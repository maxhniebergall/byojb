# Tucows Domains — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:domains | company_type: product

What they do: Tucows Domains is, in its own words, "the world's largest **wholesale domain
registrar**, responsible for maintaining the health, neutrality, and openness of an important — but
largely invisible — part of the Internet: the domain name system (DNS)." It is the second-largest
registrar globally and operates through the **OpenSRS**, **Enom**, **Ascio** and **Hover** brands,
supplying thousands of website builders, hosting companies and other resellers. It is one business
unit of Tucows Inc. (NASDAQ: TCX), an American-Canadian publicly traded Internet services company
whose other subsidiaries are **Ting Internet** (fiber ISP in the US) and **Wavelo**
(telecom/communications software).

How they describe themselves: "As part of Tucows — one of the world's largest Internet companies —
Tucows Domains has a rich history of helping make the Internet better." Company framing: "at heart
we are advocates of the Open Internet, on a mission to push for positive change at every level… a
team of smart, skilled people united in their belief in the power of the open Internet, working at
the foundation to make the Internet better." On working there: "We embrace a **people-first
philosophy that is rooted in respect, trust, and flexibility**. We believe that whatever works for
our employees is what works best for us. It's also why the majority of our roles are remote-first,
meaning you can work from anywhere you can connect to the Internet! Today, over one thousand people
from over 20 countries are part of our team." The careers site adds "a people-first culture built on
trust, inclusivity, and transparency", employee resource groups, and explicit commitment to
flexibility for caregiving, health and accessibility needs. Their values statement closes with:
"At Tucows we care about protecting the open Internet, narrowing the digital divide, and supporting
fairness and equality."

Size / stage / funding: **publicly traded** (Tucows Inc., NASDAQ: TCX), founded 1993 — 30+ years old.
Over one thousand employees across more than 20 countries. This is an established public company
rather than a venture-stage bet; note that Tucows Inc. as a whole carries the capital burden of the
Ting fiber build-out, so group-level financial pressure is a factor worth checking against current
filings even though the Domains unit is the mature, cash-generative part of the business.

Locations / HQ: Toronto, Ontario, Canada (Tucows Inc. HQ). Offices in Germany, Denmark, the USA and
Canada; the Enom team is largely Bellevue WA and Toronto; the customer-service team is in
St. Catharines, Ontario. Staff work remotely across the globe.

Remote policy: **remote-first**, stated plainly — "the majority of our roles are remote-first,
meaning you can work from anywhere you can connect to the Internet". They also run a **"Nomad
program"** allowing up to 90 days of work abroad per year. Most positions offer remote flexibility.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, unambiguously.** The live "Senior Backend Engineer"
posting states directly: "This is a **remote position for applicants based in Canada**." Its listed
location in the registry is simply "Canada" — no city, no province restriction — and the company is
headquartered in Toronto with a long-standing Canadian entity and payroll. The JD also asks for
"experience working in a remote, distributed team". Mountain Time is a non-issue: the company spans
20+ countries, and even the Canada-anchored teams are already split between Toronto and Bellevue
(Pacific). This is the cleanest Canada eligibility in the batch.

Engineering & tech: DNS, domain registration and registry protocols. The Senior Backend Engineer
role is a hands-on IC role that also carries technical direction across multiple systems: designing,
building and maintaining secure, scalable backend services, databases and APIs; leading architecture
and design discussions and documenting decisions; breaking down initiatives, coordinating technical
work and managing dependencies; designing APIs, database schemas, integrations and data contracts;
improving performance, security, observability and maintainability; debugging complex production
issues and leading incident response; and writing automated tests, technical documentation and CI/CD
workflows, plus mentoring. Stack: **Python or Go**, REST API frameworks such as FastAPI or Gin,
relational databases (PostgreSQL preferred) with schema design and query optimisation, HTTP/REST/
JSON/XML. Nice-to-haves reveal the environment: RabbitMQ or other message brokers, AWS/Azure/GCP,
Docker, Docker Swarm, Nomad or Kubernetes, OAuth 2.0/JWT/OpenAPI and API security, and networking,
Linux, infrastructure operations and Internet protocols such as **DNS, EPP and RDAP**. Minimum
qualifications ask for 6+ years of backend experience and experience providing technical direction
while remaining hands-on.

Compensation: Tucows publishes ranges on its Canadian postings. Senior Backend Engineer: "The base
salary range for this position is **$145,000 – $155,000 CAD** for Canadian residents. Other
countries will differ. Range may vary on a number of factors including, but not limited to:
location, experience and qualifications. Tucows believes in a total rewards offering that includes
fair compensation and generous benefits" (benefits detailed at tucows.com/careers/benefits).

Notable / other: strong open-Internet advocacy identity, explicit diversity and accommodation
commitments ("we will ensure that individuals with disabilities are provided reasonable
accommodation"), and a culture of promoting from within with in-house leadership programs,
mentorship and learning benefits. The recruiting voice is warm and low-hype — "If this sounds
exciting to you, join the herd!"

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Backend Engineer (remote, Canada). The registry also carries
AI-flavoured Tucows titles — "Senior Engineer - Artificial Intelligence" and "Intermediate Software
Engineer - Artificial Intelligence (AI)".
