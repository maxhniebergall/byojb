# Dandy — research
Provider/key: ashby:dandy | company_type: product

What they do: Dandy is a vertical-SaaS-plus-manufacturing company for dentistry. In their own words: "Dandy is transforming the massive and antiquated dental industry—an industry worth over $400B… we're on an ambitious mission to simplify and modernize every function of the dental practice through technology. As we expand our reach globally, Dandy is building the operating system for dental offices around the world—empowering clinicians and their teams with technology, innovation, and world-class support to achieve more for their practices, their people, and their patients." Practically, they run digital dental labs: intraoral scans come in, and Dandy designs and manufactures crowns, partials and other prosthetics using additive (3D printing / SLA / DLP) and subtractive (milling, grinding, polishing) processes, with software driving the pipeline.

How they describe themselves: Mission-and-scale framing — "massive and antiquated," "$400B," "ambitious mission," "Backed by some of the world's leading venture capital firms," "building the operating system for dental offices around the world." Engineering framing in the live JD is high-visibility and strategy-owning: the role is described as "the multiplying factor in our manufacturing strategy," "a high-visibility role where you will be the architect of our competitive edge," and "You'll be working on the most interesting manufacturing problems in the industry."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, late-stage growth ("some of the world's leading venture capital firms"), several hundred to low thousands of employees when lab/manufacturing staff are counted. HQ New York City.

Locations / HQ: New York City HQ, with named US manufacturing sites in **Salt Lake City, Dallas and Indianapolis**. No Canadian site appears in their materials.

Remote policy: The live posting is tagged "Anywhere - Remote," but that is qualified inside the JD itself: "Open to Travel: Flexibility to travel frequently (up to 30%), balancing on-site project needs at our Salt Lake City, Dallas and Indianapolis sites." So it is a remote role tethered to frequent US on-site work.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, and effectively problematic.** The location tag says "Anywhere - Remote" but no Canadian entity or Canadian benefits are mentioned anywhere, the comp is posted in USD, and the job requires up to 30% travel to three US manufacturing sites. For a Canadian citizen without US work authorization, regular on-site work at US production facilities is a real friction point, and nothing on the posting indicates they employ Canadians. Treat as not verified Canada-eligible.

Engineering & tech: For this role — C++ and TypeScript; Three.js, WebGL, WebGPU, WGSL; WASM/Emscripten and React; ROS, custom 6DoF control stacks and proprietary toolpath engines; computational geometry and mesh processing with CGAL / Open CASCADE. The work is toolpath generation, 5-axis CNC and 6DoF robotic control, kinematics, path planning, collision avoidance and toolpath simulation for hybrid additive/subtractive manufacturing.

Compensation: The Senior Software Engineer II, Toolpath Algorithms posting states US$198.4K – US$248K.

Notable / other: The role is explicitly greenfield: "This role is about 0 to 1 development of novel toolpath and robotic control mechanisms as well as yield boosting optimizations to existing toolpath infra," and they want "A '0 to 1' mindset. You thrive in startup environments and enjoy defining the roadmap rather than just following one."

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer II, Toolpath Algorithms. (The registry also lists a non-engineering "Fulfillment Supervisor" req on this board — Dandy posts lab-operations roles alongside engineering.)
