# Ping Identity — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:pingidentity | company_type: product

What they do: Enterprise identity and access management (IAM) — a cloud identity platform covering
authentication, SSO, authorization, identity governance/provisioning (IDM), and, more recently,
"identity for AI agents." Customers are large enterprises; the company states it serves "more than
half of the Fortune 100." Sold B2B, mission-critical security infrastructure.

How they describe themselves: The recurring framing across every JD is "digital freedom" —
"we believe in making digital experiences both secure and seamless for all users, without
compromise… And it's not just something we provide our customers. It's something that inspires our
company. People don't come here to join a culture that's built on digital freedom. They come to
cultivate it." Their values page (`championing-every-identity.html`) centres on diversity, equity
and inclusion — "we champion every identity" — and names **Respect Individuality** as a core value
("celebrate differences so you are empowered to bring your authentic self to work"), alongside a
stated culture of belonging for employees ("Identians") and an ESG framework covering
"environmentally sustainable business practices, impactful social change, and responsible and
ethical corporate governance." The full formal value list was not published on the pages reachable
here — only Respect Individuality is quotable verbatim, so treat the rest as unverified.
Tone is corporate-enterprise, not startup-hustle: no "fast-paced", "wear many hats" or
rocketship language appears anywhere in the nine live JDs.

Size / stage / funding: Private. Headquartered in Denver, Colorado, with "offices and employees
around the globe." Ownership/headcount are not stated on the pages fetched; widely reported to be
private-equity owned (Thoma Bravo) and combined with ForgeRock, but that is **unverified from
first-party sources here** — do not treat it as confirmed.

Locations / HQ: HQ Denver, CO. Live postings anchor to: USA (remote), UK (remote), Canada —
Vancouver BC, and JDs reference distributed team members "in the Portland, OR metro area, Canada,
and Europe."

Remote policy: Genuinely remote-friendly for some geos — 6 of 9 live relevant postings are
explicitly "USA - Remote" or "UK - Remote." No company-wide remote/async policy statement was
found on the pages fetched.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — not verified, and the evidence points the other way.** Both
Canadian postings are titled "Canada - Vancouver, BC" with no "Remote" qualifier, in contrast to
the same board's explicit "USA - Remote" / "UK - Remote" labels. The company clearly knows how to
label a remote role and did not do so for the Canadian ones. The US-remote and UK-remote roles are
presumably restricted to those countries' entities. Conclusion: Vancouver-anchored, and Kimberley
BC is a 9-hour drive from Vancouver — effectively disqualifying unless Ping confirms otherwise.

Engineering & tech: AWS-primary multi-account estate managed with AWS Control Tower and Terraform,
with "a growing focus on GCP as we build out that practice"; Kubernetes-era SRE work described in
detail — deployment tooling, fault-injection/chaos harnesses, capacity planning, observability
pipelines, "identifying toil and automating it away." Product engineering covers API development,
identity protocol implementation, authentication systems, security-vulnerability mitigation, and
"performance optimizations at a large scale." They run an integrated DevOps model: "Development and
Operations teams are integrated, running continuous deployments daily." A Platform Engineering team
"is focused on building Ping's identity for AI offerings to secure and govern AI agents."

Compensation transparency: Unusually good — they post statutory ranges under both Colorado's Equal
Pay for Equal Work Act and Canada's pay-transparency rules. See `data/company-comp/` rows.
Observed: Senior SWE (CO) USD 110,000–137,000; Staff SWE IDM (CO) USD ~132,000–165,000; Senior
Staff SWE IDM (CO) USD ~151,000–185,000; SRE II (Canada) CAD 87,000–105,000; SWE I (Canada)
CAD 70,505–88,131. Note the CO ranges for the Staff/Senior Staff roles contain an obvious typo in
the source ("$132,00", "$151,00") — read as $132,000 and $151,000.

Notable / other: The Canadian bands are conspicuously low relative to the US ones for adjacent
levels — a Canada-based hire here should expect materially less than a US-remote peer.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Site Reliability Engineer (UK - Remote); Site Reliability
Engineer II (Canada - Vancouver, BC); Senior Staff Software Engineer, IDM Engineering (USA -
Remote); Staff Software Engineer, IDM Engineering (USA - Remote); Senior DevOps Engineer (UK -
Remote); Senior Software Engineer (USA - Remote / UK - Remote).
