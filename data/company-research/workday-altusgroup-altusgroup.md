# Altus Group — research
Provider/key: workday:altusgroup/altusgroup | company_type: product

What they do: Altus Group is a commercial real estate (CRE) software, data and analytics company, best known for the **ARGUS** product family — now the "ARGUS Intelligence Platform" — used for valuation, asset management and investment analysis of commercial property. Their own framing describes "one of the most sophisticated commercial real estate data ecosystems in the industry", serving "institutional investors, lenders, owners, and operators" globally, where model outputs "directly support high-value, real-world financial and investment decisions." Publicly traded Canadian company; alongside software they historically run a CRE advisory/valuation services business.

How they describe themselves: Culture language, verbatim from their own posting: "Our high trust, high performance culture prioritizes progressive programs that empower you to deliver your greatest performance while promoting collective success where everyone is recognized and connected." They advertise "boundless opportunities in a rapidly growing global business: mentorship from the best and brightest in CRE, access to our Altus Intelligence Academy, and the chance to work with the best data sets, tools and technology in the industry", and "an industry that is transitioning for greatness." They describe the engineering environment as "a culture that values curiosity, experimentation, continuous learning, and pragmatic problem-solving", and the target person as someone who "enjoys operating in environments where ambiguity exists, priorities evolve quickly, and innovation happens through rapid iteration and experimentation." On inclusion: "Altus Group is committed to fostering an inclusive work environment where all clients and employees feel welcomed, accepted and valued."

Size / stage / funding: Large, established, publicly listed Canadian company (TSX). Mature revenue base; not venture-funded or runway-dependent. Global operations.

Locations / HQ: Toronto, Ontario (downtown Toronto office), with global offices.

Remote policy: They describe a "Flexible work model: our Activity-Based Work model provides flexibility to align your work location to the needs of the work — use the office for collaboration and remote work for focused tasks." In practice the Lead AI Engineer req is explicit: "This is a remote-first role **based in Toronto**, with the opportunity to collaborate in person at our downtown Toronto office for team sessions, innovation workshops, and key collaboration moments approximately 1–2 times per month."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — Canadian, but Toronto-anchored.** The role is typed "remote-first" yet explicitly "based in Toronto" with an expected in-person cadence of 1–2 times per month, and the published interview process includes "An in-person interview in Toronto with the hiring manager" plus a final on-site-style technical presentation and panel. Eastern Time. For a Kimberley BC candidate who cannot relocate, monthly Toronto attendance and an in-person interview round make this effectively disqualifying, not merely inconvenient.

Engineering & tech: The Lead AI Engineer reports to the Principal Architect and covers "LLM-powered applications, agentic workflows, data-driven analytics, and domain-specific AI models" across the ARGUS Intelligence Platform — "conversational AI experiences for asset valuation, agentic workflows, intelligent data extraction, and AI-driven analytics capabilities." Named technical areas: "transformers, prompt engineering, RAG architectures, embeddings, semantic search, fine-tuning, agentic systems, distributed training, or model optimization", plus large-scale SaaS platforms, AI infrastructure and cloud environments. The role also includes establishing "standards, frameworks, and best practices for AI and machine learning development… including experimentation frameworks, model deployment approaches, governance, testing strategies, and operational reliability", mentoring engineers, influencing build-vs-buy, and "guiding outsourced engineering resources." Level expectation: "Experience operating at a Staff Engineer, Principal Engineer, Technical Lead, or similar level is strongly valued."

Notable / other: Compensation is posted: **$200,000 – $230,000 CAD base**, with the disclaimer that "the range does not include additional compensation such as bonuses, equity, benefits, or other incentives." Benefits framing: "competitive compensation, incentive and bonus plans, and a total rewards package prioritizing mental, physical, and financial well-being", and "over 150,000 hours of learning content" via the Altus Intelligence Academy. Hiring process is unusually heavy: a recorded BrightHire screen, an in-person Toronto hiring-manager interview, a virtual technical/leadership panel, and a take-home technical assignment presented to a panel. They disclose that "AI tools may be used to support the initial stages of screening for this role" and that background checks (employment, education, criminal, sometimes credit) may follow a conditional offer.

Open relevant roles (sample): Lead AI Engineer (Toronto, Ontario).
