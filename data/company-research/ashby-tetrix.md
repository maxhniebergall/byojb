# Tetrix — research
Provider/key: ashby:tetrix | company_type: product
What they do: Tetrix builds an "AI-native" data and analytics platform for institutional investors in private/alternative markets — they describe it as "Bloomberg, but for private markets." The product does automated document retrieval from dozens of private LP/GP portals, AI-powered extraction and normalization of messy PDFs/Excels/reports, and on-demand portfolio analytics. Customers are described as "some of the world's leading capital allocators."
How they describe themselves: "A fast-growing startup on a mission to empower institutional investors to confidently invest in alternative markets." "We are a small but mighty startup with deep industry background and expertise in both financial markets and modern technologies. We've worked hard to build a culture of trust, high ownership, and curiosity—where everyone feels empowered to contribute, challenge ideas, and grow together." The candidate profile they publish is the fullest statement of their values: "proactive and hands-on – someone who takes initiative, figures things out, and keeps things moving"; "you learn and adapt quickly … resourceful, adaptable and excited by tough challenges"; "you're customer-oriented … you keep customers front of mind"; "you're collaborative – someone who values transparency, good communication." JDs also say explicitly: "We're not looking for someone to just take tickets—we want you in the room helping us shape product direction and tech strategy," and "You'll work with clients – from pitching…".
Size / stage / funding: Small ("small but mighty"), founded Summer 2023 by two Stanford MBA co-founders, Olivier (CEO) and Naunidh (CTO). Backed by Innovation Endeavors (Eric Schmidt's VC firm) plus angels from BlackRock, CPPIB, SoftBank, Lending Club and Plaid. Exact headcount, round and runway are NOT stated on any source available here — unverified.
Locations / HQ: All five live postings are tagged "New York City, Montreal". No corporate website was verifiable from the registry (the only URL on record is the Ashby board), so HQ is inferred from the JDs only.
Remote policy: Not stated anywhere in the available material. The postings name two specific cities rather than "Remote", which reads as onsite/hybrid in NYC or Montreal — unverified either way.
Remote-Canada eligibility: UNCLEAR, leaning NO. They clearly can employ in Canada (Montreal is a listed location), but nothing indicates they hire remote outside those two metros, and nothing indicates BC/Mountain-Time support. No evidence found for remote-anywhere-in-Canada.
Engineering & tech: Python-first backend (both backend roles say "Strong Python skills are required" / "asynchronous Python"). Work described: agentic AI workflows for automated document retrieval, a proprietary extraction/normalization engine over complex PDFs/Excels, intelligent validation with full audit trails, event-driven processing systems, scalable APIs, data ingestion pipelines, LLM agents and evals. They ask for "5+ years building production-grade AI systems, particularly around LLMs, agents, evals, or data extraction/normalization pipelines," and give bonus points for open-source contributions. No public engineering blog or handbook was found.
Notable / other: The pay is posted openly and is high for the stage. Benefits stated on every posting: equity, 401k matching, healthcare insurance, unlimited PTO. Note the 401k reference implies the US entity is the primary employer.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior AI/ML Engineer; Senior Backend Engineer (Python); Staff AI/ML Engineer; Staff Backend Engineer (Python); Senior Infra/DevOps Engineer (no longer live).
