# Arctic Wolf — research
Provider/key: workday:arcticwolf/external | company_type: product

What they do: Arctic Wolf is a cybersecurity company providing cloud-native security operations — Managed Detection and Response, Managed Risk and Managed Cloud Monitoring — "each delivered through the industry's original concierge security team." Their stated mission is to "END CYBER RISK." They operate a security operations centre model on top of a large-scale telemetry and detection platform.

How they describe themselves: Four stated values, in their own words: Security — "Our first responsibility is to our customers and their security"; People — "We delight our customers, deliver value to our partners, and work to foster a diverse and inclusive team"; Innovation — "We focus on technology and solutions that secure customers today and in the future"; Transparency — "We share information, ideas, feedback, and concerns." Their culture shorthand is "We Run as a Pack," with the principles "Stronger Together," "Own The Outcome" and "Lead The Way," promoted with hashtags (#RunWithThePack, #OwnTheOutcome, #LeadTheWay, #EndCyberRisk). Their recruiting copy describes "a global team... made up of people who thrive on solving hard problems, moving fast, and building technology that protects organizations around the world."

Size / stage / funding: Large, late-stage private cybersecurity company — roughly 2,000+ employees, heavily venture-funded at multi-billion-dollar valuations, widely expected to be IPO-track. Not an early startup, but growth-stage and funding-driven rather than demonstrably profitable.

Locations / HQ: Global headquarters in Eden Prairie, Minnesota (8939 Columbine Rd). Additional offices in San Antonio TX, Waterloo ON (Canada), Pleasant Grove UT, and Temple Terrace FL. India presence as well (Haryana appears on remote requisitions). Waterloo is the main Canadian engineering site.

Remote policy: Not stated on the careers page. Evidence comes from the requisitions themselves, which are mixed: some are explicitly remote ("Remote - United States", "Remote - IND - Haryana"), while the Canadian engineering requisitions are anchored to the Waterloo, Ontario office in the Workday URL path.

There is also a VP, Endpoint Platform Engineering requisition (Remote - United States). No public engineering blog or handbook surfaced.
