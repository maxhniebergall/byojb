# FirstPrinciples — research
Provider/key: ashby:firstprinciples | company_type: product

**Entity confirmation:** this is FirstPrinciples the AI-for-science research organization (firstprinciples.com, formerly firstprinciples.org, "Theo, the AI Physicist") — **not** FirstPrinciples.io, the unrelated software development agency of a similar name. Confirmed by matching the Ashby board slug and the "Theo" product line named in the live JDs.

What they do: "FirstPrinciples is a research company building AI for scientific discovery. It began with **Theo, the AI Physicist**, and has since grown into a product-focused company with two additional systems including **Theo Conjecture**, built for automated conjecturing, and **Theo Collaborator**, an adaptive environment for doing complex research with AI." Theo is described as a modular reasoning system of five specialized models, 120B+ parameters, trained on 3M+ scientific papers, built to support development of original theoretical frameworks in physics with auditable reasoning and open scientific collaboration.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "Understand the nature of reality by advancing AI-driven discovery in fundamental science." Three stated principles: **scientific advancement** (rigorous, original research contributions), **shared understanding** — treating knowledge "as a public good" rather than proprietary advantage — and **responsible AI infrastructure** built with the scientific community. From the JD: "We're a **fast-growing, remote-first team** of builders, researchers, engineers, and thinkers working across Canada, the US, the UK, and expanding globally. What brings us together is a shared curiosity about how the universe works… We spend our time working on questions that don't have clear answers… This is work that sits somewhere between creativity and rigorous thinking, and often requires **comfort with ambiguity and iteration**. If you're someone who enjoys tackling big, abstract problems and exploring ideas that don't yet have a defined path forward, you'll likely find the work here interesting."

Size / stage / funding: Not disclosed on their own site — no headcount, no funding figures. Described as "an independent, global research company" and self-described as fast-growing. Reporting indicates it operates as a **global nonprofit structure with a Canadian foundation and a US-based 501(c)(3)** (the FirstPrinciples Foundation), alongside the research company. Funding model and financial runway are therefore opaque, and philanthropic/grant-funded rather than revenue- or VC-driven.

Locations / HQ: No offices listed. Team distributed across **Canada, the US and the UK**, expanding globally. Leadership presence in the Greater Toronto Area.

Remote policy: **Remote-first**, stated directly in the JDs. All four relevant live postings are remote.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** All relevant postings are listed as "**Ontario, Canada – Remote**", and the team is described as working across Canada. The company clearly employs Canadians. The one caveat is that the postings name **Ontario** specifically rather than Canada broadly — whether British Columbia is equally acceptable is unconfirmed and should be asked. Given a fully distributed team spanning Canada/US/UK, Mountain Time is workable, though a UK-spanning team implies some early-morning overlap.

Engineering & tech: A mix of research and engineering. The Member of Technical Staff, Engineering role covers "the design and coding of the AI Physicist, a **closed-loop research engine**" — tooling for hypothesis generation, peer review, physics-based simulation, validation against experimental data and self-revision; writing "safe, testable code" with automated unit tests; integrating SOTA tools into existing platforms; adding "self-improvement features that pass automated checks before deployment"; and "track runtime metrics and suggest optimizations that keep the project efficient and sustainable." The AI & HPC Infrastructure Engineer role is the clearest infrastructure position — high-performance computing and AI infrastructure for large-model training and inference. Roles reference leveraging gen-AI tooling directly.

Notable / other: The "knowledge as a public good" and open scientific collaboration stance is a genuine differentiator, and pairs with the nonprofit foundation structure. Job titles use the research-lab convention "Member of Technical Staff" rather than conventional engineering ladders, which makes levelling and scope hard to read from the outside.

Open relevant roles (sample): AI & HPC Infrastructure Engineer (Ontario, Canada – Remote); Member of Technical Staff, Engineering (Ontario, Canada – Remote); Member of Technical Staff, Research (Ontario, Canada – Remote); Member of Technical Staff, Staff Physicist (Ontario, Canada – Remote).
