# TailorCare — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:tailorcare2023 | company_type: product
What they do: TailorCare is a value-based musculoskeletal (MSK) care platform combining clinical expertise, digital tools and what it calls a "yield-based operating model". They are rolling out "Operating Model 3.0", a shift from uniform care delivery to yield-based alignment where each patient receives investment proportional to a real-time Yield Score (Probability x Value x Addressability), with an "ROI Gate" continuously calibrating "Wallet" (financial investment) and "Muscle" (operational intensity).

Engineering & tech: Healthcare data platform architecture end-to-end — ingestion, event bus, identity resolution (deterministic + probabilistic, Datavant-style tokenization), lakehouse (Snowflake / Databricks / BigQuery), feature store, ROI Gate decisioning, action orchestration, observability, event taxonomy, schema registry and data contracts. Heavy healthcare interoperability: X12 EDI (270/271/276/277/278/834/835/837), FHIR R4, HL7 v2 (ADT), CCD/C-CDA, NCPDP; integration with payers, EHRs, clearinghouses and HIEs. Managed Kafka or equivalent, dbt-style orchestration. ML/AI systems: feature stores, point-in-time correctness, model lifecycle, NLP on clinical text, conversational AI in contact centres. HIPAA/PHI governance, field-level access control, BAA scope, training-data lineage, consent chains. Preferred exposure to claims datasets (Komodo, Truveta, HealthVerity, Optum, IQVIA) and clinical data networks (Health Gorilla, Particle).
Notable / other: The role reports to the Director, Data and is explicitly "a hybrid strategy-and-architecture role" requiring 10+ years of healthcare data experience including 4+ in architecture/strategy leadership, plus "demonstrated executive presence". Year-one deliverables are specific and aggressive: canonical patient master service in production with >=95% deterministic match rate, real-time event bus with >=10 production event types, real-time ADT/eligibility/prior-auth integrations with sub-second response, ROI Gate v1 routing >=80% of newly-identified patients.
