# Nomic Bio — research
Provider/key: ashby:nomic | company_type: product

**Entity note / possible registry confusion:** the registry records this company only as "nomic". The Ashby board `jobs.ashbyhq.com/nomic` belongs to **Nomic Bio** (formerly nplex biosciences), a Montreal **proteomics/bioengineering** company. It is **not** Nomic AI, the Delaware/NYC company behind the `nomic-embed` embedding models and Atlas — a much more plausible target given this profile's ML-infrastructure focus. Anyone reading the registry name alone would likely assume the wrong company.

What they do: Bioengineering / life-sciences tooling. Nomic is "working to make the human proteome as broadly and easily accessible as the human genome," built around its proprietary **nELISA®** platform — "an end-to-end technology that delivers high-throughput, quantitative, and affordable protein data at unprecedented scale." With the launch of **Omni 1000** they say they "are making the $50 proteome a reality, breaking down barriers that have limited the field for decades. For the first time, large-scale, quantitative proteomics is accessible to any scientist, supporting everything from drug discovery and translational research to AI-enabled therapeutic development." Revenue comes from running protein assays for pharma/biotech customers, not from selling software.

How they describe themselves: Mission-forward and scientific in tone. They highlight partnerships "with leaders in pharma, biotech, and academia—including **GlaxoSmithKline** and the **Broad Institute**"—and that they "have profiled more than 500,000 samples across diverse applications." On capacity: "Our state-of-the-art facility is capable of profiling over **2.5 million samples a year, generating 500 million protein assays**." The engineering roles are framed as sitting "at the intersection of our lab technology-development efforts, our work to improve our data-processing algorithms and infrastructure, and our manual analysis of complex experimental datasets" — building internal tools that let their own scientists visualize and analyze datasets more automatically.

Size / stage / funding: **Series B of $42M** recently closed; "over $60 million in investment from top-tier backers" in total. Y Combinator alumnus. Small — likely well under 150 people, a large share of them wet-lab scientists rather than engineers. Capital-intensive (they operate physical lab facilities) and venture-stage.

Locations / HQ: **Headquartered in Montreal, Quebec**, with labs and offices in **Boston**. Postings are bilingual (English/French), consistent with a Quebec employer.

Remote policy: Mixed and not clearly documented. Third-party profiles variously describe hybrid arrangements, an "OnSite Workspace," and "the majority of the team is based in Montreal" working under "an in-person, but flexible work-from-home, policy." The Ashby postings themselves are more permissive than that, listing remote options. Given a lab-anchored business, the centre of gravity is physically in Montreal.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes, formally.** Both live engineering postings list Canada-inclusive remote scope: "Remote (Canada or US)" and "Remote (Canada or US), Remote (Canada), Greater Boston, Remote (United States), Montreal". Timezone is Eastern-anchored (Montreal/Boston), a workable 2-hour offset from Mountain. Caveat from their own JD text: "**Fluency in English is required as our customers and vendors are primarily located in the USA. In addition, this position will interact with our team members within our USA entity.**" Note also the tension between the postings' remote scope and the third-party reports of an in-person Montreal default — worth verifying directly.

Engineering & tech: Data-processing algorithms and infrastructure for high-throughput assay data, plus internal analysis and visualization tooling for scientists. The roles are posted at combined "Mid to Sr Levels" and blend data science with data engineering — scientific-computing and bioinformatics-flavoured rather than distributed-systems or platform work. Stack is not publicly detailed; Python is the near-certain default for this domain.

Notable / other: Roles are advertised through Y Combinator's Work at a Startup as well as Ashby. Neither live posting states a compensation range. The combined "Mid to Sr" banding suggests a small, still-forming engineering ladder.

Open relevant roles (sample): Data Scientist/Data Engineer (Mid to Sr Levels) — Remote (Canada or US); Software Engineer (Mid to Sr Levels) — Remote (Canada or US) / Montreal / Greater Boston.
