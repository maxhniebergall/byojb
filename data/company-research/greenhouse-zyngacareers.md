# Zynga — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:zyngacareers | company_type: product
What they do: Zynga is a mobile/social games publisher and a label of Take-Two Interactive (public, NASDAQ: TTWO). Its own words: "a global leader in interactive entertainment... our games have been downloaded over 6 billion times — connecting players in 175+ countries." Portfolio spans casino/slots, puzzle, strategy and word games: FarmVille, Words With Friends, Zynga Poker, Game of Thrones Slots Casino, Wizard of Oz Slots, Hit it Rich! Slots, Wonka Slots, Top Eleven, Toon Blast, Empires & Puzzles, Merge Dragons!, CSR Racing, Harry Potter: Puzzles & Spells, Match Factory, Color Block Jam.
How they describe themselves: "At Zynga, we bring people together through the power of play." Recruiting tone is upbeat and gamified — "Level Up Your Career with Zynga!", "where fun meets innovation — and where you can take your career to the next level", "Join us and be part of the play!" The engineering JD adds: "Our team encourages critical problem solvers who enjoy fast-paced change and innovation" and asks engineers to "be a thought leader for the team".
Size / stage / funding: Founded 2007; public via parent Take-Two Interactive. Large (thousands of employees across North America, Europe and Asia); not runway-dependent.
Locations / HQ: Headquartered in California; "teams span North America, Europe, and Asia". The Canadian roles in the registry are attached to a Toronto office; other postings list Austin TX, Bay Area CA and Chicago IL alongside Toronto.
Remote policy: Not stated in any of the JDs held locally, and no company page was fetched to confirm. Postings are enumerated by named office cities (Austin / Bay Area / Chicago / Toronto) rather than as "Remote", which reads as office-anchored, but this is inference, not a stated policy.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, and the Canadian roles are Toronto-anchored.** Zynga clearly employs in Canada (Ontario pay-transparency ranges are quoted on the JDs, so there is an Ontario employing entity), but every Canadian posting names Toronto as the location and none states remote or work-from-anywhere. There is no evidence of eligibility for a fully remote worker in British Columbia on Mountain Time.
Engineering & tech: The Toronto Senior Software Developer role sits on the Data Ingestion team within the Analytics organization — SDKs and near-real-time ingestion pipelines feeding reporting, BI, player engagement, ML and user acquisition; "millions of requests per minute and terabytes of data per day". Stack: RESTful backend services in Java, Go and/or Python; SQL and NoSQL, Redshift a plus; AWS highly preferred; CI/CD and DevOps practices a plus. Other open engineering work spans payments/FinOps backend and game-studio DevOps.
Notable / other: Ontario pay ranges are posted on the JDs (see the comp record). Benefits are described at https://www.zynga.com/jobs/careers/#benefits (cited from the JD; not fetched). Some roles are studio-specific (Gram Games, Wizard of Oz Slots).
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Developer (Data Ingestion, Toronto); Principal Software Developer - FinOps (Austin/Toronto); Principal Software Developer - Payments; Sr DevOps Developer - Wizard of Oz Slots.
