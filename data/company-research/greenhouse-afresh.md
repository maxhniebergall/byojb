# Afresh — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:afresh | company_type: product

What they do: Afresh builds AI-driven inventory and ordering software for the fresh and perishable sections of grocery retail — produce, meat, seafood, deli, bakery, and the distribution centres behind them. Fresh categories are hard to forecast because of short shelf lives, shifting demand and poor data visibility, so Afresh's models drive ordering, merchandising, store-operations and supply-chain decisions. It is live in more than 12,500 departments across 40 US states, with customers including Albertsons Companies, Meijer and Wakefern. Claimed outcomes include up to 25% shrink reduction, a 3% sales lift and a 7% improvement in inventory turns.

How they describe themselves: Mission framing is food waste and sustainability — fresher food, stronger margins, less waste — combined with the climate angle (the most recent round was co-led by a climate fund). Careers/JD material describes itself as early-stage in its own words, offering "meaningful early-stage equity".

Size / stage / funding: Private venture-backed. Raised $34M in April 2026, co-led by Just Climate and High Sage Ventures, on reported 70% year-over-year revenue growth in 2025. Growth-stage but still explicitly self-described as early-stage in compensation framing; not stated to be profitable.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco, California.

Remote policy: Remote-friendly but jurisdiction-restricted, and the restriction is stated in a hard list. From their own JD boilerplate: "Here at Afresh, many of our employees work remotely provided that they reside in one of the following states: AL, AR, CA, CO, FL, GA, IL, KY, MA, MI, MT, MO, NV, NJ, NY, NC, OR, PA, TX, WA, UT, VA, WI."

Remote-Canada eligibility: LIMITED AND PROVINCE-SPECIFIC — effectively NO for British Columbia. Afresh does employ in Canada: one live posting is location "Remote - Ontario, Canada" and quotes a CAD salary range, so an Ontario entity or PEO clearly exists. But the posting is scoped to Ontario, and the company's own remote-eligibility boilerplate lists US states exclusively with no Canadian provinces at all. There is no evidence Afresh can employ a remote worker resident in British Columbia. This should be treated as disqualifying unless directly confirmed otherwise with a recruiter. The parallel US posting for the same role is "Remote - United States".

Engineering & tech: Applied ML and data engineering for demand forecasting and replenishment, plus an ML Platform team and distribution-centre systems. Registry titles include Senior Data Engineer, Software Engineer ML Platform, Staff Software Engineer ML Platform, and Staff Software Engineer Data / Tech Lead (Distribution Center). The ML-platform work is genuine infrastructure; the tech-lead framing on the DC role implies leadership scope alongside it.

Compensation (see data/company-comp/): Afresh posts ranges under pay-transparency rules on both sides of the border for the same role. The Canadian posting of Staff Software Engineer Data / Tech Lead (Distribution Center) states "Salary Range in CAD: $148K - $222K". The US posting of the same role states "Salary Range in US: $168K - $253K + meaningful early-stage equity + benefits". Benefits described as "Competitive base salary, meaningful equity (U.S. employees), and a 401(k) program with a generous company match" — note that the equity and 401(k) language is explicitly scoped to US employees, which suggests the Canadian employment package is thinner.

Notable / other: The climate/food-waste mission is substantive rather than decorative and is reflected in the investor base. The domain (grocery replenishment) is unglamorous and operationally critical. Counterweight: a $34M round at 12,500 departments is a growth-stage venture posture, and the JD's own "early-stage equity" phrasing signals the company still frames itself that way.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer Data / Tech Lead (Distribution Center) — Remote Ontario, Canada; the same role — Remote United States; Senior Data Engineer; Software Engineer, ML Platform; Staff Software Engineer, ML Platform.
