# ServiceCore — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:servicecore | company_type: product

What they do: ServiceCore is vertical SaaS for the portable-sanitation and waste-rental trades — portable toilet, roll-off dumpster and septic businesses. The product is field-service operations software: customer and job management, scheduling and route optimization, inventory/asset tracking for units in the field, billing and accounting, and a mobile app for drivers. Customers are small and mid-sized US service operators, many moving off spreadsheets and paper for the first time.

How they describe themselves: They market as "The #1 Portable Toilet, Septic & Dumpster Business Software" and, on careers, "Work For The #1 Software Company In Portable Sanitation." Their differentiator claim is domain familiarity: they say they hire more people out of the portable sanitation and dumpster industries than any other software company in the space, on the reasoning that people who understand the business give better training and support. Culture copy is small-company and informal — "a great place to be yourself, have fun, and grow your skillset," with the company investing in bringing remote and local employees together virtually and in person twice a year.

Size / stage / funding: Small private company — roughly the tens-of-employees scale — headquartered in Colorado (Fort Collins). No public funding or ownership disclosure was found; there is no press coverage of large financing rounds, which is consistent with a bootstrapped or modestly-backed niche SaaS business rather than a venture rocket. That means the "boring, mission-critical domain" quality is real, but so is thin engineering depth.

Locations / HQ: Fort Collins, Colorado. A mix of local and remote employees, with twice-yearly in-person gatherings.

Remote policy: Remote-friendly with a Colorado office anchor. Postings are typed by US geography — their other current engineering opening (Senior Software Engineer – Mobile) is advertised as "United States Remote / Colorado Remote."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Almost certainly not, and unverified as anything better.** Every ServiceCore engineering posting located in this research is typed to the United States or Colorado; no Canadian requisition, Canadian entity, or non-US hiring statement was found anywhere. The BambooHR posting itself returns HTTP 403 to automated fetching and the company careers/about pages did not render for automated retrieval, so this could not be confirmed from a first-party statement — but the balance of evidence is a US-only employer. For a candidate who cannot relocate from British Columbia, this should be treated as disqualifying until a recruiter says otherwise.

Engineering & tech: Not published. No engineering blog, handbook or public stack information was found, and the one relevant JD could not be retrieved. Known from the product surface: a web application plus a driver-facing mobile app, scheduling/routing logic, and billing/accounting integrations. Engineering team size is small enough that a "Senior Software Engineer" here should be assumed to be a generalist across the whole product.

Notable / other: Available compensation figures come only from aggregators (Glassdoor, Indeed, Himalayas, Salary.com) and are wildly inconsistent with each other — one puts ServiceCore senior engineers around $209K, another around $119K, and a remote-median around $145K USD. These are not company statements and are not reliable enough to record as a band. No first-party pay disclosure was found, notably not even a Colorado pay-transparency range, despite Colorado law generally requiring one on postings.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer — ServiceCore; Senior Software Engineer – Mobile (United States Remote).
