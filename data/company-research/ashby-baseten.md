# Baseten — research
Provider/key: ashby:baseten | company_type: product

What they do: Baseten runs an "inference cloud" — a platform for deploying and serving AI/ML models in production. Products span dedicated inference deployments, hosted Model APIs, training, and cloud / self-hosted / hybrid deployment topologies. Customers are companies embedding specialised models into products (their public materials lean on latency-sensitive workloads such as voice AI and LLM serving). This is infrastructure-as-product: GPU scheduling, kernels, inference runtimes, networking and observability are the actual roadmap, not an afterthought.

How they describe themselves: Their mission framing is that "inference will be the largest market ever created" and that their job is to make inference "as reliable, fast, cost-effective, and high-quality as possible" so that millions of specialised models can be embedded into products. Culturally they say they are "customer obsessed", that "the bar is high", and that the team "work[s] hard, move[s] fast, and care[s] deeply about quality". They describe themselves as "an interdisciplinary team of researchers, engineers, and operators" attacking "a hard systems problem that requires first-principles thinking across the entire stack", with engineers owning features end to end and minimal bureaucracy.

Size / stage / funding: Hyper-growth private company. Raised a $300M Series E at a ~$5B valuation in January 2026, then a $1.5B Series F at up to a $13B valuation announced 22 June 2026 (led by Altimeter, Conviction and Spark, co-led by Sands Capital and Wellington) — roughly a 160% valuation increase in five months. Headcount reported around 300+ and the company has publicly stated it plans to triple headcount this year across engineering, research, operations and commercial.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco. Their live postings list San Francisco, New York, Toronto and Montreal as hiring locations, with a small number additionally tagged "Remote".

Remote policy: Not stated on the about page. Their own job postings are the best evidence and they are location-listed rather than remote-first: most roles enumerate San Francisco / Toronto / New York / Montreal, and only a few (Cloud Platform Engineer, Site Reliability Engineer, Software Engineer - Infrastructure) add "Remote" to that list. This reads as an office-hub company with selective remote, not an async/handbook culture.

Remote-Canada eligibility: Verified that they employ in Canada — Toronto and Montreal are standing hiring locations on essentially every engineering posting, so a Canadian employment entity exists. NOT verified for British Columbia or Mountain Time: no posting lists a Canadian location other than Toronto/Montreal, and the handful of "Remote" roles do not state which countries or timezones qualify. A Toronto- or Montreal-anchored role would be effectively disqualifying for a Kimberley BC candidate; the "Remote"-tagged infra/SRE roles are the only plausible route and need confirmation.

Engineering & tech: Inference-stack systems work: GPU kernels, model-performance and inference-runtime engineering, GPU networking and distributed systems, GPU fabric observability, training infrastructure, an enterprise/cloud platform layer, and SRE. Public engineering blog with deep dives on inference optimisation, GPU utilisation and model-serving architecture. They also run a "forward deployed engineering" function (customer-embedded), which is the one customer-facing pocket of the org.

Notable / other: Pay is posted on every JD (see below) and is unusually transparent for a private company — bands run $165K–$330K with equity for most engineering roles, $180K–$360K for model-performance/kernels/model-products, and $200K–$380K for GPU fabric observability. Currency is not stated on the postings but the SF anchor implies USD.

Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineer - Infrastructure; Cloud Platform Engineer; Site Reliability Engineer; Software Engineer — GPU Networking & Distributed Systems; Software Engineer - Model Performance; Software Engineer - Training Infrastructure; Software Engineer - Enterprise Platform; Software Engineer - GPU Kernels.
