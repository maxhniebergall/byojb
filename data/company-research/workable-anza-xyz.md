# Anza — research
Provider/key: workable:anza-xyz | company_type: product

What they do: Anza is a **Solana R&D lab / core protocol engineering firm**. In their own words:
*"Anza is a Solana R&D lab pushing the boundaries of blockchain performance and scalability. Anza
was founded by experienced executives and core engineers solving the toughest problems in Web3."*
Their site describes the company as *"the leading Solana-focused software development firm,
building resilient, elegant, and impactful protocols."* Anza maintains the **Agave validator
client** — one of the primary Solana validator implementations — spun out of the original Solana
Labs engineering group.

How they describe themselves: Open-source and multi-contributor by conviction: *"Crypto ecosystems
rely on robust protocols, and we believe those are best built out in the open, with multiple
contributors. We pioneer advanced solutions to meet the evolving demands of decentralized
applications."* The careers framing is candid about pace: *"We offer a dynamic, fast-paced
environment where innovation and problem-solving are at the heart of everything we do."*

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture/ecosystem-backed, small and senior-heavy (founded by
"experienced executives and core engineers"). Headcount and funding are not disclosed on their own
site. Economically tied to the health of the Solana ecosystem rather than to conventional
enterprise revenue.

Locations / HQ: New York, United States is the location on every live posting. No office list is
published on the site.

Remote policy: **Remote.** Every live posting carries `Workplace: remote` and the location string
"New York, United States (Remote)".

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, and the evidence leans US-only.** The postings are remote but
anchored to a US location, and the compensation clause is scoped explicitly to US candidates: *"The
salary range for US-based candidates will be determined throughout the interview process depending
on experience, skill, and location."* No Canadian entity, no Canadian range, and no country list
appears anywhere. A crypto R&D lab may well hire contractors internationally, but nothing here
affirms Canadian employment — do not treat this as Canada-eligible without asking.

Engineering & tech: This is genuinely deep systems infrastructure work — arguably the deepest in
this batch. Named engineering areas on the site: **validator infrastructure (Agave client),
consensus protocols (Alpenglow), Solana program frameworks (Pinocchio), developer tooling (Codama,
Solana Kit SDK), and on-chain program optimization**. The Block Production team *"owns the core
pipeline between networking and the runtime, responsible for processing ingested transactions and
coordinating block-production and verification."* Responsibilities include throughput and stability
optimization, scheduling mechanisms for fair resource allocation and predictable performance,
security hardening of the pipeline, scalability under rising transaction volume and validator
count, stress testing / fault injection / performance benchmarking, and *"high-performance
shared-memory interfaces between scheduling and execution stages, enabling low-latency coordination
and efficient data movement across the validator pipeline."* Stack: **Rust, C, C++**, distributed
systems, Linux, systems automation. Requirements ask 3+ years hands-on with core infrastructure
software and distributed systems — a modest bar for the depth of the work. Documentation and peer
code review are explicit responsibilities.

Notable / other: Work is open-source and publicly visible, which is good for portfolio and
credibility. Active participation in Bitcoin/Ethereum/blockchain or open-source projects is a
listed preferred qualification — an ecosystem-insider hiring filter.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Block Production; Senior Software Engineer,
Consensus; Senior Software Engineer, Generalist; Software Engineer, Security.

Pay (stated on their own JD): *"Candidates should anticipate an annual base salary (not including
incentive compensation or other benefits) of $180,000 USD to $300,000 USD"* — scoped to US-based
candidates. Very strong for the level; likely supplemented with token/equity incentives.
