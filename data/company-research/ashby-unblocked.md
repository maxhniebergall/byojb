# Unblocked — research
Provider/key: ashby:unblocked | company_type: product

What they do: Unblocked builds an AI "context engine" for software teams — a developer tool that answers questions engineers have about their own codebase and applications, so teams spend less time in meetings and interruptions and more time shipping. It ingests structured and unstructured data across a team's code and knowledge sources and serves answers through LLM pipelines. Customers are engineering teams; the product is developer tooling, not an end-user application.

How they describe themselves: "A high-growth, well-funded SaaS company." "We're a small team with a track record of building developer tools that solve real problems." They explicitly invite participation "in the entire product development process." Their stated cultural criteria (verbatim from both live JDs) are: **"Driven with a bias for action: You prototype in hours, not weeks. You ship, watch users, and iterate. You're a maker, not a deliberator"** (the Senior SWE variant reads "Anxious to design, build, and ship solutions on aggressive timelines"); **"AI-native: Modern AI developer tools are muscle memory for you, not novelty"**; **"Able to play well with others"** — collaborative, good communicator, "humble enough to actively solicit and incorporate feedback"; **"A passionate, tireless, selfless advocate for the end-user"**; **"Different"** — "unique perspective, skill set, or way of working that up-levels an already strong team… willing to work outside your comfort zone"; and **"An ambitious malcontent: Dissatisfied with the status quo in the world around you."** Tone is high-energy, maker/shipping-oriented, startup-forward.
