# Glydways — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:glydways | company_type: product
What they do: Glydways builds an autonomous public-transit system: fleets of standardized small autonomous vehicles running on dedicated narrow guideways, orchestrated by AI, offering on-demand personalized trips 24/7. In their words: "Glydways is reimagining what public transit can be. We believe that mobility is the gateway to opportunity—connecting people to housing, education, employment, commerce, and care." They claim the system moves 10,000 people per hour, costs ~90% less to build than conventional transit modes and operates at ~30% the cost of alternatives, "without the burden of heavy upfront infrastructure costs or ongoing taxpayer subsidies."
How they describe themselves: Mission: "to revolutionize transit with a solution that delivers high capacity, exceptional user experiences, unmatched affordability, and minimal environmental impact." Stated principles on their site: access to mobility equals access to opportunity; transit should be universally accessible; quality transit reduces cars on streets; transit must be financially sustainable. Closing line of their own boilerplate: "we're building more than a transportation system; we're creating a future where everyone, everywhere, has the freedom to move." Investor Vinod Khosla is quoted on their site: "Only Glydways style public transit can eliminate congestion and be better than self-driving cars."
Size / stage / funding: Series C — an oversubscribed $170M round closed April 2026. Investors include Khosla Ventures, Apollo Global, Suzuki and ENEOS. Headcount is not disclosed. Well-funded but capital-intensive and pre-revenue-at-scale: this is infrastructure construction, not SaaS.
Locations / HQ: Richmond, California (one requisition is explicitly Richmond, CA, and a "Compute Platform Engineer" role is tagged Hybrid). Deployment projects are launching in Atlanta, Contra Costa County, San Jose and Abu Dhabi from 2026.
Remote policy: Mixed. Some engineering requisitions are posted "Remote" (Data Platform Engineering Lead, Software Engineer - XIL Platform, Autonomy Integration Software Engineer) while others are onsite Richmond CA or explicitly Hybrid (Compute Platform Engineer). Robotics/vehicle work naturally pulls toward the hardware.
Remote-Canada eligibility: UNVERIFIED, likely no. The remote requisitions state no eligible-country list, and no JD or site page mentions Canada. All deployments and offices named are US or UAE, the company is Richmond CA-headquartered, and an autonomous-vehicle company with export-controlled/safety-critical work typically restricts hiring to the US. Treat as US-remote unless confirmed otherwise.
Engineering & tech: The Data Platform team sits inside the Autonomy Software organization and is a mix of applied data scientists and data engineers "responsible for building robust data pipelines to keep tabs on our robot software," running an in-house data platform with cloud-native workflows. Stack named in the JD: Spark, Hadoop, Hive, dbt; Airflow or Argo Workflows for orchestration; Bazel or CMake multi-language builds; Docker and Kubernetes; AWS/Azure/GCP; Python, Shell, SQL; Git; YAML and CUE configuration. Other roles cover autonomy integration, an XIL (hardware/software-in-the-loop) simulation platform, and onboard vehicle integration.
Notable / other: The Data Platform Engineering Lead is a hands-on lead with people responsibility — owning the technical roadmap, mentoring analytics and data engineers, partnering with engineering leadership on planning, prioritization and headcount, and making build-vs-buy calls. Data quality, validation, governance and observability across the data lifecycle are called out explicitly.
Open relevant roles (sample): Data Platform Engineering Lead [Remote]; Software Engineer - XIL Platform [Remote]; Software Engineer, Onboard Integration [Richmond, CA]; Compute Platform Engineer [Hybrid]; Autonomy Integration Software Engineer.
