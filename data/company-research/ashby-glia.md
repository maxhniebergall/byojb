# Glia — research
Provider/key: ashby:glia | company_type: product
What they do: Describes itself as "the #1 Banking AI platform", selling to community and regional banks and credit unions. Its "Banking AI Operating System" is pitched as "a central intelligence layer on top of existing tech stacks, activating an AI workforce of specialized agents that draw from banking data, interaction history, and integrated systems of record", automating front- and back-office workflows across voice and digital ("the Universal Banker model"). Originally a digital customer-service / co-browsing company (SaleMove) that has repositioned around banking AI. "Trusted by 700+ banks and credit unions."
How they describe themselves: Ironclad-security framing plus "the industry's first contractual no-hallucination guarantee". Their values are the **PACE** framework, verbatim from their site: **Proactive** — "Think ahead, act now"; **Adaptable** — "Embrace change as fuel for growth"; **Curious** — "Master our craft and relentlessly seek new knowledge"; **Excellent** — "Deliver high-quality work with urgency and impact". They publish Great Place To Work survey numbers: 89% say it is a great place to work (vs a 57% US baseline), 99% feel welcome, 95% feel trusted with responsibility, 94% view management as competent. Note "urgency" is baked into a stated value.
Size / stage / funding: Founded 2012; private, venture-backed (it has raised a large late-stage round historically and reached unicorn valuation, though I could not re-verify the figures first-party this session — treat funding specifics as unverified). Employees "representing 35 countries". 36 open roles at scan time — a substantial hiring posture.
Locations / HQ: **HQ New York City**; engineering is heavily Estonia-centred with offices in **Tallinn and Tartu**. Distributed across 35 countries.
Remote policy: The Infrastructure team's own words: "We are a **remote-first** team distributed across Portugal, Poland, Estonia, and **Canada**, working across **EET and EST timezones**. While we embrace remote collaboration, we have offices in Tallinn and Tartu for those who prefer them. We also gather the full team in Estonia twice a year to innovate and connect in person."
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Mixed — company yes, these roles no.** Glia demonstrably employs people in Canada (the Infrastructure team itself includes Canada). But all three relevant open roles are posted as "**Estonia - Remote**", and the team's operating timezones are EET and EST. EET is UTC+2/+3 — 9–10 hours ahead of Mountain Time; even the EST end of that range is 2 hours ahead. A BC-based hire would be at the far edge of an Estonia-anchored team, and the postings themselves are location-restricted to Estonia. Twice-yearly travel to Estonia is expected. Worth a direct question if ever pursued, but do not score these postings as Canada-eligible.
Engineering & tech: Published stack, verbatim from the Infrastructure JD — Infrastructure: AWS, Kubernetes (EKS), Istio, APISIX. Persistence: Amazon RDS for PostgreSQL, Apache Druid, Snowflake. Cache: ElastiCache for Redis. Event streaming: Apache Kafka. Message broker: RabbitMQ. Monitoring: DataDog. CI/CD: Jenkins, GitHub Actions. IaC: Terraform, Ansible. Languages: Golang, Python. The Infrastructure team's charter is core reliability and security hardening, CI/CD and telemetry for developer enablement, and third-party vendor integrations — "bridge the gap between high-velocity development and rock-solid reliability". They ask for "proficiency in using AI tools thoughtfully, maintaining ownership of the final output while recognizing the tools' limitations" — a notably measured framing.
Notable / other: Hiring process published: introduction → practical and team interviews → decision and offer; recruiting only from @glia.com and @gliatalent.com addresses. Only 2 of 6 relevant roles are remote-flagged in the registry.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Infrastructure Engineer, Platform (Estonia - Remote); Senior Software Engineer, CI/CD & Developer Tooling (Estonia - Remote); Senior Software Engineer, SRE / Observability Tooling (Estonia - Remote).
Pay: **no compensation stated on any posting**, and no first-party pay page found. Estonia-scoped roles would be paid on a European scale that is not comparable to the North American slots anyway — no band written rather than manufacture a misleading number.
