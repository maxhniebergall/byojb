# Fingerprint — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:fingerprint | company_type: product
What they do: Fingerprint (formerly FingerprintJS) builds device-identification and fraud-detection APIs for developers. Its identification API gives applications a stable visitor identifier used to stop account takeover, payment fraud, bot traffic and multi-accounting. Customers range from solo developers to publicly traded companies. Its flagship open-source project, FingerprintJS, has ~27K GitHub stars.
How they describe themselves: "Fingerprint empowers developers to stop online fraud at the source." Four stated values: "No Nonsense" (direct communication), "Fail. Learn. Grow" (encouraging calculated risks), "Don't take yourself too seriously" (authentic culture), and "Be level 5 helpful" (going the extra mile for peers and customers). Tone is engineer-to-engineer and understated — developer-tools framing, not enterprise hype.
Size / stage / funding: 200+ employees. Has raised $77M total (a $32M Series B is highlighted on the careers page), backed by Craft Ventures, Nexus Venture Partners and Uncorrelated Ventures (prior investors in Redis, MinIO, Postman, Druva, Gradle).
Locations / HQ: Globally dispersed with no meaningful HQ concentration; the careers page calls out Toronto, London and Delhi as team locations.
Remote policy: "We are a globally dispersed, 100% remote company." Careers page: "100% remote, global team" that "works asynchronously and respects time-zone differences," with flexibility on where and when people work. Annual company retreats; MacBook provided (kept after 4 years); flexible time off with minimum vacation targets.
Remote-Canada eligibility: Verified yes (strong). The company is 100% remote globally, explicitly names Toronto as a team location, and the live Senior Backend Engineer, Core APIs posting is listed simply as "Remote". Async-by-policy also removes the Mountain-Time overlap risk.
Engineering & tech: Backend/API-centric — fraud-detection services and "Core APIs" team. Strong open-source culture (FingerprintJS and the fingerprintjs org); engineers are given freedom to work on open-source projects. $600/yr learning budget.
Notable / other: Publishes an anti-recruiting-scam notice on its JDs (all recruiting mail from @fingerprint.com). Product is developer-first with public docs and SDKs.
