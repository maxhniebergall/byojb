# Hims & Hers — research
Provider/key: ashby:hims-and-hers | company_type: product

What they do: Hims & Hers is a direct-to-consumer telehealth and wellness company — online consultation, prescription and fulfilment for personalised health and wellness treatments, shipped to the customer. Their own words: "Hims & Hers is the leading health and wellness platform, on a mission to help the world feel great through the power of better health. We are redefining healthcare by putting the customer first and delivering access to care that is affordable, accessible, and personal, from diagnosis to treatment to delivery. No two people are the same, so we provide access to personalized care designed for results. By normalizing health & wellness challenges and innovating on their solutions, we're making better health outcomes easier to achieve."

How they describe themselves: The recurring self-description is customer-first, personalisation, and destigmatising ("normalizing health & wellness challenges"). They describe their employment model as a "talent-first flexible/remote work approach". Beyond that, the full values list could not be retrieved — their careers page (www.hims.com/careers-professionals, the URL their own JDs cite) returned HTTP 403 to an automated fetch this session, so the stated value set is UNVERIFIED and deliberately not paraphrased here.

Size / stage / funding: Public company — "Hims & Hers is a public company, traded on the NYSE under the ticker symbol 'HIMS.'" That is a meaningful stability signal relative to the venture-stage companies in this batch: audited financials, no runway question. Exact headcount not verified this session.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco, California (general knowledge — unverified this session). The registry shows engineering roles uniformly as "US Remote", plus one physical operations site in Canada: a Pharmacy Fulfillment Associate role in North York, Ontario. They also operate pharmacy/fulfilment facilities in the US.

Remote policy: Engineering is remote — every engineering posting in the registry is tagged "US Remote", and their JDs describe a "talent-first flexible/remote work approach". Operations/pharmacy roles are naturally site-based. The specifics of the flexible-work policy could not be read (careers page 403).

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO for engineering. All 12 engineering postings are "US Remote"; a full-text scan of every fetched Hims & Hers JD body returned zero occurrences of "Canada"/"Canadian". The one Canadian posting is "Pharmacy Fulfillment Associate" in North York, Ontario — a physical warehouse/pharmacy role, not remote and not engineering. So Hims & Hers demonstrably HAS a Canadian operating presence (which means a Canadian employing entity plausibly exists), but it employs no Canadian engineers and advertises none.

Engineering & tech: Unusually well documented in the JDs. The data platform runs on "GCP BigQuery, Airflow on Astronomer/EKS, dbt, Confluent Kafka, Databricks Delta Lake, and Terraform/OpenTofu", plus Fivetran and Hightouch (reverse ETL). A CDC-based streaming platform (Kafka to Flink to BigQuery) is being built net-new, alongside a lower-environments strategy from scratch. Governance is formalised: an Architecture Review Committee (ARC), architecture decision records, RFCs, solution design docs, data contracts, Schema Registry governance, dbt anomaly detection, schema validation, data drift alerting, production-readiness criteria, blameless RCAs. Teams visible: Data Platform Engineering (DPE), Patient Platform (Backend), Subscription and Membership Platform, Growth, Customer Retention, AI & Recommendations Platform, ML Systems (MLOps), and an "AI-Assisted Engineering Practices" team. Scale claim: "Our platform serves millions of patients across telehealth, prescription, and wellness products."

Notable / other: Pay is posted and strong: Senior Platform Engineer $140-165K USD base; Senior SWE (App) $175-200K; Staff SWE (Growth) $220-250K; Staff ML/MLOps roughly $199-247.5K. The Principal Data Engineer scope is genuinely senior-IC — chairing the ARC, org-wide architectural direction, cost governance of BigQuery slot reservations, and platform-level P1/P2 incident escalation. Being a regulated healthcare business (prescriptions, patient data) means compliance and privacy constraints are real engineering work.

Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Data Engineer (US Remote); Staff Data Engineer (US Remote); Sr. Staff Engineer (Backend) (US Remote); Sr. Platform Engineer (US Remote); Staff Machine Learning Systems Engineer (MLOps) (US Remote); Staff Software Engineer, AI & Recommendations Platform (US Remote); Software Engineer II, Patient Platform (Backend) (US Remote); Pharmacy Fulfillment Associate (North York, Ontario, Canada).
