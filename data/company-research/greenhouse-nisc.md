# NISC (National Information Solutions Cooperative) — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:nisc | company_type: product

What they do: NISC builds and implements enterprise and customer-facing software for **utilities and broadband/telecom providers across North America** — billing, CIS, accounting, operations, GIS and engineering analysis. Their own words: "For more than 50 years, NISC has developed and implemented enterprise-level and customer-facing software solutions for over 960+ utilities and broadbands across North America… Our Members have over 18 million end customers (residential and businesses who receive power, internet, television and/or telephone services)." The role in scope sits on **DEW (Distributed Engineering Workstation)** — an integrated platform for electric-system engineers and operators to aggregate and analyse grid data, with power-flow algorithms used to assess grid status and plan future load for renewables and EV adoption.

How they describe themselves: NISC is a **cooperative — owned by the Members it serves**, and it leans on that hard: "It also means that our focus is on taking care of our Members and our employees, rather than having a big bottom line. Quality service and innovative technology start with happy and dedicated employees." Their careers page calls the company "a bit of a unicorn in the technology industry" because of the co-op model, and emphasises "a superior work/life balance". Mission: "to deliver technology solutions and services that are Member-focused, quality driven and valued priced." Full stated Shared Values (their wording; the careers page notes they were developed by employees themselves):
- **Integrity** — "We are committed to doing the right thing – always."
- **Relationships** — "We are committed to building and preserving lasting relationships."
- **Innovation** — "We promote the spirit of creativity and champion new ideas."
- **Teamwork** — "We exemplify the cooperative spirit by working together."
- **Empowerment** — "We believe individuals have the power to make a difference."
- **Personal Development** — "We believe the free exchange of knowledge and information is absolutely necessary to the success of each individual and the organization."
Tone is notably free of hustle/rocketship language.

Size / stage / funding: Private member-owned cooperative, 50+ years old, not venture-funded and not runway-dependent. Serves 960+ member utilities/broadbands. Exact headcount not stated on any page fetched (**unverified**; publicly it is on the order of a thousand-plus employees, but that is not corroborated here). Ranked in ComputerWorld's "Best Places to Work" for twenty-two years, per their own JD.

Locations / HQ: US offices named on the live JD — **Blacksburg VA; Cedar Rapids IA; Lake Saint Louis MO; Mandan ND**. The careers page separately mentions a Lake St. Louis office (mailing-services team). No Canadian office is mentioned anywhere, despite "North America" market language (their members include Canadian utilities, but employment locations listed are all US).

Remote policy: **Hybrid, office-anchored, and specific.** The JD states: "Hybrid from the following office location" (the four above), "Hybrid Schedule: Minimum of working 3 days per week out of an office location and ability to work up to all 5 days a week from an office location", with "Required Days from an Office Location: Tuesday and Wednesday — the third required day will be up to the candidate and their supervisor to choose."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — verified from the JD.** Although the posting's location field reads "Any NISC Location or Virtual", the body overrides that with a mandatory 3-day-per-week in-office schedule tied to one of four US offices, two of them fixed weekdays. There is no Canadian office and no statement of hiring outside the US. Note the data-quality wrinkle: the ATS location string "Any NISC Location or Virtual" is misleading relative to the JD body.

Engineering & tech: **C++ and C#** primary, plus Java/other OO languages; responsive **desktop** applications for electrical-engineering use; relational databases (PostgreSQL, SQL Server, Oracle); asynchronous workflows; Git with branching/merging; Atlassian toolchain (Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket, Bamboo). Agile development teams working cross-functionally with product, QA and support. Domain depth wanted: power-flow algorithms, optimization algorithms, iterative methods for nonlinear systems, GIS and network modelling, distributed/data-intensive systems. The role asks for **8+ years** experience despite the "Developer III" title. Keyword footer also lists Kubernetes, Docker, AWS Cloud, Spring Boot and microservices, which suggests a broader platform elsewhere in the org.

Notable / other: Benefits are unusually detailed and stable-employer flavoured — medical/dental/vision (zero-premium medical per the careers page), HSA with $100/month employer contribution plus up to $800/yr wellness rewards, dependent-care FSA, fully covered life insurance at 3× base salary, fully covered short- and long-term disability, 401(k) traditional or Roth with 6% match **plus** a 4% employer base contribution, PTO scaling with service, 120 "Life Leave Event" hours, a paid holiday week and 9 paid holidays, sabbatical options (careers page), a $2,500 interest-free technology loan, a $25,000 educational-assistance program, an Employee Assistance Program, and a Benevolence Committee for employees facing financial hardship. DEW has been featured in an academic journal (nisc.coop blog).

Compensation: **No band on record and none found.** The live JD states no salary range (Virginia, Iowa, Missouri and North Dakota are not pay-transparency jurisdictions), and no company pay page was found. No row written — see below.

Open relevant roles (sample): Software Developer III — Distributed Engineering Workstation (Power Flow Software); Software Developer III — DEW/Electrical Engineering Software (C#, Big Data).
