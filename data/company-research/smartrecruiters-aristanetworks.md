# Arista Networks — research
Provider/key: smartrecruiters:aristanetworks | company_type: product
What they do: Arista builds cloud/data-center networking hardware (Ethernet switches, routers) and the software that runs them. Its core software product is EOS (Extensible Operating System), a Linux-based network operating system that runs across the entire switch line, plus CloudVision, a SaaS network-management/telemetry product. Customers are hyperscalers, large enterprises and AI/data-center operators.
How they describe themselves: The JDs frame the work as owning projects "from definition to deployment", being "responsible for the quality of everything you deliver", and describe the EOS team as "a fast-paced, high caliber team-building features to run the world's largest data center networks." The Layer1 posting states Arista "prides itself on a supportive and growth oriented culture" and that engineers "will have the opportunity to switch from Layer1 to other areas" as interests change. Note: the full corporate values page was not fetched (WebSearch budget exhausted this session), so the values quoted above are only those stated inside their own live JDs.
Size / stage / funding: Public company (NYSE: ANET), long-established and profitable. Exact current headcount not verified this session.
Locations / HQ: HQ in Santa Clara, California (not re-verified this session). Live engineering postings in the registry span Vancouver BC (Canada), Dublin/Ireland, Poland, Romania, Greece, Spain, Hungary and Sydney, Australia.
Remote policy: Multiple engineering roles are posted explicitly as remote — the Canadian postings carry the location string "Vancouver, BC, ca (remote)" and the EMEA ones are posted as country-level remote ("Poland - Remote", "Ireland, Greece, Spain, Hungary or Romania").
Remote-Canada eligibility: VERIFIED YES. Five+ live senior/principal engineering postings are listed as "Vancouver, BC, Canada, Remote", and each states a base pay range "across Canada", which is a direct statement that the role is a Canadian employment relationship rather than a US one. Caveat not resolvable from the JD text: the postings name Vancouver specifically and do not say whether a candidate elsewhere in BC / Mountain Time is acceptable — that needs to be asked. Time-zone-wise the team anchor appears to be Pacific/Santa Clara, which is Mountain-Time-workable.
Engineering & tech: Deep systems work — C, C++ and Python; Linux kernel development (including upstream mainline contribution); PCIe driver and link bring-up work; Layer1/optics control software; packet-forwarding-engine and ASIC-adjacent programming; L2/L3 routing protocols (BGP, OSPF, IS-IS, PIM, spanning tree); distributed-systems infrastructure (messaging, signalling, databases). Engineers write functional specs, design specs and test plans, build their own automated tests, and mentor juniors. There is a recurring expectation of working with Customer Support Engineers to triage problems found in customer networks, and of reporting a weekly schedule to a project lead.
Notable / other: Requirements are unusually credential-heavy (e.g. "BS + 8 years", "BS + 12 years" for Expert/Principal). Compensation is stated openly on the Canadian JDs. Roles are eligible for discretionary bonus and equity, plus medical/dental/vision, income protection and a Group Retirement Savings Plan.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior/Lead Software Engineer (EOS) — Vancouver BC remote; Expert/Principal Software Engineer — Vancouver BC remote; Senior Software Engineer, Linux Kernel — Vancouver BC remote; PCIe Senior/Lead Software Engineer — Vancouver BC remote; Senior/Lead Software Engineer, Layer1 — Vancouver BC remote; Senior/Lead Software Engineer, Network Systems; Site Reliability Engineer (SRE/DevOps), Engineering Productivity — Dublin/Poland remote; Software Engineer - SONiC.
