# iFIT — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:ifit | company_type: product
What they do: Connected-fitness company (legal entity "iFIT Inc.") selling fitness equipment — treadmills, bikes, rowers — plus subscription software and interactive content. Their stated vision: "to create the world's most holistic health and fitness platform, integrating all elements of health - physical fitness, mental health, nutrition and active recovery - into a seamless interactive experience. We develop proprietary software that learns and adjusts to the habits of each person as it delivers immersive content that guides them on their own individual fitness journey." Public-facing tagline on their site: "Redefining Fitness".
How they describe themselves: The JD's self-description is aspirational and pace-oriented: they are "currently seeking an ambitious pace-setter", and state "iFIT is an AI-first engineering company — we expect our engineers to build with AI, not just around it, and a senior engineer here helps set that bar for the team." Role commitments are written as product-mission statements ("so no one ever sees a generic, one-size-fits-all experience again"). Their careers landing page (fetched) carries essentially no culture or values content beyond the tagline.
Size / stage / funding: Not stated on any source read. Private. Headcount, funding and profitability unverified from first-party sources in this session.
Locations / HQ: Not stated on the careers page or the JD. The site footer indicates consumer operations in at least Australia, the United Kingdom and the United States. (iFIT is commonly associated with Logan, Utah — UNVERIFIED here.)
Remote policy: Remote within the US. The JD says the role is "remotely in the US" and includes an explicit state-eligibility list.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — definitively. The JD's own disclaimer enumerates the jurisdictions they will hire in and they are all US states: "Current list of states where iFIT may consider hiring: AK, AL, AR, AZ, CA, CO, CT, FL, GA, ID, IL, IN, KS, KY, LA, MA, MD, MI, MN, MO, MS, NC, NH, NJ, NV, NY, OH, OK, OR, PA, RI, SC, SD, TN, TX, UT, VA, WA, WI, WY." Canada does not appear, and the same clause warns "work in a particular state is not guaranteed". Employment is stated as at-will, a US construct.
Engineering & tech: The Data Services team "builds the backend systems and data pipelines behind the core entities that power Fitness Journeys across our mobile apps and connected equipment" — workout telemetry from treadmills, bikes, rowers and the app is "captured, stored, aggregated, and delivered back as the activity history, biometrics, leaderboards, and insights". The senior role is framed as primary owner of critical domain systems: ingestion, aggregation, streaming, integrations and content delivery, plus low-latency streaming across "millions of connected machines and apps", and a re-architecture of foundational data pipelines. AI-adjacent work includes the backend for an on-equipment "AI coach" producing adaptive workout plans, and AI-generated workout summaries. Nice-to-haves name IoT/MQTT and real-time low-latency systems, and integrations with Strava, Garmin, Apple Health, Google Fit and Terra.
Notable / other: Pay posted on the JD ($130,000-$160,000 USD) with the disclaimer that "Compensation may vary based on the job level, your geographic work location, position incentive plan, and exemption status." This is the lowest posted band in this batch. Employment is explicitly at-will.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Data Platform (Remote, US).
