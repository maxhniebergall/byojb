# Pano AI — research
Provider/key: ashby:pano-ai | company_type: product

What they do: Pano AI is "the leader in AI-powered wildfire detection and intelligence, helping fire professionals detect, respond to, and contain wildfires faster and more safely." The platform combines a network of ultra-high-definition 360-degree cameras mounted on high vantage points with satellite imagery, AI detection and other data sources to give fire agencies real-time situational awareness and confirmed ignition locations. Customers are government agencies, utilities, insurers and private landowners. They monitor **more than 50 million acres** across the United States, Australia and **Canada**.

How they describe themselves: The problem framing is climate-and-public-safety, not hype: "Every minute matters in wildfire response… Today, many wildfires are first reported by members of the public, meaning it can take valuable time to detect a fire, confirm its location and size, and mobilize responders." Their about page lists five stated values:
- **Impact** — scaling solutions for emergency managers
- **Service** — supporting clients and teammates
- **Trust** — earning reliability through integrity and quality
- **Excellence and Speed** — delivering precision work with urgency
- **Innovation** — applying cutting-edge technology
Mission framing on the site: "support the dedicated professionals in the utility and fire industries in their efforts to protect lives, property, and livelihoods." The engineering JD adds: "At Pano, we strongly believe in allowing team members to take ownership of what they do, and our approach to problem-solving relies heavily upon creativity, communication, and collaboration", and describes wanting "somebody with the wisdom and experience to build mature operational processes for the future but also comfortable with rolling up their sleeves, writing code, and building systems in a startup environment."
