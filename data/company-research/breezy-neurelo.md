# Neurelo — research
Provider/key: breezy:neurelo | company_type: product

What they do: Neurelo is a developer-infrastructure company for working with data. In its own words: "We are on a mission to disrupt the modern technology stack for developers when working with data. With Neurelo, developers can build their software faster and better with an elegant experience, run and scale them with predictable performance, and have total control throughout their software life cycle journey." And: "We are defining and shaping the modern workflow for developers, enabling them to enjoy working with data and data sources from within their applications." In practice this is an API/data-access layer over databases (auto-generated APIs, schema/data modelling, observability) — a developer-tools/data-platform product.

How they describe themselves: "Backed by leading investors, Neurelo is growing rapidly and looking to expand our team of disruptors, doers, and dreamers!" The role wants someone who "can challenge the status quo, thinks big picture", is "a champion of building things that are highly reliable, performant and extremely scalable", "loves to code", and "can effectively champion and evangelize ideas across all facets of software development" — including representing "the company, architecture, and the product vision internally and externally, at all levels". It closes with "High energy and the ability to work collaboratively in a fast-paced environment" and "a can-do attitude and strong execution focus". Tone is classic early-stage disruptor framing.
