# SS&C Technologies — research
Provider/key: workday:ssctech/ssctechnologies | company_type: product

What they do: SS&C is a financial-services and healthcare technology company — fund administration and accounting software, transfer agency, insurance and healthcare claims platforms, and (via the Blue Prism acquisition) intelligent process automation. It sells software plus large-scale outsourced operations to asset managers, insurers and healthcare payers. In their own words: *"a leading financial services and healthcare technology company based on revenue… headquartered in Windsor, Connecticut, and has 27,000+ employees in 35 countries. Some 20,000 financial services and healthcare organizations… rely on SS&C for expertise, scale, and technology."*

How they describe themselves (their own JD boilerplate, quoted): *"SS&C prides itself on hiring diverse, honest, dynamic individuals, who value collaboration, accountability, integrity, and innovation… We are a **fast-paced environment**, who promote flexibility, authenticity and offer a **hybrid working model**."* Elsewhere in the same JD: *"You will be working in a **high-priority, results-driven culture**."* Perks listed are conventional-enterprise (RRSP matching, hybrid + business-casual, SS&C Learning Institute, fitness/travel discounts).

Size / stage / funding: Public (NASDAQ: SSNC), 27,000+ employees in 35 countries per their own copy. Highly acquisitive (Advent, Intralinks, DST, Blue Prism). Stable and profitable; not runway-dependent. Note that a large fraction of headcount is operations/BPO rather than engineering — the software business sits alongside a services business.

Locations / HQ: HQ Windsor, Connecticut. Live relevant postings: **Toronto ON (3), Union NJ (2), San Francisco CA, Kansas City MO, London UK, Hyderabad and Pune India, Navi Mumbai India, plus "Remote — Texas US", "Remote — New York US" and "Remote — Poland" designations.**

Remote policy: Hybrid is the stated default (*"offer a hybrid working model"*). Some postings do carry explicit "Remote — <state/country>" locations, but they are **state- or country-scoped remote** (Texas US, New York US, Poland), and at least one so-labelled req contradicts itself in the body ("Remote — Texas, US" posting whose body states *"Locations: Kansas City, MO"*).

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No.** The three Canadian roles are all **Toronto, Ontario, hybrid** (the Canadian JDs specify the hybrid model and require you to be *"legally eligible to work at the location(s) specified above"*). The remote-designated roles are scoped to US states or Poland, not Canada. Toronto hybrid is Eastern Time and requires office presence — effectively disqualifying from Kimberley BC.

Engineering & tech: Broad enterprise stack — C++ and Progress ABL on the Toronto fund-accounting products, Java/AWS, .NET, Azure cloud engineering, SRE/L3 support, DevOps, and a Generative AI Developer req (Remote — Poland). The Blue Prism / Chorus intelligent-automation line accounts for several of the US senior roles. Much of the senior work is described in terms of architectural vision and stakeholder partnership across many products, with L3-support duties attached to the SRE reqs.

Notable / other: Several req titles carry internal codes (`PA2026Q2JB011`, `PA26ITOFFQ3071`, `PA2026Q1JB050`) — enterprise requisition bookkeeping. The Toronto Senior Software Engineer, C++ JD frankly states the culture is *"high-priority, results-driven."* The presence of "L3 Support" in SRE titles indicates a support-rotation component rather than pure platform engineering.
