# Syndica — research
Provider/key: ashby:syndica | company_type: product

What they do: In their own words, "Syndica supplies the most critical applications in Web3 with enterprise-grade RPC infrastructure and developer tools tailored for the Solana ecosystem." Concretely this is hosted Solana RPC nodes / node infrastructure-as-a-service sold to Web3 application teams. A second, engineering-heavy line of work is **Sig** — "a new open-source Solana validator client… using the Zig programming language", an independent reimplementation of the Rust `Agave` validator aimed at client diversity and performance.

How they describe themselves: "Joining our team means you'll be held to a high standard, technically challenged, and grow close to a group of individuals passionate about building new infrastructure technologies." They frame the mission as "the decentralization of blockchain ecosystems". No broader published values list was obtainable (see Sources). Their JD is unusually concrete about expectations over time rather than culture slogans: at three months "you have become one of our core Zig developers… taken on independent code review responsibilities"; at six months "delivering tasks through the entire SDLC… with minimal guidance, and… helping to effectively mentor new engineers"; at twelve months "you have established a cadence of predictable, on-time delivery without cutting corners."

Engineering & tech: Rust and **Zig** are the core languages; the role is explicitly "designing and implementing a high-performance Solana validator client from scratch". Stated responsibilities are systems-level: "Understanding and Communicating Rust Code: mapping and accurately communicating how the existing Rust client works"; "translating the Rust code into Zig while maintaining high-quality code, including performance and readability"; "Performance Research: iteratively analyzing and optimizing software bottlenecks in Zig while leveraging high-performance tools such as SIMD operations"; "Code Ownership: lead the implementation, benchmarking, and testing of your validator components from start to finish."
