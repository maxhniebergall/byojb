# Aledade — research
Provider/key: lever:aledade | company_type: product

What they do: In their own words, "Aledade, a public benefit corporation, exists to empower the most transformational part of our health care landscape - independent primary care." Founded 2014. They partner with independent primary-care practices, health centers and clinics, and "by creating value-based contracts across a wide variety of health plans, we aim to flip the script on the traditional fee-for-service model." They describe themselves as "the largest network of independent primary care in the country." The business is value-based care enablement: data, analytics and contracting that let small practices succeed under risk-bearing contracts. Engineering builds a web application plus large healthcare data pipelines, and increasingly an AI/agent data platform.

How they describe themselves: Mission-first and explicitly non-hustle. "If you want to help create a health care system that is good for patients, good for practices and good for society - and if you're eager to join a collaborative, inclusive and remote-first culture - you've come to the right place." On culture: "you will be part of a creative culture that is driven by a passion for tackling complex issues with respect, open-mindedness and a desire to learn," with team members "united by a shared passion for public health." Their engineering framing is notably conservative and quality-oriented: "We're looking for engineers who know that writing new code is not always the solution to a problem," and who "embrace strategies that minimize risk, leaning towards observability, alerting, metrics, high test coverage, and frequent releases that incrementally build value." That is unusually close to a utility/reliability engineering ethos.

Size / stage / funding: Private, US public benefit corporation, founded 2014, well past early stage (large national network; dozens of engineering teams referenced in the Principal JD — "guiding dozens of engineering teams", "guiding large teams (50+ engineers) across multiple product areas"). Exact headcount and current funding round not verified — aledade.com returned HTTP 403 to the fetch, so this is from JD text only.

Locations / HQ: US. Bethesda, Maryland is the historically stated HQ but this was NOT verified in this pass (site 403'd) — treat as unconfirmed. A Washington DC posting appears in the registry (Salesforce DevOps Analyst). All engineering roles are listed as "Remote, United States."

Remote policy: Explicitly "remote-first" in their own boilerplate. Benefits stated on the JD: "Flexible work schedules and the ability to work remotely are available for many roles," 21 days PTO in year one, 11 paid holidays, two paid volunteer days, 12 weeks paid parental leave for all new parents, six weeks paid sabbatical after six years of service, 401(k) with up to 4% match, stock options, health/dental/vision paid up to 80% for employees, dependents and domestic partners. The sabbatical and the parental-leave terms are strong sustainable-pace signals.

A US 401(k), a US public benefit corporation structure, and a business that exists entirely inside US Medicare/Medicaid value-based-care contracting all point the same way. Engineering & tech: AWS (deep), Kafka event streaming, Databricks, large-scale distributed systems, Docker/Kubernetes, CI/CD, SQL over large multi-table datasets, server-side languages (Java, Python, Scala, C#, C++, Go). Named platform concerns: "platform reliability, observability/OTEL, auth + networking integrations, runtime scalability, and future memory/governance platform work"; auth/authz (IAM, OAuth, API gateways, service-to-service auth); database replication and sharding; observability platforms and performance monitoring. AI/agent stack named explicitly: "Bedrock, LangGraph, LiteLLM, MCP, OpenAI APIs, Claude Code." They are building "next-generation agentic data platforms" and a "unified data platform designed for an AI-first future." Regulated-data engineering throughout (PHI, EHR/clinical data, enterprise security/compliance).

Notable / other: The AI roles are a genuine mix — the Principal roles are architectural data-platform/infrastructure work, but the Senior SWE II (AI Core Platform) JD blends real platform work (OTEL, auth, scalability) with product/applied-AI bullets ("prompt engineering strategies," "image synthesis, multimodal models, and personalized content creation," working with "Sales, Customer Success"). That JD reads like two roles merged. Note also that the Principal JD says "scaling our infrastructure for millions of users worldwide" — marketing overreach for a US-only primary-care network; discount it.
