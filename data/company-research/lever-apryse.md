# Apryse — research
Provider/key: lever:apryse | company_type: product

What they do: Document-processing SDKs and infrastructure for developers — viewing, editing, annotation, conversion and data extraction across 100+ file formats. Products include WebViewer SDK (browser), Server SDK, Mobile SDK (iOS/Android), Document Scanner and Barcode Scanner SDKs, Fluent (Office report generation), iText (PDF library), and Xodo (end-user PDF app) with Xodo Sign as the eSign product. Customers are described as "over 20,000 innovative start-ups, governments and Fortune 500 companies."

How they describe themselves: Positioning is "The only SDK built for the entire document lifecycle." They frame the value as "clarity, control and trust" in document workflows, lean hard on proprietary (non-wrapper) technology, data sovereignty via "true client-side processing", and security certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 2). They cite "25+ years of continuous innovation" inherited from the companies rolled up into the brand. A separate values/culture page was not fetched, so the full stated value set is unverified.

Size / stage / funding: Private; formed by consolidation of PDFTron, iText, Scanbot and Accusoft under the Apryse brand — a private-equity-style rollup rather than a venture rocketship. Exact headcount, funding stage and profitability were not verified from a first-party page this session.

Locations / HQ: Not stated on the homepage. Postings imply Canadian west-coast roots (PDFTron was Vancouver-based) plus European presence — one open req lists "Ghent, remote, United Kingdom, Germany" and the other lists "Ontario, remote, British Columbia, Alberta".

Remote policy: Not stated on a corporate page, but the postings themselves are listed as remote within named jurisdictions — a province/country list rather than a city, which reads as remote hiring within specific legal entities.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes — verified, and specifically British Columbia.** The live req "Senior Backend Engineer, PHP" (https://jobs.lever.co/apryse/dc331b78-f22d-4711-ae3b-ff378c405ab3) has location "Ontario, remote, British Columbia, Alberta" and posts a CAD salary range, which is strong evidence of a Canadian employing entity covering BC. This is one of the few companies in this batch that can actually employ a Kimberley, BC resident. A parallel EU/UK req exists for the same title.

Engineering & tech: The open backend role is PHP, working on the Xodo Sign eSign product — "creating innovative new features, and maintaining our existing codebase". The JD says the engineer "will be involved in all aspects of the software development process and encouraged to provide input on future projects and the company's direction", and that "a love of learning new things is a must". Core SDK products are C++/native with JS, .NET, Java and mobile bindings, though that was not verified from a fetched engineering page.

Notable / other: Product identity is genuinely developer-infrastructure (SDKs and document pipelines), which is unusual and favourable. The specific open role, however, sits on the acquired Xodo Sign SaaS rather than on the core SDK.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Backend Engineer, PHP (Ontario/BC/Alberta, remote); Senior Backend Engineer, PHP (Ghent/UK/Germany, remote).
