# Counterpart Health — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:counterparthealth | company_type: product
What they do: Counterpart Health is a wholly owned subsidiary of Clover Health Investments, Corp. (NASDAQ: CLOV). It builds Counterpart Assistant, an AI-powered physician-enablement platform that sits inside primary-care physicians' workflows to surface clinical insights, support earlier diagnosis, and drive longitudinal management of chronic conditions. Originally built for Clover's own Medicare Advantage plan (from 2018), it is now sold externally to payors, ACOs, practices and value-based-care enablers.
How they describe themselves: "At Counterpart Health, we are transforming healthcare and improving patient care with our innovative primary care tool, Counterpart Assistant. By supporting Primary Care Physicians (PCPs), we are able to deliver improved outcomes to our patients at a lower cost." Engineering expectations are stated bluntly in the JDs: "You won't just write code to spec — you'll understand the business problem, engage with stakeholders, and shape the solution"; "Exercise agency: make informed technical and product decisions on your own"; "Adopt and evangelize AI both in your own work and in the solutions you build"; "Engage directly with users and stakeholders to understand their needs — don't wait for requirements to be handed to you"; "You care about outcomes, not just output."
Size / stage / funding: Subsidiary of a publicly traded insurer (Clover Health), so financials are public and there is no runway risk in the startup sense, but Clover has a history of losses and has restructured before. Engineering hiring is shared with Clover: their own JDs state "We're hiring Software Developers across multiple teams at Clover and Counterpart Health — our recruiting team will assess your background and connect you with the best-fit opportunity."
Locations / HQ: Distributed / remote-first; Clover Health is US-headquartered (Nashville, TN). Postings are US-remote and Canada-remote.
Remote policy: Remote-first with flexibility on location. Stated perks include flexible time off, No-Meeting Fridays, monthly company holidays, a monthly cell phone and internet stipend, and office-setup reimbursement.
Remote-Canada eligibility: Verified yes, for part of the req list. "Senior Software Developer" is posted "Remote – Canada" with a CAD range, and "Software Engineer – Engineering Productivity" is posted "Remote – Canada; Remote – USA". However "Senior Machine Learning Engineer", "Senior Software Engineer" and "Senior Site Reliability Engineer" are all "Remote – USA" only, which he cannot take. No timezone requirement is stated on the Canadian roles.
Engineering & tech: Distributed systems, full-stack (they explicitly want "proficiency in one or more common languages… various databases and web frontend technology"), heavy internal AI-agent adoption ("You know how to build systems with AI agents as your partners and team members"), plus an SRE function and an engineering-productivity team.
Notable / other: Pay ranges are posted on every req under US transparency law: Senior Software Engineer $195,000–$220,000 USD; Senior Site Reliability Engineer $160,000–$208,000 USD; Senior Machine Learning Engineer $150,000–$200,000 USD; Software Engineer – Engineering Productivity $146,200–$190,000 USD; Senior Software Developer (Canada) $134,000–$175,000 CAD.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Machine Learning Engineer (Remote–USA); Senior Site Reliability Engineer (Remote–USA); Senior Software Developer (Remote–Canada); Software Engineer – Engineering Productivity (Remote–Canada/USA).
