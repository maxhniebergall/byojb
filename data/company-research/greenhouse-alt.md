# Alt — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:alt | company_type: product
What they do: Alt (alt.xyz) is a marketplace and financial-services platform for alternative assets, starting with trading cards. Their own words: "Alt is unlocking the value of alternative assets, starting with the $5B trading-card market. We let collectors buy, sell, vault, and finance their cards in one place." Core products are the Alt Vault (physical custody + digitization) and "Alt Value", a real-time pricing engine "that powers every trade, loan, and product on the platform." Expanding into watches, art and sealed wax, plus a "Vault-as-a-Service" B2B offering.
How they describe themselves: Investor-forward framing — "backed by leaders at Stripe, Coinbase, Seven Seven Six, and pro athletes like Tom Brady and Giannis Antetokounmpo." Culture language in JDs: "If you think like an owner, care about operational excellence, and want to build the infrastructure behind the largest alternative asset vault in the world, you'll thrive here"; "A seat at the table to help shape the future of Alt"; "Autonomy and ownership on projects that matter." One role is explicitly framed as being "the founding engineer of a business unit."
Size / stage / funding: Venture-backed private startup (Seven Seven Six and Stripe/Coinbase-affiliated angels named by the company). Exact stage, headcount and profitability not stated in the material available — unverified.
Locations / HQ: Not stated in the fetched material. All live postings are listed simply as "Remote". 401(k) and HSA benefits indicate a US-based employer.
Remote policy: "100% remote working environment", plus a $100/month WFH stipend, a $200/month wellness stipend, a WeWork office stipend and a flexible vacation policy.

Engineering & tech: Python, Postgres, AWS; React / React Native, JavaScript/TypeScript. Roles are explicitly full-stack ("Own the full stack: take features from technical design through polished UI"). Work includes vault-intake automation, scan orchestration, imaging/digitization pipelines, ingestion/grading/pricing workflows, and partner-facing account/billing APIs. A separate ML track exists around the Alt Value pricing model.
