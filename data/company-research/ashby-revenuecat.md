# RevenueCat — research
Provider/key: ashby:revenuecat | company_type: product
What they do: "RevenueCat removes the headaches of building and scaling in-app subscriptions." It is developer infrastructure for mobile monetization — SDKs and a backend platform for subscription purchase handling, entitlements and revenue analytics. Their own numbers: "we're in >40% of newly shipped subscription apps, we process $12B+ in annual purchase volume", customers ranging "from a solo dev in Brazil to the OpenAI mobile team".
How they describe themselves: "We're a remote-first crew of 150+, spread across 25+ countries, and guided by values we actually practice: Customer Obsession, Always Be Shipping, Own It, and Balance." Their hiring blog states the values are assessed throughout the interview process as "a strong performance predictor", and describes an "incredibly ambitious goal: to build a public company by the end of the decade."
Size / stage / funding: 150+ employees; Y Combinator S18; Series C ("Competitive equity in a fast-growing, Series C startup backed by top-tier investors, including Y Combinator"). Profitability not stated.
Locations / HQ: No HQ emphasised; distributed across 25+ countries. Comp is benchmarked to San Francisco (see below).
Remote policy: Remote-first and "Fully remote and flexible work environment", "4-5 weeks of suggested time off annually", $2,000 USD workspace setup and $1,000 USD annual learning stipend. 10-year option exercise window.
Remote-Canada eligibility: UNCLEAR, leaning workable. The company employs across 25+ countries and the hiring page does not enumerate them, so Canada is not confirmed. The specific DevOps/DevEx posting is scoped to "APAC, Americas (EST only), EMEA" — the "(EST only)" qualifier on the Americas is the real constraint: Mountain Time is UTC-7 vs EST UTC-5, a two-hour gap, so a BC-based candidate would be working shifted hours and may fall outside the intended band. Needs direct confirmation before applying.
Engineering & tech: AWS, Kubernetes, Docker, GitHub, Python. IaC is Pulumi + Python "to describe k8s services and associated infrastructure in a single place". They have built in-house: CI/CD, "automated one-push-per-commit releases with automatic canaries", feature flags, DB query approval workflows, VPN access workflows. Stated intent: "provide autonomy, build a self-serve culture that allows teams to move fast but ensures safety and best practices." Role includes on-call training within the first 3 months and leading AI/agentic tooling adoption.
Notable / other: Compensation philosophy is unusually transparent — salary bands are "benchmarked... based on similar stage companies located in San Francisco", they practice "equal pay for equal work regardless of geographical location" (no geo discounting), they include salary in the JD, and they do not negotiate offers "to preserve fairness and internal equity". The DevOps/DevEx JD posts a flat "$230K" salary (USD) plus equity.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior DevOps / DevEx Engineer; Senior Backend Engineer; Senior Software Engineer, Product; Senior Software Engineer, Agents.
