# General Motors — research
Provider/key: workday:generalmotors/careers_gm | company_type: product

What they do: General Motors is a global automaker (Chevrolet, GMC, Cadillac, Buick) that has become, in hiring terms, a large software employer. The engineering roles on this board cluster into several distinct software organisations: GM Energy (home/commercial energy management and V2H products), the software-defined-vehicle platform, autonomous-vehicle / AV cloud and ML infrastructure (much of it absorbed from Cruise), in-vehicle and connected services (billing & subscription platform, notification platform, mapping, diagnostics infrastructure), digital commerce and CMS, motorsports engineering, and a large internal AI/developer-productivity organisation. Public company (NYSE: GM).

How they describe themselves: GM's public framing is a transformation from automaker to a platform/software and electrification company. Their JDs are corporate-standard rather than values-heavy; the recurring distinguishing feature is an explicit and formalised work-arrangement statement on every posting, plus US/Canadian pay ranges.

Size / stage / funding: ~160,000+ employees globally; public, profitable, and about as far from runway risk as an employer gets. Also subject to conventional large-corporate volatility — GM has run repeated restructurings and has substantially reorganised its AV/software groups in recent years.

Locations / HQ: HQ Detroit, Michigan. Software engineering hubs appearing on this board: Warren MI, Milford MI, Austin TX, Mountain View CA, Sunnyvale CA, Concord NC (Motorsports), Roswell GA, plus **Markham, Ontario, Canada** — the "Markham Elevation Centre (MEC)", GM's Canadian technical centre. A handful of roles are posted as "Remote - United States".

Remote policy: Formalised and stated per-posting, in three categories that appear verbatim in the JD bodies: **Hybrid** — "This role is categorized as hybrid. This means the successful candidate is expected to report to [site] three times per week, at minimum"; **Onsite** — "This role is categorized as onsite" (e.g. Motorsports in Concord NC); and **Remote/Hybrid** — "This role is based remotely but if you live within a 50-mile radius of [Sunnyvale/Mountain View/Warren/Austin], you are expected to report to that location three times a week, at minimum." A small number of ML/AV roles are categorised "fully remote or hybrid". The genuinely remote postings are tagged "Remote - United States".

Remote-Canada eligibility: VERIFIED NO for a BC-based remote worker. The Canadian roles exist and are real — a dozen-plus postings at Markham, Ontario with CAD salary ranges — but each one states: "Work Arrangement: This opening is hybrid, the successful candidate is expected to report to the office - Markham Elevation Centre (MEC) three times per week, at minimum." That is a hard three-day-a-week onsite requirement in the Greater Toronto Area. Meanwhile every fully-remote posting is scoped "Remote - United States", which requires US work authorization. There is no path here for someone in Kimberley BC: the Canadian roles are Toronto-hybrid and the remote roles are US-only.

Engineering & tech: Substantial, genuinely infrastructural work. ML/AI infrastructure: ML Infrastructure Engineer (Compute), Staff ML Infra Pipeline Engineer (Embodied AI, offboard perception), AI/ML CI platform work including a Remote Build Execution (RBE) service, AI capacity and performance engineering. Platform/cloud: AV Cloud Engineering, Virtual Cloud Platform, Cloud Engineering & FinOps, Notification Platform, Billing & Subscription Platform, MBFF platform microservices, Nova cloud platform. SRE/DevOps: Hybrid Services & Reliability (SRE), Staff Software DevOps Engineer, Staff Software Architect - DevOps, DevOps & Configuration Management. Backend: Go (Golang), .NET, GM Energy backend and SRE. Data: Data Engineer, Data Plane, Diagnostics Infrastructure. AV network engineering. This is a wide, deep, well-resourced infrastructure surface.
