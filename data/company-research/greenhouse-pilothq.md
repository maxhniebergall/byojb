# Pilot.com — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:pilothq | company_type: product

What they do: Pilot (launched 2017) provides outsourced back-office finance for small businesses and
startups — bookkeeping, financial management ("CFO services") and tax. The differentiator is the
blend of software and staff: Pilot hires its finance experts as full-time U.S.-based employees, and
its platform integrates directly with the billing, banking, expense and payroll systems customers
already use. Their stated aim is "to provide our customers with the same superpowers as large
companies with full finance teams — superpowers that include having a deep understanding of financial
health of the business, the machinery to constantly improve it, and the predictive abilities to
thoughtfully plan for the future… at a fraction of the cost a large company would pay for this, by
delivering these capabilities through powerful software."

How they describe themselves: The eng-facing pitch is explicitly "a successful startup." Stated
reasons to join: "We invest in our employees' development and happiness because our employees are the
keys to our success and ensuring happy customers"; "The opportunity to join a seasoned founding team
that has led companies through two prior successful startups and acquisitions (by Oracle and
Dropbox)." Benefits listed: flexible vacation/time-off, all federal holidays observed, competitive
benefits with additional wellness benefits, 100%-paid 12-week parental leave for birthing and
non-birthing parents, 401(k). Notably they disclose "light identity verification steps, such as
briefly waving on camera and/or recording interviews" as part of "fair and transparent remote hiring,"
and note finalists may be invited onsite to the San Francisco office.

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed. Over 3,000 customers; **$170M+ raised** from Sequoia,
Index Ventures, Stripe, Bezos Expeditions and Whale Rock, with angels including Patrick and John
Collison and Drew Houston; the most recent round doubled the valuation to **$1.2B** (unicorn). No
profitability claim. (Pilot's own blog post "Meet Fintech's Newest Unicorn" is the cited source; the
round is not recent, so current stage/runway is unverified.)

Locations / HQ: San Francisco, CA. Finance staff are U.S.-based full-time employees. NYC is named as
a second high-cost pay geography.

Remote policy: Remote-friendly but **legally scoped to a short list of U.S. states.** The
Sr. Software Engineer, Infrastructure JD states as a hard requirement: "This position is only
available for remote employment in the United States in the following states: CA, GA, NY, NC, TN, TX,
and WA." Onsite finalist interviews in San Francisco are possible.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — disqualifying, verified from the JD.** Employment is restricted to
seven named U.S. states. There is no Canadian entity, no EOR provision, and no non-US hiring language
anywhere in the posting. A Canada-based worker cannot be employed.

Engineering & tech: Backend is **fully typed modern Python**; frontend is JavaScript, TypeScript and
Vue.js. Infrastructure is **Terraform**-managed, Docker containers deployed to **AWS ECS** via AWS
CodeDeploy, **Honeycomb** for observability, **Postgres** as the database. They deploy "daily or more
often." The Infrastructure team's charter is genuinely platform-shaped: "accelerates the rest of R&D
by providing scalable, efficient, and high-quality building blocks for product development, while
also ensuring we can support business growth in a secure and reliable way… responsible for building
simple, scalable abstractions and owns the full Infrastructure stack from the AWS infra that our
systems run on to the product platform that our product engineers use." Explicitly internal-customer
facing: "Working with internal customers and stakeholders to ensure we're solving the right problems."
They want "senior infra engineers who are generalists comfortable working across the full
infrastructure stack, with a product platform bent and full stack familiarity" — 5+ years, production
Python, Terraform/AWS, plus frontend-ecosystem familiarity. Mentoring is an expected responsibility.

Compensation (objective): The live Sr. Software Engineer, Infrastructure JD posts a base range of
**$133,000 – $267,000 USD in most remote locations**, and **$166,000 – $267,000** in San Francisco and
New York City, plus equity, 401(k) and health benefits. This is a company-stated range on its own JD
(direct), and supersedes the existing inferred jd_rollup row for platform_infra/senior. A band row was
written to /tmp/res-bands-6.json. Note the range is unusually wide (a 2x spread) because it is stated
as covering the seniority described rather than a narrow level.

Notable / other: Founding team has two prior startup exits (to Oracle and Dropbox). Publishes
recruiting notices, EEO policy, E-Verify information and a job-candidate privacy policy.

Open relevant roles (sample): Sr. Software Engineer, Infrastructure (remote US-7-states, live); a
second Sr. Software Engineer, Infrastructure posting (no longer live); Software Engineer; Sr. Software
Engineer.
