# AeroVect — research
Provider/key: ashby:aerovect | company_type: product

What they do: AeroVect builds autonomous driving for airport ground support equipment (GSE) — the tugs, loaders and baggage vehicles that operate airside. Their product is the "AeroVect Driver", retrofitted onto ground vehicles to automate airside operations for airlines and ground handling providers. In their own words: "AeroVect is transforming ground handling with autonomy, redefining how airlines and ground service providers around the globe run day-to-day operations." Site framing is "built for safety and reliability", "engineered for aviation", "enterprise ready", claiming "the world's largest airside driving dataset", 360° perception, and "tens of thousands of live aircraft crossings" handled in production.

How they describe themselves: Safety, reliability and cost-efficiency are the stated pillars; the marketing site is operations-focused rather than culture-focused, and no values list or careers-culture page content was found. Notably, the extracted facets on two of their own JDs (Staff SWE Localization, Staff Cloud SWE) were classified as culture: "hustle" during earlier posting research — a signal from their JD language, not a company statement.

Size / stage / funding: "We are a Series A company backed by top-tier venture capital investors in aviation and autonomous driving." Customers are described as "some of the world's largest airlines and ground handling providers." Headcount not disclosed; investors not named; profitability unstated.

Locations / HQ: HQ in Silicon Valley per their site. Their postings collectively name a wide geography: San Francisco / South San Francisco (hybrid), New York City (hybrid), Atlanta (hybrid and onsite), Toronto (onsite and "Toronto - Remote"), Vancouver - Remote (on one closed role), plus Europe — London, Paris, Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt, Amsterdam, Dublin, Stockholm, Italy, Poland and "United Kingdom (Remote)".

Remote policy: Mixed and role-by-role, and mostly not remote. Locations are tagged individually: several are "- Hybrid", one is "Atlanta - Onsite", the Toronto ML Ops role's own JD states "role will be based onsite in Toronto", and only some carry "- Remote". No company-wide remote policy is published.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Partially, but not for anything currently live, and not for the roles that would fit.** Canada does appear in their hiring footprint: the Toronto ML Ops role is Canadian but explicitly onsite in Toronto; several closed roles listed "Toronto - Remote" and one listed "Vancouver - Remote". Meanwhile their Staff Cloud Software Engineer JD — the closest infrastructure role — states "US work authorization and residency required" (facet geo_eligibility: us_only). The single live role in scope, Senior Staff Software Engineer, Perception, lists SF plus European cities and generic "Remote" with no Canadian location at all. So: remote-BC is unsupported today; a Toronto-remote arrangement has existed historically and might return, but nothing live matches.

Engineering & tech: Robotics/AV stack. Perception: 2D/3D object detection, multi-modal camera/LiDAR/radar fusion, detection and tracking, proprietary multi-modal dataset construction, metrics and evaluation pipelines, production model deployment and monitoring; Python. Localization: modern C++, ROS/ROS2, LiDAR-inertial-GNSS state estimation, Ceres/GTSAM/g2o, SLAM and factor graphs, Linux. Cloud/platform: AWS (ECS, ECR, DynamoDB), Terraform, CI/CD, Python and C++, ROS2. MLOps: Python, ROS2, rosbag, Docker, AWS S3/EC2, dataset versioning, training and evaluation pipelines, Weights & Biases, large-scale sensor data.
