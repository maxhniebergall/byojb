# Kalkomey Enterprises — research
Provider/key: rippling:kalkomey | company_type: product

What they do: Kalkomey is an outdoor-safety education and agency-management software company. Since 1995 it has partnered with government agencies across North America (wildlife, parks, boating, snowmobile regulators) to deliver the certification courses that legally gate hunting, fishing, boating and snowmobiling. Today it runs "more than 360 online learning experiences supported by integrated web and mobile applications." Public-sector B2G plus direct-to-consumer course sales.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "Empowering every outdoor journey with knowledge, confidence, and respect for the places we explore." In the JD: "Kalkomey Enterprises is the trusted guide to outdoor success… We believe education builds confidence, and confidence opens the door to the outdoors." Stated values, full set, from the careers page: **"Trust is Our North Star"** (accountability), **"Champion Collaboration"** (people and community first), **"Live to Explore"** (curiosity and innovation), **"Blaze a Trail"** (progress through resourcefulness). Marketing framing to agencies: "Your partner in creating safe, confident, and responsible participation," with "Streamlined Solutions" and "Trusted Standards" grounded in compliance. Tone is calm and mission-oriented; no hustle/rocketship language anywhere on the site or in the JD.

Size / stage / funding: Private; no headcount or funding disclosed. Scale markers they publish: "23M+ Certifications Issued," "1.3M+ Students Served Per Year," "60+ State, Provincial, Territorial and Federal Government Partners," "30+ Years Supporting Public Safety and Outdoor Education." 30 years in a regulated, recurring, government-contract domain reads as an established, non-runway-dependent business, though profitability is unverified.

Locations / HQ: Richardson, Texas (224 W. Campbell Rd. #512, Richardson, TX 75080). No other offices listed — the company is distributed.

Remote policy: Explicitly "Remote First" — "Work where you're most effective with a team built for async collaboration." The JD confirms: "We are a fully distributed company – unless specifically indicated in the job description, this is a work from home position," and asks for "Comfort working in a remote, async-first environment with a high degree of ownership and autonomy."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified NO for British Columbia.** The live Principal Backend Engineer JD states the hiring geography verbatim: "You must reside in one of these US states: AZ, CO, FL, GA, IL, IN, KY, MA, MD, MI, MN, NC, NV, OR, PA, RI, TX, VA, VT, WI, or one of these provinces in Canada: Ontario." Canada hiring exists but is Ontario-only; BC is not an eligible province. (Not verified whether this list is per-role or company-wide, but it is the only geographic statement available.)

Engineering & tech: Backend is "built primarily on Ruby on Rails"; the org has backend, product, data and platform teams, with a Principal IC reporting to the SVP of Technology. Practices named in the JD are unusually concrete and documentation-heavy: architecture decision records (ADRs), design reviews for major initiatives, a published "engineering playbook" covering repository health, testing, performance, security, experimentation and product analytics. Cloud is AWS-preferred, with CI/CD and infrastructure/platform practice as a bonus qualification. Experimentation (Optimizely / LaunchDarkly / VWO) and analytics (Mixpanel / Amplitude) are in the stack. Strong stated emphasis on AI-assisted development as a productivity multiplier ("AI-Forward Practice"), explicitly *not* AI/ML system-building.

Notable / other: Benefits per the JD — employer-matched 401(k), medical/dental/vision with employer HSA contributions, parental leave, three weeks paid vacation plus 12 paid holidays, a paid community service day, flexible schedule, annual wellness allowance, an annual paid mental health day, an automatic WFH stipend per paycheck, and an EAP. HR contact is pops@kalkomey.com. No recent funding, layoff, or acquisition news was verifiable from the pages fetched. The careers page listed "No items found" for open roles at fetch time even though the Rippling board carries a live posting — the site's job widget appears stale or client-rendered.
