# Xplor — research
Provider/key: smartrecruiters:xplor | company_type: product

What they do: Xplor (Xplor Technologies) builds vertical SaaS plus embedded payments for "everyday life businesses" — fitness studios, childcare, golf clubs, field services, recreation centres, laundry. "More than 130,000 businesses in 72+ countries rely on Xplor to run their day and get paid, processing over $47 billion in payments annually." The Canadian engineering role sits in Xplor Pay, its payment gateway used by integrators, partners and merchants.

How they describe themselves: "Technology that powers everyday life", with technology that should "run quietly in the background, so human connection can take center stage" and "When our customers thrive, communities thrive." Careers copy: "You'll be part of a global network of talented colleagues who support your success. We look for commonalities and shared passions and give people the tools they need to deliver great work and **grow at speed**." The JD asks that you "align with our core values and you're simply a good human" and are "comfortable asking questions and working through ambiguity in a fast-paced environment", with "initiative & ownership" and "a bias for action".

Size / stage / funding: Roughly 3,000 employees; private, assembled through "strategic acquisitions" that "united the brightest companies". Backed by Advent International, Battery Ventures and Silver Lake (stated on its own JD). 15 global offices; four consecutive BuiltIn Best Places to Work awards claimed.

Locations / HQ: Global; 15 offices, 72+ countries. No single HQ is stated on the about page. The relevant posting is Toronto, ON, Canada (remote).

Remote policy: No company-wide policy published on the about page. The Toronto posting is explicitly remote — "Able to work remotely and have a dedicated work space."

Remote-Canada eligibility: YES for this role, with a caveat. The posting is "Toronto, ON, Canada, Remote", the extracted timezone is America/Toronto, and the JD states "you must be legally authorized to work in the location (country) you're applying for. Xplor does not sponsor visas" — a Canadian citizen qualifies. Nothing states the province must be Ontario, but the team's timezone is Eastern; a Mountain-Time candidate would be three hours behind, and the role includes "a 2-week on-call rotation" for production support.

Engineering & tech: Java + Spring Boot microservices as the core, with JUnit, Maven, RabbitMQ, RESTful services, SQL and distributed-system architecture; some JavaScript/Angular 9+ solutions. Agile/SCRUM, peer review, on-call rotation, cross-functional work with QA, DevOps and Product. Reports to a Senior Manager of Software Engineering.

Notable / other: The open Canadian role asks for "1 to 5 years" of Java experience — a mid-level maintenance-and-growth role on an existing microservice fleet ("help maintain and grow our fleet of microservices"), with mentorship framed as something you receive. Second live posting is Newcastle, UK (remote).

Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineer (Java) — Toronto remote; Software Engineer — Newcastle UK remote.
