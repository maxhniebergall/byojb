# Zip — research
Provider/key: ashby:zip | company_type: product

ENTITY CHECK: "Zip" is a badly colliding name (Zip Co the Australian BNPL lender, Zip the UK water-tap maker, and others). The entity behind `ashby:zip` is confirmed from its own JD text as **Zip, the AI platform for enterprise procurement** ("Zip is the AI platform for enterprise procurement — built for humans and agents working together"), Y-Combinator-backed, US-headquartered, with a Toronto engineering office. The registry also holds two junk duplicate rows for this same key under the names "Zipio" and "Zip Phone" — see the data-quality note below; neither is a real distinct company, they are the same Ashby board.

What they do: Enterprise procurement orchestration — software that routes and approves purchasing requests across a company's teams, tools and suppliers, increasingly with AI agents doing the orchestration. Customers named in their own JD: T-Mobile, OpenAI, AMD, Mars, Dollar Tree. They claim customers have "saved over $8 billion and processed over $500 billion in spend."

How they describe themselves: "By orchestrating procurement across teams, tools, and suppliers with the help of AI agents, companies can secure the resources they need to innovate faster than ever before." On hiring, verbatim: "We're looking to hire Zipsters and that means hiring people who take ownership, communicate openly, have an underdog mindset, and are excited to increase the pace of innovation for every business in the world." They also emphasise pedigree: "Zip's team includes product leaders from Apple, Airbnb, and Meta, as well as former procurement leaders from United Health, Sanofi, MGM Resorts, Discover, and NASA." The Developer Platform JD adds a values-in-practice line: "we operate from a position of high leverage... While we are an experienced team that can provide constant guidance and mentorship, we value engineers who can autonomously scope and solve difficult technical challenges."

Size / stage / funding: Late-stage private, stated first-party in the JD: "Backed by Adams Street, Alkeon, BOND, CRV, DST, Tiger Global, and Y Combinator, Zip has raised $371 million, most recently at a $2.2 billion valuation." Recognised by Forbes Fintech 50, Fast Company Most Innovative, Inc. Best in Business, LinkedIn Top Startups. The Developer Platform team states it supports "the 100s of engineers" at Zip, which puts engineering headcount in the several hundreds.

Locations / HQ: US-headquartered (San Francisco) with a Toronto engineering office; every posting in the registry for this key is located "Toronto". The Core Infrastructure JD mentions "effective collaboration with internal stakeholders across multiple time zones", consistent with a distributed US/Canada org.

Remote policy: HYBRID, and stated first-party in the benefits list of every JD: "🏠 Hybrid model + 5 flexible remote days per quarter". Five remote days *per quarter* is a strict in-office policy with a token flexibility allowance. Reinforced by the on-site-only perks they advertise: catered breakfast, lunch and dinner; monthly commuter benefit; team building events and happy hours.

Remote-Canada eligibility: VERIFIED NO for a remote worker. The company hires in Canada — the roles are Toronto-based and Canadian employment is clearly available — but they are office-attendance roles in Toronto, not remote-Canada roles. "5 flexible remote days per quarter" plus a commuter benefit and three catered meals a day is unambiguous. This is disqualifying for a Kimberley, BC candidate who cannot relocate.

Engineering & tech: Two distinct infrastructure teams appear in the registry, both genuinely infra rather than product. **Developer Platform**: internal tooling and velocity — test-suite performance ("low-level systems optimizations for speeding up tests"), AI-assisted codemods and large-scale rewrites, CI pipelines, development environments, feature-flag conventions, deprecating legacy patterns, and "a new deploy and release process, enabling daily deployments... through canaries, traffic replay, and bundling feature releases for Enterprise customers." Tools named: GitHub, GitLab, Buildkite, Jenkins, Docker. **Core Infrastructure**: "multi-region growth by building and operating global multi-cell architectures", end-to-end ownership of "Kubernetes platforms, AI Infra, observability, deployment pipeline and cost optimization". Stack named: AWS, Datadog, Kubernetes, Celery, Redis, Docling, DBOS. Bar is 4+ years and a CS/physics/maths degree is listed as a requirement, not a preference.

Notable / other: "🤖 Unlimited AI token usage" is listed as a staff benefit, and the Developer Platform role is explicitly chartered to "drive adoption of AI-driven tools, from IDEs to code reviews to test generators" — this is an org pushing AI-assisted development internally, not only selling it. Other benefits: start-up equity, 100% health/vision/dental options, flexible PTO, ClassPass, home-office stipend, phone/internet reimbursement, paid parental leave, fertility benefits, EAP.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Developer Platform (Toronto, live); Software Engineer, Core Infrastructure (Mid-Senior level) (Toronto, live); Software Engineer, Backend (All Levels) (Toronto, live); Senior/Staff Software Engineer, Core Infrastructure; Software Engineer, Identity; Senior Software Engineer, Online Storage; Software Engineer, AI Product / AI Foundation.
