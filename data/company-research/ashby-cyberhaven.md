# Cyberhaven — research
Provider/key: ashby:cyberhaven | company_type: product

What they do: AI-native enterprise data security. Its platform ("Cyberhaven Flow") is built on **data lineage** — tracing how data actually flows and is used across an organization rather than pattern-matching content — and from that delivers data loss prevention (DLP), data security posture management (DSPM), insider risk management (IRM), AI/shadow-AI security, and data classification. Coverage spans endpoints, SaaS, PaaS and IaaS. Its own words: it "analyzes billions of workflows to understand data, detect risk, and stop threats," offering "security that moves at business speed" and "complete data control" without disrupting legitimate work.

How they describe themselves: Four stated values, verbatim: **Act Boldly** (high standards, meaningful outcomes pursued with conviction); **Innovate to Win** (take smart risks, learn quickly, keep trust); **Earn Trust Everyday** (secure systems, transparent execution); **Prioritize & Execute** (results-driven activity, customer impact). Culture is summarized as a "tracer mindset — curious, intentional, human," emphasizing disciplined innovation. Recruiting copy: "Joining Cyberhaven is a chance to revolutionize data security. Traditional tools fall short, but we've reimagined protection with AI-enabled data lineage."

Size / stage / funding: Private, late-stage venture. Its own JD states **$250M raised**, naming Khosla and Redpoint as leads; the about page adds Costanoa, Vertex Ventures, Adams Street and Crane, plus angel advisors from Google, CrowdStrike, Cisco and Meta. Leadership: Nishant Doshi (CEO), Dr. Volodymyr Kuznetsov (CTO), Edward Sharp (CFO). Team described as including people who built products at CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks, Meta and Google. Headcount not disclosed on any page fetched — unverified.

Locations / HQ: **Not stated on the site pages fetched** — the about and home pages give no HQ or office addresses. Postings imply engineering presence in Bangalore/India, Romania (Cluj and Bucharest), the EU, and the US.

Remote policy: No stated remote policy on the site. From postings: heavily distributed, with roles tagged "EU Remote", "India Remote", "Romania Remote" and "US Remote".

Engineering & tech: Three named engineering surfaces — a **browser extension** team (Chrome/Edge, Manifest V3, JavaScript/TypeScript, secure handling of untrusted web content), an **endpoint agent** team (macOS endpoint work), and a **backend** team (Go is named for backend integration). Emphasis on secure coding practices, performance and reliability across diverse environments, cross-functional work with product/design/backend, and keeping pace with evolving browser APIs and extension-ecosystem changes. Principal-level bar is 5+ years of engineering with 3+ specifically in browser extensions.

Notable / other: The company discloses that it uses AI-powered recruiting tools for scheduling, note-taking and workflow automation, and lets candidates opt out of AI-assisted interview note taking — an unusually transparent disclosure. No pay range is posted on any of its three relevant JDs.
