# Latitude.sh — research
Provider/key: rippling:latitudesh-jobs | company_type: product

What they do: Latitude.sh provides automated, global **bare metal infrastructure** with cloud-like flexibility — server provisioning, network management, billing, all driven through a public API, SDKs and a Terraform provider. Launched in 2019. Their own words: they combine "the performance and security of bare metal with the automation capabilities of the cloud," and are "on a mission to make the internet faster." Their JD confirms the core API is "a Ruby on Rails application with Go services" and is "the backbone of everything Latitude offers."

How they describe themselves: Three stated principles on their about page — **Straightforward** ("we handle the complexity of infrastructure so customers don't have to"), **Adaptable** (powerful yet flexible solutions), **Global** (servers positioned near end users). The JD adds culture language: "a lean, agile team where your work truly matters," "ownership, autonomy," "a collaborative, low-hierarchy culture where curiosity drives growth," "customers are at the center of everything we do." Notably developer-empathetic framing: "you care about API design as a craft — our customers are developers and they notice."

Size / stage / funding: Private, small/lean by their own description. **Recently acquired by Megaport** (ASX-listed network-as-a-service provider) per their own JD text — "we are now integrating high-performance compute with world-class connectivity to power the next generation of AI and enterprise workloads at scale." Headcount not stated on any page reached; unverified.

Locations / HQ: HQ not stated on the pages reached. Strong Brazil centre of gravity — the Backend JD is filed under "São Paulo, Brazil Remote" and the location tag on the posting is simply "Brazil." Megaport is Australian (Brisbane).

Remote policy: Remote roles are posted, but geo-scoped per role: the Backend Engineer role is Brazil-remote, the Senior SRE role is "United States Remote." No global-remote statement found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No evidence of Canada eligibility — likely no.** The two live relevant roles are explicitly scoped to Brazil and to the United States. Additionally the Backend JD's benefits section leads with "**Contractor (PJ) status**" — PJ is the Brazilian *pessoa jurídica* contractor arrangement, i.e. that role is a Brazil-domiciled contractor engagement, not employment. Unverified whether any Canadian arrangement exists; nothing on their pages says so.

Engineering & tech: Ruby on Rails core API with Go services; REST APIs, webhooks, SDKs, a Terraform provider; relational databases, background jobs, distributed-systems fundamentals. Small team structure — the JD says you "work with our platform architect and lead backend engineer," implying a handful of backend engineers total. Engineers "own reliability of the services you build."

Notable / other: Genuine infrastructure-utility identity (bare metal + networking) rather than an application-layer product. The Megaport acquisition is the dominant recent event and shifts them toward being a compute arm of a connectivity company — plausibly stabilising, though integration risk is real. Benefits mentioned: PTO, "competitive compensation," Wellhub/Gympass (a Brazil-centric benefit), annual performance bonus, flexible hours.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Site Reliability Engineer (United States Remote); Software Engineer - Backend (São Paulo, Brazil Remote).
