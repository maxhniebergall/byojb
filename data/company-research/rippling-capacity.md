# Capacity — research
Provider/key: rippling:capacity | company_type: product

What they do: Capacity sells an AI-powered support-automation platform: it connects to a customer's tech stack to "answer questions, automate repetitive support tasks, and build solutions" across chat, email, SMS, voice and social channels, and to assist live agents and analyse conversations. Founded 2017 by David Karandish and Chris Sims (previously Answers.com), and it has grown substantially by acquisition of smaller support/automation tools.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "help teams do their best work" by reducing time spent on information searches, repetitive questions and tedious tasks. Values as stated on their about page: creating amazing products; genuinely helping customers; doing the right thing while having fun. They say they recruit "high achievement individuals with a distinctively low ego". They also emphasise community: "Although we are a remote workforce, we remember the neighborhood where we started. We still strive to elevate our community by furthering access to education and careers in the tech space. Our affiliated nonprofit, Create A Loop, brings rigorous computer science courses to underserved communities."

Size / stage / funding: Private. Their own JDs state: "Capacity has raised over $100 million dollars from over 150 investors, giving us the opportunity to make ambitious investments in our team and big bets on our future. Our total addressable market is enormous." Described on the about page as "part of the Equity.com incubator". Headcount not disclosed on any page read; the registry shows only 7 total postings, suggesting a small company. Profitability unknown.

Locations / HQ: "Proudly headquartered in St. Louis," Missouri.

Remote policy: Remote workforce, stated in their own words ("Although we are a remote workforce…"). Reqs are posted as "Remote (United States)", "Spain Remote", "Switzerland Remote; United Kingdom Remote" — so they do employ remotely across several countries, not only the US.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — not offered. Despite hiring remotely in the US, Spain, Switzerland and the UK, no posting lists Canada, and the US reqs are explicitly scoped "Remote (United States)". They clearly have some multi-country employment capability, which makes the absence of Canada a choice rather than an incapacity, but as of this scan there is no Canada-eligible req.

Engineering & tech: The live reqs are AWS infrastructure, speech systems, retrieval, and "enablement". "Senior Infrastructure Engineer, AWS" is real platform ownership, and a "Software Engineer, Retrieval" role implies genuine RAG/search work rather than pure prompt plumbing. That said, the product itself is an AI support-chatbot/automation platform — the category the profile flags as a thin-wrapper anti-target — so the infra roles are supporting a chatbot product.

Notable / other: Comp is posted on the reqs: Senior Infrastructure Engineer (AWS), US remote, $125,000-$150,000; Software Engineer III (Speech Systems) $115,000-$158,000; Software Engineer (unlevelled) $150,000-$180,000; Software Engineer II, Spain Remote, €35,000-€45,000. Benefits include employer-paid health insurance for employee and eligible dependents, "Company Profit Interest Units eligibility", and unlimited PTO.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Infrastructure Engineer, AWS (United States Remote); Software Engineer, Retrieval (Switzerland/UK Remote); Software Engineer III, Speech Systems (US Remote); Software Engineer, Enablement (US Remote); Software Engineer II (Spain Remote).
