# Zeta Global — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:zetaglobal | company_type: product

What they do: Zeta Global (NYSE: ZETA) is an "AI-Powered Marketing Cloud that leverages advanced artificial intelligence (AI) and trillions of consumer signals to make it easier for marketers to acquire, grow, and retain customers more efficiently." Core product is the Zeta Marketing Platform (ZMP), unifying "identity, intelligence, and omnichannel activation into a single platform – powered by one of the industry's largest proprietary databases and AI." Customers are enterprise marketers across multiple verticals. Zeta Actions is a business unit encompassing the acquired Sailthru, LiveIntent and WhatCounts platforms.

How they describe themselves: Their own words on the Staff Engineer JD are unusually candid and IC-focused: "This is a deeply technical IC role. You'll spend most of your time in the code, the architecture, and the hard problems." They emphasize "Real scale, real complexity — billions of messages, live traffic, meaningful performance and reliability constraints" and "Real autonomy — you won't be handed a backlog. You'll be expected to find the important problems, make the case for solving them, and drive them to resolution." Culture language stresses curiosity ("engineers who read widely, ask uncomfortable questions"), "Bias for action… you'd rather move with imperfect information and course-correct," and "Comfort operating without a playbook." They state a commitment to "building a workplace culture of trust and belonging" and run ERGs.

Size / stage / funding: Public company (NYSE: ZETA), founded 2007 by David A. Steinberg and John Sculley. Established, not runway-dependent. Exact headcount not verified from fetched material.

Locations / HQ: Headquartered in New York City "with offices around the world." Live engineering postings sit in two geos: Remote–United States, and London, England (UK).

Remote policy: Stated explicitly on the Staff Engineer JD for the Zeta Actions unit: "We're remote-first with engineers across the US." Benefits include unlimited PTO.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — effectively US-only (or UK for the London roles).** All six US-side postings are titled "Remote - United States" and the company's own words scope remote to "engineers across the US." No Canadian entity, location or eligibility statement appears anywhere in the registry's JD bodies for this company. Verified negative from their own JD text.

Engineering & tech: AWS and cloud-native architecture; JVM-first (Java and Kotlin primary), Kubernetes in production, large-scale databases, distributed systems (fault tolerance, consistency tradeoffs, service coordination). PHP/Python a plus; Databricks and data engineering (pipelines, lakehouses, batch and streaming) a bonus. On-call rotation exists — Staff Engineers act as "second-level escalation point for complex engineering incidents." Notable: fluency with AI-assisted development tools (Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, Codex) is "a firm requirement" — they have adopted AI-assisted development across the whole engineering org and expect staff engineers to model "prompt discipline, output validation, and security."

Notable / other: Real legacy-system content — "Comfort working in and around legacy codebases… without needing a rewrite" — consistent with a roll-up of acquired platforms (Sailthru/LiveIntent/WhatCounts). Domain is adtech/martech: identity resolution and consumer-signal data at massive scale.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer (Remote – US); Lead Software Engineer, Identity (Remote – US); Lead Software Engineer - Core AI (Remote – US); Principal AI/ML Engineer - AdTech (Remote – US); Principal DevOps Engineer (Remote – US); Lead Compute Platform Engineer (London); Senior Software Engineer — Real-Time Ad Serving (London).
