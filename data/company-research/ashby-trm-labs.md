# TRM Labs — research
Provider/key: ashby:trm-labs | company_type: product

What they do: TRM Labs is a blockchain-intelligence company. It indexes and traces on-chain activity in real time across 65+ chains and 1.9B+ assets, with risk screening covering 184+ blockchains, and sells that intelligence to financial institutions, crypto businesses and government agencies to detect and investigate crypto financial crime, fraud and sanctions evasion. Founded 2018 by Esteban Castano and Rahul Raina; HQ San Francisco. Customers include PayPal, Visa, Uniswap, Anchorage and federal agencies such as the FBI and IRS.

How they describe themselves: TRM frames its values as three **Leadership Principles** — **Master Craftsperson** (excellence in the quality of the work), **Inspiring Colleague** (collaboration and how you treat people), and **Impact-Oriented Trailblazer** (results and ownership) — and states that commercial success emerges as a consequence of these rather than being the goal itself. The culture page is explicit that speed is treated as a competitive advantage: projects move "faster than typical startups" via "80-20 solutions", new hires get "no honeymoon period" and "run at the same fast clip" immediately, and JDs refer to "delivering results at TRM speed". Balanced against that, they describe deliberate prioritisation ("deep work on high-impact initiatives rather than scattered tasks"), heavy documentation, and a culture "grounded in trust, transparency, and adaptability".

Size / stage / funding: Private unicorn. Reached $1B valuation on a $70M Series C led by Blockchain Capital. Several hundred employees spread across many countries.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco. Distributed across "several countries and time zones"; live reqs are geo-tagged including Canada, LATAM and India.

Remote policy: Remote-first and explicitly async-leaning. Their culture page names "structured communication practices" over Slack, Loom and Notion, with documentation of decisions, meetings and processes as a cornerstone. Performance feedback every 6 weeks; biannual engagement surveys. Benefits: 25 days PTO plus 14 country-specific holidays, generous parental leave, a sabbatical programme, cash + equity benchmarked against similar-stage companies.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** Multiple live reqs are titled explicitly "- Canada" (e.g. *Senior Software Engineer, Backend | Product Engineering - Canada*, *Senior Software Engineer, Data Product - Canada*) with location field "Canada". The company clearly runs Canadian employment. **Timezone caveat, stated in the JD:** "the team's timezone is a mix of EST and PST with at least 6 hours of overlap with PST (8 am to 2 pm PST)". From Kimberley BC (Mountain Time) that window is 9 am – 3 pm local — entirely workable.

Engineering & tech: Large data-infrastructure engineering surface — the open reqs are dominated by data platform, data lakehouse infrastructure, graph analytics, RDBMS/data infrastructure, MLOps/LLMOps and SRE-style infrastructure work rather than product/frontend. Real-time indexing across 65+ chains is a genuine large-scale distributed-data problem. There is a Staff MLOps (LLMOps) ladder alongside senior, and separate AI-automation engineering roles.

Notable / other: Domain is compliance/law-enforcement crypto forensics — mission-critical and regulation-driven, so demand is less tied to crypto price cycles than a trading-adjacent company would be, but it is still crypto-sector-exposed. Recognised by the World Economic Forum. Note TRM also maintains a separate Greenhouse board (job-boards.greenhouse.io/trmlabs) alongside the Ashby board in the registry.

Compensation (stated on their own live JDs; the JDs say these figures "reflect the US base salary only"):

No Canadian band is published; the Canada-titled reqs carry the US figures with the US-only disclaimer, so the actual CAD offer is unknown.
