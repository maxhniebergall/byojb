# Quartermaster — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:quartermaster | company_type: product
What they do: Quartermaster is an early-stage Toronto consumer marketplace: "We're building Toronto's dedicated platform for home management to empower the city's homeowners and to better connect our neighbours and the local Pros they trust." It is a two-sided home-services marketplace matching homeowners with local trades. Scope is deliberately hyperlocal: "Our focus is purely on the GTA market right now. We build our property base one street at a time, and our marketplaces one neighbourhood at a time. We're a local tech company in every sense of the word."
How they describe themselves: Mission-and-values heavy, with an explicit intensity pitch. Stated team values are Integrity ("We prioritize integrity over comfort, convenience, and the short-cuts of success that drive metrics but undermine trust"), Trust ("We cannot win unless we win on trust. Trust is hard to earn and easy to lose"), Competitiveness ("We believe that competitiveness is a critical complement to integrity and trust... we need to be uniquely competitive to win in this space"), and Empowerment ("Any win we achieve needs to be first and foremost a win that we deliver to others, starting with our customers"). Their business beliefs: "We believe in high-trust hyperlocal communities. We believe in low commission marketplaces that the best Pros can actually say yes to... we believe that it's good business to care." The self-selection language is unusually direct: "If this resonates with you and you're at a point in your career and your life where you want to do something uniquely difficult for a meaningful outcome, you've found your next home", and the role fit criteria include "You're at a point in your life and your career where you want to tackle a uniquely demanding job", "You're keen to work in-person with a team", "You show up like an owner", and "You want to value the equity and solve for others".
Size / stage / funding: Early-stage startup, small, pre-scale — single-city focus and "higher than usual employee ownership in the company" in place of cash. "We expect a high degree of ownership from each other and we pair that with higher than usual employee ownership." Headcount and funding not disclosed; UNVERIFIED.
Locations / HQ: Toronto, Ontario (GTA only).
