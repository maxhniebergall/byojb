# Cylinder Health — research
Provider/key: ashby:cylinderhealth | company_type: product

What they do: Cylinder (formerly Vivante Health, rebranded 2023) is a virtual digital-health company focused specifically on **digestive / GI health**. Founded 2021 and sold as an **employer benefit** (B2B2C): they partner with employers and health plans, who offer Cylinder to their members. In their words, Cylinder "delivers personalized, clinician-backed care to those suffering from digestive issues through its virtual health platform. Cylinder's tech-forward, human-first program connects members to the right level of support at the right time through an easy interface, bringing the healthcare ecosystem together to align around member needs. Cylinder's dedicated care teams help members proactively address their gut health from every angle – decreasing costs, reducing absenteeism, and improving their quality of life, at scale." Stated vision: "to create a world where people receive convenient, personalized, and affordable access to the quality digestive healthcare they need."

How they describe themselves: The careers page lists six principles — **member-first care**, **continuous learning**, **innovation**, **collaboration with accountability**, **transparency**, and **embracing diversity**. Employees quoted on the page describe it as collaborative and mission-driven ("everyone really cares about what we're doing and how there's such a strong focus on our members"). The engineering JD adds their own culture statement: "We are proud of the team culture that we foster today, which is **extremely friendly and supportive while constantly reaching to raise our own standards of engineering**. We're really excited about what we're building, and usually having fun building it together." They are candid about stage: "We're still a small company, which means that **everyone still wears a lot of hats** and we need engineers who are comfortable with **ambiguity** and working across a wide variety of challenges. That said, our existing engineering team has built an extremely efficient working environment based on best-in-class hosted SaaS frameworks that greatly minimize the overhead in deploying and supporting software in production." And: "It's still early enough that there's a lot of decisions and new discoveries to be made, so this is your chance to get in early and help shape our future." Mission language is strong and personal — "Most of us also have personal (direct/family/friend) connections to GI health problems … we're on a mission to build something much larger than ourselves here."

Size / stage / funding: ~**104 employees**. Private, venture-backed: **$52.8M raised across 3 rounds**, most recently a **$31.0M Series B in September 2023**. That round is now ~3 years old at time of research, which is a notable runway question. Chicago-based.

Locations / HQ: Chicago, Illinois. Remote-first workforce; one annual in-person all-hands gathering.

Remote policy: **Remote-first**, with an annual in-person summit for all full-time employees ("to foster collaboration, encourage team-building, build connections, and celebrate another great year"). The engineering JD expects candidates "comfortable with a distributed workforce. We interact with each other via video chat, Slack, and other collaboration tools" — video-chat-inclusive rather than strictly async. The relevant posting is tagged Remote.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes, and unusually specifically.** Their careers page states a "**Remote first work environment within the U.S**" with limited exceptions — and names the exception directly: **some technical positions are available in British Columbia, Canada, for Canadian citizens only**. Consistent with this, the Lead Software Engineer, Backend posting carries a dual-currency range including **CAD $157K–$213K** alongside the USD range, which is a strong corroborating signal that this specific req is open to a Canadian hire. This is the only company in the batch with a *British-Columbia-specific* hiring carve-out.

Engineering & tech: Backend services "developed mostly in **Go** and run in **GKE (k8s)** or directly on other **GCP** serverless frameworks, with **GitHub and Terraform** powering the DevOps environment." Also relevant: Firebase, IAM, Pub/Sub. A significant part of the advertised role is AI: "leading your team in building AI-powered features, collaborating with our data and clinical teams on **LLM-based experiences and model integrations**", plus "experience building and integrating AI-assisted tools and practices into the SDLC, including engineering productivity, code generation/review, DevOps workflows, documentation, test automation, and operational governance." Data side is genuinely interesting: "bringing together data sources that have never been joined, building models of a GI patient that have never been built". Desired familiarity with healthcare data standards — **OHDSI, FHIR** — and HIPAA-regulated environments with security and privacy controls. They are explicit about security: "We take the stewardship of our customer's healthcare data seriously, and take no shortcuts to protect it."

Role shape: The open role is **Lead Software Engineer, Backend** — a hands-on engineering-leadership role, not a pure IC one. Required: "5+ years in Software Engineering with **2+ years in an engineering leadership role**", delivering complex projects "with both **onshore and offshore** developers"; responsibilities include technical leadership and vision for a squad, defining the technical roadmap with product management, "resource allocation, and project management", SQA practices, and cross-functional collaboration. They stress staying "close enough to the code to earn your team's respect".

Compensation (stated on their own live JD): **$165,000–$210,000 USD**, plus equity, with **CAD $157,000–$213,000** shown alongside.

Notable / other: Benefits per careers page — vacation plus your birthday off, sick leave and 12 national holidays, **16 weeks paid parental leave**, employer-subsidised medical/dental/vision, 401(k) and FSA/HSA, technology stipend for remote setup, professional-development stipend, equity for full-time staff, and a service-recognition gift at 4 years. Equal-opportunity statement emphasises "safe spaces where everyone can be their authentic selves". The mention of managing "offshore developers" indicates part of engineering delivery is outsourced/augmented.

Open relevant roles (sample): "Lead Software Engineer, Backend" (Remote, $165K–$210K USD / CAD $157K–$213K); "Enrollment Marketing Specialist".
