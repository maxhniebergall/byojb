# Camunda — research
Provider/key: ashby:camunda | company_type: product

What they do: Camunda builds a process orchestration and automation platform. In their own framing, it lets organizations "design, orchestrate and improve these processes — no matter where they are and what they entail," coordinating work that spans people, systems, APIs, RPA bots, microservices, IoT devices, machine learning and AI. The technical lineage is BPMN-standard workflow engines — the open-source Camunda engine and the Zeebe distributed workflow engine — sold as a commercial platform. This is orchestration middleware: infrastructure that other teams' systems depend on.

How they describe themselves: The name derives from Latin roots meaning "to capture" and "clean," which they present as capturing opportunities while maintaining ethical standards, backed by a Code of Ethics and Business Conduct. The dominant current message on their JDs is a strategic one, repeated verbatim across postings: "Fully remote and global, we are in the middle of something bigger: transforming into an **AI-first organisation, built on our own platform**. We use Agentic AI to automate, orchestrate intelligent processes, and elevate human contribution across every team." They also state a compensation philosophy directly — "We offer competitive, fair, and transparent compensation" — and describe hiring "in the first half of the range to allow room for growth."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, Berlin-headquartered, and well established. Described as "a diverse, distributed and global organization" supported by "a vibrant, global developer community of more than 100,000 developers." Headcount, current round and profitability are not published on their own about page.

Locations / HQ: Headquartered in **Berlin, Germany**, operating globally distributed. Their reqs enumerate hiring geographies including Germany, the United Kingdom, Czechia, Ireland, US East, and **Canada**, plus Singapore as a named comp market.

Remote policy: **Fully remote and global**, stated repeatedly. Benefits are built around it: "Work from anywhere with the setup that suits you," home-office budget, co-working space support, flexible time off, and up to $/€/£1,000 per year for self-directed learning. Where a country is not a direct-employment geography, they hire through **Remote.com as an employer-of-record partner**, and supply a personalized Total Rewards Calculator after the first interview. Asynchronous work is an explicit hiring criterion, not just a perk — the Infrastructure req requires "proven ability to thrive in remote, asynchronous teams."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, with a Canadian compensation band published.** The "Senior Software Engineer, Backend" posting lists Canada among its locations (Germany, United Kingdom, Czechia, USEAST, London, Ireland, Canada) and states a Canada-specific range of **C$152,700.00 to C$251,200.00** Annual Total Target Cash. A company that publishes a CAD band is employing in CAD, which is the strongest possible form of this evidence. No province restrictions are stated. Other reqs are tagged simply "Remote" without a country list; for those the Remote.com EOR path applies.

Engineering & tech: Distributed-systems work at the core — the open roles are Senior Software Engineer Backend (Core/API & Process Automation), Software Engineer Backend – Distributed Systems, Senior Software Engineer Kubernetes, and Software Engineer – Infrastructure. The Zeebe engine is a genuinely hard distributed-systems artifact (partitioned, replicated, high-throughput workflow state), and the stack is JVM-centric with Kubernetes-based deployment. Customers are major global enterprises across financial services, insurance, telecom, retail, manufacturing, tech and the public sector — named examples include Atlassian, Deutsche Telekom, Autodesk, Lufthansa, ING and Universal.

Notable / other: Compensation is published per market as **Total Target Cash (base salary + 100% variable target)** rather than base alone — an important distinction when comparing to base-only bands elsewhere, since some portion is variable. Published markets on live reqs: Canada C$152,700–C$251,200; United States $149,800–$241,500; Singapore S$186,100–S$279,100. Ranges span "from the minimum in a Standard market to the maximum in a Major market," so they are wide by construction and a given location sits in only part of the range.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Backend - Core/API & Process Automation; Software Engineer, Backend - Distributed Systems; Senior Software Engineer, Kubernetes; Software Engineer - Infrastructure.
