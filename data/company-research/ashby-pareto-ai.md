# Pareto.AI — research
Provider/key: ashby:pareto-ai | company_type: product

What they do: Pareto builds the human-data and evaluation infrastructure that frontier AI models are trained on — "the platform that turns [expert human judgment] into the data, evals, and RL environments frontier models learn from." Their own site frames it as "expert data for artificial general intelligence (AGI)", built around "human-based training signals through products and research to improve AI models". They state they work with **leading frontier labs including Anthropic and Google DeepMind**, and that "This RL environment and human-data infrastructure is already in production. Our job now is to scale it." The second half of the business is a marketplace angle: "we give skilled people everywhere a way to shape the future of AI and share in what it creates."

How they describe themselves: The mission framing on both the site and the JD is the "virtuous cycle" thesis — verbatim from the site: "Human data improves models, models help humans do more sophisticated things, which in turn improves models"; and from the JD: "Humanity is in a virtuous cycle: human insight improves AI, and better AI expands what people can do. Sustaining it depends on the one input that can't be automated: expert human judgment." No formal values list is published on the pages fetched. The JD's cultural signals are mostly about operating style: "A track record of operating in ambiguity — shipping fast, pivoting when wrong, and moving on without ego"; "juggle multiple workstreams, make smart tradeoff decisions as priorities shift, and translate ambiguous business needs into concrete technical architecture"; "Rapidly assess whether a technical idea is worth building — get early signal, align stakeholders, and kill or accelerate accordingly"; "engineering guidelines that let the team move fast without breaking things"; and "Ability to reason about requirements with a bias for Essentialism". Also notable: "Daily proficiency with agentic coding tools (Claude Code, Cursor, or equivalent) — you use these to multiply your output, not pad it", and "Exceptional written and verbal English communication skills… Communication cannot be a bottleneck."

Engineering & tech: Stack named as a nice-to-have: "Python, TypeScript, AWS, GCP, Terraform, Temporal Cloud, containerization, LLM gateways, RAG frameworks, and data pipeline tooling". The role (Applied AI Engineer, 8+ years) is architecture-heavy: designing the pipelines that generate synthetic tasks and evaluation environments for model training — described as "the factory floor of AI development, producing training fuel for next-generation models, not the models themselves" — and architecting human-in-the-loop workflows, "deciding what gets automated, what requires human intervention, how state is preserved across handoffs, and how the whole system stays reliable at scale". Also expected: owning "the most complex system design discussions", producing one-page technical scoping documents, defining technology stacks and engineering guidelines, and building reusable frameworks. Required background: agentic workflows, multi-agent orchestration, HITL pipelines, LLM applications in production (RAG, vector stores, semantic search, multi-model stacks — "not just demos"), context engineering, and distributed systems architecture applied to AI or data platforms. The JD explicitly wants "a software engineering foundation first — you think in systems, architecture, and engineering tradeoffs, not in models and experiments". Ideal background is stated as prior work at an AI data company (Scale AI, Surge, Snorkel, Labelbox), plus familiarity with RLHF/RLVR preference data and reward models.
