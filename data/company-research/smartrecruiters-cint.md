# Cint — research

Provider/key: smartrecruiters:cint | company_type: product

What they do: Cint runs the world's largest programmatic **research marketplace** — the
**Cint Exchange** — connecting survey demand (brands, agencies, market-research firms) with
millions of survey respondents across ~130 countries. Alongside the panel exchange it sells
digital survey and ad-effectiveness measurement products, letting customers "post questions
and get answers from real people" to build strategy and measure advertising impact. Founded
1998 in Stockholm as a platform for consumers to share product feedback.

How they describe themselves: "The foundation for continuous research and measurement. Get
reliable data from the world's largest network of high-quality sources." The self-description
is product/marketplace-led rather than values-led; the about page emphasizes scale, data
quality and global reach over culture statements. External recognition cited: Newsweek Global
Top 100 Most Loved Workplaces 2025.

Size / stage / funding: Cint Group AB (publ), Stockholm-listed (ticker CINT), grown by
acquisition (notably Lucid and GapFish). Reported workforce is variously ~800–1,000+ people
across 18 offices plus remote staff. Note: Cint's own about page presents an investor-relations
section without describing itself as listed — treat the exact listing status as worth
re-verifying, but it is a large, established, non-startup company either way.

Locations / HQ: Global HQ **Stockholm, Sweden**. Offices in 9 further countries —
Germany (Berlin, Hamburg), Spain (Barcelona), India (Gurugram), UK (London), USA (New York,
New Orleans), Czech Republic (Prague), Japan (Tokyo), Singapore, Australia (Sydney).
**No Canadian office.**

Remote policy: Genuinely remote-friendly — the large majority of its 38 live engineering
postings are tagged Remote, and it maintains named remote hiring regions (it publishes a
"remote-US" careers location). Remote is offered *within* the countries where it has an
employing entity, not globally.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — effectively ineligible.** Across all 38 live postings in
the registry, not one is located in or open to Canada. Every relevant remote role is anchored
to Spain, Czechia, the UK, Germany or Brazil; the extracted eligibility on most is `eu_only`.
The North American presence is US-only (New York, New Orleans, remote-US) with no Canadian
entity. A Canada-based candidate has no obvious employment path here.

Engineering & tech: Teams are organized around the marketplace's functional problems —
Data Solutions & Measurement (DSM: Integrations and Methodology sub-teams), Fraud & Quality,
Yield Management, and an AI/ML Platform group. That is a genuinely infrastructure-flavoured
org: fraud detection at panel scale, yield/marketplace optimization, measurement methodology,
and an MLOps platform. Ladder observed: Sr. Software Engineer II → Senior → Staff.

Notable / other: Benefits cited for eligible (US) staff include medical/dental/vision,
401(k) matching, PTO with unlimited sick days, and paid parental leave. JD tone is consistently
balanced — no hustle language in the scanned bodies. **No salary ranges are posted on any of
the 38 live postings**, which is consistent with an EU-centric posting footprint outside
US pay-transparency jurisdictions.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer - Data Solutions & Measurement;
Staff MLOps Engineer (AI/ML Platform); Sr. Software Engineer [Fraud & Quality Expert Team];
Sr. Software Engineer - Yield Management Team; AI/ML Engineer (Synthetic Data);
Sr. Software Engineer II - (DSM Integrations Team).
