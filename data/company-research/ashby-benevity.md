# Benevity — research
Provider/key: ashby:benevity | company_type: product

What they do: Benevity builds corporate purpose / CSR software — the platform enterprises use to run workplace giving, volunteering, grantmaking, employee resource groups, "micro-actions" and impact reporting. Nearly 1,000 enterprise customers including most of the Fortune 100; named customers include Levi's, Merck, Cisco, Prudential, Visa, Starbucks, UPS and GE Appliances. Founded 2008.

How they describe themselves: Mission framing is "Benevity is the way the world does good" — providing companies and their employees with technology to take social action on the issues they care about, and "infusing a culture of goodness into the world." They are **one of the first B Corporations in Canada**: "we're as committed to purpose as we are to profits." Their stated core cultural value is **"we-are-we"**, and the JD describes "an outcome-driven environment where collaboration with your product, design and engineering counterparts is paramount." Employees are called "Benevity-ites"; the careers copy leads with "Innovative work. Growth opportunities. Caring co-workers. And a chance to do work that fills us with a sense of purpose." DEIB is stated as "part of Benevity's DNA," with employee resource groups and stated diversity on leadership and tech teams. Notably absent: hustle, rocketship or "fast-paced" language.

Size / stage / funding: Private, roughly 800–900 employees. Funding history: $38M minority investment from JMI Equity (2015), $40M Series C from General Atlantic and JMI Equity (2019), then a majority investment from **Hg** (2020) — so it is a **PE-backed**, established growth-stage SaaS business rather than an early startup.

Locations / HQ: HQ **Calgary, Alberta**. Offices/presence in Canada, Spain, Switzerland, the United Kingdom and the United States; UK presence established via the TrustCSR acquisition (2018). The two live AI/ML reqs are anchored to Calgary AB / Vancouver BC and to Toronto ON.

Remote policy: Stated verbatim on their JDs: "At Benevity, we embrace a **flexible hybrid approach** to where we work… For those located near one of our offices, **while there's no set requirement for in-office time**, we do value the moments when coming together in person helps us build connection and collaboration… we trust our people to make thoughtful decisions about when showing up in person matters most." They also say "We have people working all over the world."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes as an employer, but the specific reqs are city-anchored — treat as partially unclear.** Benevity is a Canadian company that plainly employs Canadians, and its policy explicitly imposes **no set in-office requirement**, which is the strongest positive signal available. However both live Staff Developer - AI/ML postings are labelled with named metros (Calgary AB / Vancouver BC, and Toronto ON) rather than "Remote - Canada," so whether they will hire someone in the BC interior with no office within reach is not stated and would need to be confirmed with the recruiter. Timezone is ideal: an Alberta/BC-anchored req is Mountain/Pacific time.

Engineering & tech: Front end mainly VueJS (desktop + mobile web). Back end is a mixed stack — some **Java Spring Boot**, some **PHP** — handling data processing, external provider interfaces and security. They run in **both GCP and AWS**, leaning on cloud-native technology where possible, and emphasise "clean, maintainable code" with Git. The platform integrates with external services for email, content storage and server-to-server interactions.

The live Staff Developer - AI/ML req is a genuine ML-platform charter: own end-to-end ML architecture from data ingestion and **feature stores** to model serving and monitoring; define the roadmap for **AI infrastructure and orchestration**; set the MLOps standard (testable, maintainable, scalable); architect **LLM applications** (copilots, search, assistants, agents) including **RAG pipelines, tool integrations and multi-step reasoning**; build **evaluation frameworks** with offline benchmarks, online metrics and human-in-the-loop; establish **guardrails and safety mechanisms** (prompt-injection defence, hallucination mitigation); own model versioning, A/B testing and automated rollback for drift. Asks 7+ years engineering with 4+ years deploying ML in production, plus Python, TensorFlow/PyTorch/scikit-learn, feature stores, experiment tracking, model registries and orchestration frameworks.

Notable / other: B Corp certification. Accommodation contact published for candidates with disabilities.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Developer - AI/ML (Calgary AB / Vancouver BC); Staff Developer - AI/ML (Toronto ON).

Pay: No range posted on either live req (Ashby comp field is empty), and no company pay-transparency page was found. Note that Alberta and Ontario do not mandate posted ranges, so the absence is expected rather than evasive.
