# Knock — research
Provider/key: ashby:knock | company_type: product
What they do: Notifications-as-infrastructure. Their framing: "Knock is on a mission to help products communicate with their users in a more thoughtful way. Building product notifications in-house takes months, often leading to poor user experiences." They sell an API-first notification engine — the same category position as Stripe for payments or Algolia for search, a comparison they draw themselves: "We're APIs all the way down at Knock—Stripe for payments, Algolia for search, WorkOS for SSO. We're excited to add Knock to that list and to push forward the API-first movement."
How they describe themselves: Developer-tools identity, engineer-led. Verbatim culture signals: "We're an engineer-led team that obsesses over the reliability and availability of our service"; "This position is both high autonomy and high accountability"; "We love to write long-form documents for us, our future selves, and our AI companions." They also say plainly, "As an early stage company, everyone (including you) is involved in building every part of the company," and list responsibilities as "a collection of hats we need you to be OK with wearing." Strong explicit commitment to inclusive hiring and to off-spec applicants ("you can be a great fit even if you don't perfectly match what's described below").
Size / stage / funding: **Series A, 20+ employees.** Investors named in their own JD: Craft Ventures, Afore Capital, Preface Ventures, Worklife Capital, plus angels Guillermo Rauch (Vercel), Scott Belsky (Adobe), Adam Gross (Heroku), John Kodumal (LaunchDarkly), Nate Stewart (Cockroach Labs), Charley Ma and Zach Holman. Early-stage and runway-dependent by definition.
Locations / HQ: "remote-first (with a NYC base)". Both relevant roles are posted as "**New York or Remote**".
Remote policy: Remote-first with an NYC anchor; they describe "a fully distributed, remote-first team" and a written, long-form documentation culture — the closest thing to an async signal in this batch. On-call rotation shared across the whole engineering team.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unverified.** The postings say "New York or Remote" with no country qualifier — neither confirming nor excluding Canada — and no first-party careers/handbook page was reachable this session (their web domain does not appear in the JD text and web search was exhausted, so I did not guess one). A 20-person US Series A frequently cannot employ in Canada without an EOR. Ask directly; do not assume yes.
Engineering & tech: Terraform-backed **EKS** (Kubernetes) being modernized for elastic scale; complex multi-account AWS with VPC structure supporting EKS; **Aurora Postgres, MongoDB and ClickHouse**; queues and streams across SQS, Kinesis and Kafka; observability via Datadog, AWS CloudWatch and Honeycomb; deployment work on canaries, cycle time and blue/green deploys. Stated **>99.95% uptime** track record they intend to keep. Scaling target framed as "another 10x to 100x". They have "fully embraced AI tools… but remain clear-eyed about where they shine and where the pitfalls lie" and are "not overly prescriptive about the tools you can use".
Notable / other: Only 5 open roles total, 2 of them infrastructure — the platform team is a meaningful share of a 20-person company, which is a genuine signal that infra is first-class rather than an afterthought.
Open relevant roles (sample): Infrastructure Engineer (New York or Remote); DevOps Engineer (New York or Remote).
