# FirstPrinciples — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:firstprinciples | company_type: product (non-profit research organization)
What they do: FirstPrinciples is a non-profit building an "autonomous AI Physicist" — an intelligent system intended to explore theoretical frameworks, reason across disciplines and generate novel insights in theoretical physics. In their words it combines "AI, symbolic reasoning, and autonomous research capabilities" into "a closed-loop research engine" covering hypothesis generation, peer review, physics-based simulation, validation against experimental data and self-revision.
How they describe themselves: "a global nonprofit organization, with a Canadian foundation, a US-based 501(c)(3)". Mission framing is explicitly science-advocacy: "accelerate progress on the questions that have captivated humanity for centuries", "a transformative journey where science drives progress and unlocks the potential of humanity". Stated candidate qualities include "Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced environment" and "A passion for advancing knowledge and science advocacy".
Size / stage / funding: Non-profit, funded by donation/grant rather than venture capital. Headcount, budget and funders are NOT stated in any JD and could not be verified — no company domain appears in the registry (only the Greenhouse board), so nothing was fetched. Treat size and financial runway as unknown.
Locations / HQ: Not stated. Postings are location-tagged "Canada - Remote" and "Anywhere - Remote"; a Canadian foundation and a US 501(c)(3) both exist.
Remote policy: Fully remote — all three live roles are remote-tagged, one Canada-specific and two "Anywhere".
Remote-Canada eligibility: YES, verified from the postings themselves — "Member of Technical Staff, Engineering" is posted as "Canada - Remote", and the organization operates a Canadian foundation, so a Canadian employment relationship exists. No province restriction is stated; Mountain Time should be workable given the "Anywhere - Remote" postings.
Engineering & tech: Software work is building the AI Physicist platform: designing and coding the closed-loop research engine, tools for hypothesis generation and peer review, physics simulations, automated unit tests, self-improvement features gated on automated checks, runtime-metric tracking and optimization, custom research tooling. Explicit asks are web development and API integrations, software-architecture patterns, and proficiency with gen-AI tools (ChatGPT, AlphaCode, Claude); cloud infrastructure familiarity is a plus. The Research role goes deeper — novel model architectures, NLP + symbolic reasoning, custom tokenizers for LaTeX symbols and physical units.
Notable / other: No compensation is stated on any of the three postings, and none could be inferred from any company source. A third role is "Member of Technical Staff, Staff Physicist, Quantum Information and AI".
