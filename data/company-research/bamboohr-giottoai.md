What they do: Giotto.ai builds "sovereign" AI systems for Switzerland and Europe — new architectures for reasoning and efficient inference. Their site describes a portable AI model plus an operating system that runs advanced reasoning **on a single GPU**, deployable in the cloud, on private infrastructure, or as a certified hardware appliance. In other words: on-prem/edge-deployable reasoning models, sold on data-sovereignty grounds.

How they describe themselves: From the JD: "Our mission is to build AI capabilities that enable Switzerland and Europe to preserve their **strategic independence, cultural identity, and core values** while achieving world-class performance in advanced reasoning systems." One JD variant uses the phrase "build **sovereign** AI capabilities." From the site: "AI access should not depend on someone else's platform," with emphasis on user control over where models run and how data is handled. They cite research standing in international AI benchmarks, specifically **ARC-AGI**, and say the work "results from years of collaboration between industry and academia."

Signals point to small and early: four named executives (CEO Aldo Podestà, CTO Matteo Caorsi, COO Francesco Palma, CAIO Wallyson Lemes de Oliveira), 5 total live postings in the registry, "a team of qualified professionals across Europe," and one of the two ML roles is offered as a **contractorship** rather than employment. Funding stage and profitability unknown.
