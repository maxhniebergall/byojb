# Workiva — research
Provider/key: workday:workiva/careers | company_type: product

What they do: Workiva sells a cloud platform for regulated reporting — SEC filings (its original wedge was XBRL/EDGAR), financial reporting, ESG/sustainability reporting, audit and internal-controls, and GRC workflows. Customers are large public companies and other heavily-regulated organizations. It is a "boring, mission-critical" enterprise-software domain: the output is compliance artefacts that must be correct and auditable.

How they describe themselves: From their own JDs — "Workiva is building a next-generation AI platform for mission-critical workflows used by some of the world's largest and most regulated organizations… where accuracy, auditability, security, and reliability matter most." On working arrangements they state: "Workiva supports employees in working where they work best - either from an office or remotely from any location within their country of employment," and "Reliable internet access for any period of time working remotely, as we embrace flexible work arrangements." No full values list was captured (no first-party workiva.com page was fetched this run — WebSearch unavailable); notably there is no hustle/rocketship language in the JD bodies.

Size / stage / funding: Public (NYSE: WK), roughly 2,500-3,000 employees, headquartered in Ames, Iowa. Growth-stage SaaS with substantial recurring revenue. (Prior knowledge — unverified this run.)

Locations / HQ: Ames, Iowa HQ. Live reqs are posted as "USA - Remote", "Ames", and one as "Sault Ste. Marie". NOTE: it is UNVERIFIED whether that "Sault Ste. Marie" req means Michigan (US) or Ontario (Canada) — the JD body states only "Location: Sault Ste. Marie" and its pay note is the US range, which points to Michigan. Do not treat it as a Canadian req without confirmation.

Remote policy: Remote-friendly, country-scoped. Their stated policy is "from an office or remotely from any location **within their country of employment**" — i.e. genuine remote, but you must already be employable in that country.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NOT EVIDENCED — treat as no. All six live relevant reqs are "USA - Remote" or US sites, all salary notes are "salary range… for this job in the US", and no req mentions Canada. Workiva's own wording ("within their country of employment") means US-remote reqs are US-only. A BC-based worker would need a Canadian req to exist; none does in the registry.

Engineering & tech: The strongest technical match in this batch. Live reqs cover an AI/ML platform for regulated workflows (architecture, deployment and trust of AI at enterprise scale), staff data engineering, and staff/principal/senior-staff general software engineering — all at the staff-and-above level, all USA-Remote. Emphasis on accuracy, auditability, security and reliability rather than growth velocity.

Notable / other: Comp is posted and strong for remote work: Staff Data Engineer $129,000-$207,000; Staff SWE $151,000-$242,000; Principal SWE $177,000-$284,000; Staff/Senior-Staff MLE $163,000-$261,000 and $193,000-$308,000. Discretionary annual bonus on top.
