# BDC — Business Development Bank of Canada — research
Provider/key: workday:bdc/bdc_careers | company_type: product (Crown corporation / development bank)
What they do: BDC is "the bank for Canadian entrepreneurs" — a federal Crown corporation providing financing (small-business loans, working capital, equipment, real estate), advisory/consulting services, and capital (venture capital and growth equity) to Canadian businesses. It runs dedicated programs for Black, Indigenous, women, young (18–39), and newcomer entrepreneurs, and for technology companies. Note: BDC *sells* consulting to clients; as an employer it is a bank, not a consulting/staffing firm.
How they describe themselves: "We are banking at another level." "Choosing BDC as your employer means working in a healthy, inclusive, and skilled workplace that puts forward the best conditions to bring together unique teams where employees are empowered to act. It also means being at the centre of ambitious economic and financial projects to see further and to do things differently, to fuel the success of Canadian entrepreneurs." Careers page themes: "Progress without boundaries", talent as "our most valuable asset", and recognition as one of "Canada's Best Diversity Employers" for over a decade. They publish a "BDC Way" Culture Book.
Size / stage / funding: "2,900 employees, each talented in their own way." Federal Crown corporation — about as stability-secure as a Canadian employer gets, with no funding-runway risk.
Locations / HQ: HQ Montreal, Quebec, with offices across Canada. Both live engineering postings are Montreal.
Remote policy: "A hybrid work model that truly balances work and personal life" (stated in the JD benefits block). The specific in-office day count is not published on the pages reachable this session.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No, not fully remote.** BDC states a hybrid model and both live engineering roles are located in Montreal, so a Montreal-area base is required. Not workable from Kimberley, BC.
Engineering & tech: .NET / C# backend, Microsoft Dynamics 365 / Dataverse CRM customization (plugins, workflows, integrations), REST and event-based API development and enterprise system integration, Azure DevOps CI/CD, automated testing, a formal Secure SDLC (SSDLC) process, agile squads (Product Owner, Scrum Master, BA, Tech Lead, plus ~4 engineers). Work is maintaining and evolving business-critical systems already in production — root-cause analysis, refactoring, reliability and maintainability. Seven to ten years of experience required.
Compensation: No pay stated on either posting; no bands on record. Benefits are unusually strong: Defined Benefit Pension Plan, Employee Savings and Investment Plan with employer match, $750 wellness/health-care spending account, paid vacation plus five personal days, sick days as needed, and offices closed December 25 – January 1.
Notable / other: **Bilingual (French/English) candidates are explicitly sought** — "we are looking for passionate, bilingual candidates". Internal-customer engineering (account managers and client journeys) rather than sales-driven.
Open relevant roles (sample): SOFTWARE ENGINEERING SPECIALIST (.NET Core/CRM), Montreal; APPLICATION ENGINEERING – SOFTWARE ENGINEERING SPECIALIST, Montreal.
