# SUSE — research
Provider/key: workday:suse/jobsatsuse | company_type: product

What they do: SUSE is one of the oldest enterprise open-source software vendors (operating since 1992). Its products are enterprise Linux (SUSE Linux Enterprise, openSUSE), Kubernetes management (SUSE Rancher, acquired 2020 — "over 100 million downloads to date"), edge and AI infrastructure, and zero-trust security tooling (NeuVector). Customers are large enterprises and Fortune 500s running mission-critical infrastructure, on-prem, multi-cloud and at the edge. This is textbook infrastructure/utility software — the opposite of a product/growth shop.

How they describe themselves: The about page frames the mission as "We believe in building a future for secure, open source innovation," with the positioning lines "we make Linux and manage many," "we make Kubernetes and manage many," "we make zero trust security the default," and the claim that customers can "escape vendor lock-in and regain control of their IT destiny." Open source is described as "an ethos that influences everything we do" and "the community is our past, present and future"; their tagline is "Choice Happens," built on community, interoperability and "relentless innovation." The Life at SUSE careers page adds the internal maxim "nobody is smarter than everybody," asks people to "bring your full, authentic self to work," values different perspectives and out-of-the-box thinking, and describes a fun, social, collaborative atmosphere. They run a career-mentoring program with leadership and give every employee one paid volunteering day per year.

Size / stage / funding: Established, ~30+ years old, roughly 2,000 employees globally. Formerly listed in Frankfurt; taken private again by EQT. Mature and revenue-generating rather than runway-dependent, though it has been through repeated ownership changes (Novell → Attachmate → Micro Focus → EQT), which is the main stability caveat.

Locations / HQ: HQ Nürnberg, Germany. Global footprint including ~20 US offices, plus Czech Republic (a major EMEA engineering site), Brazil, UK, and Asia-Pacific.

Remote policy: Flexible by role — "unless roles require a specific work model, SUSE offers flexibility and choice in how employees work — home-based, office-based, or hybrid." Culturally they also emphasise meeting up and in-person collaboration. Engineering is distributed and open-source-native (public repos, upstream work), which is inherently async-friendly.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, and the currently live relevant roles are not Canada-eligible.** Both live postings in the registry are country-scoped to non-Canadian entities: Staff Software Engineer (core Rancher team) is posted under **Brazil**, and Platform Engineer, AI / "Agentic AI Solutions Engineer" (SUSE Internal IT, Shared Services) is posted under **Czech Republic, EMEA**. SUSE hires through local country entities, and no Canadian entity or Canada-located posting was found. Third-party aggregators list SUSE roles with large "N locations + Remote" pickers, and one aggregator page suggested a Vancouver listing, but that page 404s and could not be verified — treat Canada hiring as unproven.

Engineering & tech: Kubernetes, container orchestration and distributed systems (Rancher: security, scalability, authn/authz), Linux distribution engineering, Go, Helm, infrastructure-as-code, observability, GPU/on-prem LLM inference (vLLM) for the internal Agentic AI Platform. The internal-IT Platform Engineer role is explicitly plumbing work: secrets management, policy enforcement, observability, delivery automation, workload scaling, AI traffic routing, certificate lifecycle, runbooks, peer-reviewed IaC, shared on-call. The Staff Rancher JD is written at a very senior "recognized authority / mastery" register and asks for a Master's or PhD or equivalent recognition.

Notable / other: Open-source-first means much of the engineering work is publicly visible, and career progression includes a real Staff-level IC track. Small two-engineer platform team on the AI platform side means shared ownership and on-call rotation. SUSE's marketing leans on "sovereign" and vendor-lock-in-escape themes, which plays well in EU public sector.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer, core Rancher team (Brazil); Platform Engineer, AI — internal Agentic AI Platform (Czech Republic, EMEA).
