What they do: TRADER Corporation operates Canada's automotive marketplace and dealer-software stack. Flagship brands from their own JD copy: AutoTrader.ca (Canada's largest automotive marketplace, "over 25 million monthly visits"; their marketing page says 26M+ and 7M+ app downloads), AutoHebdo.net (French-language), AutoSync (dealer software for 3,500+ dealers covering operations, marketing and sales), Dealertrack Canada ("the country's top automotive financing portal, processing more than 6.5 million credit applications each year"), and Collateral Management Solutions/CMS (national lien and registration services, recovery services and insolvency management for Canadian lenders). Parent AutoScout24 is Europe's largest online car marketplace.

How they describe themselves: "A Canadian Success Story Since 1975." From the Platform Engineering JD: "We build the internal platform that 600+ developers rely on to ship software quickly and safely: centralized Kubernetes, managed CI/CD, project templates, and the developer tooling that runs on top of AWS. Our customers are developers. We live 'you build it, you run it' and we invest in self-service and a product mindset so a small team can have a big impact." On AI: "The AI is the means, not the end. What we care about is a faster, smoother day-to-day for every engineer, and AI is the biggest lever we have." On culture: "At AutoScout24 / Trader we celebrate diversity as a strength. Our organization comprises 50+ nationalities working together inclusively and respectfully." Other JDs use "you build it, you run it", "owns results, not just implementations", and "If you can think it, you can build it. If you build it, you own it." There is a mild bias-for-shipping streak: "you'd rather build something and learn from it than research it forever", "A bias for shipping."

Size / stage / funding: Established (marketplace operating since 1975), 600+ developers internally per their own JD, owned by AutoScout24 GmbH (Germany), which is itself Hellman & Friedman-backed. Mature, revenue-generating, not runway-dependent.

Locations / HQ: Toronto, Ontario (Canada HQ). Parent in Munich, Germany. All postings in this registry entry are tagged "Toronto, ON (Canada)".

Languages: Python, Go, or TypeScript. (2) CMS/product engineering — TypeScript, React/Next.js and .NET (Core/modern), rebuilding auto repossession, remarketing and lien-registry workflows, explicitly "isn't afraid of legacy systems", with LLMs used for data validation and document processing. They also expect demonstrated use of GenAI/agentic coding tools (Claude, Codex) in day-to-day work.
