# Prenuvo — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:prenuvo | company_type: product

What they do: Prenuvo operates preventative-health screening clinics built around a fast (<1 hour), radiation-free, contrast-free whole-body MRI protocol used to detect cancer, aneurysms and other disease early. It sells directly to consumers (paid screening, largely out-of-pocket) and to employers as a health benefit. The product combines MRI hardware protocols, in-house software (patient platform, scheduling, reporting) and increasingly AI-assisted image analysis. Founded by CEO Andrew Lacy and founding radiologist / biomedical engineer Dr. Raj Attariwala.

How they describe themselves: Their stated purpose is shifting healthcare "from reactive sick-care to proactive health care," and making MRI "seamless and more widely accessible." Culture language on their careers material is mission-forward and growth-flavoured — "embrace bold thinking, action, and experimentation to transform healthcare," and JD copy says "Help reshape the world through proactive healthcare while working with cutting-edge technology and high performing teams." Their Staff Backend JD explicitly frames the role for someone who "thrives in ambiguity, moves pragmatically" and describes the company as "at a critical stage of growth, scaling our platform, modernizing legacy services."

Size / stage / funding: Private, VC-backed growth-stage healthtech. Investors include Felicis Ventures plus notable angels (Anne Wojcicki, Tony Fadell, Eric Schmidt). Headcount is in the several-hundreds range across clinics + corporate; clinic expansion is ongoing and capital-driven rather than profitability-driven. Actively recruiting radiologists at scale.

Locations / HQ: HQ Redwood City, California, with a large Canadian engineering/operations presence rooted in Vancouver, BC (the founding radiologist's practice). Clinics across the US and Canada, with stated plans for domestic and international expansion.

Remote policy: Remote-friendly rather than remote-first. For the engineering role in scope the JD states verbatim: "We have a strong preference for candidates based in Vancouver, BC who are able to work in a hybrid capacity. However, we are open to remote candidates across Canada." Radiologist roles are explicitly remote with company-provided workstations.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes** — the Staff Engineer (Backend) posting is listed in both Vancouver, BC and Toronto, ON, states openness to remote candidates across Canada, and requires legal authorization to work in Canada with no visa sponsorship. A BC-based remote worker is directly hireable, and Pacific-time-anchored teams are workable from Mountain Time. Caveat: Vancouver hybrid is the stated preference, so a fully remote Kootenays candidate is the second choice, not the first.

Engineering & tech: Patient Platform team owns APIs, orchestration services and patient communication infrastructure (email/SMS/notifications). Stack: Python (FastAPI/Flask), AWS event-driven (EventBridge, SQS/SNS, Lambda, ECS), async task processing (Celery-class), REST and/or GraphQL API governance, BFF/orchestration patterns. Work explicitly includes modernizing tightly-coupled legacy services into event-driven components, reliability/observability/fault tolerance, and secure handling of PHI/identity data in a regulated environment. Staff role is positioned as "technical authority for the domain" with mentorship and design-review duties — real architectural scope, not a full-stack catch-all.

Notable / other: Consumer-pay preventative imaging is a contested clinical space (professional bodies have questioned population-level whole-body screening), so regulatory and reputational risk is a real business factor. Employee benefit: two whole-body scans per year (employee + partner) plus a flexible vacation policy. Business is capital-intensive (clinic buildout), which ties stability to continued fundraising.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Engineer – User Experience (Backend) — Vancouver BC; Staff Engineer – User Experience (Backend) — Toronto ON.
