# LifeWorks (TELUS Health) — research
Provider/key: workday:lifeworks/external | company_type: product
**Entity note:** the registry calls this "Lifeworks", but the JD body identifies the employer as **TELUS Health** — LifeWorks was acquired by TELUS and the Workday tenant simply kept the old name. Postings are branded TELUS Health.
What they do: Employee health, well-being and **benefits administration** at national scale. In their words: "TELUS Health is empowering every person to live their healthiest life ... a global-leading health and well-being provider - encompassing physical, mental and financial health." The specific team hiring is Benefits Administration: "the backbone of how millions of Canadians access and manage their employee benefits. We maintain and evolve the platforms that serve some of Canada's large enterprises."
How they describe themselves: Mission-led health framing ("Guided by our vision, we are leveraging the power of our leading edge technology and focusing on the uniqueness of each individual to create the future of health"). The team's own stated value is end-to-end ownership: "We value developers who own their work end-to-end. You won't just write code — you'll analyze tickets, understand business context, collaborate directly with stakeholders, implement solutions, and deploy them to production." They also state a clear communication-over-brilliance preference: "We value a developer with great communication over one who is technically brilliant but isolated," and ask for "Resourcefulness and initiative ... push solutions forward without constant oversight."
Size / stage / funding: Part of TELUS (TSX/NYSE: T), a large public Canadian telecom. Established and stable; not funding-dependent. TELUS Health is a global division.
Locations / HQ: Toronto, Ontario for these postings; TELUS is headquartered in Vancouver/Calgary with TELUS Health operating globally.
Remote policy: Role-dependent, and only one posting states it. The Intermediate Backend Developer is titled "- Remote" and is filed under "2 Locations" with a Toronto anchor. The Senior Lead Software Developer and Senior Software Developer - Configuration postings are listed plainly as "Toronto, Ontario, Canada" with no remote statement.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Partial and unclear.** Canadian employment is certain (Canadian employer, Toronto roles). Only the *Intermediate* Backend Developer role is marked Remote, and even that is Toronto-anchored — whether it is open to a BC/Mountain-Time worker is not stated anywhere in the posting. The two senior roles show no remote designation at all and should be treated as Toronto-based.
Engineering & tech: Legacy-forward Microsoft stack. Two named platforms: "Powerflex, our legacy system that powers critical client configurations, and ABL (Area Benefits LifeWorks), our modern, next-generation platform." Required: "Strong expertise in C# and deep familiarity with the .NET 4.8 ecosystem", "Advanced relational database skills with hands-on experience writing and optimizing T-SQL queries. SQL Server is preferred; Oracle experience is equally valued", APIs and backend service layers, CI/CD deployment, Agile ceremonies with Business Analysts. Asks 5+ years but is explicitly an *intermediate* posting: "We value demonstrated ability to code and communicate over raw years of experience."
Notable / other: The domain is genuinely mission-critical and unglamorous — "Your code powers how Canadian employees access their healthcare, dental, pension, and welfare benefits." Work is ticket-driven with direct business-stakeholder contact.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Lead Software Developer (Toronto); Senior Software Developer - Configuration (Toronto); Lead Software Developer - Configuration; Intermediate Backend Developer (C# & SQL Server) - Remote.
