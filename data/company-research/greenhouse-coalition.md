# Coalition, Inc. — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:coalition | company_type: product

What they do: Coalition sells "Active Insurance" — cyber insurance bundled with the security technology used to prevent the loss in the first place. Founded 2017, it underwrites cyber and technology risk for small and mid-sized businesses (distributed through brokers) while running Coalition Control, an attack-surface-management product that continuously scans the internet and customer environments. The scanning/threat-intelligence data feeds directly into underwriting, so the security engineering is the pricing engine, not a side product.

How they describe themselves: Their own JD copy: "Coalition is the world's first Active Insurance provider designed to help prevent digital risk before it strikes… combines comprehensive insurance coverage and innovative cybersecurity tools." Careers material lists values around being customer-obsessed, integrity and excellence, "the collective good," and protecting "the unprotected"; also diversity, passion, innovation, respect, empathy, impact and team spirit. Culture framing is "unafraid to take risks," employees encouraged to "share ideas, speak up, and push boundaries." JDs ask for "comfort operating in a fast-paced, evolving environment," and marketing copy says "opportunities to make an impact with bold thinking are real—and happening daily."

Size / stage / funding: Private, late-stage and heavily capitalized; grown to 650+ employees across six countries, with roughly 70% headcount growth in a recent year. Named to the Forbes FinTech 50, Fast Company's Most Innovative Companies, and Inc.'s Best Workplaces (2023 and 2024). An insurance carrier/MGA model means regulated, recurring premium revenue rather than pure SaaS burn.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco, with "global office spaces" and distributed teams across six countries; engineering explicitly spans the US, Canada and the EU.

Remote policy: Remote-first since inception, per their own careers page, with flexible working hours, a work-from-home stipend, flexible PTO, and offices available but not required. Distributed-team practices include deliberately sharing insights/data broadly, plus offsites and in-person meetups.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, strongly.** Five of their currently-open engineering postings are listed with location "Any location, Canada" (Senior Software Engineer; Senior Software Engineer, Underwriting; Senior Software Engineer, Scanning Engine; Senior Software Engineer, AI Platform & Agents; Principal Software Engineer, Security), each mirrored by a US-located twin. The Scanning Engine JD states the team is "a distributed team spanning the US, Canada, and EU." Nothing restricts to a metro, so a BC-based worker is directly hireable; the EU+US spread implies working hours skewed earlier than Mountain Time but with real overlap.

Engineering & tech: Scanning Engine & Data Collection team owns the distributed systems that continuously scan the internet and customer environments — large-scale cloud services, internet-wide scan orchestration, third-party threat-intel ingestion and normalization, high-throughput pipelines under strict SLAs. Languages are Python and Go ("proficiency in at least one, willingness to work in both"). Cloud-native: microservices, containers, CI/CD, monitoring/logging. Roles are described as owning end-to-end systems design, driving architecture and roadmap, code-review rigour, design documentation, secure-coding practice and mentorship. Separate open tracks for security engineering, underwriting systems, and an AI Platform & Agents team.

Notable / other: The underwriting-plus-scanning loop is an unusual and durable technical moat — security data quality directly changes loss ratios. As a regulated insurer, correctness and auditability matter more than shipping speed in parts of the stack. Compensation philosophy is "competitive salaries and equity compensation for every full-time employee," and US JDs disclose geographic-tiered bands under transparency law.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (Scanning Engine) — Any location, Canada; Senior Software Engineer, Underwriting — Any location, Canada; Senior Software Engineer, AI Platform & Agents — Any location, Canada; Principal Software Engineer, Security — Any location, Canada.
