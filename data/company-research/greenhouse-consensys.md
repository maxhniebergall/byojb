# Consensys — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:consensys | company_type: product

What they do: In their own words, "Consensys is the leading blockchain and web3 software company.
Founded by Joe Lubin, CEO of Consensys and Co-Founder of Ethereum in 2014." Its flagship products are
**MetaMask** — a crypto wallet and gateway to the decentralized web, "over 30 million monthly active
users", a dApp development platform, an aggregator of decentralized exchanges, and a decentralized
identity manager — plus **Infura** (blockchain node/RPC infrastructure) and **Linea**, described as
"the only 100% proven zkEVM rollup and an emerging home for institutional ETH capital." Newer MetaMask
work includes consumer money movement and a MetaMask Card (crypto-to-fiat payments).

How they describe themselves: Mission — "to unlock the collaborative power of communities by making
the decentralized web universally easy to access, use, and build on." They frame the market as "the
financial system is being rebuilt on open, programmable infrastructure, and Consensys is helping power
that transition." On the team: "Joining Consensys means working with a fully remote, globally
distributed team of technologists, designers, cryptographers, product thinkers, and researchers who
are building the next layer of the internet. You'll be exposed to new ideas, emerging technologies,
and complex challenges that push you to stay at the top of your game." MetaMask's own framing: "a
future where the internet and world economy empowers people through interactions based on consent,
privacy, and free association." The DevSecOps JD lists the qualities it wants as "technological
skills, organizational skills, thinking strategically, security first mindset, pro-activity, problem
solving & creativity, pedagogical methodology, team spirit and strong documentation practices" — an
unusually documentation- and security-forward description.

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2014. Private, venture-backed at multi-billion valuation (raised at
~$7B in 2022). Roughly 600–900 employees after several rounds of workforce reductions in 2023–2024.
Revenue is real (MetaMask swap fees, Infura, Linea) but strongly correlated with crypto market cycles.

Locations / HQ: Nominally New York, but effectively HQ-less — "fully remote, globally distributed."
Hiring geos on the live postings: United States, **Canada**, EMEA and LATAM.

Remote policy: **Fully remote by design**, globally distributed, no office requirement stated
anywhere in the postings.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **YES — verified, first-party and explicit.** Three of the four relevant
live roles list "CANADA - Remote" directly in the location field: Senior Software Engineer - MetaMask
Card (Canada/EMEA/LATAM/US remote), Senior Staff DevOps Engineer - MetaMask (US/Canada/LATAM remote),
and Staff Software Engineer - MetaMask Money Movement (Canada/LATAM/US remote). The exception is
Staff Software Engineer: Consumer (Money), which is US/EMEA only. Being globally distributed across
US, EMEA and LATAM means timezone expectations are genuinely flexible; Mountain Time is unremarkable
here. Note: Canadian compensation is explicitly *not* the posted US range (see Pay).

Engineering & tech: A real infrastructure surface alongside consumer product work. The MetaMask &
Infura DevSecOps role covers delivering, upgrading and maintaining infrastructure to ISO/SOC2
cybersecurity standards; CI/CD pipelines; setting up, configuring and running dev/test and
staging/production infrastructure "across multiple products and critical applications and multiple
cloud providers (AWS, Azure)"; and driving DORA-metric improvement across the group. It collaborates
with developers, SREs, product managers, a Customer Success SRE function and cross-cutting
infrastructure teams. Elsewhere: zkEVM/cryptography (Linea), node infrastructure (Infura), browser
extension and mobile clients (MetaMask), and payments/money-movement backends. Background checks
(employment, education, criminal) are a stated condition of employment for some roles.

Notable / other: Consensys has run multiple layoff rounds (2023 and 2024) and its fortunes track the
crypto cycle closely; it has also been in litigation with the SEC over MetaMask. It publicly warns
about recruitment fraud impersonating the company. The alumni network is strong ("Consensys alumni
have moved on to become tech entrepreneurs, CEOs, and team leads").

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Staff DevOps Engineer - MetaMask (US/Canada/LATAM remote); Staff
Software Engineer - MetaMask, Money Movement (Canada/LATAM/US remote); Senior Software Engineer -
MetaMask Card (Canada/EMEA/LATAM/US remote); Staff Software Engineer: Consumer, Money (US/EMEA
remote); Senior Data Engineer.

Pay: All posted ranges are **US-only**. Every JD carries: "The salary range listed for this role
applies to US-based candidates only. Compensation for candidates based outside the US (including
Canada, EMEA, and LATAM) will be determined based on location, experience, and skills during the
interview process, and may differ from the listed US range." Posted US base ranges: Senior Staff
DevOps Engineer - MetaMask $164,000–$218,000; Staff SWE - MetaMask Money Movement $146,000–$218,000;
Staff SWE Consumer (Money) $164,000–$196,000; Senior SWE - MetaMask Card $130,000–$187,000. A
Canadian offer should be assumed lower than these, by an amount the company does not disclose.
