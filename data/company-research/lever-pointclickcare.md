# PointClickCare — research
Provider/key: lever:pointclickcare | company_type: product
What they do: PointClickCare is a healthcare SaaS vendor for the senior-care / long-term and post-acute-care sector, running one of North America's largest care-collaboration networks. Its cloud EHR and care-coordination platform connects skilled nursing and senior-living providers with payers, hospitals and physicians. Founded 2000; a mature, revenue-generating enterprise vendor rather than a startup.
How they describe themselves: Purpose-led — "we make a real difference in the lives of people" — with stated emphasis on inclusivity, innovation and community impact, and "a culture of empathy and accountability where people consistently show up for one another." The careers material foregrounds work-life flexibility: "we recognize the value of work-life flexibility and encourage discovering a balance that meets your unique and personal needs." Tone is steady enterprise-healthcare, not hustle.
Size / stage / funding: Large private company (thousands of employees across North America), long-established (2000), backed historically by Dragoneer and Hellman & Friedman. Mission-critical regulated healthcare software — a "boring", sticky domain.
Locations / HQ: HQ Mississauga, Ontario, Canada; major second site in Salt Lake City, Utah; additional US presence.
Remote policy: Mixed by role and stated per posting. Many engineering roles are posted explicitly as "Remote – Canada", "Remote – US" or "Remote, USA"; a separate tranche of Mississauga roles are hybrid. Remote employees are told to expect travel to the Mississauga and/or Salt Lake City office for onboarding, team events and semi-annual/annual team meetings.
Remote-Canada eligibility: Verified yes for specific roles. Live postings "Senior Applied Research Data Engineer (Canada)" and "Senior Applied Researcher AI/ML ( CAD)" are listed as "Remote- Canada" with CAD salary ranges. Note carefully: the Principal-level and general Software Engineer roles (Principal Software Engineer, Principal Software Engineer – Authorization Platform, Senior SWE java/react, Intermediate SWE) are Mississauga, Ontario **hybrid** and are not workable from British Columbia. Several other strong roles (Principal Data Engineer, Principal AI Platform Engineer, Senior SWE – AI Platform) are US-remote only.
Engineering & tech: Java-heavy enterprise backend, big-data and streaming/real-time data systems, plus a newer AI/ML platform group (AI Platform, applied research, AI-enabled DevOps). Titles indicate a real ladder to Principal.
Notable / other: Compensation is posted on essentially every JD, in the currency of the hiring geography (CAD for Canadian roles, USD for US roles) — unusually transparent for a private company. Twelve company-stated pay bands are already on record in the registry from those postings.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Applied Research Data Engineer (Canada); Senior Applied Researcher AI/ML (CAD); Principal Data Engineer (streaming and real-time data systems); Principal AI/ML Platform Engineer; Principal Software Engineer – Authorization Platform; Senior Software Engineer – Big Data & Java.
