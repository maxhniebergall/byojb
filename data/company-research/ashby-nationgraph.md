# NationGraph — research
Provider/key: ashby:nationgraph | company_type: product

What they do: NationGraph is a public-sector sales-intelligence platform. In their own words: "NationGraph is making public sector data accessible and actionable for businesses selling to cities, counties, state agencies, schools, and special districts. NationGraph's data and intelligence engine provides buying signals derived from millions of public sector sources. Founded in 2024, NationGraph is dedicated to making uncommon knowledge common, because public data should actually be public." Their site adds the customer-facing framing: it surfaces buying signals before RFPs become public, automates prospecting and research, and supplies verified decision-maker contacts, with "110,000 agencies covered" and the pitch "Most vendors find out about opportunities when they're already public. NationGraph gives you the intelligence to compete earlier and win more."

How they describe themselves: Team credentials, quoted: "Has successfully built, scaled, and sold companies in the past. Built software infrastructure processing billions of dollars in transactions. Is backed by world-class VCs and operating partners who've invested in, and built, iconic companies." Hiring principles, quoted: "Speed: We aim to move from round to offer quickly"; "Respect: Every round has a clear purpose. No redundant interviews."; "Signal over ceremony: We care more about what you've built and how you think than where you went to school."; "Mutual evaluation: This is a two-way process. You're interviewing us, too!"; "Bar-raising: Every hire should make the team stronger. When in doubt, we pass." On scope and stakes: "Own problems end to end in a small, senior team... Every decision you make materially changes the trajectory of the company."

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2024. Early-stage, venture-backed ("backed by world-class VCs and operating partners"); specific round, amount and headcount are not disclosed on the site or in the postings. Described as "a small, senior team".

Locations / HQ: Toronto, Ontario. Both tracked postings are Toronto.

Remote policy: Explicitly in-person by default, in their own words: "We believe in-person work is our default. We're building a team of people who desire to work side-by-side and shape the culture of a growing company. In our experience, some of the best ideas come from being in the room, feeling the pain, hearing the nuance, and catching the details that are easy to miss. At the same time, we're flexible and supportive when working-from-home is needed to maintain a healthy, balanced work environment." The flexibility offered is occasional WFH, not distributed work.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO. The company states in-person Toronto work as its default and frames remote only as an accommodation "when working-from-home is needed". A Kimberley-based, Mountain-Time, fully-remote arrangement is contrary to their stated model. Canadian employment is not the issue — location is.

Engineering & tech: TypeScript monorepo (API plus React frontend) on PostgreSQL. The Senior Software Engineer, Data Engineering role is substantive pipeline and LLM-infrastructure work in their own words: "Own the systems behind NationGraph's unique differentiated asset: the data acquisition platform that collects public information/records at scale, (purchase orders, contracts, various procurement documents) from 100k+ government entities across the country"; "This is real data engineering, not dashboard plumbing. You'll build high-volume interaction and ingestion pipelines with retries, dead-letter handling, checkpointing, and resumability; turn messy government documents (PDFs, spreadsheets, scans, portals) into clean structured data"; "Build with AI at the core: LLM-driven document extraction, classification, and form processing at scale are already in production here, but there's a wide-open opportunity to make them more modular, rigorous, cheaper, and faster." Requirements name idempotent processing, retries with backoff, dead-letter handling and recovery, checkpoint/resume for long-running jobs, backpressure, graceful degradation, Postgres schema design and safe live migrations, document processing at scale with deduplication, and "LLM integration - calling model APIs at scale for extraction and classification, managing rate limits and cost across providers, structuring prompts and outputs so they're testable". They also run human-in-the-loop operations tooling under SOC 2 constraints (access controls, audit logging). Bar: 5+ years building production backend systems.

Notable / other: Compensation is posted and strong for the stage: Senior Software Engineer, Data Engineering at CA$150,000–200,000 plus equity; a (now-closed) Senior Software Engineer posting listed CA$180,000–230,000 plus equity. Hiring process is four rounds ending with the founders, with a take-home or live case exercise.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Data Engineering (Toronto, CA$150–200K + equity); (PHD) Applied Machine Learning Engineer.
