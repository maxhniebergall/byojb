# Binary Stream Software — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:binarystream | company_type: product
What they do: Independent software vendor (ISV) and SaaS company building financial/ERP extensions for the Microsoft Dynamics ecosystem (Dynamics 365 Business Central, Dynamics GP/FO). Products cover multi-entity management, subscription billing, property management and materials management for Dynamics customers. Over 25 years in business with thousands of customers worldwide.
How they describe themselves: "An award-winning ISV and SaaS company with over 25 years of experience delivering solutions built for Microsoft Dynamics." Core values stated as **Team, Innovation, and Growth**. They describe themselves as "a collaborative, inclusive team" that stays "focused on what matters most - our people, our culture, and the impact of the work we do." Stated culture points from the JD: "Wellness and work life balance is prioritized", "We work to support your growth", "Hybrid work environment", "Our CEO leads by example and truly cares about employees", "Culture of respect, integrity, continuous improvement, and transparency."
Size / stage / funding: Private, established (founded ~2000, "over 25 years"). "Global team and thousands of customers worldwide." Recognised as a **BC Top Employer for 2026** (fifth consecutive year) and one of **Canada's Top Small & Medium Employers for 2026** — both awards imply a headcount in the small/medium band (roughly 100-500). No funding/venture stage indicated; appears bootstrapped or self-sustaining.
Locations / HQ: Head office at 7300 Edmonds St #800, Burnaby, BC V3N 0G8, Canada. Global team referenced but no other offices confirmed.
Remote policy: **Hybrid, head-office-anchored.** The JD states plainly: "This position requires the individual to work at our Burnaby head office on a hybrid basis." Listed as a benefit: "Hybrid work environment."
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — not remote-eligible.** Verified from the company's own JD: presence at the Burnaby BC head office is a stated requirement. A Canadian citizen elsewhere in BC (e.g. Kimberley, ~9h drive) cannot satisfy this without relocating.
Engineering & tech: Microsoft Dynamics stack — AL development language for Business Central extensions, the Business Central AL testing framework, AppSource and PTE extension deployment, CI/CD release process, MS SQL Server and T-SQL stored procedures. Actively pushing AI: "Design, develop, and maintain AI agents and Copilot-enabled features within Business Central extensions" using the Business Central AI Development toolkit, plus expected proficiency with AI-assisted dev tools (GitHub Copilot, Claude Code or equivalent). Engineers report to a Senior Development Team Lead; the role includes mentoring juniors, code reviews, and leading approved R&D initiatives.
Notable / other: Hiring process is unusually gated — applicants must complete "a comprehensive behavioural assessment" plus a technical assessment, and successful applicants must pass a criminal background check and education verification. The Software Developer role additionally **requires two live Microsoft certifications** (MB-800 Business Central Functional Consultant Associate and MB-820 Business Central Developer Associate) — a hard credential gate, not a nice-to-have. Accounting certification listed as an asset.
Compensation (as posted): $52.40 per hour, stated flatly on the JD. At 2,080 h/yr that is approximately **$109,000 CAD/year**. Benefits: dental, extended health, on-site parking.
Open relevant roles (sample): Software Developer (Burnaby, BC — hybrid); Software Developer – Dynamics 365 Business Central.
