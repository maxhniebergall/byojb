# Five9 — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:five9 | company_type: product

What they do: Five9 sells cloud contact-center software (CCaaS) — "a leading provider of cloud contact
center software, bringing the power of cloud innovation to customers worldwide", with AI, automation and
omnichannel capabilities layered on top. Their about page tagline is "Creating Powerful Customer
Connections Since 2001". They serve "3,000+ enterprise, mid-market, and SMB customers worldwide".
Underneath the CX product is a large, genuinely heavy private-cloud infrastructure estate (voice
platforms, OpenStack, software-defined storage, Kubernetes), which is where most of the relevant
openings sit.

How they describe themselves: "Join us in bringing joy to customer experience." On culture, from their
own JD boilerplate: "Living our values everyday results in our team-first culture and enables us to
innovate, grow, and thrive while enjoying the journey together. We celebrate diversity and foster an
inclusive environment, empowering our employees to be their authentic selves." Their about page frames
the mission as being "driven by a passion to transform contact centers into customer engagement centers
of excellence, coupled with a deep understanding of the cost and complexity involved in running a
contact center." Tone is warm-corporate; no hustle/rocketship vocabulary, though one senior JD does say
"all while keeping shipping velocity high."

Size / stage / funding: Public (NASDAQ: FIVN, IPO 2015), "2,600+ employees around the world". Not
runway-dependent, though CCaaS is a competitive market facing AI-driven disruption of seat-based pricing.

Locations / HQ: Worldwide HQ in San Ramon, California — "opened new worldwide headquarters in San Ramon,
CA, to accommodate rapid company growth" (2013). Registry postings are located "United States (Remote)",
"India (Remote)" and "Colombia (Remote)". No Canadian location appears anywhere in the registry.

Remote policy: Roles are posted as location-scoped remote — every live relevant posting is labelled
"<country> (Remote)". The about page does not state a company-wide remote policy. So: remote, but within
specific countries.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO. All nine live relevant postings are "United States (Remote)",
"India (Remote)" or "Colombia (Remote)". The extracted facets on the US roles record geo_eligibility
"us_only". There is no Canadian posting, no Canadian office reference, and no language suggesting
Canada-based hiring. A US-remote role requires US work authorization, which is a hard blocker here.
The only Canada-adjacent note found anywhere is a "Canada login portal" for a product (Studio IVA) —
customer-facing, not an employment signal.

Engineering & tech: This is the strongest infrastructure content in the batch. From live JDs:
"build, automate and operate large-scale Software Defined Storage (SDS) and Kubernetes platforms in a
private cloud environment... with Infrastructure-as-Code and GitOps practices while ensuring
scalability, resilience, and performance", "enterprise storage platforms (Pure Storage, HPE, NetApp) and
SDS solutions (Ceph, Longhorn)", "self-service storage workflows for Kubernetes CSI and OpenStack
consumers (VM and Baremetal)", Ansible/Terraform/Helm/Git with Python/Bash automation, and observability
on "capacity, latency, IOPS, recovery health". Another role: "own the transformation of existing
internal tools to be 12-factor compliant... embedding world-class telemetry (Grafana, Prometheus, Loki)
throughout the platform... transition legacy pipelines into Kubernetes-native GitOps workflows, and
mentor mid-tier developers". Other stacks named: Go, Ceph, Pulumi, vSphere, KVM, Infoblox/BIND,
Ubuntu/RHEL/CentOS, C++ for the Global Voice team, ITIL methodology. Roles ask 6+ years and are
explicitly on-call.

Notable / other: Hiring is heavily distributed to lower-cost geographies — of the live relevant roles,
several are India- or Colombia-based, and the Indian/Colombian mirrors of US titles suggest ongoing
offshoring of the DevOps function. Posted US bands are extremely wide (a $90K-$250K spread on a single
senior req), which is a geo-tiered national range rather than a real band for one location. Benefits
cited on a JD: "Paid Time Off", "12 weeks paid parental leave".

Pay (first-party, stated on their own live/recent JDs):
