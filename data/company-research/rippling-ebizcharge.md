# EBizCharge — research
Provider/key: rippling:ebizcharge | company_type: product
What they do: EBizCharge develops integrated electronic payment-processing solutions embedded inside ERP, CRM, mobile and eCommerce systems. Their gateway/plugin suite is PCI compliant and integrates with QuickBooks, Sage, SAP Business One, Microsoft Dynamics, NetSuite, Epicor, Acumatica, Magento and WooCommerce. Customers are B2B merchants that want card/ACH acceptance inside the accounting system they already run. Revenue model is payment processing plus per-ERP integration software.
