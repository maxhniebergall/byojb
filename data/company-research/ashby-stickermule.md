# Sticker Mule — research
Provider/key: ashby:stickermule | company_type: product

What they do: Sticker Mule is a custom-printing and e-commerce company ("the Internet's most 'kick ass' brand") that prints stickers, labels, magnets, packaging and promotional products. In their own words they are "building the Internet's most lucrative commerce platform by combining software, manufacturing, and AI into one fully integrated stack. No print company, or pure software company for that matter, can match what we're doing." The engineering org is now also building **Sticker Mule Pro**, a suite of SaaS tools for creators and businesses: Stores (a Shopify alternative), AI tools (internal and customer-facing), Notify (a Mailchimp alternative), Reply (a Zendesk alternative), Hire Me (a Contra alternative), and mobile apps.

How they describe themselves: The careers page names five characteristics they hire for and try to embody — **Humble** ("modest view of one's abilities and importance"), **Thoughtful** ("concerned about the well-being of others and the company"), **Independent** (capable of working autonomously while still collaborating well), **Autodidactic** (self-directed learners willing to continuously improve), and **Logical** (strong decision-making). They explicitly say they aim for a "**stress-free work environment**" that attracts like-minded people doing meaningful work without excessive pressure. On going remote: "people make businesses and going remote" is the most efficient way to hire "the best possible people", who are "more productive than local teams". The engineering JD's "How we work" list is blunter: "Small, autonomous teams that own products end-to-end"; "Prioritize what matters right now and **pivot instantly** when needed"; "Use AI aggressively to push the limit of what we can do"; "**Move fast**, build for the masses, and **deprioritize edge cases**."

Size / stage / funding: **Privately owned, profitable, 9-figure annual revenue, no outside investors** — their own words: "no meddlesome investors." This is a bootstrapped, financially independent business, not a venture-runway company. Founded 2010.

Locations / HQ: HQ Amsterdam, New York (upstate NY), with manufacturing/print facilities including Amsterdam NY, Albany NY, Gaffney SC, and Pisa, Italy. Corporate presence spans **17 countries across 4 continents** and 11 US states; the engineering org describes itself as "fully remote from 40+ countries".

Remote policy: **Fully remote** for engineering — "Fully remote from 40+ countries and collaborate using Asana and Slack." Async-leaning tooling (Asana + Slack, no meeting-heavy signal). Flexible work hours. Note the tension: the manufacturing side is necessarily onsite, but software roles are not.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes.** Canada is explicitly on their published list of countries where they employ people (US, Argentina, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Brazil, Canada, Croatia, Finland, France, Germany, Guatemala, India, Italy, Poland, Slovakia, Spain, Sweden, UK), and the software engineer req is "Remote" with a 40+-country footprint. High confidence.

Engineering & tech: **Go, TypeScript, React, Expo, GraphQL, Postgres, running on GCP.** One codebase shipping to both web and mobile. Small autonomous teams own products end-to-end. Heavy internal AI-tooling adoption is a stated requirement, not just a perk ("You use AI aggressively"). The advertised role is explicitly full-stack: "You are an exceptional full-stack software engineer."

Compensation (stated on their own live JD): **$150,000–$250,000 USD salary plus a $20,000 signing bonus**, 4 weeks vacation plus country-specific holidays, fully remote. Careers page adds: "above market compensation to hire the best people", signing bonuses for all new hires, health/dental for US employees, hardware/software provided, ergonomic equipment (standing desks, premium chairs).

Notable / other: Unusually transparent about pay for a private company. The founder/CEO (Anthony Constantino) has been publicly outspoken on political topics, which has drawn periodic controversy and boycott campaigns — worth being aware of as a cultural/reputational factor. The business is genuinely durable (profitable, no investors) which is rare in this queue.

Open relevant roles (sample): "Software engineer" (Remote, $150K–$250K + $20K signing bonus).
