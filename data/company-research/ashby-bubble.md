# Bubble — research
Provider/key: ashby:bubble | company_type: product

What they do: Bubble is a no-code/visual app-development platform. In their own words: "We built Bubble with a clear mission: to empower everyone to create software. Our AI visual development platform lets anyone, from first-time entrepreneurs to enterprise teams, take an idea from prompt to fully-functional, scalable app across web, iOS, and Android." Reach: "With over 6 million users in more than 100 countries, Bubble is breaking down the barriers to entrepreneurship and innovation worldwide." The product is positioned as a hybrid of AI generation and a structured visual editor — "the only fully visual AI app builder that lets you vibe code without the code to go beyond prototypes and launch real apps to real users. Chat with AI when you want speed, edit directly when you want control." It bundles hosting: "a unified web and native mobile editor, enterprise-grade hosting, security, database management, and automatic scaling."

How they describe themselves: Mission-forward and, at present, explicitly framed around the AI moment. "The rise of AI-generated software has validated everything Bubble has been building toward for over a decade. But pure AI-generated code is fragile, hard to debug, and rarely production-ready. Bubble bridges that gap." The recruiting pitch: "The people who join Bubble right now will help define what that means for millions of builders around the world. If you've ever wanted to work on something that genuinely changes who gets to build, this is your moment." Closing: "Let's democratize access to technology together!" They are notably inclusive in hiring language: "If you don't meet all of the qualifications but think you could be a match, we'd still love the chance to review your application. At Bubble, we encourage people from all ages, abilities, and experiences to apply." No formal values list or engineering handbook was located.

Size / stage / funding: Private, more than a decade old ("building toward for over a decade"), with 6M+ users across 100+ countries. Headcount, funding round and profitability are **not stated in the available material and were not verified.** The company is well past early stage but its financial position is not documented here.

Locations / HQ: New York City, Manhattan. "Our team is based in NYC and works Eastern Time." They mention "our Manhattan office" and offer relocation assistance.

Remote policy: **Remote-friendly, US-only, with an in-office preference.** Their own words: "This is a remote-friendly role open to candidates authorized to work in the U.S. Our team is based in NYC and works Eastern Time." And: "We prefer hiring within commuting distance of our Manhattan office because we value getting together in person regularly — folks who come in enjoy catered lunches, team events, and other perks." Remote is supported but the company states a preference for proximity, and the perks accrue to those who come in. No async practices are described; the team "works Eastern Time."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — verified negative from the company's own words.** The posting states the role is "open to candidates authorized to work in the U.S." and then goes considerably further, enumerating exactly which US states they can and cannot employ in: "We're currently set up to hire across most U.S. states. States we're not currently set up to employ in: AK, AL, AR, DE, HI, ID, IA, KS, KY, LA, ME, MT, NE, NH, NM, ND, OK, RI, SC, SD, VT, WV, WY." A company that tracks its employment footprint at US-state granularity and lists 23 US states it cannot hire in has no Canadian employment vehicle. This is unusually clear evidence. Disqualifying. (Separately, the team works Eastern Time — a three-hour offset from Mountain.)

Engineering & tech: Stated company stack, verbatim —
- Frontend: TypeScript, SolidJS, React Native
- Backend: TypeScript, Node.js, Rust
- AI/ML: Python, LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.), LangChain
- Infrastructure: AWS, CDK / Terraform, ECS, Redis, Postgres
- Data & AI Workflows: JSON-based app representations, proprietary datasets

Engineering is organized into "pods." The open role sits on a newly formed one: "The Core Developer Experience (DX) Pod is a new team dedicated to improving the productivity, quality, and satisfaction of engineers working on Bubble's core product and monolith. Our mission is to modernize the developer environment, reduce friction in day-to-day workflows, and unlock sustainable long-term velocity for the entire Core org." Notably, the core product is described as a monolith carrying real legacy weight — the pod's work includes "upgrading tooling and libraries, migrating off deprecated frameworks, strengthening support for our internal design system, speeding up testing and bundling, and addressing technical debt that slows down product delivery." The pod deliberately works through leverage: "Some initiatives we drive directly, while others involve building standards, frameworks, and example implementations that empower other feature teams to adopt best practices." The role requires "5+ years of professional software engineering experience" and "Strong proficiency with modern frontend frameworks (TypeScript, SolidJS/React, etc.)."

Notable / other: Customer success stories are cited as proof of the platform: "Mailead grew a $10K investment into a $2M valuation, and Faceless.video went from zero to $1M+ ARR in under a year." Compensation is disclosed: "We offer competitive compensation aligned to tier one markets. Our estimated salary for this role at Bubble ranges from $183,000 to $237,000." Benefits are quantified: "a robust benefits package equating to roughly twenty thousand in additional annual compensation," including "Comprehensive health coverage," "401(k) matching," "Wellness and work enablement stipends," "Generous PTO," and — unusually — "A Sabbatical program." A sabbatical program at a decade-old company is a meaningful tenure and sustainability signal. Relocation assistance to NYC is offered.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Core Developer Experience — Remote / NYC, $183K–$237K + equity. Also live per the registry: Senior Software Engineer, Backend; Senior Applied AI Engineer.
