# Curve Dental — research
Provider/key: rippling:curve-dental | company_type: product

What they do: Curve Dental builds cloud-based dental practice-management software — an all-in-one platform covering scheduling, clinical/perio charting, imaging, treatment planning, billing, insurance workflows, payments and patient engagement. Customers are independent dental practices and multi-location DSOs across the US and Canada; the company states it serves "over 80,000 dental professionals within the United States and Canada." Founded 2004, launched commercially at the Waypoint Conference in 2009. It is currently building what it calls a "shared intelligence platform" bringing agentic AI into revenue-cycle management, patient communications, scheduling, clinical documentation, insurance workflows and operational automation.

How they describe themselves: The company positions itself as "singularly focused on the dental industry" and "committed to delivering the best all-in-one dental practice management, imaging, and patient engagement software for practices of all sizes." On the AI push its own JDs say: "We believe the future of dental software isn't simply AI-powered, it is AI-enabled… our goal is to create intelligent systems that help practices operate more efficiently while allowing dental teams to focus on delivering exceptional patient care." On working style, the JDs state plainly: "We move quickly, collaborate closely, and take ownership. Whether you're designing a new platform capability, reviewing code, troubleshooting a production issue, or helping another team solve a difficult problem…" No formal published values list was found.

Size / stage / funding: Privately held, PE-backed. Battery Ventures invested in 2018; in 2022 leadership announced a commitment to reinvest $60 million into the platform over three years. Not venture-runway dependent — an established, revenue-generating vertical SaaS business, 22 years old. Exact headcount not disclosed publicly.

Locations / HQ: HQ Alpharetta, Georgia. Offices in Atlanta GA; Provo UT; Calgary, Alberta; and Aberdeen, Scotland.

Remote policy: Not stated as a company-wide policy on the website. However, the ATS itself is the evidence: current engineering reqs are posted in two parallel, explicitly remote variants — "United States Remote" and "Calgary, Alberta, Canada Remote." The Canadian engineering presence is anchored on the Calgary office.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** Two of the four live relevant reqs are posted as "Calgary, Alberta, Canada Remote" (AI/ML Software Developer, AI/ML Platform Software Developer), duplicated against US-remote twins. Calgary is Mountain Time, so the working-hours overlap is exact rather than merely workable. Whether "Calgary remote" means Alberta-only or Canada-wide is not stated in the JD text and would need confirming with the recruiter — the province requirement is the one open question.

Engineering & tech: Cloud-native SaaS (multi-tenant practice-management platform, HIPAA-scoped). The current build-out is an agentic-AI platform layer: production AI capabilities, ML model deployment, intelligent workflows and agent orchestration across the product, with roles described as working "alongside a highly experienced architecture team" and helping "establish the development patterns and practices that enable intelligent applications across our products." Historic technical milestones include patented cloud-based digital image capture (2011) and mobile access (2013). Recent AI integrations with third parties Pearl AI (radiographic diagnostics) and Bola AI (voice charting).

Notable / other: Multiple "Best of Class" dental technology awards. The domain is boring-but-mission-critical infrastructure — practice-management software is the system of record a dental office cannot run without, which implies stability and low churn. No public engineering blog or handbook found.

Open relevant roles (sample): AI/ML Platform Software Developer (Calgary, Alberta, Canada Remote); AI/ML Software Developer (Calgary, Alberta, Canada Remote); AI/ML Platform Software Developer (United States Remote); AI/ML Software Developer (United States Remote).
