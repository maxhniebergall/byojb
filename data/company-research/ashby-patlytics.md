# Patlytics, Inc. — research
Provider/key: ashby:patlytics | company_type: product

What they do: Patlytics builds an AI-native patent-intelligence platform for intellectual-property professionals. In their own words they are "the fastest-growing AI-native patent intelligence platform, transforming how IP is protected and monetized at scale. Our advanced LLMs and generative AI engine — custom-built for intellectual property — power every phase of the patent workflow from invention disclosure through litigation, delivering citation-backed insights with unprecedented accuracy and speed." Product surface named on their site: patent application drafting, infringement detection, invalidity analysis, claim charting, office-action analysis, patent portfolio management, and a purpose-built AI agent for patent work. Customers are patent attorneys, IP counsel and law firms.

How they describe themselves: Their site emphasises security and data handling — SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001 and ISO 42001, GDPR compliant, and an explicit commitment that "your data is not used to train or tune models" — plus "built by IP experts with real-world patent experience" and "move faster without lowering standards". The JD is considerably more high-energy: "we're scaling fast across product, engineering, and go-to-market. **If you want to build at the inflection point, this is it.**" "With teams across the globe we're building a global company fueled by exceptional talent and bold ideas. Our rapid expansion is driven by people who bring **entrepreneurial energy**, intellectual curiosity, and a shared commitment to setting the global standard for AI-powered IP intelligence." On hiring bar: "**We hire for trajectory, not just credentials. If you're 70% of this list and hungry to close the gap, apply.**" On scope: "these responsibilities are just the beginning! As we continue to grow, we encourage you to **contribute wherever you observe opportunities in the business that align with your interests.**"

Size / stage / funding: Venture-backed growth-stage startup. They state a **$40M Series B led by SignalFire**, with participation from N47, Myriad Venture Partners, Liquid 2 Ventures, BAM Corner Point, Alumni Ventures, Antiportfolio Ventures and Relativity — explicitly to "triple down on the product, accelerate growth globally, hire future mission-aligned colleagues". Headcount not published; the language ("scaling fast", "rapid expansion") suggests active hypergrowth. Series B means this is funding-driven rather than profitability-driven.

But the requisition is city-scoped ("New York, Toronto") with no "remote" qualifier and no remote-policy statement anywhere on the site, which normally indicates an in-office or hybrid expectation at one of those two hubs. Engineering & tech: The AI Engineer role has genuinely substantive LLM-systems content, well beyond prompt-tuning:
- Multi-step LLM pipelines for patent claim analysis, infringement detection and prior-art search, including "model selection, prompt architecture, and structured output contracts that define product quality".
- **A rigorous evals framework** — success metrics per pipeline stage, golden datasets curated from real IP cases, continuous regression testing "to catch degradation before users do". The JD reinforces this culturally: "You think in evals first. Before writing a prompt, you ask 'how will we know if this is better?'"
- **Retrieval systems** — vector search, BM25 hybrids, re-ranking, tuned for patent-corpus characteristics: long documents, technical claim language, biological sequence identifiers.
- **Agentic workflows** in their Agent layer — coordinating tool use, memory and multi-turn reasoning over complex IP research tasks.
- Data classification and post-training on current LLMs; fine-tuning datasets encoding expert judgment from patent attorneys, then measuring whether it worked.
- **Production inference economics** — owning cost, latency and quality tradeoffs: prompt-caching strategies, context-window management, batching, and model-migration planning across AWS, GCP and other LLM providers.
- Monitoring tooling for data-pipeline performance, data quality and production models; unit testing, profiling, parameter tuning; partnering with data engineers and the platform team on real-time and batch pipelines; ongoing R&D on new technologies and tooling.
Bar: "You've shipped LLM-powered features that real users depend on, not just demos or side projects… a track record of turning prototype prompts into production pipelines with monitoring and fallbacks."
