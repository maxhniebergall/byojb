# OPAL-RT Technologies — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:opalrt | company_type: product
What they do: OPAL-RT Technologies builds **real-time simulation** hardware and software — hardware-in-the-loop and power-system/power-electronics simulation platforms used for testing control systems before they touch physical equipment. In their own words their ambition is "to democratize real-time simulation in order to make it accessible to every engineer and researcher". Their tools are used "in a multitude of sectors such as automotive, aerospace, energy, transportation, industrial manufacturing, etc.", and they name their customers directly: **NASA, Bombardier, ABB, EMBRAER, MIT, GE, Ford, GM, NREL, RTE and SNCF**.
How they describe themselves: The JD sells the company through a "A career with OPAL-RT will suit you if:" list, which is the fullest statement of values available: "You are seeking **stimulating challenges** that will constantly push you to do your best"; "You dream of a **family environment** that is also professional, cooperative and friendly"; "You need a **flexible schedule and the ability to telecommute** in order to balance work and home life"; "You want to continue to expand your knowledge and know-how by working side-by-side with researchers, innovators and scientists who share one thing; **Passion**"; "You have chosen to be an important contributor to technological progress in a multitude of industries"; "You want to be **recognized for your ideas, creativity and talents**"; "You know your worth and seek a **globally competitive salary**"; "You don't just want a job, you want a career!" They also write "Founded in Quebec and now with a global presence, OPAL-RT has the wind in its sails", and refer to employees as "OPALIENS". Equal-opportunity statement present; the JD is published bilingually (French first, English following).
Size / stage / funding: Established private Quebec company with a global presence and blue-chip industrial/research customers. Headcount, ownership and financials **could not be verified** — attempts to fetch opalrt.com and opalrt.com/company both failed at the network level (ECONNREFUSED), so nothing beyond the JD is confirmed.
Locations / HQ: Founded in and headquartered in **Quebec** — the posting is Montreal, Quebec, Canada. "Global presence" is claimed but the offices are not enumerated in reachable material.
Remote policy: Partially flexible. The JD's own pitch offers "a flexible schedule and the ability to telecommute in order to balance work and home life", but the posting is explicitly located in Montreal, Quebec, and the role is described as working "under the supervision of the Team Lead / MACHLAB" on internal CI infrastructure and on-premise virtualisation (Proxmox, VMware, physical/virtual machines) — work that is naturally tethered to a lab and its hardware. Telecommuting here should be read as flexibility within a Montreal-based job, not as location-independent remote.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No.** Two independent blockers, both stated in the company's own JD. (1) The role is posted "Montreal, Quebec, Canada, Permanent" with telecommuting framed as work-life flexibility rather than remote hiring, and the underlying work is on-premise CI/lab infrastructure. (2) **"Language abilities: French mandatory."** — with only "functional English" needed for communication outside Quebec. A Montreal-anchored, French-mandatory position is not workable from Kimberley BC.
Engineering & tech: Internal developer-infrastructure and CI work, reporting to the MACHLAB team lead, in an Agile environment. Build and evolve internal tools in **Python, Groovy, Shell, Batch**; automate build, test, deployment and environment management; maintain and evolve CI environments and DevOps tooling (**Jenkins, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI**); manage virtual infrastructure including VMs, on-premise environments and cloud (**Proxmox**, VMware, Azure or AWS); analyse and improve build systems, dependency trees and packaging; document tools and practices and train internal users. Assets listed: Kubernetes/Docker, Ansible/Terraform/OpenTofu, Conan/Artifactory/Maven/npm, build reproducibility and artifact management, complex multi-platform/multi-compiler builds, Grafana/Prometheus observability, Scrum/Kanban, Git/GitLab, Linux. Required experience is **3 to 5 years** — a mid-level rather than senior posting.
Notable / other: The substance of the work is genuinely appealing on the utility/infrastructure axis — internal developer platform and build infrastructure serving the company's own engineers, i.e. predictable internal customers and no sales pressure, in a safety-and-engineering-critical simulation domain. The registry records no comp slots and no salary is posted; the JD only offers the aspirational "globally competitive salary".
