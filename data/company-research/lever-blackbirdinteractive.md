# Blackbird Interactive — research
Provider/key: lever:blackbirdinteractive | company_type: product

What they do: An independent video game development studio. Their own words: "Blackbird Interactive is a creative-fueled, future-driven, independent game studio where the best talent in the industry can find long-term careers to help grow their abilities while working on a wide range of projects, which encompass world-class IPs as well as our own creations across multiple genres." So the business is a mix of work on licensed/major IP and original titles — i.e. both co-development/porting work for other publishers and their own games.

How they describe themselves: The recurring self-description emphasises independence, creative drive, breadth of projects, and — notably — "long-term careers" and growth of abilities, which is unusually stability-flavoured language for the games industry. Full formal values/principles list: NOT FOUND (no company site was fetchable — see Sources).

Size / stage / funding: UNVERIFIED. Independent (i.e. not publisher-owned) per their own wording; no headcount, funding, or profitability figures were obtainable. Five simultaneous open engineering roles suggests active hiring rather than contraction, but that is an inference, not a fact.

Locations / HQ: Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. All five live postings carry the registry location "Vancouver, BC, Canada, remote".

Remote policy: Remote-first with flexibility, stated in their own words: "Our studio operates as a remote-first workplace, offering flexible work arrangements." No async-specific claims or core-hours statement was found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: VERIFIED YES, and explicitly Canada-restricted. From the JD, verbatim: "PLEASE NOTE: At this time, we are only able to hire candidates who are legally eligible to work in Canada and reside within Canada." Combined with "remote-first workplace", this is a genuine Canada-resident remote employer. Being Vancouver-based, it is also the best timezone match in this batch for a Kimberley BC / Mountain Time candidate (one hour offset from Pacific, no Eastern-hours problem).

Engineering & tech: Unreal Engine 5 is the central technology — open roles cover UE5 rendering, gameplay AI, and platform/porting work. There is a distinct Platform Engineering (porting / co-dev) function and a DevOps/Tools function, so engineering is organised with real internal-infrastructure teams alongside game teams rather than gameplay-only. No public engineering blog or handbook was located.

Notable / other: Comp is posted on every live role, which is unusually transparent and is the reason this company already has three band rows on file. Posted CAD base ranges, taken directly from their own live JDs:

Sector context worth recording: the video game industry has been through a sustained contraction with heavy studio layoffs, and co-development/porting revenue depends on external publisher contracts. Neither risk is stated by the company; both are external context.
