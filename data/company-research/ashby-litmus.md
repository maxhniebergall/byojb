# Litmus (Litmus Automation Inc.) — research
Provider/key: ashby:litmus | company_type: product
What they do: Litmus builds an industrial data platform for manufacturing: connecting machines/PLCs/sensors at the edge, normalising and governing operational data, and making it usable for analytics and AI. Products are Litmus Edge, Litmus Edge Manager, Litmus Unify (unified namespace), Litmus Data Catalog and a Litmus MCP Server. Customers named on their site include Philips, JLR, SLB, Parker, Pfeifer & Langen and NatureFresh Farms; partners include Google, Microsoft, Dell, Oracle and Mitsubishi.
How they describe themselves: "Litmus is building the data foundation that powers industrial AI... AI doesn't work without real-world, contextualized data." On culture: "collaborative, curious, and low ego. People are scrappy, take ownership, and look for ways to make an impact. We value empathy just as much as execution." Also: "We're building a team that holds a high bar and pushes each other to improve... We hire people who take ownership, move quickly, and care about outcomes. No passengers." And: "We're a growing company, so things move quickly and not everything is perfectly defined."
Size / stage / funding: ~100 employees (schema data on litmus.io); describes itself as a "growth-stage software company", "Backed by leading investors". Exact round/funding total not verified.
Locations / HQ: HQ Silicon Valley (Santa Clara), CA; offices in Toronto ON, Munich, Tokyo and Pune. Co-founders: Vatsal Shah (CEO), John Younes (COO), Sacha Sawaya (CFO).
Remote policy: Not stated on the public site. All three live engineering postings are tagged Toronto, Canada (one dual-listed Santa Clara), and the registry's extraction reads them as hybrid. The Lead DevOps role explicitly leads "a distributed DevOps team spanning North America and India".
Remote-Canada eligibility: Canada-eligible YES (Toronto entity, CAD salary bands) but REMOTE-Canada is UNVERIFIED and the postings read as Toronto-office/hybrid — effectively disqualifying for someone in British Columbia unless the role is confirmed fully remote.
Engineering & tech: Edge computing, distributed systems, real-time industrial data. DevOps stack disclosed in the Lead DevOps JD: self-hosted GitLab CI/CD with a runner fleet, multi-cloud AWS + GCP, Kubernetes (EKS), an on-premises VMware estate, infrastructure security sub-team, DORA metrics (lead time, deployment frequency, MTTR, change fail rate). Also Golang backend and a Java principal role.
Notable / other: Compensation is posted on their Ashby JDs in CAD for Toronto and USD for Santa Clara — Senior SWE Golang CA$120K–150K; Senior SWE Industrial CA$125K–170K (Toronto) / US$160K–200K (Santa Clara). The Lead DevOps role reports directly to the Head of Technology.
Open relevant roles (sample): Lead DevOps Engineer (Toronto); Senior Software Engineer – Golang (Toronto); Senior Software Engineer – Industrial (Santa Clara / Toronto); Principal Software Engineer – Java.
