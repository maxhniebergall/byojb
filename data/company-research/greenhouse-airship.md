# Airship — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:airship | company_type: product

What they do: Airship is a customer-engagement / cross-channel customer-experience platform, historically the mobile push-notification infrastructure company (formerly Urban Airship). Its own site describes a no-code, AI-powered platform covering push notifications, SMS/RCS/MMS, email, mobile wallet, in-app experiences, journey orchestration, data collection and analytics. Its JD states it is "trusted by world's leading brands such as Alaska Airlines, BBC and The Home Depot"; the site lists Alaska Airlines, AccuWeather, Orange, SXSW, Chipotle, BBC and PayPal across retail, travel, media, finance and telecom. Underneath the no-code surface it runs a genuinely large-scale distributed data system — the JD says it processes "billions of mobile app interactions."

How they describe themselves: Positioning is that "brands are challenged to deliver seamless, unified customer experiences across a fragmented array of channels and devices," and Airship's platform "was designed with non-technical, growth-focused teams in mind." Its stated work philosophy is a named "Digital First" approach: "Airship believes that flexible work contributes to a more productive and more equitable work environment, and that Airshippers are able to collaborate, innovate, and support one another across different locations and timezones." Hiring commitment language is a standard broad EEO statement plus explicit Fair Chance consideration of applicants with criminal histories. It also publishes an unusually candid AI-in-hiring disclosure: AI tools are "decision-support tools only", People Ops reviews all AI-generated scores, "no applicant is rejected solely on the basis of an AI-generated output", and candidates may request human review or opt out.

Size / stage / funding: Private, long-established (Urban Airship dates to ~2009), well past startup stage — but headcount, ownership and profitability are **not disclosed** on the fetched homepage and remain unverified. The role offers stock options ("a direct opportunity to share in Airship's success"), so it is still equity-issuing private.

Locations / HQ: Portland, Oregon is the long-known HQ but was **not confirmed** on any page fetched this session; the homepage discloses no addresses. The posting is served from Greenhouse's EU board (job-boards.eu.greenhouse.io), which suggests an EU entity exists, but that is inference, not a stated fact.

Remote policy: Explicitly remote-friendly by policy. "Airship's 'Digital First' approach to work means that for the majority of our roles, work can be performed remotely, either some or most of the time." Some roles are location-bound; this one is "fully remote and may require up to 10% travel." Benefits include "a digital-first work environment and a monthly stipend to support remote work."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Not for this role — the registry records the posting's location as "Remote, United States."** The JD text itself says "fully remote" without naming a country, but the recorded location field, the USD-denominated band and the US-centric benefits (medical/dental/vision, CCPA notice) all point to US-remote. No Canadian entity or Canada-eligible language was found. Treat as US-remote unless Airship states otherwise; unverified whether it employs in Canada at all.

Engineering & tech: Java is the primary language. The stack named in the JD is Java services, Google Bigtable, Pub/Sub, CloudSQL and BigQuery — i.e. GCP-centric, streaming plus batch, data-intensive. The hiring team is "Shared Services", a small internal-platform team that "works to improve the efficiency, reliability, and visibility of that system through shared libraries, platform improvements, and automation/tooling." The work described is investigative rather than feature-driven: "hypothesizing, testing theories, documenting observations, and leveraging changes to improve our entire architecture, not just implementing targeted features," plus performance/cost-inefficiency diagnosis, cost modelling and reporting, and JVM memory/performance tuning. Airship maintains a public blog at airship.com/blog.

Compensation: The JD posts a starting base range of **$145,000 – $165,000 USD** plus stock options (already on record in data/company-comp.jsonl as a direct jd_posted band for platform_infra/senior). Benefits: medical/dental/vision for employee and dependents, flexible time off, company holidays, paid parental leave, paid volunteer time, mental-health/wellness resources, employer-subsidised life and short/long-term disability, remote stipend, mentorship.

Notable / other: Security notice that Airship only contacts candidates from @airship.com addresses. Legal/privacy material at airship.com/legal, including a California employee/candidate privacy notice.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Distributed Systems Engineer (Remote, United States).
