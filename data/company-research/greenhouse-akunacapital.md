# Akuna Capital — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:akunacapital | company_type: product

What they do: Akuna Capital is a proprietary trading firm — an options market maker. Their own
description: "Akuna Capital is an innovative trading firm with a strong focus on collaboration,
cutting-edge technology, data driven solutions, and automation. We specialize in providing liquidity
as an options market maker – meaning we are committed to providing competitive quotes that we are
willing to both buy and sell. To do this successfully, we design and implement our own low latency
technologies, trading strategies, and mathematical models." Their careers page calls them "a leading
proprietary trading firm specializing in options market making". They trade their own capital; there
are no external customers, which makes the engineering entirely internal-facing. The firm organises
itself into four areas: **Technology, Trading, Quantitative research, and Operations**.

How they describe themselves: "We provide our employees with endless opportunities for individual
growth and ownership." Recruiting framing: "Own your impact on day one", "team up with top minds in
trading and technology, take on meaningful challenges, and quickly see the direct impact of your
work." The Platform Engineer JD adds: "This is an exciting role where you will be given the
opportunity to take ownership of projects and make them your own. At Akuna, we are constantly looking
for ways to improve our efficiency as we continue to grow." On inclusion: "Akuna values the unique
perspectives people can bring to the table to collaboratively solve complex problems and drive Akuna
forward. We want everyone to feel empowered to apply." No formal enumerated values list was found.

Size / stage / funding: Private partnership-style prop trading firm, self-funded from trading
profits rather than venture capital — structurally stable in a way VC-backed companies are not, though
revenue is inherently tied to market volatility. Founded conceptually in Sydney; first office opened
**2011** in Chicago. Headcount not disclosed on the pages available.

Locations / HQ: **HQ Chicago** — "Our Founding Partners first conceptualized Akuna in their hometown
of Sydney. They opened the firm's first office in 2011 in the heart of the derivatives industry and
the options capital of the world – Chicago." Additional offices: **Sydney, Shanghai, London,
Singapore**. No Canadian office.

Remote policy: Not stated anywhere in first-party text. The careers page is a job portal and
discloses no remote policy; the Platform Engineer posting is tagged "Remote" by the ATS. Prop trading
firms of this type are overwhelmingly office-based, and the role's own description — working "closely
with our Trading, Development, Infrastructure, and Operations teams" and monitoring "our
hybrid-cloud environments", including **on-premise Kubernetes** — implies proximity to physical
infrastructure. Treat the "Remote" tag with scepticism; it is unsupported by anything the company
itself says.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, leaning no — and effectively disqualifying regardless.** The
posting is tagged "Remote" but the compensation is disclosed under the **Illinois Equal Pay Act**,
which anchors the role to Illinois; there is no Canadian office or entity; and no statement about
hiring outside the US exists. Beyond eligibility, the role states as an *essential function*: "The
ability to react quickly and accurately to rapidly changing market conditions, including the ability
to quickly and accurately respond and/or solve math and coding problems are essential functions of
the role." That ties the working day to US market hours (Chicago, Central Time — markets open 08:30
CT, i.e. 07:30 Mountain) and to real-time incident response during them.

Engineering & tech: Genuine infrastructure and platform work, internally facing. Scope: "design and
implement cloud solutions, automate infrastructure as code, improve cloud governance and security,
and monitor our hybrid-cloud environments." Specific responsibilities: ensure cloud services (AWS,
Azure, Google Cloud) "are leveraged to their full potential and best practices are being followed";
"manage our public cloud growth and continuously improve Akuna's hybrid-cloud operating model";
"evaluate proposed solution architectures and provide recommendations"; "provision, configure,
upgrade, and monitor technologies such as **Kubernetes (EKS and on-premise), Spark, Kafka**, and other
large-scale distributed databases"; "automate infrastructure management and development pipelines
with tools like **Terraform, Ansible, Jenkins and GitLab**"; and "explore new technologies in the
cloud-native/container ecosystem". Also required: "proficiency in automation and scripting with
Python, Bash, or similar languages"; "experience with cloud-native data services, including Amazon
RDS, Aurora, Redshift, and DynamoDB"; and "demonstrated ability to lead technical initiatives,
influence architectural decisions, and mentor engineers". Elsewhere the firm hires heavily for C++
(low-latency trading systems), MLOps and data engineering — the broader technology org is substantial
and is treated as core to the business rather than as a cost centre.

Notable / other: Level and title are deliberately deferred: "Please note that level/title will be
determined upon the conclusion of the interview process." They run distinct Entry Level and Intern
pipelines. Total compensation includes "a discretionary performance bonus" plus "a comprehensive
benefits package that may encompass employer-paid medical, dental, vision, retirement contributions,
paid time off, and other benefits" — at prop trading firms the discretionary bonus is typically a
large fraction of total comp, so the disclosed base substantially understates the package.

Open relevant roles (sample): Platform Engineer (IT Infrastructure, tagged Remote); MLOps Engineer;
Software Engineer - C++; Software Engineer - C++, Trading Strategies; Software Engineer - Data
Engineering.

Compensation on record: **US$145,000 minimum base**, disclosed by Akuna on the Platform Engineer JD:
"In accordance with Illinois Equal Pay Act, the minimum base salary starts at $145,000. Exact
compensation offered may vary based on many factors including, but not limited to, the candidate's
experience, qualifications, and skill set." This is a stated **floor**, not a range or a midpoint —
the actual band extends upward by an unknown amount, and excludes the discretionary bonus.
