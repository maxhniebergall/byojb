# IFS — research
Provider/key: smartrecruiters:ifs1 | company_type: product

What they do: IFS is an enterprise software vendor — ERP, enterprise asset management (EAM), field service management and, increasingly, "industrial AI"/agentic products — sold to asset- and service-intensive industries (aerospace & defense, energy/utilities, manufacturing, telecom). It operates several acquired product brands under the IFS umbrella, visible directly in its own job titles: IFS Copperleaf (asset investment planning, Vancouver), Softeon (supply chain/WMS, Reston VA), and "Nexus Black" and "Loops" units for agentic-AI products. Registry shows 252 total postings on its board — a large, multi-geography organisation.

How they describe themselves: No corporate values page was reachable this session (see Sources), so this is from their own JD text. The tone in the Copperleaf SRE posting is notably substantive and anti-fluff: the role is "a hands-on technical owner of our cloud infrastructure … you take full ownership of every system within Cloud Operations' purview", the CI/CD ownership is "active ownership, not support", and they want someone who works with R&D "as a technical peer, not a service desk", who "raise[s] the technical bar through the quality of your engineering work". On working style they state: "We embrace flexibility and hybrid work opportunities to support diverse needs and lifestyles, while also valuing inclusive workplace experiences. By fostering a sense of community, we drive innovation, strengthen connections, and nurture belonging." They also disclose AI use in hiring: "we may use automated tools, including artificial intelligence, to help screen and assess applications … These tools do not make hiring decisions."

Size / stage / funding: Large established enterprise-software vendor (hundreds of open roles across Europe, North America and Asia; multiple acquired product lines). Exact headcount, ownership and financials were NOT verified first-party this session — treat any figure as unverified.

Locations / HQ: Postings cluster in Staines-upon-Thames and London, England; plus Copenhagen (Denmark), Itasca, Illinois and Reston, Virginia (US), and Vancouver, British Columbia (the Copperleaf unit). The English postings dominate, which suggests the UK is a principal operating centre; the legal HQ was not verified.

Remote policy: Hybrid-flexible, stated as a company-wide boilerplate ("We embrace flexibility and hybrid work opportunities…") but not as remote-first. Two postings are explicitly tagged "Remote" in their location line (Staines-upon-Thames UK Remote; Itasca, Illinois US Remote). The Canadian role is not.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO, for the roles currently open. The only Canadian posting is "Senior Lead Site Reliability Engineer | IFS Copperleaf", located Vancouver, British Columbia, with no remote designation and only the generic hybrid-flexibility boilerplate; the Stage-3 extractor read it as hybrid/Canada. The two roles marked Remote are UK-remote and US-remote respectively, not Canada. There is no evidence of a remote-anywhere-in-Canada arrangement.

Engineering & tech: The Copperleaf SRE role is a strong, deep infrastructure spec: Azure-based multi-tenant SaaS; Terraform and/or ARM/Bicep IaC; Ansible for configuration management and drift remediation; CI/CD across Jenkins, Azure DevOps and GitHub Actions (including pipeline architecture, build performance, secrets handling and toolchain migration); AKS operation end-to-end (node pools, autoscaling, network policy, ingress, workload identity, persistent storage, upgrade lifecycle, capacity planning); observability across Azure Monitor, Log Analytics, Application Insights, Prometheus, Grafana and Pingdom; PagerDuty owned end-to-end including escalation policies and on-call schedules; incident command and post-mortem remediation; certificate lifecycle, secrets management and SOC 2 Type II / ISO 27001 / ISO 9001 compliance work. Elsewhere the org is hiring heavily into "Agentic AI", identity and access management platform work, and Java/Python data and ML engineering.

Notable / other: The Vancouver SRE posting states its salary range openly ("$133k and $151k CAD"), which is not required in BC for all employers and is a good transparency signal. A large share of current openings are AI/agentic-branded, including sales roles ("AI Sales Executive - Agentic AI Loops"), which reads as an active company-wide AI product push. Roles carry unusual title formatting with double pipes ("Principal Platform Engineer || Agentic AI || Identity and Access Management").

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Lead Site Reliability Engineer | IFS Copperleaf (Vancouver, BC); Principal Platform Engineer || Agentic AI || Identity and Access Management (Staines-upon-Thames, UK, Remote); Senior Principal Platform Lead || AI & Agentic Systems (London); Principal AI Engineer - Aerospace & Defense (Itasca, IL, Remote); Cloud Engineer I - Softeon (Reston, VA).
