# BigCommerce — research
Provider/key: workday:bigcommerce/commerce | company_type: product
What they do: BigCommerce is a SaaS e-commerce platform for merchants — building and running online stores — describing itself as "empowering a community of merchants to reach their fullest potential." It also owns Feedonomics, a product-feed management business whose engineering team is called out in its JDs as "fully remote."
How they describe themselves: Four stated values — "Stronger Together" (collaboration and diverse perspectives), "Keep It Real, Always" (honesty, trust, authenticity), "Own It to Win It" (accountability and data-driven decisions), and "Customer Compass" (customer-focused decision-making). It highlights growth "from a 2-person startup to a global team of hundreds" and being "recognized as one of the Best Places to Work in Austin, San Francisco and Australia." Infrastructure JD language is notably good: "We are a remote-first team that values collaboration and prioritizes team over individual achievements, innovative problem solving, and operational efficiency."
Size / stage / funding: Publicly traded on NASDAQ (IPO 2020); its own page gives the ticker as CMRC (commonly listed as BIGC — treat the ticker as unverified). "A global team of hundreds." Public-company stage, past the runway-risk phase, though e-commerce SaaS has been a soft market.
Locations / HQ: HQ in Austin, Texas (The Domain). Additional offices in San Francisco CA, Sydney Australia, London UK, and Kyiv Ukraine.
Remote policy: Remote-first in language, hybrid in practice near offices. Its JDs state: "For candidates based in the Austin or Atlanta metro area, the position follows a hybrid work model with three days per week in the office"; and for the Lead Infrastructure role, "This role is open for remote work within U.S. time zones or can be based in our Austin, Texas office." Postings are tagged #LI-REMOTE. Distributed team members are in "Austin, San Francisco, Sydney and remote."
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO on current evidence. The US-remote roles state "Applicants must be authorized to work in the U.S." and are scoped to "U.S. time zones"; other remote roles are Australia-remote or London-based. Canada is not listed as a hiring geography in any registry posting, and no Canadian entity was found. Mountain Time is within US time zones, so the constraint is legal work authorization, not timezone.
Engineering & tech: Stack named in its own infrastructure JD: "predominantly working in Golang, Ruby, and Scala, backed up by Terraform and Puppet for provisioning and configuration management." Also PHP (the Feedonomics backend senior role) and a substantial AI/agents engineering push (multiple "AI Agent"/"AI Specialization" backend roles). Ladder: Software Engineer II → Senior → Lead. Feedonomics engineering is explicitly fully remote and works on "core infrastructure components and services that power our robust, high-performance, data-intensive SaaS platform."
Notable / other: Strong pay transparency — ranges posted on US JDs (e.g. Lead Software Engineer USD 110,000-216,000; Senior Backend PHP USD 135,960-203,940; Backend SWE II AI USD 95,000-160,000). UK and Australia postings state no range. Kyiv office implies a wartime-resilient distributed setup.
