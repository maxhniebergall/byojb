# Radformation — research
Provider/key: lever:radformation | company_type: product

What they do: Radformation builds automation software for radiation oncology — tools that automate and standardize the radiotherapy treatment-planning workflow (contouring, planning, plan evaluation, treatment assessment, machine QA). Customers are cancer clinics and their medical physicists; the company reports 2,000+ clinic installations as of 2024. Founded 2016 by clinical physicists. Acquired Limbus AI in 2024 (AI auto-contouring).

How they describe themselves: "Radformation is transforming the way cancer clinics deliver care." Their stated focus is three-fold: "Time savings through automation", "Error reduction through automated systems", "Increased quality care through advanced algorithms and workflows". Mission framing on their own site: "By automating and standardizing the treatment planning workflow, Radformation aims to help create a system where any patient anywhere can receive the same high-quality care." Their JD says "We are a fully remote, mission-driven team united by a shared goal: to reduce cancer's global impact and help save more of the 10 million lives it claims each year." Traits they say make someone successful there: enjoys complex mathematical/computational problems; balances algorithmic innovation with practical engineering; cross-disciplinary collaboration; ownership and maintainable, well-tested code; curiosity and continuous learning. Notably absent from their own copy: hustle/"fast-paced"/rocketship language. They also publish an "AI & Hiring Integrity" policy asking candidates not to use real-time answer-generating tools during interviews.

Size / stage / funding: ~200 employees as of 2025, having grown 25 (2018) → 50 (2021) → 100 (2023) → 150 (2024) → 200 (2025). Private; funding stage not stated on the pages read (unverified). Steady multi-year headcount growth and a clinical install base suggest a real revenue business rather than a runway-driven startup, but profitability is unverified.

Locations / HQ: HQ 261 Madison Avenue, 9th Floor, New York, NY 10016. No other offices stated; team is distributed.

Remote policy: Fully remote. "Fully remote work environment with virtual events and yearly retreats." Self-managed PTO + 10 paid holidays, monthly internet stipend, company laptop plus one-time home-office setup stipend. Async practices not explicitly described (unverified).

Remote-Canada eligibility: Yes — well evidenced. The live "Senior Software Engineer, Adaptive" posting lists its location as "Remote US, remote, Remote Canada", and the benefits section distinguishes "For US teammates (via TriNet)" from "For Global Teammates (via Deel)", stating they work "with our Employer of Record (EOR) partners" so non-US teammates get locally compliant benefits. So a BC-based hire is contemplated by their own hiring infrastructure. Time-zone expectations are not stated (HQ is Eastern; unverified whether Mountain Time is an issue).

Engineering & tech: The flagship open role is C#/WPF production desktop software for adaptive radiotherapy — DICOM integration and networking, 3D medical image processing, image registration (including deformable registration), coordinate transforms, dose sampling/visualization, DVH evaluation, machine positioning, all under near-real-time latency constraints. Preferred extras: .NET Core, C++, Python, Domain-Driven Design, caching/latency optimization, and "using AI or LLM-assisted development tools as part of a professional software engineering workflow." Engineers work directly with physicists and product leaders; the JD names architecture, testing, code review, and mentorship as expected contributions. No public engineering blog or handbook was located (unverified).

Notable / other: Leadership is domain-heavy — co-founders Kurt Sysock (CEO), Elisabeth Van Wie (CXO), Alan Nelson (CTO) are all medical physicists; Karl Otto, inventor of VMAT, is Chief Scientific Officer. Ten software products across the RT workflow. 401(k) with immediately vested employer match, annual professional-membership reimbursement, conference budget. They state they do not accept unsolicited agency resumes and warn that legitimate outreach only comes from @radformation.com.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Adaptive (Remote US / Remote Canada).
