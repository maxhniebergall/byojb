# Flex — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:flex | company_type: product
NOTE ON IDENTITY: "Flex" is a heavily colliding name (Flex/Flextronics the manufacturer, Flex the HR/payroll vendors, etc.). The JD bodies confirm this registry entry is the **NYC rent-payment fintech** — "Flex is a growth-stage, NYC headquartered FinTech company that is creating the best rent payment experience."
What they do: Consumer fintech that lets renters pay rent throughout the month on a schedule that fits their cash flow rather than in one lump sum. Mission as stated: "to empower as many renters as possible with flexibility over their most significant recurring expense." Money movement, payment rails and partner (property-manager) integrations are the core engineering surface.
How they describe themselves: "It's hard to believe that it's 2026 and paying rent on time is expensive, inflexible, and difficult. We're here to change that!" They describe a period of deliberate restraint before scaling ("After deliberately keeping…" — growth-stage framing). Engineering culture is explicitly iterative: "You'll roll out changes to our customers in an agile manner with a delivery cadence that favors small, frequent iterations."
Size / stage / funding: Growth-stage private fintech; specific headcount and funding not stated on the pages available and not verified in this pass.
Locations / HQ: HQ New York City. Their own words: "Our HQ is located in New York City, but we have employees located throughout the US, Australia, Canada and South America." Offices in New York, San Francisco and Salt Lake City.
Remote policy: Mixed and location-dependent, stated explicitly: "Roles posted in New York, San Francisco, and Salt Lake City are hybrid positions with on-site expectations of 2-3 days per week in our local offices. For candidates outside of these areas, you may be eligible for our relocation assistance program." The four engineering roles in the registry are posted Remote.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Plausible but unconfirmed for these roles.** Flex states it already has employees in Canada, and its benefits block distinguishes "full-time U.S. employees" (medical/dental/vision, 401k match, unlimited PTO, 13 holidays, parental leave) from "full-time non-U.S. employees" (competitive compensation + equity, unlimited PTO) — so a non-US employment path demonstrably exists. However, the posted salary tiers on these specific engineering roles are US-only (Tier 1 NYC/Bay/LA/Seattle, Tier 2 named US cities, Tier 3 "Salt Lake City, all other USA cities"), with no Canadian tier, which suggests these particular reqs are scoped to the US. Worth asking; do not assume.
Engineering & tech: Backend-heavy — a FinPlatform team, a Money team (money movement), and Partner Integrations. Agile, small frequent iterations, consumer-facing delivery cadence.
Notable / other: Compensation philosophy is explicitly market-based and geo-tiered, with internal leveling guidelines and benchmarks referenced in the postings.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Backend FinPlatform; Senior Software Engineer, Backend Money Team; Staff Software Engineer, Backend, Money Team; Staff Software Engineer, Partner Integrations.
Pay (from their own JDs, USD base): Senior Backend — Tier 1 $172,000–215,000, Tier 2 $154,800–193,500, Tier 3 $146,200–182,750. Staff Backend and Staff Partner Integrations — Tier 1 $200,000–250,000, Tier 2 $180,000–225,000, Tier 3 $170,000–212,500.
