# Anchorage Digital — research
Provider/key: lever:anchorage | company_type: product

What they do: Anchorage Digital operates a regulated digital-asset platform for institutions: custody, staking, trading, governance, settlement, and security infrastructure. In their own words, "we are building the world's most advanced digital asset platform for institutions to participate in crypto." **Anchorage Digital Bank N.A. is the first federally chartered crypto bank in the U.S.** Other entities/offerings named: Anchorage Digital Singapore, Porto by Anchorage Digital.

How they describe themselves: Heavy internal culture vocabulary — employees are "**Villagers**" and the culture is "**the Village**"; they publish "**Factors of Growth & Impact**" as the framework for measuring impact and giving coaching/feedback within each role. On the industry: "The crypto industry is one of the most exciting industries in tech today, and it is constantly changing! At Anchorage Digital, we are building foundational technology to help the crypto industry evolve in a **safe, regulated and highly secure** manner." On remote: "We are a **remote friendly, global team**, but provide the option of working in-office in New York City, Sioux Falls, Porto, Lisbon, and Singapore. For our colleagues not located near our beautiful offices, we encourage and sponsor **quarterly in-person collaboration days**." All engineering ICs carry the title "Member of Technical Staff" — a flat, non-laddered title scheme.

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2017 in San Francisco. Funded by Andreessen Horowitz, GIC, Goldman Sachs, KKR and Visa; **Series D valuation over $3 billion**. Private, late-stage, well-capitalized. Ten-plus relevant engineering roles open simultaneously, which indicates active expansion rather than contraction.

Locations / HQ: Founded San Francisco; offices in New York, NY; Porto, Portugal; Lisbon, Portugal; Singapore; Sioux Falls, South Dakota.

Remote policy: **Remote friendly with optional offices**, per their own JD boilerplate, plus company-sponsored quarterly in-person collaboration days for remote staff. One hard requirement is stated explicitly and repeatedly: "**we have a security policy mandating all new hires complete an in-person onboarding process — no exceptions**" for physical identity verification and secure IT setup. That is a one-time travel obligation, not relocation.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Partial — verified per posting, and mostly NO.** The country lists come straight from the Lever board:
- **Canada is listed on:** Member of Technical Staff, Custody (Backend Engineer) — "United States, remote, Brazil, Argentina, Portugal, Canada".
- **Canada is NOT listed on** the other nine relevant postings, which are "United States, remote" (Asset Data, Atlas, Agentic Banking, Reporting & Statements, Security Engineering, Security Operations, Trading (Derivatives)) or "United States, remote, Portugal" / "United States, remote, Argentina, Brazil, Portugal" (Transparency Billing & Revenue; Wealth Technical Lead).
So a Canadian hire is possible in principle — the company clearly has a mechanism for it — but **only one of ten open roles currently admits it**, and applying to the US-only ones would be wasted effort. Timezone: no requirement stated; the office spread (Portugal/Singapore/US) implies broad tolerance, but Custody/banking operations may carry US-market-hours expectations. Unverified.

Engineering & tech: The Custody organization owns "the entire custody experience, including the management and transferring of cash, coins and NFTs, staking assets, interacting with onchain smart contracts, fiat transfers, and institutional platform solutions." Other org surfaces visible from titles: Asset Data, Atlas, Financial Infrastructure, Reporting & Statements (backend and data-engineering variants), Transparency/Billing & Revenue, Trading (Derivatives), Wealth, Security Engineering, Security Operations, Agentic Banking, and Technical Program Management. Stack details were not stated in the JD bodies captured. Security is positioned as the company's foundation ("Security is our foundation"), driven by "an increasing volume of sophisticated attacks by malicious actors."

Notable / other: Federally chartered bank status means a genuine regulatory perimeter around the engineering — audit, controls and change management are real, which tends to slow the pace relative to unregulated crypto. Conversely the business is exposed to crypto market cycles. No compensation figures appear in any of the nine captured JD bodies, despite the roles being US-remote (Anchorage does not appear to be posting transparency-law ranges on these Lever listings).
