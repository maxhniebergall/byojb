# BIMM — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:bimm | company_type: consulting

**Classification note: BIMM is a client-services marketing/technology agency, not a product company.**
It describes itself in its own words as "a Toronto-based agency" that works "with clients as
partners", and the posting reviewed here is a fixed-term contract staffing a named client account
(Audi). Under the pipeline's rules this is agency/consulting work and the company is excluded from
product-company research.

What they do: "BIMM is a Toronto-based agency that lives at the intersection of data, technology, and
creativity to provide clients with high-performing campaigns and digital products that shape
connected customer experiences. We've helped our clients implement fully integrated CRM customer
journeys, built robust digital experiences, and created national loyalty programs with decades of
experience in sectors of automotive, financial, telco and retail." So: CRM, loyalty programs and
digital campaign delivery, sold as billable engagements to large Canadian brands.

How they describe themselves: "We are creative realists, which means our ideas and solutions are
grounded in the realities of our clients' business. We believe big ideas are nothing without big
results (and flawless execution); high-performing agencies deliver high-performing campaigns,
products, and experiences; and the best solutions happen when we collaborate with clients as
partners." Named cultural values include "Having Fun Everyday" and "Love 2 Learn". Culture blurb:
"Our squad is fun, friendly and entirely egoless. Our social committee plans BIMM bashes, Tasty
Thursdays and quarterly outings like candle making workshops, board game nights, Jays games and
cooking classes."

Size / stage / funding: Private agency; headcount not stated. **Part of the kyu collective** — "BIMM
is part of kyu collective which gives our employees access to the network's resources, training,
offices, and more." (kyu is a holding company of creative/strategy firms.) So BIMM is owned within a
larger agency network rather than independent.

Locations / HQ: Toronto — "our beautiful dog-friendly office located in the Distillery District,
stuffed with snacks."

Remote policy: **Hybrid, mandatory.** "This is a hybrid role requiring two days per week in our
Toronto office" and "We believe in working & meeting with purpose. We observe a hybrid working
model." They also impose managed-device requirements on contractors: a Mac or Windows machine (Linux
explicitly not supported), Windows 11 or macOS Sonoma/Sequoia or newer, device less than four years
old, and agreement "to have us install and maintain BIMM's security tools on your device… must remain
active, with all permissions and configurations unchanged for the duration of the contract."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No.** Although the ATS tags the posting "Canada", the JD states plainly
that it is a hybrid role requiring two days per week in the Toronto office. That is not workable from
Kimberley, British Columbia and is disqualifying regardless of the country tag — a clear example of a
country-level location tag masking an in-office requirement.

Engineering & tech: The advertised role is Senior Agentic Developer, and the technical content is
substantive for an agency: "design and build agentic systems that can plan, use tools, generate
outputs, self-check, detect failures, retry where appropriate, escalate when needed, and fail
safely"; "develop agents and skills that support application generation, automation, and delivery
workflows"; and notably "build reliable evaluation pipelines for agent and skill outputs, including
golden datasets, regression suites, LLM-as-judge scoring, and measurable quality metrics." Breadth
expected across "application logic, cloud infrastructure, model integration, evaluation pipelines,
and guardrails", plus REST APIs and connecting front-end or agentic applications to back-end
services. The work directly supports the client Audi's digital platform.

Notable / other: Employment terms are the defining fact — a **contract ending Dec 24, 2026**, paid
hourly. Benefits described are social and developmental rather than material (kyu network training,
Lunch n' Learns, conferences, online courses, DEI committee). They state: "While we are exploring
ways to best utilize AI in our hiring processes, BIMM currently does not use AI-enabled tools in our
hiring process. All candidates are screened by humans, and approved by humans."

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Agentic Developer (Contract) — Toronto hybrid, contract to
Dec 24 2026.

Compensation on record: BIMM posts an hourly contract rate on its own JD: "The range of hourly pay
for this position is below… Total compensation range: $60—$80 CAD" per hour. No annualised band has
been filed: the figure is an hourly contractor rate with no benefits, no employer contributions and
a fixed end date, so annualising it would produce a number not comparable to the salaried bands this
registry holds. Recorded here as prose only, deliberately.
