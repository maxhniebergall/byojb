# Devoted Studios — research
Provider/key: workable:devoted-studios-1 | company_type: outsourcing

What they do: Devoted Studios is a globally remote game-development *services* company — an external development (co-dev) vendor, not a product company. Their own words: they specialize in "Co-development, Porting, and End-to-End Art Production for the global gaming industry," and operate "as a trusted, flexible extension of our partners' internal teams," with expertise in "external development, pipeline optimization, and creative problem-solving." Work is organized around client engagements: the backend JD refers to confirming which IaC tooling "is in use on the engagement."

How they describe themselves: A development partner of choice, listing Epic, Xbox, Meta, Obsidian Entertainment, Riot and Gearbox Software as clients. Emphasis on world-class talent and flexibility as an extension of client teams — classic outsourcing/co-development positioning.

Size / stage / funding: Not disclosed. Globally remote by design.

Locations / HQ: Globally remote; the backend posting is listed as Montreal, Canada (Remote), with benefits stated as "standard benefits in Quebec Province."

Remote policy: Remote-first ("globally remote game development company"). The specific posting is remote but tied to Quebec employment.

Remote-Canada eligibility: PARTIAL / NOT BC. The role is remote and Canadian, but framed as Quebec employment ("full-time employment role with standard benefits in Quebec Province"), which implies a Quebec-resident hire rather than remote-anywhere-in-Canada. From Kimberley, BC that is not clearly eligible.

Engineering & tech: The backend posting is substantive and infrastructure-heavy: Java and/or Scala services for game clients across PC, console and mobile; polyglot data stores (MongoDB, DynamoDB, Redis); REST API design with versioned contracts; AWS via CDK/Terraform/CloudFormation, EC2, ECS/EKS, Lambda, API Gateway, ElastiCache, SQS/SNS, CloudWatch, S3; VPC/security groups/IAM least privilege/secrets management; structured logging, metrics, distributed tracing, health checks and alerting; on-call ownership and post-mortems; Kafka or SQS/SNS event-driven communication; platform SDK constraints for PlayStation Network, Xbox Live, Nintendo NSO and mobile. Requires 5+ years backend with 3+ at live production scale. Explicitly demands end-to-end ownership: "an engineer who architects the service, deploys it, monitors it in production, responds when it breaks, and improves it after every incident."

Pay: Not stated on the posting.
