# Stellar Cyber — research
Provider/key: workable:stellar-cyber | company_type: product

What they do: Stellar Cyber sells an Open XDR (extended detection and response) security platform — unifying threat detection, incident correlation, threat intelligence and asset management for security operations teams. Their own words: "Join a fast-growing global leader in cybersecurity, trusted by some of the biggest names in the industry. In addition to some of the world's largest enterprises and government agencies, more than 30% of the world's top MSSPs rely on our platform. We're at the forefront of protecting organizations against sophisticated cyber threats using cutting-edge AI and automation technologies." The MSSP (managed security service provider) channel is clearly a core go-to-market motion.

How they describe themselves: "Our culture is built on diversity, openness, and collaboration, fostering creativity and innovation that drives real impact in the market." Their "Why join us?" pitch: "Work at the forefront of cybersecurity innovation within a dynamic, fast-growing team"; "Opportunity to significantly influence and shape the integration architecture of a next-generation SecOps platform powered by AI and automation"; competitive salary. No published values list was available. Language is growth-flavoured — "fast-growing" twice, "dynamic".

Size / stage / funding: Private, pre-IPO. The benefits list includes "Pre-IPO Stock Options (equity opportunity)", so equity is a stated part of the package and an IPO is the framed outcome. Exact headcount and funding stage were NOT verified this session — no first-party stellarcyber.ai URL appears in the registry, so nothing was fetched. Treat size and funding as unverified.

Locations / HQ: HQ is in Silicon Valley (San Jose, California) per general knowledge — unverified this session. The live posting set reveals the actual engineering footprint clearly: United States (remote, with one East-Coast/New York-scoped role), Spain (remote), Hungary (remote), and Taiwan (remote). Taiwan and the EU sites carry the bulk of the SRE and parser work.

Remote policy: Remote-normal but country-scoped. Every one of the nine live postings is tagged "(Remote)" with an explicit country: "United States (Remote)", "Spain (Remote)", "Hungary (Remote)", "Taiwan (Remote)". One US role is further narrowed to the East Coast ("Senior DevOps Engineer/Site Reliability Engineer-East Coast", New York), implying timezone-aligned coverage expectations. Benefits listed: pre-IPO stock options, medical/dental/vision, life insurance, 401(k), EAP, paid time off, referral program, rewards and recognition program.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO. All nine live postings are scoped to the US, Spain, Hungary or Taiwan; a full-text scan of every fetched Stellar Cyber JD body returned zero occurrences of "Canada"/"Canadian". The North American remote roles are "United States (Remote)", one of them East-Coast-restricted. There is no Canadian posting and no evidence of a Canadian employing entity.

Engineering & tech: Backend and data-intensive systems work. From the Senior Staff SWE JD: "Design and implement scalable data processing components and supporting features for Stellar Cyber's cybersecurity applications, including but not limited to, threat detections, incident correlation, threat intelligence, and asset management", plus "Monitor and resolve production stability issues". Required: "developing backend functionalities, distributed systems, APIs (REST / gRPC), and microservices for machine learning or other data intensive projects"; cloud, containerization and orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes); Python, Java or Go. Preferred experience includes "developing backend components, such as threat detection engines", "working on machine learning platform teams and/or building tools and frameworks for researchers", and "observability frameworks and practices in machine learning systems". They also value written communication explicitly: "Proficiency in communicating over a text-based medium (e.g., Slack) and can succinctly describe, discuss, and document technical details." Teams visible: SRE/DevOps, the Parser Team, and general platform engineering.

Notable / other: One notable JD detail is a red flag for scope predictability: "Candidates who successfully pass all the interviews will be assigned to a project area in need." Team assignment happens after hiring, based on organisational need. Also note a title/content mismatch in their own posting: the req is titled "Senior Staff Software Engineer" but the body opens "We are looking for a highly skilled Senior Machine Learning Software Engineer", and the minimum bar is only "3 years of experience with software development" — unusually junior for a Senior Staff title. Posted US pay: Senior Staff Software Engineer $150-200K USD base; Senior DevOps/SRE (East Coast) $165-215K USD base. No pay is posted for the Spain, Hungary or Taiwan roles.
