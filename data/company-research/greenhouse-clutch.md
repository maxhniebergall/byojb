# Clutch Technologies Inc. — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:clutch | company_type: product

**Entity confirmation:** "Clutch" is a heavily colliding name (there is a well-known US B2B ratings site clutch.co, and a US fleet-services "Clutch Technologies"). The registry entity is confirmed by the JD text itself: "Clutch is Canada's largest online used car retailer… Headquartered in Toronto, Clutch was founded in 2017… To learn more, visit clutch.ca". This is the Canadian online used-car retailer.

What they do: Clutch is Canada's largest online used-car retailer — a direct e-commerce model where "Customers can browse hundreds of cars from the comfort of their home, get the right one delivered to their door, and enjoy peace of mind with our 10-Day Money-Back Guarantee". Consumer commerce with heavy logistics and financing components. It currently services Ontario, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, British Columbia and Prince Edward Island.

How they describe themselves: Mission framing is "We're on a mission to reinvent the way people buy, sell, and own cars. Are you game?" They say they are "looking to add curious, hard-working, and driven individuals to our growing team". The engineering identity is explicitly AI-velocity-first: "At Clutch, we believe advances in AI are fundamentally changing how great products are built. Traditional handoffs between product, design, and engineering are giving way to smaller, faster teams where builders can take ideas from concept to production in days instead of months." And: "Our engineers don't just write code, they own outcomes… We're looking for people who see a broken process or an unanswered question and feel compelled to fix it, not wait for it to land in their queue." Also "If you're energized by ambiguity, moving fast, and building products with real business impact, you'll thrive at Clutch." Stated reasons to join: "Autonomy & ownership – opportunity to build your process and influence the company workforce and at every level"; "We are dedicated to attracting and retaining top talent with top-of-market compensation packages"; "Above market health & dental benefits."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, founded 2017, growth stage. Investors named in their own JDs: D1 Capital, Canaan, Real Ventures, BrandProject and Upper90. Recognition claimed: five consecutive years on the Globe & Mail Top Growing Companies in Canada list, plus Deloitte Technology Fast 50 and Fast 500, and LinkedIn's Top Canadian Startups list. Headcount not stated; unverified. Profitability unverified — a capital-intensive used-car e-commerce model with debt-flavoured backers (Upper90) is inherently funding-dependent.

Locations / HQ: Headquartered in Toronto, Ontario. Operations across ON, NB, NS, BC and PEI. Engineering postings are all Toronto.

Remote policy: **Hybrid, three days a week in downtown Toronto — stated explicitly.** The Staff Platform Engineer JD's Employment Type section reads: "New, Permanent Full-Time" / "Hybrid, Downtown Toronto three days a week". The Staff Software Engineer and Team Lead reqs carry the same Hybrid employment type.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No.** Canadian employer, Canadian roles — but every engineering req is Toronto hybrid with a stated three-days-per-week downtown office requirement, in Eastern Time. Not compatible with a Kimberley BC candidate who cannot relocate.

Engineering & tech: Product stack is TypeScript, React, Express, PostgreSQL and AWS, with the caveat "we're far more interested in exceptional engineers than perfect technology matches". Platform engineering is being stood up now: the Staff Platform Engineer is described as "our **first dedicated Staff Platform Engineer**", with a mandate to "define and execute Clutch's Platform Engineering strategy", "build and evolve our Internal Developer Platform to improve developer productivity through self-service tooling", own "the architecture and evolution of our CI/CD platform and deployment workflows", and "design and scale cloud infrastructure using AWS, Terraform, and GitHub Actions", plus platform standards for reliability, observability and security. Nice-to-haves: feature-flagging platforms, monorepo tooling, prior IDP design. They frame it as "a rare opportunity to build Platform Engineering from the ground up, defining the strategy, establishing the function, and growing the team". AI-assisted development is an explicit expectation in the product-engineering reqs ("Use AI as part of your everyday engineering workflow").

Notable / other: Compensation is posted openly and is high for the Canadian market — Staff Platform Engineer "$200,000+ per annum", Staff Software Engineer "$205,000 annually". They state "Pay decisions reflect objective criteria: skills, performance, market benchmarking, responsibilities and working co[nditions]". Benefits start day one (health, dental, vision, prescriptions, life insurance, dependent coverage) for permanent full-time staff.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Platform Engineer, Toronto — $200,000+ CAD; Staff Software Engineer, Toronto — $205,000 CAD; Senior Software Engineer, Toronto; Team Lead, Software Engineering, Toronto.
