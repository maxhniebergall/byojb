# Kong — research
Provider/key: ashby:kong | company_type: product

What they do: Kong Inc. builds API and AI connectivity infrastructure. Its flagship open-source Kong Gateway is one of the most widely deployed API gateways; the commercial platform is Kong Konnect, a multi-region SaaS control plane for securing, managing, governing and monetizing API and AI-model traffic. Related products include Kong Mesh (service mesh) and an AI Gateway for routing/governing LLM traffic. Customers are Fortune 500 enterprises and startups alike. Founded 2017, headquartered in San Francisco.

How they describe themselves: Kong's careers page leads with "No AI without APIs. Join the revolution!" and describes staff as "makers, thinkers, and doers focused on helping today's developers build tomorrow's technology." Its seven stated values, in its own words:
- "We are CUSTOMER CHAMPIONS" — customers at the center; everyone is empowered to make an impact.
- "We are OWNERS" — "drivers, not passengers"; own quality and outcomes.
- "We are UNSTOPPABLE" — "work with purpose, obsession, and grit."
- "We are REAL" — genuine, principled, confident; show respect and kindness.
- "We are ACCELERATORS" — prioritize speed, iteration, results.
- "We are GLOBAL" — "work from anywhere"; differences make us stronger.
- "We are EXPLORERS" — ideas over hierarchy; make bold bets, fail, learn.
Tone is high-energy and enterprise-sales-adjacent ("obsession and grit", "accelerators"), balanced by an explicit remote/global and open-source-developer identity.

Size / stage / funding: 600+ employees across 25+ countries. Private, venture-backed (SF-headquartered, founded 2017); Great Place To Work-certified. Established enough to be well past seed-stage instability; heavy enterprise revenue base.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco. Offices in key geographies across the U.S., Europe and Asia serving as in-person collaboration hubs. A substantial Canadian engineering presence in **Toronto** — many of its current engineering openings are posted as "Toronto, Canada" or plain "Canada". Shanghai is a second visible engineering hub (several AI Gateway / Gateway roles).

Remote policy: Remote-first. Kong states it has "developed a thriving remote culture and become a truly global company", offering flexible and hybrid arrangements, with physical offices as hubs rather than requirements. It runs company-wide "unplugging days" where the entire team is off.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** Multiple live engineering postings are located "Canada" or "Toronto, Canada" (e.g. Senior Site Reliability Engineer, Kong Konnect — "Canada, Toronto, Canada"; Software Engineer, Core Platform — "Canada"), and Kong posts salary ranges for these roles in **CAD**, which is direct evidence of a Canadian employing entity and Canadian payroll.

Engineering & tech: Platform SRE is a global team operating Konnect as a multi-region, multi-cloud SaaS across AWS, GCP and Azure. Stack visible in JDs: Kubernetes (multi-region clusters), Terraform/Terragrunt, Helm, ArgoCD, GitOps, PostgreSQL, Redis, ClickHouse, Druid, Kafka, Datadog/Prometheus/Grafana/Thanos with formal SLOs, and Go/Python/Bash for automation. Core gateway work is in Rust and Lua/OpenResty; there is an explicit "Software Engineer - Rust - Event Gateway" opening. Engineering is organized into product-aligned Konnect teams (Core Platform, Admin/Billing, Service Catalog, Managed Gateways, Identity & Access Management) plus a horizontal Platform SRE group. JDs are candid about operational load: "Participate in a global 24/7 on-call rotation" and "Experience working in a 24/7/365 production support environment." JDs also carry an explicitly inclusive application note: "If you don't think you meet all of the criteria below but are still interested in the job, please apply. Nobody checks every box."

Notable / other: Kong positions itself as "building the infrastructure that powers the agentic era" — its AI Gateway extends API-management primitives (auth, rate limiting, observability, governance, cost control) to LLM traffic. The open-source Kong Gateway gives it a genuine developer-infrastructure identity rather than a marketing one.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Site Reliability Engineer, Kong Konnect (Toronto/Canada); Senior SRE, Managed Gateways (Canada); Senior Software Engineer, Konnect Core Platform (Toronto); Software Engineer, Core Platform (Canada); Staff Software Engineer, Identity and Access Management (Toronto); Software Engineer - Rust - Event Gateway; Staff Site Reliability Engineer — Project Volcano.
