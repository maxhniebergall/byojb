# vCluster Labs (formerly Loft Labs) — research
Provider/key: ashby:vclusterlabs | company_type: product

What they do: vCluster Labs builds **virtual Kubernetes clusters** — it invented the category. Its
open-source `vcluster` project lets a team run a fully isolated, API-server-level virtual Kubernetes
cluster inside a namespace of a host cluster, giving self-service, cheap, tenant-isolated clusters
instead of provisioning real ones. Around that it sells vCluster Platform (formerly Loft), the
commercial control plane for multi-tenancy, self-service cluster access, sleep-mode cost control and
governance, and it also created **DevPod**, an open-source tool for codifying reusable dev
environments on any infrastructure. It also has a "vMetal" bare-metal line (there is a live "Senior
Software Engineer (vMetal)" role). Positioning is "platform engineering building blocks" — the
customers are platform teams at other companies. The company renamed from Loft Labs to vCluster Labs
to match the flagship project.

How they describe themselves: values are summarised on the careers page as **"Own it. Ship it. Wow
them."**, presented as five principles that "guide daily decision-making rather than serving as mere
wall decorations". Elsewhere they state: "Responsibility from day 1" — your work has immediate
impact and you shape the future of the company; **meritocracy**, "the strongest ideas win, no matter
who or where they come from"; and "open source is part of our DNA". They pitch career velocity —
"a small company growing incredibly fast", where joining early means advancing up the ladder faster
than at a larger company — and describe themselves plainly as "a VC-backed tech startup in
hyper-growth".

Size / stage / funding: founded 2019. Series A of **$24M led by Khosla Ventures** (April 2024), with
Fusion Fund, Surface Ventures, Emergent Ventures and Berkeley SkyDeck Fund participating plus an
angel investment from Kit Merker (one of Kubernetes' first PMs at Google). Total financing ~$28.6M
(the careers material rounds this to "+$30M"). Reported 4.6x ARR growth, vCluster past 40 million
image pulls, and the launch of the commercial vCluster Pro edition. Small — plausibly well under 100
people. Series-A stage means real runway dependence.

Locations / HQ: HQ is San Francisco (Salesforce Tower), but that is nominal — the team is fully
distributed across roughly **8 timezones**, and the careers page lists open roles in North America,
Europe, Asia Pacific, MEA and South America.

Remote policy: **remote-first and fully distributed**, explicitly "built for global talent". No
relocation, no commuting, "work from anywhere". Scheduling is flexible and judged on **output rather
than hours logged**, with employees free to handle personal appointments during business hours.
Benefits: competitive "data-driven" compensation, health/dental/vision/life insurance (coverage
varies by country), equity and bonus on the engineering roles. Hiring process is deliberately fast
and structured — decisions "in days, not months", standardised bias-reduced interview questions, and
practical work samples/presentations rather than trivia.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** The live engineering requisitions list their locations
as "**United States, Canada, EMEA**" — specifically "Senior Software Engineer (Backend)", "Staff
Software Engineer" and "Senior Software Engineer (vMetal)". (A separate "AI Infrastructure Engineer"
req is scoped "Remote, Australia, Germany, United States" and does **not** include Canada.) The
US/Canada/EMEA roles quote a USD range for the North American zone and a EUR range for EMEA, which
indicates a real North-American hiring entity rather than a token mention. Timezone: the team spans
8 zones and meetings are output-oriented, but the HQ and NA anchor is Pacific, so Mountain Time is
an easy overlap.

Engineering & tech: deep Kubernetes internals — virtualizing the API server, control planes,
multi-tenancy, isolation, bare-metal provisioning (vMetal). Overwhelmingly Go, CNCF-ecosystem, and
heavily open-source in public (github.com/loft-sh). This is infrastructure software sold to platform
engineers; there is essentially no product-growth surface, and roles are split into recognisable
specialisms (backend, platform, vMetal, AI infrastructure) rather than generalist squads.

Compensation transparency: vCluster posts base ranges on its own Ashby JDs, in two currencies by
zone. Senior Software Engineer (Backend): €80K–€115K (EMEA). Senior Software Engineer (vMetal):
€80K–€110K (EMEA). Staff Software Engineer: **$160K–$200K USD** (North America). AI Infrastructure
Engineer: $150K–$200K. All offer equity and bonus on top ("Multiple Ranges" is the Ashby marker for
zoned pay).

Notable / other: the founders' credibility in the Kubernetes ecosystem is the moat — vCluster is a
genuinely widely adopted open-source project, not a marketing artifact. Open-source contribution is
part of the day job. The flip side is that "hyper-growth", "responsibility from day 1" and "ship it"
are exactly the startup-intensity signals to weigh.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer (US/Canada/EMEA); Senior Software Engineer
(Backend) (US/Canada/EMEA); Senior Software Engineer (vMetal) (US/Canada/EMEA); AI Infrastructure
Engineer (US/Australia/Germany — not Canada).
