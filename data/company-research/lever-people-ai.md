# Backstory (formerly People.ai) — research
Provider/key: lever:people-ai | company_type: product

Note on identity: the registry key is `lever:people-ai` and the display name is `Backstory`. These
are the same company — **People.ai rebranded to Backstory in April 2026** — and the Lever board slug
is still `people-ai`. Not to be confused with any other "Backstory".

What they do: Backstory is a **revenue-intelligence platform for enterprise sales organisations**.
In their words: "Backstory helps companies understand the state of their revenue business by
answering questions that span customer interactions, sales activity, pipeline health, and deal
execution. The platform operates over trillions of sales activities, millions of deals, and more
than 160 million business contacts, giving customers a unified view of the signals that drive
revenue." It automatically captures activity from email, calls and CRM and turns it into deal-risk
monitoring, pipeline health, forecasting and next-step recommendations, delivered inside the CRM and
AI tools teams already use. Named customers include Nvidia, OpenAI, AMD, Red Hat and Databricks. The
rebrand signalled a shift from "data and analytics" toward "actionable answers" — the "Revenue
Answers Platform".

How they describe themselves: careers-page tagline is **"Good people. Hard problems. No
bureaucracy."** On engineering: "We value ownership highly — the ability to take an idea through all
the stages from conception to shipping a product. This reflects throughout our company, but is
especially true in engineering. As an engineer at Backstory, you'll be a part of a highly
independent and autonomous team. Since we're building out a robust data layer that needs to be
presented elegantly to the end-user, you'll be working with a large array of different technologies
and fields. Expect lots of interesting challenges." On the technical problem: "We work at the
intersection of large language models, retrieval, evaluation, distributed data systems, and product
design, turning billions of signals from across the enterprise into insights people can trust. The
underlying challenges span reasoning, search, ranking, data quality, knowledge representation, and
operating AI systems against continuously evolving real-world information."

Size / stage / funding: founded 2016 as People.ai; venture-backed and past the early stage, though
current headcount and the latest round are not published on their site. The heavy concentration of
open engineering roles in Poland, Romania and Czechia indicates a deliberate cost-centred European
engineering build-out.

Locations / HQ: **San Francisco** headquarters. Engineering hiring is concentrated in **Eastern
Europe** — the live board shows roles in Poland (multiple), Romania and Prague — plus a single
Canadian role anchored on Toronto.

Remote policy: **remote-first with flexibility** — employees can work from home or use the San
Francisco HQ. Benefits: equity ("compensation packages that allow you to share in our long-term
success"), flexible PTO, inclusive parental leave (birthing, non-birthing, adoption, foster),
monthly phone/internet allowance plus home-office setup reimbursement, and synchronised company
holidays.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Partially yes, but narrow and Toronto-anchored.** Exactly one of the
live engineering requisitions is Canadian — "Senior Software Engineer — Matching (**Canada**)",
listed as "**Toronto, remote**". Every other open engineering role is EU-scoped: "Senior Software
Engineer — Data Modeling (Europe)" (Poland, remote), "Senior Software Engineer — Matching (Europe)"
(Poland, remote), "Senior Software Engineer — XCore (Europe)" (Poland, remote), "Software Engineer —
XCore (Europe)" (Poland, remote), and "Senior Software Engineer — Admin Platform" in Romania, Prague
and Poland. So the company can employ in Canada, but the location tag is Toronto rather than
"Canada — anywhere", and it is unconfirmed whether British Columbia is in scope. More materially,
the engineering centre of gravity is Central/Eastern Europe (UTC+1/+2), which is **8-9 hours ahead
of Mountain Time** — a Kimberley-based engineer on a team whose colleagues sit in Poland would have
almost no overlap window, and the Toronto anchor suggests they expect Eastern Time at minimum.

Engineering & tech: substantive data/ML infrastructure — entity matching and resolution at very
large scale ("matching business activities to business context"), microservice architecture,
machine-learning and automation code, ranking and search, retrieval and evaluation, LLM systems
operating against continuously changing real-world data, and a large distributed data layer. Teams
are named and reasonably scoped: Matching, Data Modeling, XCore, Admin Platform. The JD text is
notably short on stack specifics, requirements lists or salary.

Compensation: **nothing published.** No range on any of the live postings, no pay-transparency page.
The Canadian and European roles are all silent on pay, and the registry has zero comp rows for this
company. Equity is mentioned generically in the benefits list.

Notable / other: the customer set (Nvidia, OpenAI, AMD, Red Hat, Databricks) is genuinely
blue-chip, and the matching/entity-resolution problem at trillions of activities is real
distributed-data engineering. But the product exists to serve **sales organisations**, and the
company frames engineering ownership as "conception to shipping a product" across "a large array of
different technologies and fields".

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer — Matching (Canada) [Toronto, remote];
Senior Software Engineer — Matching (Europe); Senior Software Engineer — Data Modeling (Europe);
Senior Software Engineer — XCore (Europe); Senior Software Engineer — Admin Platform
(Romania/Prague/Poland).
