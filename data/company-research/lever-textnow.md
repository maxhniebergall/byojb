# TextNow — research
Provider/key: lever:textnow | company_type: product

What they do: TextNow is an all-IP, cloud-based phone carrier that provides free and low-cost phone service over Wi-Fi, with no SIM card required, monetized largely by advertising. Founded 2009, it serves millions of users across the US and Canada. The company positions itself as an affordable alternative to incumbent carriers — "Communication belongs to everyone."

How they describe themselves: Their JD opens "We believe that communication belongs to everyone. TextNow is redefining how people connect by combining simplicity, intelligence, and accessibility. We're a team of curious builders using technology to make communication more affordable and powerful for millions of users every day." The careers page states five values: purpose-driven work ("energized by the millions of people who depend on us daily"), innovation ("not afraid of challenging the status quo"), growth ("nurturing talent and empowering our employees"), an inclusive environment where "diversity is celebrated as a strength", and work-life balance ("productivity and grit are balanced with flexibility"). Engineering is framed around "Members of Technical Staff" who "combine leadership and hands-on coding to enable the highest leverage opportunities possible" and are expected to "operate strategically, as well as diving into the lowest-level details."

Size / stage / funding: Private, established (founded 2009, ~15 years old). Headcount not disclosed on the careers page; historically a few hundred employees. Revenue-generating consumer carrier rather than a runway-dependent startup.

Locations / HQ: HQ in Waterloo, Ontario, Canada, with a US office in San Francisco, California.

Remote policy: Remote-friendly. TextNow operates a "Work Best" philosophy rather than a conventional work-from-home policy — employees choose how they work best, and those who work from home may relocate to another city. The careers page itself only says "flexible working arrangements."

Remote-Canada eligibility: VERIFIED YES. The live backend role is explicitly titled "Software Developer — Backend - Canada" and its ATS location field reads "Open- Canada, remote" — a Canada-wide remote requisition, not a Waterloo hybrid one. A parallel US-only requisition exists separately, which confirms the Canadian one is genuinely Canada-scoped. Mountain Time is workable against a Waterloo/Eastern-anchored company (2h offset).

Engineering & tech: Backend services for a carrier-scale communications platform — systems, architecture and tooling across mobile, web and data. The role emphasizes "technical ownership of key backend domains" and building "faster, smarter, more reliable systems." AI and automation are described as "at the core of how we build," used to accelerate development, improve performance and detect/resolve issues. They hire the backend role across intermediate/senior/staff+ levels and align title and scope to experience. A Site Reliability Engineer requisition also runs on a separate Greenhouse board (greenhouse:textnowinc).

Notable / other: Recognized as one of Achievers' "50 Most Engaged Workplaces" and among the University of Waterloo's profiled "Top Employers." Employee testimonials emphasize care for well-being and mutual support.
