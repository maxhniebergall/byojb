# DoorDash Canada — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:doordashcanada | company_type: product

What they do: DoorDash is a US-listed technology and logistics company operating an on-demand local-delivery marketplace. In their own words they are "building the world's most reliable on-demand, logistics engine for delivery," powering "a three-sided marketplace of consumers, merchants, and dashers." They describe an ambition to move "from a company that is known as the place you order food to a company that people turn to for any and all goods." This Greenhouse board is the Canadian hiring entity.

How they describe themselves: The mission is "to empower local economies." Their stated framing of how that shapes work: it "shapes how our team members move quickly, learn, and reiterate in order to make impactful decisions that display empathy for our range of users — from Dashers to merchant partners to consumers." They also state that "DoorDash is growing rapidly and changing constantly, which gives our team members the opportunity to share their unique perspectives, solve new challenges, and own their careers." The JD boilerplate refers to a "fast-growing engineering team." The full corporate values list was not reachable — the careers site (careers.doordash.com → careersatdoordash.com) returned HTTP 403 — so the above is everything stated in their own JD text.

Size / stage / funding: Large public company (NASDAQ: DASH), tens of thousands of employees globally including a large contractor/Dasher network. Not runway-dependent. Precise Canadian headcount unverified.

Locations / HQ: HQ in San Francisco. This entity hires into **Toronto, Ontario**; all three relevant postings are Toronto-located. The compensation boilerplate on one JD references "our lowest geographical market up to our highest geographical market **within British Columbia**," which indicates DoorDash also maintains a BC pay market — though the posting itself is Toronto-located, so this is a boilerplate artifact rather than evidence of a BC-remote role.

Remote policy: **Not verified.** No remote/hybrid/in-office language appears anywhere in the three captured JD bodies, and the careers site was unreachable (403). The postings are location-tagged "Toronto, ON" with no remote qualifier. Publicly DoorDash has been widely reported to operate an in-office-majority model, but I could not confirm that from a first-party source this session, so it is recorded here as **unverified**.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, leaning no.** The company plainly can employ Canadians — this is a dedicated Canadian entity with a Canadian benefits package (RRSP with employer match, paid parental leave, mental-health program). What is *not* established is whether any of these roles can be done remotely from British Columbia. All three are tagged Toronto with no remote designation and no stated work-model language. Treat as a Toronto-attached role unless a posting says otherwise.

Engineering & tech: Backend at large scale — "develop, release and run large-scale web applications," "define the backend architecture and tech stack for a product area," and an in-flight migration "transitioning our monolithic codebase to a microservice-based architecture." Named technologies: service-oriented architecture, REST APIs, SQL and NoSQL (Postgres, Redis, Elasticsearch, RabbitMQ), Java/Kotlin (listed as a nice-to-have). Explicitly a **product-engineering** framing: "Product engineers work at the intersection of product and infrastructure to solve key business problems with elegant technical solutions… evaluating and executing on trade-offs to maximize business impact for the company." Bar is set high on experience — the Staff JD asks for 8+ years and a CS degree or equivalent.

Notable / other: Benefits described for Canadian regular employees include paid time off, paid parental leave, a wellness benefit, several paid holidays, and for full-time staff medical/dental/vision, disability and basic life insurance, an RRSP with employer match, and a mental-health program.

Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineer, Backend - Unified Gateway (Toronto, ON); Staff Software Engineer, Backend (Toronto, ON) — two separate postings; Software Engineer, Backend (All Levels / All Teams).

Comp (already on record; both slots covered, no new band written):
- `backend` / `mid-senior` — CAD $120,000–$150,000 base, direct from the Unified Gateway JD.
- `backend` / `staff` — a rollup band on record, CAD $136,250 / $171,625 / $211,125. **Caveat, see data-quality note:** the Staff JD 8072115 states CAD $185,000–$231,500 for BC, and JD 5693362 carries three separate ranges in one posting (120–150K, 149.5–187K, 185–231.5K CAD) because it covers multiple levels. The rollup has therefore mixed sub-staff ranges into the staff band and its minimum is too low; the honest staff figure from a single-level posting is **CAD $185,000–$231,500 base**, plus equity.
