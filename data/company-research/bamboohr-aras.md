# Aras — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:aras | company_type: product
What they do: Aras develops Aras Innovator, an enterprise PLM (Product Lifecycle Management) platform for managing complex engineered products. In their own words: "Aras Innovator is a dynamic product that has a flexible system of customization in accordance with specific needs and specific requirements, and this versatility is one of the reasons why our software is so successful around the world." Named customers in their own JD: "Microsoft, Airbus, Boeing, General Motors, Kawasaki, Motorola, Honda, Freudenberg, Imperial Tobacco Group, Schaeffler, ECCO, Carestream Health, GETRAG and other Fortune members." This is heavy-industry enterprise software — automotive, aerospace, industrial manufacturing.
How they describe themselves: The JDs describe cross-functional teams that "develop reliable and scalable solutions using Aras Innovator infrastructure to meet the business requirements and needs of our customers." Stated soft-skill expectations: "Collaboration & teamwork; a growth mindset and commitment to continuous learning; flexibility in adapting to change; excellent planning and organizational skills; strong problem analysis and solution-solving capabilities; attention to detail." No formal published values list was reachable — the only registry URL is the BambooHR board.
Size / stage / funding: Not disclosed in any reachable source. Aras is an established private enterprise-software vendor with a Fortune-500 customer base; specific headcount, funding and ownership were NOT verified this session.
Locations / HQ: Not stated in the reachable sources. The open engineering requisitions are explicitly Poland-based; the JD names the "ASF team" as one development group.
Remote policy: Not stated. Requisitions are country-scoped rather than remote-anywhere; the JD gives the location as "Available to candidates located in Poland."
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO. Both senior software engineering requisitions state "Available to candidates located in Poland" / "Poland Based" and one adds the explicit condition "Must be eligible to work in Poland." The (m/f/d) suffix on every title is German-language job-posting convention, indicating the hiring entity is European. There is no Canadian or North American engineering requisition on this board. Not reachable from British Columbia.
Engineering & tech: .NET, C#, JavaScript, TypeScript and SQL; strong OOP, SOLID and design patterns; performance optimisation and analytics; plus a stated expectation of AI-assisted development and "LLM Fundamentals (Understanding model types, capabilities, limitations, and selecting the right [model])". Process is agile with 3-month rough planning, sprint goals, research and prototyping, product architects refining technical solutions, automated tests and code reviews. There is also a Site Reliability Engineering role and a Cloud/DevOps Architect/Tech Lead track (the latter now closed).
Notable / other: Engineers work with a Product Owner on user stories and acceptance criteria; explicit emphasis on knowledge sharing and continuous process improvement. The domain — PLM for aerospace and automotive manufacturers — is about as stable and unglamorous as enterprise software gets.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (m/f/d) - Poland [.NET / Azure]; Senior Software Engineer (m/f/d) [Poland]; Senior Site Reliability Engineer (m/f/d).
