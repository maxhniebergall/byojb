# Omnissa — research

What they do: Omnissa is an enterprise digital-workspace software company — the former VMware End-User Computing business, now standalone. In their own words: "Omnissa is the first AI-driven digital work platform, built to support flexible, secure, work-from anywhere experiences. We integrate industry-leading solutions—including Unified Endpoint Management, Virtual Apps and Desktops, Digital Employee Experience, and Security & Compliance—into a seamless, autonomous workspace that adapts to how people work. Our platform boosts employee engagement while optimizing IT operations, security, and cost." Products visible in role names include Workspace ONE (UEM), Horizon (VDI/virtual desktops) and a "Freestyle" workflow product.

How they describe themselves: Their stated core values, quoted from their own JD boilerplate: "Guided by our Core Values— Act in Alignment, Build Trust, Foster Inclusiveness, Drive Efficiency, and Maximize Customer Value —we're growing rapidly and committed to delivering meaningful impact." Role-level framing: "Engage with a passionate, start-up-minded team focused on innovation and customer success"; "Prioritize customer needs to deliver high-quality, impactful solutions"; and a stated ownership model — "Embrace a service ownership mindset—managing automation, CI/CD, monitoring, and quality of the services you build." Their JDs also carry an unusual hiring-integrity paragraph: "we use standard interview and verification practices designed to ensure alignment and protect both candidates and the organization."

Size / stage / funding: Large established enterprise-software vendor (a divested VMware business unit, privately held). Self-described as "growing rapidly". Customer base framing from their own JD: "a platform used by every Fortune 500 company". Exact headcount not verified here.

Locations / HQ: Multi-site. Offices named across the live postings: Mountain View, California; Atlanta, Georgia; Vancouver, BC, Canada; plus Canada-Ontario and Remote-Mexico hiring locations. A substantial Vancouver, BC engineering site is evident from three separate Staff Software Engineer postings there.

Remote policy: Mixed and role-specific. Some roles are fully remote by geography ("Remote - Canada", "Remote - Mexico"), several are office-attached (Vancouver BC Office, Mountain View Office, Atlanta Office), and at least one DevOps role is extracted as hybrid and US-restricted.
