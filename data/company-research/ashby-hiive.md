# Hiive — research
Provider/key: ashby:hiive | company_type: product

What they do: Hiive operates an institutional-grade marketplace for private (pre-IPO) stock. It connects accredited investors with shareholders of venture-backed private companies — founders, employees and early investors seeking liquidity before an IPO — and brings price transparency, standardization and automation to what has historically been an opaque, broker-mediated secondary market. It serves buyers, sellers and issuers, and publishes market reports and pricing data on the private-company asset class.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "to unlock the value of the private market," delivered as "a centralized, standardized liquidity solution for private companies and their shareholders." The founding story frames the company against incumbent inefficiency: the founders are former principals of a leading PE/VC secondary-market brokerage who spent 14+ years navigating opaque private markets and built Hiive to fix the traditional inefficiencies of pre-IPO secondary trading, "while retaining best practices from global secondary markets." In its own JDs: "Recognized as one of Canada's fastest-growing companies and backed by leading U.S. investors, Hiive is profitable, well-capitalized, and building a high-performance team to meet growing demand and pursue new market opportunities." No formal enumerated values list is published on the careers page (the page is a client-rendered shell with no substantive text).

Size / stage / funding: 51-200 employees. Explicitly **profitable and well-capitalized** by its own statement, backed by leading U.S. investors, and cited as one of Canada's fastest-growing companies. A Canadian-domiciled company rather than a US company with a Canadian satellite.

Locations / HQ: HQ in downtown Vancouver, BC. Hires remote across Canada and the US.

Remote policy: Hybrid-by-choice with a genuine remote track. The Senior Software Engineer req is posted to "Vancouver, BC (HQ), Remote (US), Remote (Canada)" and states "Open to Canada and US-based remote candidates." Vancouver-based staff get a dedicated desk downtown with an onsite gym and rooftop amenity; the benefits copy is explicitly conditional — "If you are based in Vancouver, enjoy a dedicated desk in our Vancouver, BC HQ" — which confirms non-Vancouver staff are a supported category rather than an exception. Two all-team "onsite" retreats per year, plus a dense in-person social calendar (bi-weekly catered lunches, bi-weekly "Friday bar," team workouts, summer and holiday parties) that is Vancouver-centric.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes — first-party and unambiguous.** The Senior Software Engineer posting lists "Remote (Canada)" as a location, states "Open to Canada and US-based remote candidates," and — decisively — publishes a *separate compensation tier titled "Remote, Canada"* at CA$150K–CA$250K base plus equity, distinct from the Vancouver tier (CA$150K–CA$275K) and the US-remote tier (US$150K–US$225K). A company does not publish a dedicated remote-Canada pay tier unless it employs remote Canadians. BC HQ also means Pacific Time — one hour off Mountain, the easiest possible overlap. Note the *other* two engineering reqs (Intermediate Software Engineer; Senior Software Engineer, Elixir) are posted Vancouver-HQ-only, so remote-Canada applies to some but not all openings.

Engineering & tech: Elixir is a named part of the stack (a dedicated "Senior Software Engineer, Elixir" req). CPTO Stuart Eccles is a serial founder with 25+ years in software product design who co-founded the agency Made by Many, suggesting a product-design-led engineering organization. Marketplace/trading systems: order handling, pricing/market data, and the compliance surface of a regulated broker-dealer. No public engineering blog or handbook found.

Notable / other: Regulated — member of FINRA/SIPC in the US and a registered exempt market dealer in multiple Canadian provinces, which implies meaningful compliance, auditability and data-integrity engineering. Leadership: Sim Desai (CEO, pioneered VC-backed pre-IPO share trading at a major incumbent), Sarah Huggins (COO & General Counsel), Stuart Eccles (CPTO), Prab Rattan (Head of Institutional Markets, with transaction experience on SpaceX, Stripe, Palantir and Zoom). Benefits: 100% employer-paid health & dental premiums plus a Health/Personal Spending Account for Canadian employees; sponsorship, immigration and relocation offered for exceptional candidates.
