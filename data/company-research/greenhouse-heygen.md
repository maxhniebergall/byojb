# HeyGen — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:heygen | company_type: product

What they do: HeyGen is a generative-AI video company. Its products create and animate synthetic presenters ("avatars") and translate video across languages — the named products in its live JDs are Avatar IV, Photo Avatar, Instant Avatar, Interactive Avatar and Video Translation. In their own words: "At HeyGen, our mission is to make visual storytelling accessible to all. Over the last decade, visual content has become the preferred method of information creation, consumption, and retention. But the ability to create such content, in particular videos, continues to be costly and challenging to scale. Our ambition is to build technology that equips more people with the power to reach, captivate, and inspire audiences." They describe themselves as "at the forefront of developing applications powered by our cutting-edge AI research."

How they describe themselves: Careers copy offers "Competitive salary and benefits package," a "Dynamic and inclusive work environment," "Opportunities for professional growth and advancement," and a "Collaborative culture that values innovation and creativity." They maintain a public "Mission and Culture doc" (a Google Doc linked from every JD) — it is linked but was not readable via fetch, so their fuller values statement remains unverified here. The JDs' preferred-qualifications sections ask for "Prior experience in a startup or fast-paced tech environment."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, rapidly scaling AI startup. Widely reported as one of the faster-growing AI-native revenue stories of the 2024–2026 cycle. Small relative to its revenue; still firmly in the hyper-growth phase and dependent on continued momentum in the generative-video market.

Locations / HQ: Roles are posted across **San Francisco, Palo Alto, Los Angeles, Toronto** and (for one req) **Singapore**. Toronto is a real HeyGen engineering location.

Remote policy: **Not stated as remote anywhere.** Every live posting on record lists a set of physical office cities as its location, with no "Remote" designation on any of them — this is an office-based company with several sites, and candidates are expected in one of those cities.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Effectively no.** HeyGen does have a Canadian presence and does hire in Canada — Toronto appears as an eligible location on four of the five live engineering reqs, and one existing comp record is denominated in CAD, so a Canadian employing entity plausibly exists. But the location is specifically **Toronto**, listed as an office alongside SF/Palo Alto/LA, with no remote tag on any posting. From Kimberley, British Columbia that is a relocation requirement, which is disqualifying. There is no evidence of remote-anywhere-in-Canada hiring.

Engineering & tech: Genuinely substantial infrastructure work despite the consumer-facing product. The Data Infrastructure Engineer req covers "Large scale data acquisition," a "Multi-modal data processing framework," "Storage and computation efficiency," and "AI model evaluation and productionization infrastructure," with preferred experience in GPU computing, distributed data processing systems and "building large scale batch inference system." The Dev Productivity req is a classic developer-platform role: "Design, build, and maintain high-performance CI/CD pipelines," "Optimize build and test infrastructure for faster feedback loops," internal tooling and developer environments, with GitHub Actions / Jenkins / GitLab CI / Buildkite / CircleCI, build systems (Bazel, CMake, Make, Gradle), Python dependency management (poetry, uv), containerization and Kubernetes, and Python/Bash/Go. Docker image build optimization, Bazel and GPU experience are called out as preferred.

Compensation: Existing registry records show a posted range around US$180,000–US$240,000 for platform/infrastructure at the mid-senior level, and a CAD-denominated record at the senior ML-engineering level in the same numeric band. The Data Infrastructure Engineer JD states no range ("Competitive salary and benefits package").

Notable / other: The AI Compute Infrastructure req adds Singapore, suggesting a growing APAC presence. Model evaluation and batch-inference infrastructure are explicitly named as owned surfaces, which is unusually infra-mature for a company of this stage.

Open relevant roles (sample): Data Infrastructure Engineer; Software Engineer, AI Compute Infrastructure; Backend Engineer - Infrastructure; Senior Software Engineer, Dev Productivity; Software Engineer, Data.
