# Rival Technologies (Rival Group) — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:rivaltechnologies | company_type: product

What they do: Rival Technologies is the technology arm of **Rival Group**, describing itself in its own JD as "the world's best conversational research platform and one of Vancouver's fastest-growing tech companies". The group has two sides: **Rival Technologies** (the SaaS product — conversational market-research / customer-insights software) and **Reach3 Insights**, "our consulting side… an award-winning market research agency with offices in Chicago, Toronto, and Vancouver". Named brand customers in their own words: Coca-Cola, Kellogg's, Dell Technologies, REVOLT TV and Roblox. The group's stated purpose is "helping brands like [those] get better insights they can use to deliver better products, create more impactful marketing campaigns, and improve the end-to-end customer experience."

**Company-type note:** the group contains a market-research *agency* (Reach3 Insights), but the hiring entity here is Rival Technologies, the software platform, and the role builds and operates that platform's own infrastructure. Classified `product`, not consulting — though the agency half of the business is worth knowing about, since agency work brings client-deadline pressure into the wider org.

How they describe themselves: "When you join Rival Group, you're not just accelerating your career—you're also helping shape the future of customer insights and market research." They call themselves "a forward-thinking, results-driven organization obsessed with helping innovative brands get closer to their customers in a way that feels fresh, organic, and fun", note "Our customers LOVE our tech and services. We're growing fast—and we're inviting great people to join us on the ride", and close with "If making measurable, immediate impact while having fun sounds like your jam, then we invite you to apply!" — emoji included. Their full stated values are four words: **Courage, Results, Energy, Kinship**, which "guide how we work together, make decisions, and show up for customers and each other".

Size / stage / funding: Private, growth-stage, self-described "one of Vancouver's fastest-growing tech companies". Headcount, funding round and profitability are **unverified** — no first-party page was reachable this session.

Locations / HQ: Vancouver, British Columbia is the technology home base; the Reach3 agency has offices in Chicago, Toronto and Vancouver.

Remote policy: The JD calls the DevOps role "a remote opportunity", but the benefits section lists a "Hybrid working model and home office benefits", and the extracted facets record `remote_policy: hybrid`. The two statements are in tension — read as remote-capable with a hybrid default, and confirm directly.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes, with a timezone caveat.** The JD opens: "Rival is looking for a **Canada based** (Ideally East Coast as you will be working EST hours) DevOps Engineer", and the fine print states "You must be eligible to work in Canada to be considered for this role." So Canada-remote is explicitly on offer — but **EST working hours are stated as the expectation**, which from Mountain Time means a 6am–2pm day. Note the company's own base is Vancouver (Pacific Time), so the EST framing appears to follow the customer/agency side rather than the whole company. Worth confirming.

Engineering & tech: The role reports to the VP of Engineering and involves "leading the DevOps team, designing and implementing scalable infrastructure, ensuring smooth deployment processes, and monitoring and maintaining the stability and performance of our production systems". Scope: AWS cloud infrastructure and third-party integrations; IaC via Terraform, CloudFormation and AWS CDK; CI/CD pipelines with risk assessment across the delivery lifecycle; system performance monitoring and high availability; incident troubleshooting and root-cause analysis; regular security audits with the security team; and representing DevOps at Change Advisory Board (CAB) meetings and signing off releases. Required: 5+ years DevOps/systems engineering with at least 2 years leading (for the senior level), AWS, JavaScript/Node/TypeScript or Python or Go, Terraform/CloudFormation/AWS SDK/shell, CI/CD (Bitbucket Pipelines, Jenkins, GitLab CI or CircleCI), Docker with AWS ECS/Kubernetes, cloud networking and pipeline security, Datadog and AWS X-Ray, security controls as code, databases and big data sets, and **"Experience in a 24x7 production environment"**.

Notable / other: They state explicitly "We personally review every application and do not use AI or automated systems to screen or filter resumes" — unusual and candidate-friendly, and the opposite of the disclosures at HOOPP, Canadian Tire and FutureFit AI. They hold security and compliance certifications, and "final applicants may be required to undergo a background check". Benefits: comprehensive paid time off, volunteer and professional-development days, competitive health/vision/dental, a health spending account, "generous sick, personal, and mental health days", and additional mental-health benefits through Canada Life. "No recruiters and no phone calls, please."
