# NVIDIA — research
Provider/key: workday:nvidia/nvidiaexternalcareersite | company_type: product
What they do: In their own words, NVIDIA "engineers advanced chips, systems, and software for artificial intelligence and accelerated computing." Focus areas span AI infrastructure (GPUs, DGX/HGX systems, DGX Cloud, NIM inference microservices, CUDA and the surrounding software stack), graphics (RTX, GeForce NOW), industrial digitalization (Omniverse), autonomous vehicles, and healthcare. Customers are hyperscalers, enterprises, governments, OEMs, researchers and consumers. Leadership describes the company as "reshaping the future of computing."
How they describe themselves: A learning-oriented, mission-led meritocracy. Their own phrasing: "a culture where people can do their life's work", "everyone has a voice", and "the mission is boss" — the last being a well-known internal principle that priority flows from the work rather than the org chart. The about page is largely promotional and does not enumerate a formal values list or an HR policy.
Size / stage / funding: Public (NASDAQ: NVDA), among the largest companies in the world by market capitalisation, deeply profitable, roughly 30,000+ employees. Stability risk is effectively nil; the risk is the opposite — extreme scale and internal competition.
Locations / HQ: HQ Santa Clara, California. From the live requisition set, engineering is concentrated in US-CA-Santa Clara, with substantial sites in Israel (Tel Aviv, Yokneam, Raanana — the Mellanox heritage), India (Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad), China (Shanghai, Shenzhen, Beijing), Vietnam (Ho Chi Minh City), Ukraine (Kyiv), Poland (Warsaw), Germany, Finland, Sweden, and US-NC-Durham. There is a **Toronto, Canada** site, but in the current live set it carries exactly one relevant requisition (Senior ASIC Infrastructure Engineer), and it is location-bound rather than remote.
Remote policy: Not stated on the about page. The requisition data is the real evidence: postings are overwhelmingly tied to named offices, with a small number of US-state-scoped remote reqs (US-CA-Remote, US-TX-Remote, US-NY-Remote) and a few European remote reqs (Germany-Remote, Finland-Remote, Sweden-Remote). NVIDIA is broadly an in-office-first employer.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified NO.** Across 30 live relevant requisitions there is no Canada-remote posting of any kind. The only Canadian requisition is Senior ASIC Infrastructure Engineer tied to the Toronto office, and it is chip-design infrastructure rather than software platform work. Remote requisitions that do exist are scoped to specific US states or specific European countries, none of which a Canada-based worker can occupy. A candidate in Kimberley, British Columbia is not eligible for any live role here.
Engineering & tech: Extremely deep infrastructure organisation and, on technical merit, close to an ideal match for a platform/ML-infrastructure profile. Live groups include **DGX Cloud** (production engineering, orchestration, distributed systems, storage, resilience/reliability engineering, attestation services, network infrastructure, AI infrastructure, data services), **NIM** (inference microservices — platform SDK/framework, factory container and cloud infrastructure), deep-learning libraries and compiler infrastructure (CUTLASS, compiler CI), AI safety and security harness/platform engineering, AIOps, data-platform observability, MLOps, and large DevOps/build-infrastructure functions. Also large VLSI/CAD, physical-design and STA organisations, which is what most of the "Backend Engineer" titles in this registry actually refer to.
Notable / other: **Title collision warning for this registry** — a large share of NVIDIA's "Backend Engineer" titles (STA Backend Engineer, Physical Design Backend Engineer, Senior Compiler Engineer - Backend GPU) mean *chip-design back end* or *compiler back end*, not server-side software. These should not be read as backend-software matches. Pay transparency is strong: US requisitions post explicit USD ranges (California/Colorado/New York transparency law), and equity is attached to essentially every senior+ req.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer - NIM Factory Container and Cloud Infrastructure (US-CA-Santa Clara); Principal Software Engineer, DGX Cloud Production Engineering; Senior Software Engineer, DGX Cloud Orchestration; Senior Platform Engineer, Network Infrastructure - DGX Cloud; Senior System Software Engineer - Data Platform Observability; Senior ASIC Infrastructure Engineer (Toronto, Canada).
