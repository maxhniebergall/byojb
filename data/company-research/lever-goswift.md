# Swiftly, Inc. — research
Provider/key: lever:goswift | company_type: product

What they do: Swiftly is "the leading transit data platform for agencies to share real-time passenger information, manage day-to-day operations, and improve service performance." Its mission, in its own words, is "to help cities move more efficiently." Over **200 transit agencies in 12 countries** — including LA Metro, MARTA, SEPTA and MBTA — use Swiftly, which the company says improves on-time performance by up to 40% and passenger information accuracy by up to 50%. The product ingests billions of data points from vehicle fleets and turns them into real-time arrival predictions, operational dashboards and feeds for partner rider apps.

How they describe themselves: "Engineering at Swiftly is not only about writing code – we believe in creating empowered product teams that work together to conceptualize new features and bring them to life. Each team aims to strike a balance between delivering incremental improvements, creating prototypes to test new ideas and mitigate risks, and building scalable software using industry best practices. We're guided by a mission to positively impact transit riders, and we embrace **humility and intentionality** in how we make technical decisions so that we best meet our customers' needs." They also state they value candidates who "angle toward growth" and encourage applying at half the listed requirements, and note "we know the tech landscape is ever-changing and strongly support individual growth."

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2014 in San Francisco by Will Dayton, Jonny Simkin and Michael Smith. 51-200 employees. ~$13M raised; Series A investors include Via ID and Plug and Play Tech Center, plus a **strategic growth investment from JMI Equity** — a growth-equity firm rather than a pure venture round, which typically indicates real revenue traction. Publicly stated intent to roughly double headcount and expand customer-facing teams.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco, CA. Distributed hiring across most of the US.

Remote policy: Distributed/remote hiring across a defined geography. From the JD: "Even though Swiftly's HQ office is located in San Francisco, CA, we are open to candidates in **most locations across the U.S. as well as Ontario and British Columbia, Canada**. At this time we are unable to provide Visa sponsorship."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, and specifically British Columbia.** Both live engineering postings list "British Columbia, remote, Ontario, United States" and the JD text names Ontario and BC explicitly as eligible provinces. BC is one of only two eligible Canadian provinces, and the candidate is in BC. Timezone is favourable — HQ is Pacific, so Mountain Time is only one hour ahead of the company's centre of gravity.

Engineering & tech: Real-time data systems. The backend role owns "the real-time source of truth for our system, developing predictive algorithms that power accurate passenger information" — map-matching, vehicle location processing, ETA prediction, disruption detection. Work includes real-time data pipelines, backend services, cloud APIs, and notably **data infrastructure for algorithm development**: "pipelines for capturing and replaying real-time data, offline evaluation frameworks, and shadow testing infrastructure." Stack signals: **cloud-based Java services and APIs**, real-time stream processing. The second role is an IoT-flavoured senior engineering position. Teams are described as full-stack, spanning frontend and backend.

Notable / other: Customers are public transit agencies — a slow-moving, procurement-driven, mission-critical public-sector customer base. The domain is genuinely "boring" in the desirable sense: reliability and data correctness matter more than novelty.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer – Backend (US / Ontario / British Columbia, remote); Senior Software Engineer (IoT) (US / Ontario / British Columbia, remote).
