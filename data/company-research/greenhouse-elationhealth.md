# Elation Health — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:elationhealth | company_type: product

What they do: Elation Health builds a cloud-based, "clinical-first" electronic health record (EHR) and billing platform purpose-built for **independent primary care** practices in the United States. The product spans the clinical record, patient engagement, and — since the acquisition of LightningMD — integrated medical billing, delivered as a unified EHR + billing platform. It also ships Elation Note Assist, an AI-powered ambient scribe that drafts documentation from the visit to cut post-visit paperwork. Customers include traditional independent practices, on-site employer clinics, at-home care models and hybrid teams. Elation serves roughly 24,000 clinicians caring for more than 12 million Americans.

How they describe themselves: "Our team is committed to enhancing physician and patient quality of life through Elation, a SaaS cloud-based clinical platform. Since inception, we've been focused on building an intuitive, reliable experience that empowers physicians to deliver better care to patients." The broader company framing is that Elation "supports primary care clinicians by relieving administrative burden and restoring the doctor-patient relationship so that they can return to the craft of medicine," and its mission is "helping independent primary care thrive."

The hiring language is notably low-pressure and inclusive rather than hype-driven: "If you're excited about building AI-powered tools that help doctors and patients, we want to hear from you — even if you don't check every box below!" and "Please don't see everything in this post as a 'must have'… We especially encourage applications from women, people of color, the LGBTQ+ community, people with disabilities, neurodivergent people, parents, carers and people from lower socio-economic backgrounds. If you have any requirements or accommodations that would help you interview or work comfortably, please let us know." There is no "fast-paced," "rocketship" or "wear many hats" language anywhere in the JD.

Size / stage / funding: Private, founded **2010** — an unusually long-established company for this registry, well past the fragile stage. San Francisco origin. Growth-stage per the JD's extracted company stage; acquisitive (LightningMD). No public funding or profitability figure found.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco, California (nominal HQ). The engineering organisation is distributed.

Remote policy: **"Our engineering team is fully remote"** — stated verbatim in the JD. Teams are cross-functional and small: "A typical team at Elation includes 3–5 engineers, an engineering manager, a product manager, a UX designer, and QA working together on a specific product area."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, explicitly and in writing.** "This role is open to candidates in the US, Canada, and New Zealand." The posting further quotes a distinct Canadian salary band in CAD, which is strong evidence of a real Canadian employment arrangement rather than an aspirational geography list. A US/Canada/New Zealand span also implies genuine timezone tolerance, which is a good sign for Mountain Time.

Engineering & tech: Stack is **Python, MySQL, React, AWS**, plus LLM integration patterns (OpenAI, Anthropic named). The open Staff role sits on the *application* team building AI-driven product experiences — "deeply involved in the system design, development, and maintenance of these products." Ownership model is end-to-end: "You'll own features end-to-end — from architecture and design through deployment and production support," and "Engineers have autonomy over technical implementation decisions while partnering with Product on feature scope and priorities." The JD's 6–12 month expectations include shaping technical direction for AI-native product experiences, mentoring engineers, and building partnerships with support and infrastructure to triage bugs and resolve production issues. Requirements are 5+ years of professional development, API building on modern backends, and "enthusiasm and interest in building systems using AI and large language models" (this one is Essential, not optional). DevOps/observability experience is listed as valued but not required. The JD includes a well-structured first-60-days plan, which suggests a considered onboarding culture.

Compensation: The Staff Software Engineer posting states its band openly and in both currencies: **"$170,000 - $200,000/yr USD OR $185,000 - $215,000/yr CAD"** (base). Note that the CAD figure is a genuinely separate, higher-numbered Canadian band, not a conversion artefact.

Notable / other: The domain is about as "boring, mission-critical" as software gets — clinical records for primary care, where correctness, uptime and regulatory compliance dominate. Independent primary care as a market segment is under pressure from consolidation, which Elation positions itself against ("independent primary care is resilient" is one of its own research themes). The company publishes survey research on the independent-primary-care market.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer (US, Canada, OR New Zealand — Remote).
