# BMO (Bank of Montreal) — research
Provider/key: workday:bmo/external | company_type: product (bank / financial institution)
What they do: BMO Financial Group is one of Canada's Big Five banks (founded 1817), offering personal and commercial banking, wealth management and capital markets across Canada and the US. Its technology org builds and runs internal banking platforms: payments, cloud (Azure/AWS), data platforms, RPA and a growing "Agentic AI"/GenAI engineering group.
How they describe themselves: JD boilerplate emphasises "boldly grow the good in business and life", inclusion, and standard large-bank purpose language. Individual JDs stress independence ("works independently with minimal supervision", "regularly handles non-routine situations"), accountability and process improvement rather than startup urgency.
Size / stage / funding: Public (TSX/NYSE: BMO), ~55,000 employees, long-established and profitable. Maximum stability; no runway risk.
Locations / HQ: HQ Toronto/Montreal. Registry postings are overwhelmingly Toronto, ON; some Calgary, AB; a few US (Chicago, IL); two listed as "REMOTE/TELETRAVAIL, ON, CAN".
Remote policy: Predominantly hybrid out of Toronto (BMO's stated norm since 2023 is multiple in-office days). Two postings carry the Workday location "REMOTE/TELETRAVAIL, ON, CAN" and address "VIRTUAL(R)59 - REMOTE/TELETRAVAIL - ON - BMO", i.e. a virtual role attached to the Ontario region.
Remote-Canada eligibility: PARTIAL / mostly no. 18 of 20 live relevant roles are Toronto or Calgary hybrid-in-office, which is disqualifying for a BC-based candidate who cannot relocate. The two "REMOTE/TELETRAVAIL, ON, CAN" roles (Software Developer R260000290; Senior Cloud Engineer R260015831) are genuinely virtual but are filed to the Ontario region — whether BC residency is acceptable is UNVERIFIED from the JD text.
Engineering & tech: AWS (Lambda, API Gateway, ECS/EKS, RDS/DynamoDB, SQS/SNS, Cognito, CloudFront), Azure cloud platform engineering, Node.js/TypeScript, React/Angular, Java payments systems, Oracle DBA/RHEL sysadmin work, RPA + agentic AI automation, MLOps. Work is internal-facing platform/plumbing — a good structural match to a utility/infrastructure preference.
Notable / other: Compensation is posted directly on Canadian JDs by policy. Observed CAD bands: $61,600–$113,900 (mid), $75,900–$141,900 (senior), $94,600–$176,000 (principal). US-posted equivalents are higher.
