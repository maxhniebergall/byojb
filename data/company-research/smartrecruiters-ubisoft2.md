# Ubisoft (Montréal) — research
Provider/key: smartrecruiters:ubisoft2 | company_type: product

Entity check: the `ubisoft2` SmartRecruiters board matches Ubisoft, the video-game publisher —
confirmed from the JD bodies, which reference "Ubisoft Montréal", "Ubisoft's Data Office", "Ubisoft's
online services across all studios and titles" and "Ubisoft games". No name collision.

What they do: Ubisoft develops and publishes video games. The engineering roles captured here are
not game-development roles but platform/backend roles supporting them: the Online Services group,
which runs the gateway and hosting platforms "responsible for fronting Ubisoft's online services
across all studios and titles" and whose work "directly impacts the reliability, latency and security
of the player-facing traffic for hundreds of millions of users"; and the Player Data domain within
Ubisoft's Data Office, "whose mission is to use data to support players throughout their journey in a
safe and respectful environment" (trust and safety).

How they describe themselves: The available JD text is role-focused rather than values-focused. The
closest to culture language, verbatim: "You will work closely with a multi-disciplinary team of
programmers, SREs and game studios where everyone shares knowledge and is encouraged to evolve every
day", and a "collaborative DevOps culture". No Ubisoft careers/values page was fetched this session,
so the corporate values set is UNVERIFIED.

Size / stage / funding: Large, publicly traded French multinational (Euronext Paris) with studios
worldwide; Ubisoft Montréal is one of its largest studios. Not verified from a fetched source this
session. Note that the games industry broadly, and Ubisoft specifically, has been through a period of
restructuring and cost reduction — this is general knowledge and was NOT verified this session, so
treat it as unconfirmed context rather than a finding.

Locations / HQ: Ubisoft is headquartered in France; the relevant studio here is Ubisoft Montréal. All
four live postings are "Montreal, QC, Canada".

Remote policy: Not stated in any of the four captured JDs — the word "remote" does not appear in the
bodies at all. Postings are location-tagged Montreal with no flexibility language. UNVERIFIED beyond
that absence.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO / unsupported. All four live roles are Montreal, QC, with no remote or
distributed-work language anywhere in the JD text. Quebec-based roles at Canadian employers also
commonly carry French-language obligations. Nothing supports employment from British Columbia.

Engineering & tech: Genuinely infrastructural for a games company. Online Services / Gateway: Go,
TypeScript/Node.js and Lua; distributed gateway services optimising high availability, low latency,
scalability and security; an "API-driven Gateway in AWS" with self-serve onboarding for internal
customers; security by design (threat modelling, OWASP API Security Top 10, WAF and edge protection,
rate limiting); infrastructure-as-code with Terraform, AWS CDK or CloudFormation; observability with
metrics, structured logs, traces and SLO-driven alerting. Crossroads team: .NET Core, Docker, AWS,
microservices, IaC, CI/CD, working on services "with a significant legacy footprint" — restructuring,
optimisation and modernisation of critical distributed systems. MLOps role: Python or Rust, web
service APIs, Kubernetes, ArgoCD, AWS, Terraform, large-scale data pipelines, and deploying
predictive models to production for trust-and-safety use cases.

Notable / other: No compensation figure appears in any of the four JDs, and none was sourced.
Ubisoft's Montreal reqs give no pay range.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Developer (Online Services - Crossroads); Senior
Software Developer (Online Services - Hosting Platforms); Software Engineer - MLOps; Site Reliability
Specialist.

Sources: the 4 live JD bodies under https://jobs.smartrecruiters.com/ubisoft2 (cited; already captured
in data/posting-research/). No first-party Ubisoft page was fetched this session.
