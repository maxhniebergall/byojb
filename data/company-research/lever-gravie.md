Provider/key: lever:gravie | company_type: product

What they do: Gravie sells health benefits (employer-sponsored health insurance alternatives) to small and midsize US businesses and their employees. Its stated mission is "to create health benefits that actually benefit small and midsize businesses and their employees." It runs its own benefit products and services rather than acting as a broker or consultancy. Domain is US health insurance/claims/enrollment, i.e. HIPAA-regulated operational data.
