# Stitch Fix — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:stitchfix | company_type: product

What they do: Stitch Fix (NASDAQ: SFIX) is an online personal styling and retail service: clients
receive curated shipments ("Fixes") of clothing selected by a combination of human stylists and
algorithms. They cover women's, men's and kids' categories. Self-description: "Stitch Fix is
redefining retail by combining human creativity with advanced data science and Generative AI." They
describe having "disrupted the market by creating an entirely new category within retail" over
"fifteen years".

How they describe themselves: The careers site names a five-part values set, the "Stitch Fabric":
- "Be the Change: Everyone leads at Stitch Fix"
- "Win Together"
- "Commit to Excellence"
- "Be Client Obsessed"
- "Be a Trendsetter"
The JD copy adds its own framing: "We are a group of bright, kind people who are motivated by
challenge. We value integrity, innovation and trust... We cultivate a community of diverse
perspectives — all voices are heard and valued... We win as a team, commit to our work, and celebrate
grit together because we value strong relationships... We boldly create the future while keeping
equity and sustainability at the center of all that we do. We are the owners of our work and are
energized by solving problems through a growth mindset lens." On hiring they also say: "we're equally
committed to building yours. We believe in investing in our team as much as our technology."

Size / stage / funding: Public company, NASDAQ: SFIX. ~15 years old. Certified a "Great Place to
Work" for 2026-2027. Exact current headcount not stated on the pages available. Note: the retail
business has been through a well-documented multi-year contraction — but nothing about current
financial health was verifiable first-party this session, so treat stability as UNVERIFIED.

Locations / HQ: US company (San Francisco roots). Both live engineering roles are listed "Remote, USA".

Remote policy: The careers landing page does not state a remote policy. The engineering postings
themselves are labelled "Remote, USA", and compensation is described as spanning "our three geographic
areas" for "US-based employees" — so remote work is normal but explicitly bounded to the United
States.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **NO (effectively).** Roles are posted "Remote, USA" and the compensation
language is written entirely around "US-based employees hired into this role". No Canadian entity or
international hiring is mentioned anywhere in the JD or careers copy.

Engineering & tech: The ML Platform Engineer role is real infrastructure work — "building and
maintaining the critical infrastructure that powers machine learning and AI across our organization":
scalable, resilient services and frameworks for ML model training and deployment, feature engineering
and serving, candidate generation, and AI agent deployment and observability. The team supports
"full-stack data scientists" as its internal customers, offering consultation and platform support,
and the role is described as carrying "significant autonomy" to "shape the future of ML and AI at
Stitch Fix", codify best practices and guide team investments. Stack named: Python and Golang as
primary languages, plus Kafka, Postgres, Redis, FastAPI, Ray, AnyScale, LangGraph, LangSmith, and AWS.
Bar: 5+ years in software development focused on data, ML or AI infrastructure, with a track record of
scalable distributed production systems. Stitch Fix's data-science organisation has historically been
one of the larger and more publicly-documented ones in retail.

Notable / other: Compensation is posted under pay transparency and spans three US geographic areas.
The role is eligible for new-hire and ongoing RSU grants "depending on employee and company
performance", plus medical/dental/vision. Careers-page benefits include flexible time off, equity
eligibility, wellness programs and style discounts.

Open relevant roles (sample):
- ML Platform Engineer — Remote, USA — $136,000–$167,000 USD
- Platform Engineer — Remote, USA — $88,100–$147,000 USD
