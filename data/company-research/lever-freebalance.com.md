# FreeBalance — research
Provider/key: lever:freebalance.com | company_type: product (with a large services/advisory arm)

What they do: FreeBalance sells Government Resource Planning (GRP) software to governments. The flagship product is the **FreeBalance Accountability Suite™**, which the company describes as "the only Public Financial Management (PFM) solution designed exclusively by government, for government." Around the product sits a substantial services business — advisory, implementation and training — aimed at helping finance ministries modernise budgeting, treasury and financial-reporting systems. They call themselves "a specialist Business-to-Government (B2G) firm."

Customers: National governments and finance/budget ministries. Named recent clients on their site include the Philippines' Department of Budget and Management and the Autonomous Bougainville Government, alongside a broad international roster.

How they describe themselves: Mission is "PFM reform that matters" — achieving "public financial management reform through digital transformation" in order to "improve citizens' wellbeing, combat corruption and enable sustainable growth." They are a signatory of the UN Global Compact, and the site's stated commitments centre on transparency, accountability, governance improvement and sustainability. Note: this is a *social-impact* value set, not an engineering-culture value set — I found no published engineering principles, handbook, or statements about pace or how the work is done.

Size / stage / funding: Private, roughly 40 years of operation. Headcount and ownership structure were **not stated** on the pages reachable and are unverified. Longevity and a government customer base imply a stable, non-runway-dependent business rather than a venture-backed one, but I could not confirm profitability.

Locations / HQ: Head office in **Ottawa, Canada**, with additional offices "across North and South America, Europe, Africa and Asia." The live postings corroborate a globally distributed delivery footprint: Ottawa; Managua, Nicaragua; Bangalore, India; Colombo, Sri Lanka.

Remote policy: **No remote-work policy is stated** anywhere I could reach. The postings themselves point the other way — every one of the four in the registry is explicitly marked *onsite*.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified NO for the relevant role.** The registry's location field for the Senior AWS Platform Engineer reads "Ottawa, Ontario, Canada, onsite." The three Senior Java Software Developer postings are likewise onsite in Managua, Bangalore and Colombo. There is a Canadian entity (Ottawa HQ), so Canadian employment is clearly possible in principle — but the open infrastructure role requires physical presence in Ottawa, which does not work from Kimberley BC and cannot be reconciled by timezone or negotiation.

Engineering & tech: From the one substantive JD — the Senior AWS Platform Engineer is a "redesign our AWS environment" mandate: lead the design and implementation of a secure, scalable **multi-account AWS architecture**, enable **cost chargeback**, and establish long-term operational best practices. Explicitly "infrastructure-first, requiring strong operating system, networking, and security fundamentals, in addition to deep AWS experience." Elsewhere the stack is Java (the Accountability Suite and its lottery-of-modules is Java-based, per the "Senior Java Software Developer" postings). No engineering blog or public handbook found. The JD itself is unusually short (three sentences) — there is no information in it about team size, scope boundaries, on-call, or process.

Notable / other: The mix of a long-lived product plus heavy advisory/implementation services means engineering here is likely closer to enterprise delivery than to a product platform org, and government procurement cycles set the pace. Offshore delivery centres (Nicaragua, India, Sri Lanka) are a meaningful part of the org. The company is *not* a staffing or outsourcing firm — it sells and implements its own software — so `company_type` is left as product.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior AWS Platform Engineer (Ottawa, onsite); Senior Java Software Developer (Managua / Bangalore / Colombo, onsite).

Comp: No pay data found.
