# Inspiren — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:inspiren | company_type: product

What they do: Inspiren builds a connected sensing-and-care platform for senior living. Its hardware
(the AUGi wall-mounted sensor unit and AUGi Call emergency-call system) plus software give care teams
real-time behavioral and emergency alerts, fall detection, live video triage with two-way voice, and
community-wide care-utilization analytics. In their own words: "Inspiren offers the most complete and
connected ecosystem in senior living… Our integrated solutions seamlessly fit into existing
workflows, capturing everything happening within a community. Backed by nurse specialists and
powerful analytics, we provide the data operators need to make informed clinical and operational
decisions - driving efficiency, profitability, and better care outcomes." Customers are senior-living
operators; deployed in 230+ communities.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "to build a world where technology augments and empowers
human care." Founded by Michael Wang, "a former Green Beret turned cardiothoracic nurse", and they
lead with the claim that "compassionate care and technology can coexist - bringing peace of mind to
residents, families, and staff." Stated culture: "care drives everything"; the team "combines
clinical, senior living, and healthcare expertise with top-tier engineering and data science to build
technology that's both smart and compassionate." They describe practising "context-driven leadership
to ensure every decision is grounded in both clinical and technical reality," twice-annual
performance reviews tied to clear leveling criteria, fast action on engagement-survey feedback, and
dedicated manager training. JDs also ask for people who "thrive in a fast-paced environment."

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2016. ~150 employees, ~54 in product and tech, 3 offices. Private,
venture-backed: $35M Series A (March 2025) then a **$100M Series B (2025) led by Insight Partners**
with Avenir, Primary Venture Partners, Scale Venture Partners, Story Ventures, Third Prime and
Studio VC — roughly $155M total raised. Growth-stage and capital-rich, but still a startup.

Locations / HQ: New York City HQ; three offices total.

Remote policy: Remote, with a stated NYC preference. Postings read "Location: Remote, US or Canada.
NYC preferred."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **YES — verified, first-party.** Both relevant engineering postings state
"Location: Remote, US or Canada." Canada is explicitly in scope, not an exception. Caveat: "NYC
preferred" appears alongside it, so a Mountain-Time candidate is the non-preferred profile, and the
company runs on Eastern time (2 hours ahead).

Engineering & tech: Genuine backend/data-infrastructure substance. The Intelligence & Integrations
team owns "the core systems that enable our platform to ingest, process, and make sense of real-time
data from our hardware devices" — streaming ingest from IoT hardware, data pipelines, and
integrations with customers' Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems. Stated architectural direction:
"transitioning from our current point-to-point architecture to a more resilient, event-driven
system," evolving core data models and APIs. Stack: AWS-native and serverless-heavy — Lambda,
Fargate, SQS/SNS, Kinesis, DynamoDB, RDS; TypeScript and Node.js; PostgreSQL and DynamoDB; React a
plus. They ask for distributed-systems and secure-development mentorship, and they explicitly require
heavy AI-coding-tool use: "You actively use AI coding tools (Cursor, Claude Code) as a core part of
how you work… well beyond basic prompting and into automating tasks, long-running processes, and
complex integrations."

Notable / other: Named to Inc.'s 2026 Best Workplaces list. Clinician-led product model with nurse
specialists embedded. Healthcare data means HIPAA/PHI handling and hardware-in-the-loop reliability
constraints. Note a registry hygiene issue: this company appears under two provider keys,
`greenhouse:inspiren` and `ashby:inspiren`, with duplicate comp rows.
