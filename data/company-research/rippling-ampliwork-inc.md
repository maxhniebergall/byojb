# Ampliwork, Inc — research
Provider/key: rippling:ampliwork-inc | company_type: product (enterprise AI agents; borders on solution-delivery consulting — see below)
What they do: Ampliwork builds "Enterprise-Grade AI Agents that amplify Human Potential™", automating complex multi-step workflows in highly regulated industries — its JD names Financial Services specifically, and its site adds Professional Services, Transportation and Government. Marketing emphasises regulatory compliance, process optimisation and data analysis, with named clients Direxion, Natixis, Hilton Capital Management and LNER.
How they describe themselves: Trademarked slogans "Reimagining Work™" and "Amplifying Human Potential™", positioning AI as complementary to humans rather than a replacement. Recurring themes are compliance rigour, security-first design and measurable ROI. The JD asks for "a senior practitioner... with the autonomy to operate without close supervision from day one."
Size / stage / funding: Operating "since 2017". Headcount, funding and revenue are not disclosed anywhere on the site or the JD. The presence of named enterprise logos alongside a single open engineering role suggests a small company (likely tens of people). Stability unverified.
Locations / HQ: Offices listed as Boston, New York, London and Montreal. The one live engineering role is Montréal, Quebec, Canada.
Remote policy: Not disclosed on the website or the JD. The posting states a Montréal location with no remote language.
Remote-Canada eligibility: UNCLEAR, and effectively no. There is a Canadian (Montréal) entity, so Canadian employment is possible, but nothing states remote work and the role is posted to a specific office city. A Montréal-anchored role is disqualifying for a candidate in Kimberley BC who cannot relocate, unless remote is confirmed — and there is no evidence it is.
Engineering & tech: The Senior SecDevOps role is AWS-centric: VPC, EC2, S3, RDS, Lambda, ECS/EKS, ALB, Route 53, reserved and spot instances (Spotinst), Cost Explorer for spend optimisation, Terraform + AWS CLI IaC, Docker, CI/CD via GitHub Actions / Jenkins / CircleCI / GitLab CI, monitoring via CloudWatch, Datadog, New Relic, Prometheus, ELK and Grafana. Security scope covers IAM, encryption, secrets management, VPN, federated auth/SSO, API-key hygiene and compliance with SOC 2, ISO 27001 and GDPR. Applications deployed are Python and Next.js.
Notable / other: The JD is structurally sloppy — the requirements list ("4+ years of experience...") is filed under the heading "What you'll do", and the nice-to-haves under "What we're looking for". Combined with the breadth of the ask (cloud cost, security, compliance, networking, CI/CD, monitoring, SSO) this reads as a one-person infrastructure function. No compensation is stated and no company pay data exists; no band is recorded because a parametric guess for an unrecognised small company would be pure anchoring.
