# Comfy — research
Provider/key: ashby:comfy-org | company_type: product

What they do: Comfy is the company behind ComfyUI, the open-source node-graph workflow engine for visual/generative AI, plus commercial products built on it (Comfy Desktop, Comfy Cloud, an API and an Enterprise offering). Their own framing: "Comfy is the AI creation engine for visual professionals who demand control over every model, every parameter, and every output. The most powerful workflow engine for visual AI. Unlike tools that hide everything behind a prompt box, Comfy lets you connect models, processing steps, and outputs on a canvas where every decision is visible and every step is inspectable."

How they describe themselves: Two threads. Publicly, control, transparency and community — "complete control over every model, every parameter, and every output", users as "creative technologists" who "build what doesn't exist yet". Internally, per their own JD: "We're a small, intense team in San Francisco. Our team comes from Stability AI and Google, and many contributed to the ComfyUI ecosystem long before working here. Low ego, high ownership. We work hard and demand a lot of each other, but we have fun. Everyone here is building something meaningful that will end up being our life's work." They also state a learning-over-expertise hiring philosophy: "we care way more about your ability to learn and ship than whether you've used these exact languages before… We're outpacing the industry by learning what we need to solve problems, not by sticking to what we already know." The JD closes with "if you view yourself as top-tier talent, your future latent self is waiting for you at Comfy."

Size / stage / funding: "We recently raised $30M at a $500M valuation. The round was led by Craft, with participation from Pace Capital, Chemistry, TruArrow, and others." Team described only as "small". Started as an open-source project in 2023. Scale claims: 4 million users, 60,000+ community-built nodes, 150,000+ daily downloads, ~123K GitHub stars; used by teams at OpenAI, Netflix, Amazon Studios, Ubisoft, EA and Tencent.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco, USA (stated on both the site and the JD).

Remote policy: Mixed signals. The company describes itself as "a small, intense team in San Francisco", but the open Senior Software Engineer, Backend Generalist posting is listed simply as "Remote". No policy page, no async or handbook culture described.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear.** The posting's location is the bare word "Remote" with no country list, work-authorization clause or restriction stated anywhere in the JD — so Canada is neither included nor excluded on the evidence. Given an SF-centred "small, intense team", a US-default employment path is the more likely reality, but that is inference, not fact. Must be asked directly. Mountain Time is one hour off SF, so hours would not be an obstacle.

Engineering & tech: Stated stack is "Python, Go, Kubernetes, and Postgres on the backend". The backend role is substantively distributed-systems infrastructure: "building rock solid distributed systems, designing data models that support explosive growth, and solving difficult scaling and optimization problems", spanning both open-source ComfyUI and Comfy Cloud. Sample projects they list are strong plumbing work — "deploy ComfyUI's diverse custom nodes written by the open source community in a secure and maintainable way" (a genuinely hard multi-tenant sandboxing problem), "write algorithms to schedule a diverse and heterogenous set of workflows on GPU nodes, optimizing for both latency and throughput", and "build a notification system from scratch in one week". Kubernetes/Helm depth is a nice-to-have. Open source is at https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI with a blog at https://blog.comfy.org.

Notable / other: Business model is hybrid open-source core plus commercial cloud/enterprise. The role is pitched as leading "critical backend initiatives for the millions of creatives using ComfyUI". No compensation is posted. No benefits are described in the JD. The JD explicitly sets an ideas-to-production expectation of "days not weeks".

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Backend Generalist (Remote); Software Engineer, Core ComfyUI Contributor; Software Engineer, ComfyUI Desktop.
