# Connor, Clark & Lunn Investment Management — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:cclim | company_type: product

What they do: Connor, Clark & Lunn Investment Management (CC&L) is a **quantitative equity
investment manager** — part of the Connor, Clark & Lunn Financial Group, a Canadian multi-boutique
asset management firm. In their own words on the JD: *"We deliver foundational systems that shape
the future of how technology is used in a top performing quantitative equity fund that manages over
$75 billion in financial assets."* Investment decisions are made programmatically: *"Quantitative
equity funds use programmed investment strategies and as a result, our technology team is crucial
to its success."* The technology group is an in-house engineering function for the fund, not a
software vendor.

How they describe themselves: *"This is a fantastic opportunity in the exciting intersection of
finance and technology where investment decisions are made using technology."* On culture and
place: *"The team is headquartered and deeply rooted in West Coast Vancouver. We place high value
on maintaining an entrepreneurial spirit and creating a culture where each of us has opportunities
to succeed."* The stated hiring values are unusually explicit and technically framed:
- **Programming Knowledge** — CS degree or "exceptional programming skills gained through your work
  experience"
- **Passion for Technology** — *"we work on low-level foundational systems and software... Upfront
  knowledge is preferred but not required; if you are passionate about technology, learning new
  concepts is natural and fun."*
- **Collaborative Communication** — working across many teams and stakeholders
- **Project Ownership Mindset** — *"We own different technology stacks and are often responsible for
  exploring new technology to modernize existing systems. Our team needs to take initiative and
  proactively drive the execution of systems projects that we own."*
- **Troubleshooting and Problem Resolution** — lateral thinking, root-cause diagnosis
- **Growth Mindset** — lifelong learning
Notably absent: any "fast-paced", "hustle", "rocketship" or hype language.

Size / stage / funding: Established, privately held Canadian asset manager; the quant equity fund
alone manages **over $75 billion**. Revenue is fee-on-AUM — highly stable, not runway-dependent,
and about as far from startup risk as this batch gets.

Locations / HQ: **Vancouver, British Columbia** — explicitly "headquartered and deeply rooted in
West Coast Vancouver."

Remote policy: **Hybrid.** The posting is tagged `#LI-HYBRID` at the foot of the JD. No remote or
distributed-work language appears anywhere in the body.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — Vancouver hybrid.** The role is BC-based and paid in CAD, but it
is explicitly a hybrid Vancouver office role at a team that describes itself as deeply rooted in
that city. Kimberley (East Kootenays) is not commutable to Vancouver, so this is effectively an
onsite role requiring relocation.

Engineering & tech: Genuinely low-level infrastructure work on a **hybrid on-premise and
cloud-based platform**. Named areas: *"distributed computing, petabyte-scale data storage,
containerization, non-traditional high-performance databases, process orchestration, monitoring,
data visualization and DevOps."* The stated ownership scope is broad: *"You own the entire
technology infrastructure life cycle"* — engineering and supporting software/systems infrastructure,
introducing new foundational technologies, collaborating with software development teams on
infrastructure tools and processes, acting as a conduit between application development and IT /
network security, improving reliability, and gathering OS/application metrics for performance
tuning, fault finding and business continuity planning. Languages: **Kdb+/Q, Python, Java, C#,
Angular/TypeScript**. Also AWS, systems engineering, distributed systems, scripting, Windows and
Linux, databases, host-based networking.

Notable / other: Kdb+/Q is a distinctive, high-value niche skill in quant finance. The hiring bar is
deliberately open on experience level: *"We welcome applications from candidates with different
levels of experience, including interns, new graduates, and experienced professionals."* The
application asks for resume, cover letter **and academic transcripts** — an unusually formal,
finance-industry process.

Open relevant roles (sample): Infrastructure Software Engineer (Vancouver, BC). Note the registry
carries an empty `titles` list for this company despite a live posting.

Pay (stated on their own JD): *"The base salary range for full-time roles is $125,000 – $200,000
annually plus a competitive performance bonus. Compensation will be determined based on experience
and qualifications."* This is a single band spanning intern-to-experienced hiring, so it is not
level-specific; the performance bonus at a quant fund can be a material addition.
