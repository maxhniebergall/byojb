# Analog Devices — research
Provider/key: workday:analogdevices/external | company_type: product
What they do: Analog Devices (ADI, NASDAQ: ADI) is a large US semiconductor company designing and manufacturing analog, mixed-signal, power and digital-signal-processing integrated circuits for industrial, automotive, communications, instrumentation, aerospace and healthcare markets. Its software openings are largely in support of that hardware business — design tooling, manufacturing systems, internal infrastructure and, increasingly, internal AI/ML platform work.
How they describe themselves: The registry's JD bodies read as a mature large-cap engineering organization rather than a values-forward startup. The AI Infrastructure JD frames the work as building "reusable, org-wide architectural patterns and platforms" that "absorb operational complexity on behalf of AI builders across the organization" — explicitly internal-platform framing. No hustle or hype vocabulary. No values page was fetched.
Size / stage / funding: Large public company (tens of thousands of employees; multi-billion-dollar revenue), long-established and profitable. Not runway-dependent.
Locations / HQ: US Massachusetts (Wilmington / Boston area) dominates the software postings; other posted geos include US-NJ Somerset, US-OR Beaverton, US-CO Colorado Springs/Centennial, US-PA, and India (Hyderabad, Bangalore). No Canadian location appears in the 26 registry postings.
Remote policy: No remote-first policy evident. Every relevant posting is anchored to a named US or India site; several are semiconductor-fab-adjacent (physical design, packaging, test, facilities) and inherently onsite.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO / effectively disqualifying — all live relevant roles are US-site or India-site anchored, with no remote-Canada listing found and no stated remote policy. A BC-based candidate who cannot relocate is not eligible for these postings on the evidence available.
Engineering & tech: Heavy silicon focus (ASIC physical design, digital IC design and architecture, design verification, test engineering, semi packaging). The software-adjacent work includes AI/ML infrastructure spanning on-prem GPU clusters, hybrid cloud-edge and cloud-native (AWS/Azure) — model serving, orchestration, compute optimization, cost efficiency and governance — plus software infrastructure, DevOps/software support, SAP/database engineering and manufacturing (MES) systems.
Notable / other: Pay is genuinely transparent in their Workday postings (Colorado and Massachusetts ranges appear), which is why the registry already carries five band rows collected directly from their own JDs.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Engineer, Software Infrastructure; Senior Engineer, AI/ML Software; Principal AI Infrastructure Engineer; Senior Backend Software Engineer; Senior Engineer, Software Support Engineering (DevOps); Staff Engineer, Digital Design Engineering.
