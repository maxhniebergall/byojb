# Overstory — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:overstory | company_type: product
What they do: Overstory applies machine learning to satellite and aerial imagery to give electric utilities real-time vegetation intelligence — locating and prioritising trees and brush that threaten power lines, which is a leading cause of wildfires and outages. It sells to utilities as a vegetation-management platform and works with 40+ utilities worldwide, including 4 of the top 10 US utilities.
How they describe themselves: Climate-first. "The climate crisis is the defining challenge of our time—but it's also the greatest opportunity for innovation." Five stated values, verbatim: (1) "Tackling the climate crisis is our greatest mission. We act with urgency."; (2) "We're rooted in diversity. Just as ecosystems need biodiversity to thrive, our resiliency comes from our differences."; (3) "Trust is fundamental. We assume the best in everyone, and we share ideas openly so that we have a positive impact."; (4) "Our curiosity fuels our growth. We recognize that change is constant, and we find joy and power in exploration."; (5) "We care for each other. We love the power of machines but we nurture each other as humans." They also write: "We're outdoor enthusiasts, musicians, artists, athletes, parents, and adventurers… What unites us is a passion for solving complex problems, a commitment to climate action, and the belief that technology should be a force for good."
Size / stage / funding: Founded 2018 in Lisbon by Indra den Bakker and Anniek Schouten; now 100+ people across Europe and North America, twelve nationalities. Venture-backed and has closed a Series A (announced on their own blog); private, growth-stage. Leadership: CEO Fiona Spruill, CFO Doug Perrygo, COO Tamara Mendelsohn, VP Product & Strategy Brian Lafayette, Head of People Peternille Dekker.
Locations / HQ: Founded in Lisbon; also associated with Amsterdam and a US presence (Greentown Labs member). Effectively distributed across the Americas and Europe.
Remote policy: Remote-first, with people "living all over the U.S. and Europe" and an explicit belief that "people do their best work when they organize work around their life."
Remote-Canada eligibility: Partially — and the specific live role is a problem. The Staff Machine Learning Engineer – Wildfire posting is listed "Remote: United States | Canada", so Canadian employment is offered. However the same JD states a hard constraint: "Time zone requirement: Eastern North America (NST, AST, EST)." Kimberley, British Columbia is Mountain Time, two hours outside that window, so this posting is not workable as written without an explicit exception.
Engineering & tech: Large-scale geospatial ML — satellite imagery pipelines, vegetation-structure and fuel-load models, production model deployment. The Staff MLE role reports to the VP of Product Engineering, works alongside data scientists and ML engineers, and covers "architectural decisions" and "standards for modeling, experimentation, and deployment across Overstory" — i.e. genuine ML-platform and MLOps content, "balancing scientific rigor with practical engineering excellence," plus mentoring.
Notable / other: Climate-tech mission with a concrete, physical outcome (fewer wildfires, fewer outages). Utility customers make revenue durable and slow-moving. The one open engineering role is titled Staff, so the ladder tops out visibly.
Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Machine Learning Engineer – Wildfire (Remote: United States | Canada, Eastern North America time zones).
