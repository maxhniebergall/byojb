Entity check: registry name "Citi", Workday tenant `citi.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/2`. This is **Citigroup Inc.** (NYSE: C), the global bank.

What they do: Citi is a global systemically important bank organized into five business divisions, serving both institutional and consumer clients and specializing in cross-border activity. From its own about page: it "enables clients to grow across borders in the digital economy", moves **nearly $6 trillion in financial flows every day**, serves **19,000 institutional clients including 85% of the Fortune 500**, and operates in **more than 180 countries with physical presence in 90+ markets**. Its Services franchise (treasury and trade solutions, securities services) is essentially payments and custody infrastructure; Markets covers trading and execution; plus Banking, Wealth and US Personal Banking.

How they describe themselves: Stated mission, verbatim — "Citi's mission is to serve as a trusted partner to our clients by responsibly providing financial services that enable growth and economic progress." Stated principles: **taking ownership, delivering with pride, and succeeding together.** The tone is institutional, responsibility- and trust-framed; no growth-hacking or disruption language.

Size / stage / funding: Public (NYSE: C), roughly 220,000-240,000 employees worldwide, a globally systemically important bank. Maximum institutional stability in the sense of not going away, but the bank has run multi-year cost-reduction and organizational-simplification programs with associated headcount reductions, including in technology — so "stable" is true of the institution, not necessarily of any given team.

Locations / HQ: HQ New York City. Very large technology footprint. Relevant to this search: a substantial Canadian technology centre in **Mississauga, Ontario**, plus US technology hubs in Jersey City and New Castle, Delaware, Irving/Fort Worth Texas, and Tampa, with major offshore centres in India, Poland and Northern Ireland. The registry's postings resolve predominantly to Mississauga (Canada), Jersey City, New Castle and Irving.

Remote policy: Office-anchored hybrid. Citi operates a hybrid model built around a defined number of in-office days per week at a named location; there is no remote-anywhere posture, and the requisitions in the registry are each tied to a specific city. The corporate about page states no workplace policy.
