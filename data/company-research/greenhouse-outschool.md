# Outschool — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:outschool | company_type: product

What they do: Outschool operates a consumer marketplace of live, small-group online classes for kids, delivered over video by independent teachers. Launched in 2017, it found its first audience among secular homeschoolers, expanded into enrichment learning, and post-pandemic pivoted toward academic learning, AI-assisted features, and international expansion. Customers are families (two-sided marketplace: parents/learners on one side, independent teachers on the other). Y Combinator alum. Current build focus per their live Staff SWE JD: a new platform helping families discover and access **public-funded** educational resources, with an "AI-guided planning journey" covering discovery, payments, reimbursement workflows and supply eligibility.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "inspire kids to love learning." Careers-page values: *learning by doing with startup methods*, *scaling to grow impact globally*, *disrupting the status quo in education*. Their JD is explicit about the culture: "Driving disruptive, positive change in education is rewarding and hard. Outschool team members are encouraged to challenge themselves, take risks, and grow in their careers. We look for talented people whose **sense of urgency**, innate curiosity, and determination to drive impact will help Outschool achieve **outsized results**." Leadership principles emphasize bias toward action, hands-on ownership, being opinionated and determined. Benefits framing: "taking care of one another enables us to do our best work."

Size / stage / funding: ~175 people. Private, VC-backed (Lightspeed Venture Partners among investors); reached unicorn valuation during the 2021 edtech boom. Self-reported traction: 1.5M+ learners across 195 countries, >$100M annual bookings, $488M+ paid out to teachers, business grown 16x since 2019, 26.5M+ courses. Growth-stage, not stated as profitable.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco office (HQ). Team distributed across the U.S., Canada and South Korea; a coworking space in Busan for the Korea-based crew. Annual all-hands retreat.

Remote policy: Remote-first and distributed since day one — "no headquarters to commute to" in their own framing, though the current careers copy is more precisely **hybrid-for-the-Bay-Area, fully-remote-elsewhere**: "Bay Area-based employees work in the office part of the week and receive commute-related benefits. Team members outside the Bay Area remain fully remote." Home-office setup stipend plus monthly remote-work/internet stipends.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, unambiguously.** The live Staff Software Engineer JD is posted as "Remote (U.S. & Canada)", states "Outschool recruits across the US and Canada", publishes a dedicated Canadian salary range, and the legal footer says "Full-time employees must be a citizen or legal residents of the U.S. or Canada and reside in U.S. or Canada." No timezone requirement stated; team already spans US, Canada and Korea, so Mountain Time is unremarkable.

Engineering & tech: TypeScript, React, Node.js, GraphQL, PostgreSQL, AWS. Full-stack-oriented engineering (their staff-level JD asks for proficiency across the whole stack, not depth in one layer). Strong current push on "AI-native development" — the JD requires a "proven track record of demonstrated proficiency in AI-native development, and leading teams into it," and an "agentic-first development approach" to building the new platform. Standard scrum-ish process: stand-ups, sprint planning, retros, production deployments, and **on-call rotations**. Andy Chiu recently hired as Head of Engineering. Adjacent problem domains: search and discovery systems, marketplace technologies, payments.

Notable / other: Runs a non-profit arm, Outschool.org, with employee donation matching. Benefits include stock options, retirement plans, wellness stipend, coaching/therapy access, pet wellness plans, generous PTO and family leave, annual professional-development and DEI learning budgets, plus class budgets for employees' children. Pay is geo-tiered: US Tier 1 (SF Bay/NYC), Tier 2 (rest of CA, Seattle, Boston, Chicago, Denver, Austin, Portland metros), Tier 3 (everywhere else), with a separate Canadian band.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer (Remote US & Canada); Senior Software Engineer, Infrastructure.

Comp (stated on their own JD, Staff Software Engineer): **CAD $148,000 – $203,500 base** for Canada-based candidates, plus equity and benefits. US equivalents: Tier 1 $186,400–256,300; Tier 2 $158,440–217,855; Tier 3 $139,800–192,225.
