# CAE — research
Provider/key: workday:cae/career | company_type: product
What they do: CAE designs, integrates and delivers simulation and training systems — flight simulators, mission-readiness and training solutions for civil aviation, defence and security, and healthcare. In its own words: "Renowned globally for designing, integrating, and delivering state-of-the-art simulation and training systems"; "At CAE, our mission is clear: to help make the world a safer place. For nearly 80 years, we've driven innovation in simulation, training, and mission readiness to support critical operations worldwide. By leveraging advanced technologies, we empower our customers to operate smarter, faster, and more sustainably."
How they describe themselves: They frame engineers as "the technical visionaries driving our innovative solutions... you'll tackle complex challenges, collaborate across functions, and lead advancements that shape the future of our industry. Supported by a culture that fosters **flexibility, growth, and technological excellence**, our engineers are empowered to make a meaningful impact every day." Also: "Join a purpose-driven organization where **bold ideas are encouraged, collaboration drives progress, and your growth fuels our shared success**." One JD does use "fast-paced environment" as a requirement. They disclose using "AI-supported tools to help review applications, with human decision-making at every step".
Size / stage / funding: Large, long-established (≈80 years), publicly traded Canadian company. Benefits signal a mature employer: group insurance, telemedicine, EFAP, **group RRSP plus a CAE Pension Plan / Defined Benefit Plan**, **sabbatical leave**, flex time, enhanced parental leave. A defined-benefit pension is a strong stability marker and is rare in tech. Exact headcount/financials not verified from a first-party page here.
Locations / HQ: Montreal (St. Laurent), Quebec — Canadian HQ and the main Canadian engineering site. Live relevant postings also sit in **Binghamton, NY (USA)**, **Tampa, FL** and **Montevideo, Uruguay**, indicating a genuinely global engineering footprint.
Remote policy: Location-anchored, not remote. Benefits mention "flex time" but no remote-work policy is stated. One JD states residency in the strongest possible terms.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No (verified from the company's own JD).** The Senior Data Engineer posting opens with "***YOU MUST RESIDE IN MONTREAL***" in emphasised capitals. The other Canadian roles are likewise Montreal (St. Laurent). The remaining relevant openings are Binghamton NY, Tampa and Montevideo. There is no Canada-wide remote option evident, and nothing workable from Kimberley BC / Mountain Time. Note also that Montreal roles at a Quebec-headquartered employer typically carry a French-language expectation, though these particular JDs do not state one.
Engineering & tech: Data-platform work at CAE is on the **Microsoft stack** — Microsoft Fabric (Lakehouses, OneLake, pipelines, notebooks), medallion architecture across Bronze/Silver/Gold layers, Python, Spark, SQL, Kafka/Hadoop, Terraform, Azure Data Factory, Airflow, Databricks, Purview, ARM, SQL Server/PostgreSQL/Cosmos DB, Power BI, Kubernetes familiarity, Git and VS Code. Responsibilities are proper platform ownership: build and maintain the enterprise data platform (warehouses and lakes), ingest/transform/deliver from internal, external and IoT sources, apply DevOps practice to data delivery, monitor pipelines, enforce data governance/architecture/security standards, and provide technical leadership through code review and best-practice sharing. Elsewhere the portfolio spans SRE (including a messaging-platform SRE role centred on IBM MQ / Amazon MQ), cloud and infrastructure engineering, cybersecurity DevOps integration, and aircraft-dynamics simulation software.
Notable / other: This is close to an archetypal "boring, mission-critical" infrastructure employer — safety-critical simulation, an ~80-year history, a defined-benefit pension and sabbaticals — and the data-platform role is well-scoped internal plumbing with internal customers (analysts, data scientists, business users). The only structural problem is geography. Recorded band: Senior Software Engineer USD 153.3–169.5K, direct from their own Binghamton NY JD (a US-geo number, not applicable to Canadian roles).
