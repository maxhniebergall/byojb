# LangChain — research
Provider/key: ashby:langchain | company_type: product

What they do: Developer infrastructure for AI agents. Stated mission (verbatim): "to make intelligent agents ubiquitous. We build the foundation for agent engineering in the real world, helping developers move from prototypes to production-ready AI agents that teams can rely on." Started as widely adopted open-source libraries and now also sells a commercial platform. Product surface: **LangSmith** (observability, evaluation, deployment, Fleet, Sandboxes), the open-source frameworks **LangChain**, **LangGraph** and **Deep Agents**, plus a newly launched **LangSmith Engine** for autonomous agent improvement, and **SmithDB**, a purpose-built database for agent observability.

How they describe themselves: Four operating principles, verbatim: **Build something great**; **Embrace hot takes**; **Maximum agency**; **Run to the roar**. Role copy reinforces this: "you build it, you run it, you own it," "operational mindset and high agency," and "all team members have meaningful impact on what we build and how we work together."

Size / stage / funding: **$125M raised at Series B** from IVP, Sequoia, Benchmark, CapitalG and Sapphire Ventures. Self-reported traction: 100M+ monthly open-source downloads, 6,000+ active LangSmith customers, 5 of the Fortune 10 and ~35% of the Fortune 500 using LangSmith in production, with named customers including Klarna, Coinbase, Workday, Lyft, Cloudflare, Harvey, Rippling, Vanta, LinkedIn, Monday.com, Nvidia and Bridgewater. Headcount not published — unverified. 2025 recognition: Enterprise Tech 30, IA40, InfraRed 100, Forbes AI 50.

Locations / HQ: HQ not stated on the careers page; postings cluster on **Boston, MA**, with San Francisco, CA and New York, NY also listed. One role is Europe-remote.

Remote policy: Mixed and role-specific rather than company-wide. Most engineering roles are tied to a US office city; the Ingestion platform role states verbatim: **"This role is fully remote within Europe, excluding France."** Compensation copy notes "team members in the EU, UK, and APAC receive locally competitive benefits aligned with regional norms," implying employment entities in EU/UK/APAC.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No.** The only fully-remote posting is scoped to Europe (minus France); the rest are Boston/SF/NY. Canada appears nowhere in the postings, and the benefits language enumerates EU, UK and APAC without mentioning North America outside the US offices. Treat as not Canada-eligible.

Engineering & tech: The LangSmith team owns the core platform for observability, evaluation and production reliability of AI systems end-to-end — tracing, annotation, run rules, evaluations. The Ingestion platform role owns high-throughput, data-intensive ingestion and trace-query systems built on SmithDB, plus API/SDK/CLI standards and CI for SDK generation across Python, TypeScript, Go and Java, plus framework integrations. Stack: Go, Python, TypeScript (also Java), PostgreSQL, Redis, Kubernetes, cloud object storage, GCP and AWS, observability stacks (Datadog, Prometheus/Grafana, OpenTelemetry); columnar/OLAP knowledge is a nice-to-have. On-call rotation exists, framed around "post-incident learning, automation, and prevention." Hiring copy explicitly says "we don't expect every box checked."

Notable / other: Compensation philosophy is published in prose but without company-wide bands: "competitive compensation that includes base salary, variable compensation for relevant roles, meaningful equity, benefits, and perks. Actual compensation and offerings will vary based on role, level, and location." Individual US JDs do post ranges. "Background in high-growth startups" is listed as a nice-to-have.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Platform Engineer, Ingestion (Remote - Europe); Senior Backend Software Engineer, AI Observability & Evals Platform / LangSmith (SF, Boston, NY); Principal Software Engineer, AI Observability & Evals Platform (Boston); Senior Backend Engineer, LangSmith Deployments (Boston); Senior Software Engineer, SmithDB; Senior Backend Engineer, Enterprise Readiness & Identity.
