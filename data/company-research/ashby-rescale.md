# Rescale — research
Provider/key: ashby:rescale | company_type: product

What they do: Rescale sells a "digital engineering" / HPC-in-the-cloud platform: running large-scale simulation and CAE (computer-aided engineering) workloads on cloud compute, plus data management and an integrated catalogue of third-party engineering and R&D applications. From their own JD: "the leader in digital engineering… transforming how products are developed—through intelligent automation, applied AI, data management, and the integration of the world's largest network of engineering and R&D applications." Named target industries: aerospace, energy, life sciences, manufacturing. They are now layering agentic/AI-physics capabilities on top of the HPC platform.

How they describe themselves: "Rescale is pioneering the future of engineering and scientific discovery… Joining Rescale means becoming part of a diverse, collaborative, and mission-driven team that's unlocking faster innovation… We're solving complex challenges that traditional HPC can't—and we're seeking passionate, curious minds to help build the next wave of breakthroughs." Their stated DEI/culture principles are spelled out at length on the backend JD: "Welcome ALL and ALL are welcome"; "Show up with our whole selves"; "Be ready to learn, unlearn, and learn again"; "Respect others and their opinions… practice empathy… celebrate uniqueness"; "Seek to understand - ask more questions than one makes statements"; "Provide safety from judgment and preserve a collaborative environment." That is an unusually explicit and non-hustle culture statement.
