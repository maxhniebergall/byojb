# Fox (FOX Corporation) — research
Provider/key: workday:fox/domestic | company_type: product

What they do: FOX Corporation is a major American mass-media company (founded 2019 in the Disney/21CF split), operating FOX News, FOX Sports, the FOX Broadcasting Company, FOX Entertainment, Tubi and its streaming properties. The technology org builds and runs the consumer streaming products — video delivery, apps across every platform, identity, and the data/BI systems behind the business. Public, profitable, ~$14B revenue scale.

How they describe themselves: The engineering site frames the org as "a start-up with the resources and stability of an established, profitable company." Culture pillars stated there: **Innovation** — "fast paced, smart ideas, entrepreneurial spirit"; **Collaboration** — team motto "Test, Iterate, Deploy — together"; **Impact** — employees "actually make a difference and be part of bettering our products"; and work/life balance ("we work hard but also play hard, striving to provide a healthy work/life balance").

Size / stage / funding: Public company (NASDAQ: FOXA/FOX), profitable, ~10,000+ employees. Not funding-dependent — the most stable employer in this batch.

Locations / HQ: HQ New York City (1211 Avenue of the Americas); major sites Los Angeles, Tempe AZ; **remote roles posted in Canada (CAN – ON – Remote)**.

Remote policy: Mixed. Most technology roles are tied to a US office (LA, Tempe, NYC) with multi-location listings; a subset are posted as fully remote. The tech site mentions team experiences "in person or virtually" but publishes no formal remote policy.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes, partially — verified from their own job board.** Two live postings carry the location "CAN – ON – Remote" (Staff Data Engineer R50033264, Senior Software Engineer R50032287), so FOX does employ Canada-based remote engineers. Caveat: the eligible-location string names **Ontario** specifically, so a British Columbia hire is not established and would need confirming; and the remaining platform/backend roles in this batch are Los Angeles / Tempe / New York and are not remote-Canada. Hours would run Eastern or Pacific rather than Mountain.

Engineering & tech: Publicly listed stack — Clients: iOS/Swift, Android/Kotlin, tvOS, Android TV, Roku, Fire TV, React, Vue.js, TypeScript, Node.js. Backend: **Go**, AWS Lambda, Elasticsearch, Redis, Kinesis, MySQL, MongoDB, PHP, C#/.NET Core. Video: AWS Elemental, Fastly, Akamai, HLS, MPEG-DASH, 4K/HDR. Infrastructure: Docker, AWS, GCP, Terraform, Kubernetes (linkerd). Distinct teams exist for media platform, identity & SaaS platform, media DevOps, and a "Business and Data Platform" team (BI/analytics on Tableau + Redshift/Snowflake/Databricks).

Notable / other: FOX publishes salary ranges on US postings under state/local pay-disclosure law, with an annual discretionary bonus on senior/principal roles, 401(k), medical/dental/vision. Some roles are union-represented (benefits per the applicable collective bargaining agreement). Note a data-quality wrinkle: the "Staff Data Engineer" (CAN-ON-Remote) JD is actually written as a **Tableau BI developer** role ("looking for a data-driven BI Engineer… Senior BI Developer: 4+ years… preferably using Tableau"), not a data-infrastructure role — the title oversells the content.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Media Platform Engineer (Tempe/LA); Senior & Principal Identity & SaaS Platform Engineer (LA/NYC); Senior Developer, Back-End (LA); Media DevOps Engineer (LA); Staff Data Engineer (CAN-ON-Remote); Senior Software Engineer (CAN-ON-Remote).
