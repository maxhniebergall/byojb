# Clario — research
Provider/key: workday:clarioclinical/clarioclinical_careers | company_type: product

**Registry naming note:** the registry lists this company as "Clarioclinical" — that is the Workday tenant slug, not the trading name. The company is **Clario** (formerly ERT / Bioclinica, merged 2021). Correct entity; the display name in the registry should be fixed to "Clario". Not to be confused with **Clarios** (automotive batteries) or **Clario Tech** (consumer security software, clario.co) — both are different companies that surface on the same search terms.

What they do: Clario supplies digital endpoint data solutions for clinical trials — it captures, integrates and analyses evidence from devices, sites and patients (eCOA, cardiac safety, medical imaging, respiratory, precision motion/wearables) so pharma, biotech and medical-device sponsors can generate regulator-grade trial endpoints. It serves 500+ life-science organisations including the top 20 biopharmaceutical companies and the leading CROs, across North America, the UK, Europe and Asia-Pacific. Roughly 30 years of trial-data history sits behind the platform.

How they describe themselves: Careers messaging centres on the value **"People First Always"** — "Everyone working at Clario is passionate about being here because they know how much it matters to patients," and the company frames success as its people and customers working together collaboratively, with employees choosing Clario "not only as a job, but as a place to work to improve the lives of others." Career framing is "everyone at Clario shapes their own career," with stated investment in people and tooling. The tone across the JDs is corporate-clinical and mission-by-patient-outcome rather than startup hype; no "rocketship" or "hustle" language appears, though the Angular JD does say "fast-paced Agile environment."

Size / stage / funding: ~4,000 employees. **As of 2026 a wholly-owned subsidiary of Thermo Fisher Scientific**, which completed its acquisition of Clario Holdings for **$8.875 billion in cash** (announced 2025, closed 2026), plus $125M payable January 2027 and up to $400M in earn-outs tied to 2026–2027 performance. Previously private-equity/sponsor-owned and long rumoured for IPO. This makes it about as far from runway-dependent as a company gets — its parent is a ~$40B-revenue S&P 100 life-sciences instrument and services group.

Locations / HQ: HQ in Philadelphia, PA. Global footprint with sites and remote staff across North America (including Canada and Costa Rica), the UK, Germany (Estenfeld), Hungary, and India (Bangalore); Seattle office also listed.

Remote policy: Substantially remote-friendly and geographically distributed. A large share of the live engineering postings are explicitly location-typed as fully remote — "Canada Remote", "United Kingdom Remote", "Hungary Remote", "Costa Rica Remote" — rather than hybrid office roles. Employee reports describe flexible scheduling with remote accommodation. No published company-wide RTO mandate found; note that Thermo Fisher, the new parent, is generally more office-oriented, so post-integration policy is a live unknown.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** Two live engineering postings carry the location "Canada Remote" as the ATS location value — *Software Engineer - Python* (R17731, a GenAI/agentic-AI backend role despite the title) and *Senior Software Engineer (Angular/TypeScript)* (R17859). These are Canada-domiciled remote requisitions, not US roles open to Canadians. Specific province eligibility (BC) is not stated. Mountain Time works against a Philadelphia-anchored org (2 hours behind) but the GenAI team's other postings are UK/Hungary, so some European-overlap expectation is plausible for that team specifically.

Engineering & tech: Two distinct engineering worlds visible in the postings.
- **GenAI department** (the interesting one): Python-first, "agentic and multi-modal AI systems," end-to-end ownership from prototyping to production monitoring, AWS preferred, Kubernetes-based backend services, model serving in cloud environments, data pipelines for training/evaluating/serving models, GitLab CI/CD. Explicitly senior-scoped with mentoring and technical-roadmap responsibility (the "Software Engineer - Python" requisition opens "Join Clario… as a Senior Software Engineer in our GenAI department").
- **Platform / product engineering**: Angular + TypeScript front ends over C#/.NET microservices, REST APIs, automated testing and CI/CD, Agile/**SAFe** process. Legacy .NET/C#/ASP.NET MVC work also exists (Costa Rica). Company-wide push to adopt AI-assisted coding tools (GitHub Copilot, Tabnine named).
Bar is moderate: 4+ years for the GenAI senior role, 3+ years for the Angular senior role — a notably low senior threshold.

Notable / other: Regulated domain (GxP, FDA/EMA submissions) — mission-critical, "boring" in the good sense, with change control and validation overhead that slows deployment cadence. R&D Software Engineering roles sit in Germany and India. SAFe adoption signals large-enterprise process weight. Integration into Thermo Fisher over 2026–27, with earn-outs tied to performance, is the main organisational variable.

Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineer - Python (Canada Remote, GenAI); Senior Software Engineer (Angular/TypeScript) (Canada Remote); AI Engineering Team Lead (UK Remote / Hungary Remote); Backend Software Engineer (GenAI); Sr Engineer, R&D Software Engineering (Germany/India); Lead Data Engineer (SQL & Python); Sr Software Engineer (.NET/C#/ASP.NET MVC) (Costa Rica Remote).
