# AVEVA — research
Provider/key: workday:aveva/aveva_careers | company_type: product
What they do: AVEVA is a global industrial-software vendor. Its products span the full industrial lifecycle — engineering design and capital projects, then operations, monitoring and optimization — for energy, chemicals, minerals, manufacturing, marine and infrastructure customers. It describes its solutions as "used by thousands of enterprises to deliver the essentials of life – such as energy, infrastructure, chemicals and minerals – safely, efficiently and more sustainably."
How they describe themselves: Four stated corporate values: **Aspiration, Impact, Curiosity, Trust**. The mission framing on their own site is "Technology with humanity at its core" and "powering radical collaboration", helping industries "design, build, operate, and optimize" by "harnessing industrial intelligence" for reliability, safety and profitability. Their JD boilerplate adds: "We're the first software business in the world to have our sustainability targets validated by the SBTi, and we've been recognized for the transparency and ambition of our commitment to diversity, equity, and inclusion. We've also recently been named as one of the world's most innovative companies." and asks for someone "curious and collaborative … who wants to make a big impact through technology."
Size / stage / funding: Established vendor with 50+ years in industrial software (their own claim). Exact current headcount and ownership structure were not confirmed from a first-party page this session — the About page fetched did not state them, and the search budget was exhausted, so ownership is recorded here as UNVERIFIED rather than asserted.
Locations / HQ: HQ not confirmed from the pages fetched. Live engineering postings in the registry are located in Hyderabad and Bangalore (India), Madrid (Spain), London and Cambridge (UK), Derry (Northern Ireland), Shanghai/Beijing (China), and Lake Forest CA / Houston TX / Philadelphia PA (US).
Remote policy: Hybrid, stated explicitly and per-role in their own JDs — "Employment Type: Full Time (Hybrid role)" on the Cloud Engineer, Data Platforms posting, and "Cambridge, United Kingdom or Derry, Northern Ireland – Hybrid (3 days at the office)" on the Cloud Software Developer in Test posting. Roles are tied to a named office city, not to a country.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — and this is verifiable from the registry rather than inferred. Across all 14 live AVEVA postings captured there is not one Canadian location, and the postings that state a work model state hybrid with a specific office (up to 3 days in office). Nothing on the pages fetched suggests Canada-based remote employment is offered.
Engineering & tech: Cloud and data-platform engineering on Azure/AWS, AI platform and cloud services, C++ and C# product development, MES (manufacturing execution systems), cloud operations and infrastructure, and test engineering. Internal engineering ladder uses "Member of Technical Staff" nomenclature — "R&D Member of Technical Staff II" (mid-senior), "R&D Senior Member of Technical Staff", and separate "Principal" and "Distinguished" AI Engineer titles. The bulk of R&D headcount in these postings sits in Hyderabad and Bangalore.
Notable / other: Partners with Microsoft, AWS and NVIDIA and works with 6,000+ system integrators and OEM partners. Their US postings comply with pay transparency: the Lake Forest CA Senior Software Engineer posting states a salary range of $124,200 – $207,000 (USD).
Open relevant roles (sample): Principal AI Engineer; Distinguished AI Engineer; Senior Software Engineer - AI Platform & Cloud Services (Bangalore); Cloud Engineer, Data Platforms (Madrid/London, hybrid); R&D Senior Member of Technical Staff, Cloud Operations & Infrastructure (Hyderabad); Senior Software Engineer (C++ and C#); Senior MES Software Engineer; Cloud Software Developer in Test (Cambridge/Derry, hybrid).
