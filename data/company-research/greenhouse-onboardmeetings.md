# OnBoard — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:onboardmeetings | company_type: product
What they do: OnBoard sells board-management software — the "board intelligence platform" used to run board and committee meetings, agendas, minutes, and secure document distribution. In their words: "Boards set the standard for what organizations can achieve. At OnBoard, our board management software helps boards function at a higher level so every organization can make a bigger difference in the world." They claim "more than 5,000 organizations and their 12,000 boards and committees in 60 countries", across higher education, nonprofit, healthcare systems, government and enterprise. The eScribe product line specifically serves public-sector/municipal meeting management.
How they describe themselves: Company history is a point of pride — "grown from a class project at Purdue University in West Lafayette, Indiana in 2003 into the world's leading board management software platform today". Culture language centres on a "culture of togetherness": "we acknowledge that uniqueness is powerful, and we welcome, foster, and appreciate all. Diversity, Equity, and Inclusiveness fuel the Pathfinder atmosphere and all our efforts. Our power is in our people and we Pledge 1% to give back to our communities and across the globe." Employees are called "Pathfinders"; the employer pitch is "a growing, casual, fun, philanthropic minded company". Their listed role competencies read as a formal corporate framework: Accountability, Adaptability, Applied Learning, Business Acumen, Dealing w/Ambiguity, Collaboration, Customer Focus, Decision Making, Driving for Results, Initiating Action, Creating an Inclusive Environment, Technical/Professional Skills, and "AI Adoption". The legal entity behind it is still Passageways (accommodation requests go to peopleops@passageways.com).
Size / stage / funding: Private equity backed — "Backed by JMI Equity and the acquisitions of eScribe and Govenda, OnBoard is positioned to become the industry leader in Board Management and Meeting Solutions for private and public sector entities." A PE-owned roll-up rather than a venture-runway startup. Headcount not disclosed; mid-size is implied by the multi-country benefits structure. Founded 2003, product launched 2011.
Locations / HQ: Indiana, USA (West Lafayette/Indianapolis roots, Passageways entity). Distributed employee base with formal benefit programs for US, Canada, Australia and UK employees.
Remote policy: Remote-first in practice. The benefits list leads with "Fully remote work with company provided equipment (laptop, software, etc.)". They also disclose AI use in hiring: "We use video/audio recordings and artificial intelligence (AI) tools during our interview process to transcribe responses, evaluate skills, and streamline evaluations."
Remote-Canada eligibility: YES — verified from their own JD. The role is titled "Senior Software Engineer, eScribe - Remote" and posted with location "Canada", and the benefits section carries a dedicated "CAN Based Employees" block (employer-paid life and AD&D, health-care spending account, dependent and optional life, LTD, drug and paramedical, dental, vision, EAP). That is a real Canadian payroll entity, not an EOR afterthought. The role is fully remote with no province named, so BC/Mountain Time should be workable, though hours expectations are unstated and the company is Eastern/Central-anchored.
Engineering & tech: Microsoft-centric. C# and .NET Core web applications (3-5 years required), Vue.js front end (or React/Angular 2+), RESTful design and microservices, "high-scale cloud mechanisms like event hubs, messaging systems, and data lakes", CI/CD "ideally in Azure DevOps", strong CSS/JS. They operate under SOC 2 and ISO 27001 in "a high transaction, high security environment". Agile with sprint planning, standups and retrospectives; the senior role "reports to Director, Engineering" and includes mentoring junior engineers, code review, coding standards, architecture influence and technical documentation. Heavy stated emphasis on AI-assisted development — they name Claude Code and PlayerZero as tools engineers are expected to adopt, and want contribution to "AI-powered product features", with familiarity with the Anthropic Claude API or OpenAI listed as a plus.
Notable / other: The domain (board governance, municipal meeting records) is genuinely boring-and-necessary, with sticky institutional customers and compliance requirements — a stability positive. The PE roll-up structure cuts both ways: capital and consolidation, but also cost discipline and integration work. Seniority bar is high (7-10 years).
