# Broadridge Financial Solutions — research
Provider/key: workday:broadridge/careers | company_type: product

What they do: Broadridge is a large financial-technology and investor-communications provider. In their
own words: "We power investing, corporate governance, and communications to help improve business
performance and transform investor experience." It runs proxy voting and shareholder communications for
much of the North American market, plus post-trade processing, clearing and settlement, wealth-management
platforms and data conversion services for banks, brokers and wealth firms. Classic
mission-critical financial plumbing.

How they describe themselves: "an S&P 500 Index company operating in over 100 countries and with
$6 billion in revenue", with "a global culture of innovation", "pushing the boundaries of technology to
solve the industry's mission-critical challenges." Their own JD adds the internal culture line: "At
Broadridge, we've built a culture where the highest goal is to empower others to accomplish more. If
you're passionate about developing your career, while helping others along the way, come join the
Broadridge team."

Size / stage / funding: Public (NYSE: BR), S&P 500 constituent, "over 15,000 associates supports clients
in over 100 countries", ~$6B revenue. Extremely stable, recurring-revenue business.

Locations / HQ: Lake Success / Long Island, New York (HQ not stated on the fetched page). Registry
postings are at Edgewood NY, Newark NJ, New York NY, Toronto ON, Vancouver (555 Burrard Street), and
Bangalore, India.

Remote policy: Predominantly hybrid and office-anchored. Job titles themselves carry the policy:
"Senior Software Engineer (Hybrid - Edgewood, NY)", "Professional Software Engineer - AI Driven
Engineering (Hybrid - Newark, NJ, NYC or Edgewood, NY)", "Senior Cloud Engineer (Hybrid)", "Technical
Lead - Cloud Engineer (Hybrid)". Two VP-level roles are titled "(Remote)". A JD notes the interview
process itself is hybrid: "Throughout your application process, you may be asked to contact us virtually
or in-person." No company-wide remote policy was found on the about page.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO for the engineering roles. Both Canadian engineering postings are
office-anchored: "DevOps Engineer" and "Senior Software Developer" are both at "Vancouver - 555 Burrard
Street" and extracted as hybrid, and "Associate Software Engineer" is at "Toronto, ON". Vancouver hybrid
is not workable from Kimberley. The only postings titled "(Remote)" are US VP-level engineering-management
roles ("VP, AI Quality Software Engineering (Remote)", "VP, Quality Software Engineering (Remote)"),
which are neither IC roles nor established as Canada-eligible.

Engineering & tech: Enterprise financial systems. The Toronto Associate role is representative of the
Canadian work: "support our data conversion projects, supporting the onboarding of new clients in the
Canadian financial and wealth management industry... Implement and optimize complex data conversion
logic, primarily using Oracle database and advanced PL/SQL techniques... Identify opportunities for
performance tuning and optimization." Elsewhere the registry titles cover Cloud/DevOps/SRE, AWS cloud
engineering, AI/ML engineering and "AI Driven Engineering", plus a Bangalore DevOps/SRE tech lead. The
work is client-onboarding and platform operations for regulated financial infrastructure — highly
predictable, compliance-bound, and change-controlled.

Notable / other: A meaningful share of the engineering openings are in Bangalore, and the US roles skew
toward the New York/New Jersey corridor with named-office hybrid requirements. Several senior titles are
"VP, ..." which at Broadridge denotes a management band rather than an IC role.

Pay (first-party, per existing registry rows sourced from their own JDs): Senior Software Engineer
(Hybrid - Edgewood, NY) USD $120,000-$130,000 base; Associate Software Engineer (Toronto) CAD
$60,000-$80,000 base.

Open relevant roles (sample): DevOps Engineer (Vancouver - 555 Burrard Street, hybrid); Senior Software
Developer (Vancouver, hybrid); Associate Software Engineer (Toronto, ON); Senior Software Engineer
(Hybrid - Edgewood, NY); Technology Lead - DevOps/SRE (Bangalore).
