# Marqeta — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:marqeta | company_type: product

What they do: Marqeta is a modern card-issuing and payments platform. It provides the APIs and infrastructure that let other companies issue physical and virtual payment cards and control authorization in real time (just-in-time funding, spend controls, tokenization), plus banking and money-movement services. Customers are other businesses — fintechs, on-demand platforms, banks and large enterprises — so the product is itself payments infrastructure sold to builders. Public company on NASDAQ (MQ).

How they describe themselves: Values stated in their own words on their JDs — **"Do What's Right"** ("Knowing businesses and livelihoods depend on us, we pursue solutions that disrupt responsibly and deliver high-quality results that our customers count on. We own our work from start to finish"), **"Simplify and Innovate"** ("We approach challenges with curiosity and take smart risks. Innovation comes from finding better, simpler ways to achieve extraordinary outcomes"), **"Win as a Team"** ("We succeed together by embracing diverse perspectives and pushing each other to raise the bar. We lead with humility and set aside hierarchy"), **"Make it Count"** ("We drive forward with focus and agility. With a sense of urgency and purpose, we get the job done, and done right"), plus a customer-empathy value ("Earning and keeping their trust guides everything we do"). On work location: "Marqeta is a Flex First company which allows you to choose your best working environment, whether that be from home or at a company office."

Size / stage / funding: Public (NASDAQ: MQ), ~848 employees as of December 2025 — a mid-size public fintech, materially smaller than at IPO. It went through a 15% workforce reduction (~150 people) announced in May 2023 as part of a cost-cutting plan targeting profitability, saving $40–45M in annual operating expense; the company has been on a path to profitability since.

Locations / HQ: Global HQ in **Oakland, California**; international offices in **London** and **Manchester** (UK); Canadian hiring in **Toronto** and **Vancouver**.

Remote policy: **Flex First** — remote work is supported at all levels, with leaders explicitly modelling it by working remotely themselves. Offices exist for those who prefer them (Oakland, London, Manchester), but attendance is a choice, not an expectation. Pay is location-calibrated: "To support Flex First, we calibrate pay to a competitive value according to working location."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes, with one caveat to confirm.** The Staff Software Engineer – Banking and Money Movement requisition is posted for "Toronto, Canada; Vancouver, Canada", is remote-marked, and quotes its base range in **CAD** — so a Canadian employment relationship clearly exists. Under Flex First the office is optional, so working from elsewhere in Canada should be acceptable, but the posting names two metro areas rather than "Canada" generally, so residency outside those metros is worth confirming explicitly. Vancouver anchoring means Pacific-time team norms; Mountain Time is one hour ahead and unproblematic.

Engineering & tech: Payments infrastructure at scale — card issuing, authorization, banking and money movement, plus a data platform. Named in the JDs: Java and other modern languages, **AWS**, **Terraform**, and Kubernetes operations (they have recently run a "Senior Software Engineer - Kubernetes Operations" requisition). Interview process is published on the JD: application → recruiter video call → coding interview and hiring-manager interview → virtual "onsite" of four 45–60 minute rounds → offer.

Notable / other: Compensation transparency is good — ranges are posted in the local currency for both US and Canadian requisitions. Beyond base they list annual bonuses for eligible employees, multiple health insurance options, flexible vacation, a retirement savings program with company contribution, equity in a publicly traded company, and a monthly stipend. Publicly traded equity is liquid, which is a meaningful difference from private-company options.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer - Banking and Money Movement (Toronto/Vancouver, CAD band); Principal Software Engineer (Remote, USA); Senior Staff Software Engineer - Data Platform; Senior Software Engineer - Kubernetes Operations (recently closed).
