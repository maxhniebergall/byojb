# Eleven — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:eleven | company_type: product
What they do: Eleven operates a captive-portal WiFi and central authentication platform for hospitality and multi-dwelling properties. In their words the platform is "used around the world to effortlessly and securely connect guests and residents (and all their devices) to the internet with an at-home Wi-Fi experience", serving "hotels and multi-family residences to campgrounds and college campuses", and they state that "every day, tens of millions of hotel guests across major hospitality brands benefit from the services we provide." Product surface includes integrations with hotel Property Management Systems (room-number auth, posting charges to guest folios) and loyalty-program enrolment.
How they describe themselves: Their JD banner is "We Move Fast, Think on Our Feet, and Have Fun Doing It!" and continues: "At Eleven, we thrive on solving complex challenges and pushing boundaries. We work at a rapid pace, embracing change, and staying laser-focused on delivering exceptional value to our customers. We're looking for visionary problem-solvers who excel in dynamic environments, embrace AI tools in streamlining work processes, adapt quickly, and drive innovation." No separate published values list was found.
Size / stage / funding: Not stated in the available material — headcount, ownership and funding stage are unverified. The stated reach ("tens of millions" of guests, major hospitality brands) implies an established vendor rather than an early startup.
Locations / HQ: Not stated. Fully distributed; roles are US-based.
Remote policy: "Eleven is a remote work employer." Roles operate "remotely, in a home office environment, with occasional travel for team meetings or company offsites."
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — explicitly excluded.** Their own JD states: "Candidates for this role must be based in the United States and legally eligible to work for any US employer. Eleven is not able to sponsor candidates at this time." The posted salary range is stated to apply "in all geographic locations in the United States."
Engineering & tech: C#/.NET (".NET 8 or recent versions preferred") on AWS Serverless — Lambda, SQS-triggered message pipelines, DynamoDB, API Gateway, SSM Parameter Store, Step Functions, CloudWatch; CloudFormation/SAM IaC; REST and SOAP third-party integration with OAuth2/Cognito; SQL. A network-facing track covers MikroTik RouterOS scripting/APIs, RADIUS, 802.1X, EAP, certificate-based auth, and hardened containerized services for resource-constrained Linux devices. Agile/Scrum with two-week sprints, engineers report to Engineering Managers, security requirements (PII handling, obfuscation, credential storage) are part of the SDLC, and every engineering role "participates in on-call rotation". They explicitly expect daily use of AI coding assistants ("GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Claude").
Notable / other: Pay is posted on their JDs. Benefits: medical/dental/vision, 401(k), "attractive paid time off policy". Compensation is stated as geography-independent within the US.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior AI Engineer; Senior Software Engineer-Network Protocols & Devices; Software Engineer-Authentication; Senior Software Engineer -Reporting.
