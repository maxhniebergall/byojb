# WEX Inc — research
Provider/key: workday:wexinc/wexinc | company_type: product
What they do: WEX is a corporate payments and financial-technology company built around three business lines, in their own words: **fleet/mobility** — "Save and modernize with fuel cards, driver support, and fleet solutions" (they note "20M business vehicles that already fuel with WEX"); **employee benefits** — "Easily manage employee benefits administration, including HSAs, FSAs, and COBRA"; and **business payments** — "Boost your revenue, reduce risk, and go digital with your business payments". Customers span small businesses, large enterprises, trucking and logistics, financial institutions, health plans and the public sector. Their positioning line is "Complexity is the enemy of progress."
How they describe themselves: WEX publishes six named values — "Put ingenuity to work", "Stick to it", "Team up", "Act with integrity", "Be a positive force", and "Stay open". The careers pitch: "For passionate problem-solvers, WEX is the place that provides exciting opportunities to make a difference because of our familial culture, entrepreneurial spirit, and ambitious attitude." "International growth" is listed as an employee benefit.
Size / stage / funding: Public company (NYSE: WEX) with an investor-relations portal at ir.wexinc.com. Established and profitable rather than venture-stage; roughly 7,000 employees. Headcount is not disclosed on the careers page.
Locations / HQ: HQ **Portland, Maine**, USA. Global operations across North America, Europe, Asia Pacific and Latin America. From the live requisition set the two engineering geographies that actually appear are the **United States** (US - Remote, Portland ME, San Francisco CA) and **Brazil** (São Paulo and Brazil - Remote — a significant offshore engineering hub).
Remote policy: Remote-friendly. Their careers page offers a "Flexible work environment – in an office or remote", and the majority of live engineering requisitions are posted as "US - Remote" or "Brazil - Remote" rather than office-bound. However, the page "does not specify which countries WEX hires in".
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified NO — no Canadian requisitions exist.** Across 16 requisitions in the registry (8 live and relevant), every single one is scoped to either "US - Remote" / a named US office (Portland ME, San Francisco CA) or to Brazil ("Brazil - Remote", "Brazil Sao Paulo - Remote Office"). There is no Canadian entity in the posting data and no "remote - Canada" or "North America - Remote" scope anywhere. "US - Remote" means remote *within the United States* and requires US work authorization, which the candidate does not have. So despite being a genuinely remote-friendly employer, WEX is not reachable from British Columbia on the current live set.
Engineering & tech: Meaningful data-platform and AI-infrastructure investment for a payments company. Live requisitions include a **Data Platform** track (Sr Staff Software Engineer - Data Platform, Senior Software Engineer - Data Lake Engineering, Senior Software Engineer - Semantic Data Lake, Principal Software Engineer - Data), an **AI/agentic** track (Staff Backend Engineer (AI & Agentic Systems), Software Development Engineer 4 - AI & Agentic Systems, Sr Staff AI Engineer - Context Engineering, AI Principal Engineer), and an SRE track (SRE Architect, AI-Powered Reliability). Backend stacks named in requisitions include Java, Python and C#/.NET. The presence of "Context Engineering" and "Agentic Systems" as named team charters suggests real agent-platform work rather than a bolted-on chatbot.
Notable / other: Pay transparency is partial — requisitions in transparency jurisdictions post USD ranges (e.g. the San Francisco Principal Software Engineer - Data req posts $200,600–250,400 USD with equity; a Semantic Data Lake req posted $121,500–145,500 USD with equity), while most US - Remote reqs state no pay.
Open relevant roles (sample): Sr Staff Software Engineer - Data Platform (US - Remote); Senior Software Engineer - Data Lake Engineering (US - Remote); Principal Software Engineer - Data (San Francisco CA); AI Principal Engineer (US - Remote); SRE Architect, AI-Powered Reliability; Senior Backend Software Development Engineer (Java/Python) (Brazil São Paulo - Remote); Lead Software Engineer (Brazil - Remote).
