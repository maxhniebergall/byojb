# Metabase — research
Provider/key: lever:metabase | company_type: product

What they do: Metabase makes open-source business-intelligence and analytics software — in their words, "the easiest way for people to get insights from their data, from tiny startups who get up and running quickly to major corporations with tens of thousands of users." They describe the strategy as bringing "data tools with the elegance and simplicity of consumer products to the crufty world of enterprise business intelligence," providing "an opinionated open source starting point for how companies should measure, analyze and share their data." The self-hosted open-source product is used by tens of thousands of companies; they also run **Metabase Cloud**, a hosted/managed offering launched more recently, which is "new and quickly growing" and is what the SRE/DevOps hiring is aimed at.

How they describe themselves: The recurring self-description is "small, engineering-led," looking for "folks who care about building a great product and team." Their public positioning is strongly opinionated-open-source and anti-enterprise-bloat. Culturally the loudest and most consistent claim is **async-first**: engineering, product and ops all run async-first, and they say they explicitly hire for work that can be done asynchronously, "with plenty of uninterrupted time." Team members define their own work schedules and work from wherever they want.

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-funded, founded 2014. Roughly **140 employees**. About **$46M raised** in total — modest for the age and headcount, which is consistent with a capital-efficient open-source company rather than a blitzscaler. Profitability not publicly stated, but the long history, wide install base and low burn profile suggest a stable business rather than a runway-driven one.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco (a downtown SF office exists and is referenced, somewhat tongue-in-cheek, as "our beautiful downtown San Francisco office Zoom channel"). The company is **fully distributed and global** — around **50% of staff are located outside the United States**, "from California to Thailand."

Remote policy: Fully distributed, **async-first**, work-from-anywhere. Roles are posted as "Global Remote." Employees set their own schedules. They have a public track record of hiring 100%-work-from-anywhere positions across engineering, product, ops, support, writing, design and sales. This is one of the clearer genuine async-remote cultures in the batch — it is stated as an operating model, not a perk.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** Both live engineering reqs are located "Global Remote, remote", and Metabase's own Software Engineer (Backend) req posts its salary range in **CAD ($110,000–$210,000)** — a company posting a Canadian-dollar band on its own JD is direct evidence it employs Canada-based people. Combined with the stated 50%-outside-the-US workforce and work-from-anywhere policy, Canada eligibility is not in doubt. Mountain Time is workable given the global spread and self-defined schedules.

Engineering & tech: Metabase is a Clojure-and-React application — the open-source core is well known for its Clojure backend, a SQL query engine and query builder that compiles to many database dialects (Postgres, MySQL, BigQuery, Snowflake, Redshift, Mongo and dozens more), with a React/JS frontend. The current infrastructure hiring is for **Metabase Cloud**: building out and running the hosted multi-tenant product, which is classic platform/SRE work — provisioning, orchestration, scaling, upgrades across a large fleet of customer instances, and operational ownership. Engineering is described as engineering-led with plenty of uninterrupted maker time. The codebase being open source means the actual work and code-review culture are publicly inspectable.

Notable / other: Metabase has one of the strongest brand positions in open-source BI, competing with Looker, Superset, Redash and Preset. The "opinionated open source" stance and consumer-grade UX in an enterprise category is the durable differentiator. The company has been going since 2014 without a mega-round, which reads as deliberate capital efficiency. Note there is a well-known naming collision risk: "Metabase" the BI company is distinct from unrelated "Meta"/metaverse entities — the entity here is metabase.com, jobs at jobs.lever.co/metabase.

Comp: The Software Engineer (Backend) req posts **CAD $110,000–$210,000 base** on the company's own JD — an unusually wide band, but Canadian-dollar and first-party. The Senior SRE/DevOps req posts no range.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior SRE/DevOps Engineer (Global Remote); Software Engineer (Backend) (Global Remote).
