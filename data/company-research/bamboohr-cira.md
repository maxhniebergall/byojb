# CIRA (Canadian Internet Registration Authority) — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:cira | company_type: product

What they do: CIRA is the not-for-profit member-based organisation that operates Canada's `.ca` country-code top-level domain and a set of national internet-infrastructure and cybersecurity services. The products named in their own JD are CIRA Cybersecurity Awareness Training, CIRA Canadian Shield and DNS Firewall ("securing household and enterprise networks"), and Anycast DNS ("defending against DDoS attacks"). The relevant engineering group is the "Cyber-DNS team", described as "dedicated to protecting Canadians online by delivering cutting-edge solutions" and building "extended detection and response (XDR) and DNS-based cybersecurity products and backend services".

How they describe themselves: "At CIRA, we're driven by a passion to make a positive impact on Canada's digital future. We're not just asking, 'What more can we do?' — we're actively exploring new frontiers to enhance and secure the internet for all Canadians." On culture: "We believe in fostering an environment where collaboration and candour are second nature and where diverse perspectives are integral to our success, because we know that great ideas come from everywhere." They describe the target engineer as someone who "thrive[s] in a collaborative environment that values open discussion, shared problem solving and diverse perspectives" and who "actively share[s] your knowledge, mentor[s] those around you and champion[s] initiatives to improve our software quality, resiliency and security." Mission framing is public-interest rather than commercial: "making a meaningful difference in how Canadians and global citizens alike experience and trust the internet."

Size / stage / funding: Not-for-profit, member-funded through `.ca` domain registrations — no venture funding, no runway risk, no acquisition pressure. This is about as structurally stable as an employer gets. Headcount not stated on the postings (unverified); CIRA is a small-to-mid organisation by any measure.

Locations / HQ: Ottawa, Ontario. Both open engineering reqs list "Location: Ottawa, Ontario, Canada". They cite "Our recognition as one of the National Capital Region's Top Employers for ten years."

Remote policy: Hybrid, anchored on Ottawa. Their own words: "CIRA embraces a blend of remote and IRL in-office work to keep our team connected and engaged. Our Ottawa headquarters is a hub for regular events and social activities that bring our team together… No matter where you work from, you'll always feel part of our vibrant team and our shared mission." The closing phrase gestures at flexibility, but the policy as written is a remote/in-office blend built around physical presence at the Ottawa HQ.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Partial — Canadian employer, but Ottawa-anchored and Eastern Time.** Employment in Canada is obviously not an obstacle (Canadian non-profit, CAD salary posted). But both reqs name Ottawa as the location and the stated policy blends remote with in-office at the Ottawa HQ, so a fully-remote BC hire is not supported by the evidence — treat as **not verified as remote-Canada eligible**. Even if remote were negotiable, the role includes "a rotating 24/7 on-call schedule to support service uptime and incident response" run out of an Eastern Time organisation, three hours ahead of Mountain Time.

Engineering & tech: Java (Spring or Play Framework) or Python at advanced proficiency; Linux and Bash automation; secure application design, threat mitigation, authentication/authorization; CI/CD with GitLab CI, Jenkins or TeamCity; Docker containerisation; Terraform or Ansible infrastructure-as-code; "cloud and hybrid environments"; PostgreSQL, MySQL, DynamoDB and non-relational stores; OWASP secure-coding standards; security stack spanning EDR/XDR, vulnerability scanners, firewalls and MITRE ATT&CK. Responsibilities include architecture participation, code review and mentoring, and "Write and maintain clear technical documentation for systems, processes and deployments." Requires a bachelor's degree and 7+ years of professional software development.

Notable / other: Both open engineering roles are **fixed-term contracts**, not permanent — "Senior Software Developer (12 months)" and "DevOps Specialist (6 months)". Compensation is posted transparently: CAD $117,000–$130,000 for the senior role. CIRA also publishes an unusually candid note on AI in hiring: recruitment platforms "include artificial intelligence-enabled features… these features are intended to assist our recruitment activities and do not replace human judgment. All applicant screenings, interviews, evaluations and selection decisions are conducted by our staff. Artificial intelligence is not used to make autonomous or final hiring decisions." They confirm "This posting is for an existing vacancy."
