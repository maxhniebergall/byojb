# The Voleon Group — research
Provider/key: ashby:voleon | company_type: product (quantitative asset manager)
What they do: A quantitative investment firm that applies machine learning to markets. In their own words: "Voleon is a technology company that applies state-of-the-art AI and machine learning techniques to real-world problems in finance. For nearly two decades, we have led our industry and worked at the frontier of applying AI/ML to investment management. We have become a multibillion-dollar asset manager, and we have ambitious goals for the future."
How they describe themselves: Research-institution framing rather than startup framing. "Your colleagues will include internationally recognized experts in artificial intelligence and machine learning research as well as highly experienced finance and technology professionals. In addition to our enriching and collegial working environment, we offer highly competitive compensation and benefits packages, technology talks by our experts, a beautiful modern office, daily catered lunches, and more." The emphasis is on collegiality, expertise and technical depth; there is no "fast-paced", "scrappy" or "rocketship" language anywhere in their postings. On the SRE side they use operational-rigour language instead: "rigorous SLAs", "uptime (measured in multiple nines)", "engineering systemic improvements and architectural fixes to prevent recurring issues."
Size / stage / funding: Private, ~two decades old, self-described multibillion-dollar asset manager. Not venture-runway dependent — revenue comes from AUM. This is about as far from "early/unstable startup" as a private company gets.
Locations / HQ: Berkeley, CA (HQ, "a beautiful modern office") plus New York City. Remote-US is offered broadly, and at least two roles are open to Remote Canada.
Remote policy: Genuinely remote-friendly per posting. Most engineering roles are listed as "Remote, United States" often alongside NYC and Berkeley. Two FinOps Automation Engineering roles are listed as **"Remote, Canada"**. Async-ness is not stated; the Cluster SRE role is explicitly the opposite — "a product that operates 24/7", "Be a first responder in the event of cluster outages."
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes, verified — but only for specific roles.** Two live postings state their location as "Remote, Canada": Senior Software Engineer, FinOps Automation Engineering and Software Engineer, FinOps Automation Engineering. The senior one posts a base range of $175K-$220K (USD) with bonus. All other engineering roles are listed as "Remote, United States" (± Berkeley/NYC) and should be assumed US-only unless the company says otherwise. So Voleon demonstrably *can* employ a Canada-based remote worker; the question for any given role is whether that role is one of the Canada-open ones.
Engineering & tech: A real Software Platform organization with distinct teams — "the FinOps Automation Engineering team sits within Voleon's Software Platform organization alongside teams that own our data infrastructure, developer experience, and compute platform." Work includes HPC/batch compute ("Slurm, Kueue, AWS/GCP Batch"), ML training systems ("Kubeflow, MLflow, Horovod"), infrastructure-as-code and configuration management (Terraform), observability and custom telemetry, on-prem *and* cloud infrastructure, data pipelines, reconciliation frameworks and internal APIs. The research cluster is described as "a world-class HPC platform for researchers to focus on cutting-edge machine learning problems at scale."
Compensation (from their own live JDs, USD base + bonus): Staff SWE Batch/Realtime Streaming $315-405K; Senior ML Engineer $290-395K; Senior/Staff SWE Data Engineering & Data Infrastructure $225-310K; Senior SWE Platform Team / Post-Trade $225-255K; Senior Linux Infrastructure Engineer $215-250K; Senior SWE Strategy Research Analytics $200-255K; Senior SWE FinOps Automation (Remote Canada) $175-220K; SWE DevOps $165-195K. Voleon posts ranges on most roles, which is unusually transparent.
Notable / other: The FinOps Automation team's customers are internal — "our primary customers are our financial operations teams, who are responsible for trade accounting, cash reconciliation, regulatory reporting, and investor-facing analytics" — and its mission is "to eliminate the manual, error-prone parts of their workflows." That is internal-platform work with internal customers. The Cluster Operations team, by contrast, is explicitly frontline: "works on the frontline to triage and mitigate real-time operational issues ... solving day-to-day issues with high urgency."
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Cluster Site Reliability Engineer; Senior Linux Infrastructure Engineer; Senior Software Engineer, Platform Team; Senior/Staff Software Engineer, Data Engineering Team; Staff Software Engineer, Batch and Realtime Streaming; Senior Software Engineer, FinOps Automation Engineering (Remote, Canada); Software Engineer, FinOps Automation Engineering (Remote, Canada); Site Reliability Engineer.
