# Appfire — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:appfire | company_type: product

What they do: Appfire builds and acquires software apps for the Atlassian ecosystem (Jira, Confluence) and adjacent collaboration platforms — one of the largest vendors in the Atlassian Marketplace, grown substantially by acquisition. Named flagship products: Appfire Flow, JXL, Comala Document Management, 7Pace Time Tracker, Jira Misc Workflow Extensions, and BigPicture. They report serving over 20,000 customers, including 55% of the Fortune 500.

How they describe themselves: "At Appfire, we believe that great work happens when people get to choose how they work. After 20 years of creating software that empowers teams to break silos and collaborate seamlessly, we've learned that one size does not fit all." Employees are called "fireflies". Their data-team JD language is direct about engineering standards: "ruthlessly eliminate technical debt, resolve edge cases, and introduce modern, AI-driven development practices… architecting a highly secure, resilient, and scalable foundation that empowers company-wide decision-making", and asks for "pragmatic, cost-effective architectures from scratch that prioritize security, scalability, and high performance without over-engineering".

Size / stage / funding: **850+ employees across 28 countries.** Private, private-equity backed — TA Associates invested USD $100M in March 2021, which funded an ongoing acquisition strategy. Mature (20 years of operating history) rather than venture-stage; not runway-dependent, but PE-owned with a roll-up growth model.

Locations / HQ: Boston, Massachusetts HQ on paper, but the company is distributed by design across 28 countries with no office requirement.

Remote policy: **Remote-first since inception** — "a team of 800+ employees, working remotely across 28 countries", and the posting is tagged `#LI-Remote`. Choice of working arrangement is presented as a founding principle rather than a concession.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** The Senior Data Engineer – Data Insights requisition is posted with location "Canada", is remote-tagged, and its benefits section is Canada-specific: Empire Health medical/dental/vision coverage and a "yearly sport allowance of CAD 600". That is a Canadian employment relationship, not a US role open to Canadians. No city is named, so any Canadian province including BC should be workable; Mountain Time within a 28-country distributed company is unremarkable.

Engineering & tech: The role sits on a central data-insights team owning a custom data lake called **"firelake"**, built on **AWS and Snowflake**. Stack named explicitly: advanced **Python** and **SQL**, **Airflow** for orchestration, **dbt** for transformation, **Fivetran** plus custom pipelines for ingestion (including reverse ETL and "atypical APIs"), **Terraform** and **Docker** for infrastructure. Governance is a first-class concern: automated data classification, PII masking, row-level and column-level security, monitoring. They are pushing AI into both the engineering workflow (automated PRs, CI/CD, test coverage, release management) and the data products themselves (LLM interfaces, "intelligent assistants and agents"). MLOps is listed as a bonus/expansion area they want guidance on.

Notable / other: Benefits are unusually concrete and generous: company equity for every team member, 25 days PTO rising to 30 after five years, comprehensive health coverage, life/critical-illness/AD&D/long-term-disability insurance, CAD 600 sport allowance, and 3 fully paid volunteering days a year under an "Appfire Town" CSR program. Recognition cited by the company: Deloitte Technology Fast 500, Inc. Best Workplaces, BuiltIn Best Places to Work, Inc. 5000. The role explicitly requires leading "3-5 concurrent cross-departmental data projects" autonomously.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Data Engineer - Data Insights (Canada, remote); Software Engineer.

Pay: No figure disclosed on the Canada posting and none found first-party; equity is offered to all employees. No band recorded.
