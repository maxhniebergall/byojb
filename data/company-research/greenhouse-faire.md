# Faire — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:faire | company_type: product

What they do: Faire operates a B2B wholesale marketplace connecting independent brands (many of them small or handmade lines) with independent retailers and boutiques. It replaces the trade-show and sales-rep model with a data-driven online marketplace, adding net-60 payment terms, free returns on first orders, discovery/search, and sales, marketing and analytics tooling for both sides. Revenue is take-rate on wholesale orders.

How they describe themselves: The founding vision is stated as building "a wholesale marketplace that would help small brands and retailers… compete on a more level playing field with Walmart and Amazon." Marketing language centres on "powering the shop local movement" and community — its JDs close with "We're looking for smart, resourceful and passionate people to join us as we power the shop local movement. If you believe in community, come join ours." The stated approach is data-driven: eliminating unnecessary obstacles for retailers, helping businesses find the right products, and providing sales, marketing and analytics tools that simplify wholesale. No enumerated public values list was located.

Size / stage / funding: Large late-stage private company; widely reported at a ~$13B valuation. Not an early or unstable startup, but venture-backed rather than a disclosed-profitable business. Nearly 250 engineers are based in Canada.

Locations / HQ: US HQ in San Francisco. Canadian engineering is substantial and split across two offices: Kitchener-Waterloo (the original and larger Canadian site) and a downtown Toronto office in the Fashion District opened in 2022 with room for at least 150 staff, housing marketing, design, data and engineering teams.

Remote policy: **Predominantly hybrid, stated explicitly and identically at the foot of every JD:** "Hybrid Faire employees currently go into the office 3 days per week on Tuesdays, Thursdays, and a third flex day of their choosing (Monday, Wednesday, or Friday). Additionally, hybrid in-office roles will have the flexibility to work remotely up to 4 weeks per year. Specific Workplace and Information Technology positions may require onsite attendance 5 days per week as will be indicated in the job posting." A minority of reqs are posted as genuinely remote, but each carries a hard geographic allow-list.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — not for a British Columbia resident.** Of the 10 live relevant reqs, 8 are hybrid roles tagged "Kitchener-Waterloo, ON; Toronto, ON" or "Toronto, ON," which means 3 days a week in an Ontario office. The two remote reqs are both the Senior Staff Data Engineer role, and each names its jurisdiction explicitly: one is "a remote role open to candidates located anywhere in **Ontario, Canada**," the other is "a remote role open to candidates located in Arizona, California, Colorado, … Wisconsin" (a 33-US-state list). There is no remote-BC, remote-Alberta or Canada-wide option anywhere in the current req set. Faire employs remote Canadians, but only in Ontario.

Engineering & tech: Marketplace engineering at scale with a well-developed platform and ML surface. Distinct teams visible in the req set: Platform Data and Analytics (data engineering), Machine Learning Platform, Applied AI/ML Science (ads bidding, fulfillment, listing quality, search, Compass, retailer growth), Search FX / Discovery Experience, Brand Platform, Growth Platform, Ads Formats, and Product Security. The Search FX role asks for "knowledge of search/query processing, including traditional, semantic, and hybrid frameworks (e.g., Elasticsearch, Lucene)" — a direct overlap with Elasticsearch experience. Titles run to Staff and Senior Staff, indicating a real IC ladder. No public engineering blog located.
