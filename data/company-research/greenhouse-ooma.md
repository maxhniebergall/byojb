# Ooma — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:ooma | company_type: product

What they do: Ooma is a cloud communications provider. In their own words: "we empower people to connect in smarter ways… through our cloud-based platform to bring people together at work and at home." Product lines: Ooma Office (small-business VoIP/UCaaS with mobile management), Ooma Enterprise (customized unified-communications solutions), residential VoIP phone service (they describe it as "the #1 rated VoIP phone service available"), and a smart-home security product. Pricing positioning is explicitly value-led: "all our products and services are priced competitively, because we believe advanced technology should be accessible to all."

How they describe themselves: The self-description is short and product/customer-framed rather than values-listy — smarter connection, accessibility of advanced technology, peace of mind for consumers, small-business owners "staying connected with their customers… from anywhere." Notably free of hustle/rocketship language. The JD adds an interesting operational note: Ooma discloses that it uses AI tooling in hiring (resume review, scheduling, interview note-taking/transcription), states all employment decisions are made by human reviewers, and commits to notifying candidates and obtaining consent before recording interviews — a candidate-transparency signal.

Size / stage / funding: Publicly traded (Ooma, Inc.). Exact headcount and financials not verified in this session — no company page was fetched (only the ATS board is in the registry, and ATS boards are not fetchable). Treat "public company, established, not runway-dependent" as the confident part and specific numbers as unverified.

Locations / HQ: HQ not verified from a first-party page this session. The infrastructure role is anchored to on-premises datacenter operations, so they run their own physical infrastructure in addition to cloud.

Remote policy: The relevant posting is listed "Remote, US". No broader company-wide remote policy statement was verified.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — verified negative for the live role. The posting's location is "Remote, US" and its pay-transparency block states "The base salary range for candidates within the United States". This is a US-remote seat, not Canada-eligible.

Engineering & tech: Infrastructure Services team owns availability/scalability of infrastructure behind all production services. Stack and scope from the Principal JD: on-prem network engineering (switching, routing, firewalls, load balancing; BGP, OSPF, TCP/IP, Juniper Junos, Arista EOS), Linux systems administration and performance tuning, virtualization and server hardware, Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Ansible), scripting (Python/Bash), Kubernetes and Docker "strongly preferred", hybrid/cloud (AWS/GCP/Azure) "valuable", and monitoring/observability/capacity planning. They are also explicitly investing in AI-assisted operational tooling: "Evaluate, design, and implement AI-assisted tooling to improve reliability, performance, and operational efficiency."

Notable / other: The Principal role is described as a senior technical leader and escalation point spanning both network and systems engineering, expecting 12+ years of experience — a deep-specialist IC ladder seat rather than a manager track. Compensation is disclosed on the JD (see data/company-comp).

Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Infrastructure Engineer.
