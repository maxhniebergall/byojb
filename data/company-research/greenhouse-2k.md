What they do: 2K is a major video-game publisher (a Take-Two Interactive label). The postings in this registry all belong to **2K Sports Lab**: "Founded in 2023, 2K Sports Lab is the newest stand alone studio at 2K looking to push game development boundaries to bring 2K's next groundbreaking sports franchise to life." The work is AAA sports-game development — gameplay systems, animation, rendering, online services, engine/systems programming.
How they describe themselves: "We're building a world-class team at our new Vancouver, BC location where our goal is to deliver an exceptional experience through bold ideas, technical excellence, and creative collaboration." They ask for "passionate and driven professionals", people "interested in tackling unique challenges" who can "level up our team", and — repeatedly — "A deep love for video games (not just ours!) and a passion for sports culture."
Size / stage / funding: 2K is part of Take-Two Interactive (public, NASDAQ: TTWO), so the parent is established; the Vancouver studio itself is a 2023 greenfield build-out. Note the games industry's ongoing layoff cycle as a stability caveat (not verified against 2K specifically this session).

Remote policy: Not stated in the JDs. A physical studio build-out in Vancouver with heavy animator/designer/production collaboration ("work closely with Animators, Design and Production", "content creation tools and animation review") strongly implies onsite/hybrid studio work.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, and unlikely.** All postings are located at the Burnaby/Vancouver studio; no remote language appears in any JD. AAA game development at a new studio is characteristically onsite. Treat as onsite-Vancouver unless the studio states otherwise.
