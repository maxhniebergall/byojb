# Albert Invent Corp — research
Provider/key: rippling:albert-invent-corp | company_type: product

What they do: Albert Invent builds a software platform for R&D chemistry and materials science. In their own words, "Albert's mission is to digitalize the world of chemistry. Using data and machine learning, Albert enables R&D organizations to dramatically accelerate the invention of new materials." The platform helps scientists "build structured data foundations, digitize formulation and testing workflows, and apply AI to innovate faster, smarter, and at scale." Customers are described as "researchers at the world's largest chemical companies."

How they describe themselves: "At Albert Invent, we value curiosity, rigor, and openness. We're writing the playbook for AI-augmented science together." They frame the platform work as building "the lab of the future" and note "our platform is robust but still in its early days — you'll have the opportunity to shape architecture decisions, introduce new technologies." Also a rigor value stated plainly: "AI makes mistakes confidently, and our customers won't accept hand-waving — neither should we." Tone is earnest/scientific rather than hype; there is no "fast-paced/hustle" language in the JD.

Engineering & tech: Python backend (async, performance optimization), FastAPI-style REST APIs, workflow orchestration with **Ray** and **Temporal**, AWS, Docker/Kubernetes. Data stores: AWS **Neptune** (graph), Redis, DynamoDB. Work includes real-time model inference serving, batch inference, long-running numerical-optimization ("inverse design") pipelines, orchestrated multi-step agent workflows, and observability (logging, metrics, tracing, alerting) plus CI/CD. Eng is organized with an AI/ML team containing an "Active Learning" team lead and an "LLM/Agents" team lead; the platform role sits between them and product engineering. Bonus signals sought: GPU workloads and scheduling, Ray/Dask/Spark, chemistry/scientific-computing background.

Notable / other: The role is explicitly framed as infrastructure rather than modelling — "you will build the foundational systems that enable AI at the bench — the APIs, data pipelines, and workflow orchestration." Requirements are 7+ years (BSc) or 5+ years (MSc/PhD).
