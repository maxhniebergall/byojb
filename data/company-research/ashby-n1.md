# N1 — research
Provider/key: ashby:n1 | company_type: product

Entity confirmation: the registry name "N1" and the Ashby slug `n1` match the JD's own self-description of a layer-1 blockchain network. This name is highly collision-prone (there are unrelated companies called N1 in healthcare and finance) — the entity researched here is specifically the blockchain-network company described in the JD body, and no external source was used, so there is no risk of cross-contamination from a same-named company.

What they do: In their own words: "N1 is the first hyper-performant and horizontally scalable blockchain network designed to enable trustless computing at internet scale." It is a new layer-1 blockchain protocol — the product is the network itself and its node software, developed as "a prominent open-source project."

How they describe themselves: The self-description is almost entirely a credentials pitch rather than a values statement: "N1 is comprised of a team spanning backgrounds from Harvard, CMU, DFINITY, VMWare, Galaxy Digital, and more, and is backed by top-tier institutions like Founders Fund, Kraken Ventures, SALT, Amber Group, and more." No mission, values or culture principles are stated anywhere in the JD. The only culture signal offered is a requirement: "Creative, independent, and collaborative team player with the ability to work in a **fast paced environment**." Engineering-quality expectations are stated: "maintaining and enhancing high-quality software engineering practices", "experience writing correct, reliable software."

Size / stage / funding: Venture-backed, clearly early-stage (a new L1 network still building core backend components). Investors named by the company: **Founders Fund, Kraken Ventures, SALT, Amber Group**. Team backgrounds cited: Harvard, CMU, DFINITY, VMware, Galaxy Digital. Headcount, round size and stage label are UNVERIFIED — not stated in any reachable source.

Locations / HQ: New York City and San Francisco Bay Area are the named office geographies; the salary language anchors on New York ("for the position of a candidate based in New York"), suggesting NYC is the primary base.

Remote policy: Remote-friendly within two named regions. Both live postings list: "New York City, San Francisco Bay Area, **Remote/US, Remote/Europe**".

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — verified negative.** The remote options are enumerated explicitly as "Remote/US" and "Remote/Europe". Canada is not among them, and the company's stated pay methodology is anchored to a New-York-based candidate. Canada is neither in the US nor in Europe, so these postings do not cover a Kimberley BC worker. (Note: some US-anchored crypto firms will engage Canadians as contractors, but nothing in the company's own words supports that here and it should not be assumed.)

Engineering & tech: **Rust** is the primary language ("designing and coding core backend software components primarily in Rust"), with C, C++ and Zig named as acceptable adjacent low-level experience. The work is core protocol/backend engineering focused on "networking, reliability, and scalability" — creation, testing, debugging and deployment of production-grade components. They explicitly seek deep systems-programming background: "Strong signals include experience writing compilers, operating systems, kernels, machine simulators." Open-source contribution to Bitcoin/Ethereum/blockchain projects is "highly desirable." 5+ years designing/testing/implementing scalable backend systems required; BSc CS or equivalent.

Notable / other: The company posts an unusually wide single band across all levels and is transparent about why — see compensation below. Compensation explicitly excludes tokens: "the compensation details listed in role postings reflect the base salary only and do not include equity, **tokens**, or benefits", confirming token-based compensation is part of the package.

Compensation stated on their own JD: "The base salary range for this full-time position is **$120k - $260k**." They add: "The range displayed on each job posting reflects the minimum and typical maximum target for new hire salaries for the position of a candidate based in New York **at any level**... our range may be higher than what is displayed. Our base salary ranges are determined by experience and location, and we hire at all levels for multiple roles." The identical range is posted on both the Senior and the non-senior Backend Rust role, which confirms it is a company-wide catch-all rather than a level-specific band.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (Backend Rust); Software Engineer (Backend Rust).
