# Aerospike — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:aerospike | company_type: product

What they do: Aerospike builds a real-time NoSQL database. In their words: "Aerospike is the
real-time database for mission-critical use cases and workloads, including machine learning,
generative, and agentic AI. Aerospike powers millions of transactions per second with millisecond
latency, at a fraction of the total cost of ownership compared to other databases." Their careers
page frames the same claim as enabling companies to "perform millions of transactions at
sub-millisecond speed, even on massive amounts of data." Alongside the core database they sell a
managed cloud offering: "a multi-cloud, multi-tenant platform that brings the power of Aerospike to
customers with maximum simplicity, speed, and scale."

Named customers (their own list): Adobe, Airtel, Barclays, Criteo, DBS Bank, Experian, Grab, HDFC
Bank, PayPal, Sony Interactive Entertainment, The Trade Desk, Wayfair. Stated use cases: customer
360, fraud detection, real-time bidding, profile stores, recommendation engines. This is a classic
infrastructure/utility vendor selling to large enterprises, heavy in adtech and financial services.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "unleash the power of the world's real-time data with a
database built for infinite scale, speed, and sustainability." Tone is confident-to-boastful: "At
Aerospike, we dream big and deliver even bigger", "If you're ready to shape the future of data, join
us." Their careers page lists **eight core values**: Customer First, Integrity, Accountability, Be
Bold, Empathy, Transparency, Collaboration, Results Focused. Engineering-specific culture language
from the JD: "we value curiosity, courage, and creativity… you'll bring new ideas to the table,
prototype rapidly, and be empowered to experiment and lead with technology", "Produce quality code,
raising the bar for team performance and speed", "champion code quality and test coverage… help us
build systems that just *work* — even under extreme load."

Size / stage / funding: Private, established, and past the startup phase — founded well over a decade
ago with a large enterprise customer base. Their careers page cites growth recognition: named to the
Inc. 5000 fastest-growing companies list and a Deloitte 2020 Technology Fast 500 Award. They describe
themselves currently as "growing fast and investing deeply in our Cloud Platform". Exact headcount and
funding round are not stated on the pages fetched; profitability not disclosed.

Locations / HQ: **HQ Mountain View / Silicon Valley, California**, with global offices in **London,
Tel Aviv, Bangalore, and Singapore**. No Canadian office is listed.

Remote policy: Hybrid, anchored to offices. The careers page does not state a formal remote policy
and instead markets "lively office life" and office amenities. The JD is explicit for the Bay Area:
"To foster strong team alignment and productivity, Bay Area–based team members are expected to work
from the office 3 times per week—typically Tuesday through Thursday." Individual roles are
nonetheless posted as remote *within a country* — the Control Plane role is tagged "Remote, USA".
On-call is part of the job: "Join the on-call rotation for services in your focus area as a technical
expert to help address customer-impacting issues." No async-culture signals were found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No, on current evidence.** The relevant live posting is geo-tagged
"Remote, USA"; the pay disclosure is given specifically for "California Based Applicants" under
California transparency law; the office footprint (Mountain View, London, Tel Aviv, Bangalore,
Singapore) contains no Canadian entity; and their careers page says nothing about geographic hiring
eligibility. Nothing indicates they can employ a Canada-based remote worker. Timezone would be
workable if they could (Pacific is one hour off Mountain) — the obstacle is the employing entity, not
the hours.

Engineering & tech: The open role sits on the **Cloud team**, building "the next generation of our
platform services" for the multi-cloud, multi-tenant managed offering — explicitly platform work, not
product surface. Scope: "build core backend systems that are foundational to our multi-cloud SaaS
platform", "optimizing performance… designing for multi-tenancy and auto-scaling", writing
"production-grade code that's secure, maintainable, and blazing fast". Required: 5+ years; "excellent
foundation in computer science and algorithms"; experience "developing distributed, multi-tenant,
auto-scalable backend services"; a statically typed backend language (**Go, Kotlin, Java, Scala**);
at least one major public cloud (AWS/GCP/Azure); NoSQL *and* relational databases with "data storage,
data replication, data modeling, and data access patterns"; Docker plus Kubernetes/EKS/GKE; Git and
CI/CD; and authn/authz/encryption standards. Preferred: **Go**, NoSQL database development,
Terraform/Ansible, the **Kubernetes Operator pattern**, and a workflow engine (**ArgoCD, Temporal**).
Collaboration model is cross-functional with "product managers, architects, QA, and other developers"
against "a long-term technology roadmap".

Notable / other: Equal Opportunity Employer statement present. The Cloud Platform is described as a
strategic investment area, which suggests the team is growing and funded rather than maintenance-mode.
The database itself is the kind of deep, well-scoped systems product where engineers own narrow
components for a long time.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Control Plane (Remote, USA — Engineering);
Senior Engineer, Observability and Manageability.

Compensation on record (posted by Aerospike on its own JD, USD base): **$169,000 – $195,000** for the
Senior Software Engineer, Control Plane role, disclosed as the "Salary Range for California Based
Applicants" with the note that "actual compensation will be determined based on experience, location,
and other factors permitted by law". This is a California-anchored figure, not a Canadian one.
