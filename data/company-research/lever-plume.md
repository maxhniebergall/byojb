# Plume — research
Provider/key: lever:plume | company_type: product

What they do: Plume (getplume.co) is a US telehealth provider of gender-affirming care for trans and non-binary people — hormone therapy (MTF, FTM and non-binary), mental-health support, lab monitoring, and adjacent clinical services such as acne treatment and smoking cessation. Its own site emphasises "no gatekeeping or barriers" and making "accessing transgender healthcare and hormone therapy more affordable", removing requirements like therapist letters or insurance. Services, pricing and minimum-age rules vary by US state (Florida, for instance, requires in-person informed-consent forms).

How they describe themselves: "Plume is a passion-fueled, mission-driven company that is trans-founded with a vision to transform healthcare for every trans life. We hope to make gender-affirming hormone therapy easily accessible at the touch of a button in every state of the US." They describe the work as "deeply personal and heart-driven", want "teammates who care about the mission and the people we serve", and offer "an affirming, trans-centered, culturally inclusive, and fun work environment filled with purpose" — "a rare opportunity to do well by doing good". They strongly encourage trans and gender-nonconforming applicants, particularly People of Colour. Their site markets an "award-winning journey"; the specific awards were not verifiable.

Size / stage / funding: Series B — the JD offers "Ground-Floor Equity (Series B)". Headcount not stated on any page reachable. Private. Uses offshore contractors for part of the data work.

Locations / HQ: US-only operations, state-by-state. HQ city not verified. The company operates region-tiered compensation: "we structure our compensation to reflect cost-of-living differences across regions. Our salary tiers are based on the cost of living relative to the national average… Tier placement will be discussed during the offer stage."

Remote policy: The posting is listed as remote, but remote **within the US only**.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — explicitly disqualifying.** The JD states verbatim: "Only those legally authorized to work in the USA are eligible to apply; residency in the USA is a requirement of employment for this role."

Engineering & tech: Data and AI team under a Director of Data and AI. Stack: BigQuery (or Snowflake/Redshift), dbt with bronze/silver/gold layers and incremental models, Apache Airflow, Looker, Python (Pandas/PySpark), dimensional modelling (star/snowflake, SCD 1/2), GitHub Actions and dbt Cloud CI, OpenMetadata for governance, HIPAA-compliant handling (PHI/PII masking, tokenisation, audit logging, RBAC). Applied AI is growing but secondary: RAG pipelines with LangChain/LangGraph, plus MLOps (training pipelines, deployment support, monitoring, retraining triggers). Nice-to-haves include Kafka/Kinesis/Pub-Sub, vector DBs, lakehouse formats (Delta/Iceberg/Hudi), Monte Carlo/Bigeye/Soda, SOC2 or HITRUST. Notably the role "takes well-defined architectural direction" from the Director and reviews offshore contractors' work.

Notable / other: Benefits include free medical/dental/vision from the first of the month after start, unlimited PTO, 11 paid holidays plus a December company shutdown, 401(k), free Plume and BetterHelp subscriptions. They warn about recruiting scams and only contact from @getplume.co addresses. The JD's stated range is $158,000-$168,000.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Data Engineer (Data + Applied AI).
