# Clearwater Analytics (CWAN) — research
Provider/key: workday:clearwateranalytics/clearwater_analytics_careers | company_type: product

What they do: Clearwater Analytics runs a front-to-back investment operations platform for institutional investors — insurers, asset managers, corporates and governments. The product spans portfolio management, trading, risk analytics, investment accounting, compliance and investment data management across asset classes. They report serving "2,400 clients globally" with "$10 trillion" in assets on the platform. It is a SaaS, multi-tenant, aggregate-and-reconcile-daily business: mission-critical financial back-office infrastructure rather than a consumer or growth product.

How they describe themselves: Their stated principles, as published: "Infectiously passionate about what we do," "Intensely committed to our clients," a focus on execution and results, being driven by continuous innovation, and operating "beyond reproach" with integrity and transparency. The tone is client-service-forward and intensity-flavored; the live JD echoes it — "driven by client satisfaction," "Strong focus on client satisfaction and user experience," "address issues with empathy and a sense of urgency," and "deliver results within deadlines."

Size / stage / funding: Formerly NYSE-listed as CWAN; **now private** — the company completed an $8.4 billion take-private acquisition by Permira and Warburg Pincus as of June 2026. Headcount is not published on the pages fetched; the office footprint below implies several thousand. As a PE-owned post-LBO business, expect cost discipline and integration activity; the specifics were not verifiable from first-party pages.

Locations / HQ: Global HQ in Boise, Idaho (777 W. Main Street, Suite 900). North America: Chicago, New York City, San Jose, Seattle, Washington DC. Europe: Dublin, Edinburgh, Frankfurt, London, Luxembourg, Paris. Asia-Pacific: Bengaluru, Hong Kong, Mumbai, New Delhi, Singapore, Sydney, Tokyo. **No Canadian office is listed.**

Remote policy: Not stated on any page fetched. The company presents as office-centric — its roles are posted against named offices in Workday, and the relevant live posting carries the location "Office - London" with no remote designation.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified NO for this role, and no evidence of any Canadian hiring.** The live Beacon Core Engineer posting is located "Office - London" — a UK in-office seat, seven to eight time zones from Mountain Time. The company lists no Canadian office anywhere in its global footprint. This is disqualifying for a BC-based candidate who cannot relocate.

Engineering & tech: The Beacon Core Engineering team builds "large part of Beacon Cloud, just above the Platform abstraction" — genuinely platform-flavored work: developer tooling and SDLC offered both internally and to clients (IDE with Cline integration, code review, test framework, release process), plus runtime execution (batch scheduler, "super computer scheduler," containerization) and the coding libraries behind the dependency graph and deal model used by the financial layer and by clients directly. Stack: Python primary, with TypeScript/JavaScript and Rust "a strong plus" and C++ a plus. Practice emphasis on multi-level automated testing (unit, integration, contract, end-to-end), design and feature leadership, mentoring, and production incident response. They are visibly AI-agent-forward: "We use AI agents to speed up development safely and at same time help clients achieve the same," and the role includes advising clients on safe agent use. No public engineering blog or handbook was located from the pages fetched.
