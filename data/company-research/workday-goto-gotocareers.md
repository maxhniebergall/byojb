# GoTo — research
Provider/key: workday:goto/gotocareers | company_type: product
What they do: GoTo builds remote-work and IT software — the GoTo Connect telephony/UCaaS platform, GoTo Meeting/Webinar, GoTo Resolve and LogMeIn Rescue remote support and remote access. Customers are SMB and mid-market IT teams. The telephony side includes provisioning, configuring and updating physical desk phones for global customers, per their own JD.
How they describe themselves: In their own words: "We're trailblazers in remote work technology—building powerful, flexible solutions that empower everyone to live their best life, both at work and beyond." On AI: "For us, AI isn't just a buzzword; it's a tool we use to deliver real, practical value to our customers and teams. We focus on solving meaningful problems, not just adding features for the sake of using AI." On growth: "Here, growth takes many forms: you can expand your skills, take on new challenges, lead initiatives, and explore creative ideas." No formal values list was retrievable — https://www.goto.com/company returned HTTP 403.
Size / stage / funding: Unverified from a fetchable source. Private (post-LogMeIn); no headcount or ownership detail could be confirmed this session.
Locations / HQ: HQ not verifiable this session. Live engineering requisitions are posted for Remote (Hungary), Remote (Quebec), Quebec City office, and Remote (Guatemala) — i.e. a distributed cost-centre model across Hungary, Quebec and Central America.
Remote policy: Genuinely remote-friendly, but remote *within a named country/province*. The Quebec JD states the work location as "N'importe où au Québec" (anywhere in Quebec). Benefits listed for the Quebec req are Canadian: RRSP, telemedicine, monthly remote-work allowance, quarterly wellness days, no-meeting days, tuition reimbursement, employee resource groups.
Remote-Canada eligibility: PARTIAL, and effectively disqualifying for this candidate. GoTo does employ Canadians, but the Canadian requisitions are Quebec-scoped and the JD states: "La connaissance du français est requise pour les postes situés en permanence au Québec" — French is required for permanently Quebec-based positions. There is no British Columbia or Canada-wide requisition live. A candidate in Kimberley BC is not eligible for the Quebec reqs, and the other openings are Hungary- and Guatemala-based.
Engineering & tech: Java and the Spring framework, backend microservice architecture, REST API development, agile remote teams. The UA team owns services that manage, configure and update hardware desk phones on the telecom platform — real infrastructure-flavoured work with on-call rotation ("Participer à l'alternance du soutien sur appel"). Deployment and monitoring responsibilities are explicit.
Notable / other: Benefits package is unusually detailed and humane in the JD: generous PTO, volunteer leave, quarterly personal wellness days, no-meeting days, Thrive Global wellbeing program, confidential EAP, one-on-one wellbeing coaching, charitable donation matching, gym reimbursement, performance bonus program. Roles are posted bilingually for Quebec.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer [Remote Hungary]; Principal Software Engineer / Technical Lead [Remote Hungary]; Développeur Logiciel / Software Developer [Remote Quebec]; Senior Data Engineer; Senior DevOps Engineer – IAM & ZTNA; Senior Machine Learning Engineer.
