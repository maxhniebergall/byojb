# Durable — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:durable | company_type: product

What they do: Durable (durable.co → durable.com) is an "all-in-one AI business builder" for very small businesses: an AI website builder that "launches a website in seconds", plus CRM, booking, invoicing, AI marketing, SEO/GEO, image studio, logo generator and a 24/7 AI business assistant. Customers are solo operators and micro-businesses — landscapers, handymen, cleaners, health & wellness practitioners, professional services, food & events, pet care, creative/digital services. Self-reported scale: ~3 million business owners on the platform and 10–11 million websites generated; 4.8 stars on Trustpilot. Entry plan $25/month, pitched against "$396/month in separate tools".

How they describe themselves: Product-side positioning is speed and accessibility — "Launch in 30 seconds", all-in-one consolidation, no technical expertise required, 24/7 AI automation. Hiring-side, the JD's own words are unusually craft-focused rather than hype-focused: "You craft fast, polished, reliable software and you're drawn to problems that are genuinely unsolved"; "building systems that stay reliable when the model is unpredictable, writing evals that surface real signal, designing agentic workflows that hold up under production conditions"; "you know where each one breaks down". On the frontend side: "In a product where AI does most of the heavy lifting, the interface is where trust is built or broken — and you hold that line." Success is defined as "Features that are fast, reliable, and polished. Architectural and interaction decisions that make the next thing faster to build. You raise the bar for the people working alongside you." Their hiring process is portfolio-first: a 2–3 minute unscripted Loom, something you've built, and three short questions (why Durable / where are you located / salary expectations) — "Your work speaks louder than your resume."

Size / stage / funding: Private venture-backed startup; headcount, round and investors are not published on the pages read (UNVERIFIED). Consumer-scale usage numbers above are the company's own claims.

Locations / HQ: Vancouver, British Columbia — "We are a Vancouver-based company".

Remote policy: Remote within Canada, with mandatory periodic travel: "candidates need to be based in Canada. Remote is fine - you can expect travel to Vancouver once per quarter (minimum) to work in-office with the team."

Remote-Canada eligibility: YES — verified in their own JD text, Canada-only hiring with remote permitted. Same province as the user (BC) and quarterly Vancouver travel is feasible from Kimberley. Note the compensation caveat: the posted band is stated "for Vancouver-based candidates" and "Actual salary takes experience, location, and other factors into account", so a location adjustment for interior BC is possible.

Engineering & tech: TypeScript-first. Languages: TypeScript, SQL, Python, Bash, HTML5, CSS3. Frameworks: React, Next.js, React Query, React Native, Expo, TailwindCSS, RadixUI, DrizzleORM, tRPC, Node.js, Bun, WebRTC, PyTorch. Tooling: Turborepo, ESLint, Prettier, Git, AI codegen. Databases: Postgres including PGVector and JSON types, object stores. Infrastructure: Docker, Cloudflare (Workers, Durable Objects, Pages, DNS, CDN), Vercel, Inngest, Render, Trigger.dev, Datadog, Together.ai, LangSmith, Hugging Face, Replicate, Langfuse. AI engineering: LLMs, multi-modal and image-generation models, in-painting, voice models; concepts they expect people to grow into include tool calling, ReAct, advanced agentic patterns, evals, memory, generative UI, streaming, multi-agent systems, guardrails, citations, vector DBs, RAG, embeddings, knowledge graphs, query routing, synthetic data, fine-tuning, RLHF, diffusion models, MCP, computer use, and serving multi-modal OSS models. They hire two shapes for the same req: "full stack" and "leans more towards frontend".

Notable / other: The role is explicitly customer-facing feature work — "You'll build customer-facing features from scratch, while also helping us integrate and scale the AI infrastructure that power them."

Open relevant roles (sample): AI Engineer (Vancouver / remote in Canada).

Pay (stated on their own JD): $180,000–$225,000 CAD base plus equity, for Vancouver-based candidates.
