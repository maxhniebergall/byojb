# Appspace — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:appspace | company_type: product

What they do: Appspace sells a "workplace experience platform" — one SaaS suite combining employee communications apps and intranets, digital signage and content management for screens, space/desk reservation, and visitor management. It grew out of the enterprise digital-signage market and expanded into hybrid-workplace tooling. Customers are large enterprises: 2,700+ subscription customers, 160+ of the Fortune 500, reaching a claimed 12 million on-site, remote and frontline employees.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "help organizations connect their people, places, and spaces to create a workplace employees love." Values are the **SPACE** acronym: **S**ervice excellence, **P**rincipled, **A**daptable, **C**amaraderie, **E**mpowerment. Careers copy: "we're passionate about creating better work experiences for people everywhere… Our global office locations and flexible work culture help you work wherever and however you're at your best. Plus, we take the time to help you enjoy your work, build lasting connections, and grow your role. Join the Appspace team and be a part of a culture that's helping people everywhere love where they work." Tone is HR-warm and benefits-forward; notably free of "rocketship"/"hustle" vocabulary. The development team is described only as adhering to "best practices in software development" using "latest technologies."

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2002 by Brandon Miles and Stan Stephens during the early enterprise-digital-signage build-out. Privately held; no publicly disclosed venture rounds or funding stage found. A ~24-year-old private enterprise-software business with a large paying customer base — the profile of a steady, cash-generating company rather than a burn-rate startup, though profitability is not disclosed.

Locations / HQ: US-headquartered. Offices in the US, UK, UAE and Malaysia (Kuala Lumpur), with staff also in Canada, Spain (Barcelona) and Portugal, plus individuals in roughly a dozen further countries.

Remote policy: Hybrid/flexible rather than declared remote-first. Their about page emphasises "flexible working hours and remote working" and "global office locations and flexible work culture," and the JD perks list includes "Flexible work schedules," "Remote work opportunities," "Appspace Quiet Fridays (No non-essential internal meetings scheduled)," generous PTO, 5 extra days off for training, and paid company holidays. No async-first or handbook culture is claimed. Quiet Fridays is a genuine, if narrow, sustainable-pace signal.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Likely yes, but not explicitly stated.** The relevant posting — Senior DevOps & Site Reliability Engineer - Americas — is ATS-located "Toronto, Ontario, Canada" and its benefits paragraph is written specifically for Canadian employees: "For all our Canadian based team members, we offer a variety of benefits from competitive salaries, medical, dental and vision coverage… and a fully paid maternity and parental leave program." So this is a Canada-payroll requisition and they demonstrably employ Canadians. What is *not* established is whether it is fully remote anywhere in Canada or Toronto-anchored hybrid: the title says "Americas," the perks say "remote work opportunities" (permissive, not guaranteed), and the ATS location is a specific city rather than "Canada Remote." A BC-based candidate would need to confirm. Toronto anchoring would also mean a 2-hour offset from Mountain Time, relevant because the role carries a **weekly on-call rotation**.

Engineering & tech: The Cloud Operations team runs a global platform footprint that is explicitly a mix of legacy and modern — "bridging the gap between legacy VM-based architectures and modern cloud-native standards through aggressive automation." Stack: Microsoft Azure (IaaS, PaaS, App Services, Networking) and Google Cloud Platform; Kubernetes (AKS/GKE) and Helm; Infrastructure as Code with Bicep and Terraform; CI/CD via GitHub Actions and Bamboo; PowerShell and Python at expert level; Windows and Linux server administration; middleware/PaaS including Event Hub, Service Bus, CosmosDB, RabbitMQ, MongoDB; databases SQL Server, MongoDB, MySQL; Atlassian suite (Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket). Observability work spans metrics, logs and traces across Azure and GCP. Compliance regimes: SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO 27001, GDPR. The role is framed around ROI and "automating away manual toil," with AI-enhanced operations (automated code review, predictive log analysis) as a stated initiative. Senior bar: 6+ years in DevOps/SRE. On-call coverage required on a weekly rotation.

Compensation: Not disclosed. The JD carries an explicit pay-transparency dodge: "For jurisdictions that require pay scale disclosure, a general compensation range may be provided during the initial stages of the interview process… If you are located in a jurisdiction with specific pay transparency requirements, we will be happy to discuss the relevant range during your application process." No number is published; no company pay page found.

Notable / other: Canadian benefits package is specifically enumerated (medical/dental/vision, gym-membership reimbursement, mental-health resources, ongoing training, fully paid maternity and parental leave). Bamboo (rather than only GitHub Actions) and ASP-era Windows tooling in the stack corroborate the stated legacy-modernisation reality.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior DevOps & Site Reliability Engineer - Americas (Toronto, Ontario, Canada).
