# Basis — research
Provider/key: lever:basis | company_type: product

Entity confirmation: the registry key is `lever:basis` and the name is ambiguous, but the JD bodies self-identify as "Basis Technologies" ("Basis Technologies' innovative Engineering team..."), the Chicago-headquartered advertising-technology company. This is NOT Basis the crypto/stablecoin project, nor Basis Theory, nor Basis (the AI-accounting startup). Confirmed from the JD text itself.

What they do: Their own words: "At Basis, we empower agencies and brands with cutting-edge software that automates digital media operations. Our all-in-one platform supports seamless planning, reporting, and financial reconciliation across direct, programmatic, search, and social media." The product combines a media-planning/reconciliation platform (Basis Platform) with an owned Demand-Side Platform — "a real-time auction engine that competes in billions of ad auctions a day, each with a sub-100ms latency". Customers are advertising agencies and brands.

How they describe themselves: Verbatim: "We're not just building advanced technology; we're also building a culture where passionate, motivated individuals come together to drive meaningful change. Our commitment to inclusivity and growth ensures our employees thrive both professionally and personally." Also, on the engineering side: "Technology is at the core of what we do." No formal values or principles list was obtainable (no company domain appears in the registry or the JD text, and no first-party page was fetchable this session). Tone is mainstream corporate with light motivational framing; no overt "fast-paced"/"scrappy"/"hustle" vocabulary, but the platform team's work is described as "high-visibility, revenue-impacting, and customer-facing".

Size / stage / funding: UNVERIFIED as to headcount, ownership and profitability. Established rather than early-stage — an owned DSP running billions of daily auctions, a full data-science/BI organisation, and a Chicago headquarters with US and Canadian operations all indicate a mature company, but no figures were obtainable.

Locations / HQ: Chicago, Illinois, USA. Their own words: "With our headquarters in Chicago, we offer flexible work options across the U.S. and Canada, including remote, hybrid, or on-site at headquarters." The registry lists the senior platform role's location as "United States, remote, Toronto, ON", confirming a Canadian (Toronto) presence.

Remote policy: Flexible/hybrid-optional. Verbatim: "we offer flexible work options across the U.S. and Canada, including remote, hybrid, or on-site at headquarters." Remote is one of three explicitly offered options, not the default. No async-culture or core-hours statement was found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: LIKELY YES, with a caveat. The company states flexible work options "across the U.S. and Canada, including remote", which is an explicit statement that remote employment in Canada is available, and the senior role's location field includes both "remote" and "Toronto, ON". However, the location field pairs Canada specifically with Toronto, so it is not confirmed whether Canadian remote hiring extends beyond Ontario or whether a Toronto-anchored arrangement is expected — this is worth confirming directly before relying on it for a British Columbia-based hire. It is not stated as Toronto-hybrid-only.

Engineering & tech: Real-time, high-throughput systems. From the JDs: the DSP is "a real-time auction engine that competes in billions of ad auctions a day, each with a sub-100ms latency", and the wider platform "processes over 300 billion events per day and uses AI and machine learning to automate and simplify the entire digital campaign process". Engineering is organised with dedicated Data Engineering, Data Science and Business Intelligence teams alongside product engineering. The senior platform role sits "at the nexus between the flagship Basis Platform application and our Demand-Side Platform", building integrations across "internal and external boundaries, services, and orgs", with explicit attention to "resources, performance, and scalability". The Tools & Services role is test-framework and tooling work — "participate in the creation of a framework that will be used to test our products for many years to come". No public engineering blog or handbook was located.
