# Whippy — research
Provider/key: ashby:whippy | company_type: product

What they do: Whippy (whippy.ai) sells AI-powered business communication — an omnichannel inbox plus Voice AI and Chat AI agents that automate customer support, sales outreach, marketing and recruiting workflows over SMS, voice and chat. Typical customers are staffing agencies, pharmacies, law firms and service businesses using it to respond to inquiries, nurture leads, screen job applicants and onboard clients at scale. Founded 2021. It serves 1,000+ paying businesses.

How they describe themselves: In their own words, Whippy is "leading the way in AI-powered business communication, transforming how companies engage with customers using intelligent AI Agents... By combining AI-driven messaging with omni-channel automation, Whippy replaces outdated tools with a single, powerful platform that helps businesses work smarter, faster, and more efficiently." No published values list. Where the JD is genuinely distinctive is a section headed "What to know?" that states its terms with unusual bluntness:
- "We are a startup, and our team dynamics differ from those of companies in different sizes and stages. Our team is distributed across the globe... **there is always something happening**, so effective time management in a remote and asynchronous engineering environment is essential."
- "While English is the second language for many engineers on the team, fluency in written English is a requirement."
- "We do not ask applicants to complete take-home assignments or participate in 'leetcode' style interviews. However, we do require a **two-week full-time or four-week part-time fully paid 'work trial'** of 'mutual assessment.' If you are unable to commit to this, please do not apply."
- "We hire globally regardless of timezone, but we have **team meetings on Mondays, Wednesday and Fridays at 2:30 pm GMT**. If you are unable to be online during these hours, please do not apply."
Also candid about the trial's intent: "If you are asking questions like 'I'm applying for multiple jobs what happens if I get one can we speed up the trial?' Then you're probably not going to be the right fit and you should take another role."

Size / stage / funding: Small, **bootstrapped and profitable** — Whippy has never raised outside capital. Revenue grew $1M (2022) → $2M (2023) → $4M (2024), reported at $5M+ ARR since. Headcount is not disclosed but is presumed very small given the revenue and bootstrapped history. The JD advertises "working hands-on with serial entrepreneurs who have created $100M+ valued companies" (CEO David Daneshgar, previously of BloomNation).

Locations / HQ: Distributed globally; no office geography stated on the posting. US-registered.

Remote policy: Fully remote and global — "We hire globally regardless of timezone." Explicitly async-oriented: "a remote and asynchronous engineering environment", and the role asks for "great written and verbal communication skills for expressing ideas, designs, and potential solutions in mostly-asynchronous manner." The one synchronous requirement is the three weekly meetings at 14:30 GMT.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes.** The Software Engineer: Backend req is posted "Remote - Worldwide" and the company states it hires globally regardless of timezone, so a Canada-based worker is eligible. The employment structure for non-US staff is not stated; a globally-distributed bootstrapped startup of this size most likely engages international staff as contractors rather than employees, but this is inference, not a company statement. The 14:30 GMT meeting slot is 07:30 Mountain Time (08:30 during GMT/MDT offset shifts) — early but workable from Mountain Time.

Engineering & tech: Backend engineering centres on API design — "Craft highly scalable and intuitive APIs to rapidly deploy new products and services." Named systems built by backend engineers: "A Public API and webhook interface to process 100s of millions of messages", "A query language on top of **Postgres and OpenSearch**", and "a production link shortening URL service with custom domains support". Engineers are expected to own the whole lifecycle — "Write Engineering Requirement Documents to take something from idea, to defined tasks, to implementation, to roll out and evaluating its success" — plus test suites and product analytics instrumentation. The messaging-scale and query-layer work is real backend infrastructure; the surrounding product is an AI agent application.

Notable / other: **Compensation is stated on the posting as $60K–$80K/year based on experience** — a global-rate band, far below North American market for this work. The hiring process is: CV screen → video call → **two-week full-time paid work trial** → meet the team and review work → offer. No take-homes or leetcode. Whippy is unusual in this space for being profitable and bootstrapped rather than venture-funded.

Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineer: Backend (Remote - Worldwide, $60K–$80K).
