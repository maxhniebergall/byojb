# Orb — research
Provider/key: ashby:orb | company_type: product

What they do: Orb builds billing / "revenue design" infrastructure — a platform for usage-based and hybrid pricing, metering, invoicing, subscription management and revenue recognition. Named customers on their own JD text: Vercel, Pinecone, Perplexity, Replit. The domain is genuinely infrastructural (event ingestion at high volume, real-time metering, correctness-critical invoicing).

How they describe themselves: From their own JD: "Orb is redefining what billing software can be, turning one of the most complex parts of running a business into a strategic driver of growth. We're building the revenue design platform that helps teams automate billing, evolve pricing, and grow revenue with confidence." They state four values verbatim: **customer centricity, minutes matter, run with it, attention to detail**. They also self-describe as "a team that moves fast, holds itself to high standards, and is serious about changing how companies grow."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed. Their own JD states **$44.1M raised** from Mayfield, Menlo Ventures and Greylock — roughly Series B scale. Headcount not stated on any page reachable from the registry; the Pricing Lifecycle JD says "As an early engineer at Orb," implying a small eng org. Profitability unknown.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco; a New York office is listed as a work location on the Pricing Lifecycle posting.

Remote policy: Stated on their own JDs: "For SF Bay Area and NYC residents, we've built a hybrid work culture where we are in office 3 days/week. For remote roles, we ask you to join us in person 1x per quarter." So: hybrid-by-default in the two hub cities, remote allowed elsewhere with mandatory quarterly onsite. No async/handbook signals found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — verified negative.** The one remote posting is titled and located "Software Engineer, Infrastructure - **Remote (USA)**" / location field `Remote-USA`. The other relevant role requires being "Based in the San Francisco Bay Area or willing to relocate." Nothing in the registry or on any reachable page indicates Canadian employment. Treat as US-only.

Engineering & tech: Stack stated verbatim on their JDs — Frontend: TypeScript + React + Tailwind; Backend: **Python**; Datastores: PostgreSQL + Apache Druid + ClickHouse; Streaming: Kafka + Spark Streaming; Cloud: AWS. The infrastructure role scope is real platform work: resiliency and recovery mechanisms, tenant isolation, load-spike handling, observability/operability, real-time event processing performance, scaling planning. They explicitly say "great engineers can learn any stack." Infra role asks 5+ yrs SWE with 4+ yrs specifically in infrastructure.

Notable / other: Benefits stated on their JDs: medical/dental/vision, One Medical, unlimited PTO plus a company week off between Christmas and New Year, 401k, 16-week paid parental leave with equity vesting, commuter stipend, catered lunches, annual L&D stipend, stock options.
