# Honor Technology — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:honor | company_type: product

What they do: Honor Technology builds the technology and services platform for in-home care of older adults. In 2021 it acquired **Home Instead**, the global in-home-care franchise network, and now runs both: Honor supplies the software/operations layer and Home Instead is the consumer care brand. In their words: "Honor provides the technology, tools, and services that empower older adults to live life on their own terms… Home Instead delivers over 50 million hours of personalized care annually" through "a global franchise network and more than 100,000 Care Pros."

How they describe themselves: Mission is "to change the way society cares for older adults" — "setting a new standard for aging in place, backed by powerful technology, compassionate care, and a commitment to aging on your own terms." They publish 13 **Leadership Principles** (honorcare.com): Are Mission First; Relentlessly Focus on Clients ("start with the client and work backwards"); Always Push ("set a high bar for themselves and others"); Identify Problems; GSD ("make tangible progress, delivering quality and timely results"); Think Systemically; Are Brutally Honest with Themselves; Tend to be Right; Balance Incremental and Radical Change; Find Those Better than Themselves; Fly High & Dive Deep; Build Trust; Disagree and Commit. The framing is visibly Amazon-derived (customer-obsession, dive deep, disagree and commit, high bar).

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed (Andreessen Horowitz among earlier investors), San Francisco origin; combined with the Home Instead franchise network it is a large operation, though Honor's own corporate headcount is far smaller than the 100k+ Care Pro figure. Exact headcount, current funding stage and profitability were NOT verified this run.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco Bay Area origin; Home Instead is headquartered in Omaha, Nebraska. All five live engineering reqs are posted simply as "Remote Position".

Remote policy: Remote-first, stated in their own words on the pay-transparency block of every JD: "We use national average to determine pay as we are a remote first company." No office-attendance requirement appears in any JD.

Remote-Canada eligibility: UNCLEAR, leaning no. All reqs say "Remote Position" with no country qualifier, which is genuinely ambiguous — but the pay language references a US "national average" and the posted ranges are USD, which is how a US-only remote employer writes a req. No JD mentions Canada or work authorization. This one would need a direct question to a recruiter to resolve; do not assume Canada-eligible.

Engineering & tech: Backend-heavy and reasonably substantive: "core services that power how families find care and how Care Professionals do their work". Stack per JDs — Python (primary), relational data modelling "at scale", AWS, service-oriented architecture, API design, distributed systems; frontend familiarity (React/Next.js/Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind) is asked for even on backend reqs. They explicitly want "experience with using AI tooling to assist with productivity in both coding and deployment". The Staff SWE role sits on the **DemandGen Team** — "Honor's public digital experiences and growth channels" — i.e. growth/marketing-adjacent engineering, not internal platform.

Notable / other: All five reqs post USD ranges: Senior Backend and Senior Applied AI at $160k-$180k; Staff Data Platform, Staff Platform (Infra+DevOps) and Staff SWE at $194k-$220k / $194.4k-$216k. Values language contains a moderate amount of push/pace signalling ("Always Push", "GSD", "set a high bar").

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Backend Engineer; Senior Engineer, Applied AI; Staff Data Platform Engineer; Staff Platform Engineer - Infra + DevOps; Staff Software Engineer.
