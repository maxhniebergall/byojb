# Pluxee — research
Provider/key: workday:pluxee/pluxee_career_site | company_type: product
What they do: Pluxee is an employee-benefits and engagement company: meal & food benefits, wellbeing, lifestyle, reward & recognition, and public benefits programs, sold B2B2C. Its own JD text describes "a highly interconnected B2B2C ecosystem made up of more than 500,000 clients, 36 million consumers and 1.7 million merchants." Engineering builds the payment and benefits platform behind those programs.
How they describe themselves (their words, from their JDs): "Pluxee is a global player in employee benefits and engagement that operates in 31 countries... Powered by leading technology and more than 5,000 engaged team members... Conducting its business as a trusted partner for more than 45 years, Pluxee is committed to creating a positive impact on all its stakeholders." Culture section: "1) A meaningful job: Be the change!... 2) A great culture: People matter – a lot! Be part of a multicultural team that moves as one in a fast paced and innovative environment. We respect and care authentically about our people, we embrace wellbeing and work-life balance, new ideas and we have a lot of fun! 3) An empowering environment: Be yourself!" Note the explicit "fast paced" self-description. Engineering framing in the Payments Squad JD is genuinely substantive: "We don't just write code; we design resilient, mission-critical systems where transactional precision, idempotency, and fault tolerance are at the heart of everything we do."
Size / stage / funding: 5,000+ employees, operating in 31 countries, ~45+ years of operating history under its predecessor. Publicly listed (spun out of Sodexo) — NOTE: the ownership/listing detail could not be verified in this session because pluxee.com was unreachable; treat as unverified.

Engineering & tech: Stated daily stack (Madrid Payments/Benefits squads): TypeScript, Node.js, React, React Native, Auth0, MongoDB (Atlas); Sentry for logging; GitLab CI with multiple deploys per day; GitHub/GitLab, Docker, Kubernetes; JIRA and Notion; Slack and Google Meet. Strong stated testing discipline: "We add tests to everything" — unit, acceptance, integration, load and performance tests. Squad-based organisation (Payments Squad, Benefits Squad). Interview loop for the Paris role: video or in-person with the Lead Engineer, then the Tech Lead. Some roles require English AND French.
