What they do (their own words): "Oneleet is one of the fastest-growing security and compliance platforms in history. We are on a mission to change the compliance and security industry by making cybersecurity and compliance effective, easy, and painless. We provide a platform that helps companies build, manage, and monitor their cybersecurity programs and achieve compliance standards such as SOC 2 and ISO 27001 efficiently, without cutting corners." Their site frames it as "compliance done fast and secure" so teams can "get back to building," positioning explicitly against "compliance theater." The platform bundles program management, access reviews, risk management and vendor management, and their marketing claims it replaces roughly six separate vendors in a typical compliance stack and gets teams audit-ready "70% faster." A Vanta/Drata competitor whose differentiator is that it pairs the compliance automation with actual security work (including pentesting).
