# TMX Group — research
Provider/key: workday:tmx/tmx_careers | company_type: product

What they do: In their own words, "The TMX group of companies includes leading global exchanges such as the Toronto Stock Exchange, Montreal Exchange, and numerous innovative organizations enhancing capital markets." TMX operates Canada's principal equity and derivatives exchanges plus clearing, depository, market data and analytics businesses. The technology they run is, literally, national market infrastructure — they describe themselves as "powering some of the nation's most critical systems, fueling capital formation and innovation."

How they describe themselves: The careers boilerplate opens "Venture outside the ordinary - TMX Careers" and continues: "United as a global team, we're connecting cross-functionally, traversing industries and geographies, moving opportunity into action, advancing global economic growth, and propelling progress. Through a rich exchange of ideas, meaningful collaboration, and a nimble operating model, we're powering some of the nation's most critical systems... bringing increased opportunity to business visionaries, product ingenuity to consumers, and career exploration to our team. Ready to be part of the action?" The engineering-org framing is notably service-oriented: Global Enterprise Services (GES) is described as "one of the foundational divisions of the TMX Group, that empowers internal TMX business lines for their technology needs, operations and digital innovation... a client centric organization [that] focuses on building technology capabilities... The cost effective operation is a key attribute of the GES execution." In the Platform Engineer role's own words: "You aren't just managing servers; you are architecting the resilient, elastic, and secure container ecosystems that drive the global financial markets. You will bridge the gap between legacy stability and future-forward innovation."

Size / stage / funding: Public company (TMX Group Limited, TSX: X). Long-established, profitable, regulated financial-market-infrastructure operator with recurring listing, trading, clearing and data revenue. Exact headcount not verified in this pass.

Locations / HQ: Toronto (100 Adelaide St W) for all observed engineering postings; Montreal is the other principal centre via the Montreal Exchange.

Remote policy: Hybrid, Toronto-office-anchored. Both live Data Engineer postings extract as `remote_policy: hybrid`, `geo_eligibility: canada`, `timezone: America/Toronto`, and every posting's location string is the literal Toronto street address.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Canada yes, remote no.** TMX employs in Canada without question, but the roles are hybrid out of the Toronto office, in Eastern Time. A Kimberley, BC candidate cannot satisfy a Toronto in-office cadence, and Mountain Time is two hours behind an exchange operation whose workday is anchored to market hours. Treat these roles as effectively disqualifying on location.

Engineering & tech: The Platform Engineer role sits in infrastructure engineering within GES and is squarely target-archetype work: "Design and maintain a multi-cloud Kubernetes ecosystem (EKS, GKE, OpenShift, and Rancher) with a focus on high availability and cost-efficiency"; Infrastructure as Code ("Treat infrastructure like software... build, configure, and monitor environments across the SDLC using programmatic, repeatable methods"); Service Mesh and loosely coupled architectures for service discovery and security; CI/CD pipeline construction with container registries; partnering with Security and Data Architects on "bulletproof failover/failback strategies and data protection protocols"; and evaluating CNCF-landscape technology. They ask for "systems thinking," troubleshooting distributed environments "under pressure," and continuous learning. The other roles are Lead Data Engineer, Big Data Engineer and DevOps Engineer.

Compensation: No figure is published by TMX on these postings. `data/company-comp.jsonl` holds two `estimated` (llm_prior) bands — data_eng/senior at CAD 100,000-120,000 and platform_infra/senior at CAD 100,000-110,000 — both Stage-3 extractor guesses with no company source. The one comp slot in the work-list is marked `needs_comp: false`, and nothing found in this pass constitutes better evidence, so no new band row was written. If those estimates are anywhere near right, the pay sits below the walk-away number; but they are guesses and should be treated as such.

Notable / other: The GES framing — an internal-services division measured on cost-effective operation serving internal business lines — is unusually explicit about the "internal customers, well-scoped plumbing" model. The trade-off is that a cost-centre framing tends to correlate with cost-centre pay.

Open relevant roles (sample): Platform Engineer (Toronto); Data Engineer / Big Data Engineer (Toronto, x2); DevOps Engineer; Lead Data Engineer.
