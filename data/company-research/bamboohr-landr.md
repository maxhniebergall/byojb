# LANDR — research

What they do: LANDR is a music-creation platform for independent musicians and producers, best known for AI audio mastering ("Studio-quality, AI mastering in your web browser") delivered both in-browser and as a DAW plugin. Around that it has assembled an all-in-one creator suite: distribution to "150+ streaming platforms", 70+ audio plugins, "3M+ royalty-free samples", stem separation, collaboration/video-chat and networking features, and 200+ music courses. Customers are self-releasing artists and producers, sold as a subscription "starting at USD $8.25/month".
