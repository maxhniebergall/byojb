# Tyk Technologies — research
Provider/key: workable:tyk-technologies | company_type: product

What they do: API management — an open-source API gateway written in Go (supporting REST, GraphQL, TCP and gRPC), plus a commercial control plane, developer portal and managed Tyk Cloud offering. In their own words the company "was born out of frustration with rigid, expensive, and overly complex API management solutions," and the founders set out to build a gateway that was "lightweight, performant, and genuinely developer-friendly." B2B platform with "many thousands of users… across the globe." Named customers include Lotte, Bell, T-Mobile, RBS, Capital One and Vinci. Current positioning adds "AI-ready API management." As they put it: "If you've banked online, used an app to check the news, or perhaps even driven a connected car, APIs, and by extension, Tyk, make that possible."

How they describe themselves: The culture is published as "The Tyk Way," headlined **"Total flexibility, default remote, radical responsibility."** Five stated principles:
- **Embracing failure** — "It's OK to screw up"
- **Innovation** — "The only stupid idea is the untested one"
- **Trust** — "Trust starts with you, make it count"
- **Collaboration** — "Assume best intent"
- **Continuous improvement** — "Make things better"
They also state: "there are no stupid questions," and "trust is a two-way street… we have each other's back, we're all on the same team." Everything is "grounded in a developer-first philosophy: building for the people who actually use their platform."

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2015. Privately held, bootstrapped for its early years before taking growth investment (Scottish Equity Partners / MTS). Order of a few hundred employees. Established, revenue-generating, with a large enterprise customer base rather than a runway-driven startup.

Locations / HQ: Registered/rooted in **London, UK**, with offices in **London, Ontario (Canada)**, Atlanta, and Singapore. Despite the offices, the operating model is remote-first rather than office-anchored.

Remote policy: Among the most explicit in the batch. They describe themselves as **"the first API company to embrace international remote-first working"** and offer **"unlimited paid holidays and remote working from anywhere in the world, for everyone."** Their reasoning, verbatim: "Tyk was founded on the principle of offering flexibility and autonomy to our employees, we believe this allows our employees to achieve their best results. It also means we can build the best possible team, **location and working hours are no barrier.**" The SRE JD adds: "As a remote first company, you will have the opportunity to work with an industry leading distributed team." Benefits include unlimited paid holiday, flexible hours, parental leave, a **three-month sabbatical after five years**, volunteering days and an employee share scheme. The founders themselves started out "12 hours apart," and the company is Flexa-certified and a Great Place to Work®.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** The live Site Reliability Engineer posting is listed directly as **"Canada (Remote)"** with workplace type "remote" (posted 2025-11-06), and the company operates an office in London, Ontario, which establishes a Canadian presence. A separate APAC-scoped SRE role exists for India, confirming they staff the SRE function region-by-region rather than US-only. Their stated policy that "location and working hours are no barrier" is unusually strong support for a Mountain Time worker.

Engineering & tech: Go is the primary language — the gateway is a well-known open-source Go project (github.com/TykTechnologies/tyk). Around it: Kubernetes and operators, cloud infrastructure across multiple providers for Tyk Cloud, Redis, and the usual observability stack. The SRE role sits on the Cloud platform team. Open-source development happens in public, so the actual engineering standards are inspectable.

Notable / other: One of the small set of credible independent API-gateway vendors (alongside Kong and Apigee), with a genuine open-source-first heritage rather than an open-core afterthought. Unusually broad geographic user base — they note users "hailing from every continent – even Antarctica."

Open relevant roles (sample): Site Reliability Engineer — Canada (Remote); Site Reliability Engineer - APAC — India (Remote).
