What they do: Jonas Club Software is the private-club vertical (golf, country, athletic and city clubs) of **Jonas Software**, which describes itself as "a leading provider of enterprise management software solutions, serving a wide range of vertical markets including hospitality, healthcare, construction, education, personal care, fitness, leisure, moving and legal services." Jonas is a roll-up: "over 65 distinct brands, each a respected leader in its domain."

How they describe themselves: "Jonas' vision is to be the branded global leader across these verticals and to be recognized by customers and industry stakeholders as the trusted provider of **'Software for Life.'** We are committed to technology, product innovation, quality, and exceptional customer service." The competencies listed on the JD are the closest thing to stated values on the role side: Data Storytelling, **Fast Learner** ("curious and fast paced learner that can adapt to change"), **Bias for Action** ("ships iteratively, validate quickly and improve continuously"), Quality & Accuracy, Customer Focus, and "Ownership & Scope Discipline." They also disclose "AI may be used during the hiring process."

Size / stage / funding: **Very stable and public-backed.** Their own words: "Jonas Software supports over 60,000 customers in more than 30 countries. We employ over 6,000 skilled professionals… Across our broader network, we support a global workforce of more than 30,000 employees." Critically: "Headquartered in Canada, Jonas Software has a global footprint with offices around the world. We're a **100% owned subsidiary of Constellation Software Inc.**, based in Toronto, publicly listed on the TSX (CSU.TO), and a member of the S&P/TSX 60 Index." Constellation's model is buy-and-hold of profitable vertical-market software — the opposite of burn-rate risk.

Locations / HQ: Canada-headquartered (Toronto area); the Club business unit role is in **Markham, Ontario**. Global offices.

Remote policy: **Hybrid, stated on the JD:** "Location: Markham, ON (Hybrid)." Scheduled weekly hours 37.5, regular worker type.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified NO for a BC-based worker.** The one live relevant role is explicitly Markham, ON hybrid — physical attendance in the GTA is required, and it is Eastern Time. Canada-eligible in the trivial sense (Canadian employer), remote-BC-eligible: no.

Engineering & tech: The live role is a **BI Data Engineer / BI-reporting developer**, not platform engineering: AWS QuickSight dashboards (parameters, controls, calculated fields, SPICE, row-level security), heavy SQL (complex joins, window functions, CTEs, aggregation, query performance tuning), dimensional modelling and semantic layers, data pipelines/orchestration in the cloud, AWS S3/Lambda/IAM, Git. It also carries a web-app maintenance component: "Application layer built with OAuth, Node.JS, Express.JS, MongoDB, SQL and .NET." Nice-to-haves stretch across Power BI/Looker, Snowflake/Databricks, Docker, Service Bus, pandas, CI/CD for BI assets and "building interactive chat type solutions over existing data assets."

Notable / other: Seniority is stated as **"Intermediate (2–5 years)"** and the posted salary range is **CA$100,000–$115,000** base. It is a newly created role reporting to the Director, BI/Analytics, with 1 opening. The role is broad ("full stack engineer for BI / Reporting solution") — dashboards + pipelines + web presentation layer in one seat.
