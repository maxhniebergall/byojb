# Spotify — research
Provider/key: lever:spotify | company_type: product

What they do: The global audio streaming service — music, podcasts and audiobooks — plus the
advertising and subscription businesses that fund it. Their own JD language sets the scale: "an
ambitious path to reach one billion users and $100 billion in revenue", and a Subscriptions data
platform covering "more than 200 million Premium subscribers around the world." Publicly listed
(NYSE: SPOT); Stockholm-founded, dual-headquartered Stockholm/New York.

How they describe themselves: The JD bodies captured in the registry are intro paragraphs only (see
data-quality note), so the full values set could not be quoted first-hand. What their own words do
show is a strong platform-engineering identity: "The Platform team creates the technology that
enables Spotify to learn quickly and scale easily… creating the infrastructure, tooling, frameworks,
and capabilities needed to welcome a billion customers." And on the developer-experience side:
"This is not just a tooling migration. It's a rethinking of how thousands of engineers build, test,
and ship software every day." Framing throughout is engineering-craft rather than hustle — no
"fast-paced", "wear many hats" or hustle vocabulary appears in any of the 24 captured postings.

Size / stage / funding: Large public company, roughly 7,000 employees, profitable in recent years.
It did cut ~17% of staff in December 2023 alongside two smaller 2023 rounds — widely reported but
**not verified from a first-party source in this run**; treat as context, not fact.

Locations / HQ: Stockholm and New York, with major engineering hubs in Stockholm, New York, London,
Gothenburg and **Toronto**. Live postings are anchored to: New York NY (remote), Toronto (remote),
London (remote or hybrid), Stockholm and Gothenburg.

Remote policy: Spotify posts most roles with an explicit work-mode tag, and in this batch the split
is visible: New York and Toronto roles are tagged **remote**, while most London/Stockholm roles are
tagged **hybrid**. Spotify has publicly run a "Work From Anywhere" programme since 2021 — but that
could not be confirmed first-hand here (see sourcing note) and its current terms are unverified.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Likely yes, but unverified — with one important caveat.** Six live
postings are tagged "Toronto, remote", and Spotify has a real Canadian engineering presence and
entity in Toronto, so a Canadian employment relationship is clearly possible. What is NOT
established is whether "Toronto, remote" means remote *anywhere in Canada* or remote *within the
Toronto/Ontario area*. That distinction decides whether Kimberley BC works. Timezone is favourable
either way: Toronto is ET, two hours ahead of Mountain — a normal working overlap. The
London/Stockholm hybrid roles and the New York roles are not Canada-viable.

Engineering & tech: Deep, well-resourced infrastructure org — this is the standout signal.
- **Platform / Starfleet**: owns internal platforms including **Fleetshift**, "the engine powering
  large-scale automated code refactoring across Spotify", and **Production Policy Enforcer**, "which
  secures and streamlines our production fleet". Currently "migrating from a polyrepo ecosystem to a
  unified monorepo model."
- **Data Infrastructure**: "We build the infrastructure and tooling that powers data pipelines used
  in some of Spotify's most loved experiences, like Discover Weekly and Wrapped… especially as AI
  and intelligent agents become more central to how we work with data."
- **Subscriptions data platform**: "the foundational data platform that helps Spotify understand
  conversion, retention, engagement, and subscriber health… enabling better decisions across
  analytics, experimentation, machine learning, and product development."
- **Personalization ML**: embedding models for catalogue discovery, candidate generation and
  ranking, and "a unified transformer-based generative personalization model"; products include
  Home, Search, Discover Weekly, daylist, AI DJ and AI Playlists.
Spotify is also the origin of Backstage, the widely adopted open-source developer portal, and runs a
substantial public engineering blog.

Compensation: No range appears on any of the 24 captured postings, including the New York ones
(which would normally carry an NYC pay-transparency range — another sign the captured bodies are
truncated). No first-party band could be established; see `data/company-comp/` for parametric
estimates, which are explicitly marked as estimates.
