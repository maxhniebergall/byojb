# CertifID — research
Provider/key: lever:certifid | company_type: product

What they do: CertifID is anti-wire-fraud infrastructure for real-estate transactions. In their own words: "Cybercrime is rising, reaching record highs in 2024. According to the FBI's IC3 report, total losses exceeded $16 billion… the real estate sector remains a lucrative target for cybercriminals. At CertifID, we take this threat seriously and provide a secure platform that verifies the identities of parties involved in transactions, authenticates wire transfer instructions, and detects potential fraud attempts." Customers are title companies, lenders and other parties to real-estate closings. They also move money themselves — the payments side handles ACH/RTP/FedNow rails through sponsor bank partners.

How they describe themselves: Mission-led with a single sharp vision statement — "our vision of a world without wire fraud" — and "We are guided by our core values" (the values themselves are not enumerated in the JD; their careers site was not reachable to confirm them). Culture framing: "We offer a dynamic work environment where you can contribute to meaningful impact and be part of a team dedicated to enhancing security and fighting fraud."
