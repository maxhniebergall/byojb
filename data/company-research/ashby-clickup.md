# ClickUp — research
Provider/key: ashby:clickup | company_type: product

What they do: ClickUp sells a work-management / productivity SaaS platform. Their own framing has moved
hard toward AI: "At ClickUp, we're building the future of work: the first truly converged AI workspace
unifying tasks, docs, chat, calendar, and enterprise search, all supercharged by context-driven AI. We
are an AI-native company. Every team member is expected to leverage AI daily, and we evaluate AI fluency
as part of our hiring process." Scale claims from their careers page: "20M+ users globally", "800K+ new
team signups monthly", and "$300M+ in annual recurring revenue", described as "one of the fastest-growing
companies in SaaS."

How they describe themselves: The stated values are unusually blunt. Their careers page leads with
"Normal f*cking sucks" and continues with challenging norms for greatness, embracing hard work and
resiliency, driving urgency, growing "1% every day", being fun to work with, attention to detail,
staying hungry, delivering excellent customer experience, and direct communication. They describe
themselves as product-led, valuing "ownership, speed, and craftsmanship", with teams that are "lean and
cross-functional" enabling "outsized impact". The live JD reinforces the tone: "This is a high-leverage,
high-autonomy role where you'll set the technical bar for the team... This role is for someone who
doesn't just build great systems, but makes the engineers around them better."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed — "$535M in funding from investors including Andreessen
Horowitz and Tiger Global" (a Series C-plus profile), $300M+ ARR. Late-stage growth company, not a
public or profitable one; ClickUp has had public layoff rounds in the past (not verified this session).

Locations / HQ: San Diego, California (main HQ), with additional offices in Dublin (European HQ),
San Francisco, and London.

Remote policy: Mixed and role-dependent, in their own words: "Our work model varies by role and
location. Some teams are fully remote, while others follow a hybrid schedule." They add "a co-working
program for remote employees" seeking flexible workspace, and note that specific arrangements appear in
individual job postings.

Remote-Canada eligibility: LIKELY YES for the role in question, though not stated in the JD text. The
one live relevant posting, "Staff Data Engineer", carries the ATS location "United States, Canada" —
i.e. Canada is an explicitly listed hiring geography. The JD body itself says nothing about location,
hybrid expectations, or time-zone requirements, and the careers page notes the work model "varies by
role and location", so whether this specific role is fully remote or tied to a hub is unverified. The
visa note in the JD ("we are unable to sponsor or take over sponsorship of an employment visa for roles
outside of engineering and product") does not apply to a Canadian citizen working from Canada. Overall:
Canada is listed, which is a real positive; hub/time-zone expectations remain unconfirmed.

Engineering & tech: The Staff Data Engineer role is a substantive platform-architecture position:
"Own the technical architecture of ClickUp's data platform, making design decisions that balance
scalability, cost, reliability, and velocity", "Design systems at scale: build frameworks, abstractions,
and patterns that other engineers use daily", "Drive cost optimization across cloud infrastructure and
compute", "Design and maintain infrastructure for AI/ML workloads, including LLM frameworks, feature
pipelines, training data systems, and model monitoring." Stack: AWS serverless (Lambda, Fargate, Step
Functions, Kinesis, S3, DynamoDB, Aurora), Snowflake, dbt, Terraform/CDK, Airflow/Dagster/Prefect,
Kafka, Docker, CI/CD, feature stores, data mesh, FinOps. Requirements include "at least 3 years operating
at a senior or staff level", "Expert-level SQL and Snowflake... including performance tuning and cost
optimization", and "Prior experience in a technical leadership role without direct reports
(staff/principal IC track)" as a desirable.

Notable / other: The wider title list shows a large AI-engineering hiring push (Senior/Staff AI Engineer
across Multi-Agent Frameworks, AI Platform, AI Product, Voice Platform) plus backend roles for Hierarchy,
Fields, Search and Collaboration Platforms — i.e. real agent-infrastructure teams exist, not only
product-surface AI. Only one of these currently reads as relevant in the registry, and it states no
compensation.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Data Engineer (United States, Canada); Senior AI Engineer - AI
Platform; Staff AI Engineer - Multi-Agent Frameworks; Principal Engineer, Collaboration Platforms;
Senior Backend Engineer, Search.
