# Cockroach Labs — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:cockroachlabs | company_type: product

What they do: Cockroach Labs builds **CockroachDB**, a distributed SQL database, and **CockroachDB Cloud**, its managed multi-cloud service. Their pitch: "CockroachDB makes it easier for companies to build and scale apps," aimed at companies that "disrupt… but many fail when they try to scale." The Cloud product covers customer-facing capabilities including backup and restore, disaster recovery, private connectivity, and AI features.

How they describe themselves: Careers tagline is "Category-defining tech. Career-defining work." Their stated posture: "We tackle problems head-on and focus on solutions that create lasting impact," and "Because when our customers win, we all win." On engineering norms they state a preference for collaboration explicitly — "A collaborative approach to engineering. You do code reviews, you pair on hard problems, and you help the people around you get better." Onboarding is unusually deliberate for a startup and they say so: "In your first 30 days, you'll onboard and be oriented… **We want you to take the time to learn before you start building.**" There is no hustle or "fast-paced" language anywhere in the JD.

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, long-established in database terms (founded 2015). EVP of R&D Isaac Wong previously spent 16 years at Medidata Solutions "from a 30 person startup to more than 2000 people worldwide." Headcount and current funding stage were not verified this session.

Locations / HQ: HQ in **New York, NY**, with a **Toronto, Canada** office. The relevant posting is dual-listed "New York, NY; Toronto, Canada," and the JD narrows it: "We're looking for a Software Engineer **based out of Toronto**."

Remote policy: **Hybrid, stated explicitly and in detail.** Quoting their own JD boilerplate: "Cockroach Labs has a hybrid work model, with Roachers that are local to one of our offices coming in on **Mondays, Tuesdays, and Thursdays** and working flexibly the rest of the week. While we've learned valuable lessons working remotely, nothing can replace the connection, creativity, and fun that occurs when Roachers get together and we are committed to fostering a workplace that encourages collaboration."
Countervailing signal in the same JD: the team itself is globally distributed — "Comfortable working in a geographically distributed team. **Most of your teammates will be in different time zones**," and "Experience on globally distributed teams" is listed as a bonus.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified NO for this role.** The company has a Canadian entity (Toronto office), so Canadian employment is possible, but this posting is explicitly "based out of Toronto" and the company-wide policy requires three in-office days a week (Mon/Tue/Thu) for anyone local to an office. There is no remote-Canada designation on the posting. Not workable from Kimberley BC. The distributed-timezone language above suggests the *team* would tolerate a Mountain Time member, but the stated work model does not.

Engineering & tech: The open role sits on the **Production Services** team, which "owns the customer-facing capabilities of CockroachDB Cloud… backup and restore, disaster recovery, and private connectivity," plus customer-facing AI features. They draw the boundary sharply and unprompted: **"We don't build tooling for other engineering teams to consume. The features we ship go straight to customers."** Work includes managed backup/restore pipeline reliability, observability and SLA compliance, instrumenting metrics and alerts to reduce MTTR, automation to reduce operational toil, applying AI to operational workflows (alert triage, automated diagnostics), and **participating in on-call for production systems**. Requirements name reliability principles, SLOs/SLAs, error budgets, multi-cloud experience, networking fundamentals (TCP/TLS/DNS), and prior on-call experience; ~5+ years and a CS degree or equivalent. Adjacent teams named: Production Infrastructure, Production Orchestration. Reporting line: Steve Tidwell, Senior Manager Engineering, within the Production Engineering organisation.

Notable / other: The JD publishes named bios for the hiring manager and the EVP of R&D — a transparency signal. Benefits listed: stock options, medical/vision/dental, life and disability insurance, professional development funds, flexible time off, paid holidays, paid sick days, paid parental leave, retirement benefits, mental wellbeing benefits. Explicit accessibility/accommodation contact published.
