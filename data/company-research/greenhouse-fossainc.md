# FOSSA — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:fossainc | company_type: product

What they do: Software Composition Analysis (SCA) — FOSSA scans a company's codebase and build artifacts to inventory third-party and open-source dependencies, then enforces license compliance, security (vulnerability) policy, and SBOM generation. The platform analyses packages, containers, SBOMs, binaries and code snippets. Customers are enterprises that must prove what open source is inside their shipped software; 80+ enterprise contracts plus partnerships with global systems integrators and CI/CD vendors. Developer-tooling / supply-chain security, sold to engineering and legal/compliance orgs.

How they describe themselves: They lead with being "a rapidly growing series-B company with a truly distributed team. While our roots are in San Francisco, our growing team has members in several countries across many time zones. We're always working to find the perfect balance of effective approaches to distributed work, while maintaining the direct personal connections that help us work better together and love our jobs." On the role itself they say: "you'll be working closely with the whole company to bring features from ideation to implementation. Your participation will have a significant impact on our company roadmap, and you'll be able to see the outsized impact that your work has on our revenue. Your day-to-day will be largely determined by what you want to work on: in a team of our size, our engineering team also helps own our product, customer success, and growth functions."

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2015 in San Francisco by Kevin Wang. Roughly 70 employees across five countries. Series B (most recent round July 2025); ~$43.6M raised in total, investors include Bain Capital Ventures, Costanoa and Canvas Ventures. Estimated ARR in the high single-digit millions. Venture-stage and growth-driven, not profitable-and-boring.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco roots, but the team is genuinely distributed across five countries. Postings are city-anchored-but-remote (Toronto; Vancouver).

Remote policy: Distributed / remote by default — "a truly distributed team… members in several countries across many time zones." They explicitly frame it as an ongoing balancing act rather than a settled async-first doctrine, and value "direct personal connections," so expect meaningful synchronous overlap rather than a handbook-driven async culture.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** Both live Senior Software Engineer (Analysis) postings are Canadian: "Toronto, Ontario, Canada - Remote" and "Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada - Remote". Compensation is quoted in CAD, which confirms a Canadian employing entity rather than a US role that merely tolerates Canadians. The Vancouver posting is in-province for a BC-based candidate; Pacific-anchored hours are an easy overlap from Mountain Time.

Engineering & tech: The "Analysis" team is the technical core of the product — dependency resolution and build-system introspection across many language ecosystems, license-text detection, snippet matching and binary analysis. This is genuinely deep static-analysis work. FOSSA maintains a well-known open-source CLI and publishes regularly on open-source licensing and supply-chain topics. Small enough (70 people) that engineers span multiple concerns.

Notable / other: Strong name recognition in the open-source licensing space; their license-compliance content is widely cited. Benefits include "competitive salary and equity package". The candid admission in their own JD that engineering "also helps own our product, customer success, and growth functions" is an unusually honest scope disclosure.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (Analysis) — Toronto remote; Senior Software Engineer (Analysis) — Vancouver remote.
