# GoDaddy — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:godaddy | company_type: product

What they do: GoDaddy is one of the world's largest domain registrars and small-business web-services companies — domains, hosting, website building, commerce and security tooling. Their stated mission is "to radically shift the global economy toward small businesses by empowering people to easily start, confidently grow, and successfully run their own ventures. We are dedicated to giving our customers the tools, insights, and the people to transform their ideas and personal initiative into success." Public company (NYSE: GDDY), tens of millions of customers.

How they describe themselves: On the engineering side the tone is craft-oriented rather than hype-driven: "We're looking for a Staff Software Engineer who loves to dive into technology and pull it all apart to see how it works, then put it back together again better than it was before. We treasure curiosity, communication, a bias toward collaboration and action, and the desire to embrace and extend guidelines and patterns. If you're the kind of person who can't see something that isn't right without a compulsion to make it at least just a little bit better before you put it back, we want to talk to you!" They point candidates at their public engineering blog (godaddy.github.io / godaddy.com/engineering), a Jekyll site maintained by engineers covering their tooling and open-source work.

Size / stage / funding: Large, public, established and profitable — roughly 9,000 employees. Not runway-dependent; this is a mature utility-like business rather than a startup.

Locations / HQ: HQ in Tempe, Arizona, with offices globally. Canadian remote hiring is scoped to British Columbia and Ontario on the current requisitions. AWS footprint spans 12 regions.

Remote policy: Team-by-team rather than company-wide. Their own wording: "At GoDaddy the future of work looks different for each team. Some teams work in the office full-time; others have a hybrid arrangement (they work remotely some days and in the office some days) and some work entirely remotely." The relevant engineering requisitions are explicitly in the fully-remote category: "This is a remote position, so you'll be working remotely from your home. You may occasionally visit a GoDaddy office to meet with your team for events or meetings."

Remote-Canada eligibility: VERIFIED YES, and specifically British Columbia. The Staff Software Engineer requisition states "Location Details: BC or ON - Canada, Remote" and the registry records its location as "British Columbia, Canada"; the Senior Software Engineer - Backend/Infrastructure requisition is scoped to "Canada". This is one of the few companies in this batch that names BC explicitly rather than defaulting to Toronto. No relocation implied; occasional office visits only.

Engineering & tech: The relevant roles sit on the CAPE team within Global Platform Engineering, which "owns GoDaddy's internal developer platform at the infrastructure layer — the foundation that every engineering team builds on." Two named systems: the Golden Images system (hardened, patched, security-scanned container images and AMIs distributed to all GoDaddy AWS accounts across 12 regions — GCIs, GCRIs, GAMIs) and CertAPI, "GoDaddy's centralized certificate lifecycle service" automating "TLS/mTLS issuance, renewal, and governance for thousands of internal services." Work includes automated build/harden/scan/distribute pipelines, certificate issuance/renewal/revocation and policy enforcement, and security posture work: CVE triage, remediation SLA enforcement, SBOM generation and supply-chain hardening. They explicitly own "the specifications, documentation, and reference implementations for these foundational systems" plus the pipelines and cloud infrastructure. Stack is AWS-centric and multi-region.

Compensation (posted, Canada): Staff Software Engineer CA$153,500–230,500 base, plus a discretionary cash bonus of 15% of base and eligibility for the equity plan. Senior Software Engineer - Backend/Infrastructure CA$107,000–161,000 base, plus a 10% discretionary bonus and equity eligibility. Both ranges are stated in the postings themselves under BC pay-transparency requirements. Benefits listed: health/dental/vision, life insurance, critical illness, AD&D, health care spending account, EAP, paid sick/personal/parental leave, paid holidays and wellness days, employee stock purchase plan.

Notable / other: The postings carry an unusual explicit disclosure that "AI will not be used to screen, assess or select applicants," and confirm "This job posting relates to an existing vacancy" — i.e. not a pipeline-building ghost req. Public engineering blog and GitHub orgs are active.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer (BC/ON remote); Senior Software Engineer - Backend/Infrastructure (Canada); Senior Site Reliability Engineer; Senior SRE - Database Services; Principal Software Engineer.
