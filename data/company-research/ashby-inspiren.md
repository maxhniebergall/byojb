# Inspiren — research
Provider/key: ashby:inspiren | company_type: product

What they do: Inspiren sells "a complete and connected technology ecosystem for senior living" — a hardware-plus-software platform for senior-living operators. In their words the "integrated solutions seamlessly fit into existing workflows, capturing everything happening within a community", giving operators "greater visibility into what is happening across their communities." The product combines in-room sensing hardware with a data platform and analytics, "supported by nurse specialists", so operators can "make informed clinical and operational decisions that improve efficiency, outcomes, and quality of care." Customers are senior-living / assisted-living operators; the engineering surface includes real-time device telemetry ingestion and bidirectional EHR (Electronic Health Record) integrations.

How they describe themselves: Founded by Michael Wang, "a former Green Beret and cardiothoracic nurse", and "built on the belief that compassionate care and technology can and should coexist" — Inspiren "proves that compassionate care and technology can coexist - bringing peace of mind to residents, families, and staff."
