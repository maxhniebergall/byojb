# Nubank (Nu) — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:nubank | company_type: product

What they do: Nu Holdings ("Nubank") is a digital bank / fintech, the largest neobank in Latin America — credit cards, deposit accounts, personal loans, investments, insurance and crypto, serving well over 100M customers across Brazil, Mexico and Colombia. It is publicly listed (NYSE: NU) and profitable, which puts it firmly outside the runway-dependent startup category. It has been standing up international engineering hubs, including one in Toronto, to build products that generalize across markets.

How they describe themselves: The stated mission is to "fight complexity and empower people" — the company frames technology as core rather than supporting, combining "efficiency, scalability, and impact." Their Toronto announcement says they chose the city for "a mature, diverse, and multicultural market that values autonomy, collaboration, and purpose — values that align with Nubank's culture." Engineering messaging emphasizes state-of-the-art software practice, autonomy and ownership rather than hustle language.

Size / stage / funding: 9,000+ employees from 50+ nationalities; public company (NYSE: NU), profitable. Not funding-driven.

Locations / HQ: HQ São Paulo, Brazil. Hubs/entities in Brazil (São Paulo, Campinas, Belo Horizonte, Rio), Mexico, Colombia, Argentina (Buenos Aires), the United States (Durham, Miami, Palo Alto, Washington DC), Germany, and Canada (Toronto).

Remote policy: Nubank states it "operates in a hybrid model, where teams collaborate remotely and periodically come together for about one week of in-person sessions." Their careers content describes a "hybrid structure across all locations." Critically, their own Canadian JDs state: "For Canadian team members, these sessions typically take place in one of our hubs (Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, or the United States) and are communicated well in advance to allow proper planning, with travel support provided."

Remote-Canada eligibility: Partially verified. The day-to-day model for Canadian staff is remote work plus periodic (~1 week) travel to a foreign hub, which is compatible with living outside Toronto. However every Canadian posting is labelled "Canada, Toronto" and no source states that a candidate elsewhere in Canada (e.g. British Columbia) is eligible, nor whether Mountain-Time hours are workable against a Brazil-centric (UTC-3) organisation. Treat as UNCLEAR leaning workable-if-they-accept-BC; the timezone gap to São Paulo is 4-5 hours, which is a real working-hours concern.

Engineering & tech: Cloud-native distributed architecture on AWS; Clojure for backend services (chosen for immutable concurrency and maintainability); Python for AI/agentic systems; 3,000+ microservices across 85 Kubernetes clusters; Datomic as the transactional database. Public engineering blog at building.nubank.com. Current Toronto-facing work: scaling systems for tens of millions of users, multi-market/multi-language product design, and native AI banking experiences.

Notable / other: Acquired Cognitect (the Clojure/Datomic company) in 2020, which is why the stack is unusual for a bank. Heavy current investment in AI agents and orchestration, reflected in open roles for AI Core / agentic orchestration.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (Canada, Toronto); Staff Software Engineer — Digital Experience Platform; Staff Software Engineer | Credit Cards; Staff Software Engineer — Insurance; Staff Software Engineer | Crypto; Lead Software Engineer, AI Agents & Orchestration; Lead ML Data Engineer, AI Core.
