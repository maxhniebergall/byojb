# Talkiatry — research
Provider/key: ashby:talkiatry | company_type: product
What they do: Talkiatry is a technology-enabled psychiatry practice delivering in-network telepsychiatry in the United States — treating ADHD, anxiety, depression, bipolar disorder, PTSD, OCD and insomnia through psychiatrists, PMHNPs and therapists on its own platform. In their words: "Talkiatry is transforming mental health care by making high-quality psychiatry more accessible, human, and sustainable for both patients and clinicians. Co-founded by a patient and a triple-board-certified psychiatrist, we were built to solve one of healthcare's most urgent challenges."
How they describe themselves: Mission framing is care-and-access first: "millions of people struggle to access mental health care, while clinicians face growing administrative burden and burnout. Through our technology-powered, in-network platform, we're changing that." Culture claims from their own JD: "Top-notch team: we're a diverse, experienced group motivated to make a difference in mental health care"; "Collaborative environment: be part of building something from the ground up at a fast-paced startup"; "It all comes back to care: we're a mental health company, and we put our team's well-being first." Careers page: "great care starts with great people, in every role."
Size / stage / funding: Scale is stated in their own JD: 750+ psychiatrists, 250+ therapists, more than two million patient visits delivered nationwide; "the largest dedicated psychiatry practice in the U.S." They still self-describe as a "fast-paced startup" and say they are "growing quickly." Funding round and clinical-vs-engineering headcount split are not disclosed on any page reached.
Locations / HQ: New York-centred (recognised in "Fortune Best Workplaces in New York 2025"); a single HQ is not explicitly stated. Service delivery is nationwide across the US.
Remote policy: Engineering roles are posted as "Remote". No written remote-work policy was found on the careers page.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO. The Site Reliability Engineer JD states "Talkiatry participates in E-Verify and will provide the federal government with your Form I-9 information to confirm that you are authorized to work in the U.S." — a US I-9/E-Verify requirement means US work authorisation is mandatory. Benefits listed are US-only (401K with match, US medical/dental/vision effective day 1). "Remote" here means remote-within-the-US. Not reachable from British Columbia.
Engineering & tech: TypeScript/Node.js and React, AWS EKS and RDS, Terraform for infrastructure-as-code, Python or TypeScript for tooling, and observability via Datadog/Prometheus/Grafana. Engineering is organised as six product teams plus a central DevOps team. Product teams retain their own on-call — the incoming SRE is explicitly "not the pager" and works as a force multiplier establishing SLOs/SLIs, error budgets, observability, production-readiness reviews and toil reduction. HIPAA/regulated-environment experience is a nice-to-have.
Notable / other: The SRE role is a founding one, measured on two concrete outcomes: reducing production outage frequency, and increasing the share of incidents caught by monitoring before stakeholders report them. Awards: Great Place to Work Certification, Forbes America's Best Startup Employers, Fortune Best Workplaces in New York 2025. Benefits: medical/dental/vision day 1, 401K with match, generous PTO plus paid holidays, paid parental leave, a Learning team for skill development.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Site Reliability Engineer [Remote US, $160K–$185K]; Senior AI Engineer [Remote US, $170K–$195K]; Data Engineer [Remote US, $130K–$145K].
