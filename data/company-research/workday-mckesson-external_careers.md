# McKesson — research
Provider/key: workday:mckesson/external_careers | company_type: product
What they do: McKesson is a pharmaceutical and medical-supply distributor and healthcare-services company — in its own words "the logistical and operational backbone of the U.S. healthcare system." It distributes pharmaceuticals and medical supplies and runs oncology, pharmacy and biopharma solutions businesses across North America: 344+ US distribution facilities, ~40,000 deliveries a day to care sites, support for ~3,300 independent oncology providers across 29 states, and patient medication access "over 100 million times annually." McKesson Canada is "a leading diversified healthcare provider in the country," with heritage "back to the early 1900s," spanning pharmaceutical distribution, specialty health, pharmacy services and automation.
How they describe themselves: "An impact-driven organization that improves care in every setting – one product, one partner, one patient at a time," with the stated purpose "advancing health outcomes for all." The Canadian site names the **I²CARE and ILEAD principles** as its guiding values (the individual principles are not spelled out on the page read). Corporate-citizenship commitments cover "employees, our communities, our partners and the environment."
Size / stage / funding: Publicly traded (investor.mckesson.com), Fortune-scale, decades established, not funding-dependent. Exact headcount/revenue not stated on the pages read.
Locations / HQ: HQ in Las Colinas / Irving, Texas. Large US footprint (Columbus OH, Atlanta GA, Richmond VA, Irving TX appear in live reqs), a substantial India engineering centre in **Bangalore**, a UK/London presence, and Canadian operations with engineering roles in **Mississauga, Ontario**.
Remote policy: Hybrid, office-anchored. A US DevOps req states plainly: "This role is a hybrid position. The selected candidate is expected to work on-site at our Las Colinas office a minimum of two (2) days per week, with the remaining days worked remotely." A Mississauga data req lists "Working Conditions: Environment – Traditional office environment." Registry facets across the Canadian reqs read hybrid/onsite/unclear. One req (Senior Software Engineer, AI Platform, "5 Locations") is facet-tagged remote.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Effectively no for a BC-based candidate.** Every Canadian requisition is anchored to CAN, ON, Mississauga with hybrid or traditional-office working conditions; none is posted as Canada-remote. Nothing found offers a remote arrangement from British Columbia / Mountain Time. Unverified whether exceptions exist.
Engineering & tech: Broad enterprise engineering — cloud/platform (Sr. Cloud Engineer, Lead Network and Cloud Architect), CI/CD and DevOps platform engineering, data engineering (data governance, "data literacy and data democratization across enterprise"), identity & access management, data-platform security architecture, and a growing agentic-AI group ("Senior Software Engineer, AI Platform", "Agentic AI Specialist", "Digital Agent Technology Specialist" — the Workday slugs show several of these reqs were retitled). Much of the senior IC volume sits in Bangalore.
Notable / other: Compensation is unusually well disclosed: nearly every req carries an explicit "Our Base Pay Range for this position" block plus the boilerplate that pay is set by "performance, experience and skills, equity, regular job market evaluations, and geographical markets," with annual bonus and long-term incentive on top. Canadian ranges are CAD, US ranges USD. 17 live relevant reqs at scan time — high volume, so re-checking periodically is cheap.
Open relevant roles (sample): Sr. Cloud Engineer (Mississauga); Lead, Data Engineer (Mississauga); Lead Network and Cloud Architect (Mississauga); Senior Agentic AI Specialist (Mississauga); Senior / Lead Data Engineer (Mississauga + 1); Senior Software Engineer, AI Platform (5 locations, US); DevOps Engineer (Irving TX, hybrid).
