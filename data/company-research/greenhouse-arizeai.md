# Arize AI — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:arizeai | company_type: product
What they do: AI/agent observability and evaluation platform — monitoring, tracing, troubleshooting and evaluating ML and LLM systems in production. Commercial product is "Arize AX" (build/test/run in one workspace); they also steward a widely used open-source project (Phoenix) for LLM tracing and evals. Customers cited on their own JDs: Booking.com, Uber, Siemens, PepsiCo; "150+ leading enterprises and Fortune 500 companies".
How they describe themselves: Mission stated verbatim as "to make the world's AI work—and work for people." They call themselves "the leading AI & Agent Engineering observability and evaluation platform". Their about page lists three core values verbatim: **"Do > Say"** (execution over discussion), **"N+1"** (continuous improvement), **"Bring Solutions"** (ownership-focused problem solving). The OSS JD says outright they want someone who "thrives in a fast-paced environment where 'everyone wears multiple hats'". They highlight responsible-AI/bias work, culturally conscious events, and a "Lady Arizers" employee subgroup.
Size / stage / funding: **Series C**, "over $135M in funding" per their JDs; the $70M Series C is announced on their own blog. Investors listed on their site: Battery Ventures, Foundation Capital, TCV, Adams Street Partners, Microsoft Venture (M12), Sinewave Ventures, OMERS, Datadog. About page shows 200+ people. Venture-funded and growth-stage, not profitable-by-design.
Locations / HQ: Offices in Berkeley/SF Bay Area and New York City. Team pages also reference EMEA and APJ regional staff.
Remote policy: "remote-first company" with the two offices as an option for those who live near them; WFH monthly stipend for a co-working space otherwise.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No evidence of it, and the postings argue against it.** The two relevant engineering roles are both posted as "Remote (United States)". Their other DevOps roles are country-scoped to Argentina, South Korea, Malaysia and Singapore — i.e. they open a role per country where they have coverage, and Canada is not among them. Treat as not Canada-eligible unless they say otherwise.
Engineering & tech: Go is the primary language for the distributed backend, plus Python, Java and TypeScript services. They build high-volume analytics systems, their own OLAP database architecture, real-time evaluation infrastructure ("millions of annotations per second"), distributed stream processing (Kafka/Gazette), Prometheus-style observability, and dimensionality-reduction/visualization algorithms in a distributed environment. Strong public open-source presence and public docs; they contribute back to open-source OLAP and message-queue projects. Clouds: AWS/GCP/Azure + Kubernetes.
Notable / other: 37 open roles at scan time — a fast-hiring posture. Benefits: professional-development stipend, open vacation, sabbatical leave, 100% parental-leave coverage, wellness programs, 401(k), medical/dental/vision. Press: TechCrunch (Feb 2025) framing them as a first mover in AI observability.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior AI Product Engineer, Backend (Remote US); Open Source AI Engineer (TypeScript) (Remote US); DevSecOps Engineer (TypeScript & Agentic AI); Senior DevOps Engineer, APJ (South Korea / Malaysia / Singapore); DevOps Support Engineer (Argentina).
Pay: posted on their own US JDs — Senior AI Product Engineer Backend $125,000-$225,000 + equity; Open Source AI Engineer (TypeScript) $185,000-$200,000 + equity. Both are unusually wide/US-national ranges.
