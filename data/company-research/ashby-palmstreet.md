# Palmstreet — research
Provider/key: ashby:palmstreet | company_type: product

What they do: Palmstreet runs a live-shopping (livestream social commerce) marketplace — in their words "the #1 live shopping platform for rare plants, handmade crafts, & unique goods". Buyers watch daily live shows from independent sellers and bid/buy in real time. Founded 2020 by Instagram and Apple veterans, it began as a plant-identification app and pivoted into live commerce starting with plant sellers, then expanded into reptiles, vintage decor, fashion and beauty. Consumer marketplace, take-rate/GMV business model; they cite over 2 million collectors and sellers.

How they describe themselves: Vision, verbatim: "to become the 'World's Online Main Street,' bringing the charm of in-person shopping to engaging, social online experiences." The about page frames the mission as celebrating "uniqueness, connections, and bring[ing] the warmth of Main Street to the digital world, making every purchase meaningful", and describes the brand around connection, growth and community — "community-obsessed" is their own recurring adjective. No formally enumerated value list is published. The recruiting tone is high-energy startup: "Fueled by a passionate, plant-loving community", "we've grown our GMV by 500x in the last 18 months", "Be part of a dynamic team revolutionizing the online shopping experience", "You'll take on complex problems that others haven't yet been able to solve", and "Thrive in a flat, startup environment: Take full ownership of your projects".

Size / stage / funding: Private VC-backed startup, founded 2020, "powered by a nimble, empathetic team". Backed by "top Silicon Valley VCs" — named on their about page as a16z, Craft Ventures and Headline. No disclosed headcount, round size or profitability. Growth-stage and runway-dependent by every available signal.

Locations / HQ: US-headquartered (LA-area roles appear in third-party listings); genuinely distributed. They state they have "a truly global team."

Remote policy: Remote-friendly with country-typed requisitions. About page: "you have the flexibility to live and work anywhere." No async/handbook or documented working-hours policy found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** Their live backend req is titled "Senior+ Backend Software Engineer - Canada", location "Canada", and the JD states under *Your Location*: "Remote - Anywhere in the Canada". No metro attached and no stated timezone band, so a BC-based candidate is eligible. Caveat: the role also lists "Bilingual proficiency in English and Mandarin" under Qualifications — a stated requirement, not a nice-to-have, which gates this particular req independent of geography.

Engineering & tech: Node.js and TypeScript backend services and APIs powering web and mobile; Kubernetes preferred; payment-system experience preferred. Domain problems named in the JD are genuinely infrastructural for a consumer product: "instant bidding and live interactions", "scalable architecture to handle millions of users", "live streaming and checkout processes", and "a reliable deployment pipeline". Senior scope is described as "Establish self as an owner of a particular component, feature, or system with expert end-to-end understanding" while working "closely with PM, design, and infrastructure teams". Asks BS+ in CS and 5+ years building "highly scalable and reliable infrastructure". No public engineering blog found.

Notable / other: Pay is posted openly and is high for the Canadian market: "Pay Range: $200,000 - $250,000 CAD", plus "Equity grants will also be provided as part of the total compensation package." They comply with the Employment Equity Act for Canadian roles. Careers hub at palmstreet.app/careers; they warn that application volume may prevent individual responses.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior+ Backend Software Engineer - Canada; Senior Backend Software Engineer.
