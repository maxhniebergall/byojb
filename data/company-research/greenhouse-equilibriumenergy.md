# Equilibrium Energy — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:equilibriumenergy | company_type: product

What they do: Equilibrium Energy ("EQ") describes itself as "a team of technologists, power market experts, and AI pioneers reimagining how the world's most critical industry operates. We're building a first-of-its-kind **AI operating system for the power sector**, uniting cutting-edge science with real-world purpose to enable a cleaner, more resilient energy future." Concretely it operates at the intersection of AI, power systems and modern software — optimizing battery energy storage and renewables across the grid, and running energy trading, forecasting and portfolio management. Its flagship product, **EQ Mission Control**, delivers real-time enterprise insights, granular forecasting, decision support and AI copilots trained on proprietary data, applied to battery storage and renewables optimization in markets like ERCOT and CAISO.

How they describe themselves: "Equilibrium is composed of deeply knowledgeable industry experts across all our functions, with decades of experience in energy-specific commercial structuring, power systems engineering, machine learning, computational research, operations research, distributed and compute-intensive infrastructure, and modern software & ML engineering. Our experience in the space means we've previously built versions of nearly every technical component of our platform. We are now designing them better, and combining them in a holistic and novel way, to achieve global scale and climate impact. We pride ourselves on our deeply empathetic & collaborative culture, **honest and direct but respectful communication**, and our **balanced, flexible, and remote-first work environment**." They also describe "a tight-knit group of brilliant, curious, and adventurous people." Recruiting language calls for "collaborative, talented, passionate and resourceful folks."

Size / stage / funding: **Series B**, self-described as "well-funded… backed by some of the most prominent institutional investors in climate." ~$100M raised in total: a $39M Series B led by DCVC with Breakthrough Energy Ventures and Valo Ventures, followed by a **$28M Series B2 led by GS Energy with participation from NRG Energy** (announced March 2025). Strategic energy-industry investors (GS Energy, NRG) alongside climate VCs.

Locations / HQ: US-centred, with San Francisco Bay Area and New York City as the stated preferred hubs. Team works internationally across timezones.

Remote policy: **Remote-first**, in their own words — "balanced, flexible, and remote-first work environment", with JDs asking for "strong communication skills for collaborating within a remote-first team that works internationally across timezones" and describing collaborating "asynchronously with engineers, researchers and product managers across time zones." Async collaboration is named explicitly, which is a good sign.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Partially — one of three relevant roles, and only that one.** The postings differ and must not be treated uniformly:
- *Software Engineer – Data Engineering (Staff/Sr Staff)*: "San Francisco or NYC preferred, **Remote – US, Canada, and Europe**" → **Canada-eligible**, remote, with US hubs merely "preferred" not required.
- *Software Engineer – ML Platform (Staff/Sr Staff)*: "San Francisco or **US Remote**" → **not Canada-eligible**.
- *Software Engineer (Staff/Sr Staff)*: "SF Bay Area or NYC preferred, or Remote" → ambiguous; remote is open-ended but the eligible geography is unstated, so Canadian eligibility is unconfirmed.
There is no evidence of a BC-specific restriction on the data engineering role, and remote-first plus international timezones makes Mountain Time workable. Benefits language mentions a **401k**, i.e. a US-centric package — worth confirming what the Canadian employment arrangement actually is (direct entity, EOR, or contractor).

Engineering & tech: Modern, substantial data and ML platform work. The Data Engineering role covers long-term data architecture, "scalable ETL/ELT pipelines in **Python, SQL, and dbt** — ingesting data via APIs, web scraping, and streaming sources", orchestration with **Temporal and Dagster**, data modelling and schemas for a **Databricks** cloud warehouse and relational databases, contributing to an **ML feature store**, cost/performance optimization, and mentoring. The company also cites "distributed and compute-intensive infrastructure" and operations research as in-house competencies.

Notable / other: Real-time decision-making for energy trading means correctness and latency genuinely matter — a mission-critical, physically-grounded domain rather than a hype AI wrapper. The "AI copilots" element is one feature of a much larger forecasting/optimization platform.

Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineer – Data Engineering (Staff/Sr Staff) — Remote US/Canada/Europe; Software Engineer – ML Platform (Staff/Sr Staff) — US only; Software Engineer (Staff/Sr Staff).
