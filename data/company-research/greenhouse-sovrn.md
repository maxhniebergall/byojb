# Sovrn — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:sovrn | company_type: product

What they do: Sovrn is a software and data business serving Open Web publishers — adtech. In their
words: "Sovrn is a Software and Data business that helps Open Web businesses be and remain
independent. We help them understand their business better, operate more efficiently, and make & keep
more money." Product surface spans software products and data solutions for publishers, including a
"Data Collective". The engineering domain is programmatic advertising infrastructure: SSP/DSP/exchange
/ad-server systems, OpenRTB bid request-response flows, auction mechanics, supply path optimisation.

How they describe themselves: Their stated beliefs, verbatim: "We believe in the freedom and free-flow
of information. We believe the Open Web is the largest source of this information. We believe in
helping Open Web businesses be and remain Independent." Customer promise: help customers "Understand
their business better", "Operate their business more efficiently", "Make (and Keep) more money, so
they control their own destiny."
Core values, verbatim and complete: **Candid, Customer Empathy, Learning, Scrappy, Second Order
Thinking.**
On hiring: "We understand that no candidate is perfectly qualified for any job... Even more important
than your resume is a clear demonstration of accountability and the ability to thrive in a fluid and
collaborative environment."

Size / stage / funding: Not stated in available material. Established private adtech company; no
headcount, funding stage or profitability figure was verifiable this session. UNVERIFIED.

Locations / HQ: "Sovrn offices are located in Boulder, CO and New York, NY."

Remote policy: Office-default hybrid, stated verbatim: "We believe collaboration and shared purpose
lead to deeper relationships, more creative problem-solving, and more learning, faster. For employees
located near one of our offices, working in-person is our default. We trust people to use personal
agency when focused work or life circumstances call for flexibility." Fully-remote hiring is an
exception, not the norm: "For exceptional candidates we would consider remote locations in these
states: AZ, CA, CO, GA, MD, MA, MI, MO, NJ, NY, NC, OK, TX, UT, WA." The postings carry both
#LI-Hybrid and #LI-Remote tags.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **NO — verified negative.** Remote hiring is restricted to an enumerated
list of 15 US states. Canada is not among them and no country outside the US is contemplated. A
Canada-resident candidate is not eligible.

Engineering & tech: Stack named on the Principal Data Engineer role: Python, PySpark, Kafka,
Databricks, AWS; also Snowflake as a "second-layer big data platform". The role is genuinely senior
data-platform architecture: own the design and evolution of data platform systems "at exchange scale
— high throughput, real-time streaming, and always-on batch pipelines"; lead architecture across
pipeline design, data modelling, lakehouse architecture and data-services layers; define pipeline
observability at massive scale; drive technical standards and design reviews; mentor engineers.
A second, substantial half of the role is AI/agentic engineering leadership: "establish and champion
AI engineering practices across the team, from prompt engineering and RAG patterns to agentic
workflow design, LLM evaluation, and progressive implementation of agentic design patterns";
identify AI leverage in the data stack (intelligent pipeline optimisation, anomaly detection,
automated data quality, forecasting, LLM-powered data services); "set standards for how we evaluate,
trust, and operate AI-powered systems in production, including observability, fallback behavior, and
model governance". They state they already "use LLMs and agentic tooling across our data stack" and
want to move "from general adoption to intentional practice".
Bar: 10+ years software engineering, and **5+ years specifically in adtech data infrastructure**
(SSP, DSP, exchange or ad server), plus deep OpenRTB/programmatic fluency; data security and
compliance (PII, CCPA, GDPR).

Notable / other: Compensation is posted openly — base $200,000–$240,000 for both Principal roles,
plus bonus, equity and benefits. Benefits: stock options, medical/dental/vision, short and long-term
disability, life insurance, 11 paid holidays, flexible vacation, commuter benefits, 401(k) with match,
paid parental leave. The Principal Software Engineer posting states "Applications will be accepted
until July 1, 2026" — i.e. the listing is past its own stated close date as of this research, and the
registry last saw this posting on 2026-06-26. Note also that the "Principal Data Engineer" posting's
body opens by calling the role "Principal Software Engineer (Data)" — the two Principal listings are
close variants of one another. Sovrn does not accept agency resumes.
