# Teladoc Health — research
Provider/key: workday:teladoc/teladochealth_is_hiring | company_type: product

What they do: Teladoc Health describes itself in its own JDs as "the global virtual care leader, offering comprehensive virtual care solutions spanning virtual care including primary, mental health, expert medical, devices and licensed platform services." Customers are insurers, employers and health systems rather than individual consumers — a B2B2C model. Their own stated scale: serving "over 9 million Canadians", "more than 20 years" of Canadian operations, and operations in 175 countries worldwide. Domain is healthcare delivery and health data — regulated, mission-critical, and about as far from hype-cycle as software gets.

How they describe themselves: Mission framing is "transforming how better health happens" and "helping millions of people live their healthiest lives." Their JD boilerplate states: "At Teladoc Health, you are empowered to bring your true self to work"; "Here you will be part of a high-performance culture where colleagues embrace challenges, drive transformative solutions, and create opportunities for growth"; "Chart your career path with meaningful opportunities that empower you to grow, lead, and make a difference"; "Join a multi-faceted community that celebrates each colleague's unique perspective and is focused on continually improving, each and every day"; "Contribute to an innovative culture." Tone is large-enterprise HR boilerplate — "high-performance culture" and "fast-paced environment" appear, but not startup hustle language. Notably they also publish an anti-fraud clause: "As part of our hiring process, we verify identity and credentials, conduct interviews (live or video), and screen for fraud or misrepresentation."

Size / stage / funding: Large, mature, publicly traded (NYSE: TDOC). Multinational — 175 countries, 20+ years in Canada. This is an established public company rather than a funding-dependent startup; exact current headcount not verified from the pages available here.

Locations / HQ: US-headquartered multinational with a long-standing Canadian business. The ATS shows distinct country-scoped remote requisitions: "CAN - Any Location (Remote)", "ARG - Any Location (Remote)", "IND - Any Location (Remote)".

Remote policy: Remote-friendly and explicitly country-scoped. Requisitions are posted per-country as "Any Location (Remote)" and JDs carry the `#LI-Remote` tag. Roles are open to a whole country rather than tied to an office, but each requisition is restricted to its named country.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes — verified.** The live Data Engineer requisition (JR20936) is posted as "CAN - Any Location (Remote)", i.e. remote anywhere in Canada, which includes British Columbia and Mountain Time. This is the strongest Canada-remote evidence in this batch. **Caveat:** it applies to the Canadian requisition only. The DevOps Engineer III (JR20710) and Site Reliability Engineer (JR20931) are "ARG - Any Location (Remote)" — Argentina-only — and the Software Engineer, EPM Developer (JR18141-2) is "IND - Any Location (Remote)" — India-only. Those three are not open to a Canadian applicant.

Engineering & tech: Mixed and enterprise-flavoured rather than product-engineering-flavoured.
- *Data Engineer (Canada)* — batch, incremental and file-based pipelines supporting analytics, reporting and "AI-enabled initiatives" across Teladoc Health Canada. Heavy emphasis on data migration from legacy systems to modern platforms with reconciliation; enterprise ETL tooling, **Talend** specifically named (maintaining existing Talend jobs and migrating/re-implementing them elsewhere); establishing dev/test/prod separation, access controls, naming standards, source control, deployment and monitoring; ingestion of unstructured and semi-structured data (documents, PDFs, text extracts, metadata) alongside relational; supporting and optimising **Power BI** data models; curating "analytics- and AI-ready datasets" for AI & Automation Specialists; data quality checks, validation, reconciliation and monitoring; source-to-target mappings, data definitions and lineage documentation; working with data governance and security on privacy and regulatory compliance. Requires a bachelor's degree, 4+ years in data engineering / analytics engineering / BI / data platform roles, 4+ years with data integration and ETL. Explicitly *not* a supervisory role.
- *DevOps Engineer III (Argentina)* — IaC, automated environment provisioning, automated deployment and monitoring; CI enablement paired with dev and QA; **"Participate in a 24/7 on-call rotation"**; automation to reduce toil; SLO/SLA work with cross-functional business teams; documentation of operational practices. Requires 5+ years DevOps, containers and Kubernetes, cloud environments, coding/automation scripting, alerting-and-metrics systems (New Relic, Zenoss, Sensu, Nagios, Graphite, Collectd, Ganglia, Datadog), CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins, Bamboo CI, Docker), Git/SVN/Jira. Preferred: **Microsoft Azure**.

Compensation: The Canadian Data Engineer requisition posts a range and it is already on record — CAD $110,000–$140,000 base (see data/company-comp.jsonl, `data_eng`/`mid-senior`, derivation `direct`, sourced from the JD itself).

Notable / other: The Canadian data role's centre of gravity is BI/reporting and legacy-migration work (Talend, Power BI) rather than distributed-systems or ML platform engineering — "AI-enabled" here means preparing datasets for other people's automation, not building LLM infrastructure. The Argentina DevOps role carries an explicit 24/7 on-call rotation. Entity confirmed from JD text (virtual care, 9 million Canadians) against the registry record.
