# Hasbro — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:hasbro | company_type: product

What they do: Hasbro is a large, publicly traded (NASDAQ: HAS — background knowledge, not verified here) toy and games company. Every engineering posting in this batch is actually for Wizards of the Coast, its games subsidiary, tagged "#Wizards" in the JD body. Wizards publishes Magic: The Gathering and Dungeons & Dragons and runs AAA digital game studios; the engineering roles support that — cloud platform, build engineering, data platform, FinOps and applied AI.

How they describe themselves: The Hasbro banner text is heavy on play metaphors: "We take play seriously. We're looking for curious adventurers ready to find their party, fueled by imagination and drive to build what's never been built before... This is your chance to shape legendary play that lasts a lifetime." Wizards' own framing: "At Wizards of the Coast, we harness the power of imagination and connection to create unforgettable experiences... In every pursuit our mission is to inspire a lifetime love of games. Whether it's through the strategic depth of Magic: The Gathering, the rich storytelling of Dungeons & Dragons, or our AAA digital game studios, we build worlds that bring people together." On inclusion: "At Wizards, we believe in the magic of diversity—great ideas come from unique perspectives. We are committed to building an inclusive, welcoming workplace where everyone belongs." The tone is themed and enthusiastic but not hustle-coded — no "fast-paced", "wear many hats" or "rocketship" language appears.

Size / stage / funding: Large, established, publicly traded multinational. Not runway-dependent. Hasbro has had well-publicised restructuring and layoffs in recent years including at Wizards — worth noting as a stability caveat, though it was not verified from a source fetched here.

Locations / HQ: Hasbro HQ Pawtucket, Rhode Island; Wizards of the Coast is Renton, Washington. Roles in this batch sit in Renton WA, Boston MA, and one in Montréal, Quebec.

Remote policy: Not stated on any posting read. Every role carries a specific city location (Renton, Boston, Montréal) with no remote designation, no "#LI-REMOTE" tag and no remote-work language — the working assumption is office-based or hybrid at the named site.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO, effectively. There is exactly one Canadian posting — Sr Cloud Platform Engineer, Montréal, Quebec — and it names Montréal as the location with no remote or distributed-work language anywhere in the JD. Montréal is Eastern time and a four-hour flight from Kimberley; a Montréal-anchored role is not workable from British Columbia. Every other role is Renton WA or Boston MA. No Canada-wide-remote posting exists here.

Engineering & tech: The Montréal Sr Cloud Platform Engineer sits on the "Central Technology Build Engineering team as we develop and curate an Unreal Engine ecosystem to share across our internal game studios" — "a specialized team responsible for unblocking developers, ensuring build stability, and optimizing iteration workflows across multiple concurrent titles... design, build, and run scalable AWS architectures that serve as the backbone for deploying our Unreal Engine build systems and tools." Stack: AWS, infrastructure-as-code (AWS CDK, CloudFormation or Terraform), gitops, Perforce administration, C#/Golang/TypeScript plus Bash/Python/JavaScript, Unreal Engine. Other roles cover a Data Platform, Cloud FinOps (cost allocation, tagging, chargeback/showback), Applied AI, and two Principal Software Engineer tracks including a consumer-focused platform.

Notable / other: Pay is disclosed on US postings under Washington/Massachusetts transparency law, and the ranges are strong for a non-tech-sector employer. Observed: Applied AI Engineer $109,200-$163,800; Cloud FinOps Platform Engineer $124,800-$155,100; Data Platform Engineer $108,300-$162,500; Principal Software Engineer and Principal Software Engineer, Consumer-Focused Platform both $172,600-$259,000; Principal Platform Engineer, AI $205,100-$307,600. The Montréal posting states no range (Quebec has no equivalent disclosure law).

Open relevant roles (sample): Sr Cloud Platform Engineer (Montréal); Principal Platform Engineer, AI (Renton); Principal Software Engineer (Renton); Data Platform Engineer (Boston); Cloud FinOps Platform Engineer (Renton); Applied AI Engineer (Boston).

Sources: JD bodies for the seven live postings under https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/hasbro (entity confirmed as Hasbro/Wizards of the Coast from the "#Wizards", Magic: The Gathering and Dungeons & Dragons references in the JD text).
