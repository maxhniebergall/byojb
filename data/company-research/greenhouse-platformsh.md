# Platform.sh / Upsun — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:platformsh | company_type: product

What they do: Platform.sh (now doing business as **Upsun**) is a cloud application PaaS — managed infrastructure for building, deploying and scaling web applications without teams having to run the backend themselves. Its pitch for Upsun: "the cloud application platform humans and robots love… built for today's hybrid teams, where AI agents write and test code and humans focus on solving the problems that really matter." Customers are developers, DevOps engineers and platform teams. The product IS infrastructure — this is a developer-tools/PaaS company, not a product-growth SaaS.

How they describe themselves: Core belief is that software should power brighter solutions and greater innovation, with a continued commitment to enterprise reliability, customer trust, and ESG leadership. Stated values: **make a positive impact, aim for the stars, care for each other**. On remote: "At Upsun, remote work isn't just a trend — it's our foundation. The freedom of remote work with the support of a diverse, global team has been our successful model for over a decade." Also: "Upsunners are a remote, global workforce, and we thrive in a multicultural team. We are committed to open source and an open, welcoming environment. Our team spans the globe and the experience spectrum."

Size / stage / funding: ~290 employees ("Upsunners") across 40+ nationalities. Private. Certified **B Corporation**, ESG-led. Over a decade old (Platform.sh founded ~2014), rebranded to Upsun.

Locations / HQ: Dual HQ Paris, France and San Francisco, USA. Team members in 30+ countries. Healthcare coverage offered in UK, Canada, France, Spain, USA — indicating established employing entities in those countries.

Remote policy: **100% remote**, global, for over a decade. Recognized by Forbes' Top 30 Companies for Remote Jobs and France's Best Workplaces for Women. Has a remote-work travel program. Multicultural/distributed by design, which implies async working norms.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes — and Canada is the explicit target geography for this role.** The live Senior Software Engineer JD states they are "currently focused on hiring for this role in **Canada**," and that no visa sponsorship is available (i.e. they want someone already authorized to work in Canada). Canada is one of the five countries where they provide healthcare coverage. No office-attendance requirement anywhere; BC/Mountain Time is workable given the 30+ country spread.

Engineering & tech: PaaS/cloud infrastructure — container orchestration, build and deploy pipelines, multi-language runtime support, managed services. Strong open-source commitment. The Upsun positioning adds AI-agent-facing developer workflows (agents writing and testing code against the platform). Runs a public Developer Center (devcenter.upsun.com) with engineering content, including material on remote work practice.

Notable / other: Certified B Corp is an unusual and meaningful stability/ethics signal for a PaaS. Rebrand from Platform.sh to Upsun is a repositioning toward AI-era developer workflows rather than a distress signal. Award-winning workplace recognitions listed on their own JD.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (Remote • Canada).
