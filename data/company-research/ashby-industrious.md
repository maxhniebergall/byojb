# Industrious — research
Provider/key: ashby:industrious | company_type: product
What they do: Industrious is a premium coworking / "workplace-as-a-service" operator — flexible office space and workplace experience for teams of all sizes. Their words: "Industrious is the largest premium workplace-as-a-service provider and home to the highest-rated workplaces in the industry. Everything we do comes down to creating great days for teams of all sizes and stages, including our own."
How they describe themselves: "We believe that what makes a great day at work is the people on your team and the problems you get to solve together. We're looking for people who love thinking outside the box and thrive in a truly collaborative setting. As teammates, we encourage new ideas and toast every win." They put weight on inclusion: "Great days also start when everyone can be their authentic self at work... Industrious is committed to creating an inclusive, respectful environment that embraces your individuality and quirkiness. You are valued for who YOU are." Internally they use the hashtag #SoIndustrious and describe the team as "fun, low-ego, high-talent".
Size / stage / funding: Self-described as "at a once in a lifetime inflection point, transitioning from being a late stage growth startup to an independent business unit of the world's largest real estate company" — i.e. a late-stage startup now owned by a major real-estate group (CBRE, per public reporting; not verified from their own pages here). That is a stability positive relative to a standalone startup.
Locations / HQ: US-centric coworking operator with locations across many cities; the engineering role in the registry is based in Montreal, Canada.
Remote policy: **Hybrid, stated explicitly in the JD: "This role is based in Montreal, Canada and is a hybrid role."**
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No.** The single relevant posting requires Montreal hybrid presence. This is disqualifying for a Kimberley-BC-based candidate who cannot relocate — and Montreal is Eastern Time, compounding the mismatch.
Engineering & tech: The Tech Lead, DevOps Engineer role owns the deployment ecosystem: AWS (GCP a bonus), Terraform and Terragrunt ("an IaC purist"), CI/CD via GitHub Actions, distributed systems and event-driven architectures with Kafka/RabbitMQ, Python and TypeScript/Node.js, application monitoring and security posture. Framing is unusual: they want to "architect the 'Vibe Coding' ecosystem" — internal platform and AI-leveraged automation "that allows our employees to create applications... at the speed of thought" — plus building "an AI-native data platform". 7+ years DevOps with 2+ years leading teams; mentoring and "a culture of radical transparency, continuous learning, and high-performance 'vibes'".
Notable / other: Compensation is described only as "a competitive base and bonus" with subsidized healthcare, generous PTO, wellness programs, professional development grants and a retirement plan — no numbers posted.
Open relevant roles (sample): Tech Lead, DevOps Engineer (Montreal, hybrid).
