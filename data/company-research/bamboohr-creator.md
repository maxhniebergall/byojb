# Creator.co — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:creator | company_type: product

What they do: Influencer-marketing and creator-commerce SaaS. In their own words: "At creator.co, we build the cutting-edge infrastructure that bridges social content and direct commerce. Our platform enables top brands and agencies to manage massive influencer marketing and affiliate campaigns, automate outreach, and process global payments." Features include an AI outreach agent ("London"), a creator database they size at "400M+ influencers across YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok", campaign/brief/approval management, content usage rights, and conversion/ROI attribution. Named customer logos include Nike, Groupon, Mercedes and Pokemon.

How they describe themselves: Tagline "We Are All Creators™". Marketing emphasis is on transparency, ease of collaboration, measurable results and removing friction from influencer-marketing workflows. Internally the JD describes "our daily Scrum meetings, sprint planning, and code review processes to foster a highly collaborative, transparent, and agile engineering culture", and asks the engineer to "Own Engineering Documentation … to keep our distributed team aligned". Growth framing is prominent: "We are experiencing explosive growth."

Size / stage / funding: Small private company; headcount, funding stage and profitability were not verified from a first-party page this session (**unverified**). "Distributed team" is stated in the JD. Only one relevant engineering req is open, which itself suggests a small engineering org.

Locations / HQ: Vancouver, British Columbia — the sole open engineering req is "Location: Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada", Permanent Full-Time.

Remote policy: Not stated on the website or in the JD. The JD references a "distributed team" and heavy documentation, but the posting itself names Vancouver as the location rather than "Remote". Remote status is therefore **unclear** — the posting reads as Vancouver-based.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, leaning no.** The employer is Canadian (Vancouver) and pays in CAD, so a BC-based hire is legally trivial — this is the strongest point in its favour. But the req names Vancouver as the work location, not remote, and no remote policy is published, so Kimberley-based work is unverified rather than confirmed. Timezone would be ideal (same province, one hour offset).

Engineering & tech: Node.js backend (3+ years required), PostgreSQL with emphasis on indexing, connection pooling and aggregation tuning for read-heavy dashboards; Python ETL pipelines for asynchronous background collection; AWS (API Gateway, SQS, Docker, auto-scaling groups); an in-flight monolith-to-microservices migration with "crisp service boundaries and dedicated data isolation"; REST/gRPC/message brokers. The stated engineering milestone is "evolving our data layer to seamlessly ingest, track, and display deep social media metrics (from millions of creators) alongside real-time conversion and e-commerce attribution data" while "scaling our platform to support hundreds of thousands of concurrent users". Tooling: GitHub, Jira. Nice-to-haves include AI-assisted coding (Copilot, Claude Code, Codex) and "a conceptual understanding of modern AI systems, including Model Context Protocol (MCP), prompt engineering…".

Notable / other: The JD asks for "architectural empathy" — "someone who writes clean Node.js code but keeps a sharp eye on how that code impacts database load, data ingestion pipelines, and AWS cloud-native scaling limits." The role is explicitly scoped as **Mid-Level** despite the ingestion/microservices framing, and pay reflects that.

Open relevant roles (sample): Backend Engineer (Vancouver, BC) — CAD $90,000–$105,000, permanent full-time.
