# Buzz Solutions — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:buzzsolutions | company_type: product

What they do: Buzz Solutions applies **computer vision to electrical grid inspection and maintenance**. Their own words: "Buzz is revolutionizing the analytics and maintenance of power grid infrastructure through our advanced AI solutions. Our computer vision systems analyze critical infrastructure to enhance safety, reliability, and operational efficiency across the power grid network." The concrete inspection tasks named across their JDs are **equipment defect detection, thermal anomaly identification, vegetation encroachment monitoring, and surveillance of closed areas for human and animal intrusions** — i.e. automated analysis of imagery (including drone- and GIS-derived, metadata-enriched imagery) of transmission and distribution assets. Customers would be utilities, though none are named.

How they describe themselves: There is no values list, mission page or culture section in any of the three postings — the "About Us" paragraph above is the entirety of their self-description, repeated verbatim across roles. The one culture signal they do offer is about team structure and autonomy, and it is a positive one: "You'll work within a team of experienced ML engineers with the **autonomy to drive your own projects and the support to keep growing**." "Revolutionizing" is mild hype framing, but no "fast-paced", "hustle", "rocketship", "scrappy" or "wear many hats" language appears anywhere.

Engineering & tech: Two tracks — an ML/CV research-to-production track and an ML platform/infrastructure track.
- **Applied ML Platform Engineer** (entry/mid, 2–4 years): "build tooling and infrastructure to help scale our training and data pipelines". Scalable training infrastructure for CV workloads; **distributed multi-GPU, multi-node training pipelines** and hyperparameter tuning; robust data pipelines; database schema and storage strategy for large training datasets, annotations and model artifacts; **feature stores, data versioning, experiment tracking**; workflow orchestration and automation; documentation of platform components, data contracts and deployment processes; code review and integration tests for ML pipelines. Named stack: Python (idiomatic, type hints, async, packaging), Pytest, UV, FastAPI, Pydantic; Git, Docker; MLflow or Weights & Biases; GitHub Actions, CI/CD, Prefect; **AWS, GCP, Kubernetes, Helm, Terraform**; Postgres, DynamoDB, Bigtable.
- **CV/ML Engineer and Senior CV/ML Engineer** (7–10 years at senior, plus 2–3 years leading technical teams): object detection, semantic segmentation, image classification, similarity search, vision-language models; PyTorch, Lightning, OpenCV, Scikit-Learn; FastAPI and Pydantic; reading and adapting papers, benchmarking against baselines, structured error analysis by failure mode and data slice; production-grade Python libraries for the full ML lifecycle; mentoring and establishing best practice for model development, evaluation, deployment and monitoring. Desired: multi-modal CV, edge deployment, model quantization, GIS/drone metadata, generative models for data augmentation.
Engineering-practice signals are unusually strong for a company this size: TDD with Pytest, type hinting, CI/CD, code review, ablation studies, and explicit "ML devops". No public engineering blog or handbook was found.
