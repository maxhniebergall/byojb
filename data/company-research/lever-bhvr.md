# Behaviour Interactive — research
Provider/key: lever:bhvr | company_type: product
What they do: Behaviour Interactive is Canada's largest independent video-game developer and publisher, founded 1992 and headquartered in Montreal. The business has two halves: **Original Games** (their own IP, headlined by *Dead by Daylight*, which has passed 60 million players) and **Services** — work-for-hire co-development for "gaming and entertainment giants like Disney, Sony, Activision, and Nintendo", plus location-based entertainment (LBE). They have shipped over 250 titles since 1992. Note that the Services half is a work-for-hire/co-development business; several of the open engineering roles sit on confidential co-development projects for external partners rather than on Behaviour's own IP.
How they describe themselves: "A dynamic, independent studio of over 1200 passionate employees." Their stated cultural stance: work "hard" and "play hard" while maintaining work-life balance and explicitly avoiding crunch; innovation balanced with disciplined execution; collaborative teamwork without egos; learning from mistakes through calculated risk-taking. The postings add a formal inclusion statement: "At Behaviour Interactive, we believe in giving everyone a fair shot. We embrace a diverse and creative environment where everyone is celebrated. We value folks of all ages, sex, ethnicity, colour, ethnic or national origin, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, civil status, disability, or any other protected characteristics as per applicable law."
Size / stage / funding: 1,200+ employees; independent (privately held); founded 1992. Recognized as one of Canada's 50 Best Workplaces. Funding stage not applicable/undisclosed. Note that the games industry broadly saw large layoffs in 2023–2025; whether Behaviour was affected was not verifiable in this session.
Locations / HQ: HQ Montreal. Studios in Toronto (Behaviour Toronto), Rotterdam (Behaviour Rotterdam), and the UK (Behaviour UK–North and UK–South).
Remote policy: The About page does not state one. Every live engineering posting in the registry is tagged **"Montreal, QC, hybrid"** and "Permanent Full-Time | Permanent temps-plein". So the operative policy for these roles is Montreal hybrid.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO for the current openings. All four relevant live postings are Montreal hybrid, which requires residence in the Montreal area. This is a Canadian employer (so the legal entity is not the obstacle), but the roles themselves are not remote and are on Eastern Time. Effectively disqualifying for a Kimberley BC / Mountain Time candidate.
Engineering & tech: Cloud-heavy backend and DevOps for live-service games. The Core Technologies (Core Tech) team "develops both backend and frontend services that support and enhance Behaviour games. Our services, which are primarily based on cloud technology, are utilized by games such as Dead by Daylight to develop in-game features, including matchmaking, player progression, in-game purchases, friends' systems, and messaging." Core Tech "fully embraces the DevOps philosophy to achieve world-class reliability, scalability, and security." The backend roles cover "implementing backend services and building the game-side APIs that communicate with those services… solid experience with cloud technologies and DevOps principles," and for the unannounced project, architecting "the backbone of our online infrastructure. From early conception to live deployment… backend systems that power both new and evolving features… at a global scale." The DevOps roles call for "deep expertise in cloud technologies, DevOps principles, Infrastructure as Code, and cloud environments." Postings are bilingual FR/EN, so French is likely an asset or requirement in the Montreal office.
Notable / other: *Dead by Daylight* is a long-running live-service title, which means the backend/Core Tech work is genuinely long-lived platform work rather than project-churn — a real point in its favour. No compensation is posted on any Behaviour JD.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Backend Programmer – Confidential Co-Development Project; Senior Backend Programmer – Unannounced project; Senior DevOps Programmer – Core Technologies; Senior DevOps Programmer – Confidential Co-Development Project.
