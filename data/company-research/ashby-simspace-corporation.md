# SimSpace Corporation — research
Provider/key: ashby:simspace-corporation | company_type: product

What they do: SimSpace builds high-fidelity cyber range / simulation environments — in their words "an AI Proving Ground where organizations can confidently train, test, and outmaneuver adversaries in any environment." Customers are "allied governments, militaries, enterprises, and research institutions worldwide." The platform "unifies training, testing, and validation in a realistic, live-fire simulation—helping teams evaluate security investments, optimize performance, and compress cyber readiness cycles from months to days." Practically: they emulate networks, attacks and human user behaviour so security teams can rehearse against realistic adversaries.

How they describe themselves: Their JD boilerplate is unusually value-forward and worth quoting. "Why join SimSpace? We are an organization that is focused on building our culture and mindfully enhancing our atmosphere every day which is why we have collaborated on an integral value system. Our governing philosophy of being Human Centered is deeply embedded within our value system. We apply this philosophy to every one of our internal team members, external clients, and their customers." On working style: "We believe that people are at the center of everything we do. SimSpace fosters a culture of continuous learning, curiosity, and professional growth. That belief shows up in action: in-house training, internal and external learning platforms, cyber conferences, industry events, and dedicated time for skill development. Our people are empowered to shape their careers - and it shows. Year over year, SimSpace consistently outperforms industry benchmarks in internal mobility, promotions, and total rewards growth." And: "We are a team of innovators, protectors, and problem-solvers. We believe diversity of thought and experience fuels better solutions... Whether you're remote or office-based, you'll collaborate with talented colleagues across departments and time zones, united by the mission to create a safer digital world." The governing stated value is "Human Centered". Notably absent: hustle, "fast-paced", or hype language.

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2015 by "experts from U.S. Cyber Command and MIT Lincoln Laboratory". Private, venture-backed. Exact headcount and funding stage not verified this session — the registry shows only 11-12 live postings, and no first-party simspace.com page appears in the registry to fetch. Treat size/stage as unverified.

Locations / HQ: HQ is Boston, Massachusetts (from general knowledge — unverified this session). The live posting set shows exactly two hiring geographies: "Remote - U.S." and "Remote - Costa Rica". The Costa Rica cluster looks like a deliberate nearshore engineering hub.

Remote policy: Remote-friendly and distributed — the boilerplate says "Whether you're remote or office-based, you'll collaborate with talented colleagues across departments and time zones", and every engineering posting in the registry is explicitly remote. But remote is country-scoped: US or Costa Rica.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO. All 12 live postings are "Remote - U.S." (8) or "Remote - Costa Rica" (4). A full-text scan of every fetched SimSpace JD body returned zero occurrences of "Canada"/"Canadian". Additionally the customer base is "allied governments, militaries" with founders out of U.S. Cyber Command, which in this sector typically brings US-person / clearance constraints on the US roles. No Canadian route is visible.

Engineering & tech: Backend stack is explicitly Java/Spring Boot microservices with event-driven messaging and containerization — one JD asks for "expertise in microservices, event-driven messaging systems, Spring Boot, and containerization" building "containerized microservices that provide the core emulation services of our cyber ranges." Teams named in the postings: AUE (Attack and User Emulation), Control Plane & Orchestration, Core Services, Core UI, Network Simulations, Identity & Access Management, and SRE. Senior/Staff scope is described as genuinely broad — "Lead end-to-end technical projects, from requirements gathering and architecture design to deployment", mentorship, code review, monitoring/alerting strategy, security and compliance controls, and architecture documentation.

Notable / other: The technical domain — network simulation, attack emulation, control-plane orchestration — is unusually deep, well-scoped systems work with internal-facing "make the simulation real" problems rather than sales-driven feature pressure. Pay is transparent and strong: US Staff software engineering rolls up to roughly $185-260K USD base.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer, Identity & Access Management (Remote - U.S.); Staff Software Engineer (Attack & User Emulation Team) (Remote - U.S.); Staff Site Reliability Engineer (Remote - U.S.); Senior Software Engineer, Network Simulations (Remote - U.S.); Backend Senior Software Engineer (Control Plane & Orchestration) (Remote - Costa Rica).
