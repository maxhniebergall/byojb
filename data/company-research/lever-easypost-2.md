What they do: "Founded in 2012, EasyPost is a **YC unicorn** whose mission is to make shipping simple for businesses—from garage startups to the Fortune 500." They sell "the first developer-friendly **REST API for shipping**" — carrier integrations, label generation, rate shopping, tracking, address verification and insurance — and operate the **USPS PC Postage system**, described in their own JD as "EasyPost's most critical, highest-traffic platform." Framing: "Shipping, now more than ever, is the backbone of the global economy, but integrating the technology-enabled operations of a modern business with the low-tech and complex shipping industry has always been a challenge."
