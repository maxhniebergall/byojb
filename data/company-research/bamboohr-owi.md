# Offworld Industries — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:owi | company_type: product
What they do: Offworld Industries Ltd. is an independent video-game studio and publisher. Their words: "the independent studio behind the successful military first-person shooter game, 'Squad'. Offworld was formed in 2014 by more than 15 developers who had worked together on the well known Project Reality mod for Battlefield 2. The founding purpose of the team was to bring a standalone successor of this acclaimed mod to market and build a game development studio and a livelihood from that foundation." They have since expanded "into serving as a publisher supporting mods" and released "Starship Troopers: Extermination", a 16-player PvE FPS based on the 1997 film.
How they describe themselves: "Fast forward to 2026, Offworld has over 150 employees and is rapidly growing." "We are built on passion, unified spirit, and continuing efforts to break the mold. We strive to become a leader in creating large-scale multiplayer games and supplying others with the ability to do the same." They close postings with "Wonderful colleagues!" as a listed benefit and are an equal-opportunity employer.
Size / stage / funding: 150+ employees as of 2026, "rapidly growing"; independent (self-funded from Squad's success rather than an announced VC stage, as far as their own text says). Live-service revenue plus publishing.
Locations / HQ: New Westminster, British Columbia, Canada (Greater Vancouver). A partner relationship exists with Tencent's GCloud/InGame infrastructure teams in China.
Remote policy: Office-anchored. The Senior Game Backend Engineer posting states "the requirement to work from our office three days per week"; the DevOps & Build Engineer posting is listed at the New Westminster location and includes on-call as the "primary point of contact for critical build and infrastructure incidents", with on-prem infrastructure to maintain alongside AWS — which implies physical proximity.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No for the backend role (explicit 3 days/week in the New Westminster office). Unclear-to-unlikely for the DevOps role**, which is location-listed in New Westminster and involves on-premise infrastructure and firefighting duties. Both are in BC, so timezone (Pacific) would be workable, but the office requirement is not.
Engineering & tech: DevOps & Build Engineer — Unreal Engine build pipelines (Automation System, BuildGraph, UnrealGameSync), Jenkins with Groovy/JobDSL, Python automation, Perforce (essential) and Git, AWS plus on-premise infrastructure, Terraform/Ansible IaC, Docker/Kubernetes, Linux (Ubuntu/CentOS) and Windows Server, monitoring/logging/alerting (Grafana, Prometheus, OpenTelemetry desirable), release management across hardware platforms and storefronts, LiveOps workflows, DR/HA strategies. Senior Game Backend Engineer — Node.js/TypeScript stateful microservices on the Tencent "InGame" PaaS; stateful scaling, consistent hashing, distributed caching, graceful shutdown and dirty-cache sync; TcaplusDB; Tencent SDKs (Midas payments, WeGame auth, ACE moderation); replacing legacy shell-script deploys with containerized CI/CD; building integration test suites toward a "zero-failure" testing culture.
Notable / other: The backend role is a **12-month fixed-term contract** (possible extension or conversion) and requires **native-level Mandarin** to work with Tencent's GCloud teams plus willingness to take meetings in China morning hours from Pacific time. Both roles post CAD salary ranges: DevOps & Build Engineer $95,000-$120,000; Senior Game Backend Engineer $105,000-$125,000. Benefits: extended health and dental, wellness/health spending accounts, retirement savings plan, 3 weeks vacation plus 2 weeks paid studio shutdown in December (DevOps role); the fixed-term backend role offers only 2 weeks vacation.
Open relevant roles (sample): DevOps & Build Engineer (New Westminster BC); Senior Game Backend Engineer (New Westminster BC, 12-month fixed term).
