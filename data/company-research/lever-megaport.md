# Megaport — research
Provider/key: lever:megaport | company_type: product

What they do: Megaport is the global leader in Network as a Service (NaaS). It operates a Software Defined Network that lets businesses provision private, on-demand connectivity between data centres, cloud providers and each other — replacing long-lead-time traditional circuits with API/portal-driven elastic interconnection. The SDN reaches 850+ enabled data centres across 25+ countries in North America, Asia Pacific and Europe. Partners and destinations include AWS, Microsoft, Google, Oracle and IBM. Revenue is recurring connectivity/port subscription.

How they describe themselves: "We're not your typical tech company – and we don't want to be." On culture: "We're a team of problem solvers, pixel pushers, code slingers, and cloud fanatics. Culture is more than a poster on the wall here – collaboration beats hierarchy, curiosity fuels our growth, and everyone's voice matters. We take our work seriously, but not ourselves. We work across time zones to execute on our global vision, trust each other to get things done, and never compromise our values for commercial gain. Most importantly, we place our customers at the center of everything we do." Their engineering JDs describe an environment with "no ego" teams, ownership of work, and an explicit expectation of balancing technical debt against feature delivery. They also state "Don't meet every requirement? That's okay."

Size / stage / funding: Publicly listed on the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX: MP1) following IPO — no venture runway dependency. "A crew of over 600 people spread across Asia-Pacific, Europe, and the Americas."

Locations / HQ: Headquartered in Brisbane, Australia. Distributed across APAC, Europe and the Americas.

Remote policy: Remote-first with coworking options available. Historic and current postings include fully-remote roles with up to 100% telecommuting. Culture statement explicitly acknowledges distributed working: "We work across time zones to execute on our global vision, trust each other to get things done." Benefits noted publicly: four weeks paid annual leave, parental leave, birthday leave, health-and-wellness support, learning support.

Remote-Canada eligibility: LIKELY YES. The live Senior Backend Software Engineer posting carries the location string "Canada, remote, California", so Canada is an accepted remote location for this role and no Canadian city is specified. The residual uncertainty is timezone, not geography: the company is Brisbane-headquartered and describes working across time zones, so an Americas-based engineer may face occasional APAC-overlap meetings. No mandatory-hours statement appears in the JD (unlike, say, Lightspeed's EST/EDT clause).

Engineering & tech: Core network/SDN engineering — the product IS infrastructure. Backend services for provisioning, orchestration and billing of virtual cross-connects, plus network automation as its own discipline (a Senior Network Automation Engineer role is publicly listed alongside the backend role). Live relevant titles are Senior Backend Software Engineer and Senior Site Reliability Engineer, so backend and SRE are distinct tracks.

Notable / other: Partner ecosystem with every major hyperscaler. Domain is carrier/interconnect infrastructure — a mature, unglamorous, mission-critical market rather than a hype cycle. The JD's own framing does lean product-and-customer-facing in places ("You find joy in making a product that customers love", "we place our customers at the center of everything we do"), which is worth noting for anyone optimizing for internal-facing scope.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Backend Software Engineer (Canada, remote / California); Senior Site Reliability Engineer; Senior Network Automation Engineer.
