ENTITY NOTE: the registry calls this company "Kcura" because the Workday tenant slug is `kcura`. The company rebranded from kCura to **Relativity** years ago, and every JD body in the registry self-identifies as Relativity. Same entity, stale name.

What they do: Relativity is a legal-data-intelligence company. Its core product is RelativityOne, a cloud e-discovery / legal-data platform used for litigation, investigations, regulatory inquiries and data-breach response, plus the "aiR" suite of generative-AI products (aiR for Review, aiR for Privilege, aiR Assist, aiR for Case Strategy, aiR for Data Breach Response). Customers are AmLaw 200 law firms, Fortune 500 corporations and government agencies.

How they describe themselves: From their JDs — "a leading legal data intelligence company building technology that helps users organize data, discover the truth, and act on it with confidence"; "At Relativity, engineers don't just write code. They build the systems that power AI-driven workflows at massive scale." They also highlight Relativity Academic (access for academic institutions) and Justice for Change (pro bono support). No explicit values list was captured; no hustle/rocketship language appears in the JD bodies.

Size / stage / funding: Private, Chicago-based; majority investment by Silver Lake since 2021 (prior knowledge — not verified from a fetched page this run). Roughly 1,500+ employees.

Locations / HQ: HQ at 231 South LaSalle St., Chicago, IL. Live engineering reqs are split between Illinois (US) and Kraków, Poland. Reqs are listed against many "Locations" counts (up to 23), which appear to be multi-city US/EU listings rather than remote.

Engineering & tech: Substantive platform work. The aiR Services team "builds the shared backend platform that powers Relativity's generative AI products… secure and scalable access to foundation models across the aiR Suite while handling limited model capacity, global data privacy requirements, and fair usage across products and customers", described as "high-throughput, distributed systems that route AI traffic across regions and providers". Stack across reqs: C#/.NET, Python, Java, Go, Azure and/or AWS. This is close to the candidate's Elasticsearch inference-API/model-routing background.
