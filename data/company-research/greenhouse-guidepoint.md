# Guidepoint — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:guidepoint | company_type: product

What they do: Guidepoint is an expert-network / research-enablement firm. It connects institutional
investors, corporations and consultancies with a global network of subject-matter advisors through
expert calls, surveys, custom research and data products, and is building an AI-powered "Next-Gen
research enablement platform" on top of its proprietary knowledge repositories. Their stated mission,
in their own words, is "making individuals, businesses, and the world smarter through personalized
knowledge-sharing solutions." Note: Guidepoint brokers access to third-party experts — it is an
information-services business — but the engineering roles here are **internal product/platform
roles**, not client-placement consulting.

How they describe themselves: "Guidepoint's Technology team thrives on problem-solving and creating
happier users… the engineering team is taking on challenges to improve our internal application
architecture and create new AI-enabled products to optimize the seamless delivery of our services."
The Toronto build-out is framed as "a strategic investment… integral to Guidepoint's vision for the
future," and the AI work is repeatedly described as "responsible AI" with "best-in-class MLOps."

Size / stage / funding: Private, established (founded 2003), roughly 1,000+ employees globally, with
offices across North America, Europe and Asia. Mature revenue business rather than a venture
runway story; the expert-network sector as a whole is stable but competitive and compliance-heavy.

Locations / HQ: HQ New York City, with a global office network. The engineering centre relevant here
is the **Toronto Technology Hub**, described as "the base of our Data/AI/ML team."

Remote policy: **Hybrid, office-anchored.** The postings state plainly "This is a hybrid position
based in Toronto" and carry the `#LI-HYBRID` tag. All four relevant openings are listed "Toronto,
Ontario, Canada."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **NO — this is Canadian, but not remote-Canada.** Every relevant role is
an in-person-anchored hybrid role tied to the Toronto Technology Hub. There is no remote option
stated on any posting. For a candidate in Kimberley, British Columbia who cannot relocate, this is
disqualifying regardless of the work's quality.

Engineering & tech: Genuinely substantial AI-platform engineering — arguably the strongest technical
content in this batch. The Toronto Data/AI/ML team is "dedicated to building a modern data
infrastructure for advanced analytics and the development of responsible AI." Named scope includes:
scalable low-latency backend services and APIs serving GenAI features; retrieval-augmented generation
pipelines and agentic systems; retrieval optimisation (k-value and chunk-size tuning, re-rankers,
advanced chunking, prompt engineering for hallucination reduction); hybrid search over vector
databases (**Pinecone, Elasticsearch**); multi-agent systems with tool calls; LLMOps with automated
evaluation via LLM-judge frameworks and MLflow, AI observability and monitoring; end-to-end lifecycle
ownership including CI/CD. Platform stack: **Databricks**, **Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS)**, Python.
There is also an internal-developer-platform (IDP) track — "Senior Platform Engineer (IDP)" and
"Principal Platform Engineer" — i.e. a real platform-engineering function alongside the AI team.

Notable / other: The Elasticsearch + vector-search + LLM-serving + eval-harness combination maps
unusually closely onto prior Elastic ML Core / Chat Completion API experience. Expert networks carry
significant compliance obligations (MNPI/insider-trading controls), which shapes internal tooling.

Open relevant roles (sample): AI Engineer (Toronto, hybrid); Senior Data/AI Engineer (Toronto);
Senior AI Platform Engineer (Agentic IDP); Senior Platform Engineer (IDP) (Toronto); Principal
Platform Engineer (Toronto); Senior Python Engineer – Data Platform.

Posted pay (their own JDs, CAD base, plus discretionary annual bonus): Senior Data/AI Engineer
$135,000–$210,000; Principal Platform Engineer $175,000–$200,000; Senior Platform Engineer (IDP)
$115,000–$140,000. Bands are wide and the senior platform band is notably low for Toronto.
