# Amplify (Amplify Education) — research
Provider/key: ashby:amplify | company_type: product

What they do: K–12 education technology. Amplify builds curriculum, assessment and supplemental-learning programs for literacy, biliteracy, math and science, delivered through the "Amplify Classroom" platform (curriculum + assessment + supplemental learning in one instructional system). Founded/pioneering in education since 2000; per its own JD copy it "serves more than 18 million students and teachers across all 50 states and on six continents." Customers are US school districts and states.

How they describe themselves: "Amplify helps teachers bring delight and rigor to students every day… building inspiring teaching and learning experiences based on research." The careers page frames the company as purpose-driven — improving "the lives of teachers and students" — with cross-functional collaboration and an "agile approach" to adapting programs to real classrooms. JD language asks for "ability to work in a fast paced, collaborative setting" and "a passion for working on education products."

Size / stage / funding: Private, ~25 years old, backed historically by News Corp then Emerson Collective. Exact current headcount not stated on the pages fetched — unverified.

Locations / HQ: HQ Brooklyn, NY (DUMBO).

Remote policy: Self-described as "Remote first. Brooklyn based. In person sometimes." Cites workplace awards for both fully-remote and hybrid arrangements.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — not eligible.** Every relevant open posting in the registry is explicitly "Remote - United States" (4/4). The careers page does not enumerate hiring countries, but the postings themselves are US-scoped. Treat as US-only.

Engineering & tech: TypeScript across front and back end; Node.js, Express, Nx, React, ESBuild; MongoDB as the datastore; AWS with Datadog, Terraform, Docker; Jest and WebdriverIO for testing. Engineers sit on cross-functional teams with PMs, designers, data scientists and education experts, and contribute "from initial ideation through production deployment." A "Learning Experience" team owns content authoring, the in-lesson student/teacher experience, and teacher tooling — explicitly both front and back end. Current AI work: integrating models into the platform for classroom orchestration, lesson planning/customization and coaching; JDs mention LLMs, Model Context Protocol, ASR and computer vision as nice-to-haves. A separate DevOps team owns deployment/infrastructure (engineers "collaborate with" it rather than own it).

Notable / other: Bar for the senior role is modest (3+ years professional experience, 2+ years React) relative to the "Senior" title. Roles are product-team-shaped rather than platform-shaped.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (×3, Remote - United States); Software Engineer (Remote - United States).
