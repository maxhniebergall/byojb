# Alpaca — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:alpaca | company_type: product

What they do: Alpaca is a US-headquartered, licensed **self-clearing broker-dealer and brokerage-infrastructure API company** — the "AWS for brokerage." It provides institutional-grade APIs for stocks, ETFs, options, crypto, fixed income and 24/5 trading, which other financial institutions embed to offer investing to their own users. Their own copy: "serving hundreds of financial institutions across 40 countries with our institutional-grade APIs. This includes broker-dealers, investment advisors, wealth managers, hedge funds, and crypto exchanges, totalling over **10 million brokerage accounts**." Recent positioning has shifted to "global leader in **agent-first** brokerage infrastructure." They are self-clearing (they run their own clearing, not a third party's), which is a substantial regulatory and systems undertaking.

How they describe themselves: Mission is "opening financial services to everyone on the planet." Core values, stated verbatim and repeated on every JD, are three: "**Stay Curious, Have Empathy, and Be Accountable**." The careers page adds four cultural pillars: career development ("build and scale your career" through ambitious problem-solving and attention to detail); a goal-oriented approach where teams "care about doing the work and solving problems together" and "welcome questions from all levels because they know that no one knows all the answers"; a global perspective, actively recruiting "agile talent with vibrantly diverse cultural experiences"; and integrity, valuing "honesty and clarity" in communication combined with empathy across multicultural teams. They emphasise deep work ("Alpaca values uninterrupted deep work" — employee testimonial) and open-source contribution: "deeply committed to open-source contributions and fostering a vibrant community." Counter-signal in the same documents: JDs ask for the "ability to work independently in a fully remote, globally distributed team, and **under pressure in a fast-paced environment**," and describe candidates as "passionate individuals eager to contribute to Alpaca's **rapid growth**."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, **Series D**. Funding statements are inconsistent across JD vintages: older postings cite "over $320 million" total investment and a **$1.15B valuation** at the Series D; newer postings say "**proudly backed by $400 million in funding**." Investors: Portage Ventures, Spark Capital, Tribe Capital, Social Leverage, Horizons Ventures, Opera Tech Ventures, SBI Group, Derayah Financial, Unbound, Peak XV, Elefund, and Y Combinator. Headcount is also stated inconsistently — newer JDs say "**400+ globally distributed members**", the about page says "more than 250 employees in 25 countries." Not stated to be profitable. As a licensed broker-dealer it is regulated and its subsidiaries are capital-constrained, which imposes a stability floor that a typical startup lacks.

Locations / HQ: Headquartered in California, USA. **100% remote, globally distributed** — team members in 20-25 countries, with the USA, Canada, Japan, Hungary, Nigeria, Brazil and the UK named explicitly. Leadership comes from Robinhood, Strike, TD Securities and TMX Group among others.

Remote policy: "100% remote company" across 20+ countries and "nearly every continent." Roles are posted against **regional time-zone bands rather than countries** — live examples include "Remote - Canada", "Remote - Americas", "Remote - North America (EST)", "Remote - North America - LATAM", "Remote - North America - South America - (MST Time Zone)", "Remote - Europe", "Remote - EMEA", "Remote - APAC", "Remote - Japan - APAC", "Global Anywhere", and "Global Anywhere - Eastern or European Time Zones." Collaboration is described as a mix: "Collaborate with tech leads in other regions both synchronously and asynchronously, using Slack and **occasional meetings outside of regular working hours** to align with global teams." Senior roles carry regional operational duties — "serve as the operational reliability and engineering lead and the **incident commander during regional business hours, ensuring proactive 24x7 stability**" — and "you may be asked to be on-call."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, directly.** A live posting — Senior Software Engineer - New Markets (job 5655807004) — is located "**Remote - Canada**". Canada is also named in their own team-geography list ("teammates spanning the USA, Canada, Japan, Hungary, Nigeria, Brazil, the UK"). Several other live roles are "Remote - Americas", "Remote - North America" or "Global Anywhere", which also encompass Canada. One Mountain-Time caveat: the North-America roles are frequently pinned to a specific zone — one is "(EST)", one is "(MST Time Zone)". MST is explicitly served by at least one live role; EST-pinned roles would mean an early start from BC. The employment model for international hires (direct employee vs. employer-of-record vs. contractor) is **not stated anywhere on their public pages** and should be asked about — it materially affects the value of the "Competitive Salary & Stock Options" line.

Compensation: **Alpaca publishes no pay ranges at all.** All 19 live postings in the registry, across every region including US-facing ones, state no salary; benefits are described only as "Competitive Salary & Stock Options" plus "Health Benefits", a one-time **USD $500** new-hire home-office setup and a **USD $150/month** stipend via a Brex card. No pay-transparency page, no compensation philosophy page, no levels/handbook page exists publicly. Because every role is posted as a multi-country remote band rather than against a US state, none of them trip CO/NY/WA/CA/IL pay-transparency disclosure. This is the least transparent compensation posture in the batch, and it is deliberate rather than accidental: pay is almost certainly geo-adjusted per region, which is also why no single range would be publishable. The $500/$150 perk figures are on the thin side and are a weak signal about overall cash generosity.

Engineering & tech: **Go** is the primary backend language ("proficiency in Go or similar backend languages"). Architecture is **gRPC microservices** with **domain-driven and event-driven design**, plus React-based internal admin UIs and third-party vendor integrations. Work spans the core trading engine, clearing, market data, tax platform, payments and treasury, advisory suite, and new-market expansion (multi-language, multi-currency, multi-market support in the core framework). Explicitly open-source-committed with a public developer community around the API. Engineering is organised regionally, with senior engineers acting as regional leads — expected to handle "regional recruitment initiatives, leading cross-functional team projects, mentoring talent on our engineering principles and practices, and fostering Alpaca culture in the region." Domain requirements are heavy: financial-services experience, double-entry accounting, trade lifecycle and settlement, order management, execution, market-data processing, real-time trade processing, and regulatory compliance in financial markets. Note the New Markets JD asks for an "experienced **full-stack** engineer" despite the backend framing.

Notable / other: 24/5 (moving toward 24/7) trading means genuine round-the-clock system-criticality — an outage is a financial-markets incident, not a degraded web page. The Japan business is significant enough that two live roles require **JLPT N1 fluency**. Equal-opportunity employer; a published Recruitment Privacy Policy. Hiring volume is high — 15+ relevant engineering roles open simultaneously — which is both an opportunity signal and a churn/growth-pressure signal.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer - New Markets (Remote - Canada); Senior AI Platform Engineer (Remote - North America EST); Senior Data Engineer (Remote - North America - LATAM); Senior Software Engineer - Core Trading / Trading / Clearing / Market Data / Platform / Tax Platform / Payments & Treasury; Senior Software Engineer, Quality Engineering (Global Anywhere); Site Reliability Engineer (Remote - EMEA); DevOps Team Lead, Core Foundation (Remote - EMEA); Staff Software Engineer - New Markets Japan (JLPT N1).
