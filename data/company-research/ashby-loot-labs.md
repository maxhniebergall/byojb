# Loot Labs — research
Provider/key: ashby:loot-labs | company_type: product

What they do: Loot Labs is a **Seattle-based consumer digital-collectibles company**. Its flagship product is **Boxed.gg**, a "mystery box" / loot-box platform for trading cards and gaming collectibles: users digitally purchase and open boxes, draw and authenticate cards (Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Magic: The Gathering, Counter-Strike 2 cosmetics), then either keep them digitally or have the physical merchandise shipped from a US fulfillment facility. In their own words: "Loot Labs is on a mission to make collecting fun. We believe that every collectible tells a story: of passion, nostalgia, and discovery. Through our flagship product, Boxed.gg we are reimagining how the world collects by blending digital entertainment with tangible rewards, delivering nostalgia and discovery straight to the fans' doorsteps."

How they describe themselves: Three self-stated pillars, verbatim from the JD:
- "**Community First**: We've served 500k collectors and foster an active community of over 125k+ Discord members."
- "**Ambitiously Led**: Led by veterans from Microsoft, Twitch, 2K, and Boeing."
- "**Remote-First**: We value high autonomy, low ego, and trust our team to build from anywhere in the world."
On the working environment: "You'll own features end-to-end and see your impact in **days, not months**"; "Work alongside people who **ship fast** and care about the details"; "Take ownership in ambiguous situations"; must be "comfortable working in a **fast-paced**, collaborative environment"; "Balance speed and robustness, making pragmatic trade-offs to deliver value quickly." Growth framing: "Since our inception in 2023, we've seen significant year-over-year growth. And now we're gearing up for our **most ambitious year yet**, with major expansions planned across product offerings, strategic partnerships, and next-generation collector experiences."

Size / stage / funding: **Very early and small.** Founded 2023 by former Cool Cats (NFT project) leaders. Funding: **$1.5M pre-seed** (May 2023, BITKRAFT Ventures leading, with Polygon Ventures, Mechanism Capital, Lofty Ventures Syndicate and streamer "Yassuo") → **$6M seed** (October 2024, BITKRAFT again, with Sfermion, Fabric Ventures, Everyrealm). Roughly $7.5M raised in total — a seed-stage company, almost certainly under 50 people.

Locations / HQ: **Seattle, Washington** (Pacific Time). A US fulfillment facility handles physical shipping (the board also carries an on-site "Fulfillment Associate" role). No Canadian office.

Remote policy: **Remote-first and genuinely global.** "We value high autonomy, low ego, and trust our team to build from anywhere in the world." Restated in the comp section: "Loot Labs is a remote-first company, and this role is open to candidates globally. To support efficient collaboration across teams, we will prioritize applicants who have working hour overlap with U.S. and E.U. time zones." Two caveats attached to the engineering role: "**Time Zone Requirement: Must have 5+ hours of overlap with US Pacific Time (9am–6pm PT)**," and "**Occasional U.S. travel is required** for in-person collaboration, team offsites, and company events."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified YES.** There is a dedicated req — "Senior Backend Engineer (**Canada**)" — posted alongside the US equivalent, so hiring a Canada-based worker is an established, deliberate arrangement rather than an exception. Location is listed simply as "Remote," and the company states the role is open globally with North America preferred. **The Mountain Time requirement is comfortably met**: MT is one hour ahead of PT, so a Kimberley-based 8am–5pm MT day gives roughly seven hours of overlap with the required 9am–6pm PT window — well past the 5-hour minimum. The only friction is the occasional required US travel.

Engineering & tech: A **TypeScript / AWS serverless** shop. Named stack: AWS (**DynamoDB, Lambda, S3, AppSync, Cognito, SQS**), **SST v2 and v3** for infrastructure-as-code, **GraphQL** APIs and application logic in TypeScript, CI/CD, observability and incident response. Responsibilities include system design, data modelling, architectural decisions for scalability and maintainability, security and authn/authz, and mentoring. Bonus skills sought: e-commerce and payment processing, marketplaces, order/fulfillment workflows, blockchain/cryptocurrency/digital collectibles, **OpenSearch/Elasticsearch**, internal tooling (Retool), real-time and event-driven systems, data scraping and browser automation.

Notable / other: The business began as an explicitly **Web3/NFT** venture — the original Boxed.gg pitch was NFT-based mystery boxes — and has since pivoted away from NFTs toward physical trading-card fulfillment, though crypto-adjacent investors (Polygon Ventures, Mechanism Capital, Sfermion, Everyrealm) remain on the cap table and blockchain experience is still listed as a bonus skill. Worth noting that loot-box mechanics attract ongoing regulatory scrutiny in several jurisdictions as gambling-adjacent, which is a business-model risk distinct from the engineering. The JD also asks for "strong product sense and user empathy… build with the why in mind," signalling a product-facing rather than platform-facing engineering role.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Backend Engineer (Canada) — remote, no posted range; Senior Backend Engineer (US) — remote, **US$135K–200K** base posted; Fulfillment Associate — on-site (not relevant).
