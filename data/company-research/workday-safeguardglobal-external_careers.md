# Safeguard Global — research
Provider/key: workday:safeguardglobal/external_careers | company_type: outsourcing

What they do: Safeguard Global is a **global workforce-solutions / Employer of Record (EOR) company**. Its own about page lists Employer of Record, contractor management, payroll processing, recruitment and HR outsourcing as its core offerings, under the tagline "global expansion without the risk". Its careers copy puts it as: "we enable global expansion without the risk by helping 1,500+ customers to hire, manage, and pay their employees in nearly 190 countries." Its business is therefore employing and administering *other companies'* workforces — an outsourcing/EOR provider. (Note: the engineering roles listed below appear to be **internal** platform/data roles building Safeguard's own systems, not client placements; the `outsourcing` classification describes the company's business, not the role.)

How they describe themselves: Six stated values — **Caring, Accountability, Integrity, Disrupt and empower, Passion and talent, Fun.** Employment-brand copy: "We want to help you 'work in any way' — that means making time for family, commitments, and life outside of work so that you can have the best of both worlds. When you own what you do and are driven to deliver, you have the flexibility to decide where and how you work." And: "Our roles aren't just remote and hybrid first — they break free from the traditional ways of doing things, paving a way for a job that works for you."

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2008 by Bjorn Reynolds (still CEO). Private; took "significant growth equity capital from Accel-KKR" in 2019. Self-reported scale: 187 countries served, 400+ experts worldwide, 1,500+ organizations served. In 2025 it "sold [its] enterprise payroll division to focus on… serving small- to medium-sized organizations" — a notable recent divestiture and strategic narrowing.

Locations / HQ: Distributed across multiple international hubs; no single HQ emphasised on the about page. Live engineering postings are tagged Portugal, Spain, United Kingdom and India.

Remote policy: Remote-first and explicitly flexible — "a flexible, remote-first culture dedicated to helping our people do their best work"; "Autonomy and Flexibility (work in any way): Remote first, with the flexibility to include life needs like school runs and gym breaks in your schedule." Benefits include a competitive leave package with paid bonding leave and 2 paid charitable days off.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — verified negative for these roles.** Canada appears on their marketing site only as a *country they provide EOR services in* for customers, not as a hiring geography for themselves. Every live engineering posting is scoped to a specific non-Canadian country: Principal Engineer (Portugal – remote, "3 Locations"), Principal Data Engineer (Spain – remote, "3 Locations"), Senior Platform Engineer (United Kingdom – remote), Platform Engineer (India – remote). The Principal Data Engineer JD confirms this by quoting starting salaries only for Spain and Portugal. Remote-first, but not remote-*Canada*.

Engineering & tech: Data platform work is substantive and infrastructure-flavoured. Principal Data Engineer owns "the architecture and evolution of our data platform… how data is ingested, modelled, governed, and consumed"; sets technical direction and standards for the data engineering team; drives governance (quality, lineage, ownership, cataloguing); and partners with the AI engineering team so "the data platform is AI-ready: clean, structured, and consumable at scale," working closely with a Head of AI. Stack: **Apache Iceberg** (or equivalent modern table formats) in production, **dbt** at scale, AWS data services (S3, Glue, Athena), **Terraform** IaC. Nice-to-haves: platforms serving AI/ML workloads, cataloguing/governance tooling, compliance-heavy multi-jurisdictional data environments (payroll, HR, finance), and event-driven/streaming architectures. Explicit emphasis on "building a team capability that is durable and not person-dependent."

Compensation transparency: The Principal Data Engineer JD states, "We are hiring this position across multiple countries; compensation varies by hiring location. For target markets located within the European Union (EU), base salaries start from (Gross): Spain: €89,000; Portugal: €89,000." It adds, "We are committed to pay transparency and equal pay for equal work… Compensation decisions are based on objective criteria including skills, experience, scope of responsibility, and market benchmarking, in line with internal salary structures." Note this is a stated **floor**, not a range.

Notable / other: Domain is payroll/HR/global-employment compliance — genuinely "boring", mission-critical infrastructure. Multi-jurisdictional regulatory complexity is a core engineering constraint.

Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Engineer (Portugal – remote); Principal Data Engineer (Spain – remote); Senior Platform Engineer (UK – remote); Platform Engineer (India – remote).
