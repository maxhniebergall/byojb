What they do: Free ad-supported streaming television (FAST) service — a large on-demand movie and TV catalogue monetised entirely through advertising rather than subscriptions. The engineering problem set is video delivery at scale, ad serving and experimentation, plus the compliance/privacy machinery a media business needs.

Engineering & tech: Rust-centred backend platform work — "developing and maintaining critical Rust-based tools and infrastructure which could bring Tubi to its next era". Named Rust stack: "Tokio, Axum, Tonic, Hyper, Bytes, Serde, Tracing", with "Experience with pingora stack is a big plus". Beyond Rust: "Deep understanding of distributed systems, event-driven architectures, and performance engineering"; cloud-native tooling "Kubernetes, gRPC, CDN, S3, Terraform" and "modern observability stacks"; datastores "PostgreSQL, DynamoDB, Redis, Clickhouse". The role collaborates with "teams focused on compliance, privacy, experimentation, and infrastructure" and is expected to "Design and optimize systems for reliability, observability, and scale, with an emphasis on clean APIs and modular components" and to "Partner with engineering leadership and cross-functional partners to drive technical vision and influence roadmap decisions." Requirement bar: "4+ years of backend software development experience, including 1+ years using Rust in production environments."
