# Affinity.co — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:affinity | company_type: product

What they do: Affinity builds a "relationship intelligence" CRM for the private capital industry — venture capital, private equity, private credit and investment banking. It ingests the data exhaust from email/calendar/interaction data across a firm's team and stitches it into a professional relationship graph, then surfaces deal and network insights on top of it. They state 3,000+ customers worldwide and claim their platform drives insight on "over 450,000 deals every month".

How they describe themselves: Mission framing is "make it possible for anyone to cultivate and fully harness their network to succeed." Their careers page states five named values, quoted from their own pages: **Playmakers** (initiative, going above and beyond), **Growth Mindset** ("A growth mindset drives our grit and drive to improve. We embrace feedback, learn fast, and keep moving forward"), **Owners** (accountability for outcomes), **Respectful Candor** (direct honest communication with empathy), and **"Taste the Soup"** (diving into the firsthand experience rather than theorizing). Their JD's "How we work" section adds three operating statements in their own words: "We iterate quickly. As such, you must be comfortable embracing ambiguity, be able to cut through it, and deliver value to our customers"; "We are candid, transparent, and speak our minds while simultaneously caring personally"; "We make data-driven decisions and make the best decision for the moment based on the information available." Tone is startup-professional rather than hype-y — no rocketship/hustle vocabulary, but "iterate quickly / embrace ambiguity" is present.

Size / stage / funding: Private, VC-backed; "$120M raised", "backed by some of Silicon Valley's best firms". Headcount not stated on their careers page. They cite Inc. and Fortune Best Workplaces awards and Great Place to Work certification "for the last 5 years running".

Locations / HQ: Hub offices in San Francisco, New York and Barcelona. Additional operating entities in the United Kingdom, Canada and Spain.

Remote policy: Describes itself as remote-first with hub offices. Employees attached to a hub (SF / NY / Barcelona) are hybrid with a stated 2-days-per-week in-office expectation; staff in other regions are fully remote.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** Two signals: (a) their careers page explicitly lists Canada among the countries where Affinity has a hiring entity; (b) a live posting — "Senior Machine Learning Engineer, AI Platform", https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/affinity/jobs/7628830003 — carries location "Canada (Remote); Toronto, Ontario", posts a CAD salary range, and lists Canada-specific benefits (RRSP plan, extended health premiums). No Toronto-office attendance requirement is stated on that posting, and no Canadian hub is listed among the 2-day hub offices, which implies a Canadian hire is remote. *Unverified:* whether they hire in British Columbia specifically or restrict to Ontario — the posting names Toronto as a co-location but does not state a provincial restriction.

Engineering & tech: The AI Platform team owns the AI services behind the relationship-intelligence product: information extraction, storage and retrieval over "billions of structured and unstructured data points". Stated stack, in their words: "Our ML pipeline manages multiple Python services that support various AI features, including utilizing OCR to extract information from unstructured data, serving embedding models to vectorize chunks, and ranking a list of recommendations based on relevance and user preference." Python + PyTorch/Scikit-learn; vector and graph databases; streaming and batch inference serving; observability for online/offline model evaluation, A/B testing and LLM tracing. They are explicit that the ML work is "an applied machine learning position with a strong emphasis on engineering, rather than research", and that ranking/recommendation infrastructure will be built "from scratch, initially via integrated off-the-shelf models".

Notable / other: Benefits stated on the Canadian posting: full extended-health premium coverage for employee and dependents, RRSP plan, annual education budget and L&D program, monthly reimbursement for home internet / meals / wellness, flexible personal and sick days. They disclose that they "may use artificial intelligence (AI) tools as part of our recruitment process to help screen and evaluate candidate applications", stating it never replaces human decisions.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Machine Learning Engineer, AI Platform (Canada Remote / Toronto); Senior Machine Learning Engineer, AI Platform (San Francisco / USA Remote); Senior AI Engineer, AI Platform; Senior Software Engineer; Software Engineer II.

Pay: both live ML Platform postings state ranges directly — CAD 160,000–220,000 base on the Canada posting and USD 160,000–235,000 base on the US posting (base only, excludes equity and benefits). See data/company-comp.jsonl.
