# Remote (Remote.com) — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:remotereferralboardinternaluseonly | company_type: product

**Identity note — important.** The registry name "Remote - Referral Board" and the Greenhouse slug `remotereferralboardinternaluseonly` are misleading. This is **not** an aggregator or a third-party referral/staffing board: it is the *internal employee-referral job board of Remote Technology, Inc. (remote.com)*, a real product company hiring for its own headcount. Every JD on it opens "About Remote" and describes Remote's own global-employment platform, its own interview loop ("Interview with the recruiter … Bar Raiser Interview … Offer + Background check (Veremark & Remote)"), and its own benefits handbook at remote.com/r/benefits. Postings here are duplicates of Remote's public reqs, surfaced through the referral pipeline. Treat the employer as **Remote**.

What they do: Remote is a global employment platform / Employer of Record. In their words: "Remote is solving modern organizations' biggest challenge – navigating global employment compliantly with ease. We make it possible for businesses of all sizes to recruit, pay, and manage international teams." The product handles payroll, benefits, taxes, compliance, contractor management and stock options across ~100+ countries; it is a multi-tenant HR/fintech platform.

How they describe themselves: Five stated core values — **Care, Ownership, Innovation, Transparency, Excellence** (the careers site renders the set as Care, Innovation, Transparency, Intensity, Excellence — "Intensity" and "Ownership" appear interchangeably across pages). Their own text: "With our core values at heart and future focused work culture, our team works tirelessly on ambitious problems, **asynchronously**, around the world. You can find Remoters working from 6 different continents (Antarctica left to go!) and **all of our positions are fully remote**." They describe themselves as "one of the world's largest remote-first, fully asynchronous global companies", with a 24-hour async reply norm, a Notion-based "search first" documentation culture, and a **public handbook** they publish deliberately "to inspire other remote-first companies". Also stated: "With Innovation as one of the core values, we have built Automation and AI capabilities into the requirements for every role." Recruiting tone: "If you are energetic, curious, motivated and ambitious, be part of our world."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed unicorn (founded 2019; large Series B/C rounds). Represents "more than 90 nationalities" and "more than 50 languages spoken" across its workforce. Growth-stage HR-fintech; the sector has seen consolidation pressure.

Locations / HQ: No headquarters in the conventional sense — fully distributed across six continents. Legally San Francisco-registered.

Remote policy: **Remote-first and async-first by construction** — it is literally the product. "All of our positions are fully remote", "work from anywhere", "flexible working hours (we are async)". Benefits stated in the JDs: work from anywhere, flexible paid time off, flexible working hours, **16 weeks paid parental leave**, learning budget, home-office setup allowance, co-working allowance, mental-health support, company stock options, country-specific packages.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes in principle, but req-dependent.** Remote employs globally and operates its own Canadian EoR entity, and one of the two SRE reqs states **"Location: Anywhere in the World"** — that is unambiguous Canada eligibility with Mountain-Time-compatible async hours. However, the *other* Senior SRE req states **"Location: For this hire, due to diversity and timezones requirements, we're prioritising Europe"**, so eligibility must be checked per-posting rather than assumed. The Backend Engineer reqs are tagged "Global" / "Remote".

Engineering & tech: From the Senior SRE JD — Kubernetes (operating and scaling production clusters), Docker, **AWS**, **Terraform** IaC, reliability frameworks (SLOs, SLIs, error budgets, alerting strategy), observability with **OpenTelemetry, Grafana/Prometheus**, CI/CD via GitLab CI or GitHub Actions, **Golang** plus Bash. Backend elsewhere is **Elixir** (there is a "Senior Backend Engineer (Elixir)" req), with Node.js and Python around it. Scope language is unusually good: "you'll work with a high degree of autonomy on complex reliability and platform problems, owning the plan and execution of features and projects within our SRE/Platform domain … contribute to the platform's architecture and reliability strategy, translating ambiguous requirements into robust, maintainable solutions … in an async-first, fully remote environment." On-call rotations are explicit. Strong stated expectation of AI-native working: "Practical, embedded use of AI in infra/ops/dev work, agentic workflows with concrete, observable results, not just familiarity with the tools" and "build reusable AI workflows that make the whole team faster".

Compensation — stated on their own live JDs (geo-banded):
- Senior Site Reliability Engineer: **$53,300 — $119,850 USD**
- Senior Site Reliability Engineer (Remote Build): **$54,000 — $150,000 USD**
Their stated philosophy: "Remote's Total Rewards philosophy is to ensure fair, unbiased compensation and fair equity pay along with competitive benefits in all locations in which we operate. We do not agree to or encourage cheap-labor practices and therefore we ensure to **pay above in-location rates**." They explain the very wide bands directly: "At first glance our salary bands seem quite wide — here is some context. At Remote we have international operations and a globally distributed workforce. We use **geo ranges** to consider geographic pay differentials as part of our global compensation strategy." So the band spans low-cost and high-cost geographies; a Canadian offer would land in the upper-middle of the range, not the bottom.

Notable / other: Publishes an open benefits/compensation handbook — genuinely transparent. Interview loop includes an async hiring-manager interview and a time-boxed infrastructure exercise ("you're not expected to spend more than 2 - 4 hours"), which is a respectful-of-candidates signal. Applications accepted on an ongoing basis; CVs must be in English.

Open relevant roles (sample): "Senior Site Reliability Engineer" (Europe-prioritised); "Senior Site Reliability Engineer (Remote Build)" (Anywhere in the World); "Senior Backend Engineer" (Global); "Senior Backend Engineer (Elixir)" (Remote).
