# Nova Credit — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:novacredit | company_type: product

What they do: Nova Credit builds financial data and analytics infrastructure for credit underwriting. Its products let banks, fintechs, credit unions and other lenders see an applicant's full financial picture beyond a domestic credit score — most distinctively Credit Passport, which translates a consumer's foreign credit bureau history into a locally-interpretable score so newcomers are not treated as credit-invisible, plus cash-flow and income-verification analytics. Credit Passport coverage spans more than 20 origin countries and has been extended to reach Canada. Customers are financial institutions; the business is B2B data infrastructure.

How they describe themselves: Mission framing is "Building a Better Credit System" and to "power a fair and inclusive financial system for the world". Their stated values are four:
- "Build Bridges" — creating relationships with colleagues and mission partners.
- "Challenge Without Ego" — intellectual honesty from a place of humility.
- "Chug Like a Train" — a bias for action; velocity determines outcomes.
- "Make it Your Mission" — taking ownership to advance company goals.

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed and well capitalized. Raised a Series D (a $35M round is cited on their careers material). Investors include Kleiner Perkins, Y Combinator and General Catalyst. Headcount not disclosed publicly; roughly mid-size (low hundreds).

Locations / HQ: HQ in New York City, with additional offices in San Francisco, London and Toronto. The Toronto office matters here: it is the likely employing entity behind the Canadian-dollar salary ranges.

Remote policy: Remote-friendly and distributed. Public careers aggregators list Nova Credit as hiring remotely, and the company describes itself as open to talent regardless of location. Both relevant live engineering postings carry the location "Remote" with no city attached. Remote-supporting benefits are stated: a monthly contribution toward cell phone and internet bills, and a flexible time-off policy "based on trust".

Remote-Canada eligibility: PROBABLE YES, though not stated in policy prose. The strongest evidence is first-party and quantitative: both live engineering JDs post dual-currency ranges, quoting Canadian-dollar figures alongside US-dollar figures, which means Nova Credit is prepared to pay a Canadian-resident hire in CAD for these roles. Combined with a Toronto office (so a Canadian entity exists) and postings listed simply as "Remote", a Canada-based remote hire looks well supported. What is NOT confirmed is whether they employ outside Ontario specifically — nothing in the postings restricts it to a province, but nothing confirms British Columbia either. Timezone: NYC-centred with a Toronto office, so Eastern is the centre of gravity and Mountain Time means an early day; no core-hours requirement is stated in the JDs. Note also that the US benefits paragraph (401(k), US medical/dental/vision) is scoped "In the U.S.", implying a separate Canadian package.

Engineering & tech: Backend and data-platform work in fintech — ingesting and normalizing heterogeneous international credit-bureau data, cash-flow analytics, and underwriting-decision APIs consumed by financial institutions. Live titles are simply Senior Software Engineer and Staff Software Engineer, so the ladder runs at least to Staff. Regulated-lending context implies strong requirements around data correctness, auditability, privacy and reliability.

Compensation (see data/company-comp/): Nova Credit posts dual-currency ranges on its own JDs, which is unusually candid, and explains that "the above compensation ranges are dependent on individual experience, skills, education, location, and qualifications. We consider all elements of compensation to be a part of the value we provide to Novans. This may include base salary, equity grants, incentive compensation for eligible roles, professional development, flexible PTO, and tenure rewards." Observed:
- Senior Software Engineer: $184,000 - $218,500 USD per year / $163,852 - $194,574 CAD per year.
- Staff Software Engineer: $212,500 - $260,200 USD per year / $190,000 - $233,489 CAD per year.
Other stated benefits: subsidized medical/dental/vision and a 401(k) in the US, mental-health support (Spring Health, Headspace+), 16-week parental leave, flexible PTO, a professional development budget with Udemy Business access, phone/internet stipend, and a "Fly Anywhere" subsidy of one round-trip flight annually on each work anniversary.

Notable / other: The immigrant-credit-access mission is genuinely distinctive and the product is data infrastructure rather than a consumer app. Fintech in lending is cyclical and regulation-sensitive, and Series D venture-backed means an eventual liquidity expectation, but the company is not early-stage.
