# Safari AI — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:safariai | company_type: product

What they do: Safari AI sells vision-AI software that runs on enterprises' *existing* camera infrastructure to measure and automate physical operations. The platform measures real-time metrics (occupancy, throughput, queue times, staff engagement), raises alerts, recommends actions and tracks outcomes through what they call an "Action Center" automation loop. Their stated vision is to "Automate Action of the leading companies in the physical economy, from Entertainment, Live Venues, QSR's, Retail and beyond."

Customers: Named on their site and JD — Charlotte Hornets, Calgary Flames, Herschend Family Entertainment (Dollywood, Silver Dollar City), Merlin/Legoland, Taco Bell, 7-Eleven, Tanger Outlets, Tennessee Titans, Barcelona Aquarium, plus real-estate, parking, shipping/logistics and government operators. Segments: theme parks, stadiums and venues, museums, QSR/drive-thru, retail, CRE, parking, logistics.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "to provide enterprises the ability to quantify their physical space reliably and affordably using computer vision." They emphasise measuring outcomes in dollars rather than vanity metrics, and needing no custom hardware. The JD's culture section is candid about being early-stage: "You're excited to build a product and invent a market that hasn't existed before… You're looking to take ownership/run a singular project and take advantage of the freedom and culture an early stage startup can provide… Enjoy a small team size ~10, project team of 1-3." Also: "You are looking to see through the fluff of the modern AI landscape and explore new ways of achieving measurable ROI."

Size / stage / funding: Small — the JD states a total team of roughly 10 people, with project teams of 1–3. Venture-funded ("a fast-growing, venture-funded start up"); the JD says they "developed the tech and data pipelines over three years and invested several million dollars and is now ready to scale". Specific round/stage and investors are not disclosed on the pages read. Founded by Ali Vahabzadeh, described as having "a meaningful exit under his belt".

Locations / HQ: Headquartered in Miami, with the **engineering team co-located in Vancouver**. Site lists a New York (+1 212) phone number. The Greenhouse posting's location field reads "North America/Remote".

Remote policy: Contradictory and unresolved. The posting is listed as "North America/Remote", but the company explicitly describes its engineering team as "co-located in Vancouver". No written remote policy exists on their website. 401k is offered only "for US team members", implying a mixed US/Canada payroll.

Remote-Canada eligibility: Canada employment is clearly possible (engineering is in Vancouver, and the extracted facets read the JD as Canada-eligible). Whether a *fully remote* engineer in interior BC would be accepted is UNVERIFIED — "co-located in Vancouver" points the other way, and this should be asked directly before applying.

Engineering & tech: Python-first backend; migration of hot paths to Rust "for improved performance and reliability"; live data streaming and real-time analytics on Kafka / Pulsar / InfluxDB; AWS; Kubernetes and orchestration tooling; an ML data streaming-analytics platform and reliability backend services; computer-vision models over camera feeds. SOC 2 Type II certified.

Notable / other: Explicitly refuses recruiters/agencies. Benefits listed: competitive salary and "meaningful equity for early team members", unlimited vacation, health/vision/dental, 401k (US only), new hardware. No compensation figure is published on the JD or the site.

Open relevant roles (sample): Backend Developer (listed North America/Remote; engineering is Vancouver-based).
