# Float (Float Financial) — research
Provider/key: ashby:float | company_type: product

What they do: Canadian fintech — "Canada's intelligent financial operating system", combining financial services and software for Canadian businesses. Products: high-limit corporate cards, automated expense management, next-day bill payments, high-yield accounts. States it is trusted by more than 7,500 Canadian companies, "all built in Canada, for Canada."

How they describe themselves: Mission is "to simplify finance for Canadian businesses, empowering them to eliminate complexity and unlock new opportunities." Self-described as "customer-obsessed, passionate and entrepreneurial." Stated culture principles from the JD: "At Float, everyone is an owner"; "we take having a culture based on feedback seriously"; "We openly share our thoughts and differing opinions"; "we keep our decision-making decentralized." Hiring philosophy: "We hire for character, curiosity, and learning velocity before credentials. Titles matter less than impact, and every hire is expected to raise the bar." Explicit ownership/ambiguity framing: "You'll thrive here if you're energized by ambiguity, default to action... This role is best suited to builders who own outcomes — not people who execute tasks assigned to them." Also a "high-trust, high-performance culture" line and "build, grow, and win together."

Size / stage / funding: Private, Series C — announced an $85M Series C (per their own JD). Investors named: Inovia Capital, Growth Equity at Goldman Sachs Alternatives, OMERS Ventures, Silicon Valley Bank. Team includes people from Uber, Stripe, Shopify, Top Hat, Ada, DoorDash, Snowflake, Wealthsimple. Headcount not stated. Described as "one of Canada's fastest growing companies and top-rated startups in 2025, 2024 and 2023."

Locations / HQ: Toronto, Canada.

Remote policy: HYBRID. Their own benefits list: "Hybrid work model – we are based in Toronto with in-office days for connection and collaboration." Catered team lunches Tuesday/Wednesday/Thursday and a dog-friendly office — i.e. in-office attendance is expected on those days.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO for a BC-based worker (verified from the JD itself). The company is Canadian and hires in Canada, but the role is Toronto-hybrid with required in-office days — not workable from Kimberley, BC. This is location-disqualifying, not merely a preference.

Engineering & tech: Data platform on Snowflake with dbt for transformation; ELT/ETL ingestion from banking partners, card processors, CRM, marketing platforms and product systems; orchestration, data observability/alerting, data contracts, CI/CD for data code, lineage/metadata tooling; Python and SQL. Existing data org is small — the Senior Data Engineer listing is explicitly "Float's first dedicated data engineering hire," paired with one Analytics Engineer who owns dbt, plus data scientists. ML pipelines for training/inference are in scope. Regulated/fintech data (transaction, card, banking) with governance and compliance as nice-to-haves. Heavy stated expectation of daily AI tooling use across coding, monitoring and incident triage.

Notable / other: The JD discloses AI use in hiring — "AI-assisted scheduling and candidate communications, and AI-generated interview notes, guides or summaries," with human final decisions. Accessibility/accommodation contact named in the posting. Role framing is high-autonomy greenfield: own Snowflake end-to-end, produce a target-state architecture and phased migration roadmap in the first 60 days.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Data Engineer (Toronto, Canada).
