# Customer.io — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:customerio | company_type: product

What they do: Customer.io is a customer-engagement / marketing-automation platform. Customers build data-driven messaging workflows — email, push, SMS, in-app and webhooks — triggered off behavioural event data streamed from their own products. It is a developer-adjacent product: the value proposition is fine-grained event data and programmable segmentation rather than a drag-and-drop marketing suite. The product surface includes a high-volume email-sending channel (there is a dedicated Email Channel engineering team) and a backend platform group.

How they describe themselves: Remote-first, built on "trust, autonomy, transparency, and collaboration." Their values are reported as **empathy, transparency, responsibility, and "awkwardness"** — the last one explicitly celebrating the individual quirks that make each person distinctive, which is an unusually self-aware framing. They emphasise async communication through documentation and written updates, with deliberately reduced meeting load and "collaboration hours" that overlap across time zones; in-person connection happens through annual company summits and team events rather than daily presence.

Size / stage / funding: Private. Notably **profitable** — the company stage extracted from its own Senior Backend JD is "profitable," which places it outside the burn-rate/runway category. Mid-size (low hundreds of employees), with staff distributed across 40+ countries.

Locations / HQ: No headquarters in a meaningful sense — fully distributed. Team members span the US, Nigeria, Poland, Australia and beyond. Roles are posted by region, e.g. "Americas Remote" and "EMEA Remote."

Remote policy: **Fully remote / remote-first, and long-established as such**, not a post-2020 conversion. Async-by-default with documentation culture, reduced meetings, and collaboration-hours overlap rather than fixed-timezone attendance. Benefits are built for distributed staff: 100% covered medical/dental/vision for the employee and family, unlimited PTO with a recommended *minimum* of 20 days, 16 weeks paid parental leave, and monthly home-office and coworking stipends plus a professional-development budget.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** The live engineering roles are posted as "Americas Remote," and the extracted geo eligibility on the Senior Software Engineer, Backend req is explicitly `canada` with `location_hints: ["Canada"]`. An "Americas Remote" posting with Canada named in the JD is about as clean a signal as this pipeline gets. Timezone extracts as "unclear," which for an async company with collaboration-hours overlap is a mild positive rather than a risk — Mountain Time sits comfortably inside the Americas band.

Engineering & tech: Backend is **Go**, running scalable distributed systems in cloud-native environments across AWS and GCP, with MySQL as the relational store and a real investment in observability. The Senior Backend req asks for "7+ years of experience building scalable distributed systems in Go" — a high, specialist bar rather than a generalist one. Teams are organised by system rather than by feature: Backend, Backend Platform, and Email Channel are separate reqs, which indicates well-scoped ownership. There is also AI-agent work appearing in the stack and a GTM AI Engineer role. Culture on the JD extracts as "balanced" and autonomy as "high."

Compensation: Customer.io posts salary ranges on its engineering roles. Senior Software Engineer, Backend: US$150,000–200,000 base (the JD-extracted range; the registry rollup across the two Senior Backend reqs puts the midpoint at US$175,000). Senior Software Engineer – Backend Platform: US$150,000–200,000. Senior Software Engineer – Email Channel: US$150,000–200,000. The company appears to run a single consistent senior-engineering band in USD regardless of region.

Notable / other: Unlimited PTO with a stated recommended minimum (20 days) is a meaningfully better version of the policy than the usual unlimited-PTO trap. 16 weeks of paid parental leave and 100% family health coverage are both above market for a company of this size. Annual company summit is the main in-person ritual.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Backend (Americas Remote); Senior Software Engineer, Backend (EMEA Remote); Senior Software Engineer – Backend Platform (Americas Remote); Senior Software Engineer – Email Channel (Americas Remote); GTM AI Engineer (Americas Remote).
