# OnePay — research
Provider/key: ashby:oneapp | company_type: product

What they do: OnePay (ATS slug "oneapp") is a US consumer fintech building a single app that bundles banking, lending, payments, risk and crypto features. The engineering surface visible in the registry covers banking, lending, risk, crypto and a platform group — i.e. a consumer money app with the regulated infrastructure that sits behind it.

How they describe themselves: The recruiting boilerplate on every posting is "Competitive base salary, stock options, and health benefits from Day 1" and "Remote-friendly (US), flexible time off (FTO), and opportunities for growth." They describe themselves repeatedly as "a growing, remote organization" and treat documentation as load-bearing because of it: "Create and maintain clear documentation — a must-have for a growing, remote organization" and "As a growing and remote organization, having the necessary documentation is key to our success." That documentation emphasis is a genuine positive signal for async work. No mission statement or values page was fetched — the ATS board is client-rendered and the corporate domain is not derivable from the "oneapp" slug, so the culture read here is JD-only and partial.

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, growth-stage — described in its own JDs as "a growing... organization" and offering stock options on every role. Funding stage, investors and headcount were not verified from any source read here.

Locations / HQ: HQ not verified. All nine engineering postings on record are "United States (Remote)."

Remote policy: Remote-friendly and self-described as a remote organisation, with a documentation-first working style. The qualifier is always "(US)".

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — as close to explicit as the JDs get. Every posting's benefits block states "Remote-friendly (US)", and every location field reads "United States (Remote)". No Canadian posting or Canadian entity appears in the registry.

Engineering & tech: Teams map to product/regulatory domains — Backend Engineer Crypto; Platform Engineer; Platform Engineer (AI and Automation); Platform Engineer, Builder Experience; Software Engineer Banking; Software Engineer Risk; Software Engineer Product Facing; plus recently-closed Lending and Quality Engineering roles. The three Platform Engineer variants (general, AI/automation, Builder Experience) suggest a real internal platform group including developer experience. Specific stack details were not stated in the parts of the JDs read.

Notable / other: Pay is posted on nearly every role and equity is offered on all of them. Observed base ranges: Platform Engineer $170K-$210K; Platform Engineer (AI and Automation) $170K-$210K; Software Engineer, Banking $170K-$210K; Software Engineer, Product Facing $125K-$190K; Backend Engineer, Crypto $140K-$170K; Software Engineer, Risk $130K-$160K. The platform ladder is the best-paid, which is unusual and favourable.

Open relevant roles (sample): Platform Engineer; Platform Engineer (AI and Automation); Platform Engineer, Builder Experience; Backend Engineer, Crypto; Software Engineer, Banking; Software Engineer, Risk.
