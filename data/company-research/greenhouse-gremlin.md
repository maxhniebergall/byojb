# Gremlin — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:gremlin | company_type: product

What they do: Gremlin sells the Gremlin Reliability Platform — chaos-engineering and reliability-testing software. In their words: "Today's complex, fast-paced systems have become a minefield of reliability risks, any of which could cause an outage that costs millions and destroys customer confidence. That's why high-availability teams use Gremlin to find and fix reliability risks before they become incidents." The platform "helps software teams proactively monitor and test their systems for common reliability risks, build and enforce reliability standards, and automate their reliability practices organization-wide." They position themselves as "the industry leader in Chaos Engineering and reliability testing," working with "hundreds of the world's largest organizations where high availability is non-negotiable" — customers range from the Fortune 500 to smaller organizations.

How they describe themselves: The JDs carry a "What Do We Care About" section with three stated principles, quoted:
- **We Care about our People** — "People are our critical differentiators. The company strives to treat our people with respect, empathy, and dignity. We expect that our people will treat each other similarly. In both cases, we will assume good intent. All are welcome at Gremlin. We know our differences make us stronger and that our best ideas and contributions can come from anyone at any level."
- **We Care about Collaboration** — "Gremlin is strongest when we come together as one team with shared goals. Be the glue, not the glitter. But as a remote company, teamwork and collaboration won't happen by accident. We approach every challenge as a shared challenge. We rely on each other for diverse perspectives and creative ideas. We celebrate our wins as a team."
- **We Care about Results** — "Be high productivity, low drama. Results matter. To keep our pace, everyone owns the outcomes of their actions and takes action when needed. We reward speed over perfection. We empower each other to iterate and experiment."
Also: "Gremlin is a team of industry veterans and people eager to learn from one another. We set the standard for reliability…"

Engineering & tech:
- Backend: Java (5+ years required) and Go with "Systems Level Programming"; AWS incl. Lambda/serverless (Google, Oracle clouds also considered); DynamoDB and/or other NoSQL, or major relational DBs; Linux, Docker, Kubernetes, OpenShift; "architecting complex distributed systems and integrating with external systems." Strong stated emphasis on automated testing, CI/CD and engineering best practices. Modern JS (React, TypeScript) is a bonus, not a requirement.
- ML/data: they hold "a proprietary dataset of millions of chaos engineering experiments" and are building automated failure analysis and remediation on top of it — pretraining and fine-tuning models to "detect, classify, and explain failures," "scalable data pipelines and feature stores… for both model training and real-time inference," and techniques including "causal inference, graph ML, time-series modeling, and reinforcement learning," plus "causal AI and agentic systems." MLOps tooling (model serving, monitoring, feature stores) is listed as bonus experience.
- On-call: "Has been on-call and participated in an incident management program" appears as bonus experience in both roles, so an incident-management program exists.
