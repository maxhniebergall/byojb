# Centari — research
Provider/key: ashby:centari | company_type: product

What they do: Centari builds a **deal intelligence platform** for legal and financial professionals. Its proprietary **Deal Reasoning Engine** turns a law firm's private corpus of transactional documents into structured, queryable strategic insight — "unlocking your firm's private deal data" so deal teams negotiate "with the full force of their organization's collective knowledge." Coverage spans M&A, credit finance, fund formation and real estate, and the pitch is a compounding data flywheel: "Every deal a firm runs through Centari makes the next one sharper." Customers include Ropes & Gray, Willkie, Fried Frank, Wilson Sonsini and practices across the AmLaw 25 and Magic Circle; press reporting says it serves nearly half of the AmLaw 10. It reportedly reached seven-figure revenue within four months of going to market.

How they describe themselves: From their own JD — "We are drawn to complexity because that is where true innovation happens. If you're a builder who thrives in **ambiguity** and operates with **radical ownership**, we'd love to meet you." The engineering framing: "You'll be a key player in designing and building Centari's core AI technology, working alongside engineers who share a **bias for action**. As one team, you'll write code, shape the product, **interact with customers**, and drive initiatives from concept to completion." Requirements repeat this: "**Willingness to roll up sleeves and get things done** - our engineers are comfortable **switching between different technical domains** as needed," and "Strong collaboration skills and ability to work effectively in a **fast-paced environment**." Offered: "Build at the frontier of AI for knowledge work, with a highly engaged user base from top firms. **Flat organizational structure** promoting autonomy and direct impact. **Meaningful equity** in a high-growth startup. Competitive salary. **100% remote. Flexible PTO.**"

Size / stage / funding: **Early-stage startup.** Founded 2023 by Kevin Walker, a former Paul Hastings M&A lawyer; team blends former attorneys (Kirkland & Ellis, Davis Polk) with ML engineers. Raised an oversubscribed **$8.4M Series A** (September 2025) led by Sentinel Global, bringing **$14M total** raised, with participation from GTMfund, South Park Commons, Alt Capital, RiverPark Ventures and Recall Capital. Angel/strategic backers named on the JD include Jeremy Kranz (former DoorDash board), Andy Appelbaum (Seamless) and Jack Altman. Small headcount; runway-dependent.

Locations / HQ: New York, NY. Canadian reqs are labelled "Canada - Toronto, Canada - Ottawa."

Remote policy: The JD states "**100% remote. Flexible PTO.**" and "Flat organizational structure promoting autonomy and direct impact."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** Centari runs a dedicated Canadian hiring track — two live reqs are titled "Senior Software Engineer, Backend **(Canada)**" and "Senior Software Engineer, Product **(Canada)**", priced in CAD, with a Canada-specific compensation tier in the ATS. The JD says 100% remote. The city labels (Toronto, Ottawa) appear to be nominal anchors rather than office requirements given the stated remote policy, but they hint at where the existing Canadian cluster sits — worth confirming, since a NY-HQ startup with an Eastern-time Canadian cluster will run on Eastern, two hours ahead of Mountain.

Engineering & tech: **Go** on the backend with **React/TypeScript** on the front end, "leveraging cutting-edge LLM technologies across infrastructure, search, and persistence layers." Domain content includes LLMs, **knowledge graphs**, **semantic search** and retrieval over complex legal documents. Asks for 5+ years building production applications in Go or another strongly typed language, tech-lead or end-to-end project ownership experience, and lists as nice-to-haves enterprise SaaS or regulated-industry experience (legal, financial services, healthcare), startup experience, and specialisation in either full-stack product development or data/ML infrastructure.

Notable / other: Engineers are expected to interact with customers directly. Equity is offered on the Canadian reqs.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Backend (Canada) — Toronto/Ottawa; Senior Software Engineer, Product (Canada) — Toronto/Ottawa; Senior Software Engineer, Backend; Senior Software Engineer, AI.

Pay: Posted directly in the ATS on both Canadian reqs — **CAD $130,000–190,000** base, plus "Offers Equity" (Canada compensation tier).
