# DELFI Diagnostics, Inc. — research
Provider/key: lever:delfidiagnostics | company_type: product

What they do: Cancer-diagnostics company building blood-based ("liquid biopsy") early-detection tests. Its platform is built on "fragmentomics" — cell-free DNA fragmentation patterns read from whole-genome sequencing and classified with machine learning. First product is FirstLook Lung, a laboratory-developed screening test for lung-cancer-screening-eligible patients (not FDA cleared/approved). Also works on treatment-response monitoring.

How they describe themselves: Mission is to "make cancer less deadly through widely accessible early detection." Three stated values, quoted from their own pages/JD:
- "Lead with Science, Anchor in Pragmatism: We pioneer life-changing science by ensuring quality, transparency, and rigor at all times. We explore thoughtfully, experiment smartly, and deliver impact with conviction."
- "Build With & For All: We embrace diverse backgrounds to innovate and achieve together. We are not just building a product - we aim to disrupt the path of cancer for all - no matter geography or socioeconomic class."
- "Put We over I: We are a home for high-performing people. Through teamwork, we build collective intelligence... We show up with empathy, humility, and integrity at every step of the journey."
Tone is mission-led and science-first rather than hustle/rocketship.

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2019. Private. Over $325M raised — $100M Series A (Jan 2021) and $225M Series B (Jul 2022); strategic investors include Eli Lilly, Merck and OrbiMed. Headcount not disclosed; the about page describes executive, clinical/data science, engineering, lab ops, medical affairs, product, quality, regulatory, finance, HR and commercial functions.

Locations / HQ: HQ and CLIA laboratory at 1810 Embarcadero Road, Suite 100, Palo Alto, CA. Labs/offices also in Baltimore, MD.
