# Pinterest — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:pinterest | company_type: product

What they do: Pinterest is a public (NYSE: PINS) visual discovery and inspiration platform where users save and discover ideas ("Pins"). Revenue is almost entirely advertising — Ads Delivery, Ads Measurement, Monetization and Shopping/Merchant surfaces are the largest engineering investment areas. It also runs tvScientific (a connected-TV ad business appearing under the same Greenhouse board), whose roles are posted alongside Pinterest's own.

How they describe themselves: "Millions of people around the world come to our platform to find creative ideas, dream about new possibilities and plan for memories that will last a lifetime. At Pinterest, we're on a mission to bring everyone the inspiration to create a life they love, and that starts with the people behind the product." Careers copy frames the pitch as "Discover a career where you ignite innovation for millions, transform passion into growth opportunities, celebrate each other's unique experiences and embrace the flexibility to do your best work. Creating a career you love? It's Possible." They now foreground AI in hiring: "At Pinterest, AI isn't just a feature, it's a powerful partner that augments our creativity and amplifies our impact… what matters most is that you can always explain your approach, showing us not just what you know, but how you think," and they publish a separate "philosophy on AI in hiring" page.

Size / stage / funding: Large public company, roughly 4,500–5,000 employees, profitable on a non-GAAP basis with multi-billion-dollar annual revenue. Not runway-dependent — a stability green signal.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco. Other engineering geos in the registry's live postings: Palo Alto, Seattle, Chicago, and **Toronto, ON** (the Canadian office).

Remote policy: "PinFlex." Their own page: "For roles without a regular office requirement, employees may live anywhere within their country or region of employment," but "for positions that require regular in-office collaboration, employees are expected to live within a specific area or region," and "Leaders define when in-person connection is essential and how often teams will meet in-person." Every employee is expected in an office for in-person collaboration moments during the year; Pinterest provides WiFi/mobile/commuter subsidies and a home-office stipend, and covers travel for outlying employees.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Mostly NO, with one exception.** Of the ~52 live postings on record, the overwhelming majority are tagged "Remote, US" — US-only remote. The Canadian roles are almost all tagged plainly "Toronto, ON, CA", and at least one (Sr. Software Engineer, Backend, gh_jid 5572858) carries `#LI-HYBRID` and "This position is not eligible for relocation assistance" — i.e. a Toronto-area hybrid role, not remote-BC. The clear exception is **Staff Machine Learning Engineer, Shopping Merchants (gh_jid 8049465)**, tagged "Toronto, ON, CA, Remote, Canada" and carrying a "Canada based applicants only" CAD pay range — that one is genuinely remote-within-Canada. A handful of others are tagged "Remote, CA, US" (gh_jid 7494938, 7958766), where "CA" is ambiguous between California and Canada and should be verified per-posting. Net: Pinterest can employ a Canada-based remote worker, but only on the small subset of reqs explicitly opened to Canada; the deep-infrastructure roles are US-remote.

Engineering & tech: Very large, well-differentiated infrastructure organization — live reqs include Batch Processing Platform, Big Data Storage, Capacity Engineering, Observability, Service Communications, Edge, Compute Platform, Online Systems, Data Product Platform and AI Platform, plus a separate security engineering org (Application Security, Detection & Response, Corporate Security, Security Operations). ML engineering is a first-class ladder of its own (Applied Science, Computer Vision, Responsible AI, Ads Delivery, Agentic Ads, Content Ecosystem). This is a company where platform/infra is genuinely well-resourced and roles are narrowly scoped, which is unusual and valuable.

Compensation transparency: Pinterest posts a salary range on essentially every JD, including geo-specific ones — US reqs post USD ranges, Canadian reqs post a "Canada based applicants only" CAD range. Ranges are base salary; the JD notes the offer depends on "location, travel, relevant prior experience, or particular skills and expertise," with equity and benefits separate. See `data/company-comp/greenhouse-pinterest.md`.

Notable / other: The hiring process explicitly expects candidates to work with AI tools and explain their reasoning. tvScientific postings on this board are a distinct CTV-advertising business and should not be read as core Pinterest infrastructure.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer, Batch Processing Platform; Staff Software Engineer, Big Data Storage; Staff Software Engineer, Observability; Staff Software Engineer, Capacity Engineering; Principal Engineer, Compute Platform; Staff Machine Learning Engineer, Shopping Merchants (Remote, Canada); Sr. Software Engineer, Backend (Toronto, hybrid).
