# DuckDuckGo — research
Provider/key: ashby:duck-duck-go | company_type: product

What they do: DuckDuckGo is a consumer online-privacy company. It operates a privacy-preserving search engine, a browser on Mac/Windows/iOS/Android, and a paid "DuckDuckGo subscription" (VPN, personal-information removal, identity restoration). It also ships privacy-oriented AI products, notably Duck.ai, which brokers private chat with third-party models (ChatGPT, Claude, others) without tying conversations to a user identity. Founded 2008; revenue is advertising- and subscription-based rather than data-brokerage-based.

How they describe themselves: Mission is "to raise the standard of trust online." Their framing of the product thesis is stated plainly: "the best way to protect your personal information from hackers, scammers, and privacy-invasive companies is to stop it from being collected at all." Their stated culture pillars are trust, inclusivity, and "empowered project management" — described as "each team member takes full ownership of their projects, from scoping and execution to postmortem," and pitched to candidates as "if you're seeking end-to-end ownership of your work, you've come to the right place." Hiring emphasises "hardworking, self-motivated individuals," diversity, and self-direction. Notably absent from their own copy: hustle/rocketship/fast-paced language.

Size / stage / funding: 300+ team members (their careers page says 335+ across 15+ countries). Private, independent, **profitable since 2014**, annual revenue now exceeds $100M USD. Not venture-runway dependent. They report 95%+ average retention over several years while roughly tripling in size.

Locations / HQ: Nominally Paoli, Pennsylvania, USA, but functionally a distributed company — team members in 15+ countries.

Remote policy: Fully remote / remote-first, and long-established as such (not a post-2020 conversion). **No core hours** — team members work when they are most productive, with an average full-time commitment of ~40 h/week. Time off is taken as needed. Two in-person trips per year are expected: an all-hands meetup and a team retreat, ~4-5 days each. Meetings are on-camera video.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** Their live Senior Backend Engineer posting lists "Canada (Remote)" among 25 eligible countries (alongside USA, UK, Ireland, most of the EU, Brazil, Mexico, Argentina, Uruguay, South Africa, Israel). One caveat they state explicitly: "Eligibility for company-sponsored health benefits is limited to team members based in the United States. This program does not extend to team members located in other countries, such as Canada or the UK." So Canada is hireable, but health coverage is not provided the way it is for US staff.

Compensation & transparency: Unusually transparent. The JD states the number directly: "$178,500 USD annually and stock options. Compensation is transparent across the organization, and all team members within the same professional level and global region receive the same compensation." Their careers page restates the philosophy: "Everyone within the same professional level and global region earns the same pay, regardless of factors like race, gender, and age." Practical implication: pay is a fixed level-and-region number, not negotiated — and the posted figure may be region-adjusted outside the US. Additional stated benefits: paid parental leave, office-setup and co-working allowances.

Engineering & tech: Backend Functional Team works in **Perl 5, Go, and Node.js**. Engineering is organised into "functional teams" with project-based ownership end to end (scoping → execution → postmortem). Recent stated project areas: AI-assisted answers integrated into Search while preserving the privacy model; competitive improvements to Duck.ai for free and paying users; large-scale data-processing pipelines maintaining search relevancy. The senior JD asks for 7+ years backend on consumer-facing products, familiarity with AI agent tooling (eval frameworks, RAG pipelines, prompt engineering), strong DS&A, and mentoring experience.

Notable / other: Hiring process includes a **paid test project** and is explicitly two-way. They publicly disclose that AI tooling is used in application/test-project review, with human final decisions, and offer an alternative selection process on request. Background check required. A "Team Member Support Guide" is published covering wellbeing, parental leave, and allowances.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Backend Engineer (Perl/Go/Node; USA, Canada, UK, EU and 20+ other countries, remote).
