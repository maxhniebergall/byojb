# Magnet Forensics — research
Provider/key: lever:magnetforensics | company_type: product

What they do: Develops digital-investigative / digital-forensics software that acquires, analyzes and shares evidence from computers, smartphones, tablets and IoT devices. Customers are law-enforcement agencies, national-security bodies and corporate investigation/incident-response teams ("thousands of customers globally"). The Griffeye product line (referenced in an open AI/ML role) is their media-analysis/CSAM-investigation suite.

How they describe themselves: From their own JD text — "a global leader in the development of digital investigative software... We are continually innovating so our customers can deploy advanced and effective tools to protect their companies, communities, and countries." On engineering: "The Engineering Organization is focused on producing software that solves the most important problems facing digital forensics and incident response professionals today. Our algorithms make sense of data that is often hidden, corrupted, or incomplete. We write well-tested, performant code and build intuitive UIs because we know that minutes can make a difference in national terrorism cases and life-or-death situations." Culture in their words: "a community of smart, passionate, humble people who recognize that solving the industry's toughest problems requires constant collaboration, continuous learning, and frequent doses of humour." Notably absent from their JD language: any "fast-paced / hustle / rocketship" framing.
