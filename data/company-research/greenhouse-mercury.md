# Mercury — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:mercury | company_type: product

What they do: Mercury provides banking and financial software for startups and small businesses — business checking and savings, payments, corporate cards, treasury/investments, and related financial workflows. Their JDs carry the standing disclaimer "*Mercury is a fintech company, not an FDIC-insured bank," but that is changing: Mercury announced on December 19, 2025 that it had submitted an application to the OCC for a national bank charter and applied for FDIC deposit insurance, and on April 27, 2026 announced conditional approval. In their words, "Mercury has the universe's cosmic blessing to build a bank, and we are full speed ahead." A major current engineering thrust is connecting "our modern, product-focused engineering systems to enterprise core banking systems and payment networks built in a different era, with different assumptions and different interfaces."

How they describe themselves: The JD voice is distinctive and literary — requisitions open with extended historical or scientific analogies (the Space Shuttle's Rendezvous, Proximity Operations and Docking subsystems for the Banking Integration Platform team; William Gilbert's 1600 *De Magnete* for the AI Engineering team). Stated stance on AI: "At Mercury, we're making a deliberate, company-wide bet on AI… to take those scattered experiments and turn them into shared infrastructure, shared context, and shared capability. The goal is a multiplier effect." On the bank build: "Building Mercury Bank is the most important adventure the company is embarking on." They describe the integration work as "some of the most consequential infrastructure work at Mercury," and value engineers who build "systems that are technically correct and operationally trustworthy" and "clean abstractions that hide the complexity underneath." They also say plainly that priorities move: "This list is illustrative. Priorities will shift as we learn; the right person will help choose the next highest-leverage work," and they ask for someone "high-agency and self-directed" who "can operate effectively without tightly-defined scope." Mercury "values diversity & belonging and is proud to be an Equal Employment Opportunity employer."

Size / stage / funding: Private, late-stage and well capitalized; widely reported as profitable, with a national bank charter conditionally approved as of April 2026. Not an early-stage or runway-dependent company. The charter process itself brings heavy regulatory scrutiny and a long, deliberate build.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco, CA, with offices in New York, NY and Portland, OR.

Remote policy: Every relevant live requisition carries the same location line: "San Francisco, CA, New York, NY, Portland, OR, or Remote within Canada or United States." Compensation is quoted in two currencies with the explicit qualifier "any location" on both — "US employees (any location)" and "Canadian employees (any location)" — indicating no geographic pay tiering within a country and no city or time-zone restriction.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, unrestricted.** "Remote within Canada or United States" appears on all nine live relevant postings, and every one of them publishes a distinct Canadian base-salary range in CAD for "Canadian employees (any location)". British Columbia and Mountain Time are unproblematic; no time-zone constraint is stated anywhere.

Engineering & tech: Two clearly infrastructure-shaped tracks in the current openings.
- **Banking Integration Platform**: building Mercury's integration with an FFIEC-approved bank core and connections to payment networks; designing internal APIs that "give product teams simple, consistent interfaces to complex external systems"; handling retries, failures, format mismatches and downtime; building data pipelines that keep Mercury's systems in sync with the bank core; owning monitoring, alerting and recovery for the most critical external connections; defining clean boundaries and reliable contracts with other teams. Asks for 7+ years building and operating backend infrastructure and distributed systems at scale, preferably integrating modern systems with legacy infrastructure in a highly-regulated environment. "Every account opening, every monetary transaction, and every balance call will flow through the systems you build."
- **AI Engineering (internal AI platform)**: building and evolving MCP servers "that connect internal systems and data sources into a coherent interface for agents and engineers"; expanding and operating "our LLM gateway infrastructure: routing, rate limiting, cost attribution, and observability across teams"; shared prompt libraries, guardrails and policy-as-code; a structured, agent-consumable company knowledge layer with retrieval; sandbox environments and self-service scaffolding; "playgrounds and evaluation harnesses so internal AI agents can be tested and iterated in controlled environments before hitting production." Asks for 5+ years backend in complex production systems, fluency across languages, platform/infra/devtools range, and shipped LLM systems (RAG, agents, eval frameworks) with real understanding of "cost modeling, observability, latency, and safety — not just the exciting parts."
Other open areas: Investments, Stability, Fraud, and general Product engineering. Mercury's backend is well known publicly for its heavy use of Haskell alongside other languages.

Notable / other: Mercury is unusually transparent on pay, publishing paired US and Canadian ranges on every requisition.
