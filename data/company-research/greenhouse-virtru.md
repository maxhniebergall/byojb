# Virtru — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:virtru | company_type: product

What they do: Virtru is a data-centric security company: instead of locking data down, it encrypts data itself so it can be shared with persistent access control. Products are end-to-end encryption and access control layered over Gmail/Outlook, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, files and SaaS apps, plus an open platform built on the Trusted Data Format (TDF) open standard and the OpenTDF open-source project. Customers are Fortune 500 enterprises and US federal/government agencies (6,100+ customers, 1M+ active users, ~6M emails/files protected weekly per their own site).

How they describe themselves: Mission on their own site — "Unlock the power of data by creating a world where it is always under your control, everywhere," with the tagline "Respect the People. Respect the Data." In their JD they frame themselves against the industry: "While the rest of the security industry obsesses over locking data down to prevent it from being lost or stolen, we're doing something fundamentally different at Virtru. We're setting data free… without sacrificing security, privacy, or control," and describe the platform as "the Android of data protection: a robust platform with an open core." The JD lists "Virtruvian qualities": thinking outside the box to "respectfully challenge your teammates and managers in the pursuit of excellence"; "strong sense of urgency with an action-oriented mindset"; "able to collaborate and adapt to shifting priorities as business needs evolve"; and "comfortable with asynchronous communication including slack, email, zoom." Wellbeing framing is explicit and structured (Occupational, Mental, Social, Physical, Environmental Wellness), and they run a DB&I Council with psychological safety named as a priority.

Size / stage / funding: Founded November 2012, private, VC-backed. Engineering & tech: Go is the core backend language; the platform services are Go-based and tied to the open-source OpenTDF project. Also in play: gRPC/ConnectRPC and REST APIs, both microservice and monolithic architectures, distributed systems, Docker + Kubernetes in production, CI/CD and GitOps (Helm, ArgoCD), Terraform IaC across multi-cloud, and multi-language SDKs (Go, Java, JavaScript). Deployment targets are unusually demanding: on-premise, air-gapped, sovereign cloud and bare-metal alongside cloud-native. Nice-to-haves point at cryptographic primitives/protocols/key management, identity federation (OIDC, SAML, federated trust systems), OCI container standards, and Rust/Python. The eng culture described is design-doc-driven ("develop well-defined written design documents," "a culture of technical excellence") with mentoring via code review and pairing. They explicitly expect engineers to "leverage AI coding agents to accelerate development."

Notable / other: Real open-source identity — OpenTDF is theirs and the TDF format is an open standard, so a meaningful share of the work is public. Team structure for this role is "a small, focused team" inside a broader engineering org.
