# Liquid AI — research
Provider/key: ashby:liquid-ai | company_type: product

What they do: Liquid AI is a foundation-model company. Their own description: "Spun out of MIT CSAIL, we build general-purpose AI systems that run efficiently across deployment targets, from data center accelerators to on-device hardware, ensuring low latency, minimal memory usage, privacy, and reliability. We partner with enterprises across consumer electronics, automotive, life sciences, and financial services. We are scaling rapidly and need exceptional people to help us get there." Their models are branded Liquid Foundation Models (LFMs); the technical thesis is efficiency and edge/on-device deployment rather than maximum-scale frontier models.

How they describe themselves: There is no published values list in the material available — the JDs instead state per-role expectations that read as de facto culture. Quoted: "Takes ownership: Owns customer recommendation system engagements end-to-end, from requirements through delivery and evaluation."; "Thinks at scale"; "Is pragmatic: Optimizes for measurable customer outcomes (engagement, conversion, revenue lift) over theoretical novelty."; "Communicates clearly: Can translate between customer business metrics and internal technical decisions, and push back when needed." Note "We are scaling rapidly" — growth-stage framing. The recurring job title for essentially all technical staff is "Member of Technical Staff", the frontier-lab convention that deliberately flattens levelling.

Size / stage / funding: Early/growth-stage venture-backed AI lab, spun out of MIT CSAIL. Only 16 total postings. Exact headcount and funding stage were NOT verified this session — no first-party liquid.ai URL appears anywhere in the registry, so nothing was fetched. Treat size, funding and runway as unverified. What is clear from their own text is that they are "scaling rapidly", i.e. in an expansion phase rather than a steady state.

Locations / HQ: Boston, Massachusetts (the MIT CSAIL lineage) with a San Francisco presence. Postings list "San Francisco, Remote, Boston" or, for two roles, "San Francisco, Boston" only.

Remote policy: Partially remote. Seven of the nine live postings include "Remote" alongside San Francisco and Boston; two (Applied ML RecSys, and Multi-Modal Audio) are office-only. No stated async/handbook culture; benefits mention 401(k) matching up to 4% of base pay, which is a US-payroll indicator.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO / unverified-negative. Although "Remote" appears in most postings, it is unqualified — no country scope is stated — and a full-text scan of every fetched Liquid AI JD returned zero occurrences of "Canada"/"Canadian". The only benefits mentioned are US-style (401(k)), which strongly implies US-payroll-only remote. No evidence of a Canadian employing entity. Treat as not Canada-eligible absent direct confirmation.

Engineering & tech: The work is model-centric rather than infrastructure-centric. Open roles cover distributed training, GPU performance, edge inference, post-training (applied, audio, vision), multi-modal (vision and audio), ML research engineering for data, RecSys, and a Japanese-localization ML role. Stack signals: Python and PyTorch, with "autonomous coding and debugging ability"; sequential recommendation architectures named explicitly (HSTU, SASRec, BERT4Rec); large-scale data pipelines for user interaction data and feature engineering; task-specific evaluation design (ranking quality, latency, throughput); serving under latency and throughput constraints.

Notable / other: A significant structural feature: the applied roles are customer-delivery roles, not product-platform roles. The RecSys JD says the person will "Act as the technical owner for enterprise customer engagements involving recommendation and ranking workloads", "Translate customer requirements into concrete specifications", and, between engagements, "build reusable applied tooling and workflows that accelerate future delivery". Success in year one is defined as "Independently owns and delivers enterprise recommendation system engagements with minimal oversight" and being "trusted by customers as the technical owner". That is a forward-deployed / solutions-engineering shape wrapped in a research-lab title.
