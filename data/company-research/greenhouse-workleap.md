# Workleap — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:workleap | company_type: product

What they do: Workleap is a **Montreal-based software company, founded in 2006** (formerly GSoft). In their own words: *"We're builders at heart, we make simple products that actually matter to the people who use them."* They run **two product lines**: (1) **The Workleap Agent**, described as *"our agentic HR platform that helps managers become better leaders"*; and (2) **ShareGate**, *"the world's leading solution for Microsoft 365 migration and governance."* They state that *"more than 15,000 companies worldwide"* use their products. ShareGate is the more infrastructure-flavoured line — tenant migration, governance, protection, and now a new **Backup** capability described as *"one of our customers' top requests"* and *"a first for ShareGate in the migration space."*

How they describe themselves: The careers site names five principles: **Keep it simple; Make customers fall in love with us; Do what it takes; Move fast; Raise the bar.** Hiring tone is selective — *"We're intentional about who joins us. If you're the kind of person who gets excited by a hard problem and wants to help shape what comes next, there's a place for you here."* Alongside that, they describe deliberate communication design: they have *"created specific principles to maximize our communications and collaborations,"* prioritising **written, asynchronous communication**, with office attendance optional outside required gatherings. The Staff JD backs this up concretely: *"With a light meeting load and mostly async collaboration, you will have the space to prioritize deep work and problem solving."*

Size / stage / funding: Private, ~20 years old, Montreal. Headcount not stated on the pages reached (commonly reported in the several-hundred range — unverified here). No funding, burn or runway language anywhere; the 2006 founding date and 15,000-customer base point to an established, self-sustaining business rather than a venture-timeline company. Recognised as a **Canada Top 100 Employer** and a **Best Managed Companies** designate.

Locations / HQ: HQ Montreal, Quebec, with a physical Montreal office. Hiring is Canada-wide.

Remote policy: **Remote-first across Canada**, stated on their careers page — employees can *"work from anywhere in Canada,"* and may work abroad up to **150 days annually**. Office attendance is optional except for **two mandatory annual in-person gatherings**: *"We host some must-attend events throughout the year where employees can get together."* Async-and-written is the stated default mode.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified YES — the strongest in this batch.** Every live relevant posting is tagged **"Canada - Remote"**, salaries are quoted in CAD, and the JDs state the range *"reflects our Canada-wide compensation scale"* with *"final offers may be adjusted based on the candidate's region to align with local market conditions."* That last clause is worth noting: a Canada-wide scale with regional adjustment means a Kimberley BC offer may land below the Montreal/Toronto midpoint. Mountain Time is two hours behind Montreal, which is workable for an async-first org; the two mandatory annual gatherings imply travel to Montreal twice a year.

Engineering & tech: ShareGate is a Microsoft-ecosystem product suite (M365 migration, governance, protection, backup) with both desktop and web surfaces — the open roles span "Migrate [Core]", "Migrate [Desktop]", "Migrate [Web Experience]", "Platform", "Protect" and "Backup", so the org is split into product-area squads with a distinct Platform team (React on the front end for that role). The Staff Backup role is architecture-first: *"own the architecture, define the long-term technical vision, and help build the team,"* designing *"a new backup system that is reliable, automated, and easy to use, capable of meeting operational needs and recovery requirements"* with *"scalability, security, and long-term maintainability."* Most distinctive practice: they are pushing **spec-driven development** — *"features are designed upfront through detailed technical specifications before any code is written, enabling agentic AI-assisted development, faster reviews, and higher quality delivery"* — and expect the staff engineer to *"champion"* it. Weekly rhythm is described as writing specs, using agentic AI dev tools, reviewing code and designs, facilitating design discussions, and writing code.

Notable / other: **Pay is posted in CAD on their own JDs**, which is unusual for Canadian postings and makes them unusually comparable: **$100K–$130K CAD** for Software Developer II roles and **$140K–$180K CAD** for the Staff Software Developer. The Staff role also carries team-building duties (hiring, onboarding, mentoring in partnership with a Software Development Manager). Ladder naming is "Software Developer II" → "Staff Software Developer" — note there is no "Senior" title visible in the current openings between those two rungs.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Developer - ShareGate Backup (Canada - Remote); Software Developer II (React), Platform - ShareGate; Software Developer II - ShareGate Migrate [Core] / [Desktop] / [Web Experience]; Software Developer II - ShareGate Protect.
