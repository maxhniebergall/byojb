# Nucs AI — research
Provider/key: recruitee:nucsai | company_type: product

What they do: Per their own posting, "Nucs AI is revolutionizing prostate cancer care through cutting-edge AI and medical imaging technology. Founded in 2024 by a multidisciplinary team of oncologists, AI researchers, and healthcare innovators, we're tackling one of medicine's most pressing challenges: the growing demand for accurate, timely cancer diagnostics in the face of rising scan volumes and limited radiologist capacity." Stated mission: "to enhance diagnostic precision and expand access to expert-level prostate cancer screening, ultimately improving patient outcomes worldwide."

How they describe themselves: Impact-led framing — "Your work will directly contribute to advancing cancer care technology. Every system you build, every optimization you make, and every integration you design helps medical professionals provide better care to patients facing one of life's most challenging diagnoses." No separate values list was available. Language is mission/urgency-flavoured ("revolutionizing", "cutting-edge") rather than process- or craft-flavoured.

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2024 — a very early-stage company. No headcount or funding figure available from any reachable page. Registry shows 15 live postings across the org (3 counted relevant), which for a two-year-old company indicates rapid hiring.

Locations / HQ: Multi-city, Europe-weighted. The posting lists Berlin, Warsaw, London, New York, Munich, Budapest, Milan, Prague, Paris, **Toronto (Ontario, Canada)**, Boston, "+10 more". The application form's phone-number default is Germany, and the work-location question offers a fixed list of those cities. HQ not stated; the German default and the Berlin/Munich weighting suggest a German or wider-European base.

Remote policy: Listed as "Remote, Hybrid" — i.e. mixed, and tied to one of the named city locations rather than location-independent.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Partial and probably not workable from Kimberley.** Canada does appear — the location picker offers "Canada (Toronto, Ontario, Canada)" — but it is offered as a *city* option within a "Remote, Hybrid" posting, not as remote-anywhere-in-Canada. A Toronto-anchored hybrid role is effectively disqualifying for someone in British Columbia who cannot relocate. There is also no evidence of Mountain-Time-friendly hours: the centre of gravity is European, which would put core hours 8-9 hours ahead of MT.

Engineering & tech: Explicitly full-stack despite the "Senior Software Engineer" title — the body opens "Join our engineering team as a Full Stack Engineer". Backend: Python with Flask or FastAPI, SQL/relational modelling, RabbitMQ event-driven processing, microservices on Kubernetes, GCP, CI/CD with testing/monitoring/rollback, monitoring-logging-alerting, healthcare integrations over HL7 and FHIR. Frontend: TypeScript and React for clinical workflows. Bar is 3+ years of full-stack experience. Bonus: healthcare/medical software, GDPR and ISO 13485 regulatory knowledge, ML/analytics in production workflows, threat modelling and compliance, Terraform or Pulumi, and medical-imaging/graphics frameworks (WebGL, WebGPU, Cornerstone3D, VTK.js).

Notable / other: The interview process is published up front: a 10-minute phone screen on career highlights, a technical interview with live problem-solving, and a cultural interview. No compensation is stated anywhere in the posting.
