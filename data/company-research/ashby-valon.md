# Valon Tech — research
Provider/key: ashby:valon | company_type: product

What they do: Valon builds what it calls "the AI-native operating system for regulated finance, starting with mortgage servicing." Unusually, it does not sell software on top of incumbents — it built and operates its own mortgage servicing business, currently managing "$110+ billion in loans," and uses that operational depth to build the product. Its platform, ValonOS, is pitched as a unified system that makes "every process structured and programmable," with AI agents layered on the resulting structured data. Enterprise contracts are described as "now deploying across the industry."

How they describe themselves (their own words, from their live JDs): "Valon is building the AI-native operating system for regulated finance, starting with mortgage servicing." / "We're a Series C company backed by a16z, transforming industries that others have written off as too complex to innovate." / "Rather than build on top of broken legacy systems, we took a different approach: we built and operate our own mortgage servicing business... This wasn't the end goal, it was how we deeply understood the complexity needed to build software that actually works in regulated industries." / "We've transformed mortgage servicing from a 0% margin business into 60%+ margins while dramatically improving customer experience." / "Mortgage servicing is just the beginning of our vision to transform regulated industries and beyond."
