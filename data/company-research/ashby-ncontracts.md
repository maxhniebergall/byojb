# Ncontracts — research
Provider/key: ashby:ncontracts | company_type: product

What they do: Ncontracts sells integrated risk-management and compliance (GRC) software to financial institutions — enterprise risk management, vendor/third-party risk, compliance and lending compliance, audit management, findings management. It serves over 5,000 financial-industry clients: banks, credit unions, mortgage companies, wealth-management firms and fintechs. Founded 2009.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "to provide actionable guidance and intelligent software that enables financial organizations of all sizes to thrive by proactively managing risk and compliance." In their JDs — "Headquartered in Nashville, Tennessee, Ncontracts leads the industry in integrated risk management and compliance solutions… As a seven-time Inc. 5000 Fastest Growing Companies honoree and consistent year-over-year recipient of 'Best Places to Work' awards, we offer a thriving work environment where career growth and life-work balance go hand in hand." The engineering ladder JDs list explicit expected behaviours: **Honesty** ("Whether reviewing another developer's code, participating in retrospectives, or shaping technical direction, we expect openness and honesty. Honesty creates trust, and we believe all great teams are built on trust"); **Low ego** ("Strong opinions, loosely held – and the maturity to build consensus rather than win arguments"); **Deep curiosity**; and for L5, Leadership beyond your team, Architecture at scale, Force multiplier, Strategic ownership, and "AI-forward engineering" (defining how teams use AI assistants "ensuring acceleration never comes at the cost of quality or security").

Size / stage / funding: ~350 employees, private, backed by **Gryphon Investors** (private equity). Seven-time Inc. 5000 honoree — i.e. sustained high growth, PE-owned rather than VC-runway-dependent.

Locations / HQ: Nashville, Tennessee.

Remote policy: All seven engineering postings in the registry are listed as "Remote". The about page does not state a formal remote policy, so the evidence is the reqs themselves rather than a policy statement.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NOT EVIDENCED — treat as no. Engineering & tech: A clearly defined internal ladder — L4 = "Senior Developer" who "owns the architecture and delivery of large features within a squad"; L5 = operates across squads, "defining the standards, patterns, and platform capabilities that multiple teams build on… architect complex, distributed systems that span multiple services and teams, design for high availability and resilience". Stack: C#, JavaScript/TypeScript, Python; "cloud architecture expertise in Azure and/or AWS, including infrastructure-as-code"; "fluency with event/message-driven architecture and distributed systems". The published level definitions are an unusually good documentation signal.
