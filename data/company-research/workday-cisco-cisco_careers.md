# Cisco Systems — research
Provider/key: workday:cisco/cisco_careers | company_type: product
What they do: Cisco is the incumbent enterprise networking vendor — routing/switching, data-centre networking, observability (ThousandEyes, Splunk), and cloud-native security (Isovalent/Cilium, Tetragon, Hypershield). The engineering roles in the registry are concentrated in exactly the infrastructure end of that: eBPF/Tetragon security engineering, Kubernetes control planes in Go, SRE fleet and Kubernetes-platform work, network data-plane engineering, Splunk analytics/search, and data engineering.
How they describe themselves: The Workday JDs carry standard large-enterprise framing (inclusion, "Cisco is an Affirmative Action and Equal Opportunity Employer") rather than a distinct culture statement; the product-line JDs (Isovalent, Hypershield, ThousandEyes) read as deep-systems engineering rather than growth/hustle. No first-party culture page was fetched.
Size / stage / funding: Public (NASDAQ: CSCO), roughly 90,000 employees, decades-old, consistently profitable and dividend-paying. Maximum stability, with the usual large-cap caveat of periodic restructurings.
Locations / HQ: HQ San Jose, CA. Live relevant postings span San Jose, San Francisco, Seattle, Milpitas, RTP NC, Boston, Annapolis Junction MD, Miami, London UK, Krakow, Zurich, Bangalore and Hyderabad. Several postings are multi-location umbrellas ("133 Locations", "52 Locations").
Remote policy: Predominantly hybrid. Extracted policies on the roles that state one are "hybrid, us_only" (Senior SWE Backend; Principal Tetragon), "remote, us_only" (Senior SWE Control Plane – Hypershield), and "hybrid, eu_only" (SWE Technical Leader, Isovalent). One SRE role is titled "(Remote)" but is anchored to Boston, MA.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO, on the evidence available. Not one of the 31 live relevant postings is Canadian, and the roles that state geography state us_only or eu_only. Cisco does operate Canadian offices (Toronto/Ottawa) as a matter of general knowledge, but no Canada-eligible opening appears in this registry, and the US-remote roles are explicitly US-only — so from Kimberley BC this board is effectively closed.
Engineering & tech: Go and Kubernetes control planes, eBPF (Cilium/Tetragon), Rust, network data plane, SRE at fleet scale, Kubernetes platform engineering under IRAP, data warehousing and cloud data platforms, Splunk analytics and search. This is deep, well-scoped infrastructure specialisation of exactly the kind targeted.
Notable / other: Cisco posts US pay ranges under state transparency law. Observed on live JDs: Senior Software Engineer, Backend US$146,700–$277,600 + equity; Principal Tetragon Software Engineer US$222,900–$381,600 + equity; Senior SWE Control Plane (Hypershield) US$183,800–$263,600 + equity. Equity is included on senior+ roles.
Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Tetragon Software Engineer; Senior Software Engineer – Control Plane (Go/Kubernetes) – Hypershield; Software Engineering Technical Leader, Backend Services – Isovalent; Lead Site Reliability Engineer, Engineering Enablement (Remote); Senior SRE – Technical Leader, Kubernetes Platform (IRAP); Principal Software Engineer, Splunk Analytics and Search; Senior Cloud Infrastructure Engineer.
Sources: registry postings and JD bodies at data/posting-research/ (cited: https://cisco.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/cisco_careers/...). No cisco.com marketing pages were fetched — the JDs already carry the substantive information and web search was unavailable.
