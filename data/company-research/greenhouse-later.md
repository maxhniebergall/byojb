# Later — research

Provider/key: greenhouse:later | company_type: product

What they do: Later is an influencer- and social-marketing technology company. It sells a
social media management / scheduling platform (the original Later product, first-to-market
with the "link in bio" tool) plus influencer marketing software and services (Mavrck, and
the Mavely creator-commerce network). Customers are brands, agencies, creators and
influencers. Its own framing: "the world's most intelligent influencer marketing company,
built to give brands the confidence to create unforgettable campaigns by combining real
creator relationships, trusted intelligence, and expert guidance", "built on a native,
AI-powered platform and more than a decade of proprietary data—including billions of social
interactions, impressions, and $2.4B+ in verified influencer-driven purchases".

How they describe themselves: Mission — "building the future of social marketing technology —
empowering brands, creators, and influencers through Later and Mavely." Five stated values,
verbatim headings with their own gloss:
1. **We work hard** — "bring passion, grit and accountability to everything we do"
2. **Innate curiosity** — "approach every challenge, conversation, and opportunity with genuine curiosity"
3. **Customer & creator obsessed** — "customers are at the center of everything we do"
4. **Fearless honesty** — "communicate with transparency and compassion"
5. **Succeed together** — "believe in the power of collaboration, trust, and shared pursuit"
They also state they "openly build and maintain unbiased hiring, pay, and promotion practices
to create a foundation for an equitable workplace."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, growth stage. Formed from the 2024 merger of
two 2014-founded businesses — Mavrck (influencer marketing) and Later (social media
management) — which "officially joined together as one unified business in 2024", later adding
Mavely. Roughly mid-size (low hundreds of employees). Merger-integration risk is the main
structural consideration; no public profitability statement.

Locations / HQ: Hub cities — **Vancouver, BC**; **Boston, MA**; **Chicago, IL**; and
**Vancouver, WA**. Offices in Boston, Vancouver BC and Vancouver WA.

Remote policy: Hybrid-default with selective fully-remote hiring. Their own words: "We have
offices in Boston, MA; Vancouver, BC; and Vancouver, WA. For select positions, we are open to
hiring fully remote candidates. We post our positions in the location(s) where we are open to
having the successful candidate be located." The careers page describes a "hybrid work
environment". In practice their JDs are tagged either `#LI-Hybrid` or `#LI-Remote` — the tag
is the reliable signal.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes, but only for the specific postings tagged `#LI-Remote`.**
Verified from their own live JDs: *Senior Engineer (Product)* (`#LI-Remote`, location
"Remote") and one *Staff Engineer (Platform)* (`#LI-Remote`, location "Remote", eligibility
listed as Canada + United States) are genuinely remote. The `#LI-Hybrid` postings —
*DevOps Engineer* (Vancouver BC), the other *Staff Engineer (Platform)*, *Senior Engineer
(Platform)*, *Staff Engineer (Product)* — enumerate Vancouver BC / Boston / Chicago / LA /
NY / Vancouver WA and expect hub-city presence. Being BC-based helps only if one is willing
to be in Vancouver; from Kimberley (an 11-hour drive / Mountain Time), only the `#LI-Remote`
postings are workable. Company is unambiguously set up to employ Canadians (Vancouver BC hub,
CAD payroll, CAD salary bands quoted).

Engineering & tech: Split into Platform and Product engineering tracks with a distinct
DevOps role; ladder observed is Senior → Staff. Platform roles are the infra-facing side of a
social-data product built on "more than a decade of proprietary data". Salary structure notes
some roles are OTE (base + variable) rather than salary-only — worth checking per role.

Pay transparency: They post ranges on every JD by policy — "To comply with local legislation
and ensure transparency, we share salary ranges on all job postings", benchmarked "against
market Total Cash Compensation for the geographic location of our job posting", with zoned
ranges by location.
- DevOps Engineer (Vancouver BC): **$120,000 – $140,000 CAD**
- Senior Engineer (Platform / Product): **$110,000 – $160,000 CAD** (US: $125,000 – $205,000 USD)
- Staff Engineer (Platform): OTE **$170,000 – $200,000 CAD** (US: OTE $175,000 – $250,000 USD)
- Staff Engineer (Product): **$170,000 – $200,000 CAD** (US: $175,000 – $250,000 USD)

Open relevant roles (sample): DevOps Engineer; Senior Engineer (Platform); Senior Engineer
(Product); Staff Engineer (Platform); Staff Engineer (Product).
