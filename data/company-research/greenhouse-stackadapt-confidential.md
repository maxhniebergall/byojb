What they do: StackAdapt is a Toronto-founded self-serve programmatic advertising platform (DSP) — native, display, video, CTV and audio — for agencies and brands. Its own words on this board: "StackAdapt is the leading technology company that empowers marketers to reach, engage, and convert audiences with precision. With 465 billion automated optimizations per second, the AI-powered StackAdapt Marketing Platform seamlessly connects brand and performance marketing to drive measurable results across the entire customer journey. The most forward-thinking marketers choose StackAdapt to orchestrate high-impact campaigns across programmatic advertising and marketing channels."

How they describe themselves: Same as the main board — "a diverse and inclusive team of collaborative, hardworking individuals trying to make a dent in the universe. No matter who you are, where you are from, who you love, follow in faith, disability (or superpower) status, ethnicity, or the gender you identify with (if you're comfortable, let us know your pronouns), you are welcome at StackAdapt." Careers-site pillars: Diversity, Equity and Inclusion; Flexible Work; Learning and Development; Collaboration. Tagline "Innovate the future of adtech" / "The globe is our office now."

Size / stage / funding: Private, founded 2014, headcount reported in the 1,001–5,000 range. Scaled and well past runway dependence; no public round or profitability figure on first-party pages.

Locations / HQ: HQ Toronto; hubs in Toronto and London; offices/operations across the US, UK, Singapore and Australia.

Remote policy: Remote-first. Stated on this very req: "**StackAdapt is a remote-first company with teams around the world. Our teams work in a fully distributed environment, and we are open to candidates based in Canada and the United States.**" Perks elsewhere include work-from-home reimbursement and an optional global WeWork membership.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** The req states outright that they are open to candidates based in Canada, and its location field is "Canada; United Kingdom; United States". StackAdapt is a Canadian employer with a Canadian entity; sibling reqs on the main board list Alberta, Calgary, BC and Vancouver explicitly, so Mountain Time and a BC residence are workable.

Engineering & tech: The role on this board is a **Machine Learning Engineer on the Data Science team**, working "directly with our Data Scientists, Machine Learning Engineers, Engineering teams, and our CTO/Co-Founder on building pipelines and ad optimization models. With databases that process millions of requests per second, there's no shortage of data and problems to tackle." Stated responsibilities: "Design modular and scalable real time data pipelines to handle huge datasets"; "Understand and implement custom ML algorithms in a low latency environment"; "Work on microservice architectures that run training, inference, and monitoring on thousands of ML models concurrently." Requirements emphasise algorithm and software design, concurrency, data structures, probabilistic/ML algorithm implementation, interest in scalable distributed systems, and — unusually — "A high GPA from a well-respected Computer Science program." Public artefacts they link for the team: an AI in Action podcast episode with Ned Dimitrov, the stackadapt.com/careers/data-science page, and an Amazon Tech Talks recording.

Notable / other: Awards cited in the boilerplate — Ad Age Best Places to Work 2024; G2 Top Software and Top Marketing & Advertising Product 2024; Campaign's Best Places to Work 2023 (UK); Great Place to Work® 2024 Best Workplaces for Women and Best Workplaces in Canada; #1 DSP on G2 across several categories.

Compensation: This req states **no salary range**. StackAdapt posts a band on only a small minority of its reqs.
