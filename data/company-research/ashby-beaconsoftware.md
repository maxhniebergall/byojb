# Beacon Software — research
Provider/key: ashby:beaconsoftware | company_type: product (software-first permanent-capital holding company operating a vertical-SaaS portfolio)

What they do: Beacon acquires and operates a portfolio of vertical SaaS companies, and — this is the differentiator in their own framing — builds an internal software platform to run that portfolio rather than adding headcount. Their words: "Most private equity firms scale by adding people. We are building Beacon to scale by adding software. The thesis is simple. Portfolio operations, value creation, and deal sourcing are bottlenecked by human attention, and the right software platform can lift that ceiling by an order of magnitude." They also describe themselves as "a permanent capital holding company which acquires and grows essential businesses… a profitable series B+ firm that combines great technologists, operators and M&A professionals." They state that "by the next phase of buildout we will have **100+ portfolio companies** running on this platform."

Note on classification: Beacon is a holding company, but it is **not** a staffing/consulting/outsourcing firm — the engineering roles build Beacon's own internal platform (and, separately, product engineering inside owned portcos such as 99Pledges). One posting, "Oracle Cloud Architect - System Dynamics", is openly hiring *on behalf of* a portfolio company ("One of our portfolio companies is seeking…"), so postings under this key can be for different employers/products with different conditions. Flagging as a registry data-quality wrinkle rather than a disqualifier.

How they describe themselves — the full stated values ("Our Values at Beacon Software", quoted from their JDs):
- **Humility**: "We acknowledge that the path to getting to the right answer involves being wrong along the way. We have strong beliefs which are weakly held. We actively seek new ideas and believe we can learn from anyone at any time."
- **Honesty**: "We are truth seeking in our approach to business problems. Business is a repeat game and we believe that human relationships generate alpha. We understand that trust is earned over a lifetime and can be lost in an instant."
- **Hunger**: "We play to win. We hold ourselves to high standards and **will not be outworked**. We take pride in having a deep sense of responsibility… We believe to whom much is given much is expected."
- **Horizon**: "We seek to build a generational software company. This will take decades. We manage our expectations and those of our partners to take advantage of the 8th wonder of the world - compounding growth."
Engineering tone is unusually concrete and anti-hype: "The bar here is not novelty. It is correctness, latency, observability, and the kind of API design that ages well"; "This is the unglamorous infrastructure work that determines whether we can onboard portco 50 as fast as portco 5"; "ML and agentic systems are part of the stack. They sit on top of a foundation that has to be solid first."

Size / stage / funding: Raised **$550M+** from General Catalyst, Lightspeed, D1 Capital, CPMG, and the family offices of the founders of Stripe, DoorDash and Ramp. Self-described "profitable series B+ firm" and "permanent capital" — i.e. no forced exit clock. Headcount not stated.

Remote policy: No company-wide policy stated. Remote-Canada eligibility: **Mixed, and only partly verified.** The Oracle Cloud Architect posting is located "Canada" and explicitly 100% remote — that one is genuinely remote-Canada. The Member of Technical Staff posting is located "Toronto, Ontario" with no remote flag, so remote-from-BC is **unverified and should not be assumed**; the 99Pledges role is dual-city SF/Toronto, same caveat. Canadian employment clearly exists as a mechanism.

Engineering & tech: Detailed and specific. Platform surfaces an MTS can own end-to-end: (1) **Data platform** — cross-portco data lake on **Iceberg** with **Snowflake or Databricks** as query engine, per-portco **S3 and KMS isolation**, ingestion from QuickBooks, HubSpot, Salesforce, PostHog, Intercom, Linear, Slack, Gmail, Postgres, Stripe, Zendesk; canonical data model, catalog and semantic layer. (2) **Core services and APIs** — identity, access control, audit, workflow orchestration, internal APIs. (3) **Multi-tenant isolation** — per-portco data/compute/credential boundaries, cross-cloud AWS + Azure connectivity, regional data residency for regulated verticals. (4) **Workflow and action runtime** — typed action surfaces, idempotency, retries, rollback, human-in-the-loop approval gates, audit trails; scripted and model-driven workflows as variations of one primitive. (5) **Observability and evals** — traces, metrics, structured logs, replay infrastructure, regression suites, safe A/B testing, for both traditional services and model-driven workflows. (6) **Safety and blast radius** — autonomy tiers, kill switches, per-action-class blast-radius caps, audit surfaces. Bonus points listed: Iceberg/Polaris/Snowflake/Databricks at scale, multi-tenant SaaS or platform infra, production LLM systems including evals and observability, offline RL / contextual bandits / sequential decision-making, open-source contributions to data infra or developer tooling.
Portfolio-company stacks differ sharply: **99Pledges** is Ruby on Rails 7 / Ruby 3.1, PostgreSQL, HAML/SCSS/Bootstrap/jQuery, Delayed Job, Braintree/PayPal/Dwolla payments, Heroku + AWS S3 + Redis, Postmark, Honeybadger/New Relic/Datadog, PostHog. The Oracle role is Oracle 19c multitenant CDB/PDB, APEX, legacy Forms/Reports, OCI (VCNs, load balancers, cloud storage), Oracle Linux/RHEL, Windows Server, IPSec VPN, DR via native replication, supporting a proprietary 36-module ERP.

Notable / other: 99Pledges' engineering practices are stated plainly and are unusually disciplined: "We review designs before writing code and favor explicit, well-engineered solutions over clever ones. We prefer DRY code, thorough testing, and small, reviewable changes. We take production seriously: careful migrations, monitoring, and a bias toward correctness." Beacon discloses that it "uses Artificial Intelligence and AI-enabled tools to assist with screening, reviewing, organizing and highlighting profiles and applications", while stating AI makes no hiring decisions and every application is human-reviewed.
