# StackAdapt — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:stackadapt | company_type: product

What they do: StackAdapt is a self-serve, AI-powered programmatic advertising platform (DSP) that lets marketers plan, run and measure multi-channel campaigns — native, display, video, connected TV (CTV) and audio. Its own JD boilerplate claims "465 billion automated optimizations per second" and describes the platform as "seamlessly connect[ing] brand and performance marketing to drive measurable results across the entire customer journey." Customers are agencies and brands; the company has been rated the #1 DSP on G2 for six consecutive years. Founded 2014.

How they describe themselves: The careers site's tagline is "Innovate the future of adtech," and "The globe is our office now." The stated culture pillars on that page are **Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion; Flexible Work; Learning and Development; Collaboration**. There is no published values manifesto beyond those four pillars. JD closing boilerplate describes the company as "a diverse and inclusive team of collaborative, hardworking individuals trying to make a dent in the universe. No matter who you are, where you are from, who you love, follow in faith, disability (or superpower) status, ethnicity, or the gender you identify with … you are welcome at StackAdapt." JDs also disclose that "We use artificial intelligence (AI) to streamline the resume reviews of candidates and assess their fit based on the criteria outlined in the job posting. We do not use AI to make any final hiring or interview decisions."

Size / stage / funding: Private. Headcount reported in the 1,001–5,000 range. Scaled and well past the runway-dependent stage; no public funding round or profitability figure found on first-party pages.

Locations / HQ: Founded and headquartered in Toronto, Canada. Offices/hubs now span the US, UK, Singapore and Australia; client-support operations across North America, EMEA, APAC and LATAM. JDs mention "hubs in London and Toronto."

Remote policy: Remote-first / globally distributed. JD perks include "Work from home reimbursements" and an "Optional global WeWork membership for those who want a change from their home office and hubs in London and Toronto." Engineering JDs carry the `#LI-REMOTE` tag. Benefits are explicitly caveated: "Benefits and perks may vary depending on your country of employment… In locations where StackAdapt does not have a legal entity, employment and benefits are administered in accordance with local regulations and partner policies."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, and unusually strongly.** StackAdapt is a Canadian employer of record, and most of the open engineering roles are Canada-first or Canada-only. Registry location strings from live postings: "Canada" (DevOps Engineer; Senior Software Engineer, Integrations); "Alberta, Canada; British Columbia; Calgary; Ontario; Toronto; Vancouver" (Senior/Staff Machine Learning Engineer; Software Engineer, Backend; Software Engineer, Stats & Analytics); "Canada; United States" (Staff Engineer FinOps; Staff/Senior Staff Software Engineer, Data Delivery; Staff/Senior Software Engineer, Programmatic Bidding; Senior Big Data Engineer & DBA; Automation Engineer, Agentic Orchestration). Several roles list Alberta and Calgary explicitly, which makes Mountain-Time hours workable. This is one of the most Canada-friendly employers in the registry.

Engineering & tech: Engineering is a first-class, deep platform org rather than a thin product layer — the open reqs are for real distributed-systems surfaces: programmatic bidding, data ingestion, data delivery, user-profile systems, stats & analytics, big-data engineering/DBA, EngOps, DevOps, and a FinOps/cost platform. The FinOps JD describes "a founding individual contributor role" that will "build the internal cost platform and reporting model for engineering spend," "own analysis and optimization of AWS and engineering-owned vendor spend," and "define and implement showback, chargeback, and unit-cost models" — i.e. AWS-based infrastructure, telemetry-backed cost models, internal tooling and dashboards. Roles are well-scoped and specialized (a whole team per data-pipeline stage), which is a strong signal of depth rather than generalist churn. No public engineering blog or levels handbook was found.

Compensation & pay transparency: StackAdapt includes a pay-transparency module on *some* JDs but not most. Its boilerplate reads: "The compensation range listed for this role reflects the expected base salary for candidates located in Ontario, Canada. It is informed by market data and the approved budget for this position. StackAdapt maintains different compensation ranges for roles across other countries and regions, and final offers will be aligned to the candidate's current location." and "This range represents base salary only. Depending on the role, candidates may also be eligible for additional compensation such as annual bonuses, commissions, equity awards, and a comprehensive benefits package." Of the 17 scraped engineering JD bodies, only **one** carried an actual range: Senior Big Data Engineer & Database Administrator, "Ontario Salary Band $100,000 — $125,000 CAD" (the same page also renders an identically-valued "Ontario Hourly Band", which appears to be a template artefact). The other 16 engineering roles state no pay at all. JD perks list "Highly competitive salary," "Retirement/401K/Pension Savings globally," health benefits from day one, mental-health program, "Competitive Paid time off packages including birthday's off!", parental leave, conference/course/book budget, and internal programmatic certifications.

Notable / other: Awards cited on their own JDs — G2 Top Software for 2026; Great Place to Work® 2026 Best Workplaces™ for Young Talent and Best Workplaces in Canada; #1 DSP on G2 across several categories. Ad Age named it a Best Place to Work in 2024. Runs a "Conversion" customer conference. Recruiting-fraud notice: "StackAdapt will always contact job candidates via an official @stackadapt.com email address."

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Engineer, FinOps & Cost Platform; Staff / Senior Staff Software Engineer, User Profile; Staff II Software Engineer, Data Ingestion; Staff/Senior Software Engineer, Programmatic Bidding; Senior Staff Software Engineer, Data Delivery; Senior/Staff Machine Learning Engineer; Machine Learning Engineer (Remote); Senior Software Engineer, Integrations; Senior Software Engineer, EngOps; DevOps Engineer; Software Engineer, Backend; Software Engineer, Stats & Analytics; Senior Big Data Engineer & Database Administrator.
