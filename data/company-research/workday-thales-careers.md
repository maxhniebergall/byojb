# Thales — research
Provider/key: workday:thales/careers | company_type: product

What they do: Thales is a French multinational in aerospace, space, ground transportation, defence & security, and digital identity/cybersecurity — avionics, radar and mission systems, rail signalling, satellites, secure communications, and (via Thales Canada) long-running defence programs for the Canadian Armed Forces and Coast Guard. Its output is safety- and mission-critical systems engineering, not commercial SaaS.

How they describe themselves (their own JD boilerplate, quoted): *"In fast changing markets, customers worldwide rely on Thales. Thales is a business where brilliant people from all over the world come together to share ideas and inspire each other. In aerospace, transportation, defence, security and space, our architects design innovative solutions that make our tomorrow's possible."* The Canadian defence postings add that Thales is *"a trusted partner to DND, enabling the Canadian Armed Forces and the Canadian Coast Guard to achieve mission success."* Canadian postings are bilingual (FR/EN) throughout. No hustle or rocketship language; tone is institutional-engineering.

Size / stage / funding: Very large European public company (Euronext: HO), tens of thousands of employees, majority state-adjacent shareholding in France. Long program cycles, government and prime-contractor customers — about as far from runway-dependent as it gets.

Locations / HQ: HQ Paris, France. Live relevant postings cluster in the **UK (Doncaster, Crawley/Manor Royal, Cheadle)** and **Canada (Montréal QC, Québec City QC, Ottawa ON)**.

Remote policy: Onsite/hybrid, site-anchored. The Canadian JDs state it explicitly: Montréal role is *"Montréal, QC (Hybride/Hybrid)"*, Québec City role is *"Ville de Quebec, QC (Sur Site/Hybride) / Quebec City, QC (On Site/Hybrid)"*, Ottawa role is *"This position is Hybrid Ottawa, Ontario."* The UK infrastructure/DevOps roles are tied to named UK sites. There is no remote-designated posting.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — and doubly constrained.** Thales genuinely employs engineers in Canada (Montréal, Québec City, Ottawa), but every Canadian role is hybrid/onsite at a Quebec or Ottawa site — Eastern Time, in-office, disqualifying from Kimberley BC with no relocation. On top of location, the Canadian defence roles require export-control eligibility and *"a Canadian Enhanced Reliability security clearance"* (Controlled Goods Program / US EAR references), which further ties the work to a controlled site.

Engineering & tech: Two clusters. (1) **Canada** — applied AI research (frontier AI, computer vision, generative and agentic AI, data science) in Montréal and Québec City, plus a **C5ISRT & Cloud Architect** role in Ottawa driving *"the technical vision and strategic execution for secure, scalable defence"* cloud architecture. (2) **UK** — Infrastructure Engineers (6 reqs across Doncaster, Crawley, Cheadle) and DevOps Engineers, including *"Dev Ops Software Engineer – Maritime Mission Systems."* Work is defence-systems engineering with secure/classified constraints rather than internet-scale platform work.

Notable / other: The AI roles in Quebec are titled *Research Scientist*, not engineer — closer to applied research than to the ML-infrastructure archetype. The Ottawa architect role is a technical-vision/architecture position rather than hands-on platform build. Canadian postings are fully bilingual, and the Quebec sites will in practice expect French.
