# KoBold Metals — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:koboldmetals | company_type: product
What they do: Applies machine learning, data science and scientific computing to mineral exploration — finding new deposits of copper, lithium and other metals needed for electric vehicles and renewable energy. Their framing: the mining industry "has steadily become worse at finding new ore deposits, requiring >10X more capital to make discoveries compared to 30 years ago… the industry has chronically under-invested in new exploration technology, relying on the manual techniques of yesteryear." They run real exploration projects worldwide alongside the software.
How they describe themselves: Technology-led exploration company. They emphasize a proven track record over promises: "KoBold has proven its first discovery with materially less capital than the industry average and found one of the best copper deposits ever discovered: the copper is far more concentrated than the global average of copper mines, and this asset alone is expected to generate meaningful revenue for decades." Engineers get "hands-on exposure to our exploration projects around the world". The Client Platform JD does use fast-growth language ("eagerness to take on an evolving range of responsibilities at a fast-growing company").
Size / stage / funding: Very well capitalized private company; investors named in their own postings include T. Rowe Price and Canada Pension Plan Investments (and KoBold is publicly associated with Breakthrough Energy Ventures). A proven revenue-generating asset is claimed. Not runway-fragile by the standards of an early startup.
Locations / HQ: Berkeley/California-rooted, but operationally remote-first with global field sites; postings list Remote (US/Canada) and a separate UK track.
Remote policy: Stated verbatim: "KoBold is a remote-first workplace." Field sites exist but engineering roles are posted Remote.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes — verified, stated in their own JDs.** "Location: Remote, Candidates can be located anywhere in the United States or Canada" and "All candidates must be legally authorized to work in the United States or Canada." The Staff Client Platform Engineer posting repeats this ("We are open to candidates currently residing anywhere in the United States or Canada") but adds "CST or EST time zone preferred" — a preference, not a requirement, though Mountain Time is a two-hour gap from EST. The Decision Science and Scientific Computing roles state no timezone preference.
Engineering & tech: Decision Science and Scientific Computing engineering teams (numerical/scientific software, data pipelines, ML for subsurface prediction); an Infrastructure Engineering track; a Client Platform / IT-operations track moving "from reactive, break/fix helpdesk support toward proactive, automated IT operations that work seamlessly across geographies, including remote field sites with limited connectivity". Google Workspace, CrowdStrike, Datadog appear as tooling in the CPE role.
Notable / other: Roles are explicitly full-time exempt with equity and 401k matching. A UK hiring track exists separately (Software Engineer (All Levels), United Kingdom).
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Decision Science; Senior Software Engineer, Scientific Computing; Staff Client Platform Engineer (CPE) (Remote); Software Engineer (All Levels); Senior Infrastructure Engineer (recently closed).
Pay (from their own JDs): Senior SWE Decision Science / Scientific Computing $170–215K USD base; Staff Client Platform Engineer $150–180K USD base + equity + 401k match; Software Engineer (All Levels) $150–240K; Senior Infrastructure Engineer $170–230K (posting now closed).
