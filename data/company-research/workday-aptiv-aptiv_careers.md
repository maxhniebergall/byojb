# Aptiv (Wind River) — research
Provider/key: workday:aptiv/aptiv_careers | company_type: product

**Entity note / registry data-quality flag:** the registry name is "Aptiv" and the portal is Aptiv's corporate Workday tenant, but effectively **every** relevant engineering req in this batch is a **Wind River** role — the location field reads "CAN Kanata (2), ON - **WR**" (WR = Wind River) and req titles reference "Wind River Next Gen RTOS", "Senior Member of Technical Staff - Cloud Product Support - WR" and "Member of Technical Staff - Software Engineering - WR OpenStack". Aptiv PLC acquired Wind River from TPG, announced January 2022 and completed December 2022, for $3.5B (revised down from the $4.3B originally agreed). Wind River continues to hire through Aptiv's Workday. Judge these roles as Wind River roles, not automotive-Aptiv roles.

What they do: **Aptiv PLC** is a large global automotive technology supplier — electrical architecture, advanced safety and user-experience systems, and its Smart Vehicle Architecture for software-defined vehicles. **Wind River**, the subsidiary these reqs belong to, is a long-established provider of intelligent-edge and embedded software: VxWorks RTOS, Wind River Linux, and Wind River Studio, a cloud-native platform for developing, deploying and operating distributed edge systems. Wind River's largest commercial market is telecom 5G edge/RAN infrastructure; Aptiv's rationale for the acquisition was to integrate Wind River Studio into its Smart Vehicle Architecture to provide "a cloud-native software architecture and an edge-to-cloud DevOps platform that speeds software development, streamlines deployment to vehicles and enables lifecycle management."

How they describe themselves: The JDs are engineering-forward and specific rather than mission-flavoured. The Kanata cloud team is described as working "with large-scale projects including Kubernetes, Docker, OpenStack and fast-paced Open-Source technologies that are in high demand from their customers," and as "a leading contributor to the Open Source StarlingX cloud native project, to manage and maintain the critical infrastructure for highly distributed Edge solutions, primarily 5G." Recurring candidate-attribute language across reqs: "Strong communication skills and ability to work effectively in team environments; Self-motivated, fast learner with the desire and ability to master new technologies." No enumerated values list appears in the postings.

Size / stage / funding: Aptiv PLC is a publicly traded (NYSE: APTV) Fortune 500 automotive supplier with roughly 200,000 employees globally. Wind River was founded in 1981, previously owned by Intel and then TPG, now an Aptiv subsidiary. This is a mature, stable, public-company employer, not a startup.

Locations / HQ: Aptiv is Ireland/Dublin-domiciled with major US operations. Wind River is headquartered in Alameda, California. The relevant engineering site here is **Kanata, Ontario** (Ottawa's tech west end), which hosts the Wind River Cloud Platform team; one req is in Swindon, UK.

Remote policy: **Hybrid, stated explicitly.** Every Kanata JD carries the same benefits block, which includes "BENEFITS - Canada … • **Workplace Flexibility: Hybrid Work**." Roles are anchored to the Kanata office with a hybrid split; no remote-anywhere option is offered on any req.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Effectively no.** Every relevant req is tagged "CAN Kanata (2), ON - WR" with "Workplace Flexibility: Hybrid Work". This is Canadian employment, but it is Ontario-office hybrid — it requires living within commuting distance of Kanata. There is no remote-BC, remote-Alberta or Canada-wide posting. A British Columbia resident who cannot relocate is not eligible for these roles as posted.

Engineering & tech: Genuinely deep infrastructure work — arguably the purest "plumbing" in this batch. The Kanata cloud team owns StarlingX (an open-source distributed edge cloud platform), OpenStack, Kubernetes, Docker, container orchestration, storage, cloud networking, and the DevOps/CI systems around them, targeting 5G telecom edge infrastructure. Named stack elements across reqs include Go, C/C++, Kubernetes operators, Ansible/Puppet, Linux, and RTOS. Reqs span Cloud Platform (Storage, Networking, Virtualization), DevOps, SDET/test automation, Linux application development, and a Principal Architect role for enterprise private cloud infrastructure and AI workloads. Open-source contribution is explicitly valued ("Contribution to the open-source community would be an asset").
