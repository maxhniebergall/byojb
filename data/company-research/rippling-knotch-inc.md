# Knotch — research
Provider/key: rippling:knotch-inc | company_type: product

What they do: Knotch is a New York–based marketing-technology company. Its original product, "Knotch One", is a Content Intelligence & Optimization platform that lets enterprise marketing teams plan, measure and optimize content across channels. As of 2026 the company has repositioned around a newer product, **Ace**, described on its own homepage as "AI experience infrastructure for the conversational web" — it composes personalized web pages based on visitor intent and is pitched as optimizing sites "to convert humans and machines" (i.e. LLM/agent traffic). Customers named on their site include Google (Think with Google), Zillow (Zillow Rentals), Square, Ally, Cox Automotive, GEICO, Synchrony, Dropbox, FOX, Chime and Deloitte.

How they describe themselves: The homepage frames the pitch as "The AI-native customer journey is here. But your website was built for a different era," and invites candidates to "come build the conversational web." The messaging is entirely product/marketing-outcome oriented (conversion, personalization, intent); no engineering values, handbook or principles are published.

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed. Founded 2013 by Anda Gansca and Aron Tzimas. ~67 employees (Tracxn, May 2026); ~$49.8M raised across 6 rounds. Not profitable/established — runway-dependent.

Locations / HQ: New York City, NY (US).

Remote policy: No published remote policy. The ATS shows role-level geography only.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes, for the DevOps role specifically.** Their Rippling ATS lists "DevOps Engineer" with location "Canada Remote; United States Remote". The "AI Engineer" posting is "United States Remote" only, so it is not Canada-eligible. Evidence: registry postings, ats.rippling.com/knotch-inc.

Engineering & tech: No engineering blog or public handbook found. Product implies a web-delivery/personalization stack with an LLM/agent layer; the open DevOps role is the only infra-flavoured position.

Notable / other: The company has clearly pivoted from content-marketing analytics toward "AI experience infrastructure", which is the current market cycle. Small eng org implied by ~67 total headcount against a Fortune-1000 enterprise customer list — meaning heavy enterprise-customer-facing pressure per engineer.

Open relevant roles (sample): DevOps Engineer (Canada Remote; US Remote); AI Engineer (US Remote).
