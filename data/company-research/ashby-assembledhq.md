# Assembled — research
Provider/key: ashby:assembledhq | company_type: product

What they do: Assembled sells a unified platform for running customer-support operations — workforce management (forecasting contact volume and scheduling thousands of agents), plus AI Agents that resolve cases end to end and an AI Copilot for human agents. In their own words: "Great customer support requires human agents and AI in perfect balance, and Assembled is the only unified platform that orchestrates both at scale." Named customers: Canva, Etsy, Robinhood. They describe the product as "a single operating system" spanning in-house agents, BPOs and AI.

How they describe themselves: Positioning is around human/AI collaboration — "we're building the platform that makes AI and human collaboration actually work" — and around AI-native internal workflows (the Design Systems role is framed as shaping "how AI coding agents build UI"). On hiring they are explicitly inclusive: "We know great candidates don't always meet every requirement listed in a job description… We value diverse perspectives and are committed to building an inclusive workplace where everyone feels like they belong." No separate public values list was located; no non-ATS company URL appears in the registry or in the JD text, and web search was unavailable this session, so this section is drawn from JD text only.

Size / stage / funding: "Backed by $71M from NEA, Emergence Capital, and Stripe." Headcount not stated. Growth-stage private company; profitability unstated.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco office (JD references "Catered lunches every day (M–F) and fully stocked SF office"). Both live postings are listed as "United States, Canada". They also hire in the UK (the Forecasting JD includes a UK right-to-work clause).

Remote policy: Not stated explicitly. The postings carry a country-level location of "United States, Canada" rather than a city, which reads as distributed hiring within those two countries, alongside a physical SF office. Async practices not described.

Remote-Canada eligibility: Yes on the evidence available — both live postings are geo-scoped "United States, Canada", and the JD carries separate US (E-Verify) and UK (right-to-work) compliance clauses, implying real multi-country employment infrastructure. What is NOT verified is whether the Canadian option is remote-anywhere-in-Canada or tied to a city; the stated benefits block is labelled "Our U.S. benefits", so the Canadian package is unknown. Mountain Time against an SF-centred company is workable.

Engineering & tech: The Forecasting & Scheduling team does statistical/ML work with real substance — "developing forecasting interfaces, data pipelines, and inference servers to predict support contact volume", solving a constrained scheduling problem (the JD links the Nurse scheduling problem on Wikipedia) across thousands of agents subject to preferences and labour law, plus "MLOps: enhancing machine learning efficiency and operations to support rapid model deployment and iteration." Python, pandas, SciPy, seaborn. The Design Systems role is frontend: TypeScript, React, Storybook, visx/D3/Chart.js, token architecture, visual regression testing, CI/CD and versioned releases.

Notable / other: Both live engineering postings state pay ranges: Forecasting & Scheduling at $135K–$280K USD base and Design Systems at $160K–$270K USD base, both plus stock options. The unusually wide bands are described as varying "depending on location, job-related knowledge, skills, and experience". US benefits include medical/dental/vision, flexible time off, monthly credits for professional development, wellness, Assembled customers and commuting, paid parental leave, and daily catered lunches.

Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineer - Forecasting & Scheduling; Software Engineer, Design Systems; Software Engineer - Platform; Software Engineer - AI Agents & Platform.
