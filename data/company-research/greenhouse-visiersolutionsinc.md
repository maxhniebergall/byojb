# Visier Solutions Inc — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:visiersolutionsinc | company_type: product

What they do: Visier is a people-analytics and workforce-intelligence platform. In their own words: "Visier is the global leader in Workforce Intelligence that powers every people decision. We bring Workforce AI to life for HR departments through our award-winning, agentic AI technology by surfacing the insights leaders need to plan, decide, and act with confidence in the moments that matter most. As the market leader in people analytics, workforce planning, organizational design, and manager effectiveness solutions, we fuel smarter decision-making for organizations across the globe. Our mission is to help businesses lead with insight at scale as they continuously transform." The site's framing is "See the workforce you have. Build the one you need" — detecting attrition risk and skill gaps, scenario-testing hiring and restructuring, and tracking programs against business outcomes.

How they describe themselves: Emphasis on data security and compliance ("Your sensitive workforce data, protected"), integration breadth ("pre-built connectors and APIs"), and moving customers "from insight to impact." They lead with pedigree: "Founded in 2010 by the pioneers of business intelligence."

Size / stage / funding: Private, well-established Canadian software company, founded 2010, roughly 15–16 years old. Their own copy claims "over 85,000 customers in 75 countries" and "2,000,000+ users globally" — the 85,000 figure counts organizations reached through embedded/partner distribution rather than direct enterprise contracts. Named customers include BASF, Panasonic, Domino's Pizza, Experian, Amgen, eBay and Ford Motor Company. Heavily venture- and growth-equity-funded (a large Goldman Sachs-led round in 2021 valued it above US$1B). Mature and substantial rather than early-stage, but still private and growth-funded rather than demonstrably profitable.

Locations / HQ: **Vancouver, British Columbia** — a genuinely BC-headquartered company, which is rare in this registry. Both live postings are tagged "Vancouver, BC, Canada."

Remote policy: Not stated on the public site or on the requisition. Both live reqs carry a specific city location (Vancouver, BC) rather than a "Remote" or "Remote - Canada" tag, which indicates an office-anchored or hybrid arrangement rather than distributed hiring.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Canadian employment yes; remote-from-Kimberley unclear and probably no.** Visier is a Canadian employer headquartered in the same province, so work authorization, payroll and benefits are all trivially fine — there is no immigration or entity obstacle at all. But the requisition is tagged to the Vancouver office with no remote designation, and Kimberley is in the East Kootenays, roughly 800 km and a full day's travel from Vancouver, in a different time zone (Mountain vs Pacific). A Vancouver-anchored hybrid role is not commutable from Kimberley. This needs to be confirmed directly with the recruiter before investing in an application — it is the single question that decides whether Visier is viable, and it is genuinely open rather than settled.

Engineering & tech: The Staff DevOps Developer requisition is unusually well-matched and worth quoting at length. "Visier is building the centralized control and data plane—the infrastructure, pipelines, and governance layer—that powers our internal AI transformation (Vector) across professional services, customer success, and internal knowledge. As the Staff DevOps Developer on this initiative, you will own the platform and integration layer that the entire system runs on. You will define how cloud infrastructure is designed, write production-grade Python application code, and build the Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers and RAG retrieval services that enable AI tools and agents to reliably query and act on organizational knowledge." Named deliverables: **Platform & Network Architecture** — "Define and operate a multi-cloud infrastructure across AWS and Azure—specifying compute, storage, VPCs, subnets, private endpoints, and load balancing with clear architectural rationale"; **Production Python & Integration Layer** — "Write tested, maintainable Python application code, build resilient API integrations with core source systems (Salesforce, ServiceNow, Gong, Gainsight), and develop internal tooling and automation workflows"; **RAG Context Engine & MCP Servers** — "Design, build, and optimize the inference-time retrieval service—from query embedding and vector search to re-ranking—and expose this via Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers for governed agent access." The role also asks the holder to "evaluate emerging agent frameworks, set platform-wide engineering standards, and engineer secure, resilient architectures designed to scale," blending "software engineering discipline, deep cloud networking and Infrastructure as Code (IaC) expertise, and an eye for emerging AI agent architecture."

Compensation: No range posted on the requisition. The registry carries an estimated band of roughly CA$130,000–CA$180,000 for sre_devops/staff. Visier is a Vancouver company and Vancouver staff-level engineering compensation is materially below Toronto and far below US levels, so the estimate is plausible but the midpoint sits at the bottom of the $160–200K CAD target.

Notable / other: Note that the "Vector" initiative described in the requisition is **internal** AI transformation — the platform serves professional services, customer success and internal knowledge teams, i.e. internal customers rather than external ones. The other live posting is a "Software Developer (Talent Pool)" evergreen requisition, not a real opening.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff DevOps Developer (Vancouver, BC); Software Developer (Talent Pool) (Vancouver, BC).
