# Clover Health — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:cloverhealth | company_type: product

What they do: A technology-driven **Medicare Advantage health insurer** (NASDAQ: CLOV). Clover sells MA PPO plans to Medicare-eligible seniors in the US and differentiates on software rather than network narrowing. Its platform, **Clover Assistant**, "is designed to aggregate patient data from across the healthcare ecosystem to support clinical decision-making and improve health outcomes through the early identification and management of chronic disease" — surfaced to primary-care physicians at the point of care. While most of the industry "shifts toward narrower HMO networks, Clover continues to offer a robust portfolio of PPO plans" with $0-to-low premiums. So: an insurance business whose thesis is that better data and ML on chronic-disease management lowers medical cost.

How they describe themselves: Careers material is benefits-forward and organized around "well-being" pillars. On compensation: "Our commitment to attracting and retaining top talent begins with a competitive base salary and equity opportunities. Additionally, we offer a performance-based bonus program and regular compensation reviews to recognize and reward exceptional contributions." On culture: "We understand the importance of mental health in fostering productivity and maintaining work-life balance. To support this, we offer initiatives such as **No-Meeting Fridays**, company holidays, access to mental health resources, and a generous annual leave policy. Additionally, we embrace a **remote-first culture** that supports collaboration and flexibility, allowing our team members to thrive from any location." Postings also list "Remote-first culture, enabling collaboration with global teams" and a "generous flexible time-off policy."

Size / stage / funding: Publicly traded (NASDAQ: CLOV), roughly 500–800 employees. **153,000 Medicare Advantage members as of 1 January 2026, up 53% year over year**, and the company "expects first-ever full year GAAP Net Income profitability in 2026." Post-SPAC (2021) with a rocky market history, now on a stated path to profitability — improving stability, but reaching profitability for the first time rather than long-established.

Locations / HQ: US — historically Nashville, TN and Jersey City, NJ. Plans are "accessible by 5.2 million Medicare-eligible beneficiaries in 2026, within 203 counties across 5 states." Employment is remote-first rather than office-anchored, with roles posted as "Remote - USA" or "Remote - Canada."

Remote policy: Remote-first and stated as such repeatedly across postings, with No-Meeting Fridays, flexible time off, and "#LI-REMOTE" tagging on every relevant role. Postings are country-scoped, however — a role is either Remote-USA, Remote-Canada, or both.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Partial — verified yes, but only for a minority of roles.** Of the 7 relevant live postings, only **two** include Canada: "Senior Software Developer" (Remote - Canada) and "Software Engineer - Engineering Productivity" (Remote - Canada; Remote - USA). The Canadian roles quote pay in **CAD** ($134,000–$175,000), confirming a genuine Canadian employing entity. Critically, **all of the infrastructure-flavoured roles are Remote - USA only** — Principal Engineer Cloud Infrastructure, Senior Site Reliability Engineer, Senior Machine Learning Engineer and both Senior Software Engineer postings are US-scoped and E-Verify-tagged, i.e. require US work authorization. Timezone is not an obstacle (US-hours company, Mountain Time is fine).

Engineering & tech: Cloud infrastructure, SRE, ML engineering and engineering-productivity functions all exist as distinct teams, which indicates a real platform organization rather than an insurer with a small IT group. ML work centres on chronic-disease risk identification feeding Clover Assistant. Postings reference collaboration with "global teams."

Notable / other: E-Verify participant, with San Francisco Fair Chance Ordinance language on postings. Pay transparency is applied consistently — every relevant JD posts a base range. Compensation includes equity plus a performance-based bonus and 401k matching on US roles.

Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Engineer, Cloud Infrastructure (Remote-USA, $195K–$270K USD); Senior Site Reliability Engineer (Remote-USA, $160K–$208K USD); Senior Machine Learning Engineer (Remote-USA, $150K–$200K USD); Senior Software Engineer ×2 (Remote-USA, $195K–$220K USD); **Senior Software Developer (Remote-Canada, $134K–$175K CAD)**; **Software Engineer - Engineering Productivity (Remote-Canada / Remote-USA, $146.2K–$190K USD)**.
