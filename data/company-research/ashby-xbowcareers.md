# XBOW — research
Provider/key: ashby:xbowcareers | company_type: product

What they do: XBOW builds an AI system for offensive security — autonomous penetration testing. In their words: "Attackers are already using AI to move faster than defenders can react—we're creating the platform that puts security ahead in the arms race. Our AI-powered system autonomously discovers, validates, and even exploits vulnerabilities, giving organizations proof-backed results in hours instead of weeks." The product is delivered both as SaaS and on-premises, across multiple clouds.

How they describe themselves: "We're a team of builders, hackers, and researchers who thrive on solving problems others think are impossible." The platform role adds a clear engineering philosophy: "This is a role for someone who sees infrastructure as a product, loves clean abstractions, and knows how to dig into performance issues across layers," on "a high-trust, high-velocity team." They also state plainly that the environment is unformed: "Navigate ambiguity and help define how we build as much as what we build," and want "a thoughtful communicator who values clarity and simplicity and is comfortable working in a fast-pac[ed]" environment. No separate values page was retrievable (see Sources).

Size / stage / funding: Venture-backed startup, roughly two years old. From their own JD: "Founded by Oege de Moor, creator of GitHub Copilot, and backed by Sequoia, Altimeter, and other leading investors." Also from their own claims: "In just over a year, our AI... has uncovered thousands of real-world zero-days across the software billions rely on, and achieved the #1 ranking on HackerOne's global leaderboard." Headcount not published; the hiring pattern (a handful of open engineering roles spanning Americas and EMEA) suggests a small team, likely well under 100.

Locations / HQ: HQ not verified. The company hires internationally by region rather than by office — live postings enumerate US remote, Canada remote, Argentina, and a long list of European countries (UK, Ireland, Spain, Switzerland, Austria, Greece, Sweden, Denmark, Malta, Brazil).

Remote policy: Remote-first in practice. Roles are split by region ("- Americas" / "- EMEA") and every location string in the live postings is a country-level remote designation rather than an office. The "US East Coast" tag on two roles indicates some timezone-band preference for collaboration.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes — verified from the posting itself.** "Software Engineer - Platform / Core Infrastructure - Americas" lists its locations as "US remote, Canada remote, Remote Argentina." Canada is named explicitly as a hiring geography, which is the strongest eligibility signal in this batch. Caveat: two of the four roles carry a "US East Coast" tag, so there may be an Eastern-timezone overlap expectation; Mountain Time is a two-hour offset from Eastern, which is workable but should be confirmed. The AI Systems and Console roles are EMEA/US-East/Argentina and do not list Canada.

Engineering & tech: The platform role is squarely infrastructure. Stated work: "design and implement the complex distributed infrastructure that powers our core AI engine and distributed analysis systems, enabling XBOW to run seamlessly across multiple cloud providers (AWS, Azure, OCI etc.) and contexts (SaaS, on prem)"; tuning "cloud services across compute, storage, networking, and observability"; supporting "large-scale systems with event driven architectures"; and "disaster recovery and business continuity strategies." Core services are "written in TypeScript, Kotlin and Go (and pick them up quickly if you haven't used them before)." Wanted experience: distributed systems on AWS or similar, Docker and Kubernetes, infrastructure as code (Terraform, CDK), event-driven architectures and message queues such as Kafka, and "a track record of performance tuning across cloud services, databases, and compute layers." Ownership is end-to-end: "from design through deployment to production support."

Notable / other: Compensation is posted on all four live roles as an unusually wide, level-agnostic band: "$100K – $350K • Offers Equity," described on two roles as "Open to various levels." This is a genuine posted range but is too wide to be informative about any particular level — it spans junior through staff. Equity is offered on every role (equity percentage on the platform roles, equity cash value on others).

Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineer - Platform / Core Infrastructure - Americas (US remote, Canada remote, Argentina); Software Engineer - Platform / Core Infrastructure - EMEA; Software Engineer - AI Systems (multi-country EMEA + US East Coast); Software Engineer - Console.
