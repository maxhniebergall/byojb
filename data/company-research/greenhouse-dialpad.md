# Dialpad — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:dialpad | company_type: product

What they do: In their own words, "Dialpad is the AI platform for customer experience, built to resolve customer problems in real time across voice and digital." The product combines cloud telephony/UCaaS with contact-centre AI: "Our AI agents learn from your best human agents and improve with every interaction, helping organizations understand their customers, deliver better experiences, increase operational efficiencies, and build a lasting competitive advantage." They position against two incumbents: "Unlike legacy systems built to route and answer, or standalone agentic bot vendors built to deflect, Dialpad was built to **resolve**. Our AI agents and human agents operate on a single platform with shared context, allowing Agentic AI to resolve issues, advance deals, and eliminate busywork through automation while seamlessly handing conversations to humans when needed, with full context preserved." Named customers: Randstad, Motorola Solutions, Netflix, the San Diego Padres, the Colorado Rockies Baseball Club, Cal Athletics.

How they describe themselves: Employees are "Dialers." Their stated core traits, verbatim: "We look for people who are **intensely curious and hold themselves to a high bar**. Our ambition is significant, and achieving it requires a team that operates at the highest level. We seek individuals who embody our core traits: **Scrappy, Curious, Optimistic, Persistent, and Empathetic**." On AI internally: "At Dialpad, AI isn't just a feature; it's how our teams do their best work every day. We put powerful AI tools in every employee's hands so they can move faster, think bigger, and achieve more." On offices: "We have designed our offices to be inclusive, offering a vibrant environment to cultivate collaboration and connection. Our exceptional culture, repeatedly recognized as a Great Place to Work…"

Size / stage / funding: Private, late-stage, well capitalized. Backed by **Andreessen Horowitz, GV, ICONIQ Capital, and T-Mobile** — the T-Mobile investment is a strategic telecom tie. Established (founded 2011 as Switch.co) with a large enterprise customer base; not an early-stage risk.

Locations / HQ: HQ **San Ramon / Bay Area, California**. Substantial Canadian engineering footprint in **Vancouver, BC** and **Kitchener, Ontario**; also **London, UK**. The Canadian sites carry most of the currently open engineering roles.

Remote policy: **Hybrid, office-anchored.** Stated explicitly on the Developer Platform req: "This position reports to our Developer Platform Director and will **require a hybrid working arrangement based out of our Vancouver office**." Company copy reinforces the office as the intended default ("we have designed our offices to be inclusive, offering a vibrant environment to cultivate collaboration and connection"). No fully-remote engineering track was found on the Canadian reqs.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **NO — effectively disqualifying.** Every relevant Canadian opening is anchored to a specific office city (Vancouver or Kitchener), and at least one states the hybrid requirement outright. Although the roles are in Canada — and Vancouver is only one time zone from Kimberley — a hybrid arrangement "based out of our Vancouver office" requires living within commuting distance of Vancouver, roughly 850 km from Kimberley. This is not workable without relocation. Note that BC placement does mean **British Columbia pay-transparency law applies**, so the Vancouver reqs post real salary ranges (see below).

Engineering & tech: Substantive platform and ML-infrastructure work across the open roles. Named signals: public **API and developer-platform** engineering (APIs, events, and frameworks enabling third-party integrations and plug-ins), **data pipelines (ETL)**, **high-throughput async systems (Redis, message queues)**, an **ML Inference Platform** team, agentic runtime work, telephony core and cloud ("Tel Core," "Tel Cloud"), search, and observability. The Ontario Staff Platform req names **Datadog and Grafana** for monitoring plus a **Vue.js/Vuex frontend running natively in Electron** and "numerous integrations with third-party services." Real-time voice at scale is a genuinely hard distributed-systems domain.

Pay transparency (first-party, posted on their own JDs):
- Software Engineer, Developer Platform — **British Columbia: CA$147,000–174,000** base
- Software Engineer, ML Inference Platform — **Ontario: CA$111,000–133,500** base
- Staff Software Engineer (Platform) — **Ontario: CA$194,500–226,750** base
Their standard wording: "Our salary ranges are determined by role, level, and location… compensation details listed reflect the base salary only, and do not include bonus, equity, or benefits."

Notable / other: Repeatedly recognized as a Great Place to Work. The "Scrappy" core trait is worth flagging — it is one of the profile's explicit red-signal words, and Dialpad lists it first among its five traits. The heavy internal-AI mandate ("AI isn't just a feature; it's how our teams do their best work") is a strong cultural expectation, not just a perk. Ten relevant open roles make this one of the higher-volume employers in the registry.

Open relevant roles (sample): Sr. Software Engineer, Developer Platform (Vancouver); Software Engineer, Developer Platform (Vancouver); Software Engineer, ML Inference Platform (Kitchener); Staff Software Engineer (Platform) (Kitchener); Sr. Software Engineer (Tel Cloud) / (Tel Core) (Vancouver, Kitchener); AI Engineer, Agentic Voice (TTS) (Vancouver, Kitchener); Software Engineer (Search).
