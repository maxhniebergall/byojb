# Akuity — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:akuity | company_type: product

What they do: Akuity builds an enterprise GitOps / software-delivery platform on top of Argo CD and Kargo — the open-source CD tooling its founders created while scaling Kubernetes at Intuit. The Akuity Platform lets teams manage development and deployment across hundreds-to-thousands of Kubernetes clusters from a single control plane. Argo CD is the third most popular CNCF project and is used by ~70% of companies running Kubernetes in production; named users include Intuit, BlackRock, Tesla, MLB and Peloton. Customers are enterprise DevOps / platform-engineering teams.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "to simplify the software delivery process so that DevOps and Platform Engineering teams can move fast, and deploy code effortlessly without the fear of breaking things," and to "modernize the cloud-native application delivery landscape" with an exceptional developer experience. JD language emphasizes "developers who build tools with an empathic mindset," cross-team collaboration, mentoring on engineering best practices, and "a genuine interest in designing a great end-to-end developer experience." The SRE JD frames the work as "high-ownership… you won't just respond to incidents, you'll shape how we define and defend reliability across the entire platform," with blameless post-mortems and toil reduction. Benefits page lists "flexible time off and a culture that respects it."

Size / stage / funding: Private. $20M Series A (May 2022) led by Lead Edge Capital with Decibel Partners; $24.5M total across 2 rounds. Roughly 40 employees (most recent public headcount data, Dec 2024). No publicly reported round since 2022. Founded 2021 by Argo co-creators Hong Wang (CEO), Jesse Suen (CTO), Alexander Matyushentsev (Chief Architect). Advisors include executives from Google Cloud, Elastic, Cisco and Intuit. SOC 2 Type 2 compliant with a public trust report.

Locations / HQ: Sunnyvale, California. Distributed team; no other offices advertised.

Remote policy: Fully remote. Stated verbatim in the JDs: "Fully remote: work from anywhere within US time zones (Pacific through Eastern), including Canada and other regions." Requirement line: "Live within US time zones (Pacific through Eastern), including Canada and other regions." Home-office stipend and equipment budget; flexible time off.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** Both live JDs name Canada explicitly as an eligible region provided the candidate is in a Pacific-to-Eastern time zone. Mountain Time (British Columbia interior) falls inside that window.

Engineering & tech: Go is the primary server-side language. Stack per their JD: Kubernetes (EKS) multi-region enterprise-grade clusters serving Argo CD and Kargo workloads; AWS as primary cloud across production and DR; Argo CD and Kargo run in-house; Prometheus, Grafana and OpenTelemetry for observability; Terraform plus GitOps-driven infrastructure management; Postgres/MySQL. Backend role asks for 5+ yrs experience, 3+ yrs Go, and experience building Kubernetes controllers/operators at scale. Advantages listed: multi-region/multi-cluster Kubernetes, compliance-adjacent infrastructure (SOC 2, ISO 27001, ISO 28000:2022, HIPAA, PCI DSS), and prior experience operating infra for other platform/developer-tooling companies. SRE role owns SLI/SLO/SLA definition, observability, on-call/incident command, runbooks and alerting fidelity.

Notable / other: The company's core asset is open-source credibility — you would be working directly with the engineers who built Argo CD and Kargo. Neither live JD posts a salary range; comp is described only as "competitive compensation, commensurate with experience" plus "equity participation in a well-funded, growing company."

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Backend Engineer (Remote — US Timezones); Senior Site Reliability Engineer (Remote — US Timezones).
