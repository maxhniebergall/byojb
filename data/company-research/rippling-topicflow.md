# Topicflow — research
Provider/key: rippling:topicflow | company_type: product

What they do: Topicflow is an AI-powered "memory management" platform for people managers. It ingests work signals from the tools a team already uses — chat, meeting notes, issue trackers, CRM — and synthesises them into a unified running record of team activity, so a manager can prepare for 1:1s and performance reviews without manually reconstructing what happened. The customer is the individual manager or the people-ops function; the product is a management/HR-adjacent SaaS application, not infrastructure or developer tooling.
