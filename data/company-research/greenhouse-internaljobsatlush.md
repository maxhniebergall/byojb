What they do: Lush is a cosmetics manufacturer and retailer — "You might know us as the inventors of the bath bomb, but there's more to this great-smelling, partly employee-owned, family-run company than pioneering cosmetics." Lush Digital is its in-house technology division, which per its own site builds "till systems, apps, and digital innovations" and runs the global digital estate: "a range of exciting online and in store experiences for our customers ... providing and maintaining a global digital estate across multiple platforms and devices."
How they describe themselves: Ethics-first, with an explicit anti-big-tech strategy. "We believe our business should put more back into the world than it takes and demonstrate that capitalism can be a force for good. We put our people first and fight cruel practices like animal testing, promote regeneration, and show that it is what's inside that counts - whether that's an ingredient in a product, or the minerals in a smartphone." On technology specifically: "Tech doesn't have to be unethical; it can be built for the greater good and impact positive social change", and technology "should give more than it takes from society and the environment" — they champion "ethical hardware, ethical data, and ethical design". On open source: "We love open source! The Cloud Native Computing Foundation is always our first port of call when a requirement for a new tool comes up. Whenever we can, the code we produce is made public for all to use", and they "work with the open-source community, build in-house or connect with third parties who follow the same best practices." They also publish a detailed land acknowledgement and a commitment to "reconciliation and decolonization ... guided by the Truth and Reconciliation Commission of Canada: Calls to Action #92".
Size / stage / funding: Established, "partly employee-owned, family-run" private company — explicitly not venture-funded or runway-dependent. Global retail footprint. Exact headcount unverified; the tech team is "an in-house tech and digital team, based across the globe".
Locations / HQ: Lush Cosmetics North America is headquartered in Canada (Vancouver area — their own text places their largest facilities "on the unceded territories of the Musqueam, Squamish and Tsleil-Waututh"), with a Toronto presence ("the lands of the Mississaugas of the Credit"). The Senior DevOps Engineer posting is Toronto, Ontario. Lush globally is UK-headquartered (Poole, Dorset).
Remote policy: Not stated. Their own Lush Digital page says only that the team is "based across the globe"; the posting is location-tagged Toronto with no remote provision given.
Remote-Canada eligibility: Canadian employment is certain (Lush Cosmetics North America is a Canadian-headquartered entity with a Toronto office). **Remote eligibility from BC is unverified** — the posting is tagged Toronto and states no remote arrangement, so it should not be assumed. Note the role does require "on-call support for global production environments during emergency scenarios", which implies out-of-hours coverage across global timezones.
Engineering & tech: A genuinely modern, open-source cloud-native platform stack — Terraform for IaC, Docker/Podman + Kubernetes + Helm for orchestration, GitLab CI/CD and ArgoCD (plus "custom in-house tooling"), an observability stack of Loki/Prometheus/Grafana, and Cloudflare for "proxying, geo-aware load balancing, and workers". Google Cloud is used, chosen partly for "100% renewable energy". Architecture is "microservices, APIs, and cloud infrastructure". Asks "5+ years of experience in DevOps or a related technical role". The role reports to the Senior Backend Engineering Manager.
Strategic direction worth noting: "A key strategic priority for LUSH Digital is our transition away from big-tech reliance; you will play a pivotal role in this journey by researching, scoping, and implementing open-source solutions that enhance our independence and development velocity." The stated focus is internal enablement — "building and refining the tools that empower our engineering team to reliably deploy, monitor, and scale their applications."
Compensation: The Senior DevOps Engineer posting states $91,000-$99,000 CAD base.
Notable / other: Publishes code publicly where it can; CNCF-first tooling policy; strong stated ethical positions (anti-animal-testing, ethical data and hardware, decolonization commitments).
