# Conga — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:conga | company_type: product

What they do: Conga sells a unified "revenue lifecycle management" / commercial-operations SaaS
platform — pricing, CPQ (configure-price-quote), contract lifecycle management (CLM), rebates and
document automation on one platform. Its own site describes it as "Unified Pricing, CPQ, CLM, and
Document Automation" and says the point is to "align pricing, quoting, and contracting with shared,
AI-driven intelligence so teams stay aligned and move from decision to execution with clarity and
speed." Customers are large enterprises; the company claims "over 10,000 customers worldwide,
including over 50% of the Fortune 100," and names Twilio, T-Mobile, LinkedIn, Adobe and Nestle.

How they describe themselves: The careers copy leads with "A career that's the whole package!" and
"At Conga, we've built a community where our colleagues can thrive. Here you'll find opportunities
to innovate and support growth through individual and team development, all within an environment
where every voice is heard." The site refers to a values framework it calls "The Conga Way," but the
homepage does not enumerate the individual values, so the full value set is UNVERIFIED here.
Marketing tone is enterprise/steady ("trusted decisions," "consistent buyer experiences,"
"enterprise-grade infrastructure," "purpose-built AI with embedded governance") rather than
hype-driven. The JD itself uses no hustle language: no "fast-paced," "wear many hats," or "scrappy."

Size / stage / funding: Private company (no investor-relations presence found). Scale indicators are
customer-side rather than headcount-side: 10,000+ customers, >50% of the Fortune 100. Exact
headcount, ownership and profitability are UNVERIFIED — the homepage does not state them and web
search was unavailable this session.

Locations / HQ: Not stated on the homepage — UNVERIFIED. The company operates globally and hires in
Canada (see below). Historically associated with Colorado (Broomfield), but that is not confirmed
from a fetched source and should be treated as unverified.

Remote policy: At least partly remote-friendly at the role level. The live Staff DevOps JD states
plainly: "**Location:** Canada (Remote)". No company-wide remote/hybrid policy statement was found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **VERIFIED YES** for the relevant open role. The JD
(https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/conga/jobs/5182920007) header reads "Location: Canada (Remote)",
with no city named and no in-office requirement anywhere in the posting. Timezone expectations are
not stated; the reporting line is to a "Senior Director, Cloud Operations."

Engineering & tech: The DevOps/platform stack is explicitly AWS **and** Microsoft Azure, Terraform
and CloudFormation for IaC, Kubernetes and Docker, enterprise-scale CI/CD, microservices /
multi-tenant SaaS-PaaS-IaaS architecture, observability and incident response, disaster recovery and
fault-tolerant design, cloud security (OAuth, WS-Security, encryption at rest/in transit, IAM),
Agile with Atlassian Jira. Application languages named are .NET Core/.NET Framework, Java and
JavaScript. The Staff role is framed as cross-org technical leadership: "define architectural
standards," "drive modernization initiatives," "provide mentorship and technical guidance to
engineers across the organization," and explicitly "goes beyond operational support." SRE practices
and observability frameworks are called out as an edge. No public engineering blog or handbook was
located (unverified rather than absent).

Notable / other: Publishes an Applicant Privacy Statement (https://conga.com/applicant-privacy-
statement) and states it does not accept third-party agency resumes. Standard EEO language plus an
explicit "don't meet every requirement? apply anyway" encouragement. Product messaging is heavily
AI-flavoured but positioned as governed AI inside an established enterprise product, not as an
AI-wrapper startup.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer, Dev Ops (Remote Canada). Registry also lists
Senior Software Engineer, Senior Software Engineer AI, and Staff Software Engineer- AI under this
portal.
