# TENEX.AI — research
Provider/key: ashby:tenex | company_type: product
What they do: TENEX is a Managed Detection and Response (MDR) cybersecurity provider — they describe themselves as "AI-native, automation-first, built-for-scale." The product is an AI layer over security operations: autonomous detection, RAG-backed investigation and auto-remediation workflows running over billions of security events, sold as a managed service to organizations wanting improved threat detection and response. Note this is a *managed service* business (a SOC delivered as a service) rather than a pure software product, though the engineering roles are product/platform engineering.
How they describe themselves: "We are a force multiplier for defenders, helping organizations enhance their cybersecurity posture through advanced threat detection, rapid response, and continuous protection." On team and stage: "Our team is composed of industry experts with deep experience in cybersecurity, automation, and AI-driven solutions. Backed by leading investors, we are rapidly growing and seeking top talent to join our mission of revolutionizing the AI-Native MDR landscape." On joining early: "We're a fast-growing startup ... As an early employee, you'll play a meaningful role in defining and building our culture. Get in on the ground floor. We're a small but well-funded team that just raised a substantial round – joining now comes with limited risk and unlimited upside." On culture and location: "Culture is one of the most important things at TENEX.AI—explore our culture deck at culture.tenex.ai to witness how we embody it, **prioritizing the irreplaceable collaboration and community of in-person work**." They state pay transparency as a practice: "We practice pay transparency and reward performance."
Size / stage / funding: Early-stage and explicitly self-described as small. Seed round led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z); a later, larger round led by Crosspoint Capital Partners, with Shield Capital, DTCP (formerly Deutsche Telekom Capital Partners), Deepwork Capital and the Florida Opportunity Fund also backing. Exact headcount and round sizes not verified.
Locations / HQ: HQ is Sarasota, FL. Other offices: San Jose, CA and a Kansas City, MO SOC. Some roles list "Remote, USA."
Remote policy: Hybrid-mandatory and office-centric. JDs state: "This role will require Monday - Thursday onsite in any of our locations. WFH Friday," and on other roles "This role will require a hybrid work schedule in any of our locations." Postings tagged "Remote, USA" sit alongside the same in-person requirement, so the "remote" tag should not be read as remote-eligible. They explicitly prioritize "the irreplaceable collaboration and community of in-person work."
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — verified negative.** Four days per week onsite at a US office (Sarasota FL, San Jose CA, or Kansas City MO) is required; all location tags are US. There is no Canadian office and no indication of non-US employment.
Engineering & tech: Python, Go, Rust or Java; agentic systems design including centralized and decentralized multi-agent (MAS) architectures; graph structures and graph databases; LLM productionization, fine-tuning, prompt libraries; RAG; tool-calling agents; multi-modal models over text, logs and graphs; streaming feature pipelines at billions-of-events scale; evaluation and reliability work (red-team testing, latency budgets, fallback strategies). Engineers partner tightly with Product, Detection Engineering and Customer Success to convert attacker behaviour into ML and rule-based detections. Notably the "AI/ML Engineer" role asks only "1 - 3+ years" of software experience while describing deep agentic-systems ownership.
