# Langfuse — research
Provider/key: ashby:langfuse | company_type: product

What they do (their own words): "Open Source LLM Engineering Platform that helps teams build useful AI applications via tracing, evaluation, and prompt management." They position it as "the 'Datadog' of this category; model capabilities continue to improve, but building useful applications is really hard, both in startups and enterprises." It is observability and evaluation infrastructure for LLM applications — traces, evals, prompt management — offered both as Langfuse Cloud and as a self-hostable open-source deployment.

How they describe themselves: Engineering-dense and scale-proud rather than culture-declarative. Traction claims, all first-party: "Largest open source solution in this category: trusted by 19 of the Fortune 50, >2k customers, >26M monthly SDK downloads, >6M Docker pulls," and "Langfuse processes over a billion trace events per month." On the work: "This isn't a 'maintain what exists' role. We're scaling fast, and you'll be the person who makes sure the infrastructure grows ahead of demand — not behind it," and "You'll be thinking ahead about what breaks at 10x scale and building the foundation before we get there. **10x is always just one quarter away here at Langfuse.**" They describe the team as "small, engineering-heavy, and experienced." They maintain a public "how we work" document (linked from their JDs) and a stated mission/product page, though those were not fetched here.

Size / stage / funding: **Acquired — now part of ClickHouse.** Their own words: "We are now part of ClickHouse... We joined ClickHouse in January 2026 because LLM observability is fundamentally a data problem and Langfuse already ran on ClickHouse. Together we can move faster on product while staying true to open source and self-hosting, and join forces on GTM and sales to accelerate revenue." Previously: Y Combinator, with backing from Lightspeed and General Catalyst. Headcount not stated beyond "small... team." The ClickHouse acquisition materially changes the stability picture — this is no longer an independent runway-dependent startup, though it is also very recent (Jan 2026, ~7 months old at time of writing).

Locations / HQ: Team is in **Berlin and San Francisco**. Postings are tagged "Europe, Zurich, Munich, Paris, Berlin, London."

Remote policy: Remote **within EU timezones, with mandatory monthly office attendance**. Stated verbatim: "We're a small, engineering-heavy, and experienced team in Berlin and San Francisco. **We are also hiring for engineering in EU timezones and expect one week per month in our Berlin office**."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — explicitly ruled out.** The company states it hires engineering "in EU timezones" and requires one week per month physically in Berlin. Kimberley, BC is Mountain Time, 8-9 hours behind Berlin, and monthly transatlantic travel from interior BC is not a viable arrangement. Every relevant posting is tagged with European cities only. This is a clear, evidence-backed no.

Engineering & tech: Very strong infrastructure substance. Langfuse Cloud runs on **AWS ECS Fargate and ClickHouse Cloud, with Datadog as the observability backbone**. The Senior Cloud Infrastructure Engineer owns "deployments, autoscaling, capacity planning, and cost optimization," the full Datadog setup "end to end — dashboards, alerts, and SLOs," and the public self-hosted distribution: "our Helm chart, Docker Compose configuration, and deployment documentation... from a single-node setup to a multi-region enterprise deployment." Also CI/CD, infrastructure-as-code, zero-downtime deploys, and security/compliance hardening for enterprise adoption. Requirements: AWS (ECS/Fargate, networking, IAM, S3), Kubernetes and/or ECS, Helm, Docker, Terraform/Pulumi/CloudFormation, and strong monitoring instincts. Forward-looking product directions named: "complex long-running agent observability and real-time evaluation." Being inside ClickHouse is pitched as a technical advantage: "the team behind the database at the core of our stack is one channel away."

Compensation (first-party, posted on every relevant JD): **€90K – €160K base, plus equity** — the same band is stated across all four senior engineering roles (Data Infrastructure, IAM & Billing, Cloud Infrastructure, SDK). Unusually transparent; note it is a single wide company-wide senior band rather than role-specific.

Notable / other: Open-source-first with a genuinely large distribution footprint (26M monthly SDK downloads, 6M Docker pulls), which means a lot of the infrastructure work is public and portable as a portfolio. The product category — LLM tracing, evals, prompt management — is precisely the "AI infrastructure not AI wrapper" line the profile draws.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Cloud Infrastructure Engineer; Senior Backend Engineer (Data Infrastructure); Senior Backend Engineer (IAM and Billing); Senior Software Engineer (SDK).
