# DexCare — research
Provider/key: lever:dexcarehealth | company_type: product

What they do: DexCare sells a "digital care orchestration" / patient-navigation platform to hospital systems. It ties together provider search, scheduling, provider data management and virtual care so health systems can align patient demand against clinician capacity — predicting constraints, scheduling precisely, and cutting operational cost. Product surfaces named on their site: Search and Schedule, PDM+ (provider data management), Optimize AI (capacity and routing optimization), Virtual On Demand (telehealth), plus a federal-health offering. Their JD states the platform currently serves 57 million patients.

How they describe themselves: Mission stated as "Nobody waits for care"; site framing is "making care findable" via navigation infrastructure. They also state a DEIB mission: "Creating an inclusive workplace where diversity drives innovation, equity ensures fairness, and belonging strengthens collaboration, enabling everyone to thrive." The SRE JD's tone is unusually craft-oriented rather than hustle-oriented — they ask for "someone who takes pride in a clean Terraform module, cares about observability because they've felt the pain of flying blind, and believes good documentation is an act of kindness for your teammates," and describe postmortems "with a focus on learning, not blame." Explicitly: "You don't need to have all the answers."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed; headcount and funding stage are not disclosed on the pages fetched. Originally incubated inside Providence health system (not restated on the current site — treat as unverified background). Customer base is large health systems, which implies enterprise-sales-cycle revenue rather than consumer burn.

Locations / HQ: HQ location is not stated on the pages fetched (historically Seattle, WA — unverified). Named customers span the US: Providence, SSM Health, Kaiser Permanente, Piedmont Healthcare, Texas Health Resources, El Camino Health, MUSC.

Remote policy: The open SRE role is posted as "Remote". No company-wide remote-policy statement was found on the site.

Remote-Canada eligibility: UNVERIFIED — leaning no. The posting says "Remote, remote" with no country named, but every named customer is a US health system, the posted band is USD, and US health-system data (PHI/HIPAA) work is commonly restricted to US-based staff. No Canadian entity or Canada hiring language found.

Engineering & tech: Concrete and infrastructure-heavy from the JD — AWS with EKS, IAM and RBAC; Terraform/Terragrunt; GitHub Actions CI/CD; Datadog for observability; Python for automation; runbooks and postmortems. Agile with an on-call rotation. Infrastructure sits in a department literally named "Infrastructure."

Pay: The Senior Site Reliability Engineer JD posts $125,000–$165,000 USD base (ATS-posted).

Notable / other: Customer-outcome claims on their site: SSM Health quadrupled online scheduling; Providence increased patient acquisition 30%; typical clients see 40% more appointments without adding staff.
