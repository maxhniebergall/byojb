# SecurityScorecard — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:securityscorecard | company_type: product

**Registry naming note:** the registry stores the display name lowercase as "securityscorecard"; the trading name is **SecurityScorecard**. Cosmetic, but worth fixing.

What they do: SecurityScorecard rates the cyber-health of organisations and their vendors. It collects outside-in, non-intrusive signals about a company's internet-facing posture and assigns a security rating of "A" through "F", packaged with data analytics and remediation guidance. The primary use case is third-party / supply-chain risk management — enterprises monitoring the security posture of their vendors in real time. In their own words: "We measure your and your vendors' cyber-health by assigning a security rating of 'A' through 'F' based on outside-in, non-intrusive data. Our Comprehensive security ratings, advanced data analytics, and actionable insights discover Third-Party Vulnerabilities & Security Gaps In Real-Time." They claim to rate 1.5 million companies and serve 1,000+ enterprise customers.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "to make the world a safer place." Vision — "to create a new language for companies and their partners to communicate, understand, and improve each other's security posture." Self-positioning is category-creation plus hype markers: "We have created a new category of enterprise software, and our culture has helped us be recognized as one of the 10 hottest SaaS startups in NY for two years in a row." Note the boilerplate is stale — it still says "over 200+ employees" and "raised over $110M USD," figures that predate the current $292M total. No values list is published in the JD boilerplate.

Size / stage / funding: Private, NYC-headquartered. Total funding ~$292M; investors named in their own copy are Google, Sequoia and Riverwood. Most recent round was roughly five years ago (Series E, 2021) — a long time without a raise. Current valuation reported around $0.9B, i.e. below the peak-cycle unicorn mark. Headcount is in the several-hundreds; the self-description's "200+" is outdated.

Locations / HQ: Headquartered in New York City, with employees globally. The live postings cluster across the US (NY, SF, Boston, Austin, Dallas, Salt Lake City), Canada (Halifax NS, Montreal QC, plus a generic "Remote (Canada)"), and Latin America (Brazil, Argentina, generic LATAM).

Remote policy: Historically remote-friendly rather than remote-first, headquartered in NYC. Employee reporting indicates a shift away from that — remote work "was offered but that started changing with mandatory RTO." The live postings still carry remote location tags in several geographies, so the practice appears inconsistent or in transition. Benefits are country-specific: "we offer a competitive salary, stock options, Health benefits, and unlimited PTO, parental leave, tuition reimbursements."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes at the posting level.** Multiple live requisitions are explicitly Canada-located and quote CAD ranges — Senior Site Reliability Engineer "Remote (Canada)", Senior AI Engineer "Remote (CAN)", Staff AI Engineer "Remote (CAN)", and Senior Software Engineer postings for Halifax NS (Remote) and Montreal QC (Remote). CAD-denominated compensation ranges on those requisitions confirm Canadian payroll rather than a US role open to Canadians. Province is not restricted in the generic "Remote (Canada)" posting. Caveat: the reported RTO drift means the durability of this is uncertain, and NYC anchoring puts core hours 2 hours ahead of Mountain Time.

Engineering & tech: Security-data platform work — large-scale internet-wide scanning and data collection, analytics over that corpus, ratings computation, and the SaaS delivering it. The open role families are Senior/Staff AI Engineer, Senior Site Reliability Engineer, Senior Data Engineer and Senior Software Engineer, indicating a real data-engineering and platform-infrastructure org rather than a thin product team. Detailed stack is not published in the JD boilerplate captured.

Compensation (from their own live JDs, all stated as **total compensation, base plus bonus**, not base alone):
- Senior AI Engineer: USD 160,000 – 185,000 (US) / CAD 140,000 – 170,000 (Canada)
- Staff AI Engineer: USD 180,000 – 220,000 (US) / CAD 170,000 – 200,000 (Canada)
- Senior Site Reliability Engineer: USD 152,000 – 195,000 (US) / **CAD 197,500 – 225,000** (Canada)
- Senior Software Engineer: roughly USD ~172,500 midpoint across US metros; CAD postings in a similar band
Standard qualifier on every posting: "Actual compensation for the position is based on a variety of factors, including, but not limited to affordability, skills, qualifications and experience, and may vary from the range." The word "affordability" appearing first in that list is an unusual and telling inclusion. Equity is stock options; PTO unlimited.

Notable / other: The significant negative signal is workforce stability. Employee-reported layoffs occurred in **May 2026**, and reviews describe layoffs recurring roughly quarterly, "sometimes indiscriminate of performance and completely cost cutting measures," with staff let go despite positive performance reviews, and leadership repeatedly promising no further layoffs before conducting more. Glassdoor sits at 3.4/5 (360 reviews) and Blind at 2.9/5 (9 reviews); RepVue carries a review titled "Cut throat culture and constant layoffs." No raise since ~2021 against a ~$0.9B valuation is consistent with a company under sustained cost pressure. These are third-party aggregator and review sources, cited as such — weak evidence individually, but consistent across four independent ones.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior AI Engineer (Remote CAN / Remote US); Staff AI Engineer (Remote CAN / Remote US); Senior Site Reliability Engineer (Remote Canada / US / Brazil / Argentina); Senior Data Engineer (Remote Brazil / LATAM / Argentina); Senior Software Engineer (Halifax NS, Montreal QC, and several US metros, all Remote).
