# Found — research
Provider/key: ashby:found | company_type: product

What they do: Found builds business banking and financial tooling for self-employed people, freelancers and small business owners — banking, bookkeeping, invoicing, expense tracking and automated tax handling in one app. Found is a financial technology company, not a bank; banking services are provided by Lead Bank (Member FDIC). The core account has no fees, no monthly minimums and no credit check; revenue comes from optional paid tiers (Found Plus at $35/month or $315/year; Found Pro at $80/month or $720/year). The product serves tens of thousands of weekly users.

How they describe themselves: Mission — **"We're on a mission to simplify finances for small business owners."** In their own words: "Small business owners are the backbone of the American economy. But running a small business comes with challenges: taxes, accounting, bookkeeping, and banking are just a few. That's where we come in." They state they are "looking for kind, resourceful, and passionate people to join us in building the financial foundation small businesses deserve", that "everyone does their best work when they feel healthy, happy, and supported", and that "everyone on our team should feel and act like an owner." They also state plainly that "diversity, equity, and inclusion are crucial to our success." The tone is notably calm and low-hype for a fintech — the language is about support and peace of mind rather than disruption or velocity. The one exception is a JD phrase describing Found as "a fast-paced company" and asking for someone "capable of balancing moving fast while moving safely."

Size / stage / funding: **Series C**, private. Investors include Sequoia Capital, Founders Fund, Lightspeed Venture Partners and Khosla Ventures — a top-tier cap table. Total headcount is not disclosed, but the engineering organization is stated in the Platform JD as **30+ engineers**, so this is a mid-sized startup, not a large company.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco HQ, with a second office in New York City. Canadian presence in **Toronto and Vancouver**. Fully remote working is supported for team members outside those cities.

Remote policy: Remote-friendly with office hubs. In their own words: "We have Found offices in SF and NYC. For team members who work outside those cities, Found also supports fully remote working." The careers page frames it as "fully remote work across the US, with an SF hub for those who thrive on in-person collaboration." Not a documentation/handbook-async company as far as is publicly visible, but remote is a supported first-class arrangement rather than an exception.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, and structurally so.** The live Staff Software Engineer, Platform req lists its locations as "**Vancouver, Toronto or Remote (Canada)**, San Francisco, New York or Remote (USA)" — Remote (Canada) is named explicitly, not as an afterthought. The compensation statement confirms a Canadian employing entity: "The anticipated salary range for this role is $210,000 - $278,000 (**USD or CAD**). The salary range listed represents the low and high end of the anticipated salary range for this position across all locations **in the corresponding country**." Found runs country-specific payroll and has named Canadian office cities. Note that the benefits list published on the JD is labelled "Perks & Benefits of Found (**USA**)" — 401K, FSA, commuter, 16 weeks flexible parental leave, medical/dental/vision 100% covered for employees and 90% for dependents, meaningful equity, generous/unlimited vacation — so the Canadian benefits package is a separate schedule that would need to be asked about directly.

Engineering & tech: A ~30-person engineering organization taking "products from ideation to being used by tens of thousands of users every week", responsible for "everything from building the product to reliable and performant technical operations." The **Platform team** is described as "a small but high-impact group" with "broad ownership across reliability, security, compliance, and developer experience" supporting the 30+ engineers. Stated stack and concerns from the Platform JD: public cloud, databases, distributed job processing, CDN/WAF, load balancing, backend services; OpenTelemetry / statsd / Prometheus for observability; infrastructure as code (Terraform, CloudFormation); Sidekiq and Redis for async jobs (implying a Ruby/Rails backend); Capacitor or React Native for mobile app infrastructure. Compliance frameworks PCI and SOC2 are in scope. The team runs formal SLAs/SLOs and owns incident response and on-call experience. Staff-level expectations are architecture, mentorship, technical vision and cross-team quality/tech-debt work; the bar stated is 8+ years of backend experience in a public cloud environment.

Notable / other: Compensation is unusually transparent and high for the stage — a stated $210K–$278K band for a Staff platform role, in the candidate's own currency. Found publishes an explicit invitation to ask questions in the application ("If you have any questions please include them in your application"), and uses `#LI-DNI` on the req. The domain — banking, bookkeeping and tax for the self-employed — is a "boring", mission-critical, regulated area rather than a hype-cycle product, and there is no visible AI-wrapper positioning.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer, Platform (Vancouver / Toronto / Remote Canada / SF / NY / Remote USA); Senior Software Engineer (Full-Stack).
