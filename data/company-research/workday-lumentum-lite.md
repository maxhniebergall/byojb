# Lumentum — research
Provider/key: workday:lumentum/lite | company_type: product
What they do: Optical and photonic components and modules. Their own mission line: "Illuminating the networks of tomorrow with breakthrough optical and photonic technologies that power innovation across AI, cloud, telecommunications, industrial, and sensing applications." Product lines cover data-centre optical connectivity (a major AI-infrastructure supply position), telecom optical components, industrial lasers, and 3D-sensing VCSELs. This is a hardware/photonics manufacturer — software roles exist to support process, test and network-OS work rather than as the product itself.
How they describe themselves: The about page foregrounds four commitments — **Sustainability, Quality, Supply Chain (resilience), and Diversity & Inclusion ("Together")**. Careers benefits framing lists "Health & Wellness Programs," "Financial Security," "Work-Life Balance," and "Learning & Growth." No engineering handbook or eng blog was found.
Size / stage / funding: Public company (investor.lumentum.com referenced); headcount not stated on the pages read. As an optical-component supplier its results track the telecom/datacom capex cycle — this sector has historically been volatile, though nothing about layoffs or financial stress was verified this session.
Locations / HQ: Headquartered in **San Jose, California**, with "R&D, manufacturing, and sales locations" worldwide (a Slovenia facility is pictured). The about page does not list Ottawa, but the live requisitions confirm an **Ottawa, Ontario** site ("Canada - Ottawa (Bill Leathem)") and a UK site (Towcester, Northamptonshire).
Remote policy: Not stated corporately. The evidence in their own JDs points the other way: the Ottawa Senior Process Automation Software Engineer req states "Location: Ottawa (On-Site)." The Senior DevOps Engineer and the (now-closed) Senior Software Engineer (SONiC) reqs are facet-tagged hybrid.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No.** The Canadian roles are tied to the Ottawa Bill Leathem site and are explicitly on-site or hybrid — consistent with a photonics manufacturer whose engineers work near lab and fab equipment. Nothing supports a fully-remote BC hire.
Engineering & tech: Process-automation software and test/calibration engineering supporting optical manufacturing; a network-OS role (SONiC — the open-source switch operating system, i.e. genuine data-centre networking software); a Senior DevOps Engineer at the same Ottawa site. The "backend" in "Senior Process Development Engineer (Backend)" (Towcester, UK) refers to semiconductor **back-end process** — packaging/assembly — not backend software.
Notable / other: Lumentum publishes pay ranges on its Canadian reqs, which is unusual for a hardware company: the Ottawa Senior Process Automation Software Engineer states "Salary Range: $93,000 - $133,000 CAD (flexible)" with "annual bonus, equity, and comprehensive health and welfare benefits" on top. Registry title drift is significant: the Ottawa Senior Process Automation Software Engineer sits at a URL slugged "Test-Engineer--Optical-Calibration-and-Test-".
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior DevOps Engineer (Ottawa); Senior Process Automation Software Engineer (Ottawa); Senior Process Development Engineer (Backend) (Towcester, UK); Senior Software Engineer (SONiC) (Ottawa) — closed.
