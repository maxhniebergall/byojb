# Fora Financial — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:forafinancial | company_type: product
What they do: Technology-enabled provider of flexible financing (working capital) to small and medium-sized US businesses. In their own words: "Since 2008, we have supported more than 55,000 merchants nationwide with over $4 billion in capital for operating expenses, cash-flow management, and growth." Their proprietary technology "helps deliver capital through a streamlined process that can be completed in as little as 24 hours, compared with the weeks or months often required for a traditional bank loan."
How they describe themselves: "Fora has grown from two founders in a small Manhattan workspace to a company of nearly 200 employees with a collaborative, partner-centric culture." The JD's framing of the work is measured and specific rather than hype-driven: they want "a systems thinker who understands business impact and operational burden", and describe the role as a "hands-on Staff IC" who will "make strategic architecture calls ... and then get into the weeds to build, harden, or rebuild pipelines". No slogan-style values list was obtainable (no first-party values page was reachable).
Size / stage / funding: ~200 employees (stated on the JD). Private, founded 2008, 18 years old. Funding stage not stated; the $4B cumulative origination figure and the age suggest an established lender rather than an early-stage startup. Profitability unverified.
Locations / HQ: New York City (originated in "a small Manhattan workspace"). The JD lists onsite perks ("Weekly breakfast, snacks, and Friday lunches provided onsite"), so at least one physical office is active.
Remote policy: Stated verbatim on the JD: "Fully remote within the US; Eastern or Central time zones preferred." The benefits list also says "Remote working model" alongside onsite food perks — remote-first with an NYC office.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — explicitly. "Fully remote within the US" is a first-party statement on their own live JD. The Eastern/Central time-zone preference additionally works against Mountain Time.
Engineering & tech: Small Data & AI team; the Staff Data Engineer reports to a VP of Data & AI. Stack and direction from the JD: Snowflake (warehouse design, RBAC, performance tuning, cost controls) as the target platform; retiring legacy Azure Data Factory and SQL-backup reporting paths; Azure data services (Event Hubs, Blob Storage, Azure SQL, Azure DevOps) as the incumbent environment; CDC/Debezium, dbt Cloud, Dagster or Airflow; Monte Carlo/Elementary for observability; Apache Iceberg as a nice-to-have. Explicit platform discipline: data contracts, schema-change handling, upstream SLAs, freshness/volume/null/duplicate monitors, backfills, runbooks, incident response. They are "modernizing its data stack to build the foundation for agentic products and analytics" and want engineers who "build data products that agents can consume safely" — semantic layers, MCP servers and governed context retrieval are named explicitly.
Notable / other: Compensation posted openly on the JD ($175,000-$200,000 base) with the note that "Final compensation is based on scope of past ownership, technical judgment, and ability to set direction independently." Benefits: 401(k) match, employer-paid life insurance, subsidised medical/dental/vision, health-care and dependent-care FSAs, commuter benefits, generous time off with rollover vacation days.
Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Data Engineer (Remote, US only).
