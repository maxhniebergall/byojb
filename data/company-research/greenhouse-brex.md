# Brex — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:brex | company_type: product

What they do: Brex is "the intelligent finance platform that enables companies to spend smarter and move faster in more than 200 markets" — global corporate cards and business banking combined with spend management, bill pay, and travel software. Their pitch is AI-native automation that eliminates manual expense and accounting work. Customers include DoorDash, Coinbase, Robinhood, Zoom, Plaid, Reddit and SeatGeek — "tens of thousands" of companies.

How they describe themselves: "Working at Brex allows you to push your limits, challenge the status quo, and collaborate with some of the brightest minds in the industry. We're committed to building a diverse team and inclusive culture and believe your potential should only be limited by how big you can dream." On engineering, in their own words: "Engineering at Brex is about building systems that scale with **speed and intention**. Our teams span Software, Data, Security, and IT, and operate with high autonomy and deep collaboration. We tackle hard technical problems, own our outcomes, and push for excellence at every level — from architecture to deployment. It's an environment where engineering is a craft, and builders become leaders."

Size / stage / funding: Late-stage fintech, formerly one of the highest-valued private fintechs. **Materially changed status: the JD footer states "Brex LLC is a wholly owned subsidiary of Capital One, N.A."** — Brex is now under Capital One rather than an independent venture-backed startup, which is a significant stability (and culture) signal in both directions. Roughly 1,000+ employees.

Locations / HQ: US HQ San Francisco; engineering hubs in **Seattle, WA** and **Vancouver, BC**, plus New York and a São Paulo presence. The Vancouver office is a real, staffed engineering site — most Canadian roles are mirrored 1:1 with Seattle.

Remote policy: **Hybrid, in-office by default.** Stated verbatim on the JD: "This role will be based in our Vancouver office. We are a hybrid environment that combines the energy and connections of being in the office with the benefits and flexibility of working from home. We currently require a minimum of **three coordinated days in the office per week, Monday, Wednesday and Thursday**. As a perk, we also have up to four weeks per year of fully remote work."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No.** Brex hires in Canada, but only into the Vancouver office on a 3-day-a-week hybrid schedule. There is no remote-Canada pathway in any live posting. For a candidate in Kimberley, BC who cannot relocate, the Canadian roles are effectively out of reach despite being nominally "in BC."

Engineering & tech: Backend in **Java, Kotlin, Python**; SQL and NoSQL databases; teams organised across Software, Data, Security and IT. Named team areas from the live board: Backend / Product Engineering, Product Data Platform, Data Enablement Platform, Cloud Infrastructure, Release Infra, Credit Limit Engineering, Banking, Travel & Expense, and an AI Engineer (Ecosystem) role. Senior JDs ask 7+ years of full-stack design/deploy experience and describe building "customer-facing products that will drive direct business impact."

Notable / other: Compensation is posted openly and is high: Senior backend $192,000–$240,000 CAD (identical figure in USD for Seattle), Software Engineer II $152,000–$190,000 CAD, Staff $240,000–$285,000 CAD; equity and other components on top. Brex explicitly warns about recruiting scams — their recruiters only contact via LinkedIn or a brex.com email domain.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Backend (Vancouver / Seattle); Senior Software Engineer, Product Data Platform (Vancouver / Seattle); Senior Software Engineer, Data Enablement Platform (Seattle); Senior Software Engineer, Release Infra (Seattle); Staff Software Engineer, Banking (Vancouver / Seattle); Staff Software Engineer, Travel & Expense (Vancouver / Seattle); Software Engineer II, Backend / Cloud Infrastructure (Vancouver); AI Engineer, Ecosystem (Seattle).
