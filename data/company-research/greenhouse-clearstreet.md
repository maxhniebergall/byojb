# Clear Street — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:clearstreet | company_type: product (financial services / brokerage infrastructure)
What they do: "Clear Street is modernizing the brokerage ecosystem. Founded in 2018, Clear Street is a diversified financial services firm replacing the legacy infrastructure used across capital markets." They built a cloud-native clearing and custody system from scratch: "Our platform is fully integrated with central clearing houses and exchanges to support billions in trading volume per day. We've agonized about our data model abstractions, created horizontal scalability, and crafted thoughtful APIs." Products include Clear Street Studio (portfolio management, real-time risk, analytics, P&L) and a new cloud-based active-trading platform. Their site frames the mission as giving "every sophisticated investor access to every asset, in every market", replacing 1970s-era infrastructure.
How they describe themselves (values, verbatim from a JD): "Engineers who are biased toward action and can make thoughtful trade off decisions with incomplete information"; "High ownership and accountability across the full software lifecycle"; "Strong communication and collaboration across engineering, trading, and operations"; "A willingness to challenge assumptions and continuously improve how we build". Also: "The opportunity to join a small and growing team of good people, where you can make a difference. A new, high-quality code base with little technical debt... A meritocratic philosophy that champions collaboration." And: "Our top priority is our people. We're continuously investing in a culture that promotes collaboration."
Size / stage / funding: 800+ employees; ~700 institutional clients; ~$16B customer balances; ~550M shares/day and ~$28.4B notional/day; 94% YoY transacted growth; "$1.0 billion in capital raised from some of the most prominent investors". Private, founded 2018.
Locations / HQ: HQ at 4 World Trade Center, 150 Greenwich St, Floor 45, New York, NY. Global clearing connections across North America, Europe, Middle East and Asia Pacific; a London office is implied by a "Software Engineer, Regulatory Reporting — London, UK" posting.
Remote policy: Not stated on their site. Two engineering postings carry the location "Remote", but every JD's benefits section is office-centric: "In-office benefits include lunch stipends, fully stocked kitchens, happy hours, a great location, and amazing views." Read that as remote-tolerant with a strong NYC office centre of gravity.
Remote-Canada eligibility: UNVERIFIED, lean NO. The postings say "Remote" without naming a country, and the salary ranges are quoted in USD with US-style disclosure language ("The Base Salary Range for this role is $170,000 - $240,000"), which points at US-remote. Nothing in any source states Canadian employment. Would need direct confirmation; do not assume.
Engineering & tech: Rust is the primary backend language for the Active trading platform; stack listed as "Rust, Aeron, Kafka, PostgreSQL, Kubernetes, gRPC, Snowflake". Also Postgres, TypeScript, React/React Native and "LLM APIs / model serving" on the AI Platform side. Work described: "mission-critical order and execution management infrastructure", "systems that balance low-latency execution, fault tolerance, and strict correctness guarantees", "efficient, audit-ready risk controls", and translating "evolving regulatory and market structure requirements (CAT, Reg NMS, MiFID) into reliable, testable platform capabilities". The AI Platform role is genuine platform work — "streaming responses, low-latency tool execution, caching, and reliable orchestration of model + tools", "authentication/authorization, rate limits, auditing, tracing, retries, and fallbacks", "deterministic order previews, confirmation flows, idempotent tool calls, and comprehensive audit logs" — not a chatbot wrapper.
Notable / other: Pay is disclosed on the JDs under NYC transparency rules: Senior/Staff Software Engineer $170,000-$240,000 base; Senior/Staff AI Platform Engineer $200,000-$350,000 base. Both explicitly base only — "The range stated does not include other factors of total compensation such as bonuses or equity." Benefits: company equity, 401k matching, gender-neutral parental leave, full medical/dental/vision. The AI Platform role asks for "At least Eight (8) years of experience".
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior / Staff AI Platform Engineer; Senior / Staff Software Engineer; Software Engineer - Market Data; Software Engineer - Low Touch Desk; Backend Software Engineer - Reference Data Services; Software Engineer, Regulatory Reporting (London).
