# Scribe — research
Provider/key: ashby:scribe | company_type: product

What they do: Scribe sells a "Workflow AI platform" that "automatically captures and optimizes how work gets done" — it records a user performing a process and turns it into step-by-step documentation/guides, then layers analytics and AI on top. Customers are enterprises and SMBs: per their own JD, "94% of the Fortune 500 use it, and 45% are paying customers", across "600,000 businesses".

How they describe themselves: "Scribe is where exceptional people come to do the best work of their careers… We're builders who hold a high bar, move fast, and care deeply about each other and our customers." A full published values list was not obtainable (their marketing site was not fetched — see Sources), so this is the complete set of self-description available: high bar, move fast, care deeply. They also state "At Scribe, we celebrate our differences and are committed to creating a workplace where all employees feel supported and empowered to do their best work."

Size / stage / funding: Stated in their own JD (as of the posting): Series C, valued at $1.3 billion, "$100M ARR in May 2026", "over 5 million daily active users across 600,000 businesses". Headcount not stated. Growth-stage and clearly well capitalized; profitability not claimed.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco. The JD's Location section reads: "Hybrid. Based in San Francisco. We work from the office 3 days per week." Other offices UNVERIFIED.

Remote policy: **Hybrid, not remote.** Three days per week in the San Francisco office, stated explicitly. Note that the Ashby board metadata for this posting carries `location: "Remote"`, which contradicts the JD body — the JD body is authoritative here (see Data-quality note).

Remote-Canada eligibility: **NO — verified.** The role is San Francisco hybrid, 3 days/week onsite. Not workable from Kimberley, BC.

Engineering & tech: Python, TypeScript and Go; AWS. The backend org owns "large-scale data ingestion and processing pipelines", "services that power AI inference with our in-house models" (i.e. they train/host their own models, not purely a vendor-API wrapper), and microservices in production. Message queues and orchestration named: Celery, SQS, RabbitMQ, Kafka. Django is a nice-to-have. The senior bar is high: "8+ years of experience building backend applications with Python, Typescript, or Go" plus "deep experience designing and operating distributed systems in production".

Notable / other: Benefits stated — health/dental/vision for employee and dependents, flexible PTO and company holidays, 401(k), paid parental leave, daily catered lunch (SF office), commuter benefits, home-office stipend. Equity: "All full-time employees receive equity in Scribe." Comp policy: "Salary varies by location… Final offers depend on experience and scope" — no range published on this JD despite the CA/SF posting, so no band is citable from it.
