# Skio — research
Provider/key: lever:skio | company_type: product

What they do: Skio builds subscription-management software for Shopify brands — the billing,
subscription-lifecycle and merchant-facing tooling that lets DTC brands sell recurring products.
Founded 2021. Their positioning is explicitly as the modern replacement for legacy subscription
apps: "Build the subscription platform that fast-growing brands actually want to use." Customers
are Shopify merchants — 1,000+ subscription brands per their careers page ("hundreds of brands"
per the JD).

How they describe themselves: From their own JD: "we're on a mission to help Shopify brands grow
faster by making subscriptions simple, powerful, and delightful… We run lean, move fast, and obsess
over impact. With a minimal-meeting culture and a bias for action, we make reversible decisions
quickly and reserve thoughtfulness for the ones that matter. We value **integrity, agency, and
grind**. We look for **drivers, not passengers**. Everyone **wears many hats**, takes ownership, and
pushes themselves and the team to be world-class. No micro-management. You have full autonomy and
responsibility." The careers page calls the team "the most execution-obsessed team in
subscriptions" and cites weekly feature releases as the competitive edge against legacy platforms.

Size / stage / funding: 67 team members. Founded 2021, so ~4 years old. **Profitable** — their JD
states "$30M+ ARR on just $8.4M raised. And we're profitable." Capital-efficient rather than
burn-driven. **Corporate status change: Skio joined Recharge, announced 2025** (Recharge is the
larger incumbent subscriptions platform), so Skio now sits inside a bigger parent rather than
operating as an independent startup.

Locations / HQ: No single HQ emphasized. The posting lists "San Francisco, New York, or Remote".
Team members are distributed globally — Portugal and Montreal are named on the careers page. The
2025 annual team offsite was in Montreal.

Remote policy: Global remote-first. Remote is an explicit option on the open posting, and the
careers page describes a globally distributed team with no geographic restriction stated.
Minimal-meeting culture is stated as a value, which is an async-friendly signal.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Likely yes, but not stated in policy form.** Evidence: the careers
page names Montreal among the places team members work and held the 2025 company offsite there;
the posting offers "Remote" alongside SF and NY; the company describes itself as globally
remote-first with no stated geographic restriction. Additionally, the salary band captured from
their own Lever posting is denominated in **CAD** ($150K–$250K), which is strong circumstantial
evidence that they hire and pay Canadians directly. Not a written policy, so worth confirming.

Engineering & tech: TypeScript and React heavily, on top of Shopify's infrastructure; a public API
at code.skio.com and docs at help.skio.com. The engineering shape is full-stack product work: the
JD asks you to "build entire product features end-to-end and own their success", "handle product
ambiguity and bring clarity, obsessing over user details to ship experiences that delight merchants
and customers", and build "scalable systems that keep us fast today and resilient tomorrow".
Notably, AI-assisted development is an explicit job expectation: "Use AI tools (like Claude Code,
Cursor, and others) to supercharge your output, improve quality, and automate the boring parts of
software development." Weekly release cadence. Engineers are also expected to "contribute to our
engineering culture… raising the bar for technical quality, team collaboration, and speed of
execution."

Notable / other: Benefits — "We pay well" with market-rate salaries, 100% employer-paid health,
dental and vision premiums (employee covers family add-ons), unlimited PTO, equity for all
employees, a MacBook, and an annual team offsite. No micro-management and full autonomy are stated
explicitly. The capital efficiency ($30M+ ARR on $8.4M raised, profitable) is unusual and is the
single most distinctive objective fact about the company.

Pay (from their own posting): $150,000–$250,000 CAD base for the Software Engineer role.

Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineer.
