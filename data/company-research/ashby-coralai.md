Entity check: The JD names its own domain — "At Coral AI (trycoral.ai)" — and the fetched trycoral.ai site matches (healthcare document automation), confirming this is the registry entity. Note the name collides with several unrelated things: Google's Coral edge-TPU hardware line, and at least one other "Coral AI" PDF-chat product. This is the healthcare-workflow company.

What they do: Agentic document-processing automation for US healthcare administration. Their words: "we're engineering the intelligent operating system for a more efficient healthcare industry... tackling a massive administrative bottleneck, a $450 billion crisis that consumes 25% of U.S. healthcare spending and forces patients to wait weeks for life-saving treatment." The product ingests the messy inputs of healthcare intake — "handwritten faxes, complex medical records, and blurry scans" — claiming "an unprecedented 98.7% accuracy", extracts clinical data and syncs it into EHR systems. Their site describes consolidating faxes, emails and CSVs into an intelligent queue, and cites "500,000+" cases processed and customer claims of "90%" reductions in administrative overhead. Customers: DME (durable medical equipment) providers, infusion centers, radiology, specialty pharmacy, clinics.

How they describe themselves: High-velocity, mission-heavy startup framing. "Backed by Lightspeed and founded by a team with deep roots in AI and robotics, we've scaled to multi-million dollars in ARR in less than six months by delivering immediate, Day-1 value." Impact framing: "By slashing patient wait times from five days to under three hours, we're doing more than just automating paperwork; we're returning time to clinicians." The candidate profile they ask for is explicit: "If you're a high-ownership builder who wants to solve mission-critical problems in OCR and NLP." Stated offer: "Opportunity to work on cutting-edge platform engineering challenges", "Collaborative and growth-oriented work environment", "Competitive salary and benefits", "Modern office in the heart of Bengaluru".

Size / stage / funding: Early-stage venture startup, Lightspeed-backed, self-reporting multi-million ARR reached in under six months and featured in Forbes. Headcount not disclosed and not verified. By its own account it is roughly a year or less into commercial scale — the definition of an early, funding-driven company.
