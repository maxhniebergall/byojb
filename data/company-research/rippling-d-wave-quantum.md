# D-Wave Quantum — research
Provider/key: rippling:d-wave-quantum | company_type: product

What they do: D-Wave (NYSE: QBTS) builds and sells quantum computing systems, software, and services. It describes itself as "the world's first commercial supplier of quantum computers, and the first and only to offer dual-platform quantum computing products and services, spanning both annealing and gate-model quantum computing technologies." Delivery is both on-premises enterprise systems and the Leap™ quantum cloud service (they claim 99.9% availability/uptime). More than 100 commercial, government, and research organizations are cited as customers. Additional offerings: the Ocean™ open-source developer tool suite, D-Wave Launch™ professional services, and training/research support.

How they describe themselves: Positioning is "the practical quantum computing company" — "find better answers to your toughest problems and put them to work faster" — with a mission to "help customers realize the value of quantum today". Their stated values, published in the JD as "A D-Waver's DNA", are: (1) "We look at the future and say 'why not'; we see possibilities where others see problems or routines. We show the way ahead and are committed to achieving ambitious goals."; (2) "We practice straight talk and listen generously to each other with empathy. We value different opinions and points of views. We ensure that we connect outside as well as inside to learn from others and inspire each other."; (3) "We hold ourselves accountable for delivering results. We make decisions & take responsibility so that we can act & support each other."; (4) "As leaders we motivate & engage our teams to undertake beyond what they originally thought possible, by developing our teams & creating the conditions for people to grow and empower themselves through enabling & coaching." On inclusion they note a team ranging "from distinguished domain experts with decades of experience in their respective fields, to bright and motivated graduates". Compensation philosophy, in their words: "We believe providing D-Wavers with company ownership, competitive pay, and a range of meaningful benefits is the start of creating a culture where people want to give the best they've got" — total rewards are base + equity + bonus + perks, reviewed by the recruiter, and "the final offer is determined by your proficiencies within this level", implying a defined levelling ladder.

Size / stage / funding: Publicly traded on the NYSE under QBTS (the legal entity behind the Canadian operation is D-Wave Systems Inc.). Headcount and profitability are not stated on the pages read (unverified). As a public quantum-hardware company it is R&D-heavy and capital-market dependent rather than a "boring, profitable" business — treat stability as unverified.

Locations / HQ: Canadian roots and presence in Burnaby, British Columbia (surfaced in their own site footer); a US office in New Haven, Connecticut, which is where the current relevant opening sits. The DevOps role reports to a "DevOps Engineering Manager" in New Haven.

Remote policy: Role-dependent, not company-wide remote. The live DevOps Engineer II posting says: "This role is remote-friendly, with a preference for candidates located within a few hours of our New Haven office to support occasional on-site collaboration", and the posting's own footer classifies it as "Hybrid (New Haven, CT, US)". No company-wide remote or async policy statement was found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No for the current opening** — it is a US hybrid role anchored to New Haven, Connecticut, with a stated preference for candidates within a few hours' travel of that office. Kimberley, BC is not compatible. Separately, D-Wave does have a Burnaby, BC presence, so Canadian *employment* clearly exists at the company; whether they hire remote (non-Burnaby) staff in Canada is unverified — no such statement was found, and Burnaby-based roles would be Pacific-time office roles, not remote-BC roles.

Engineering & tech: The DevOps team operates hybrid infrastructure spanning on-prem environments (including hardware-integrated quantum systems), Kubernetes clusters (both on-prem and cloud), AWS, and CI/CD. Named stack: Zabbix, OpenSearch, CloudWatch and Grafana for observability; GitHub Actions for CI/CD; Terraform and Ansible for infrastructure-as-code; Docker for containers; PostgreSQL, MySQL/InnoDB and Amazon Aurora for stateful services; Python and Bash for automation. The remit is explicitly reliability/observability for the QCaaS (Leap) platform — logging, metrics, dashboards, alerting, alert-noise reduction, incident support, deployment improvement. The JD asks for "comfort working in a fast-moving environment with evolving systems and priorities." No public engineering blog or handbook was located (unverified), though Ocean is open source on the developer side.

Notable / other: Named to the Leaders category in IDC MarketScape's 2026 Quantum Computing Vendor Assessment. Recent expansion agreement with AT&T to deploy quantum technology in network operations. Standard EEO statement; explicitly "No 3rd party candidates will be accepted."

Open relevant roles (sample): DevOps Engineer II (New Haven, CT — hybrid); Quantum Software Engineer II; Senior DevOps Engineer.
