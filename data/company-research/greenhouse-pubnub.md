# PubNub — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:pubnub | company_type: product
What they do: PubNub is "a San Francisco-based technology company that powers real-time interactive experiences that drive engagement, retention, and monetization." It sells a real-time messaging/eventing platform (pub-sub, presence, storage/history, search, fanout) as developer infrastructure — "Our platform enables organizations to efficiently build, manage, and optimize real-time applications used by millions of people around the world."
How they describe themselves: Infrastructure-first and operationally serious: "Engineering teams at PubNub build and operate the systems behind our globally distributed platform. Our infrastructure processes trillions of transactions each month across 15+ data centers worldwide." They describe a "Remote-first environment with autonomy and ownership," engineers who "own services from development through production operations, including monitoring, incident response, and capacity planning," and an explicit "Use AI tools thoughtfully to improve engineering workflows."
Size / stage / funding: Founded 2012; private, venture-backed; serves "more than 2,000 companies globally, including Amazon, RingCentral, Gameloft, and DAZN." Headcount and current funding status not stated on the material available.
Locations / HQ: San Francisco, California; 15+ data centers worldwide.
Remote policy: "Remote-first environment with autonomy and ownership." The Core Systems JD says "Bay Area candidates preferred. Remote candidates will also be considered," and the posting location reads "US - Remote (Bay Area preferred)."

Deep emphasis on fault tolerance, concurrency, observability (metrics, dashboards, alerts, runbooks), latency/throughput optimization and capacity planning.
