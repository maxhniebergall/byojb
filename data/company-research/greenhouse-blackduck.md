# Black Duck Software, Inc. — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:blackduck | company_type: product
What they do: Black Duck is an application-security vendor. In its own words it "helps organizations build secure, high-quality software, minimizing risks while maximizing speed and productivity. Black Duck, a recognized pioneer in application security, provides SAST, SCA, and DAST solutions that enable teams to quickly find and fix vulnerabilities and defects in proprietary code, open source components, and application behavior. With a combination of industry-leading tools, services, and expertise, only Black Duck helps organizations maximize security and quality in DevSecOps and throughout the software development life cycle." Its site claims service to "over 4,000 organizations worldwide" and describes it as an "eight-time Leader in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for AST". Current positioning is "True Scale Application Security" aimed at AI-generated code and regulatory compliance.
How they describe themselves: Four stated values on their company page — "**WE ARE BOLD**: Challenging conventions and pushing boundaries"; "**WE PUT CUSTOMERS FIRST**: Listening deeply and delivering customer value"; "**WE SCALE UP**: Thinking big and moving fast"; "**WE LEAD WITH INTEGRITY**: Acting with honesty and transparency". JD tone is engineering-serious rather than hype-driven — it asks for someone "passionate about software security and quality" who "enjoys solving complex engineering problems at scale", and leans on academic credentials (MS or PhD in CS, Programming Languages, Static Analysis or Compilers, with equivalent industry experience considered).
Size / stage / funding: Established enterprise vendor, not a startup — thousands of customers and a long product history. This is the former Synopsys Software Integrity Group, carved out and rebranded as Black Duck; **the ownership/divestiture detail is from background knowledge and was NOT verified by the pages fetched** (the company page footer still carries Synopsys references, which is ambiguous). Treat exact headcount, ownership and profitability as unverified.
Locations / HQ: HQ at 800 District Ave, Ste 201, Burlington, MA 01803, USA. A significant **Calgary, Alberta** engineering presence — two of the four live relevant postings are Calgary. Multiple other offices exist but are not enumerated on the fetched page.
Remote policy: **Not stated** on the company page fetched, and not stated in any of the JD bodies. All four live relevant postings carry a concrete city ("Burlington, MA" or "Calgary, AB") rather than a remote designation.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, leaning no for BC specifically.** The company clearly *employs* in Canada — the Calgary roles post CAD salary bands, so Canadian payroll exists. But both Canadian postings are pinned to Calgary with no remote qualifier, and no remote policy was found on any first-party page. Calgary is at least Mountain Time, so timezone would be ideal if a remote arrangement were possible; that is speculation until confirmed. The US postings (Burlington MA) are not relevant.
Engineering & tech: Genuinely deep systems work. The Calgary "Software Engineer 3 (C++/Rust)" role sits on the **Static Analysis team**: enhancing the static-analysis engine, expanding vulnerability coverage, improving scalability and performance in cloud-native environments, developing new analysis algorithms, and writing detection rules in Rust. Required background is programming-language theory, compiler design, parsers, ASTs, language-analysis frameworks, and dataflow / control-flow / taint analysis or symbolic execution — 5+ years in C/C++ and/or Rust on Linux/UNIX. There is a real AI angle, but of the substantive kind: integrating static analysis into AI-assisted development workflows for automated security insight, triage and remediation, and applying analysis to AI-generated code. The DevOps/SRE roles run Kubernetes (GKE) clusters across multiple regions on GCP, own deployment pipelines, monitoring, alerting and incident response, GCP-native observability, autoscaling, and "end-to-end operational responsibility for all customer-facing SaaS" plus hosted offerings.
