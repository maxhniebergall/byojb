# 3M — research
Provider/key: workday:3m/search | company_type: product

What they do: 3M is a very large, publicly traded (NYSE: MMM) diversified industrial materials and manufacturing conglomerate — adhesives, abrasives, films, personal safety equipment, electronics materials, industrial and consumer products. Software/data engineering here is **internal enterprise IT and corporate R&D**, not a software product line: the live roles build data platforms "that power advanced analytics and machine learning applications across the organization."

How they describe themselves: The careers boilerplate embedded in every JD is the clearest statement of self-image: *"Collaborate with Innovative 3Mers Around the World… With a wide variety of people, global locations, technologies and products, 3M is a place where you can collaborate with other curious, creative 3Mers."* They explicitly court career-changers: *"This position provides an opportunity to transition from other private, public, government or military experience to a 3M career."* Tone is corporate, benefits-forward and stability-forward rather than mission-hype; there is no hustle or rocketship language anywhere in the JDs. They state they *"regularly benchmark with other companies that are comparable in size and scope"* to keep pay competitive.

Size / stage / funding: Public, ~$30B+ revenue scale, tens of thousands of employees globally, well over a century old. Maximum stability by the profile's definition; the counterweight is that 3M has been through significant corporate restructuring in recent years (Solventum healthcare spin-off, large legal settlements) — this was not verified from their own pages in this pass and should be treated as unverified background.

Locations / HQ: HQ in St. Paul / Maplewood, Minnesota. Global sites; the registry's live relevant roles are anchored to Minnesota (US) and to Hwaseong, Gyeonggi-do, South Korea.

Remote policy: Role-by-role, not company-wide. The Lead Data Engineer is posted as "Remote - Minnesota" and the body states plainly: **"Work location: Remote in the US"**, "Travel: May include up to 5% primarily domestic travel", "Relocation: Not authorized."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified NO for the live relevant roles.** The Lead Data Engineer JD states "Work location: Remote in the US" and "Must be legally authorized to work in country of employment without sponsorship." The other relevant role is in South Korea. 3M does operate 3M Canada (London, Ontario) and posts Canadian roles generally, but none of the currently scanned relevant engineering roles are Canada-eligible, and no work-from-anywhere-in-Canada policy is stated.

Engineering & tech: Azure-centric data platform work — Python, SQL, Spark/Databricks/Synapse, Data Lake, Data Factory, Cosmos DB; Airflow orchestration; Kafka/Event Hub streaming; feature stores and model-training pipelines; data governance, lineage and compliance; and explicitly "vector databases, embeddings, or LLM oriented data pipelines" plus MLOps practice. Lead roles include mentoring juniors and owning technical roadmaps. Requires a completed and verified Bachelor's in CS and 7+ years for the Lead level.

Notable / other: Pay is posted under US transparency rules — $188,251–$230,084 for the Lead Data Engineer, described as base plus variable incentive. Benefits list is extensive (medical/dental/vision, HSA, FSAs, disability). There is a "Max" virtual recruiting assistant on 3M.com/careers. Corporate Research Laboratory is a real internal research org (the Asia data-engineer role sits in it), which is an unusually deep-tech corner of a manufacturing company.

Open relevant roles (sample): Lead Data Engineer (Remote - Minnesota); Senior Data Engineer, Corporate Research Laboratory-Asia (Hwaseong, KR); Backend Software Engineer; Architect - Agentic AI; Sr. Data Engineer - HANA/Snowflake/SAP BW/ABAP.
