# Novara — research
Provider/key: lever:novara | company_type: product

⚠️ **Entity confirmation**: "Novara" is a common name (an Italian city, plus several unrelated firms and a UK media outlet, Novara Media). The registry entity is the **Colorado EHS software company at novara.com**, confirmed by matching the JD's named products (Flex, Risk Management Center) to that site's product line. No mismatch with the registry.

What they do: Novara sells **environmental, health and safety (EHS) and operational risk-management software, training and services** to industrial employers. Their framing: software that "empowers organizations to identify and resolve issues before they become incidents," addressing operational risk proactively "by unifying data, increasing workforce engagement, and proactively managing risk." Product lines:
- **Flex** — the flagship, a mobile-first SaaS EHS platform used by workers on manufacturing floors and in field environments; audits and inspections, incident management, flexible training, SDS management, asset/equipment inspection, contractor management, reporting and insights.
- **Flex Sustainability** — sustainability goal tracking, carbon accounting, supplier requests.
- **Risk Management Center** — a version aimed at insurance brokers to manage their insured base.
- **Training** — 1,000+ safety and compliance courses via an integrated LMS.
Customers span construction, manufacturing, energy, utilities and mining; named references include Quality Electric, Alleguard, Expand Energy, Rappahannock Electric Cooperative and Burleson Sand Mine. They claim "thousands of customers, 8 million employees trained."

How they describe themselves: Positioned as built "by safety experts," focused on helping clients "identify, remedy, and prevent workplace safety and compliance problems," with the combination of "training, software, and tools" putting "people and safety first while protecting critical operations." No published values list or engineering handbook was found. The JD's own tone is the better signal and it is intense: it seeks someone "highly motivated, hands-on, production-proven" to "drive the technical realization of our Agentic AI Platform" and to **lead** its design, development and scaling.

Size / stage / funding: **A brand-new standalone company as of January 2026.** Novara spun out of the established EHS company **KPA** after six years as a division within it. Private. No headcount or funding figures disclosed publicly. Actively acquisitive: on **9 June 2026** it acquired **Ensogo**, a Toronto-founded AI firm, as its entry point into the Canadian manufacturing, construction, energy and mining market.

Locations / HQ: HQ **Westminster, Colorado** (11080 Circle Point Road, Suite 200) — Mountain Time. Canadian presence via the Ensogo acquisition in **Toronto**.

Engineering & tech: The role is explicitly about **transforming a mature, traditional Enterprise EHS/ESG SaaS system (Flex) into an "AI-native system of intelligence and action."** Concretely, the JD names: complex **state-machine routing code in Python**; auto-scaling **serverless pipelines on AWS**; secure **vector search engines**; and dynamic widget-rendering APIs for the frontend. It asks for someone who has "built and shipped enterprise-grade AI products to production, managing the real-world challenges of **multi-tenancy, PII redaction, token costs, latency, and model hallucination**." That is a substantive applied-LLM-platform brief rather than prompt-tuning. Described as "developer-first."

Notable / other: The combination of a January 2026 spin-out and a June 2026 acquisition means the org is mid-reorganisation; team structure, engineering leadership and process are likely still forming.
