# Momentum Financial Services Group — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:momentumfinancialservicesgroup | company_type: product
What they do: Momentum Financial Services Group (MFSG) is the parent of **Money Mart**, described in its own words as "Canada's largest non-bank branch network". It provides short-term/alternative lending, money transfers, prepaid cards and related financial products to consumers who are underserved by traditional banks, and runs the technology and operations behind that branch and digital network. In-house engineering supports "digital banking and financial services platforms".
How they describe themselves: From their own JD: "At Momentum Financial Services Group, we help people move forward by reimagining how money works for those who need it most. With more than 40 years of experience, we're the team behind Money Mart... From short-term loans to money transfers and prepaid cards, we power the products, technology, and operations that connect millions of customers each year to the money they need, when they need it. At MFSG, we work together across teams and functions to create something bigger than ourselves: solutions that remove barriers and give people access to money they might not get anywhere else." Stated employment offer: a "Compensation Philosophy – Competitive pay aligned with experience and market standards", discretionary annual bonus, fully-paid health/dental premiums plus EAP, retirement plans, tuition reimbursement and professional development, Perkopolis discounts and recognition programs. No separate published values manifesto was reachable.
Size / stage / funding: Private; 40+ years of operating history; described as the largest non-bank branch network in Canada, serving "millions of customers each year". Exact headcount and ownership not verified from a first-party source.
Locations / HQ: Toronto, Canada — all live engineering postings are Toronto. They reference "our corporate HQ spaces designed for teamwork and creativity".
Remote policy: **Hybrid, stated in their own JD**: "Hybrid Work Environment – Flexibility to balance remote and in-office collaboration; enjoy our corporate HQ spaces designed for teamwork and creativity."
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No (verified from the company's own JDs).** Every live posting is located "Toronto, Canada" and the benefits block explicitly describes a hybrid remote/in-office split anchored on the Toronto HQ. Not workable from Kimberley BC.
Engineering & tech: The SRE role is a genuinely substantive reliability/platform brief — SLOs, error budgets, blast-radius assessment before production changes, end-to-end service lifecycle ownership, CI/CD pipeline ownership across staging/UAT/production, Git Flow enforcement, zero-downtime deployments, rollback/failover/DR runbooks, observability, and audit-ready change documentation. Heavy regulatory/compliance overlay (financial regulations, data protection, internal and external audits), and coordination with engineering, DevOps, QA, cybersecurity and compliance including formal go/no-go decisions. Other openings span cloud engineering, Power Platform, and principal-level application development.
Notable / other: This is a "boring, mission-critical domain" in the infrastructure sense — regulated financial plumbing with predictable internal customers — which is normally a positive signal. The counterweight is the business itself: high-cost short-term consumer lending (the Money Mart / payday-adjacent segment) is an ethically contested domain and carries regulatory risk. Pay is posted on some roles (a Principal Software Developer band is on record at CA$160–175K base), which is unusually transparent for a Canadian employer.
Open relevant roles (sample): Site Reliability Engineer; Senior Cloud Engineer; Cloud Engineer; Principal Software Developer; Lead Software Engineer; Senior Software Engineer; Power Platform Engineer — all Toronto.
