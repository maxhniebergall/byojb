# ClassDojo — research
Provider/key: ashby:classdojo | company_type: product

What they do: ClassDojo operates a K-12 communication network connecting teachers, children and families, and is now extending into learning products. In its own words: "ClassDojo's flagship app is the #1 communication app connecting K-12 teachers, children, and families globally. Teachers use it to share what's happening throughout the day through photos, videos, and messages that make parents feel like they're there." Reach claimed on its JDs: "actively used in over 95% of US schools, reaching over 45 million children in 180 countries, with a team of just around 200 people." Its own about page describes "a global community platform connecting over 50 million teachers and families." Strategy: "We are now beginning to use this network to give kids the best learning experiences in the world, far beyond those a standard school can provide" — i.e. expanding "beyond communication into building the best possible learning experiences for kids everywhere."

How they describe themselves: Mission stated as "**every kid on earth should get an education they love**" / "to give every child on Earth an education they love." Stated principles from their about page: equal learning opportunities regardless of zip code or income; connection between school communities; learning through play; safety and trust as the foundation of a kids' online community.
On hiring and culture, from the JDs: "We hire for talent density. Our team comprises the most talented, entrepreneurial, and innovative teammates from around the world, with experience in education and large scale consumer internet companies, including Instagram, Netflix, Dropbox, Stripe, Uber, Y Combinator, and more. We're building a company where the most talented people want to work." Also: "We're a small, high-talent-density team with experience from leading technology companies. We value **ownership, speed, and thoughtful decision-making**, and we aim to create an environment where people can do the best work of their careers."
A named cultural norm appears in the leadership JD: "**Embrace Candor > Harmony**: give the hard message early, comfortable with disagreement." Engineering practices are named explicitly: "flow-based delivery, vertical slicing, WIP discipline, Definition of Done, observability," and "AI-native development."

Size / stage / funding: Private, roughly 200 people, and **profitable** — its own JD links a "TechCrunch Article: Second Act comes with First Profits." Small headcount against very large reach (45–50M+ users), which implies a high-leverage platform organization. Funding stage and investors are not stated in the sources read.

Locations / HQ: A distributed company with no office requirement stated. San Francisco is referenced only via the SF Fair Chance Ordinance boilerplate, implying a California nexus. Compensation tiers name CA, WA, NY, NJ and CT as high-cost US states.

Remote policy: Remote-first and distributed, stated verbatim: "We are a distributed company, so we hire regardless of location, as long as you are willing to have significant hours overlap with one of the Americas time zones." Postings are tagged `#LI-Remote` and listed "Remote." Written communication is explicitly weighted — "Are an excellent written communicator; can articulate strategy and trade-offs clearly in writing."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Likely yes, but not stated for Canada by name.** The company's own words are "we hire regardless of location, as long as you are willing to have significant hours overlap with one of the Americas time zones." Mountain Time is an Americas time zone, so Kimberley BC satisfies the only stated condition, and there is no country restriction or work-authorization language on either posting. Caveat: the compensation section is explicitly headed "Salary ranges (**United States**)" with US state tiers and no Canadian band published, so how a Canadian hire is paid and contracted (employee vs. contractor vs. EOR) is unverified. Worth one recruiter question, but this is the strongest Canada-compatible language in the batch after Trellis.

Engineering & tech: The registry's relevant roles are platform and developer-experience — directly the internal-plumbing archetype.
- **Senior Software Engineer, Developer Experience**: a newly formed DevEx team. "Every minute an engineer spends waiting on builds, troubleshooting local environments, repeating manual workflows, or navigating unnecessary complexity is a minute they aren't building… Developer Experience Engineering exists to remove that friction." Scope: internal platforms and tooling across "developing, testing, reviewing, deploying, and operating software"; "trusted paved paths"; reusable tools, automation, documentation and self-service capabilities; AI-native developer workflows; measurement via "engineering metrics, developer feedback, and adoption data." Nice-to-haves name CI/CD, release engineering, developer environments, software delivery systems, and DORA/SPACE productivity frameworks. The stated stance is "treat engineers as customers."
- **Engineering Leader, Platform Engineering**: manager-of-managers owning "infrastructure, data, developer experience, and AI tooling" with 2–4 EMs reporting in; owns build-vs-buy and architecture trade-offs, SLAs/SLOs and incident response, paved roads and golden paths ("earn adoption rather than mandating it"), and deliberate tech-debt/reliability trade-offs. Requires 2+ years managing managers and 10+ years total.
LLM tooling is a stated expectation across both: "Use LLM-based tools daily and push your teams to adopt them."
