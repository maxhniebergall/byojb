What they do: Red Cell Partners describes itself as "an incubation firm building and investing in rapidly scalable technology-led companies that are bringing revolutionary advancements to market in three distinct practice areas: healthcare, cyber, and national security. United by a shared sense of duty and deep belief in the power of innovation, Red Cell is developing powerful tools and solutions to address our Nation's most pressing problems." Engineering & tech: The two live engineering roles are both for **Trase Systems**, a Red Cell portfolio company (the Red Cell JD carries an "About Trase" section verbatim). Content: Trase OS, an "agentic operating system" — core execution model (state machine, lifecycle, resource model, failure semantics), platform APIs/SDKs with versioning and compatibility, correctness via idempotency, deterministic replay, compensating actions and data integrity; concurrency controls, rate limits, backpressure, sharding/partitioning, workload isolation; RBAC/ABAC, policy enforcement, fine-grained audit and lineage; distributed tracing, structured logs, metrics, evaluation hooks and an "explainable trail" of agent actions; test strategy spanning unit, property and chaos testing, plus SLOs, incident response and postmortems.
