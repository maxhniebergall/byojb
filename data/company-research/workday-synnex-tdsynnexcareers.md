# TD SYNNEX — research
Provider/key: workday:synnex/tdsynnexcareers | company_type: product
What they do: TD SYNNEX is a very large IT distributor and solutions aggregator — it sits between hardware/software vendors and the channel of resellers and MSPs. In its own words: "We're TD SYNNEX, a leading IT distributor and solutions aggregator for the IT ecosystem. We're 23,000 of the IT industry's best and brightest... We're an innovative partner that helps our customers maximize the value of IT investments, demonstrate business outcomes, and unlock growth opportunities." The engineering roles in the registry are internal IT/platform functions supporting that distribution business, not a software product sold to customers.
How they describe themselves: Values are stated on their JDs as "Together, We Own It, We Dare to Go, We Grow and Win, and above all, We Do the Right Thing." Their JDs are unusually blunt about scope, which is a positive signal: the Senior Platform Engineer (Cloud) posting has a "What This Role Is Not" section — "This is not a Cloud Architect, Solution Architect, or Enterprise Architect role... You will not set the overall cloud strategy... If you are looking forward to spending your day building and automating, please read on." The Senior Infrastructure Platform Engineer JD similarly says "Not a ticket-based or operations role... Not a senior-level architecture or platform ownership role." Benefits language is corporate-global (leadership programs, "Life Empowerment Assistance Program", DEI, onboarding network).
Size / stage / funding: ~23,000 employees by their own count (some sources put total headcount higher). Publicly traded (NYSE: SNX), Fortune-500-scale revenue, profitable and long-established — the opposite of runway-dependent. Operates the legacy Tech Data brand ("At Tech Data, a TD SYNNEX Company...").
Locations / HQ: Global. Dual HQ Fremont, CA and Clearwater, FL. The engineering reqs in the registry sit in Barcelona (Spain), Chennai (India), San Jose (Costa Rica), Bogota (Colombia), Prague (Czechia), Warsaw (Poland), and one in Mississauga, Ontario, Canada.
Remote policy: Not stated on any JD captured. Roles are posted against specific city offices, and several carry shift requirements ("Senior Platform SRE - US Shift"), which implies office- or region-anchored work rather than remote-first.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — effectively disqualifying. Of 12 live relevant reqs, exactly one is in Canada: "IT Business System Engineer – DevOps for EDI and Microservices" posted to Mississauga, Ontario, and posted bilingually (French/English), i.e. an Ontario-anchored role. Every other platform/SRE req is anchored to Chennai, Barcelona, Costa Rica, Bogota, Prague or Warsaw. Nothing found supports hiring a remote engineer based in British Columbia, and the "US Shift" variants point the other way on hours.
Engineering & tech: Multi-cloud platform work, primarily Azure with AWS where applicable; Terraform module authoring ("You write modules, not just consume them"), Ansible playbooks/roles/collections, Python/PowerShell/Bash, Azure DevOps pipelines, Linux (RHEL/Debian/Ubuntu) and Windows, VMware/Nutanix, Kubernetes/containers. Also operating "AI-ready services" on the consumer side — provisioning Azure AI Foundry and AWS Bedrock for application teams, explicitly "you are not expected to design AI/ML systems". Additional SAP BW/BDC data-engineering and credit-data-engineering roles in Europe.
Notable / other: The technical substance of the platform roles is a good match on paper — hands-on IaC, day-2 ownership, explicitly de-scoped away from architecture theatre. The blocker is purely geographic. Entity check: the Workday tenant `synnex.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/tdsynnexcareers` and the JD self-description both confirm TD SYNNEX; no collision.
