# DoubleZero — research
Provider/key: ashby:doublezero | company_type: product

What they do: DoubleZero is "a protocol for building and operating high-performance, permissionless
networks" — in practice a **global dedicated-fiber network for distributed systems, primarily
blockchains**, delivering low-latency connectivity and real-time data infrastructure. Its own
description: "DoubleZero is a high-performance, permissionless global network purpose-built for
distributed systems. The mission is simple: increase bandwidth and reduce latency. DoubleZero
increases bandwidth by filtering junk data that currently plagues validators and other network
operators. DoubleZero reduces latency by connecting these filters via underutilized private fiber
links, forming a mesh network. By combining these improvements and routing the right data through
optimized, low-latency paths, the DoubleZero protocol empowers blockchains and other systems to
achieve performance levels previously impossible. In short, DoubleZero is a new Internet for
distributed systems." It is a DePIN (decentralized physical infrastructure network) with its own
token (2Z), and it borrows the private-fiber playbook of HFT trading firms.

How they describe themselves: "It is supported by many independent contributors. Among them,
**DoubleZero Foundation** drives the adoption, decentralization, security, and advancement of the
protocol. **Malbec Labs** develops the technical components of the protocol itself." So the entity
you would actually be employed by is likely Malbec Labs rather than "DoubleZero" as such — worth
clarifying before applying. The public site states no mission page, no values page and no team page;
messaging is purely performance-and-permissionlessness. The engineering voice in the JD is blunt and
opinionated: "We're looking for an SRE who thinks like a systems architect and builds reliability
through automation. You should have lived inside large production environments and be **allergic to
manual processes**."

Size / stage / funding: early. Raised a **$28M seed at a ~$400M valuation** (announced March 2025),
co-led by Multicoin Capital and Dragonfly Capital, later reported around $28M for network expansion.
Founded by **Austin Federa** (former Head of Strategy at the Solana Foundation) with **Mateo Ward**
and **Andrew McConnell** of Malbec Labs; Jump Crypto is a core contributor. Team is small and
combines Solana-ecosystem people with hardware/edge/distributed-systems engineers. Revenue model is
evolving (a "DoubleZero Edge" revenue model has been discussed publicly); economics are substantially
token-based.

Locations / HQ: no stated HQ. Testnet presence in seven cities — Singapore, Tokyo, Los Angeles, New
York, London, Amsterdam and Frankfurt — which reflects network points of presence, not offices.

Remote policy: the SRE requisition is tagged simply "**Remote**" with no country qualifier and no
further detail; the public site has a careers page but publishes no remote-work policy, benefits, or
locations.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear — not verified.** The posting says only "Remote" with no
country list, no timezone requirement and no mention of Canada or of employment entity. Crypto
protocol organisations of this shape commonly engage people as contractors across borders rather
than through local payroll, which can work for a Canadian but has real tax/benefit implications.
Because network operations span Asia, Europe and North America, an SRE on-call footprint could also
mean awkward hours from Mountain Time. Nothing here is disqualifying, but nothing is confirmed
either — eligibility, entity and pay currency would all need a recruiter conversation.

Engineering & tech: **Go** is the stated core language. The SRE / Production Engineer role is
automation-first infrastructure work: "Design and build automation-first reliability systems in Go";
"Treat infrastructure as pipelines: provisioning, observability, deployment, failover"; "Build
internal tooling, control planes, and reliability infrastructure"; "Own production readiness,
alerting, and operational maturity"; "Work across protocol, networking, and hardware boundaries."
They want a "Big tech SRE / Production Engineering background or similar", strong production
Golang, a systems thinker who designs for scale, reliability and operational simplicity, experience
**building internal platforms rather than just operating existing ones**, and comfort with
distributed systems, networking and low-level system behaviour. Technical documentation lives at
docs.malbeclabs.com.

Compensation: **nothing published.** No range on the JD, no pay page. Token-denominated
compensation is a realistic possibility given the structure.

Notable / other: the underlying engineering problem — physical fiber, mesh routing, latency
optimisation, filtering at the network edge — is genuinely deep infrastructure work and unusually
hardware-adjacent for a software role. The context, however, is squarely crypto: the customer base
is Solana validators, the value accrual is a token, and the organisational shape (foundation plus
labs plus "independent contributors") is a crypto-native structure rather than a conventional
employer.

Open relevant roles (sample): Golang SRE / Production Engineer (Remote).
