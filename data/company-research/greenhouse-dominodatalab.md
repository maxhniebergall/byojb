# Domino Data Lab — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:dominodatalab | company_type: product
What they do: Domino builds an enterprise AI / data-science platform for large, regulated organisations — a streamlined model development environment, MLOps capabilities, and features for collaboration, reuse, reproducibility and governance. In their words the platform makes "data science teams more productive, reduce time to value, and ensure compliance". Named customers include Johnson & Johnson, GSK, Bristol Myers, UBS, FINRA and the US Navy, used for "developing new medicines, securing our financial markets, or protecting our country". Verticals: life sciences, finance, manufacturing, government.
How they describe themselves: Their stated values, quoted from the JD: "We strongly believe in the value of growing a diverse team…"; "We value a growth mindset. High-performing creative individuals who dig into problems and see the opportunities for success"; "We believe in individuals who seek truth and speak the truth and can be their whole selves at work"; "We value all of you that believe improving is always possible. At Domino, everything is a work in progress – we can do better at everything"; "We emphasize an environment of teaching and learning to equip employees with the tools needed to be successful in their function." Self-description of stage: "we have been in business for a decade but are still a small team operating with the spirit of a startup… the future is still being invented — and we want to be the ones building it."
Size / stage / funding: Founded 2013. Private, late-stage, heavily backed — Sequoia Capital, Coatue Management, NVIDIA, Snowflake, NetApp and other investors. Headcount not disclosed on their about page; they self-describe as "a small team".
Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco. No other offices listed on the about page. Postings are remote (US and Argentina), implying a distributed workforce.
Remote policy: Remote-friendly — SRE and engineering roles are tagged "#LI-Remote" and posted as "Remote US" or "Remote Argentina".
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO evidence. The four live engineering postings are "Remote US" (three) and "Remote Argentina" (one). Pay is published as "The annual US base salary range". Canada is never mentioned. The existence of a LatAm remote hub shows they can employ outside the US, but nothing first-party supports Canada — treat as unverified/unlikely as posted.
Engineering & tech: Kubernetes, Linux, cloud platforms, observability tooling; Python and Go for internal tools and services; single-tenant SaaS deployments with customer-managed upgrades. The SRE practice is being deliberately rebuilt: "the people who respond to incidents also build the systems that make future incidents shorter, rarer, and less painful", with AI-assisted reliability tooling that "analyzes tickets, logs, traces, and documentation" to speed diagnosis. Scope includes SLO/SLI frameworks for priority services, end-to-end incident ownership, customer-facing observability features, and mentoring. They explicitly want "sound judgment about AI/LLM tooling: you know where it genuinely helps in operational workflows and where it adds noise".
Notable / other: Compliance and governance are a core product concern (regulated pharma, financial and defence customers), which tends to mean deliberate, documented engineering. Compensation for Staff SRE is posted at $200,000-$230,000 USD base, plus equity, company bonus, 401(k), medical/dental/vision and wellness stipends.
