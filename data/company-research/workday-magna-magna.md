# Magna International — research
Provider/key: workday:magna/magna | company_type: product

What they do: Magna International is "one of the world's largest automotive suppliers" — a tier-one contract manufacturer and systems supplier to global automakers. Business lines span complete-vehicle engineering and assembly, body & chassis, electrical/electronics including ADAS, exteriors and interiors, powertrain (BEV/hybrid/ICE), seating, lighting, mirrors and glass. Two divisions appear in the current postings: Magna New Mobility (urban mobility, industrial intralogistics, commercial and autonomous vehicles, "advanced software solutions that integrate with next-generation hardware") and Magna Electronics (automotive electronic systems, computer-vision R&D).

How they describe themselves: Stated corporate values are "Think Big, Take Responsibility, Never Settle, Be Collaborative." Careers language: "At Magna, you can expect an engaging and dynamic environment where you can help to develop industry-leading automotive technologies. We invest in our employees… As a member of our global team, you can expect exciting, varied responsibilities as well as a wide range of development prospects. Because we believe that your career path should be as unique as you are." Magna Electronics adds: "Our name stands for quality, environmental consciousness, and safety. Innovation is what drives us and we drive innovation."

Size / stage / funding: Public company, listed on NYSE (MGA) and TSX (MG). 155,000+ employees, operations in 28 countries, 321 manufacturing and assembly facilities. Long-established, mature, cyclical automotive industrial — not runway-dependent.

Locations / HQ: Canadian-headquartered (Aurora, Ontario — company is TSX-listed and its Canadian engineering roles cluster in Newmarket, Ontario; the specific HQ address was not confirmed on the page reached). Relevant postings: Newmarket, Ontario CA; Troy, Michigan US; Timisoara, Romania.

Remote policy: No corporate remote policy is published on the pages reached. The Romanian Machine Learning role offers "Flexible working hours and hybrid work"; the North American postings are tied to named plant/office locations, and the Industrial IoT role requires "Travel requirement Domestic and International – 25%".

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Effectively no.** Every relevant posting is anchored to a physical site (Newmarket ON, Troy MI, Timisoara RO); nothing is advertised as remote, and the one flexibility statement found is hybrid-in-Romania. Magna is a Canadian employer, so employment in Canada is possible in principle, but only from Ontario on-site — not from British Columbia.

Engineering & tech: Automotive/embedded flavoured. Computer vision and ML for object detection, classification, segmentation and tracking; PyTorch/TensorFlow/Keras, Python (numpy, OpenCV, scikit-learn), C/C++, embedded and ARM development, ROS2, MLflow/MLOps, AWS, Databricks. Industrial IoT roles are Industry 4.0 / IT-OT infrastructure oriented and are largely customer-support engineering rather than product development.

The Romanian ML role quotes "annual gross salary starting from 140,000 RON" plus meal tickets (35 RON/day).
