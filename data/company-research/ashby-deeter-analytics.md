# Deeter Analytics — research
Provider/key: ashby:deeter-analytics | company_type: product

What they do: Deeter Analytics is building a large-scale AI trading system. In their own words: "Our goal is to create a fundamental trading model as capable as today's most advanced AI systems — but applied to global markets. Not incremental signals or isolated strategies, but a system that can continuously interpret, learn from, and act on the evolving state of the world." Their data thesis is social data: "We train on large-scale, real-time social data — capturing how narratives form, how sentiment propagates, and how collective behavior drives markets." Functionally this is a quantitative trading firm with an in-house frontier-style model training operation.
