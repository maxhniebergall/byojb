# Cartography Biosciences — research
Provider/key: rippling:cartography-biosciences | company_type: product
What they do: Cartography Biosciences is a therapeutics company building, in its own words, "the first atlas to identify targets that are specific enough to only engage cancerous cells, broad enough to work across cancer cells and patients, and safe enough to sidestep toxic side effects." It bridges immunology and computation to find safe, selective targets for immunotherapeutics. Founded by Kevin Parker, Howard Chang and Ansu Satpathy.
How they describe themselves: Mission-led and science-first — "bridging immunology and computation to understand the critical differences between normal and cancerous cells". The JD is notably free of hustle language: no "fast-paced", no "wear many hats"; instead it stresses reproducibility, cost efficiency, security, observability, "clear documentation and post-mortems that improve the system over time".
