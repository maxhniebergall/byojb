# OP Labs — research
Provider/key: ashby:oplabs | company_type: product
What they do: OP Labs is "the team behind Optimism's technology" — it builds the OP Stack, the open-source software behind Optimism, an Ethereum Layer-2 blockchain network described as powering "the next generation of financial infrastructure". Companies use the OP Stack to launch their own chains; named users include Sony, Coinbase, Kraken, Uniswap, OKX and Bitpanda. The company claims "over 65% of Layer 2 activity on Ethereum runs through the OP stack" (its site says "over 50%" — the two figures come from different pages and are not reconciled). It is also building OP Enterprise, letting major institutions launch and operate OP Stack chains, and it ships security and performance upgrades to those chains.
How they describe themselves (their own words): Mission: "Scale Ethereum, Build Optimism". "We are a small team building at the speed of the ecosystem we support." "We have our work cut out for us and are looking for exceptional people to join us." They seek "mission-driven contributors who thrive on tackling complex computer science challenges while delivering high quality code with high-velocity". Engineering expectation: "Use AI-assisted engineering tools effectively while maintaining rigorous standards for correctness, security, review, and technical ownership."
Size / stage / funding: Small team, venture-funded — "We've raised $178M from a16z, Paradigm, and others." Headcount not disclosed. Revenue model is tied to the Optimism/L2 ecosystem and the newer OP Enterprise line; crypto-market dependent.
Locations / HQ: New York City is the stated centre of gravity. No other offices named.
Remote policy: Stated plainly in their own words: "We're remote-friendly but the NYC office is our center of gravity." That is remote-tolerated rather than remote-first, with an explicit in-office nucleus.
Remote-Canada eligibility: LIKELY YES on paper — the live Senior Software Engineer, Protocol (Rust) req is posted "Remote (Global)", which does not exclude Canada. But this is remote-friendly-with-an-NYC-centre rather than a distributed-by-design org, and no Canadian entity, employer-of-record arrangement or timezone expectation is documented; contractor engagement is common in this sector. Treat Canadian employment terms as unverified.
Engineering & tech: Rust is the focus of the open role — "kona-node and related Rust-based consensus-client infrastructure as a fully compatible and production-ready alternative to op-node". Also: deep Ethereum internals (EVM, JSON-RPC, clients), distributed systems, Go (reading production Go while migrating behaviour into Rust), Solidity for system-level smart contracts, and familiarity with op-node, kona or Reth. Practices called out: owning features "from architecture, implementation, and docs to deployment and monitoring", "rigorous conformance and adversarial tests to ensure implementation compatibility", and executing "safe protocol upgrades" plus resolving "complex availability/performance issues". Near-term work also covers native account abstraction across the stack and premium protocol capabilities for OP Enterprise. The Protocol Team "designs and operates the core specifications and clients defining the stack" — everything is open source.
