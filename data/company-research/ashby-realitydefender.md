# Reality Defender — research
Provider/key: ashby:realitydefender | company_type: product

What they do: Reality Defender is a cybersecurity company that detects deepfakes and AI-generated
media. In their words: "an award-winning cybersecurity company helping enterprises and governments
detect deepfakes and AI-generated media. Utilizing a patented multi-model approach, Reality Defender
is robust against the bleeding edge of generative platforms producing video, audio, imagery, and text
media." The product is API-first: "Reality Defender's API-first deepfake detection platform empowers
teams and developers alike to identify fraud, disinformation campaigns, and harmful deepfakes in real
time." Customers are named as "leading enterprise clients, financial institutions, and governments".

How they describe themselves: The available first-party text (their live JDs) is product- and
mission-framed — preventing AI-generated media from being "used for malicious purposes", detecting
"fraud, disinformation campaigns, and harmful deepfakes". They cite an award: "Reality Defender Wins
RSA Most Innovative Startup". A full stated values/principles list was NOT obtainable — no corporate
about/values page was reachable or derivable this session, so this heading is incomplete rather than
absent by choice.

Size / stage / funding: Venture-backed startup. Their own words: "Backed by world class investors
including DCVC, Illuminate Financial, Y Combinator, Booz Allen Hamilton, IBM, Accenture, Rackhouse,
and Argon VC." A prior Stage-3 facet extraction on their Senior SWE (Enterprise Integrations) JD also
classified company_stage as "startup". Headcount and funding round size UNVERIFIED. Note the investor
mix is unusual and informative: two large systems integrators (Accenture, Booz Allen) and IBM
alongside DCVC and YC — consistent with an enterprise/government go-to-market.

Locations / HQ: HQ in New York City ("employees working from Reality Defender's HQ in NYC"). Postings
carry "New York City, USA Remote" and "USA Remote".

Remote policy: Remote-capable but US-scoped. Roles are posted as "USA Remote". They run a hybrid NYC
office with in-office perks (daily UberEats lunch, commuter benefits) and, notably, "Remote Fridays"
for HQ staff — i.e. NYC employees are expected in the office most days. Benefits are explicitly
split into "all our employees, regardless of location" vs. NYC-office-only. Async culture: no
evidence either way; one annual "company-sponsored offsite".

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No / not evidenced.** Every live posting is geo-tagged "USA Remote" or
"New York City, USA Remote", and the universal benefits package is US-instrument-specific — 401k,
FSA/HSA, US healthcare premium coverage. There is no Canadian entity, Canadian benefit, or non-US
geo mentioned anywhere in their own text. Stage-3 extraction on the Enterprise Integrations JD
recorded geo_eligibility as "unclear", but the location tags and the benefits list both point to
US-only employment. Treat as US-only until they say otherwise.

Engineering & tech: Genuinely infrastructure-heavy for a detection product. The Data Engineer role
covers "large-scale data processing pipelines handling multi-terabyte and streaming datasets,
including audio/video transcoding, feature extraction, and preprocessing workflows", Kubernetes and
AWS in production, distributed compute with **Spark and Ray**, workflow orchestration with **Airflow**
(or Dagster/Prefect/Luigi) including "dependency management, retries, monitoring, and alerting",
enterprise database administration and tuning, and MLOps support — "training pipelines, model
retraining triggers, feature stores". Languages: Python and SQL, "Golang is a plus". The Senior SWE
(Enterprise Integrations) JD adds Python, REST APIs, event-driven systems, AWS, Kubernetes/Docker,
Kafka/RabbitMQ/NATS, and SIP/VoIP/WebRTC telephony — i.e. real-time audio integration work. Team
structure: data engineers "work closely with ML engineers and researchers".

Notable / other: Benefits are unusually strong for a startup and are stated plainly: 100% premium
coverage on healthcare (partial for dependents), 100% on dental/vision including dependents, 100% on
short/long-term disability and life, FSA/HSA and 401k, equity, **20 days PTO**, 12 weeks parental
leave, learning & development budget, monthly wellness benefit, annual offsite. The Enterprise
Integrations JD (now closed) asked only 3–6 years for a "Senior" title, which suggests a compressed
levelling ladder.

Open relevant roles (sample): Data Engineer ($140K–$180K + equity + bonus, NYC/USA Remote); Applied
AI Engineer II (Audio); Staff AI Engineer (Audio). Recently closed: Senior Software Engineer,
