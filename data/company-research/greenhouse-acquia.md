# Acquia — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:acquia | company_type: product

What they do: Acquia is the commercial company behind **Drupal**, founded in 2007 by Drupal's creator Dries Buytaert. It sells a digital experience platform (DXP): Drupal Cloud hosting, site building, content management, digital asset management, personalization and marketing tooling — "a portfolio of products to help companies maximize the value of their content across multiple channels." Customers are large brands and public-sector organizations; more than 4,500 organizations use Acquia and it works with 700+ global partners.

How they describe themselves: In their own words on the JD, "Acquia is an open-source digital experience company. We provide the world's most ambitious brands with technology that allows them to embrace innovation and create customer moments that matter… we believe in the power of community and collaboration - giving our customers the freedom to build tomorrow on their terms." The about page frames the mission as helping customers "build a better digital world for your customers — one that's frictionless, relevant, accessible, and safe," and describes the workplace around **creativity, individuality, collaboration and continuous learning**. Their JDs lean on accolades: "named as one of North America's fastest growing software companies, as reported by Deloitte and Inc. Magazine," "rated a leader by the analyst community," "one of the Best Places to Work by the Boston Business Journal." They also state openly that they are "an organization that embraces innovation and the potential of AI to enhance our processes and improve our work."

Size / stage / funding: Private; **Vista Equity Partners** acquired a majority stake in 2019 in a roughly $1bn deal. No new funding since a 2015 Series G and no IPO/exit signal — a margin-focused PE hold. Headcount is around 1,100 (reported ~1,097 as of April 2026, up slightly from ~1,077 in December 2025). **This follows multiple consecutive years of layoffs**: third-party reporting and employee reviews describe onshore layoffs across 2024–2025 with widening scope each round, significant loss of Drupal expertise, and a deliberate shift of headcount to offshore hubs in India (Pune, Delhi/NCR) and Costa Rica (Heredia).

Locations / HQ: HQ Boston, MA (53 State Street). Offices in Sydney (Australia), Reading (UK), Tokyo (Japan), Heredia (Costa Rica), Ballerup (Denmark), plus large India engineering presence (Pune, Delhi/NCR).

Remote policy: Not stated on the about page. Reported as remote-friendly across most US divisions, and their live reqs are explicitly location-scoped per role (e.g. "Pune or Delhi/NCR Remote," "Remote-Canada").

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Nominally yes, with a caveat.** The Senior Software Engineer (Golang + Kubernetes) req is tagged **"Remote-Canada"** on their board. However, the body of that same JD asks for a "BTech/BE/BS in computer science or similar" — a credential phrasing used almost exclusively for Indian reqs — and the companion Staff Software Engineer req is scoped "Pune or Delhi/NCR Remote." That combination suggests the Canada tag may be a duplicated/mis-scoped req rather than a genuine Canadian opening; verify with the recruiter before investing. No Canadian office is listed on their about page.

Engineering & tech: Golang and Python, Docker and Kubernetes, AWS/GCP (EC2, EKS/ECS), Terraform, Jenkins/GitHub/Artifactory, Puppet for configuration management, Git branching strategy depth, observability tooling (SignalFX, Grafana), centralized logging (Sumologic), Unix shell. Also the classic Drupal stack — Drupal, MySQL, Apache. The Senior Golang + K8s role sits in "an AI Agentic team" building and maintaining a new CI/CD pipeline used by multiple Acquia products and services, with an explicit expectation to "take over scrum master role." Teams own and operate what they build, and there is an after-hours on-call rotation "as needed for emergency or urgent situations." The role also includes "product support to internal and external stakeholders."

Notable / other: Strong open-source lineage and a genuinely infrastructure-flavoured platform (cloud hosting, CI/CD, containers). Competitive pressure in the DXP market is significant, and its AI/DAM roadmap is being publicly questioned by industry press. The 5+ years DevOps and 3–5 years monitoring requirements make the Senior role a build/release-engineering job more than an application-backend one.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (Golang + Kubernetes) — Remote-Canada; Staff Software Engineer — Pune or Delhi/NCR Remote; Staff AI Engineer.
