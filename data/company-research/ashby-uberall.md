# Uberall — research
Provider/key: ashby:uberall | company_type: product

What they do: Uberall operates a multi-location ("near me") marketing platform for enterprise brands — managing digital presence across search, AI discovery, business listings, reviews and local engagement channels. They report managing **1.3M+ locations across 194 countries**. Customers are large multi-location brands and franchises; the product is a marketing SaaS, though the internal engineering platform is substantial.

How they describe themselves: Four stated values, quoted from their own about page:
- "Be Customer Obsessed" — prioritising client success
- "Grow Together" — collaboration and learning
- "Demonstrate Passion & Performance" — accountability and excellence
- "Be Bold & Drive Change" — courageous innovation
Their engineering JD tone is markedly more sober and craft-oriented than the marketing site: "a high-leverage, builder-and-multiplier role", "You know what makes a foundation pleasant to build on versus painful", "Raise the bar on developer experience, clear APIs, good defaults, useful errors, and documentation people actually read".

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2013 by Florian Hübner and David Federhen; **€143M total funding raised**. Headcount not disclosed on the about page — teams span multiple continents. Private, venture-backed but well past seed stage (Uberall has grown partly by acquisition in the location-marketing space).

Locations / HQ: **Berlin, Germany** (Hussitenstrasse 33). Global operations across 194 countries of coverage, with engineering concentrated in Europe. Live engineering postings list "Europe, Armenia, Hungary, North Macedonia" and "Philippines, Indonesia".

Remote policy: Not stated on their about page. The postings themselves are the only signal and they are region-scoped rather than city-scoped ("Europe, Armenia, Hungary, North Macedonia"), which implies distributed/remote hiring — but strictly within those regions. One role is explicitly a "Contractor role" (Senior QA Engineer — Data Platform, Philippines/Indonesia), indicating they also use contractor arrangements in lower-cost geographies.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — no evidence of any kind.** Both live relevant postings are geo-scoped to Europe/Caucasus or Southeast Asia. Uberall is a Berlin-headquartered company with no North American engineering location found on its own pages, and nothing states or implies a Canadian entity or Canada-eligible hiring. Absent a Canadian entity, the Kimberley BC constraint cannot be met. Additionally, Berlin (CET) to Mountain Time is an 8-hour gap — a hostile-timezone red signal even if employment were possible.

Engineering & tech: Genuinely strong platform engineering. From the AI Platform & Frameworks JD: "our mission is to make AI agents and high-throughput backend systems easy for internal teams to adopt". Two halves —
- **Agentic frameworks:** "the libraries, starters, and orchestration patterns that internal teams use to ship LLM-powered agents (Python/LangChain/LangGraph on the agent side, Kotlin/Spring Boot on the platform side), plus the supporting frameworks (evaluations, guardrails) that prove those agents actually work"; agent orchestration, tool use, memory, "message routing, human-in-the-loop workflows, MCP integration"; leading "the evaluation and guardrail strategy so we can measure agent quality, reliability, and regressions".
- **Core backend platform:** Kotlin services, shared libraries, Spring Boot starters and templates, client libraries.
Nice-to-haves list messaging/queues (SQS, Redis), gRPC, event-driven architectures "including the failure modes and operational realities", AWS and infrastructure-as-code, open-source library maintenance, and evaluation/experiment-tracking/observability tooling ("Langfuse, MLflow, or similar"). They also run a Data Platform with a QA function. Seniority bar: "7+ years of professional software engineering experience, including a track record of building libraries, frameworks, or platform components that other engineers built on."

Notable / other: The AI-platform role is explicitly a standards-setting, multiplier role — "Set the patterns and standards other engineers follow", "Drive adoption and multiply other engineers", "establishing standards, golden paths, and adoption across multiple teams" — with a mandate to "become our go-to expert" in agentic AI. No compensation is posted on either live role (the Ashby comp object is empty).

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer - AI Platform & Frameworks (Europe, Armenia, Hungary, North Macedonia); Senior QA Engineer — Data Platform, Contractor (Philippines, Indonesia).
