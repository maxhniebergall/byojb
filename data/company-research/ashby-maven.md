# Maven — research
Provider/key: ashby:maven | company_type: product

(Entity note: this is **Maven Learning, maven.com** — the cohort-based-course marketplace founded by Gagan Biyani. It is *not* Maven Clinic (women's health), Maven AGI (`ashby:maven-agi`, a separate registry entry), or Apache Maven. Confirmed via the JD's own domain extraction of "edtech" plus creator-economy and marketplace nice-to-haves.)

What they do: Maven is a marketplace for live, cohort-based online courses. Independent experts — practitioners in product, design, marketing, engineering, data science and AI — build, market and teach time-boxed cohorts to paying students; Maven provides the tooling, discovery and payments layer and takes a cut. It is a two-sided creator-economy marketplace: the instructor is the supply side and the professional learner the demand side.

How they describe themselves: The mission is to "democratize education by empowering the world's experts to offer cohort-based courses directly to their audience." Their stated beliefs are that **community is foundational to online learning**, that **active, hands-on learning through projects is the most effective form**, and that **access to expert instructors should be global and universal**. The team is described as "entrepreneurial, strategic doers."

Size / stage / funding: Series A, founded 2020. Roughly 339 people reported as of mid-2026 (a figure that likely includes marketplace-side staff rather than a large engineering org). Raised ~$30M from First Round Capital and Andreessen Horowitz, with angel backing from creator-economy figures (Sahil Lavingia, Ankur Nagpal, Tiago Forte, Li Jin, David Perell). Founded by Gagan Biyani (co-founder of Udemy), Wes Kao (co-founder of altMBA) and Shreyans Bhansali (co-founder of Socratic).

Locations / HQ: No headquarters — "Remote, United States" is the registered location. Company offsites are the in-person ritual (a February 2025 offsite was held in San José del Cabo).

Remote policy: **100% remote**, stated plainly on their about page. Multiple company offsites per year. Their engineering JD lists "fully remote" and **"no-meeting Wednesdays"** as explicit work-life-balance signals, and the JD's culture extracts as "sustainable" with high autonomy. The timezone requirement is specific, however: **"US time zones +/- 1 hour."**

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes.** The Senior/Staff Software Engineer req extracts `geo_eligibility: canada` with `location_hints: ["US", "Canada"]`. The timezone constraint of US time zones ±1 hour is satisfied by Mountain Time without difficulty.

Engineering & tech: A small, full-stack, product-facing team. Languages: Python, JavaScript, TypeScript. Frontend: React (hooks) and Next.js. Backend: FastAPI on Postgres, with OpenSearch for search and OpenAI API integrations. Data stack: dbt and Snowflake. The single open req asks for "5+ years of experience shipping real features to a real user base," comfort "with React hooks, data model design, and Postgres performance," and "a high degree of agency and conscientiousness." Nice-to-haves are creator-economy/marketplace experience and OpenSearch or OpenAI API work. This is generalist product engineering across the whole stack, not specialist infrastructure ownership.

Compensation: The Senior/Staff Software Engineer req posts **US$170,000–220,000 base** with equity, described as "strong benefits and equity." Note the Ashby structured-compensation field on this posting is empty — the range comes from the JD body text itself.

Notable / other: Benefits include health/dental/vision, parental leave, competitive compensation with equity, unlimited PTO with a stated three-week minimum, free access to Maven courses, and a home-office stipend. The unlimited-PTO minimum is a meaningful detail. Only one relevant engineering role is live, so the surface area at this company is thin.
