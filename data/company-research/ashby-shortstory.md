# Short Story — research
Provider/key: ashby:shortstory | company_type: product

What they do: Short Story is a San Francisco direct-to-consumer apparel retailer built exclusively for petite women 5'4" and under. It runs a clothing discovery, curation and delivery service (originally a styling/subscription box, now a broader e-commerce retailer) and describes itself in its own JD as "an award winning, technology powered retailer" using "a data driven learning system that leverages customer feedback to create tailored products". It designs and manufactures its own product line rather than reselling. Press coverage from Forbes and Fortune; Y Combinator alumnus.

How they describe themselves: Stated mission — "to create a seamless shopping experience for millions of petite women so they can dress with effortless confidence." The full stated value set, verbatim from their own posting:
- **Transform Lives** — "We're customer-first. Everything we create exists to give her confidence."
- **Demand Excellence** — "We deliver only our best work, in Fit and Finish. We obsess about details others overlook."
- **Embrace Challenges** — "We see obstacles as opportunities to invent better solutions."
- **Think Differently** — "We get a thrill from finding better solutions others haven't seen."
They also state their culture "thrives on data insights, exceptional commitment to fit, and the urgency to move quickly on what matters", describe the engineering team as "fast-moving", say they "value engineers who take ownership, move quickly, and are comfortable figuring things out as they go", and describe the company as "an early stage start-up" staffed by "a group of smart, passionate hackers".

Size / stage / funding: Private, seed stage. Founded April 2019 by Isabella Sun and Samuel Hoffstaetter. ~$3M seed (Sept 2021) led by True, with Tribe Capital, Y Combinator, Hyphen Capital, Wedbush Ventures, Operate Venture Studio, Bossanova, plus angels including Zendesk's CTO and a Shopify director of engineering. Reported eight-figure annual revenue with ~95x growth since the pandemic. Small team.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco, CA. Warehouse/fulfilment operations (they post Fulfillment Associate roles).

Remote policy: Role-level. The backend req states: "Either in-person in San Francisco, or remote in US or Canada." They post the same Senior Backend req twice — one San Francisco edition and one explicitly "(Remote Canada)".

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes — explicitly and unambiguously.** A dedicated requisition titled "Senior Software Engineer, Back-End (Remote Canada)" is live with ATS location "Canada (Remote)", and the JD body repeats "remote in US or Canada". Note the benefits line scopes 401k as "(US)", implying a separate Canadian arrangement. Evidence: https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/shortstory/469112a4-4c3a-4a09-80ca-852c9cd8fe0b

Engineering & tech: Python (a Python web framework), Postgres/SQL, Docker containerized workloads, CI/CD and monitoring, REST APIs. Domain systems named in the JD: orders, inventory, logistics/warehouse operations, personalization/fit, and a data platform. Sprint-based environment; TDD and tech-lead experience listed as preferred. Engineers "own meaningful parts of the system end-to-end", deciding data models, APIs and system behaviour. Requirements: 5+ years experience, 2+ years Python. No engineering blog or public handbook found.

Notable / other: The engineering pitch is explicitly "the standard e-commerce playbook doesn't work for our customers, so you build your own" — i.e. in-house rebuilds of order/inventory/logistics systems. Compensation & benefits stated: generous PTO, health/vision/dental, 401k with match (US), employee merchandise discount. No salary range disclosed on either edition of the req.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Back-End (Remote Canada); Senior Software Engineer, Back-End (San Francisco); Fulfillment Associate.
