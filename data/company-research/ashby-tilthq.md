# Tilt Finance — research
Provider/key: ashby:tilthq | company_type: product

What they do: Tilt is a **consumer fintech lender** — mobile-first credit products for people underserved by traditional credit scoring. In their words: "At Tilt, we see a side of people that traditional lenders miss. Our mobile-first products and machine learning-powered credit models look beyond credit scores, using over 250 real-time financial signals to recognize real potential. With millions of customers worldwide, we're not just changing how people access financial products — we're creating a new credit system that backs the working, whatever they're working toward." They operate a regulated, PCI-DSS-scoped estate across multiple regions, including named APAC businesses in **India and the Philippines** (the Philippines business is branded **Cashalo**).

How they describe themselves: Under "The Tilt Way": "We're looking for people who chase excellence and impact. Those who stand behind their work, celebrating the wins and learning from the missteps equally. We foster an environment where every voice is valued and mutual respect is non-negotiable — **brilliant jerks need not apply**. We're in this together, working to expand access to fair credit and prove that people are incredible." Hiring philosophy is explicitly potential-over-pedigree: "Don't meet every qualification? We care about potential over your past"; "we know careers don't all look the same — if you're close, let's talk"; "Growth opportunities follow your contributions, not rigid promotion timelines." They also push an AI-first working expectation: candidates should be "able to effectively leverage AI-powered development tools (e.g., Cursor, Augment, Factory)", and the SRE role says "Work AI-first: use coding, observability, and IaC assistants to move faster and cut toil."

Size / stage / funding: Private; "millions of customers worldwide". Headcount, funding stage and profitability are **unverified** (WebSearch exhausted this session; no tilt domain appears in the registry so none was fetched). The team is distributed "across 14 countries, 12 time zones, and counting". Scale signals — regulated fintech, PCI-DSS programs, multiple regional businesses, a dedicated global SRE/Platform team — suggest a mid-size scaled company rather than an early startup, but this is inference, not a verified figure.

Locations / HQ: No HQ is named. Distributed across 14 countries / 12 time zones. Regional businesses in India and the Philippines (Manila office exists — a closed posting listed "Hybrid - Manila"). Live postings are anchored to **Remote - US** and **Remote - Australia**. Past onsites have been held in Vail, San Diego and Mexico City.

Remote policy: **Remote-first / "virtual-first"**, stated verbatim: "Tilt is a remote-first company. We drive connectivity through regular company offsites. Travel for company offsites is expected at a minimum 2 times a year." And: "Virtual-first teamwork: The Tilt team is collaborating across 14 countries, 12 time zones, and counting. You'll get started with a WFH office reimbursement." The APAC SRE role adds "This is a 100% remote role that requires international travel at least twice a year for onsites." So: genuinely remote-first, but with a mandatory twice-yearly international travel commitment.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, leaning no — not verified.** The company is remote-first and employs across 14 countries, which makes Canadian employment structurally plausible. However, **not one live or recently-closed posting is open to Canada**: the backend roles are "Remote - US", the data platform role is "Remote - US", the SRE role is "Remote - Australia", and a closed backend role was "Remote - Australia, Hybrid - Manila, Remote - Singapore". Treat Canada eligibility as **unverified and probably unavailable** unless a Canada-tagged posting appears.

Engineering & tech: **.NET / C#** is the primary backend stack ("Experience with .NET (C#), or similar frameworks"). Infrastructure is **Azure-first**, with AWS and legacy services still in the estate and active cross-cloud migration underway. Terraform/IaC fluency is expected at module-and-environment level, not one-off scripts. Networking depth is called out: site-to-site VPN, VNet design and peering, DNS, TLS/certs, firewall and IP allow-listing, edge/CDN. Scripting in PowerShell, Bash, Python, Azure CLI. Practices: incident response with root-cause analysis and blameless post-mortems, runbooks, PCI-DSS hardening, vulnerability scanning, least-privilege access, secret management. Teams are organised around domains — decisioning, payments, APIs — plus a global SRE/Platform team. ML shows up in the credit-decisioning models (250+ real-time financial signals) rather than as a platform-engineering specialty. No public engineering blog or handbook was found.

Notable / other: Benefits and perks stated: WFH office reimbursement, "competitive compensation packages and generous equity", flexible health plans at every premium level with substantial subsidies "that stand up to global standards", direct exposure to the leadership team, paid global onsites twice yearly (Vail, San Diego, Mexico City cited), and growth tied to contribution rather than promotion cycles.
