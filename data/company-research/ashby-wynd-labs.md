# Wynd Labs — research
Provider/key: ashby:wynd-labs | company_type: product

What they do: Wynd Labs builds web-data infrastructure for AI training. In their own words: "We
build infrastructure that delivers massive amounts of web data to the companies training the
world's most powerful AI models." They are the team behind **Grass** — a bandwidth-sharing
network (a DePIN / crypto project with its own token) whose participants contribute unused
residential bandwidth, which lets Wynd operate a massive distributed web crawler with "unique
access to high-quality public web data at global scale." On top of the crawl they run pipelines
"for ingesting, segmenting, and annotating billions of videos, transcripts, and audio files,
powering dataset creation for frontier labs." The public site frames the commercial offering as
"Internet-scale web data for Business Intelligence." So: a crawler + dataset-curation business
whose supply side is a token-incentivized consumer network and whose demand side is frontier AI
labs.

How they describe themselves: "We're lean, technical, and move fast. No red tape, no slow
decision-making; just a team of builders pushing to expand what's possible for open web data and
AI." On culture: "We're a lean team with a high bar. We come to work not to be comfortable, but to
find out what we're capable of and to do work that matters. We're not calling for people who keep
things moving. We're calling for people who make everyone around them better. We prioritize low
ego and high output. This is a fully remote team." From another JD: "We operate with a lean, highly
motivated team who revel in the responsibility that comes with autonomy. We have a flat
organizational structure, the people making decisions are also the ones implementing them. We are
driven by ambitious goals and a strong sense of urgency. Leadership is given to those who show
initiative, consistently deliver excellence and bring the best out of those around them. Join us if
you want to set the tone for a fair and equitable internet." JDs also require "excellent work ethic
and the ability to thrive in a fast-paced startup environment" and "experience working in a high
output team."

Size / stage / funding: Small and early. Reported headcount is in the 13–45 range depending on
source and whether contractors are counted (a Grass token-holder disclosure referenced ~45 FTE;
other trackers list ~13 employees). Total equity funding reported at roughly $8M: a $3.5M seed for
Grass (led by Polychain Capital and Tribe Capital) and a later ~$10M bridge round co-led by
Polychain and Tribe with Hack VC involved. Other investors named across trackers include Brevan
Howard, Delphi Digital/Delphi Ventures and Bluegrass Capital. Note the funding picture is partly
crypto-native: the Grass token is a separate economic layer from the equity, and Wynd Labs is paid
out of network spend, which is an unusual and less predictable revenue structure than ordinary SaaS.

Locations / HQ: No office or HQ is stated on their pages or postings. Every relevant posting on
both their Ashby and Greenhouse boards is listed as "Remote", and the JD says plainly "This is a
fully remote team."

Remote policy: Fully remote, stated explicitly in the JDs. Flat org, decision-makers are also the
implementers. No stated timezone requirement or async-vs-sync policy on any page reviewed.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Likely yes, but not stated.** No JD, careers page or site text names
Canada or any geographic restriction — the only geographic statement anywhere is "fully remote
team." The circumstantial evidence is the compensation data captured from their own postings:
four of the five bands on record (Machine Learning Engineer, Software Engineer (Backend), Data
Engineer, and Backend SWE (Systems & Chromium Infrastructure)) are denominated in **CAD**, while
Senior Rust Engineer (Backend) is denominated in USD. A company posting most of its engineering
ranges in Canadian dollars is very likely hiring Canadians directly. Not a written policy —
confirm with a recruiter.

Engineering & tech: Substantial data and systems infrastructure. Across the JDs: large-scale ETL
and pipeline orchestration in Python, Scala or Java; Docker and Kubernetes for data-infrastructure
deployment; data governance, data security and disaster recovery designed into the infrastructure;
infrastructure-as-code and pipeline automation. The ML side is dataset engineering rather than
modelling research — "building scalable pipelines for filtering, deduplicating, and improving
large-scale text datasets used for LLM training", text deduplication, dataset filtering, corpus
curation, data distillation, NLP pipelines, OCR, and time-series analysis. There is also serious
low-level systems work: one role is dedicated to Chromium infrastructure — "managing the relentless
intake of upstream Chromium releases… ensuring our custom patches play nice with the latest Google
updates" and "building the CI/CD and QA infrastructure required to compile, test, and deploy a
massive C++ codebase across multiple platforms." A Rust backend role exists as well. Stated
engineering values include an "infrastructure mindset: you don't just want to fix a bug; you want
to build a test suite that ensures that bug never comes back," comfort with automated QA and
deployment pipelines, and "write clear, well-tested, and well-documented code."

Notable / other: Two ATS boards are in use (Ashby `wynd-labs` and Greenhouse `wyndlabs`).
Compensation is described in JDs only as "a competitive salary, benefits and equity package," but
actual ranges are posted on the individual listings. The Grass network has a public token-holder
call cadence and published network disclosures, so some financial detail about the business is
unusually public for a company this size.

Pay (from their own postings): a $180,000–$300,000 base range is posted on Machine Learning
Engineer, Software Engineer (Backend), Data Engineer and Backend SWE (Systems & Chromium
Infrastructure), all in CAD; the same $180,000–$300,000 range is posted in USD on Senior Rust
Engineer (Backend).

Open relevant roles (sample): Machine Learning Engineer; Senior Rust Engineer (Backend); Software
