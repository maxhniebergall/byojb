# Astronomer — research
Provider/key: ashby:astronomer | company_type: product

What they do: In their own words, "Astronomer empowers data teams to bring mission-critical software, analytics, and AI to life and is the company behind Astro, the industry-leading unified DataOps platform powered by Apache Airflow®. Astro accelerates building reliable data products that unlock insights, unleash AI value, and powers data-driven applications." They are the commercial steward of Apache Airflow, the dominant open-source workflow-orchestration project. Product surface is Astro (managed Airflow PaaS), Observe (data observability) and an IDE. Customer base is stated two ways in their own material: "Trusted by more than 800 of the world's leading enterprises" (JD boilerplate) and "900+ enterprise customers" (careers page) — plus "80K+ organizations use Airflow."

How they describe themselves: The Platform JDs use named internal principles, quoted verbatim as headings:
- **"Blaze a Trail"** — "Own and develop our platform infrastructure strategy, with the sponsorship and responsibility to match. Map out what we need, make the calls, and own the outcomes."
- **"Be an Owner"** — "Be directly involved in deciding what we work on and how we work on it. Make promises, and keep them."
- **"Do Sensible Things"** — "Make principled build vs. buy assessments and advocate for the right tools for the right job — not the fashionable ones, not the ones already in the estate just because they're there."
- **"Garage Door Open"** — "Create and maintain comprehensive internal documentation and decision records for systems and processes. Participate in architectural forums and make principled, open decisions that the rest of the organisation can learn from and hold us to."
The careers page adds: "Join a team across 4 continents, 20+ countries," and emphasises reliability, iterative shipping, cross-department collaboration and "responsibility as stewards of Apache Airflow" — "Engineering, product, sales, marketing, and customer success operate as one team."

The Platform Infrastructure JD is unusually honest about what the work is and isn't: "The work ahead on our Platform team isn't about managing data or curating pipelines; it's about building the foundational systems that everything else runs on — the kind of systems where the failure modes matter, the latency budgets are real, and getting it wrong has visible consequences for hundreds of enterprise customers." And: "We're not looking for someone to write strategy documents; we're looking for someone who writes the strategy and the code." Also notable: "A passion for reliability and operational excellence. A low tolerance for toil and other nonsense." Astronomer describes its own estate candidly as "a healthy and complex infrastructure estate spanning multiple cloud providers, a mix of managed and self-hosted systems."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, late-stage growth (Series D-era; exact current stage and headcount not disclosed on the careers page and not verified here). Established commercial open-source company, not a seed-stage risk. The careers page discloses customer counts but no headcount.

Locations / HQ: **HQ New York City**, described as "NYC with offices in major cities globally," and a workforce "across 4 continents, 20+ countries." The concrete engineering-role geos in the registry are narrower: New York City, London, and Ireland.

Remote policy: Mixed by role, stated plainly: "Some roles are in-office, others are flexible — but our spaces are always there when you want them." Not a remote-first company by declaration. The JDs do assume distribution — both Platform roles require "experience in working with a globally distributed team in delivery" — and the Staff role notes documentation and decision records as a first-class expectation ("Garage Door Open"), which is an async-friendly signal. The Senior Platform role "involves periodic on-call for the services we own."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No, for these roles — and unclear as a company.** Every open Platform posting is pinned to New York City, London and/or Ireland; none lists a Canadian or remote-North-America location. The careers page says the team spans "20+ countries" but does not name them and makes no mention of Canada. So Astronomer plausibly employs internationally in some form, but there is no evidence it hires in Canada, and the specific roles in this registry are city-pinned to NYC/London/Ireland. Treat as not Canada-eligible unless directly confirmed with their recruiting team.

Engineering & tech: Multi-cloud PaaS — "considered architectural decisions across AWS, GCP, and/or Azure," with the Senior role calling out "the innards and quirks of AWS, GCP and (particularly) Azure." **Go is the platform team's production language** ("The platform team writes production Go. You should be fluent"); Python also required at the Senior level. **Kubernetes at operator depth** — "You know what happens inside the scheduler and the control loop when things go wrong, because you've been there. You've operated clusters under real load, not just deployed workloads onto them." Distributed-systems depth framed concretely: "consistency and availability tradeoffs, failure cascades, backpressure, graceful degradation," with **NALSD** (Non-Abstract Large System Design) named as the expected design method. Observability with SLI/SLO definition and tracking. Storage primitives — relational vs object store tradeoffs. Named stack components: "CircleCI, Chronosphere (Prometheus), Splunk, Bazel, Istio, Playwright, Karpenter, Github [Actions]." Platform/Reliability practice reports to a **VP of Reliability**; the Staff role shapes "what Astronomer's products — Astro, Observe, and our IDE — are capable of over the next several years."

Notable / other: This is one of the most substantive and least hype-laden JD sets encountered — no "fast-paced," no "rocketship," explicit rejection of fashionable tooling, explicit preference for documented and contestable decisions. Astronomer is also the primary corporate steward of Apache Airflow, which is genuine open-source infrastructure work. The Staff role is described as "a foundational role at a consequential moment" on "a small but growing team," so the platform practice is being built out rather than inherited mature.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer, Platform Infrastructure (London / New York City / Ireland — four near-duplicate listings); Senior Software Engineer, Platform (New York City + London; and a London-only listing); Software Engineer - Airflow Infrastructure; Software Engineer, Astro Core Services.

Pay: The Senior Software Engineer, Platform JD states its own range: "The estimated total compensation for this role ranges from $168,000 - $230,000 based on leveling and geography, along with an equity component and a comprehensive benefits package. This range is merely an estimate; actual compensation may deviate from this range based on skills, experience, and qualifications." Note this is **total compensation**, not base, and it is explicitly self-described as an estimate. No range is stated on any of the Staff Platform Infrastructure postings.

Data-quality note: The registry holds four near-identical "Staff Software Engineer, Platform Infrastructure" postings and three "Senior Software Engineer, Platform" postings that differ only in the ordering of the same location list (e.g. "London, New York City, Ireland" vs "New York City, London, Ireland"). These are almost certainly one or two real requisitions duplicated per-location by Ashby, not six distinct openings — the `live_relevant: 6` count for this company is inflated.
