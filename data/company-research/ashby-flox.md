# Flox — research
Provider/key: ashby:flox | company_type: product

What they do: Flox builds developer tooling for reproducible software environments, powered by Nix. Their own words: they are "building the next generation of software development lifecycle tooling" and help "teams create secure software by construction—managing all software and its dependencies down to the smallest package, with SBOMs and provenance included." The About page frames the mission as helping teams create reproducible environments across the SDLC by bundling tools, frameworks and software together. Products are "developer tools at their core: command-line-first, systems-level, powered by Nix, built for engineers who care about how software gets made." Supply-chain security (SBOM, provenance) and enterprise environment management are the commercial wedge.

How they describe themselves: The About page centres on determinism and repeatability — the questions "what exactly made that happen?" and "how can I make exactly that happen again?" — and on championing open-source principles through Nix adoption. The engineering JD adds a strong stated stance on AI-assisted development: agentic coding tools (Claude Code, Cursor) as "your primary development workflow", building "harnesses, context engineering (CLAUDE.md, skills, rules files), automated feedback loops, and evaluation frameworks", and "exercise judgment on when AI agents accelerate real work and when they introduce risk, complexity, or false confidence." They also state a quality bar: "make architectural decisions that balance speed with long-term system health" and "discipline to maintain quality when code generation is fast and cheap." Note the velocity language: "You ship at high velocity because of how you use AI."

Size / stage / funding: Private venture-backed startup, small. The About page lists roughly 22 team members across engineering, product, operations and marketing. Leadership: Ron Efroni (CEO), Michael Brantley (CTO). Investors named on their own site: DESCOvery (the D. E. Shaw group's venture studio), NEA, Hetz Ventures, Addition Ventures, plus angels Thomas Dohmke (former GitHub CEO), Guy Podjarny (Snyk founder) and James Turnbull (former VP at Docker). Exact round size/stage and headcount are not stated on the About page; the small team plus this investor set reads as early (seed/Series A) — treat as UNVERIFIED. Origin story: the company was built to solve problems D. E. Shaw's own developers hit adopting Nix at scale, described as "one of the largest and most impactful enterprise deployments of Nix."
