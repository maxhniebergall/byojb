# Tempus — research
Provider/key: workday:tempus/tempus_careers | company_type: product

What they do: Tempus AI is a precision-medicine / health-technology company. In their own JD words: "Recent advancements in underlying technology have finally made it possible for AI to impact clinical care in a meaningful way. Tempus' proprietary platform connects an entire ecosystem of real-world evidence to deliver real-time, actionable insights to physicians, providing critical information about the right treatments for the right patients, at the right time." The business combines genomic sequencing/diagnostics with a very large clinical and molecular data library, sold to physicians, health systems and pharma R&D partners. Work spans clinical workflow optimization, clinical trial matching and medical research.

How they describe themselves: The recurring self-framing is mission-and-impact: "Passionate about precision medicine and advancing the healthcare industry?" and "revolutionize healthcare and make a significant impact on people's lives". The SRE team describes itself as: "The Site Reliability Engineering team works with all departments and business units to provide dependable cloud infrastructure solutions, along with support, and services which allow teams to innovate, experiment and deliver software while maintaining a high level of quality. We focus on providing a platform and knowledge to help teams develop resilient and scalable cloud systems and services, ensuring quick detection and recovery from failures, and cultivating a culture of continuous enhancement. Our team is built on collaboration, offering robust mentorship opportunities and the flexibility to explore every facet of our business through our cloud computing initiatives." Their hiring framing is notably low-gatekeeping: "You meet the following requirements, or believe you have transferable skills in all of these areas." Standard EEO statement on every posting.

Size / stage / funding: Publicly traded (NASDAQ: TMPS) — a large, late-stage health-tech company, not a venture startup. Engineering & tech: Two distinct relevant orgs.
- **SRE / cloud platform (Calgary):** AWS, GCP and Azure; containerised applications; "Configure and deploy cloud infrastructure using Terraform (our favorite) and CI tools like Concourse"; Kubernetes, CloudFormation, Docker, Ansible, Salt, Packer, Puppet, Chef; AWS Aurora MySQL and Postgres tuning; Composer and Dataproc for workflow orchestration; Bash plus Python/Ruby/Go; dashboards, monitoring and alerting; HIPAA / HITRUST / ISO compliance work. Explicitly framed as enabling other teams and advocating best practices.
- **Clinical AI / ML platform (Chicago + remote-USA):** "Build and operate production AI pipelines: LLM-powered extraction, batch orchestration, and inference, with a focus on reliability, cost, and latency"; Airflow-based orchestration; observability "that catches regressions before they reach downstream consumers"; "Build and maintain eval infrastructure that measures clinical model output quality continuously: regression detection, drift, gold-set management, dashboards"; "Ship platform tooling and SDKs that accelerate Machine Learning Scientists"; LangGraph as the primary ML framework (PyTorch/spaCy welcome); GCP; microservices; design docs (RFCs/PRDs). On-call: "Participate in the pod's on-call rotation." Teams are organised into "pods" with cross-pod design review.

Notable / other: Pay transparency is applied across jurisdictions on the Clinical AI role — New York $170,000–$230,000 USD; California $170,000–$230,000; Illinois $150,000–$210,000; Remote-USA $150,000–$210,000 — plus "incentive compensation, restricted stock units, medical and other benefits". They include the Los Angeles Fair Chance Ordinance criminal-history disclosure. Regulated healthcare domain (HIPAA/HITRUST) means genuinely mission-critical, compliance-bound infrastructure work.

Open relevant roles (sample): Site Reliability Engineer (Calgary); Staff/Senior Machine Learning Engineer, Clinical AI (Chicago / 5 locations); Senior Software Engineer (Barcelona); Translational Scientist, Applied Machine Learning and Agentic AI, Pharma R&D.
