# GlossGenius / Genius AI — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:glossgenius | company_type: product
What they do: GlossGenius is an all-in-one business management platform for appointment-based service businesses — beauty, wellness, medspa, health and fitness. It bundles scheduling, payments, marketing, client management, finances, staff and inventory management into one system (its site claims 100+ features) and monetizes partly through payment processing at a flat 2.6%. Its own JD text says it serves 120,000+ businesses and processes "billions in payments." The corporate identity is currently being rebranded upward to "Genius AI," with GlossGenius and a product called Reception as sub-brands.
How they describe themselves: Its JD opens: "Genius AI is building technology to help local businesses run themselves. Genius AI products do the admin work that keeps practitioners from the work they love and the people they serve." It cites impact metrics — 106M admin hours saved, $2.0B additional revenue earned for businesses, 624M+ client touch points. The engineering pitch is aggressively AI-forward: "Genius AI is building an agentic workforce: AI systems that act on behalf of appointment-based business owners, not just assist them. The engineers who join now will be the ones who architect it." It splits hiring into "product engineers" and "platform engineers who build the scalable, reliable infrastructure that makes those AI products possible," and states the expectation that "you build with AI as the default layer underneath how you work, not a tool you reach for occasionally." It also insists the AI work is "not wrappers around existing tools, but net-new capabilities." The consumer site markets "AI-powered agents for 24/7 business automation."
Size / stage / funding: Private, growth stage (the registry's extraction labels company_stage "growth"). 120,000+ business customers. Specific headcount and funding round were not disclosed on any page read this run.
