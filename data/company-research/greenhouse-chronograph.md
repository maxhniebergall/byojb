# Chronograph — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:chronograph | company_type: product
What they do: Cloud-based portfolio-monitoring, valuation, analytics and data-management software for private capital markets — venture capital, private equity, private credit and related strategies, serving both LPs and GPs. Founded 2016 "to bring next-generation technology to private capital markets." Scale claimed on their own site and JDs: **over 258,000 private companies and 15,000 private capital funds monitored**, a client base "monitoring more than $5.9 trillion in invested capital", with "8 of the 10 largest GPs and 5 of the 10 largest LPs" as users. Recently launched a Private Credit Platform.
How they describe themselves: Positioning is "Built by private capital investors for private capital investors", with three stated differentiators — investor domain expertise on the team, a client-centric partnership approach, and modern cloud technology. Marketing line: "Transform your portfolio monitoring, valuations, and analytics with a technology partner that understands the nuances of your business." The careers framing in their JDs: "a team that values creativity, open communication, cutting edge technology, striving for excellence in all things - and having fun along the way. We want you to be happy for the long-term." Engineering culture is described as "centered on continuous learning, collaborative knowledge sharing, and both personal and team development", and they say they "hire engineers with a broad set of technical skills, who are highly cross-functional" whose engineers "execute across the stack."
Size / stage / funding: Private, growth stage. Backed by **Sixth Street Growth, Summit Partners, Carlyle AlpInvest and Nasdaq Inc.**; the site cites **$140M+ in growth equity** from Sixth Street Growth. Describes "continuous rapid growth since its founding in 2016." Institutional, later-stage investor base rather than early venture — a relatively stable capital position, though still growth-funded rather than stated-profitable.
Locations / HQ: HQ in **Brooklyn, New York** (Ten Grand Street, Floor 4); international office in **London, UK**.
Remote policy: Stated in JDs as "Flexible work arrangements (including remote / hybrid)". Not remote-first: "Team week events in HQ (Brooklyn, NY) three times annually for all employees" — so periodic in-person travel to Brooklyn is expected of everyone. Both live engineering postings are listed as "Remote".
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear — not verified, and the available evidence leans US.** Neither the company site (no remote-work policy published) nor the JDs name Canada or list eligible countries. Counter-signals: salary quoted in USD, benefits are US-specific (401(k)), HQ Brooklyn with a London office and no Canadian entity mentioned. Three mandatory HQ team weeks a year would also mean regular cross-border travel. Treat Canada eligibility as an open question to confirm with a recruiter before investing effort — do not assume yes.
Engineering & tech: Core services in **Ruby on Rails and Node.js**. Infrastructure team runs **AWS, production Kubernetes clusters and managed database systems**, infrastructure-as-code, CI/CD workflows, and local dev environments. A notable in-house platform: a PR-environment system that "allows developers to quickly spin up an entire application stack with its own production-scale, ZFS-based database clone". Observability tooling is developer-owned (logging, metrics, tracing, alarming). They are building AI-assisted tooling "to automate operational work, with sound judgment on safety and cost". **On-call rotation is explicit** for the infrastructure role, including production incident response and post-mortem-driven observability work. The infra team's stated mission is "to scale the infrastructure that powers our platform and to empower developers throughout the software development lifecycle."
Notable / other: The JD is candid about being "a rapidly growing startup environment". They encourage under-qualified applicants to apply ("Even if you don't meet all the criteria"). Benefits listed: 401(k), unlimited and flexible vacation, generous health benefits, fully-paid parental leave.
Compensation (as posted): Both live senior engineering JDs post a pay-transparency range of **$175,000 - $215,000 USD** base, "dependent on experience". Already on record in data/company-comp.jsonl for both software_general/senior and platform_infra/senior.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (Remote); Sr. Software Engineer, Platform Engineering (Remote).
