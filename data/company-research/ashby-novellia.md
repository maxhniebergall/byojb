# Novellia — research

What they do: Patient-controlled health-records and real-world-data (RWD) platform. Consumer side: "Novellia is the first and only company that lets anyone in the US gain access to nearly a decade of their health data in under 30 seconds, 100% free. All your health records, across doctors, all in one place, always up to date." Business side: "the only patient-powered real world data platform delivering comprehensive, patient-authorized longitudinal health insights to accelerate biopharma innovation" — patients unlock 15+ years of their own records free, and Novellia turns those complete health journeys into "fit-for-purpose datasets for evidence generation, regulatory submissions, and market access" sold to pharma. Explicitly contrasted with "traditional RWD providers who provide fragmented institutional data".
