# Fullscript — research
Provider/key: lever:fullscript | company_type: product

What they do: Fullscript is a Canadian health-technology company. In its own words: "We're an industry-leading health technology company on a mission to help people get better. We started in 2011 with one simple idea. Make it easier for practitioners to access the products they trust so they can deliver better care. That simple idea grew into a platform that powers every part of care. Today, more than **125,000 practitioners** use Fullscript for clinical insights, lab interpretations, patient analytics, education, and access to high-quality supplements. Over **10 million patients** rely on Fullscript to stay connected to their care plans and follow through on treatment." The business began as practitioner-dispensed supplement fulfilment and has expanded into clinical data, labs and patient analytics.

How they describe themselves: The mission framing is "help people get better" and "shape the future of care" — "We build tools that make care smarter and more human. Tools that save time, simplify decisions, and help practitioners stay closely connected to the people they care for." The invitation to candidates is "Bring your ideas, your grit, and your care for people." Engineering has its own public site (builders.fullscript.com) built on four stated pillars: **Speed & Iteration** ("Deploy From Day One", 20+ deployments per day, "speed, failing fast, and iterating often" while keeping "high-quality code that keeps security, scalability, performance, and UX in mind"), **Impact** (code reaching 125,000+ practitioners and 10M+ patients), **Innovation** (hackathons and flex time for creative projects) and **Flexibility** (choice in work location and setup). The platform team describes itself as caring about craft: "Our team cares about doing things well. That means improving the systems developers work in every day, reducing friction, modernizing our stack, and making thoughtful technical decisions that support Fullscript's long-term growth." Culture rituals named: Town Halls, "Fullhouse", offsites, teams and clubs, a DEIB Council and Employee Resource Groups.

Size / stage / funding: Private, founded 2011, Canadian. Roughly 1,000 employees. Established and revenue-generating (a fulfilment + SaaS business with a decade-plus of operating history) rather than an early venture bet; it merged with Emerson Ecologics in 2020 to form one of the largest practitioner-dispensed platforms in North America.

Locations / HQ: HQ **Ottawa, Ontario**, downtown in Constitution Square, plus a Kanata office. Second physical office in **Manchester, New Hampshire**. **Virtual hubs: Toronto ON, Kelowna BC, and Phoenix AZ.**

Remote policy: **"Wherever You Work Well"** — the stated core of the philosophy is *choice*, with physical offices in core locations and virtual hubs elsewhere. Fullscript's own careers material notes that most employees choose a hybrid setup, so this is flexible/hybrid-leaning rather than remote-first. Engineering reqs nevertheless list "remote" alongside named cities.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes — it is a Canadian employer, so there is no work-authorisation question at all.** BC is explicitly part of the footprint: **Kelowna, BC is a named virtual hub**, and several live reqs list Vancouver, BC directly (Senior DevOps Engineer; Senior Machine Learning Engineer; Staff Machine Learning Engineer). **One caveat:** the *Senior Developer, Backend - Developer Infrastructure* and *Senior Data Engineer* reqs list "Ottawa, ON, remote, Toronto, ON, Calgary, AB" — BC is **not** named on those two, though "remote" is. Calgary AB being listed does confirm Mountain-Time working is normal here. Worth confirming BC eligibility on the specific req rather than assuming it.

Engineering & tech: Stated stack — backend **Ruby on Rails** with a **GraphQL API** and **MySQL**; frontend **React, TypeScript, Apollo, Next.js**; infrastructure **Docker** and **OpenSearch**; data work in cloud warehouses, **Snowflake** or comparable. Ships 20+ deployments per day. Two distinct relevant teams:
- **Developer Infrastructure** (the platform team): "the infrastructure team responsible for the long-term health, scalability, and developer experience of Fullscript's core application… Our team enables fast, reliable product development by defining and upholding engineering standards for quality, scalability, and maintainability. We take on foundational upgrades, cross-team technical work, and infrastructure projects that help product teams move quickly without compromising the stability of the platform." Internal customers, explicitly.
- **Data Engineering**: "part of a small team that turns messy practitioner and clinical data into reliable, analysis-ready assets that enable causal outcome modeling, lab normalization, and strategic patient and prescribing insights." Requires healthcare-specific data experience. Work includes ingesting and normalising heterogeneous clinical records, lab results, intake forms and semi-structured artefacts; building reproducible ELT pipelines for longitudinal patient-level datasets; applying OCR and NLP to unstructured clinical documents; implementing data-quality frameworks, testing, version control and CI/CD for all ingestion and transformation workflows; optimising warehouse pipeline performance; and producing "clear documentation and operational runbooks that enable internal consumers to trust and act on healthcare datasets."
There is also a public developer handbook (fullscript.com/developer-handbook) and an engineering site at builders.fullscript.com.

Notable / other: Healthcare data brings regulatory weight (PHI/PIPEDA/HIPAA-adjacent), which pushes toward rigour and documentation. Benefits/programmes named: educational budgets, mentorship programmes, hackathons, wellness initiatives, DEIB programmes. The ML ladder goes to Staff, so there is senior-plus room.
