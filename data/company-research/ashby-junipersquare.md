# Juniper Square — research
Provider/key: ashby:junipersquare | company_type: product

What they do: Juniper Square builds software for **private markets** — the fund administration, investor management, reporting and capital-raising workflows used by private equity, venture capital and commercial real estate GPs and their LPs. It combines a SaaS platform with an outsourced fund-administration service. Its current strategic bet is **JunieAI**, "our purpose-built AI platform… reimagining how private markets operate, embedding intelligence across every workflow." The engineering problems are document-heavy and regulated: structured extraction from unstructured financial documents, RAG pipelines, agentic workflows, LLM evaluation, data lineage and contracts, semantic layers/metrics stores, and payment/fintech data pipelines.

How they describe themselves: "Founder-led since 2014… we're building a company designed to shape the future of private markets for decades to come." Culture statement, in their own words: "Our culture is built for people who want to do ambitious, meaningful work alongside exceptionally talented teammates. We **think like owners, move with urgency**, and take pride in solving hard problems that truly matter to our customers and the future of private markets. We believe the best ideas come from **open debate, deep collaboration**… which is why we believe **transparency is the default and feedback makes us stronger**. If you're energized by **high standards, rapid growth**, and the opportunity to help define a category at a pivotal moment, come join us!"

Size / stage / funding: **1,000+ employees**, founder-led since 2014, private, **$350M+ raised** (Series D territory; extracted facets tag it "late_stage"). Large enough to be past startup fragility, still on a growth/category-definition footing.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco (HQ), plus physical offices in New York City, Mumbai and Bangalore. Employees distributed across **27 U.S. states, 2 Canadian provinces, India, Luxembourg and England**. Significant India engineering presence — several open reqs are explicitly India-based (Lead Site Reliability Engineer – India, Principal Engineer (Platform) – India, QA Automation Lead (Data Engineering) – India).

Remote policy: "Juniper Square offers employees a variety of ways to work, ranging from a fully remote experience to working full-time in one of our physical offices. We invest heavily in **digital-first operations**, allowing our teams to collaborate effectively across [those geographies]." Offices are opt-in for those who prefer them. So: remote-permitted and digital-first by design, but not remote-only.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes, with a caveat to verify.** All the AI/data engineering reqs are posted as "Americas (USA or Canada)", and every one of them publishes an explicit **Canadian base salary range in CAD** alongside the U.S. range — the strongest possible evidence they employ Canadians directly. **The caveat**: the company states it operates in only "**2 Canadian Provinces**" and does not say which. If those are Ontario and British Columbia, a BC-based hire is straightforward; if they are Ontario and Quebec, it is not. One JD's extracted location hints mention Ontario and another mentions EST — worth confirming with a recruiter before investing in a process. No hard timezone requirement is stated in any JD.

Engineering & tech: Python-forward web stack (FastAPI, Flask, Django) with React on the front end; AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, containers, service mesh, serverless, Terraform/Pulumi. Data platform: Kafka, Flink, Redshift, dbt, Airflow, PostgreSQL, real-time analytics and event-driven architectures, semantic layers and metrics stores, data lineage/contracts/metadata management. AI stack: RAG pipelines, vector stores, agentic workflows, LLM orchestration, MLOps tooling and experiment tracking, and **LLM evaluation frameworks named explicitly (RAGAS, custom evals, human-in-the-loop review)**. Cursor is named as a tool. JDs mention large-scale cloud cost optimization and scaling relational databases under high concurrency. Ladder runs senior → staff → senior staff → principal, with an explicit TLM (tech-lead-manager) track referenced as a nice-to-have.

Notable / other: Benefits listed on every JD — health, dental and vision for you and your family; life insurance; mental wellness coverage; fertility and growing-family support; Flex Time Off in addition to standard holidays; retirement/savings. Compensation is base + equity + benefits, with location-adjusted bases and dual US/Canada ranges published as a matter of course.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (AI); Senior Software Engineer, Data (AI); Staff Software Engineer (AI); Staff Software Engineer, Data (AI); Senior Staff Software Engineer, Data; Senior Staff Software Engineer (AI). India-only: Lead Site Reliability Engineer, Principal Engineer (Platform), QA Automation Lead (Data Engineering).
