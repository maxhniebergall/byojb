# Cadence Design Systems — research
Provider/key: workday:cadence/external_careers | company_type: product
What they do: Cadence is a major electronic-design-automation (EDA) vendor — software, IP and hardware systems used to design semiconductors, electronic systems and, increasingly, multiphysics/system-analysis and computational-biology products. Customers are chip and systems companies. Visible product lines in the open roles include verification IP, the Jasper formal-verification R&D line, automotive solutions, AI compiler / ChipStack AI tooling, and a sheet-metal cost-planning and simulation platform. Engineering is deeply technical: compilers, simulation kernels, distributed systems and verification.
How they describe themselves: The recurring JD boilerplate is "At Cadence, we hire and develop leaders and innovators who want to make an impact on the world of technology" and the sign-off "We're doing work that matters. Help us solve what others can't." Accessibility statement: "We welcome applications from candidates with disabilities and in equity seeking groups." A striking current theme is an AI-first engineering mandate — candidates "are expected to be AI-native developers, leveraging modern AI tools, coding assistants, agent frameworks, and structured reasoning techniques throughout the software lifecycle—from requirements analysis and system decomposition to implementation, testing, and maintenance." No separate values page was fetched.
Size / stage / funding: Large, profitable, publicly traded US company (NASDAQ: CDNS), tens of thousands of employees worldwide. Not runway-dependent; among the most stable employer types in this queue. Financials not verified in this pass.
Locations / HQ: HQ San Jose, California. The 11+ live relevant postings span San Jose, Cork (Ireland), Feldkirchen/Munich, Noida and Bangalore (India), Belo Horizonte (Brazil), Mont-Saint-Guibert (Belgium), and in Canada **Burnaby, BC and Port Moody (Vancouver), BC**, plus one listed as "HOME CANADA_ON" (home-based, Ontario).
Remote policy: Predominantly site-based — nearly every posting is bound to a named office. One exception in this scan: the "HOME CANADA_ON" Software Engineer II, which is a home-based Ontario role. No company-wide remote or async policy was found, and none is stated in the JD text.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Mixed, and effectively negative for Kimberley.** Cadence clearly employs in Canada (BC and Ontario), so work authorization is not the issue. But the BC roles (Distributed Systems Engineer, Burnaby / Port Moody) are Lower-Mainland office roles, not remote — not workable from the Kootenays. The one genuinely home-based Canadian posting is anchored to **Ontario** (Eastern Time) and is an early-career role. No remote-BC senior infrastructure role exists in the current set. Treat as not remote-Canada-eligible for this candidate.
Engineering & tech: Genuine systems depth across the open roles — Compiler/LLVM lead work, distributed systems engineering, cloud infrastructure at staff level, AI DevOps for the ChipStack AI product, AI-systems principal roles, and verification-IP development. Languages and tools named across postings: C++ (dominant), Python, Qt, VBA, RESTful APIs, distributed systems, agent-based architectures, Azure DevOps, Git, VS/VS Code, Agile. Domain-adjacent asks include finite-element methods, numerical methods and physics-based modelling for the simulation products. Several roles require a Master's degree in engineering, applied mathematics, physics or CS.
Notable / other: The Canadian home-based role is unusually specialized — "an advanced software platform focused on cost planning, simulation, and early feasibility evaluation of sheet metal components", wanting "a highly motivated early-career engineer", i.e. below the target level.
