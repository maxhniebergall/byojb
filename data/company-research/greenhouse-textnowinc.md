# TextNow — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:textnowinc | company_type: product

What they do: TextNow provides free and low-cost phone service in North America — free calling and
texting over data/WiFi with a real phone number, plus paid wireless plans. It operates as an MVNO
(riding on a US carrier's network) with an ad-supported free tier, so the business combines
telecommunications infrastructure with a large-scale consumer advertising engine. Their careers page
describes the offering simply as free phone service — calls, texts, phone numbers and wireless plans.

How they describe themselves: The careers page organizes its pitch around five themes — purpose-driven
work, innovation in action, growth opportunities, an inclusive environment, and work-life balance —
and characterizes the culture as one where "productivity and grit are balanced with flexibility and
understanding. We're more than our titles." "Flexible working arrangements" is listed as a benefit.
The mission framing is affordability/access: connectivity for people priced out of conventional
carrier plans. Note the word "grit" — the only hustle-adjacent term found, and it is explicitly paired
with flexibility rather than standing alone.

Size / stage / funding: Private, Canadian-founded (out of the University of Waterloo, originally
Enflick). Several hundred employees. Long-established (founded 2009) with a large consumer user base
in the tens of millions and real advertising revenue — a revenue-generating, mature business rather
than an early-stage startup. The registry's extracted facets record stage: growth.

Locations / HQ: Waterloo, Ontario is the headquarters and the location string on every live engineering
posting ("Waterloo, ON"). A US presence exists (the SRE JD's benefits section splits "RRSP matching
(Canada) | 401(K) (USA)", confirming both Canadian and US employment entities).

Remote policy: **Unclear, and not stated on any fetched page or JD body.** The careers page mentions
only "flexible working arrangements" as a benefit with no geographic detail; neither the SRE nor the
Backend JD bodies contain any remote, hybrid, in-office, or "days per week" language at all. All live
postings are labelled "Waterloo, ON". The extracted facets for the SRE role record remote_policy:
unclear with location hints "Canada, United States".

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Canada-eligible as an employer, but geographically unverified.** They
plainly employ Canadians (CAD salary bands, RRSP matching) — the country is not the problem. What is
unverified is whether a role tagged "Waterloo, ON" can be held from British Columbia. Nothing found
states remote-anywhere-in-Canada, and the uniform Waterloo tagging on every posting points to a
Waterloo-anchored (likely hybrid) expectation. Treat as needing direct confirmation before applying;
do not assume a BC-remote hire is on offer. Timezone would be Eastern, a 2-hour offset from Mountain —
workable but not ideal.

Engineering & tech: Backend, SRE and AI engineering across a telecom/consumer-scale stack. Live roles
cover site reliability, backend services, a backend Ads team, a Labs (experimental) backend team, and
Senior Software Developer, AI. The SRE role's extracted facets read autonomy: high, on-call: true,
culture: balanced. Real infrastructure surface here — carrier integrations, VoIP/messaging at scale,
and an ad-serving pipeline are all genuine distributed-systems problems.

Notable / other: TextNow posts full CAD salary bands by level directly on its JDs, which is unusually
transparent for a private Canadian company. From the Site Reliability Engineer posting, verbatim:
- "Canada Intermediate: CAD $113,400 - $162,000 annually"
- "Canada Senior: CAD $158,000 – 207,000 annually"
- "Canada Staff: CAD $200,400 – 248,000 annually"
The JD adds: "Final compensation will be determined based on a number of factors, including skills,
experience, location and on-the-job performance. We're committed to paying competitively to hire and
retain high-caliber talent." Other benefits: $750 annual wellness benefit or healthcare spending
account, RRSP matching (Canada) / 401(K) (USA). These are among the strongest CAD bands in this batch.

Open relevant roles (sample): Site Reliability Engineer; Software Developer - Backend; Software
Developer - Backend (Labs); Software Developer, Backend Ads; Senior Software Developer, AI.
