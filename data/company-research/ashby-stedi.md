# Stedi — research
Provider/key: ashby:stedi | company_type: product

What they do: Stedi is building "the first new healthcare clearinghouse in decades" and describes itself as "the world's only programmable healthcare clearinghouse." US healthcare law (HIPAA, 1996) requires insurance payers to exchange claims, eligibility checks, prior authorizations and remittances in a standardized EDI format called X12 HIPAA; clearinghouses process the bulk of those transactions and provide consolidated payer connectivity. Stedi offers a modern API interface — eligibility checks, claims processing, ERA ingestion — alongside traditional real-time and batch EDI, so healthcare technology businesses can transact with payers without handling the EDI protocol or carrier-specific connectivity themselves. In their own words: "Until Stedi, the space was occupied entirely by a small group of legacy players, built on outdated, often pre-internet technology." The company pivoted into healthcare from a general-purpose EDI/B2B data platform.

How they describe themselves: The **Stedi Standards** are published on their careers page and alignment to them is tested throughout the technical interview process. Verbatim, in two groups:
- *Ownership*: "Constantly deliver business value"; "Be customer obsessed"; "Everything is important"; "Be present"; "Be negative maintenance"; "Be curious".
- *Operational obsession*: "Eliminate toil wherever you find it"; "Apply the minimum effective dose"; "Create and execute your own work"; "Write important things down".
The JD adds: "We have lightning in a bottle: engineers and designers shipping products week in and week out; a lean business team supporting the company's infrastructure; passion for automation and eliminating toil." Engineers are described as shipping weekly with high autonomy and minimal process overhead. They also state that "joining a startup shouldn't require quality of life tradeoffs" and encourage taking time to travel and rest.

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, founded 2017 by Zack Kanter. **$142 million raised** from Stripe, Addition, Union Square Ventures, Bloomberg Beta, First Round Capital and others. Small, lean team (they emphasize a "lean business team"). Ranked by Ramp as one of the fastest-growing SaaS vendors. Not profitable/established in the conventional sense, but very well capitalized with strong investors and real transaction revenue.

Locations / HQ: No office-anchored engineering. Nominally US-headquartered; the team is globally distributed. A daily-lunch benefit implies at least one physical location exists.

Remote policy: **Remote-first.** The careers page states Stedi is a "remote-first employer" with a globally distributed team that hires "regardless of location." Documentation culture is explicit and load-bearing — "Write important things down" is one of the ten Standards.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes — explicitly.** The live requisition is titled "Backend Engineer: **US & Canada**" and its ATS location field reads "Remote in the USA, Remote in Canada." That is a purpose-built Canada-eligible remote role, not a US role with vague flexibility. Combined with the stated remote-first, hire-regardless-of-location policy and the written-communication culture, this is the strongest remote-Canada signal in the batch. Engineering & tech: Stated verbatim under "How we build":
- "We use AWS exclusively for our customer-facing backend infrastructure. We use tools like GitHub, Stripe, and PagerDuty, but all of our application work happens on AWS."
- "We use serverless technologies almost exclusively: Lambda, API Gateway, SQS, SNS, DynamoDB, Aurora Serverless, and more. **We don't run a single server.**"
- "We use CDK (TypeScript) to define infrastructure as code."
- "We write the majority of our API backends and EDI parsing engine in **Rust**."
The role: design and develop complex, large-scale web applications and APIs; work closely with AWS to improve use of their services; contribute to engineering rigor "through both coding and writing high-quality, effective communication"; build the broadest-reaching commercial trading network at the lowest price. Requirements: 4+ years coding (Rust and TypeScript beneficial), 4+ years with AWS services, commitment to AWS serverless in production, plus "high bandwidth; thoughtful attention to many areas simultaneously" and "ability to context switch throughout the course of the day or week as priorities shift."
