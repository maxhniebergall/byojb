# Plooto — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:plootocareers | company_type: product

What they do: Plooto is a **Canada-born B2B fintech** offering an accounts payable / accounts receivable and payments platform for small and mid-sized businesses and their accountants — "mission control for your AP and AR." It handles domestic and international business payments, approval workflows, cash-flow management and CRA payment processing, and integrates with QuickBooks and Xero. They state they power payments and cash flow management for **14,000+ businesses across North America**.

How they describe themselves: In their own words: "Plooto is a Canada-born fintech company on a mission to give small and mid-sized businesses the financial power to succeed on their own terms. We make payments secure, accurate, and stress-free so businesses and their accountants can spend less time in the books, confidently manage cash flow, and make smarter decisions every day. We're a team that builds with integrity, agility, and heart, and we're looking for customer-minded pros who share our passion to join us as we scale." The engineering JD's culture statement is blunt: "We move fast. We debate ideas, not egos. We hold a high bar. And we win as a team," and "a team that cares deeply about customer impact, takes pride in our craft, and moves with urgency." They pitch the role as "not a feature-factory role" with "real ownership, real scale, real impact," and describe a culture of "experimentation, ownership, and continuous improvement" plus regular Lunch & Learn sessions. They claim "strong product-market fit, a massive opportunity ahead, and the backing of experienced fintech leaders."

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2015; small — roughly **92 employees**. Private, venture-backed and still in growth/scaling mode ("we're just getting started," "high-growth environments"), currently rebuilding "the next generation of our fintech platform."

Locations / HQ: HQ Toronto, Ontario, Canada. No other offices identified.

Remote policy: Effectively **remote-first despite a Toronto HQ** — the Built In Toronto company profile lists an "OnSite Workspace" with "typical time on-site: None," and all their currently posted roles are listed as Remote. Third-party career listings describe Plooto as supporting "hybrid and remote-friendly ways of working," and they have previously posted 100%-remote roles.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes, verified and unambiguous.** The Senior Backend Engineer req is posted as **"Remote - Canada"** by a Canadian company with a Canadian entity — no work-authorization or entity questions at all. This is the cleanest geographic fit in the batch. Time-zone expectations are not stated; the team is Toronto-centred (Eastern), so a Mountain-Time worker would be two hours behind, which is workable but means morning-heavy overlap.

Engineering & tech: **.NET / C#** on **Azure** — this is a Microsoft-stack shop. Event-driven, distributed backend services processing high volumes of financial transactions; API-first services "with strong contracts, reliability, and observability"; cloud-native architecture (Kubernetes, serverless, managed Azure services); event streaming (Kafka or similar); RDBMS and NoSQL data storage patterns; automated testing at unit, integration, contract and end-to-end levels. Asks for 5+ years backend experience, a CS degree or equivalent, and explicitly "ability to effectively use AI tools towards increasing velocity, efficiency and quality of deliverables." Responsibilities include leading complex technical initiatives from design through production rollout, making architecture decisions, code review and mentorship, and partnering closely with Product and Design.

Notable / other: They disclose that "we may use AI tools to support candidate review and selection" as part of recruitment, and note there is currently exactly one open position for this role. Accommodations are offered throughout the recruitment process (hr@plooto.com). One artifact worth noting: the JD's Qualifications section still contains an unedited internal template instruction ("Outline the experience, technical skills, and attributes required to succeed in the role…"), a small sign of hiring-process haste.
