What they do: Companion Group is a AAA **co-development gaming studio** — it does not ship its own titles. It sells engineering capacity to game publishers/studios, specialising in multiplayer/online backend work (matchmaking, lobbies, session management, anti-cheat, server orchestration, player-density scaling).
