# Lambda — research
Provider/key: ashby:lambda | company_type: product

What they do: Lambda sells AI cloud infrastructure — GPU compute for training and inference, from single instances to full clusters and supercomputers, plus orchestration on top. Their own words: "Lambda, The Superintelligence Cloud, is a leader in AI cloud infrastructure serving tens of thousands of customers. Our customers range from AI researchers to enterprises and hyperscalers. Lambda's mission is to make compute as ubiquitous as electricity and give everyone the power of superintelligence. One person, one GPU." They also physically build and operate data centres.

How they describe themselves: Seven stated values from their careers page, quoted/paraphrased closely: (1) "Customer Obsession" — "Does the customer care about this work? Will this delight the customer?"; (2) "First Principles Thinking" — reasoning from timeless business truths (quality plus low price; people determine output); (3) "Team Over Individual" — a "One Team" mentality putting customer and company above personal gain; (4) "Care Deeply" — taking pride in the work and going the extra mile; (5) "Low Ego" — outcomes over titles, valuing anonymous feedback and humility; (6) "Hacker Culture" — rooted in open-source values, with "Do-ocracy" (if you want it done, do it); (7) "Speed" — making quick, reversible decisions before larger competitors can react. They also state a written-communication norm — "If you can't write it in one-two pages, you haven't thought it through" — plus "be excellent to each other" and explicit bans on politics and crypto discussion at work. Hiring tests for deep expertise.

Size / stage / funding: Headcount and funding were not disclosed on the pages read this session. Lambda (formerly Lambda Labs) is a well-known, heavily-funded late-stage private AI-cloud company that has raised substantial capital and taken on large debt facilities to buy GPUs — but no figure was verified here, so treat stage and size as UNVERIFIED. The GPU-cloud business model is notably capital-intensive and exposed to GPU supply, depreciation and price competition.

Locations / HQ: Offices at San Jose, CA (primary operations), San Francisco, CA, and Bellevue, WA. HQ not explicitly designated on the careers page. In addition they operate or deploy into data centres in Austin TX, Quincy WA, Vernon CA, Atlanta GA, Kansas City MO, Ashburn VA, Bluffdale UT, Dallas TX and Columbus OH.

Remote policy: Effectively in-office for engineering. Their careers FAQ lists the question "What's Lambda's stance on hybrid and remote work?" but the answer was not present in the fetched content. The postings settle it in practice: TEN of the eleven live engineering postings list only physical offices — "San Francisco Office (Fremont St)", "San Jose Office (First St)" / "(Zanker)", "Bellevue Office" — with no remote option at all. The single posting tagged "Remote, USA" is the AI Infrastructure Data Center Deployment Lead, which is a hands-on data-centre role tied to the physical sites listed above.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — this is the clearest disqualification in the batch. Every software/platform/SRE role requires presence at a San Francisco, San Jose or Bellevue office. A full-text scan of every fetched Lambda JD returned zero occurrences of "Canada"/"Canadian". Their FAQ question "Do you hire outside the US?" was likewise unanswered in the fetched content, but the posting evidence is unambiguous: there is no remote engineering role, let alone a Canadian one.

Engineering & tech: Roles cover core cloud platform, managed Kubernetes, SDN (software-defined networking), fleet management, compute, infrastructure storage, platform engineering and SRE/observability — genuinely deep systems infrastructure. The data-centre side involves cluster deployment, network configuration, power/cooling requirements, cabling, vendor management and hardware-engineering/supply-chain interfaces. Culture is engineering-led with a strong written-docs norm and open-source/hacker roots.

Notable / other: Compensation is the highest in this batch by a wide margin — Senior Software Engineer (Fleet) $203-300K USD base; senior platform/infrastructure rolls up to roughly $266-395K; Staff Software Engineer (Infrastructure Storage) $314-465K USD base. Frontier-lab-adjacent pay. Note the tension between "Speed" as an explicit value ("quick, reversible decisions before larger competitors can react") and a preference for negotiable deadlines and rare emergencies; a GPU cloud with physical data-centre build-outs is an operationally intense environment.
