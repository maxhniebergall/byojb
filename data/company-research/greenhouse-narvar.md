# Narvar — research

Provider/key: greenhouse:narvar | company_type: product

What they do: Narvar is a post-purchase customer-experience platform for e-commerce retailers and
brands — order tracking, delivery estimates, proactive notifications, returns/exchanges, fraud
protection and delivery-claims management. Six named products: Promise (AI-powered delivery
estimates), Track, Notify, Shield (returns/exchanges), Secure (fraud), Assist (claims). It sits in
the logistics/retail-data plumbing layer between retailers and carriers. Founded 2012.

How they describe themselves: Four stated values on their careers page — **"Live customer
obsession"** ("Our customers are at the heart of everything we do"), **"Collective strength"**
("Together, our peak is limitless"), **"Accountability"** (owning both successes and setbacks), and
**"Purposeful innovation"** (encouraging "bold, calculated leaps that drive growth"). Tone on the
careers site is warm/people-first ("Life @ Narvar to me means always growing and learning new ways
to succeed with people I love working with"). Engineering JDs strike a notably different, more
senior-autonomy tone: the Rust role says "This role is for engineers who want agency: people who
enjoy identifying the right problems, choosing the right tools, and owning systems end-to-end. You
won't be handed a narrow ticket queue," and "We care more about judgment and ownership than
credentials."

Size / stage / funding: 250+ global employees. Private; funding stage and profitability not
disclosed on their own pages. Claims scale of 74 billion interactions processed annually, 1,500+
brands, ~125 million consumers reached annually, and that "over 90% of U.S. adults" have touched
Narvar services. Customers named: Gap, Levi Strauss & Co., Puma, Lululemon, Sephora, Glossier,
Dyson.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Mateo, California. Offices listed across San Francisco Bay Area, Atlanta,
New York, Bangalore (India) and London (UK).

Remote policy: The careers page frames the company as "hybrid flexibility" anchored on the Bay Area
HQ plus global offices. However, the engineering JDs themselves are explicitly and fully remote:
"We are fully remote" appears verbatim in the Rust JD, and every relevant open engineering posting
is tagged `#LI-Remote`.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** All six relevant open engineering roles are posted with
location "Remote - Canada". The Senior Software Developer, Rust JD states directly: "For this role,
you should be located in Canada and able to work within EST/EDT or PDT/PST hours. We are fully
remote." Pay ranges on these JDs are quoted in CAD. Timezone note: EST/EDT **or** PDT/PST is
acceptable, so Mountain Time is workable against the PDT/PST option.

Engineering & tech: Polyglot backend; explicitly a **GCP shop** ("We have deployed and operated
services in AWS or GCP (We are a GCP Shop though!)"). Currently introducing **Rust** into the
critical path — "We're introducing Rust where correctness, performance, and reliability truly
matter… the parts that must be fast, safe, and correct under real-world scale." Work described
includes distributed systems, concurrency/async boundaries, data integrity and failure modes, API
and data-contract design, relational and NoSQL databases at scale, observability, and on-call
ownership of production systems. Open roles span Distributed Systems, Data, Machine Learning and
AI. JDs endorse AI-assisted workflows "to increase leverage, not outsource thinking."

Notable / other: Recently launched **Narvar Agentic** ("the future of agentic post-purchase") and
**IRIS™** (Intelligent Retail Insights Service), a personalization/optimization layer built over
their 74-billion-datapoint corpus — so the ML/AI roles are attached to a real data asset rather
than a thin wrapper. JDs do note "You've worked in startup or high-ownership environments" and
"comfortable operating with ambiguity" as positive indicators, and mention being "backend-first,
full-stack aware."

Compensation (posted, first-party): Narvar publishes a pay-transparency block on its own JDs, in
**CAD**. Senior-level engineering roles (Senior AI Engineer; Senior Software Engineer II, Data;
Senior SWE II, Distributed Systems; Senior SWE II, Machine Learning; Senior Software Engineer,
Data) post **$180,000 – $230,000 CAD** base; Staff Software Engineer, Distributed Systems posts
**$195,000 – $273,000 CAD**. Their own caveat: the number "does not include the other components
that make up a Narvar offer including: annual bonus, equity, and benefits."

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior AI Engineer; Senior Software Developer, Rust; Senior Software
Engineer II, Data; Senior Software Engineer II, Distributed Systems; Senior Software Engineer II,
Machine Learning; Staff Software Engineer, Distributed Systems — all "Remote - Canada".
