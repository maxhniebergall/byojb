# Clariti (Clariti Cloud Inc.) — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:clariticloudinc | company_type: product

What they do: Govtech SaaS. Clariti sells community-development, permitting and licensing software to local and state governments across North America — "we empower governments to deliver exceptional citizen experiences." Three products: **Enterprise** (large local governments), **Launch** (small to mid-sized governments), and **CivCheck** (AI-assisted plan review). Named customers include Phoenix AZ and Orange County CA, plus state governments in Ohio, Michigan and Massachusetts. The product is delivered as **Salesforce managed packages installed into customer orgs**, supported by off-platform Java- and Node-based services.

How they describe themselves: Mission framing — "We envision a future where government services are as straightforward and user-friendly as the best consumer applications," and "Join our mission to provide governments with exceptional experiences so they can do the same for their communities." Their positioning themes are disruption / doing things differently, a refusal to accept that permitting software must be painful, configurable and user-friendly solutions, being a one-stop shop for community development, and acting as a supportive long-term partner. The JD states the culture values explicitly: team members **take ownership** over projects, tasks and outcomes; bring a **growth mindset** for continuous learning; **communicate courageously** in a direct but respectful way; and are **customer-focused**, keeping the customer at the heart of decision-making. They advertise a Great Place to Work certification (2024 and 2025 badges on the posting).

Size / stage / funding: Private, PE-backed, mid-size. Founded 2008 as **BasicGov** — one of the first cloud-based permitting/licensing solutions and the first on the Salesforce platform — acquired by investment firm **Explora Partners** in 2020 and rebranded to Clariti. Acquired **Camino Technologies** in 2023 to serve smaller governments. Raised **$18M from Vistara Growth** and **$10M in growth capital from CIBC Innovation Banking** (2024). Growth-capital / debt financing rather than large equity rounds suggests a reasonably capital-efficient business.

Locations / HQ: Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. No meaningful office requirement.

Remote policy: **Remote-first**, with employees working remotely across Canada and the United States. The JD adds: "Although we operate as a remote company, all roles are expected to participate in occasional travel for in-person company-wide or departmental meetings, typically **1–2 times per year**."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes — and Canada is in fact mandatory for this req.** The JD states in bold: "**Please note: for this role you must be living in and working out of Canada.**" The registry location is "CANADA (Remote)." Vancouver HQ means Pacific time, one hour off Mountain — an ideal overlap.

Engineering & tech: The senior role sits "at the intersection of Salesforce, distributed SaaS architecture, and off-platform services." Core stack: **Salesforce (Apex, LWC, APIs, security, governor limits, data modeling)** with managed-package and multi-org distribution concerns — package-safe patterns, versioning strategies and upgrade paths — plus **Java and Node.js** services, RESTful and asynchronous APIs, and event-driven integrations. Modern practice expectations: CI/CD, observability, testing strategies, system design. They explicitly encourage **AI-assisted development tools** (Copilot, Codex, Claude Code) to improve productivity and code quality, and list AI-assisted development experience as a bonus. Role includes leading technical design discussions and mentoring across Salesforce and backend domains.

Notable / other: Background checks are required for all successful candidates (customers trust them with sensitive information), scoped to global sanctions and employment-relevant criminal offences. Accommodation contact published (hr@claritisoftware.com).

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (CANADA, Remote).

Pay: The live Senior Software Engineer JD posts its own range — **CAD $103,000 – $160,000 base**, with the note "If you have questions about compensation as we move through the process, we're happy to discuss further." Benefits stated to depend on employment type.
