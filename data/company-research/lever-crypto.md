# Crypto.com — research
Provider/key: lever:crypto | company_type: product

What they do: Crypto.com is a global cryptocurrency exchange and financial-services platform (spot and derivatives trading, custody, a Visa prepaid card, DeFi wallet, NFT marketplace, and increasingly regulated brokerage/IRA products via Foris Capital US and Foris DAX Trust Company). Mission tagline: "Cryptocurrency in Every Wallet™". Customers are retail and institutional crypto traders worldwide; the postings in scope are for the internal Trading Infrastructure team building the exchange's front-to-back multi-asset trading platform.

How they describe themselves: The careers site states six values — **Think Clearly** ("clarity of thought to be the single most important trait"), **Be Resourceful** (find solutions with the resources at hand, but ask for help), **Stay Determined** (perseverance through challenges, with mutual support), **Dream Big** (take smart risks, pursue unprecedented goals), **Take Ownership** (empowered to make decisions and act as owners), and **Remain Humble** (company over ego, learn from mistakes). The tone is ambition-forward: an "open and dynamic culture" of "talented, interesting, ambitious, and humble people", with employees encouraged to "think and do things that have never been done before" and close access to executive leadership.

Size / stage / funding: Private, founded 2016 (Hong Kong / Singapore origins, Foris DAX group entities). ~4,000 employees as of mid-2023; user base reported over 100 million by mid-2024. In 2026 the company announced a **12% workforce reduction**, with CEO Kris Marszalek framing it around "integrating enterprise-wide AI". Still hiring heavily — roughly 860 open reqs listed publicly at the time of research.

Locations / HQ: Singapore-headquartered with major hubs in Hong Kong, London, Poland, Taiwan, and the US; the trading-platform reqs in the registry are geo-specific clones (Singapore, Hong Kong, UK/London, Poland, Taiwan, Canada, United States).

Remote policy: Predominantly **hybrid/onsite by hub**. The registry's location strings for these reqs are explicit: Singapore "onsite"; Hong Kong, London, and Canada all "hybrid". Only one req — "Senior Java Developer (Trading Platform)", United States — is marked "remote". No async-first or remote-first language anywhere on the careers site.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Partial / weak.** There is a Canada-designated req ("Senior Java Developer (Exchange/Trading Platform), Canada" and "Senior Java Developer (Trading Platform), Canada"), so the company can employ in Canada — but both are tagged **hybrid**, not remote, which conflicts with a fully-remote requirement from Kimberley BC. Their offices are not in BC. The only fully-remote req is US-based. Verdict: can hire in Canada, but not verified as remote-in-Canada.

Engineering & tech: The Trading Infrastructure team builds "a high-performance, front-to-back Trading Platform that supports multi-asset trading … low-latency execution, robust risk controls, and seamless integration across trading, risk, operations, and finance workflows." Modular architecture: market-data feeds, order gateways, execution algorithms, risk engines, UI dashboards, middle-office reconciliation, account infrastructure. Stated emphasis on "event-driven, deterministic system design, real-time observability, and strong security." Stack: **Java (low-latency), Python, React/Ag-Grid, Aeron, ClickHouse, Kubernetes**, modern CI/CD; AI-assisted development tooling is used across the team. This is genuinely deep infrastructure work — Aeron + ClickHouse + deterministic event-driven design is real low-latency systems engineering, not CRUD.

Posted compensation (from their own live JDs): Senior Java Developer (Trading Platform), US remote — $100,000–$230,000 USD. Senior Java Developer (Trading Platform), Canada — up to $230,000 CAD (lower bound not stated). Senior DevOps Engineer (Exchange/Trading Platforms), United Kingdom — up to £80,000.

Notable / other: Heavily regulated and regulator-exposed business; the crypto sector's cyclicality is the dominant stability risk. The 2026 12% layoff is recent and material. Brand/marketing spend is large (arena naming rights, sports sponsorships), which is a product/growth signal at the company level even though the trading-platform roles themselves are infrastructure.

Open relevant roles (sample): "(Senior) Cloud Infrastructure Engineer / System & Cloud Engineer"; "Senior DevOps Engineer (Exchange /Trading Platforms)" (HK, Poland, Singapore, Taiwan, UK variants); "Senior Java Developer (Exchange/Trading Platform), Canada"; "Senior Java Developer (Trading Platform)" (US remote); "Trading Java Developer".
