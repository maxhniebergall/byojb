# GTP Software Inc. ("Stratus") — research
Provider/key: rippling:gtp-software-inc | company_type: product

**Entity note (read first):** the registry name is "Gtp Software Inc", but every word of the company's own JD text brands the product and team as **Stratus**. GTP Software Inc. appears to be the legal/ATS entity behind the Stratus product; the JD never uses the GTP name. No independent confirmation was possible — WebSearch was exhausted for this session and no company domain appears anywhere in the registry (the only URLs on record are Rippling ATS board links, which are client-rendered and not fetchable). So: **nothing below is verified against a company website — it is all sourced from the JD body already in the registry.** Also note "Stratus" is a heavily-colliding name (there is an unrelated Stratus Technologies in fault-tolerant servers); do not merge this record with any other Stratus without checking.

What they do: Stratus sells MEP-specific (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) construction software — a "software layer an MEP Contractor needs to optimize profits with true 'Data Driven Contracting'." In their words the product "offers an advanced set of MEP specific solutions that seamlessly layer across a contractor's entire workflow from design to fabrication to installation", covering piping, plumbing, sheet metal and electrical contracting. Customers are MEP contractors, described as "some of the most innovative and largest MEP contractors". The name derives "from the Latin term meaning 'layer'". Adjacent ecosystem is Autodesk/BIM: AutoCAD, Revit, Fabrication, BIM 360 Docs, Forge are all listed as nice-to-have familiarity, as is modernizing legacy ASP.NET MVC applications.

How they describe themselves: "Our team of seasoned industry experts, skilled technology leaders, innovators, and entrepreneurs understands that fabrication does not occur in isolation." The architectural philosophy stated for the AI platform is an explicit set of principles: "Compounding Intelligence" ("every AI and agentic project deepens the domain substrate that makes the platform defensible"); "simplify the core, customize at the edge"; "architect agentic UX and enable just-in-time interfaces — the platform generates tailored views; we do not over-invest in heavy custom UI work"; and "Foundational stability. Drive reliability and the architectural foundation customers can trust for production AI workloads." The senior architecture role is framed as "a player-coach role. Most time is spent hands-on." No general company values/culture statement was available (no fetchable site).

Size / stage / funding: Unverified. The only signal is a nice-to-have qualification — "Prior experience in a Series B / growth-stage company navigating the transition from product-market fit to scale" — which strongly implies Stratus is itself a Series B / growth-stage private company. Headcount, revenue, profitability and investors are all unknown.

Locations / HQ: Not stated anywhere in available material (unverified). US-based, per E-Verify participation and the US-only hiring requirement.

Remote policy: "Primarily remote work with occasional annual team onsites." 20 days annual PTO, matching 401k, health benefits.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — explicitly excluded.** The JD states verbatim: "This is a remote role, but candidates must be based in the U.S." The posting footer likewise says "Remote (United States)", and the company participates in E-Verify with I-9 work-authorization verification post-hire. A Kimberley, BC candidate is ineligible; this is a hard, stated bar, not an inference.

Engineering & tech: Polyglot AI/ML stack on a .NET foundation. Python plus "the modern AI/ML stack (LangChain, LangGraph, PyTorch, sklearn, vector stores, embedding pipelines)"; C#/.NET and **Azure** for integration with the existing Stratus platform; RAG, fine-tuning, prompt engineering, model training and eval-harness design; agent frameworks (LangGraph, AutoGen, CrewAI, Semantic Kernel) in production; vector search, embeddings pipelines, model lifecycle, MLOps. They are building "agent-friendly platform surfaces (MCP, Agent, or what fits at the time)" and treating AI agents as "first-class consumers: MCP servers, agent-facing APIs, retrieval-grounded interfaces". Serious emphasis on evaluation and observability: "evaluation frameworks measuring model quality, agent reliability, drift, and platform effectiveness. Make AI workloads observable to engineering and product." Organizationally the platform is split into pillars — "Intelligence" and "Agentic Workflows" versus a "Fabricate" pillar — with a Senior Data Architect owning the canonical data layer and an AI/ML Engineering Lead owning capability execution; architects coordinate across pillars. Standards are set "through working reference implementations, in-repo templates, and patterns AI/ML teams can copy." Security architecture and threat modeling for AI systems (prompt injection, data exfiltration, agent abuse) is a listed nice-to-have. There is legacy ASP.NET MVC to modernize.

Notable / other: The AI/ML Architect role expects "10+ years software engineering experience, with 5+ years in Principal, Staff+, or Architect roles at a B2B SaaS or AI/ML platform company" and a "track record of leading AI/ML and agentic architecture across multiple teams or product lines", plus direct customer engagement and mentoring of senior engineers. A second posting, "Senior Software Engineer with AI plus Revit" (US Remote), is on record but is no longer live.

Open relevant roles (sample): Principal or Senior AI/ML Architect (Remote, United States); Senior Software Engineer with AI plus Revit (US Remote, no longer live).
