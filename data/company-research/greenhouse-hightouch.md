# Hightouch — research

Provider/key: greenhouse:hightouch | company_type: product

What they do: Hightouch is an "Agentic Marketing Platform powered by the industry-leading
Composable CDP" — it activates a company's customer data out of the warehouse (Snowflake,
Databricks, BigQuery) into the marketing tools that act on it, and layers AI agents on top. In
their own framing: "With complete brand context, customer data, and performance history in one
place, every marketer finally has the power to build and ship end-to-end campaigns themselves."
The platform lets organizations build audiences, sync data, personalize experiences in real time,
and orchestrate campaigns. Their AI agent product is pitched internally as "Cursor for Marketers".
Founded 2019, headquartered in San Francisco.

How they describe themselves: Seven stated values, quoted from their careers page —
1. **Forever Hungry**: "We are hungry and ambitious. We celebrate our accomplishments, but we're never fully satisfied."
2. **Kindness**: "People who aren't kind aren't tolerated — it's just not worth it."
3. **Efficient Execution**: "Speed matters. We don't have time for endless deliberation — most decisions are two-way doors."
4. **Compassion**: "We listen to everyone and try to put ourselves in their shoes, regardless of our initial reaction."
5. **Impact Driven**: "Everyone should be intrinsically motivated by business impact."
6. **Raising the Bar**: "We have high expectations for performance and believe in exceptional talent in every position."
7. **Humility**: "We are humble. Listening is mission critical — we are open to others' perspectives and ideas."

They describe the company as "a small team of kind and talented people" in a "fast paced
environment that values creative thinking and innovation." Their JDs repeat the framing: "The team
is ambitious, impact-driven, efficient — and we believe humility, kindness, and compassion are
essential to our success. If you're energized by velocity, obsessed with raising the bar, and want
to build alongside people who care deeply about each other and our customers, we'd love to meet
you."

Size / stage / funding: Series D. 803 paying customers. Investors include **Databricks**, ICONIQ,
Sapphire Ventures, Goldman Sachs, **Snowflake Ventures**, Capital One, Amplify Partners, HubSpot
Ventures and Bain Capital Ventures — notably strategic money from both major warehouse vendors.
Headcount not disclosed on their own pages ("a small team"). Profitability not disclosed. They
state their streaming products' revenue "has 10x'd over the past year" and that a newly added
real-time computation engine is "seeing 7-figure market response in the first few months."

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco. Offices in San Francisco, New York City, London, plus five or
more additional locations globally. Hiring is described as remote across North America, EMEA and
APAC.

Remote policy: **Remote-first, and unusually concrete about it.** Every relevant engineering JD
states the salary "is location independent in accordance with our remote-first policy" — i.e. no
geographic pay banding, which is a strong first-party remote-first signal rather than a slogan.
All eight relevant open engineering roles are posted as "Remote (North America)".

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Likely yes, but not stated in so many words.** The evidence: all
relevant postings are "Remote (North America)" (Canada is in North America), the careers page lists
remote hiring across North America, and pay is explicitly location-independent. What is missing is
an explicit "we hire in Canada" / list-of-eligible-countries statement or a Canadian entity, and
no CAD pay figures appear anywhere. Worth confirming with a recruiter before investing in an
application, but nothing on their pages excludes Canada.

Engineering & tech: Deep streaming and distributed-systems work behind a marketing-facing product.
The Streaming Systems team runs "the high-throughput systems that collect, forward, and perform
realtime computation on billions of behavioral events from our customer's users" — ingesting
clickstream, product-interaction and transactional data. Stated scale requirement: "millions of
messages per second at single second latency end to end." Named open problems include "improving
caching strategies for our online computation service", "architecting a system that can deduplicate
message delivery at 1,000,000 messages / second", and "patterns for elegant and zero-downtime
deployments in our online computation service." Other teams: Control Plane, Customer Studio
Backend, Native Delivery, Distributed Systems, AI Agents, AI Productivity. Autonomy is emphasized:
"We believe in enabling our engineers to do their best work for our customers by giving them
extremely high levels of ownership and autonomy. You will own and deliver projects from start to
finish, work directly with customers to solve their hardest scaling problems, and have significant
influence over our technical roadmap." Several roles are explicitly greenfield: "We are building
new products from scratch, so we need someone who enjoys greenfield work and leading other
engineers."

Notable / other: Named a **Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Customer Data
Platforms**. Enterprise customer list: Spotify, DoorDash, Domino's, Grammarly, PetSmart, Warner
Music Group, Autotrader, Cars.com, Ramp, Aritzia. Equity is a meaningful part of the package and
the terms are employee-friendly by startup standards: ISO options with **early exercise and a
10-year post-termination exercise window**.

Compensation (posted, first-party): Hightouch posts an identical, very wide, location-independent
band of **$180,000 – $400,000 USD base** on essentially every engineering JD, from Software
Engineer through Staff and Principal — including Principal Engineer, Streaming Systems; Software
Engineer, Control Plane; Customer Studio Backend; Distributed Systems; Native Delivery; Streaming
Systems; and Staff Engineer, AI Productivity. Their wording: "The base salary range for this
position is $180,000 - $400,000 USD per year, which is location independent in accordance with our
remote-first policy. A large portion of the compensation will be in the form of equity via ISO
options." Because the same range is pasted across all levels, it functions as a company-wide
envelope rather than a level-specific band — the real level signal is where in the range an offer
lands, which they do not disclose.

Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Engineer, Streaming Systems; Software Engineer, Streaming
Systems; Software Engineer, Distributed Systems; Software Engineer, Control Plane; Software
Engineer, Customer Studio Backend; Software Engineer, Native Delivery; Software Engineer, AI
Agents; Staff Engineer, AI Productivity — all "Remote (North America)".
