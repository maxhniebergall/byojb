# GHGSat — research
Provider/key: workable:ghgsat | company_type: product

What they do: GHGSat is a global emissions-intelligence company providing site-level greenhouse-gas (primarily methane, also CO₂) detection, measurement and monitoring. In its own words it "offers greenhouse gas detection, measurement, and monitoring services to industrial and government customers around the world. We use our own satellites and aircraft sensors, combined with third-party data, to help industrial emitters better understand, control, and reduce their emissions." Its differentiator: "GHGSat's capability is unique: we provide high-resolution, local measurements of atmospheric methane and carbon dioxide concentration from space." Product lines are DATA.SAT (satellite), DATA.AIR (airborne sensors) and DATA.GS (third-party global survey data). Customers span oil & gas, landfills, government, finance, mining and agriculture.

How they describe themselves: Positioning centres on **independence and repeatability** of measurement — "independent, repeatable" data that lets customers make confident operational and regulatory decisions, supporting ESG reporting and climate action. Careers copy is mission-forward and candid about scale: "This is a rare opportunity to work on urgent problems. You'll join a small but high-impact team and help scale a company doing meaningful work for the planet." They also state "GHGSat offers a creative and highly motivating work environment" with "flexible work arrangements, and opportunities for ongoing development."

Size / stage / funding: Private, Canadian, venture/growth-backed; exact headcount and funding not disclosed on the pages reachable. Scale markers they publish: active across 127 countries; 702 MtCO₂e of methane emissions detected in 2025; 21 MtCO₂e mitigation facilitated since inception. Recognised on the Deloitte Fast 500 and Deloitte Fast 50 Canada lists — a growth-stage signal. Careers copy describes engineering as "a small but high-impact team".

Locations / HQ: **Headquartered in Canada (Montreal)**, with offices across North America, Europe and the Middle East. Listed phone is a Montreal (+1 438) number.

Engineering & tech: Backend/data work on the **Payload Planner** — satellite planning and tasking software — reporting to the Mission Platform lead within a cross-functional Agile Scrum team. Responsibilities include building tools for satellite planning/tasking workflows; evaluating, deploying and maintaining databases, data-transformation frameworks and APIs; optimising data structures, schema and queries; and maintaining high availability of the GHGSat data platform, with system architecture designed "collectively with the team, revisit[ed] regularly". Stack: Linux, Python, PostgreSQL, MongoDB; scalable backend services and APIs — REST/GraphQL, event-driven architecture, microservices, cloud deployment; Docker and Kubernetes nice-to-have; TypeScript/React nice-to-have. Assets: atmospheric science / remote sensing / satellite operations interest, GIS and mapping (Mapbox, Leaflet, GeoJSON, GeoTIFF), and bilingual French/English. Requires a BSc/MSc in CS or software engineering and 5+ years' experience, and "ability to work autonomously, with minimum direction."
