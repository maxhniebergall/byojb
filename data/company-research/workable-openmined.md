# OpenMined — research
Provider/key: workable:openmined | company_type: product (non-profit foundation)

What they do: The **OpenMined Foundation** is a **501(c)(3) non-profit** building open-source privacy-enhancing technology (PETs). Its core idea is a network in which "data stays where it lives" — enabling researchers and auditors to get answers from non-public data without obtaining a copy of it or direct access to it. In practice this is AI-governance and AI-safety infrastructure: letting external researchers study models and datasets held by large organisations under privacy and IP constraints. Their flagship open-source work is the PySyft/SyftBox line of tooling, used by leading organisations worldwide.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "Create the public network for non-public information." Stated values, verbatim from their careers page: **Mission-Driven, Never Give Up, Teamwork, Ingenuity, Candor, Pragmatism, Humility.** They pitch the work as collaborating "with talented engineers, researchers, and advocates from around the world who are shaping the future of privacy-preserving AI," with engaging projects, experienced mentors, and contributions to widely-used open-source software.

Size / stage / funding: Non-profit, **donation-funded** — the careers page itself solicits one-time gifts, monthly giving and major-gift enquiries alongside the job listings. Community of 16,000+ members worldwide, but the paid staff org is small. No published headcount, budget or endowment. Financially this is the least stable profile in the batch: grant- and donation-dependent rather than revenue-generating.

Locations / HQ: Fully distributed, no HQ. Contributors worldwide.

Remote policy: **Fully remote**, with a hard timezone constraint: current postings state **"ET ± 1hr."**

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear-to-negative for this candidate.** Both live relevant postings in the registry are listed as **"United States (Remote)"**, and the careers page never addresses Canada. Even setting the country question aside, the stated **ET ± 1hr** window excludes Mountain Time by a clear margin (MT is ET − 2). A Kimberley, BC candidate would need both a country exception and a two-hour timezone exception — treat as effectively ineligible unless they say otherwise.

Engineering & tech: Open-source Python privacy/ML infrastructure — federated and remote data science, differential privacy, secure enclaves and data-governance tooling. Stack is not published on the careers page beyond the open-source repositories themselves. Roles on offer skew senior/staff with a QA-lead component.

Notable / other: No compensation philosophy or salary ranges are disclosed anywhere on their site or postings; non-profit scale means pay should be assumed well below commercial fintech/media benchmarks. Also runs a placements programme (placements.openmined.org) and a large volunteer/community pipeline, so much of the "16,000 members" figure is unpaid community rather than staff.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer (United States, Remote); Staff QA Software Engineer (United States, Remote).
