# Revic — research
Provider/key: ashby:revic | company_type: product

What they do: Revic sells an "AI-native sales acceleration engine" — software that runs AI agents
against a company's revenue data to tell sales reps who to sell to and to fire off workflows on their
behalf. In their words: "Revic is an AI-native sales acceleration engine built to uplift sales
professionals. We handle the complexity: analyzing data, identifying opportunities, triggering the
right workflows, so reps can focus on the human side of sales." And: "Our platform learns what's
working, defines who to sell to, and gives reps the context they need to move with confidence and
precision." Buyers are enterprise revenue/sales organisations. Category: GTM / revenue-operations AI.

How they describe themselves: Mission framing is sales-empathy — "Great sellers are the lifeblood of
every business… too often, they're buried under administrative work, disconnected tools, and
processes that pull them away from what they do best: selling. We believe salespeople deserve
better." Closing thesis: "Sales will always be human. Revic exists to make sure it stays that way, by
letting AI handle everything else."

A formal values list was not obtainable, but the JD contains an unusually explicit self-description
of working norms, quoted verbatim because it is the most informative thing on record:

  "This is not for you if: You need perfect requirements before starting · You're looking for
  work-life balance (we offer work-life integration) · You want to work on one narrow piece of the
  stack · You prefer meetings over shipping"

  "This is for you if: You want to look back in 5 years and say 'Damn! I built the platform that
  powered all of that' · You're ready to work harder than you've ever worked and be challenged well
  beyond your comfort zone · You believe great infrastructure is invisible to the people who depend
  on it · You want equity that could actually change your life"

Other cultural self-descriptions from the same page: "You're energized by debugging hard problems
under pressure", "You're Intellectually Honest — you question everything, including your own
assumptions", "Ship daily, learn instantly", "Uncapped growth, the kind that comes from building, not
waiting."

Size / stage / funding: UNVERIFIED — no funding, headcount or stage is stated and no corporate page
was reachable this session. The signals point to early-stage: equity is pitched as the headline
upside ("meaningful equity — we're not another 0.001% shop", "equity that could actually change your
life"), the platform team is being hired for the first time ("Own the platform… from the ground up",
"Work directly with engineering leadership"), and quarterly all-hands offsites imply a small company.

Locations / HQ: HQ not stated. The Platform Engineer posting lists **Vancouver, New York, San
Francisco** — three named cities, no "Remote" tag. Quarterly in-person offsites: "we trade slack for
sunshine, every quarter we meet in-person."

Remote policy: Not stated as remote. The posting names three offices/hubs rather than a remote
designation, and the company commits to quarterly in-person gatherings. There is no async language
anywhere; the stated cadence is the opposite ("Ship daily", "debugging hard problems under pressure").

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, leaning no.** Vancouver is one of the three listed locations,
so a Canadian employment relationship is plausible — but the posting reads as location-anchored
(city list, no remote qualifier), and Vancouver is a ~10-hour drive from Kimberley, so a
Vancouver-hub expectation is not workable without relocation. No statement about hiring remotely
within Canada exists in their own text. Do not treat as remote-Canada-eligible on current evidence.

Engineering & tech: The role itself is real infrastructure work. Scope: "Design, build, and maintain
the cloud infrastructure and developer platform"; "Own CI/CD pipelines, deployment automation, and
environment management"; "Build and operate observability, monitoring, and alerting systems";
"Improve developer experience by reducing friction in local development, testing, and production
debugging"; "Champion infrastructure-as-code, automated testing, and operational best practices."
Stack: AWS/GCP/Azure, Docker and Kubernetes, Terraform or Pulumi, Python/Bash/Go scripting, with
"Familiarity with data infrastructure (Spark, Databricks, Airflow, or similar)" as a strong plus.
Bar: 5+ years operating cloud infrastructure. The stated technical problem is genuinely interesting:
"How do you build infrastructure that supports thousands of concurrent AI agent runs, each pulling
from dozens of data sources, while keeping latency low, costs reasonable, and reliability high?" They
claim their agents "process terabytes of data, orchestrate dozens of integrations, and make real-time
decisions that impact enterprise revenue pipelines."

Notable / other: Benefits stated are thin and generic — "Competitive base + meaningful equity",
"Premium health, dental, vision", quarterly offsite. No PTO policy, no parental leave, no retirement
contribution, no learning budget mentioned. The absence of a stated pay range is itself notable given
the role lists San Francisco and New York, both pay-transparency jurisdictions.

no pay posted.
