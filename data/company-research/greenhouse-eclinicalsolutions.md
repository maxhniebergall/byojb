# eClinical Solutions — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:eclinicalsolutions | company_type: product

What they do: eClinical Solutions is a clinical-trial data software and services company. Its own description: "a global leader in clinical data intelligence, delivering the elluminate® Clinical Data Cloud® — an AI-powered platform driving agentic data review, SDTM automation, and RBQM for 18 of the top 50 pharmaceutical companies worldwide." Elsewhere: "helping life sciences organizations unify, analyze, and unlock the value of their data faster than ever before. By combining a modern cloud platform with expert data services, we empower smarter decisions across the clinical trial lifecycle." So the business is two-sided: a SaaS data platform (elluminate) plus a professional data-services arm around it — but the engineering roles in the registry are product engineering on the platform, not client consulting.

How they describe themselves: "Our engineering teams build enterprise-grade, revenue-generating products at the intersection of cloud, data, analytics, and emerging AI technologies. If you're excited about building sophisticated software that makes a real-world impact on healthcare, this is the place to do it." They repeat a career-growth refrain across postings: "Accelerate your skills and career within a fast-growing company while impacting the future of healthcare" and "Take the first step towards your dream career."
There is a named internal AI program: **"our eNOVA AI-First transformation"** — "we are embedding AI across every pillar of clinical development and enterprise operations — compressing cycle times, improving data quality, and redefining how work gets done." The AI engineering function is described as deliberately small and delivery-focused: "builder-first (70–80% hands-on development)… within a small, high-output team (2–4 engineers)… This is not a program management or oversight role. You are expected to build, ship, and iterate continuously." Operating cadence is stated bluntly: "Deliver continuous output with weekly or bi-weekly releases" and "Operate with minimal process and high accountability in a fast-paced environment."

Size / stage / funding: Private and **PE-backed** — the AI role lists "Experience in high-growth or PE-backed SaaS environments" as a preferred qualification, which is effectively a self-description. Customer proof point: 18 of the top 50 pharmaceutical companies. Headcount, revenue and investor identity are not stated in the sources read and were not verified.

Engineering & tech: Two distinct role shapes.
- **Principal AI Engineer – Automation & AI**: agentic AI workflows and enterprise automation across R&D, Engineering, Professional Services, IT, Finance, Sales and Marketing. Builds "multi-agent workflows, RAG-based applications, document intelligence and summarization pipelines, workflow and process automation." Frameworks named: LangChain, LangGraph, LlamaIndex "or equivalents"; assistant tooling: Codex, Claude Code, Gemini, NotebookLM. Python, API development and system integration, cloud (AWS, Azure or GCP). Owns "prompt engineering strategies, orchestration logic, API integrations and data pipelines, testing, validation, and monitoring frameworks," plus reusable prompt templates, agent design patterns and integration utilities, and lightweight standards for security, responsible AI and evaluation/performance monitoring. Preferred: RAG architectures and vector databases, AI evaluation frameworks, guardrails and monitoring; regulated-environment experience; clinical data / CDISC standards. Reports into the CTAO. 6–10+ years experience.
- **Principal Software Engineer** (posted as Principal *Full Stack* Software Engineer in the body): Microsoft stack — "expert-level proficiency in C#, .NET, and ASP.NET (MVC/Web APIs)" plus "full-stack web applications (Angular, TypeScript, HTML, CSS)," Microsoft SQL Server with performance tuning, microservices and distributed systems, AWS preferred. 10+ years. Also carries AI integration work: "integrating LLMs and AI services into .NET- and Python-based systems," "agentic AI patterns (task orchestration, tool-using agents, workflow automation)," prompt engineering, evaluation and guardrails — with the explicit caveat "Deep ML expertise is not required — we're looking for strong engineers who understand how to apply AI capabilities in real products."
