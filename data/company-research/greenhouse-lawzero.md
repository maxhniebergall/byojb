# LawZero — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:lawzero | company_type: product (non-profit research organization)

What they do: LawZero is a Montreal-based non-profit AI safety research organization, in their words "committed to advancing research and creating technical solutions that enable safe-by-design AI systems." Its scientific direction comes from Professor Yoshua Bengio, and its programme is to build "non-agentic AI that learns primarily to understand the world rather than to act in it, giving truthful answers to questions based on transparent and externalized probabilistic reasoning" — a "Scientist AI" that is "disinterested" and "predict[s] the truth without pursuing goals of its own". Intended applications: accelerating scientific discovery, providing oversight for agentic AI systems, and advancing understanding of AI risk. It was incubated at Mila — Quebec AI Institute.

How they describe themselves: Tagline "Protecting human joy and endeavour." They describe themselves as "a nonprofit startup" deliberately "insulated from market pressures that often prioritize speed of deployment over safety and trustworthiness", and state that "AI should be cultivated as a global public good—developed and used safely towards human flourishing." On culture: "At LawZero, diversity is important to us. We value a work environment that is fair, open and respectful of differences. We welcome applications from highly qualified individuals interested in working towards our mission in a respectful, inclusive and collaborative setting." Notably, none of the usual startup urgency vocabulary appears anywhere in their own copy or JDs.

Size / stage / funding: Non-profit, launched 2025, still small (order of tens of staff). Philanthropically funded rather than venture-funded; named supporters include the Gates Foundation, Future of Life Institute, Founders Pledge, Coefficient Giving, Schmidt Sciences, Silicon Valley Community Foundation, and NVIDIA. Not revenue-dependent, but grant-dependent.

Locations / HQ: Montreal, Quebec. Their own benefits copy describes "our vibrant office space in the heart of Little Italy, in the trendy Mile-Ex district, close to public transportation" — the Mile-Ex AI cluster next to Mila.

Remote policy: No remote policy is published, and the signals point the other way. All current engineering and research postings are located "Montreal", and the benefits list sells the physical office as a perk. Treat as onsite/hybrid Montreal.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No / effectively negative.** Canada-based, yes — but the roles are Montreal-located, in-office-flavoured, and Eastern Time. Nothing in the postings or on lawzero.org offers remote or distributed work. For a Kimberley BC candidate who cannot relocate, this is disqualifying in practice, not merely a mild concern. Would need explicit confirmation from them that a fully-remote BC arrangement is possible before spending any effort.

Engineering & tech: The relevant role, "Senior ML Data Platform Developer", is a strong infrastructure charter: "architect, implement, scale, and maintain the data engine powering our next-generation frontier models", explicitly "treating the data platform as an internal product with our researchers as your primary customers". Responsibilities include a "multi-tiered, ultra-low-latency storage architecture", "automated, petabyte-scale data processing pipelines", efficient GPU utilisation for data processing, and dataset versioning/reproducibility/traceability with datasheets under internal Data Governance policies. Named technologies: Lustre, Ray, Apache Spark, workflow orchestrators, Apache Arrow, Parquet, Kubernetes, SLURM, Docker, Enroot, vector databases. Asks 5+ years and a bachelor's degree (required). They state "Our technical environment is not fixed and will evolve… We expect someone capable of evolving it, not only following industry trends, challenging it, and making sustainable decisions in close collaboration with our Research and Product teams."

Notable / other: Stated benefits are unusually concrete and stability-flavoured for an AI lab: "Comprehensive health benefits (including mental health and wellness management account)"; "20 days of vacation per year upon start"; "Employer contribution of 4% to your retirement savings, with no required employee match"; "Additional compensation totaling 8% of your salary to apply towards additional retirement savings or bonuses (independent of group and individual performance)" — i.e. a non-performance-contingent 8%, which is a deliberate anti-pressure design. No salary figure is posted on any req.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior ML Data Platform Developer (Montreal); ML Research Scientist, probabilistic inference (Montreal).
