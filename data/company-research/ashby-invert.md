# Invert — research
Provider/key: ashby:invert | company_type: product

Entity check: The JD's self-description (bioprocess data for biopharma, "at invert") matches the registry name. The word "invert" collides with many unrelated companies; this is the bioprocess/biomanufacturing data platform, confirmed from the JD text itself, not from any external source.

What they do: A data platform for biopharmaceutical process development and manufacturing. Their words: "Drug development is one of the most data-intensive endeavors in human history. Yet scientists spend more time finding and cleaning data than actually using it, slowing down drugs that should reach patients faster and more affordably. Biopharma has seen billions invested in software. Almost none of it was built for the scientists actually running experiments." The technical problem they name is squarely a data-engineering one: "Bioprocess data is high-frequency, multi-source, and inconsistent. It's spread across dozens of systems that are rarely connected or clean. We make it usable." On top of the pipeline sits an LLM agent: "our AI agent gives scientists something they've never had: the ability to query their full experimental history, catch deviations in real time, and make decisions that inform million-dollar manufacturing outcomes." They also state an explicit reliability posture: "We think carefully about model behavior, reliability, and what it means for AI to be trustworthy in a regulated industry. Every engineer here owns that problem."

How they describe themselves: Emphatically a high-ownership, high-velocity startup. "This is a place for builders — people who want to own hard problems, ship fast, and work with others who hold themselves to a high bar." Their stated competencies, verbatim: "Ownership: We all own the results... People who succeed here don't wait for orders, they get to work."; "Strong Communication: In a remote environment, communication can be the difference between a project finishing in days instead of weeks."; "Adaptable: This is a fast paced environment and plans change when they need to."; "AI-First: At Invert, we're committed to making sure every team member benefits from how the software industry is changing. Adopting AI isn't optional — it's a shared responsibility and a growth opportunity."; "Ambitious: Intrinsically motivated, driven to succeed."; "Mentors effectively." Their hiring bar, verbatim: "Deep backend and system design instincts, you've built things at scale"; "Strong opinions on code quality, held loosely"; "You actively seek out new AI tools... and have a point of view on what good AI product looks and feels like"; "You don't wait to be told what to own, you identify it and go"; "You over-communicate in writing because you know silence creates drag"; "You find the hard problems fun."

Size / stage / funding: Self-described "High-growth startup". Round, valuation and headcount are not disclosed in the JD and were not verified. The presence of both a Head of Engineering and multiple founders in the interview loop, plus separate EU and North American engineering teams, suggests a small but structured organization rather than a handful of people.

Locations / HQ: No HQ stated; the company runs distributed teams "across US and European timezones", with separate "North America" and "EU" engineering reqs.

Remote policy: Fully remote, first-party: "Fully remote, work with great people distributed across US and European timezones". Communication is explicitly written-first ("You over-communicate in writing because you know silence creates drag"). Other stated package items: "Competitive salary, equity, and benefits"; "Unlimited credits on your chosen AI coding tools (Claude Code, Devin, Codex, etc.)"; "New laptop, monitor, and accessories of your choice"; "Frequent team offsites"; "Unlimited PTO".

Remote-Canada eligibility: Verified YES. The registry location for the North America posting is "Remote, Canada, United States" — Canada is named explicitly — and the JD confirms a fully-remote distributed model. Timezone caveat: the team spans "US and European timezones", so the centre of gravity skews eastward; Mountain Time is the far western edge and morning overlap with EU colleagues would be limited. That is a scheduling friction, not an eligibility problem.

Engineering & tech: Stated stack, verbatim: "Backend: Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL"; "Frontend: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Vercel"; "Infrastructure: Terraform, AWS"; "AI: Bedrock, Modal, LiteLLM"; "Streaming data: Kinesis, Greengrass". The Greengrass/Kinesis pairing indicates edge-to-cloud ingestion from lab and manufacturing equipment — genuine high-frequency streaming data engineering. LiteLLM plus Bedrock is a model-routing/serving posture that maps closely to prior unified-inference-API work. Role scope: "ship new features, maintain existing ones, and push our technical [bar] towards excellence... you will deliver on this mission through both hands-on delivery and project leadership."

Regulatory context: Biopharma manufacturing is a regulated environment (GxP), which the JD acknowledges — "what it means for AI to be trustworthy in a regulated industry". That implies validation, audit trails and change control obligations around the platform.

Hiring process (stated, unusually transparent): five stages — 30-minute discovery with the hiring manager; a 90-minute technical coding challenge with two employees; a 60-minute technical leadership working session with two employees; a 30-minute founder chat; a 30-minute Head of Engineering chat. "We aim to complete all stages within two weeks."

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (North America) — Remote, Canada/United States; Senior Software Engineer (EU).
