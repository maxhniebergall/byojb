# Moxie — research
Provider/key: ashby:moxie | company_type: product
What they do: Moxie is a SaaS platform for aesthetic-medicine entrepreneurs ("MedSpas"). Its "Moxie Suite" handles scheduling, medical purchasing, payments and invoicing, bookkeeping and analytics end-to-end for independent practices. Their JD states they support "700+ practices nationwide" (US) and grew "from an idea to a global, remote-first team" in a few years.
How they describe themselves: Purpose — "to unlock sustainable success for aesthetic entrepreneurs, at every stage of their journey"; they "empower ambitious aesthetic entrepreneurs to build profitable, independent practices—without burnout, overwhelm, or guesswork." Stated values, verbatim: "act as owners, give more than we take, move with speed and care, and simplify and learn every day." They self-describe as "a fast-growing company focused on building reliable infrastructure, strong developer experience, and operational excellence as we scale."
