# Nerdy (Varsity Tutors) — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:nerdy | company_type: product

What they do: Online learning marketplace and platform. From their own JD: "At Nerdy (NYSE: NRDY) — the company behind Varsity Tutors — we're redrawing the blueprint of learning. Our Live + AI™ platform fuses real-time human expertise with proprietary generative-AI systems, setting a new bar for measurable academic impact at global scale." Customers are students, educators and institutions; the product is live tutoring plus AI-assisted classroom experiences.

How they describe themselves: The JD is unusually explicit about culture, and the wording matters. Under "How we compete" they list:
- "**AI-Native at every level** — From the CEO to day-one hires, everyone builds and ships with generative AI. If you're not wielding AI, you're not done."
- "**Entrepreneurial velocity** — Move at founder speed, prototype in hours, and measure in real user outcomes. Slow teams die."
- "**Free-market rig[our]**" (text truncated in the local body).
Elsewhere: "We recruit the kind of technologists and operators you'd bet on as solo founders — people who turn ambiguous problems into shipping code, iterate faster than markets move, and compound their advantage with every data point. In an era where great employees can deliver 10-times the leverage of the merely good, we back those who play to win." Closing line: "Fortune favors the bold. Join us."

Size / stage / funding: Public (NYSE: NRDY). Not an early startup, but a small-cap consumer-education company. Headcount and financial position not verified from a first-party page this session.

Locations / HQ: St. Louis, Missouri (unverified from a fetched page). The open engineering reqs are entirely offshore-remote.

Remote policy: Remote, but geographically restricted to specific non-North-American markets and structured as contracting. Every relevant posting is titled "(Full-time Contractor)" or names an Indian city, with locations "Remote - Brazil", "Remote - Colombia", "Remote - Costa Rica", "Remote - Mexico", "Remote - Chile", "Remote - Bengaluru", "Remote - Hyderabad", "Remote - Pune". The JD states plainly: "We are looking for Senior Software Engineers in LATAM."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — verified negative.** All 20 relevant reqs on record are LATAM or India, and the offshore roles are contractor engagements rather than employment. There is no Canada-eligible opening, and no Canadian or US employment req in the registry at all.

Engineering & tech: JavaScript/TypeScript plus "a core Object-Oriented Programming language like Java/C++/C#", "a complete CI/CD software lifecycle and an evolving services-oriented architecture hosted in AWS". The stated differentiator is agentic coding-tool fluency: "Proficiency in utilizing various Agentic coding tools to significantly amplify impact and accelerate implementations is essential for this role." The described work is product surface — "building a seamless class discovery flow, improving accessibility and performance, or integrating AI into real-time classroom experiences" and "fast, elegant, and responsive interfaces" — i.e. frontend/product engineering with AI features, not AI infrastructure.

Notable / other: Despite the "AI-Native" branding, no posting on record describes model serving, retrieval infrastructure, evals or agent runtimes; "AI-Native" here means engineers use AI coding tools, not that they build AI platforms. No compensation is stated on any of the 20 reqs.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (AI-Native) — Remote Brazil / Colombia / Mexico / Chile / Costa Rica / Bengaluru / Hyderabad / Pune; Staff Engineer (AI-Native) (Full time contractor, India).
