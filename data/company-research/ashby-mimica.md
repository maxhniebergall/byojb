# Mimica — research
Provider/key: ashby:mimica | company_type: product

What they do: Mimica ("Mimica Automation") sells AI-powered **task mining / process intelligence**. Its agent observes employee actions across the desktop and categorises them into detailed process maps; the product then highlights inefficiencies, prioritises improvements by ROI, recommends the right automation technology (RPA, intelligent document processing, GenAI), and produces a blueprint for building the automation. Customers are enterprises running process-transformation programmes.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "empower enterprises, teams, and individuals to reclaim their most precious resource — time and work more efficiently, with greater purpose and impact," and "drudgery should be left to machines; humans deserve better." Stated principles from the careers page: trust in each individual's abilities and a commitment to frequent, open communication; transparency and inclusion ("knowledge is accessible to all to make informed decisions"); "diversity is our strength"; feedback and growth through "open discussions and respectfully challenging each other's ideas"; and "going above and beyond for our customers."

Their stated approach to engineering (quoted from the backend JD): "We prioritize customer needs first · We work in small, project-based teams · We have flexibility in terms of the problems we work on · We own the full lifecycle of our projects · We avoid silos and encourage taking up tasks in new areas · We balance quality and velocity · We have a shared responsibility for our production code · We each set our own routine to maximize our productivity."

Size / stage / funding: Venture-backed scale-up (Entrepreneurs First portfolio origin); headcount and funding stage not published on their own pages. JDs describe an "early engineering team" and a "lean but highly capable" platform team, and say "Mimica is growing fast" — small-to-mid scale-up, not an established profitable company.

Locations / HQ: Distributed, UK roots. Team locations advertised include Vancouver and Montreal (Canada), plus San Francisco, San Diego, Austin, New York, Raleigh, Newport, and a broad European footprint (London, Warsaw, Amsterdam, Berlin, Lisbon, Dublin, Stockholm, Spain, Estonia, Czechia) and Brazil.

Remote policy: **Remote-first and distributed** — "Work from anywhere — fully remote, in our hubs, or a mix," with flexible schedules and location, company laptop, remote setup stipend and co-working budget. The backend JD states the constraint explicitly: "This is a fully remote position. You can be based anywhere in the UK, Americas, or Europe within a UTC-6 to UTC+1 timezone."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes.** Canada is listed directly among the eligible locations on the Staff/Lead Platform/DevOps JD, and Toronto on both backend JDs; Vancouver and Montreal appear as existing team locations on the careers page. Timezone caveat: the stated window is UTC-6 to UTC+1. Kimberley, BC is Mountain Time — UTC-6 in summer (MDT), UTC-7 in winter (MST) — so it sits at the extreme western edge and drops just outside the window during standard time. Worth confirming with them, but Canada eligibility itself is not in doubt.

Engineering & tech: Platform/DevOps side — GKE (multi-region), Terraform (migrating IaC to **Crossplane**), Terragrunt, ArgoCD/GitOps, GCP IAM (AWS acceptable), Honeycomb/Grafana/Loki/Alloy observability, Temporal, Python platform tooling, MongoDB/PostgreSQL/SQL Server, scheduled on-call rotation, GPU workloads on Kubernetes for ML infra, SOC 2 hardening. Current ambitious projects: multi-region and single-tenancy environments, BYOC (Bring Your Own Cloud) deployments, Terraform→Crossplane migration, and a FinOps push. Backend side — MERN/Node.js + TypeScript, Docker/K8s, real-time collaborative graph editing that feeds AI model retraining, APIs into the ML components; asks 8+ years.

Notable / other: Hiring process is published and deliberately time-respectful: recruiter screen (30–45 min), live triage & debug technical challenge (1 h), system design interview (1 h), behavioural interview. Benefits include generous comp + stock options "aligned with our internal framework, market data, and individual skills," ample PTO, enhanced parental leave, health & retirement, annual L&D budget, annual workaways. Security note on JDs: they only contact candidates from @mimica.ai addresses. Engineering off-sites (e.g. Bilbao).

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff/Lead Platform/DevOps Engineer (Europe/Canada/UK etc.); Staff/Senior Backend Engineer, Node.js (Toronto among locations); Staff/Lead Backend Engineer (FastAPI, High-Throughput) (Remote Anywhere / Toronto).
