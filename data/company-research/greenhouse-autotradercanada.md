# AutoTrader.ca (TRADER Corporation) — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:autotradercanada | company_type: product

What they do: In their own words, "We are a Canadian leader in digital automotive solutions." The business is a portfolio of four brands that "help Canadians buy, sell, and finance vehicles with confidence":
- **AutoTrader.ca** — Canada's largest automotive marketplace, with "over 25 million monthly visits."
- **AutoSync** — dealer software (operations, marketing, sales) serving "3,500+ dealers."
- **Dealertrack Canada** — the country's top automotive financing portal, "processing more than 6.5 million credit applications each year."
- **Collateral Management (CMS)** — a national platform for lien and registration services, recovery services, and insolvency management for Canadian lenders.
The legal entity is TRADER Corporation. Notably, the marketplace is only one part of the business; the CMS and Dealertrack lines are B2B financial/registry infrastructure for lenders, which is where most of the currently-open engineering roles sit.

How they describe themselves: The self-description is corporate and restrained — no values manifesto surfaced in the JDs, and no hustle or rocketship language either. The recurring framing is Canadian market leadership plus a European parent: "As part of **AutoScout24 group**, Europe's largest online car marketplace, we're shaping the future of automotive retail in Canada and beyond… Join us on our journey as we bring together the best of Europe and North America to shape the future of automotive retail."

Size / stage / funding: Private, and **owned by AutoScout24 group** (Europe's largest online car marketplace, itself Hellman & Friedman-backed). This is an established, revenue-generating, mature business rather than a venture-stage company — a marketplace with 25M monthly visits and a lender-facing SaaS arm processing 6.5M credit applications a year. Stability is a genuine strength here.

Locations / HQ: **Toronto, Ontario.** Two named office locations: 20 Queen St W and 405 West Mall. All eight currently-open relevant engineering roles are posted as "Toronto, ON (Canada)."

Remote policy: **Hybrid, office-anchored.** The company moved to a hybrid model at the start of 2024. The prevailing requirement is **2–3 days per week in office** at the Toronto locations, and some roles are advertised at **3–4 days in office**. Requirements vary by role, but no fully-remote engineering track was found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **NO — effectively disqualifying.** Every open engineering role is anchored to Toronto with a mandatory 2–3 (sometimes 3–4) day in-office expectation. A candidate in Kimberley, British Columbia who cannot relocate is not eligible for these roles in practice, regardless of the "Canada" country match. This is not a borderline case: it is a physical-presence requirement, not a preference. It also means the eight relevant open postings and the five comp bands on record for this company are not actionable for this candidate.

Engineering & tech: The open roles imply a broad enterprise stack across marketplace and lender-facing systems — platform engineering, CMS modernization (registry, auto repossession and remarketing), and a "Tech Transformation" stream that suggests active legacy-modernization work. Titles range from Software Engineer up to "Software Engineering Expert." No public engineering blog or handbook was found. Comp is posted on the JDs, which is a positive transparency signal and unusual for a Canadian private company.

Notable / other: This is one of the more genuinely stable, non-hype employers in the batch — a profitable Canadian market leader in a boring, mission-critical domain (vehicle registry, lien management, credit applications), with posted salary ranges and a European strategic parent. The single blocking issue is location. If AutoTrader ever opens a remote-Canada engineering track, it would be worth immediate re-evaluation on the merits.

Open relevant roles (sample, all Toronto, ON): Senior Platform Engineer (CA$130–160K posted); Senior Software Engineer, CMS (Auto Repossession & Remarketing); Senior Software Engineer, CMS (Modernization of Registry); Senior Software Engineer, Tech Transformation; Software Engineer, CMS (Registry Support); Software Engineering Expert.
