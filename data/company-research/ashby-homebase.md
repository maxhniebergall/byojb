# Homebase — research
Provider/key: ashby:homebase | company_type: product

What they do: Homebase is, in their words, "the everything app for hourly teams—built to simplify the day-to-day and superpower local businesses." The product bundles scheduling, time clocks, payroll, team communication and HR for small businesses that employ hourly staff. Scale claims from their own JD: "over **150,000** small (but mighty) businesses rely on us," and "together, we've tracked over **a billion hours for 2M+ workers**—and we're just getting started."

How they describe themselves: Opening line of every JD is "Hi, Future Homie!" — employees are "Homies." Their stated posture: "you'll join a team that's **bold, fast-moving, and obsessed with helping small businesses thrive**. We build with empathy, **act with urgency**, and take **big swings** that drive real-world impact. Here, every Homie shows up to **raise the bar**, support one another, and celebrate wins as a team. We're not just building an app—we're building unstoppable teams. So what do you say, are you in?"
Their published operating principles, "The Homie Way," verbatim:
- 💡 **Be Customer Obsessed** — "Solve problems with empathy and creativity."
- ⚡ **Move Fast, Learn Fast** — "Experiment, take action, and grow every day."
- 🎯 **Own Your Impact** — "Think big, focus on what matters, and make decisions you stand behind."
- 🏆 **Master Your Craft** — "Excellence fuels impact—show up, step up, and make your mark."
- 🏅 **Win Together** — "Put goals over roles, lead with trust, and connect to our mission and each other."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, well past early stage (150K+ business customers). Headcount, funding round and profitability were not stated in the JDs and are **unverified**.

Locations / HQ: US-headquartered with a San Francisco nexus (the JD cites the San Francisco Fair Chance Ordinance). Office geos evidenced by the postings: **Toronto** and **Denver**, with a Canadian benefits package (TFSA/RRSP with 4% match, EI top-up for parental leave) confirming a real Canadian entity. Every relevant open engineering role is Toronto-located.

Remote policy: **Hybrid, with named required days.** From their own benefits section: "💼 **Our Hybrid Rhythm**: We believe collaboration drives impact. That's why **Tuesday and Wednesday are our required in-office days**—a time to move faster as a team, build deeper connections, make better decisions, and build together." Every relevant posting carries "(Hybrid)" in its title. Countervailing flexibility perks: a "**Work From Anywhere Month**" and "**meeting-free weeks**" annually, plus "designated focus periods" alongside flexible PTO — genuine (if bounded) async/focus signals.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified NO for the current openings.** Canadian employment is clearly available — Toronto office, CAD-denominated pay tier, Canadian benefits — but two in-office days per week are company policy and every open role is title-tagged "(Hybrid)" in Toronto. Not workable from Kimberley BC. The "Work From Anywhere Month" is an annual perk, not a remote arrangement.

Engineering & tech: The most relevant opening is **Senior Software Engineer, Infrastructure/Developer Tooling** on the **platform infrastructure team** — "writing the internal tooling, automation, and services that power how our engineering organization builds and ships software every day." The JD is explicit about the internal-platform framing: "This is a role for someone who **thinks of internal engineers as customers**, cares deeply about developer experience." Responsibilities: build and maintain internal developer tools, CI/CD pipelines and deployments; "own the implementation of **well-scoped features end-to-end**"; use data and metrics to prioritise developer-experience improvements; "contribute to the reliability and scalability of our AWS-based infrastructure **within defined domains**"; participate in on-call rotations.
Stack: **Ruby or Golang** (production-quality code in at least one is a hard requirement), **AWS**, **Terraform** (IaC, preferred), containerised deployment, CI/CD systems, observability tooling. Requirement is 5+ years in software development or infrastructure engineering — "your roots are in writing software, and your infra experience grows from there." They also expect candidates to "use AI tools to drive personal effectiveness."

Notable / other: Benefits (Canadian tier): stock options; TFSA/RRSP with 4% company match; comprehensive medical/dental/vision including dependents; flexible PTO plus company holidays and designated focus periods; maternity/parental leave EI top-up after 6 months; life insurance and short/long-term disability; meals provided, team offsites, "Customer Days"; and notably "**AI access, for real** — paid AI tools with minimal restrictions." The interview process is published in the JD: Talent Acquisition → Hiring Manager → technical interviews → background check + offer.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Infrastructure/Developer Tooling (Hybrid, Toronto/Denver); Staff Engineer, Core Platform (Hybrid); Senior Software Engineer (Hybrid, Toronto); Software Engineer, Infrastructure/Developer Tooling (Hybrid); Applied AI Scientist (Hybrid, Toronto).
