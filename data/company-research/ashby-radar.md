# Radar — research
Provider/key: ashby:radar | company_type: product
What they do: Radar is a geolocation infrastructure company — geofencing SDKs, maps APIs and AI-enabled products for marketing, fraud and operations teams. They self-describe as "the global leader in geolocation" and are pursuing a product vision of "full-stack location infrastructure". They also run their own geospatial database, HorizonDB.
How they describe themselves (their own words, from the SRE JD): "We're trusted by some of the world's best companies, from high-growth startups to the Fortune 500"; "We have incredible scale: We're processing over 1 billion API calls per day from hundreds of millions of devices"; "We're well-resourced"; "We have a high-performance culture, with ambitious and entrepreneurial teammates in every role"; "We recently moved into an amazing new office in Flatiron, Manhattan, NYC"; "named a top 10 best place to work in NYC by Crain's". On working style: "We care a lot about shipping fast and talking to customers… customer feedback is a treasure map to gold." "Even though Slack is the brain of our company, working together in-person in our NYC HQ is the fastest way for us to get things done." "To us, a week is a long time, and we expect to ship big things every week." Their signature practice is "Walk A Mile" — literally walking a mile dogfooding the SDK, "because you can't create location infrastructure behind a desk".
Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed; $85.5M raised from Accel and Insight Partners (stated in their JDs). Headcount not stated; the JDs describe a startup that is past early stage but explicitly "high growth". They ask candidates to "have experience at a high growth startup".
Locations / HQ: HQ in Flatiron, Manhattan, NYC. All five live engineering postings read "New York , US Remote".
Remote policy: Nominally NYC + US Remote, but the JD is unusually candid that in-person NYC is preferred: weekly Monday in-person planning in small groups, in-office framed as "the fastest way for us to get things done", and the final-round interview is onsite at NYC HQ. Most engineers are in an on-call rotation (one week every 1-2 months).
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO (as posted). Every live posting says "US Remote". There is no mention of Canada or of non-US employment anywhere in the JD text, and benefits (401(k) 4% match, commuter benefits) are US-shaped.
Engineering & tech: Terraform-managed AWS, deployed via EKS; MongoDB on Atlas (large sharded clusters, heavy read/write); CircleCI for CI/CD; CloudWatch, Grafana, Pingdom, PagerDuty for monitoring; Cloudflare DNS. Server languages TypeScript and Rust; data pipelines in Airflow and Scala Spark. Multi-AZ today, with multi-region as a major initiative and a 99.99%+ availability goal. They sponsor OpenStreetMap, MapLibre and OpenAddresses. Team profile is described as "former technical co-founders or former Radar interns from schools like Waterloo and CMU"; engineers are expected to be either "Staff level expertise in one stack, or Multi-Stack at any level".
AI usage (their own words): engineers pick their own tools, Claude most popular; they are "actively building Claude skills — for example we've taught it how to debug HorizonDB"; Claude/Cursor/Copilot review all PRs alongside a human reviewer knowledgeable in the area; "we are excited about what AI can do, but we also recognize the risks and don't compromise our coding standards."
Notable / other: Projects are run by a trio — an Engineering lead, an executive and a Go-to-Market lead — and engineers are expected to "talk to customers, talk to prospects, help close them, get them live and make them successful". Linear for planning. Hiring: recruiter call → code screen → coding round → system design → onsite NYC final. Perks: 401(k) with 4% match, catered lunches, unlimited PTO, 100% employee-covered health/dental/vision, 12 weeks paid parental leave, commuter and fitness benefits.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior / Staff Site Reliability Engineer; Senior / Staff Platform Engineer; Senior / Staff Data Platform Engineer; Senior / Staff Product Engineer, Backend; Staff / Principal Engineer, Product Security. All post $200K-$300K + equity (Product Security $250K-$300K).
