# Remitly — research
Provider/key: workday:remitly/remitly_careers | company_type: product

What they do: Remitly is a digital cross-border remittance and financial-services company — immigrants and migrant workers send money home through its app to recipients in ~170 countries, with an expanding set of adjacent products (Remitly Business, high-value sends, receiver-side products). The engineering problem space is payments, verification/KYC, fraud and risk decisioning, pricing, and a large data/ML platform behind all of it.

How they describe themselves: The publicly notable framing in current JDs is their **"Connected Work Culture: Driving Innovation, Together"**, stated in their own words: "we believe that true innovation sparks when we come together. Our Connected Work Culture fosters dynamic in-person collaboration, where ideas ignite and challenging problems find solutions faster… These intentional in-office moments are vital for deepening relationships, fueling creativity, and ensuring your impact is felt where it matters most." Benefits they list: four weeks vacation, health benefits, mental-health and family-forming benefits, RRSP with company match, ESPP, life insurance and disability, continuing education and travel benefits.

Size / stage / funding: Public company (NASDAQ: RELY), several thousand employees, well past the startup stage. Not runway-dependent.

Locations / HQ: HQ Seattle, Washington. Major Canadian engineering site in **Burnaby, British Columbia**. Also London (UK) and other international offices.

Remote policy: **Hybrid, and explicitly so.** "For corporate team members, we have an in-office expectation of at least 50% of the time monthly, typically achieved by coming in three days a week… Managers also have the flexibility to set higher expectations based on their team's specific needs." Every posting in the registry for this company is tied to a physical site — Burnaby BC, Seattle WA, or London UK — and the ones with extracted facets are all marked `hybrid`.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — verified negative for a fully-remote Canadian hire.** Canada roles exist in volume, but they are Burnaby, BC positions under a three-days-a-week in-office expectation. Burnaby is ~800 km from Kimberley, BC and is in Pacific, not Mountain, time. Nothing in the postings or the stated culture supports a remote-from-elsewhere-in-BC arrangement; the in-office language is unusually assertive and is presented as a core cultural commitment rather than a default that can be waived.

Engineering & tech: Substantial platform and ML surface — ML Platform, Decision Intelligence Platform, Customer Data Platform, Verification Platform (IAM, OAuth, KYC pipelines), Transaction Monitoring, Pricing Platform, Core Transfer Experience. A large recent hiring push around "AI Native Software Engineer" and "Agentic Software Engineer" titles at every level (II / Senior / Staff / Principal), plus Forward-Deployed and Knowledge-Development Engineer variants — a deliberate, well-funded AI-engineering build-out. Ladder is Amazon-style: SDE2 / SDE3 / Staff / Principal.

Notable / other: Comp is transparent and strong. The Principal Engineer, Verification Platform (Burnaby) JD states a starting base range of **CAD 220,000 – 275,000**, plus equity ("In Canada, Remitly employees are shareholders in our Company and equity is part of our total compensation plan"). Remitly is an E-Verify employer in the US. The AI-native roles are clustered in Seattle ("2 Locations") rather than Canada; the Canadian site skews toward core platform, data platform and ML platform work.

Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Engineer, Verification Platform (Burnaby BC); Senior Software Development Engineer, ML Platform (Burnaby BC); Senior SDE (SDE3) – Customer Data Platform (Burnaby BC); Staff Software Engineer, Core Transfer Experience (Burnaby BC); Principal Software Engineer, Receiver Products (Seattle); Senior/Staff AI Native Software Engineer (Seattle).

Pay (see data/company-comp.jsonl): Principal, Verification Platform (Canada) — base **CAD 220,000 – 275,000**, stated on their own JD.
