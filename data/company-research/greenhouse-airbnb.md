# Airbnb — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:airbnb | company_type: product

What they do: Airbnb operates the global marketplace for short-term stays and experiences. From their own JD boilerplate: "Airbnb was born in 2007 when two hosts welcomed three guests to their San Francisco home, and has since grown to over 5 million hosts who have welcomed over 2 billion guest arrivals in almost every country across the globe. Every day, hosts offer unique stays and experiences that make it possible for guests to connect with communities in a more authentic way." Public company (NASDAQ: ABNB); the business is consumer marketplace with a very large supporting infrastructure org.

How they describe themselves: "Airbnb is a mission-driven company dedicated to helping create a world where anyone can belong anywhere. It takes a unified team committed to our core values to achieve this goal. Airbnb's various functions embody the company's innovative spirit and our fast-moving team is committed to leading as a 21st century company." Inclusion statement: "Airbnb is committed to working with the broadest talent pool possible. We believe diverse ideas foster innovation and engagement..." Note the explicit self-description as a "fast-moving team" and repeated "fast paced, high growth tech environment" phrasing in requirements.

Size / stage / funding: Large public company, tens of thousands of hosts-facing operations plus a several-thousand-person engineering org. Well past venture stage; profitability and headcount not restated on the JD pages.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco HQ. Engineering hiring in the registry spans Remote-USA, Remote-Bangalore (India) and Brazil-Remote.

Remote policy: Remote-eligible by country, with an explicit legal-entity constraint. Verbatim from their Cloud Networking JD: "This position is US - Remote Eligible. The role may include occasional work at an Airbnb office or attendance at offsites, as agreed to with your manager. While the position is Remote Eligible, you must live in a state where Airbnb, Inc. has a registered entity... This list is continuously evolving... If your position is employed by another Airbnb entity, your recruiter will inform you what states you are eligible to work from." Airbnb publicly runs a "live and work anywhere" style flexibility programme for existing employees, but hiring eligibility is still gated to the entity's registered jurisdictions.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No.** Every relevant live posting in the registry is either "Remote - USA"/"Remote - US" (extracted `geo_eligibility: us_only`), "Remote - Bangalore, India", or "Brazil - Remote". The one role whose title line reads "Remote - USA or Canada" (Software Engineer, Cloud Networking) contradicts itself in the JD body, which states plainly "This position is US - Remote Eligible... you must live in a state where Airbnb, Inc. has a registered entity" and quotes a USD pay range only. Treat the "or Canada" in that location string as a data-quality artifact, not as evidence of Canadian eligibility. No Canada-eligible engineering posting was found.

Engineering & tech: Deep, well-resourced infrastructure organisation. The Cloud Infrastructure org's Networking team "owns the entire Airbnb production network infrastructure... traffic proxy and load balancer, service mesh, VPC/backbone and cross-region connectivity, network monitoring and security systems", working "from Layer3 to Layer 7" on "global traffic load balancing and disaster recovery, next-gen service mesh, cross-region gateways and edge security". "Airbnb is a member of the Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) end user community, and we work closely with the open source community (e.g. k8s, istio) and peer companies." Other live infra work: Bazel build systems, Ads platform backend, payments, host pricing/availability serving, ML platform (Growth Platform Engineering, Post Training, Trust), and Community Support AI/ML. Engineers are "full-cycle developers" with on-call responsibility for production infrastructure.

Notable / other: Pay is posted on US roles under transparency law, with equity and "Employee Travel Credits"; they note "Our job titles may span more than one career level. The actual base pay is dependent upon many factors... The base pay range is subject to change." They provide an accommodations process (reasonableaccommodations@airbnb.com). The infra ladder runs to Principal (e.g. Principal Software Engineer, Performance at $292K–$365K USD).

Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineer, Cloud Networking (Remote-US); Staff Software Engineer, Build (Bazel) (Remote-USA); Senior Staff Machine Learning Engineer, Post Training / Trust / Growth Platform (Remote-USA); Staff Backend Engineer, Host Serving and Access (Remote-USA); Principal Software Engineer, Performance (Remote-US); Senior Software Engineer, Infrastructure (Brazil-Remote).
