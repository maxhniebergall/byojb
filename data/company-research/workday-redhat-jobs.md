# Red Hat — research
Provider/key: workday:redhat/jobs | company_type: product

What they do: In their own words, "Red Hat is the world's leading provider of enterprise open source software solutions, using a community-powered approach to deliver high-performing Linux, cloud, container, and Kubernetes technologies." The product portfolio visible across the live postings: Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL), OpenShift (including OpenShift Managed Cloud Services and hosted control planes on GCP), Red Hat Certificate System / Identity Management (PKI, upstream Dogtag PKI), an observability stack (storage and UI), virtualization, and an emerging AI/agentic engineering line. A separate Technology Services / Consulting arm delivers implementation engagements to customers.

How they describe themselves: "Red Hat's culture is built on the open source principles of transparency, collaboration, and inclusion, where the best ideas can come from anywhere and anyone." And: "Red Hatters are encouraged to bring their best ideas, no matter their title or tenure. We're a leader in open source because of our open and inclusive environment. We hire creative, passionate people ready to contribute their ideas, help solve complex problems, and make an impact." On work location, quoted directly: "Spread across 40+ countries, our associates work flexibly across work environments, from in-office, to office-flex, to fully remote, depending on the requirements of their role."

Size / stage / funding: A wholly-owned subsidiary of IBM (acquired 2019). Large, established, revenue-generating; no runway risk. Operations in 40+ countries per their own boilerplate. Exact headcount not stated in the postings.

Locations / HQ: Historically headquartered in Raleigh, NC. Live postings span Raleigh, Boston, Brno (Czech Republic, "Tech Park Brno"), Remote Brazil, Remote Spain, Remote UK, Remote Australia, New Delhi, and Ireland.

Remote policy: Officially a three-mode model — "in-office, office-flex, or fully remote, depending on the requirements of their role" — and several current reqs are labelled fully remote by country (Remote Brazil, Remote Spain, Remote UK, Remote Australia). Others are office-attached and say so in the title (e.g. "Brno Office, Czech Republic"). One Boston ML role carries the restriction "*Telecommuting permitted: work may be performed within normal commuting distance to the Red Hat, LLC office in Boston, MA" — i.e. remote-but-local. Remote roles are scoped **per country**, not globally.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, and NO among the current openings.** None of the 15 relevant live postings in the registry is Canadian or Canada-remote. The countries named across the current req set are the US, Czech Republic, Ireland, UK, Spain, Brazil, Australia and India. Red Hat's remote model is explicitly country-scoped, and its benefits boilerplate on the US reqs states "These benefits are only applicable to full time, permanent associates at Red Hat located in the United States." The Raleigh Senior Software Engineer req adds "Red Hat will not be providing visa sponsorship for this position." Red Hat does operate in Canada as a corporate matter, but **nothing in the registry or in any fetched/available first-party page verifies that they currently hire remote engineers in Canada**, and I did not verify it independently — treat this as unverified rather than as a yes.

Engineering & tech: Deep systems and open-source work, which is the strongest signal here.
- **Identity Management / PKI (Raleigh, senior)**: "designing, developing, testing and collaborating with other contributors over the world to bring features on existing components or bootstrap new ones… contributing to open source projects, and services for enterprise & public sector critical infrastructure." Java/Rust, Linux internals and dev tools, PKI/cryptography/software security, Fedora/RHEL package maintenance, CI/CD, OpenShift/AWS/Azure. Notably they list mentoring, design documents and blog posts as part of the role: "share expertise by producing comprehensive design documents, writing blog posts, and mentoring others on documentation and communication best practices" — a genuine documentation-culture signal.
- **Observability (Brno)**: storage and UI engineering for the observability stack.
- **OpenShift platform**: hosted control planes on GCP, managed cloud services SRE, DevOps/OpenShift+Python integration.
- **AI / ML**: Principal Software Engineer – Agentic AI; Principal Software Engineer – AI Experiment Tracking (Ireland); Machine Learning Engineer (Boston), whose actual content is Python refactoring and productionizing research code — "Translate experimental or research-oriented code into maintainable, production-quality Python modules" — a fairly junior req (2 years' experience) despite the title.
- **AI-assisted development** is now an explicit requirement even on core systems roles: "Experience using AI agents to accelerate development efforts" is listed as required on the Raleigh PKI role, with "Experience with an autonomous agentic software defined lifecycle" as a plus.
- **Consulting arm**: "Consultant - Cloud Infrastructure Services" (New Delhi) and "Platform Engineering Consultant" (Remote Spain) are customer-delivery roles — "perform on-site delivery and implementation of modern Red Hat technology… Deliver quality results both on-site and post-engagement that are on time and within the scope of the assigned project." These are structurally different from the product-engineering roles and involve travel/on-site customer work.

Notable / other: US benefits listed: comprehensive medical/dental/vision, FSA and HSA, 401(k) with employer match, PTO and holidays, paid parental leave for all new parents, disability/paid family medical/military leave, employee stock purchase plan, family planning reimbursement, tuition reimbursement, transportation expense account, EAP. Red Hat states it does not accept unsolicited agency resumes and provides an application-assistance channel for accommodations.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer – Identity Management/PKI (Raleigh); Principal Software Engineer (Boston); Principal Software Engineer - Agentic AI; Principal Software Engineer - AI Experiment Tracking (Ireland); Senior Software Engineer - Observability Storage (Czech Republic); Senior Software Engineer - GCP Hosted Control Planes (UK/Ireland/Czechia); Senior DevOps Engineer OpenShift & Python Integration (Remote Brazil); Customer SRE - OpenShift Managed Cloud Services (Remote Australia); Machine Learning Engineer (Boston); Telco Cloud Architect; Platform Engineering Consultant (Remote Spain).

Pay: Red Hat posts US pay-transparency ranges on its US reqs and states, "Red Hat determines compensation based on several factors including but not limited to job location, experience, applicable skills and training, external market value, and internal pay equity. Annual salary is one component of Red Hat's compensation package. This position may also be eligible for bonus, commission, and/or equity." Ranges on record from live JDs: Senior Software Engineer (Raleigh) USD 118,600–195,680; Machine Learning Engineer (Boston) USD 136,320–225,090; Principal Software Engineer (Boston) USD 193,390–318,980. The non-US reqs (Brazil, Spain, Czechia, UK) post no ranges, so no band could be derived for the sre_devops or platform_infra slots.
