# Fleetio — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:fleetio | company_type: product

What they do: Fleetio is a SaaS fleet-management platform — "a modern software platform that helps
thousands of organizations worldwide manage their fleet operations." Maintenance scheduling, work
orders, parts/inventory, fuel, inspections, telematics integrations, a mobile app (Fleetio Go), a
parts-and-service Marketplace with payments, and a public API (developer.fleetio.com). Customers are
commercial fleet operators across transportation, construction, utilities and government.

How they describe themselves: "Transportation technology is a hot market, and we're leading the
charge with raving fans and new customers signing up every day." They are a "proud founding member
of the Rails Foundation." Publicly stated values center on diversity, inclusion and belonging, open
communication, allyship and advocacy "so that every voice/idea is heard and valued"; respecting and
trusting teammates "to do the right thing"; sincere and honest dialogue; taking responsibility and
holding themselves accountable. Their engineering culture states that "indecision is the worst
decision" and that in ambiguity they choose to take action using intuitive intelligence, course
correcting as necessary and learning from mistakes. Their AI engineering expectations emphasize
"translating ambiguous AI opportunities into clear experiments, balancing user value with technical
feasibility, and creating feedback loops through customer feedback, usage signals, evaluations, and
quality reviews."

Size / stage / funding: Founded January 2012. Private, venture-backed; raised a **$450M Series D in
March 2025** — a very large, recent round, so well capitalized but growth-expectation heavy. Several
hundred employees; over 50% of the team is remote.

Locations / HQ: Headquartered in Birmingham, Alabama (US Central Time). Distributed team across the
US, Canada and Mexico.

Remote policy: Remote-first in practice — "Remote working friendly since 2012", `#LI-Remote` on
postings, flexible hours, and over half the company remote. Engineering is described as remote-first
with flexible hours. No stated async-by-default doctrine (unlike, say, a public handbook company).

Remote-Canada eligibility: **YES — verified, first-party and unambiguous.** Every relevant
engineering JD states: "This is a remote opportunity and is open to candidates in the United States,
Canada, or Mexico." Canada is a named hiring geography, not an exception. HQ is US Central Time,
one hour ahead of Mountain — the most Mountain-Time-workable spread of any company in this batch.

Engineering & tech: Ruby on Rails monolith plus React/TypeScript front end — Vite, Storybook,
Tailwind, a shared component system. Rails APIs, database-backed product workflows, AI integration
points, prompts, evaluation flows and observability. Organized into product "squads" (Actionable
Intelligence, Fleet Maintenance, Growth, Marketplace Payments, Integrations, Leverage), each
partnering closely with Product, Design, Data and Customer Success. Engineers are expected to work
across the stack including customer-facing UI, to prototype quickly, and to "decide when to iterate,
harden, or discard an approach." They publish engineering-culture and interview-process material at
fleetio.com/careers/engineering and culture videos at fleet.io/culture.

Notable / other: Founding member of the Rails Foundation — a genuine open-source community
commitment and a signal they are staying on Rails long-term. Public API and developer docs.
Their own careers pages (fleetio.com/careers, /careers/engineering) return HTTP 403 to automated
fetching, so the culture material was read via their JD bodies and secondary summaries.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Actionable Intelligence; Senior Software
