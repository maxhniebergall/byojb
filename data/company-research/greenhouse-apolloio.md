# Apollo.io — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:apolloio | company_type: product

What they do: In their own words, "Apollo.io is the leading go-to-market solution for revenue teams, trusted by over 500,000 companies and millions of users globally, from rapidly growing startups to some of the world's largest enterprises." The product "provides sales and marketing teams with easy access to verified contact data for over 210 million B2B contacts and 35 million companies worldwide, along with tools to engage and convert these contacts in one unified platform. By helping revenue professionals find the most accurate contact information and automating the outreach process, Apollo.io turns prospects into customers." Domain: B2B sales intelligence and outbound go-to-market automation. Customers are sales and marketing organisations.

How they describe themselves: Their stated operating values, quoted from their own careers copy: "we **take extreme ownership** of our work, **move with focus and urgency**, and **learn voraciously** to stay ahead"; "Collaboration is at our core—we're **all for one**"; "We encourage **bold ideas and courageous action**, giving you the freedom to experiment, take smart risks, and drive big wins"; "We invest deeply in your growth, ensuring you have the resources, support, and autonomy to **own your role and make a real impact**." They lead with an explicit AI identity: "**We are AI Native.** Apollo.io is an AI-native company built on a culture of continuous improvement. We're on the front lines of driving productivity for our customers—and we expect the same mindset from our team. If you're energized by finding smarter, faster ways to get things done using AI and automation, you'll thrive here." Their we-are-apollo page adds a remote-first, recognition-focused framing: "You can feel the impact of your work—your ideas matter, your contributions are recognized, and you can see how your efforts directly shape Apollo's success."

Size / stage / funding: "Founded in 2015… one of the fastest growing companies in SaaS, raising approximately $250 million to date and valued at $1.6 billion. Apollo raised a series D in 2023 and is backed by top-tier investors, including Sequoia Capital, Bain Capital Ventures, and more, and counts the former President and COO of Hubspot, JD Sherman, among its board members." Their careers page states **700+ employees operating across 30 countries**. Private, venture-backed, late stage. Profitability not stated.

Remote policy: **Remote-first.** Their careers page describes "a remote-first culture with distributed team structure", including "virtual clubs connecting global colleagues". Postings are geo-tagged as remote by country rather than by office.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **VERIFIED YES.** The live Senior Software Engineer posting's location field reads "Remote, Canada" — a country-level remote listing with no city or office attached, at a company that describes itself as remote-first and hires across 30 countries. This is one of the few companies in this batch that is unambiguously open to a Canada-based remote worker; Mountain Time specifically is not addressed, and the JD's mention of "a large global team, attending scrum ceremonies" implies synchronous meetings whose timezone anchor is unstated.

Engineering & tech: Their stated stack: "React.js / TypeScript · Python · GoLang · MongoDB · Elasticsearch · Redis · Kafka · Kubernetes / Docker · GCP · Terraform / Ansible · Grafana / Kibana · GitHub Actions". The researched role spans "two high-impact domains: data platform architecture and integrations with distributed systems building the infrastructure that powers Apollo's AI-driven internal products. On the data side, you'll organize and make sense of data at scale, working closely with our data engineering team to structure pipelines and enable intelligent product experiences. On the integrations side, you'll own the connections layer and the distributed architecture that keeps Apollo reliably linked to the broader ecosystem at high throughput." Responsibilities include designing "highly scalable backend services, data pipelines, and integration endpoints", writing design documents, and building "systems with observability as a first-class concern proper monitoring, alerting, and dashboards so issues are caught before customers notice". Process is scrum-based with "scrum ceremonies, team events, and manager 1:1s". Requirements: minimum 5 years backend, distributed web applications at scale, SQL or NoSQL modelling, RESTful services.

Notable / other: The JD demands AI-tool fluency as a hiring criterion, not a perk: "Proven experience leveraging AI tools to enhance software development processes, including code generation, debugging, and productivity optimization" and an "AI-native working style: habitually leverages AI tools across coding, debugging, review, and documentation". It also asks for "Background in fast-scaling startup or high-velocity environments where **going beyond your job title was the norm**", and lists "Dealing with Ambiguity" and "Navigate ambiguity and roadblocks proactively" among must-haves. Reported recognition: a 4/5 Glassdoor happiness score and Forbes recognition as one of America's best startup employers (their claim, on their own page).
