# Asana — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:asana | company_type: product

What they do: Asana is a work-management / team-collaboration SaaS platform — projects, tasks, workflows, goals and reporting — now positioning itself as "a leading platform for human + AI collaboration" with AI teammates and AI chat layered onto the collaboration graph. Customers are enterprise and mid-market teams; millions of teams use it. Publicly traded (NYSE: ASAN).

How they describe themselves: From their own JD boilerplate: "Asana is a leading platform for human + AI collaboration. Millions of teams around the world rely on Asana to achieve their most important goals, faster. Asana has been named to Fortune's Best Workplaces for 7+ years and recognized by Fast Company, Forbes, and Gartner for excellence in workplace culture and innovation. We offer an exceptional office-centric culture while adopting the best elements of hybrid models to ensure that every one of our global team members can work together effortlessly. With 13+ offices all over the world, we are always looking for individuals who care about building technology that drives positive change in the world and a culture where everyone feels that they belong." They also state a commitment to pay equity: "we're committed to looking at market value which is why we check ourselves and conduct a yearly pay equity audit."

Size / stage / funding: Public company (NYSE: ASAN), roughly 1,700-1,900 employees, 13+ global offices. Established rather than early-stage, though not yet consistently profitable — it has been in a cost-discipline phase common to post-IPO SaaS.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco. 13+ offices worldwide. The Canadian engineering presence relevant here is Vancouver, BC — every posting in this registry entry is tagged "Vancouver, BC". Other engineering locations in their board include Reykjavik.

Remote policy: EXPLICITLY OFFICE-CENTRIC HYBRID, stated verbatim on their own JDs: "This role is based in our Vancouver office with an office-centric hybrid schedule. The standard in-office days are Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday. Most Asanas have the option to work from home on Wednesdays. Working from home on Fridays depends on the type of work you do and the teams with which you partner." Their company boilerplate reinforces "an exceptional office-centric culture". Postings are tagged #LI-Hybrid.

Remote-Canada eligibility: VERIFIED NO for a remote worker. The roles are Canadian (Vancouver, BC, paid in CAD) but require physical attendance in the Vancouver office three fixed days a week (Mon/Tue/Thu). Vancouver is a ~10-hour drive or a flight from Kimberley BC; this is not commutable and there is no remote option stated. Effectively disqualifying.

Engineering & tech: Web-app performance, APIs, payments/billing platform, data engineering, and a substantial AI push (AI Chat, AI Retrieval, AI Developer Experience). The Vancouver site carries a broad mix of product-engineering and platform teams. Roles are conventional product-SaaS engineering with some genuine platform surface (Payment Platform, APIs, Data Platform).

Notable / other: Pay transparency is strong — Vancouver JDs post CAD base ranges. Observed on their own live postings: Senior Software Engineer roles (Account Management; Consumption Based Billing & Licensing; Payment Platform) at CAD $176,000 - $200,000 base; Senior Data Engineer at CAD $138,000 - $168,000 base. Total package additionally includes equity and benefits. In-office culinary/dining perks are listed, consistent with the office-centric model.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Payment Platform; Senior Software Engineer, APIs; Senior Software Engineer, AI Chat; Senior Software Engineer, AI Retrieval; Senior Data Engineer; Senior Infrastructure Engineer (Software-Focused); Senior Software Engineer, Web App Performance — all Vancouver, BC.
