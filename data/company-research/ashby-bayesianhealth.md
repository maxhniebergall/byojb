# Bayesian Health, Inc. — research
Provider/key: ashby:bayesianhealth | company_type: product

What they do: Clinical AI for hospitals. In their own words, "Bayesian Health's mission is to improve patient outcomes by empowering clinicians with the insights they need to make the right decision for the right patient at the point-of-care." The product is a real-time predictive system embedded in clinical workflow — the JD describes "training and tuning ML models that predict clinical conditions in patients" and a "live, clinical AI product" whose value depends on "the timeliness of your model's predictions." The company frames its purpose as making "healthcare proactive by empowering physicians, nurses, and care team members with real-time data to save lives." Customers are health systems; the JD references "working with enterprise customers, and the agility and responsiveness they require."

How they describe themselves: "We're an early-stage startup on a mission to make healthcare proactive." On composition: "We're a diverse team of clinicians, engineers, machine learning experts, product designers, and performance improvement leaders committed to enabling smarter, patient-specific care delivery through unlocking the power of data." The one explicit cultural expectation stated is "Excitement for Bayesian's mission and being a bar raiser so we can accelerate the pace at which we create value." There is no published values list, handbook, or engineering blog in the available material. Tone is mission-and-credentials forward rather than process-forward.

Size / stage / funding: Self-described as "an early-stage startup." Named investors, in their own words: "We're funded by top tier tech and biotech investors: Andreessen Horowitz, American Medical Association's venture arm, Catalio Partners, and LifeForce Capital." Headcount, round size and profitability are not stated and were not verified.

Locations / HQ: **Not stated in the available material and not verified.** No office city appears anywhere in the postings; all three registry postings are remote-flagged.

Remote policy: Remote. The live Staff Machine Learning Engineer posting has location "Remote." Two other (now closed) postings in the registry — Software Engineer, Analytics and Infrastructure Engineer — both carry the location "Remote - US Only." So the company operates as a distributed team, but its own historical postings define what "remote" means to them. No async working practices are described.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Almost certainly no.** The live Staff MLE posting says only "Remote" without qualification, so on that posting alone it is unstated. However, the registry holds two other Bayesian Health engineering postings whose location field reads verbatim "Remote - US Only." That is the company's own established pattern for engineering roles, and it is the strongest available evidence of what employment vehicle they actually have. The unqualified "Remote" on the current posting is far more likely to be shorthand for the same policy than a deliberate widening. Treat as US-only unless the company says otherwise directly. Additionally, the product is a US healthcare product deployed in US health systems with US regulatory exposure, which reinforces a US-only employment posture.

Engineering & tech: Python and SQL are the core languages — "writing production-grade Python and SQL code to implement and evaluate ML models in production systems," repeated several times as the central skill. Cloud is AWS: the JD describes "debugging a specific patient case by following patient data as it moves through our AWS services." MLOps tooling is named explicitly: "Experience using MLOps tools such as SageMaker and MLFlow." The role's own framing of the job shape is unusually explicit and worth quoting in full: "Part Data Scientist (building models), part Applied Scientist (productionizing models), and part MLE (deploying, maintaining), also known as 'Full Stack Data Scientist' – someone who wants to own the end-to-end effectiveness of their real-time models in a live, clinical AI product." Listed responsibilities span Model Prototyping, Productionizing, Deploying and "MLOps: Build infrastructure that enables ML model development and deployment in production systems." The bar is set at "Ph.D. in a relevant field plus 3+ years relevant experience, or a relevant Master's degree and 5+ years experience shipping ML based software products."

Notable / other: Substantial external validation for an early-stage company. In their own words: "Our company has won many awards; most recent recognitions include: Forbes AI Top 50, World Economic Forum Tech Pioneer, Time Best Inventions, BioTech AI Company of the Year." They also cite peer-reviewed clinical evidence — "our recent publication in Nature Medicine that associates our products with lives saved." Standard US EEO language appears at the foot of the posting, referencing "federal, state or local laws," another US-employment indicator. Data work involves protected health information and "messy health data," with the JD naming "familiarity working with messy health data" as a preferred qualification.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Machine Learning Engineer — Remote, no salary posted (live). Previously and now closed: Software Engineer, Analytics — Remote - US Only; Infrastructure Engineer — Remote - US Only.
