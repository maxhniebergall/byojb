# AlphaSense — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:alphasense | company_type: product

What they do: AlphaSense is an AI-powered market- and business-intelligence platform. It indexes a
content universe of 500M+ premium business documents (earnings-call transcripts, SEC/company
filings, broker research, expert-call transcripts, news, and customers' own internal content) and
layers domain-specific AI search, summarization and agentic "GenSearch" workflows on top. Customers
are financial institutions and corporates — it claims over 70% of the S&P 500 and nearly all of the
world's largest financial institutions. It acquired Tegus in 2024, adding the expert-call library.

How they describe themselves: mission is "Shape how the world makes decisions." Stated values on
the careers site are **People First** ("lead with empathy", inclusion), **Product-led** ("obsess over
user needs", innovate through experimentation), and **Growth Mindset** ("curiosity and resilience").
They describe the culture as one that "blends ambition with humility, and autonomy with
collaboration", emphasising psychological safety, career development and work-life balance, and
"show up as your full self, every day". Tone is polished corporate-scaleup, not hustle-coded — the
JDs do not use "fast-paced"/"wear many hats" language and instead describe scoped subsystem
ownership at Staff/Principal level.

Size / stage / funding: ~2,900 employees. Private, late-stage. Raised $350M in June 2026 at a $7.5B
valuation (roughly double the prior $4B), led by Vitruvian Partners, Accenture Ventures and J.P.
Morgan Asset Management, with D. E. Shaw Ventures and Pinegrove Opportunity Partners joining and
existing backers CapitalG, Goldman Sachs Alternatives and Viking Global participating. Total funding
now over $1B. Surpassed $600M ARR in Q1 2026, up from $500M in October 2025. Not a runway-risk
company; growth is revenue-backed rather than purely burn-funded.

Locations / HQ: Global HQ moved to Hudson Yards, New York City. Offices in the US, Canada, Helsinki,
London, Waterford (Ireland), Bengaluru, Pune, New Delhi and Singapore. Headcount across EMEA and
APAC has more than doubled recently.

Remote policy: Careers site advertises "hybrid flexibility" plus relocation assistance for
office-based roles, but a large share of engineering requisitions are posted as fully remote by
country — the live board carries "Remote - United States", "Remote - Canada", "Remote - United
Kingdom" and "Remote - India" postings. Remote is country-scoped, i.e. hired through a local
entity rather than "work from anywhere".

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** Multiple live requisitions are posted explicitly as
"Remote - Canada", including "Staff Software Engineer" (job 8403920002), "Staff Software Engineer,
Identity & Access Management" (job 8632155002) and "Principal Software Engineer" (job 8403928002),
each with a CAD salary range attached. No province restriction is stated on those postings, and the
company already has a Canadian entity/office. Working hours are not stated, but the roles are
US/Canada-anchored so Mountain Time should be workable; the India/UK requisitions are separate
listings, so there is no implied EU/IST overlap requirement on the Canadian ones.

Engineering & tech: Search/retrieval and LLM systems are the core of the product, so the eng org is
unusually infrastructure-heavy for a "market intelligence" vendor — live roles cover a Cloud
Platform / Cloud Developer Experience team, a Core Developer Experience (DevEx) team, an Identity &
Access Management platform team, Site Reliability Engineering, a Financial Data Platform &
GenSearch Agent team, an AI Platform team, and Search. That is a real platform organisation with
named, well-scoped areas rather than generalist product squads. Ladder runs SWE → Senior → Staff →
Senior Staff → Principal → Senior Principal; the volume of open Staff/Principal reqs suggests a
genuinely deep IC track.

Compensation transparency: AlphaSense states on its JDs that "in accordance with legislation, as
well as to provide greater transparency to candidates, we share salary ranges on all job postings
regardless of desired hiring location" — so nearly every posting carries a stated base range in the
local currency. This is a company-wide policy, not just CO/NY compliance.

Notable / other: Domain is financial research/market intelligence — a stable, mission-critical,
"boring in a good way" enterprise workflow rather than a consumer AI toy, though the product is very
much positioned in the current AI cycle. The Tegus acquisition means part of the business is a
human expert-network operation alongside the software.

Open relevant roles (sample): Principal AI Platform Engineer; Principal Engineer, Developer
