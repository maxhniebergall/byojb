# Recharge (Recharge Payments) — research
Provider/key: ashby:recharge | company_type: product

What they do: "Recharge is the subscription platform for the world's fastest-growing and most innovative brands. Shopify's premier subscription solution, Recharge is on a mission to build the future of commerce around customer retention." It provides recurring-billing, subscription management, loyalty and retention tooling for e-commerce merchants, plus customer-facing analytics. Self-reported scale: 20,000+ brands, "data from over 100 million shoppers", $1B+ processed monthly, and "71% of subscriptions on Shopify stores". Named customers include quip, Dr. Squatch, Blueland, Estrid, Oats Overnight and Bobbie.

How they describe themselves: Stated company values, quoted verbatim from the JD, are **#day-one, #ownership, #empathy, #humility** — the JD asks candidates to "live by and champion our values". Site messaging centres on three founding principles: hands-on partnership with merchants ("a hands-on partner to over 20,000 brands globally"), relentless product innovation ("104 major updates in the last year"), and proven enterprise-scale performance. Notably candidate-respectful policy detail: recording, transcription and AI notetakers in interviews are prohibited without prior written consent, with an accommodation path via recruiting@getrecharge.com.

Size / stage / funding: Private, late-stage and well established (founded 2014; previously valued in the low billions after a 2021 round). Headcount not published on the pages read — treat specific figures as unverified. Ecommerce SaaS with substantial recurring revenue rather than a runway-stage startup.

Locations / HQ: **Santa Monica, California** per its own site footer; additional office locations not detailed. Postings are listed as "Remote".

Remote policy: Roles are posted "Remote" and the benefits list includes a "Monthly Remote Life and Merchant stipend", which is strong evidence of an established distributed workforce. No formal remote-first policy statement was found on the site.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes — verified from their own JD.** The Senior Data Engineer posting publishes two hiring ranges side by side: "Hiring range in the US $148,000 USD - $185,000 USD" and "**Hiring range in Canada $108,000 CAD - $136,000 CAD**". A Canada-specific band is direct evidence they employ Canadians. Time-zone requirements are not stated (HQ is Pacific Time, which is favourable for Mountain Time). Caveat: the posting states "Application window anticipated to close: 7/1/2026" and the registry last saw it live on 2026-06-29 — it may no longer be open.

Engineering & tech: The role sits on "the centralized Data and Analytics team… [which] delivers critical analytic capabilities and insights for Recharge's business and customers". Work: build data pipeline, ELT and infrastructure powering both internal analytics/science and external customer-facing data products; automated monitoring, auditing and alerting for data quality and consistency; conceptual/logical/physical data modelling with application teams; optimise existing ETL; continually improve warehouse operations and performance; and "redesign and optimize existing data processes using AI (e.g., LLM-assisted development, automated documentation, issue resolution)". Stack: Snowflake (preferred), Redshift, Databricks, MySQL/Postgres/Oracle/RDS, AWS and GCP; Kimball/Inmon dimensional modelling; Fivetran, dbt, Python; Airflow and Cloud Composer; Kubernetes and Docker; expert SQL. Requires ~5+ years data engineering and 3+ years building pipelines into a star-schema warehouse/lake.

Compensation: Base ranges published on the company's own live JD — US $148,000–$185,000 USD; **Canada $108,000–$136,000 CAD**, exclusive of equity and benefits. Their stated philosophy is "grounded in a pay-for-performance philosophy… Salary ranges are designed to be competitive and aligned with country specific practices." The CAD band is recorded in `data/company-comp.jsonl` (data_eng / senior, direct).

Notable / other: Benefits — medical/dental/vision, retirement plan with employer contribution, Flexible Time Off, paid parental leave, monthly remote-life and merchant stipends. The Canadian band is markedly lower than the US one (roughly 55–65% of the US figures at typical FX), i.e. Recharge applies steep local-market geo-pay rather than a global band.
