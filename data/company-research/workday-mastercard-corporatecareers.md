# Mastercard — research
Provider/key: workday:mastercard/corporatecareers | company_type: product

What they do: Mastercard is a global payments-technology company (NYSE: MA) operating the card
network and a large portfolio of adjacent products — real-time payments, decision management,
fraud/identity (Ethoca), data and services. Engineering is a first-class, very large in-house org:
the registry currently holds 34 live software postings under this single Workday portal, spanning
backend Java, C/C++, SRE/performance engineering, data engineering, HPE NonStop platform work, and a
sizeable agentic-AI/AI-platform push.

How they describe themselves: NOT VERIFIED from a first-party page this session —
https://www.mastercard.com/ returned HTTP 403 to the fetcher and web search was exhausted, so the
corporate mission/values language ("decency quotient", "Doing Well by Doing Good") is deliberately
NOT asserted here. What can be quoted is from their own JDs: standard corporate benefits framing
("401k with a best-in-class company match", "deferred compensation for eligible roles", "fitness
reimbursement or on-site fitness facilities", tuition reimbursement). Notably, the recurring
mention of **on-site fitness facilities** is itself an office-centric signal.

Size / stage / funding: Very large, profitable, publicly traded (NYSE: MA). Maximum stability; no
funding-runway risk. Exact headcount not verified from a fetched source.

Locations / HQ: US-centric with heavy concentration in O'Fallon, Missouri (the technology hub — the
single largest source of live postings), plus Arlington VA, Purchase NY, Washington DC and Atlanta
GA. Canadian engineering exists in **Toronto** (including the Ethoca unit) and **Vancouver**.

Remote policy: Effectively office-anchored. Every live posting is filed against a named office
(O'Fallon, Arlington, Atlanta, Toronto, Vancouver, Purchase). Across 34 live postings only ONE
remote pay line appears anywhere — the Principal AI Platform Engineer JD lists "Remote - Virginia:
$170,000 - $281,000 USD" as a location-specific band, i.e. a US-state-scoped remote arrangement, not
a location-agnostic one. No general work-from-anywhere policy is stated on any JD.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **NO / effectively disqualifying for this candidate.** The Canadian roles
are Toronto-based and Vancouver-based office roles ("Senior Software Engineer (Java)" — Vancouver;
"Senior Software Engineer (Backend - Java)" — Toronto, Ethoca; "Principal AI Engineer" — Toronto;
"Lead Software Engineer (Java - SDET)" — Vancouver; "Vice President, AI Software Engineering" —
Toronto). None is advertised as remote-within-Canada, and the only remote band on record is scoped
to Virginia. A candidate in Kimberley BC who cannot relocate is not served by these.

Engineering & tech: Java backend (dominant), C/C++, full-stack Java/React, data engineering (big
data & analytics), site reliability and performance engineering, HPE NonStop platform engineering
(classic payments mainframe-adjacent infrastructure), and a distinct agentic-AI track — "Backend/
Platform Agentic AI" roles at II/Senior/Lead, plus a "Principal AI Platform Engineer - AI Center of
Excellence". The AI work is framed as backend/platform engineering rather than model research, which
is substantively the interesting kind.

Compensation transparency: Unusually good. Mastercard posts statutory pay ranges directly on its
JDs, per location, which is what makes the bands below directly sourced rather than estimated.
Observed on live JDs: O'Fallon Software Engineer II $92k–$147k USD; O'Fallon Senior SWE $115k–$184k
USD; Arlington Senior SWE $132k–$212k USD; Atlanta/Arlington Lead SWE $161k–$266k USD; O'Fallon Lead
(C/C++) $140k–$231k USD; Principal AI Platform Engineer $195k–$323k USD (Arlington) / $196k–$323k
(Purchase NY, Washington DC) / $170k–$281k (Remote-Virginia); Vancouver Senior SWE $111k–$160k CAD;
Vancouver Lead SWE (SDET) $127k–$203k CAD; Toronto Principal AI Engineer $138k–$221k CAD. The
Canadian bands are markedly lower than the US ones for the same nominal level.
