# NextPatient — research
Provider/key: ashby:nextpatient | company_type: product

What they do: NextPatient sells patient-experience software to medical practices. Their product plugs
into a doctor's office's existing legacy practice-management system and layers a modern experience on
top: online scheduling, paperless check-in, interactive reminders, an automated waitlist, and
payments. Customers are medical practices ("thousands of offices across the country" — i.e. the US);
the pitch is a better patient experience and less busy-work for staff.

How they describe themselves: "We're looking for engineers to upgrade the patient experience." They
emphasise deliberate smallness and independence: "We're just 40 people, but we're the top-rated
solution in our space... We do things differently than most: we're smaller (by choice), and
self-funded. This allows us to stay focused on our practices instead of trying to impress VCs. As an
engineer joining the team, this means greater ownership." They also ask for "a start-up friendly
temperament (comfortable with ambiguity and rapid change)". Note: these are the only self-descriptions
available — see Sources; no company site was fetched, so the full values set is unknown.

Size / stage / funding: ~40 people, self-funded / bootstrapped (their own words), no VC. Claims to be
the top-rated solution in its niche. No revenue or profitability figure stated.

Locations / HQ: Not stated in any material available locally. UNVERIFIED. Company trips referenced
are all US locations (Park City, Miami, Austin, Chicago, SF, Vegas), and benefits are US-shaped
(401k with match, "11 federally recognized holidays"), which strongly implies a US entity.

Remote policy: Both live postings are listed simply as "Remote", and perks lead with "Freedom to work
from home". No hybrid or office requirement mentioned. They do run a mandatory-feeling year-end
company-wide trip and a mid-year team trip with partners.

Remote-Canada eligibility: UNCLEAR / likely US-only. The postings say "Remote" with no country
qualifier — but the benefits stack (401k, US federal holidays) points at US-only employment. Nothing
in the registry or the JDs confirms Canadian hiring. This must be asked before applying.

Engineering & tech: "Python, JavaScript and Postgres on Linux, with AI growing in importance." Team
structure for an engineer: supported by co-founders, a product manager, a graphic designer / UX
designer, and other experienced engineers. Roles are explicitly full-stack, and each engineer is
made "the go-to person for a slice of the product". Engineers join occasional client calls to gather
feedback directly, and the Staff role stays in close contact with account managers and the sales team.
Seniority bar is high: ~7+ years for Senior, ~10+ years for Staff. Healthcare domain experience is
explicitly not required.

Notable / other: Pay is unusually high for a 40-person bootstrapped company and is stated openly on
the JDs. Benefits: comprehensive health/dental/vision, 401k with match, generous PTO plus an
encouragement to take a real 2-week vacation, 11 US federal holidays, fully paid parental leave after
12 months tenure, equity offered.

Open relevant roles (sample):
- Senior Software Engineer — Remote — $225,000–$265,000 + equity
- Staff Software Engineer — Remote — $250,000–$300,000 + equity
