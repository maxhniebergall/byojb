# Carta — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:carta | company_type: product

What they do: Carta builds software and services for private capital markets — cap-table management, equity/ownership administration, valuations, and fund administration for venture capital, private equity and private credit. In its own words it "connects founders, investors, and limited partners through world-class software, purpose-built for everyone in venture capital, private equity and private credit," and is "creating the end-to-end ERP platform for private markets." Its Fund Administration platform "supports 9,000+ funds and SPVs, representing nearly $185B in assets under management, with tools designed to enhance the strategic impact of fund CFOs." Its pitch is that "Traditional ERP solutions don't work for Private Funds… Private capital markets need a comprehensive software solution to replace outdated spreadsheets and fragmented service providers" — "a new category of software to make private markets look more like public markets."

How they describe themselves: Mission — "our employees set out on a mission to unlock the power of equity ownership for more people in more places. We believe that the problems we solve today unlock the opportunities of tomorrow." On hiring philosophy, its JDs describe a distinctive pooled model: "You'll enter our engineering interview process as part of a pooled hiring model… We're excited to meet people who are energized by complex, ambiguous problems. We look for owners and problem-solvers who are eager to dive into the details of their craft… As you move through our process, we'll transition from the general pool to a team-matching phase." For Staff roles: "We believe in hiring for Carta first, focusing on your core strengths and technical craft rather than a specific team's immediate gap. Staff Engineers at Carta are technical anchors for our business. You don't just own features, you take long-term accountability for the technical health and strategic direction of different domains." No enumerated public values list was retrievable (carta.com/careers returns HTTP 403 to automated fetches).

Size / stage / funding: Large late-stage private company. Trusted by "65,000+ companies in 160+ countries." Recognized by Fortune, Forbes, Fast Company, Inc. and Great Places to Work. Venture-backed; not disclosed-profitable.

Locations / HQ: US-headquartered (San Francisco). Relevant to this search, it runs established Canadian engineering sites in **Toronto** and **Waterloo, Ontario**, plus a **London, UK** office. All current relevant reqs are tagged to one of those three cities.

Remote policy: Office-anchored. Every live relevant req is tagged to a specific city (Toronto, Waterloo, or London) and — decisively — carries a **city-specific salary band** ("$205,600 - $257,000 CAD in Waterloo, Ontario, Canada"). Location-indexed pay tied to named offices is the signature of an in-office or hybrid model rather than a distributed one. Carta's CEO Henry Ward published a public return-to-office plan (2021) and the company has been office-oriented since. Third-party employer profiles list Carta's arrangements as a mix of "Remote or Hybrid · Hybrid · In-Office" depending on role, so some remote arrangements exist somewhere in the company, but none are advertised on the current engineering reqs.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No / not offered on any current req.** All Canadian openings are tagged Toronto or Waterloo, Ontario, with Ontario-city-indexed CAD bands and no remote designation. A Kimberley, BC resident would be applying to an Ontario office role — effectively a relocation-or-nothing proposition. Nothing in the JD text mentions remote work, distributed teams, or timezone flexibility. Treat as not Canada-remote-eligible unless a recruiter says otherwise.

Engineering & tech: Platform and infrastructure work is real and first-class. The req set includes Senior Site Reliability Engineer — "Build and scale our internal platform offerings (compute, storage and networking services) to ensure the reliability and performance of our applications. Design and implement monitoring, alerting…" — plus Developer Experience, Design Systems, Machine Learning Engineer II, and Staff Software Engineer. The Staff description emphasises long-term technical accountability for domains rather than feature ownership. The domain (equity ledgers, valuations, fund accounting) is correctness-critical financial record-keeping.

Compensation (posted on its own live Canadian JDs, CAD base):
- Senior Software Engineer II — $181,050–$213,000 (Toronto and Waterloo, identical)
- Staff Software Engineer — $205,600–$257,000 (Toronto and Waterloo, identical)
The London and SRE reqs post no range.

Notable / other: Carta drew significant public criticism in early 2024 over allegations that it used cap-table customer data to solicit secondary-share trades, after which it announced it was exiting the secondary-trading business. This is a reputational data-governance episode worth knowing about. Carta's own blog also publishes research on remote pay and compensation practices.
