# Voltus — research

Provider/key: lever:voltus | company_type: product

What they do: Voltus is a **virtual power plant (VPP) operator** and distributed-energy
platform. In its own words: "Voltus is a leading company for Distributed Energy Resources
(DERs). Our mission is to be the platform that fulfills the promise of the energy transition.
The Voltus platform connects any DER to any energy market across the US and Canada, providing
flexibility, reliability, and resilience to the grid while generating revenue for our
partners." Commercially it pays energy users to reduce or shift electricity consumption in
response to grid stress, high prices and high emissions, and monetizes that flexibility in
wholesale energy markets (demand response). It reports having brought over 15,000 MW to
market.

How they describe themselves: Three values, stated verbatim on the careers page:
- **Bright** — "You're uncommonly intelligent and committed to applying that gift every single day."
- **Gritty** — "You recognize that working hard is a proven way to get ahead in life."
- **Good** — "You're authentic, honest, and have a positive outlook on life."
Every JD repeats: "We hire teammates who are bright, gritty, and good and who have a genuine
passion for delivering a cleaner energy future and for working in a **fast-paced,
entrepreneurial environment**. We are a remote and virtual company, distributed across US,
Canada, and abroad."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, growth/late-growth stage — recognized on
Forbes' Best Startup Employers list 2022–2024 and the Deloitte Technology Fast 500. Team
described as combining "150 years of energy experience". Still startup-shaped, so
funding-dependence is a live consideration despite the maturity of the market it serves.

Locations / HQ: No office-centric HQ. Team members concentrated in New York, San Francisco,
Boston, **Toronto**, Chicago, Los Angeles and Washington DC, "and other cities".

Remote policy: **Fully remote by design** — "We are a remote and virtual company"; "Voltus is
fully remote, allowing their team to live wherever they want while working towards their
mission." Hiring is restricted to the **US and Canada**, employees are expected to work
primarily from their home country, and the company does not sponsor visas or transfers.
Culture rituals are remote-native: weekly "Veritas" all-hands, annual VoltusFest company
summit, speaker sessions, learning stipends.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** The careers page states hiring is restricted to
the US and Canada; the JDs state the company is "distributed across US, Canada, and abroad";
the Senior Software Engineer JD extracts as `remote` / `canada`; and Toronto is a named team
concentration. Fully remote with no office anchor means a Kimberley, BC base is workable, and
the company already operates in Canadian energy markets (IESO roles are posted). Mountain Time
sits inside the US/Canada spread the team already covers.

Engineering & tech: Backend-led — "core APIs", "data pipelines", customer interfaces, and
"core systems that decarbonize the grid" on a "modern tech stack". Senior engineers are
expected to "assume a technical leadership role in our backend solutions and front end
technologies where appropriate to the team", i.e. teams are backend-primary with some
full-stack expectation. Roles are hired at the senior level onto one of several teams; there
is a dedicated **Senior Data Infrastructure Engineer** line. Domain is grid/energy-market
integration — mission-critical, regulated, and structurally "boring" in the good sense.

Notable / other: Benefits — 12-week parental leave, medical/dental/vision/life, 401(k)
matching, flexible PTO, learning stipends. Voltus posts pay ranges on its JDs.

Pay (from their own live JDs, USD base, per year):
- Software Engineer (early career): **$110,000 – $140,000**
- Senior Software Engineer: **$165,000 – $205,000**
- Senior Data Infrastructure Engineer: **$160,000 – $190,000**

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Data Infrastructure Engineer; Senior Software Engineer;
Software Engineer; Energy Markets Analyst - IESO.
