# Epoch AI — research
Provider/key: lever:epoch-ai | company_type: product (nonprofit research organization)

What they do: Epoch AI is a nonprofit research organization producing data-driven analysis of AI progress. Three stated research areas: what drives AI progress (compute, hardware, investment), measuring AI capabilities through independent evaluations and benchmarks, and forecasting AI's economic and societal impact. They publish research openly, build interactive public data tools (their AI Benchmarking Hub, compute-trend databases), and also do some consulting.

How they describe themselves: Mission is to "help people understand what is happening in AI from a neutral perspective and grounded in the best possible evidence." Stated values: remain relentlessly curious, publish openly, stay impartial, focus on pressing questions, communicate clearly, act with urgency, and prioritize team wellbeing. Notably they pair "act with urgency" with an explicit wellbeing commitment. Their hiring copy is deliberately bias-reducing: "Please do not include a cover letter, photograph, or headshot of yourself, or any personal information that is not relevant to the role," and "We invite anyone who is interested to apply, regardless of background, experience, or credentials."

Size / stage / funding: Began 2021 as a volunteer group; formally established as Epoch AI after their 2022 paper on training-compute trends. Independent nonprofit funded by donors; no funding amounts or headcount disclosed. Described as a "multidisciplinary, global" team. Being donor-funded rather than VC-funded is a different — not necessarily lower — stability profile: no growth-at-all-costs pressure, but grant-cycle dependence.
