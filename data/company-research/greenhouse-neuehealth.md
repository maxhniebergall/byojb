# NeueHealth — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:neuehealth | company_type: product
What they do: Value-based healthcare company. In their own words: "NeueHealth is a value-driven healthcare company grounded in the belief that all health consumers are entitled to high-quality, coordinated care. By uniquely aligning the interests of health consumers, providers, and payors, we help to make healthcare accessible and affordable to all populations across the ACA Marketplace, Medicare, and Medicaid." Two business lines are described: direct clinical care through owned clinics (Centrum Health and Premier Medical) plus affiliated provider partnerships, and an enablement business — "a suite of technology and services scaled centrally and deployed locally" that helps providers "succeed in performance-based arrangements".
How they describe themselves: "Through our value-driven, consumer-centric approach, we are committed to transforming healthcare and creating a better care experience for all." On engineering culture, from the JD: senior engineers "assist in the continuous improvement of the team's productivity and overall design of our systems" and "senior talent plays a key role in building and maintaining a culture which focuses on making sure every engineer progresses in their career in line with personal goals/expectations." They also state they are "building and owning a culture of DevOps and Quality Assurance" and expect engineers to "continuously document your code, framework standards, and team processes". No broader values list was obtainable.
Size / stage / funding: Not stated on any source read. NeueHealth is the renamed successor to Bright Health Group, a US insurtech that exited the insurance market and went through severe financial distress and restructuring — RECALLED, NOT VERIFIED in this session; no first-party page or filing was fetched, so treat the stability picture as unconfirmed but a live concern worth checking before any application.
Locations / HQ: HQ not stated on the sources read. The JD says "Preferred locations for candidates are MN and TX" (Minnesota and Texas). Owned clinics named — Centrum Health and Premier Medical — operate in the US; the business is defined entirely by US programs (ACA Marketplace, Medicare, Medicaid).
Remote policy: The posting is listed "Remote" in the registry, but the JD qualifies it: "Preferred locations for candidates are MN and TX." So remote is tolerated with a strong geographic preference toward two US states.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO, on the available evidence. The entire business is US healthcare-program-specific (ACA/Medicare/Medicaid), the stated location preference is Minnesota and Texas, and no non-US hiring is mentioned anywhere. Not explicitly stated as US-only, but there is nothing to support Canadian employment.
Engineering & tech: Primarily Scala, with C# and SQL also in use. Work spans APIs, data microservices and ETL pipelines "to share data with internal and external partners", plus "interfaces to public data sets to enrich our analytics data stores", and Data Ingestion frameworks deployed in Azure. Batch and streaming pipelines in Scala. Preferred experience: Databricks, Hive and Spark; functional programming in Scala and C#; microservices/SOA distributed systems; hands-on Azure admin and DevOps; Docker/Kubernetes; CI/CD; event sourcing and Big Data architectures; serverless functions. There is a genuine ML/LLM component: "Develop and optimize processes for fine-tuning large language models (LLMs) and implementing Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) frameworks to enhance AI-driven solutions." Around five years of enterprise/commercial experience expected; healthcare IT background "highly preferred"; bachelor's in CS/CE/IS or equivalent required.
Notable / other: No compensation is posted on the JD — this is the only company in this batch with no pay data from its own postings.
