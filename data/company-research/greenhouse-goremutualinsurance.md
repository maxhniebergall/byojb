# Gore Mutual Insurance — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:goremutualinsurance | company_type: product
What they do: Gore Mutual is a Canadian property-and-casualty mutual insurer (founded 1839, one of Canada's oldest). Their words: "At Gore Mutual, we've always set ourselves apart as a modern mutual that does good. Now, we're proudly building on that legacy to transform our company — and our industry — for the better."
How they describe themselves: Mission framing is mutual-first and long-horizon: "Every decision and investment remains anchored in long-term benefits to customers, members, and communities. Come join us." The "modern mutual that does good" line is the central self-description on their postings.
Size / stage / funding: Mutual insurer (no shareholders). **Effective January 1, 2026, Gore joined Beneva — "the country's largest mutual insurance company" — as part of its Property & Casualty operations in Ontario and Western Canada.** During 2026 Gore is combining operations with Unica Insurance, Beneva's Ontario-based subsidiary specializing in niche commercial and personal insurance, "creating a stronger, more diversified mutual insurer with greater scale and long-term stability." So: financially stable and not runway-dependent, but actively mid-integration after an acquisition/merger.
Locations / HQ: Cambridge, Ontario (historic home) and Toronto, Ontario. Both open engineering roles list Cambridge and Toronto.
Remote policy: **Hybrid** — stated as such on the posting.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No.** Canadian employer, but the roles are Ontario hybrid (Cambridge / Toronto). Eastern Time and required office presence make this unworkable from Kimberley BC.
Engineering & tech: The Senior Software Engineer, AWS role is contact-centre platform engineering rather than general backend: designing, developing and maintaining Amazon Connect and supporting AWS services (Lambda, Lex, DynamoDB, API Gateway, S3, CloudWatch). Duties include building contact flows, queues, routing profiles and IVR menus; Lambda functions and Lex bots powering self-service and agent-assist; CloudWatch dashboards, alarms and logging; production troubleshooting across the telephony stack with root-cause analysis; **participation in an on-call rotation and P1/P2 incident triage**; and integrations between Amazon Connect and enterprise systems including Guidewire (ClaimCenter/PolicyCenter) and CRM. The role explicitly partners with Operations, Engineering and vendor teams. A Data Engineer role is also open.
Notable / other: Both engineering postings carry Ontario pay-transparency ranges: Senior Software Engineer, AWS CA$79,500-129,500; Data Engineer CA$64,500-114,500. One relevant posting in the registry ("Cloud Engineer") is a **12-month contract**.
