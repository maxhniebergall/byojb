# Robinhood — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:robinhood | company_type: product

What they do: Robinhood is a **publicly traded US retail brokerage and financial-services platform** (NASDAQ: HOOD), best known for commission-free stock, options and crypto trading via a mobile-first app. The business has broadened well beyond trading into retirement accounts, credit cards and banking, and Web3/crypto infrastructure — all of which are represented in the current engineering openings. Stated mission: "**democratize finance for all**." Their own framing of the opportunity: "An estimated $124 trillion of assets will be inherited by younger generations in the next two decades. The largest transfer of wealth in human history. If you're ready to be at the epicenter of this historic cultural and financial shift, keep reading."

How they describe themselves: The engineering self-description is explicitly high-intensity, quoted from their own JD:
- "We are building an **elite team**, applying frontier technologies to the world's biggest financial problems."
- "We're looking for **bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact.**"
- "**Robinhood isn't a place for complacency**, it's where **ambitious people do the best work of their careers**. We're a **high-performing, fast-moving team** with ethics at the center of everything we do. **Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.**"
On offices: "At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams."

Size / stage / funding: **Public company (NASDAQ: HOOD)**, thousands of employees, well past venture stage. Financially the most established employer in this batch. Note the historical volatility: the 2021 meme-stock episode and subsequent large layoffs in 2022, though the company has since returned to growth and profitability.

Locations / HQ: HQ **Menlo Park, California**. Significant engineering presence in **Toronto, Ontario** — every Canadian req in the registry is Toronto-based — plus **London, UK** and other US sites.

Remote policy: **Hybrid, and firmly so.** Stated verbatim in the Toronto JDs: "This role is based in our **Toronto office, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week**," followed by an explicit statement of belief in in-person work. There is no remote track for these roles.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **NO — disqualifying.** All Canadian openings are Toronto-office roles carrying a stated minimum of three days per week of in-person attendance. A Kimberley, BC candidate who cannot relocate is ineligible in practice. Beyond the office requirement, the company culture explicitly valorizes in-person work, so a remote exception is very unlikely to be granted. Timezone would also be unfavourable: Toronto is Eastern, two hours ahead of Mountain, and HQ is Pacific.

Engineering & tech: The Toronto site hosts genuinely strong infrastructure work — the **Software Platform** group "accelerates developer velocity and increases system reliability by building the foundational platforms and tools that power Robinhood engineering," and within it the **Kubernetes Compute team** builds and operates "a highly available, scalable Kubernetes-powered container platform," including an active **migration to Amazon EKS**, multi-region scalability, and platform integrations for networking and security. Other Toronto reqs cover Infrastructure, Provisioning, Developer Experience (DevX), Developer Productivity & AI Tooling, Ops Platform, and Web3. Stack signals: Kubernetes, AWS/EKS, container provisioning, high-throughput financial systems. This is exactly the platform/infrastructure profile the candidate targets.

Notable / other: Comp is strong and posted (Canadian ranges are stated on the Toronto reqs — e.g. Senior Software Developer bands around CA$166–195K base, Staff around CA$200–235K, Senior Staff CA$234–275K), comfortably above the candidate's target range. Retail brokerage is a heavily regulated domain. The consumer-facing, engagement-driven nature of the product and the "expectations are high" framing are the cultural counterweights.

Open relevant roles (sample, all Toronto): Senior Software Developer, Kubernetes Compute; Senior Software Developer, Infrastructure; Senior Software Developer, Developer Experience (DevX); Software Developer, Provisioning; Staff Software Engineer, Web3; Senior Staff Software Developer, Developer Productivity & AI Tooling.
