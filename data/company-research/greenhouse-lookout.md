# Lookout — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:lookout | company_type: product

What they do: Lookout, Inc. is an enterprise mobile-security company. Its platform protects mobile endpoints — Mobile Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) that continuously monitors phones and tablets for social-engineering and phishing attacks, malware, software vulnerabilities and anomalous activity. In their own words, the company delivers "advanced protection for the most vulnerable element of any enterprise security strategy — human error and manipulation," positioning the mobile device as "the frontline of human-centric attacks."

How they describe themselves: "Lookout, Inc. is a globally recognized cybersecurity leader… Cloud-native by design, the Lookout platform offers rapid, scalable deployment and simplified security operations." They emphasize the scale and credibility of their analysis platform: it is "capable of analyzing millions of apps on a daily basis," and "our analysis is trusted by Google to help identify potentially harmful applications before they enter the Play Store and by the world's leading telecommunications providers to protect their customers." Engineering framing is research-flavoured — "these are challenging problems and you must be open-minded to tackling new exploratory areas," plus hackathons and internal innovation fairs.

Size / stage / funding: Private, long-established (founded 2007), heavily VC-funded historically, and now materially smaller than at its peak after two deliberate divestitures. It sold its consumer mobile-security business to F-Secure (completed 2023) and its Cloud Security / SSE business to Fortra (May 2025), stating it would "focus its resources and investments on Endpoint Security." The result is a focused pure-play enterprise mobile-security company, but one that has shrunk by shedding two whole business lines rather than by growing into one.

Locations / HQ: US-headquartered (Boston, having relocated from San Francisco) with a globally distributed workforce; engineering roles are posted by country rather than by office.

Remote policy: Remote-friendly and posted by country. The relevant requisition is explicitly location-typed "Remote, Canada." No published async/handbook culture material was found (their site returned 403 to automated fetching), so remote practice beyond the posting itself is unverified.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** The Staff Backend Software Engineer (Java/Scala) posting is titled "Remote, Canada" and includes a Canadian pay-transparency block stating the range applies "across all Canadian locations." That is a country-wide remote req with no metro attached, so a British Columbia resident is directly eligible. The team collaborates with security research, data ingestion and ML groups whose centre of gravity is US-based, so expect US-hours overlap; nothing indicates a hard timezone requirement.

Engineering & tech: The role sits on the Security Intelligence team, which owns "the detection engines, analysis systems, and other core intellectual property at Lookout" — described as "the fundamental technological building blocks of the Security Platform." Concretely: a massively scalable platform performing static analysis of iOS and Android applications and applying behavioural and malware-detection policy; continuous acquisition and processing of mobile apps; threat hunting against state-level actors and their infrastructure; inventing new app-analysis techniques, engines and domain-specific languages. Stack and skills: Java/Scala with functional programming "highly desired," high-volume low-latency distributed SaaS, large-scale production systems on AWS and GCP, strong emphasis on automated testing. Work spans cloud systems and, at times, mobile platforms and reverse engineering of mobile runtimes.

Notable / other: Compensation is disclosed on Canadian postings. Their stated structure is "base + bonus + equity + benefits," with the posted number being base only, and individual pay set by work location within the national range. Google Play integrity and carrier partnerships are meaningful external validation of the analysis platform.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Backend Software Engineer (Java/Scala) — Remote, Canada ($130,000–$170,000 CAD base); Staff Software Engineer – ML/AI.
