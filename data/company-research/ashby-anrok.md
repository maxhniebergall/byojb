# Anrok — research
Provider/key: ashby:anrok | company_type: product

What they do: Anrok is a **sales-tax automation platform for software businesses**. In their words: "Anrok is the leading tax automation platform enabling businesses to expand globally without compliance complexity." They connect to a customer's billing and payment systems to automate tax monitoring, rate calculation and return filing end-to-end across "50 US jurisdictions and 100+ countries". Their framing of the problem: "As the digital economy has grown 6x over the last decade, software businesses have gone from not worrying about sales tax to needing to monitor exposure, calculate rates, and file returns… This creates a critical bottleneck for companies that should be able to transact with customers everywhere."

How they describe themselves: Mission framing is "making compliant digital commerce a reality for companies big and small". No formal values list is published in the JD text. The engineering-culture signals they choose to state are unusually substantive and read as anti-hustle: they want someone who will "own their impact and find simple solutions to complex problems that are tailored to our needs"; who "think[s] through problems thoroughly to ensure we're building the right solution to the right problem at the right time, and you will push back when necessary"; whose "designs take into account current and future needs, technical debt, and maintenance, and you can clearly articulate the tradeoffs"; and who "level[s] up the people around you". They also want evidence of hands-on AI experimentation: "you've built something with it… that you can walk us through." Notably absent: "fast-paced", "wear many hats", "rocketship", "scrappy".

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed — "backed by over $100M from leading investors including Sequoia, Spark, Index, and Khosla Ventures." They describe themselves as having "the equity upside of an early-stage startup with the product-market fit of a later-stage company", i.e. self-identifying as early-stage by equity terms while claiming established PMF. Exact headcount and funding round are **unverified** (WebSearch exhausted this session). Customer traction claimed: "40% of Forbes Top 50 AI companies", "20% of Forbes Top 100 Cloud companies", named logos include Notion, Anthropic and Cursor.

Locations / HQ: Three office "hubs" — **San Francisco, New York City, and Salt Lake City**. San Francisco is where daily lunch is provided, suggesting it is the primary office.

Remote policy: Stated verbatim: "At Anrok, we embrace a dynamic and flexible hybrid work environment based out of our growing office hubs — San Francisco, New York City, and Salt Lake City where we collaborate in-person 3 days per week. **For employees located outside of these hub cities, we offer a fully remote setup U.S only.**" So: hybrid 3 days/week in-hub, or fully remote but restricted to the United States. Home-office setup stipend provided; annual team offsites and in-person gatherings around the hubs.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified no.** The company's own JD text states the remote option is "U.S only". Postings list "Remote, San Francisco, Salt Lake City, New York City" — the bare "Remote" there means US-remote, per the policy paragraph in the same document. There is no mention of Canada or Canadian entities anywhere in the three live JDs. This is an explicit, first-party disqualification rather than an inference.

Engineering & tech: **GCP, Pulumi, Postgres, TypeScript** — and they explicitly say "prior experience is not required" with that specific stack. The Infrastructure role owns "Anrok's reliability, security, scalability, and performance" and drives infrastructure technical decisions. Named example project areas are genuinely infrastructural: "Improve system observability and provide tools to enable product engineers to own application observability"; "Scale our database layer through sharding, replication, and splitting out services when it makes sense"; "Refactor existing systems to take into account present and evolving needs." Requirements: 5+ years software engineering, 5+ years operating cloud-deployed software at scale using config-as-code, relational-database-at-scale experience a plus, a security mindset. No public engineering blog or handbook was found.

Notable / other: The company posts pay ranges on its own JDs (see Open roles), which is a transparency positive. Benefits: medical/dental/vision covered 100%, One Medical membership, flexible sick benefits, annual learning-and-development stipend for books/courses/conferences, home-office setup stipend, annual team offsites, daily lunch and snacks in the SF office. They append an anti-recruitment-fraud notice: "Anrok does not recruit via WhatsApp or any third-party messaging platforms. All official communication… comes exclusively from @Anrok.com email addresses."

Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineer, Infrastructure — $140K–$220K + equity (Remote); Software Engineer (7+ years of experience) — $165K–$250K + equity; Software Engineer (4+ years of experience) — $140K–$220K + equity. All USD.
