# ANVIL — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:anvil | company_type: product

What they do: ANVIL is a Canadian defence-technology company building analytical tooling, automation and machine-learning capability for the defence industry, extended to public safety, law enforcement and national-security organizations. Its stated purpose is to help those organizations "discover, manage, enrich, fuse, and exploit" the information available to them, in support of "Information Dominance and Decision Advantage". The product is data-platform / analytics software rather than hardware.

How they describe themselves: From their own JD text — "ANVIL is a trusted partner in the defence industry, delivering cutting-edge solutions that enhance military capabilities and operational effectiveness." On mission: "This is more than just a job; you'll be part of a team of dedicated professionals who share a common goal: to increase the safety and security of Western democracies through the effective use of data. Our workplace is not just a job; it's a community of like-minded people working together to make a positive impact on the world we live in." On the people they want: "We value people who have an ingrained sense of accountability to the team around them. As an ideal candidate you are not only technically qualified, but you demonstrate a strong work ethic and take pride in your craft." Notably absent from all three live JDs: any "fast-paced", "hustle", "rocketship", "wear many hats" or "move fast" language.

Size / stage / funding: Not stated on any fetched or local source. Uses BambooHR for recruiting (typical of small-to-mid private companies) and has a single named office. Private; funding stage unverified. Headcount unknown — do not assume.

Locations / HQ: Ottawa, Ontario, Canada — 55 Murray Street office is the one named location. No other offices mentioned.

Remote policy: Their own JDs state "Job Type: Full Time Remote (Hybrid option available for those in the Ottawa area - 55 Murray Street Office)" and, under Work Environment, "Hybrid work model combining remote flexibility with…" (truncated in the captured body). The default is full-time remote; hybrid is the *option*, not the requirement.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** This is a Canadian company headquartered in Ottawa, salaries are quoted in CAD, roles are posted as Full Time Remote, and the prerequisites require the candidate be "Eligible for Government of Canada Secret or Top Secret security clearance" — which effectively requires Canadian residency/citizenship rather than excluding it. Timezone: Ottawa is Eastern; a Mountain-Time worker is 2 hours behind, which is workable. Caveat: the security-clearance requirement is a real gate (clearance processing can be slow, and some defence work may be facility-bound) — the JDs do not say whether any on-site presence is needed for cleared work. That specific point is **unverified**.

Engineering & tech: Backend is Python-centric — Django/DRF, Flask, FastAPI, Sanic; PostgreSQL with query optimization and schema design called out as expert-level; Elasticsearch as the NoSQL store; Redis for caching; Celery/RQ for async task processing; Docker with multi-stage production builds; REST and WebSockets APIs. Desired: AWS/GCP/Azure, microservices and distributed systems, Prometheus/Grafana observability, Kafka and Airflow stream processing, pytest, CI/CD. The senior backend role explicitly includes advising engineering leadership on architecture, mentoring, code review and internal engineering standards — i.e. scoped ownership of backend systems, not full-stack sprawl. Separate frontend, data-science and DevOps functions are named as collaborators, which indicates specialised rather than generalist roles. No public engineering blog or handbook was found.

Notable / other: Domain is mission-critical and "boring" in the good sense — defence/national-security data infrastructure, not a consumer growth product. Prerequisite for the senior role is a CS/SWE degree *or* 10+ years of backend experience. Benefits stated: flexible health package through Equitable, "industry-leading employer retirement contributions match", competitive salaries. Ethical note for the reader's own judgement: this is defence/law-enforcement/surveillance-adjacent work ("exploit the information available to them", "Information Dominance").

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Backend Engineer (CAD $125,000–$165,000, remote); Intermediate Backend Engineer (CAD $105,000–$145,000, remote); Intermediate DevOps Engineer (CAD $120,000–$160,000, Ottawa, Ontario).
