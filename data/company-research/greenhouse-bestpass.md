What they do: Fleetworthy sells a fleet-readiness software suite for commercial trucking — safety & compliance (DOT/audit readiness), toll management, and weigh-station bypass. Their own words: "We bring together fleet safety, compliance, toll management, and weigh station bypass to help fleets operate more efficiently," and their JDs claim they are "Trusted by 75% of the top fleets in North America," with "the most adopted toll management solution and largest weigh station bypass network," supporting "millions of vehicles and drivers."
