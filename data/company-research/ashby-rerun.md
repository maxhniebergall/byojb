# Rerun — research
Provider/key: ashby:rerun | company_type: product
What they do: Building "a new multimodal data stack for physical AI" — tooling for extraction, ingestion, storage, querying, streaming and visualization of temporal multimodal data (robotics/AR-VR/simulation logs). Their open-source visualizer is widely used ("already loved by some of the best teams in the world"). The commercial product, **Rerun Hub**, is a catalog and storage engine that they say "already handles petabytes of robot training data for early customers", now being opened to more teams.
How they describe themselves: "Large parts of the physical-world economy will be transformed by these technologies, and we're building the data infrastructure and tools that enable it to happen." On culture, verbatim from their JDs: "We've put together an uncommonly talented tech team, value agency and helpfulness highly, and expect everyone to take broad responsibility for what they build"; they want people "energised by ambiguity and iteration, and who enjoy turning rough ideas into solid systems"; "a helpful attitude and a curious mind: we're all figuring this out together!". Notably low on hustle language — no "fast-paced"/"rocketship" phrasing at all.
Size / stage / funding: Small — 4 total open roles at scan time, teams described as "small" and "newly formed". Exact headcount, funding stage and investors NOT verified: no first-party domain appears in their JD text and web search was exhausted this session. Early-stage with paying early customers is the honest read.
Locations / HQ: Remote company **headquartered in Stockholm, Sweden**. The team is spread across European and US-East timezones; they meet in person for a week roughly once a quarter.
Remote policy: Remote-first by construction. Core constraint stated verbatim: "Everyone is available for synchronous work between 1:30–5:00pm CET." Quarterly week-long in-person offsites. Follow-the-sun on-call for the infrastructure role.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, and structurally unattractive.** Their own text: "Given our current team distribution, candidates outside of Europe or the US will be considered on a case-by-case basis to ensure there's enough overlap for effective collaboration." Canada is neither included nor excluded — it would be a case-by-case exception. The harder problem is the clock: 1:30–5:00pm CET is **4:30–8:00am Pacific/5:30–9:00am Mountain**, so the mandatory synchronous window lands before the start of a normal Kimberley BC workday. Roles are additionally tagged "East Coast, US" and "Remote Europe".
Engineering & tech: Rust throughout (async Rust is the core stack), Go for the Kubernetes control plane, Kubernetes operator pattern (CRDs, controllers, controller-runtime/kubebuilder/operator-sdk), AWS, Terraform/OpenTofu, production observability, CI/CD. Data-side concepts: high-throughput time-series/analytical databases, indexing and query execution, Apache Arrow / DataFusion, GPU workloads. They frame infrastructure as software: "You think of infrastructure as software you build, not just systems you operate."
Notable / other: Six weeks paid vacation, competitive cash and equity, any hardware/software you need. Infra role is customer-adjacent — deploying Hub into customer cloud accounts and doing "deep plumbing" for onboarding. They explicitly hire across levels for the infra role and invite off-spec applications.
Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineer, Infrastructure (Remote, East Coast US); Software Engineer (Rust) - Backend (Remote / Remote Europe / East Coast US); Robotics Machine Learning Engineer.
Pay: **No compensation stated on any posting** and no first-party pay page reachable this session. No band written — a Stockholm-HQ seed/early company whose numbers I cannot verify is exactly where a guess would be worthless.
