# Alteryx — research
Provider/key: workday:alteryx/alteryxcareers | company_type: product

What they do: Alteryx sells an analytics automation platform — data preparation, blending, analytics and reporting workflows aimed at business analysts and data teams, with a long-standing desktop Designer product plus a cloud platform. Its engineering problems are genuinely infrastructural: a data plane, an orchestration and job-execution layer, distributed systems, and an internal developer platform.

How they describe themselves: The recruiting language is restrained and unusually free of hustle framing — postings simply describe the team and the systems ("Alteryx is searching for a Senior Software Engineer. This position is remote-friendly"). On pay they state: "Alteryx is committed to fair, equitable, and transparent compensation. Final compensation will be determined by various factors such as your relevant work experience, education, certifications, skills, and geographic location." No values manifesto was fetched (the company is geographically disqualifying, so fetch budget was spent elsewhere) — treat the culture read as JD-only and therefore partial.

Size / stage / funding: Formerly NYSE-listed (AYX); taken private in 2024 by Clearlake Capital and Insight Partners. That is from background knowledge rather than a source fetched here — treat as unverified. Private-equity ownership is worth noting as a stability signal in both directions: no runway risk, but PE-owned software companies commonly run cost-reduction programmes.

Locations / HQ: HQ Irvine, California. The live engineering roles are posted as "US - Remote" with one "NSW, Australia - Remote".

Remote policy: Genuinely remote for engineering — eight of the ten postings on record are "US - Remote", one is "NSW, Australia - Remote", and one Principal role carries "#LI-REMOTE" plus a $150/month "Connectivity Plus" remote-work stipend and a $200/year home-office reimbursement. So the company is comfortable with distributed engineers; the constraint is the country, not the arrangement.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO (as posted) — verified from the JDs. Every remote engineering posting is scoped "US - Remote" (or Australia), and each carries "This position involves access to software/technology that is subject to U.S. export controls. Any job offer made will be contingent upon the applicant's capacity to serve in compliance with U.S. export controls." Salary language is likewise stated as "the salary range for this role in the United States." No Canadian entity or Canada-eligible posting appears in the registry.

Engineering & tech: The role set is unusually infra-heavy: Senior Software Engineer - Orchestration & Job Execution, Sr. Software Engineer Dataplane, Principal Software Engineer/Architect Distributed Systems, Sr. Developer Platform Engineer, Lead Site Reliability Engineer, Staff Python Software Engineer, and a Principal Agentic Product Design Engineer. Stack signals: C++/Python hybrid repositories, CI/CD pipelines and code-quality tooling (docs jobs, coverage, static analysis), distributed systems, developer platform.

Notable / other: Pay is disclosed on US postings. Observed base ranges: Software Engineer $103,300-$133,850 (plus a 15% target bonus); Senior Software Engineer - Orchestration & Job Execution and Sr. Software Engineer both $119,000-$153,000; Sr. Software Engineer, Dataplane $118,000-$154,000; Sr. Developer Platform Engineer $136,000-$177,000; Principal Software Engineer, Distributed Systems $215,000-$300,000. The jump from senior to principal is very large.

Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Software Engineer, Distributed Systems; Sr. Developer Platform Engineer; Senior Software Engineer - Orchestration & Job Execution; Sr. Software Engineer, Dataplane; Lead Site Reliability Engineer; Principal, Agentic Product Design Engineer.
