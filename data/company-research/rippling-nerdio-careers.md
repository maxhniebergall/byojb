# Nerdio — research
Provider/key: rippling:nerdio-careers | company_type: product

What they do: Nerdio builds **management and automation software layered on top of Microsoft Azure Virtual Desktop and Windows 365 Cloud PC**. The platform lets enterprises and managed service providers deploy, provision and operate virtual desktop estates without hand-rolling Azure infrastructure — automating user provisioning, application deployment, image management, and (a headline feature) **cost optimization / auto-scaling of Azure compute**. Two product lines: Nerdio Manager for Enterprise and Nerdio Manager for MSP. Customers are enterprise IT departments and the MSP channel; the go-to-market is tightly coupled to the Microsoft ecosystem.

How they describe themselves: A "remote-first team that values innovation, transparency, and collaboration." They describe thinking big "while maintaining a culture that feels like a small group of friends," and invite people to "bring your whole self to the table, own what makes you unique, embrace big ideas and solve complex challenges — whether in cloud automation, problem-solving, design, storytelling, or strategy." Community giving runs through a "Nerdio Cares" program with dedicated volunteer days off. The tone is notably free of hustle/rocketship framing.

Size / stage / funding: Private. Originally founded **2005 as Adar**, an IT-services and virtual-infrastructure management provider; the Nerdio product was spun out into its own company in **January 2020**. Funding: $8M Series A (2020) → **$117M Series B** (Updata Partners, Dec 2022) → **$500M Series C (March 2025)**, which pushed the valuation past **$1B — unicorn status**. Roughly **300 employees**, with stated plans to scale headcount for a growing global customer base. A twenty-year operating history and a very large, recent war chest make this materially more stable than a typical Series C.

Locations / HQ: HQ **Chicago, Illinois**. Team distributed globally; no office attendance requirement surfaced.

Remote policy: **Remote-first.** The team is "spread across the globe, working from wherever they do their best thinking." They explicitly operate on video calls plus **async communication**, with in-person connection handled through optional local group meetups, one-on-one coffees, and periodic company events rather than mandated office days. Internal IT is built for remote (endpoint management via Intune), and they state the practice is designed to "ensure effective operation across time zones."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified YES.** The live Senior Software Engineer req lists its location as "United States Remote; **Canada Remote**; United Kingdom Remote" — Canada is a named, first-class hiring geography, not an exception. No city or office anchor. HQ in Chicago is Central Time, one hour ahead of Mountain, so working hours are close to ideal. The only mild caveat is that a UK-inclusive posting implies some meetings may be pulled earlier to catch European colleagues.

Engineering & tech: Deeply **Microsoft/Azure-native** — the product is essentially cloud infrastructure automation, so the engineering work is infrastructure work: Azure resource orchestration, VM/session-host lifecycle management, autoscaling logic, cost modelling, identity (Entra/Intune), and multi-tenant management planes for MSPs. Predominantly a .NET/C# and PowerShell shop given the Azure and Windows-management surface area. They are also building an **internal AI Enablement Platform**, with senior engineering roles attached to it that collaborate directly with executive leadership. Remote engineering practice is supported with tooling explicitly chosen for distributed work.

Notable / other: The business is unusually "utility"-shaped — customers buy Nerdio to make their infrastructure cheaper and less painful to run, which is a durable, non-hype value proposition and a defensive-spend category. The main strategic risk is **platform dependency**: Nerdio's fortunes are tied to Microsoft's Azure Virtual Desktop / Windows 365 roadmap, and Microsoft could absorb parts of the management layer natively. Also note the ATS is Rippling (`ats.rippling.com`), which is a real, fetchable board rather than a client-rendered one.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (United States Remote; Canada Remote; United Kingdom Remote).
