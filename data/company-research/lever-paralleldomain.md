# Parallel Domain — research
Provider/key: lever:paralleldomain | company_type: product

What they do: Parallel Domain builds **synthetic data and simulation platforms for autonomous
vehicle and robotics teams**. In their own words on the JD: *"Parallel Domain builds synthetic data
and simulation platforms for autonomous vehicle and robotics teams."* The product line is
photorealistic reconstruction from real-world capture data plus deterministic sensor simulation
(camera, lidar, radar), used to test perception stacks at scale. Two named products: **PD Replica**,
which converts drive/flight logs into simulation-ready neural reconstructions, and **PD Sim**, which
runs test scenarios with configurable sensor simulation at scale. The company frames its mission as
*"closing the gap between simulation and reality"* — turning messy fleet data into production-grade
testing assets with measurable sim-to-real validation.

How they describe themselves: Engineering-led and technically substantive rather than hype-forward.
The Product Engineering team *"owns the customer-facing product and service layer: the self-serve
platform for productizing ML reconstruction pipelines, our high-fidelity simulation-as-a-service
product, the web applications and dashboards customers interact with directly, and the centralized
backend services and APIs that connect them."* Expectations are ownership-flavoured: *"design and
build features and services end to end - from API design through to production - and take real
ownership of the reliability and quality of what you ship"*; engineers who thrive there *"are
comfortable across multiple layers of the stack, move well from a requirement to a design, and take
operational ownership of what they build."* No hustle/rocketship language appears in the JD.

Size / stage / funding: Venture-backed private company; small engineering org (only two live
relevant roles). Serves automotive, drone, robotics, agriculture, trucking and defense markets.
Customer logos published on their site include **Zenseact, Toyota, Woven Planet and Humble Motors**
— serious industrial buyers. Specific headcount and funding stage are not disclosed on the company
site.

Locations / HQ: US-founded (Bay Area lineage in the AV-tooling space) with a **Vancouver, British
Columbia** engineering presence — both live relevant roles are posted there.

Remote policy: **Hybrid.** Both live postings carry the location string "Vancouver, British
Columbia, Canada, hybrid". No remote-anywhere or distributed-team language appears.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Canada yes, but Vancouver-hybrid — not remote.** This is a Canadian
employment relationship (roles are posted in BC, paid in CAD), but the postings are explicitly
hybrid against a Vancouver office. Vancouver is roughly a 10-hour drive or a flight plus connection
from Kimberley, so a hybrid office expectation is not satisfiable without relocation.

Engineering & tech: **Python backend services, RESTful and gRPC APIs, relational databases, React
frontends, and data pipelines**, plus ML reconstruction pipelines and sensor simulation underneath.
The Development Platform role is internal-platform work; the Product role is the customer-facing
service layer. Domain is genuinely deep — neural reconstruction, deterministic multi-sensor
simulation, sim-to-real validation — and the work is data/ML-infrastructure shaped rather than CRUD.

Notable / other: The defense vertical is listed among served industries, which may or may not
matter to a candidate. Company publishes pay ranges on its own JDs, which is a good transparency
signal.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer - Product; Senior Software Engineer,
Development Platform; Senior Site Reliability Engineer.

Pay (stated on their own JDs): **$155,000–$175,000 CAD** base for both live senior roles
(Product and Development Platform) — competitive for Canadian senior engineering.
