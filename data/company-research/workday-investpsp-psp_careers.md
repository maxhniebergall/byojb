# PSP Investments (Public Sector Pension Investment Board) — research
Provider/key: workday:investpsp/psp_careers | company_type: product

**Registry data-quality flag:** the registry name is "Investpsp" (derived from the Workday tenant slug). The correct entity name is **PSP Investments / Public Sector Pension Investment Board** (Investissements PSP). Worth correcting in the registry.

What they do: PSP Investments is one of Canada's largest pension investment managers — a Crown corporation that invests the funds for the pension plans of the federal public service, the Canadian Forces, the Royal Canadian Mounted Police and the Reserve Force. In its own words: "We're one of Canada's largest pension investors, with **CAD$320.6 billion of net assets as of March 31, 2026**." It invests across public markets, private equity, credit investments, real estate, infrastructure and natural resources.

How they describe themselves: The technology pitch in its own JD: "You will have the chance to shape a flagship AI platform at one of Canada's largest pension investment managers, with direct impact on how investment decisions are researched, with access to frontier models and proprietary data at scale, within a high-caliber, collaborative team." On rewards: "We offer a tailored employee experience and competitive total rewards and benefits package designed to attract and retain global diverse talent, reward performance, and reinforce business strategies and priorities." No enumerated values list appears in the postings.

Size / stage / funding: A federal Crown corporation managing CAD$320.6B in net assets. Institutionally about as stable as an employer gets in Canada — no funding risk, no runway, no layoff-cycle exposure of the kind that affects venture-backed tech. Roughly 1,000+ employees.

Locations / HQ: Headquartered in **Ottawa**, with its **principal business office in Montréal** (where both relevant tech roles sit), plus offices in New York, London and Hong Kong.

Remote policy: **Hybrid, stated explicitly in the benefits list of its own JD:** "• A hybrid work model with a mix of in-office and remote days." Anchored to the Montréal office. No fully remote option is offered on either relevant req. PSP has been publicly recognised for supporting employees through the shift to remote work and "in preparation to the adoption of a hybrid workplace environment," confirming hybrid is the settled end state rather than a transitional stance.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No.** Both live relevant roles (Senior Applied AI Engineer; Developer, GenAI and Digital Workplace) are posted to **Montréal** under an explicit hybrid model requiring in-office days. This is Canadian employment but Québec-office-anchored. A Kimberley, BC resident who cannot relocate is not eligible, and the Mountain-to-Eastern timezone gap (2 hours) would additionally shift working hours early. Treat as not remote-eligible.

Engineering & tech: A small but genuine in-house technology function building what the JD calls "a flagship AI platform" for investment research, with "access to frontier models and proprietary data at scale." The two relevant reqs are a Senior Applied AI Engineer and a Developer for GenAI and Digital Workplace. The work is applied LLM/GenAI engineering serving internal investment teams as customers rather than external product users. Scale of proprietary financial data is a genuine engineering asset. No public engineering blog or handbook found; no stack details disclosed in the postings.

Notable / other: Named a **Montréal Top Employer for five consecutive years**. Benefits are strong and family-oriented: gender-inclusive paid family leave (up to 26 weeks for primary caregivers, 5 weeks for secondary), personalized family-building support, vacation days available from day one, and summer Friday afternoons off.
