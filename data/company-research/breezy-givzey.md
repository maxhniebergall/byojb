# Givzey — research
Provider/key: breezy:givzey | company_type: product
What they do: Givzey sells fundraising technology to nonprofits — a Gift Agreement Platform for "smart gift agreements, dynamic workflows, automated pledge reminders, and intelligent invoicing" that "modernizes the multi-year giving process, enabling nonprofits to secure, manage, and forecast commitments". It also owns Version2.ai, which it calls "the world's first and only fully autonomous fundraiser, the Virtual Engagement Officer (VEO)" — AI workers that "operate donor portfolios, build relationships, and secure gifts on their own." Customers are universities and health foundations: William & Mary, Texas State University, Boston Children's Hospital.
How they describe themselves: Heavy growth/mission framing — "Join the Future of Fundraising at Givzey!", "one of the fastest-growing and most innovative technology companies serving the nonprofit sector, on a mission to unlock more generosity through AI-powered donor engagement." Claimed traction: "In just three years, Givzey's platform has already helped organizations raise $10M+ through autonomous engagement, including individual gifts as large as $100,000"; "72% of Smart Gift Agreements are confirmed within 24 hours". On the role: "This is not a 'keep the lights on' devops role"; "You're comfortable wearing multiple hats."
Size / stage / funding: Not disclosed on their site — no headcount or funding stage stated. Roughly three years old by their own account; the AI Platform role is described as helping "scale the engineering organization", implying a small team.
Locations / HQ: Boston, Massachusetts.

The role explicitly spans cloud infra, IaC strategy, CI/CD, AI platform and DR/resiliency; example day: "debugging IAM permissions… building a Pulumi module, improving GitHub Actions, tuning ECS workloads, or helping an ML engineer deploy a SageMaker endpoint."
