# Holepunch — research
Provider/key: recruitee:holepunch | company_type: product

What they do: Holepunch builds peer-to-peer infrastructure. Its own docs call it "the P2P Company" and position its platform, Pear, as the entry point to "the Internet of Peers." Per its JD: "we are not just developing software; we are redefining the internet's architecture. Our pioneering P2P development platform, built on our open-source technology stack, Pear, eliminates the need for traditional server infrastructure. Pear platform facilitates direct deployment of applications from the developer's machine to the user." Their flagship application is Keet, a P2P communications app (messaging, file sharing, collaboration) "all while maintaining user sovereignty over personal data."

How they describe themselves: Mission-framed around autonomy and privacy — "Work on groundbreaking technology that prioritizes user autonomy and privacy", "Collaborate with a team of thinkers and innovators dedicated to reshaping the digital landscape", "Contribute to a future where technology serves humanity, not the other way around", and "join Holepunch. Together, we can create a more secure, inclusive, and P2P decentralized future." The tone is ideological/technical rather than commercial; there is no sales or customer-pressure language anywhere in the JD.

Size / stage / funding: Not verified. No funding, headcount or ownership information appears on docs.pears.com or in the JD, and no other first-party page was reachable this session. Registry shows only 2 live postings. Treat all commonly-repeated claims about its backing as unverified — none could be confirmed here.

Locations / HQ: No HQ stated on any page reached. The team is described as "remote, globally distributed."

Remote policy: Fully remote, worldwide. The posting title itself is "Senior Node.js Software Engineer (100% Remote, Worldwide)" and the requirements include "Comfort working in a remote, globally distributed team."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes, as far as the company's own words go.** The role is advertised "100% Remote, Worldwide", and the application form asks "Considering the role is remote, from which country will you be working?" — i.e. country is an open question rather than a filter. It also asks for expected salary in annual USD, implying a USD-denominated global pay model rather than country payroll. Not proven to be Canadian *employment* (it may be contractor-style engagement); worth confirming.

Engineering & tech: Node.js-centric P2P stack, all open source, "over 1500 public npm modules". Named components from their docs: **Pear** (installable peer-to-peer runtime, development and deployment platform for mobile, desktop and terminal), **Bare** (cross-platform standalone JavaScript runtime), **Hypercore** (distributed append-only log for data replication), **Hyperbee** (database layer over shared data structures), **Hyperdrive** (P2P filesystems), and **Hyperswarm / HyperDHT** (peer discovery and connection networking primitives). The advertised role wants production-scale Node.js, modular/maintainable code, authoring and maintaining reusable npm modules, testing and debugging, and "deep interest or experience with P2P technologies or distributed systems." Preferred: native Node.js extensions in C/C++ or binding JS to lower-level code, optimizing network protocols for latency and throughput, advanced networking concepts, and prior OSS contributions in P2P/decentralized tech. The application form also asks about Chromium experience, suggesting desktop-runtime work.

Notable / other: Documentation lives at docs.pears.com; Keet at keet.io. Public artefacts (npm modules, docs) make the engineering culture unusually inspectable before applying. The application asks unusually substantive questions (an interesting recent project, networking/distributed-systems knowledge, OSS links) rather than boilerplate.
