# Inngest — research
Provider/key: ashby:inngest | company_type: product

What they do: Inngest is a developer-tools company selling durable execution — a queueing and workflow engine that lets application developers write long-running, retryable, step-based functions without managing queues, state stores or schedulers themselves. Their words: "Systems Engineers at Inngest build the core of our product: the durable execution layer, queueing system, state stores, and the distributed systems that connect them together." It has both an open-source project and a hosted cloud platform (cloud dashboard, dev server, CLIs, SDKs).

How they describe themselves: "Inngest is solving long standing developer problems in a novel way which means we're creating first-of-it's-kind solutions. We're building tools that developers will use in their everyday workflow so efforts to improve UX and performance are primary. Importantly, you'll need a strong product mindset and an interest in developer tools." The engineering values stated in the JD are unusually aligned with deep systems work: "You understand engineering trade-offs and can make correct judgement calls on approaches available"; "You understand how to observe, monitor, and maintain the systems you design"; "You appreciate simplicity, even if it's harder to design and build." Notably absent: hustle/rocketship language. Present instead: an expectation of breadth beyond engineering — by day 90 "You'll have learned where you can contribute outside of the engineering domain (growth, UX design, DevRel, etc.) and contributed ideas around product and growth."

Size / stage / funding: Small venture-backed developer-tools startup (seed/Series A scale). Headcount not disclosed; the JD's reference to "our existing systems engineering team is in San Francisco" implies a small, co-located core team. Funding specifics were not verified in this pass.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco, California. The systems engineering team is SF-based and "works in person several days a week".

Remote policy: Partially remote within the US only, with a strong in-person default for this specific team. Verbatim: "This role requires working time overlap with US PST. Ideally, you'd live in the SF Bay Area, but for strong candidates you may live anywhere in the United States. Our existing systems engineering team is in San Francisco and works in person several days a week." The registry's location field, "US/Remote, San Francisco", is accurate.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — verified negative from the JD. Remote is bounded explicitly to "anywhere in the United States", with SF Bay Area preferred and the team in-office several days a week. The PST-overlap requirement itself would have been workable from Mountain Time (a one-hour offset); it is the US-only employment boundary and the in-person team norm that disqualify it.

Engineering & tech: Stack, verbatim: "Backend: Go, Postgres, Redis, Clickhouse, PubSub/Kafka, k8s"; "APIs: gRPC internally, GraphQL and REST APIs for UI"; "Hosted on AWS, GCP and Bare Metal"; tooling "Github, Linear, Slack, Notion, Figma". The work is genuine distributed-systems depth: "the inner workings of our queueing system (eg. working on debounce, concurrency over time), state store (handling terabytes to petabytes of storage), the API layers in-between, and sometimes the high-level SDKs"; "Plan and implement improvements on throughput, and latency at hundreds of thousands to millions of requests per second". Ideal candidate: "several years" on distributed systems, "Go or similar statically typed languages professionally for two years or more". Bonus: compliance experience (SOC2, ISO27001, HIPAA), networking fundamentals, SRE background. The role also includes user-facing open-source duties: "Communicate with our users through Github, email and Discord" and writing technical specs and user documentation.

Notable / other: Onboarding expectations are written out in a 30/60/90 structure, which signals decent internal process for a company this size. The product mindset requirement is repeated three times across the JD — this is a developer-tools company where systems engineers are expected to care about product and growth, not purely internal plumbing.

Open relevant roles (sample): Distributed Systems Engineer - Platform (US/Remote, San Francisco).
