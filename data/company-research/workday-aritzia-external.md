# Aritzia — research
Provider/key: workday:aritzia/external | company_type: product

What they do: Aritzia is a Canadian women's fashion retailer — a vertically-integrated "house of brands" designing and selling its own in-house labels through boutiques across Canada and the US plus a large e-commerce business. Publicly traded (TSX: ATZ). The engineering roles in scope sit in its internal Technology and Digital Technology divisions, building and running the retail/e-commerce platform and, in one case, an enterprise AI platform.

How they describe themselves: Their careers copy, verbatim from their own Workday postings: "We are Everyday Luxury™. It's what we do and how we do it — together. It's working alongside world-class talent in pursuit of world-class results. It's **maximizing your potential in a high-performance culture**. It's knowing that wherever you are, you belong. It's elevating your world — and the world of those who love and will love Aritzia. It's You. And an endless runway of opportunity." The AI Enablement team's stated mission: "to empower domain teams to deliver AI solutions that unlock business value through strategic guidance and AI infrastructure." Note the culture framing is explicitly performance-oriented — "high-performance culture", "world-class results", "pay-for-performance" (the last picked up from the Data Engineer posting).

Size / stage / funding: Large, established, publicly traded (the Stage-3 extraction labelled all three postings `company_stage: public`). Thousands of employees across retail and support functions. Not runway-dependent in any sense. Exact headcount unverified here.

Locations / HQ: Stated first-party: "With a Global Support Campus in **Vancouver** and additional Support Office hubs in major cities across North America — including **Toronto, New York, Los Angeles and Seattle** — our workplace network is strategically designed to support our People and serve our Clients a frictionless experience." The three live technology postings are split between the Vancouver and Seattle support offices.

Remote policy: **Office-based.** Their own copy centres on the "Global Support Campus" and "Support Office hubs", and the perks advertised are all physical-premises perks: "in-office fitness studio and gym open 7 days a week", "subsidized dining". The Stage-3 extraction read the Seattle postings as `onsite` and the Vancouver Principal role as `hybrid`. No remote-work provision is stated anywhere in the postings.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **NO for a remote worker.** Aritzia is a Canadian employer and clearly hires in Canada — the Principal Platform Engineer role is at the Vancouver Support Office — but these are support-office roles requiring attendance at a specific campus, not remote roles. Vancouver is in Pacific Time, a manageable one hour from Kimberley's Mountain Time, but the office requirement is the blocker: Kimberley is roughly an 800 km / 11-hour drive from Vancouver, so a Vancouver hybrid role is not commutable and Max cannot relocate. The two other live postings are worse — they are located at the **Seattle** support office and were read as onsite and US-only. Effectively disqualifying.

Engineering & tech: Two quite different profiles on the board. The **Principal Platform Engineer, Enterprise AI Enablement** (Vancouver) is a genuine AI-platform architecture role: "architect and oversee the foundational infrastructure that powers Aritzia's AI evolution... establish the overarching philosophy, principles, and technical guardrails that shape how we design, deploy, and scale AI. You will own our AI platform roadmap — curating a network of world-class partners and building robust development platforms, multi-modal AI pipelines, intelligent autonomous agents, and self-service tools... With a secure-by-design mindset, you will establish the critical infrastructure and monitoring frameworks required to ensure our AI usage is responsible, ethical, resilient". It also carries explicit evangelism duties — "fostering enterprise-wide AI literacy and supporting a culture of continuous learning". The **Senior Backend Developer** (Seattle, Digital Technology) is DevOps-flavoured: "CI/CD pipelines, Terraform, infrastructure as code, automated testing, Cloud Logging/Monitoring, observability" with "solid DevOps fundamentals" required. A Senior/Data Engineer role also sits in Digital Technology.

Notable / other: Benefits mentioned across the postings: 16-week "baby leave", in-office fitness studio and gym open seven days a week, subsidised dining, and an explicitly pay-for-performance philosophy. The phrase "an endless runway of opportunity" and the "Everyday Luxury™" trademarked framing set the tone: this is a brand-first company where technology is a support function, not the product.

Open relevant roles (sample): Information Technology - Principal Platform Engineer, Enterprise AI Enablement (Vancouver Support Office, live); Digital Technology - Senior Backend Developer (Seattle Support Office, live); Digital - Senior Data Engineer / Data Engineer, Digital Technology (Seattle, live).
