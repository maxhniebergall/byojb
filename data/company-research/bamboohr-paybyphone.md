# PayByPhone — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:paybyphone | company_type: product

What they do: PayByPhone runs a mobile parking-payment app and platform. Drivers pay for and extend parking sessions from their phone (credit/debit card, Google Pay, Apple Pay) instead of using a meter. Own-site figures: available in **over 1,200 cities across North America and Europe**, "**more than 125 million all-time users**". The JD gives the active-user figures: "already help more than 60 million users across North America and Europe" and a platform "enabling over 17 million users to conveniently pay for parking." Customers are municipalities and parking operators on one side, consumer drivers on the other.

How they describe themselves: "At PayByPhone, our strength is our people. Behind our product is a talented, creative, and driven multi-disciplinary team united by a shared ambition: to make everyday mobility simpler. We believe innovation should be collaborative, learning should be constant, and work should be enjoyable. As we grow, we're looking for people who want to grow with us." And: "Together, we're on an ambitious mission to create intuitive technology solutions that deliver world-class user experiences. We are a **fast-growing, forward-thinking company**." Value proposition framed around consumers paying "quickly, easily, and securely — without waiting in line, carrying change, or worrying about costly fines." No formal published values list was reachable.

Size / stage / funding: Not verified this run. The company website's about page returned 404 and the homepage carries no ownership, headcount or investor information. PayByPhone has historically been reported as a subsidiary of a large automotive group, but **no first-party source confirmed that on the pages fetched — treat ownership as unverified.** The recruiting text self-describes as "fast-growing".

Locations / HQ: Vancouver, British Columbia is the location of all three live engineering postings and the JD names "Vancouver, BC" as the role location. Operations span North America and Europe. HQ not independently confirmed beyond the postings.

Remote policy: **Hybrid, stated explicitly in the JD** — "Location: Vancouver, BC (**Hybrid**)". All three live engineering postings are located Vancouver, British Columbia. On-call is a stated obligation: "Provide on-call, critical response on a rotational basis" and "You must maintain a personal data plan to fulfill your responsibilities for on-call support."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **NO — verified from the JD.** The role is explicitly Vancouver hybrid, which requires regular physical presence in Vancouver. Kimberley BC is roughly a 10-hour drive / one flight from Vancouver and is in Mountain Time, so a hybrid Vancouver role is not commutable. This is Canadian employment but not remote-Canada employment.

Engineering & tech: From the Vehicle Services JD — the senior developer "develop[s] major components that power our consumer apps and global platform." Stated expectations include architecting infrastructure, designing automated test suites and test plans, code review including **PCI compliance** verification, using and improving an automated **CI/CD pipeline**, provisioning and maintaining **cloud infrastructure** for the team's products, monitoring product health via application and system metrics, and owning production support during outages. Mob- and pair-programming are offered where appropriate. Agile (Kanban/Scrum), automated unit testing, interest in TDD/BDD. Hard skills asked for are notably mobile-first: "**Expert-level ability to code in Flutter, Swift or Java/Kotlin**, specific role dependant"; databases relational and non-relational (MySQL, MongoDB, DynamoDB). Teams are product-aligned (Data Team, Vehicle Services, Payments & Financial).

Notable / other: PCI scope is real — the business processes card payments at consumer volume — which gives the payments and data work genuine correctness constraints. The Senior Software Developer (Vehicle Services) posting states its pay openly: "**Compensation: Hiring Range: 125,000 - 135,000 CAD**".
