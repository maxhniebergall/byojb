# Amplemarket — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:amplemarket | company_type: product

What they do: Amplemarket sells an AI-powered B2B sales platform — lead generation, data enrichment,
hyper-personalized sequencing, multichannel outreach and "AI-powered smart actions". Their own framing:
"Amplemarket's premise is that a lot of the playbooks used by B2B sales teams that worked 5 years ago
don't cut it anymore... sales teams need to have a tremendous amount of information about their prospects
to decide when is the best moment and channel to reach out in the most relevant way. You can't do this
with 5 different data/sales enablement point solutions that don't share the same data schema and barely
communicate with each other. Amplemarket is the all-in-one compound solution that enables this." Named
product surfaces include Duo Copilot, Searcher, Multichannel Sequences and deliverability optimization.
Customers cited on their own JD: Deel, Moveworks, H1, Vanta.

How they describe themselves: Four stated core values, verbatim from the careers page:
- "Mission focus"
- "Pro sports team mentality" — seeking people who "raise the bar and play to win"
- "Curious optimism"
- "Make something people want" (the Y Combinator motto)
On working model: "As a remote-first company, we believe freedom and autonomy go hand-in-hand with high
performance", and "Work from anywhere in the world (except for region-specific roles)". The engineering
JD emphasizes founder proximity and velocity: "The three co-founders have an engineering background
(João, Luís and Mica studied Physics & Computer Science in university) and deeply value the work that
engineers are responsible for"; the role promises you will "Work directly with the co-founders", "Have
lots of autonomy and impact", "Ship new features every week", "Own central, complex components of the
application", and "Drive and deliver on our product roadmap, turning rough specs into features". It
describes the hire as "an early member of engineering team" working "cross functionally in a fullstack
team and in close collaboration with product, design, customer success, support, and sales functions."

Size / stage / funding: Y Combinator-backed ("We are backed by Y Combinator" on their own JD),
venture-stage, small engineering team — the JD's own "early member of engineering team" phrasing is the
best available size signal. Extracted stage: growth. Headcount is not disclosed on the careers page.
Founded by three Portuguese engineers; the careers page references Portuguese and Brazilian team members,
indicating an Iberian/Portuguese origin with a distributed team.

Locations / HQ: No HQ stated on the careers page. Distributed/remote-first; team references point to
Portugal and Brazil, with US-based commercial staff (a US-based Head of Sales appears in testimonials).
The Greenhouse board is on the EU instance (job-boards.eu.greenhouse.io), consistent with an EU-rooted entity.

Remote policy: Remote-first and explicitly global — "Work from anywhere in the world (except for
region-specific roles)". The backend posting narrows this: "This position is available for candidates
based in EMEA, LATAM or North America."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes, in principle — Canada falls inside the stated North America window.**
The Senior Backend Software Engineer JD states verbatim: "This position is available for candidates
based in EMEA, LATAM or North America", and the extracted facets record geo_eligibility: canada with
location hints "Canada, EMEA, LATAM, North America". Caveat: nothing states a Canadian employment
entity, so this is likely a contractor/EOR arrangement rather than Canadian payroll, and the other two
open roles are narrowed to EMEA/LATAM only. Timezone: with the engineering centre of gravity in Europe
and roles open to EMEA, Mountain Time is the far edge of the company's span — expect early-morning
overlap pressure with a European core team.

Engineering & tech: Stack stated on the JD: "Ruby on Rails, React, PostgreSQL, Elasticsearch, Redis",
with the note "We don't require you to have experience in our stack to apply." Genuine data-scale
problems are described — "working on NLP models that can extract information from a thread of emails to
scaling an Elasticsearch cluster holding terabytes of data" and "We keep and enrich valuable data that
is critical for sales reps' success. We need to keep these TB of data organized and easily accessible."
But the role itself is defined as fullstack product work: "you will build reliable backend systems and
design state-of-the-art APIs to enable new product features. You will work cross functionally in a
fullstack team." Extracted facets: autonomy high, culture balanced.

Notable / other: The JD carries a long recruitment-scam warning ("Beware of Recruitment Scams... there
are fraudulent activities targeting job seekers, where individuals are impersonating Amplemarket"),
confirming amplemarket.com as the only legitimate hiring domain. Compensation is stated only as
"Competitive Salary" — no figures anywhere on the JD or careers page. Two of the three open engineering
listings are "Spontaneous Application" pipelines (Data Engineer, Infrastructure Engineer) rather than
funded live openings.
