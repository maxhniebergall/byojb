# EQ Bank | Equitable Bank — research
Provider/key: lever:eqbank | company_type: product

What they do: Equitable Bank is a Canadian Schedule I bank — "Canada's Challenger Bank™" — offering residential and commercial lending, savings products (high-interest savings, GICs) and a digital banking platform, EQ Bank (launched 2016). From their own JD: "Our company continues to grow, and today we serve more than 800,000 customers across Canada through Equitable Bank... and have been around for more than 50 years. Equitable Bank's wholly-owned subsidiary, Concentra Bank, supports credit unions across Canada that serve more than six million members. Together we have over $142 billion in combined assets under management and administration, with a clear mandate to drive change in Canadian banking to enrich people's lives." Their about page states "over $144 billion in combined assets under management and administration" and describes EQB as Canada's seventh largest independent Schedule I bank by assets.

How they describe themselves: "Join a Challenger. Being a traditional bank just isn't our thing, so we challenge ourselves to get creative in providing innovative banking solutions for Canadians. How do we get there? With a talented team of inquisitive and agile challengers that break through the status quo. So, if you're passionate about redefining the future of banking—while having fun—this could be your next big opportunity." Corporate framing: "financial services are changing" and a belief in "better service and a better deal for our customers through digital banking". They also publish an unusual hiring-process statement: "candidates selected for our comprehensive interview process may be considered for a different level — either higher or lower — based on their interview performance... rather than relying solely on perceived experience. The hiring committee will thoughtfully assess each candidate's skills and potential, and adjust the level of the role as needed."

Size / stage / funding: Public — EQB Inc. trades on the TSX under "EQB"; Equitable Bank is its wholly-owned subsidiary, with Equitable Trust beneath that. $144B AUM/AUA, 800,000+ customers, 50+ years of operating history. Established and regulated; no runway risk. Headcount not disclosed on the pages reached (in the low thousands for a bank of this size, but that is an inference).

Locations / HQ: Head office at EQ Bank Tower, 2200–25 Ontario Street, Toronto, ON M5A 0Y9, with lending operations across Canada. Their careers postings for engineering are Toronto-based.

Remote policy: **No first-party remote-work statement was found** — the equitablebank.ca careers landing page returned only navigation, and the about page says nothing about work arrangements. The postings themselves are the evidence: both live engineering roles are labelled "Toronto, hybrid" in the ATS.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No, for practical purposes.** As a Canadian bank, employment authorization is a non-issue, but both relevant live roles are explicitly **Toronto, hybrid**, meaning regular in-office attendance in Toronto. There is no evidence of a fully-remote engineering arrangement. Regulated Canadian banks also commonly restrict where staff with access to systems and customer data may work from. For a candidate in Kimberley BC who cannot relocate, hybrid Toronto is closed; Eastern-to-Mountain time would add a two-hour offset on top.

Engineering & tech: **Microsoft-centric enterprise IT.** The Staff Engineer, Cloud Engineering role is explicitly split: "Power Platform (30%), Azure Cloud Engineering (30%), AKS and DevOps Enablement (20%), and Platform Process Improvement (20%)", reporting to the Director, Cloud Operations, and tasked with designing "reusable platform capabilities that empower both professional and citizen developers across the bank", working "hands-on with Azure services, PaaS workloads, Kubernetes, and DevOps toolchains, while also contributing to Power Platform governance and lifecycle enablement", collaborating with "engineering, security, operations, and business stakeholders to drive platform maturity, automation, scalability, and compliance". The Senior DevOps Engineer role is described only in outline: "EQ Bank's IT team is starting to evolve and Needs a talented Senior DevOps Engineer with a solid passion in this space. Experienced and knowledgeable about the end to end software build chain, operations and automated build and release process." No engineering blog or public handbook was found.

Notable / other: The phrase "EQ Bank's IT team is starting to evolve" indicates an org early in a modernization journey rather than a mature platform organisation. "Citizen developers" and Power Platform governance signal a low-code/IT-services orientation alongside the cloud work. No compensation is posted on either role. The level-flexible interview process means the advertised level is not guaranteed.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Engineer, Cloud Engineering (Toronto, hybrid); Senior DevOps Engineer (Toronto, hybrid).
