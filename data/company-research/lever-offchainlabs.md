Provider/key: lever:offchainlabs | company_type: product

What they do: Offchain (formerly Offchain Labs; the offchainlabs.com domain now redirects to offchain.io) builds blockchain scaling and infrastructure software for the Ethereum ecosystem. Their own words: "We built the Arbitrum stack that powers Arbitrum One, the most widely adopted Ethereum scaling solution that exists today. Arbitrum's ecosystem is undergoing tremendous growth with hundreds of projects and dApps on Arbitrum One today. Over 100 different teams have used Offchain technology to build their own Arbitrum chains. Major players in the space, Robinhood, BlackRock, Ethena Labs, Securitize, Aave, and Apechain are all using the Arbitrum stack." Product suite per their site: **Arbitrum** (scaling platform "handling billions in value"), **Prysm** (Ethereum consensus client "powering ~25% of network nodes"), **ZeroDev** (smart-account infrastructure), **Offchain Labs** (research division "focused on protocol breakthroughs"), **Tandem** (venture/partner studio) and **Onchain Labs** (Arbitrum ecosystem innovation). They position as the "Enterprise infrastructure partner for the world's most ambitious companies" building "the most trusted interconnected blockchain product suite".
