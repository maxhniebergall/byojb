# Freenome — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:freenome | company_type: product

What they do: Freenome develops **blood-based early cancer screening tests**. In their own words: "Detecting cancer early can make a world of difference. Freenome is setting a new pace for early cancer detection by combining the latest in science and artificial intelligence, with the ease of a standard blood draw." Their first product is **SimpleScreen™ CRC**, an FDA-approved blood test for colorectal cancer screening, with stated plans to expand to other cancer types. The underlying approach is **multiomics** — combining multiple biological signal types — with AI/ML models on top. They operate a **CLIA-certified** clinical laboratory for high-complexity testing.

How they describe themselves: Philosophy — "cancer screening should be **simple, accessible and effective**" — with explicit recognition that patients and cancers differ individually. Careers framing: the team are "**passionate creators and ambitious builders**" who are "**relentless in pushing for what's possible** in early cancer detection". The engineering JD adds a mission hook: "You are passionate about software development best practices and will have a significant impact on the continued growth of an organization dedicated to **changing the entire landscape of cancer**."

Size / stage / funding: Founded **2014**. Their site states the company **recently became publicly traded** — a material stability signal relative to a private biotech, though it also means quarterly-earnings pressure and, in the diagnostics sector, exposure to reimbursement and commercial-ramp risk. Headcount not disclosed on the pages fetched.

Locations / HQ: **Brisbane, California** (3300 Marina Boulevard, Brisbane, CA 94005). Plus the CLIA lab operation. No other offices disclosed.

Remote policy: Not stated at company level on the website. The JD is explicit for this role: "The role reports to our engineering management team. **This role will be a Remote role.**" Both live Senior Software Engineer II postings are listed as Remote.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — effectively US-only, and the JD says so implicitly.** The compensation paragraph reads "The **US target range** of our base salary for new hires is $161,925 - $227,325", with no other geography given. Combined with a California HQ, a CLIA-certified US clinical lab and an FDA-regulated product (the JD lists "Experience working in an **FDA regulated environment**" as a nice-to-have), there is no indication of a Canadian hiring entity. Treat as US-remote only.

Engineering & tech: Backend platform engineering supporting a diagnostics business. From the Senior Software Engineer II JD: design, develop and deploy "reliable, maintainable, scalable, and fault-tolerant **backend services** that power both our internal and external systems"; **Python**; **Docker and Kubernetes** (stated as a strong requirement); cloud on AWS, Azure or GCP; contribution to "**data infrastructure for machine learning applications**, ensuring efficient data processing and integration"; leading code and design reviews; driving "engineering hygiene practices". Nice-to-haves: statistical/ML methods applied to real problems, **ETL or data pipelines at scale using Flyte, Airflow or similar**, computational biology / genomics / bioinformatics domain experience, and FDA-regulated-environment experience. Requires a BSc or equivalent and **at least 8 years** of professional experience including end-to-end project leadership and mentoring.

Notable / other: Heavy interdisciplinary collaboration is a core expectation — "work closely with an interdisciplinary team of **engineers, scientists, and product managers**". The role explicitly includes mentoring junior engineers and championing best practices, i.e. a senior IC with organisational influence rather than pure heads-down work. Compensation is disclosed on the JD and also includes equity, cash bonuses and a full benefits range. **Registry data-quality note:** both live postings share the identical title "Senior Software Engineer II" and near-identical bodies (two requisitions), and the comp slot for them is classified `ladder_level: mid` despite an 8-year minimum and a $162-227K USD band — the level classification looks too low for the actual scope.
