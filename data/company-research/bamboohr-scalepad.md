# ScalePad — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:scalepad | company_type: product
What they do: ScalePad builds SaaS for managed service providers (MSPs) — it calls itself "the MSP operating system for stronger client relationships." Five integrated products: Lifecycle Manager (asset lifecycle), ControlMap (compliance/GRC), Quoter (CPQ), Backup Radar (backup monitoring), and Cognition360 (ConnectWise BI). Customers are MSP firms (Integris, Thrive, Ntiva, TeamLogic IT, Executech, CMIT Solutions, AIR IT Group, Courser).
How they describe themselves: Marketing line is "Invisible work doesn't build trust. Visible impact does." — the pitch is making an MSP's work "visible to every client, every quarter" and helping partners become each client's "most valuable partner." They stress the suite-over-point-tool angle: "each one does its job alone. Together they run your customer success program." No published values list or engineering handbook was found on the site's front page; a fuller values statement is unverified.
Size / stage / funding: Self-reported scale metrics: 12,000+ partners globally, 370+ integrations, 90M+ assets managed, 3M+ backups monitored, 250K+ QBR hours saved. Headcount, funding stage and profitability are NOT disclosed on the site and were not verified. Private; known in the MSP tooling market as a consolidator (several of the five products are acquisitions), which implies PE/growth-equity backing, but that is unverified here.
Locations / HQ: The BambooHR careers board and the live posting place the role in Vancouver, British Columbia. The registry posting's extracted location hints also list Toronto, Montreal and Phoenix, suggesting multiple offices. HQ not stated on the site page fetched; Vancouver is the strong inference from the postings.
Remote policy: The live Senior Software Developer [Platform] posting is classified **hybrid**, tied to Vancouver, BC. No remote-first statement found.
Remote-Canada eligibility: Canada — YES as an employer (Canadian company, BC-based role). Fully-remote-from-interior-BC — **NO / unverified**: the role is a Vancouver hybrid posting, which requires being in the Lower Mainland. Not workable from Kimberley.
Engineering & tech: From the platform JD's extracted facets — React, MariaDB, OpenSearch, AWS and Azure, REST APIs, MCP, OLTP/OLAP workloads, CI/CD, data-ingestion pipelines, and AI-assisted development. Compliance posture matters to the product (SOC 2, ISO 27001 referenced). Reasonable amount of genuine data/platform work behind an MSP-facing product.
Notable / other: Product line is a suite assembled around MSP customer-success workflows rather than a single core platform, so engineering is split across five acquired/parallel products. No engineering blog was located.
