# Poolside — research
Provider/key: ashby:poolside | company_type: product
What they do: Frontier AI lab building foundation models and agentic systems for software development. Their own framing: "poolside exists to be this company: to build a world where AI will be the engine behind economically valuable work and scientific progress. We believe the fastest way to reach AGI lies in accelerating software development itself, by reshaping the developer experience with agentic systems, coding assistants, and the frontier models that power them. We deploy these systems directly into the development environments of security-conscious enterprises." Their site shows open-weight models — Laguna S 2.1 (118B parameters, "frontier-class reasoning") and Laguna XS 2.1 (33B, "lightest and fastest agentic coding model") — distributed via OpenRouter and Vercel AI Gateway, alongside desktop and CLI developer tools and runtime systems for training and operating agents. They publish research: "we work in the open", with a blog covering reinforcement learning from code execution and GPU optimization, and a Laguna M.1/XS.2 technical report.
How they describe themselves: Heavily AGI-race framed. "In this decade, the world will create Artificial General Intelligence. There will only be a small number of companies who will achieve this. Their ability to stack advantages and pull ahead will define the winners. These companies will move faster than anyone else. They will attract the world's most capable talent... They will be given the right to raise large amounts of capital along their journey." On culture: "Our team is a multidisciplinary blend of research, engineering, and business experts. What unites us is our deep care for what we build together. We're in a race that requires hard work, intellectual curiosity, and obsession; to balance this intensity, we've assembled a team of low ego and kind-hearted individuals who have built the special culture poolside has."
Size / stage / funding: Private AI lab; they state they expect to "raise large amounts of capital" and are pre-AGI-scale training. Exact headcount, round and valuation UNVERIFIED this session (no search budget). They operate large GPU clusters and train foundation models, so capital intensity is high.
Locations / HQ: "We were founded in the US and have our home there, but our team is distributed across Europe and North America." Paris is the recurring in-person hub; London, UK appears as a listed location on the posting.
Remote policy: "Fully remote work & flexible hours" — but with a hard in-person cadence: "We get our fix of in-person collaboration (and croissants) in Paris each month for 3 days, always Monday-Wednesday, with an open invitation to stay the whole week. We also do longer off-sites once a year."
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO (as posted). The live posting's location field is "Remote (EMEA/East Coast), Remote (EMEA), London, UK" — EMEA or US East Coast only. British Columbia is neither, and Mountain Time is three hours further from the stated overlap band. The monthly three-day Paris trip is an additional structural obstacle from interior BC.
Engineering & tech: The Pretraining Data team builds the "Model Factory" — pipelines transforming "trillions of raw tokens" into training datasets. Concretely: petabyte-scale ingestion, deduplication and streaming systems bridging raw web crawls to GPU clusters; data modelling, algorithmic sorting, distributed pipeline optimization. Stack named: orchestration via Slurm, Airflow or Dagster; observability via CI/CD, Grafana, Prometheus; infra via Git, Docker, Kubernetes and cloud managed services; batch inference with vLLM; Polars, Dask or PySpark; expert-level Python. Nice-to-haves: trillion-scale SOTA pretraining datasets, research-to-production translation, OCR, web crawling, evals, prior LLM pre-training. Works alongside Pretraining, Posttraining, Evals and Product teams.
