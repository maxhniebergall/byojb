# BitGo — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:bitgo | company_type: product

What they do: BitGo is a digital-asset infrastructure company — institutional custody, wallets, staking, trading, financing and settlement from regulated cold storage. Founded 2013 by Mike Belshe and Ben Davenport. It describes itself as "the operational backbone of the digital economy", handling a significant share of Bitcoin network transactions and claiming to be the largest independent digital-asset custodian and staking provider. Customers are institutions (exchanges, platforms, brands) plus millions of retail investors via partners.

How they describe themselves: Corporate values stated on their JDs: "open communication, collaboration, accountability, craftsmanship, and a client first approach", plus "transparency" and "taking ownership". Mission framing: "deliver trust in digital assets". Their site states five principles: transparency (on-chain auditability), monetary freedom, borderless transactions, security through cryptography, and innovation in digital-asset infrastructure. JD tone is mission/disruption-flavoured — "Disrupting an industry takes vision, innovation, passion, technical chops, drive to deliver"; "Join us and you'll be able to look back and say you were part of the team that transformed finance." One JD asks for "ability to work independently in a fast-paced environment".

Size / stage / funding: Publicly traded on NYSE under ticker BTGO; OCC federally chartered. Parent BitGo Holdings, Inc. incorporated in Sioux Falls, South Dakota. Multiple regulated Trust companies and international subsidiaries (Switzerland, Germany, Singapore, UAE). Headcount not stated on the pages read.

Locations / HQ: US parent (Sioux Falls, SD incorporation; historically Palo Alto operations — not verified here). Global presence with entities in Switzerland, Germany, Singapore, UAE. Engineering office in Toronto, Ontario.

Remote policy: For the Toronto roles, explicitly NOT remote. All three live Canadian JDs carry the identical line: "This role will require being full-time onsite at our Toronto office to support collaborative team dynamics and innovative problem-solving." No company-wide remote policy statement was found on bitgo.com.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO for the current Canadian openings. They employ in Canada (Toronto), but the roles are full-time onsite in Toronto — unworkable from Kimberley, BC.

Engineering & tech: TypeScript / Node.js and Golang backends; PostgreSQL with deep transaction/locking work; Kafka and event-driven microservices; state machines; double-entry accounting and settlement workflows; React for full-stack reach. Infrastructure side: Linux, AWS plus bare-metal Kubernetes, Bash/Python/Go, observability on Grafana/Mimir/Tempo/Pyroscope, terabyte-scale data-warehouse pipelines, distributed databases and Kafka clusters, ArgoCD and GitHub Actions, on-call operations-response rotation. The DataOps team claims "a proven track record of zero-downtime critical changes and upgrades". They explicitly require AI-assisted development fluency (Copilot/Cursor) with rigorous validation of generated code in financial contexts.

Notable / other: Strongly regulated/mission-critical domain (custody, clearing, settlement) — the substance is infrastructure, not product surface. JDs emphasise correctness over speed, invariant checking, audit trails and financial reconciliation. Crypto-market exposure is the main sector risk.

Open relevant roles (sample): Infrastructure Engineer - DataOps Team (Toronto); Senior Software Engineer - Post Trade (Toronto); Software Engineer - Trade (Toronto); Data Engineer E2/E3; DevOps Engineer E2 - InfraOps; Backend Engineer E2 - Trade.

Pay (stated on their own JDs, CAD base): Infrastructure Engineer - DataOps $180,000–200,000; Senior Software Engineer - Post Trade $180,000–220,000; Software Engineer - Trade $180,000–200,000. All say equity + annual performance bonus on top.
