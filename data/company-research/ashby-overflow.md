# Overflow — research
Provider/key: ashby:overflow | company_type: product

What they do: Overflow is a faith-and-philanthropy giving platform — "a vertical fintech and SaaS platform unlocking more ways to give — cash, stock, crypto, and beyond — through a beautifully designed ecosystem built for high-impact churches, nonprofits, and mission-driven institutions." Their stated mission is "to inspire the world to give", and they describe themselves as "building the infrastructure that makes generosity frictionless at scale." The differentiating product capability is non-cash giving (notably stock and crypto donations).

How they describe themselves: The founding principles section is explicitly religious: "Overflow was founded on the belief that generosity transforms people and communities. Inspired by Proverbs 11:24 — 'The world of the generous gets larger and larger' — we operate with integrity, stewardship, service, and excellence." Those four words — **integrity, stewardship, service, excellence** — are the stated values. Tone elsewhere is mission-forward and warm rather than aggressive; the JD does use "energized by building from scratch, comfortable with ambiguity", "fast-growing", and "startup DNA… you thrive in ambiguity and love the 0-to-1 journey", but there is no hustle/24-7/rocketship vocabulary.

Size / stage / funding: **Internally inconsistent in their own JD — flag this.** The opening line says "Overflow is a Series C, growth-stage technology company", while the compensation-philosophy section later says "As a Series B company, we believe equity is a meaningful part of building long-term ownership". One of the two is stale copy; the actual current round is therefore UNVERIFIED. Investors named: Uncork Capital, Craft Ventures, Salesforce Ventures, R7, and WIF ("one of the largest lenders to faith and philanthropic organizations in the country"). Headcount not stated. Profitability not stated.

Locations / HQ: Offices in **Los Gatos, CA** and **Birmingham, AL**, described as being "for team collaboration, coworking, meetings, and intentional in-person connection".

Remote policy: Stated verbatim: "Overflow operates with a flexible work model. While there is no company-wide in-office requirement, some teams may establish in-person collaboration rhythms based on their function, goals, and team needs… Candidates should be comfortable participating in occasional in-person gatherings or team-specific onsite expectations, depending on their role and department." So: flexible/remote-tolerant, but with an explicit caveat that a given team may impose onsite rhythms, plus "Team retreats and intentional in-person gatherings throughout the year" in the benefits list.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unverified, and the evidence leans no.** The posting is listed simply "Remote", but every concrete detail is US-anchored: a 401(k) retirement plan, both offices in the US, Carta US benchmarks for pay, US-centric customer base (churches and nonprofits, WIF as a US lender). No Canadian entity, EOR or non-US hiring is mentioned anywhere. Also note the "occasional in-person gatherings" expectation across Los Gatos CA and Birmingham AL — workable from BC only as travel, not commuting.

Engineering & tech: This is the company's **first dedicated Data Engineer** — "You will be the primary architect and builder of our data infrastructure, taking ownership of a rapidly expanding ecosystem of data." Named technology: AWS data services, Databricks (including **Unity Catalog** for governance, Delta Lake, Databricks SQL/BI), Change Data Capture pipelines, NoSQL and relational SQL databases, OLAP schema design for financial transactions. Requirements: 5+ years data engineering, "ideally acting as the first or lead Data Engineer at a fast-growing tech, fintech, or SaaS company". Scope in the first 90 days is explicitly BI and data governance — "ensuring our Sales, CS, Finance, and Success teams have reliable, governed access to the precise metrics they need" — then OLAP architecture for financial transactions, then partnering with the Head of AI to organize agentic conversational data for "MLOps, RAG pipelines, and agent memory". Daily collaboration with Software Engineering and Business Operations engineers is stated. Nice-to-haves include fintech ledgering/reconciliation and SOC 2 aligned environments.

Notable / other: Compensation philosophy stated verbatim: "Overflow's compensation philosophy is rooted in Carta Total Compensation benchmarks, ensuring pay that is competitive by role, geography, and function." No numeric range is posted.
