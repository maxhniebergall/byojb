# Atticus — research
Provider/key: ashby:atticus | company_type: product

What they do: Atticus is a Los Angeles-based legal-tech marketplace that connects sick, injured or disabled Americans with attorneys and government benefits. Its two main lines are Social Security / disability benefits ("the leading platform connecting people with disabilities to government benefits") and compensation claims for victims of accidents, misconduct and violence. It operates as a two-sided marketplace: consumers on one side, a vetted network of law firms on the other, with internal tooling built for both.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "tear down barriers between people in crisis and the aid they deserve." They frame the market opportunity as "at any given time, 16 million Americans are experiencing a crisis that requires urgent help from our legal system or government... today, most never get it," and claim "hundreds of thousands of people access over $10 billion in life-changing aid" plus "over 20,000 five-star reviews." They repeatedly stress being "a certified B Corporation" and "a fast-growing Series C startup that doubles as a B-corp social enterprise," and describe the incumbent industry as "dominated by outdated technology... massive call centers, antiquated CRMs, and paper checks." Their careers page leans on equity ownership for all employees, education stipends and comprehensive benefits. Tone is mission-forward and explicitly startup-flavoured: the Growth pod JD says "we run fast, experiment constantly"; the infrastructure JD asks for someone "excited by environments with little structure — where you're expected to... cut through ambiguity."

Size / stage / funding: Series C; "raised more than $100 million" from Fika, Forerunner, Google Ventures and True Ventures. Engineering is explicitly "a 20-person engineering team." Certified B Corporation. Company-wide headcount not stated on any source read.

Locations / HQ: HQ Los Angeles, California. No other offices mentioned; the workforce is distributed.

Remote policy: Fully remote / fully distributed. Every JD states: "This job is fully remote and we're committed to empowering everyone with flexibility. Work remotely, and travel to LA (on the company dime) as needed to be with your colleagues — usually quarterly, plus offsites." The careers page describes annual in-person retreats plus regular travel for problem-solving sessions.

Remote-Canada eligibility: UNVERIFIED — leaning no. Nothing on the careers page or in any of the four live JDs states a country restriction, but nothing states Canada eligibility either. All product, customers and regulatory context are US-only ("any sick or injured American", US Social Security benefits), the ATS lists the roles simply as "Remote", and the careers page references serving clients "across the US" from an LA HQ. Treat as probably US-only pending a direct question to the recruiter.

Engineering & tech: ~20 engineers organised into cross-functional "pods" (Growth Pod, Representation Pod) of engineers, PMs and designers. Stack signals from JDs: Go, GCP, Kubernetes, Terraform, Docker; data stack is BigQuery (or comparable), dbt, and Dagster/Airflow orchestration, with Salesforce/CRM data pipelines. The Lead Infrastructure Engineer would be the second infra hire, reporting to the VP of Engineering, and the role is framed as "infrastructure as a product, serving Atticus's product engineering teams as your customers" — infra roadmap, security and operational foundations, SLOs, MTTR reduction, CI/CD, observability, database ops. The Lead Data Engineer is the first in-house data engineer, taking over data infrastructure that appears to have been outsourced/contractor-run (a listed nice-to-have is "experience transitioning data infrastructure from an outsourced or contractor model to an in-house team").

Notable / other: B-Corp status is a genuine differentiator in an industry of call centres. Benefits stated in JDs: $600/year internet stipend, $1,000/year education/training reimbursement, up to $1,200/year student-loan repayment assistance, equity. All four engineering roles state a salary band openly even though the roles are not tied to a transparency-law jurisdiction.

Open relevant roles (sample): Lead Infrastructure Engineer ($170,000-$220,000); Lead Data Engineer ($174,000-$230,000); Senior Backend Engineer - Representation ($157,250-$230,000); Senior Backend Engineer - Growth ($157,250-$230,000). All listed "Remote".
