# Recast — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:recast | company_type: product

What they do: Recast builds **marketing mix modeling (MMM)** software — a Bayesian statistical platform that measures the incremental impact of marketing spend across channels and optimizes budget allocation. Two products: **Recast MMM** (Bayesian model with weekly automated refreshes, forecasting, budget optimization, goal tracking) and **GeoLift by Recast** (standalone geo-based incrementality testing). Customers are Fortune 500, CPG, fintech, pharmaceutical and DTC brands; they claim their models govern over $5 billion in annual marketing spend. Positioned as the modern replacement for legacy consultant-run MMM and for post-iOS14 attribution loss.

How they describe themselves: Their public methodology page is built around a **"Nowhere to Hide"** philosophy — explicitly rejecting black-box solutions. Concretely they say they publish model documentation publicly, run weekly automated refreshes with no manual adjustments, surface uncertainty intervals rather than false precision, and validate through weekly automated backtesting (claiming >95% cumulative forecast accuracy). Models are described as having "40,000+ parameters re-estimated weekly," with time-varying parameters, hierarchical modeling, saturation curves and multi-stage funnel analysis. On the hiring side the JD states values plainly: shipping where "'good enough to learn from' is a real engineering value, not a compromise"; being "a remote, async-first team spread across time zones where clear written communication is essential"; "an async-first culture with a focus on documentation over standing meetings"; and "autonomy and support to do your best work in your own time." They also state candidly that "startup pace is real: things move fast, not everything is written down, and priorities shift."

Size / stage / funding: Early-stage and small. Total funding roughly **$4.5M**, including a $3.4M round (announced Dec 2022) led by Good Friends (the Warby Parker / Allbirds / Harry's founders' fund) and Lerer Hippeau, with Data Tech Fund, Vibe Capital and angels Jonah Goodhart and Adam Grenier. Proceeds earmarked to scale software engineering and data science. More than 20% of employees hold PhDs in math, engineering or statistics — a research-heavy team. Founded/led by Michael Kaminsky.

Locations / HQ: Officially US-based, HQ Brooklyn, New York. One founder is in Brooklyn, the other in Mexico City, with team members in **6+ countries**.

Remote policy: **Fully remote and async-first.** From the JD: "Work wherever you're happiest. We're fully remote"; "an async-first culture with a focus on documentation over standing meetings"; "your local holidays plus unmetered PTO (minimum 2 weeks mandatory PTO!)". Clear written communication is called an essential requirement.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes.** The JD states directly: "While Recast is officially based in the US, we're a fully remote team… We can't sponsor visas at this time, however we are happy to have you join remotely from wherever you are." Existing team members span 6+ countries including Mexico. Async-first working with distributed time zones means Mountain Time is a non-issue. Note the pay caveat below — the stated range is US-based and they say ranges "may have the salary range adjusted based on their local markets."

Engineering & tech: Python (primary — "you write idiomatic Python, design and ship production APIs in it, and it's your natural default") and Ruby on Rails (the existing app), REST APIs and **MCP**, AWS and Heroku, **Bedrock AgentCore, Anthropic SDK, LangChain/LangGraph**, PostgreSQL and Redis, Docker, Terraform, Kubernetes, GitHub Actions. The Apps & APIs team owns the customer-facing surface: the flagship MMM application, agentic apps, public APIs and the company's **MCP server** — the JD's headline first-six-months item is taking that MCP server "from early-stage to production-grade." Hiring process is five stages: hiring screen, hiring-manager interview with the Head of Engineering, technical interview with a short take-home plus code walkthrough, team interview, final interview with the CEO. They run identity verification and background screening.

Notable / other: Statistically serious product (Bayesian hierarchical modeling) with an unusually transparent public methodology. Their users are technical — "developers, marketing analysts and data scientists who will notice when something isn't right." Committed to diverse hiring; explicitly encourage under-represented applicants to apply without meeting every requirement.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Apps & APIs (Remote).
