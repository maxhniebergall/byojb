# Northbeam — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:northbeam | company_type: product

What they do: Northbeam builds a marketing-intelligence / multi-touch-attribution platform for eCommerce brands — in their words, "the world's most advanced marketing intelligence platform, providing top eCommerce brands a unified view of their business data through powerful attribution modeling and customizable dashboards. Our technology helps customers accurately track ad spend, understand the full customer journey, and drive profitable growth." Founded 2019. It tracks over $25B in ad spend, $130B in attributed revenue and 2.1T+ impressions for 800+ brands. In October 2025 it launched a "Clicks + Deterministic Views" attribution model connecting ad views and clicks across platforms to revenue.

How they describe themselves: The JD is unusually candid about identity and about pace. On identity: "**Northbeam is fundamentally a data product** - the whole company. We don't sell shoes, ads, games, or database technologies. We sell data: quality integrations with a variety of platforms, fresh and reliable data pulls, correct aggregations, and algorithmic insights on top of that data" — and therefore "our data systems team is foundational and load-bearing." On pace and culture: "We're experiencing rapid growth, have strong product-market fit… This is a rare chance to make a meaningful impact at a fast-moving, high-growth company. At Northbeam, you'll join a team of driven, collaborative, and talented individuals who value personal growth and excellence." And bluntly: "**This is a startup. The one thing that's constant is change.**… If this sounds like **your kind of chaos**, we'd love to hear from you." They also say "You will work with great people who have done this many times before."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, roughly 80–120 employees (~100). ~$30M raised in total: a $15M Series A led by Silversmith Capital Partners in 2022 and a $15M growth round in 2025 led by HighPost Capital alongside Silversmith, earmarked for growing the ~100-person team in engineering and go-to-market. Early-growth stage, not late-stage or confirmed profitable.

Locations / HQ: HQ El Segundo, California, with roots and a second office in San Francisco (some sources also list a New York presence).

Remote policy: In their own words, "We're a remote-friendly company with offices in San Francisco and Los Angeles." Reqs carry `#LI-Remote`. Note the phrasing is *remote-friendly*, not remote-first — offices exist and are named.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** They run two parallel copies of the same req, one located "Remote - USA" and one "Remote - Canada" (job 4687847006), so Canadian remote employment is explicitly on offer for this role family. Nothing in the JD imposes a timezone overlap requirement, though the company is California-anchored so expect Pacific-leaning hours — easy from Mountain Time.

Engineering & tech: Stated stack for the data systems team — **Python** (primary), **SQL and BigQuery**, **Airflow** for orchestration, **Docker/Kubernetes**, REST/GraphQL/Webhook-based API ETL, OAuth 2.0 / API-key auth and secrets management, event-driven plus batch architectures, and multi-tenant secure architecture. The work described is: designing scalable ingestion and transformation pipelines with reliability/observability/maintainability; building tenant-aware integration APIs; implementing observability, monitoring and alerting for data freshness and failures; and optimising data flows for cost and efficiency in a cloud-native environment. Data sources span ad platforms, order-management systems (Shopify, Amazon) and their own real-time event collection. Asks for 5+ years in data/software/integration engineering. The team is explicitly cross-functional — "you will work with a cross-functional team of product managers, product engineers, and business leaders to translate our customers' feedback into scalable data pipelines and products."

Notable / other: The domain is ad-tech/marketing attribution, which is cycle-exposed and dependent on the continuing viability of cross-platform tracking signals — a structural risk for the product independent of company execution. Against that, the engineering problem itself (a "labyrinth of integrations and transformations" over trillions of impressions) is real large-scale data infrastructure.

Compensation (posted on their own live JD): Senior Software Engineer, Data Systems (Python) — **$170,000 – $200,000 USD base**. Plus equity, medical/dental/vision, 401(k), flexible PTO, 12 company holidays, 12 weeks paid parental leave and a $500 WFH stipend.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Data Systems (Python) — posted twice, once Remote-USA and once Remote-Canada.
