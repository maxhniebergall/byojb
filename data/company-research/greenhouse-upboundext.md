What they do: Upbound builds control-plane infrastructure for cloud platform teams. They are the creator and primary maintainer of **Crossplane**, the open-source CNCF framework for building cloud-native control planes, and they sell the **Intelligent Control Plane** / **Upbound Spaces** — multi-control-plane management software, available both as SaaS (Upbound Cloud) and as self-hosted distributions for on-premises customers. Commercial products named on their site include Upbound Crossplane 2.0 and UXP 2.0 (an enterprise upgrade from open-source Crossplane). Customers are Fortune 500 companies and platform engineers in 100+ countries; they also partner with cloud providers, ISVs, and open-source communities.

How they describe themselves: Their stated mission is "to power the AI-native enterprise with a foundational platform layer that helps teams provision, operate, and adapt infrastructure at scale — so platforms are ready for both humans and AI agents." They pitch "complete declarative control" and declarative APIs "rather than manual workflows", and position themselves as "pioneering infrastructure platforms for the Agentic AI Era". Their careers page states seven principles: **accountability, decisive collaboration, craftsmanship, community care, customer advocacy, ownership mentality, and creating psychological safety through vulnerability.** The JD echoes the last one directly — "help create a safe environment where everyone can contribute, learn from failures, share on-call knowledge, and help each other grow" — and adds "reliability and debuggability are features". Their site signs off "Crafted with love by our globally distributed team."

Size / stage / funding: **Series B**, $69M raised to date, backed by **GV (formerly Google Ventures), Altimeter Capital, and Intel Capital**. Traction figures they state themselves: Crossplane past **100M+ downloads**, adopted by **1,000+ teams worldwide**, customers across 100+ countries including Fortune 500s. Total headcount is not published on any page read (unverified). As a Series B company they are venture-runway dependent rather than known-profitable.

Engineering & tech: **Go** against the **Kubernetes API** — operators, controllers, add-ons — with explicit emphasis on observability, debuggability and operational excellence. Multi-tenant SaaS operated in production plus self-hosted customer distributions. Observability stack named: **Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry, distributed tracing**. Practices named in the JD: design documents for new initiatives, detailed post-incident reviews, runbooks, user guides and blog posts authored by engineers, and support for the full lifecycle (discovery, analysis, architecture, design, review, documentation, building, migration, automation, deployment, production-readiness, ongoing operational support). Engineers **participate in an on-call rotation** for Upbound Cloud and work directly with customers to reproduce and resolve issues in their environments. Open-source contribution to Crossplane is part of the job. They are an active CNCF contributor and run a community Slack.

Notable / other: The concrete engineering challenges stated are attractive and specific — scaling to "thousands of control planes", diagnosing reconciliation-loop problems, managing API rate limits, and optimizing controller performance.
