# Harvey — research
Provider/key: ashby:harvey | company_type: product
What they do: Harvey builds AI for legal and professional services. In its own words: "we're transforming how legal and professional services operate. By combining frontier agentic AI, an enterprise-grade platform, and deep domain expertise, we're reshaping how critical knowledge work gets done for decades to come." Customers are law firms and enterprise legal/professional-services organisations; the company states "2400+ customers in 70+ countries".
How they describe themselves: Explicitly and at length. "This is a rare chance to help build a generational company at a true inflection point. With … strong product-market fit, and world-class investor support, we're scaling fast and defining a new category in real time. The work is ambitious, the bar is high, and the opportunity for growth — personal, professional, and financial — is unmatched." On culture: "Our team moves fast, takes ownership, and is deeply committed to the mission — operating with intensity, staying close to our customers, and pushing each other for excellence." The three stated values are **Decisiveness, Simplicity, and Job's Not Finished** — glossed by the company as "We act quickly on clear judgment over perfect information, we believe simplicity is what scales, and we're never satisfied with where we are." It closes: "At Harvey, the future of professional services is being written today — and we're just getting started."
Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, high-growth AI company; describes itself as scaling fast with "world-class investor support" and claims strong product-market fit and 2,400+ customers across 70+ countries. Headcount and funding round were not verified from a first-party source this session (search budget exhausted).
Locations / HQ: US-headquartered (not verified this session). The postings in this registry are for a **Toronto, Canada** engineering site.
Remote policy: In-person, and it differs by level in their own words. Senior: "This role is based in Toronto, Canada. We use a hybrid, 3+ days-per week in-person work model and offer relocation assistance to new employees." Staff: "This role is based in Toronto, Canada. We use an in-person work model and offer relocation assistance to new employees" — i.e. the Staff role is fully in-office. Both offer relocation, which confirms the roles are expected to be filled by someone physically in Toronto.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — verified from their own JD text. Canadian employer, but Toronto-in-person: 3+ days a week at senior level and fully in-person at staff level, with relocation assistance offered rather than remote work.
Engineering & tech: The Toronto backend team "owns and operates the platform layer that enables secure, reliable, and flexible experiences for our customers including notifications, permissions and feature flag infrastructure", and also builds "enterprise-grade collaboration experiences for law firms". Named scope: "foundational backend infrastructure including authentication, permissions, feature flagging, notifications, and document management integrations", plus building and evolving "Harvey's internal product platform, accelerating development across multiple product areas". The role is explicitly framed as straddling both sides: "ideal for engineers who are excited by the opportunity to move between deep platform thinking and hands-on product iteration", designing systems that "serve as critical infrastructure for the rest of the company" while "directly building user-facing features for some of the world's leading law firms".
