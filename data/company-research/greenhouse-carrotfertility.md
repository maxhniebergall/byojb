# Carrot (Carrot Fertility) — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:carrotfertility | company_type: product

What they do: Carrot is a global fertility and family-care benefits platform sold to employers,
health plans and health systems. Their framing: *"the leading global fertility and family care
platform, built on intelligent care orchestration: the right clinical guidance, at the right
moment, in the context of each member's life."* They claim more than a thousand multinational
employer/health-plan/health-system customers and support for millions of members across 195
countries, spanning pre-pregnancy through menopause. The value proposition to buyers is explicitly
cost savings for plan sponsors plus member outcomes.

How they describe themselves: The engineering org is organized around three stated ideas, quoted
verbatim from the JD: **"AI has changed what a small team can ship."**, **"Have a bias for
action."**, **"Own your outcomes."** The role is framed for engineers who *"thrive in high-autonomy
environments"* and *"enjoy operating with urgency, navigating ambiguity, and delivering
production-ready solutions without waiting for perfect conditions."* Other stated expectations:
own high-stakes work as the DRI end-to-end; *"Make the technical calls... Make speed-vs-durability
tradeoffs and live with the consequences"*; *"Operate what you build"* (reliability, observability,
incident response); *"Use AI as leverage"* — strong opinions about AI tooling and raising the org's
bar for it; *"Be a rising tide"* (mentoring); *"Drive measurable outcomes"* tied to a member
metric, cost line, reliability target or revenue lever. Culture is described as collaborative,
inclusive and values-driven, with Flexible Time Off and a stated culture of recharging.

Size / stage / funding: Venture-backed private healthtech, self-described as *"a high-growth
environment where leaders are empowered to shape strategy and drive impact."* Offers equity.
Recognition includes Fast Company Most Innovative Companies and World Changing Ideas, CNBC 100
Barrier Breaking Startups, Inc. Power Partners, Modern Healthcare Innovators, Fortune Best
Workplaces in Healthcare, Great Place to Work and Age-Friendly Employer certifications.

Locations / HQ: US-headquartered; operates globally by serving members in 195 countries (that is
member reach, not employment geography). The live engineering posting is listed simply as "Remote".

Remote policy: The relevant posting is listed as **Remote**. No geographic scope statement appears
in the JD body.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, and unverified.** The posting says "Remote" with no country
list. The compensation section quotes a single US-style ladder (Sr. Software Engineer I / II) with
no currency marker and no Canadian equivalent, and all company communication is stated to come from
@get-carrot.com. Nothing in the posting affirms Canadian employment. Treat as probably US-only
until confirmed; do not assume Canada eligibility.

Engineering & tech: Small-team, high-leverage model explicitly premised on AI-assisted development
(*"What used to take a team of engineers a quarter..."*). Engineers are DRIs owning problems from
product 1-pager through design, build, ship, monitor, fix, iterate, including partner integrations,
and are on the hook for reliability, observability and incident response in their domain. Work is
sequenced for incremental value delivery. No specific stack is named in the posting.

Notable / other: Security notice in the posting warns that all legitimate recruiting comes from
@get-carrot.com. Benefits include comprehensive parental leave and family-forming assistance,
retirement savings, short- and long-term incentives.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer; Applied AI Engineer, Enterprise.

Pay (stated on their own JD): starting base salary ranges from **Sr. Software Engineer I
($140,000–$160,000)** to **Sr. Software Engineer II ($160,000–$200,000)**. The JD does not label a
currency; given a US-headquartered employer and a US-style posted range, USD is the reading.
