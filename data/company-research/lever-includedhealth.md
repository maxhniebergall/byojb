# Included Health — research
Provider/key: lever:includedhealth | company_type: product
What they do: Included Health is a healthcare navigation and virtual-care company serving employers and health plans — care navigation, advocacy and integrated virtual care for member populations. Their own framing: they are "propelled by the opportunity to redefine healthcare for all", delivering "high-quality, integrated care" using "data-driven insights and human-centered design." Their engineering framing: "we're building technology that makes healthcare more accessible, personalized, and effective for our members."
How they describe themselves: Five stated values, in full — **Member First** (prioritize member outcomes and advocate for accessible healthcare), **Outcomes-Focused** (impact over effort, accountability for results), **Diverse Perspectives** (space for different viewpoints and candid debate), **Value Change** (embrace adaptation and lifelong learning in ambiguous situations), and **Lead the Way** (innovate, solve problems unconventionally). JD language emphasizes "ownership and leadership—making thoughtful technical decisions, setting direction, and mentoring others."
Size / stage / funding: Large private company formed from the Grand Rounds / Doctor On Demand merger; exact current headcount, funding position and profitability are not stated in the fetched material — unverified. They list workplace and innovation awards spanning 2024-2026.
Locations / HQ: HQ not stated on the careers page fetched; US-focused, with enterprise customers referenced as having "U.S. employees".
Remote policy: Explicitly blended rather than remote-first: they describe a workforce of "remote, hybrid, and in-office talent", enabling "team members to work in the ways that support their own success and flexibility." The individual engineering postings in the registry are all listed as "Remote, remote", and the Cloud Security JD states "This is a remote role reporting to the Chief Information Security Officer."
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, leaning no.** The roles are genuinely remote, but the business is US healthcare (HIPAA/PHI, US employer customers) and the careers page references US employees only. No Canada statement exists anywhere in the fetched material or the JD bodies. Treat as unverified and likely US-only.
Engineering & tech: The clearest technical picture comes from the Staff Cloud Security JD: "engineering, implementing, and automating robust security controls within our cloud environments (AWS primarily, with GCP considerations)", building "advanced security solutions, often through code (Python, Go, etc.) and automation (Terraform)", covering access control, development-environment security and infrastructure hardening, with an explicit goal of "prevention of unauthorized PHI exfiltration" and reducing HIPAA incidents. That role is "a key technical peer to our infrastructure software and engineering teams". Product engineering reports to Engineering Managers and partners with PMs and designers.
Notable / other: PHI/HIPAA compliance is a first-class engineering constraint, which tends to mean disciplined change management and durable internal-platform work. Note the registry also carries a non-engineering posting ("Sr. Provider Enrollment Specialist") under this company.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Backend; Staff Software Engineer, Cloud Security; Staff Software Engineer, Product.
