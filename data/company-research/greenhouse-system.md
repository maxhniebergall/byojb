The fetched site (System Inc., healthcare/knowledge-graph, Public Benefit Corporation) matches the JD text exactly — same PBC framing, same healthcare-first positioning, same "see and solve anything as a system" language. Entity confirmed.

What they do: System builds a quantitative model of how the world's systems interconnect, starting in healthcare. Named products from their site: **System Graph** (a continuously evolving model integrating scientific evidence and the relationships between entities), **System Health** (clinical decision-support applications for providers), and **System Apps** including *WholeHealth* (disease determinants and molecular pathways) and *Synthesis* (literature synthesis). From their JD: "System builds software to help the world see and solve anything as a system, starting in healthcare." The site frames the mission as moving knowledge "from isolated silos to interconnected systems" to address health, climate, migration and inequality.

How they describe themselves: A **Public Benefit Corporation** "driven by purpose and shaped by values." From the JDs: "We aspire to help the world see itself differently and build a more responsible and values-driven model of a tech company… We believe in the power of autonomous, interdisciplinary, and diverse teams; in agile development; and in leading with values, first principles, and clear high-level priorities backed by data. We believe in cultivating a growth mindset." Their "you might be a fit if you" list is effectively their full values statement, quoted: think in systems (feedback loops, interdependencies, second-order effects); motivated by purpose-driven work at the intersection of technology and healthcare; believe technology should be a force for good and are drawn to the PBC model; "hold yourself and your work to a **values-first standard, not just a deliverables-first one**"; lead with first principles and question assumptions; "see complexity as an invitation, not an obstacle"; care about downstream effects on users, systems and society; "operate with **intellectual humility** — always learning, always open to being wrong."

Size / stage / funding: Startup, venture-backed — "backed by top-tier VCs in Silicon Valley and New York and leading angel investors and **founded by the former VP Data at Spotify**." Engineering & tech: The Data & AI/ML Engineering track is a good technical match on paper: "designing the pipelines, platforms, and **model-serving systems** that power our healthcare data products — reliably, responsibly, and at production grade." Scope: scalable data pipelines and ETL/ELT; infrastructure for **training, deploying and serving ML models in production**; **feature stores, vector databases** and other AI-enabling data infrastructure; reliability, low latency, high availability; moving research findings into production with Research/Data Science; monitoring and observability for data and model health; IaC and documentation on cloud platforms. Asks 4+ years, strong **Python and SQL**, with Spark/dbt/Airflow a plus, cloud data infra on AWS/GCP/Azure, ML lifecycle management, model versioning and deployment patterns. Bonuses: Docker/Kubernetes, **knowledge graphs / graph databases / semantic data models**, live data migrations, clinical/healthcare data, **LLMOps or AI governance frameworks**.
The Software Engineering track asks 3+ years, Python/TypeScript/Go, REST APIs and distributed systems, systems design fundamentals, cloud + IaC, with scope including "resilient infrastructure for serving data at scale," CI/CD, code review and — notably — "Influence culture, **recruit new engineers**, and help shape a rapidly growing startup."

Notable / other: Partnerships and validation cited on their site: the **Novartis Foundation** collaborates with System on its AI4HealthyCities cardiovascular-health initiative, and a **Mass General Brigham** expert panel identified "systems-level disease views" as a top healthcare need.
