# Anaplan — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:anaplan | company_type: product
What they do: Anaplan sells an enterprise connected-planning platform — scenario planning and analysis for finance, supply chain, sales and workforce planning. In its own words it is "a team of innovators focused on optimizing business decision-making through our leading AI-infused scenario planning and analysis platform so our customers can outpace their competition and the market." Customers are large enterprises; it cites "2,400+ global companies" including Coca-Cola, LinkedIn, Adobe, LVMH and Bayer, and says they "rank among the who's who in the Fortune 50."
How they describe themselves: The dominant self-description is the "Winning Culture", which they call "the engine that drives our teams of innovators": "We champion diversity of thought and ideas, we behave like leaders regardless of title, we are committed to achieving ambitious goals, and we love celebrating our wins – big and small." Their operating principles are stated as "being strategy-led, values-based and disciplined in execution." The careers site adds a values acronym, "I ACT REAL", and describes the work model as "One Connected Team". Recurring emphasis on "our collective commitment to our customers' success."
Size / stage / funding: Not stated on the pages fetched. NOTE — from prior knowledge rather than a fetched source, and therefore UNVERIFIED here: Anaplan was formerly NYSE-listed (PLAN) and was taken private by Thoma Bravo in 2022, making it PE-owned rather than publicly traded. The careers page fetch did not confirm this either way; treat the ownership question as open.
Locations / HQ: Global, with regions listed as North America, EMEA (France, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Spain, Sweden, UK) and APAC (Australia, India, Indonesia, Japan, Philippines, Singapore). The careers page's own location list does not name a Canadian office, yet the registry contains a live posting located in Ottawa, Canada — so a Canadian presence exists that the careers site does not advertise.
Remote policy: Hybrid. The careers page describes a hybrid "One Connected Team" model and positions flexibility as part of that model rather than as remote-first. Some individual US postings are location-tagged as remote within a named state, e.g. "Pennsylvania-Remote, United States".
Remote-Canada eligibility: NOT ESTABLISHED — treat as no. There is exactly one Canadian posting (Principal Engineer, Product Security & Identity) and it is location-tagged "Ottawa, Canada" with no remote qualifier; the JD body captured in the registry contains only corporate boilerplate and no work-model statement. The US remote roles are scoped to a US state ("Pennsylvania-Remote"), which is a US employment relationship, not a Canadian one. Nothing found states that Anaplan hires Canada-based remote employees outside Ottawa.
Engineering & tech: Not well characterised from the material available — the JD bodies captured in the registry are almost entirely corporate boilerplate (winning-culture blurb, EEO statement, and a long recruitment-fraud disclaimer) with little substantive role content. The role titles indicate the shape of the org: a Principal-heavy AI and data-engineering push (Principal Engineer - AI, Principal Machine Learning Engineer, Principal Data Engineer, Principal Data Engineer - AI, Senior Data Engineer - AI), plus Kubernetes platform engineering, FinOps data engineering and a Salesforce-flavoured platform-experience track.
Notable / other: The JD boilerplate carries an unusually prominent recruitment-fraud disclaimer, warning that fraudulent Anaplan job offers circulate and that Anaplan never sends job offers by email. The AI/data hiring is concentrated in London and US-remote.
