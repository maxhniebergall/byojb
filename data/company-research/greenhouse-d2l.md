# D2L — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:d2l | company_type: product (EdTech SaaS)
What they do: Cloud learning-management software (Brightspace) for schools, campuses and companies. In their words: "D2L is a cloud company that is modernizing education and building the Future of Work ... New models of teaching and learning enable a personalized, student-centric experience - and deliver improved retention, engagement, satisfaction, and results for learners of all ages - in schools, campuses, and companies."
How they describe themselves: Mission-driven with growth framing. "D2L has had a singular mission for 25 years and is dedicated to that same mission in the years ahead: to transform the way the world learns - and by doing so, we will help improve human potential globally." Also "D2L is disrupting the way the world learns" and "No other company provides a solution as robust and innovative as D2L." On its careers page it calls itself "a passionate, collaborative, mission-driven company" with "a culture of recognition, fun, trust and collaboration", seeking people who are "driven, passionate, mission aligned, curious, and excited to make a impact on global education", and describes itself as "established but continuing to innovate", "at a stage of incredible growth and boundless market potential".
Size / stage / funding: Established — 25 years old, publicly traded (D2L Inc., Waterloo). Multiple global offices. Not early-stage or runway-dependent.
Locations / HQ: Headquartered in Waterloo/Kitchener, Ontario. Canadian offices in Kitchener, Winnipeg and Vancouver; also Melbourne, Australia. Their own benefits list mentions "Dog Friendly Offices Spaces at our HQ in Kitchener, Winnipeg, Vancouver and Melbourne offices."
Remote policy: **Hybrid, three days per week in office** — stated flatly in the JD body: "D2L operates in a hybrid work style, with expectation of 3 days per week in office." The careers page softens this to schedule flexibility only: full-time staff work 40 hours a week but "when you arrive and when you leave each day is flexible", with employees working "around a schedule that works for them", though "some roles may require specific coverage."
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No, despite appearances — this is a data-quality trap.** The scanner records these postings' location as "Kitchener, Toronto, Winnipeg, Vancouver, Canada Remote", which reads as remote-eligible. The JD body of the same posting contradicts it: three days per week in office is expected. With offices only in Kitchener, Winnipeg, Vancouver and Melbourne, none of which is reachable from Kimberley BC, these roles are effectively disqualifying. Treat the "Canada Remote" string in the registry as unreliable for D2L.
Engineering & tech: Broad product engineering rather than specialised infrastructure — "a wide range of technologies including, but not limited to: distributed systems, machine learning, user interfaces, mobile and cloud computing." Required: "Experience with OO design and proficient coding skills (ie: C++, C# or Java)", "Strong understanding of Computer Science topics including: data structures, algorithms, databases, distributed systems", plus "Acumen with Artificial Intelligence tools". Senior expectations are leadership-flavoured: "Provide technical leadership across teams, through mentoring, influence and leading by example" and "Solves ambiguous multi-system problems across teams/initiatives", typically at "8+ years experience". Hiring runs a structured technical screen and coding assessment.
Compensation (posted on their own JDs, CAD base): Software Developer $70,000-$105,000; Senior Software Developer $90,000-$130,000; Senior Software Developer 2 $110,000-$155,000; Senior Software Developer 2, Artificial Intelligence $130,000-$160,000. Their pay-transparency block adds that base "is aligned to ensure both internal and external competitiveness using market data for the geographic location and industry" and that roles "may be eligible for additional benefits including a Wellness Subsidy, Equity Grants, Variable Incentive, and more."
Notable / other: They state a human-review hiring policy: "Every application we receive is personally reviewed by a member of our Talent Acquisition team - yes, a real person looks at your resume! While we use AI tools internally to streamline tasks like meeting notes, summaries, and administrative work, these tools never rank resumes, make hiring decisions, or influence candidate evaluations." Benefits include a SkillsWave continuing-education programme with 2 paid days for exams, 2 paid volunteer days, home-internet reimbursement, wellness reimbursement and retirement planning.
