What they do: Fintech operating an electronic corporate-bond trading platform. In their own words: "Trumid is a dynamic fintech revolutionizing the landscape of fixed income trading. With intelligent, easy-to-use, electronic solutions, we are rapidly growing... Founded in 2014 by a team of fixed income market experts, Trumid has quickly become one of the top three corporate bond e-trading platforms in the U.S. Today, over 1,300 traders from an extensive and expanding client network of 890+ buy-and sell-side institutions transact on Trumid monthly." Engineering & tech: The Data & Intelligence team builds "the analytics platform that powers reporting, decision-making, and data delivery across the organization". Stated stack: "BigQuery, dbt, Fivetran, SQL-based data modeling, and modern analytics and semantic layer tools. We rely heavily on AI-powered IDEs and agentic workflows for development." Role scope: curated dbt/BigQuery data models defining consistent business metrics; data-delivery systems for dashboards, client-facing reporting, compliance datasets and other external data products; governed datasets and documentation enabling self-service analytics; query performance and cost optimisation in BigQuery; data governance including documentation, lineage, testing and quality standards; mentoring engineers and analysts. They note the role "focuses primarily on building the data models and datasets that power analytics and reporting, rather than working exclusively on dashboards", though engineers "may occasionally contribute to reporting or visualization work". A separate group within the same platform team builds real-time data systems and infrastructure. They are "hiring for multiple roles and levels on this team".
