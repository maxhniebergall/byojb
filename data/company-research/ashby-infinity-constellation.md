# Infinity Constellation (Supernal) — research
Provider/key: ashby:infinity-constellation | company_type: product
What they do: Infinity Constellation operates as a multi-brand holding/venture structure — its Ashby board carries requisitions for at least three distinct brands: Supernal, Labrynth and Everest. The brand behind all the senior engineering roles is Supernal, which in its own words "help[s] SMBs hire their first AI employee. Our AI teammates are built with intelligent, agentic workflows and deployed on our proprietary platform. We don't build tools — we deliver working, value-generating AI Employees." The commercial model is delivery-led: engineers "translate customer requirements and SOWs into working systems, own delivery timelines," and act as "technical owner for client delivery." So it is a proprietary agent platform sold as implemented outcomes rather than as software.
How they describe themselves: Engineers are branded internally as "Masons" ("Our AI Platform Engineers, known internally as Masons, are the builders behind these systems"). Tone is high-intensity craft/ownership language: "This is a hands-on role. You're not just reviewing PRs or sitting in meetings — you're building, debugging, and shipping, while raising the engineering bar through crisp technical judgment and strong ownership," and they want "an elite debugger." "These are not demos or experiments — they are production systems that customers rely on." Locations / HQ: Not stated. All roles are posted as "Remote (Global)".
Remote policy: Fully remote, global. Roles report to a Director of Engineering.
Remote-Canada eligibility: YES, effectively — the Senior AI Engineer requisition states "Location: Remote (Global)" and "Type: Contractor or EOR FTE," meaning they engage internationally via contractor agreements or an Employer of Record. A British Columbia candidate would be reachable. Note the flip side: contractor/EOR engagement means no employment benefits and self-managed taxes.
Engineering & tech: The Staff role is substantive platform work — Django/DRF/ASGI backend, distributed task infrastructure across Celery + Dramatiq + Temporal/Cortex, resilient workflow patterns (retries, circuit breakers, graceful degradation), PostgreSQL/pgvector query tuning, Kubernetes and Helm, event-driven architecture, and an observability stack. The client-facing Senior role covers LLM orchestration, RAG design, workflow decomposition, multi-turn conversation state management, and production voice agents with telephony/streaming-audio integration (ElevenLabs, Retell, Nextiva), including latency budgets, interruption handling and fallback paths.
Notable / other: Compensation is stated openly on the Senior AI Engineer requisition as "Rate: $35-50/hr" — for a role asking 4+ years of production experience plus deep agentic/voice/RAG expertise. Annualised at 2080 hours that is roughly USD $73k–$104k with no benefits. Testing discipline is emphasised (unit plus end-to-end as a condition of shipping), as is error taxonomy (retryable vs fatal, no silent failures). English fluency is explicitly required, which alongside the global-contractor model suggests a globally arbitraged talent pool.
