What they do: Hopper is a travel platform and travel fintech company. In their own words: "we are on a
mission to become the leading travel platform globally – powering Hopper's mobile app, website and our
B2B business, HTS (Hopper Technology Solutions). By leveraging massive amounts of data and advanced
machine learning algorithms, Hopper combines its world-class travel agency offering with proprietary
fintech products to bring transparency, flexibility and savings to travelers globally." Its fintech
products (Cancel for Any Reason, Flight Disruption Assistance) price and absorb travel risk. HTS, the B2B
arm, embeds those products in partners' own channels and now represents "more than 75% of the business";
named partners include Capital One (co-developed Capital One Travel), Air Canada, Uber, CommBank, Nubank,
Flair Airlines and Spirit Airlines.

How they describe themselves: "Well-funded and proven startup with large ambitions"; "Entrepreneurial
culture where pushing limits and taking risks is everyday business"; "Open communication with management
and company leadership"; "Small, dynamic teams = massive impact". Engineering JDs ask for a "bias for
action", self-direction, comfort with "technical ambiguity", and a "commercial mindset" / "product
mindset". They describe the working environment as "remote-first". Claimed scale: 120M+ app downloads,
"hundreds of millions of travelers", 70% Gen-Z/millennial users, "billions of dollars worth of travel and
travel fintech" sold annually, ~30% fintech attach rate; named "#1 most innovative company in travel by
Fast Company".

Size / stage / funding: Private, late-stage — "well-funded and proven startup" offering "pre-IPO equity
packages". Exact headcount, latest round and profitability UNVERIFIED from local sources.

Remote policy: Remote-first, stated in their own JD text ("collaborate with cross-functional squads in a
remote-first environment"). Perks include a work-from-home stipend and on-demand co-working space via
FlexDesk, which is consistent with distributed work rather than an office mandate.

Remote-Canada eligibility: YES. Roles are posted as "Toronto - Remote, Vancouver - Remote" with CAD
compensation, and the benefits are Canadian-specific (RRSP plan with automatic pre-tax withdrawals,
Dialogue telemedicine, HSA). A Vancouver-remote listing is a good sign for a BC-based candidate, though
whether they hire outside the listed metros — and specifically into Mountain Time — is UNVERIFIED.

Engineering & tech: Substantial distributed-systems and production-ML work.
- Backend: Scala (primary) or other JVM languages (Java, Kotlin), microservices, gRPC, Pub/Sub, REST,
  high-throughput low-latency booking services, third-party GDS/lodging/cars partner integrations,
  production troubleshooting in complex existing codebases, full ownership from implementation through
  deployment.
- ML/fintech platform: the Fintech Foundation team owns "the pricing engine that balances customer demand
  against financial risk across dozens of partners". Work described is real MLOps — "automated, reusable
  training pipelines", ETL and feature engineering, real-time ML pricing deployed to production, and
  monitoring "for latency, drift, and training-serving skew", plus champion-challenger testing. Stack:
  Python, Scala, SQL, distributed data-processing frameworks.
This is model-systems infrastructure, not prompt-wrapper work.
