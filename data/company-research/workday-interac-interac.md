# Interac Corp. — research
Provider/key: workday:interac/interac | company_type: product
What they do: Interac operates Canada's domestic payment and verification infrastructure — Interac Debit at point of sale, Interac e-Transfer for bank-to-bank money movement, Interac Direct for online payments, and a digital identity verification service. It is effectively a national utility for money movement: its customers are Canada's banks, credit unions and merchants rather than end consumers directly. 2024 volumes cited on its own site: 6.6B Interac Debit transactions, 1.4B e-Transfer transactions, 568M verification transactions.
How they describe themselves: Its homepage frames the purpose around helping Canadians "stay in control of your money" and providing "convenient digital access to verify and authenticate your data so you can get more out of life." Tone is institutional and security-first rather than growth/hype — no rocketship language anywhere on the public site. A fuller values statement lives at interac.ca/en/company/careers/careers-at-interac/, which was not fetched this run.
Size / stage / funding: Private Canadian corporation owned by a broad membership of Canadian financial institutions (recalled, not verified this run). Not venture-funded; revenue comes from per-transaction fees, so the business is structurally profitable and non-runway-dependent. Its own JD text describes a "profitable" established company.
Locations / HQ: Toronto, Ontario. All four live engineering postings in the registry are Toronto-based.
Remote policy: Hybrid, Toronto-anchored. The "Senior Software Developer" JD explicitly advertises a "flexible hybrid work model for better work-life balance" — i.e. hybrid, not remote. The platform postings do not state a policy but are all located in Toronto.
Remote-Canada eligibility: Canada-only employment, YES — but geographically pinned to Toronto/GTA. No evidence of a remote-anywhere-in-Canada option in any of the four live JDs. Treat as NOT eligible for a BC-based remote worker unless confirmed otherwise with the recruiter.
Engineering & tech: Two distinct groups visible. (a) On-prem platform: OpenShift + OpenShift Service Mesh (Istio), RHEL administration, Docker golden-image lifecycle, CockroachDB / EDB Postgres, Ansible, Jenkins/GitHub Actions/JFrog, TLS & certificate/key management, observability. (b) Cloud platform: AWS (IAM, VPC, S3, KMS, CloudWatch, PrivateLink, Transit Gateway), Terraform, GitHub Actions, Artifactory, SonarQube, CyberArk, SRE practice. Application side is Java/Spring Boot/Hibernate/Netty, REST microservices on Istio/Envoy, Oracle + PostgreSQL, Hazelcast/Memcached, Maven, Bamboo. Real-time, high-availability payment systems; on-call rotations and after-hours production work are stated explicitly on the cloud platform role.
Notable / other: Benefits mentioned in its own JD text: generous vacation and wellness days, pregnancy and parental leave top-up. Domain is genuinely mission-critical national infrastructure — the "boring, load-bearing" category rather than product/growth.
Open relevant roles (sample): Platform Engineering, Senior Specialist (REQ-2244); Platform Services & Cloud Engineering, Senior Specialist (REQ-2291); Senior Software Developer (REQ-2090); Senior Data Engineering Developer, 8-month fixed term (REQ-2290).
Comp on record: two of its own Toronto JDs state CAD ranges — CAD 110,000–130,000 (Platform Services & Cloud Engineering, Senior Specialist) and CAD 120,000–150,000 (Senior Software Developer). The fixed-term Senior Data Engineering Developer states CAD 125,000–145,000. These are modest for senior platform work.
