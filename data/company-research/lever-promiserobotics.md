# Promise Robotics — research
Provider/key: lever:promiserobotics | company_type: product
What they do: Promise Robotics builds an AI-powered offsite construction platform for homebuilding — "Factory-as-a-Service®". The system converts architectural designs into manufacturing instructions and drives robotic production of home components (framing/panelized structures) inside AI-powered factories, with streamlined on-site assembly. Around that core it offers push-button manufacturing, automated cost estimation, integrated procurement, production management, real-time analytics, and logistics coordination. They claim homes delivered up to 60% faster with roughly double the labour productivity, positioning against North America's construction labour shortage.
How they describe themselves: From the JDs: "Promise Robotics is pioneering Physical AI for homebuilding — a rapidly growing, venture-backed company bringing together AI, robotics, and manufacturing to help the homebuilding industry meet the growing demand for housing." And the differentiator they lead with: "We're building physical AI for housing — and unlike most of the field, our robots are already in production, fabricating real structures and generating real operational data every day across multiple factories." The site's framing is "built by builders, for builders", emphasising operational simplicity, cost certainty and scalable housing production. "Our team spans AI researchers and roboticists to factory operators, technicians, and production teams."
