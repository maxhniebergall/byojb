# GoodShip — research
Provider/key: ashby:goodship | company_type: product

Entity check: the GoodShip here is the freight/transportation-procurement software company
(FreightTech), confirmed from the JD's own description. No first-party website could be verified
this session — the only registry URL is an Ashby board page (client-rendered, off-limits to fetch)
and web search was exhausted. Everything below is from GoodShip's own job description text as
captured in the registry.

What they do: In their words, "GoodShip is the AI engine powering modern transportation networks,
empowering transportation teams to optimize spend and service while driving seamless collaboration
across internal stakeholders and external partners." Concretely: they "unify data from loads,
tenders, contracts, real-time tracking, and market benchmarks to deliver a comprehensive,
intelligent view of the transportation network," then let shippers act on it — "renegotiating rates,
refining routing guide logic, triggering carrier performance alerts, or running off-cycle bids and
full-network RFPs through purpose-built procurement tools." Customers are enterprise transportation
/ logistics teams.

How they describe themselves: "We're backed by A-list venture firms and founders of industry-leading
FreightTech companies, and were recently recognized in the TechCrunch AI Disrupters 60. With ample
financial backing and strong traction, we're seeking a driven and experienced [candidate] to join us
on the ground floor and help bring ultra-modern transportation technology to market." The role copy
itself is more measured and platform-minded: "This is a highly leveraged engineering role where your
customers are other engineers"; the goal is to "remove operational bottlenecks, improve engineering
productivity, and establish a culture where operational excellence and engineering velocity
reinforce one another." Notably absent: "fast-paced", "wear many hats", "hustle". Present: "join us
on the ground floor", "driven", "high-growth" — early-stage framing.

Size / stage / funding: Venture-backed early-stage startup (facet extraction: `company_stage:
startup`; "ground floor" language). Investors are described only as "A-list venture firms and
founders of industry-leading FreightTech companies" — no named investors, round or headcount.

Engineering & tech: AWS production workloads; Infrastructure as Code with Terraform, Pulumi or
CloudFormation; modern CI/CD pipelines; observability with Datadog, Grafana, Prometheus,
OpenTelemetry; SLIs/SLOs, dashboards, alerting and incident response; Kubernetes, ECS, Docker;
PostgreSQL; event-driven architectures; internal developer platforms and self-service tooling. The
role also carries a data-reliability mandate — "improve the accuracy, consistency, and reliability
of the data flowing through the platform... proactively identifying and preventing data integrity
issues before they impact customers." They explicitly expect AI-assisted development: "Proficiency
using AI development tools such as ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, or Claude as productivity multipliers,"
and want the platform to "further enable AI-assisted engineering." Bar is "5+ years" as a software /
platform / SRE / DevOps engineer.
