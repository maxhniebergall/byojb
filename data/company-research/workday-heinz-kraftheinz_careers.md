# Kraft Heinz — research
Provider/key: workday:heinz/kraftheinz_careers | company_type: product

What they do: Kraft Heinz is one of the largest packaged-food companies in the world (Heinz, Kraft,
Philadelphia, Kool-Aid and dozens of other brands), publicly traded and operating globally. Software
engineering here is a corporate "Digital" function supporting the CPG business, not a software product
line. The registry's engineering-titled openings are a mix of true software roles and R&D process/
packaging engineering roles that merely share the word "Engineer".

How they describe themselves: No corporate page could be read this session — heinz.com resolves to a
consumer product marketing site ("Discover what's new at Heinz(R). New products, exciting flavors, great
collabs and more.") with no corporate content, and no other first-party domain was derivable from the
Workday tenant without guessing. The only first-party voice available is their own JD, which is written
in brand-playful corporate style: sections are headed "Senior DevOps Engineer at a glance..." and
"What's on the menu?".

Size / stage / funding: Very large publicly traded multinational CPG company. Stability is not in
question; the sector has been under margin pressure and periodic restructuring, but that was not
verified this session.

Locations / HQ: Dual-HQ Chicago and Pittsburgh (not verified this session). Registry postings are at
Toronto - Queen's Quay - Headquarters (the Canadian HQ), Glenview, IL, and Ahmedabad - Venus Stratum GCC
(their Indian global capability centre).

Remote policy: Hybrid and office-anchored. Their own Toronto JD states the policy in its header:
"Location : Toronto, ON / Hybrid : 3 days/week". No remote-first or async language appears anywhere.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — the one Canadian role is explicitly Toronto hybrid at three days a week
in the Queen's Quay office. That is an in-office role for practical purposes and is not workable from
interior British Columbia. The other live roles are Glenview, IL (US) and Ahmedabad, India.

Engineering & tech: The Toronto Senior DevOps Engineer JD is a genuine, well-scoped release-engineering
role: "Design, build, and optimize automated CI/CD workflows (Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions)";
"Implement automated release validation steps including build verification, test gates, security scans,
dependency checks, and artifact promotion"; "Develop, implement, and continuously improve rollback
strategies (blue/green, canary, feature flags, versioned deployments)"; observability via "Azure Monitor,
Log Analytics, Application Insights, and/or Datadog". Stack from the extracted facets: Azure, Azure
DevOps, GitHub Actions, Kubernetes/AKS, Docker, Helm, ArgoCD, Flux, Datadog, OPA, GitOps; Python, Bash,
PowerShell. It asks for "5+ yrs DevOps/Release Engineering/SRE/Platform/Cloud". The framing is
internal-platform work serving other engineering teams — "Ensure release information is accessible and
self-service rather than dependent on manual communication."

Notable / other: Several of the highest-scoring titles in this company's list are not software at all
("Sr. Principal Engineer, R&D - Packaging Process Development & Reliability Engineering", "Principal
Engineer, R&D - Thermoforms") — a title-matching artifact worth being aware of when reading the
company's posting counts. The India GCC in Ahmedabad absorbs the data-engineering roles.

Pay (first-party): the Toronto Senior DevOps Engineer JD posts CAD $118,600 - $148,200 base, no equity.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior DevOps Engineer (Toronto, hybrid 3 days/week); Data Engineer II
(Ahmedabad GCC); Sr. Principal Engineer, R&D - E2E Complex Processing Project Delivery (Glenview, IL).

Sources: JD bodies under data/posting-research/ (Kraft Heinz Workday postings R-104445, R-104207);
https://www.heinz.com/ (consumer site, no corporate content). No corporate careers or values page was
readable this session.
