# Cribl — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:cribl | company_type: product

What they do: Cribl builds vendor-neutral telemetry infrastructure — the pipeline layer that collects, routes, reduces, enriches, stores and searches observability and security data before it reaches expensive analytics back-ends. Their own framing: "Join the company that's building the telemetry infrastructure for the AI era. At Cribl, we partner with IT and Security teams at many of the world's biggest enterprises, including half of the Fortune 100, to bridge the gap between AI ambition and infrastructure reality. As the AI Platform for Telemetry, we give customers the choice, control, and flexibility to manage and analyze telemetry for both humans and agents, so they can build what's next." The about page repeats the promise as "choice, control, and flexibility to manage, investigate, and analyze telemetry for both humans and agents, with no lock-in, no data loss, and no compromises." Products span stream/pipeline processing, edge collection, search and storage.

How they describe themselves: Five stated company values, quoted from their about page: (1) "Customers First, Always" — cross-functional collaboration centred on customer experience; (2) "Irreverent, but Serious" — a deliberately fun workplace paired with commitment to execution; (3) "Insatiably Curious"; (4) "Open and Authentic" — transparency treated as a strategic advantage; (5) "Excel Together". In JDs they add the cultural self-description: "With a global workforce, we're remote-first and grounded in a simple idea: software is a people business. Cribl is the place where curious, collaborative people can do their best work, grow fast, and bring their full selves to the herd." The "herd"/goat motif is their running in-joke ("enjoying all the goat gifs the internet has to offer"). Tone is playful but the engineering content is serious; there is some growth language ("one of the fastest-growing private companies", "grow fast") but no hustle/24-7 framing.

Size / stage / funding: 1,000+ employees. $300M+ ARR as of December 2025. Last raise was a Series E of $319M at a $3.5B valuation in August 2024. Investors: Sequoia Capital, Greylock Partners, Redpoint Ventures, Tiger Global Management, CRV, Google Ventures, IVP. Founded July 2018. Late-stage private with substantial revenue — not runway-dependent in the fragile sense.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco, California. The about page does not enumerate offices; the company describes itself as having "a global workforce". Live postings show United States and Poland.

Remote policy: Explicitly remote-first — "With a global workforce, we're remote-first". Individual JDs then scope the role by country, e.g. "This role is remote within the U.S." Note one SRE posting is "Remote - Poland", so remote hiring is not US-exclusive as a matter of company policy; it is per-req. On-call is explicit for SRE: "This position will require stand-by, on-call, or off-hours duties."

Remote-Canada eligibility: UNCLEAR, leaning no for the CURRENT openings. This is worth re-checking when new reqs post rather than treating as settled.

Engineering & tech: Node.js/TypeScript is the primary language — "Development with JavaScript/Node.js/TypeScript in a Linux/Mac environment", with "Background in Linux Systems Engineering". Infrastructure: Terraform (preferred) or Ansible, AWS and Azure, containers and orchestration, cloud SDKs. Observability tooling: New Relic, Splunk, CloudWatch, Prometheus, Grafana/Kibana, Sentry. Notably good SRE philosophy stated in the JD: "Fixing things at the operational side should always be the last resort, so our SREs are involved from conception to design to development and all the way through production and beyond", plus "sustainable incident response in a blameless environment" and "Help identify and drive down toil with creative innovation and automation". Team names visible: Core Platform, Infrastructure, Edge, Storage, Systems Insights, Cribl AI, AI Research.

Notable / other: Pay is transparent on every US req and strong: Senior SRE $141.8-195K USD base; Senior SWE ~$147.5-208.75K; Senior Infrastructure ~$162.5-207.5K; Staff Platform/Infrastructure ~$190-245K; Staff Software Engineer, Cribl AI $225-265K. The AI roles are infrastructure-flavoured (telemetry for agents, AI research on telemetry data), not chatbot wrappers. Half the Fortune 100 as customers implies an enterprise, contract-driven, relatively stable revenue base.
