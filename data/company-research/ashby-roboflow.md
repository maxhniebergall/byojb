# Roboflow — research
Provider/key: ashby:roboflow | company_type: product

What they do: Roboflow is a computer-vision developer platform — dataset management and AI-assisted annotation, hosted model training, a low-code workflow builder, and model deployment to cloud, edge, VPC or API. Their own site claims 1M+ engineers and 16,000+ organizations use it, "including those from half the Fortune 100"; cited applications include cell counting for cancer research, construction-site safety, floor-plan digitization, coral-reef monitoring and drone guidance. A large part of the business is open source: `inference` (their CV inference engine), `supervision`, `trackers`, `notebooks`, and the Universe dataset/model hub.

How they describe themselves: Mission is "to make the world programmable." From their JDs: "Roboflowers love building great things with passionate teammates. We value ownership, accountability, and a bias toward action—whether it's a big initiative or a small fix." They repeatedly emphasize a founder mindset and autonomy: "Much of our current team is made up of former founders and thrive in the level of autonomy at Roboflow. Maybe you had a side hustle in high school or college." A team member is quoted describing it as "a company full of giant brains and tiny egos." They also self-describe as "AI-native": "we expect our team to use AI to accelerate everything from writing code and fixing bugs to analyzing security, cost, and performance." Site-level values are developer-first, open source, and enterprise-grade reliability (SOC 2 Type 2, HIPAA-compatible).

Size / stage / funding: ~100 people ("Our team of ~100"). Raised "over 63 million" from Y Combinator, Google Ventures, Craft Ventures, Sam Altman and Lachy Groom. Private; profitability not stated. Salaries are reviewed every six months against market data, per their own JDs.

Locations / HQ: "Roboflow is distributed across the US and Europe." Hubs in New York City and San Francisco, with plans to open more. Postings list "NY, SF or Remote".

Remote policy: Remote-friendly with hubs, not strictly remote-first: "You can work from one of our Hubs (we offer a relocation bonus), work from home, work at co-working spaces." A $4,000/yr travel stipend and team on-sites support in-person time. On hours: "Roboflow primarily operates during the daytime hours in the US and there are some synchronous meetings you'll be expected to attend each week. Apart from that, we have a flexible schedule." Tier 1 pay rates apply to people in the SF/NY hubs 3+ days a week. One JD's 30-day plan includes "Onboard in person with your manager."

Remote-Canada eligibility: Unverified, and the wording leans against it. They state the company is "distributed across the US and Europe" — Canada is not named anywhere in either JD, and no Canada-specific hiring language exists. Mountain Time is workable against a US-daytime schedule. Do not treat this as Canada-eligible without asking; it may require a contractor arrangement or be a no.

Engineering & tech: Infrastructure team owns cloud architecture, databases, file storage, search clusters, microservices and ML pipelines. Stack signals: Kubernetes in production, Terraform, Helm, bash, Python, Node.js, AWS and/or GCP, GPUs, Docker, PyTorch/TensorFlow, GitHub Actions or Spacelift for CI/CD, SLO/SLA definition, incident response and an on-call rotation, plus SOC 2 / HIPAA / GDPR hardening. The ML Engineer role is explicitly an open-source maintainer post for `inference`: building "an agentic-driven contribution pipeline — automated and semi-automated review, triage, and CI/CD" to absorb high volumes of agent-authored PRs, growing a nightly end-to-end test suite across all targets, and moving from weekly toward daily releases; tech includes PyTorch, TensorFlow, ONNX, TensorRT, vLLM, OpenCV, DeepStream, PyAV and hardware-accelerated video decode.

Notable / other: Interview process is ~5 hours and unusually founder-led (hiring manager, CTO Brad Dwyer, a 90-minute hands-on technical infra interview, Head of Operations Kate Wagner for culture, CEO Joseph Nelson). They openly advise that the best way to stand out is to contribute to their open source or write about something built with Roboflow. Benefits: $4,000/yr travel stipend, $350/mo productivity stipend, $350/mo AI tools stipend, $150/mo team lunch stipend, up to 100% of health insurance for employee and family, unlimited PTO with a 2-week annual minimum, 12 weeks parental leave, equity. Note a typo in their own JD: the recruiting address is written "recruiting@robloflow.com".

Open relevant roles (sample): Infrastructure Engineer; Machine Learning Engineer - Inference Maintainer & Developer Experience; Data Infrastructure (no longer live).
