What they do: AVEVA is an industrial software vendor — engineering, design, simulation, operations and asset-performance software for energy, infrastructure, marine, and process manufacturing. Its own site says it works "to deliver solutions and expertise to optimize engineering, operations and performance." Customers are large industrial operators; products include the AVEVA Marine line referenced in one of the live postings, plus a growing cloud/data-platform portfolio (AVEVA Connect-style SaaS, Databricks-based data platforms).
How they describe themselves: The homepage leads on sustainability and responsible resource use — "helping you drive the responsible use of the world's resources" — and on its partner/developer ecosystem. It cites 5,500 partners and 5,700 certified developers globally alongside its own 6,500 employees. The tone is enterprise-industrial, not startup. A full values list was not on the homepage and was not fetched.
Size / stage / funding: 6,500 employees. Long-established enterprise vendor; the registry's own JD extractions classify company_stage as "public." Recalled but NOT verified this run: AVEVA was a UK-listed company that was taken private by Schneider Electric, which had been its majority shareholder — treat the ownership detail as unverified.
Locations / HQ: HQ Cambridge, UK (High Cross, Madingley Road, CB3 0HB). The live postings span Hyderabad and Bangalore (India), Manoteras/Madrid (Spain), Cambridge (UK), Shanghai (China), Lake Forest and San Leandro (California, US), and Houston (Texas).
Remote policy: Hybrid and office-anchored. The Senior Software Engineer - AI Platform & Cloud Services JD states "3 days in office" outright; the Cloud Engineer, Data Platforms JD is labelled "Full Time (Hybrid role)". Every posting is tied to a named city office.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO. Not a single one of the 14 postings in the registry is in Canada, and the two with explicit policies are office-hybrid in India and Spain. The Cloud Engineer, Data Platforms role is extracted as eu_only. There is no evidence AVEVA hires remote engineers in Canada at all, let alone in British Columbia.
Engineering & tech: Two visible clusters. Cloud/data platform: Azure and Azure Databricks, Terraform, Bicep/ARM, Azure DevOps, GitHub, CI/CD, Spark, Delta Lake, Python and SQL. Application/AI platform: C# and .NET, Azure, Docker, Kubernetes, REST APIs, plus a stated LLM/RAG/agent-orchestration line of work naming MCP and A2A standards, MLOps, prompt engineering and AI output evaluation. Engineering titles use an R&D "Member of Technical Staff" ladder (MTS II → Senior MTS → Principal), and there is a parallel Tech Support engineering ladder.
