# Curai Health — research
Provider/key: lever:curai | company_type: product
What they do: Curai Health is a virtual primary-care company that combines AI with licensed clinicians. In its own words: "we believe that access to high-quality healthcare is a fundamental human right, not a privilege. Our mission is to radically transform healthcare delivery by harnessing the power of artificial intelligence and clinical expertise to make care more affordable, accessible, and effective for everyone." Operationally: "Patients interact with advanced AI systems at every step of their care and follow-up. Licensed physicians review each case with the patient for the clinical decision in our integrated virtual clinic." The product is described as "an AI native care experience where patients get diagnosis, treatment, and prescriptions through conversation", covering labs, prescriptions and video visits. Scale as stated on their own JD: "hundreds of daily patients".
How they describe themselves: "We ground our approach in rigorous research, continuous learning, and a deep commitment to clinical integrity. We focus on making a real impact: improving health outcomes, expanding access to care, and setting new standards for what trustworthy, patient-centered healthcare is."
