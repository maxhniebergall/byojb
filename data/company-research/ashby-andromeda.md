# Andromeda (Andromeda Cluster) — research
Provider/key: ashby:andromeda | company_type: product

**Registry name correction:** the registry records this company as "Andromeda Surgical". That is wrong — the `ashby:andromeda` board belongs to **Andromeda Cluster** (andromeda.ai), an AI compute marketplace. There is a separate, unrelated medical-device company called Andromeda Surgical. All content below refers to the AI-compute Andromeda, confirmed from the company's own job descriptions and website.

What they do: Andromeda is a marketplace and orchestration layer for GPU compute. It sources, benchmarks and prices compute from 100+ providers in real time and routes customers' training and inference jobs across that global supply, handling deployment, contracts, SLAs, billing and support through a single platform — "one invoice. One support channel. Full observability." Customers are AI labs, AI startups, data centres and cloud providers. In its own framing, the long-term goal is "to build the liquidity layer for global AI compute — a marketplace that moves the infrastructure and workloads powering AGI, not dissimilar to the flows of capital in the world's financial markets."

How they describe themselves: The site tagline is **"GPU capacity is finite. Your access to it shouldn't be."** and "Access the world's compute." From their JD: "Andromeda Cluster was founded by Nat Friedman and Daniel Gross to give early-stage startups access to the kind of scaled AI infrastructure once reserved only for hyperscalers. We began with a single managed cluster — but it filled almost instantly. Since then, we've been quietly building the systems, network, and orchestration layer that makes the world's AI infrastructure more accessible." No formal published values list. The engineering tone is unusually specific and anti-generalist — the SRE JD opens "This is not a generalist SRE role" and repeatedly demands first-hand rather than theoretical knowledge ("you understand GPU memory hierarchies, ECC behavior, thermal throttling, and hardware failure modes from direct experience not documentation"). Role framing is high-autonomy: "a high-impact, senior builder's role. You'll have significant ownership and autonomy to shape how our systems run at a foundational level."

Size / stage / funding: Small and early. Launched in 2023 as a ~$100M cluster (2,512 H100 GPUs on InfiniBand), expanded past 4,000 GPUs including H100s, H200s and B200s by 2024. Spun out to stand alone with a **$60M raise from Paradigm at a ~$1.5B valuation**, followed by a **Series A of ~$150M in March 2026**. Public traction figures on the site: "billions gpu/hour liquidity" and "1000+ transactions completed." Headcount is not disclosed and is presumed small. **The founders have departed** — Nat Friedman and Daniel Gross joined Meta's AI leadership and no longer have an active relationship with the company, though the JD still leads with their names.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco. Roles are posted as "Global Remote / San Francisco, CA" or "North America Remote / San Francisco, CA".

Remote policy: No formal policy published, but remote is clearly supported and geographically broad — reqs are tagged "Global Remote" and "North America Remote" alongside the SF office.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Very likely yes, but not explicitly stated.** The Senior Site Reliability Engineer req is posted "Global Remote / San Francisco, CA" and the Software Engineer - AI Infrastructure req is "North America Remote / San Francisco, CA" — Canada falls inside both. However, Andromeda nowhere names Canada, names a Canadian entity, or states how it employs non-US staff (direct employment vs. contractor vs. EOR). Treat as unconfirmed pending a direct question; the location tags are permissive but not evidence of Canadian payroll.

Engineering & tech: Deep, low-level GPU-infrastructure systems work. From the SRE JD, the surface is: multi-provider, multi-region GPU cluster architecture with topology-aware scheduling; high-speed interconnect health (InfiniBand, RoCE, NVLink) and diagnosing degraded collectives/all-reduce performance; distributed-training internals (NCCL, CUDA, PyTorch distributed, DeepSpeed, Megatron, FSDP); expert Linux and kernel/driver work (NVIDIA drivers, CUDA toolkit, cgroups/namespaces, syscall- and hardware-level profiling); Kubernetes with GPU device plugins, topology-aware scheduling, multi-cluster federation and custom operators, plus Slurm and HPC schedulers; Terraform/Helm/Ansible; Python, Go, Bash; GPU-specific observability (DCGM, nvidia-smi, fabric-manager metrics) beyond standard Prometheus/Grafana. SLOs and error budgets are defined around GPU-specific failure modes (ECC errors, NVLink degradation, NCCL timeouts). Nice-to-haves include parallel filesystems (VAST, Weka, Lustre, GPFS), checkpoint I/O bottlenecks, MFU/straggler optimization, and physical cluster buildout (rack layout, power/cooling, network topology, hardware burn-in). The platform also runs an automated "certification engine" that validates GPU performance against enterprise benchmarks.

Notable / other: The SRE role is explicitly **customer-embedded**: "Serve as the primary technical point of contact for customers running large-scale training workloads. Onboard, troubleshoot, and optimize, often in real time." It also carries "Incident Leadership" for multi-layer hardware/network/orchestration/framework failures. Team-leadership experience is called out as a plus ("We're growing and need people who raise the bar"). Standard EEO statement present. No compensation is published on any live req.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Site Reliability Engineer (Global Remote / SF); Software Engineer - AI Infrastructure (North America Remote / SF); General Interest — Experience w/ AI Infrastructure (Global Remote / SF).
