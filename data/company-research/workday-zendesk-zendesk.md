# Zendesk — research
Provider/key: workday:zendesk/zendesk | company_type: product

What they do: Zendesk is a customer-service and support software company — ticketing, help centre and, increasingly, AI agents that resolve support tickets autonomously. Their own JD states the scale plainly: "Zendesk has 100,000+ customers, billions of support interactions, and a live product surface where agents are already resolving tickets." Customers are businesses of all sizes; the product is B2B SaaS.

How they describe themselves: Their careers site lists four core principles verbatim: **"Lead with exceptional service"**, **"Deliver the simplest solution"**, **"Take pride in our work"**, and **"Care for each other."** The JDs themselves are unusually concrete and engineering-literate — the Staff ML Engineer posting describes the actual production system, its architecture and its failure modes rather than selling a mission. Tone is substantive, low on hype. One recurring cultural theme across JDs is AI-augmented engineering: "We expect AI to be a core part of how we build and operate data systems" — using AI tools for SQL generation, pipeline development, debugging and documentation, then applying engineering judgment to validate and productionise the output.

Size / stage / funding: Large, mature, global. Taken private in 2022 by an investor group (Hellman & Friedman and Permira) after a long run as a public company — so private-equity-owned rather than venture-stage. More than 20 offices worldwide; certified as a Great Place To Work in 18 countries per their careers site.

Locations / HQ: Global HQ San Francisco, California. Named hub offices on the careers site: San Francisco, Austin (Texas), Pune (India), Kraków (Poland), Lisbon (Portugal), Singapore. "More than 20 Zendesk offices worldwide."

Remote policy: **Hybrid by design, office-anchored.** The careers site states: "In person where it helps, flexible where it doesn't, shaped by what your role and what the team actually needs." The JDs are more explicit and less flexible — the standard "Where we work" block reads: "Hybrid: In this role, our hybrid experience is designed at the team level… while also giving you flexibility to work remotely for part of the week. **This role must attend our local office for part of the week.**" So each requisition is tied to a specific local office.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — verified negative on the current openings, and no Canadian office is listed.** All eight live relevant requisitions are anchored to non-Canadian offices: Melbourne (Australia) ×4, Kraków (Poland) ×3, Berlin (Germany), Dublin (Ireland), and one "Remote, Portugal". Every one carries the mandatory local-office clause except the Portugal role, which is remote *within Portugal*. Zendesk's careers site names no Canadian office among its hubs, and no Canada-remote hiring is advertised. There is no reachable path here for a British Columbia resident. The registry's earlier note ("Strong ML/agent infra but no Canada posting") is confirmed.

Engineering & tech: Genuinely strong, and this is the frustrating part of this record. The **Custom Agent Service** described in the Staff Machine Learning Engineer JD is exactly the agent-platform work the profile targets:
- *Agent execution core* — the planning loop, tool dispatch, memory integration and error recovery on the main execution path; integrating new agent architectures into production and hardening them for real traffic; architectures range "from single-pass ReAct loops to our iterative multi-plan executor".
- *Knowledge retrieval* — embedding, reranking and context assembly balancing answer quality against latency and token cost "across thousands of heterogeneous knowledge bases per deployment".
- *Actions and connectors* — calling Zendesk APIs, third-party connectors (Shopify, Salesforce), admin-configured custom actions and increasingly other agents via **A2A**; reliable retries, timeouts, schema validation and graceful degradation on mid-execution connector failure; a Connector SDK.
- *Production instrumentation for model training* — capturing execution trajectories (reasoning steps, tool calls, outcomes, user feedback) and implicit reward signals (resolution success, escalation patterns, satisfaction) to feed a training pipeline for domain-specialised models.
- *Security and compliance* — PII filtering, audit logging, action versioning, governance to keep agents within admin-configured bounds, working directly with Product Security.
Stack (stated): Python primary with some Go for platform services; custom iterative agent architectures and ReAct; Kubernetes, Spinnaker, AWS (ECS, S3, ElastiCache); Postgres, ElastiCache/Redis for vector + KV, Kafka; **Braintrust** for evaluation (experiment tracking, scoring, CI/CD integration); MCP, REST and gRPC protocols. Requirement: 5+ years backend engineering with strong Python — "You have shipped production systems, not just models" — comfort across LLM APIs, prompt engineering, tool calling, memory management and evaluation, and building for the failure case.
Separately, the **Staff Data Engineer, Product Analytics** role (Kraków) owns product-analytics data architecture, semantic layers and core datasets, sets org-wide standards for data modelling, metric definitions, data quality and pipeline design, and drives cost/performance efficiency; stack is the modern data stack — dbt, Airflow, Snowflake or equivalent — with 8+ years required.

Compensation: Zendesk posts statutory ranges on its Polish requisitions. Staff Data Engineer (Kraków): **PLN 309,000–463,000** annualised base. Senior Machine Learning Engineer (Kraków): **PLN 302,000–454,000** annualised base. Both are company-stated on their own live JDs and are recorded as `direct`/`jd_posted` bands. No range is posted on the Melbourne, Berlin, Dublin or Portugal requisitions.

Notable / other: Eight live relevant requisitions is the largest open surface of any company in this batch, and the ML/agent-platform content is the closest match to the profile's north star of anything researched here — it is purely geography that rules it out. Entity confirmed from JD text (100,000+ customers, support ticket resolution) against the registry record.
