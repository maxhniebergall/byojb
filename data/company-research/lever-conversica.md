What they do: Conversica sells AI agents that hold automated two-way conversations with a company's customers and prospects across email, SMS, chat and messaging apps — lead qualification and follow-up, customer service, retention and revenue expansion. The agents are "trained on your policies, tone, and workflows" and integrate with existing business systems to take actions such as scheduling meetings and triggering workflows. This is a sales-and-marketing automation product with an AI-agent delivery mechanism.
