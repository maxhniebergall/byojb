# Quadrature Capital — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:quadraturecapital | company_type: product

What they do: Quadrature Capital is a quantitative/systematic investment firm (hedge fund) that runs an automated trading business end-to-end. Their own words: "we're building the ultimate automated trading system: one that ingests data, forms views, executes in the market, and learns from the result to get better and faster." The engineering surface spans research infrastructure, low-latency stream processing, distributed data systems, trade execution, and observability. Customers are internal — the trading and research desks — not external clients.

How they describe themselves: The stated goal of their platform work is that "human effort is meant to compound, rather than be spent solving the same problems again and again" — an explicitly leverage/utility framing rather than a product-growth one. Their "Working here" material leans on community and wellbeing: "We bring people together often for social events, or just to hang out. We enjoy spending time together and have a lot of fun." Notably absent from the JD is hustle/hype language — no "fast-paced", "rocketship" or "wear many hats". Platform Engineers are described as running "the internal developer platform that powers Quadrature's business-critical applications, giving engineering teams self-service tools to ship and operate their systems reliably" — i.e. an internal-customer, well-scoped platform mandate.

Size / stage / funding: Private, self-funded hedge fund; describes itself as a "growing, global business". Headcount not stated on the pages read (unverified). Not venture-runway dependent — revenue comes from trading, which is a stability positive.

Locations / HQ: HQ is London, UK — The Leadenhall Building, 122 Leadenhall Street, EC3V 4AB. Their benefits page says benefits "vary slightly by office", implying more than one office, but the additional offices are not enumerated on that page (unverified). The one live posting in the registry is located "London, England, United Kingdom".

Remote policy: In-office by default. The only stated flexibility is "flexibility to work from home occasionally" — an occasional-WFH policy attached to an office, not remote-first. No async or handbook culture signals found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — verified negative on the available evidence.** The only live relevant posting is London, England, and the company's own benefits page describes office-based employment with occasional work-from-home. Nothing on any page read indicates hiring outside the UK, and there is no Canadian entity referenced. A Kimberley, BC candidate who cannot relocate is not employable here. (I did not find an explicit "we do not hire remotely internationally" statement — the conclusion is inferred from the London-only posting plus the office-centric benefits page.)

Engineering & tech: Kubernetes in production (administration, operations, application deployment) is the headline requirement; Linux and networking fundamentals; Go or Python; GitOps, Infrastructure-as-Code, configuration management, and modern observability stacks. The role expects "a track record of leading complex technical projects end-to-end: from requirements and architecture through to implementation and delivery", and a "pragmatic, adaptable mindset — equally comfortable building global infrastructure, advising other teams, and bringing in new tools and ideas". Low-latency stream processing and distributed data systems are named as first-class parts of the platform.

Notable / other: Benefits (from their own benefits page) are unusually rich even by finance standards: 30 days annual holiday, 12 months paid parental leave, private medical/dental/GP cover, onsite gym, mental health support, annual winter and summer company trips, a retirement contribution of 10% of base salary, funded training, travel insurance, and a personal development allowance. No compensation figures are published on that page. The firm is publicly associated with large-scale charitable giving via a related foundation — not verified from a fetched page in this session, so treat as background only.
