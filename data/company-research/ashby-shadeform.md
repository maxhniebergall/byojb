# Shadeform — research
Provider/key: ashby:shadeform | company_type: product

What they do: GPU cloud aggregation and orchestration. In their words: "Shadeform provides a unified platform for deploying and managing GPU infrastructure across cloud providers, neoclouds, and data centers. Our platform gives startups, inference providers, and enterprises a consistent way to access and operate GPU capacity through a single API and service layer." The core problem is fragmented GPU supply: "You will own the systems that turn fragmented GPU capacity into a reliable, production-ready platform."

How they describe themselves: The posting is terse and engineering-first — no mission/values section, no culture bullets, no hype adjectives. What it does emphasize is technical novelty and market position: "Because of Shadeform's unique position in the market, you will work on novel solutions to some of the biggest challenges in the GPU market," and "You will have opportunities to test out bleeding edge GPU infrastructure and utilize the latest AI dev tools." Notably absent: any "fast-paced", "wear many hats" or "rocketship" language.

Size / stage / funding: NOT stated first-party and not verified this session — unverified. Locations / HQ: Not stated on the posting. The only geographic statement is the employment constraint below. No HQ city is asserted.

Remote policy: Remote, but timezone- and country-constrained. Verbatim: "Remote in a US timezone; US preferred; cannot sponsor visas at this time."

Remote-Canada eligibility: UNCLEAR, leaning no. Mountain Time is nominally "a US timezone" (US Mountain Time is the same clock as Kimberley BC), so the timezone requirement itself is satisfiable from BC. But "US preferred" plus "cannot sponsor visas at this time" indicates they are set up to employ in the US and are not looking to solve cross-border employment. No Canadian entity, no mention of contractor arrangements, no non-US hiring statement. Would need explicit confirmation that they can pay a Canada-resident employee or contractor.

Engineering & tech: Golang and Next.js, "we expect everyone to be language agnostic." AWS and GCP. Kubernetes in production (operating and debugging it is the first preferred-experience bullet). Virtualization: KVM and Kubevirt. Domain work: GPU provisioning, orchestration and virtualization; instance lifecycle management; multi-cloud environments; managed services and public APIs consumed by technical customers; "automated AI infrastructure services." This is a genuine systems/infrastructure role rather than an application role.

Notable / other: Customers are named as categories rather than logos — startups, inference providers, and enterprises. The company sits in the neocloud/GPU-broker layer of the AI infrastructure stack, which is currently capital-intensive and competitive; no profitability or funding signal was available.
