# Syndio — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:syndio | company_type: product
What they do: Syndio is a workplace-equity software company. Its platform analyses employer compensation data to find and fix pay-equity gaps, keep customers compliant with pay-transparency and reporting law across dozens of jurisdictions, and increasingly to drive "AI for Pay Decisions". It analyses pay for over 10 million employees across dozens of countries and sells to large, recognisable enterprises.
How they describe themselves: "Do you want to empower organizations to build smarter compensation strategies while ensuring fair pay for all employees?" They call themselves "a team of curious builders and bold problem-solvers who care deeply about the work and the impact it has… connected through shared values, candid conversations, and the belief that how we work together matters as much as what we build," and, on the careers page, "AI builders who move fast". Seven stated values, verbatim: "Stay Curious", "Do What Delivers", "Own the Outcome", "High Trust, Low Ego", "Name It to Tame It", "Dare to Build", "Start Simple Soon".
Size / stage / funding: Founded 2016, Seattle. Series C, backed by $83M from Bessemer Venture Partners, Voyager Capital and Emerson Collective. Private, growth-stage, enterprise-sales-led.
Locations / HQ: Seattle, Washington; distributed team. The one live engineering posting is located in Calgary, Alberta, Canada.
Remote policy: Remote-first with periodic in-person meetups. Benefits listed include flexible vacation (noted as U.S.), 90% employee / 50% dependent healthcare, stock options + 401(k), home-office and electronics stipends and paid parental leave — the benefit framing is US-centric.
Remote-Canada eligibility: Partial / unclear. The company is remote-first and the live Staff Data Engineer req is posted in Calgary, Alberta, Canada, which proves they can employ in Canada. What is NOT established is whether that req is remote-anywhere-in-Canada or anchored to Calgary, and their careers page does not publish a country-eligibility list. Alberta is Mountain Time, so timezone-wise this is the single best-aligned company in the batch; geography would need one direct question to confirm.
Engineering & tech: Data platform at the centre. The Staff Data Engineer "owns the technical direction for how data is structured, standardized, modeled, and activated across the company", building "data pipelines that produce the data products powering analytics, product experiences, and Syndio's LLM-powered systems", including "the pipelines that feed retrieval, context generation, and evaluation workflows", "multi-tenant schema standardization, automated field mapping, retrieval-ready data modeling, and data pipelines for AI evaluation and feedback loops", plus "data contracts", governance, "policy tagging, PII handling, and lineage tracking", and both batch analytics and event-driven flows.
Notable / other: Practises what it sells — "we publish our own salary ranges, CEO pay ratio, and median pay gap" internally. No public pay range appears on the Calgary JD, and no first-party number was found, so no band row was written.
Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Data Engineer (Calgary, Alberta, Canada).
