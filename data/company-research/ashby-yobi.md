# Yobi — research
Provider/key: ashby:yobi | company_type: product

What they do: Yobi (Yobi AI, yobi.ai) is a "Behavioral AI" company founded in 2019. It has
assembled what it calls one of the largest *consented* behavioral datasets in the United States
and trains foundation models of human behavior grounded in real-world actions — purchases, store
visits — rather than text. It sells three product lines: ML optimization (pre-engineered consumer
features other teams build models on), media performance (AI-driven advertising targeting), and
personalization/recommendations. Data comes from partnerships with retailers, telcos, shipping
companies and media firms; petabyte-scale. Customers are enterprise brands and agencies across
QSR, CPG, travel, entertainment, B2B and tourism. Current strategic focus is "bringing the
performance of closed-web user acquisition to the open web and connected TV." In short: an
adtech/martech data-and-modelling company, positioned as the "behavioral intelligence layer for
any system that makes a personalization decision."

How they describe themselves: Tagline "Democratize the AI Revolution"; mission "to ethically
democratize the benefits of data and AI." Recurring themes are privacy-by-design ("Our
private-by-design modeling enables state-of-the-art personalization and decisioning for leading
brands and agencies while protecting privacy, safety, and ethics"), consented data, and data as
"the original scaling law". Explicitly contrasts itself with LLM companies and with "the walled
gardens of Big Tech" — "walled-garden results without the walls". On culture, from their JDs:
"We're at an inflection point—customer adoption is accelerating, but there's still room to shape
the architecture and culture from the ground up. Engineers here own major surface areas, build
0→1 systems in large-scale data and model infrastructure, and help define how Behavioral AI scales
ethically and effectively." Hiring philosophy: "We prioritize attitude, culture, and general
(technical) fit over matching perfectly into one of our job descriptions… we encourage you to
apply" even if not a perfect fit.

Size / stage / funding: Private startup, founded 2019. Self-described "rapidly growing" and
"quickly growing startup"; funding stage, investors and headcount are not disclosed on their site
or in their JDs. Team is described as ML experts previously at Amazon, Uber, Twitter and Meta, with
a GTM team that has "taken ideas from concept to 9 figure revenue streams".

Locations / HQ: Hubs in the SF Bay Area, Seattle and NYC. No single HQ is stated publicly; a NYC
presence is listed on third-party job boards.

Remote policy: "Fully remote or hybrid from several hubs (SF Bay Area, Seattle, NYC)" — stated
verbatim in their JDs. Every relevant open posting in the registry is listed as
"Remote (US + Canada Only)".

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** All five relevant engineering postings carry the
location string "Remote (US + Canada Only)" on their own Ashby board, and the JDs state "fully
remote". Canada is explicitly named as an eligible hiring geography. Note the flip side: the
restriction is US+Canada only, so the pool is North America.

Engineering & tech: Data/ML platform stack — Airflow for orchestration, Spark for large-scale
processing, Bazel for builds, GitHub CI/CD, plus experiment tracking. JDs stress being able to
contribute across the "full stack" of the data pipeline: data orchestration, build systems,
experiment tracking. Roles are split into Core Signals (the foundation-model/embedding side),
Applied/Product (productizing the embeddings), Inference/Serving, Data Engineering, and the Yobi
Ad Platform. The stated engineering shape is a large degree of 0-to-1 development, engineers owning
"major surface areas", and applied MLEs "wearing your Product hat" and collaborating with Product
and core-signals MLEs. ML domain is recommender systems and personalization, not NLP/LLM research —
JDs say candidates need to understand "enough about machine learning to be dangerous but not
necessarily published in the field."

Notable / other: Partnerships with Microsoft and Databricks are called out as company highlights;
Microsoft specifically partnered on assembling the consented behavioral dataset. Product
integrations with The Trade Desk, Viant, Nexxen, Microsoft Advertising, Adobe and Optimizely.
Benefits per JD: competitive base, "meaningful equity & financial upside — a real % of the
company", annual bonus target on personal and company performance, health/dental/vision, unlimited
PTO ("we care about impact, not tracking days you're out"), 401k with company match, generous
parental leave. Pay transparency: base ranges are stated directly on the JDs.

Pay (from their own postings): $180,000–$275,000 base for the three MLE roles and the Ad Platform
Software Engineer; $225,000–$300,000 base for Tech Lead / Software Engineer, Ad Platform. All
plus equity. Currency USD.

Open relevant roles (sample): Machine Learning Engineer – Applied / Product; Machine Learning
Engineer – Core Signals; Machine Learning Engineer – Inference / Serving; Software Engineer –
Yobi Ad Platform; Tech Lead / Software Engineer – Yobi Ad Platform; Lead Data Engineer.
