# CloudZero — research
Provider/key: ashby:cloudzero | company_type: product

What they do: SaaS platform at the intersection of cloud cost management and FinOps. CloudZero ingests billing and usage data from all cloud, SaaS and PaaS providers, organizes it in real time according to the customer's business structure, and lets organizations tie engineering decisions to unit cost/ROI. Positions itself as "the only platform that quantifies ROI for every cloud and AI dollar spent." Founded 2019 (mission dated to 2016 in its own JD copy); reports 14B+ in total cloud spend under management.

How they describe themselves: Tagline "Connect Technical Decisions To Business Results" and "order within the chaos." Five stated values, verbatim: **Delight The Customer**; **Commit, Then Iterate** ("perfection is a process, not an instant result"); **Less Is More**; **Nothing Is Sacred** (embrace change when a better solution exists); **Wait For No One** (pursue the ideal path without seeking approval). JD copy: "CloudZero is growing fast… This is actual platform engineering work at actual scale, not a maintenance role or a backlog triage job." Also: "We believe every engineering decision is a buying decision, and we're applying proven reliability engineering principles to financial efficiency."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed. "$56 million+ raised" per its own JD; $32M Series B in June 2023. Headcount not disclosed on the pages fetched — unverified. CEO Phil Pergola (ex-CloudHealth) since Nov 2021; founder Erik Peterson is an AWS Ambassador Partner. Analyst recognition: Forrester Wave "Strong Performer" in Cloud Cost Management; Gartner MQ "Visionary" for CFM Tools (Oct 2024).

Locations / HQ: Boston, MA (named a Boston Business Journal Best Place to Work, June 2024). Second geo in postings: San Francisco.

Remote policy: The about page says "remote-first environment." However, every one of its three relevant open engineering postings is location-tagged **"Boston, San Francisco"** rather than "Remote", so remote-first in practice appears to mean US hub-anchored.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, leaning no.** No page fetched states hiring countries, and no posting is tagged remote or Canada. On the evidence available, treat as US-only unless the company says otherwise. Not verified either way.

Engineering & tech: Core platform team scaling; the "Droptable" team owns billing ingest — backend data processing, storage and query infrastructure, including a **custom-built query engine**. Stack signals from JDs: SQL, Python, cloud data warehouses (Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery), ETL pipeline frameworks, data modelling / warehouse design for analytics, AWS Step Functions and Lambda; bonus for ML pipelines and model deployment. Roles are described as owning architecture end-to-end and working directly with business stakeholders.

Notable / other: Domain (cloud cost/FinOps, unit economics, query engines over billing data) is genuine infrastructure/data work rather than product surface. Offers are contingent on background and reference checks.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Billing Ingest (Boston, San Francisco); Staff / Principal Software Engineer (San Francisco, Boston); Distinguished Engineer, Data Platform (San Francisco, Boston).

Pay on record (all direct, posted on its own JDs): software_general/senior USD 145–200k base; software_general/principal USD 220–290k base; data_eng/principal USD 275–330k base. See data/company-comp.jsonl.
