What they do: Per their own posting, "Pendulum offers unmatched social data coverage, ensuring companies never miss conversations they care about across video, audio, image, and text content – including YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat, podcasts, Reddit, and more. Based on proprietary machine learning (ML) technology, the Pendulum Intelligence platform has the most comprehensive social media coverage to help enterprise brands proactively identify opportunities and mitigate risks, enabling better decision-making across the organization." In short: enterprise social-media monitoring, brand risk and intelligence, sold to risk/intel, communications and marketing teams at large companies and organizations.
