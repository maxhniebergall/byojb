# FacilityOS — research
Provider/key: ashby:facilityos | company_type: product
What they do: FacilityOS is a B2B SaaS company "redefining how facilities operate—bringing safety, security, and daily operations into one unified platform used by organizations around the world." It describes itself as "headquartered in the heart of Toronto's vibrant tech ecosystem" and its platform as "market-leading", helping businesses "improve their safety, security, compliance, and efficiency across many critical facility touchpoints" (visitor management, contractor/safety compliance and related facility workflows).
How they describe themselves: The self-description is consistently growth-and-energy flavoured. "FacilityOS is a fast-growing company"; "As we continue to scale globally, we're building a team of driven, curious people who want to make an impact"; "You'll be part of a dynamic, collaborative culture where individuals are trusted to take ownership, solve meaningful problems, and grow in their careers"; "If you're looking to do your best work alongside a great team in a high-growth environment, FacilityOS is the place to build your career." On culture specifically: "We are a team of passionate, driven individuals committed to excellence, innovation, and redefining our industry", "we are focused on creating an innovative and collaborative working culture where we value the contribution of each individual", "Employee engagement is a key focus area for us and we encourage participation and the sharing of information and ideas", and — verbatim from the Software Developer posting — "We work hard and play hard and we do both with passion and respect for one another. Our company promotes a fast-paced, fun, friendly [environment]".
Size / stage / funding: Not stated on the material available; presented as a private, fast-growing / high-growth B2B SaaS company. Headcount, funding stage and profitability are UNVERIFIED — the ATS board is client-rendered and cannot be fetched, and no first-party corporate page was fetched this session (search budget exhausted).
Locations / HQ: Toronto, Ontario, Canada. All three live postings are Toronto-located.
Remote policy: Hybrid, stated explicitly and twice. The company-wide boilerplate says "Our team comes together in-office twice a week to connect, collaborate, and build momentum", and the Software Developer posting spells it out: "This is a hybrid position where you'll be required to be in office at least two days a week with the flexibility to work from home the remaining days if desired."
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO. It is a Canadian employer, but every role requires being in the Toronto office at least two days a week, which is not workable from British Columbia without relocating.
Engineering & tech: Two distinct tracks. (1) The legacy platform is **full-stack .NET** — "full-stack .NET/.NET Core Web Application Development (C#)", MS SQL Server (T-SQL), design patterns and OOP, cloud-native PaaS on Azure (preferred) or AWS, web services, code review and static analysis. (2) A newly-formed AI/data function reporting to the VP of Engineering: an **AI Engineer** who is "FacilityOS's hands-on technical anchor for AI agent development", owning RAG pipelines, agent frameworks and orchestration layers, agent-orchestration runtimes coordinating "multi-step agent workflows, tool calls, and task hand-offs", business-system integration into Microsoft 365 / Outlook / Teams via Microsoft Graph API and OAuth, and evaluation infrastructure — "eval harnesses, regression testing for prompts and workflows, and quality/accuracy metrics" plus "guardrails and mitigations for hallucination, unsafe outputs, and failure modes". And a **Senior Data Engineer** owning the data warehouse and data lake, schema design, pipeline quality, lineage tracking, access controls, and platform cost/health, partnered with the AI Engineer. Compliance targets are ISO27001 and SOC 2 Type 2, with a VP Infosec in the org.
Notable / other: The AI operating model is unusual and explicitly documented: "Department leaders own the discovery, tooling choices, behaviour, and outcomes of their own agents", while the central AI Engineer builds "the underlying technical architecture … that supports that department-level ownership, not to run agents on departments' behalf." There is an "AI Tech/Integration SWAT" team, an "AI Program Lead" with a portfolio, and an explicit "AI before headcount" evaluation policy — i.e. proposed roles are tested against whether automation could do the work first. The AI Engineer JD names "OpenClaw or comparable agent orchestration/runtime frameworks".
Open relevant roles (sample): AI Engineer (Toronto); Senior Data Engineer (Toronto); Software Developer — full-stack .NET (Toronto), posted at CA$85K – CA$135K.
