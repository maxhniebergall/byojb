# AppDirect — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:appdirect | company_type: product

What they do: AppDirect operates a B2B subscription-commerce and marketplace platform — software that lets telcos, resellers and other channel partners sell, provision, bill and manage cloud software subscriptions to their own business customers. It is a platform/marketplace business serving the indirect channel, with billing, provisioning and catalogue management as the core surface.

How they describe themselves: The engineering JDs emphasise operating "in a hybrid organization where flexible schedules require asynchronous communication," and look for candidates who are "self-motivated with strong organizational skills, a positive and solution-oriented mindset." Careers material centres on a hybrid model framed around employee choice.

Size / stage / funding: ~1,870 employees as of December 2025, with roughly 2.0% year-over-year growth — a large, mature private company that has stopped growing quickly. Doubled headcount during the COVID period; growth has since flattened.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco. Substantial Canadian footprint with offices in **Montreal, Calgary, Trois-Rivières and Kirkland (QC)**, plus a significant employee population in the Toronto area. Essentially all of the relevant engineering postings are Montreal-based, with one in Calgary.

Remote policy: **Hybrid.** Company careers material states a hybrid model "that puts employee choice at the center" and says it is not mandating when people are in the office, and that over 50% of employees work remotely. However, the engineering JDs themselves are tagged `#hybrid` / `#LI-Hybrid` and are posted to specific Canadian cities (Montreal, Calgary) rather than as remote roles. The hybrid framing is office-anchored — flexibility about *which days*, not about *which city*.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — effectively disqualifying for a candidate outside a hub city.** Every relevant live posting is tied to Montreal (or Calgary) and carries a hybrid tag. AppDirect can obviously employ Canadians, but these roles expect the employee to live within commuting distance of a Quebec or Alberta office. There is no evidence of a Canada-wide remote posting in this set. Kimberley, BC is not workable for these listings.

Engineering & tech: Subscription commerce and billing platform work — data platform and data engineering roles, .NET and PHP services alongside general software development, indicating a heterogeneous and partly legacy stack accumulated over a long company history.

Compensation (first-party): The Senior Data Engineer JD posts a **Canada Compensation Band of $100,000–$135,000 USD**, described as "the expected annual base salary or OTE (on-target earnings) for this role at AppDirect," with performance bonuses and benefits separate. The OTE ambiguity means the figure may blend base and variable pay for some roles.

Notable / other: Glassdoor reviews raise a recurring stability concern — "Annual layoffs in the 3rd or 4th quarter make it hard to trust in leadership" — and a separate review references a layoff of roughly 50 employees concentrated in the San Francisco office. Treat as employee-reported, not company-confirmed.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Data Engineer (Montreal); Data Platform Engineer (Montreal); Senior Software Developer (Montreal); Staff Software Developer (Montreal); Software Developer (Calgary); Senior Software Developer – PHP (Montreal).
