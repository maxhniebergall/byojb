# Restaurant Brands International (RBI) — research
Provider/key: workday:rbi/rbi_external_career_site | company_type: product

What they do: Restaurant Brands International Inc. is one of the world's largest quick-service restaurant companies, with (per its own JD boilerplate) "nearly $45 billion in annual system-wide sales and over 32,000 restaurants in more than 120 countries and territories." It owns four brands — Tim Hortons, Burger King, Popeyes and Firehouse Subs — operated as independent brands under shared corporate ownership. The engineering roles in the registry are in-house corporate digital/data teams supporting guest-facing ordering, loyalty and promotions, plus franchisee-facing operational tooling.

How they describe themselves: "Ready to make your next big professional move? Join us on our journey to achieve our big dream of building the most loved restaurant brands in the world." They frame their social program as the "Restaurant Brands for Good framework, improving sustainable outcomes related to its food, the planet, and people and communities." Engineering framing on the Firehouse Subs role: "This is not a traditional people-management-only role… contributes production code daily (50–70% hands-on), drives architectural direction, elevates engineering standards." The Tim Hortons data JD asks for people who can "manage in an environment of quick change" and "work in a fast-paced Agile environment."

Size / stage / funding: Public (NYSE/TSX: QSR). Large, established, profitable multi-brand operator; ~$45B system-wide sales per its own boilerplate. Headcount not stated in the sources read.

Locations / HQ: Toronto (Corp — Toronto King St corporate office) and Jacksonville, FL (Firehouse Subs). Global brand offices in 120+ countries; the registry's live engineering roles are Toronto and Jacksonville.

Remote policy: **Explicitly in-office five days per week.** Stated verbatim on multiple JDs: "RBI follows a 5 day, in-office work schedule to support collaboration. Candidates should be comfortable working onsite 5 days per week out of our office in Toronto, ON" (Data Engineer III) and the identical sentence naming Jacksonville, FL on the Firehouse Subs lead role.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No.** Canadian roles exist (Toronto), but they are 5-day onsite in Toronto, stated on the JD itself. There is no remote option and no BC/Mountain-Time-workable arrangement in any live posting.

Engineering & tech: Two distinct stacks visible. Guest-facing digital (Firehouse Subs): JavaScript/TypeScript, React, Node.js, GraphQL with Apollo Federation, AWS (Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB), Terraform, CircleCI/Jenkins/GitHub Actions, Datadog observability, ADR-driven architecture, TDD/BDD, active legacy-to-cloud-native modernization. Data (Tim Hortons): Python, SQL, Scala, Apache Spark/Hadoop/Hive, AWS (S3, EC2, DynamoDB, Glue, Lambda, IAM), Snowflake warehouse, Terraform + Git CI/CD, Databricks as an asset, loyalty-program data.

Compensation transparency: RBI posts CAD ranges on its Canadian JDs — Data Engineer III, Tim Hortons: "Base salary range of $110,000 – $130,000 CAD," plus an annual discretionary bonus and possible equity under the long-term incentive plan. The Jacksonville lead role posts no range.

Notable / other: Benefits described as "focused on physical, mental and financial wellness… a comprehensive global paid parental leave program… free telemedicine and mental wellness support." The Tim Hortons Data Engineer III posting notes it is "for an existing vacancy" (i.e. backfill, not new headcount).

Open relevant roles (sample): Sr. Software Engineer Lead, Firehouse Subs (Jacksonville); Data Engineer III, Tim Hortons, Canada (Toronto); Data Engineer II, Tim Hortons, Canada (Toronto); Machine Learning Engineer III, Tim Hortons; Principal Software Engineer, Popeyes.
