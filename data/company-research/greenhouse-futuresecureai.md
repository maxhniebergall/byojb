# Future Secure AI — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:futuresecureai | company_type: product (with a strong professional-services / customer-engagement component — see note)

What they do: Future Secure AI builds an enterprise AI platform — the marketing term used throughout their JDs is "**AI Co-Workers**" — that lets large enterprises "deploy a diverse and dynamic range of AI agents from multiple third-party platforms (including models, LLMs, micro-services and even orchestration layers) through their own secure and scalable platform." In practice the offering is a secure, compliance-oriented (SOC2 is named) layer for running third-party AI agents inside enterprise environments, delivered to "reputable global clients" across multiple industries.

Classification note: this sits on the line between product and services. The AI Engineer (Software) role states plainly: "you'll **lead customer-facing engagements, collaborating closely with customer and partner technical teams**, alongside our internal squad to enhance tech solutions", and asks the engineer to "navigate between detailed coding and broader business and product strategies." That is delivery-engineering against named clients, not pure product work. They do describe "the product and platform" as a thing they are building, and the SRE role is a genuine internal platform role, so **`company_type` is left as product** rather than consulting — but the engineering culture should be read as engagement-driven.

How they describe themselves: "At Future Secure AI, we're building something genuinely new — and we're looking for people **bold enough to build it with us**. We work at the frontier of AI, tackling big, real-world problems for global enterprises across multiple industries, armed with state-of-the-art technology and a culture that prizes **courage, rigor, and relentless curiosity**. Our **BRAVER** values aren't just words on a wall — they describe the kind of people we are and the standard we hold ourselves to every day. Our leadership team is entrepreneurial, experienced, and accessible, with an **open-door policy** that means you'll never be just a number here. We invest seriously in your growth because we know our success depends on yours. If you're ready to work alongside some of the brightest minds in the industry, **push into uncharted territory**, and do work that genuinely matters, Future Secure AI is the place for you." The BRAVER acronym is referenced but never expanded in any JD, and no company site was reachable to expand it.
