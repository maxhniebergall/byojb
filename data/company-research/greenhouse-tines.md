# Tines — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:tines | company_type: product
What they do: Tines sells an "intelligent workflow platform" that "applies AI, automation, and integration with human ingenuity to drive real business results" — a vendor-agnostic integrator across the whole tech stack, integrating with any API-enabled service. It grew out of security operations and is still popular with security, IT, engineering and finance teams; it is "secure and private by design". Customers named in their own JD: Canva, Databricks, Elastic, Kayak, Intercom, McKesson.
How they describe themselves: Mission — "To power the world's most important workflows." Three stated values: **Simplicity** (removing unnecessary barriers), **Speed** (executing with purpose and iterating), **Soundness** (integrity and transparency in how goals are achieved). JDs add: "committed to delivering exceptional customer experiences while fostering a company culture that nurtures individual curiosity, growth, and integrity", "a hugely supportive and collaborative engineering team that takes great pride in the work we do".
Size / stage / funding: Founded 2018 by Eoin Hinchy (CEO) and Thomas Kinsella (CCO), both former senior security operators. VC-backed by Accel, Index Ventures, Felicis, Addition and Blossom. Eight-person executive team. Headcount not disclosed on their about page; the JD calls the engineering org "still a small but growing software engineering organization".
Locations / HQ: Co-headquartered in Dublin and Boston. Office space in Boston is available to engineers.
Remote policy: Fully remote within defined regions rather than globally. The staff backend JD states: "Location: US Eastern states ideally… You can work fully remotely from anywhere in EST states, with a preference for the Boston area." Other live reqs are "United States (Remote)", "United States - East (Remote)" and "Ireland (Remote)".
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO evidence of it, and the stated geographies are exclusionary — US Eastern states, US-wide, and Ireland. Nothing on the about page or in any live JD mentions Canada. Treat as not Canada-eligible unless they say otherwise.
Engineering & tech: Backend job-processing system and code-execution engines are the deep-infrastructure core: high-availability systems with massive data throughput, action-throughput scaling, job prioritisation controls, fair scheduling on multitenant stacks, and customer code execution. Skills sought: Linux internals (namespaces, file descriptors, Unix sockets, mounts, networking, process isolation), container runtimes/orchestration, filesystem and storage (POSIX behaviour, content-addressed storage, crash recovery), low-level primitives in Rust or Go, deep debugging with logs/traces/metrics. They ship fast — "see them live in production minutes after merging". Public engineering blog and docs. Explicitly: "This role is broad, not specialized… you'll work across our stack, from infrastructure changes to the frontend."
Notable / other: A separate Government Cloud line of work (FedRAMP-style US public-sector deployment) accounts for two of the live senior reqs; those posted US ranges of $210,000-$220,000 base.
Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer (backend); Senior Software Engineer - Government Cloud; Senior Site Reliability Engineer - Government Cloud; Software Engineer (back-end), Ireland.
