# Material Security — research
Provider/key: ashby:materialsecurity | company_type: product
What they do: Material Security provides cloud workspace security for Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 — protecting the email, files and accounts that live there. In their words: "Your fundamental material — the emails, files, and accounts that your company is made of — lives in Google Workspace and Microsoft 365." Products cover email security, account/OAuth protection, and detection/investigation/remediation of data exposure in Drive and similar surfaces, increasingly via LLM-agent workflows.
How they describe themselves: Deliberately anti-hype and pragmatic: "We believe in common sense and not silver bullets, snake oil, or 'next generation' technobabble." They frame their goal as usable security — "usable security means making people safe and productive" — and "security that works — and works simply." JD language asks for people who are "smart, hard-working, humble, and not afraid to fail," "team-first but willing to take the lead with minimal guidance."
Size / stage / funding: Founded 2017; venture-backed, later-stage security startup. Investors: Andreessen Horowitz (Martin Casado), Founders Fund (Trae Stephens), Elad Gil, plus angels including Andy Bechtolsheim, Susan Wagner and Drew Houston. Customers named on their site include OpenAI, Figma, Mars, Lyft and MassMutual. Exact headcount not stated.
Locations / HQ: San Francisco, California office; leadership drawn from Dropbox, Microsoft and Okta. Founders: Ryan Noon (Chairman), Abhishek Agrawal (CEO), Chris Park (VP Engineering).
Remote policy: "Material Security is a remote-first workplace with an office in San Francisco, California" (stated verbatim on both live JDs). Both live engineering roles are listed simply as "Remote."
Remote-Canada eligibility: UNCLEAR / likely US-only. Nothing on their JDs or site says they hire in Canada. The Senior SWE JD carries a "California Candidate Privacy Notice" and both roles quote USD ranges with no Canadian equivalent — the usual signature of US-only employment. Not stated explicitly either way, so this is inference, not verified fact.
Engineering & tech: Backend/systems work integrating deeply with Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 APIs at scale — "rate limits, retries, idempotency, and messy data are familiar territory" — plus TypeScript/React on the frontend. Heavy current investment in production LLM agents: "Design and ship agentic workflows that automate detection, investigation, and remediation of security risks," with explicit attention to "prompt design, tool use, and evaluation" and to "correctness and trust." Small team framing: "an early, remote-first team of experienced engineers."
