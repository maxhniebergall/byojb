# CIBC — research
Provider/key: workday:cibc/search | company_type: product
What they do: CIBC (Canadian Imperial Bank of Commerce) is one of Canada's Big Five banks, offering personal banking, business and commercial banking, wealth management and capital markets, with operations in Canada, the US (CIBC Bank USA) and other regions.
How they describe themselves: Their brand promise is "Ambition™": "At CIBC, we aim to help our clients, employees, communities and shareholders achieve theirs." The recruiting boilerplate on every posting reads: "We're building a relationship-oriented bank for the modern world. We need talented, passionate professionals who are dedicated to doing what's right for our clients. At CIBC, we embrace your strengths and your ambitions, so you are empowered at work. Our team members have what they need to make a meaningful impact and are truly valued for who they are and what they contribute." The corporate site also emphasises integrating sustainability and ESG into operations. Leadership includes Victor Dodig as President and CEO.
Size / stage / funding: Large, publicly traded Canadian chartered bank. Headcount and asset figures were not stated on the About page fetched — unverified here, though CIBC is well known to be a tens-of-thousands-employee institution. Maximum stability; no runway risk.
Locations / HQ: Toronto, Ontario (Eastern Time), with US and international operations. All four live relevant postings are tagged Toronto, ON.
Remote policy: Hybrid, and stated explicitly in the JDs. The Consultant, Analytics Data Engineer posting is the most specific: "At CIBC we enable the work environment most optimal for you to thrive in your role. You'll have the flexibility to manage your work activities within a **hybrid work arrangement where you'll spend 1-3 days per week on-site**, while other days will be remote." The Sr. Infrastructure Engineer and Applied AI Research Scientist postings use the softer variant: "details on your work arrangement (proportion of on-site and remote work) will be discussed at the time of your interview" — i.e. an on-site proportion is assumed, only the amount is negotiable.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO as posted. CIBC is obviously a Canadian employer, but every relevant role is Toronto-based with a stated on-site component of 1–3 days per week (or an on-site proportion to be negotiated). A Toronto hybrid role is not workable from Kimberley BC, and the work is on Eastern Time. Effectively disqualifying.
Engineering & tech: The most relevant role, **Sr. Infrastructure Engineer – Patching**, is substantial platform-automation work: "design, build, secure, and continuously improve the enterprise patch automation ecosystem used to remediate vulnerabilities and maintain platform currency at scale… engineer automated patching workflows for Windows, Linux, middleware, and platform services using orchestration and configuration management capabilities such as **Ansible Automation Platform, PowerShell, Bash, and APIs**… build integrations with vulnerability management, CMDB, change management, monitoring, and service management platforms so that patching is risk-informed, policy-driven, observable, and auditable." It covers JBoss, Oracle Database and MQ, and explicitly includes "identifying manual patching steps and other repetitive operational activities, then designing and delivering automations that reduce manual effort." The data roles sit in Marketing Data Strategy & Governance building "Client-Centric Data Products" on the **Databricks** Analytics Platform with Agile, DevOps, CI/CD and built-in governance. The Applied AI Research Scientist sits in the Advanced Analytics and AI team — "the centre of AI excellence within CIBC" — covering foundation models, multi-agent AI and responsible AI, with academic collaboration and research publication expectations.
Notable / other: No compensation is posted on any CIBC req (Ontario has no salary-disclosure requirement in force for these postings). Note the title/level mismatch on one posting: it is titled "Consultant, Analytics Data Engineer" but the underlying Workday URL is "Manager-Analytics-Engineer_2614374" — a registry data-quality wrinkle worth knowing when reading the level.
Open relevant roles (sample): Sr. Infrastructure Engineer – Patching; Consultant, Analytics Data Engineer; Consultant, Data Engineering & Analytics; Applied AI Research Scientist — all Toronto, ON.
