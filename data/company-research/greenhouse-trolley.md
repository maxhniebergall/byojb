# Trolley — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:trolley | company_type: product

What they do: Trolley is a global **payouts platform** — the infrastructure businesses use to onboard, verify, tax-document and pay recipients worldwide from one system. It is explicitly not a payment processor; it specialises in payout and recipient operations: collecting recipient tax and banking information, identity verification, invoice approval automation, fraud prevention, and sending payments to 210+ countries via local bank transfer, PayPal, Venmo and others. Founded 2015 in Montréal as **Payment Rails**, rebranded to Trolley in 2021. Since inception it has processed billions in payouts and filed over 1.2 million tax statements.

How they describe themselves: Trolley frames itself as building "the global infrastructure behind how people get paid" — infrastructure language in the company's own words, not a marketing gloss. The market focus is the "internet economy": marketplaces, on-demand and sharing-economy platforms, ad networks, affiliate platforms, app stores, and music/creator royalties.

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed. $7M USD Series A (Dec 2021, alongside the Payment Rails → Trolley rebrand), followed by a **$32M CAD Series B** to expand the payout platform's global reach. Recognised on the Deloitte Fast 500 (2024) and Canada's Top Growing Companies (2025) — growth-stage with external validation of revenue trajectory.

Locations / HQ: HQ **Toronto, Canada** (1 University Ave, 3rd Floor). Additional offices in Montréal QC, Halifax NS, London UK, Miami FL, San Francisco CA, Singapore, and Vilnius, Lithuania. Roughly 83% of staff are Canadian, with the largest concentration historically in Montréal.

Remote policy: The relevant engineering role is posted as a fully remote Canadian role — the JD opens "Remote role, based in Canada" and closes with the `#LI-remote` tag. Benefits include "30 days remote work from anywhere," indicating an established distributed-work posture rather than an office-default one, despite the office footprint.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** The live Senior Software Engineer posting is listed with location "Canada" and the JD's first line is "Remote role, based in Canada." Engineering & tech: Payments infrastructure — recipient onboarding and KYC/identity verification, tax reporting (1099/1042-S-scale volume), multi-rail payment routing across 210+ countries, fraud prevention, and compliance. Regulated: Trolley US Inc. is a licensed money transmitter (NMLS ID 2309944) with deposits held at Cross River Bank. This is correctness-critical, audited, back-office systems work.

Notable / other: Clients include Envato, Canva, SoundCloud, Epic Games and Bugcrowd. Recognised as a Great Place to Work Best Workplace in Quebec (2023) and Best Workplaces for Start-ups (2024). Compensation package includes meaningful equity (ESOP).
