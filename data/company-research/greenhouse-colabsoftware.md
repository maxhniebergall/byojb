# CoLab Software — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:colabsoftware | company_type: product

What they do: CoLab makes a design-review and engineering-knowledge platform for **mechanical engineering teams** — CAD design reviews in the browser, with the resulting feedback, decisions and lessons learned accumulating into a knowledge repository. In their words: "CoLab is the AI platform for driving stronger engineering decisions. Every design review in CoLab builds a knowledge repository of design feedback, decisions, and lessons learned — which AI agents draw from to flag issues on future designs before they compound." Named customers: **Ford, Komatsu, Johnson Controls**. The AI layer is branded **Operator**, and is explicitly not a bolted-on chatbot: "Operator isn't a chatbot bolted onto our product. It's becoming the intelligence layer across CoLab."

How they describe themselves: "At CoLab, we help mechanical engineering teams bring life-changing products to market years sooner." They are candid about the nature of the work rather than promotional: "The work is complex and often ambiguous. This role exists because there often isn't an existing pattern to follow." They publish an anti-fit list in their own JD — you likely won't enjoy the role if "you prefer working primarily on frontend applications", "you want highly defined requirements before starting work", or "you'd rather optimize existing systems than design entirely new capabilities" — and a fit list: "you enjoy designing backend systems from first principles", "you like difficult infrastructure and data problems", "you're curious about AI, search, and knowledge systems", "you balance technical excellence with pragmatism and collaboration".

Size / stage / funding: Founded in St. John's, Newfoundland; first customer in 2019; a Y Combinator alumnus. Raised a USD $21M / CAD $28.6M Series B led by Insight Partners (with Killick Capital and Pelorus VC), which took headcount from 86 to about 125; secured a further CAD $5.6M in 2025 from the Hibernia and Hebron projects for local AI talent and the ReviewAI roadmap; and raised a **USD $72M round on AI demand**, for roughly **$108M total across six rounds**. Recognized on Deloitte's Fast 50 and Fast 500 and named a company to watch by The Globe and Mail and Financial Post. A well-funded scale-up, not an early startup, but still venture-backed and growth-stage.

Locations / HQ: Headquarters in **St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador** (Atlantic time), with flexible hybrid and remote options and remote hiring across Canada and the United States.

Remote policy: Remote-friendly with an office-optional HQ: "Our headquarters are in St. John's, Newfoundland, with flexible hybrid and remote options." The backend role is posted "Canada, Remote"; other roles are posted "North America, Remote".

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, explicitly.** The Senior Back End Developer JD states in bold: "**This role can be performed remotely from anywhere in Canada.**" That is as unambiguous as this gets, and it is a Canadian company with Canadian benefits (extended health, unlimited paid vacation, RRSP matching). Timezone note: HQ is Newfoundland time, which is 4.5 hours ahead of Mountain — the largest timezone gap in this batch, so overlap hours will skew early for a Kimberley-based employee.

Engineering & tech: The Build Operator team owns the backend behind the AI layer: scalable **retrieval pipelines**, AI search infrastructure, integration of structured and unstructured engineering data, and the services powering intelligent experiences. Roadmap named: company-wide knowledge retrieval, dynamic reporting, AI-generated dashboards, personalized engineering insights. Stack and interests named: **Python**, APIs, distributed systems, cloud-native applications, databases, event-driven architectures, and on the bonus list **vector databases and search infrastructure (TurboPuffer named specifically)**, embeddings, ranking, information retrieval, RAG architectures, LLM workflows, enterprise SaaS. There is also a simulation/3D engineering side to the product (Senior Software Developer - Simulation, Senior 3D & AI Systems Software Developer). Bar is 7+ years of production backend.

Notable / other: Full-time permanent roles with competitive compensation and **stock options**; extended health benefits, **unlimited paid vacation**, and **RRSP matching**. The company is a rare Atlantic-Canadian scale-up with genuine enterprise industrial customers, and the AI work is grounded in a real proprietary data asset (years of accumulated design-review decisions) rather than a generic wrapper. There is an open VP of Software Engineering role, so the engineering leadership layer is in flux.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Back End Developer (Canada, Remote); Senior Software Developer - Simulation (North America, Remote); VP of Software Engineering (North America, Remote); Senior 3D & AI Systems Software Developer (recently closed).

Pay: No figure disclosed on any posting and none found first-party — only "competitive compensation and stock options". No band recorded.
