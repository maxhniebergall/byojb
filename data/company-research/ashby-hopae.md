# Hopae — research
Provider/key: ashby:hopae | company_type: product

What they do: Digital-identity infrastructure. Hopae's site describes "a global eID verification hub for enterprises" — connecting to government-issued digital IDs across 65+ countries through a single API. Two products: **Connect** (an API for accessing global eIDs) and **Wallet** (an SDK for building eIDAS 2.0-compliant digital wallets). Their JD frames it as "the world's most trusted digital identity platform — private, secure, and built for real life… Hopae Connect lets businesses verify real people across borders through a single API — no need to build country-by-country integrations themselves." Customers/verticals: IDV providers, e-signature platforms, fintech, credentials, supply chain, gaming, travel, telecom.

How they describe themselves: "We believe identity should be in your control — not locked behind outdated systems or centralized gatekeepers." Culture bullets, in their words: "Global DNA" (peers across Seoul, Paris, San Francisco); "High Impact"; "Ownership Culture: No micromanagement — full trust, full accountability"; "Elite Standards: Work fast, learn fast, and grow with world-class peers"; "Inclusive & Respectful: 10+ nationalities, diverse perspectives, one vision"; "Growth Mindset." Also "We move fast, think globally, and build products that define the future of identity." Site positioning is "designed by developers, for developers," emphasizing speed, security and compliance, with "government-grade security," SOC 2 and GDPR certifications.

Size / stage / funding: Early stage. First-party "Fast Facts": "$13.5M+ raised from top global investors", "2.6M+ downloads of our sd-jwt-js library", "10+ nationalities across 3 hubs", "82 eID research projects completed", "3 projects accepted into the OpenWallet Foundation." Headcount not disclosed anywhere first-party — unverified, but a seed/Series-A-scale company on the funding figure.

Locations / HQ: "operates across Seoul, Paris, Luxembourg, and San Francisco." No single HQ is stated; the site does not name one. Korea/Europe are the centre of gravity given the eID/eIDAS focus.

Remote policy: Stated as "Remote-friendly work model" with "Unlimited PTO" and "Real ownership from Day 1." The posting's location field is simply "Remote." No country list, no timezone-overlap statement.

Hiring bar stated as "Solid understanding of Node.js and TypeScript, with at least 2 years of backend development experience" — i.e. a mid-level bar, not senior.
