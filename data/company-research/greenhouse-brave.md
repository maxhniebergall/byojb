# Brave (Brave Software) — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:brave | company_type: product

What they do: Brave Software builds the Brave web browser — a Chromium-based browser that blocks ads and trackers by default — plus Brave Search (a private search engine with "a truly independent index"), a browser-native crypto wallet, Brave Rewards / a private opt-in ad network, and premium products (VPN, Leo AI). Revenue comes chiefly from its own ad network, search API/ads and premium subscriptions; estimated ~$100M annual revenue. Over 121 million people use the browser by the company's own count.

How they describe themselves: "Brave is on a mission to protect the human right to privacy online… The internet is a sea of ads, hackers, and echo chambers. Big Tech makes huge profits off our data, and tells us what's true and what's not. Brave is fighting back." Working-at-Brave points they list themselves: "Industry-leader in privacy, with a research and engineering team that's innovating everyday"; "Highly competitive salaries & benefits, and generous home-office stipends"; "Fully remote team, though we have offices and gatherings in a few locations around the world"; "Welcoming, humble, ridiculously smart teammates, and a truly flat org structure"; "Opportunity to get in early at a hyper-growth company". They note co-founder/CEO Brendan Eich invented JavaScript.

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2015, private, HQ San Francisco. Roughly 340 employees as of 2026 (up from ~290 in 2025); estimated ~$100M ARR. Not a fresh-funding-dependent startup — it has a real revenue base — but it is still private and its ad/crypto revenue model is unconventional.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco HQ; a handful of offices and gathering locations worldwide; the team itself is distributed globally.

Remote policy: **Fully remote**, and unusually explicit about async working norms. From the JD, in their words: "Our processes are light and we have no mandatory weekly meetings, 0 on-call, no need to be in an office (unless you really want to) and no strict core hours. Work is autonomous (collaborative at times), asynchronous, flexible." The DevOps posting is banded "Remote - Anywhere (American timezones preferred)".

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, with a caveat.** The role is "Remote - Anywhere", American timezones preferred — Mountain Time is ideal for that. The JD says "No visa sponsorship provided", which is irrelevant for a Canadian citizen working from Canada. The one unknown is the employment vehicle (employer of record vs contractor) for a Canadian hire; that is not stated and should be asked.

Engineering & tech: Build/release and CI at real scale — "hundreds of builds running every day for over 1000 GitHub repositories (many open source)" in C++, Rust, Go, Python, JavaScript, Ruby, Kotlin, Bash/Shell, HCL. Multiple CI systems with acceleration techniques. Named tools: AWS + Terraform, TeamCity (Kotlin DSL), Jenkins (Jenkins Job Builder), gn/ninja/siso/reclient, Remote Execution API, EngFlow, ccache/sccache, Gerrit, code signing, app-store distribution and packaging across Android, iOS, Linux, macOS, Windows. The DevOps team is described as "a team of 8 seniors, each with 15-20+ years of experience". They expect "responsible AI usage and able to use and review generated code". Much of the codebase is open source (brave-browser on GitHub).

Notable / other: Flat org structure and no on-call are both rare and directly stated. The company's identity is adversarial toward Big Tech; the crypto/BAT component of the business is a genuine differentiator and a genuine risk factor depending on one's view. The DevOps requirement bar is high: "10+ years of experience".

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior DevOps Engineer (browser CI/CD + AWS) — Remote, Anywhere.
