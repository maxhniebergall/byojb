# Rumble — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:rumble | company_type: product

What they do: Rumble Inc. (NASDAQ: RUM) is a Canadian-American online video platform and cloud
services business. Two business lines: **Rumble Services** (Rumble Video, Rumble Studio, Rumble
Advertising Center/RAC, Rumble Wallet — a non-custodial crypto wallet) and **Rumble Cloud**, an
IaaS offering explicitly positioned as resilient against deplatforming. Founded 2013 by Chris
Pavlovski; went public via SPAC in September 2022.

How they describe themselves: Their own JD boilerplate opens: *"Rumble is **the** Freedom-First
technology platform. We proudly offer a video platform, cloud services, advertising solutions,
and a non-custodial cryptocurrency wallet."* The founding rationale, per public profiles, was that
incumbent video platforms prioritized large brands and major influencers over smaller independent
creators. The identity is explicitly free-speech / anti-deplatforming and politically salient —
this is a values-forward positioning, not a neutral infrastructure one.

Size / stage / funding: ~278 employees (May 2026). Public company (NASDAQ: RUM) since Sept 2022.
Not a startup; revenue-generating but historically loss-making and capital-markets dependent.

Locations / HQ: Toronto, Ontario (Canadian HQ/eng) and Longboat Key, Florida (US HQ, opened 2023).
Additional posting geos: Miami, Sarasota, Washington D.C., New York City.

Remote policy: Not stated on any of the six live JDs. Every relevant posting names concrete office
cities as the location — no "remote", "hybrid", "distributed" or "work from anywhere" language
appears anywhere in the JD bodies (grep for remote/hybrid/onsite returns nothing).

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, and the evidence points to no.** The Canadian roles are
posted as "Toronto, ON"; the multi-city roles list Miami / Sarasota / Toronto / Washington D.C. as
alternatives, i.e. a choice of offices, not remote. There is no statement supporting a
British-Columbia-based remote hire. Treat as Toronto-office-based unless recruiter confirms
otherwise.

Engineering & tech: Backend is **PHP 8+ / MySQL / Memcached / Redis / Linux / NGINX**, with a
substantial acknowledged **legacy-modernization** component ("maintaining and modernizing legacy
code", PHPStan, PHPUnit, server-side rendering). Infra side is more interesting: a real CDN team,
a **DBaaS platform team**, a platform-security team, and video/codec/live-streaming work at
"millions of users" scale. Senior backend asks 8+ years backend and 10+ years OOP with 5+ years
PHP — an unusually high bar, and a stack-specific one. JDs also say "willingness to jump into any
project when needed".

Notable / other: Rumble Cloud makes this genuinely an infrastructure company in part, not only a
media company. The crypto wallet and ad-network lines add product/growth surface. The brand is
politically polarizing, which is a real career-narrative consideration independent of the work.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Back-End Developer; Senior Back-End Developer, Rumble Studio;
Senior Data Engineer; Senior Infrastructure Engineer (CDN); Senior Platform Engineer (DBaaS);
Senior Platform Engineer - Security.

Pay (stated on their own JDs): Senior Back-End Developer — **$135,000–$154,000 CAD base + benefits
+ equity**, with a note that the range is a good-faith estimate. Other senior roles carry posted
USD ranges (data eng ~$149–170k USD; platform security ~$165–205k USD) — see
`data/company-comp.jsonl`. Note the CAD roles pay materially less than the US-located ones.
