# IntellectEU — research

Provider/key: bamboohr:intellecteu | company_type: product

**What they do:** IntellectEU is "an international technology company focused on digital finance and emerging technologies" — fintech spanning payments, blockchain and capital markets. Two intertwined businesses: (1) **client delivery / integration** — "Since 2006 we have been focused on financial messaging and integration, being a SWIFT service partner", and "Over the past two decades, we have earned our expertise in financial technology, executing on hundreds of complex integrations and co-creating new digital asset platforms alongside our clients"; and (2) a **product line, CatalyX** — "the leading blockchain operations platform", whose suite (CatalyX Blockchain Manager, CatalyX Package Manager, CatalyX Tokenization Explorer, and an upcoming DAML Agent and Wallet Manager) "supports 100+ active validators and enables customers to successfully deploy and operate production Canton infrastructure." They also contribute directly to the **Splice codebase on the Canton Network**, "a next-generation privacy-preserving distributed ledger being adopted by major market infrastructure providers globally."

**How they describe themselves:** "At IntellectEU we are on a mission to become the top-of-mind fintech company. We are committed to our people and believe in building a better future with the newest technologies. We give rising talent and industry veterans the space to explore, launch ideas, and collaborate closely with our clients. With our technical hands-on experience in payments, blockchain and capital markets, there's no challenge that's left unsolved." On engineering standards: "Our blockchain practice is built around production-grade frameworks where **security, scalability, and correctness are non-negotiable**", and they ask for "high standards for code quality, test coverage, and documentation **in a codebase where correctness is critical**." Notably calm, craft-oriented language — **no "fast-paced", hustle or hype phrasing anywhere in the four JDs.** They also call for "honest, actionable feedback that motivates the team."

**Size / stage / funding:** Private, founded 2006 (two decades old) — established rather than early-stage. Headcount, ownership and funding are **not stated and unverified**. The engineering org is described as "a distributed team of senior engineers" / "globally distributed development environment."

**Locations / HQ:** No HQ stated in the postings; the name and SWIFT-partner history indicate a European base (unverified). Teams are globally distributed.

**Remote policy:** Remote, and explicitly **async-first**: "Work within a globally distributed Agile team, coordinating across time zones with **asynchronous-first communication**." But hard geographic and overlap constraints are stated per role: **"Location — EU countries, remotely"** (Senior Software Engineer (Java), req 441) and **"Location — EU, remote. 5-hour overlap with EDT time is required"** (req 451). Employment models differ per posting — the BambooHR employment-type field reads "**Consultant**" on req 441 and "**Employee**" on reqs 448/450, so at least some roles are contractor engagements.

**Remote-Canada eligibility: No.** The Java roles state remote hiring is restricted to **EU countries**; Canada is never mentioned in any posting, and there is no evidence of a Canadian or North-American hiring entity. The one stated overlap requirement is 5 hours with EDT — from Mountain Time that means a working day pushed early, and it does not create eligibility in any case. Treat as disqualifying unless a non-EU-scoped posting appears.

**Engineering & tech:** Serious, correctness-first distributed-systems work. Java/Kotlin and other JVM languages; **Scala with functional programming depth** ("typeclasses, effect systems, immutability, referential transparency", implicits/givens, leveraging the compiler for correctness guarantees); distributed systems fundamentals (consensus, fault tolerance, eventual consistency, partition handling); gRPC and Protobuf, service-oriented architectures; REST APIs; relational databases (PostgreSQL, strong SQL), message queues, **Public Key Infrastructure**; Kubernetes and containerized deployment; OAuth/OIDC (Keycloak, Auth0); DevOps — CI/CD, GitOps, IaC, observability; networking fundamentals (TLS, DNS, load balancing, service-to-service comms). Domain: Canton/Daml and enterprise blockchain, a production collateral-exchange platform "actively evolving with new business use cases", plus refactoring and redesign work "to improve scalability, maintainability, and long-term performance." On-call is explicit: "Participate in on-call support rotations, troubleshooting and resolving production issues to ensure system stability and high availability." They actively encourage AI coding tools with judgment — "Experienced user of AI coding tools (e.g. Claude Code, Copilot, Cursor) with sound judgment on where they accelerate delivery and where they introduce risk."

**Notable / other:** Seniority bar is high (8+ years for the Senior Java role, 7+ with team leadership for the Lead role, 5+ years of Scala for the "Software Engineer (Scala)" role — so that title's ladder level is understated). Roles include real mentoring/capability-building mandates. Compensation is not posted on any listing.

**Company-type note:** This is a genuine hybrid. The SWIFT-service-partner / "hundreds of complex integrations… alongside our clients" history is consulting-shaped, and one req is posted as a **Consultant** engagement; but CatalyX is a real owned product with its own roadmap and 100+ validators, and the Splice/Canton work is upstream open-source contribution. Recorded as `product` rather than `consulting` on that basis — but a client-services centre of gravity is a live risk and should be re-checked if it ever resurfaces in the queue.
