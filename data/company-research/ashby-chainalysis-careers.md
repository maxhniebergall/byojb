# Chainalysis — research
Provider/key: ashby:chainalysis-careers | company_type: product

What they do: Blockchain data and investigations software. Their own framing: "Our data and solutions have been used to solve some of the world's most high-profile criminal cases and grow consumer access to cryptocurrency safely." The flagship investigative product is **Reactor**. Customers span government/law-enforcement agencies (including highly security-sensitive ones running air-gapped deployments) and commercial crypto businesses. Stated current mission: "to build a flexible, AI-driven platform that automates entity resolution, optimizes data labeling pipelines, and creates predictive models that identify illicit patterns before they escalate" — and "by pairing the industry's most trusted blockchain data with agents that reason, investigate, and act, we help our customers scale their workflows as the cryptocurrency economy becomes increasingly mainstream." Recent acquisitions are visible in the org structure: **Hexagate** and **Alterya** each have their own engineering postings.

How they describe themselves: "The engineering team at Chainalysis is inspired by solving the hardest technical challenges and building products that establish trust in cryptocurrencies. We're a global organization that thrives on challenging work and doing it alongside exceptionally talented teammates. Our industry evolves rapidly..." Engineering-craft expectations are stated concretely: "you write code you're proud to operate, think about failure modes before they happen," "a strong sense of ownership and effective cross-team collaboration," and "well-tested, well-documented code with unit tests, integration tests, and CI-driven quality gates." Note the mild intensity signals — "thrives on challenging work", "our industry evolves rapidly" — though there is no explicit hustle/rocketship vocabulary.

Size / stage / funding: Large, well-established private company and the dominant vendor in blockchain analytics; "a global organization" in its own words. Engineering & tech: Go, Java, JavaScript/TypeScript, React, Node.js, and Bash. Strong Kubernetes emphasis — "deployments, networking, storage, Helm, and troubleshooting workloads in production", with custom operators (Operator SDK / controller-runtime) as a bonus. Terraform IaC on AWS for dev, acceptance-testing and data-delivery environments. Distinctive specialisation: on-premises and **fully air-gapped** deployment — a Go-based installer CLI doing "provisioning, manifest rendering, image bundling, and end-to-end deployment", OCI registries and image mirroring, plus a Java blockchain-data delivery pipeline that "packages blockchain data in the cloud, delivers it to customer sites, and unpacks it for ingestion into the on-premises system". Compliance engineering is first-class: FedRAMP, audit logging, RBAC policies, package signing, security hardening, multi-tenant isolation. Linux fundamentals (RHEL). The Government Systems team is described as owning "the critical path from 'Chainalysis builds software' to 'a government customer is running it'", partnering with Reactor product teams, Security, Compliance and Customer Success.

Notable / other: The Government Systems team sits within the Investigations group. Reactor product teams own their application code and depend on Government Systems for installer, data delivery, multi-tenant configuration and the admin portal — an unusually clean internal-platform boundary. Broader open roles show data-platform and infrastructure investment beyond gov systems.

Compensation stated on their own JDs: Senior Infrastructure Engineer, Government Systems — "$150K – $200K • Offers Equity • Offers Bonus" (USD base, described as "Estimated base salary based on location & level of experience"). The Software Engineer II, Gov Systems posting states no range.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Infrastructure Engineer, Government Systems; Software Engineer II, Gov Systems; Senior Infrastructure Engineer; Senior Data Platform Engineer, Data Cloud; Senior Software Engineer, Hexagate; Senior Software Engineer, Alterya; Staff Software Engineer, Graphs.
