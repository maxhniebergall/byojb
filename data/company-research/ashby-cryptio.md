# Cryptio — research
Provider/key: ashby:cryptio | company_type: product

What they do: In their own words, *"Cryptio is the leading enterprise-grade financial data transformation and digital asset ERP platform for accounting, financial reporting, tokenization compliance, and loan management solutions. It is a complete back and middle-office platform that delivers financial integrity and accountability for businesses in crypto. Its platform allows users to transform on-chain and off-chain crypto activity into auditable records for accounting, treasury, tax filings, license applications, loans activity, and regulatory reporting."* In plain terms: crypto sub-ledger and accounting infrastructure — it ingests blockchain and exchange activity and turns it into audit-grade financial records for institutions, funds, exchanges and their auditors.

How they describe themselves: The culture statement is short and explicit: *"At Cryptio, we move fast and take ownership of outcomes. We learn from failures, celebrate wins, and let humility, curiosity, and a passion for crypto guide how we work. If you value collaboration and want to build with purpose, you'll feel right at home here."* The role framing emphasizes autonomy and small-team surface area: *"We're hiring our first Senior Software Engineer – Intelligence… **The team is small on purpose.**"* and *"This is a **high-autonomy seat** with real room to grow. You'll experiment, ship, and shape the toolkit's direction. As the practice scales, the right person grows into a technical leader for the function."* They also expect pushback on specs: *"push back on specs that miss"*; *"When the spec is wrong, you propose alternatives. When something's broken, you don't wait to be asked."* Cross-functional exposure is stated as a requirement, not a perk: *"Finance, product, design, leadership are all on your week, not just other engineers."*

Size / stage / funding: **Series B**, announced at **$45M** — *"Having recently announced our $45m series B, now couldn't be a better time to join!"* Roughly 100-person scale. Venture-funded, not profitable, in a domain whose customer base (crypto firms) is itself cyclical.

Locations / HQ: London, UK-founded with a European (UK/France) centre of gravity. The Advisory Intelligence role is posted for **"Canada East Coast, Ontario, Toronto."**

Remote policy: Stated as *"👩‍💻 Remote or Hybrid working."* Benefits: *"25 days paid holiday plus bank holidays"*, *"One additional day of annual leave each year, up to 30 total days"*, *"Your birthday off"*, *"Mental health resources, wellbeing programs, and professional coaching"*, *"Family-friendly policies"*, *"Fitness and wellness budget"*, *"MacBook Pro"*, *"$200 home office setup budget"*, *"Training and development budget"*, with *"additional benefits depending on location."* The PTO baseline is genuinely good (a real 25–30 days, not "unlimited").

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes for Canada, but explicitly scoped to Eastern Canada — not British Columbia.** The posting's own location field is *"Canada East Coast, Ontario, Toronto"*. That is a deliberate geographic restriction on a company that could have written "Canada"; combined with a UK/EU-headquartered team it implies an Eastern-Time-or-later overlap expectation. Kimberley BC is three hours behind Toronto and eight behind London, so this role as posted does not cover the candidate's location. Worth one clarifying question, but do not score it as Canada-wide.

Engineering & tech: Full-stack **TypeScript**. Backend **Node.js** — *"REST APIs, middleware auth, async pipelines with batching, concurrency limits, retries, timeouts"*. Frontend **React + TypeScript** with **Zustand** state management and **Zod** validation. **Postgres** is central and treated seriously: *"data modeling for workflow and run-oriented schemas, indexing strategy, query troubleshooting at table scale"* and *"designing versioned config and audit-trail schemas. Append-only matters in compliance contexts and you've built for it."* Distributed-systems literacy expected (partial writes, retries, ordering, observability). Production LLM work with **AWS Bedrock, OpenAI or the Anthropic SDK**; multi-step AI pipelines with *"prompt chaining, structured output parsing, confidence scoring, failure recovery, and audit-trail logging"*; an **AI output evaluation framework** with *"structured test inputs, expected outputs, scoring rubrics"*. Nice-to-haves: Next.js App Router, **BullMQ / pg-boss** background jobs, AWS/Docker/CI-CD ownership, parsing Excel/CSV/ERP exports into typed pipelines, GAAP/IFRS and month-end-close familiarity.

Notable / other: The architecture philosophy is unusually well articulated and sensible: *"Each tool combines deterministic logic (the parts where being wrong has financial consequences) with AI layers that draw on a structured library of real client engagements"*, and the engineer is expected to *"maintain and extend the deterministic pre-processing and heuristic layers that make AI output reliable enough for audit."* That is a mature stance on where LLMs belong in a correctness-critical system. The role reports into a VP of Advisory Intelligence and works alongside a single Advisory Intelligence Product Engineer — a two-person function.
