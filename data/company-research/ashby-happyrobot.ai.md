# HappyRobot — research
Provider/key: ashby:happyrobot.ai | company_type: product

What they do: HappyRobot builds "the infrastructure for enterprises to build and orchestrate AI workforces" — AI agents ("AI workers") that communicate over voice, email and chat and autonomously execute operational tasks: negotiating freight rates, scheduling shipments, chasing check-calls, collecting payments. They started in freight/logistics and are expanding into utilities, airlines, finance, insurance, manufacturing and retail — what they call "the AI operating system for the real economy". 70+ enterprise customers including DHL, Ryder, Schneider and Werner.

How they describe themselves: From their own JD and manifesto: their AI workers "don't just communicate — they make decisions, take action, and run operations autonomously"; the platform is "battle-tested in the most demanding environments — where AI has real consequences"; they "built our own voice stack, models, and orchestration layer from the ground up". The Series B post frames the mission as "closing the circuit between intelligence and action". Their stated **Operating Principles** lead with **Extreme Ownership** — "We take full responsibility for our work, outcomes, and team success. No excuses, no blame-shifting — if something needs fixing, we own it and make it better." The ML JD's "Must Have" list ends with "**Founder mindset**: ownership, independence, and willingness to go deep", and the perks list leads with "Opportunity to work at a **high-growth AI startup**", "Take full ownership of projects and **ship fast**", and "Work With the Best". Tone is high-intensity, ownership-maximalist, YC-inflected.

Size / stage / funding: Y Combinator S23. Series A $15.6M; **Series B $44M (Sept 2025, led by Base10 Partners**, with a16z, Array Ventures and YC participating), ~$500M reported valuation. **$62M raised total** (their JD rounds this to "over $60M"). Early-stage, venture-funded, growth-mode; not profitable. Small — the ML JD says the hire would be "one of our first ML hires".

Locations / HQ: US-founded via YC, Spanish founders; HQ not stated on the pages fetched. Serves "global enterprises worldwide".

Remote policy: The single relevant registry posting ("Machine Learning Engineer") is tagged **Remote**. No remote-first / async / handbook language anywhere in their own materials; the culture text ("ship fast", "go deep", "founder mindset") reads as high-synchronicity startup rather than async-documented.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear.** The posting says only "Remote" with no geography qualifier and no stated country list; nothing on their own pages confirms Canadian employment. Would need to be asked directly.

Engineering & tech: Own voice stack, in-house models, and an orchestration layer built from scratch. The ML role covers the full lifecycle: "Design, build, and maintain scalable ML systems — from data ingestion and preprocessing to training, testing, and deployment"; "Develop and optimize end-to-end ML pipelines (data collection, labeling, training, validation, monitoring)"; "Implement robust MLOps practices, including model versioning, experiment tracking, CI/CD for ML, and continuous monitoring in production"; "Ensure data quality, observability, and performance across all AI systems." Required: ML/DL/NLP depth, MLOps and data-pipeline background, **Python plus familiarity with Go**, ML lifecycle tooling (MLflow, Kubeflow, Weights & Biases). Nice-to-have: speech recognition/TTS/audio, LLMs and **real-time inference systems**, orchestration frameworks (Airflow, Prefect, Dagster), cloud (AWS/GCP/Azure) + Docker/Kubernetes.

Notable / other: Vertical-AI-agent company at the peak of the agent funding cycle; deep-tech credibility comes from owning the voice/model/orchestration stack rather than wrapping a vendor API. Benefits stated: competitive salary + equity, healthcare/dental/vision. Rapid round cadence (Series B only ten months after Series A) signals aggressive scaling.

Open relevant roles (sample): "Machine Learning Engineer" (Remote).
