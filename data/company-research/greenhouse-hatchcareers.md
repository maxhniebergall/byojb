# Hatch — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:hatchcareers | company_type: product

What they do: Hatch is a consumer **sleep-wellness hardware + app company**. It makes connected smart sleep devices — Rest, Rest+ and Rest Go for babies and kids, and Hatch Restore, a customisable smart sleep clock / sunrise alarm for adults — paired with an iOS/Android app offering sounds, routines and guidance. The business is device sales plus an app subscription. Founded 2014; Rest has helped 500,000+ families and the company says it now serves **more than 5 million people**.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "to make sleep easier than ever for everyone with innovative, all-in-one products for newborns through adults." Their positioning is deliberately counter-cultural: "The world is wired and tired… Hatch is a sleep wellness company that's betting on rest in a culture obsessed with optimization. While most technology is designed to keep you engaged, our devices use the science of habit formation to help people unplug, unwind, and recharge… (no sleep scores or wearables here)." They emphasise not tracking or selling user data. On the JD they call out being "a certified Great Place to Work" with 94% of employees agreeing, "an amazing leadership team that truly values employees and leads with our company values every day," a "collaborative, fun" team, backing by "world-class investors and growing fast," and "a remote-friendly work environment."

Engineering values as stated in the Staff Backend JD: "Automate, Automate, Automate"; **"Desire for elegance and simplicity — 'Do the simplest thing that can possibly work' is not just a slogan here; it's a way of life"**; "Judgement and Humility"; "Unafraid of new, unknown tech"; "Collaborative spirit."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed ("world-class investors"), founded 2014 by parent entrepreneurs Ann Crady Weiss (CEO) and Dave Weiss — both ex-BabyCenter — after Ann's post-partum sleep deprivation and Dave's chronic insomnia. Appeared on Shark Tank in 2016. Exact headcount and funding stage not published; JDs describe "a small team."

Locations / HQ: Headquartered in **Menlo Park, California** (some listings show San Francisco). Distributed hiring across the US and Canada.

Remote policy: **Remote-friendly / distributed.** The JD states the role's location as "Remote-friendly (US/Canada, PST hours)," with a remote office stipend and monthly internet reimbursement.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes — verified, and stated in the company's own words.** The Staff Backend Engineer posting says: "We're open to candidates across the U.S. and Canada. However, please note that Hatch is based in California, so we just ask that you're available during PST/PDT hours." The registry location field agrees: "Distributed, 🇺🇸 + 🇨🇦 (US + Canada)." Pacific hours are a one-hour offset from Mountain Time — entirely workable from Kimberley, BC. (Caveat: the JD also carries a US E-Verify notice, boilerplate that applies to US hires.)

Engineering & tech: Backend is **Java 11+ / Spring Boot / Hibernate**, Groovy, and Python services; RESTful JSON APIs; **AWS IoT** and Lambda for the device fleet; MySQL; Ansible, Jenkins, ELK, Graphite, Docker for infra and monitoring; distributed systems. The Staff Backend role reports to the **Director, Backend & DevOps** and owns architecture, delivery and reliability for the cloud backend behind the mobile apps and IoT devices — device communication, registration/auth flows, routine scheduling — working across Mobile, Embedded and Product. Continuous Deployment and automated testing are explicitly held values. **AI-assisted development is described as core to how the team works** ("we expect this senior role to… use AI fluently in their day-to-day").

Notable / other: Hiring process and role expectations are published as an unusually explicit 90-day / 6-month / 1-year ramp plan. Benefits: 100% paid medical/dental/vision for employees (80% dependents, incl. domestic partners), meaningful equity, 401(k) match with **no vesting schedule**, 16 weeks fully paid parental leave, 16 free therapy sessions (for the employee plus one other adult), family-planning support, remote office + monthly internet reimbursement, product and retail discounts. No salary range is posted on the Canada/US-distributed listing.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Backend Engineer (Distributed, US + Canada, PST hours).
