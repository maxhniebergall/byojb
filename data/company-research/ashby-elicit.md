# Elicit — research
Provider/key: ashby:elicit | company_type: product
What they do: Elicit is an AI research assistant that uses language models to help researchers "figure out what's true and make better decisions", starting with literature review and evidence synthesis. Over 2 million people use it, including pharma teams making R&D decisions "worth tens of millions of dollars", scientists and enterprise decision-makers. A single session can trigger hundreds of thousands of language-model invocations across multiple providers.
How they describe themselves — their stated aims, quoted: "Elicit radically increases the amount of good reasoning in the world." "For experts, Elicit pushes the frontier forward." "For non-experts, Elicit makes good reasoning more affordable. People who don't have the tools, expertise, time, or mental energy to make well-reasoned decisions on their own can do so with Elicit." "Elicit is a scalable ML system based on human-understandable task decompositions, with supervision of process, not outcomes. This expands our collective understanding of safe AGI architectures." On culture: "High agency, low bureaucracy. ~30 curious, slightly weird people who write things down and trust each other to run with a vague brief." "Built for the long term. We're a Public Benefit Corporation that spun out of a non-profit AI research lab. Long-term impact and AI safety are part of the corporate charter." They also say "everybody's a craftsperson who cares deeply about their field."
Size / stage / funding: ~30 people company-wide. Public Benefit Corporation, spun out of the non-profit AI research lab Ought. Funding stage not stated in the material read; enterprise contracts are described as "getting larger" with 8-figure deals as a stated goal, so revenue is real but the company is small. Head of Engineering is James (ex-Square); cofounders are Andreas and Jungwon.
Locations / HQ: Office in Oakland, CA. Otherwise distributed.
Remote policy: Remote-friendly by policy, not merely tolerated: "We have a great office in Oakland, CA, and we'd love to see you there if you're local. That said, we're just as happy for you to work remotely." Formally: "work from our office in Oakland or remotely with time zone overlap (between GMT and GMT-8), as long as you're comfortable traveling for quarterly in-person offsites." Whole team gets together for a quarterly retreat. Writing-things-down is called out as a cultural norm.
Remote-Canada eligibility: PARTIAL / UNVERIFIED — timezone yes, employment entity unknown. The stated remote band GMT to GMT-8 explicitly contains Mountain Time (GMT-7/-6), so Kimberley BC is inside their supported working hours; the Ashby location line reads "Oakland, CA (or remote within US timezones)", and MT is a US timezone, so timezone is not a barrier. What is NOT stated anywhere is whether they can employ a worker resident in Canada — benefits listed are US-shaped (401K with 6% employer match, FSA/HSA) with the caveat "some variation based on location". This must be confirmed with them; do not assume it.
Engineering & tech: Backend in Node and Python; frontend Next.js/TypeScript/Tailwind; static type checking in both Python and TypeScript; GitHub for code review and CI. Infrastructure: Terraform (primary IaC), Kubernetes across AWS (primary) and GCP, Argo CD, GitHub Actions, Karpenter autoscaling, Aurora PostgreSQL, Redis, MongoDB Atlas, Cloudflare, DataDog-class observability. Inference runs across multiple providers (AWS, CoreWeave, Parasail) with circuit breakers and inference routing. Compliance work is real: SOC 2, NIST AI framework, EU Cyber Resilience, SIEM, DR drills. Agentic coding tools (Claude Code, Cursor, Devin) are described as "how we build at Elicit", including for IaC and incident investigation.
Notable / other: The Infrastructure Engineer role is their FIRST dedicated infrastructure hire — the function has been covered part-time by one engineer (Panda) and by the Head of Engineering. There is no formal on-call rotation yet; the JD is candid that "part of this role is building the incident response practice we should have… and eventually a rotation designed so it doesn't burn anyone out. You'd design the on-call setup you'll then live with." Perks: fully covered health/dental/vision/life for the employee with generous family coverage, flexible vacation with a 20-day/year minimum recommendation, $200/month wellbeing stipend, 401K with 6% match, new Mac + $1,000 setup budget (then $500/yr), $1,000 per quarter AI experimentation & learning budget, and a team administrative assistant.
