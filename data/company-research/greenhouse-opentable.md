# OpenTable — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:opentable | company_type: product
What they do: OpenTable runs the online restaurant-reservation network — in its own words, "With millions of diners and tens of thousands of restaurant partners around the world, OpenTable is an industry leader in online reservations. Our technology helps restaurants focus on what matters most – their guests, their teams, and their business – while giving diners a reliable, delightful way to find the perfect restaurant for any occasion." Two-sided marketplace: a consumer booking product plus restaurant-side inventory, policy and table-management software.
How they describe themselves: "We believe hospitality is about taking care of others, and that ethos shapes both our products and how we work together." On engineering culture: "Foster a healthy, inclusive engineering culture: encourage thoughtful debate, blameless retros, and data-driven decision-making", alongside "Make pragmatic trade-offs between delivery speed, technical quality, operational risk, and cost". Delivery expectations are stated plainly — "Provide reliable estimates and hold yourself accountable to delivery commitments" and "Demonstrated bias for action". Inclusion statement: "We're committed to creating a workplace where everyone feels they belong and can thrive."
Size / stage / funding: Large and stable — part of Booking Holdings, sharing a global team with sister brand KAYAK: "contributing to a global team that includes our sister brand KAYAK and its portfolio of travel products." Publicly-owned parent, so no runway risk. Exact Toronto headcount not stated.
Locations / HQ: Global; the relevant roles are all in the **downtown Toronto, Canada office**. (San Francisco HQ historically, not verified here.)
Remote policy: **Hybrid, 2 days a week in office**, stated verbatim at the top of the Staff JD: "This is a hybrid schedule (2x/week) role planned for our downtown Toronto, Canada office." And in the Senior JD: "OpenTable has adopted a hybrid workplace model. This role aligns with that approach, with an expectation of coming into the office two days a week — giving employees the best of both worlds: in-person collaboration and flexibility." Tagged `#LI-Hybrid`. Partial mitigation only: "Work from (almost) anywhere for up to 20 days per year."
Remote-Canada eligibility: **NO for fully-remote.** These are Toronto-anchored hybrid roles requiring two days a week in the downtown Toronto office — not workable from Kimberley, BC without relocation, and Toronto is Eastern Time. Canadian employment itself is not an issue; the office-attendance requirement is.
Engineering & tech: The Diner Core Availability team is a genuinely infrastructural group — it "builds and operates the systems that power availability search for the core diner experience", serving "several thousands of searches per second… under strict low-latency and high-reliability requirements", and owns "the end-to-end lifecycle of availability queries: from ingesting upstream changes (inventory, policies, events) through event-driven pipelines, to maintaining highly-optimized in-memory and distributed cache layers, to responding to real-time search requests." Stack: **C#/.NET**, Redis and multi-layer in-memory/distributed caching, RESTful APIs, Kafka-based event-driven architecture, SQL/NoSQL at high read/write volume, Kubernetes deployments, service meshes, modern CI/CD, plus observability and performance tuning (profiling, capacity planning, latency/throughput trade-offs). Distributed-systems fundamentals are called out explicitly: "consistency models, failure modes, circuit breaking, timeouts, retry/backoff strategies". AI-assisted development is encouraged "in a safe and responsible way" for design exploration, code generation, test scaffolding and incident analysis. Staff expectations include design docs, cross-team forums and setting review standards.
Notable / other: Strong benefits — company-paid SpringHealth therapy sessions and Headspace, a company-wide week off each year ("the whole team fully recharges (and returns without a pile-up of work!)"), 20 days PTO on start, birthday day off, paid volunteer time, paid parental leave, private health and dental, life and disability insurance, development dollars, travel discounts, employee resource groups. Roles are "eligible to be considered for an annual bonus and equity grant". No salary range is posted on the Toronto JDs (Ontario does not compel one).
Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer (Availability), Toronto; Senior Software Engineer (C# & .Net), Toronto; Senior Engineer – Reservation, Toronto; Software Engineer II (Backend C# & .Net), Toronto.
