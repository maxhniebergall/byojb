# People Data Labs — research
Provider/key: ashby:people-data-labs | company_type: product

What they do: Data-as-a-service provider of people and company data. In their words: "People Data Labs (PDL) is the provider of people and company data. We do the heavy lifting of data collection and standardization so our customers can focus on building and scaling innovative, compliant data solutions. Our sole focus is on building the best data available by integrating thousands of compliantly sourced datasets into a single, developer-friendly source of truth." Customers use the workforce data "to enrich recruiting platforms, power AI models, create custom audiences, and more." The business is fundamentally a large-scale web-crawling, ingestion, entity-resolution and dataset-publishing operation sold via API.

How they describe themselves: "We are looking for individuals who can balance extreme ownership with a 'one-team, one-dream' mindset." "We like our engineers to be thoughtful, quirky, and willing to fearlessly try new things. Failure is embraced at PDL as long as we continue to learn and grow from it." Autonomy is emphasized: "a high level of autonomy and opportunity for direct contributions." Working norms are explicitly written/async: people thrive there who "work independently in a fast-paced, remote-first environment, proactively unblocking themselves and collaborating asynchronously," "communicate clearly and thoughtfully in writing (Slack, docs, design proposals)," and "write and maintain technical design documents, including pipeline design, schema design, and data flow diagrams." Also "balance pragmatism with craftsmanship, shipping reliable systems while continuously improving them."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed; stage and headcount are NOT stated on the posting and were not verified this session (the careers and about pages returned 404 / empty shells to a plain fetch) — unverified. Benefits list includes "Stock," so equity is part of comp.

Also "Unlimited paid time off," health/dental/vision, and health, fitness and office stipends. Asynchronous written collaboration is an explicit expectation, not an afterthought.

Engineering & tech: Python, Go, Rust ("or similar") with heavy concurrency — "async / await, coroutines, or concurrency frameworks." Large-scale distributed data acquisition: web crawling technologies, distributed job orchestration, data pipelines, large-scale asynchronous workloads, backpressure and message-driven design. Message queues and orchestration (Kafka, SQS, Airflow). Cloud: AWS preferred, GCP/Azure acceptable. Data platform: Apache Spark/Databricks, streaming (Kafka, Pub/Sub, Spark Streaming), warehousing (Snowflake, Redshift, BigQuery), modern storage patterns (parquet, Delta Lake, partitioning, incremental updates, backfills). Deep web-protocol knowledge expected: browser rendering pipeline, auth/cookies/HTTP, DNS, proxies, packet capture. Linux/Unix fluency. Data-quality monitoring across releases is a first-class responsibility. Team structure: a "Data Engineering & Acquisition Team" with mentorship from experienced teammates on the crawling domain.

Notable / other: They call out candidate-fraud defenses up front: "with the rise of fake applicants and AI-enabled candidate fraud, we have built in additional measures throughout the process to identify such candidates." A "Nice to have" is "Experience as a Red Teamer" — the acquisition side is adversarial (anti-bot evasion). Compliance is a stated part of the product positioning ("compliantly sourced datasets"), which matters in a category that attracts privacy scrutiny.

Comp (first-party): the Senior Software Engineer, Data Acquisition posting states "Comp: $160K - $200K" (USD; base, no other component named), plus stock, unlimited PTO, medical/dental/vision, and health/fitness/office stipends.
