# EverCommerce — research
Provider/key: workday:evercommerce/evercommerce_careers | company_type: product

What they do: EverCommerce is a service-commerce software group — a multi-brand operator of vertical
SaaS products for small and medium service businesses. It runs three main segments: **EverPro** (home
and field services — contractors, HVAC, landscaping, remodelling), **EverHealth** (health services —
practice management, EHR, patient engagement), and **EverWell** (wellness/fitness). Products are
delivered as a portfolio of acquired and integrated brands, with shared payments, marketing-automation
and customer-engagement infrastructure underneath.

How they describe themselves: The JD language is plain and operational rather than promotional. On the
team: "The EverCommerce team is distributed globally, with teams in the U.S., Canada, the U.K., Jordan,
New Zealand, and Australia. With a widely distributed team, we are used to working remotely across
different time zones." On working model, the benefits block states: "Flexibility to work where/how you
want within your country of employment – in-office, remote, or hybrid". On pay: "EverCommerce is
committed to equal pay and transparency." Extracted culture is "balanced" across the roles reviewed.
Notably absent: any "fast-paced", "rocketship", "hustle" or "wear many hats" vocabulary.

Size / stage / funding: **Public company** (NASDAQ: EVCM), IPO'd 2021, roughly 2,000 employees, revenue
in the several-hundred-million range. The registry's extracted facets record company_stage: public on
both roles where they were extracted. Formerly private-equity-backed (Providence Equity, Silver Lake);
now a listed, acquisition-driven roll-up with audited financials. Stability profile is that of a mature
public SaaS operator rather than a venture-stage startup.

Locations / HQ: Corporate headquarters in **Denver, Colorado**. Distributed teams across the U.S.,
Canada, the U.K., Jordan, New Zealand and Australia. Multiple office locations across North America.

Remote policy: **Genuinely distributed and remote-friendly, per country of employment.** Verbatim from
the EverPro Staff Engineer JD: "With a widely distributed team, we are used to working remotely across
different time zones. This role can be based anywhere in Canada – if you are close to one of our
offices, we can set you up in-office or you can work 100% remotely." The benefits block reinforces it:
"Flexibility to work where/how you want within your country of employment – in-office, remote, or
hybrid." The one caveat stated: "this role may require travel to our Corporate Headquarters in Denver,
Colorado, or to other office locations around North America."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes — verified and unusually explicit.** The JD states in its own words
that the role "can be based anywhere in Canada" and that a candidate not near an office "can work 100%
remotely". Two live postings are tagged "Remote (Canada)" (EverPro - Staff Engineer, and EverPro -
Software Engineering Operations Specialist (Remote, CAN)), and the salary on the Staff Engineer req is
quoted in CAD — confirming a Canadian employment entity and Canadian payroll, not a contractor
arrangement. Kimberley, BC qualifies without qualification. Requirement: "you must be eligible to work
without sponsorship" — satisfied by Canadian citizenship. Timezone: HQ is Denver (Mountain Time), an
exact match for Kimberley — the best timezone alignment of any company in this batch. Caveat: occasional
travel to Denver or other North American offices may be required.

Engineering & tech: Distributed multi-brand SaaS engineering. Live and recent roles span a Staff Engineer
(EverPro), Staff Software Engineer (EverCommerce shared), Senior Data Engineer, Senior Cloud Engineer,
a Software Engineering Operations Specialist (posted in both Canadian and US variants), and product
engineering in EverHealth/EverWell. The presence of dedicated Cloud Engineer, Data Engineer and
Engineering-Operations roles indicates a real shared platform layer beneath the brand portfolio — the
integration work of unifying acquired products onto common payments, data and infrastructure is the
central technical problem of a roll-up. Extracted autonomy: medium.

Notable / other: Pay transparency is practised across jurisdictions — Colorado is a transparency state
and EverCommerce posts ranges on both Canadian and US reqs. Posted figures from their own JDs:
- EverPro - Staff Engineer (Remote, Canada): **CAD $145,000 – $190,000** base per year
- EverCommerce - Staff Software Engineer (Remote, US): USD $150,000 – $170,000 base
- EverHealth - Software Engineer (Remote, US): USD $110,000 – $130,000 base
The Canadian Staff Engineer band sits squarely inside the profile's CAD $160–200K target at its
midpoint. Note the JDs append a boilerplate caveat that the range "combines all distinct labor markets
in the US" even on the CAD-denominated Canadian req — a copy-paste artefact, not a substantive
qualification.

Open relevant roles (sample): EverPro - Staff Engineer (Remote, Canada); EverCommerce - Staff Software
Engineer (Remote, US); EverCommerce - Senior Data Engineer; EverCommerce - Senior Cloud Engineer;
EverPro - Software Engineering Operations Specialist (Remote, CAN).
