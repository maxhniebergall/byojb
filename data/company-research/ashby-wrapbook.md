# Wrapbook — research
Provider/key: ashby:wrapbook | company_type: product

What they do: Wrapbook is a vertical fintech / "system of action" for **production finance** in the
entertainment industry — payroll, spend management, accounting, onboarding and insurance for the
cast and crew of feature films, TV and commercials. It handles the unusually messy problem of
project-based, freelance, union-governed workforces (SAG-AFTRA, DGA, IATSE, Teamsters) and
cross-border US/Canada payroll. Customers include teams at Netflix, Paramount and Anonymous
Content. The company now markets itself as "the AI platform for production finance", aiming to put
finance teams in control of payroll, spend and accounting in one place.

How they describe themselves: mission is to "increase the prosperity of the project economy" by
letting companies seamlessly onboard, pay and insure their workforces. The careers page lists five
principles: **"Win as one"**, **"Start with the customer"**, **"Say what makes us better"**, **"Act
and use good judgement"**, **"Get sharper every day."** Longer-form versions of the same values
appear elsewhere as "Succeed together. Break silos. No solo victories.", "The customer is always at
the head of the table.", "Make the call, own the outcome.", "Stay curious. Try things. Learn out
loud." They add that "judgment is never treated as a bottleneck" — engineers are trusted to
understand complex problems deeply and decide. Tone is customer-first and ownership-heavy;
"fast-growing technology company" is used explicitly, and the data-platform JD asks for people who
have built platforms "in fast-growing or early-stage environments" and are "comfortable with
ambiguity".

Size / stage / funding: 350+ employees ("Wrapbookers"). Private, venture-backed and reasonably
late-stage — investors include Andreessen Horowitz, Bessemer Venture Partners and Jeffrey
Katzenberg's WndrCo. Growing rather than contracting.

Locations / HQ: no meaningful HQ — staff span 42+ US states and Canadian provinces. Founded with
strong Canadian roots and hires across both countries.

Remote policy: **100% remote / fully distributed**, hiring throughout Canada and the United States.
Benefits explicitly include "Work from anywhere in Canada and USA", up to $1,500 USD / $2,025 CAD
toward home IT setup, up to $50 USD / $67.50 CAD per month toward internet/cell, unlimited PTO
(the careers page states a minimum of 3 weeks), health/vision/dental, RRSP/401(k) matching up to 2%,
stock options, paid parental leave, and "competitive, above-market salaries". Roles are tagged
`#LI-Remote`.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes — this is a Canada-first posting.** The live "Senior Data
Platform Engineer II" requisition is titled "Remote - Canada Only" and its own compensation block
includes "Zone C: All Locations in Canada" at CA$174K–CA$237.1K. "All locations in Canada" means no
province restriction, so British Columbia is in scope. Timezone expectations are not stated; the
team spans North America, so Mountain Time is unproblematic. The role does carry an on-call rotation
for production data systems.

Engineering & tech: The data-platform role is explicitly **~80% platform engineering (infrastructure,
tooling, automation) and 20% data engineering (pipelines and transformations)** — green-field
ownership of a unified data platform, data contracts, SLAs, observability/telemetry, and platform
roadmap, partnering with Product Engineering, Analytics, Compliance and Security. Stack named in the
JD: Python (preferred) plus Java or Scala; PostgreSQL and relational modelling; Dagster, Airbyte,
Airflow, dbt; Terraform or Ansible for infrastructure automation; AWS. The company is aggressively
AI-assisted internally — the JD requires "an AI-centric workflow… you embrace AI coding assistants
(such as Claude Code) as essential tools", and the careers page names Claude, Cursor and Dust as
standard tooling. The interview process itself includes AI-assisted technical exercises meant to
mirror real work.

Compensation transparency: Wrapbook posts geographically zoned base ranges on its own JDs —
Zone A (San Francisco, Los Angeles, Seattle, New York City, Boston, San Diego, Washington DC),
Zone B (all other US locations), and Zone C (all locations in Canada). For Senior Data Platform
Engineer II these are $196K–$267.1K USD, $174K–$237.1K USD and CA$174K–CA$237.1K respectively.
Notably Canada is not discounted relative to non-hub US pay — the CAD figure equals the Zone B USD
figure numerically, which is a meaningful nominal-CAD win.

Notable / other: The domain (union payroll, tax, insurance, compliance for film production) is
genuinely non-trivial regulated plumbing rather than a thin app, but the company positions itself
firmly inside the AI narrative. It pledges a harassment- and discrimination-free workplace across a
broad list of protected characteristics. Team includes entertainment-industry specialists alongside
engineers from Amazon, Microsoft and Facebook.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Data Platform Engineer II (Remote - Canada Only).
