# Fidelity (FMR LLC) — research
Provider/key: workday:fmr/fidelitycareers | company_type: product
What they do: Fidelity Investments is one of the largest financial-services firms in the world — brokerage, asset management, retirement/401(k) recordkeeping, clearing and custody, plus a digital-assets arm. Technology is a very large internal function: the live postings span contact-centre digital engagement services, a data & execution platform, digital assets/DeFi, Python and Rust backend services, Oracle/PL-SQL data engineering, observability, and site reliability engineering. Engineering here is internal-platform work serving Fidelity's own businesses.
How they describe themselves: No values or culture page was fetched this session. The single culture-relevant statement carried in every JD is the working-model notice, quoted verbatim below under Remote policy.
Size / stage / funding: Private (privately held by the Johnson family and employees), decades old, very large (tens of thousands of employees), profitable and not funding-dependent. Extracted facets in the registry classify it as stage "public"-equivalent i.e. established/mature — note that is an extraction artefact; Fidelity is privately held.
Locations / HQ: Boston, MA is the historic HQ. Posted engineering locations across the live roles: Boston MA, Merrimack NH, Durham NC, Westlake TX, Jersey City NJ, Smithfield RI. All US.
Remote policy: ONSITE, and actively tightening. Every live JD carries this notice verbatim: "Fidelity's Onsite Working Model — Fidelity is transitioning to a full-time onsite working model through a phased rollout across regions and roles. Currently, some roles and locations require 100% onsite presence, while others require less. Onsite expectations are likely to evolve as the rollout continues. This transition does not apply to fully remote roles." None of the live engineering postings in the registry are flagged fully remote; all name one or more physical US offices ("3 Locations", "2 Locations", "Boston, MA", etc.).
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO. Every posting is US-office-based, extracted geo eligibility is "us_only", and the company is explicitly moving to full-time onsite. Fidelity does have a Canadian asset-management arm, but nothing in the registry or in these postings offers a Canada-remote engineering role. Effectively disqualifying.
Engineering & tech: Rust, Python, Java, AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, Oracle/PL-SQL, observability tooling, cloud platform and data-execution platform engineering, React on the front end for the digital-assets group. Ladder uses Senior / Lead / Principal titling.
Notable / other: Compensation is published as broad, low-specificity bands — one Principal-level posting carries a $107,000-$216,000 USD range, which spans so wide it conveys little; a Senior Software Engineer/Developer posting sits around $136.5-141k USD. That is materially below the market rates seen elsewhere in this batch for equivalent titles, though it excludes bonus.
Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Software Engineer, Contact Center Digital Engagement Services; Lead Cloud Platform Engineer (Data & Execution Platform); Principal Python Backend Engineer; Senior Data Engineer - PL/SQL; Senior Software Engineer/Developer; Platform Engineer.
Sources: eight live Fidelity JDs captured in the registry under data/posting-research/, e.g. https://fmr.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/fidelitycareers/job/Merrimack-NH/Lead-Cloud-Platform-Engineer--Data---Execution-Platform-_2132046 . No fidelity.com page fetched this session.
