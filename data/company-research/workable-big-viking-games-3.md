# Big Viking Games — research
Provider/key: workable:big-viking-games-3 | company_type: product

What they do: Canadian live-service game studio. In their own words: "a profitable, self-funded live-service game studio based in Toronto. Since 2011, we've been building and operating virtual worlds with deep player-driven economies and long-term engagement at their core." Flagship titles are **YoWorld** and **FishWorld**, enduring social virtual worlds that "have served millions of players over their lifetime," sustained by live operations, evolving content systems and in-game economies of virtual goods.

How they describe themselves: Focused on "building, operating, and growing long-standing online game communities." Currently framing a turn: "We are entering a new phase of modernization and growth, with a focus on stronger engineering discipline, better tooling, practical AI adoption, faster iteration, and scalable systems that help our games and teams perform at a higher level." Engineers are expected to "use modern AI tools responsibly to accelerate development without compromising quality."

Size / stage / funding: **Profitable and self-funded** (their words), founded 2011 — no venture runway risk. Headcount not stated; the small number of open roles and the single-studio footprint suggest a small-to-mid studio. Unverified.

Locations / HQ: Toronto, Ontario, Canada — single studio.

Remote policy: **Hybrid, three days per week in the Toronto office.** Stated verbatim on all three relevant JDs: "This is a hybrid role based in Toronto, with an expectation of working in office three days per week" / "This is a hybrid role with three (3) days in office." The DevOps role adds that "live-service games require operational awareness outside regular business hours, including periodic on-call and incident response availability."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Canadian employer, but NOT remote-eligible.** This is the one company in the batch that can employ a Canadian — and it still doesn't work: the role requires three days a week physically in a Toronto office, which is unreachable from Kimberley, BC without relocation. Effectively disqualifying.

Engineering & tech: Unity / game development, gameplay and UI systems, live-operations systems, backend integrations and internal tooling; networked and online systems including APIs, real-time systems, client-server interaction and multiplayer/social features; performance, memory, network and reliability optimization; production debugging on large, long-lived codebases. A separate DevOps role covers cloud infrastructure for live games with on-call. Work is cross-functional with design, art, QA, product and live ops.

Notable / other: Benefits include health/dental/vision with an HSA/WSA spending account from day one. Postings are dated June–July 2026.
