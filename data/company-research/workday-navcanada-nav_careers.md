Entity check: registry display name is "Navcanada" (the Workday tenant slug). The entity is **NAV CANADA**, Canada's civil air navigation service provider.

What they do: NAV CANADA owns and operates Canada's civil air navigation system (ANS) — air traffic control, flight information, weather briefings, aeronautical information, airport advisory services and electronic aids to navigation across Canadian domestic airspace and a large portion of the North Atlantic. It is a private, non-share-capital, not-for-profit corporation created in 1996 when the federal government transferred the ANS out of Transport Canada; it is funded by charges to aircraft operators rather than by taxes or shareholders. It also commercializes air-traffic technology internationally (e.g. its space-based ADS-B stake via Aireon).

The tone throughout is institutional and safety-oriented, with no growth, urgency or disruption language of any kind.

Size / stage / funding: Roughly 5,000-6,000 employees. Not-for-profit corporation with a statutory monopoly on Canadian civil air navigation — revenue is rate-regulated service charges. About as structurally stable an employer as exists in Canada.
