# Tenable, Inc. — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:tenableinc | company_type: product
What they do: Tenable is a cybersecurity company in the exposure-management category — its platform helps organisations "find, prioritize and eliminate exposures across your attack surface". Originally known for Nessus vulnerability scanning, it now spans cloud security, identity exposure and FedRAMP-authorised government offerings. Over 40,000 organisations use it, including 65% of the Fortune 500, 50% of the Global 2000, and large government agencies.
How they describe themselves: Core belief, from their about page: "every business has the right to pursue its ambitions free from security worry." Stated values emphasise unified security solutions, delivering measurable results, caring for employees, and impactful work. Their JD boilerplate asks and answers "What makes Tenable such a great place to work?" with "Our people!" and describes "a culture of belonging, respect, and excellence where we can be our best selves… #OneTenable… We deliver results that exceed expectations and we win together!" — team/mission language rather than hustle language; no "fast-paced"/"scrappy"/"wear many hats" phrasing appears in the JDs read.
Size / stage / funding: Public company (NASDAQ, IPO 2018), founded 2002, operating in 40 countries. Headcount not stated on the about page; benefits (401(k) match, employee stock purchase plan, education assistance) are those of an established public employer.
Locations / HQ: Headquarters in Columbia, Maryland (postings read "US - Headquarters - Maryland - Columbia"). Other posted locations include a London UK office and US remote states (Massachusetts, Virginia, DC, California Bay Area). Their CTO serves as managing director in Israel, so there is a substantial Israeli engineering presence.
Remote policy: Genuinely remote-friendly for engineering. Live postings include "US - Remote" (unqualified), and several list multiple named remote states alongside the Maryland HQ. One JD is headed "*Remote Position".
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO evidence of it, and the posted geography says otherwise. Every live engineering posting scopes remote to named US states or plain "US - Remote"; the one non-US role is "UK - Office - London" (office-based). Pay ranges are published under a "US Pay Range" heading. Tenable operates in 40 countries so a Canadian entity may well exist, but nothing first-party confirms Canada-remote hiring — treat as NOT verified and likely US-scoped for these roles.
Engineering & tech: Cloud-native distributed systems for security products — cloud-based search over customer infrastructure, highly scalable microservices, Java/Kotlin distributed systems, cloud security, SRE work on a FedRAMP-authorised environment, and an Applied AI group (a Senior Localization Engineer, Applied AI role sits in it). Engineers are expected to contribute to system design/architecture, own scalability/reliability/security of global cloud infrastructure, and mentor juniors.
Notable / other: Benefits listed in JDs: medical, dental, vision, disability and life insurance; 401(k) with company match; employee stock purchase plan; referral programme; FSAs; EAP; education assistance; parental leave; PTO; company-paid holidays; wellness events and community programmes. Variable comp (bonus for non-sales roles) on top of base. Formal applicant privacy notices for General/California/EU-EEA-UK — no Canadian notice, a small extra hint that Canada isn't a standard hiring geo.
Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer (US - Remote, $188,000-$282,000); Staff Software Engineer - Java/Kotlin, Distributed Systems; Senior Software Engineer - Cloud Security; Software/Site Reliability Engineer - FedRAMP; Senior Localization Engineer, Applied AI (London office).
