# Borrowell — research
Provider/key: workable:borrowell | company_type: product

What they do: Borrowell is a Canadian consumer fintech offering free Equifax credit scores and weekly credit-report monitoring, credit-building products (Rent Advantage, Credit Builder), and a marketplace that recommends financial products from "over 75 different companies" — which is how it makes money: "Yes, it's really free. We make money by analyzing your credit profile to suggest the best financial products for you." It reports "over four million members" and positions itself as "Canada's leading credit education company".

How they describe themselves: "At Borrowell, we're on a mission to help Canadians feel confident about their money… We empower individuals to take control of their financial futures." Culture language from its own JDs: "Our team is diverse, inclusive, and driven by a shared passion for making a meaningful difference in the lives of Canadians. We pride ourselves on fostering a culture of collaboration, humility, and innovation." The engineering team publishes an explicit 10-point values list in the JD: "We work well as a team and collaborate always"; "We are humble and open to each others' ideas"; "We are not afraid of making mistakes and we are always learning"; "We are persistent about solving problems"; "Change is good and welcomed"; "We are always willing to take on new challenges"; "We are not afraid to say, 'I don't know,' or, 'I need help'"; "We take ownership over our platform and the work that we deliver"; "We communicate clearly"; "We find the simplest solutions possible for the hardest problems." Hiring is deliberately lean: "we don't think you need to jump through 8 hoops to land the role" — recruiter call, then a "practical, realistic, and open book" coding challenge.

Size / stage / funding: Growth-stage private Canadian fintech, 4M+ members. Headcount, funding and profitability are not disclosed on the site.

Locations / HQ: Toronto, Ontario, Canada ("proudly Canadian").

Remote policy: Remote-first across Canada. Workable workplace field is "remote"; extracted signals include "remote-first across Canada", "flexible working hours" and a "flexible vacation policy" — with the caveat that the role "requires attending in-person meetings and team-building events".

Remote-Canada eligibility: YES, and British Columbia is named explicitly. Both live postings are "Toronto, Canada (Remote)" and the extracted location hints on the Sr. DevOps Engineer JD are "Toronto, Ontario, British Columbia, Alberta, Canada (Remote)" — i.e. they hire remotely in BC. Caveat: periodic in-person meetings/team events are expected, meaning travel to Toronto.

Engineering & tech: Microsoft Azure is the platform of record. DevOps side: Terraform IaC with reusable modules, Azure DevOps pipelines (also GitHub Actions/GitLab experience accepted), Docker/Kubernetes, Linux, Bash, WAF/VNets/DNS, microservices, security practice. Application side: .NET 10 on Azure for backend; React, React Native and TypeScript in a Turborepo monorepo for frontend; SQL and NoSQL; Auth0 for identity. Engineering is organized into specialized pods — Frontend Platform, Backend Platform ("core business systems, security, and scalability… microservices architecture, event-driven systems, and critical infrastructure projects like identity management"), and embedded Product Development Pods; the general "Software Developer" posting funnels candidates into one of them after the coding challenge.

Notable / other: Pay is stated on the postings — Software Developer CAD 80,000–130,000, Sr. DevOps Engineer CAD 100,000–150,000. The Software Developer posting warns "A large chunk of your time will be spent experimenting and building new products from scratch rather than just maintaining old code", and lists "Experience in fast-paced Fintech or startup environments" as a nice-to-have.

Open relevant roles (sample): Sr. DevOps Engineer; Software Developer (general posting across pods).
