# Toast — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:toast | company_type: product

What they do: Toast is a restaurant technology platform — an all-in-one point-of-sale and management system (front-of-house, back-of-house, back-office, payments, payroll, guest-facing ordering, and hardware such as card readers and kitchen displays) built specifically for restaurants. Launched in 2013, HQ in Boston. Customers are restaurants of all sizes, from single locations to enterprise chains and, increasingly, retail/grocery. Publicly traded (NYSE: TOST); revenue is heavily payments-processing driven.

How they describe themselves: Careers site describes the culture as "driven, collaborative, inclusive, and fun," organized around empowering the restaurant community ("we empower the restaurant community to delight guests, do what they love, and thrive"). Stated cultural pillars on their FAQ page: deep industry expertise (they say nearly 75% of employees have restaurant-industry experience), diversity and inclusion ("building teams that reflect our diverse customer base"), internal growth (~20% of open roles filled internally), and social impact via Toast.org volunteering. Tone is mission/hospitality-flavored rather than infrastructure-flavored.

Size / stage / funding: 5,000+ employees globally ("Toasters"); public company, established and at scale (not runway-dependent). Large enough to have dedicated infrastructure, ML and platform orgs.

Locations / HQ: HQ Boston, MA. Operations in the US, Canada (Toronto), Ireland (Dublin), UK, India, Australia and Taiwan.

Remote policy: Officially a **hybrid** model — "We embrace a hybrid work model that fosters in-person collaboration while valuing individual needs"; specific arrangements vary by location and role. In practice a large share of their engineering postings are explicitly listed as "Remote, US" / "Remote, Canada," alongside Boston, MA and Toronto, ON office-based roles.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes, verified.** Multiple live engineering roles are posted as "Remote, Canada" with CAD pay ranges attached, e.g. Senior Software Engineer, Enterprise (gh_jid=7587088, CAD $115,000–$165,000), Staff Software Engineer, Tech Lead (gh_jid=7895604, CAD $142,000–$227,000), Staff Software Engineer (gh_jid=7818293, CAD $175,000–$220,000), Staff Software Engineer, Employee Lifecycle Management (gh_jid=7747181). Note that some Canada roles are Toronto-office ("Toronto, ON" / "Toronto, Canada": Senior SWE Care Experience, Senior SWE Scheduling, SWE Toast IQ) — those are not remote. The remote-Canada roles are nationwide-remote in wording, so a BC/Mountain-Time candidate should be eligible, but the org's centre of gravity is Boston/Eastern time.

Engineering & tech: Large multi-team eng org spanning application infrastructure, cloud network engineering, platform, ML/AI (a dedicated "AI Pod" and "AI Foundations" group, Voice AI Platform, Support AI, Toast IQ analytics), payments/orders APIs, hardware/firmware (Card Readers team), and Salesforce DevOps. Ladder runs Software Engineer → Senior → Staff → Principal → Senior Principal, so there is genuine depth of individual-contributor progression. Java/Kotlin-heavy backend with AWS, plus embedded/Android work on the hardware side.

Notable / other: Pay transparency is applied consistently — nearly every US JD carries zone-based ranges (Zone A/B/C) and Canadian JDs carry CAD ranges, which is unusually good disclosure. They publish an explicit statement about how AI is used in their hiring process. Very high volume of open senior/staff/principal engineering roles (12 relevant live in this registry alone), including several Dublin duplicates of US postings.

Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Infrastructure Engineer; Staff Software Engineer - Application Infrastructure; Staff Software Engineer - Cloud Network Engineering; Staff Software Engineer, Backend; Senior Software Engineer, Enterprise (Remote, Canada); Staff Software Engineer, Tech Lead (Remote, Canada); Principal Machine Learning Engineer; Senior Software Engineer, Voice AI Platform.
