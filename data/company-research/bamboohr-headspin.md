# HeadSpin (PartnerOne portfolio) — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:headspin | company_type: product

**⚠ Entity mismatch — read first.** The registry company is **HeadSpin**, but the live posting on HeadSpin's BambooHR board opens with: *"This position is with **Cincom Systems, a subsidiary of PartnerOne**, and will be on the HeadSpin payroll."* So the hiring entity, the product and the engineering org are **Cincom Systems**, not HeadSpin — HeadSpin is only the payroll vehicle. Both are portfolio companies of **PartnerOne**, a private-equity acquirer of enterprise software companies, which is evidently running a shared hiring board. Anyone evaluating this posting is evaluating Cincom/PartnerOne, not the HeadSpin mobile-testing business.

What they do:
- **HeadSpin** (the registry entity, the payroll entity): a mobile/device testing platform — *"real-world testing with SIM-enabled mobile devices, browsers, OTT media devices, Smart TVs, and more,"* letting developers test apps, detect issues and evaluate performance, deployed in cloud and on-premises across **50+ global locations**.
- **Cincom Eloquence** (what the job is actually on): described in the JD as *"a mature, enterprise-grade Customer Communications Management (CCM) platform trusted by organisations in insurance, banking, financial services, and healthcare to deliver high-volume, regulated, personalised communications across print, email, SMS, web, and mobile channels."* It combines *"a powerful document authoring environment, a sophisticated rules and conditional logic engine, batch and on-demand document generation, and omnichannel delivery — all within a compliance-ready, audit-trail-enabled architecture."*
- **PartnerOne**: a PE firm that acquires and operates enterprise software companies. (Commonly reported as Montreal-headquartered — **unverified** from first-party sources in this pass.)

How they describe themselves: There is no values or mission statement on any page reached — the HeadSpin homepage is pure product marketing and the about-us path 404s. The JD's own voice is, however, unusually specific and appealing for this kind of role: *"If you are a Java developer, with a Data & Performance focus, who likes to go beyond REST endpoints and DTOs, and you enjoy understanding how data really flows through a system, this role is for you."* It lists what the platform values as *"Databases matter / SQL matters / Performance matters / Design decisions have visible, real-world impact,"* and asks for someone who likes *"understanding existing systems instead of rewriting everything"* and *"enjoys technical challenges that require thinking, not just implementing tickets."* Crucially, it states the role's origin: *"This role exists following the retirement of a long-standing engineer with deep system knowledge. We are not looking for a like-for-like replacement, but for a sharp, curious developer who enjoys understanding complex systems and making them better."*

Size / stage / funding: PE-owned rather than venture-funded — no runway risk in the startup sense, but PE rollups of mature software assets typically optimise for cost and cash extraction, with lean teams and limited investment in new development. Headcount for either entity is **unverified**; nothing was recoverable from the pages reached.

Locations / HQ: **Not stated anywhere reachable — unverified.** HeadSpin is commonly associated with Palo Alto/Mountain View, California and Cincom with Cincinnati, Ohio, but neither was confirmed first-party here. Notably, **the posting carries no location field at all** (the registry location string is empty for all four HeadSpin postings).

Remote policy: No policy statement found. The only signal is in the JD's requirements: candidates should be *"comfortable working remotely with international colleagues"* — which implies a distributed, cross-border team, but does not state where the company will employ.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear — genuinely unverified.** Points in favour: no location is specified on the posting at all, remote work with international colleagues is an explicit expectation, and PartnerOne is (per common reporting, unverified) a Canadian firm. Points against: nothing anywhere states Canada eligibility, and "will be on the HeadSpin payroll" implies employment through a specific entity whose jurisdictions are unknown. This one needs a direct question to the recruiter rather than an assumption either way.

Engineering & tech: Java backend with a deliberate **database-first** slant — PostgreSQL and/or SQL Server (Oracle a plus), schema design and analysis for long-lived applications, SQL query optimisation, execution plans, indexes, locking and transactions, and investigating real production performance problems. Application side is the **Spring** ecosystem with **Hibernate/JPA**, with explicit judgement expected about *"when ORM is helpful and when SQL is the better tool."* Domain context is CCM (Customer Communications Management) in regulated industries. Experience bar is stated as **4–5 years**, and the role reports to the *"Head of Eloquence Product Engineering, Support & Services"* — a combined engineering-plus-support org, which usually implies some customer-escalation load.

Notable / other: Four near-identical "Java Backend Developer" postings (IDs 281, 283, 284) plus a "Data Engineer" (302) sit on this board with no locations, only one of which has a body captured — likely the same role duplicated per geography. No compensation is stated anywhere. The JD includes an anti-fraud disclaimer (*"HeadSpin does not charge a…"*), suggesting they have had recruitment-scam impersonation issues.
