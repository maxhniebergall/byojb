# Sanofi — research
Provider/key: workday:sanofi/sanoficareers | company_type: product (large pharmaceutical enterprise)
What they do: Sanofi is a global pharmaceutical company. Per its own JD copy it is "an R&D-driven, AI-powered biopharma company committed to improving people's lives and delivering compelling growth", with a focus on immunology-derived medicines and vaccines. The engineering postings sit in its internal digital organization — "Sanofi Accelerator's Global Function (GF)", which "work[s] at the intersection of technology and business, partnering closely with product, finance, procurement, and operations teams" to "streamline global operations, automate complex processes, and unlock efficiency across the enterprise", plus a Digital R&D arm building software for research workflows and data analysis.
How they describe themselves: "chase the miracles of science to improve people's lives" is the recurring purpose line. Digital ambition: "to be the leading digital healthcare platform to deliver medicine faster, enable business operations to scale seamlessly, and help Sanofi teams focus on what matters most — science and patients." JD value language emphasizes "scalability, maintainability, and reliability", "high standards in software engineering, testing, and documentation", and notably "Values unowned or less visible work that enables the team to move faster."
Size / stage / funding: Public multinational (Euronext/Nasdaq), ~90,000 employees globally, decades established — the opposite of runway-dependent.
Locations / HQ: HQ Paris, France. The engineering postings in the registry cluster in Toronto ON, Lyon and Paris (France), Barcelona (Spain), and Hyderabad (India).
Remote policy: Hybrid. The Toronto Software Engineer postings state "Location: Toronto, ON (Hybrid)" explicitly; the Data and AI Engineer (Toronto) was extracted as onsite; the Engineering Lead – AI Workflows (Toronto) as hybrid.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No, not for a fully-remote worker.** Every Canadian posting is Toronto-based and marked hybrid or onsite. There is no remote-anywhere-in-Canada option in any live posting; a Kimberley, BC candidate could not take these without relocating to Toronto.
Engineering & tech: Modern microservices and cloud-native applications; internal-platform and accelerator teams; AI/ML workflow engineering (Engineering Lead – AI Workflows, Data & AI Engineer), container platform engineering (Kubernetes/containers, Hyderabad), AWS cloud engineering (Barcelona), Windows platform engineering (Barcelona/Hyderabad). Work is internal-customer-facing (product, finance, procurement, operations, scientists) rather than sales-driven.
Compensation: The Engineering Lead – AI Workflows (Toronto) posting states a CAD range recorded in the registry as 144,100–240,200 CAD base. Other postings state no pay. European and Indian postings state none.
Notable / other: Large, stable, mission-credible, internal-platform work — a good archetype match on paper; the blocker is purely geographic. High posting volume (14 relevant live roles) makes it recur at the head of queues.
Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineer (Toronto, hybrid, x3); Engineering Lead – AI Workflows (Toronto); Data and AI Engineer (Toronto); Accelerator Senior Software Platform Engineer (Lyon); AWS Cloud Engineer (Barcelona); Container Platform Engineer (Hyderabad).
