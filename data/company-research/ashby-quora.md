# Quora — research
Provider/key: ashby:quora | company_type: product
What they do: Quora operates two platforms: Quora.com, a global Q&A / knowledge-sharing platform with over 300M monthly unique visitors, and Poe (poe.com), a consumer platform giving millions of users one place to chat with, explore and build on a wide range of AI models (Claude, GPT, image models and others). Revenue is advertising (Quora) plus subscriptions (Poe).
How they describe themselves: Mission is "to grow the world's collective intelligence." They describe "passionate, collaborative, and high-performing global teams" with "a culture rooted in transparency, idea-sharing, and experimentation that allows us to celebrate success and grow together through meaningful work." Engineering is described as "a small engineering team [that] works on challenging problems every day… rooted in constantly learning and improving," where "engineers are encouraged to think big and experiment with new ideas" and, as a remote-first company, "have a high degree of flexibility and autonomy."
Size / stage / funding: Privately held, long-established (founded 2009). Small engineering team relative to product reach; Poe is the growth vector. Not a fresh startup — no runway framing anywhere in its material.
Locations / HQ: Formerly Mountain View, CA; now effectively distributed. Direct employment entities in the United States, Canada (Quora Canada Operations Corp.) and Ireland, plus PEO employment in 13 further countries.
Remote policy: Explicitly "remote-first" (they link their own Remote-First-at-Quora page from every JD). "All of our positions can be performed remotely" from eligible countries. No stated timezone restriction on the eligibility page.
Remote-Canada eligibility: Verified yes. Canada is one of the three countries where Quora employs people directly, via Quora Canada Operations Corp.; the live ML postings list "Remote – Multiple Locations, United States, Ireland, Canada, & 13 other countries." No timezone constraint published, so Mountain Time appears workable.
Engineering & tech: Large-scale recommendation and ranking systems (feed, notifications, the Quora digest emails, A2A), plus ML applied across most of the product. Roles include "developing tools and abstractions that our other developers will build on top of" — i.e. ML platform work, not only model tuning. Poe brings multi-model LLM serving/integration work.
Notable / other: Poe integrates frontier models rapidly as they ship, so the AI work is real model-systems engineering rather than a thin prompt wrapper. Also has a Senior Infrastructure Security Software Engineer opening (US/Canada remote).
Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Machine Learning Engineer – Recommendations (Remote); Senior Machine Learning Engineer, Ranking (Remote); Senior Infrastructure Security Software Engineer (Remote).
