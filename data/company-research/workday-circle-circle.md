# Circle — research
Provider/key: workday:circle/circle | company_type: product
What they do: Circle (NYSE: CRCL) describes itself as "one of the world's leading internet financial platform companies, building the foundation of a more open, global economy through digital assets, payment applications, and programmable blockchain infrastructure." Its platform comprises USDC — "the world's largest regulated stablecoin network" — the Circle Payments Network for global money movement, and Arc, "an enterprise-grade blockchain designed to become the Economic OS for the internet." Customers are enterprises, financial institutions and developers.
How they describe themselves: Five stated values, quoted from their own pages: **High Integrity** ("open and honest communication", high ethical standards), **Future Forward** ("Agile, curious and adaptive"), **Multistakeholder** (balancing customers, shareholders, employees, communities), **Mindful** ("present and aware, respectful, active listeners"), and **Driven by Excellence** ("relentlessly pursue excellence, do not tolerate mediocrity"). They also say "Circle is committed to visibility and stability in everything we do… As we grow as an organization, we're expanding into some of the world's strongest jurisdictions. Speed and efficiency are motivators for our success," and "We have built a flexible work environment where new ideas are encouraged and everyone is a stakeholder."
Size / stage / funding: Publicly traded (NYSE: CRCL) — the most financially transparent and, in principle, most stable company in this batch. Headcount not stated on the careers page.
Locations / HQ: San Francisco and New York City are the named in-person hubs; postings list "San Francisco - remote first in US", one "Taipei City - remote first in Taiwan", and several tagged "12 Locations".
Remote policy: Remote-first — employees work from home with access to WeWork locations globally, flexible paid time off, plus annual conferences, local meetups and team offsites.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NOT ESTABLISHED, and the wording cuts against it. Every relevant posting is titled "remote first in US" (or in Taiwan for the Taipei req); the careers page references "U.S. employees" and "select countries" for benefits without listing them, and nowhere mentions Canada. Assume US-only for these reqs unless a recruiter says otherwise.
Engineering & tech: The data-platform roles are substantial infrastructure work: org-wide data quality standards, data contracts, SLAs and governance; reliability/observability frameworks with SLI/SLO models, lineage, monitoring and alerting; incident management (severity models, escalation, on-call, blameless postmortems); data-engineering SDLC with testing, CI/CD, versioning and reusable frameworks; build-vs-buy and architectural influence. Named tooling: Astronomer (Airflow), BigQuery, dbt, Dataplex, Kubernetes, Python, Go. They also explicitly want "experience leveraging AI tools and methodologies to design and implement the solutions."
Notable / other: Pay is posted on the JDs (a strength): Distinguished SWE $285-352.5k, Staff SWE $195-257.5k, Senior SWE $152.5-205k base. Domain is crypto/stablecoins — regulated, but cycle-exposed.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Staff Data Engineer; Staff Software Engineer, Data Platform; Staff Software Engineer; Senior Software Engineer; Distinguished Software Engineer.
