# Harris (Harris Computer) — research
Provider/key: workday:harriscomputer/1 | company_type: product

What they do: Harris Computer is a vertical-market software (VMS) group: it acquires, operates and
indefinitely holds mission-critical software businesses serving public sector, utilities, healthcare,
education, telecom/broadband and financial-services niches. It runs as a federation of largely
autonomous divisions/brands rather than a single product org (e.g. "MACC, a division of Harris" appears
verbatim in one of its own live JDs). Harris is widely known as one of the operating groups of
Constellation Software Inc. (TSX:CSU) — NOTE: unverified this session; harriscomputer.com returned
HTTP 403 to automated fetch, so this is background knowledge, not a fetched citation.

How they describe themselves: Not verifiable this session (corporate site blocked automated fetch).
The only first-party language available is from their own live JDs, which is notably un-hyped and
operational: "Design, develop, and maintain database solutions using MSSQL. Develop and maintain COBOL
application to support a production environment." One JD adds an "AI & Innovation Mindset" section:
"We are committed to leveraging emerging technologies to improve how we work, serve our customers, and
drive business outcomes. The successful candidate will demonstrate curiosity and a willingness to
actively adopt and leverage AI tools to improve workflows, solve problems, and increase efficiency...
while maintaining appropriate judgment, security, and compliance standards." Benefits language from the
same JD: "3 weeks' vacation and 5 personal days... Employee stock ownership and RRSP/401k matching
programs... Remote work and more!" No hustle/rocketship vocabulary anywhere in the JD corpus.

Size / stage / funding: Large and long-established; acquisitive holding-company model, cash-generative
rather than venture-funded. Exact headcount unverified this session.

Locations / HQ: Ottawa, Ontario, Canada (unverified this session). Registry job locations span Canada
(British Columbia; "Office - Blair"), the United States (Florida, Utah, Alabama, plus multi-site
postings listing "51 Locations"/"52 Locations"), the UK, and India (Pune-Baroda, Bangalore-Baroda).

Remote policy: Remote-heavy and geographically distributed. Titles and locations are explicitly
remote-labelled ("(Remote) Software Engineer", "Remote - British Columbia", "Remote - UK",
"Remote - India"). Benefits list ends with "Remote work and more!". No stated async/handbook culture
was found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: VERIFIED YES. Harris posts a "Senior Software Engineer" at
"Remote - British Columbia" (R0044915) — remote in the user's own province — and a "(Remote) Software
Engineer" whose body states: "This remote role welcomes candidates anywhere in Canada and the US.
Travel is required as needed, approximately 25%... Preference will be given to candidates who can work
in CST timezone." Canada-remote is real; caveats are (a) some divisions prefer Central Time and
(b) ~25% travel on that particular role.

Engineering & tech: Enterprise / legacy-modernization flavoured. From live JDs: Java 11/17, "JVM
internals, memory management, tuning, and concurrency/multithreading", Spring Boot, REST API design,
PostgreSQL/MySQL/MongoDB, Elasticsearch, React/Next.js/Material UI; elsewhere MSSQL, C#/.NET Core,
Python/PowerShell, COBOL maintenance, Azure/AWS, GitHub, Agile. Senior JDs ask for "8+ years of
experience in software development" and expect architectural leadership and code review. AI-assisted
development (Copilot, Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT) is explicitly encouraged. No public engineering blog or
handbook was located.

Notable / other: A large share of live openings are India-based (Pune-Baroda, Bangalore-Baroda,
"Remote - India"), so the Canada-eligible slice of the 14 relevant live roles is small. Divisions carry
their own brands (MACC), so scope and culture likely vary substantially by division rather than being
company-wide.
