# SOTI — research
Provider/key: workday:soti/careers | company_type: product

What they do: SOTI builds enterprise mobility management (EMM) software — managing, securing and supporting business-critical mobile devices and their diagnostics. In its own words: "SOTI is a proven innovator and industry leader for simplifying business mobility solutions by making them smarter, faster and more reliable. With SOTI's innovative portfolio of solutions, organizations can trust SOTI to elevate and streamline their mobile operations, maximize their ROI and reduce device downtime. Globally, with over 17,000 customers, SOTI has proven itself to be the go-to mobile platform provider." Products form the "SOTI One Platform"; the role in the registry sits on **SOTI XSight**, described as "our groundbreaking solution revolutionizing enterprise mobility diagnostics and analytics," covering advanced analytics, real-time monitoring and integrations across the platform ecosystem.

How they describe themselves: The origin story is told on every posting: "It all started with a classic story about one developer's vision. In 1995, Carl Rodrigues, President and CEO of SOTI, developed solutions for managing mobile device screens from a desktop." Their tagline on the boilerplate: "SOTI is committed to providing its employees with endless possibilities; learning new things, working with the latest technologies and making a difference in the world," and "We strive to go where nobody has gone before."
Their stated "What's in it for you" trio:
- **The People** — "From our humble origins in our founder's basement to our industry-leading position today, SOTI has worked hard to foster a company culture that we can all believe in. A culture that emphasizes personal growth, continuous innovation and fun."
- **The Growth** — "Our environment fosters new ideas, fresh perspectives, and the ability to take them over the goal line. SOTI is a **fast-paced environment** with a global reach that encourages you to make your mark and be part of something big!"
- **The Technology** — "complex and interesting projects, as part of highly collaborative and agile teams… alongside SOTI's partners which include leading tech giants."

Size / stage / funding: Private, founder-led (Carl Rodrigues, founder and still CEO), founded 1995 — roughly 30 years old, so an established company rather than a funding-dependent startup. Over 17,000 enterprise customers and "millions of devices managed worldwide" per its own boilerplate. Exact headcount and financials are not stated in the sources read and were not independently verified.

Locations / HQ: HQ is Mississauga, Ontario, Canada — the Workday location string is "Mississauga, Canada – Meadowvale Office (HQ)". Described as global with worldwide operations; the live engineering roles in the registry are all HQ-based. Ontario employment-law statements (Human Rights Code, AODA) appear on the postings.

Remote policy: **Hybrid, tied to the Mississauga office.** Stated verbatim on the JDs: "Location: Mississauga, Canada / Work Model: Hybrid" (Senior Software Developer, SOTI XSight) and "Location: Mississauga, Ontario (Hybrid)" (Senior SDET). No remote or distributed-team language appears anywhere in the postings.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — Canadian, but not remote-Canada.** SOTI is a Canadian company and the roles are Canadian, but they require hybrid attendance at the Mississauga HQ in Ontario. That is effectively disqualifying for a candidate in Kimberley, British Columbia who cannot relocate. Note: the pipeline's extracted facets for "Software Developer in Test" record `remote_policy: remote` / `geo_eligibility: canada`, which **contradicts the JD text** — treat the facet as wrong and the JD as authoritative.

Engineering & tech: Microsoft-centric full-stack. Core stack is **C#, .NET, TypeScript and Angular**, with architectural work spanning "user interfaces, middleware, backend systems, and integrations with data analytics and business intelligence platforms." Cloud: AWS and/or Azure "highly desirable," including Azure Maps services; cloud-native optimization is a bonus responsibility. AI is a "bonus" area — "leverage hands-on experience with Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to design and implement intelligent, data-driven features," with ML libraries as an advantage rather than the job.
QA/test engineering is a substantial discipline: automated frameworks for UI, API and backend; Java/Python/C#/JavaScript; JUnit, TestNG, Selenium, Cypress, RestAssured, Playwright; CI/CD via Jenkins, GitLab CI, Azure DevOps or CircleCI; performance/load testing with JMeter, Gatling or k6. Agile/Scrum. Senior engineers act as "Subject Matter Expert (SME) for one or more key system components" and mentor others — scope is defined by component ownership.

Compensation: **No salary ranges are posted on any SOTI role** in the registry, and no company pay-transparency statement was found in the sources read. Ontario does not currently compel a range on these postings. No band was recorded — nothing citable exists and a market-wage figure would not be a company band.

Notable / other: SOTI discloses AI use in recruiting: "SOTI uses AI tools to support parts of our recruitment process, such as reviewing resumes, screening qualifications, and creating interview questions. These tools assist our team only — final hiring decisions are made by people, not automation." Accommodation available under AODA via careers@soti.net. They explicitly do not accept unsolicited agency resumes. Corporate site referenced in their own text: soti.net.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Developer (SOTI XSight); Senior Software Developer in Test (SDET); Software Developer in Test; Senior Software Developer (Angular/C#); Senior Data Engineer.
