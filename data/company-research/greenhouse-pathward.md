# Pathward, N.A. — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:pathward | company_type: product
What they do: Pathward, N.A. is a US national bank and financial-empowerment company. It "builds solutions for markets where individuals and businesses are underserved," operating two divisions: Partner Solutions (card issuing, acquiring, digital payments, credit services — i.e. BaaS/sponsor banking for fintech innovators) and Commercial Finance (working capital, equipment finance, structured finance). It is FDIC-insured and is the bank charter behind many US fintech programs. Founded in 1954 as Storm Lake Savings and Loan in Iowa.
How they describe themselves: Mission is "Financial Inclusion for All™". From their own careers boilerplate: "At Pathward, we take tremendous pride in our purpose to create financial inclusion for all™… We strive to remove barriers that traditional institutions put in the way of financial access, and promote economic mobility by providing responsible, secure, high quality financial products." Their internal values framework is the **HUMBLE / HUNGRY / SMART** triad: "We celebrate, and embrace, our team members through our *HUMBLE*HUNGRY*SMART approach, and we believe that we are strongest when we embrace the voices of our employees, customers, partners, and the communities we serve." The corporate site adds emphasis on ethical conduct, employee well-being, sustainability, and "inclusion and belonging" — "We're simply better when everyone has a chance to contribute."
Size / stage / funding: Established, regulated national bank with a 70-year history; grew through acquisitions across several US states. Headcount and public/private status are not stated on the About page fetched — unverified here (the entity trades under the Pathward Financial holding company, but that was not confirmed from a fetched source, so treat as unverified).
Locations / HQ: US-based, originating in Iowa, with a national footprint including Texas, Pennsylvania and Michigan via acquisitions. Their JD boilerplate names onsite locations: Sioux Falls SD, Scottsdale AZ, Louisville KY, Troy MI, Franklin TN, Easton PA.
Remote policy: Stated verbatim at the top of every job posting: "We are a hybrid, remote-office company dedicated to growing our talent anywhere!" followed by the list of US onsite locations. So: genuinely remote-friendly, but the frame of reference is the United States.

The AI Engineer JD is broad applied-AI: model development, data prep and pipelines, training/validation/optimization, bias mitigation and "ethical AI practices," and integration "into existing systems, applications, and workflows by developing APIs, microservices, or interfaces," plus monitoring of deployed models.
