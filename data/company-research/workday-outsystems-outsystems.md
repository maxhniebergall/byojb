# OutSystems — research
Provider/key: workday:outsystems/outsystems | company_type: product

What they do: OutSystems sells an enterprise low-code / "AI Development Platform." In their own words: "OutSystems is a leading AI Development Platform built for the enterprise. Global organizations trust OutSystems to rapidly build mission-critical apps and agents, modernize legacy processes with agentic systems, and govern their entire AI portfolio across complex regulatory environments, all on one unified platform." The current strategic push is agentic AI layered onto the existing low-code platform: "As the future becomes agentic, our customers need us now more than ever... OutSystems bridges the 'enterprise gap' by combining the speed of generative AI with a deterministic, enterprise-grade framework."

How they describe themselves: Global, enterprise, and heavily transformation-framed. Careers boilerplate: "There are NO limits to your career: come shape the future and be part of a truly unique global culture at OutSystems!" Mission statement: "we are the platform enterprise leaders trust to evolve their entire business, accelerating innovation through secure, governed human-AI collaboration." They say they are "looking for passionate, talented, and motivated people." No published values list was recoverable from these pages. The internal JD prose is notably grandiose — the Lead Platform Engineer is described as one who "defines the 'Gold Standard' for platform reliability in an Agent-forward world" and is "the 'North Star' for operational excellence."

Size / stage / funding: Private. Self-reported on their JDs: **1,700 employees**, more than 600 partners, 900k+ developer community members, thousands of active customers "in over 75 countries and across 21 industries." **Founded in 2001** — a genuinely established company, not a startup. Named customers cited: Toyota, Heineken, Bosch, KeyBank, UCLA. Profitability not disclosed.

Locations / HQ: Offices in "the United States, United Kingdom, the Netherlands, Portugal, Germany, the UAE, Japan, Hong Kong, Malaysia, Australia, India, and Singapore." Portugal is the engineering centre of gravity — most relevant postings are Portugal-based. **Canada is not on the office list.**

Remote policy: Genuinely remote-friendly but geo-scoped per posting. They describe "a thriving, worldwide community of remote employees." The live relevant roles are tagged "US - Remote" (Lead Platform Engineer) and "Portugal - Remote" (Lead Salesforce Software Engineer, Agentic Tech Lead), with one multi-location posting. No async/handbook signals found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No evidence of it, and the posting geos say otherwise.** Every relevant opening is scoped to either US-Remote or Portugal-Remote, and Canada does not appear in their stated list of office countries. Treat as not Canada-eligible unless they say otherwise.

Engineering & tech: For a platform vendor the *relevant* engineering openings here are thin and oddly shaped. The "Lead Platform Engineer" (internally "Platform Operations Expert") is an **IT-operations / AIOps role, not a software platform role** — it asks for "8-10 years in IT Operations; Mastery of AIOps frameworks, private LLM hosting, and secure AI gateway management," "8+ years in platform-level technical leadership," and prefers an "OutSystems Expert" certification. Responsibilities are about "global, multi-environment OutSystems setups," "data residency protocols for AI agents," "Autonomous Platform Engineering," and designing a "Platform Workbench" using "Agentic Workflows to automate 60%+ of routine administrative tasks (upgrades, patching, scaling)." Another relevant role is a **Lead Salesforce Software Engineer** — internal business-systems work. Note the roles are also product-ecosystem-specific: expertise in the OutSystems platform itself is the currency, which is not portable.

Compensation (first-party, posted on their own JD, USD base): Lead Platform Engineer (US - Remote): **$131,750 – $160,425**. Their stated salary philosophy: "Our salary range is based on industry standards, company performance, and market conditions," determined from "Market Research... Company Performance... Job Requirements... Geographic Location." The Portugal-based roles state no range.

Notable / other: Only 1 of 29 scanned postings was classified relevant, so this is a large careers page with very little that matches an infrastructure/backend profile.

Open relevant roles (sample): Lead Platform Engineer / Platform Operations Expert (US - Remote); Senior Site Reliability Engineer; Senior AI Engineer; Agentic Tech Lead (Portugal - Remote); Lead Salesforce Software Engineer (Portugal - Remote); Digital Product Lead – AI & Agentic Transformation.
