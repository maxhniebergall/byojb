# Jane (jane.app) — research
Provider/key: ashby:jane | company_type: product

ENTITY NOTE: this key is Jane Software Inc. / jane.app — the North Vancouver, BC health-and-wellness practice-management SaaS. It is NOT Jane Street (the trading firm) and NOT Jane Technologies / iHeartJane (the cannabis e-commerce company); both of those also appear elsewhere in this registry. Confirmed from the JD body: "Jane is a founder-led, high-growth SaaS company born in North Vancouver, British Columbia."

What they do: Jane is an all-in-one practice-management platform for allied-health and wellness practitioners — physiotherapy, massage, chiropractic, mental health and similar independent clinics. The product covers branded online booking, scheduling, insurance management and billing, customizable charting, online intake forms, patient reminders, integrated payment processing and telehealth. Customers are tens of thousands of independent healthcare practices (roughly 30K) worldwide. Revenue is SaaS subscription plus integrated payments.

How they describe themselves: Mission is "Helping the Helpers"; vision is "More Happier Helpers" — "a thriving community of health care professionals who are succeeding in their businesses and providing engaging care to their patients." Their stated values are three:
- "Love Jane" — expressed through respect, commitment, empathy and action, applied to the product, customers, employees and how the company is run.
- "Work Hard" — explicitly paired with sustainability: a performance bar they call "Delight", while "enabling sustainable careers and work-life harmony".
- "Have Fun" — "if you're not having fun, there's a problem somewhere and we need to fix it."
Founder-led (Alison and Trevor). Tone across the careers material and the JDs is plain, unhyped and unusually candid — no rocketship or hustle framing.

Size / stage / funding: "a team of more than 700 people". Private, founder-led, high-growth; not presented as a venture-runway story and no funding-driven urgency language anywhere in their material. Founded 2012.

Locations / HQ: Born and headquartered in North Vancouver, British Columbia. Team distributed across Canada, the US and the UK.

Remote policy: Remote-first in practice — "We're now a team of more than 700 people working remotely across Canada, the US, and the UK." Live engineering postings carry the location "Canada" rather than a city.

Remote-Canada eligibility: VERIFIED YES, and it is the best case in this batch. The company is headquartered in British Columbia, its postings are Canada-wide with no city attached, and its salary ranges are quoted in Canadian dollars on Canadian postings. A BC-based, Mountain-Time candidate is squarely inside their normal hiring footprint; Pacific/Mountain is their home timezone, not an accommodation.

Engineering & tech: Roles in the registry are data-platform shaped — "Staff Data Engineer" and "Senior Data Engineer - Customer Signals & Instrumentation" — i.e. internal data infrastructure and instrumentation serving internal consumers, plus a Cloud Engineer track. Healthcare practice management implies PHIPA/PIPEDA/HIPAA-grade data handling, payments integration and high booking-path availability.

Compensation philosophy (see data/company-comp/): Unusually explicit and worth quoting. "At Jane, we're committed to paying fairly, clearly, and above all, paying for growth." Every posting states a full range and then explains it: the range spans the whole growth journey in the role, and "most new hires join at the accomplished stage", for which they name a specific number. They publish a video explaining how the salary bands are set and say "You're also encouraged to ask questions about compensation at any point during the interview process." Observed on live postings: Staff Data Engineer $152,000-$237,500 with an accomplished-stage hire point of $180,500; Senior Data Engineer $121,600-$190,000 with an accomplished-stage hire point of $144,400.

Notable / other: Glassdoor culture-and-values rating around 3.9/5 with roughly 71% of North Vancouver employees recommending the company. The "helping the helpers" framing is consistently applied rather than decorative — the values page ties it to how they treat employees as well as customers.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Data Engineer (Canada); Senior Data Engineer - Customer Signals & Instrumentation (Canada); Cloud Engineer.
