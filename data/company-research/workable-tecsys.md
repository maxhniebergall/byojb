# Tecsys Inc. — research
Provider/key: workable:tecsys | company_type: product

What they do: Tecsys is a Montreal-based supply-chain management software vendor. Its core products are warehouse management (WMS), distribution, inventory and supply-chain visibility systems delivered as SaaS. In its own words it is "a fast-growing innovator offering supply chain solutions to industry leading healthcare systems, hospitals, and pharmacy businesses to distributors, retailers, and 3PLs." Healthcare supply chain is its flagship vertical — named customers on its site include Intermountain Health, Mercy, Prisma Health, Sanford, St. Luke's and Vanderbilt, alongside distribution/retail customers such as Canon, Nissan and Carolina Cat. It also runs an analytics/AI product line branded **TecsysIQ**.

How they describe themselves: Corporate positioning is "the heartbeat of modern supply chains — visibility. Agility. Confidence in every outcome," helping organizations "run supply chains they can count on", with "decades of experience in complex environments." In job postings the recurring line is "If you thrive on tackling interesting challenges with continuous learning opportunities, then Tecsys could be a good fit for you", plus a diversity-and-inclusion statement and an explicit "if you don't meet every requirement, we still encourage you to apply."

Size / stage / funding: Established, publicly traded Canadian company (TSX: TCS), not venture-backed and not runway-dependent. Long operating history; mid-market scale. Recognized on Best Workplaces in Canada and Women in Leadership lists.

Locations / HQ: HQ at 1801 McGill College Avenue, Suite 1200, Montreal, QC H3A 2N4. Additional offices; roles in the registry are Montreal-based. Postings are issued in both English and French (a French twin exists for the Data Platform Engineer req), indicating a Quebec-centred workforce.

Remote policy: **Stated as "digital-first", but implemented as Montreal hybrid.** Every relevant posting carries the same preamble: "Having recognized the advantages of remote work, including employee morale, productivity, reduced commuting... we are proud to be a digital-first company... Our digital-first work environment, together with our conveniently located offices and collaborative workspaces, provide our team with the freedom and flexibility to work in the way that makes our employees most productive." However the ATS workplace field on those same reqs reads **`hybrid`** with location "Montreal, Canada (Hybrid)", and the SRE req adds a requirement of "occasional travel to the office (less than 10%)". Only the DevOps internship is listed as Montreal (Remote).

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — not for a candidate who cannot relocate to Montreal.** The company employs in Canada (which clears work authorization), but all four relevant open reqs are tagged Montreal Hybrid, not remote-anywhere-in-Canada. The "digital-first" language is about flexibility around a Montreal office, not location independence; the SRE req explicitly expects periodic office attendance. From Kimberley BC this is effectively disqualifying. Additionally Montreal is Eastern Time, two hours ahead of Mountain, and part of the posting set is French-language.

Engineering & tech: AWS (multi-account, VPC, EC2, EKS) and Kubernetes at scale; Terraform and Ansible for IaC; GitLab CI/CD (Jenkins acceptable); Datadog for monitoring, alerting, dashboards and SLO/SLI definition; Python/Bash/Java scripting; Java and .NET application environments. Data side: Databricks and Apache Spark (including Spark Structured Streaming and CDC), Bronze/Silver/Gold medallion layering, Helm and Kubernetes Jobs for customer-environment onboarding, Docker images and a Java/Maven build pipeline, PySpark, SQL, catalog/schema/access governance. Org structure visible from departments: "PT - CLOUD Infrastructure" runs a Network and Security Operations Center (NOC) that owns platform reliability, incident command and blameless postmortems; "PT - Research & Development" hosts the TecsysIQ Data & AI team. They explicitly encourage use of AI-assisted engineering tools (e.g. Amazon Kiro) "to accelerate execution while validating outputs". Agile delivery with Product Owners.

Notable / other: The SRE role includes on-call rotations, escalations and acting as Incident Commander. The Data Platform Engineer role is an unusually clean hybrid of Terraform/Kubernetes infrastructure and Spark streaming pipelines — "equally comfortable writing Terraform and debugging a Spark Structured Streaming job." Healthcare supply chain is a genuinely mission-critical, non-faddish domain.

Open relevant roles (sample): Site Reliability Engineer (Montreal, Hybrid); Data Platform Engineer (DevOps) (Montreal, Hybrid); Ingénieur(e) de plateforme de données (DevOps) (Montreal, Hybrid); Stagiaire DevOps - Automne 2026 (Montreal, Remote).
