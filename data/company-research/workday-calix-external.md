# Calix — research
Provider/key: workday:calix/external | company_type: product

What they do: Calix sells cloud software, systems and services to **broadband service providers**. Their own current positioning on calix.com is *"an AI platform company that enables service providers to transform their operations and accelerate delivery of differentiated experiences."* Customers are national and global carriers, regional telcos, cable operators, utilities, municipalities, WISPs and managed service providers. The portfolio spans broadband access hardware/systems, a cloud platform for subscriber experience and operations analytics, AI agents, and managed services. Historically Calix was primarily an access-equipment vendor (fibre/PON systems) that has been repositioning toward recurring-revenue cloud software.
