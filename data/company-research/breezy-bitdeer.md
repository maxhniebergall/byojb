# Bitdeer Technologies Group — research
Provider/key: breezy:bitdeer | company_type: product

What they do: Bitdeer is a vertically integrated compute-infrastructure company with three stated business lines: (1) **Bitcoin mining** — its own SEALMINER ASIC hardware plus hosted/cloud mining; (2) **AI Cloud** — GPU rental infrastructure ("NeoCloud") for AI/ML workloads, deploying NVIDIA GB200 NVL72, B200 and H100 fleets; (3) **Infrastructure** — data-centre design, construction and cooling (Minerbase containers). From their own JD: "Bitdeer is a world-leading technology company for AI and Bitcoin mining infrastructure… Bitdeer handles complex processes involved in computing such as equipment procurement, transport logistics, data center design and construction, equipment management, and daily operations."

How they describe themselves: The self-description is operational and unromantic rather than mission-driven — the emphasis is on owning the whole physical stack of compute. The SRE JD's own framing of the role is notably blunt and craft-oriented: "This is the **build + run** role. You don't only ship code; you ship a service that other squads, cloud-service teams, and tenants depend on. **You take the on-call pager for what you build.**" and "ride the Plugin Framework conventions, meet declared SLOs, and stay drift-free." No values page or culture manifesto was found on any reachable page.

Headcount not disclosed on reachable pages. 83 total live postings in the registry — a large, actively hiring organisation. Engineering & tech: This is the most technically detailed JD in the batch. The role builds "one or more bounded contexts of the **NeoCloud SRE platform** — the multi-region substrate that observes, protects, and operates a GPU rental fleet across self-built and OEM-rented data centers," shipped through **GitOps + CI/CD** with a Plugin Framework and declared SLOs. Owned domains include: collection & storage (collection agents, metrics/logs/traces/profiles stores, analytics lake, enrichment); alerting, correlation and SLO frameworks; topology and cluster-health rollup with collection plugins for **Kubernetes, Slurm, Ray, Volcano, Kueue, KubeRay**; **fault prediction** (GPU, link, disk, straggler, SDC, stranded-GPU predictors); remediation actuators, workflow/orchestration substrate, inspection orchestration, job scheduling, NCCL baseline probes; hardware lifecycle and DC ops (boot provisioning, rolling upgrade, BMC/IPMI bare-metal management, ZTP D0–D5); IAM/secrets/tenant-config/CMDB; customer bridge, ticketing and console BFFs; backup/DR and meta-monitoring; CI/CD, GitOps, plugin registry, image registry; a **self-improving agent** stack (agent control plane, discovery, codegen, sandbox, per-region LLM gateway); and global SRE management (maintenance windows, change management, capacity planner, cost optimiser, GPU-efficiency and network-stability dashboards, patching, compat matrix, security platform).
Requirements: **7+ years** production software engineering including 2+ years operating what you built with real on-call; production depth in **Go (preferred), Rust or Java** plus Python for tooling; distributed-systems fundamentals (at-least-once vs exactly-once, idempotency, back-pressure, leader election, consistent hashing, gossip, fan-out, CRDT vs Raft vs Paxos); production-scale experience in the ingest/query/storage paths of **Prometheus, VictoriaMetrics, Mimir, Thanos, Loki, Elasticsearch, Tempo, Jaeger or OpenTelemetry**; hands-on GitOps/CI-CD (ArgoCD).
