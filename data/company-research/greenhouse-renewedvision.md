# Renewed Vision — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:renewedvision | company_type: product

What they do: Renewed Vision builds **cross-platform desktop presentation and media software**. The exact product is not named in any of the three JDs, but the technical picture is unambiguous: native macOS (Swift/Objective-C/AppKit) and Windows (C#/WPF) applications sharing a Rust and modern-C++ core, with video and audio frameworks, low-level rendering, and OpenGL/Metal graphics work. They refer to "our platform" and "our products" without naming customers or a market segment. **Caveat: the company's own website was not reachable in this session** (see Sources), so the customer base, market and business model are **unverified** — everything below comes from the company's own job-description text.

How they describe themselves: No mission statement, values list or culture section appears in any of the three postings — unusual, and a genuine gap in this dossier. What they publish is entirely a description of the engineer they want, and it is notably craft-oriented rather than hustle-oriented:
- "**You love working on the parts of an application that are invisible to the user but make everything work.** You realize that different programming languages have their place in a complex tech stack and are comfortable working with the language best suited for the job. You are comfortable working without all the details, knowing that part of the process is discovery, which drives innovation. **You prioritize memory safety and concurrency to ensure the creation of dependable and high-performing applications.**"
- "You write code with a product focus knowing that there is a balance to be found in the tension that exists between code perfection and customer value."
- "You might even have a bit of a love-hate relationship with macOS and AppKit — knowing that the built in technologies are great, but they do have their shortcomings."
- Repeated across roles: "Understand the balance between refactor now vs. refactor later"; "Consistently seek to learn and develop both personally and professionally"; "Take ownership of projects, **work with loosely defined specs**, and drive for results"; "Can focus on details while understanding the importance of timely, high-quality updates."
No "fast-paced", "hustle", "rocketship", "scrappy" or "wear many hats" language appears anywhere. Equally, no stability, funding or growth claims appear either.

Size / stage / funding: **Entirely unknown.** No headcount, stage, funding, ownership or profitability information is stated in any posting, and no company page could be fetched. The hiring pattern — a handful of deep platform-specialist roles, one of which is a pipeline-only req — is more consistent with a small, established, product-focused software company than with a venture-backed growth startup, but that is inference and should be treated as unverified.

Locations / HQ: **Not stated.** No office, city or country appears in any of the three postings. All are tagged simply "Remote".

Remote policy: All three live postings are tagged **"Remote"** with no qualifier, no hub city, no in-office day requirement and no travel expectation. No written remote-work policy is published in the JD text.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear — genuinely unverified, in both directions.** The positive: every role is tagged plain "Remote" with **no country restriction of any kind**, and unlike Anrok ("U.S only"), Swan ("Remote (U.S.)") or Buzz Solutions (US work-authorization language), these JDs contain **no US-only clause, no work-authorization statement, and no sponsorship disclaimer**. The negative: they also contain no positive statement that they hire outside any particular country, and Canada is never mentioned. The absence of a restriction is weak evidence, not confirmation. Timezone workability is likewise unknown since no HQ is named. **This must be asked directly before investing effort** — it is the single highest-value unknown about this company.

Engineering & tech: Three distinct specialist tracks, all deep rather than broad:
- **Core (Rust)** — "develop the infrastructure and working technologies that power our platform". Rust and modern C++; cross-platform libraries consumed by multiple applications; high-performance multi-threaded library development and debugging; "multithreaded, decoupled systems using actor frameworks and async/await, emphasizing message-driven design, non-blocking I/O, and memory-safe concurrency"; familiarity with video/audio frameworks and low-level rendering. Nice-to-have: C# or Swift.
- **macOS** — Swift, Objective-C, SwiftUI, AppKit, Xcode, XPC; C/C++ interop with Swift/Objective-C required; graphics work with OpenGL or Metal; integrating cloud services into a native application.
- **Windows** — C# and WPF; interfacing C# with native languages.
The org is described as a "multi-platform cross-functional team". No public engineering blog or handbook was found.

Notable / other: The **Windows Software Engineer posting is not an active vacancy** — it opens with: "While this not a position we are currently hiring for, we are accepting resumes in anticipation of hiring in the near future. Although you may not receive an immediate response, we will carefully review your information once an opportunity becomes available." So the genuinely live count is lower than the board suggests. No benefits, compensation, or perks are stated on any posting.

Open relevant roles (sample): Core Software Engineer | Rust (Remote); macOS Software Engineer (Remote); Windows Software Engineer (Remote — pipeline only, not currently hiring). No pay range on any of them.
