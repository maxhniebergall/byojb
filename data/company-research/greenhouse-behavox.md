# Behavox — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:behavox | company_type: product

What they do: Behavox is a cloud-native AI company selling an integrated **compliance and controls platform** to global banks, asset managers, hedge funds, private equity firms, insurance businesses and commodity firms. In their words, the platform "unifies communications and trade surveillance, compliant archiving, policy management as well as front-office analytics on a single, AI-native technology stack, delivered as a globally scalable SaaS-based cloud service." In practice: ingest all employee communications and trade data for a regulated financial institution, and run surveillance/risk models over it.

How they describe themselves: The self-description leans hard on speed and intensity, quoted directly from their JD —
- "our engineering culture is built around **speed, experimentation, and technical excellence**, following agile principles and rapid iteration."
- "We constantly test and adopt the latest cloud technologies and AI tooling, optimising for **fast feedback loops and execution**."
- "We look for people who can **move fast, challenge conventional wisdom**, and who want to work at the frontier of modern AI, SaaS platforms, and distributed systems."
- "Behavox is a **high-performance organisation with a strong bias toward delivery, ownership, and responsibility. We commit, and we execute.**"
- "We are building systems that are complex, mission-critical, and global in scale; systems that many consider too large or too difficult. To do that, we seek the **smartest, most technically capable** engineers and technologists who take end-to-end responsibility and want to **win by building what others cannot**."

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2014. Private, backed by **SoftBank Vision Fund** (~$100M invested in the 2020 round). Not profitability-declared. Third-party employer-review sources report a pattern of recurring layoffs and project-cycle hiring.

Locations / HQ: HQ **London, UK**. Offices in New York City, **Montreal**, Seattle, Singapore and Tokyo. The Canadian centre of gravity is Montreal, with a Toronto office also referenced.

Remote policy: **Hybrid with a light office anchor.** Stated in the JD: "Modern, comfortable offices in Montreal/Toronto downtown, with an expectation of **working from the office 1 day per week**, reflecting our belief in strong in-person collaboration, while **remaining flexible to accommodate remote work for candidates who live in other Canadian cities**." They also note "whenever possible, we aim to conduct interviews in person at our offices."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified YES, with a caveat.** The posting is listed simply as "Canada," and the JD explicitly carves out remote work for candidates living in other Canadian cities — so a Kimberley, BC candidate is eligible on paper and the 1-day-per-week office expectation would not apply. The caveat is cultural rather than contractual: the company states a belief in in-person collaboration and prefers in-person interviews, so a fully-remote Mountain-Time hire would be an exception to their default, and the working day skews to Montreal/London hours (UTC-5 / UTC+0), i.e. an early start from MT.

Engineering & tech: Deep JVM shop. The Senior Software Engineer (JAVA) role asks for Java internals (GC tuning, Collections Framework, `java.util.concurrent` and advanced concurrency, NIO/NIO2, performance profiling, heap-dump analysis), mastery of Spring/Spring Boot, SOLID, Clean Code and GoF patterns. Architecture: monolith and microservices including migration patterns and domain-driven decomposition, REST/gRPC/messaging IPC, distributed transactions (Sagas, 2PC), CQRS, Event Sourcing. Cloud: **GCP preferred**, also AWS and Azure; IAM, VPC, encryption, JWT/JWS/JWE; Terraform/IaC; Twelve-Factor App. Reliability: full observability stacks (structured logging, distributed tracing, metrics, alerting), SLI/SLO/SLA frameworks, rolling/blue-green/canary deployment. Notably, **"AI-assisted engineering" is a mandatory requirement**, not a nice-to-have: "Practitioner-level command of agentic AI tools applied to software engineering — prompt engineering, AI context management and its limitations, sub-agents, skills and plugins, multi-agent orchestration, and team-of-agents architectures. Experience with Claude Code (Anthropic), Codex (OpenAI), or equivalent is mandatory."

Notable / other: Benefits stated as competitive cash, an equity award "aligned with long-term value creation," comprehensive family health insurance, and **30 days annual time off** plus public holidays and sick leave — a genuinely generous leave policy. On the other side, public employer-review aggregates are poor: Glassdoor ~3.3/5 across ~138 reviews, and Comparably rates overall culture 2.4/5 (bottom 5%). Recurring themes in those reviews are top-down management, a leadership "clique," chaotic and shifting priorities, and hire-for-a-project-then-cut workforce planning. Treat these as weak, self-selected sources, but the volume and consistency are notable.
