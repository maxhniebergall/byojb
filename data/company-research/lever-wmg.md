# Warner Music Group — research
Provider/key: lever:wmg | company_type: product

What they do: Warner Music Group is one of the three global major music companies, spanning **recorded music** (Atlantic, Warner Records, Elektra, Parlophone and others), **music publishing** (Warner Chappell) and artist services. Publicly traded (NASDAQ: WMG). Its technology organisation is unusually central to the business: signing and onboarding artists, delivering correct metadata and audio to Spotify, YouTube and other DSPs, royalty and distribution pipelines, and data products that drive artist and label decisions.

How they describe themselves: From their own JD copy — "At Warner Music Group, we're a global collective of music makers and music lovers, tech innovators and inspired entrepreneurs, game-changing creatives and passionate team members. Here, we turn dreams into stardom and audiences into fans." They state **three core values** that "underpin everything we do across all our diverse businesses":
- **Curiosity** — "We do our best work when we're immersing ourselves in culture and breaking through barriers. Curiosity is the driving force behind creativity and ingenuity. It fuels innovation, and innovation is the key to our future."
- **Collaboration** — "Making music and bringing it to the world is all about the power of originality amplified by teamwork. A great idea, like a great song, travels globally. We ignite passions and build connections across our diverse community of artists, songwriters, partners, and fans."
- **Commitment** — "We pursue excellence for our team and our talent. Everything in music starts with a leap into the unknown, and we're committed to keeping the faith, acting with integrity, and delivering on our promises."
They explicitly position technology as core: "Technology is one of the most important parts of our business… technology plays an invaluable role in our success. The engineering team at Warner Music Group makes all of it a reality." They also run a public engineering/product/design/infrastructure/data blog with first-hand team accounts, and pitch the environment as "the best of both worlds – an innovative global music company that retains the creative spirit of a nimble independent."

Size / stage / funding: Public company (NASDAQ: WMG), major-label scale, thousands of employees globally. Established and revenue-generating, not venture-funded.

Locations / HQ: New York HQ with global offices. The Canadian engineering reqs are posted against **Ontario, British Columbia and Alberta** with "remote" in the location list; some also list Toronto.

Remote policy: Mixed remote and hybrid depending on req. The Canadian engineering postings in this batch are explicitly geo-tagged across three provinces plus "remote," which is the pattern of a genuinely province-flexible remote req rather than an office-anchored one.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, and British Columbia is named explicitly.** Every live relevant req carries "British Columbia" in its location list alongside Ontario, Alberta and "remote" — e.g. DevOps Engineer (Ontario, remote, British Columbia, Alberta), Senior Software Engineer, Dimensional Data (Ontario, remote, British Columbia, Alberta), Software Engineer, Automated Marketing (Toronto, Ontario, remote, Alberta, British Columbia). Salaries are quoted in CAD. Alberta/BC inclusion means Mountain Time is an accepted working location. This is the cleanest geographic fit in the batch.

Engineering & tech: Data-platform-heavy. The **Dimensional Data Team (DDT)** "owns and delivers dimensional data on concepts like Artists, Participants, Albums, and Tracks to business users and internal WMG products, empowering artist and music label decisions" — the work is building and managing "data sets, pipelines, transformations, APIs, and front end products that abstract away data complexity for our stakeholders." Adjacent teams cover Data Platform, Data Operations, Distribution, Automated Marketing, and infrastructure/DevOps (including an Oracle DBA-flavoured infrastructure line). Public engineering blog exists.

Notable / other: Roles span Business Intelligence, Data Operations, Engineering, Data Platform, Data Science, Software Engineering, IT, Infrastructure and Product Management in Canada.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Dimensional Data; DevOps Engineer; Software Engineer, Data Platform; Software Engineer, Distribution; Software Engineer, Automated Marketing; Staff Backend Software Engineer - Data Platform.

Pay: WMG posts CAD ranges directly in the ATS on its Canadian reqs — DevOps Engineer **CAD 102,000–134,000**; Senior Software Engineer, Dimensional Data **CAD 112,000–152,000**; Software Engineer, Automated Marketing **CAD 115,000–165,000**; Software Engineer, Dimensional Data and Software Engineer, Distribution **CAD 90,000–135,000**; (recently closed) Senior Infrastructure Engineer - Oracle DBA **CAD 106,250–125,000**.
