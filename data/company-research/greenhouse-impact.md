# impact.com — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:impact | company_type: product
What they do: impact.com runs a partnership-management / partnership-marketing SaaS platform — brands use it to discover, manage, and measure affiliate, influencer/creator, publisher and customer-referral partnerships in one system. Its products are branded Performance (affiliate), Creator (influencer) and Advocate (referral). It says it manages "over $100 billion in partnership commerce annually" and that "over 5,000 global brands — including Walmart, Uber, Shopify, Lenovo, L'Oréal, and Fanatics" use it across "more than 350,000 partnerships".
How they describe themselves: "the world's leading commerce partnership marketing platform"; "the all-in-one partnership management platform"; positions itself around turning "discovery into performance". In the JD: "you'll be working within a small fast-paced agile scrum team building an integral piece of the client portal. We are composed of highly cohesive and loosely coupled teams to maximize productivity and encourage ingenuity. At impact.com you will not be just another cog in the machine." Sustainability has a dedicated footer page.
Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, late-stage (scale implied by 5,000+ brands). Exact headcount and funding stage not verified from the pages reachable this session.
Locations / HQ: Global, with localized sites for China, Japan, Italy, France and Germany. HQ is not stated on the homepage. The relevant engineering posting is tagged "#LI_Victoria_Canada" — a Victoria, British Columbia office/team.

Engineering & tech: Java (with "familiarity with functional style"), Spring, Hibernate, Vue.js, SQL (MySQL/MSSQL), GCP, GitHub, Linux; pluses are Kafka / Google Pub-Sub streaming, Gradle/Maven/Jenkins. Small agile scrum teams; the role is explicitly "Senior Full Stack Developer" despite the "Senior Software Engineer" title. 7+ years experience required.
