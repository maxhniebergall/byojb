# ZayZoon — research
Provider/key: ashby:zayzoon | company_type: product

What they do: ZayZoon is a Calgary-founded fintech offering an employee financial-wellness platform, best known for Earned Wage Access (EWA) — letting hourly employees draw a portion of already-earned wages before payday. It sells through employers and payroll/HR partners (integrated with payroll providers; a Scotiabank partnership brings EWA to Canadian payroll). Customers include large franchise employers such as McDonald's and Burger King franchisees, plus thousands of SMBs. Stated mission: "save 10 million hardworking employees 10 billion dollars."

How they describe themselves: Four stated core principles, in their own words —
- **Work smarter, not harder**: "Own everything you do, and take it to the next level."
- **Dependability**: "Be the person others want to lean on when the going gets tough."
- **Empathy**: "Practice empathy in service of others and aspire to make a positive impact."
- **Growth mindset**: "Adopt a growth mindset—especially when things get hard."
They also claim "74% of ZayZoon customers reporting reduced financial stress." CEO Darcy Tuer frames the org as a "high-performance culture" built by "intentional leadership." JD tone is warm and non-hustle: mentoring, "data as your north star," secure SDLC, agile ceremonies — notably free of "fast-paced"/"rocketship" language.

Size / stage / funding: Private, Series B extended to nearly **$50M USD** (initial $25.5M raise in 2022). Growth-stage, not yet profitable-declared. Recognized in the 2025 Deloitte Technology Fast 50 and Fast 500, and as a top-10 growth company in Canada by the CIX awards.

Locations / HQ: HQ Calgary, Alberta (**Mountain Time**); second office Scottsdale, Arizona. Job postings list Calgary, Edmonton, Toronto, Vancouver, Winnipeg, Ottawa, Halifax, Moncton, plus US cities (Orlando, Phoenix, Scottsdale, Pittsburgh, Columbus, St. Louis).

Remote policy: **Remote-first, permanently.** Their careers page: "We are a remote-first company. And rest assured, that'll never change." The Staff Engineer JD states plainly: "You might see a few cities listed, but no need to be in any of them! We're fully remote and hire across Canada and the U.S." Benefits: "Permanently Remote: Work from a desk, a coffee shop, or in the great outdoors — our jobs are fully remote, forever"; a "You-do-You" flexible time-off program; day-one benefits; $500 wellness budget; parental leave up to six months topped up at 80% of base.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified YES — and the strongest timezone fit in this batch.** Hires across Canada with no city requirement, and HQ is Calgary, i.e. the same Mountain Time zone the candidate works in.

Engineering & tech: Primary stack is **Ruby on Rails** (Staff role asks 6+ years of Rails). Emphasis on domain-driven design, OO, microservices where appropriate, scale/concurrency/queuing/background processing, third-party data ingestion. Cloud on **AWS** (RDS, queues, serverless). Strong data-engineering component: complex SQL, query optimization, ETL, a data warehouse and analytics tooling used directly by engineers. Security posture is explicit: OWASP, secure coding, **SOC-2** processes. AI is treated as a developer-productivity tool ("Use AI to streamline processes, reduce friction, and give time back to developers"), not as a product-hype pillar. Agile process: sprint planning, story grooming, standups. Code review and coding-standards enforcement are named responsibilities of the Staff role.

Notable / other: Regulated-adjacent fintech (EWA sits under consumer-lending scrutiny in several jurisdictions, a stability consideration). SVP People & Culture Agata Zasada named one of Canada's Most Admired Chief People and Culture Officers for 2024. Employees themselves get the ZayZoon product. Company re-domiciled/expanded back into Canada after a US-heavy growth phase; the bulk of revenue is US SMB.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior DevOps Engineer (CA$132K–160K base + CA$20.5K bonus, posted); Staff Engineer (Ruby on Rails, no posted range).
