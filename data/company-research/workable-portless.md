# Portless — research
Provider/key: workable:portless | company_type: product

What they do: Portless is a cross-border ecommerce fulfilment platform: it ships DTC orders direct-from-factory out of Asian manufacturing hubs (primarily China) to end customers in 75+ countries, using domestic last-mile carriers. The pitch is cash-flow and lead-time compression — "Cut your inventory lead time from months to days" — converting inventory to cash in days instead of months and enabling just-in-time inventory. It integrates with Shopify, WooCommerce, SellerCloud and X-Cart.

How they describe themselves: Customer-outcome marketing ("hundreds of high-growth ecommerce brands", named customers include MrBeast, The Woobles, Craft Club, Loftie, Memobottle). The engineering JD's own framing: "make our operations faster, our team smarter, and our merchants more successful"; it asks for someone who can "thrive in fast-moving environments" and "work independently in a remote environment with a strong sense of ownership and ability to ship with minimal oversight". No published values page, engineering blog or handbook was found.

Size / stage / funding: Venture-backed startup — investors listed on its own site are Commerce Ventures, Red Swan, Valor Equity Partners, Ground Up Ventures and Plaza Ventures. Headcount and funding stage are not disclosed on the site; the JD's extracted company stage is "startup". Small.
