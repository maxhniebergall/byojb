# Allocate — research
Provider/key: ashby:allocate | company_type: product

What they do: Allocate is a fintech platform for private-market investing aimed at RIAs (registered investment advisors) and family offices. In its own words: "Allocate is transforming private market investing by enabling RIAs and family offices to seamlessly discover, model, and manage their private market exposure. Our platform combines curated fund and co-investment opportunities with institutional-grade infrastructure. Through a single, data-rich digital experience, clients access top-tier opportunities across venture capital, private equity, private credit, and other private asset classes — backed by powerful tracking, analytics, and administration tools."

How they describe themselves: The postings publish a full, unusually explicit values set under "Essential Values & Culture":
- **Client obsession** — "Providing our clients with a world-class experience is our number one priority. We obsessively search for ways to improve the experience for our clients and partners. This requires extraordinary response times, proactivity, and ensuring that everything we do, from product strategy to offline communications is a top-tier client experience."
- **Challenge convention** — "Instead of detailing all the reasons why an idea may not work, we constantly question things to determine how a viable idea may be put into motion."
- **Commitment to continuous improvement** — "We find ways to personally scale each day by pushing ourselves up the learning curve."
- **Meritocracy, not politics** — "We place the utmost value on results and rewards through merit, not reward actions driven by political agendas."
- **Civil Discourse is embraced** — "open, intellectually curious conversations are required to consistently arrive at the best decisions. Respect is paramount… but our mission is always to get the right answer collectively, not to be right."
- **Embrace technological change** — "We adopt tools and techniques that make us faster, smarter, and better… especially around AI and automation, and drop outdated methods without hesitation. This includes actively integrating AI-assisted development tools into your daily workflow… Complacency kills progress, we value adaptability and curiosity."
Both postings frame the company as early in its build-out: "There's a lot for us to build, and we need a developer with a broad skillset who can jump right in to help us lay the technical foundation for the company's future."

Size / stage / funding: Not stated in the sources read. The "lay the technical foundation for the company's future" framing and the "broad skillset" ask indicate a small, relatively early engineering organization. Funding stage, investors and headcount are unverified.

Locations / HQ: HQ not stated in the postings. San Francisco Bay Area is named as an example of a "higher cost-of-living market" for pay purposes, which hints at a US west-coast centre of gravity but is not a location statement. Team and department offsites require travel, and "an in-person interview may be required during the process."

Remote policy: "Location: Fully Remote Position." Distributed by default, with periodic in-person offsites and a possible in-person interview. A broadband internet connection is required. No async or handbook culture is described.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — US work authorization required.** Both postings qualify the remote statement with "(all I-9 eligible candidates will be considered)." Form I-9 is the US employment eligibility verification form, so this is a US-authorization-only requirement. A Canadian citizen working remotely from British Columbia with no US work authorization is not eligible. This is a disqualifier, and it is stated by the company itself on both live roles.

Engineering & tech: Two distinct tracks.
- **Backend**: C#/.NET on ASP.NET Web API, SQL-based databases with Postgres preferred (explicit emphasis on schema design and indexing — "must understand how to design a schema that will result in a performant application"), REST APIs, ORMs (Dapper, Entity Framework), FluentMigrator, Docker and Docker Compose, AWS ECS/EKS. Agile squads: "work as part of an independent, cross-functional squad to deliver critical features."
- **ML/AI (posted as "Machine Learning Engineer", described internally as an "AI / Extraction Engineer")**: applied ML/LLM engineering in production with strong Python; "build, train, and improve machine-learning models for production use"; "develop evaluation and feedback loops that measurably improve model performance over time"; "stand up the tooling to measure, monitor, and track model quality"; model fine-tuning with "rigorous evaluation discipline." Nice-to-haves: applied NLP, computer vision or multimodal models; MLOps and model-monitoring tooling; fintech or financial-data background.
Both roles require hands-on use of AI coding assistants (Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Cursor) as a stated must-have, not a preference.

Compensation: Ranges are posted directly on the JDs (USD base):
- Machine Learning Engineer — "$165,000 to $185,000", seniority "Senior professional." Base only; total comp may include a discretionary performance-based bonus. "Candidates located in higher cost-of-living markets, including the San Francisco Bay Area, may be considered within the higher end of the range."
- Sr. Software Engineer II – Backend — "$145,000 to $175,000" in the JD prose, seniority "Mid-level professional." Note the Ashby structured comp field on the same posting reports **$145K–$210K**, which disagrees with the prose maximum (see data-quality note).
Benefits: medical, dental and vision; 401(k); "responsible vacation time (PTO)". Compliance with Allocate's Code of Ethics is a condition of the role.

Notable / other: The ML role's real content is document/data extraction for private-market fund data ("AI / Extraction Engineer"), which is more information-extraction pipeline work than model research.

Open relevant roles (sample): Sr. Software Engineer II – Backend (Remote); Machine Learning Engineer.
