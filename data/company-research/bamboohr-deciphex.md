# Deciphex — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:deciphex | company_type: product

What they do: Deciphex is a digital-pathology company building AI-driven diagnostic and research
software, plus a pathology services arm. In their own words the mission is to "leverage the combined
power of AI and a global network of expert pathologists to deliver faster, more accurate
diagnostics", and they describe themselves as helping "pharma accelerate essential drug development"
and helping "patients receive timely and accurate diagnosis" — i.e. two markets, preclinical/research
pathology for pharma and clinical diagnostics. Their JD states the software is built "in a regulated
environment (GLP, ISO 13485, IEC 62304, EU MDR)". Products include Patholytix (research) and
Diagnexia (clinical) — the latter confirmed by the corporate entity name "Diagnexia UK Ltd" on their
Kidlington laboratory.

How they describe themselves: "Our culture is built on trust. We give our team the space they need to
deliver results and the environment to enjoy doing it." They emphasise "collaboration, creativity,
and support" and say they value "diverse minds, unique approaches, and shared wins". Uniquely among
this batch, they publish the *behavioural* bar they hire against and state it carries equal weight to
technical skill — "Technical ability gets you in the room. How you work determines whether you're
hired & thrive here." The full stated set, verbatim:
- **Ownership:** "You follow through, raise issues, and see things to resolution. Completing the task
  is your baseline, not your finish line."
- **Proactivity:** "You flag risks and refine approaches before being asked."
- **Inquisitiveness:** "You are curious about the product, the system, and the patient impact behind.
  You are keen to continue using AI in the development process."
- **Adaptability:** "You make good judgement calls when requirements are unclear and priorities
  shift. You find a way forward without having to be told."
- **Advocacy:** "You bring a quality engineering mindset into every conversation, not just the test
  phase."
- **Root cause thinking:** "Yes finding issues matter. Understanding why it happened, & preventing it
  in future, matters more."
- **Stay steady under pressure/ regular change:** "Make sound decisions with incomplete information,
  and adapt quickly when priorities shift without losing sight of quality code."

Size / stage / funding: "Founded in Dublin in 2017, Deciphex has scaled rapidly to a team of over 230
people." Private; funding stage and profitability not stated on the pages available. Self-described
as "a fast-growth company with real momentum".

Locations / HQ: **HQ Dublin, Ireland** — DCU Alpha Building, Innovation Campus, 11 Old Finglas Rd,
Glasnevin. Other sites per their careers page: Naperville, Illinois (Digital Accessioning Center);
Exeter, UK (Digital Accessioning Center); Kidlington, UK (clinical laboratory, Diagnexia UK Ltd);
**Mississauga, Ontario, Canada** (Digital Accessioning Center); Basel, Switzerland (Digital
Accessioning Center). The JD adds that the team is "spread across Europe and North America" and
mentions offices in "Dublin, Exeter, Oxford, and Chicago".

Remote policy: Remote-friendly for engineering, but **geographically restricted**. The Senior
Software Engineer (Java) posting states its work environment as: "Remote role - living & working full
time in either Ireland or Romania only." They also offer "Flexible remote working with autonomy to
manage your own work-life balance". So the model is genuine remote work, confined to two countries.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — explicitly excluded.** The role requires "living & working full
time in either Ireland or Romania only", and adds "Regrettably, we are unable to offer visa or work
permit sponsorship for Romania OR Ireland. Applicants must already hold valid work permit." Deciphex
does have a Canadian site (Mississauga, ON), but that is a Digital Accessioning Center — a
specimen/slide-digitisation operation, not a software engineering hub — and it is in Eastern Time on
the far side of the country from Kimberley, BC. There is no evidence of any Canada-based remote
engineering employment.

Engineering & tech: Java-centric backend in a regulated medical-device context. Stated stack: Java
(certification a plus); "Strong Spring ecosystem experience — Spring Boot and Spring Cloud (Gateway,
Streams, Security/OAuth2, Spring Data)"; event-driven architecture with Apache Kafka; distributed
caching (Hazelcast); web services, microservices and REST APIs; Docker and Kubernetes; AWS; MongoDB
Atlas and Mongo Lucene; Terraform, Skaffold, Helm; CI/CD, testing frameworks and build tools; Python
and JavaScript secondary. Notably they build **both** "on-premise components" and "cloud
microservices" — a hybrid deployment model driven by hospital/pharma customers. Teams are described
as "mature cross functional agile teams" working on "a hugely successful flagship product". Mandatory
compliance training is part of the role (QMS, ISMS, GxP, SDLC). They are emphatic about AI-assisted
development: "As an AI native team we use a variety of AI tools in our SDLC. We expect you to do the
same", naming Claude Code and BMAD, and requiring candidates to have "moved beyond theory to actually
doing something with AI". Seniority bar: 6/7+ years, and a Bachelor's or higher in CS/IS is stated as
essential.

Notable / other: Stated benefits are qualitative rather than material — "A true sense of meaning in
your work by contributing to better patient outcomes", "Challenging, unique projects at the
intersection of AI, pathology, and regulated software", "Regular performance feedback and significant
career development opportunities", "A highly collaborative, multicultural team spread across Europe
and North America". Employment type is Full-Time, Permanent. Equal-opportunities employer statement
present.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (Java) — remote within Ireland or Romania
only, no pay posted.

Compensation on record: none. No range is posted on the JD (Ireland and Romania have no pay-
transparency requirement of the kind that would force one), and no company pay page was found. No
citable band exists, and none should be invented — a European-scoped salary would in any case not be
comparable to the Canadian bands this registry is built around.
