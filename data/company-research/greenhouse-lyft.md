# Lyft — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:lyft | company_type: product

What they do: Lyft is a publicly traded (NASDAQ: LYFT) North American rideshare and transportation
network company — ride-hailing marketplace, bikes and scooters, Lyft Business (enterprise/corporate
travel and healthcare transport), an advertising business (Lyft Ads / Lyft Media), and autonomous
vehicle partnerships. The engineering problems are large-scale marketplace, pricing, mapping,
fulfilment/dispatch, risk and ads systems.

How they describe themselves: Lyft's postings emphasise in-person collaboration as a cultural
commitment — roles are structured around "foster[ing] a collaborative work environment and company
culture", with office attendance framed as "an essential function of this hybrid role." Compensation
language is transparent and formulaic: "The expected base pay range for this position in the Toronto
area is… not inclusive of potential equity offering, bonus or benefits. Salary ranges are dependent on
a variety of factors, including qualifications, experience and geographic location."

Size / stage / funding: Public company, multi-billion revenue, several thousand employees. Post-IPO
and now profitable on an adjusted basis, but has been through repeated restructurings and layoffs
since 2022 and operates in a competitive duopoly with Uber. Stable in the sense of being public and
capitalised; not stable in the sense of headcount.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco. Major engineering hubs in San Francisco, Seattle, New York,
**Toronto**, and Nashville, plus an offshore presence (a Staff Software Engineer role is listed
"Ukraine Anywhere").

Remote policy: **Hybrid, mandatory in-office.** Stated verbatim on the postings: "This role will be
in-office on a hybrid schedule — Team Members will be expected to work in the office at least 3 days
per week, including on Mondays, Wednesdays, and [Thursdays]", and "working in the office at least 3
days per week [is] an essential function of this hybrid role." Recruiters are directed to describe
"in-office perks."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **NO.** All thirteen relevant live postings are "Toronto, Canada" and
every one carries the 3-days-per-week in-office mandate on fixed anchor days. It is a Canadian
employer, but there is no remote option — for a candidate in Kimberley, British Columbia who cannot
relocate, this is disqualifying. The one non-Toronto role in the set (Staff Software Engineer) is
"Ukraine Anywhere" and equally unavailable.

Engineering & tech: Substantial scale problems and several genuinely infrastructure-flavoured teams —
Marketplace, Pricing (optimisation and algorithms), Fulfillment, Core Maps, Ad Infra (advertising
serving infrastructure), Growth Platforms (backend), Data Engineering, Risk/Insurance ML, and
Autonomous Vehicle. ML is a first-class discipline with dedicated MLE roles across Recommendations,
Mapping, Lyft Business & Ads, and AI Entries. The Toronto hub carries real ownership rather than being
a satellite support office.

Notable / other: The engineering work (marketplace optimisation, pricing, mapping, ad infrastructure)
is technically strong and the ML/AI roles are real modelling and systems work rather than wrappers.
Toronto CAD bands are meaningfully below the equivalent US bands, as is standard for Lyft.

Open relevant roles (sample, all Toronto): Senior Software Engineer, Marketplace; Senior Software
Engineer, Pricing; Senior Software Engineer - Ad Infra, Lyft Ads; Senior Machine Learning Engineer,
Recommendations; Senior Software Engineer, AI Entries; Data Engineer, Pricing; Software Engineer, Core
Maps; Software Engineer (Backend), Growth Platforms; Software Engineer, Fulfillment; Machine Learning
Engineer, Lyft Business & Ads.

Posted pay (their own JDs, Toronto area, CAD base, excluding equity/bonus): Senior MLE
Recommendations $149,600–$187,000; Senior SWE Marketplace $136,000–$170,000; Senior SWE Pricing
$136,000–$170,000; Senior SWE Ad Infra $136,000–$170,000; Senior SWE AI Entries $136,000–$170,000;
MLE Lyft Business & Ads $118,800–$148,500; mid-level SWE roles (Pricing, Core Maps, Fulfillment, Ad
Infra, Growth Platforms, Data Engineer Pricing) $108,000–$135,000.
