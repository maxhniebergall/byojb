# Inspiration Commerce Group (ICG) — research
Provider/key: ashby:inspiration-commerce-group | company_type: product

What they do: A commerce holding group that buys and builds e-commerce companies — "we buy and
build companies that help people find and buy the products they love." The portfolio spans luxury
fashion marketplaces (italist, HEWI, Verishop), affiliate-marketing platforms (Stylight,
MyBestBrands), B2B technology (Bonsai), and niche marketplaces in the creator and construction
spaces (Everpress, DOZR). The engineering role in question works across three of these: italist,
HEWI and DOZR.

How they describe themselves: They publish their operating principles verbatim in the JD, and they
are unusually blunt:
- "You're competitive and love to win… You have a high bar (some would say 'unrealistic') for
  yourself and those you work with."
- "You do whatever it takes. You're willing to do what others won't to achieve what others can't.
  What we're doing is hard. Really hard. **Sacrifices are required in the pursuit of greatness**,
  and we're chasing greatness."
- "Founder-level ownership. If something breaks, you fix it… You (and we) never say 'that's not my
  job'."
- "Curiosity with teeth."
- "You ask for forgiveness, not permission."
- "You're a self-starter… You never let roadblocks get in the way."
- "You have excellent analytical skills. The term 'quantitative rigour' gets you excited."
They also state explicitly who the role is *not* for: "People who want a stable, predictable or
repetitive role/set of tasks"; "People looking for active mentorship"; "People who find comfort in
following a playbook"; "People who prefer stability over speed."

Size / stage / funding: "~50 people", "6 acquisitions in 18 months", "scaled from $0 to >$100M in
revenue." Self-describes as "early enough that everyone's work makes a big difference on the
company's trajectory." Funding structure not stated; acquisition-driven roll-up.

Locations / HQ: "A global group with businesses across the US, UK, and EU." The hiring process
anchors on Toronto (see below). The posting's location field lists "Toronto, EU, New York, UK."

Remote policy: "This is a full-time, remote-first role — you can be located anywhere in the world."
Also: "we operate Remote-first with some time spent In-Person together throughout the year as
needed." On hours: "we trust team members to manage their own schedules… That said, we're early
enough that everyone's work makes a big difference… our team finds hard work fulfilling when we
know it will be impactful."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes, apparently unrestricted** — "you can be located anywhere in the
world" is about as permissive as it gets, and Toronto is the process's home base. One caveat: the
interview loop includes "1-day working session (paid, on-site in Toronto, if you're outside of
Toronto we'll fly you in)", so expect at least one trip. Whether "anywhere in the world" means
employment or contractor engagement is not stated — unverified.

Engineering & tech: Full-stack JavaScript — React on the frontend, Node on the backend, across
"customer-facing storefront features, internal admin tools, third-party integrations, data
pipelines and automations." **There is no product manager**: "This is not a role where a product
manager hands you a ticket… You'll talk directly to the people who need something built, figure out
what actually needs to exist, then build it, test it, deploy it, and validate that it's working in
production… You own the outcome, not the pull request." Explicitly AI-native: "you'll work with
Claude Code or another AI copilot as your daily driver — not autocomplete, but genuine agentic
workflows… We expect AI to make you dramatically faster and more rigorous, and we invest in the
tooling to make that true." Engineers rotate across businesses week to week: "Three businesses,
three domains, one engineer who can context-switch between them."

Notable / other: Hiring process is fast and stated up front — "everyone will hear back within 3 days
of applying", then 2-3 calls plus the paid on-site working session. No compensation figure is stated
anywhere in the posting or in the Ashby comp fields (all null).
