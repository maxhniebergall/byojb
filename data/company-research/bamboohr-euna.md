# Euna Solutions — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:euna | company_type: product

What they do: In their own words, *"Euna Solutions® is the leading provider of purpose-built, cloud-based software designed to streamline procurement, budgeting, payments, and grants management for public sector and government organizations. Euna's AI-powered features and intelligent automation help organizations make better-informed decisions, ensure compliance, empower collaboration, and reduce administrative burden. Euna's full-cycle financial suite supports more than 3,600 organizations across North America in building trust, enabling transparency, and driving positive community impact."* It is a govtech (B2G) financial-software suite — procurement/marketplace, budgeting, payments and grants — sold to municipalities, school districts and agencies. Recognised on Government Technology's **GovTech 100** list.

How they describe themselves: their careers page frames the mission as driving *"more efficient spending of taxpayer dollars and meaningful connection between citizens and the agencies that serve them."* Full stated values:
- **We Are One** — team unity
- **We Drive Results** — goal-focused execution
- **We Are Growth Oriented** — continuous improvement and diverse perspectives

Culture language from the careers page: a culture that *"experiments, adapts, and uses AI to punch above its weight"* and is focused on *"figuring out better ways to do things"*; they emphasise flexible time off, work-life balance, and that employees should *"leave work at work."* The JD adds: *"we carefully foster a work environment where employees have a safe space for creative and intellectual freedom, and the opportunity to work cross-functionally. We offer a dynamic environment with considerable opportunities for professional growth and advancement."* Notable perk wording: *"We pay competitive wages and salaries, and we only expect an honest 40-hour week for it"*, twice-yearly wellness days appended to long weekends, a community-engagement committee, and a culture committee running frequent events.

Size / stage / funding: Headcount not disclosed on any page read. Serves 3,600+ public-sector organisations across North America. Registry shows **37 total open postings, 6 relevant** — a mid-sized company actively hiring. It is a multi-product roll-up (procurement, budgeting, payments, grants are distinguishable product lines with separate teams), which typically indicates private-equity backing, but **ownership and funding are not disclosed on the careers page and are unverified here**.

Locations / HQ: Two offices stated on the careers page —
- **US HQ:** 1155 Perimeter Center West, Suite 500, Sandy Springs, GA 30338
- **Canada:** 603 Michigan Drive, Unit 1, Oakville, ON L6L 0G2

Remote policy: **Hybrid, explicitly.** The careers page states a minimum of **three days in-office and two days remote per week, across both the U.S. and Canada.** The Software Developer JD is more specific still: *"This position will be hybrid with 3 days/week in our Oakville, ON office. (Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday)"*. The "flexible workday" perk refers to hours, not location: *"We understand that what a workday looks like differs by employee and the role requirements."*

Remote-Canada eligibility: **They employ in Canada, but the roles are not remote-Canada.** Euna clearly has a Canadian legal entity and hires into Ontario. However the stated policy is hybrid 3-days-in-office everywhere, and the live engineering posting names the specific in-office days at the Oakville, Ontario site. A candidate in British Columbia who cannot relocate is **not eligible** for this posting. Nothing on the pages read offers a fully-remote exception for engineering.

Engineering & tech: Stack named in the JD: **Node.js, TypeScript, JavaScript, React, AngularJS, PHP, Python, MySQL**, on **AWS**, with SQL throughout. Testing and accessibility tooling: **Jest, Mocha, Cypress, axe**. Process tooling: JIRA, git, scrum/Kanban. The Procurement team builds a *"first-in-class business-to-government marketplace."* Engineers are expected to work full-stack ("crafting front-end web apps or back-end services"), write tests and monitor real-time platform performance, and do code review and documentation. Bar is comparatively junior: **2+ years** of software development experience.

AI posture: unusually prominent. The JD asks engineers to *"operate, improve, and coordinate in an AI agentic workflow and AI tooling across the team and organization"* and lists **"2+ years implementing AI/ML agentic solutions in business environments"** as an essential qualification. Their stated stance: *"We believe the future of work is human + AI. At Euna Solutions, we encourage our team members to leverage AI tools to enhance creativity, efficiency, and decision-making."* They also disclose that *"Euna uses AI responsibly to assist in screening, assessing, or selecting applicants. AI helps us work smarter, but people lead the process."*

Notable / other: Pay transparency is a stated policy — *"We believe in openness, so we publish our range from the start."* The posting notes it *"fills an existing vacancy"* (backfill, not headcount growth). Accessibility accommodations are offered through recruiting@eunasolutions.com / 1.877.707.7755.
