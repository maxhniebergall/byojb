What they do: Global financial-services firm — investment banking, securities, wealth management and investment management. Several of the live engineering roles sit in **Parametric**, part of Morgan Stanley Investment Management, which builds custom/systematic portfolios and has been "a leader in custom solutions for more than 30 years". Other live roles sit in the core firm's technology org (Montreal, London, Bengaluru, Hong Kong).

How they describe themselves: Their stated core values, quoted from their own JD boilerplate: "putting clients first, doing the right thing, leading with exceptional ideas, committing to diversity and inclusion, and giving back". They add: "At Morgan Stanley, you'll find an opportunity to work alongside the best and the brightest, in an environment where you are supported and empowered. Our teams are relentless collaborators and creative thinkers… There's also ample opportunity to move about the business for those who show passion and grit in their work." Tone is large-institution, stability- and mobility-oriented, not startup-hustle.

Size / stage / funding: Public company (NYSE: MS). Their own boilerplate states "more than 80,000 employees in 1,200 offices across 42 countries" and "offices in more than 41 countries". Highly stable, profitable, established — the opposite of runway-dependent.

Locations / HQ: HQ New York. Live engineering postings in this tenant are located in Montreal (Canada), London (UK), Seattle (WA, Parametric), Bengaluru (India) and Hong Kong.

Remote policy: **Hybrid, explicitly.** The Parametric roles state: "This role is part of Parametric's hybrid working model, which includes working in the office 3 days a week and choosing to work remotely or in the office the remaining days of the week." Every Canadian posting in the registry is tagged "(Hybrid)" in the title itself and located in Montreal, and the extracted facets for the Montreal roles record `remote_policy: hybrid`.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Effectively no.** Canada-located roles exist (Montreal) and are Canada-eligible in the immigration sense, but they are *Montreal hybrid* — office attendance is required, and Montreal is Eastern Time. There is no fully-remote-within-Canada posting in this tenant. For a candidate in Kimberley BC who cannot relocate, these roles are not takeable. The US roles (Seattle Parametric) are likewise hybrid 3 days/week and would additionally require US work authorization.

Engineering & tech: Parametric's Platform Engineering team sits in "Parametric IT's Developer Experience department" and does genuinely well-scoped platform work: consulting with cloud-native (AWS, Azure) and self-hosted app teams, automating non-production environment provisioning, owning development toolchains and CI/CD pipelines, Kubernetes container orchestration, and observability via Datadog, Splunk and Sentry. Required skills: Terraform IaC, GitHub + YAML CI/CD pipelines, Kubernetes, SAST/SCA vulnerability tooling integrated into the SDLC, Python/BASH/PowerShell scripting, and "Experience using Gen AI tooling for software development and testing productivity". Elsewhere in the tenant: Java/agentic-AI lead roles (Bengaluru, London), Unix/SRE, cybersecurity data pipelines (Montreal), and a large Java developer pipeline req in Montreal.

Notable / other: Roles are graded on the bank ladder — "VP", "Associate", "Specialist" — rather than Staff/Senior IC titles, so title-to-level mapping is bank-specific (VP ≈ senior/staff IC or lead). Benefits are extensive and stated in the JDs (medical, 401(k), 20 vacation days, 10 paid holidays, parental leave, disability).

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Platform Engineer – Parametric (Seattle); Senior Research Software Engineer – Parametric (Seattle); Data Pipeline Engineering Specialist (Cybersecurity) (Hybrid) (Montreal); SRE Associate (Hybrid) (Montreal); Java Developer, multiple opportunities (Montreal); Agentic AI Java Lead Software Engineer – VP (Bengaluru); Senior Software Engineer – VP (London).
