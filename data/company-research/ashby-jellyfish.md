# Jellyfish — research
Provider/key: ashby:jellyfish | company_type: product
What they do: Jellyfish sells an engineering-management/intelligence platform — an analytics product that ingests data from engineering tooling and tells R&D leadership where effort is going and what is driving impact. Their own description: "Jellyfish is the leading intelligence platform for AI-Integrated engineering, helping more than 1,000 companies including DraftKings, Keller Williams and Blue Yonder, leverage AI to transform how they build software. By combining the industry's deepest engineering dataset with context-rich intelligence, Jellyfish helps R&D organizations understand what's driving impact, adopt proven industry best practices, and make smarter decisions across AI adoption, planning, delivery, and engineering performance." Elsewhere: "The team at Jellyfish is changing how product development happens, helping engineering teams learn how to work together and communicate more effectively," and "Jellyfish is rewriting the manual on how high-performing engineering teams actually work within an AI world." Customers are engineering/R&D organizations; the buyer is typically a VP Eng or CTO.
How they describe themselves: Their stated hiring philosophy: "A list of job experiences and qualification requirements is great, but **humility, a performance-driven attitude, and a team-player approach** are most important to us. We love to have fun and win in the process. We only hire people who have a passion for building great companies in an environment where a sense of humor is a must." They repeatedly emphasize **high agency**: "an exceptional engineering organization with high agency: where you can help shape the features your team builds and how your team builds them," and for the staff role "a high-agency role where you'll help shape not just what gets built, but how we build it — and raise the bar for the engineers around you." They also foreground AI-first internal practice: "AI-driven developer who dives deep into AI first workflows," and "develop internal tooling and agentic workflows to accelerate internal development." A recurring bonus qualification is "You have worked at a rapidly scaling startup before and loved it" / "You've thrived at a rapidly scaling startup and know how to balance speed with technical rigor."
Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, self-described as "rapidly scaling," serving "more than 1,000 companies." Founded in the Boston area. Exact headcount, funding stage and profitability not verified — no first-party page was fetched (the Ashby board is client-rendered and no company root domain is derivable from the registry).
Locations / HQ: Boston, Massachusetts (not directly verified from the JDs, which are all remote-tagged). All registry postings are tagged "Remote - US."
Remote policy: **Remote-US, with a timezone restriction.** Every posting is "Remote - US" and every JD carries the requirement "**Located in EST or CST time zone**." They also note "Occasional travel may be required."
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — verified negative, on two independent grounds.** (1) Work authorization: "Applicants must be authorized to work for any employer in the US. We are unable to sponsor or take over sponsorship of an employment visa at this time." (2) Timezone: "Located in EST or CST time zone" is a stated requirement on every posting — Mountain Time is excluded even for a US-authorized candidate. Both lines appear verbatim in multiple JDs.
Engineering & tech: Substantively a data-engineering shop despite the "backend" titles. Python is the core language ("strong programmer with extensive Python experience," "expert Python engineer"). The work: building and scaling data pipelines for storage, transformation and export; integrations with third-party developer tools (Jira, GitHub, GitLab, incident-management tools, AI coding assistants) and the API clients that ingest from them; infrastructure as code with **Terraform** (the staff role owns IaC strategy and treats "infrastructure as a product, not an afterthought"); workflow orchestration for near-real-time and batch processing using **Prefect, Airflow or Dagster**; data export pipelines serving diverse customer needs; internal tooling and agentic workflows to accelerate development. Bonus signals: Delta Lake and lakehouse architectures, AI/LLM tool integrations (Claude, Copilot). Staff-level responsibilities are explicitly architectural and organizational: defining long-term technical direction, setting integration standards, leading design reviews, mentoring, partnering with engineering leadership on roadmap prioritization and org-wide technical strategy, and writing design docs.
Notable / other: **Pay is posted on every role and is consistently good.** Live: Senior Backend Software Engineer $165K-$235K + equity; Staff Data Engineer $200K-$260K + equity. Recently-closed postings show the same ladder: Senior Data Engineer $190K-$240K; Senior Platform Engineer $165K-$235K; Senior Software Engineer, Data Products $165K-$235K; Senior Data and AI Platform Engineer $150K-$230K; Data Engineer (unspecified level) $165K-$205K. This is unusually consistent and transparent banding across a whole ladder. The volume of recently-closed data/platform postings alongside the live ones suggests steady hiring in this area.
