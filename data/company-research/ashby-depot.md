# Depot — research
Provider/key: ashby:depot | company_type: product
What they do: Depot builds developer infrastructure that makes CI and container builds fast. The product surface spans Depot CI (a programmable CI engine that runs GitHub Actions workflows, claimed up to 10x faster), remote Docker/container builds with automatic layer caching (claimed up to 40x faster), hosted GitHub Actions runners on AWS at roughly half the cost, Depot Cache (distributed remote caching for Bazel, Turborepo, sccache), a registry, a Build API for platforms that build images on behalf of their own customers, plus SDKs and a CLI. Named customers on their homepage include PostHog, PlanetScale, Semgrep, Astral, Secoda, Wistia and Poolside.
How they describe themselves: "Our job is to make hard infrastructure feel fast, dependable, and easy for developers"; "fast by design". Their "How we work" section is unusually explicit: "Depot is fully remote and distributed across the US, Canada, and Europe. We rely on clear writing, async collaboration, and people who can create clarity without waiting for perfect instructions." "We are a small team trying to do unusually ambitious work. Ownership matters here: you make choices, own outcomes, and share what you learn when those choices work or do not." "Work is not family. We care about excellent work and meeting commitments, and we also expect people to have real lives outside of Depot." "We make decisions with customer context and data. Strong opinions are useful; clear evidence and good judgment are better." On hiring they say "We care as much about how you work as what you know. You should listen well, write clearly, handle disagreement without creating drag, and build trust through judgment and follow-through" and "Direct experience with our exact stack helps, but it is not the bar."
Size / stage / funding: Small team, Y Combinator-backed. Exact headcount and funding round not verified from their own pages.
Locations / HQ: No office — fully distributed across the US, Canada and Europe.
Remote policy: Remote-only, explicitly async-first ("clear writing, async collaboration"). The posting states: "Depot is remote-only. For this role we can currently support hiring within North America and Europe."
Remote-Canada eligibility: VERIFIED YES. The company states in its own JD that the team is "distributed across the US, Canada, and Europe" and that this role can be hired "within North America and Europe". No province restriction is named. Timezone: a US/Canada/Europe spread means Mountain Time sits at the western edge, which is generally favourable for async.
Engineering & tech: TypeScript, Go, React, protobuf/gRPC-style APIs (Connect, HTTP/JSON), MySQL/PlanetScale, ClickHouse, AWS, OpenTelemetry. Work spans the web console, APIs, CLI, GitHub integrations, billing and usage, permissions, docs and internal tools. Helpful background cited: CI systems, GitHub Actions, build tooling, Docker/BuildKit, remote caching, auth/SSO, billing, observability. Practices: design docs, code review, mentorship, measuring whether work made customers faster.
Notable / other: Compensation stated on the Ashby posting: $100K–$250K USD (a very wide band that they say varies with seniority/location). Equal-opportunity statement with an explicit encouragement to apply without matching every bullet and an accommodations offer. The Staff role expects roughly 7+ years of production engineering experience.
