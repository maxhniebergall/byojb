What they do: Syllo builds an AI-powered litigation workspace for law firms. In their words: "Syllo is on a mission to transform litigation. Our product is a unified litigation platform that enables lawyers and paralegals to safely harness the power of language models and agentic AI throughout the litigation life cycle." They sell to enterprise legal: "Since going to market, we have gained a diverse group of enterprise customers, including some of the biggest law firms and corporations in the country, and we are quickly expanding."

How they describe themselves: Explicitly mission-driven, with a stated societal thesis: "By reducing the expense of litigation industry-wide, we aim to improve access to high-quality representation and promote the alignment of legal outcomes with merit," and "We are a mission-driven venture and recognize the enormous societal benefits that will result from a legal system that works better for everyone." The engineering tone is startup-paced — they seek someone who "love[s] working in a dynamic startup environment" and describe one opening as seeking a "skilled and fast-moving" senior engineer. On the platform side the framing is more disciplined: they want a Staff Engineer who will bring "a FinOps mindset, treating cost as a first-class engineering concern, while fostering a collaborative, high-performing environment where engineers can grow, learn, and thrive," with the nice internal symmetry that "just as Syllo reduces the cost of litigation for our customers, this role ensures we run our own platform with the same discipline." No formal published values list is available.

Size / stage / funding: Private venture-backed startup, post-go-to-market and "quickly expanding." Funding stage, investors and headcount are not stated anywhere in the available material — unverified. The presence of a Staff-level FinOps/cost-ownership role implies cloud spend has grown large enough to need dedicated ownership, which suggests real production scale rather than pre-product.

Engineering & tech: Named stack and practices, mostly from the Staff Systems role. Cloud is **GCP**. Infrastructure runs on Kubernetes and Pub/Sub, with "cloud tools and distributed systems, such as Kubernetes, Kafka, Postgres, and Elasticsearch." Languages named are **Python and Ruby**, plus "strong system administration experience, including fluency with Linux, networking, and bash scripting." The Systems role's work is squarely platform: "Design, implement, and manage scalable cloud-based infrastructure"; "Refactor existing systems to enhance scalability, including transforming stateful components into stateless ones, and applying extensive autoscaling and auto-repair mechanisms"; "Drive platform reliability initiatives, including enhancing alerting, health checking, and incident management"; "Collaborate with our AI team to optimize data pipelines and integrate AI technologies to remove performance bottlenecks"; and "Ensure the platform meets rigorous testing and security standards." It also states "Participate in an on-call rotation to ensure meeting SLAs." The Backend role is more product-adjacent: "Design, build, and maintain Syllo's backend systems and APIs to support our data platform" and "Collaborate closely with front-end engineers, product managers, and data teams." There is a separate AI/inference track (Staff Software Engineer, AI Inference) and a non-engineering "AI Workflows Training Lead."

Notable / other: Pay is posted on every engineering role, which is unusual and welcome — Staff Systems $190,000–$230,000 USD ("plus health insurance and equity"), Staff AI Inference $190,000–$230,000, Senior AI $170,000–$220,000, Senior Backend $160,000–$200,000. The company's cost discipline is a stated engineering value rather than just a finance concern. Legal-tech means enterprise law-firm customers, confidentiality-sensitive data and correspondingly strict security requirements ("Expertise in cloud data security" is a listed qualification).
