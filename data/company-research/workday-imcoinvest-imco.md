Provider/key: workday:imcoinvest/imco | company_type: product (in-house asset manager; not a vendor/consultancy)
What they do: IMCO is an independent investment and risk-management manager that serves Ontario public-sector institutions (pension plans, insurance funds, endowments) exclusively. It builds diversified portfolios across Fixed Income & Government Bonds, Global Credit, Infrastructure, Private Equity, Public Equities and Real Estate on behalf of its client organizations. It is a Crown-adjacent, non-commercial entity: clients are its owners, not customers it sells to.
