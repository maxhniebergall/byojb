# NetCyberOps (registry name "nco") — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:nco | company_type: product (managed security services)
What they do: NetCyberOps is a managed security services provider (MSSP). Its product is a web application customers use to manage their security posture — triaging security events, tracking remediation, and keeping visibility into active threats. Engineering work described is a Python/Flask REST API plus backend services for security-event ingestion, processing and delivery.
How they describe themselves: "a managed security services company known for the quality of our work"; "Security and quality come first here, and we prioritize them over speed." They ask for engineers who "own a feature from start to finish", care about details, and "have a point of view on good API design" — "You're expected to have opinions, not just implement what you're told." Notably free of hustle/rocketship language.
