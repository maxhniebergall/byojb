# G-Research — research
Provider/key: workday:gresearch/g-research | company_type: product
What they do: G-Research is a leading quantitative finance research and technology firm. In its own words: "We tackle the most complex problems in quantitative finance, by bringing scientific clarity to financial complexity." Engineering exists to build the research platform: "As part of our engineering team, you'll shape the platforms and tools that drive high-impact research - designing systems that scale, accelerate discovery and support innovation across the firm."
How they describe themselves: The tone is unusually aligned with deep, patient engineering: "From our London HQ, we unite world-class researchers and engineers in an environment that values deep exploration and methodical execution - because the best ideas take time to evolve." Roles are explicitly framed as leverage-building rather than feature-shipping: "suited to someone who enjoys creating leverage through shared engineering patterns rather than building a single product feature set." No hustle or hype vocabulary at all. AI-assisted engineering is an explicit stated priority ("Leveraging AI and AI assisted engineering to accelerate productivity and value").
Size / stage / funding: Large, long-established, privately held and highly profitable quantitative firm; thousands of employees. Not funding-dependent — one of the most stable employer profiles in this batch.
Locations / HQ: London, UK — stated as HQ in the JDs, and all 19 live relevant postings are listed "London, UK". (The firm also has a Dallas presence publicly, but no US or Canadian posting appears in the registry.)
Remote policy: None stated in the JDs; every relevant posting is London-anchored, and out-of-hours on-call rotations are named ("Participating on the out of hours on-call rotation"), implying a co-located team.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — all 19 relevant postings are London, UK. No remote or Canada-eligible listing exists in the registry, and nothing in the JDs suggests remote hiring. London hours are MT+7/8, which would also be a hostile timezone. Effectively disqualifying.
Engineering & tech: Platform-heavy and squarely in the candidate's territory — Kubernetes platform engineering, database platform, Elastic platform, analytics services platform, observability instrumentation (OpenTelemetry SDKs, Collectors, Prometheus/PromQL, VictoriaMetrics, OpenSearch, Grafana, distributed tracing, structured logging, Kafka telemetry pipelines), DevOps tooling (Terraform, ArgoCD, Helm, Jenkins), security software engineering, realtime quant frameworks, custom I/O, engineering tools, and ML/Core AI engineering. Primary languages C# and Python.

None of the 19 postings states pay — UK postings are not covered by pay-transparency law. Compensation at top quant firms is well above general software market, weighted heavily toward bonus.
