What they do: Property and casualty insurance in Canada (and internationally). The engineering org
in the registry is a corporate IT / in-house technology function rather than a software product
company: security product development, IAM, test automation, SRE, DevOps developer-experience, RPA
(UiPath/PowerAutomate), and an AI Engineering team building a GenAI platform.

How they describe themselves: Their own recruiting language: "a workplace where you can shape the
future, win as a team and grow with us", and "Pay at Intact is about much more than just salary."
They name "respect" as a stated corporate Value ("At Intact, our Value of respect is..."). The full
published values set and any careers/culture page could NOT be retrieved this session — no
first-party URL exists in the registry — so anything beyond the JD language is UNVERIFIED.

Size / stage / funding: Large, publicly traded, long-established Canadian insurer. This is the
"stable, boring, mission-critical" end of the spectrum rather than a venture-funded company. Exact
headcount not verified from a fetched source this session.

Locations / HQ: Canadian. The postings sit in Toronto, Ontario and Montreal, Quebec, with some reqs
spanning 2-4 Canadian locations.

Remote policy: Hybrid, company-wide. Every JD's rewards block states "Flexible work arrangements and
a hybrid work model", and every single posting in the registry carries the `#LI-Hybrid` tag. No JD in
the batch carries `#LI-Remote`.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO. All 10 captured postings are anchored to Toronto or Montreal offices
and universally tagged #LI-Hybrid; none states a work-from-anywhere-in-Canada option. The JDs do
confirm Canadian employment generally ("must be eligible to work in Canada", "No Canadian work
experience required") — but that is work authorization, not location flexibility. Additionally,
Quebec-based roles carry a hard bilingualism requirement: "for positions located in Quebec,
bilingualism is required considering the necessity to interact on a regular basis with English
speaking colleagues across the country."

Engineering & tech: AWS, MLOps, AgentCore, GenAI and agentic AI (Senior AI Developer); a GenAI
platform team with a dedicated advisor role and a Senior IT Business Analyst supporting the AI
Engineering team; SRE and DevOps within a "DX COE" (developer-experience centre of excellence); IAM
and security-product development; test automation; RPA. Orchestration frameworks named in the AI
roles: CrewAI, AutoGen "or similar".

Notable / other: No compensation figure appears in any of the 10 captured JDs — Intact publishes no
range, and the rewards block deflects on the point ("Pay at Intact is about much more than just
salary"). Stated non-salary benefits: flexible work arrangements and a hybrid model, the option to
purchase up to 5 extra days off per year, and wellbeing benefits including telemedicine and a
wellness account. Several reqs are described as newly created roles on growing teams.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior AI Developer (AWS, MLOps, AgentCore, GenAI, agentic AI); GenAI
platform Advisor; SRE specialist; Senior Software Developer - IAM; Software Developer Specialist -
Security Product; Software Developer II (Test Automation).
