# Miris — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:miris | company_type: product

What they do: Miris builds **AI-optimised 3D/spatial streaming infrastructure** — a platform that ingests 3D assets, processes them, and streams them adaptively to any device without downloads or server-side rendering. Their pitch is "**one asset, every device**". In their own words: "Miris is a cutting-edge technology company building the future of 3D content delivery at global scale. Our mission is to empower creators and developers to deliver high-fidelity, photorealistic 3D experiences to billions of users instantly, seamlessly, and across all major platforms and devices. Backed by patented innovations, Miris leverages state-of-the-art advancements in **neural rendering, including radiance fields, Gaussian splatting, and new AI-assisted graphics approaches**." A JD frames the mission as "**removing the barrier to entry for spatial content**".

Product surfaces: Platform (asset upload, processing, streaming engine), Web SDK (three.js and custom integrations), REST API and CLI, Portal (asset and viewer-key management), Player and Playground. Stated benefits: first frame in under 2 seconds, native-resolution streaming with automatic level-of-detail, cross-platform delivery without manual optimisation, and **pay-per-stream pricing "without GPU infrastructure maintenance"**. Target industries: retail/e-commerce product visualisation, media & entertainment (asset review, virtual production), and physical AI / robotics (digital-twin delivery).

How they describe themselves: On compensation — "At Miris, our compensation philosophy is designed to attract, motivate, and retain top talent. We offer **competitive and fair pay that reflects the value of each role** within our organization and the broader market. We believe that every employee who brings impact should share in our success and reflect that with **generous equity options**." On structure — "**As a remote-first company with a globally distributed team**, we tailor compensation to ensure fairness and alignment across diverse markets." Careers framing: a "**small team solving tough spatial streaming**" problems. Notably, no hustle/rocketship vocabulary appears anywhere in their own copy.

Size / stage / funding: **Early — the platform is described as in beta**, and the team is self-described as small. Funding amount and headcount are **not disclosed** on their site and were not verified. Named partners/customers/ecosystem: **Tapcart, NVIDIA, AWS, CoreWeave**, plus three.js, Unity and NVIDIA Omniverse. They claim patented innovations.

Locations / HQ: **Not disclosed** on the site. Both live postings are tagged simply "Remote".

Remote policy: First-party and unambiguous: "**a remote-first company with a globally distributed team**", and they say they "tailor compensation to ensure fairness and alignment across diverse markets" — i.e. they operate location-based pay across multiple countries. Benefits listed: medical/dental/vision, FSA, **flexible working hours**, generous PTO, 401(k), technology stipend, wellness stipend, equity options — with the caveat "**Globally, benefits may vary depending on a candidate's location**", which is a further indication of non-US hiring.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Likely yes, but not explicitly confirmed.** The strongest evidence available short of an explicit statement: "remote-first… globally distributed team", pay "tailored… across diverse markets", and benefits that "vary depending on a candidate's location". No country list, entity or EOR is named, and Canada is not mentioned by name — but unlike most companies in this batch, the US-only framing is absent and the compensation language presupposes multiple markets. The 401(k) in the benefits list indicates a US entity exists as well. Worth confirming directly; the prior probability here is materially better than for the US-only employers in this batch.

Engineering & tech: Real distributed-infrastructure work. From the SRE (Senior+) JD: cloud infrastructure automation with **Terraform**, focused on **CDN optimisation and content-delivery performance**; capacity planning and performance optimisation for "high-volume spatial content delivery"; instrumentation, monitoring dashboards and alerting with **Prometheus and Grafana**; comprehensive observability including **SLI/SLO definition and error-budget management**; **multi-tenant architecture** security, data protection and threat modelling; automated compliance monitoring for **SOC 2, GDPR, ISO 27001 and FOSS licensing**; incident response and post-mortems; establishing reliability practices and code-review/deployment-safety standards within the Web Services team; mentoring DevOps engineers. Cloud platforms named: **AWS Fargate and CoreWeave** (the CoreWeave presence confirms GPU-heavy processing) with **Kubernetes**. Requires 7+ years SRE/DevOps. There is also a graphics/R&D engineering track (Gaussian splatting, neural rendering).

Notable / other: The SRE role includes "help define escalation policies and **participate in on-call rotations to ensure the 24/7 health of Miris pipelines**". Compensation is posted with **extremely wide bands** — SRE (Senior+) $102,693-$287,488 USD, and Graphics/R&D "All Levels" $89,155-$287,488 USD. Both top out at the identical $287,488 figure, which suggests a single company-wide maximum applied across postings rather than a role-specific band; the ranges are wide enough to carry little information about what the role actually pays, and any inference from their midpoints is unreliable.
