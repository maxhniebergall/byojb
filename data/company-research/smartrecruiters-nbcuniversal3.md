# NBCUniversal — research
Provider/key: smartrecruiters:nbcuniversal3 | company_type: product

What they do: NBCUniversal is a **media and entertainment conglomerate**, a wholly-owned subsidiary of **Comcast**. Its businesses include the NBC broadcast network, cable networks (Bravo, USA, Syfy, CNBC, MSNBC), **Universal Pictures**, **DreamWorks Animation**, **Universal Destinations & Experiences** (theme parks), and the **Peacock** streaming service. Engineering spans streaming/video delivery, advertising technology, data platforms, studio and animation technology, corporate/enterprise IT, and increasingly ML/AI applied to media assets.

How they describe themselves: There is no single engineering values statement; the org is federated across business units and reqs read very differently depending on unit (DreamWorks Technology, News Digital, Digital Workplace Technology, Enterprise Data, Peacock/streaming). Roles are written in conventional large-enterprise voice. The Montréal MLOps req is representative of the AI/ML line: *"We are seeking a Staff MLOps Engineer with experience building and scaling infrastructure for large 2D and 3D media datasets. In this role, you will develop and own the backbone of our machine learning lifecycle, ensuring that data pipelines are automated, reproducible, and highly performant at scale."* It asks for *"Minimum of 5+ years of relevant industry experience, ideally within a **fast-paced, high-growth tech environment**"* and *"Proven experience as an MLOps Engineer in a **fast-paced environment**."* Desired attributes: *"Conscientiousness: High attention to detail regarding system reliability and data security"* and *"Systems Thinking: Ability to translate abstract ML requirements into concrete, scalable cloud or on-prem infrastructure."*

Size / stage / funding: Very large and mature — tens of thousands of employees; a division of Comcast (NASDAQ: CMCSA). No funding risk, but the sector is under structural pressure (cord-cutting, streaming losses, studio cost-cutting), and the media industry has seen repeated restructuring and layoffs. Comcast has publicly moved toward spinning off portions of the cable-network portfolio.

Locations / HQ: HQ at 30 Rockefeller Plaza, New York. Major hubs in New York, Universal City / Glendale California, Orlando, London, and **Montréal, Québec**. The live req inventory in the registry is almost entirely **New York** and **California**, plus one Montréal role.

Remote policy: **Mostly office-anchored with "remote" flags that are location-bound.** SmartRecruiters locations read like "New York, NEW YORK, United States, Remote" and "Universal City, CALIFORNIA, United States, Remote" — i.e. remote *within commuting distance of a named hub*, not remote-anywhere. Comcast/NBCU have publicly pushed toward increased in-office attendance. No async or handbook culture signals found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Largely no — with one Québec-specific exception.** All but one relevant live req is US-anchored (New York, Universal City, Glendale). The single Canadian opening is **"Staff MLOps Engineer - Ingénieur(e) MLOps expert(e) (niveau Staff)"**, posted as *Montréal, QC, ca (remote)* and published bilingually in French and English — a Québec role at the Montréal hub, on Eastern Time, with French-language presence in the posting itself. There is no evidence of Canada-wide or British-Columbia hiring, and no MT-workable arrangement is described. Treat this as a Montréal-region role, not a Canada-remote one.

Engineering & tech: Python, Git and Unix shell as core; **Docker, Kubernetes**, workflow orchestrators (**Airflow, Prefect, Kubeflow**); CI/CD plus **CT (continuous training)** pipelines; dataset versioning and storage strategy for massive 2D/3D media datasets; model performance and data-drift monitoring. Elsewhere: data engineering (Principal/Lead Data Engineer), SRE and DevOps (including an SAP BTP SRE lead), WordPress for News Digital, and a DreamWorks "Principal Engineer, AI" role. Ecosystem is Jira/Confluence/Slack. Prior experience in *"robotics, smart grids, precision agriculture, game development, or aerospace"* is called out as valued for the 3D-data work.

Notable / other: NBCU posts salary ranges on its US reqs (New York pay-transparency law), so comp is unusually well documented for a company this size — Principal DevOps Engineer $180k–$230k, Principal Data Engineer $160k–$200k, Staff SWE (SAP BTP SRE Lead) $130k–$170k, Data Engineer (Engineering & Operations) $115k–$145k, all USD base. The DreamWorks Technology AI role is a notable specialist opening. Note the registry shows several duplicate "Senior DevOps Engineer" reqs.
