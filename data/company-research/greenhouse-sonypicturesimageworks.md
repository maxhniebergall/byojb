# Sony Pictures Imageworks — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:sonypicturesimageworks | company_type: product (VFX/animation studio)
What they do: Visual effects and computer animation for feature films and episodic content — a Sony Pictures Entertainment studio. Engineering exists to serve artists: the open roles build "artist-facing tools that enhance the creative and production teams", specifically "playback review tools, asset browsers, and plugins", plus rigging tools.
How they describe themselves: The postings lead with a land acknowledgement — "Sony Pictures Imageworks is located on the unceded traditional territory of the Musqueam, Squamish, and Tsleil-Waututh First Nations. We are committed to respecting traditional lands, and working with communities towards reconciliation." On hiring: "We value unique perspectives, and want diverse, unique talent to work with us. We encourage candidates from all identities to apply." The senior engineering role is framed around mentorship and craft: "Mentor and guide intermediate and associate engineers", "Define initial risks and mitigation strategies for large projects", "Maintain clear documentation for code and technical processes to facilitate knowledge sharing", "Foster a collaborative and positive team environment."
Size / stage / funding: A division of Sony Pictures Entertainment (Sony Group Corporation) — large, public-parent, long-established. Not runway-dependent, though VFX generally is project/slate-driven with cyclical staffing.
Locations / HQ: The Canadian entity is **Sony Pictures Imageworks Canada Inc., 658 Homer Street, Suite 405, Vancouver, BC, V6B 2R4** — stated verbatim in the JD. A second site in Montréal, Québec carries French-language postings ("Développeur(euse) logiciel senior - d'applications").
Remote policy: Not stated as remote anywhere. Postings carry a specific street address and note "Language in work environment - English" (Vancouver) — studio/production work of this kind is conventionally on-site or studio-hybrid, and no remote provision appears in any posting.
Remote-Canada eligibility: Canadian employment is certain (Sony Pictures Imageworks **Canada** Inc., BC-incorporated with a Vancouver address, and a Québec site). **But remote eligibility is unverified and the postings read as location-based** — the Vancouver JD names a physical suite address and states nothing about remote work. Treat as Vancouver-onsite/hybrid unless confirmed. Vancouver is at least in British Columbia, but it is a ~10-hour drive from Kimberley and in Pacific Time, so it is not commutable.
Engineering & tech: Python-first in a UNIX/Linux environment — "Proficiency in Python programming, with experience in a UNIX/Linux environment", plus "Strong knowledge of C/C++ and OpenGL", Git, and the open-source VFX ecosystem ("open-source projects like OTIO and OCIO"). Work is plugin/tooling development for artist workflows, UI/UX for artist-facing tools, and pipeline troubleshooting: "Troubleshoot and resolve critical technical issues that impact the production pipeline." Senior expectation is a bachelor's plus "a minimum of four years of experience in software development".
Compensation: Stated on the JD — "The anticipated base salary for this position is $104,200.00 - $150,000.00/yr (CAD)". Benefits "per company policy: include healthcare, tuition reimbursement, RRSP's, Sick and Vacation leave, standard increases as applicable."
Notable / other: Bilingual operation across Vancouver and Montréal; Montréal roles are posted in French and English. The "production pipeline" is a real, mission-critical internal system, so there is genuine internal-platform character to the work even though the customers are artists.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Application (Vancouver, BC); Développeur(euse) logiciel senior - d'applications // Senior Software Engineer - Application (Montréal, QC); Software Engineer - Rigging; Ingénieur logiciel - Gréement; Associate Software Engineer.
