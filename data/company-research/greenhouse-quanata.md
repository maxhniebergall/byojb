# Quanata — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:quanata | company_type: product
What they do: AI-assisted insurance technology — contextual and behavioural risk modelling and scoring for underwriting, telematics-driven driver-rewards programs, customer-acquisition tooling and claims modernization. Formerly known as BlueOwl; operates the "HiRoad" insurance brand lineage. Legally "Quanata, LLC ... a separate company in the State Farm family of companies" — i.e. an independent tech subsidiary with long-term State Farm backing.
How they describe themselves: "a passion for creating innovative technologies, digital products, and brands", "customer centered". Their JDs are benefits- and structure-forward rather than mission-hype; there is no rocketship/hustle language, but there is also little published engineering-culture writing.
Size / stage / funding: Private, corporate-backed by State Farm rather than VC — unusually stable funding for a tech company of this type, with no runway dynamic. Headcount not published. 12 open roles at scan time (a small, targeted hiring posture). Note as context: the BlueOwl/Quanata group has been through restructuring in recent years; stability of individual teams is not something I could verify first-party.
Locations / HQ: "Based in San Francisco, CA." Offices in the San Francisco Bay Area and Providence, Rhode Island; employees near either may commute to them by choice.
Remote policy: **Remote-first, stated verbatim**: "We are a remote-first company for most positions so you may work from anywhere you like in the U.S, excluding U.S. territories." Travel is "occasional… requested or encouraged but is not required" for most roles. **Core meeting hours 9AM – 2PM Pacific** "for collaborating with team members across all time zones" — this is the single most Mountain-Time-friendly hours policy in this batch (10am–3pm MT). $2,000 one-time home-office equipment payment; MacBook Pro provisioned before day one.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **NO — explicitly disqualifying.** Their own remote policy limits employment to "anywhere you like in the U.S, excluding U.S. territories". A BC-based hire is not possible under the stated policy. All relevant postings are titled "[Remote-US]".
Engineering & tech: Data engineering and MLOps are first-class (dedicated Senior Data Engineer, MLOps and Senior Data Engineer roles), plus backend engineering and backend SDET. Telematics implies large-scale sensor/time-series ingestion and ML scoring pipelines. Specific stack details were not published on the pages read.
Notable / other: Strong benefits — health/dental/vision including dependents, Headspace subscription, monthly wellness allowance, 401(k) with company match, four weeks accrued PTO in year one, twelve weeks fully paid parental leave for birthing *and* non-birthing parents, up to **$5,000/year** for professional learning and development, LinkedIn Learning, BetterUp coaching.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Back End Engineer [Remote-US]; Senior Data Engineer, MLOps [Remote-US]; Senior Data Engineer [Remote-US]; SDET, Backend.
Pay: posted on their own JDs (US base) — Senior Data Engineer, MLOps $213,000–$300,000; Senior Data Engineer $215,000–$300,000; Senior Back End Engineer $195,000–$280,000. Among the highest posted ranges in this batch. Bands already on record.
