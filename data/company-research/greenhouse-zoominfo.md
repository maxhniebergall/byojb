# ZoomInfo — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:zoominfo | company_type: product

What they do: "ZoomInfo (NASDAQ: GTM) is the Go-To-Market Intelligence Platform that empowers businesses to grow faster with AI-ready insights, trusted data, and advanced automation. Its solutions provide more than 35,000 companies worldwide with a complete view of their customers, making every seller their best seller." In practice it is a B2B contact-and-company data business sold to sales and marketing organizations, plus the Chorus conversation-intelligence product. The company trades under the ticker GTM ("go to market") — the sales orientation is the identity, not a segment.

How they describe themselves: The recruiting boilerplate on every JD is explicit about pace: "ZoomInfo is where careers accelerate. We move fast, think boldly, and empower you to do the best work of your life. You'll be surrounded by teammates who care deeply, challenge each other, and celebrate wins. With tools that amplify your impact and a culture that backs your ambition, you won't just contribute. You'll make things happen–fast."

Size / stage / funding: Public (NASDAQ: GTM), and currently contracting sharply. In 2026 ZoomInfo cut 20% of its global workforce — 600 roles — as part of a cost-reduction and restructuring plan; roughly half came from closing its Israel research office, with the remainder spread across R&D, sales and marketing. It lowered 2026 revenue guidance to $1.185–1.205B, about a 4% year-over-year decline at the midpoint, citing macroeconomic pressure, AI-related buyer hesitation and weak software demand. Shares fell 28% to $4.32, the lowest since the 2020 IPO, valuing the company near $1.3B against roughly $13B at IPO. Management is replacing seat-based pricing with a platform-fee-plus-credits hybrid model from Q3 2026.

Locations / HQ: Vancouver, Washington and Bethesda, Maryland are the primary US hubs, with Waltham, Massachusetts also prominent. A substantial Toronto, Ontario engineering site is where most Canadian requisitions sit. The Israel research office has been closed.

Remote policy: Mixed and role-by-role. Requisitions carry precise geographic tags — "Remote-US-CA", "Remote-US-NY", "Remote-US-NJ", "Remote US - PST", "Remote Canada - EST" — indicating remote work is permitted but tightly scoped to named states/regions and timezones rather than offered broadly. Many engineering requisitions list a physical office (Bethesda, Waltham, Vancouver WA, Toronto) with no remote option.

Remote-Canada eligibility: LARGELY NO for this candidate. The overwhelming majority of Canadian requisitions are tagged "Toronto, Ontario, Canada" as a physical location — Senior DevOps Engineer (x3), Senior Data Engineer (x2), Senior Software Engineer, Senior Software Engineer - CICD, Principal Software Engineer (x3), Software Engineer III (x3). Those are Toronto-anchored and effectively disqualifying from Kimberley BC without relocation. The one explicitly Canada-remote tag found — on a Principal Software Engineer requisition — reads "Remote Canada - EST", i.e. restricted to Eastern Time. Mountain Time is 2 hours behind EST, so even that role is a poor match against an explicit timezone restriction. A handful of postings say only "Remote" with no country (Senior Machine Learning Engineer, Senior DevOps Engineer, Senior Software Engineer - Web Data Team, Senior Software Engineer - API & MCP Platform), which given the pattern of precise US-state tagging most likely means US-remote.

Engineering & tech: Broad surface — data engineering and web-scale data collection (Web Data Team, Contributory Network), CI/CD platform work, DevOps, machine learning, an API & MCP Platform team (the most infrastructure-flavoured group, and the one with agentic/MCP substance), and Chorus conversation intelligence. Stack signals from the JDs include React/Angular/Vue and TypeScript on the frontend, Node.js and Python backends, MySQL/MongoDB/DynamoDB, and Elasticsearch/Solr. Note that despite the "Software Engineer III" title, that requisition is a frontend/data-visualization role, not backend.

Compensation: No ranges are stated on the Canadian or US requisitions reviewed; every band on record for this company is an extractor estimate rather than a posted figure. This research pass found no first-party ZoomInfo pay disclosure.

Notable / other: The JDs disclose that "ZoomInfo may use a software-based assessment as part of the recruitment process," with a published NYC Local Law 144 bias audit. They publish a Job Applicant Privacy Notice.

Data quality note: all nine comp rows on record for this key are `estimated`/`llm_prior` extractor output with the method line "no figure in the ATS field or the JD text" — consistent with the JDs genuinely stating no ranges. They should not be read as sourced numbers.
