# The New York Times — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:thenewyorktimes | company_type: product

What they do: The New York Times Company is a public news and media organization whose business is subscription journalism, plus adjacent consumer products — NYT Cooking, Games (Wordle/Crossword), The Athletic, Wirecutter, and audio. Their stated business strategy "centers on making journalism so good that it's worth paying for", i.e. a direct-to-consumer subscription model rather than an ad-first one. Product Engineering is a large in-house org building the digital and app destination on top of a substantial internal platform.

How they describe themselves: From their own mission statement quoted on every JD: "The mission of The New York Times is to seek the truth and help people understand the world. That means independent journalism is at the heart of all we do as a company. It's why we have a world-renowned newsroom that sends journalists to report on the ground from nearly 160 countries. It's why we focus deeply on how our readers will experience our journalism, from print to audio to a world-class digital and app destination." Journalistic independence is treated as an explicit engineering value, not just an editorial one — JDs require candidates to "support our mission and values, including journalistic independence, and keep ethics and safety central to how we use AI in developer workflows". They also state an unusual hiring-process value: candidates are asked not to use GenAI tools to generate substantive content during application and interview, "to keep our hiring process as fair and authentic as possible". Standard EEO language and explicit Fair Chance / criminal-history-friendly hiring language appear on their postings.

Size / stage / funding: Public company (NYSE: NYT), profitable and subscription-funded — the opposite of runway-dependent. Their own JD states the engineering org is **750+ engineers across 100+ teams** in Product Engineering. The specific team hiring (DevEx Platforms) is **6 engineers** inside the Developer Platforms organization.

Locations / HQ: HQ New York, NY. The relevant live posting lists "New York, NY; Remote - USA".

Remote policy: Hybrid, US-anchored. The GenAI/DevEx posting is tagged `#LI-Hybrid` while simultaneously listing "Remote - USA" as a location, so the practical arrangement appears to be NYC-hybrid with a US-remote option depending on team. Benefits text distinguishes "roles in the U.S." from "roles outside of the U.S.", confirming they do employ some people internationally, but the location field on this role is explicitly USA.

Engineering & tech: Golang is named as the primary language for cloud-native applications; AWS (3+ years preferred) and Kubernetes workloads; Datadog for observability, DX for developer-productivity measurement, FinOut for cost. The Developer Platforms organization runs an internal developer platform serving 100+ teams and 750+ engineers, and the DevEx Platforms team owns developer experience and workflow automation. They are actively building a **GenAI developer platform**: LiteLLM-based routing with auth and configuration, powering both local and background/long-running agents; GenAI-powered developer tooling (prompts, IDE integrations, Cursor/Claude rules, telemetry); and guardrails plus observability for agentic workflows integrating safely and auditably with Jira, GitHub, and internal platforms. Named tools in use across the org: Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot. Stated use cases are debugging, migrations, test generation, and platform modernization / structured tech-debt paydown. The role carries "limited on-call responsibilities", with the schedule set on joining.

Notable / other: They warn publicly about fraudulent job postings using their name and direct all legitimate openings through the NYT careers site — worth noting when verifying any NYT offer. Reasonable-accommodation contact is published on postings. The engineering org is broad: the registry shows roles spanning Games, Cooking, Community, News Multimodal, ML Platform, Data Platforms and Developer Platforms.
