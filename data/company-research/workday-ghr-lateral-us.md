ENTITY NOTE — registry name mismatch: the registry stores this company as "Ghr" from the Workday tenant slug `ghr`, which is Bank of America's Global Human Resources tenant. The JD bodies identify the employer unambiguously: "At Bank of America, we are guided by a common purpose to help make financial lives better through the power of every connection." The posting locations (Jersey City, Addison, Plano, Pennington, Denver, Charlotte-area) are Bank of America technology hubs. This is NOT a staffing firm named GHR — the name collision is worth flagging, since "GHR" is also a US healthcare-staffing brand.
What they do: Bank of America is one of the largest US banks (NYSE: BAC), a global systemically important financial institution in consumer banking, wealth management, global banking and global markets. The postings are its internal technology organization: payments modernization, equities/markets technology, mainframe infrastructure, data platforms, GenAI platform engineering, SRE and cloud infrastructure.
How they describe themselves: Stated purpose: "to help make financial lives better through the power of every connection", delivered through "Responsible Growth" for "clients, teammates, communities and shareholders". They emphasize "Being a Great Place to Work", inclusion, and "supporting our teammates' physical, emotional, and financial wellness".
Size / stage / funding: Enormous, public, systemically important, profitable. Maximum institutional stability; no runway risk. Very large bureaucracy with correspondingly heavy governance, risk and compliance process.
Locations / HQ: HQ Charlotte, NC. Postings cluster in Jersey City NJ, Addison and Plano TX, Pennington NJ, and Denver CO. All US. No Canadian posting appears among the 25 registry entries.
Remote policy: Stated explicitly and verbatim in the JDs: "Bank of America is committed to an in-office culture with specific requirements for office-based attendance and which allows for an appropriate level of flexibility for our teammates and businesses based on role-specific considerations."
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — definitively. The company states an in-office culture with office-attendance requirements, and every relevant posting is anchored to a US hub. This is a hard disqualifier for a BC-based candidate who cannot relocate.
Engineering & tech: Large-scale enterprise modernization. Data platform work spans on-prem data warehouses, Informatica ETL, Hadoop, mainframe batch processing and legacy reporting, migrating toward Azure, Databricks/Delta, Snowflake, Event Hubs/Kafka, APIs/microservices, Terraform/Bicep IaC, CI/CD for data and observability. Also mainframe z/OS infrastructure and tape storage, SRE, AWS infrastructure, SIEM platform engineering, GenAI platform architecture and security, and a digital-asset/stablecoin platform. Titles use bank conventions (Software Engineer III, Vice President, Principal Engineer).
Notable / other: US pay-transparency law makes many of these postings state ranges, so the registry already holds six band rows taken directly from BofA's own JDs — roughly $103-180K USD base for Software Engineer III, $122-200K for senior engineer/infrastructure, $150-191K for a senior SIEM platform role, and $157-230K for principal engineer.
