# Praia Health — research
Provider/key: rippling:praia-health | company_type: product

What they do: Praia Health sells a "Patient Experience Orchestration Platform" to health systems. It consolidates a hospital system's fragmented digital tools behind one patient login, layers identity/access management, personalization and multi-channel engagement (email, SMS, push, chatbot) on top of the EHR, and provides plug-and-play connectors to third-party digital-health vendors. In short: a consumer-experience and patient-engagement layer for health systems, not a clinical or infrastructure product.

How they describe themselves: Marketing frames the problem as patients facing "multiple logins and dead-end landing pages," and Praia as delivering seamless, branded journeys that differentiate a health system from a generic EHR portal. The pitch is heavily ROI/conversion-flavoured — they advertise $48M in customer ROI, a 200% increase in campaign conversions, 106% lift in portal adoption and 3x higher care-gap closure. Their own framing at launch was that the company is "driven by robust and novel identity and personalization technology at its core."

Size / stage / funding: Early-stage. Spun out of Providence (the large US non-profit health system) in April 2024 with a $20M oversubscribed Series A led by Frist Cressey Ventures, with SignalFire, Epsilon Health Investors and Providence Ventures participating. Led by CEO Justin Dearborn, previously executive-in-residence in Providence's Digital Innovation Group, where the platform was incubated from 2022. Team size is small (tens of people). The platform already carried ~3.5 million user accounts at spin-out via Providence.

Locations / HQ: US-based, rooted in Providence's footprint (Pacific Northwest / Seattle-Renton orbit). Distributed/remote team; no traditional office story published. Uses Rippling's ATS, which is itself a small-company signal.

Remote policy: Effectively remote — its two open engineering postings are location-typed "Canada Remote" and "United States Remote" with no office attached. No published handbook, async policy or written remote-work philosophy was found, so remote practice is unverified beyond the postings themselves.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes for one specific role.** The open "Sr. Software Developer" is posted as "Canada Remote," so a BC-based worker is eligible. Important caveat: the more infrastructure-flavoured role, "Staff Software Engineer - Data Infrastructure," is posted "United States Remote" and is therefore not available. Canada eligibility here is role-by-role, not a company-wide policy, and only one relevant role currently qualifies.

Engineering & tech: Core stated technical differentiator is identity and personalization — identity/access management at multi-million-account scale, EHR integration (Epic-centric given the Providence lineage), engagement/notification channels, and a connector framework for third-party digital-health solutions. There is a separate Data Infrastructure track (US-only). No engineering blog, public handbook or published stack detail was found; the JDs are the only real technical window.

Notable / other: Being a health-system spin-out is a double-edged signal — it means a real anchor customer (Providence) and real production scale on day one, but also heavy concentration risk in that one relationship, and Community Health Network is the main named additional client. Ecosystem partners include Labcorp, Walgreens, Cedar, Omada, DexCare, Kyruus and others. As a Series A company roughly two years past spin-out, it is at the runway-dependent stage where the next raise matters.

Open relevant roles (sample): Sr. Software Developer — Canada Remote; Staff Software Engineer - Data Infrastructure — United States Remote.
