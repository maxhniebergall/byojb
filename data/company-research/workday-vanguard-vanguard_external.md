# Vanguard — research
Provider/key: workday:vanguard/vanguard_external | company_type: product
What they do: One of the world's largest asset managers — index funds, ETFs and retirement/advice services. Its own JD states "Vanguard manages over $11 trillion in assets across the globe, and with this comes an immense responsibility to safeguard our clients' funds from bad actors," and describes itself as "a digital-first company" with an Enterprise (anti-fraud/security) organisation built around that. Vanguard's distinguishing structural feature — that it is owned by its funds and therefore by its investors — could not be verified first-party in this session (the corporate culture page 404'd); treat the ownership detail as unverified here.
How they describe themselves: From the "How We Work" block repeated verbatim across their reqs: "We believe our mission-driven and highly collaborative culture is a critical enabler to support long-term client outcomes and enrich the employee experience." Employees are referred to internally as "crew members." Individual role pages lean on "high-impact, high-visibility" framing.
Size / stage / funding: Public-facing scale is enormous (>$11T AUM per their own JD), private/mutual structure, established since 1975 — stability is not in question. Crew headcount not verified from the pages read.
Locations / HQ: HQ Malvern/Wayne, Pennsylvania. Live reqs are concentrated in Wayne PA, Charlotte NC, **Hyderabad, India** (a large and growing centre — roughly half the live relevant reqs), London and Manchester UK, and a single **Toronto, Canada** req.
Remote policy: Hybrid, stated identically on every requisition: "Vanguard has implemented a hybrid working model for the majority of our crew members, designed to capture the benefits of enhanced flexibility while enabling in-person learning, collaboration, and connection." No remote-first or async signals; the rationale explicitly favours in-person collaboration.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No.** The only Canadian requisition (Senior Lead Data & AI Engineer) is Toronto-based and carries the same hybrid-working-model clause; the registry facet for it reads "hybrid". There is no Canada-remote posting and no stated accommodation for a candidate outside a hub city.
Engineering & tech: Deep infrastructure and platform surface — AWS and GCP multicloud, Kubernetes-adjacent virtualization platforms (Citrix, Azure Virtual Desktop), ServiceNow CMDB and infrastructure discovery, SRE, DevOps/automation platform engineering, identity & authentication backend services, and a large data/AI estate: Databricks lakehouse, Delta Live Tables, Lakeflow, Unity Catalog, AWS Glue, SageMaker and Bedrock, plus explicit agent infrastructure — "deploying AI agents using RAG, Graph RAG, MCP-enabled integrations, and agent orchestration frameworks such as AgentOps, AgentBricks, LangGraph," with "AI observability" and metadata-driven reusable frameworks. The Toronto req asks for 9–12+ years.
Notable / other: Titles in the registry drift noticeably from the Workday slugs (e.g. "Senior Cloud Platform Engineer, Google Cloud Platform (GCP)" sits at a `MultiCloud-Engineer--Google-Cloud` URL; "Technical Lead Data Engineer" at a `Data-Engineer--Senior-Specialist-WT-VISCO-Sales---Marketing` URL) — reqs are being retitled in place. **No requisition in the registry states a salary range**, including the US ones, so pay is entirely undisclosed here. India-based reqs dominate the volume.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Lead Data & AI Engineer (Toronto); Senior Software Engineer – Client Identity (Backend); Senior Cloud Platform Engineer, GCP; Senior Site Reliability Engineer; Senior Infrastructure Engineer – CMDB Discovery; Senior data Engineer Anti Fraud.
