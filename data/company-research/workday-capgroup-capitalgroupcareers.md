# Capital Group — research
Provider/key: workday:capgroup/capitalgroupcareers | company_type: product
What they do: Capital Group is one of the world's largest and oldest active investment management firms — the manager behind the American Funds family — serving individual investors, financial advisors and institutions. Technology here is internal-facing: the platforms, data infrastructure and ML systems that support the investment process, distribution and enterprise operations. Their own framing: "Through nine decades, our goal has been to improve people's lives through successful investing."
How they describe themselves: Their JD boilerplate is written as a series of first-person associate statements — "I can be myself at work," "I can influence my income," "I can lead a full life," "I can succeed as a <role> at Capital Group." They state five core values explicitly: **Integrity, Client Focus, Diverse Perspectives, Long-Term Thinking, and Community.** On belonging: "We intentionally seek diverse perspectives, experiences, and backgrounds, investing in a culture designed to celebrate differences. We believe that belonging leads to better outcomes and a stronger community of associates united by our mission." On compensation: "Your performance will be reviewed annually, and your compensation will be designed to motivate and reward the value that you provide. You'll receive a competitive salary, bonuses and benefits. Your company-funded retirement contribution will factor in salary and variable pay, including bonuses." On life outside work they cite generous time-away and health benefits "from day one, with the opportunity for flexible work options," 2-for-1 matching charitable gifts with annual grants, and on-demand professional development. And: "the success of the people who invest with us depends on the people in whom we invest." Note the tone is notably absent of hustle/rocketship language — the stated value is literally "Long-Term Thinking."
Size / stage / funding: Large, private, employee/partner-owned, ~90+ years old (founded 1931), one of the largest active asset managers globally with assets under management in the trillions of USD. Exact current AUM and headcount not verified from a first-party page in this pass. Financially this is the opposite of runway-dependent.
Locations / HQ: US offices include Los Angeles / Irvine (Southern California — the historic base), Charlotte NC, Seattle WA, and internationally London. Postings in the registry are split across Charlotte, Irvine, Seattle and London.
Remote policy: Office-anchored with "the opportunity for flexible work options." Every posting in the registry is tied to a named metro (Charlotte, Irvine, Seattle, London) and the posted salary bands are geo-differentiated by office metro, which is a strong indication that roles are assigned to an office location rather than being location-agnostic remote. No remote-first or fully-remote posting was found.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No / effectively no.** All relevant live postings are anchored to US metros (Charlotte, Irvine, Seattle) or London, with metro-indexed pay bands. Their compensation footnote refers specifically to "Temporary positions in the United States." No Canadian office or Canada-eligible posting appears in the registry. Treat as not Canada-eligible unless a Canadian posting appears later.
Engineering & tech: Two distinct tracks visible. (1) **Platform / cloud** — "As a Senior Engineer on our enterprise-wide technology team, you won't just maintain infrastructure — you'll architect the backbone of Capital Group's cloud ecosystem," building AWS cloud infrastructure automation and Infrastructure-as-Code, plus "the automation, tooling, and platform capabilities that thousands of engineers rely on every day," and infusing AI/ML into infrastructure operations. (2) **Machine learning engineering** — "build the next generation of AI products at Capital Group — including agentic systems, LLM-powered workflows, and the platform that ensures they are safe, governed, and reliable in production," operating "at the intersection of production ML, GenAI and agentic workflows, and governed data infrastructure," with high autonomy, mentoring, and setting engineering standards. A separate applied-research flavour exists on the investment side: ML engineers who "create, research, implement, and maintain state of the art machine learning models, data pipelines, and analytical systems to significantly enhance our investment processes," collaborating directly with senior investment professionals.
Notable / other: **Posted pay is strong and stated by law-driven transparency in their JDs.** Charlotte Senior Platform Engineer base $136,749-$218,798; Southern California equivalent $159,354-$254,966; Senior Machine Learning Engineer (Southern California) $201,683-$322,693; Machine Learning Engineer II (Southern California and Seattle) $159,354-$254,966. These are base only — annual bonus and a company-funded retirement contribution that factors in variable pay sit on top. An internal-platform org "thousands of engineers rely on" implies a very large technology function.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Platform Engineer (Charlotte); Senior Machine Learning Engineer (Irvine); Machine Learning Engineer II (Seattle); Software Engineer - Lead (London); Software Engineer - Senior (London).
