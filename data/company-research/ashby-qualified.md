# Qualified (a Salesforce company) — research
Provider/key: ashby:qualified | company_type: product

**Registry note:** the registry name is "Qualified" but the live JDs on this Ashby board are now Salesforce reqs. Qualified was acquired by Salesforce — definitive agreement announced 17 December 2025, acquisition **completed 1 April 2026**. Every JD opens with "Qualified has been acquired by Salesforce!" and carries Salesforce's About/EEO/compensation boilerplate. This company should be understood as a Salesforce business unit, not an independent startup.

What they do: Qualified is a San Francisco agentic-AI marketing company. Its flagship product is **Piper**, an "AI SDR" — an always-on AI worker that turns a company's website into a multi-modal conversational experience, engaging inbound visitors in real time to qualify, nurture and convert them into sales pipeline. It had raised ~$163M before the acquisition (investors include Norwest). Inside Salesforce it feeds **Agentforce Sales and Agentforce Marketing**, adding production-ready autonomous pipeline-generation agents.

How they describe themselves: Salesforce framing — "the #1 AI CRM, where humans with agents drive customer success together. Here, ambition meets action. Tech meets trust. And innovation isn't a buzzword — it's a way of life… looking for Trailblazers who are passionate about bettering business and the world through AI." Qualified's own engineering section is explicit about pace and ownership: "Engineering at Qualified is a **high-ownership, high-velocity** environment where engineers ship customer-facing product to real enterprises at scale. We're building at the frontier of AI + product: developing agentic software and embedding AI directly into our platform." And on the post-acquisition posture: "Following our acquisition by Salesforce, we continue to operate with the **pace and ownership of a startup—backed by the distribution, resources, and long-term runway of a global enterprise**. Engineers have true end-to-end ownership from idea to production: scoping, implementation, testing, deployment, monitoring, and iterative improvement based on real customer feedback… a high-talent-density team that cares deeply about craft, performance, reliability, and user experience… without layers of approvals slowing you down." One req bullet says plainly: "Embrace a culture of flexibility and efficiency, moving fast to capture opportunities while **wearing multiple hats in a startup environment**."

Size / stage / funding: Now a wholly-owned Salesforce business unit (Salesforce: NYSE CRM, ~76,000 employees, profitable, S&P 500). Pre-acquisition Qualified was a late-stage venture company with ~$163M raised. Stability risk is now integration/reorg risk rather than runway risk.

Locations / HQ: Qualified is San Francisco-based; Salesforce is San Francisco-headquartered with global offices. The relevant reqs are posted as remote.

Remote policy: The Foundations reqs are posted **"Remote (US/Canada)"**. Salesforce's compensation boilerplate on these reqs enumerates US, Ontario and British Columbia hiring ranges, confirming a real multi-geo remote hiring footprint rather than a US-only one.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, and specifically British Columbia.** Location is "Remote (US/Canada)", and the JD's own pay-transparency section states: "For Ontario-based roles, the base salary hiring range for this position is CAD 130,300 to CAD 179,200 annually. For **British Columbia-based roles**, the base salary hiring range for this position is CAD 130,300 to CAD 179,200 annually." A company that publishes a BC-specific band is unambiguously able to employ a BC-resident remote worker. Team is SF-anchored, so Pacific-leaning hours — trivial from Mountain Time.

Engineering & tech: The open roles are on the **Foundations** team, which is the platform/infrastructure function: "building highly scalable cloud infrastructure, robust CI/CD pipelines, advanced monitoring and observability, and top-tier engineering tools", with the goal to "increase deployment velocity and confidence, raise our quality bar, and ensure our systems are capable of supporting complex, real-time interactions". Named responsibilities: architecting secure, fault-tolerant, highly-available public-cloud infrastructure; system performance optimisation for highly-trafficked services; evolving CI/CD in a multi-service environment; infrastructure-as-code upgrades; observability with **DataDog and Sentry**; runbooks, operating procedures and architecture diagrams; and mentorship. Described as sitting "at the intersection of distributed systems, orchestration, and public cloud platforms". Asks 5+ years with emphasis on backend systems, cloud platforms and infrastructure. There is a visible ladder — SMTS (Senior Member of Technical Staff) and LMTS (Lead Member of Technical Staff) — using Salesforce's MTS nomenclature.

Notable / other: The product itself (autonomous AI SDR agents) is squarely in the agentic-AI go-to-market hype zone, but the advertised roles are the internal platform underneath it rather than the agent product surface. Salesforce benefits are stated: time off programs, medical/dental/vision, mental health support, paid parental leave, life and disability insurance, 401(k) and an ESPP.

Compensation (stated directly on their own live Foundations JDs, base salary only, excluding bonus/equity/benefits):
- US typical range: **$148,500 – $223,900 USD**
- SF Bay Area and NYC metro select cities: **$178,900 – $246,000 USD**
- **Ontario: CAD 130,300 – 179,200**
- **British Columbia: CAD 130,300 – 179,200**

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (SMTS), Foundations — Remote (US/Canada); Lead Software Engineer (LMTS), Foundations — Remote (US/Canada).
