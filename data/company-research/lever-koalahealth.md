# Koala Health — research
Provider/key: lever:koalahealth | company_type: product

Entity check: confirmed. koala.health is the online pet pharmacy whose Lever board (jobs.lever.co/koalahealth) carries the posting in the registry. Not to be confused with Koala (the Australian mattress retailer) or any human-health company of similar name.

What they do: Koala Health is a direct-to-consumer online pet pharmacy. Their stated goal: "to make managing your pet's health simple and delightful." The service ships prescription pet medications and supplements to the door "in presorted medication pouches" — their three-step description is "Verify & fetch prescriptions from their vet", "Sort & package their medications by date and time", and "Deliver and refill their medications for free." They sell to pet owners directly and also work with veterinary clinics through a partnership model, covering dogs and cats across a broad set of health categories. They are "LegitScript approved" and listed with Dot Pharmacy — real pharmacy regulatory compliance, which shapes the engineering (prescription verification, fulfilment, controlled workflows).

How they describe themselves: The company frames itself around an "ambitious goal" of simplifying pet health, and is now "focused on continuing to improve that experience while building a suite of new products and services that will make the pet health experience even better." No published values list, handbook, or culture page was found.
