# Pelmorex — research
Provider/key: lever:pelmorex | company_type: product
What they do: Pelmorex Corp. is the Canadian weather and environmental-data company behind The Weather Network / MétéoMédia, plus MeteoMedia, ElTiempo.es and subsidiaries. Beyond consumer media it runs a growing B2B data business: "we're on a mission to transform weather and environmental data into decision-grade insights for businesses and consumers. Our B2B products help organizations make smarter, safer, and more profitable decisions." It also operates Canada's National Alert Aggregation and Dissemination System (Alert Ready) — genuinely mission-critical public infrastructure.
How they describe themselves: Employer-brand-forward and benefits-first in their JDs — "Flexible Hybrid Work Environment", RRSP matching, a personal spending account, summer hours, 17 paid days off plus 13 personal days and a birthday day off, and an "IDEAS (Inclusion, Diversity, Equity, Awareness, Solidarity) team" ("Weather is inclusive, we will be too"). They advertise ranking "#5 Most Admired Company in Canada in Leger's 2026 Reputation Study, among 334 organizations". The data-engineering JD describes "a small, entrepreneurial team of Engineers, Product Managers, and Data Scientists".
Size / stage / funding: Private, Canadian-owned, long-established (The Weather Network dates to 1988). Not venture-runway dependent. Exact headcount UNVERIFIED — no first-party figure captured and no company site was fetched.
Locations / HQ: Oakville, Ontario (head office) plus Montreal, Quebec. Spanish operation via ElTiempo.es.
Remote policy: Hybrid, and explicitly not negotiable on location. Both live engineering JDs state it in near-identical terms: "we are only considering candidates who are eligible to work in Canada and can work in a Hybrid model at our Oakville or Montreal location" (Senior Data Engineer) and "...can work in a Hybrid model at our Oakville, ON, Canada location" (Backend Software Developer). "Flexible Hybrid" refers to the split of days, not to location freedom.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — verified negative, and stated in the JD text itself. Canadian work eligibility is required but physical presence near Oakville or Montreal is a hard filter. A candidate in Kimberley, BC cannot satisfy it without relocating, and the timezone gap (Mountain vs Eastern) compounds it.
Engineering & tech: Backend meteorological platforms in C/C++, Go, Python and Rust, running on Kubernetes (EKS), ECS and EC2; Docker, Istio, HTTP/gRPC services, Redis, cloud-native architecture on Linux. The systems "must have high availability, fast response times and scalability to support 4 billion global locations with high quality data". Separately, an Engineering Insights team building "a scalable data analytics and insights platform" — ingestion, validation, transformation and consumption of data across the B2B portfolio, with emphasis on "how data quality, latency, and design decisions directly impact products, customers, and business outcomes". Agile process.
Notable / other: The technical content is a strong match on substance — large-scale distributed data processing, real HA requirements, a serious systems language stack. One of the two live roles is also a fixed-term contract ("this is a contract role until February 2027"), which is a separate negative.
