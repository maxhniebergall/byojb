# TeamSnap — research

What they do: A sports team management and communication platform for youth/amateur sports — scheduling, rosters, availability, messaging, payments — used by families, coaches and league organisations. Scale stated first-party in the JD: systems "that serve millions of daily users and tens of thousands of amateur sports organizations."

How they describe themselves: Verbatim: "At TeamSnap, we believe when the world connects through sports; the world becomes better. TeamSnap is a sports and communication platform dedicated to taking the work out of play in youth sports. We also believe our jobs should excite us, our teammates should support us and our bosses should inspire us. We empower our people to bring big ideas and tiny egos, landing us on Outside Magazine's list of 'Best Places to Work' and Built In's '100 Best Remote-First Places to Work.'" The phrase "big ideas and tiny egos" is their signature value statement, and the two awards they choose to cite are both work-environment awards rather than growth or funding awards — a deliberate self-presentation as a place to work well rather than a rocketship.

Remote-Canada eligibility: UNCLEAR — no first-party statement either way. This is the single biggest open question. The posting is "Remote, remote" with NO country, state or timezone named, and the Stage-3 extraction correspondingly tagged `geo_eligibility: unclear` and `timezone: unclear`. A genuinely fully-distributed company is structurally the most likely kind to support a Canadian hire, but US SaaS companies of this size very frequently mean "remote within the US" without saying so. This must be asked before investing effort. Nothing in the JD excludes Canada, and nothing confirms it.

Engineering & tech: Stack stated verbatim: "Node, TypeScript, Elixir, Ruby/Rails, SQL, React, cloud-based services, event-driven systems, queues, background jobs, caching layers, and observability tooling." Architecture: "we architect and build scalable systems using service-oriented and event-driven architecture." The Staff Backend role's scope, in their words: "build and improve APIs and services, reason through production issues across the system, lead technical design in ambiguous areas, mentor engineers, and raise the quality bar across the team", with a focus on "backend systems, APIs, services, data performance, reliability, observability, and production quality." Notably explicit about boundaries: "This person should be comfortable working closely with infrastructure, platform, and data teams without being expected to own those functions directly" — i.e. dedicated infrastructure, platform and data teams exist and are separate, and this role is not expected to absorb them.

Youth-sports SaaS is seasonal (registration and season-start peaks) and consumer-facing, which shapes both traffic patterns and support load.
