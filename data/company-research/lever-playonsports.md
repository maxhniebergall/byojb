# PlayOn — research
Provider/key: lever:playonsports | company_type: product
What they do: PlayOn (formerly PlayOn! Sports) is a high-school sports platform combining ticketing, live streaming and analytics under the brands GoFan, NFHS Network and MaxPreps. From their site: they manage "the complete game lifecycle — from ticket sales and event promotion to live streaming and post-game content distribution." Scale figures they publish: ~750,000 events a year through GoFan, 7,500 schools ticketing, 8,500 schools broadcasting, 50M annual visitors, 525,000 teams, 2.5M student athletes.
How they describe themselves: The stated theme is that "every moment matters" in high school sports, connecting students, families, coaches, athletic directors and communities through integrated digital tools. No formal published values list was found on the pages fetched.
Size / stage / funding: Not stated on the pages fetched — headcount, ownership and funding stage are **unverified**. Scale metrics above suggest an established, non-seed-stage business.
Locations / HQ: HQ not stated on the fetched page (commonly associated with Atlanta, GA, but that is not verified from a first-party source here). All live engineering postings are listed as "Remote, remote".
Remote policy: Engineering roles are posted as remote. No written remote/async policy statement was found; nothing about core hours or timezone bands.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear.** Postings say "Remote" with no country scope stated, and the captured JD bodies contain no work-authorization or geography clause. A US-only default is likely for a US high-school-sports business but is not evidenced either way. Would need to be asked directly.
Engineering & tech: The SRE role describes work "at the intersection of software engineering and operations — focused on building the tools, automation, and visibility that enable our teams to deliver resilient software at scale": observability (dashboards, metrics, logs, instrumentation/telemetry), monitoring and alerting, CI/CD pipelines, defining SLIs/SLOs for core user flows, and incident response with an "Event Commander"/on-call rotation. Separate application-engineering, DevOps and QA teams exist. Specific languages/cloud not stated in the captured bodies.
Notable / other: The business is inherently event-spiky — live game broadcasts and ticketing peaks around school sports seasons, which is what the reliability investment is for. The SRE JD is written as a maturity project (observability is being established, SLOs do not yet exist for core flows).
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Site Reliability Engineer; Senior Software Engineer; Senior Software Engineer, Data; Staff Software Engineer, Growth (no longer live).
