# Suno — research
Provider/key: ashby:suno | company_type: product

What they do: Consumer AI music generation. Suno describes itself as "building the world's first creative entertainment platform, where the entire world can feel the joy and fulfilment of making music," and as "the fastest growing consumer entertainment company and the leader in AI music." Users range from "grandmothers creating songs for their loved ones, to Grammy winners using Suno Studio, our power tool." Products include the core creation surface, Suno Studio, and in-flight work on a multi-modal music agent and a realtime API for live generation and radio experiences.

How they describe themselves: Four stated values, verbatim: **Music** ("AI is a tool; music is our identity"); **Impatience** (continuous improvement through speed); **Aesthetics** (quality and taste in every detail); **Fun**. Recruiting copy is unusually blunt about intensity: "Building the future of entertainment requires ambition. The pace is fast, the problems are hard, and the work demands ownership and intensity." Also: "operate with ambiguity — scope what matters, move fast, and drive projects forward independently," and teams that "solve hard problems with autonomy and a bias toward engineering excellence."

Size / stage / funding: Private, heavily venture-backed — investors named in its own JD: Bond Capital, Menlo Ventures, Lightspeed, IVP, Forerunner, Union Square Ventures, Alkeon, Quiet, Matrix Partners, Schroders Capital, and NVentures (NVIDIA's venture arm). Engineering org stated as **70+ engineers**. Total headcount and raise amount not disclosed on pages fetched.

Locations / HQ: HQ **Cambridge, MA (Harvard Square)**; additional offices in New York, NY (Chelsea) and Los Angeles, CA (Venice).

Remote policy: **Fully in-office.** Two separate JDs state it explicitly: "This is a 5 days a week in-office role" and "This role is expected to work from the Suno office 5 days a week, per Suno's company policy." Benefits list "In-Office Lunch (5 days per week)."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — categorically.** Company policy is 5 days a week in a US office (Cambridge, NYC or LA). No remote option of any kind, let alone Canada. Disqualifying.

Engineering & tech: Platform-side org is split into Infrastructure, Platform and Leverage teams that "build the foundation upon which everything else is built." Work described includes: APIs, authentication, deployment tooling and backend-driven configuration for 70+ engineers; legacy-system migrations; shared infrastructure and agentic tooling for AI-native development plus evaluation systems for internal tooling; services for write-heavy consumer traffic; distributed databases, caching layers and storage at consumer scale; Kubernetes infrastructure and control planes; and inference infrastructure tuned for speed, reliability and cost. Team assignment is decided during the interview process based on background and fit.

Notable / other: Benefits include unlimited PTO and sick days, 16 weeks fully paid parental leave, free lunch at all offices, 3% 401(k) match, and a creative-education stipend — a US-benefits package, consistent with US-only employment.
