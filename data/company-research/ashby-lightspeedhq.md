# Lightspeed Commerce, Inc. — research
Provider/key: ashby:lightspeedhq | company_type: product

What they do: Lightspeed Commerce sells a unified commerce platform — point-of-sale, payments, e-commerce and back-office software plus hardware — to independent retailers, restaurants, hospitality and golf operators. Founded 2005 in Montreal. Dual-listed on the TSX and NYSE as LSPD. Revenue comes from software subscriptions and payment processing take-rate; the current strategic narrative is "profitable growth" / getting to sustained EBITDA-positive.

How they describe themselves: Careers material centres on "building communities through commerce" and on inclusion as an origin story — the company was founded in Montreal's gay village with founding members from the LGBTQ+ community, and they present "a workplace where everyone belongs" as an ethos rather than a policy. JDs emphasise autonomy, "upleveling engineering craft across the organization by providing technical guidance, promoting best practices, mentoring fellow engineers, while empowering teams to build towards sustainable architecture." Notably absent from the JD text: hustle/"fast-paced"/"wear many hats" language. Tone is corporate-professional rather than startup-hype.

Size / stage / funding: ~3,100-3,200 employees worldwide (Mar 2026). Public (TSX/NYSE: LSPD), no longer venture-funded. Largest employee concentration is Canada (~38%), then Germany (~9%) and the UK (~8%). Not consistently profitable; targeting EBITDA-positive.

Locations / HQ: HQ Montreal, Quebec. Canadian offices in Montreal, Toronto and Ottawa; significant presence in Germany, the UK, the Netherlands, the US, Australia/NZ.

Remote policy: Explicitly flexible — "We offer a choice of fully remote, partially remote or fully office-based work environments (in cities with local offices)." Public careers material also mentions flexible PTO and up to two months working remotely from abroad per year. Important constraint stated on every relevant JD: "For this role, you should be located in Canada, and able to work within EST/EDT hours." (Some Principal roles widen this to "Canada/USA".)

Remote-Canada eligibility: VERIFIED YES. Several live postings are literally titled location "Remote in Canada" (Senior Staff Software Developer, Staff Software Developer, Principal Software Developer). The binding constraint is not geography but hours: EST/EDT working hours from Mountain Time means a roughly 06:00-14:00 local day. That is workable but is a real, stated requirement, not a preference.

Engineering & tech: Commerce platform engineering — POS, payments, e-commerce and supply-chain systems at merchant scale. Ladder observed in live postings runs Staff → Senior Staff → Principal Software Developer, plus a Staff Site Reliability Engineer role, so there is a genuine IC track above Senior. SRE exists as its own discipline. Engineering is distributed across Montreal, Toronto and Ottawa hubs plus Canada-remote.

Compensation (see data/company-comp/): Lightspeed posts ranges on its Canadian JDs under a stated philosophy: "we carefully consider a wide range of factors when determining compensation, including your skill set, qualifications, experience, and market data." Staff Software Developer (both the Remote-in-Canada and the Toronto/Ottawa posting) states total compensation of $175,000 to $190,000 CAD. Benefits listed: medical, dental, wellness, life and disability insurance, RRSP plan with match, paid parental leave top-up, equity, flexible PTO, $500/yr health-and-wellness benefit.

Notable / other: Repeated restructuring. Public reporting documents multiple rounds of job cuts across recent years (~300, ~280, and ~200 roles in separate announcements) tied to a strategic review and a pivot toward profitable growth, plus 2026 Canadian layoffs concentrated in customer support after AI began resolving a reported 80%+ of support requests. So: not a runway-risk startup, but an org in active cost discipline.

Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Software Developer (Remote in Canada); Senior Staff Software Developer (Remote in Canada); Staff Software Developer (Remote in Canada); Staff Site Reliability Engineer (Montreal); Staff Software Developer (Toronto/Ottawa).
