# OpsMill — research
Provider/key: ashby:opsmill | company_type: product

What they do: Infrastructure data management and network automation. Their open-source platform **Infrahub** gives infrastructure and network teams "a unified, structured view of their IT environments so engineers and AI agents can automate operations safely at large scale" — effectively a source-of-truth / infrastructure-data layer that replaces fragmented CMDB and IPAM stacks. Infrahub "models infrastructure as a network of relationships rather than static assets, allowing teams to manage dependencies, validate changes, and maintain governance through a structured DevOps workflow." A companion product, Infrahub Sync, handles integration with existing systems. Positioned squarely at the NetDevOps / network-automation community.

How they describe themselves: From the live JD — "The best platforms aren't just built to work today—they're designed to scale gracefully tomorrow… Clean, extensible systems don't happen by accident—they require intentional design, deep technical leadership, and a vision that spans the entire platform." They state "We've achieved **product-market fit**, and now we're expanding—new products, growing customer deployments, and increasing demand from the market," and describe the moment as one where "the right software architectural decisions compound into sustainable competitive advantage." A recurring theme is driving "our evolution toward an **AI-first engineering culture**" — both in how the team builds (AI tooling in day-to-day development) and in making Infrahub a substrate for customers' AI-powered automation.

Size / stage / funding: Founded **2023**, headquartered in **Paris, France**. Raised a **$14M / €11.9M Series A in May 2026**, led by IRIS with participation from BGV and existing investors Serena and Partech. Very young and small — likely well under 50 people. Early-stage and venture-dependent, though freshly funded.

Locations / HQ: Paris, France. Distributed team across Europe and North America; postings are split into a "Europe" and a "North America" variant of the same Principal role.

Remote policy: Remote, but **CET-anchored**. The JD requires being "self-directed and effective in remote environments with **at least 4 hours of overlap with CET timezone (Europe, UK, or US East Coast locations work well)**." That is an explicit, quantified synchronous-overlap requirement rather than an async-first culture.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes on paper, but effectively poor.** The North America posting is scoped "US/Canada, United States, Canada", so Canada is formally in scope. However the **4-hour CET-overlap requirement is the binding constraint**: Mountain Time is CET−8 (CET−9 under some DST alignments). Four hours of overlap with a European working day means starting around **05:00–06:00 Mountain Time daily**. The JD names "Europe, UK, or US East Coast" as the locations that "work well" — Mountain Time is two to three hours further west than the westernmost location they endorse. Formally eligible, practically a hard daily-schedule imposition.

Engineering & tech: **Python is the primary language** ("expert-level Python proficiency"), with Rust or Go listed as nice-to-haves for performance-critical components, and a **graph database engine** at the core (Neo4j or similar experience is a nice-to-have). Work described includes API design, data-consistency patterns, distributed-systems trade-offs, architecture reviews, RFCs and design docs. Infrahub is developed in the open. The Principal role is explicitly an architect-plus-hands-on-coding role spanning multiple teams, reporting alongside the Head of Engineering and CTO.

Notable / other: Open-source community includes hyperscalers — **TikTok Ltd.** is cited as a community user — and the enterprise edition is used by retail, insurance, manufacturing and fintech customers across Europe and North America. Reference customer **Eurofiber Group** reportedly cut service deployment times from five days to 15 minutes after deploying Infrahub. Notable emphasis on giving AI agents a trustworthy single view of infrastructure data.

Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Software Engineer - North America (US/Canada); Principal Software Engineer - Europe.
