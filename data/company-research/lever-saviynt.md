# Saviynt — research
Provider/key: lever:saviynt | company_type: product

What they do: Saviynt sells an enterprise identity security / identity governance and administration (IGA) platform. In their own words: "Saviynt's AI-powered identity platform manages and governs human and non-human access to all of an organization's applications, data, and business processes. Customers trust Saviynt to safeguard their digital assets, drive operational efficiency, and reduce compliance costs." The platform spans internal workforce, external workforce, privileged access management (PAM), non-human identities and — the current push — AI agents. Their flagship AI-identity product is branded **Zuma**, positioned as "Enterprise AI Identity Security."

How they describe themselves: Site tagline is "Secure Every Identity," with the pitch that enterprises can "build and run AI with confidence" through central identity control. On JDs: "Saviynt is recognized as the leader in identity security, with solutions that protect and empower the world's leading brands, Fortune 500 companies and government institutions." The engineering org is branded **Saviynt Labs**: "We design, build and run the leading Enterprise Identity solutions... The engineering teams design, build and run SaaS software built on leading edge technologies. We focus on engineering excellence and we attract the best talent in our industry. Our cloud services are built on AWS, GCP and Azure with a global presence." Closing boilerplate on JDs: "Saviynt is an amazing place to work. We are a high-growth, Platform as a Service company focused on Identity Authority to power and protect the world at work." One JD explicitly asks for the "ability to work effectively and achieve results across functional areas in a work environment with a high rate of change." No published values list was recoverable from the reachable pages.

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture/growth-backed, self-described as "high-growth." Headcount and funding stage were **not disclosed** on the pages reachable here — saviynt.com's homepage does not state them. Company claims to secure "over 100 million identities" and to be a "5x Gartner Customers' Choice" for IGA (the only vendor with five). Treat scale and stability as unverified.

Locations / HQ: HQ not stated on the reachable homepage; the live engineering postings are located in **Milpitas, California**; **Vancouver, BC (hybrid)**; and **Bengaluru, India (onsite)**. Bengaluru carries a large share of the engineering headcount judging by posting volume (151 total postings scanned, 18 relevant).

Remote policy: Mixed and mostly not remote. Of the four relevant live postings: two Principal Software Engineer roles are "Vancouver, hybrid, Milpitas, California"; one Principal Engineer, Software Engineering is "Bengaluru, onsite"; only "Principal Software Engineer, AI Platform" is tagged "remote" with no geography attached. No remote-first, async or handbook signals anywhere in their material.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Legally yes (they employ in Vancouver, BC) but practically no for this candidate.** The Canadian presence is a *Vancouver hybrid office*, which requires regular in-office attendance roughly 800 km from Kimberley — effectively disqualifying, since relocation is off the table. The single "remote" AI Platform posting is the only opening that could work, and its geography is unspecified, so Canadian eligibility for that specific role is unverified.

Engineering & tech: Deeply Java/JVM. The AI Platform Principal JD asks for "10+ years... with a strong focus on Java, Spring Boot, and distributed system design," "deep understanding of the Java ecosystem, JVM internals, memory management, performance tuning, and multithreading," plus PostgreSQL/MySQL, Redis, Elasticsearch, LDAP, Kubernetes, Docker, AWS, Terraform/Helm/Ansible, Prometheus/Grafana/OpenTelemetry, ELK. Work described includes multi-tenant isolation, service orchestration, GC tuning, and "modernization of backend services, refactoring legacy Java components." The other Principal JD is heavier legacy still: "13+ yrs of experience in Java/J2ee," "Groovy and Grails preferred," SOAP APIs — and one live posting is literally titled "Principal / Senior Principal Software Engineer - (Grails Server Page)." Cloud runs on AWS, GCP and Azure. Note the Principal roles are quasi-managerial: one asks for "proven experience in team management, attracting, hiring, retaining and leading top engineering talent" and "ability to manage multiple teams."

Compensation (first-party, posted on their own Lever JDs, USD base):
- Principal Software Engineer, Connectors: $225,000 – $245,000
- Principal Software Engineer, Privileged Access Management: $160,000 – $250,000

Notable / other: Security/compliance obligations are spelled out on the JDs (annual security & privacy training, acknowledgement of a long list of InfoSec policies) — normal for the sector but indicative of a compliance-heavy internal environment. Note there is title inflation to watch: many openings are "Associate Principal," which sits below Principal.

Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Software Engineer, AI Platform; Principal Software Engineer, Connectors; Principal Software Engineer, Privileged Access Management; Principal Engineer, Software Engineering (Bengaluru); AI Platform Engineer, Training and Inference.
