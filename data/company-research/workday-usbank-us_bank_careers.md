# U.S. Bank — research

Provider/key: workday:usbank/us_bank_careers | company_type: product

What they do: U.S. Bancorp / U.S. Bank is one of the largest commercial banks in the United
States — retail and commercial banking, payments (Elavon), wealth and corporate trust. The
engineering estate in the requisitions is classic large-bank technology: technology
modernization (mainframe → cloud), Snowflake data platform work, DevOps/SRE, digital
analytics, Salesforce, and an emerging AI/ML platform and enablement function.

How they describe themselves: JDs are corporate-functional; the recurring self-presentation is
the benefits and pay-transparency boilerplate — "In addition to salary, U.S. Bank offers a
comprehensive benefits package, including incentive and recognition programs, equity stock
purchase 401(k) contribution and pension (all benefits are subject to eligibility
requirements)", and for the UK reqs "Clearly defined salary ranges aligned with industry
benchmarks and internal equity standards." No distinctive values or engineering-culture
statement surfaces in the scanned bodies.

Size / stage / funding: Public (NYSE: USB), ~70,000 employees. Maximum stability; heavily
regulated; pension and stock-purchase plan on offer. No runway risk of any kind.

Locations / HQ: HQ **Minneapolis, Minnesota**. Engineering sited across **Hopkins MN,
Minneapolis MN, Earth City MO, Irving TX, Tempe AZ, Chicago IL**, and several multi-location
reqs ("2/3/4/5/7/8 Locations"). International: **London, United Kingdom** (AI DevOps
Engineer, Senior DevOps Engineer) and a **Toronto, Ontario** requisition
(*Sr. Software Engineer (Back-end AI/ML)*).

Remote policy: Office/location-anchored. Every requisition names one or more specific US
cities (or London/Toronto), and the pay boilerplate is explicitly location-zoned — "The salary
range reflects figures based on the primary location, which is listed first. The actual range
for the role may differ based on the location of the role." No remote-first, async or
work-from-anywhere statement appears anywhere in the 22 scanned postings.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Effectively no.** The overwhelming majority of roles are US-sited
and require US work authorization, which the candidate does not have. There is exactly one
Toronto, Ontario requisition (Sr. Software Engineer, Back-end AI/ML), but it is posted to
Toronto as a location rather than as remote-Canada, and nothing in the company's postings
indicates a work-from-anywhere-in-Canada arrangement. For a Kimberley, BC candidate this is
not a viable employer.

Engineering & tech: Java/Spring Boot microservices, React and Python, Salesforce, mainframe
modernization, Snowflake platform engineering, Linux infrastructure automation, orchestration
and automation, multi-cloud data platforms, DevOps/SRE, and an AI platform line (Lead AI
Platform Engineer; Principal AI Engineer – AI Acceleration & Enterprise Enablement; AI/ML
Deployment and Enablement Leader). Ladder is numeric-plus-named: Software Engineer 1 → 2 →
Sr. → Lead → Principal.

Notable / other: U.S. Bank posts a pay range on essentially every US requisition, and the
ranges are tight and role-specific (unlike State Street's wide officer bands) — this is
unusually good comp data. UK reqs state no numbers.

Open relevant roles (sample): Snowflake Platform Engineer; Lead Snowflake Platform Engineer;
Senior DevOps Engineer (London); Sr. Infrastructure Engineer - Orchestration and Automation;
Principal Software Engineer – Technology Modernization (Chicago); Principal AI Engineer – AI
Acceleration & Enterprise Enablement; AI/ML Deployment and Enablement Leader; Sr. Software
Engineer (Back-end AI/ML) (Toronto).
