# BlackBerry — research
Provider/key: workday:bb/blackberry | company_type: product
What they do: BlackBerry Limited is now a software and embedded-systems company with two divisions: (1) **QNX** — a real-time operating system and embedded software platform, deployed in over 275 million vehicles globally and also used in robotics and medical devices; and (2) **BlackBerry Secure Communications** — end-to-end encrypted voice and messaging for governments and critical infrastructure, deployable on-premises or in sovereign cloud, built around encryption and zero-trust identity controls with data-sovereignty features. No handset business remains.
How they describe themselves: The site's messaging is security- and trust-led — "Every call. Every message. Protected." for Secure Communications, and QNX as foundational software for mission-critical embedded systems demanding high performance and functional safety. Stated organisational values as presented on the site centre on security, trust and innovation. A fuller values/handbook statement was not located in this session.
Size / stage / funding: Publicly traded (BlackBerry Limited; the site maintains an investor-relations section). Exact current headcount and listing details were not confirmed from a fetched page — unverified here. The company has been through a long multi-year transition from handsets to software, including divestitures and restructuring, so "stable" should be read as "established but shrinking/reorganising", not "growing".
Locations / HQ: Canadian company, historically Waterloo, Ontario. Registry postings sit in Waterloo Ontario, Ottawa Ontario, and Hyderabad India — Ottawa is the traditional QNX engineering centre and Hyderabad is clearly a significant QNX site now.
Remote policy: Not stated on any fetched page and not stated in the JDs. Every posting in the registry is tagged with a specific city (Waterloo, Ottawa, Hyderabad), with none tagged Remote.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NOT ESTABLISHED. BlackBerry is a Canadian employer, so there is no entity obstacle, but no posting is advertised as remote and none is in British Columbia or Mountain Time. The two Canadian-located roles are Waterloo and Ottawa (Eastern Time), and the Ottawa QNX roles are no longer live. Assume onsite/hybrid Ontario unless confirmed otherwise.
Engineering & tech: QNX systems software — board support packages (BSPs), device drivers, graphics, Wi-Fi/networking stacks for a commercial real-time OS. This is deep, well-scoped, specialised low-level C/embedded work on a long-lived product with automotive-grade safety requirements. Separately, "Intermediate Applications Software Developer" in Waterloo is general application development. No engineering blog or public handbook was found.
Notable / other: An important registry detail: of the four "live relevant" roles, three of the QNX systems-software postings are located in **Hyderabad, India**, not Canada. The two Ottawa QNX roles (Systems Software Developer II – Graphics, and Principal Systems Software Developer) are recorded as no longer live. The only live Canadian role is the Waterloo Intermediate Applications Software Developer, whose posted band is CAD $90,000–$132,000 — an intermediate-level band, below Max's senior target.
Open relevant roles (sample): Intermediate Applications Software Developer (Waterloo, ON); QNX– Senior Systems Software Developer (BSP) (Hyderabad); QNX– Senior Systems Software Developer – Wifi/Networking (Hyderabad); QNX– Systems Software Developer (BSP/Device Drivers) (Hyderabad).
