# Experian — research
Provider/key: smartrecruiters:experian | company_type: product

What they do: Experian is a global information-services / credit-bureau company. In its own words it
exists for "creating a better tomorrow by bringing financial power to all," transforming data into
actionable information for consumers and businesses across credit data, analytics and technology
services. Its stated pillars are improving financial health, "treating data respectfully",
supporting employees, operating with integrity and protecting the environment; it describes using
data and technology as "a force for good."

Size / stage / funding: Large, established, publicly traded on the London Stock Exchange (ticker
EXPN), with ADR and BDR programmes. Operations in 26+ countries. Headcount not stated on the
corporate site and left UNVERIFIED here. Maximum stability class — no runway risk.

Locations / HQ: Registered HQ at 2 Cumberland Place, Fenian Street, Dublin 2, D02 HY05, Ireland.
Operating regions: North America, Latin America, UK & Ireland, EMEA, Asia Pacific, with offices
including the US, Canada, Brazil, Australia, China, Japan, India and across Europe.

Remote policy: No company-wide policy is published on the corporate site (UNVERIFIED at that level).
At the role level the JDs are explicit and vary by region: the India roles say "This is a hybrid
remote/in-office role" / "#LI-hybrid based in Hyderabad", "requiring travelling to Hyderabad office
40% times per month"; the Costa Rica roles say "This is a fully remote job opportunity" / "#LI-
Remote"; the Brazil roles are listed as "(remote)" within Brazil; and the one US role says "This a
US based, Remote position with periodic travel."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **NO — none of the live relevant engineering roles is Canada-eligible.**
This is the decisive finding and it comes from the postings themselves, not inference. Of the 26
live relevant engineering postings in the registry, the locations break down as roughly: Hyderabad,
India (~13, hybrid, 40% office attendance); Brazil — Sao Paulo / Sao Carlos (~7, remote *within
Brazil*); Heredia, Costa Rica (2, fully remote *within Costa Rica*); and one United States-only
remote role (Vice President, Software Engineering — "This a US based, Remote position"). Experian
does have Canadian operations as a company, but it is currently advertising **zero** Canadian
engineering roles on this portal, and each remote role is explicitly scoped to its own country.

Engineering & tech: Large distributed engineering org concentrated in India, Brazil and Costa Rica.
Stack signals from live titles and JDs: Java, Microsoft .NET / Azure cloud, AWS, Python, data
platform and lakehouse engineering, SRE and DevOps at scale, QA/data-platform testing, and a GenAI
product track (GenAI Product Lead, EMAP). Levels run Mid / Senior / Lead / Staff / Senior Staff up
to VP.

Notable / other: **Data-quality note for the registry** — this company scores extremely well on
posting-rubric terms (`best_posting_score` 4.11, the highest in this batch, `live_relevant` 26) yet
is entirely unreachable for a BC-based candidate. The rubric is rewarding title/stack matches while
the geography facet is unpopulated on most of these records (only 2 of 26 have any extracted
`geo_eligibility`, and neither is Canada). Titles are also multilingual ("Especialista de SRE II",
"Desenvolvedor(a) Back-End Java Senior", "DevOps Pleno", "Software Engineer Pleno"), a reliable
tell for LATAM-scoped postings that a title-based scorer will not catch.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Site Reliability Engineer (Hyderabad); Lead Data Platform
Engineer (Hyderabad); Senior Staff Software Engineer (Hyderabad); Senior DevOps Engineer (Heredia,
Costa Rica, fully remote in CR); Especialista de SRE II (Sao Paulo, remote in BR); Vice President,
Software Engineering (US remote).
