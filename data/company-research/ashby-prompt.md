# Prompt (Prompt Therapy Solutions, Inc) — research
Provider/key: ashby:prompt | company_type: product
What they do: B2B enterprise SaaS ("EMR + practice management") for outpatient rehab-therapy businesses — clinics, their staff and patients. Describes itself as "the go-to platform in the space" with rapidly growing market share. Healthcare-adjacent, billing/patient-care/operations data.
How they describe themselves: From their own JDs — "revolutionizing healthcare by delivering highly automated and modern B2B enterprise software"; a "mission-driven startup". Their four stated "Why work for Prompt?" pillars, verbatim: **Big Challenges** ("solving complex and unique problems that have plagued the healthcare industry since the dawn of time"), **Talented People** ("incredibly talented and proven individuals who all made their mark before joining forces to build the greatest software on the planet for rehab therapists"), **Healthy Approach** ("This isn't an investment bank. At Prompt you own your workload and the entire organization takes a liking to smart work (over hard work)"), **Positive Impact** ("helps outpatient rehab organizations treat more patients and deliver better care with less environmental waste... turning a paper-heavy industry digital"). Tone is confident/superlative-heavy; the Data Engineer JD nonetheless asks for people who "operate effectively in ambiguous, fast-paced environments".
Size / stage / funding: Self-described startup; 22 open roles at scan time. Headcount, funding stage and profitability NOT verified — no company site was fetched (no first-party domain appears in the JD text and web search was exhausted). Treat as unverified.
Locations / HQ: HQ location not verified. There *is* a physical HQ — benefits list "Company-wide sponsored lunches", "commuter benefits" and a "Recovery suite at HQ – includes a cold plunge, sauna, and shower", so the culture is meaningfully office-anchored.
Remote policy: JDs say "Remote/hybrid environment" and all four relevant postings are listed simply as "Remote". No async/handbook signals stated.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Very likely NO.** The JDs state "Prompt Therapy Solutions, Inc is an E-Verify Employer" — E-Verify is a US federal employment-eligibility system, which implies US-based W-2 employment. No Canadian entity or non-US hiring language appears anywhere. Not directly contradicted, but no evidence of Canada eligibility.
Engineering & tech: AWS; Docker with ECS/Fargate; GitLab CI and GitHub Actions; Terraform/Ansible/CloudFormation; Datadog, Prometheus, Grafana; Bash/Python/Go; PHP in the application stack (listed as a preferred skill). Data side: Python + SQL, dbt, Metabase, S3/Athena/RDS/Redshift/DMS/Glue/ECS/Lambda, Postgres/Snowflake/BigQuery. They explicitly expect use of "modern AI and LLM-powered tools (code assistants, agents, automation frameworks)" in the data role.
Notable / other: Unusual hiring policy stated in every JD — they will not hire individuals employed by Prompt customers without that employer's explicit consent. Benefits are strong: flexible PTO, company-paid disability/life, company-paid family and medical leave, medical/dental/vision, 401k, FSA/DCA, discounted pet insurance, digital fitness subscription. Equity is only "potential equity compensation for outstanding performance" — i.e. not standard.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior DevOps Engineer; Senior DevOps Engineer (Infrastructure & MLOps); Senior DevOps Engineer, Team Lead; Senior Data Engineer; Senior Healthcare Integrations Software Engineer (B2B SaaS).
Pay: all four relevant roles post ranges (USD base) — DevOps $180-225K, DevOps Team Lead $230-265K, Data Engineer $170-190K, Healthcare Integrations SWE $170-200K. Bands already on record in data/company-comp.jsonl.
