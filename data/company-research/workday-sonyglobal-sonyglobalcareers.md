# Sony (sonyglobal Workday board) — research
Provider/key: workday:sonyglobal/sonyglobalcareers | company_type: product

What they do: This is the shared Workday careers board for Sony group companies in North America, not a single employer. The two relevant live postings come from two different Sony entities:
- **Sony Engagement Platform Services (SEPS)**, under Sony Corporation of America — building "the next-generation platform that powers how fans connect with the content—and the creators—they love", a data-driven engagement platform spanning Sony's games, anime, music, film and live-experience businesses, serving internal teams and external partners. It explicitly aims at "decoupling application development from platform services".
- **Sony Music Publishing (SMP)** — "the #1 global music publisher", advancing songwriters and composers for 25+ years; the Canadian role's legal employer is **Sony Electronics Inc. / Sony of Canada ULC**, with day-to-day direction from SMP.

How they describe themselves: SEPS: "innovation meets collaboration in the dynamic world of digital engagement"; "we invite you to explore the exciting opportunities within our team… we will shape the future of fan experiences". Sony Music Group: "we are shaping what's next in a way that creates impact. Forging powerful new ideas at the heart of music, technology, and culture"; "Be a part of an organization that is creator first. Committed to fueling excellence and always imagining more, while fostering a supportive culture, one where we elevate each other and act responsibly." SEPS also states "Honesty, trustworthiness and ethical conduct are material requirements".

Size / stage / funding: Sony Group is a large, stable, publicly traded multinational. Not runway-dependent. Individual teams (SEPS "AI-native tiny team") are small.

Locations / HQ: Board covers North America. Relevant postings: Culver City, CA (SEPS) and Toronto, ON (SMP / Sony Electronics Inc. as Canadian employer). Sony Corporation of America is at 25 Madison Avenue, New York.

Remote policy: Explicitly hybrid/onsite for these roles. The SEPS role: "This position is a hybrid role requiring 3 days on-site in our Culver City, CA Office." The Toronto Cloud Engineer role states no remote policy and advertises "A modern office environment designed to foster productivity, creativity, and teamwork", i.e. office-based. A previously-live "Staff Software Engineer, AI Native" was posted across 11 locations including "Remote - Texas" (now closed), so remote does exist elsewhere in Sony — but US-based.

Remote-Canada eligibility: The Toronto Cloud Engineer role IS Canada-employable — "Sony Electronics Inc., as the legal employer in Canada, will provide the employment agreement, payroll, benefits… Applicants must be legally authorized to work in Canada." But nothing states it may be worked remotely, and the benefits copy points to an office. Remote-from-BC is UNVERIFIED and should be assumed no. The Culver City AI role requires US presence and 3 days on-site — ineligible.

Engineering & tech: SEPS: Java/Go/Python, REST API design, relational and NoSQL data modelling, React/TypeScript front end, AWS, Docker/Kubernetes, Terraform/Ansible/Jenkins CI/CD — plus a heavy "AI-native" mandate: directing and governing AI coding agents (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot) across the delivery lifecycle, prompt-engineering patterns, structured output schemas, guardrails, MCP/A2A and tool-connection patterns, evals and LLM-as-judge, tracing prompt chains and agent failures, hallucination reduction, prompt caching, model fallback. On-call rotation. SMP Cloud Services: AWS operations at depth — CloudFormation, serverless, cloud networking / Cloud WAN, GitLab CI/CD, Bash/Python automation, database infrastructure, Okta, Dynatrace observability; AWS Certified Solutions Architect Professional required.

Notable / other: The Toronto role's own text warns about hours: "7.5-hour business workday but variations in work volume frequently require extended working hours for evening and late-night events." Benefits include a defined-contribution pension with matching, tuition reimbursement, winter recess, and (US) employee stock purchase plan and annual incentive bonus. Sony warns publicly about fraudulent job postings impersonating them and advises verifying anything against sonyjobs.com.

Open relevant roles (sample): Sr. Software Engineer, AI Native (Culver City, hybrid 3 days); Senior Cloud Engineer, Cloud Services (Toronto).

Pay (stated on their own JDs): Sr. Software Engineer, AI Native — $180,000–$205,000 (USD, Culver City). Senior Cloud Engineer, Cloud Services — $175,000–$200,000 (Toronto posting; the currency is not stated in the text but the role is Canadian-employed, so read as CAD), plus discretionary annual bonus, pension matching and benefits.
