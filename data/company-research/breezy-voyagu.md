# Voyagu — research
Provider/key: breezy:voyagu | company_type: product

What they do: Voyagu (Voyagu Travel Group) is a travel-technology company built around premium international air travel. Its consumer-facing business is a business/first-class flight consolidator for US premium travellers — it holds privately-supplied airline contracts and sells discounted premium fares with a human travel-advisor layer and 24/7 support. Its corporate strategy, as stated on its own company page, is broader: it "acquires, technology-enables, and scales travel agencies", operating a portfolio of travel brands on top of an in-house platform called **Radar**, described as an operating system for travel agencies.

How they describe themselves: Stated mission — "To build the technology infrastructure that makes travel agencies significantly more valuable — for their clients, their teams, and their owners." The values page emphasizes practical execution over process: "We do the work", with "no consultants, no committees"; judgment-driven decision-making; authenticity; long-term "compound thinking" where decisions build momentum. The consumer site separately promises "excellent personal service, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week."

Size / stage / funding: Early-stage growth company, private. Team described as expanding alongside a "growing portfolio of travel brands". No disclosed funding stage or headcount found. Roll-up/acquisition strategy implies capital-dependent growth.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco. Operations in Mexico City and Warsaw. **R&D in Amsterdam.** Leadership: founder & CEO Ivan Saprov, with VP Product and VP Engineering drawn from Uber, Glovo and N-iX.

Remote policy: No published company-wide remote policy. The one relevant posting is listed by the ATS as "Worldwide Remote".
