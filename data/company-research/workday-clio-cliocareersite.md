# Clio — research
Provider/key: workday:clio/cliocareersite | company_type: product

What they do: Clio builds cloud practice-management, legal-payments and client-intake software for
law firms — case management, calendaring/court rules, invoicing, fintech/payments, document and
knowledge extraction. Customers are legal professionals (small and mid-size firms up through larger
practices). Its stated purpose, repeated verbatim at the top of every JD, is to "transform the legal
experience for all" by "making legal services more accessible for legal professionals while
increasing access to justice."

How they describe themselves: Mission language in their own words: "transform the legal experience
for all", "increasing access to justice". They describe a "human and high-performing culture" and
frame benefits as a "Total Rewards program". Their hybrid model has a named ritual — "Anchor Days" —
and employees are called "Clions", with a "Clioversary" recognition programme at 3, 5, 7 and 10
years. The full published values set could not be retrieved: clio.com/about returned HTTP 403 this
session, so anything beyond the JD language above is UNVERIFIED.

Size / stage / funding: Large, late-stage private Canadian SaaS company. Exact headcount, funding
round and profitability could NOT be verified this session (no first-party page reachable). Signals
of maturity from the registry alone: 9+ concurrent live engineering reqs, seven named offices, a
formal salary-band structure with published mid-points, RRSP/RESP matching, and a UK/Ireland/US/AU
footprint.

Locations / HQ: Canadian-headquartered in the Vancouver area — the JDs reference a "Burnaby Hub" and
a "Toronto Hub". Named offices in the benefits block: Vancouver, Calgary, Toronto, Dublin, London,
New York City and Sydney.

Remote policy: Hybrid by default and hub-anchored. Verbatim: "Hybrid work environment, with
expectation for local Clions (Vancouver, Calgary, Toronto, Dublin, London, New York City and Sydney)
to be in office min. twice per week." Role JDs restate it, e.g. "This role requires you to be in
office a minimum of twice per week on one of our Anchor Days."

Remote-Canada eligibility: PARTIAL — verified yes for at least some roles. The Machine Learning
Engineer JD (BF-REQ-3169) states verbatim that the role "is available to candidates across Canada
(excluding Quebec)" and that the in-office expectation applies only "If you are local to one of our
hubs (Burnaby, Calgary, or Toronto)". A candidate in Kimberley, BC is neither in Quebec nor local to
a hub, so that role is eligible. The Toronto/Vancouver-anchored reqs (Fintech, Software Developer
Toronto, Software Developer Burnaby) are explicitly office-anchored and are NOT eligible. So
eligibility must be checked per posting; it is not a blanket policy. Timezone is favourable — the
company's centre of gravity is Pacific (Burnaby/Vancouver), one hour off Mountain.

Engineering & tech: Portfolios rather than one monolithic org — Fintech/payments, Voice & Messaging,
Invoicing, Calendar & Court Rules, Knowledge Extraction, CBS Monetization, plus a Platform group with
dedicated Azure Infrastructure and Databases engineers, a Data Engineer role (London), and a Machine
Learning Engineer role. The presence of a "Senior ML Platform Engineer" and a Knowledge Extraction
team suggests real applied-ML/document-extraction work rather than a bolted-on chatbot.

Notable / other: Clio publishes explicit three-point salary bands (min / mid / max) in CAD directly
on its Canadian JDs, and states "There are a separate set of salary bands for other regions based on
local currency" — i.e. a formal, geo-differentiated levelling system. Other stated benefits:
top-tier health/dental/vision, flexible time off with an encouraged 20 days/year, a $2,000 annual
counselling benefit, RRSP matching and RESP contribution. The UK JD lists 25 days holiday plus bank
holidays, private healthcare and pension.

Open relevant roles (sample): Machine Learning Engineer; Senior ML Platform Engineer; Platform
Engineer, Databases; Platform Engineer, Azure Infrastructure; Senior Software Developer, Fintech;
Senior Software Developer — Calendar & Court Rules; Senior Software Developer, Voice & Messaging;
Data Engineer (London).
