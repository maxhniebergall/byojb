# Disney — research
Provider/key: workday:disney/disneycareer | company_type: product

What they do: The Walt Disney Company — a publicly traded global media and entertainment conglomerate. The engineering roles on this Workday tenant sit across its technology organizations: Disney Streaming (Disney+/Hulu), ESPN, Disney Entertainment, ad platforms (linear and digital advertising), Parks & Experiences (Orlando/Celebration FL postings), and studio/production technology (Vancouver pipeline and USD-authoring roles). The engineering "customers" are internal — streaming playback, content platforms, ad serving, data platforms, personalization and recommendation systems.

How they describe themselves: The corporate values/careers pages were not fetched in this pass — the session's WebSearch budget was exhausted and guessing a URL was avoided per policy — so Disney's stated values in their own words are **unverified here**. What is verifiable from the registry is the shape of the work: heavy investment in platform, data and ML engineering as first-class disciplines, with dedicated ladders for Product Software Engineer, Data Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, Security Engineer and Site Reliability Engineer.

Size / stage / funding: Very large public company (NYSE: DIS), well over 100,000 employees globally. Stability is not in question in the runway sense; media-industry restructuring and periodic layoffs are the relevant risk instead, not insolvency. Exact current headcount not verified in this pass.

Locations / HQ: Burbank, CA (corporate). Engineering postings observed in: Glendale CA, Santa Monica CA, Burbank CA, Seattle WA, New York NY, Bristol CT (ESPN), Orlando FL, Celebration FL, and Vancouver BC, Canada. Many listings show "2 Locations"/"3 Locations"/"4 Locations", i.e. a choice among US offices.

Remote policy: Onsite / hybrid, office-anchored. Every posting in the registry with an extracted policy records either `onsite` or `hybrid` — none records `remote`. Extracted timezones are America/Los_Angeles and America/New_York. Disney has publicly required substantial in-office presence for corporate staff in recent years; the posting data here is consistent with that.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No.** Of 58 live relevant postings, every one with extracted geo eligibility says `us_only` except two Vancouver, BC "Expression of Interest" roles (Sr. Software Engineer, Pipeline Infrastructure; Sr. Software Engineer, USD Authoring), and those are recorded as `hybrid` with `timezone: America/Vancouver` — i.e. onsite-in-Vancouver studio-technology roles, not remote-in-Canada. An "Expression of Interest" listing is also a talent-pool posting rather than a live req. There is no path here for a Kimberley-based remote worker.

Engineering & tech: Broad. AWS/cloud platform engineering (cloud account management, content platform engineering, infrastructure), data engineering and data platform (Data Foundation Platform, ML Data Systems, data streaming/integration platforms), machine learning engineering with named specializations (Personalization, News, ESPN, Ad Platforms), observability and AI ("Sr Software Engineer - AI and Observability"), build tooling and developer infrastructure, network infrastructure, database administration, security engineering, and SRE up to Principal. Client-side product roles use Kotlin, Swift and Roku/BrightScript; back-end product roles use JavaScript/TypeScript.

Senior Principal Machine Learning Engineer, Ad Platforms $228,700-$306,700 USD. Senior Security Engineer $135,400-$181,600 USD. Lead Product Software Engineer - Data Systems $155,700-$208,700 USD. Senior platform/infra roles roll up to roughly $137,000-$188,100 USD. Several postings also indicate equity. The Vancouver pipeline-infrastructure role carries an estimated CAD 118,800-159,400 band (extractor estimate, not a posted figure). Pay is genuinely good in USD terms at senior level and above.

Notable / other: 58 live relevant postings and 13 comp band rows already on record — this is one of the best-documented employers in the registry, and every comp slot already has a band, so no further pay research is warranted. The ML and data-platform roles (Personalization, ML Data Systems, Data Foundation Platform) are substantively strong infrastructure work.

Open relevant roles (sample): Sr Software Engineer - AI and Observability; Lead Product Software Engineer - Data Foundation Platform; Senior Cloud Platform Engineer - AWS; Senior Machine Learning Engineer - Personalization; Principal Site Reliability Engineer; Expression of Interest - Sr. Software Engineer, Pipeline Infrastructure (Vancouver, BC).
