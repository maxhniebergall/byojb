# Tubi (Canada) — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:tubi-canada | company_type: product

What they do: Tubi is a free ad-supported streaming television (FAST) service with a very large content
library and hundreds of millions of viewers. Its engineering problems are recommendation, search,
content understanding and ads optimization at streaming scale. The Canadian entity hires into a
Toronto engineering office. Tubi Media Group is a division of Fox Corporation (stated verbatim in
every JD's benefits block), so it sits inside a large, public, profitable parent rather than being
runway-dependent.

How they describe themselves: The JDs' own words: "The Machine Learning team at Tubi drives the
innovation behind personalized user experiences. With the largest inventory in the industry and
hundreds of millions of viewers, we tackle problems in the space of recommendations, search, content
understanding and ads optimization that shape the future of streaming." No broader values/principles
statement was reachable — Tubi's own corporate site was not fetched this session (no first-party URL
in the registry), so the mission/values line is UNVERIFIED beyond the JD text.

Size / stage / funding: Division of Fox Corporation (NASDAQ: FOX/FOXA). Not venture-stage. Exact
headcount unverified.

Locations / HQ: Tubi is US-headquartered (San Francisco) per general knowledge — UNVERIFIED from a
fetched source this session. The Canadian roles are all attached to the Toronto office.

Remote policy: Hybrid, office-anchored. Every one of the 7 live Canadian postings is tagged
"Toronto, Canada (Hybrid)" and several JDs state in body text: "This is a hybrid role in our Toronto
office." One JD carries both #LI-Hybrid and #LI-Remote tags, but the body text still says hybrid
Toronto — treat the #LI-Remote tag as an ATS artifact, not a policy.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO for a BC-based candidate. Verified from the JD bodies: hybrid,
Toronto-office-anchored. There is no cross-Canada remote language anywhere in the 7 postings.

Engineering & tech: Recommendation systems and personalization at scale; deep learning
(TensorFlow/PyTorch); full-stack ML pipelines (data extraction, feature development, training,
testing, deployment); ML infra and distributed systems; content platforms; a data-platform group.
Senior ML roles ask 3+ years production ML; Staff/Principal ask 8+ years plus MSc/PhD.

Notable / other: Tubi publishes CAD pay bands on every Canadian JD under local pay-disclosure
requirements — unusually transparent. Benefits are FOX's, with Tubi-specific deviations: a Flexible
Time Off policy in lieu of FOX's vacation policy, and a more generous parental leave than FOX's.
Roles are also eligible for an annual discretionary bonus and a long-term incentive plan.

Open relevant roles (sample): Machine Learning Engineer (Staff & Principal); Senior Machine Learning
Engineer; Software Engineer, ML Infra & Distributed Systems (Staff & Principal); Senior Data Platform
Engineer; Senior Software Engineer - Content Platforms Engineering; Senior/Staff Software Engineer, Growth.
