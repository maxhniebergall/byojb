What they do: Trane Technologies is a global climate-control and sustainability company. Through the **Trane** brand it supplies HVAC for commercial and residential buildings — "energy efficient indoor environments" — and through **Thermo King** it supplies transport refrigeration for moving perishables. It is an industrial manufacturer with a large software and digital-services arm supporting connected building systems.
How they describe themselves: Sustainability is the stated organising principle — "the essential guiding principle for our business into the 21st century." Their headline commitments are the **Gigaton Challenge** (reduce one billion metric tons of carbon emissions by 2030) and the introduction of 110 new sustainable products in 2025. On people they state a focus on "uplifting culture, career growth, and opportunity for all", citing an employee engagement score of 81/100.
