# Coupa Software, Inc. — research
Provider/key: lever:coupa | company_type: product

What they do: Coupa sells a "total spend management" platform — procurement, invoicing, payments, supplier management and spend analytics — to large and mid-market enterprises. Their own positioning: "Coupa makes margins multiply through its community-generated AI and industry-leading total spend management platform for businesses large and small. Coupa AI is informed by trillions of dollars of direct and indirect spend data across a global network of 10M+ buyers and suppliers." The differentiator they claim is the aggregated cross-customer spend dataset feeding prediction/prescription/automation.

How they describe themselves: Their JD boilerplate offers three "Why join Coupa?" pillars, quoted verbatim: (1) "Pioneering Technology: At Coupa, we're at the forefront of innovation, leveraging the latest technology to empower our customers with greater efficiency and visibility in their spend."; (2) "Collaborative Culture: We value collaboration and teamwork, and our culture is driven by transparency, openness, and a shared commitment to excellence."; (3) "Global Impact: Join a company where your work has a global, measurable impact on our clients, the business, and each other." They point to a "Life at Coupa" blog. Tone is standard enterprise-SaaS corporate — notably NOT hustle/"rocketship" flavored, but also thin on engineering-culture substance.

Size / stage / funding: Large enterprise SaaS. Coupa was publicly traded (NASDAQ: COUP) and was taken private by Thoma Bravo in a ~$8B all-cash deal that closed in 2023 — so it is now PE-owned rather than public or VC-stage. NOTE: this is from general knowledge, NOT verified against a company page in this session — treat as unverified. Headcount not verified here; historically several thousand.

Locations / HQ: HQ historically San Mateo / Foster City, California. From the live posting set, real engineering geographies are: US (San Francisco, New York, Chicago — all "remote"), Mexico City, Bogotá/Colombia, Pune and Bangalore India, and Poland. The volume centre of gravity is clearly Mexico City + India, not the US.

Remote policy: Most postings are tagged "remote", but always bound to a named country (e.g. "US Remote", "Mexico City, Mexico, remote", "Poland, remote", "Colombia, remote"). At least one Pune role is explicitly "onsite". So: country-scoped remote, not location-free remote. No async/handbook culture signal found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — unverified but strongly negative. Across all 16 live postings in the registry there is not a single Canadian location, and a full-text scan of all fetched Coupa JD bodies returned zero occurrences of "Canada"/"Canadian". The North American remote roles are scoped "US Remote". No evidence Coupa employs Canada-based remote workers; nothing found to the contrary either.

Engineering & tech: From live JDs — Ruby on Rails is a significant legacy/core stack (multiple "Ruby on Rails - Lead/Sr. Lead Software Engineer" roles); cloud platform and data platform engineering teams exist ("Lead Cloud Platform Engineer", "Lead Cloud Software Engineer", "Lead Data Platform Engineer"); an active applied-AI push ("Lead AI Engineer", "Senior AI Engineer, NLP & Training Data", "Senior Lead Software Engineer, AI Systems", "Principal Agentic Architect"). Internal IT roles show they run Claude (Anthropic) and Gemini Enterprise as internal AI tooling. Title ladder is unusual: "Lead" appears to be an IC level BELOW "Sr Lead", which is below "Principal".

Notable / other: The AI roles are genuine systems/platform work (NLP training data, agentic architecture, AI systems) rather than thin chatbot wrappers — the substance is reasonable. The spend-management domain is "boring" and mission-critical in the good sense: enterprise back-office plumbing, not consumer growth.
