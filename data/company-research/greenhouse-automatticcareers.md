# Automattic — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:automatticcareers | company_type: product

What they do: Automattic builds and operates the commercial ecosystem around WordPress — WordPress.com, WooCommerce (e-commerce platform), Jetpack, Tumblr, and related products — plus heavy sponsorship of the WordPress open-source project. Its own JD frames the mission around the open web: "WordPress doesn't try to keep its users inside walled gardens, a rare thing in a world that is dangerously moving away from the open web." Customers span personal blogs to large commercial sites and stores.

How they describe themselves: The company's stated culture is codified in the Automattic Creed (https://automattic.com/creed/), which employees are expected to internalize. Its full set of principles, in their own words: "I will never stop learning."; "I won't just work on things that are assigned to me."; "I know there's no such thing as a status quo."; building the business sustainably through passionate and loyal customers; "I will never pass up an opportunity to help out a colleague"; "I am more motivated by impact than money"; treating Open Source as "one of the most powerful ideas available"; "I will communicate as much as possible, because it's the oxygen of a distributed company."; "I am in a marathon, not a sprint"; "Given time, there is no problem that's insurmountable."
The JD adds: "We're kind to each other and our users"; "We work as a global and distributed workforce"; "We offer flexible work arrangements allowing our team members to work when they feel best"; "We open-source! We're cool with open-sourcing everything except passwords, or secret keys"; "We ship often, deploying many times daily with the help of peer code review, continuous integration, and our global workforce in over 70 countries."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, long-established (founded 2005). Their own JD states a workforce spread across "over 70 countries". Exact current headcount, valuation and the scale of the 2024-2025 restructuring were NOT verified in this pass (no first-party source fetched) — treat any headcount figure as unverified.

Locations / HQ: Nominally San Francisco, but the company is fully distributed with no meaningful office-centric structure; staff in 70+ countries.

Remote policy: Fully distributed / remote-first, and among the most established examples of it. Explicit flexible hours ("work when they feel best"), written-communication-first ("the oxygen of a distributed company"). One in-person obligation stated in the JD: "Are open and able to travel 3-4 weeks per year to meet up with your teammates in person."

Remote-Canada eligibility: Verified yes, on strong evidence. The JD is titled/located "Remote", states hiring "worldwide", a workforce in over 70 countries, and a globally uniform salary band "regardless of location" paid "in local currency". Canada is squarely inside that model. Mountain Time is workable — there is no stated timezone-overlap requirement.

Engineering & tech: "We mostly use PHP and JavaScript at Automattic, we expect you to be comfortable with the idea of becoming an expert in these languages." Continuous deployment ("deploying many times daily"), peer code review, CI. Large public GitHub org (github.com/Automattic). Stated scale experience expectation: "on the backend and performance side, or via the complexity of interacting with a multi-million diverse userbase." AI is described as central to recent product work (WordPress.com AI website builder) and to internal dev workflows.

Notable / other: Hiring process is documented publicly (https://automattic.com/how-we-hire/) and the application questions are explicitly weighted: "we are paying extra attention to your answers to the questions in the application form... a significant part of the hiring process." Historically the process has included a paid trial project. The "Experienced Software Engineer" posting is an evergreen general pipeline req rather than a specific team opening.

Compensation (objective): The JD states, first-party: "Salary range: $70,000-$170,000 USD. Please note that salary ranges are global, regardless of location, and we pay in local currency." That is an unusually wide global band; placement within it reflects assessed level. Already on record in data/company-comp.jsonl (software_general / unspecified, direct, jd_posted).

Open relevant roles (sample): Experienced Software Engineer (Remote); Applied AI Engineer; Performance Engineer, Backend.
