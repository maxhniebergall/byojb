# Ramp — research
Provider/key: ashby:ramp | company_type: product

What they do: Ramp is a corporate spend-management fintech — corporate cards, bill pay, expense management, procurement, travel and accounting automation for businesses. Its pitch is that the product exists to reduce customer spend rather than grow it. Founded 2019, it enables over $35B in purchases annually and is one of the fastest-growing corporate card and bill-payment platforms in the United States. Customers are US businesses from startups through enterprise. It is expanding into stablecoin/banking rails and agentic-AI operations tooling.

How they describe themselves: The organizing principle on the careers page is a product rule — **"If it doesn't save you time or money, we don't build it."** On culture, in their own words:
- "We only hire builders."
- "Solve hard problems, build without permission, and ship work you're proud of."
- "Hands off doesn't exist at Ramp."
- "We're a proud meritocracy. Titles and roles are the least interesting thing about Ramp employees."
- Hiring bar, stated as a question: "If this person started a company would we join them?"
- "You'll be given too much responsibility. You'll never be bored. You'll be pushed to do the best work of your career — at the fastest pace of your career."
- Values referenced elsewhere: take ownership, think big, "grow without fear", "win the marathon sprint by sprint".
- "If you have talent and drive we will back you like the Medici family backed Michelangelo."
The tone is unusually explicit about intensity and low process; it is a stated selling point rather than something read between the lines.

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, founded 2019. Valuation reached $22.5B in 2025. Describes itself as "growing 10x faster than the median publicly traded SaaS company". Late-stage and extremely well capitalized; not a runway-risk company, though it is growth-stage rather than a steady-state business.

Locations / HQ: HQ at 28 West 23rd Street, Floor 2, New York, NY. Additional office presence in San Francisco and Denver, CO. Ramp Visa business cards are issued in Canada, and it maintains an engineering presence in Argentina (a dedicated "Software Engineer, Argentina" req).

Remote policy: Not stated as a formal policy on the careers page, and the culture material is NYC-centric — relocation support to NYC is offered as a benefit, and the "hands off doesn't exist" framing implies a high-contact environment. In practice, individual reqs are explicitly location-tagged and many include "Remote (US)". So: office-anchored by default (NY / SF / Denver), remote available per-req.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, but narrowly — one role.** The live "Software Engineer, Production Engineering" req is located "New York, NY (HQ), **Remote (Canada)**, San Francisco, CA, Remote (US)". Every other current engineering req is US-only ("Remote (US)" / NY / SF / Denver) or Argentina. So Ramp can and does employ Canada-based remote engineers, but the Canadian door is currently open on essentially one posting, not company-wide.

Engineering & tech: A "high-velocity and engineering-driven culture" run on a PEDD model — Product, Engineering, Design and Data treated as co-equal functions. Teams are product-domain aligned: Core Product, Banking, Credit, Accounting, Bill Pay & Procurement, Growth Platform, Data Platform, Fraud & Identity, Stablecoin, Production Engineering. Infrastructure/utility work exists as a minority of the org (Data Platform, Production Engineering, Growth Platform) alongside a large surface of customer-facing product teams. There is a visible applied-AI push — "Applied AI Engineer" and "AI Operations Specialist | Agentic Workflows" reqs.

Notable / other: Ramp posts salary ranges on its US reqs and offers equity broadly. Observed live ranges: $189K–$330K (Software Engineer, Banking / GTM Platform), $204K–$300K base for SF/NY (Software Engineer, Guest Travel), $168K–$324.5K (Software Engineer, Production Engineering). These are top-of-market US technology bands. The stablecoin and agentic-workflow reqs indicate active expansion into crypto rails and internal AI operations.

Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineer, Production Engineering (Remote Canada eligible); Software Engineer, Data Platform; Software Engineer, Banking; Software Engineer, Core Product; Applied AI Engineer; Software Engineer, Fraud & Identity; Software Engineer, Stablecoin; Software Engineer, Growth Platform.
