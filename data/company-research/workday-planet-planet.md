# Planet (Planet Labs PBC) — research
Provider/key: workday:planet/planet | company_type: product
Note on identity: the registry stores the company name as "Company Background Planet" — a scraping artifact (a JD boilerplate heading "Company Background" ran into the name). The entity is Planet Labs PBC, the Earth-observation satellite company, confirmed by the careers host `planet.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com` and the planet.com company pages.
What they do: Operates the largest commercial constellation of Earth-imaging satellites and sells the imagery plus analytics as a subscription data platform. Products span daily monitoring, tasking (high-resolution on demand), imagery analysis and a cloud insights platform. Customers are agriculture, government/civil, defense & intelligence, insurance, mapping and environmental/climate organisations.
How they describe themselves: Mission — to "image all of Earth's landmass every day, and make global change visible, accessible and actionable." Stated values are four: "Do good," "Dream big," "Drive for results," "Be collaborative," plus "Strive for openness." Ethics framing: "committed to seeking the greatest good in everything we do." Careers page emphasises "building a community where everyone belongs and invite people from all backgrounds to apply."
Size / stage / funding: Public — Planet Labs PBC, a Delaware public benefit corporation listed on NYSE (investor site investors.planet.com). Roughly ~1,000 employees (not verified from a first-party page this session). Has built and deployed 450 satellites in total, with "200+ in orbit collecting over 350M sq km of imagery daily." The company has had public cost-reduction/restructuring rounds in recent years; not verified from first-party sources in this session, so treat profitability and headroom as unverified.
Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco. Offices: Berlin, Washington D.C., Haarlem (NL), Ljubljana (SI), London (UK), Graz (AT). **No Canadian office is listed.**
Remote policy: Not stated on either the company or careers page fetched. The office list plus office-tagged reqs suggests office-anchored/hybrid rather than remote-first. Unverified.

Engineering & tech: Satellite/space hardware plus a large software side — imagery pipelines, geospatial data processing at petabyte scale, cloud platform and APIs, and increasingly ML/AI over imagery (the live req is an AI Engineer; historical titles include AI Engineering Lead). Genuinely data-infrastructure-heavy domain. No engineering handbook or public levels page located.
