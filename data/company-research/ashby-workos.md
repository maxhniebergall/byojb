# WorkOS — research
Provider/key: ashby:workos | company_type: product

What they do: WorkOS builds developer tools and APIs that let software companies become "Enterprise Ready" — authentication, user management, SSO/SAML, directory sync, identity, authorization and audit logging, sold as infrastructure APIs rather than an end-user product. Stated mission: "give every software company the ability to have their products used by the biggest enterprises." Its customer base is explicitly the AI-native software wave: OpenAI, Cursor, Perplexity, Vercel and Plaid are named on their own JDs, plus "hundreds of modern software companies." Their current strategic framing is Human **and Agent** Authentication, Identity and Access Control — "who are your agents, and what are they allowed to do?"

How they describe themselves: The careers page states the company prioritises "transparency, developer-led initiatives, and iterative processes to build high-quality products." JD copy describes the Infrastructure team as the people who "build and maintain the foundation that keeps everything running smoothly — handling millions of requests, operating at scale, and ensuring our systems are fast, reliable, and always on." The qualities they ask for read as autonomy-oriented rather than hustle-oriented: "generalist mindset," "curious and proactive," "care deeply about observability, reliability, and performance," "work independently, take ownership, and follow through — from identifying a need to shipping a solution." Notably, their own copy avoids "fast-paced"/"wear many hats" phrasing, though it does repeatedly say "fast-growing."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed. **Series C of $100M at a $2B valuation**, led by Meritech and Sapphire with Greenoaks, Craft, Abstract and Audacious participating. Headcount not disclosed on their pages; the company is comfortably funded but is a growth-stage startup, not a profitable/established business.

Locations / HQ: No single HQ stated on the careers page. San Francisco and New York are named on some roles, and commuter benefits exist "for hybrid employees in SF/NYC" — so those are the two office hubs. Everything else is distributed.

Remote policy: Explicitly a "**remote-first** team." Most positions are open to "USA time zones." Fully remote working arrangements are listed under benefits. Hybrid is an option in SF/NYC rather than an expectation elsewhere. On-call rotation is part of the Infrastructure role.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** All four live engineering postings (Infrastructure Engineer, Site Reliability Engineer, Software Engineer - Productivity, Applied AI Engineer) list their location as "**United States & Canada**." Caveat: the benefits block is headed "Benefits and Perks (US Only)" and says "Please inquire directly with our recruiting team for benefits available to those working outside the US" — so Canadian employment is in scope but the benefits package outside the US is unspecified. The "USA time zones" expectation is compatible with Mountain Time.

Compensation: Unusually open for a private company — a range is posted on every live JD: **$175,000 – $275,000 USD, plus equity**, for all four engineering roles (Infrastructure, SRE, Productivity, Applied AI). The same range across roles suggests a single broad senior-engineering band rather than per-role calibration. Other stated benefits (US): 401k matching, medical/dental/vision, FSA, ST/LT disability, voluntary life, Carrot fertility, 20 days PTO + 10 holidays + unlimited sick leave, 12 weeks fully paid parental leave, monthly fitness and wellness stipends, "unlimited token usage."

Engineering & tech: Stack is **TypeScript, React, Postgres, AWS**, with Infrastructure as Code. The Infrastructure team "designs and maintains the distributed systems that power core parts of WorkOS," writes and optimises backend services in TypeScript, evolves the AWS/IaC footprint, builds internal tooling and shared infrastructure, and runs an on-call rotation. There is a distinct **Productivity / developer-experience** role (8+ years) focused on internal tooling, codebase patterns, toolchains and CI — an internal-customer role by construction. Teams "work across the stack and partner closely with teams throughout the company."

Notable / other: They disclose that AI tools support parts of hiring (application/resume review, response assessment) with human final decisions. Equal-opportunity statement present. The Productivity role is framed heavily around interpersonal work — "building trust and credibility across the engineering team," interfacing with leadership and vendors — so it is as much an influence role as an engineering one.

Open relevant roles (sample): Infrastructure Engineer; Site Reliability Engineer; Software Engineer - Productivity; Applied AI Engineer — all US & Canada remote.
