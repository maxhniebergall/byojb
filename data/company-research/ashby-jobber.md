# Jobber — research
Provider/key: ashby:jobber | company_type: product

What they do: Jobber builds field-service management SaaS for small home-service businesses — quoting,
scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, payments and customer communication for lawn care, cleaning, plumbing,
painting, HVAC and similar trades. In their own words: "Job by job, we're transforming the way service
is delivered. Your lawn care provider, home cleaning service, plumber or painter could use Jobber to
better connect with their customers, save time in the office, invoice faster, and get paid! We're
bringing tens of thousands of people together with technology to deliver billions of dollars a year in
services to happy customers." They size their market as "a $400-billion industry that has no dominant
player...yet."

How they describe themselves: Small-business-champion framing — "Jobber exists to help make these small
businesses successful, and when they're successful we all win!" On compensation they take an explicitly
transparent stance: "At Jobber, we believe that compensation should be transparent, fair, and supportive
of your experience and growth... We believe in transparency and open conversations about compensation."
On working model: "We believe great collaboration is intentional, and sometimes that means coming
together in person to build, brainstorm, and connect." The registry's extracted facets read culture:
sustainable, autonomy: medium — the JD language is notably free of hustle vocabulary. The one growth-y
note is the "$400-billion industry with no dominant player...yet" line.

Size / stage / funding: Private, Canadian, growth-stage (extracted stage: growth). Raised a
US$100M Series D (2023, General Atlantic among investors) after a US$60M Series C. Several hundred
employees. Well capitalized and long-established (founded 2011) rather than early or precarious.

Locations / HQ: Headquartered in Edmonton, Alberta. Operates hub offices in Edmonton, Toronto,
Vancouver and Kitchener-Waterloo. Canada-only footprint for these engineering roles.

Remote policy: **Hub-city model, not location-agnostic remote.** Every engineering posting carries the
same clause verbatim: "We believe great collaboration is intentional, and sometimes that means coming
together in person to build, brainstorm, and connect. To support this, the role is open to candidates
based in one of our hub cities: Edmonton, Toronto, Vancouver, or Kitchener-Waterloo." Extracted facets
label the Senior Software Engineer as hybrid and the Staff Software Engineer as remote, but both JD
bodies carry the identical hub-city restriction, so the "remote" label is a mis-extraction — the real
policy is hub-anchored.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Canada-only, but not open Canada.** Employment is Canadian and the pay is
quoted in CAD, so the country is right — but eligibility is restricted to residence in one of four
named metros. Kimberley, British Columbia is not a hub city and is ~800 km from Vancouver, so a
Kimberley-based candidate does not meet the stated location requirement on any of the three live roles.

Engineering & tech: Product-team-aligned engineering organized around business domains rather than
platform layers. Teams named in the Senior SWE JD include QuoteMeal (quoting/estimating, "web and mobile
solutions"), Lending (access to funds, QuickBooks Online sync), and Franchise (multi-location account
architecture, Partner HQ, lead routing, royalty reporting, onboarding, partner tooling — described as
"deep product and platform challenges"). Work is squarely product/feature engineering with some platform
depth in the Franchise/account-architecture area. Rails-era SaaS stack.

Notable / other: Compensation is fully posted on every JD with an explicit min/mid/max philosophy —
"Our range is intentionally broad to support growth and long-term impact, with fully established hires
typically starting around the midpoint. The higher end of the range is reserved for those who have
demonstrated deep expertise and lasting contributions." Total comp includes equity/stock options, RRSP/
TFSA/FHSA matching, fully paid extended health premiums, health and wellness stipends, and a talent
development program with career coaching. Frequent "best workplace in Canada" style recognition.

Posted pay (CAD base, from their own JDs):

- Senior Software Engineer — min $125,800 / mid $147,900 / max $170,100
- Staff Software Engineer — min $169,200 / mid $199,050 / max $228,900 (registry, extractor-derived)

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer; Intermediate Software Engineer; Staff Software Engineer.
