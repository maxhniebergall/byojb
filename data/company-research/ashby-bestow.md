# Bestow — research
Provider/key: ashby:bestow | company_type: product
What they do: Vertical technology platform for the life-insurance industry — not an insurer itself. "Bestow isn't selling policies. We're building the infrastructure that helps an entire industry move faster." Sells a platform to large life insurance carriers that "unifies the fragmented, legacy value chain, enabling carriers to launch products in weeks instead of years", powered by AI and automation.
How they describe themselves: "Life insurance is one of the world's most important products. It's also one of the hardest to build, distribute, and modernize. Bestow exists to change that." They describe the team as one that "moves with precision, purpose, and heart" and pitch "help reimagine a centuries-old industry with lasting impact". No separate published values list was reachable this session. Notably they describe the engineering culture as "remote-first and async-heavy" and ask that technical decisions, trade-offs and status be communicated "clearly in writing".

Remote policy: "Bestow offers flexible remote/hybrid work"; "Flexible work-from-home policy and open to remote"; "Remote and WFH options". The engineering team is described as "remote-first and async-heavy".
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO (as posted). No Canadian entity is mentioned anywhere.
Engineering & tech: The IPC (Implementation & Platform Configuration) Engineering team owns the digital life-insurance enrollment experience for carrier partners. Stack: Python + PostgreSQL, Go, and a Java-based Drools rules engine; gRPC, REST and async messaging; Protocol Buffers and OpenAPI; Kubernetes on Google Cloud Platform. Practices: SLOs, observability, shared on-call ("share on-call responsibility for IPC workloads"), design review and pairing. Agentic AI coding tools (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot) are a stated requirement of daily practice, not optional.
