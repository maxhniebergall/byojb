# Slice — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:slice | company_type: product
What they do: Slice is a technology and services platform for independent pizzerias. Founder Ilir Sela "started Slice with the belief that local pizzerias deserve all of the advantages of major franchises without compromising their independence." Starting from his family's shops, it now "empower[s] over tens of thousands of restaurants with the technology, services, and collective power that owners need to better serve their digitally minded customers." Product surface covers online ordering, marketing, and group-buying/collective-supply services for shops competing against large chains and third-party delivery apps.
How they describe themselves: Mission framing is explicitly David-vs-Goliath — "our mission is to level the playing field for small businesses"; "Local pizzerias already outperform the big chains in offline sales, yet are outsold 20-to-1 online – a gap we're determined to close"; when a shop partners with Slice the goal is that it feels "as if they've just hired 1,000 people". Engineering culture is described in founder-mode terms: they want "bold, founder-like engineers who aren't afraid to challenge the status quo", an "Owner's mindset… You think like a founder… move with urgency… thrive in a fast-paced, ambiguous startup environment", people who chase "asymmetric opportunities where a small technical change can deliver disproportionate value", and closes with "If you've ever dreamed of building something big, scrappy, and impactful". Careers site is slice.careers; postings are served through Greenhouse.
Size / stage / funding: Venture-backed private startup; exact stage, headcount and funding not stated in the material reviewed and NOT verified here. The Services backend team is small and explicitly sized: "a tight-knit team of 5 to 8 backend engineers (across the US, UK, and Europe)". Benefits are quoted in GBP ("£200 per annum employee wellbeing allowance") and they cite Diversity Mark NI membership, indicating a substantial Northern Ireland / UK engineering presence alongside the US.
Locations / HQ: US (New York-associated, unverified) plus UK/Europe engineering, including Northern Ireland. Canadian hiring is limited to Ontario.
Remote policy: Distributed/remote — the team spans the US, UK and Europe, they want a "Remote-ready collaborator… comfortable collaborating with teammates across different time zones" who can communicate asynchronously, and benefits include "flexible working hours". So async-friendly in principle.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Partial — Ontario only.** The JD states verbatim: "This position is open to applicants based in Canada in the Ontario province." That is an explicit provincial restriction; British Columbia is excluded. Not workable from Kimberley.
Engineering & tech: Primary stack is **Python (Flask)** microservices on cloud infrastructure, with databases, monitoring/logging and DevOps ownership — "You build it, you own it - from writing unit tests to fine-tuning logging and alerting". Deliberately polyglot and pragmatic: "You might rewrite a microservice from Go to Python, craft a script in Ruby, or leverage a new cloud service – whatever it takes." Engineers own projects end-to-end with product and design.
Notable / other: Benefits include medical/dental/vision, flexible hours, generous time off, a wellbeing allowance and "market leading maternity and paternity schemes". Hiring process is stated up front: intro (30 min) → 90-minute live coding + 60-minute technical → 45-minute hiring manager → offer. Note the Services team posting explicitly welcomes "associate, intermediate or senior level" applicants for several openings, so leveling is fluid.
Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Developer, Services (Canada/Ontario); Software Developer, Services; Software Developer, Web.
