# Proofpoint — research
Provider/key: workday:proofpoint/proofpointcareers | company_type: product

What they do: Enterprise cybersecurity. In their own words, "Proofpoint is a global leader in human- and agent-centric cybersecurity. We protect how people, data, and AI agents connect across email, cloud, and collaboration tools. Over 80 of the Fortune 100, 10,000 large enterprises, and millions of smaller organizations trust Proofpoint to stop threats, prevent data loss, and build resilience across their people and AI workflows." Product lines visible in the postings include email security and threat intelligence, data loss prevention, and **Digital Communications Governance (DCG)** — enterprise archiving, eDiscovery and compliance. Their stated mission: "safeguard the digital world and empower people to work securely and confidently."

How they describe themselves: They publish a named values acronym, **BRAVE**, quoted in full from their JDs: **B**old in how we dream and innovate; **R**esponsive to feedback, challenges and opportunities; **A**ccountable for results and best in class outcomes; **V**isionary in future focused problem-solving; **E**xceptional in execution and impact. Culture statement: "Our culture is rooted in values that inspire belonging, empower purpose and drive success — every day, for everyone." Perks they list: "Competitive compensation; Comprehensive benefits; Career success on your terms; Flexible work environment; Annual wellness and community outreach days; Always on recognition for your contributions; Global collaboration and networking opportunities", plus (from a US pay-transparency block) "flexible time off, a comprehensive well-being program with two paid Wellbeing Days and two paid Volunteer Days per year, plus a three-week Work from Anywhere option."

Size / stage / funding: Large, established enterprise vendor — formerly NASDAQ-listed, taken private by Thoma Bravo in 2021. Global. The scale claims above (80 of the Fortune 100, 10,000 large enterprises) are the company's own. Exact current headcount and financials are *unverified* from the material available.

Locations / HQ: Multi-site global. Offices appearing in live postings: Sunnyvale CA, Draper UT, Boston MA, Utah, Toronto Canada, Bengaluru India, Hyderabad India. Their US pay bands name SF Bay Area / NYC metro, plus California, Colorado, Connecticut, Illinois, DC metro, Maryland, Massachusetts, New Jersey, Texas, Washington, Virginia and Alaska as distinct markets — a wide US footprint.

Remote policy: "Flexible work environment" and a "three-week Work from Anywhere option" are stated, but the live engineering roles are attached to specific offices or specific countries. Several India roles are labelled "Bengaluru, India - Remote". The Toronto role is explicitly office-based: "Proofpoint's Archiving Ingestion team is looking for a Staff Software Engineer to work from the Toronto office… You will be based in Toronto and be the technical lead of a hybrid development team spanning Toronto and India."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified NO for the current openings.** There is exactly one Canadian posting — Staff Software Engineer (MIP tech lead), R14422 — and it explicitly requires working from the Toronto office. Every other relevant role is a US office location (Draper UT, Boston, Sunnyvale) or India-remote. Nothing in the registry or the JD text indicates remote hiring anywhere in Canada outside Toronto, and no BC/Mountain-Time-compatible option exists. For a candidate who cannot relocate from Kimberley BC, this company currently has no eligible role.

Engineering & tech: Two clusters are visible.
- **Satori AI Platform team** (Draper UT / US): an *internal* platform "that every Proofpoint product team uses to build agentic applications — spanning AI-assisted development, evaluation-based release gates, OpenTelemetry observability for agents in production, and cross-product AuthX for AI workloads." Stated stack: agent runtimes and SDKs on LangGraph, OpenAI Agents SDK, Anthropic's Agent SDK and MCP; typed Python and TypeScript/Node; eval tooling (LangSmith, Braintrust, promptfoo); OpenTelemetry including the GenAI semantic conventions; microservices in cloud. They define the job as setting "what 'release-ready' means for an agent at Proofpoint: the eval suites, safety checks, telemetry, and AuthX contracts that gate every rollout", and note "Staff engineers at Proofpoint still write code". Their own framing: "we empower small teams to make large impacts, the systems are inherently non-deterministic, and the bar for what we ship to product teams is high."
- **Archive / DCG Ingestion team** (Toronto): "the backbone of our Digital Communications Governance product line… processing millions of messages daily across email, collaboration, and cloud connectors." Stated stack: Kafka (MSK/Confluent) consumer-group design, partition strategy, offset management, schema evolution, exactly-once semantics; strong Java; REST APIs; AWS (MSK, EKS, S3, IAM, Lambda, CodePipeline); EKS with KEDA autoscaling and Helm; Terraform/Terragrunt; RDS/MySQL, SQLite, Argo Workflows, GitOps; Kafka Streams / Flink / Spark Structured Streaming as pluses. Data-engineering principles at enterprise scale: "idempotency, deduplication, data quality validation, and observability". They emphasise SLO ownership, incident/RCA leadership, and cost-per-throughput awareness, and state on-call is limited: "Be available for very limited on-call during critical deployment activities."

Notable / other: Both teams state an explicit **AI-first development mandate** — the Toronto role lists "AI-first mindset: You excel with spec-driven development and are willing to mentor others to do the same" as a hard requirement and names Claude Code and Copilot; Red-flag-free but unusually prescriptive about tooling. Also India-heavy staffing: four identical "Backend Golang Developer" reqs (R14189–R14192) in Bengaluru plus multiple Senior Software Engineer reqs there.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer - AI Platform Team (Draper UT + 3 locations); Software Engineer - AI Platform Team (Draper UT + 3 locations); Staff Software Engineer (Boston + 4 locations); Staff Software Engineer, MIP tech lead (Toronto, office-based); Sr. Data Architect, Large Scale Distributed Systems (Sunnyvale + 2); Sr. Software Engineer (Backend) (Hyderabad); Backend Golang Developer ×4 (Bengaluru, remote); Senior Software Engineer ×2 (Bengaluru, remote).
