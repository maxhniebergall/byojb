# NerdWallet — research
Provider/key: ashby:nerdwallet | company_type: product

What they do: NerdWallet is a US consumer-finance platform — "building tools and experiences that help people make smarter financial decisions." It publishes comparison content, calculators and personalized recommendations across credit cards, banking, mortgages, loans, insurance and investing, and monetizes primarily through affiliate/referral revenue from financial-product partners. It operates a Canadian arm ("NerdWallet Canada") which is the hiring entity for the role in the registry. Publicly traded in the US (NASDAQ: NRDS).

How they describe themselves: The JD frames a team "that values curiosity, informed risk-taking, and thoughtful problem-solving," and the role as one where you "have room to bring new ideas, influence how AI and automation practices evolve." Remote stance stated verbatim: "NerdWallet is proud to be a remote-first company! We believe great work can be done anywhere. No matter where you are based, NerdWallet offers benefits and perks to support the physical, financial, and emotional well being of you and your family." Benefit headings are self-consciously playful and employee-balance-oriented: "Work Hard, Stay Balanced," a "Rejuvenation Policy" for vacation time, "Have Some Fun! (Nerds are fun, too)," and "Plan for your future." Employees are called "Nerds"; company-wide rituals include NerdLove (employee appreciation), an annual Charity Auction, and hackathons.

Size / stage / funding: US-public company (NASDAQ: NRDS), IPO'd 2021. Established and revenue-generating rather than runway-dependent, though as a public consumer-marketing business it carries quarterly-growth pressure and the sector has seen cost discipline in recent years. Precise current headcount not established from first-party sources during this pass.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco, California HQ, with a Canadian entity. Remote-first, so office geography is largely nominal.

Remote policy: Remote-first, stated explicitly in the JD. Benefits are explicitly portable ("No matter where you are based"). Canadian package includes a monthly healthcare stipend, provincial public holidays, a paid sabbatical, monthly wellness / wifi / cell-phone stipends, a work-from-home equipment stipend, and an RRSP with a 4% match eligible one month after hire.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, with one exclusion.** The JD states: "This is a remote position and a person can be located anywhere in Canada (with the exception of Quebec)." British Columbia is eligible. No time-zone restriction is stated on the Canadian posting. Note the registry also carries a US-based twin of the same role — the Canada-specific requisition is the relevant one.

Engineering & tech: The security/AI role is substantively an applied-LLM platform role, not a prompt-wrapper. Stated work: designing multi-agent LLM systems and routing logic to automate threat modeling, security design review, policy Q&A and vulnerability analysis; RAG pipelines and semantic search over large code and documentation repositories; automated code review for insecure patterns; integrations with GitHub, Slack, Jira, Confluence and cloud platforms; REST APIs and platform services with authn/authz, rate limiting, observability; scalable data-processing pipelines over large codebases (extraction, indexing, stream processing, batch jobs, parallel execution); AI application security controls including prompt-injection prevention, sensitive-data filtering and supply-chain security; and SSDLC automation. Required stack: Python or Go for production backend services; AWS, containers, Kubernetes or ECS, serverless, IaC; message queues, NoSQL; Redis and caching/performance patterns including semantic caching, TTLs and invalidation. Bar is 3+ years of software or security engineering. Reports to an AI Security Manager. Contributes to security incident response and on-call needs.

Notable / other: The role is explicitly a technical-lead position on high-priority initiatives and an internal subject-matter-expert seat on AI adoption — the internal customer is NerdWallet's own engineering org, not external users. The team is described as having "a growing backlog of high-impact work." Posted Canadian base range for the role is CAD $123,000–$188,000.

Open relevant roles (sample): Security Software Engineer, AI & Automation — Canada; Security Software Engineer, AI & Automation (US twin); Staff Software Engineer, Consumer Banking & Credit Cards.
