# Zensurance — research
Provider/key: lever:zensurance | company_type: product (InsurTech)
What they do: Zensurance is a Canadian commercial-insurance brokerage/InsurTech — "redefining commercial insurance for Canadian businesses". They sell business coverage online through a digital-first platform that combines "advanced technology with deep industry expertise to deliver tailored insurance solutions", working through "a partner network of more than 50 insurance providers". Their build is in-house proprietary technology.
How they describe themselves: Four stated values — "Invent" (solving complex problems creatively), "Deliver" (setting ambitious goals with accountability), "Include" (an open, safe space for all), and "Integrity" (doing what's right for customers and colleagues). Careers copy: "At Zensurance, we value ownership, collaboration, and innovation. Our team thrives on solving complex challenges, challenging the status quo, and making a real impact in an industry ready for change... If you're looking to build something meaningful in a fast-growing, customer-focused company, we'd love to hear from you!"
Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, high-growth; headcount and funding stage are not disclosed on the careers page. Growth is externally validated: Deloitte Technology Fast 50 (2023, 2024, 2025), Deloitte Technology Fast 500 (2024, 2025), Top Insurance Employers (2022).
Locations / HQ: Head office in downtown Toronto (Suite 800 – 111 Peter St, Toronto, ON M5V 2H1). Services are sold across Canada — Ontario, Alberta, British Columbia, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Newfoundland and PEI.
Remote policy: Remote-first in practice — every live engineering posting is titled "(Remote First)", and the careers page offers "Full-time remote work options", stating employees can work "in our head office in downtown Toronto, at a remote location, or from the comfort of your home."
Remote-Canada eligibility: YES. Roles are posted Toronto, ON with a remote flag and explicit "Remote First" titles, on a Canadian entity paying CAD. The careers page does not name eligible provinces, so BC specifically is not stated in writing — but the company writes business across BC and the remote language carries no geographic caveat, so BC/Mountain Time is very likely fine and is the thing to confirm in a first conversation.
Engineering & tech: In-house proprietary insurance platform. Live roles cover core application development, a data-focused senior role, an application-support senior role and a Staff-level integration role (integrations with 50+ carrier partners implies substantial back-end/integration plumbing). Stack details are not published.
Notable / other: Compensation is posted on every live JD in CAD — Senior Software Developer, Senior Software Developer Application Support and Senior Software Developer Data are all $104,000–$145,000; Staff Software Developer – Integration is $155,000–$180,000. That transparency plus the award record is a good stability signal for a private company.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Developer (Remote First); Senior Software Developer, Data (Remote First); Senior Software Developer, Application Support (Remote First); Staff Software Developer – Integration (Remote First).
