# RTX — research
Provider/key: workday:globalhr/rec_rtx_ext_gateway | company_type: product (aerospace & defense manufacturer)
What they do: RTX is one of the world's largest aerospace and defense companies, formed from the Raytheon/United Technologies combination. It operates three business units — Collins Aerospace, Pratt & Whitney, and Raytheon — and describes itself as delivering "mission critical technologies of the highest quality." Software engineering here is embedded/defense systems, avionics, radar and test software, plus some commercial aviation SaaS (FlightAware, ARINCDirect).
How they describe themselves: Stated values are "safety, trust, respect, accountability, collaboration and innovation." Marketing claims include "every second of every day, an aircraft carrying RTX technology takes flight," roughly 50% of the world's population protected by its defense products, and 90% of US Department of War and commercial space launches using its products. Scale framing: 60,000 engineers, 250 factories, multibillion-dollar R&D.
Size / stage / funding: Very large publicly traded prime defense contractor. Long-cycle government and commercial-aviation programs; extremely stable, but bureaucratic.
Locations / HQ: Headquartered in Arlington, Virginia. Global sites including Poland, Australia and Singapore. Registry postings cluster in Marlborough MA, Tucson AZ, Fullerton CA, Richardson TX, Ft. Wayne IN, State College PA, Wilsonville OR, Santa Isabel Puerto Rico, Hyderabad India, the UAE, and one Canadian site: Longueuil, QC (Pratt & Whitney Canada engine-test software).
Remote policy: Mixed, but strongly onsite-weighted. Of 29 live relevant postings, the great majority are explicitly "(Onsite)" at a named building address. A minority are designated Remote (US-TX-REMOTE, US-ID-REMOTE, US-AZ-REMOTE, US-VA-REMOTE). Its own JD text defines these: "Remote: Employees who are working in Remote roles will work primarily offsite (from home). An employee may be expected to travel to the site location as needed." It also notes candidates "may be asked to attend select steps of the interview process in-person at one of our office locations, regardless of whether the role is designated as on-site, hybrid or remote."
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — verified disqualifying. Even the fully-remote FlightAware posting states: "This job requires a U.S. Person. A U.S. Person is a lawful permanent resident... U.S. citizens, U.S. nationals, U.S. permanent residents, or individuals granted refugee or asylee status." This is ITAR/export-control gating and applies across the US postings. The remote roles are additionally scoped to US states. The only Canadian posting is an onsite role in Longueuil, QC.
Engineering & tech: Defense and aerospace software — secure communications, battlefield radar, mission management, test equipment software, head-up guidance systems, Java systems, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, Kubernetes/cloud administration, DevOps tooling, and Python/PySpark data engineering in Puerto Rico. Deep ladder: Software Engineer I/II, Senior, Principal, Senior Principal. Security clearance is required or preferred on many roles (the FlightAware remote role is an exception: "Security Clearance Type: None/Not Required").
Notable / other: Pay ranges are posted on essentially every US JD (see company-comp rows) and are wide — RTX states each "salary range provided is a good faith estimate representative of all experience levels." FlightAware and ARINCDirect are the commercial-aviation software arms and are the least defense-gated parts of the portfolio, but still carry the U.S. Person requirement.
