# Seeq — research
Provider/key: workable:seeq | company_type: product
What they do: Seeq builds industrial advanced-analytics and AI software for process-industry data — pharmaceuticals, energy, manufacturing. In their own words the core product is "a robust calculation engine capable of executing advanced math and machine learning algorithms on streaming time-series data", with generative AI layered on to "enhance usability and accelerate outcomes". Customers are industrial enterprises; the domain is boring-in-the-good-sense, mission-critical time-series infrastructure.
How they describe themselves: Mission — "Accelerate the Advanced Analytics journey to a modern AI-driven, sustainable industrial future"; and, from the JDs, "provide software and services that convert that data into meaningful information that the business can use to improve profitability and sustainability." Stated values cover integrity, customer success, valuing team members, agility, community support, financial responsibility, and continuous improvement. JD tone: "a wonderful, kind-hearted, talented team that loves to collaborate, lead by example, and exceed our customers' expectations"; agile principles; heavy emphasis on craft — unit testing, CI, refactoring, eliminating technical debt, pair programming and code review.
Size / stage / funding: Privately held; no headcount or funding disclosed on their about page. Founded by "serial entrepreneurs" with an executive team and board experienced in "successful startup ventures in high-growth environments". CEO Dr. Lisa Graham. Certified a Great Place to Work; listed in Deloitte Technology Fast 500 and Inc. Best Places to Work.
Locations / HQ: No single HQ — described as "a privately held virtual company with employees across the United States, Asia, Canada, Europe, and South America."
Remote policy: Remote-first and remote-only: "Seeq is a remote-first (and only) company." Collaboration runs on Zoom, Slack and their own "Qube Virtual Office". Annual company meetups; generous home-office allowance.
Remote-Canada eligibility: YES — strongest evidence in this batch. Their about page explicitly lists Canada among the countries where employees are located, and the live "Backend Software Engineer (Staff/Principal)" JD quotes its range in Canadian dollars ("$237,000 - $286,000 Canadian."). Caveat: the Workable postings themselves are labelled "United States (Remote)" and the JD says "You must be authorized to work in the country in which you reside" while declining to sponsor US F1/H-1B visas — so the location label and the CAD range disagree. Worth confirming which specific reqs are open to a Canadian resident.
Engineering & tech: JVM languages (Kotlin, Java), also Rust/C++ mentioned as acceptable expertise; Node.js, GraphQL, Postgres/MySQL, distributed computing and cloud platforms; agile/Scrum/Kanban with continuous delivery. Staff+ engineers "shape the technical direction and architecture", lead design of complex systems, and mentor. Explicit expectation that engineers engage with customers.
Notable / other: Perks — unlimited PTO, equity and cash bonus, internet/mobile reimbursement, 4-week paid sabbatical every 7 years, vacation bonus program, pet-friendly (i.e. home) workspace.
Open relevant roles (sample): Backend Software Engineer (Staff/Principal); Staff Software Engineer - Platform; Staff Software Engineer - Developer Experience.
