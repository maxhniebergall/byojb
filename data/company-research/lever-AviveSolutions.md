# Avive — research
Provider/key: lever:AviveSolutions | company_type: product
What they do: Avive Solutions, Inc. builds Automated External Defibrillators (AEDs) paired with a connected emergency-response software platform. In their own words they are "a growth stage Automated External Defibrillator (AED) company with a connected response system that is rapidly gaining market share," combining "advanced – yet still accessible – hardware, and software" to speed response to out-of-hospital sudden cardiac arrest. Buyers are businesses, schools, municipalities and EMS systems; the software side coordinates responders and device fleet telemetry.
How they describe themselves: Mission: "for all cardiac arrest victims to have rapid access to life-saving defibrillation" — they note Sudden Cardiac Arrest "is a leading cause of death in the United States, and we are on a mission to change that." Self-description: "a mission driven team that is quite literally saving lives" and "a dynamic organization that builds elegant, creative solutions to solve complex problems." The careers page emphasises employees "inspired by Avive's purpose" and "a fun and engaging work environment" with team lunches, office snacks and collaborative events. No formal values list is published.
Size / stage / funding: Self-described "growth stage." Exact headcount, funding round and investors are NOT disclosed on their careers page and could not be verified this session — treat as unverified. Equity is granted to all employees, implying venture-backed private.
Locations / HQ: Not stated on the fetched careers page. Company is US-focused (its mission statement and market framing are entirely US). The careers page references office snacks and team lunches, implying at least one physical office.
Remote policy: Requisitions are posted as "Remote, remote" on Lever, but the careers page states no remote policy at all. The office-perk language suggests a hybrid reality for some staff.
Remote-Canada eligibility: UNVERIFIED, and probably no. Nothing on the careers page or in any of the three JDs mentions Canada or an eligible-country list. Benefits named are US-specific (401(k) with 3% employer contribution, US medical/dental/vision, 11 paid holidays), which normally indicates US-only payroll. The product and regulatory market described is the United States. Do not assume Canada eligibility without asking them.
Engineering & tech: Backend services, APIs, data pipelines and event-driven systems; distributed systems, cloud-native architectures, observability, identity/access patterns; a DevOps function and a QA function exist as separate teams. There is also a Lead Backend Architect track above the senior IC role. Specific languages/cloud are not named in the JD text.
Notable / other: Benefits disclosed: comprehensive medical/dental/vision; equity for all team members; 401(k) with 3% employer contribution starting day one; 11 paid holidays plus accrued PTO and three floating holidays; employees can buy company AEDs at reduced rates. Hiring runs through Lever with a Gem talent community. No salary transparency on the careers page or the JDs.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Backend Engineer [Remote]; Lead Backend Architect [Remote]; Senior Devops Engineer [Remote].
