# Neo Financial — research
Provider/key: ashby:neofinancial | company_type: product
What they do: Neo Financial is a Canadian consumer fintech — credit cards, savings, mortgages and rewards — built as an alternative to the incumbent banks. Their framing: "Financial software runs underneath the biggest decisions people make. When it's good, it saves real money over years. When it's not, those losses compound just as quietly. Most of it was built to capture value, not create it. Neo is building the alternative." They report "over one million customers".
How they describe themselves: Engineering-autonomy-first, and explicitly filtering for a high-agency, low-process temperament. "The platform behind that growth is built entirely in-house: 150 services in production, hundreds of pull requests merged every week, tens of millions of transactions processed every month. The engineers here own their services from architecture decisions through to production. There's no QA team to hand work off to, no DevOps team to manage your deploys." They actively recruit against slow environments — "If you've spent time at a company where good ideas died in committee and you know you could be going faster, this is the environment those engineers tend to thrive in" — and state the inverse plainly: "This is not the right role if you want steady state and fully scoped requirements before writing a line. The engineers who thrive here find that kind of environment frustrating." Expectations listed include owning production and on-call-style accountability ("When something breaks on your watch, you fix it and figure out how to prevent it"), going "beyond the ask", pushing back on scope, and treating "AI as a force multiplier".
Size / stage / funding: Private, late-stage, very high growth — "Three consecutive years as Canada's fastest-growing company on the Deloitte Fast 50. $400M+ raised. Over one million customers." Venture-funded rather than profitable-by-disclosure; headcount not stated but implied to be large (150 services in production).
Locations / HQ: Calgary, Alberta (headquarters), with Toronto and Vancouver presence — the non-engineering posting lists "Calgary, AB, Toronto, ON, Remote, Vancouver, BC".
Remote policy: Office-anchored for engineering. The Senior/Staff/Principal Software Developer req is posted "Calgary, AB — Full-time" with "Relocation support available for candidates moving to Calgary." No remote option is offered on that role. Notably a non-engineering req (Mortgage Fulfillment Officer) does list "Remote" among its locations, so remote arrangements exist at the company — just not on this engineering posting.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO for the relevant engineering role — verified from the JD. It is Calgary-based with relocation support offered, which is the opposite of remote hiring. Canadian work authorization is required ("Must be a Canadian citizen, Permanent Resident or have a valid work permit") and candidates undergo "a security screening including a criminal record check and credit check". One mitigating note: Calgary is Mountain Time, so if a remote exception were ever available the hours would be ideal — but nothing on the posting supports one.
Engineering & tech: "Our stack is GraphQL, Mongo, Node.js, TypeScript, React, and React Native on AWS." 150 microservices, engineer-owned deploys ("Deploys are independent and automated. One button to ship when your work is ready, not when someone schedules a release window"), code ownership per repo, tests written as part of building, observability built in from the start ("alerting thresholds, dashboards, tracing, logging"). Tooling is generous and named: "Cursor, Claude with unlimited tokens, CodeRabbit, GitHub Enterprise, Datadog, Wiz, Sentry, Snowflake, AWS. MacBook Pros."
Notable / other: Compensation is stated openly on the JD — "Competitive salaries (up to CA$275,000) that lead the Calgary market", plus STIP cash bonuses tied to company milestones and "equity through stock options that are in the money from day one", with the pitch that "your 4-to-5-year total comp is designed to significantly outpace traditional tech tracks". Benefits: comprehensive health, 21 days PTO, parental top-up. The role spans three levels in one posting (Senior / Staff / Principal).
