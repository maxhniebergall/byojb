# StackBlitz (Bolt.new) — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:stackblitz | company_type: product

What they do: StackBlitz builds WebContainers — a WebAssembly-based OS that runs Node.js entirely inside the browser — which powers an online IDE they say is used by "over 1 million developers every month". On top of that they built Bolt.new, an AI app builder that generates, edits and deploys full-stack web and mobile apps from natural language. Their site names Google, Meta, Shopify, Salesforce, Intel, Mozilla, Cloudflare and Stripe as teams using the platform.

How they describe themselves: "We're the team that brought you WebContainers… That breakthrough kicked off our journey in 2019." On Bolt.new: "the fastest way to go from idea to production without writing traditional code… No installs. No setup. Just smart automation and instant dev environments that let you move at the speed of thought." On the team: "We're a fully remote team, globally distributed, deeply collaborative, and seriously passionate about building the future of software development. This is your chance to join a small team with a big vision. If you love shipping fast, solving real problems, and pushing the boundaries of what's possible, we'd love to meet you." No formal published values list was found.

Size / stage / funding: Self-described as "a small team with a big vision"; venture-backed private company, founded 2019. Exact headcount, funding round and profitability were NOT verified from first-party sources in this pass — treat as unknown.

Remote policy: Fully remote, globally distributed. JDs state explicitly: "You do **not** need to be located in the U.S. — we're remote-friendly" and "You do not need a college degree to apply."

Engineering & tech: TypeScript front-end and back-end plus Ruby on Rails back-end services ("We're opinionated about our stack"). Infrastructure runs across AWS, GCP *and* Azure with Terraform as the common IaC layer. AI team work is genuinely platform-flavoured: agent loops, long-running workflows, context compression across large codebases, model routing, multi-provider integration and benchmarking (OpenAI / Anthropic / Google), tool-use and workflow orchestration (Vercel AI SDK, LangGraph mentioned), guardrails, production observability, and eval harnesses/datasets. JDs state "This isn't API integration work." SRE practice: SLIs/SLOs and error budgets, launch checklists, blameless postmortems, paved-road tooling — with a stated on-call rotation of "currently one week per month" shared by every SRE.

Notable / other: The AI roles emphasise failure-mode analysis and dataset methodology rather than prompt tinkering. Staff SRE is explicitly a non-managerial high-influence IC role. Bonus points listed include open-source AI/ML contributions and fine-tuning/alignment experience (SFT, RLHF/RLAIF, DPO/ORPO).

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Applied AI Engineer (Remote); Staff Applied AI Engineer (Remote); Staff Site Reliability Engineer (Remote).
