# LanceDB — research
Provider/key: ashby:lancedb | company_type: product
What they do: LanceDB is an AI-native multimodal lakehouse built on the open-source Lance columnar format. It unifies data curation, feature engineering, search and retrieval (vector search and RAG), streaming of training data, and interactive exploration of large-scale AI datasets — the pitch being that teams stop stitching together scattered data systems. Production users named on their site include Runway, World Labs, Netflix, Luma, Uber, NVIDIA, Character.AI, CodeRabbit and ByteDance/Volcano Engine, across AI/ML, autonomous vehicles and gaming.
How they describe themselves: "The preeminent data platform for multimodal AI use cases... the best foundation for your AI application, and powers some of the most groundbreaking applications and challenging requirements today." Marketing emphasises 10x faster data experimentation through versioning and branching, up to 70% GPU utilization during training, unification of multimodal workflows, and developer experience (Python UDFs, no-rewrite schema evolution, accessible abstractions over petabyte-scale infrastructure). On the team: "You'll join a world-class team of open-source builders, including co-authors of pandas, and contributors to HDFS, Arrow, Iceberg, and HBase." They ask for "proven ability to move fast, work independently, and collaborate with a high-caliber team".
