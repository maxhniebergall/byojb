# Thinkific — research
Provider/key: ashby:thinkific | company_type: product

What they do: Thinkific is a Vancouver-based "learning commerce" SaaS platform. Founded 2012, it lets independent experts and companies build, host, sell and run online courses, communities and content — "bringing together community, courses, and content with commerce… all from one AI-powered platform." Customers are creators and learning businesses (SMB-to-mid-market SaaS), who in turn serve millions of end learners. Products span the core course platform, Thinkific Plus (enterprise), payments/commerce, and embedded customer-facing analytics.

How they describe themselves: "We're a team of 300+ Thinkers building products that matter. Every role at Thinkific contributes to raising the bar for online learning, supporting thousands of learning businesses, and creating real-world impact for millions of learners around the world. You'll work alongside curious, collaborative teammates who care deeply about what they build and who they build it for." Stated values: **succeeding together, ethics and integrity, learning and growth, passionate debate, and equality**. They lean hard on hiring-experience fairness ("We're committed to a fair, inclusive, and human hiring experience… you always know what to expect"), publish a Candidate Hub with the full interview process, and explicitly permit candidates to use AI tools when applying. Self-described as "an AI-forward company — we use AI tools across all teams," with AI-assisted recruitment operations disclosed openly and every hiring decision still made by a person. Tone is warm and low-hype; notably absent are "fast-paced", "hustle" or "rocketship" phrasings, though the Data Engineer JD does ask for someone who thrives on "undefined problems" and "a small, dynamic team."

Size / stage / funding: 300+ employees. **Publicly traded on the TSX (THNC)** — not VC-runway dependent. Roughly $60M ARR scale per public trackers. Certified Great Place to Work; named one of Canada's Most Admired Corporate Cultures by Waterstone.

Locations / HQ: HQ Vancouver, British Columbia. Distributed team across Canada, with employees in Victoria, Toronto, Calgary, Halifax and elsewhere.

Remote policy: Flexible/distributed. "Choose to work from home from anywhere in Canada, at our Vancouver HQ, a co-working space, or anywhere there's wifi for a change of scenery." Device program (company-owned MacBook Pro), monthly co-working allowance, and a home-office budget.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes — Canada is the default, not the exception.** Both live JDs are posted as "Distributed – Canada" with salary ranges quoted in CAD, and the benefit copy says work from anywhere in Canada. HQ is in the same province the candidate lives in (BC), so Pacific/Mountain hours are the native working timezone. This is about as unambiguous as remote-Canada eligibility gets in the registry.

Engineering & tech: Data stack is dbt (SQL) + Python, BigQuery, Looker (including embedded/white-labelled Looker in the product), GCP and Terraform; nice-to-haves list Airflow/Dagster and LLM/agent development for AI-powered data products. Product stack is React and TypeScript with some Ruby (Rails heritage). Engineering ladder is explicit and deep — the Principal Engineer JD references senior, staff and senior-staff engineers, a Lead Architect, Directors of Engineering and a VP Engineering, and describes principals as owning architecture and SaaS/COGs metrics for a domain, writing technical documentation, and "regularly contributing to the engineering blog, giving talks at conferences." On-call rotations exist ("participate in on-call rotations as required"). Hiring processes are short and published: Data Engineer is talent call → take-home → stakeholder interview; Principal is talent call → hiring-manager impact interview → technical interview with two engineers → VP Engineering.

Notable / other: Benefits are unusually strong for the size — Day-1 health/vision/dental for family, $3,000 mental health care, $900/yr health-or-personal spending account, flexible PTO with an encouraged **minimum four weeks** plus "Thinker Holidays" (extended summer long weekends) and December holiday closure, parental leave top-ups up to 32 weeks, fertility coverage, 3 paid volunteer days, $2,000 CAD/yr Learn-and-Grow fund, equity plus team-wide bonuses plus an Employee Share Purchase Plan. Land acknowledgement for Musqueam, Squamish and Tsleil-Waututh territories on every posting.
