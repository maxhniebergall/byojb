# IDEXX Laboratories — research
Provider/key: workday:idexx/idexx | company_type: product

What they do: IDEXX Laboratories is a publicly traded diagnostics company. Its main business is veterinary diagnostics — in-clinic analysers, reference laboratories and practice-management software for vets — plus water testing and livestock/poultry diagnostics. Their own JD boilerplate states the purpose: "we follow our Purpose and Guiding Principles to help pet owners worldwide keep their companion animals healthy and happy, to ensure safe drinking water for billions." A separate "IDEXX Veterinary Software" portfolio runs the practice-software side.

How they describe themselves: They lean on a stated "Purpose and Guiding Principles" and describe themselves as "an innovation leader in every industry we serve." On culture: "We're proud of the work we do, because our work matters... We enrich the livelihoods of our employees with a positive and respectful work culture that embraces challenges and encourages learning and discovery. At IDEXX, you will be supported by competitive compensation, incentives, and benefits." Also: "We have customers in over 175 countries and a collaborative global workforce." The tone is steady and employer-of-record-ish, with no hustle or hype language anywhere in the JDs — a genuinely low-drama read.

Size / stage / funding: Large, established, publicly traded (NASDAQ: IDXX — background knowledge, not verified here), profitable, global. Not runway-dependent.

Locations / HQ: HQ Westbrook, Maine. Postings reference Westbrook ME as the anchor plus openness to New Hampshire and Massachusetts, one role in London UK, and one "Virtual United States" posting.

Remote policy: Hybrid and explicitly not remote, stated bluntly and repeatedly. "Location: See the location requirement below. This is not a remote role." "We prefer you to be driving distance to our Westbrook, Maine Headquarters where we require a flexible hybrid 8 days per month on-site." "We are only considering candidates located in Maine, New Hampshire, or Massachusetts who are willing to be onsite eight times per month. Reduced onsite requirements may be considered for candidates located farther from the office within [the region]." Even the Senior Software Developer posted as "Virtual United States" says "The ideal candidate will have the ability to work in a hybrid capacity onsite 2x per week or 8x per month" and is tagged #LI-HYBRID. The UK Intermediate Data Engineer role also states a "Hybrid working model."

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — verified explicitly and repeatedly from the JDs. "NOTE: We will not consider anyone outside of Maine, New Hampshire or Massachusetts, at this time." Every engineering role requires monthly on-site presence at the Westbrook, Maine HQ (typically 8 days per month). There is no Canadian posting and no remote track.

Engineering & tech: Roles cover DevOps/platform (Principal Platform Engineer (DevOps), Principal DevOps Engineer II), site reliability (Senior Site Reliability Engineer), data engineering (Senior Data Engineer, AWS Data Platform; Intermediate Data Engineer, London), security tooling (DLP Platform Engineer; Senior Cyber Defense Infrastructure Engineer), and general development (Software Developer II; Senior Software Developer). Stack signals: AWS data platform, cloud environments "AWS, Azure, or hybrid", data-migration and pipeline modernisation across hybrid cloud, DLP/security tooling operations. This is a real, differentiated infrastructure org — SRE, DevOps, data platform and security engineering all exist as distinct functions.

Notable / other: IDEXX posts an unusually candid pay line on every role: "Base annual salary target: $X - $Y (yes, we do have flexibility if needed)", plus annual cash bonus, day-one health/dental/vision, and 5% 401k matching; the Principal DevOps Engineer II adds "yearly equity award." Observed targets: Software Developer II $90,000-$100,000; DLP Platform Engineer $110,000-$125,000; Senior Data Engineer, AWS Data Platform $110,000-$130,000; Principal Platform Engineer (DevOps) $120,000-$140,000; Senior Cyber Defense Infrastructure Engineer $120,000-$150,000; Principal DevOps Engineer II $140,000-$160,000; Senior Software Developer $120,000/year flat; Intermediate Data Engineer (London) £40,000-£50,000. Note the pay compresses hard at the top — a Principal DevOps role tops out at $160,000, low for the title.

Open relevant roles (sample): Principal DevOps Engineer II; Principal Platform Engineer (DevOps); Senior Site Reliability Engineer; Senior Data Engineer, AWS Data Platform; Senior Cyber Defense Infrastructure Engineer; DLP Platform Engineer.
