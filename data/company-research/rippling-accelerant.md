# Accelerant — research
Provider/key: rippling:accelerant | company_type: product

What they do: Accelerant Holdings operates a data-driven "risk exchange" for specialty insurance — a marketplace connecting selected specialty insurance underwriters (members/sellers) with risk capital providers (buyers). In its own words: "Accelerant is a data-driven risk exchange connecting underwriters of specialty insurance risk with risk capital providers. Accelerant was founded in 2018 by a group of longtime insurance industry executives and technology experts who shared a vision of rebuilding the way risk is exchanged – so that it works better, for everyone." It transacts across 20+ countries and 250+ specialty products; its insurers hold an AM Best A- (Excellent) rating.

How they describe themselves: Stated values include **"Fast & Wise"** (move quickly, with purpose) and **"Aggressively Transparent"** (honesty and clarity in everything). They call themselves "a powerhouse team blending commitment, knowledge and innovation" with a mission to "build a smarter, more connected insurance ecosystem", and emphasise serving their Members. Their engineering JDs go further on pace and on AI: "We are building engineering squads for the AI era – teams that can move quickly, own outcomes end to end and deliver with far greater leverage than traditional software teams… Our engineers use AI throughout the development lifecycle to generate code, automate testing, accelerate delivery, and improve quality." They ask for people who "take ownership, learn quickly, collaborate closely and enjoy building useful software in a fast-moving environment", who "ship early, learn quickly from users and production", and who have "a strong interest in using AI not just to assist with coding, but to rethink and improve how software is designed, built, tested, shipped, and operated."

Size / stage / funding: **Public.** IPO'd on the NYSE under ticker **ARX** on 24 July 2025, pricing an upsized offering of 34.5M shares at $21 (above the $18–20 range) to raise ~$724M; the stock rose 36% to $28.50 on debut for a ~$6.4B valuation. Financially substantive and profitable: $26.6M net income on $652.5M revenue for the twelve months ending 31 March 2025. As of 31 March 2025 it had 232 members and 96 risk capital partners on the exchange, with Exchange Written Premium growing at a 217% CAGR since inception.

Locations / HQ: Operates across more than 20 countries. Engineering reqs are tagged to US, UK/EU and (for one role) Canada; the engineering org sits under "Business Solutions and Group IT".

Remote policy: Remote-supportive — they state they "support remote work, allowing employees to work where they're most productive and fulfilled", plus flexible time off and high-quality health/dental benefits. Job locations are expressed as "United States Remote", "United Kingdom Remote", "Canada Remote", and one role as "Remote Flexible / Global remote", so it is a genuinely distributed model with per-req geo restrictions.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Partial and role-dependent — mostly NO for the roles that matter.** Of the live engineering reqs, only the **Data Engineer** is tagged "United States Remote; United Kingdom Remote; **Canada Remote**". The Senior SRE is "United States Remote" only; Senior Software Engineer and Cloud Engineer are "United States Remote; United Kingdom Remote" (no Canada); AI Platform Engineer (Global) is tagged "United States Remote" despite the "Global remote" label in the body. So the two slots that most need pay data (Senior SRE, Senior Software Engineer) are not currently open to a Canada-based worker.

Engineering & tech: Stated stack — React, TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Redis, Snowflake, AWS, Azure. Work is organised into "cross-functional product engineering squads" building "custom software, intelligent workflows, and AI-enabled product capabilities" for internal enterprise solutions, plus scalable APIs, services and modern web applications. Explicitly broad-stack: "Strength in one area of the stack, with the curiosity and ability to contribute more broadly." AI-assisted development is a stated expectation rather than an option.

Notable / other: The insurance-marketplace domain is durable and regulation-adjacent — a genuinely "boring, mission-critical" business — and the public listing plus profitability make it one of the most financially stable companies in this batch. The counterweight is that the engineering identity presented is fast-moving, AI-forward product-and-internal-tools work rather than deep platform ownership.

Open relevant roles (sample): AI Platform Engineer (Global); Senior Software Engineer; Senior SRE; Cloud Engineer; Data Engineer.
