# Thumbtack — research
Provider/key: ashby:thumbtack | company_type: product

What they do: Thumbtack runs a two-sided marketplace for home care — homeowners describe a project (plumbing, landscaping, renovation, cleaning) and are matched with local service professionals who bid on the work. The pros are the paying customers; revenue comes from lead/booking fees. They also ship a homeowner-side app that builds a maintenance plan for a specific house. Self-reported scale: 100 million projects facilitated and 15 million 5-star pro reviews.

How they describe themselves: The stated ambition is "a new era of home care" — empowering both homeowners and the small businesses that serve them. Their leadership framing is explicit and unusual: leadership is "a mindset, a commitment, and an awesome responsibility to elevate others," i.e. it is presented as an expectation of everyone rather than a title. Careers-page tone is warm and steady rather than hustle-coded; the employee testimonials emphasize "open discussions" and mutual support over speed. Notably absent from their own copy: "fast-paced," "rocketship," "scrappy."

Size / stage / funding: 1,000+ employees. Private, venture-backed, and well past early stage — the careers page frames the company as "more than a decade in" (founded 2008). No current funding round or profitability figure is published on their own pages.

Locations / HQ: US-headquartered with no central office culture; hiring geographies are the US, Canada (Ontario and British Columbia only), and the Philippines.

Remote policy: "Virtual-first, not virtual-only." Employees work remotely by default, with periodic in-person offsites and company events rather than an office return. Benefits reinforce this: a $1,000 USD annual remote-work stipend, flexible PTO plus 18 company holidays and a year-end shutdown week, 12 weeks paid parental leave (birthing parents get 8 additional weeks), and BetterUp coaching.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes — and specifically British Columbia.** This is unusually strong evidence. The careers page states hiring for virtual roles in the US, Canada and the Philippines, with remote work anywhere in Ontario and British Columbia. Independently, the registry holds a *separate Canadian posting for nearly every relevant role* — Senior Data Engineer, Senior SWE Customer Growth, Senior SWE Developer Experience, Senior SWE Site Reliability Engineering, Software Engineer AI/ML Infrastructure, and Sr. SWE Platform each exist in both a "Remote, United States" and a "Remote, Ontario, Remote, British Columbia" variant, each with its own CAD salary range. Note the one exception: "Software Engineer, AI/ML Infrastructure (US-Based)" is explicitly US-only, while the non-suffixed AI/ML Infrastructure posting is the Canadian one.

Engineering & tech: The open roles describe a mature, differentiated engineering org rather than a generalist product team — there are dedicated Developer Experience, Site Reliability Engineering, Platform, Data Engineering, and AI/ML Infrastructure functions alongside product teams like Customer Growth. That specialization is the main signal here: internal-facing platform and reliability work is staffed as its own discipline.

Notable / other: Canadian roles post real CAD ranges under BC/Ontario pay-transparency practice, which makes comparison straightforward. The AI/ML Infrastructure role is banded markedly lower (CAD 123.3k–159.5k) than the senior software/platform/SRE/data roles (CAD 180.2k–233.2k) — it is evidently a lower ladder rung despite the similar-sounding title, not a senior-equivalent posting.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Developer Experience; Senior Software Engineer, Site Reliability Engineering; Sr. Software Engineer, Platform; Senior Data Engineer; Software Engineer, AI/ML Infrastructure.
