# Wowza Media Systems — research
Provider/key: workable:wowza-media-systems | company_type: product
What they do: "Wowza powers the world's leading live and on-demand streaming experiences. From global events and enterprise broadcasts to AI-powered video intelligence at the edge, Wowza provides the most trusted, extensible, and developer-friendly streaming platform on the market." The product line is an integrated video platform plus on-premises streaming-server software with builder-focused APIs, used in surveillance, sports, entertainment and healthcare. Founded 2007; claims "35,000+ streaming implementations" and customers including ESPN, NASA, SpaceX and Sony.
How they describe themselves: "As we move into the next era of streaming, we are expanding our product organization with builders who combine technical depth, analytical rigor, and a customer-first mindset." Notably level-headed JD tone on the data role: "This is not a greenfield role. You'll be joining a team with a set of mature, business-critical systems and will be expected to understand them deeply, identify opportunities to modernize and reduce complexity, and execute those improvements with confidence and care", and "a collaborative team that values pragmatism over hype." Leadership is described as bringing "more than 200 years of collective experience" in video technology.
Size / stage / funding: Headcount, ownership and funding are not disclosed on their about page. Nearly two decades old, so an established rather than early-stage business, but stability cannot be confirmed from first-party sources read here.
Locations / HQ: Colorado-based, with "remote employees across the globe".
Remote policy: Distributed workforce — "made up of remote employees across the globe"; postings are labelled remote and the JD mentions collaborating "with remote teammates across time zones via video conference and async communication".
Remote-Canada eligibility: NOT ESTABLISHED. Despite the "remote employees across the globe" phrasing on the about page, every live posting is scoped "United States (Remote)" and all posted ranges are USD. No Canadian entity or province is mentioned anywhere. Treat as US-only for these reqs; the global phrasing is marketing, not a hiring commitment.
Engineering & tech: The data-platform role is PHP/Laravel (v11+) with Hexagonal Architecture and DDD, MySQL and relational data modelling, AWS Lake Formation and AWS Glue for the data lake and ETL, AWS generally (EC2, RDS, S3, Lambda), REST integrations to billing/CRM/payments (Zuora, HubSpot, Salesforce mentioned), plus maintaining customer-facing account-management UIs in JavaScript/TypeScript/React. Nice-to-haves include Docker/Kubernetes, Terraform/Pulumi, and AI-assisted development tools such as Claude Code. Scrum/Kanban.
Notable / other: Pay posted on all three relevant JDs: Software Engineer $140-160k, Senior Software Engineer (Data Platforms & Monetization) $145-165k, Senior Java Engineer $175-195k, all USD base. The data role's remit is usage metering into billing — "You will ensure that every byte of product usage is accurately captured, transformed, and monetized, directly impacting the company's bottom line."
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Data Platforms & Monetization; Senior Java Engineer; Software Engineer.
