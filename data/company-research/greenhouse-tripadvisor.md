# Tripadvisor — research

Provider/key: greenhouse:tripadvisor | company_type: product

What they do: Tripadvisor Group is the travel-guidance platform — user-generated reviews and
ratings for accommodation, restaurants and attractions, plus experiences booking (Viator) and
restaurant reservations (TheFork). Revenue is advertising, partnerships and booking
commissions. Engineering supports a very high-traffic consumer marketplace, its advertising
and partnerships stack, SEO, and a substantial data platform behind the review and booking
corpus.

How they describe themselves: The recurring careers/benefits language on their own JDs is
built around flexibility — **"'Work your way' with flexibility to suit your lifestyle.
Tripadvisor Group takes a remote-friendly approach to collaboration across a worldwide team,
with the option to join on-site as often as you'd like or as required by your team"** — and
around benchmarked pay: "Competitive compensation packages (routinely benchmarked against the
latest industry data), including base salary and annual bonuses". Tone across all three
scanned live JDs extracts as **balanced**; no hustle, grit or "rocketship" language appears,
though one posting does use "fast-growing team".

Size / stage / funding: Public (NASDAQ: TRIP), founded 2000, several thousand employees.
Mature, established, past its growth-story phase — a long-lived internet property rather than
a venture-stage company. Not a hypergrowth or burn-rate story.

Locations / HQ: HQ **Needham, Massachusetts**. Office hubs include Boston-area,
**London, UK**, and **Ottawa, Ontario**; the wider group (Viator, TheFork) adds European
locations. The company describes itself as having a "worldwide team".

Remote policy: **Remote-friendly, genuinely so — but tagged per requisition.** "Work your
way" allows joining on-site "as often as you'd like *or as required by your team*", so the
team-level expectation is the operative constraint. Their JDs carry explicit tags:
`#LI-Remote` / `#Remote` for the fully-remote reqs, `#LI-Hybrid` for hub-anchored ones. The
London *Software Engineer II* posting is explicit about the hybrid model: "This role is open
to candidates based within a commutable distance (generally within 1–2 hours) of our office
hubs in London … You can expect a remote-first working model with periodic in-person
gatherings at your closest office."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes for the data-engineering roles.** Two of the three
live relevant postings are Canadian and remote: *Data Engineer II* is located simply
**"Canada"** and tagged `#LI-Remote`, and *Senior Data Engineer* is located
**"Ottawa, Ontario - Remote"** and tagged `#Remote`. A country-level "Canada" location with a
remote tag is about as clean a remote-Canada signal as an ATS produces. Tripadvisor also
externally advertises an "Engineering Team Lead (Remote within Canada)" role based out of
Ottawa, corroborating that remote-within-Canada is an established arrangement rather than a
one-off. Caveat: the Ottawa hub and Eastern Time are the centre of gravity for the Canadian
org, so Mountain Time means a 2-hour early skew — workable, but not a Pacific-friendly org.
The third live role (Software Engineer II, London) is hybrid and not relevant.

Engineering & tech: Web stack is **Java, React, TypeScript, GraphQL, Kubernetes, SQL and
NoSQL databases, running on AWS**. Engineers are described as owning "the full process from
software design, to code, to test, to deployment and back again", with "most engineers
releasing code to production every few days and a daily release cycle" — continuous-delivery
culture with end-to-end ownership. Relevant open work is concentrated in **data engineering**
(Data Engineer II, Senior Data Engineer) plus backend and advertising/partnerships platform
roles. SEO engineering is a named team.

Notable / other: Benefits framing mentions competitive packages with base salary plus annual
bonus and flexible schedules. **No salary range is posted on any of the three live relevant
JDs** (the Canadian and UK postings fall outside US pay-transparency jurisdictions), and there
is no company-level band on record — this is the main data gap for Tripadvisor.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Data Engineer (Ottawa, Ontario - Remote); Data Engineer II
(Canada, remote); Software Engineer II (London, hybrid); plus Back-end Engineer, Senior
Software Engineer - Backend and Principal Software Engineer (Experiences) in the wider
requisition list.
