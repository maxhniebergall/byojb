# Wynd Labs (Grass) — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:xhiring | company_type: product
What they do: Wynd Labs is an early-stage startup whose stated mission is "to make public web data accessible for AI through contributions to Grass". Grass is a bandwidth-sharing application — in effect a residential proxy network — where users share unused internet bandwidth from personal devices and are rewarded for the traffic their node carries; the traffic is metered and rewards distributed accordingly. The broader project is described as "a web-scale crawler and knowledge graph that allows ordinary people to participate in the process, and share in the benefits of AI development". The reward/token mechanics place this in the crypto-adjacent space.
