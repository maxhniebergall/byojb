# Granum — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:granum | company_type: product
What they do: Granum is a vertical SaaS company serving landscapers and arborists across North America. It is a roll-up of three established brands — LMN (landscaping business management), SingleOps (tree care / arborist management) and Greenius (crew training) — combined "into one powerful software ecosystem". LMN Pro is described as "the platform our customers run their entire business on — from quoting and scheduling through job execution and getting paid". The company pairs software with hands-on onboarding, implementation and human support.
How they describe themselves (their own words): Mission "to help industry professionals unlock their potential and achieve their most important goals", "united by the belief that its clients' success is not just a milestone, but a shared mission", and "on a mission to reshape an age-old industry". Six core values, quoted in full: "We Before Me, Bias to Act, The Extra 1%, Accountable to Outcomes, Unconditional Transparency, and Forgiveness". Environment self-described as "highly collaborative, innovative, and supportive, with a shared commitment to having fun while making an impact". Their 2026 Engineering operating principles are published in the JD: Customer Focus; Shipping ("small changes, low risk, fast feedback; velocity with robust process"); Efficiency; "Simplicity (YAGNI) — start simple, prefer boring industry-standard tech"; "Get Good at AI — every engineer is expected to use, improve, and help shape our AI-assisted development practices"; and "Performance & Reliability — clear, measurable bars for performance, uptime, and time-to-restore; regressions are first-class work". Engineering also aligns to Product's principles: "Be the Partner, Raise the Bar, Big Vision Small Scope, Shipping is Discovery".
Size / stage / funding: Small-to-mid private company (Comparably ranks it among small/medium companies); a multi-brand roll-up, which typically implies private-equity backing — ownership not verified. Repeated Inc. 5000 fastest-growing listings (three years running, #131 Southeast Regional 2024) indicate strong revenue growth. Headcount not disclosed.
Locations / HQ: Distributed across the US and Canada. Atlanta, Georgia is a major hub (Atlanta Business Chronicle and Built In Atlanta workplace awards); Ontario is a significant Canadian presence (the postings reference AODA compliance and Great Place to Work Canada certification). LMN's roots are Canadian.
Remote policy: The live engineering req is posted "Remote" and the company hires in both the US and Canada with parallel benefit structures for each. No formal remote-first/async statement was found, but the hiring and benefits design is genuinely dual-country distributed.
Remote-Canada eligibility: YES — verified and strong. The JD states compensation separately for "Canada-based candidates" and "US-based candidates", offers "RRSP matching" for Canadians and "an employer-funded HSA-based benefits plan with drug, dental, and mental health coverage" in Canada, and holds Great Place to Work Canada 2024 certification. It explicitly does not sponsor work authorization, which is irrelevant for a Canadian citizen applying to the Canadian entity. No province restriction or timezone requirement is stated.
Engineering & tech: Backend C#/.NET with a multi-tenant service architecture, Azure SQL Server, Azure Functions. Frontend Angular with a shared component library, TypeScript. Cloud/delivery on Azure with Terraform, Cloudflare, GitHub and GitHub Actions, feature flags. Observability via Datadog (APM, RUM, logs). AI-assisted development runs through "an internal orchestration layer that enhances Claude, Cursor" and other tools. Organized into feature pods of 2-4 engineers with a PM, Designer and Test Engineer, shipping to production multiple times a week. Levelling ladder is explicit and published: P4 (Senior) → P5 → P6 → P7, with "concrete expectations for promotion to Staff and beyond". They track DORA metrics and treat post-incident learnings as follow-through work.
Notable / other: Compensation is published directly on the JD: Canada-based CAD 160,000-200,000; US-based USD 160,000-200,000 — unusually strong and transparent for vertical SaaS, and notable that the CAD figure is not discounted relative to USD. Benefits: unlimited PTO, paid company holidays, a company-wide winter break December 24 to January 1, RRSP/401(k) matching, career development budget.
