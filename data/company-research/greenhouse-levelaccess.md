# Level Access — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:levelaccess | company_type: product

What they do: Level Access sells digital accessibility software and services — a platform that helps organisations make websites, mobile apps, software and digital content accessible and legally compliant (WCAG / ADA / AODA / European Accessibility Act). The offering combines automated testing, expert manual evaluation, AI-assisted remediation, governance/reporting and accessibility training. Customers are 1,000+ (their materials also say "thousands of") corporations, government agencies and educational institutions, including Fortune 500 enterprises.

How they describe themselves: Mission stated on their own company page: "Enable all people to live their best lives through access to technology." Their four stated values are: "Be the change", "Boldly go", "Bring the energy", "Build together". They emphasise employee diversity and cite Great Place to Work and top-workplace recognitions.

Size / stage / funding: Founded 1997 as SSB Bart Group, renamed Level Access in 1999 — 25+ years in the market, so an established business rather than a startup. Private; it merged with eSSENTIAL Accessibility in 2022 and is private-equity backed. Exact headcount is not published on their own site (third-party listings put it in the several-hundred range). Not a runway-driven early-stage company, but PE ownership implies cost discipline.

Locations / HQ: HQ in Virginia, USA (their company page lists Stafford, VA; other sources say Arlington, VA). Offices also listed in Toronto, Ontario, Canada and Bristol, UK (Lewins Mead).

Remote policy: Effectively remote-first / distributed. The relevant posting is tagged simply "Remote", and employee reports describe the company as 100% remote (with the associated downside that "everything is done virtually"). The company does not publish a formal remote-policy page.

Remote-Canada eligibility: LIKELY YES, not explicitly confirmed. Positive evidence: they list a Toronto, Ontario office on their own company page, so a Canadian employing entity exists, and the Senior Data Engineer role is posted as "Remote" with no stated geographic restriction, timezone requirement, or US-work-authorization clause anywhere in the JD text. Negative/unclear: the posting does not name Canada either, and the company is US-headquartered. Province-level (BC) and timezone eligibility should be confirmed with a recruiter before investing effort.

Engineering & tech: The open Senior Data Engineer role is a genuine data-platform build-out: consolidating business systems into a single unified data platform on Databricks + AWS following Medallion (Bronze/Silver/Gold) architecture; ETL/ELT pipelines; data-quality monitoring (freshness, completeness, validity, anomaly detection) with automated alerting; Golden Record identity resolution and reverse sync; metadata/lineage/permissions via Databricks Unity Catalog; infrastructure-as-code and CI/CD for pipelines. Stack named explicitly: Python/PySpark, Databricks (notebooks, jobs, workflows, Unity Catalog, Delta Live Tables, MLflow), AWS (Lambda, ECS/Fargate, S3, Glue, Athena, DynamoDB, EventBridge, Step Functions, IAM), SQL + dbt, Delta Lake/Parquet, REST API integration, Salesforce APIs. They ask for 8+ years of data engineering.

Notable / other: The JD discloses that Level Access "uses artificial intelligence (AI) tools to support and enhance our recruitment and hiring processes" — résumé screening, skills assessments, interview scheduling and communication, with stated human review. No salary is posted on the role; benefits are described only generically ("competitive benefits package, including bonus opportunities, generous paid time off, paid holidays"). The JD uses "fast-paced environment" twice.
