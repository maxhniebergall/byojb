# Fullsteam — research
Provider/key: workday:fullsteam/external | company_type: product

What they do: Fullsteam is a vertical-software-plus-embedded-payments company operating on an acquisition/rollup model — it acquires and operates industry-specific software products and layers its own integrated payment processing across them, along with insurance, fintech, marketing services and AI tooling. In their own words: "a leading provider of vertical software and embedded payments technology dedicated to helping businesses flourish by providing their customers with seamless experiences", "committed to driving innovation and delivering best-in-class software and payment solutions that empower small and medium-sized businesses across numerous industries." Their site names 11+ verticals including hospitality, healthcare, automotive, self-storage, field services, wine, and weddings/events. Revenue is therefore a mix of software subscriptions and payment-processing take rate.

How they describe themselves: JD boilerplate opens with "**It's fun to work in a company where people truly BELIEVE in what they're doing!**" Their stated purpose is "to help our customers grow their businesses and delight their customers", and they invite candidates to "join us and be a part of a forward-thinking company that values growth, excellence, and the success of our clients." Their site describes them as "growth-oriented", with a mission to "serve our customers and employees better every day", emphasising tailored, cloud-first solutions. No formal values or principles list was published on the pages fetched. Tone is enthusiastic corporate rather than engineering-led.

Size / stage / funding: "A dynamic and growing team of over 1,900 employees" (stated in their own JD). Engineering & tech: Only one JD body is available, and it is a **talent-pipeline requisition, not an actual opening** — it states plainly: "We're building a pipeline of exceptional AI Engineering talent for future opportunities within our organization. While this is not an immediate opening, we anticipate significant hiring in this space throughout the year." It describes the company's AI direction: investing "heavily in Generative AI, Agentic Systems, and Ambient Intelligence to transform how we build products, operate internally, and serve our customers", targeting expanded product capabilities, productivity gains and measurable NPS improvement. The engineering content described is reasonable: scalable backend services, APIs and data pipelines; autonomous agents that use LLMs to reason, use tools and orchestrate multi-step workflows; architecture for real-time AI inference, conversation state and tool execution with error handling and recovery. Skills listed — system design, API design, distributed systems, concurrency and async, relational + vector databases, CI/CD and observability; agent architecture (planning, orchestration, memory, tool use), LLM integration (prompting, function calling, context management), RAG pipelines (embeddings, vector search, retrieval strategies), reliability patterns for non-deterministic systems; ML fundamentals (training, inference, evaluation, embeddings, PyTorch/Hugging Face/scikit-learn, fine-tuning, precision/recall/F1/perplexity). Nice-to-haves: LangGraph, AutoGen, vector databases, AI safety and guardrail patterns, mentoring. Note the framing throughout is applying AI to Fullsteam's vertical products and internal operations, not building AI infrastructure as a product.
