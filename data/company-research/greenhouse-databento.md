# Databento — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:databento | company_type: product

What they do: Databento sells market data as an API — real-time and historical data across futures, options and equities through a single unified interface. They describe the problem as an industry where "our data providers are still stuck in the past — with cumbersome onboarding processes, complicated APIs, slow infrastructure, and expensive licensing costs," and themselves as "the market data platform for modern finance." They own the whole stack: their own capture hardware in exchange colocation facilities through to the customer-facing API. Customers are trading firms, quant funds and fintech startups — they cite "3,000+ customers."

How they describe themselves: The self-description is engineering-first and unusually free of mission hype. They stress being "the first platform of its kind to combine the performance and reliability demanded by the world's leading trading firms with a dramatically simpler developer experience," and lean on pedigree: "Our team includes alumni of Two Sigma, Citadel Securities, Flow Traders, Tower Research, PDT Partners, SIG, and other top quantitative firms." Their marketing values are technical ones — nanosecond accuracy, direct colocation sourcing, developer experience. No published values/principles list was found; their public writing is product and engineering documentation rather than culture copy.

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed. Their own site announces a "$97M Series B" (blog dated July 2026). Headcount is not published; the engineering team is described as small and senior. Not stated to be profitable.

Locations / HQ: Not stated on the pages fetched. Their live JDs list "Boston / Salt Lake City / San Francisco / New York" (and Chicago on one), which are the office/hub cities; infrastructure sits in colocation facilities including Equinix NY4 and CyrusOne Aurora I.

Remote policy: Remote is offered — every live engineering posting is tagged "Remote /" ahead of the office list, and the JD asks for "Good communication skills and work ethic for a remote workplace." No formal remote-policy page was found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear — not verified, and the balance of evidence leans US-only.** All named locations are US cities and no posting mentions Canada or an international entity. "Remote" here most likely means remote-within-the-US. Nothing on their site states otherwise either way; treat as unconfirmed and a question to ask before applying.

Engineering & tech: This is deep systems work. Core engineering owns "all things C/C++ and Rust, and the most performance-sensitive parts of our codebase" — message parsers and feed handlers for direct exchange feeds, optimisation "for high throughput (millions of messages per second, up to 40+ gigabits) and low latency (measured in nanoseconds)," and petabyte-scale tooling. The stated scale is "nearly 20 PB of data, with thousands of bare metal CPU cores at your disposal." Publicly documented stack: Python, Rust, C++; Layer 1 switches, PTP time synchronisation, FPGAs, zero-copy and kernel-bypass networking; their own DBN binary encoding alongside CSV/JSON and a pandas integration. Wanted background is "systems programming, algorithms, data structures, multithreading, networked I/O, serialization, CPU architecture, kernel, and compilers," 5+ years, comfort with lldb/gdb.

Notable / other: The JD carries an explicit phishing warning — official mail comes only from @databento.com or us.greenhouse-mail.io, and lookalike domains (databento-careers.com, databento.online) are fraudulent. Their postings encourage applicants whose experience doesn't exactly match. Roles are pitched as "midlevel or senior IC," and no salary range is posted on any of the four live engineering roles.

Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineer (C++/Rust); Software Engineer (C++/Java); Software Engineer (C++/C#); Software Engineer (TypeScript/JavaScript) — all Remote / Boston / Salt Lake City / San Francisco / New York (or Chicago).
