# Kaseya — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:kaseya | company_type: product
What they do: Kaseya sells IT management and security software to managed service providers (MSPs) and internal IT departments, bundled as the "IT Complete" platform and, from April 2026, a "Cyber Resiliency Platform". Its portfolio is assembled largely by acquisition — Datto (acquired June 2022 for $6.2B), IT Glue, Unitrends, RapidFire, ID Agent, Graphus, and INKY (AI email security, October 2025), among others. Product areas span RMM, backup/continuity, networking, endpoint management, PSA/business management and security.
How they describe themselves: Their public material is product- and MSP-economics-led ("increase MSP efficiency and profitability", "boost innovation and lower prices") rather than values- or culture-led; no published values statement was found. Their JDs are technical and terse, e.g. "Architect and develop multi-tenant cloud-native networking and security solutions in public, private cloud, and hybrid environments."
Size / stage / funding: Large private company, thousands of employees. Private-equity owned — the Datto deal was funded by an equity consortium led by Insight Partners with TPG, Temasek and Sixth Street. Well capitalised and acquisitive; the business model is roll-up-and-consolidate.
Locations / HQ: Global HQ in Miami, Florida. Major engineering presence in Toronto, Ontario. Other sites include Norwalk, Connecticut (Datto) and India.
Remote policy: Predominantly in-office. Employee reviews report that "generally, every position is 100% in office", and the Toronto engineering reqs are listed as In-Person on external job boards. A small number of exceptions are posted explicitly as remote ("Staff Software Engineer — Canada, Remote"; "Senior Staff Software Engineer – IAM Platform — United States - Remote"; "Lead Software Engineer — India - Remote"). Reviews also report out-of-hours expectations, including Teams meetings on weekends and late evenings, and note that overall Glassdoor rating (3.7) has fallen ~9% over the last year.
Remote-Canada eligibility: Mostly no. Nine of the eleven live engineering postings are Toronto, Ontario in-person — including all four "Sr/Senior Staff Software Engineer" reqs, the Senior Backend Engineer (SaaS, Data & AI) and both IAM Platform Toronto reqs — which is effectively disqualifying for someone in Kimberley, BC who cannot relocate. Exactly one req, "Staff Software Engineer", is posted "Canada, Remote" (and it is the one with a published CAD range). The US-remote IAM req requires US work authorisation he does not have.
Engineering & tech: Multi-tenant cloud-native networking and security, identity and access management platform, payments and integrations, backend SaaS with data/AI components, and a data-engineering function. Levels run Senior → Lead → Staff → Senior Staff → Principal.
Notable / other: The "Staff Software Engineer" (Canada, Remote) req states "The base salary range for this role is $219,000 to $245,000 CAD" — an unusually high and explicitly stated Canadian band, and the only compensation figure Kaseya publishes across its live reqs. Kaseya retained the whole Datto workforce post-acquisition with no layoffs or office closures. Its acquisition strategy has drawn public criticism from competitors over customer pricing and contract terms.
Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer (Canada, Remote); Senior Staff Network Software Engineer (Toronto); Senior Staff Software Engineer – Identity & Access Management Platform (Toronto); Senior Backend Engineer, SaaS, Data & AI (Toronto); Principal Software Engineer.
