# AstraZeneca — research
Provider/key: workday:astrazeneca/careers | company_type: product
What they do: Global biopharmaceutical company (research, development and commercialisation of prescription medicines). Relevant here is **Evinova**, described in its own JD as "a global health tech business" and "a fully-owned subsidiary of AstraZeneca Group" that "delivers market-leading digital health solutions that are science-based, evidence-led, and human experience-driven" — in practice, SaaS for clinical-trial design, planning and operational optimisation.
How they describe themselves: "At AstraZeneca, we pride ourselves on crafting a collaborative culture that champions knowledge-sharing, ambitious thinking and innovation – ultimately providing employees with the opportunity to work across teams, functions and even the globe." Evinova's own framing is markedly more startup-flavoured: "You're disruptive, decisive, and transformative… Thoughtful risks and quick decisions come together to accelerate innovation," "think big, be bold."
Size / stage / funding: Very large publicly-listed pharma; stability is not a concern. Evinova is a newer internal venture rather than an independent startup — it carries AstraZeneca's balance sheet. Headcount not captured here (astrazeneca.com returned 403 to the fetch attempt).
Locations / HQ: Global. In this registry: Canada – Mississauga/Toronto (Evinova, with a "downtown Toronto Bluesky Hub office"), US – Wilmington DE and Santa Monica CA, Belgium – Mont-Saint-Guibert, and Spain – Barcelona (closed req).
Remote policy: Hybrid with an explicit in-office quota, stated in their own words: "Recognizing the importance of individualized flexibility, our ways of working allow employees to balance personal and work commitments while ensuring we continue to create a strong culture of collaboration and teamwork by **engaging face-to-face in our offices 3 days a week**." The US Wilmington req says only that "A hybrid work schedule may be permitted within a commutable distance from the worksite."
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No.** The Canadian (Evinova) role requires three days a week in the downtown Toronto office; the US role requires residence within commuting distance of Wilmington, DE. Neither is compatible with working from British Columbia.
Engineering & tech: The Evinova **ML/AI Ops team** is the interesting part — "a newly formed platform team that will spearhead the design, creation, and operational excellence of our LLM-based agent deployments, multi-agent orchestration, and conversational AI systems pipelines," "responsible and accountable for the design, implementation, deployment, health and performance of all LLM-based applications," managing "ML/AI and broader cloud resources, automating operations through infrastructure-as-code and CI/CD pipelines" on AWS, under regulated-industry standards (Good Clinical Practice, Good Machine Learning Practice). Elsewhere the stack is enterprise: Veeva CRM / OCE / Vault, Salesforce (Apex, Visualforce, Lightning), Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Snowflake, Databricks, MuleSoft and SnapLogic middleware, React/React Native, Power BI. There is also a robotics/automation principal role in Cell & Gene Therapy process sciences.
Notable / other: AstraZeneca posts pay ranges on Canadian reqs: the Evinova Generative AI Cloud Operations Engineer states "Annual base salary for this position ranges from $114,622.40 to $150,441.90" (CAD) with a second, higher range also shown (122,635.20–160,958.70, currency unlabelled in the captured text — likely a second geo/level tier), alongside "AstraZeneca is committed to providing fair and equitable compensation opportunities to all colleagues." The Wilmington Senior Platform Engineer req is a formal immigration-style posting (fixed duties, "No calls please. EOE.", 15% travel) and requires Veeva certifications.
Open relevant roles (sample): Generative AI Cloud Operations Engineer – Evinova (Canada – Mississauga/Toronto); Senior Platform Engineer – CRM Applications (Wilmington, DE); Cross-Disciplinary AI Engineer – Discovery (Belgium); Principal Engineer, Robotics and Automation, Cell and Gene Therapy (Santa Monica, CA).
