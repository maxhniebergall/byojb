# OMERS — research
Provider/key: workday:omers/omers_external | company_type: product (in-house corporate IT/eng of a pension plan)

What they do: OMERS (Ontario Municipal Employees Retirement System) is a Canadian defined-benefit pension plan and global institutional investor, managing retirement benefits for ~665,000 members (municipal employees in Ontario). Its real-estate arm is Oxford Properties. Engineering here is internal corporate/platform technology serving Investments, Pension Services, Finance and Corporate functions — not a software product sold to customers.

How they describe themselves: From their own JD text — "a purpose-driven, dynamic and sustainable pension plan. An industry leading global investor with teams in Toronto to London, New York, Singapore, Sydney"; "We embody the values of our 665,000 members, placing their best interests at the heart of everything we do"; "Choose a workplace that empowers your impact… a global workplace where employees thrive. One that embraces diversity of thought, expertise and experience." Framing is employee-development and wellness oriented ("accelerate your growth & development, prioritize wellness, build connections"). The AI/ML JD does use the phrase "collaborative, fast-paced environment".

Size / stage / funding: Institutional pension plan, not venture-funded. Very large and stable; a multi-decade institution with global offices. Exact headcount not verified.

Locations / HQ: HQ Toronto, Ontario. Other offices named in their own text: London, New York, Singapore, Sydney, plus "other major cities across North America and Europe."

Remote policy: Not stated in the JDs or verified from a first-party page. **All six live engineering postings are located "Toronto, Ontario"** with no remote designation. Treat as Toronto-based (on-site or hybrid) unless proven otherwise.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Effectively NO for a BC-based candidate — unverified but strongly negative.** Every open relevant posting is Toronto-anchored; none advertise remote or a BC/Mountain-Time option. Note: this is Canada-eligible in the trivial sense (a Canadian employer) but not remote-from-Kimberley-eligible.

Engineering & tech: The AI/ML role sits in the "AI Delivery and Innovation team within the Platform Engineering, AI and Advanced Analytics department" — described as a central hub for AI capability partnering with Software Engineering and business areas. Stated stack/practice: LLM applications, RAG pipelines, agentic workflows, traditional ML; MLOps/LLMOps/GenAIOps pipelines (experiment tracking, model & prompt versioning, CI/CD, observability, drift detection, automated retraining); Azure AI Foundry and Copilot Studio as the enterprise AI platforms; vector databases, embeddings, retrieval; AI governance / responsible-AI controls. Agile delivery. Other open roles imply data engineering, SRE/application support and DevOps functions.

Notable / other: Requires 3+ years software engineering with 2+ years production AI/GenAI for the Lead AI/ML role — a fairly low bar for a "Lead" title. Employee-referral flow runs through Workday. The domain (pension administration + institutional investing) is genuinely "boring and mission-critical", which is a positive stability signal.

Open relevant roles (sample): Lead, AI/Machine Learning Engineer; Senior Data Engineer; Lead, Site Reliability Engineering (Application Support); DevOps Engineer; Data Engineer; Automation Support Analyst (SRE) — all Toronto, Ontario.
