# Compa — research
Provider/key: ashby:compa | company_type: product

What they do: Compa is a venture-backed compensation-intelligence company. It runs a "real-time compensation data platform" that aggregates live offer/pay data and sells it to enterprise compensation teams so they can benchmark and make pay decisions. Its own JDs name customers including NVIDIA, Stripe, DoorDash, OpenAI, T-Mobile, Moderna, Workday, Ulta and Target. Positioning is explicitly AI-flavoured — "built to augment enterprise compensation teams in the era of AI".

How they describe themselves: "Compa is a venture-backed AI startup revolutionizing the future of compensation." On culture, their own words (repeated verbatim across postings): "We're a collaborative, curious, and driven team that values transparency, ownership, and continuous learning and prioritizing in person work where possible." The Staff platform JD adds a high-autonomy framing: "This is a high-ownership individual contributor role where your decisions shape how the enterprise builds and ships", and asks for someone "Comfortable with high ownership and ambiguity - you've built things without a playbook before". The IC JD asks for comfort "navigating ambiguity and working in fast-paced, iterative environments". Tone is startup-ownership, not enterprise-process.

Size / stage / funding: Venture-backed private startup, stated in their own words. Exact headcount, round and profitability were not verifiable from the sources available this session — unverified. Postings describe Irvine as HQ with "growing sites" in Denver and San Francisco, which reads as small-to-mid, expanding.

Locations / HQ: HQ Irvine (Orange County), California. Additional sites: Denver CO, San Francisco CA, Boston MA. Ashby location tags on live postings list Orange County HQ, Denver Office, Boston Office, San Francisco and "Remote - USA".

Remote policy: "Remote friendly" but explicitly office-leaning — "prioritizing in person work where possible", and the Software Engineer / Senior Software Engineer postings state "there is a preference for candidates located near one of our offices in Irvine, CA, Denver, CO or Boston, MA."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — not eligible, on their own posting metadata.** The only remote location tag on the live postings is "Remote - USA". No Canadian entity, office or location tag appears anywhere in their listings, and the roles express an in-person preference near US offices. Treated as US-only.

Engineering & tech: Backend Django/Python; frontend React, TypeScript, GraphQL; infrastructure AWS; other tooling vLLM, Linear, Slack, Notion, Metabase. The Staff Multi-Cloud Platform Engineer role is genuine infrastructure work: Kubernetes across AWS, GCP and Azure (EKS/GKE/AKS), cloud abstraction patterns, cross-cloud identity, networking, data movement, traffic routing, customer-managed encryption keys, workload isolation/tenancy, SLIs/SLOs, CI/CD and paved paths, plus "Exposure to MLOps" as a nice-to-have. Multi-cloud is described as "central to what we're building, not a future initiative". The generalist SWE roles are full-stack product work ("work across the stack, collaborate closely with product and design").

Notable / other: Pay transparency is posted on every role (fitting for a comp-data company), and all roles offer equity. Note the coincidence: the product is compensation benchmarking, so their own posted bands are likely deliberate.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Multi-Cloud Platform Engineer ($200K–$225K USD + equity); Senior Software Engineer, All Teams ($160K–$225K USD + equity); Software Engineer, All Teams ($125K–$180K USD + equity).
