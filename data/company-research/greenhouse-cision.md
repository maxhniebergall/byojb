# Cision — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:cision | company_type: product

What they do: In their own words, "Cision is the global leader in consumer and media intelligence, engagement, and communication solutions. We equip PR and corporate communications, marketing, and social media professionals with the tools they need to excel in today's data driven world. Our deep expertise, exclusive data partnerships, and award-winning products" serve the public-relations and communications market. The portfolio is a roll-up of long-established brands — most visibly **PR Newswire** (press-release distribution), plus media monitoring, media databases and social listening. Several live reqs are branded "PR Newswire Canada," indicating a distinct Canadian operating unit.

How they describe themselves: "At Cision, we believe in empowering every individual to make an impact. Here, your voice is heard, your ideas are valued, and your unique perspective fuels our collective success. As part of our global team, you'll thrive in an environment that champions curiosity, collaboration, and innovation, all while making meaningful contributions to the brands we accelerate." "Join us in shaping the future of communication and building authentic connections that matter. Whether you're solving complex problems or driving bold innovations, your growth is our success, and together, we'll create the conversations of tomorrow." Their tagline: "Empower your impact at Cision. Be seen, be understood, be you."

Size / stage / funding: Large, mature, private-equity-owned (Platinum Equity acquired Cision in 2020). Thousands of employees globally. This is a leveraged, cost-managed legacy software business rather than a growth company — stable in the sense of being established, but with the margin pressure and restructuring history typical of PE-owned SaaS roll-ups.

Locations / HQ: Chicago, US HQ, with a heavily distributed global engineering footprint. The live postings show an unusually clear picture of where they build: **Canada** (PR Newswire Canada, remote), **Bulgaria** (Sofia, remote), **Hungary** (remote), **Mexico** (remote) and **Brazil** (remote). Note the board is served from `job-boards.eu.greenhouse.io`, i.e. their EU Greenhouse instance.

Remote policy: Effectively remote-by-country. Every live engineering req on record is tagged "Remote - <country>" rather than to an office — Remote-Canada, Remote-Bulgaria, Remote-Hungary, Remote-Mexico, Remote-Brazil. No hybrid or in-office engineering reqs appear.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes, verified and unambiguous.** Three live reqs are tagged plainly "Remote - Canada": "Cision, Senior Software Developer, Software Engineer, AMER, Canada," "PR Newswire Canada, Software Developer II," and "PR Newswire Canada, Staff Software Developer." These are country-level remote roles with no city attached, so British Columbia and Mountain Time are workable. Cision clearly employs Canada-based remote engineers as a matter of course.

Engineering & tech: **.NET-centric.** The Senior Software Developer req requires ".NET 6+ with C#, SQL, Git, Unit Testing, RESTful web services, Messaging & queuing," and "Front-end framework such as React," with MySQL, Postgres and Kubernetes as preferred. Most striking is that the role is built around AI-assisted development as the primary methodology rather than as a tool: "You will work closely with product, design, and data science teams to develop and enhance applications by managing AI intent within a spec-driven development framework. As a technical expert, you will help orchestrate AI agents to generate production-ready code and automated tests, utilizing multi-LLM workflows for code verification and quality assurance." Required experience includes "Proficiency in Spec-Driven Development (SDD) and AI-native coding in VS Code" and "Expertise in GitHub Copilot Agent Mode and AI-driven debugging," with the engineer's job framed as "managing the intent of AI-generated code so that it meets the functional, security, and performance requirements of the product." Separately they run a DevOps/SRE function, but those reqs are staffed out of Bulgaria, Mexico and Brazil.

Compensation: Cision posts Canadian salary ranges on its Canadian reqs — a rare and useful transparency. Recorded direct bands: Software Developer II CA$96,395–CA$110,000; Senior Software Developer CA$119,000–CA$137,000; Staff Software Developer CA$143,000–CA$164,100. These are notably below market for equivalent Canadian senior/staff engineering roles, consistent with a cost-optimized PE-owned employer that also staffs heavily from lower-cost geographies. See `data/company-comp/greenhouse-cision.md`.

Notable / other: The distribution of reqs — Canada, Bulgaria, Hungary, Mexico, Brazil, and no US engineering postings — is itself a signal that engineering is being run as a cost centre across nearshore/offshore geographies.
