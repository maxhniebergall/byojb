# Socure — research
Provider/key: ashby:socure | company_type: product

What they do: Socure provides identity verification, fraud detection, compliance screening and authentication as a platform — what they call "AI-Native Trust Infrastructure for Identity, Risk, Compliance, Age, and Workforce." Their JD framing: "Socure is building the identity trust infrastructure for the digital economy — verifying 100% of good identities in real time and stopping fraud before it starts." Customers span fintech, banking, crypto, ecommerce, gaming, public sector and workforce verification; they claim "3,000+ customers including 19 of 20 top U.S. banks and 13 of 15 top credit card issuers."

How they describe themselves: Unusually blunt about the culture they want, and it is the most decision-relevant text on the company. From their own JD: "We hire people who want that level of responsibility. People who move fast, think critically, act like owners, and care deeply about solving customer problems with precision. **If you want predictability or narrow scope, this won't be your place.** If you want to help build the future of identity with a team that holds a high bar for itself — keep reading." Their marketing adds an urgency frame: "AI is moving faster than any technology has before. You must have a partner who can keep up and use AI on offense." Stated engineering values are "teamwork, outcome-driven development, and a commitment to best-in-class technology experiences," with an inclusivity angle on the product side — "saying yes to more real people."

Size / stage / funding: Private, late-stage, well established (named to the CNBC Disruptor 50 list). Funding round and headcount are not stated on the pages read; unverified. The customer roster (most of the top US banks) indicates a mature enterprise vendor rather than an early startup.

Locations / HQ: Not stated on the site. From live postings: a Toronto office and a London office, both explicitly hybrid. US operations exist (their entire customer base is US financial institutions) but no US role appears in the current live set. Engineering is organised partly through a "Technology Center of Excellence."

Remote policy: Hybrid, and stated as such in the location field itself — every live posting is tagged "Hybrid - Toronto" or "Hybrid - London." There is no remote designation on any live role.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — the Canadian role is Toronto hybrid, which is disqualifying.** The one Canadian posting, Senior Software Engineer – Agentic AI Foundations, is tagged "Hybrid - Toronto," meaning regular office attendance in Ontario. There is a Canadian employing entity (the role posts in CAD), so Canadian employment is possible in principle, but nothing in the live set is remote and nothing is BC-eligible. Not workable from Kimberley without relocation.

Engineering & tech: Backend and distributed systems at enterprise scale. The Staff Backend Engineer role describes "designing and implementing complex core components and distributed systems, while architecting solutions for scalability, security, modularity, and long-term maintainability," conducting "code and architecture reviews," and driving "the planning, execution, and delivery of large, complex initiatives, coordinating work across a small team of engineers and effectively managing scope, timelines, and resources." There is an explicit agentic-AI platform team ("Agentic AI Foundations") and a RiskOS backend team, plus SRE, data engineering and data science roles in the registry. Specific languages and stack are not stated in the captured JD text.

Notable / other: Compensation is posted on the Toronto role — CA$165,000–195,000 base, plus equity and bonus — which is a useful public datapoint for their Canadian senior band. The London roles post no range. The Staff Backend role's responsibilities read closer to a tech-lead/delivery-management hybrid ("managing scope, timelines, and resources") than a pure IC track.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer – Agentic AI Foundations (Hybrid - Toronto, CA$165K–195K); Staff Backend Engineer - RiskOS (Hybrid - London); Senior Software Engineer (Hybrid - London). The registry also lists Senior Backend Engineer, Senior Data Engineer, Senior Software Engineer - SRE, AI Engineer and Senior AI Engineer titles for this company.
