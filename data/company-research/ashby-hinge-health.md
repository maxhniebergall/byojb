# Hinge Health — research
Provider/key: ashby:hinge-health | company_type: product

What they do: Digital musculoskeletal (MSK) care company. In their own words they are "using technology to scale and automate the delivery of healthcare — starting with musculoskeletal (MSK) conditions, which affect over 1.7 billion people worldwide," delivering personalized, evidence-based exercise therapy through an "AI-powered human-centered care model." Their TrueMotion computer-vision technology "turns everyday mobile devices into powerful motion-tracking tools, giving members real-time feedback and guidance as they work through personalized exercise therapy." The platform spans acute injury, chronic pain and post-surgical rehab. Distribution is B2B2C: "the preferred partner to 50+ health plans, PBMs, and other ecosystem partners," available "to over 20 million people across more than 2,550 employers."

How they describe themselves: Mission framing is clinical-outcomes-led — "help people move beyond their pain," improving "outcomes, experiences, and costs." The engineering framing for this role is senior-IC-leadership: "technical leader for this team — someone who can architect end-to-end systems across mobile and backend, tackle complex scaling challenges, and raise the bar on engineering quality." The CV User Experience team's job is "making that magic feel effortless." Explicit EEO statement in both English and French, covering the Canadian Human Rights Act, the Quebec Charter, and the Employment Equity Act; accommodations offered. Company publishes an anti-phishing warning for candidates (only "@hingehealth.com" emails; verify via security@hingehealth.com).

Size / stage / funding: Large, late-stage. Not stated on the posting; publicly the company listed on US markets in 2025 — NOT verified from a first-party source in this session, so treat "public company" as unverified prior knowledge rather than fact. Scale indicators that ARE first-party: 20M+ covered lives, 2,550+ employer customers, 50+ health-plan/PBM partners, offices on three continents.

Locations / HQ: "headquartered in San Francisco with additional offices in Montreal and Bangalore." This posting is Montreal, QC.

Work is full-stack across mobile and backend, plus scaling/performance of TrueMotion-driven interactive features. Cross-functional pods: engineers work alongside product managers, designers, data scientists and computer-vision scientists. Staff-level expectations are architecture ownership, leading architectural discussion, and raising quality bars, on an explicit 3-month / 6-month ramp plan.
