# Thanx — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:thanx | company_type: product

What they do: Thanx is a loyalty, CRM and guest-engagement platform for restaurants, aimed at regional and national multi-location brands. In their own words: "Thanx helps regional and national restaurant brands grow customer lifetime value with easy-to-use lifecycle marketing automations and innovative customer loyalty tools focused on access, status, and personalization over rote discounts." The technical differentiator is proprietary **credit-card tokenization** — purchases are attributed automatically from card transactions with no check-in or code scanning, which "dramatically increases the volume and accuracy of purchase data which sits at the core of our CRM suite of tools." They also ship app- and web-based ordering experiences. Founded 2011 in San Francisco.

How they describe themselves: The stated culture is a set of six "core behaviors," which they credit with their results: **Think Boldly, Execute Reliably, Focus on What Matters, Say "Thanx" Genuinely, Encourage Diverse Perspectives, and Lead, Virtually.** ("Lead, Virtually" is an explicitly remote-native value.) They emphasise "incredibly strong employee tenure, track record of internal promotions, and impressive alumni network," and describe "a thriving culture of trust, autonomy, and internal growth." Recognition claimed: Nation's Restaurant News "2020 Power List" — one of the 50 most influential innovators in food service. Tone is confident-startup but restrained; the only hype tells are "Think Boldly" and the JD's closing "Ready to change your life? Apply now!"

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, "more than $30M raised" from M33 Growth, Ribbit Capital and Sequoia Capital. Founded 2011 — fifteen years old but with modest total funding for that age, implying either capital efficiency or slow growth rather than a hypergrowth trajectory. Headcount is small (low hundreds at most). No layoff reports surfaced.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco origin; operates as a distributed company. Live engineering requisitions are located "Remote - Canada" and "Remote - Brazil & Argentina."

Remote policy: Remote-native. "Lead, Virtually" is one of the six core behaviors, and the JD requires "Strong communication skills for remote collaboration and cross functional discussions." Compensation is explicitly location-tiered (Tier 1 / Tier 2 cities), which is a hallmark of a genuinely distributed employer. On-call rotation participation is required.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** Two live engineering requisitions are ATS-located "Remote - Canada" (Senior Software Developer 6250103003 and Software Developer 6231699003), the JD body opens with "***Remote - Canada***", and the posted salary ranges are quoted in **CAD**. A Software Engineering Manager role is also open for Remote - Canada. No province restriction stated. San Francisco anchoring means Pacific Time, one hour behind Mountain — an easy overlap.

Engineering & tech: Ruby on Rails backend (Django named as an acceptable equivalent), React and React Native on the front end, TypeScript, Angular mentioned as comparable. Full-stack expectation with optional specialisation. Infrastructure as code with AWS CDK or Terraform is a preferred qualification; data platform work with Snowflake, Fivetran and dbt likewise. Tooling ecosystem: Jira, Notion, Sentry, Datadog. Responsibilities include systematic technical-debt reduction, root-cause bug work, code review and on-call.

**Unusually aggressive AI-workflow mandate** — stated as a core requirement, not a nice-to-have: "Our engineers use **Claude Code** as their primary development tool with MCP connections to production data, Jira, Notion, Sentry, and Datadog. We expect engineers to actively leverage and improve these workflows." And: "Active daily use of AI coding tools (Claude Code, Cursor, or similar) with demonstrated productivity gains. We are looking for engineers who have integrated AI into their development workflow, not just experimented with it." Building "reusable AI workflows, skills, or tooling that others can use" is a preferred qualification. They also disclose that AI tools may assist in screening applications, with human review retained. Senior bar: 6+ years.

Compensation (stated verbatim on their own Remote-Canada JD, in CAD, with an explicit internal ladder):
- L4 – Tier 1 City: $155,000 – $190,000 CAD
- L4 – Tier 2 City: $135,000 – $165,000 CAD
- L5 – Tier 1 City: $185,000 – $220,000 CAD
- L5 – Tier 2 City: $165,000 – $200,000 CAD
"Thanx uses structured internal levelling and location-based compensation. Final levelling and compensation are determined through the interview process based on experience, scope, and work location, and any offer will align to the appropriate range." The Senior Software Developer title maps to L5; Software Developer to L4. The separate "Remote - Brazil & Argentina" requisitions post a much lower USD 75,000–95,000 range for the same titles.

Notable / other: Publishing a full four-cell CAD level/tier matrix on a Canadian posting is materially better pay transparency than any other company in this batch. The role is described as "an existing vacancy" (backfill, not speculative headcount). Early-stage startup background is a preferred qualification. Equal-opportunity statement present, including fair-chance language for applicants with arrest or conviction records.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Developer (Remote - Canada); Software Developer (Remote - Canada); Senior Software Developer and Software Developer (Remote - Brazil & Argentina); Software Engineering Manager (Remote - Canada).
