# Argonne National Laboratory — research
Provider/key: workday:argonne/argonne_careers | company_type: product (public research institution / US federal laboratory)

What they do: Argonne National Laboratory is a **US Department of Energy national laboratory**, operated for the DOE and located in Lemont, Illinois. The relevant hiring org is the **Argonne Leadership Computing Facility (ALCF)**, which operates leadership-class supercomputers for the open scientific community. In their own words, the ALCF mission is *"to accelerate major scientific discoveries and engineering breakthroughs for humanity by designing and providing world-leading computing facilities in partnership with the computational science community. We help researchers solve some of the world's largest and most complex problems with our unique combination of supercomputing resources and computational science expertise."*

How they describe themselves: Mission-driven public science rather than commercial. The current flagship programme described in their JDs is the DOE's **American Science Cloud (AmSC)** — *"a secure, federated, and science-optimized cloud environment that integrates the DOE's world-leading computing and experimental facilities, data resources, and high-performance networks,"* enabling scientists to *"create, access, and integrate AI-ready datasets, run scalable AI model training and inference on leadership-class systems, perform distributed simulations, control instruments, and move data efficiently across sites."* Tone is institutional, precise, procedural; there is zero hustle, growth or hype language. Emphasis on teamwork, communication and continuous improvement ("Define and implement KPIs, processes and drive continuous improvement").

Size / stage / funding: Federally funded national laboratory, roughly 3,000+ staff. Funding is appropriated rather than raised — extremely stable in the ordinary sense, though exposed to US federal budget cycles rather than market risk. Not applicable: stage/VC funding.

Locations / HQ: Lemont, Illinois, USA (single main campus). All relevant live postings are tagged "Lemont, IL USA."

Remote policy: Stated verbatim in the DevOps JD as **"Hybrid Remote Work – Occasionally Onsite: which applies to employees regularly scheduled for some onsite and some remote days, with employees typically working more than 60% of their time remotely."** So it is a hybrid arrangement anchored to the Lemont campus, not location-independent remote. Non-exempt employees may not split on-site and remote in the same day without supervisor approval.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified NO — hard disqualifier.** Two independent blockers in the DevOps JD: (1) hybrid work is defined relative to the Lemont, IL campus, so physical proximity to Illinois is required; (2) *"US citizenship: To perform the essential functions of this position successful applicants must provide proof of U.S. citizenship, which is required to comply with federal regulations and contract."* Postdoctoral Appointee roles are likewise Lemont-based residency positions. There is no path here for a Canada-based remote worker.

Engineering & tech: Genuinely deep platform/infrastructure work — Kubernetes cluster administration (EKS/AKS/GKE, node-group lifecycle, cluster upgrades, CNI plugins, load-balancer controllers, multi-environment promotion dev→staging→prod), Terraform, GitOps/Helm, CI/CD pipeline construction, Harbor image registries, DevSecOps security controls, instrumentation feeding monitoring/alerting, multi-cloud central hub infrastructure, and vendor coordination for hardware/software. The DevOps engineer sits in the **Data Services and Workflows team** at ALCF and interfaces with the AmSC Infrastructure Services group; downstream "L2 science service teams" deploy data catalogs, at-scale HPC compute services, APIs and AI/MLOps on top. Entry bar for the PT2 level is a CS bachelor's plus 2+ years, with prior team-lead experience requested.

Notable / other: The AI-adjacent postings are **Postdoctoral Appointee** positions (fixed-term research appointments, typically 2–3 years, requiring a recent PhD) rather than staff engineering roles — a materially different employment category and pay scale from the DevOps staff role. Existing comp on record for the DevOps/SRE slot is $69,750–$108,810 USD base, which is national-lab scale and well below commercial infra pay.

Open relevant roles (sample): DevOps Engineer (Lemont, IL); Assistant Software Engineer - Autonomous Discovery Platforms (MADSci); Postdoctoral Appointee - Building Agentic AI Platform for X-ray Science; Postdoctoral Appointee - Data Infrastructure and Software for AI.
