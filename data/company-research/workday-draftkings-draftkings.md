# DraftKings — research
Provider/key: workday:draftkings/draftkings | company_type: product

What they do: DraftKings (NASDAQ: DKNG) is a publicly traded digital sports-entertainment and gaming company: daily fantasy sports, regulated online sportsbook, iGaming/casino, and a media arm. It builds and operates its own betting platform end to end — odds/pricing, wallet and payments, risk and trading, personalization, and a very high-throughput real-time event pipeline that spikes hard around live sporting events. Revenue is consumer-facing and regulated state by state / jurisdiction by jurisdiction.

How they describe themselves: Careers copy is confident and pace-forward — "Engineering at Startup Speed and Global Scale," "A Culture Built for Bold Engineers," "Driven By People, Not Borders," and "The Crown Is Yours" as the JD opener. Their ML JD leads with an AI manifesto: "AI is becoming an integral part of both our present and future… Our teams are energized by innovation and readily embrace emerging technology. We're not waiting for the future to arrive. We're shaping it, one bold step at a time." Culture material stresses inclusion and belonging, cross-functional collaboration, and trust in engineers to bring in tools and frameworks they think solve a problem better. Roles are described as "fast-moving, high-impact."

Size / stage / funding: Public company, thousands of employees, headquartered in Boston. Mature and well capitalized rather than runway-dependent, though profitability has historically been uneven and the sector is exposed to regulatory and tax changes (state tax rate hikes on gaming revenue are an ongoing business risk). Publicly traded means quarterly-earnings pressure and, at times, cost discipline / headcount actions.

Locations / HQ: HQ Boston, MA. Careers site lists offices and hiring geos in the United States (incl. Las Vegas), Canada, Ireland, United Kingdom, Israel, Bulgaria and Ukraine. Their engineering org is genuinely multi-geo, with large teams in Eastern Europe (Bulgaria, Ukraine) and Israel.

Remote policy: Flexible hybrid, not remote-first. Company language is "a combination of remote and on-site work… work where they're most productive"; employee reports describe roughly 2 days in office / 3 remote for people attached to an office. However, individual requisitions are explicitly location-typed, and a meaningful number are posted as fully remote by country.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, at the requisition level.** Three currently-open relevant reqs are posted with location "Remote - Canada": Senior Software Engineer, Backend (JR14268); Senior Machine Learning Engineer, Personalization (JR13985); Senior Software Engineer, Platform Tooling (JR14289). These are country-level remote, not metro-hybrid, so a British Columbia resident is eligible. Caveat: most of the req volume is Boston-MA-hybrid, London, or Remote–Ukraine/Bulgaria, so the Canada-remote surface is a minority of openings and hours skew toward Eastern Time / European overlap rather than Mountain Time. Note also that as a regulated gaming company they state you "may be required to obtain a gaming license issued by the appropriate state agency as a condition of employment."

Engineering & tech: Stack spans .NET Core, Kafka, Node.js, React, Apache Flink, Kubernetes, Swift and Kotlin. Data/ML side uses Python, SQL, Spark, Databricks, MLflow, Jenkins and GitOps; the Personalization ML role covers real-time one-to-one personalization at scale, production ML pipelines, CI/CD for models, drift detection and retraining, and A/B experiment design. Backend roles are conventional large-scale distributed services; there are dedicated Data Platform, Platform Tooling and SRE tracks, so genuine infrastructure scope exists alongside product-facing work. Real-time, spiky, low-latency traffic makes reliability engineering substantive.

Notable / other: Regulated gaming is an ethical consideration in its own right — the product is consumer gambling, and engineering work on "personalization… to deepen engagement, improve retention, and drive long-term player value" is directly optimizing gambling engagement. On-call and event-driven peak load (major sporting events) are inherent to the domain.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Backend — Remote, Canada; Senior Machine Learning Engineer, Personalization — Remote, Canada; Senior Software Engineer, Platform Tooling — Remote, Canada; Senior Data Platform Engineer — London, UK; Senior Site Reliability Engineer — Boston, MA; Lead Software Engineer, Backend — Boston, MA.
