# Chamberlain Group (CG) — research
Provider/key: workday:chamberlain/chamberlain_group | company_type: product

What they do: Intelligent access hardware and the cloud platform behind it — garage door openers, commercial access control, and connected IoT cameras. In their own words: "Chamberlain Group (CG) is a global leader in intelligent access and Blackstone portfolio company. Powered by our myQ technology, we make access simple and secure for millions of homeowners, businesses, and communities worldwide. Our flagship brands, LiftMaster® and Chamberlain®, are found in 51+ million homes, and 14 million+ people rely on the myQ® app daily." The engineering centre of gravity for the relevant roles is the **Video Platform**: "a large scale, high availability system that provides video streaming, storage and computer vision for IP cameras and other IoT devices with embedded cameras."

How they describe themselves: The JD language is corporate and process-oriented rather than mission-poetic — the closest thing to stated values is a list of professional obligations: "Protect Chamberlain Group's reputation by keeping information confidential," "Maintain professional and technical knowledge by attending educational workshops, reading professional publications, establishing personal networks, and participating in professional societies," "Comply with health and safety guidelines and rules," and compliance with "the security requirements set forth by the Information Security team and the established ISO 27001 Security Roles, Responsibilities, and Authorities Document found in the ISMS Document Library." Tone is that of a mature manufacturing company with a formal engineering organisation — design review processes, communities of practice, documented standards, multiple scrum teams. There is no hustle or rocketship language whatsoever; equally, there is no async/remote-first/handbook signal.

Size / stage / funding: Private-equity owned — explicitly "a Blackstone portfolio company". Long-established consumer hardware brand (LiftMaster, Chamberlain) with 51M+ homes and 14M+ daily app users, so revenue-generating and mature rather than runway-dependent. Engineering & tech: Java-heavy enterprise stack — "Java SE, Java EE, JDBC, Hibernate", SQL and NoSQL databases, queues and messaging systems, Eclipse and Git. Video specialisation is deep and unusual: "WebRTC, H.264, HLS, ICE, video relay". Cloud is AWS: "EKS, ECR, Aurora, S3, KVS [Kinesis Video Streams], SQS, Rekognition, CodePipeline", plus APM tooling. Organisation: architects collaborating with dedicated AI/ML, UX, infrastructure, database, firmware and front-end domain teams across multiple scrum teams, with formal design-review oversight, coding standards, community-of-practice forums, and technology roadmaps reported up to manager/director stakeholders. Requirements are heavy: 10+ years relevant experience, 8 years cloud architecture and platform design.

Notable / other: The Principal Architect role is substantially a technical-leadership job — "Lead the team to design and build our next generation video platform", assess "functional team skills and process maturity, outline training plans", mentor junior and senior engineers, and "report functional team strategy and roadmap to manager and director level stakeholders".
