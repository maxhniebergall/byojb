# Manulife and John Hancock — research
Provider/key: workday:manulife/mfcjh_jobs | company_type: product
What they do: Manulife Financial is one of the largest life-insurance and financial-services groups in the world, operating as **Manulife** in Canada and Asia and as **John Hancock** in the United States. Business lines are life and health insurance, group benefits, retirement and pension administration, wealth and asset management (Manulife Investment Management), and Manulife Bank. Their consumer-facing framing is financial planning: helping people "protect, save, and grow your wealth", covering retirement and longevity preparation, tax optimization and investment education, and they position themselves as looking "at your entire financial situation to help define and achieve your financial goals."
How they describe themselves: Purpose-led and impact-oriented, with public sustainability and investor-relations programmes. Corporate language centres on making decisions easier and lives better for customers. Engineering copy is plain and non-hyped — no rocketship, hustle or "wear many hats" framing appears in their requisitions, which read as straightforward enterprise-IT descriptions.
Size / stage / funding: Very large, mature, publicly traded (TSX: MFC; NYSE: MFC; also HKEX and PSE). Roughly 38,000 employees plus a large agent force, with assets under management and administration in the trillions of CAD. Heavily regulated (OSFI in Canada), which makes it a stable, "boring", mission-critical domain rather than a runway-dependent one.
Locations / HQ: Global HQ **Toronto, Ontario** (200 Bloor Street East). Major Canadian sites in Toronto, Waterloo (Manulife Bank Technology) and Montreal. US operations under John Hancock in **Boston, Massachusetts**. Very large Asian footprint including Quezon City (Philippines — a major technology and operations hub), Hong Kong, Singapore, and other Asia markets.
Remote policy: **Hybrid, explicitly, with remote ruled out.** Their requisitions state the arrangement in their own words: "Work arrangement: Hybrid - 3 days in office (nearest office), 2 days from home; **Remote working option is not available**." Requisitions also rank office preference explicitly, e.g. "Office location: Toronto - Canada (preference - 1) or Waterloo - Canada (preference -2) or Boston - USA (preference -3)."
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified NO.** This is stated by Manulife itself, not inferred: remote working is not available, and the hybrid model requires 3 days per week at the nearest office. Every live Canadian requisition is anchored to Toronto, Waterloo or Montreal. There is no office within reach of Kimberley, British Columbia, so a non-relocating BC candidate is ineligible for the live set.
Engineering & tech: A substantial enterprise technology organisation with genuine platform depth. A **Cloud Operations** team runs multi-cloud infrastructure — the Platform Engineer role is to "build and deploy scalable, secure cloud infrastructure on Azure, AWS, or Google Cloud", "Develop and maintain Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using tools such as Terraform, CloudFormation, or Ansible", "Ensure compliance with relevant industry standards and regulations", and integrate cloud services into existing workflows across development and operations teams. Azure appears to be the primary platform (there is a dedicated Senior Cloud Architect – Azure & AI Platforms (Multi-Cloud) requisition) alongside an AI & Modernization architecture track. Other live functions: data engineering (Waterloo and Quezon City), machine-learning engineering (Boston, and a Global Marketing AI team in Asia), DevOps for Manulife Bank Technology, and an Asia Data Office.
Notable / other: Good pay transparency on Canadian requisitions, which post CAD base ranges and note that "The actual salary will vary depending on local market conditions, geography and relevant job-related factors", with incentive compensation on top of base. Benefits are enterprise-grade: health, dental, mental health, vision, short- and long-term disability, life and AD&D, adoption/surrogacy and wellness benefits, EAP, pension plus a global share ownership plan with employer matching, and generous Canadian paid time off. They disclose that they "use data and analytics technologies, such as artificial intelligence (AI), and automated processing tools, to analyze and process the information you provide" during hiring.
Open relevant roles (sample): Platform Engineer (Toronto/Waterloo/Boston); Senior Cloud Engineer (Toronto, 200 Bloor St E); Cloud & Platform Engineer (Montreal); Cloud/DevOps Engineer, Manulife Bank Technology (Waterloo); Senior Cloud Architect – AI & Modernization (Toronto); Senior Cloud Architect – Azure & AI Platforms (Multi-Cloud) (Toronto); Data Engineer (Waterloo); Senior Machine Learning Engineer (Boston).
