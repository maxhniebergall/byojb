# TetraScience — research
Provider/key: workable:tetrascience | company_type: product

What they do: TetraScience sells a "Scientific Data and AI Cloud" for life sciences — a data platform that ingests, harmonizes and governs instrument and lab data from pharma R&D, then serves it to analytics and AI workloads. Their own framing: "the industry's only purpose-built, open, and collaborative Scientific Data and AI Platform." Customers are pharma and biotech; they claim "48% of top pharma companies are TetraScience customers" and that they are "the category leader in this vital new market, generating more revenue than all other companies in the aggregate." Regulated-industry requirements (audit trail, eSignature, dataset-level access control) are first-class product concerns.

How they describe themselves: Vision — "Enable Scientific AI to solve humanity's grand challenges." Mission — "Combine deep scientific knowledge and the industry's only purpose-built, open, and collaborative Scientific Data and AI Platform to improve and extend human life." The full stated value set ("the Tetra Way") is six items: **Transparency & context; Fearlessness & resilience; Alignment with customers; Trust & collaboration; Equality of opportunity; Commitment to craft.** Notably, every candidate is required to read a values letter from the CEO before applying: "you will be asked to carefully review the Tetra Way letter, authored directly by Patrick Grady, our co-founder and CEO… It is impossible to overstate the importance of this document and you are encouraged to take it literally and reflect on whether you are aligned with our unique approach to company and team building. If you join us, you will be expected to embody its contents each day." The tone across mission and JD is high-intensity and category-leadership-flavored ("catalyzing the Scientific AI revolution," "100× growth").

Engineering & tech: The Platform Core group owns shared services every other team builds on — authorization, metadata, governance, and high-throughput event processing — with an explicit design goal to "absorb 100× growth in data volume and users." Stack from the JD: TypeScript and Python for backend services; AWS deeply (EC2, Lambda, ECS/EKS, IAM, S3) plus SQS, Kinesis, Kafka, EventBridge; infrastructure-as-code via CloudFormation and AWS CDK; API-first design across REST, GraphQL and OpenAPI; observability with SLI/SLO/SLA design and distributed tracing. Identity work is unusually deep and explicitly not integration work: OAuth 2.0, OIDC, JWT/JWKS, SAML, full token lifecycle including client-credentials and token exchange for service-to-service and workload identity, extended to non-human identities (AI agents, MCP-based tooling), with policy-driven RBAC/ABAC/ReBAC externalized from application code. The Lead seat is described as "technical design authority / SME across product and engineering." No public engineering blog or handbook was found via the pages fetched.

They state co-innovation and go-to-market partnerships with major compute/cloud/data/AI vendors, though no partner is named in the JD text.
