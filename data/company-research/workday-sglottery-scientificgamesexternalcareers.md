Entity check: the registry name "Sglottery" is a Workday tenant slug. In its own requisition copy the company identifies itself as **Scientific Games**, and the tenant is literally "scientificgamesexternalcareers". This is the lottery business that was separated from the former Scientific Games Corporation (whose gaming/slots business was renamed Light & Wonder in 2022) — so it should not be confused with Light & Wonder, nor with the unrelated SciPlay social-gaming business.
What they do: In their own words, "Scientific Games is the global leader in lottery games, sports betting and technology, and the partner of choice for government lotteries. From cutting-edge backend systems to exciting entertainment experiences and trailblazing retail and digital solutions, we elevate play every day. We push game designs to the next level and are pioneers in data analytics and iLottery." Customers are government and state lotteries — instant scratch tickets, lottery systems and terminals, iLottery digital platforms, and sports betting technology. That makes the customer base institutional and regulated rather than consumer-driven.
How they describe themselves: "Built on a foundation of trusted partnerships, Scientific Games combines relentless innovation, legendary performance, and unwavering security to responsibly propel the global lottery industry ever forward." Note the emphasis on responsibility and security, which is characteristic of regulated gaming. No formal values list is exposed in the requisition copy, and their about page could not be retrieved.
Size / stage / funding: Private and **private-equity owned** — the lottery business was acquired from Scientific Games Corporation by Brookfield Business Partners in 2022. Roughly 6,000–7,000 employees globally. Established and revenue-generating rather than venture-stage, but PE ownership brings the usual leverage, cost-discipline and eventual-exit dynamics.
Locations / HQ: HQ Atlanta, Georgia, USA, with operations worldwide. From the live requisition set: **Montreal, Canada** and **Toronto, Canada** for the ML roles, plus Alpharetta, GA in the US.
Remote policy: Not published in any first-party source that could be retrieved, and the requisitions do not describe a hybrid or remote arrangement. What they do state is office anchoring — the Senior Machine Learning Engineer requisition says plainly: "This role is based out of Toronto."
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified NO for the live roles — they are office-anchored in Eastern Canada.** All three live relevant requisitions are labelled Montreal, Canada in the registry, but the Senior ML Engineer JD body states "This role is based out of Toronto", which is a direct contradiction of its own location label. Either way the role is anchored to an Eastern-Canada office, no remote arrangement is offered anywhere in the copy, and regulated gaming employers typically impose location and background-check constraints. A candidate in Kimberley, British Columbia who cannot relocate is not eligible.
Engineering & tech: The ML platform charter here is unusually well-specified and genuinely infrastructural. From the Senior Machine Learning Engineer requisition: the mission is "to build self-service ML tooling and golden paths that enable Data Scientists to independently take models from experimentation to reliable production deployment across batch and real-time use cases", explicitly "not about creating a centralized gatekeeping team". Responsibilities include building "reusable self-service tooling for model packaging, deployment, batch inference, and real-time serving"; "foundational ML workflows including model registry, environment promotion, rollback, feature access patterns, and inference APIs"; and "CI/CD pipelines for automated training, validation, shadow deployment, canary rollout, rollback, and full production promotion workflows", plus "golden-path templates, SDKs, CLIs". Named or preferred technologies: Databricks, PySpark, Airflow or equivalent orchestration; feature stores and reusable feature-access SDKs; API-based inference services, async batch scoring and event-driven pipelines; self-service experimentation and A/B testing tooling. They want "Experience building internal ML platforms from zero to first scaled adoption" and someone with a "Builder mindset with focus on developer experience and adoption" who can "translate infrastructure complexity into simple self-service workflows". The ML platform is being built greenfield ("from the ground up", "the first generation of reusable ML infrastructure"), staffed with Staff MLEs alongside Senior MLEs and partnered with Data Scientists.
Notable / other: No compensation is posted on any of the live requisitions, including the Canadian ones. Standard EEO statement ("SG is an Equal Opportunity Employer..."). A Senior Data Engineer requisition in Alpharetta, GA has recently gone dead.
