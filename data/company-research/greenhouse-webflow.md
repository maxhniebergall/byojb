# Webflow — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:webflow | company_type: product

What they do: Webflow is a visual web-development platform — a website builder plus hosting and CMS that lets designers and marketers build production websites without hand-writing code, positioned in recent years as a "Website Experience Platform" with growing AI and optimization/experimentation capability. Customers are primarily design teams, agencies and marketing organizations. The core product is a design/marketing tool; the infrastructure roles exist to support the hosting, delivery and developer-productivity substrate underneath it.

How they describe themselves: JDs carry a recurring "**Remote, together**" section describing the remote-first model. The engineering org is organized into named product surfaces — "Delivery Loop" (deploy/delivery infrastructure), "Convert" (optimization/experimentation), "Developer Productivity", plus Cloud infrastructure and Observability functions.

Size / stage / funding: ~1,649 employees worldwide as of December 2025, up 26.1% from 1,308 in 2023 — steady, substantial growth. Private, heavily venture-funded, well past early stage. Hiring is accelerating: active job postings roughly doubled from 56 in March 2026 to 107 in April 2026. No public profitability disclosure.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco. Distributed workforce with dedicated hiring hubs, including a notable **Argentina** engineering presence and defined Canadian and US remote eligibility.

Remote policy: **Genuinely remote-first.** Reporting notes Webflow "has never issued a return-to-office mandate," in contrast to peers like Salesforce, Amazon and Shopify, and remote roles (125) substantially outnumber on-site ones (38). Postings are nonetheless geo-scoped to specific hiring countries rather than being open-globally.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Mostly no — and this is the decisive finding.** Of the five relevant live postings, **four are scoped "Remote-first (Argentina)"** and are not open to Canadian applicants: Senior Agentic Engineer, Senior Infrastructure Engineer (Cloud), Senior DevOps Engineer (Delivery Loop), Staff DevOps Engineer (Delivery Loop), and Staff Software Engineer (Convert). Exactly **one** posting is Canada-eligible — Staff Software Engineer, Developer Productivity, listed as "**CA Remote (BC & ON only); U.S. Remote**" — and British Columbia is explicitly included there. So Webflow can and does employ BC-based remote engineers, but its current infrastructure/DevOps hiring push is aimed at Argentina, not Canada.

Engineering & tech: Hosting and delivery infrastructure, cloud infrastructure, site reliability and observability, developer productivity/internal tooling, and an emerging agentic-AI engineering track (Senior Agentic Engineer). The "Delivery Loop" and "Developer Productivity" teams are internal-platform work in the desirable sense.

Compensation (first-party): The Staff Software Engineer, Developer Productivity JD posts a base range of **$212,500–$255,000 USD** — a first-party, transparency-law-style disclosure. Webflow also states that the role "is eligible to participate in Webflow's company-wide bonus program. Target amounts are a percentage of base salary and vary by career level. Payouts are based on company performance against established financial and operational goals."

Notable / other: The Argentina concentration for infrastructure roles is a visible cost-arbitrage pattern and is the main structural reason most of Webflow's relevant openings are unreachable from Canada.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer, Developer Productivity (**CA Remote — BC & ON — and U.S. Remote**); Senior Agentic Engineer (Argentina); Senior Infrastructure Engineer, Cloud (Argentina); Senior/Staff DevOps Engineer, Delivery Loop (Argentina); Senior Site Reliability Engineer, Observability; Staff Software Engineer, Convert (Argentina).
