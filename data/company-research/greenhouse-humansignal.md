# HumanSignal — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:humansignal | company_type: product

What they do: HumanSignal is the company behind **Label Studio**, the open-source data-labeling and evaluation platform, which they describe as "the open-source standard for data labeling and evaluation, used by over 1 million practitioners worldwide." The commercial product is Label Studio Enterprise. Beyond the platform, they operate as a "human data partner" — a services arm that designs and creates datasets from scratch, recruits and manages the domain experts who evaluate model output, and runs the whole pipeline through their own platform. They specialize in "the operationally complex: real-world data collection, multimodal pipelines, and multi-step workflows." Customers are ML/AI teams building models and AI products, split between enterprises running their own "data factories" on the platform and teams buying the managed services.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "We build software that helps people do what only they can do – give meaning," and to "help data scientists use data to teach ML/AI models how to make the best predictions." Their framing opens with "Real-world data is the competitive edge in AI," and their stated core belief is that "historical data needs to be accurate, comprehensive, and without bias" for ML/AI to be meaningful; they emphasize that *domain experts*, not generalists, should annotate data. Recruiting pitch: "If you want to do work that materially shapes how the next generation of AI products gets built, we'd love to talk." Their engineering JD states its posture bluntly: "This is a product-engineering role: not 'build what you're told,' but shape what we build — own the problem, propose solutions, and ship outcomes that matter to users and the business."

Size / stage / funding: Private, ~51-200 employees. Founded 2019 as **Heartex**, rebranded to HumanSignal in June 2023 to signal a shift toward enterprise. Has raised **$30M total**: a $4M seed (January 2021) and a $25M Series A (May 2022) led by Redpoint Ventures, with Unusual Ventures, Bow Capital and Swift Ventures. No publicly announced round since 2022. Profitability not disclosed.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco Bay Area. No traditional office footprint described — the company presents as distributed rather than office-centred.

Remote policy: Explicitly a "remote-first, distributed organization," with team members across North America, South America and Europe. Pay is "set based on regional compensation market rate ranges across the globe" — i.e. geo-adjusted bands. Benefits include competitive salary with equity ownership, a flexible vacation policy that recognizes local holidays, remote work from anywhere, and annual in-person team offsites.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, leaning yes.** The company states it hires "across North America, South America as well as Europe" and the Senior Backend Engineer req is listed simply as "Remote" with no country restriction. However, no posting or page names Canada explicitly; the specific hiring locations that do appear by name in their postings are the US, Mexico City and Manila. Their geo-adjusted pay policy implies the machinery to employ outside the US exists. This needs direct confirmation before applying.

Engineering & tech: Stated stack — **Python/Django, JS/TS, React, OpenAI API, Spark, BigQuery, Kafka, Jest, Cypress, AWS, Kubernetes, Postgres, Redis.** The backend role covers services and APIs in Python/Django over Postgres/Redis, data modelling and migrations, query and cache tuning, background jobs and queues (Celery/RQ/Kafka-backed workers) for high-volume workflows, and reliability work described in real terms: "timeouts, retries, idempotency, rate limits, pagination, backpressure." Also secure multi-tenant patterns — auth, RBAC, auditability, safe data boundaries. Development practice is **trunk-based with small, frequent changes**. Engineers are expected to write concise RFCs/PRDs and decision records, partner early with PM/Design, work with Support to investigate production issues, and engage the open-source community on GitHub issues and discussions.

Notable / other: Label Studio's 1M+ practitioner install base is a genuinely large open-source footprint and the company's main distribution moat. The dual model — open-source core, enterprise platform, plus a human services organisation — means part of the company is a labour-managing services business rather than pure software. The rebrand from Heartex tracks a deliberate move upmarket to enterprise. Competitive space (Scale AI, Surge, Labelbox, Snorkel) is crowded and consolidating.

Comp: The Senior Backend Engineer req posts **$140K–$200K USD base**, which is the only company-stated pay data point on record.
