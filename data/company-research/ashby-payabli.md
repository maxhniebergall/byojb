# Payabli — research
Provider/key: ashby:payabli | company_type: product
What they do: "Payabli is a next-generation Payments Infrastructure and Monetization Platform purpose-built for vertical software companies." Embedded payments for vertical SaaS: a single developer-friendly API plus low-code embedded payment components that let software platforms "embed, monetize, and operationalize payments". Unifies payment acceptance, payment issuance and payment-operations tooling into one stack. Compliance posture stated as PCI DSS 4.0 and SOC 2.
How they describe themselves: They publish "north star values" directly on their JDs, and the wording is distinctive: "Team Love = Customer Love — You understand that when you build great products for your internal partners, they deliver exceptional experiences to merchants. You obsess over the platform partners and businesses that depend on every payout landing on time." / "Run to the Fire — When ACH returns spike or a card program hits a snag, you're the first one in the war room. You don't shy away from hard problems—you chase them. You volunteer for the complex, messy challenges others avoid." / "Little Things Count — You sweat the details because you know a single decimal error in a payout can damage a business." They also pitch: "you'll have a front row seat into building a high-growth venture backed fintech company... You'll stretch yourself every day, learn a ton, grow alongside the company", and describe an "AI-first operations strategy".
Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, self-described "high-growth". Investors named on their own JDs: QED Investors, Fika Ventures, TTV Capital, Bling Capital. Headcount and round UNVERIFIED (no search budget this session).

Engineering & tech: Backend is C# / .NET — services for intelligent payment routing, connector management, token intelligence and platform configuration; RESTful and event-driven APIs; relational databases and data modelling; distributed systems; secure, observable, high-volume transaction processing; merchant dashboards, onboarding flows, embeddable components and white-label experiences consumed by platform partners. Separately they are hiring a *founding* Staff Data Engineer: warehouse/lakehouse direction, data lake and layered architecture, batch and streaming pipelines (CDC, ELT, real-time), canonical data modelling (dimensional / normalized / Data Vault), data quality and observability, RBAC/masking/lineage/auditability for regulated financial data, feature pipelines for ML/AI, plus CI/CD standards for data. Named ecosystems: Snowflake + dbt + Fivetran, or Databricks + Spark + Delta Lake + Unity Catalog; Airflow/Dagster/Prefect; AWS/GCP/Azure; Kafka/Kinesis/Flink; Monte Carlo, Great Expectations, OpenLineage, MLflow. Bars: 5+ years for the senior backend role, 8+ years for the staff data role.
