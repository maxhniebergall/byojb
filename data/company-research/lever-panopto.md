# Panopto — research
Provider/key: lever:panopto | company_type: product

What they do: Panopto is a video knowledge platform for enterprises and universities — its own words:
"the video knowledge platform that helps enterprises and universities capture, manage, and share
knowledge at scale—turning every training session, lecture, and meeting into something searchable,
reusable, and lasting." Core capability is lecture/meeting capture plus search inside video (its
"Smart Search" indexes spoken words and on-screen text). Customers are 2,000+ organizations with
11 million daily users. Classic education-technology + enterprise-knowledge-management, sold to
universities and corporate L&D.

How they describe themselves: Founding belief is that recorded knowledge should be "as searchable and
accessible as written text." Five stated core values, verbatim:
- "Customers first, always"
- "Elevate the craft"
- "Clarity over complexity"
- "Act with ownership"
- "Thrive together"
Their DevOps JD repeats this register: "we are the most customer-centric learning technology company
in the world... We don't just build software; we obsess over our users' goals to deliver solutions that
truly matter. Our mission is simple: to attract the brightest talent, people like you, to Elevate the
Craft and do the most impactful work of your career." Tone is craft/quality-oriented rather than
hustle-oriented — there is no "fast-paced", "scrappy", "wear many hats" or "rocketship" language
anywhere in the JD. Notably the JD explicitly disclaims ticket-work: "You won't just be a
'ticket-taker.' We are looking for someone with an opinion. If you see a build process that takes
40 minutes and know you can get it down to 5, we will give you the autonomy to prove it."

Size / stage / funding: ~150 distributed employees globally. Founded 2007 as a spinout of
Carnegie Mellon University. Private, private-equity-owned (K1 Investment Management). Acquired Elai
(AI video generation) in October 2024. Stephen Laster became CEO in April 2025. A mature ~19-year-old
company in a slow-moving institutional market rather than a venture-runway startup.

Locations / HQ: Headquarters Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Additional offices in London, Singapore,
Hong Kong and Sydney.

Remote policy: Explicitly remote-first. Their own words: "Remote-first team operating across five
continents"; offices exist "for those who want them—not as a mandate. We measure output, not
attendance." That last clause is an unusually direct async/output-over-presence statement.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes — verified and explicit.** The live posting is titled
"DevOps Engineer (USA or Canada)" and the registry's extracted facets record geo_eligibility: canada
with location hints "USA, Canada". Combined with the company-level "remote-first... across five
continents, offices not a mandate" policy, a British-Columbia-based remote hire is squarely within
the stated hiring geography. Timezone is not constrained in the JD; HQ is Eastern but the team spans
five continents, so Mountain Time is workable.

Engineering & tech: The open role is CI/CD and build-system modernization — "assess our current CI/CD
landscape, identify bottlenecks in our legacy pipelines, and implement modern strategies to accelerate
our delivery lifecycle. You'll be the bridge between 'how we've always done it' and 'how we should be
doing it.'" Framed as internal-facing developer-productivity work: "You will directly impact the daily
quality of life for our entire engineering team." Extracted facets: autonomy high, culture balanced,
stage growth. There is real legacy surface here (a 2007-era codebase), which is both the scope of the
job and a signal of stable, non-greenfield engineering.

Notable / other: Second Panopto-related open role in the registry is an AI Engineer, consistent with
the Elai acquisition and a push into AI video/search features. PE ownership under K1 typically means
cost discipline and profitability focus — stability-positive, but also a cap on aggressive comp growth.
Pay for the DevOps role is posted on the JD (USD $155,000–$175,000), which is above the profile's
CAD $150K walk-away by a wide margin.

Open relevant roles (sample): DevOps Engineer (USA or Canada); AI Engineer.
