# TensorWave — research
Provider/key: ashby:tensorwave | company_type: product

What they do: TensorWave runs an AI cloud built specifically on AMD Instinct accelerators — bare-metal GPU infrastructure, managed services, and support for training, fine-tuning and inference. Their pitch is a credible non-NVIDIA alternative: "avoid vendor lock-in through AMD's open ROCm platform," "leading price to performance" at scale. Customers are AI labs and companies running large training/inference workloads.

How they describe themselves: Tagline is "Train Bigger. Infer Faster" and "The Cloud Built for the Next Wave of AI." From their JDs: "Our mission is simple: deliver seamless, secure, reliable, and resilient AI compute at scale. We've built a versatile cloud platform that eliminates infrastructure barriers, empowering builders to focus on innovation instead of fighting their stack. Because breakthrough AI should move at the speed of ideas, not infrastructure." JDs repeatedly frame the moment as "an exciting phase of growth" and cite standards of "excellence, collaboration, and impact." No published values/handbook page found.

Size / stage / funding: Series B, $350M raised at a $1.55B valuation (per their own site). Private, venture-funded, growth stage — not profitable/established. Headcount not published; the JD volume (8 live relevant infra roles) suggests active rapid hiring.

Locations / HQ: HQ not stated on the site. Postings list "Las Vegas, Nevada, Remote" or plain "Remote"; Las Vegas is the recurring physical anchor.

Remote policy: Roles are posted as "Remote," but the benefits block on every JD lists US-domestic benefits (401(k), FSA, US disability/life insurance) and closes with "Other In-Office Perks" — i.e. remote with an office culture attached.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — verified negative.** Every JD body carries: "All offers of employment are contingent upon verification of identity and authorization to work in the **United States**, as required by law." Combined with 401(k)/FSA benefits, this is a US-employment-only company. A Kimberley, BC resident is not eligible.

Engineering & tech: Genuinely deep infrastructure work — Kubernetes control-plane architecture across regions, multi-tenant cluster models (vcluster, Kamaji), cluster topology and failure-domain strategy, pod-to-pod networking and CNI (Cilium preferred), ingress/egress design, storage platform, virtualization, AWS/DevOps automation, ROCm/AMD GPU stack. Roles carry on-call and incident-response leadership. Ladder goes to Senior Staff. No public engineering blog or handbook was found.

Notable / other: The AMD-first bet is the distinctive thing about them — it is a real technical specialization rather than an AI-wrapper play. Accommodations contact published (accomodations@tensorwave.com, sic).

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Staff Infrastructure Engineer – Kubernetes Platform; Senior Staff Infrastructure Engineer – Virtualization; Infrastructure Engineer – Storage Platform; Infrastructure Engineer – Virtualization; DevOps Engineer – AWS; DevOps Engineer – Platform Integrations; Senior Machine Learning Engineer; Senior Software Engineer (Back-end).
