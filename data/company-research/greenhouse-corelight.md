# Corelight — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:corelight | company_type: product

What they do: Corelight is a network detection and response (NDR) security company built on the open-source Zeek network monitoring framework (whose creators founded the company). It sells sensors and a SaaS/AI-driven NDR platform that turns raw network traffic into evidence for SOC teams, threat hunters and incident responders. Customers are enterprise and government security operations centres; it partners closely with CrowdStrike and Cisco. It is an "open-core" business — the core engine (Zeek, and the Suricata integration) is open source and community-maintained.

How they describe themselves: Three stated values on the careers page — **"Low ego results"** (execute with humility, build collaboratively), **"Applied curiosity"** (use data and evidence to drive success), and **"Tireless service"** (commitment to customers, to the open-source communities, and to colleagues). They describe a "humble, winning culture where everyone matters and everyone helps," emphasise diversity/inclusion and run ERGs (women, veterans, pride). They explicitly call out encouraging "creativity and growth while valuing a healthy work-life balance", with family-friendly policies, and make time for volunteering and meetups. Tone is service- and evidence-oriented rather than hype-driven; there is no "move fast / hustle" language on their own pages.

Size / stage / funding: Private, San Francisco HQ. ~300+ employees. Closed a $150M Series E in April 2024 led by Accel with participation from Cisco Investments and CrowdStrike Falcon Fund; ~$309M raised in total. Reported >40% YoY ARR growth and ~23% headcount growth; described as the fastest-growing scaled NDR platform. Late-stage and well-capitalised rather than runway-fragile, but not confirmed profitable.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco, CA. Regional presence across North America, EMEA and APAC (the careers page organises contacts by those four regions).

Remote policy: Remote-heavy. Their own words: the team "works remotely most of the time, but they're never remote with each other," with quarterly/bi-annual offsites and an annual company gathering. Remote WFH stipend provided. 401(k) matching in the US and pensions internationally — i.e. they employ outside the US.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Likely yes, not explicitly confirmed.** The live "Senior Backend Engineer" req is listed as *North America; Remote* and the "Lead Software Engineer - Applications" simply as *Remote*; the company runs international payroll/pension schemes. The careers page never names Canada specifically, so treat as probable-but-unverified and confirm with the recruiter. Nothing in the JDs requires an office or a US-only work authorisation. Mountain Time is unproblematic for a SF-anchored North American team.

Engineering & tech: Core engine is Zeek (C++/Spicy) plus Suricata; the product spans sensor software, a cloud SaaS analytics plane and detection content. Roles on the board are backend and applications engineering rather than full-stack product work. Being open-core, a large amount of the engineering is publicly visible (zeek.org, github.com/zeek) — an unusually good signal for judging the actual work. Detection/AI features are a stated growth area (300% YoY growth in AI/SaaS NDR).

Notable / other: Great Place to Work certified — 94% of employees say it is a great place to work vs 57% at a typical US company. Strategic investment from both Cisco and CrowdStrike makes it an obvious acquisition candidate. Its mission-critical, "boring plumbing" security domain (network evidence) is durable rather than cycle-chasing.
