# Priceline — research
Provider/key: workday:priceline/priceline | company_type: product

What they do: Priceline is a large online travel agency (OTA) — flights, hotels, rental cars, packages — and part of Booking Holdings. In their own words: *"WE ARE PRICELINE. Our success as one of the biggest players in online travel is all thanks to our incredible, dedicated team of talented employees. Priceliners are focused on being the best travel deal makers in the world."* Engineering serves high-volume consumer e-commerce: search, ranking, pricing, personalisation and intelligent automation across *"millions of traveler interactions."*

How they describe themselves: The stated values are named explicitly in the Principal Architect JD — **Customer, Innovation, Team, Accountability, and Trust** — and candidates are expected to show *"an illustrated history of living"* them. Framing is competitive and business-outcome-driven: the AI platform is *"mission-critical to our competitive edge,"* and the role exists to *"reduce architectural risk and complexity"* while *"balancing the need for rapid AI innovation with the rigor required for enterprise-grade security, reliability, and cost-efficiency."* Notably, the JD language is architecture-and-governance flavoured rather than hustle-flavoured — no "fast-paced" or "wear many hats" boilerplate appears.

Size / stage / funding: Large, established, profitable; a subsidiary of Booking Holdings (NASDAQ: BKNG). No runway risk. Consumer travel is cyclical but Priceline itself is not a startup by any measure.

Locations / HQ: US HQ in Norwalk, Connecticut. **A real Toronto engineering office exists** and is where all the currently scanned relevant roles sit — every live relevant posting in the registry is tagged "Toronto."

Remote policy: **Hybrid, stated explicitly.** The Principal Enterprise Architect JD says under Location: *"Hybrid (Two days in-office)."* That is a firm two-days-per-week onsite expectation, not a remote role with optional office access.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Canada-based yes; remote-Canada NO.** This is the important distinction. Priceline genuinely employs engineers in Canada — the roles are Toronto-based, priced in CAD, and subject to Ontario employment. But the posting is explicitly hybrid two-days-in-office in Toronto, which is **effectively disqualifying for a candidate in Kimberley, British Columbia who cannot relocate** — it is a relocation requirement in all but name, plus a three-hour time-zone offset from Mountain Time. Do not read "Toronto + Canada" as remote-eligible.

Engineering & tech: GCP-centric. The AI platform work names Vertex AI, GKE, Dataflow, Composer, BigQuery and Kafka; multi-agent frameworks (LangChain/LangGraph); vector stores; evaluation frameworks (Arize, LangSmith); model gateways; LLMOps/MLOps standardisation; LLM observability, prompt management, safety guardrails and cost awareness; RAG pipelines. The architect role is about defining *"golden paths"* and reusable frameworks for other engineering teams — i.e. genuine internal platform work — and coaching Principal and Staff engineers. Elsewhere the org runs a Java backend stack (multiple Associate Java Developer roles), an observability-focused SRE function, and cloud and data engineering teams.

Notable / other: Pay is disclosed in the JD in CAD, which is unusual and useful: **$150,000–$175,000 CAD** for the Principal Enterprise Architect, AI Platform, plus possible annual bonus and/or equity. They state the range is set from *"external market benchmark data, geographic location, and years of experience."* The Principal bar is high — 12+ years total with 5+ on AI/ML or data platforms at scale, and a Master's or PhD is preferred. Preference is stated for FinTech or high-volume e-commerce backgrounds.

Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Enterprise Architect, AI Platform (Toronto); Senior Site Reliability Engineer, Observability; Senior Cloud Engineer; Senior Software Engineer - Backend; Data Engineer; Associate Java Developer (Toronto).
