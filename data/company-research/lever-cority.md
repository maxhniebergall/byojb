# Cority — research
Provider/key: lever:cority | company_type: product

What they do: Cority is an enterprise **EHS+ (environment, health, safety, quality and
sustainability)** software company — a fully integrated SaaS platform sold to large industrial
organizations. Their own words: *"Cority helps customers see and prevent risks across their
operations in real time. Our EHS+ platform converges people, data, and AI agents to provide a clear
view of information people can trust, automate workflows that make people more impactful, and
deliver personalized insights and expertise to improve decision-making. While most solutions
respond to risks one at a time, Cority helps prevent them across environmental management, employee
health, safety, quality, and sustainability."* Verticals include aerospace, automotive, energy and
utilities, food and beverage, hospitals and medical centres, and manufacturing.

How they describe themselves: *"For 40 years, Cority has been the market leader in EHS+, recognized
by top analysts and trusted by more than 1,500 of the most complex organizations worldwide. Cority
has received many awards for its strong employee culture and outstanding business performance."*
The engineering org describes itself as *"a dynamic, fast-paced, and highly collaborative
environment"* looking for *"a passionate team player committed to the success of the collective
group."* Roles report to a Development Manager and work closely with Product Owners.

Size / stage / funding: Established private company, 35–40 years old, market leader in its
category, >1,500 enterprise customers. Mature and revenue-stable rather than venture-runway
dependent; large enough to have a global professional-services and implementation organization
alongside product engineering.

Locations / HQ: Toronto, Ontario. Also employs across the US, UK and internationally.

Remote policy: **Fully remote for these engineering roles**, stated on the JD itself: *"This is a
fully remote role open to candidates in the United States and Canada."* A second live posting is
scoped to Canada and the United Kingdom. Publicly advertised remote benefits include a one-time
home-office allowance and a subsidized monthly internet allowance, which indicates remote is a
structural arrangement rather than an exception.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes — verified.** The Senior Software Engineer JD states in its own
words that the role is fully remote and open to candidates in Canada; the registry location field
for both live postings reads "Canada, remote". No province restriction is stated, so British
Columbia should be in scope (worth a one-line recruiter confirmation, but the default reading is
yes). Toronto HQ means Eastern Time collaboration hours against Mountain Time — a 1-hour offset,
easily workable.

Engineering & tech: Product engineering teams own one or more product streams within the Cority
platform — designing, building and enhancing the core software. Scope and ownership are explicitly
calibrated to the individual: *"The scope and ownership of this role will reflect your experience —
we have meaningful work at a range of seniority, and we calibrate during the interview process."*
The platform is described as converging people, data and **AI agents**, so there is agent/workflow
automation work in the product. Ladder includes Senior Software Engineer and Staff Software
Engineer, plus Site Reliability Engineer, Senior Platform Engineer and Sr. DevOps Engineer roles
seen on their board — a real platform/infra function exists alongside product engineering.

Notable / other: Benefits publicly listed include competitive health and dental, retirement savings,
mental-health support (Calm Premium, Talkspace) and learning budgets (Udemy). Domain is regulated,
mission-critical and unglamorous — a "boring, mission-critical" business in the sense the profile
favours. The JDs state no compensation range.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer; Sr Software Engineer I; Site Reliability
Engineer II.
