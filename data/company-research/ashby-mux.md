# Mux — research
Provider/key: ashby:mux | company_type: product
What they do: "Mux is video for developers." A video API platform: video encoding and streaming (Mux Video), video monitoring/analytics (Mux Data), plus player embedding, moderation, search, transform, and AI-powered workflows ("Mux Robots"). Positioning is developer-infrastructure — easy integration, reliable scaling, cheaper than building in-house. "Our mission is to democratize video by solving the hard problems developers face when building video."
How they describe themselves: Six stated principles on their jobs page — "Care obsessively", "Be human" (life beyond work), "Do a lot with a little", "Be clear and direct", "Customers = fans", "Bias for action". The JD adds: "We're committed to building a healthy team that welcomes diverse backgrounds and experiences... believe in our values (from Be Human to Turn Customers Into Fans), and want to improve the people around them", and "We have a supportive culture that cares about both excellent work and work-life balance."
Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed. Investors named on their own JD: Coatue, Accel, Andreessen Horowitz, Y Combinator. Headcount not stated on any page fetched; funding round/stage and profitability UNVERIFIED this session (no search budget). Customers named across their own pages: Patreon, Cursor, Typeform, Fox, Substack, Shopify, Synthesia, Bandcamp, Runway, Citizen, HubSpot, Dropbox, Paramount, Glassdoor, Vercel, Yelp, Strava, Vimeo, Robinhood, PBS, Equinox.
Locations / HQ: Offices in downtown San Francisco, New York City, and London.
Remote policy: "remote-equal with offices in downtown San Francisco, New York, and London". Concrete async-supporting practices are stated: "Weekly no-meeting days + quarterly focus weeks" (the jobs page names it "No-meeting Thursdays"), flexible vacation + 11 company holidays.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO evidence of it. The live posting is "Remote, United States"; the jobs page lists open positions only under United States (Remote) and United Kingdom (London). Canada is not mentioned as a hiring location anywhere. Benefits are published in two blocks only — U.S. Benefits (401(k), FSA/HSA) and UK Benefits (matched pension, 25 days annual leave + 8 public holidays) — which corroborates two employing entities, US and UK.
Engineering & tech: Primarily a Go ecosystem, polyglot monorepo, Kubernetes at production scale, CI/CD and developer-productivity tooling, Argo CD, Terraform, skaffold. Adjacent systems: video ingest/streaming/CDN data paths, Kafka-like event streaming, distributed databases (CockroachDB, ClickHouse). Networking fundamentals are explicitly part of the bar (TCP/UDP, HTTP, TLS, DNS, Linux). The Platform Engineering team "works cross-functionally with product engineers to reduce friction", building "paved paths from local dev to prod" — an internal-platform charter with product engineers as the customer. Team pedigree cited: Google, YouTube, Twitch, Reddit, Zencoder, Fastly. Founders previously built and sold Zencoder and authored Video.js; Mux organizes Demuxed, the video-engineering conference.
Notable / other: Benefits include flexible vacation, competitive health/dental/vision including fertility, short- and long-term disability, group life, travel accident, 401(k), paid parental leave, professional development program, lunch/internet/equipment reimbursements.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Platform Engineer — CI/CD & Developer Productivity (Remote, United States); Staff Platform Engineer.
