# HOPPR — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:hoppr | company_type: product

What they do: HOPPR builds foundation-model / generative-AI technology for medical imaging. In its own words it "empowers developers to build the next generation of AI medical imaging applications, with the goal of making clinical care and workflow optimization more accessible, efficient, and equitable within the healthcare systems they serve." The product is effectively a platform other developers build on (radiology/medical-imaging AI), not a clinical end-user app.

How they describe themselves: Stated draws are "Innovation: be part of a pioneering company at the forefront of generative AI in healthcare"; "Impact: play a critical role in transforming radiology and medical imaging… directly impacting patient care"; "Growth: opportunities for professional development… in a rapidly growing industry"; "Culture: a dynamic and supportive team that values innovation, collaboration, and excellence." The role text asks for an "ownership mindset" and someone who will "thrive in a dynamic and rewarding environment that emphasizes excellence, autonomy, and impact." Tone is mission-plus-hypergrowth; "dynamic" and "rapidly growing" are the mild hustle markers.
