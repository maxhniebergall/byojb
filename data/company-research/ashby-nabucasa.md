# Nabu Casa — research
Provider/key: ashby:nabucasa | company_type: product

What they do: Nabu Casa, Inc. was "founded in 2018 by the founders of both Home Assistant, the open source home automation platform, and Home Assistant OS, the operating system that turns your device into a smart home hub powered by Home Assistant." Their commercial product is **Home Assistant Cloud**, a subscription service that gives the Home Assistant community "access to features usually reserved for power users with expensive hardware at home. Current features such as remote access and text-to-speech / speech-to-text support are made available without ever compromising on privacy." They also sell Home Assistant hardware. Scale, in their words: "More than two million households benefit from its home automation capabilities... GitHub ranked it the most active open-source project in the world in 2024."

How they describe themselves: Their founding rationale, verbatim: "After being involved in the Internet of Things industry for many years, we realized that there is a need for a cloud service that aims to put users, their privacy and their data first. Such a cloud can only be built as an extension to a platform that does the same." On governance and values: "In 2024, the Open Home Foundation was formed to protect these open-source projects and ensure they thrive into the future. Nabu Casa is a commercial partner of the foundation, and our principles are aligned with a joint commitment to **privacy, choice, and sustainability** in the smart home. These are the values that we put at the heart of every decision we make. They are woven into our architecture, licensing, community, and everything else." On employee wellbeing: "Core to the establishment of Nabu Casa was the well-being of the people building the future of the smart home."

Size / stage / funding: **Profitable, bootstrapped, user-funded** — stated twice, verbatim: "We are a profitable company with no external investors. Our funding comes directly from users who subscribe to Home Assistant Cloud and purchase Home Assistant hardware, ensuring our only stakeholders are our employees and our users." Founded 2018. Headcount is not stated but is small (the Cloud & Infrastructure team reports to a single Engineering Manager based in France, and the final interview is with the CTO and CEO directly). They also fund other open-source work: "Open hardware tools, such as ESPHome and ESP Web Tools; open standards, such as Python Matter Server, Z-Wave JS, ZigPy, BTHome, and Improv Wi-Fi; open voice, such as Rhasspy, Wyoming Protocol, and Piper; Music Assistant."

Locations / HQ: No offices — a fully distributed company employing worldwide. The Cloud team's Engineering Manager is based in France; scheduling is anchored to Central European Time.

Remote policy: **Fully remote, worldwide, with formal employment.** Verbatim: "Nabu Casa is a fully remote organization that uses Remote [the EOR] to employ people from all over the world. You will be a normal salaried employee in your country." And on hours: "This is a full-time position for 40 hours per week. Because we are a fully remote company, there is no fixed schedule. The team tries to schedule most meetings between 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM Central European Time (CET) to maximize collaboration across Europe and the Americas. While schedules are flexible, candidates should be comfortable occasionally adjusting their working hours to collaborate with teammates across time zones."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **VERIFIED YES, explicitly.** The posting location field names Canada directly, the JD requires only that you "be based in North America, Latin America, or Europe", and they name Canada as one of their "primary operating countries" with a stated Canadian salary band. They employ via an EOR as a normal salaried employee. This is the most clearly Canada-eligible company in the batch. **Timezone caveat:** meetings are targeted at 14:00–17:00 CET, which from Mountain Time is 06:00–09:00 — an early but survivable morning overlap, and they describe the schedule as otherwise unfixed with no core hours.

Engineering & tech: Backend in **Elixir** (production experience required), with TypeScript, and nice-to-haves in NestJS, Terraform, Grafana and Python. The team is named "Cloud & Infrastructure" and the work is squarely utility/plumbing: "Build and maintain backend services primarily using Elixir... Contribute to the design and implementation of scalable, high-performance, and reliable backend applications... Help operate and support production services, including troubleshooting issues, improving reliability, and participating in the operational ownership of cloud systems... Work on authentication, subscription, and payment-related services that power Home Assistant Cloud... Implement security and data protection best practices." Requirements include 5+ years backend, basic system administration, and "experience operating production systems, debugging live issues, and maintaining service reliability at scale", plus auth/identity and billing/subscription platform experience. Monitoring, observability and incident response are nice-to-haves.

Compensation (first-party, stated in the JD): Their philosophy, verbatim: "When first offering a position to a new member, Nabu Casa aims to provide a total compensation package that matches the 75th percentile for the new hire's role, seniority, and local market rates. For this role in our primary operating countries, the approximate yearly compensation will be the following: **Canada: 105,000 – 129,100 CAD**. Europe: Compensation will be determined based on the candidate's location, experience, and local market rates. Other countries: Compensation can be discussed during the first interview." Note this is stated as TOTAL compensation, not base.

Benefits (stated, and unusually generous for a small company): five weeks / 25 days paid time off; 14 days paid sick leave even where local law makes them unpaid; six weeks paid plus six weeks unpaid parental leave in the first year, topped up where local law falls short; a work-hardware budget which you keep after three years; an annual smart-home budget; 50% contribution to home internet; **"One day every two weeks to work on your personal projects"**; and paid work time for existing Home Assistant-related side projects.

Notable / other: Two open backend postings exist — one general and one explicitly "(LatAm based)" — suggesting deliberate geographic spread. The hiring process is published: apply → team + hiring manager review → screening → technical test → team interview → interview with CTO and CEO → offer. The open-source identity is not marketing: they fund an independent foundation, and the company's revenue model structurally aligns them with users rather than advertisers or investors.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Backend Engineer (Elixir) — Canada / US / EST, live; Backend Developer (LatAm based).
