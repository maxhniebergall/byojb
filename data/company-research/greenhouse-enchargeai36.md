# EnCharge AI — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:enchargeai36 | company_type: product

What they do: EnCharge AI designs **analog in-memory computing AI accelerators** — silicon plus the accompanying software stack — for edge-to-cloud AI workloads. In their own words, EnCharge is "a leader in advanced AI hardware and software systems for edge-to-cloud computing," whose "robust and scalable next-generation in-memory computing technology provides orders-of-magnitude higher compute efficiency and density compared to today's best-in-class solutions. The high-performance architecture is coupled with seamless software integration and will enable the immense potential of AI to be accessible in power, energy, and space constrained applications." The core bet is that performing computation inside memory using analog (charge-domain) techniques slashes the energy cost of inference, particularly for client, edge and power-constrained deployments. The company sells accelerators, not a SaaS product.

How they describe themselves: Framing is engineering- and mission-led — the work is about "making a difference" through innovation, driven by "AI's potential to serve humanity," with the goal of bringing "powerful AI solutions to everyone, everywhere." Stated core values: **integrity, growth, and collaboration**. They emphasize "groundbreaking R&D" that "redefines performance and efficiency," built on "innovative computation physics, ultra-efficient circuits, scalable architectures, and software." They describe themselves as "led by veteran technologists with backgrounds in semiconductor design and AI systems." A recruiting anecdote they publish: candidates repeatedly said their interviewers "genuinely seemed happy to be here at EnCharge and deeply passionate about the technology — more so than anywhere else they interviewed."

Size / stage / funding: Private, **launched 2022 as a spinout from Princeton University**, commercializing research led by CEO **Naveen Verma**; CTO is **Kailash Gopalakrishnan** (ex-IBM). Roughly **60 employees**. Closed an **oversubscribed $100M Series B in February 2025 led by Tiger Global**, with participation from Samsung Ventures and HH-CTBC; **total raised over $144M**. DARPA-backed. Well capitalized for its size, but pre-scale and hardware-capital-intensive.

Locations / HQ: **Silicon Valley HQ — Santa Clara, CA** (4500 Great America Pkwy, Ste 230; some sources also reference a San Jose office). **India office — Bengaluru, Karnataka.**

Remote policy: **Not stated on their careers pages.** The evidence is mixed and role-dependent: some roles are described as hybrid with an expectation of Bay Area presence, while the software-side reqs are posted with remote locations. As a silicon company with lab and bring-up dependencies, the hardware roles are necessarily onsite; the model-deployment and framework software roles are the ones posted remote.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes for this specific role, with a caveat.** The live "LLM Inference Deployment Engineer" req is located **"U.S.-Remote, Canada"** and its salary paragraph states the range in both currencies — "$180,000 to $240,000 USD ($175,000 to $245,000 CAD) per year" — which is direct evidence they intend to employ a Canada-based person in this role and have a CAD band for it. The caveat is that the same JD closes with "EnchargeAI is an equal employment opportunity employer **in the United States**," boilerplate that sits awkwardly against the stated Canada location; the employment entity (Canadian subsidiary vs employer-of-record) is unconfirmed. Note this Canada-eligibility is role-specific — most other EnCharge reqs are US-based or Bay Area hybrid.

Engineering & tech: Two distinct engineering worlds. The **hardware/silicon side** covers analog in-memory compute circuits, chip architecture, and physical design — live reqs include Principal Engineer and Staff/Senior Staff Static Timing Analysis (STA) Lead, plus embedded firmware and driver engineers. The **software/ML side** is a genuine ML-infrastructure stack: the LLM Inference Deployment Engineer role covers deploying and optimizing LLMs (GPT, LLaMA, Mistral, Falcon) post-training from HuggingFace; inference runtimes **ONNX Runtime and vLLM**; optimizing **batching, caching, and tensor parallelism** for scalability and low latency; and building high-performance inference pipelines with **Docker and Kubernetes**. Required expertise spans **PyTorch, ONNX Runtime, vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, DeepSpeed**, Python, containerized AI deployment (**Triton Inference Server, TensorFlow Serving, TorchServe**), LLM memory optimization for long-context, and real-time LLM applications (chat, code generation, RAG). Other software reqs include AI Compiler Engineers and Deployment & Framework Engineers — a compiler-and-runtime organization sitting between models and custom silicon.

Notable / other: The company is one of the most credible analog/in-memory compute startups, with real academic pedigree (Princeton), DARPA funding, and a tier-one Series B lead. The strategic risk is the one shared by all AI-silicon challengers: competing against NVIDIA's software moat, with revenue dependent on design wins and shipped silicon on multi-year cycles. Samsung Ventures' participation is a meaningful supply-chain signal. The Bengaluru office indicates a globally split engineering org.

Comp: The LLM Inference Deployment Engineer req states, first-party, "$180,000 to $240,000 USD ($175,000 to $245,000 CAD) per year," with actual offers "determined based on job-related knowledge, skills, and experience."

Open relevant roles (sample): LLM Inference Deployment Engineer (U.S.-Remote, Canada); AI Software Engineer; Principal Engineer, Static Timing Analysis (STA); Staff / Senior Staff Engineer: Static Timing Analysis (STA) Lead.
