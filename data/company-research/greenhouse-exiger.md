# Exiger — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:exiger | company_type: product

What they do: Exiger is a software and tech-enabled solutions company in supply-chain and third-party risk management. Its flagship platform, **1Exiger**, is an AI product that gives "instant visibility into complex supplier ecosystems, leveraging proprietary data and advanced AI to surface risk, automate compliance, and unlock efficiencies and cost savings." Customers are corporations, banks and government agencies — 550+ globally, including Fortune 500 companies and U.S. federal agencies. Exiger is a **FedRAMP-authorized** provider to the U.S. federal government, which shapes both its customer base and its security/compliance posture.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "to make the world a safer and more transparent place to succeed" (careers site: "make the world a safe and transparent place to succeed"). Their four stated core values, in their own words, are **Mission Inspired, Imagination Driven, Trust Anchored, Compassion Focused**. The careers site emphasizes "diversity of thought, inclusive and innovative problem solving". JD tone is moderately aspirational but not hype-driven: "If you are ambitious, eager to learn, and interested in developing great software solutions that help our customers, this position at Exiger has your name on it." No "fast-paced"/"hustle"/"rocketship" language; the JD does ask for the "ability to balance the quality of code and speed of delivery".

Size / stage / funding: Private. Headcount not disclosed — the careers page renders its employee-count and language-count statistics as "0" placeholders, so no figure could be verified. Established enough to be a Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader and to hold FedRAMP authorization, which implies substantial scale and a mature compliance function, but a specific headcount or funding stage is **unverified** (WebSearch was exhausted this session).

Locations / HQ: Offices in **McLean, Virginia; Richmond, Virginia; Jersey City, New Jersey; New York, New York**. The careers page states there are global offices but does not enumerate them or name an HQ. All four currently live engineering postings are anchored to the US East Coast sites.

Remote policy: **Hybrid, on-site anchored.** The Senior Software Engineer JD states explicitly: "Location: McLean, VA or Richmond, VA", "Work Environment: Hybrid", is tagged `#LI-Hybrid`, and closes with "This is a full-time hybrid opportunity in Richmond, Virginia". Benefits list "Flexible, hybrid approach to working from home and in the office where applicable". The careers landing page states no remote policy at all.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified no.** Every live engineering posting lists only US locations (McLean VA, Richmond VA, Jersey City NJ, New York NY), the roles are explicitly hybrid and tied to a named office, and neither the JDs nor the careers page mention Canada or Canadian hiring anywhere. The FedRAMP / U.S.-government customer base is a further structural reason to expect US-person and US-location requirements. A Kimberley, BC candidate cannot take these roles.

Engineering & tech: Java or another OO language (C, C++, C#, Python) is the core requirement — 6+ years. Microservices architecture and domain-driven design, API and backend service ownership, CI/CD pipeline management, process automation, feature testing. Messaging and format standards named: SOAP, REST, XML, JSON, HTML, GraphQL, RPC. Agile environment, cross-functional feature teams, 2+ years mentoring expected at senior level. Engineers "own features from conception to deployment" and maintain technical documentation. No public engineering blog or handbook was found.

Notable / other: Named a Leader in the Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Supplier Risk Management; twice selected for Fast Company's "Brands That Matter" (incl. 2024); recipient of the Third Party Risk Association's Innovator Award; 50+ awards cited overall. Benefits: discretionary time off with no maximum, health/vision/dental, 16 weeks fully paid parental leave, wellness stipends and programming, career-development programs with reimbursement for educational certifications.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (McLean VA / Richmond VA, hybrid); Backend Engineer (Jersey City NJ / McLean VA / Richmond VA); Platform Engineer (Jersey City NJ / McLean VA / New York NY / Richmond VA); Site Reliability Engineer (Jersey City NJ / McLean VA / Richmond VA).
