# Hootsuite — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:hootsuite | company_type: product

What they do: Hootsuite is a social media management SaaS platform ("a social media performance engine", founded 2008) covering social marketing/publishing, social listening, employee advocacy and social customer care. It serves "hundreds of thousands of customers globally", from SMBs to enterprises. Revenue is subscription SaaS; the product is customer-facing marketing software rather than infrastructure, though the internal platform/DevOps org is a real, separately-managed function.

- **One Team** — "Make Hootsuite a place where everyone feels safe, welcome, valued, and empowered to do their best work without compromising who they are. #OneTeam #FreeToBeMe"
- **Customer Obsessed** — "Focus relentlessly on helping our customers succeed."
- **Go Fast, Be Agile** — "Widen our competitive advantage by committing to speed and simplicity over perfection and complexity."
- **Play to Win** — "Commit to building an incredible, profitable company for our customers, our employees, and our stakeholders. #PlayToWin #NoExcuses"
- **Neighbours & Allies** — "Give back to our communities and be an ally. #SocialForGood #Allies"
Their JD "WHO YOU ARE" section adds behavioural expectations: solution seeker, lifelong learner, resilient adapter, intentional collaborator, critical challenger, active communicator, integrated thinker, accountable owner, "Bar-raiser: You step up to help your team grow and succeed, even when that means going beyond what might be expected." Tone is warm-corporate with mild velocity language ("Go Fast", "#NoExcuses").

Size / stage / funding: Not stated on the careers site and NOT VERIFIED here — the careers homepage gives no headcount. Established (founded 2008), long-standing private company; their own value statement refers to "building an incredible, profitable company", which is an aspiration statement rather than a disclosure of profitability. Treat stage/headcount as unknown.

Locations / HQ: Vancouver, British Columbia origin; the careers site references multiple global offices. Live Canadian postings sit in Vancouver BC, Toronto, Ottawa, Waterloo and Montreal.

Remote policy: Careers site: "a mix of in-person, hybrid, and remote opportunities" — teams near a global office typically work hybrid, while remote-only positions exist for people without a nearby office. The two BC engineering JDs are tagged `#LI-Remote` and say "Based out of British Columbia, you will report to the Senior Manager, Software Development, DevOps." Both mention "Participate in a flexible on-call rotation."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, and specifically British Columbia.** Both the Senior Platform Backend Developer and Senior Software Developer, DevOps JDs are `#LI-Remote`, located "Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada", state "Based out of British Columbia", and carry a "Canada Pay Range For This Role" of $115,400–$161,600 CAD. A BC-resident remote hire is exactly the stated shape of these roles. (Kimberley BC is Mountain Time while the team is Pacific — a one-hour offset in the candidate's favour.)

Engineering & tech: Backend Platform team uses **Go, Scala and PHP**, with Kafka, "various AWS services, and some infrastructure-as-code tools". Work described as "developing services and tools for our product development teams as well as modernizing our existing platform" — internal-platform/DevEx work: tools, libraries, automation, services, glue scripts; owning reliability/security/integrity of a large cloud-based platform; "Provide executable interfaces to our infrastructure platform"; diagnosing distributed systems; migrations; reducing alert noise and BAU. A separate AI Platform data-development team exists (Lead, Data Development, AI Platform).

Notable / other: They disclose AI use in recruiting — "Hootsuite uses artificial intelligence (AI) to support our recruitment process... All final hiring decisions are made by human decision-makers", with a Careers Privacy Policy at careers.hootsuite.com/privacy-policy/. Roles are posted bilingually (EN/FR) for Quebec-facing positions. Pay transparency is applied voluntarily to Canadian postings — bands are published on the JD itself.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Platform Backend Developer (Vancouver BC, remote); Senior Software Developer, DevOps (Vancouver BC, remote); Lead, Data Development, AI Platform (Ottawa/Toronto/Waterloo/Montreal).
