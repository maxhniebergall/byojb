# Trend Micro / TrendAI — research
Provider/key: workday:trendmicro/external | company_type: product
What they do: Trend Micro is a long-established global cybersecurity vendor. The postings in the registry are all for **TrendAI™**, which its own JDs describe as "the global AI security leader and enterprise business unit of Trend Micro", empowering "organizations with full AI visibility and consolidated security that inspires confidence, drives innovation, and eliminates risk." The product being built is **Trend Vision One™** (referred to in the JDs as "TrendAI Vision One") — a consolidated security platform. TrendAI states it "builds AI-powered cybersecurity solutions protecting millions of customers worldwide."
How they describe themselves: The JD boilerplate is the fullest statement available: "At TrendAI™, we're always seeking exceptional talent; people who want to collaborate with the best and push boundaries together. Here, your work goes beyond building a career. You will help protect what matters and play a vital role in shaping a safer, more trustworthy AI-powered future." Their tagline is **"AI Fearlessly."** The engineering culture is described in unusually specific terms: an explicit "AI-first approach to development" where engineers "are expected to utilize AI-enabled tools and coding assistants to accelerate feature delivery", maintaining "a high-velocity, AI-augmented development workflow", and where the senior expectation is to be "an expert in leveraging AI-enabled tools to accelerate the software development lifecycle (SDLC)". No corporate values page was fetched this session (search budget exhausted), so this is drawn from their own live JDs.
Size / stage / funding: Large, long-established, publicly traded cybersecurity vendor with a global customer base (its JDs claim "millions of customers worldwide"). Exact headcount and listing details not verified from a first-party page this session.
Locations / HQ: Global. Every posting captured in the registry is anchored to the **Ottawa, Ontario office at 40 Hines Rd, Ottawa, ON K2K 2M5**.
Remote policy: Hybrid with a hard floor, stated identically on all six postings: "This is a hybrid role with a minimum in-office requirement of 3 days per week in the Ottawa, ON office located at 40 Hines Rd, Ottawa, ON K2K 2M5."
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — verified from their own JD text. These are Canadian roles, but they are Ottawa-office roles requiring three days a week on site. That is not remote-Canada-eligible for someone in British Columbia who cannot relocate.
Engineering & tech: Substantive and modern. Backend: scalable microservices and event-driven architecture on AWS/Azure, cloud-native features with integrated monitoring and observability, APIs and data pipelines, relational and analytical data stores including Postgres, Infrastructure as Code, and automated testing with JUnit, Go testing libraries and Python frameworks. AI/ML: "sophisticated AI agent architectures, incorporating tool-calling capabilities, long-horizon reasoning, advanced memory management, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)"; multi-agent orchestration using "ReAct, Plan-and-Execute, and reflection loops"; LLM and SLM selection and fine-tuning balancing "inference latency, operational cost, and predictive accuracy"; end-to-end ML pipelines from ingestion and feature engineering through training, evaluation and deployment; inference optimization via "quantization, pruning, and TensorRT" and MLOps infrastructure; plus deep learning, time-series and Graph Neural Networks for threat detection. Named AI coding tools: Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot. Code review is explicitly framed around "validating AI-generated code for architectural fit, security vulnerabilities, and deep system context."
Notable / other: The Ottawa site is running a distinct sub-brand (TrendAI) with its own recruiting voice, and the roles are duplicated several times over in the ATS — three near-identical "Applied AI Backend Sr. Software Developer - Ottawa, ON" requisitions and two identical "Applied AI ML Engineer - Ottawa, ON" requisitions, suggesting a bulk build-out of the Ottawa AI team rather than five distinct jobs.
