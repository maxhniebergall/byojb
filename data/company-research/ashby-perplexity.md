# Perplexity — research
Provider/key: ashby:perplexity | company_type: product
What they do: Perplexity builds an AI-native answer engine and search products (including the Comet browser). Engineering owns a full search stack — crawler, index, search core — plus the LLM inference platform serving every query. From its own JD: "We build and run the inference engine behind every Perplexity query and deploy dozens of model architectures at scale with tight latency and cost budgets."
How they describe themselves: The JDs are engineering-dense rather than values-forward. Cultural asks are explicit: "You own problems end-to-end. You can read a research paper on Monday, write a kernel on Wednesday, and debug a production incident on Friday" and "Self-directed. You do well in fast-moving environments where the path forward isn't laid out for you." Compensation framing: "Final offer amounts are determined by multiple factors including experience and expertise. Equity: In addition to the base salary, equity may be part of the total compensation package." All engineering roles are flattened to "Member of Technical Staff".
Size / stage / funding: High-profile, heavily venture-funded AI company in rapid growth; valuation has escalated repeatedly through successive rounds. Private, not profitable, growth/funding-driven. Exact headcount and latest round were not verified in this session.
Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco. The 15 live relevant registry postings are anchored to London, and to Belgrade / London / Berlin trios — i.e. this portal slice is largely the European engineering footprint. No Canadian location appears.
Remote policy: No remote policy stated in the JDs; every posting names specific cities. Perplexity is publicly known for a strongly in-office, high-intensity working culture. Unverified beyond the city anchoring in the postings.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO evidence. All 15 relevant postings are city-anchored to London, Belgrade or Berlin, with no remote listing and no Canadian entity indicated. European hours are MT+7/9. Effectively disqualifying.
Engineering & tech: Inference platform in Rust, Python, CUDA and NVIDIA CuTe DSL; kernel migration to CuTe for GB200 and forward-portability to Vera Rubin; a Rust-native serving runtime; continuous batching, KV-cache management, request scheduling, API gateway; low-precision inference (INT8/FP8/FP4), speculative decoding, prefill-decode disaggregation; NCCL/NVLink/InfiniBand/RDMA and tensor/model parallelism; Nsight/CUDA-GDB/PTX profiling; Kubernetes GPU scheduling and autoscaling. Search side uses Rust and Golang (crawler, search core). Also cloud infrastructure, cloud/offensive security, and a data-flywheel team.
Notable / other: Pay is stated on many postings and is very high: the registry already holds a direct band of $250K-$485K USD base for platform_infra mid-senior (Inference & Training Platform), $200K-$400K for a software_general role, and an inferred $220K-$405K for security.
Open relevant roles (sample): MTS (AI Inference Engineer); MTS (AI Infrastructure Engineer); MTS (Backend/Infrastructure Engineer, Search); MTS (Machine Learning Engineer, Search); MTS (Rust Engineer, Search); MTS (Golang Engineer, Search).
