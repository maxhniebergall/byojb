# AIA Contract Documents (ACD) — research
Provider/key: ashby:acd | company_type: product

What they do: AIA Contract Documents publishes and sells the standard contract forms used across the US architecture, engineering and construction industry, plus the software that lets firms author, negotiate and manage them. In their own words: "For more than a century, AIA Contract Documents (ACD) has supported architecture, engineering, and construction professionals by delivering a shared industry standard to align parties on a project. What began in 1888 with the development of standardized construction contracts has evolved into a comprehensive suite of contract tools and foundational workflows." It is the commercial documents arm associated with the American Institute of Architects — an entity identity confirmed by the JD text itself, which matches the registry name.

How they describe themselves: Positioning is deliberately anti-hype and stability-first: "In a world driven by scale, fragmentation, and AI-generated decisions that prioritize speed over a clear understanding of risk, ACD serves as a trusted anchor, ensuring project participants can reduce disputes and negotiations, while achieving faster alignment and more predictable outcomes for the future."
