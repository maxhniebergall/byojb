# Nubank — research
Provider/key: ashby:nubank | company_type: product

What they do: One of the world's largest digital banks. Founded in Brazil in 2013, Nubank (Nu Holdings, NYSE: NU) offers credit cards, deposit accounts, personal loans, insurance, investments and a payments product (NuPay) to well over 100 million customers across **Brazil, Mexico and Colombia**. Built branchless and mobile-first, its original thesis was undercutting the incumbent Brazilian banking oligopoly on fees and service. Product lines visible in the current openings: Credit Cards and Credit Card Core Platforms, NuPay, Payments Gateway, Investments, Insurance, Marketing Platforms, and a business-customer (PJ) segment.

How they describe themselves: Engineering-heavy and platform-oriented — postings are organized around long-lived internal platforms (Credit Card Core Platforms, Payments Gateway, Marketing Platforms, Data) rather than feature squads, and the ladder is deep: Senior → Staff → Senior Staff → Lead → Principal. Benefits named on the JDs include **NuLanguage** (language-learning program), **NuCare** (mental-health and wellness assistance) and vacations. Compensation is stated as "Total compensation includes base salary, RSUs and benefits."

Work model — stated verbatim on the JDs: "**Hybrid 2–3 times/week: Our hybrid work model brings us to the office at least twice a week, on strategic days designed to maximize team connection and collaboration.**" (documented at https://building.nubank.com/nu-hybrid-work-model/). Roles are further pinned: "This role is based on a hybrid model at one of our Nubank offices in Brazil (São Paulo, Campinas, Rio de Janeiro, or Belo Horizonte), the United States (Palo Alto or Miami), or Canada (Toronto)."

Size / stage / funding: Publicly listed on the NYSE (Nu Holdings, ticker NU) since December 2021; profitable and among the most valuable financial institutions in Latin America. Tens of thousands of employees. Berkshire Hathaway has been a notable investor. Very stable — this is a large, profitable public company, not a startup.

Locations / HQ: HQ São Paulo, Brazil. Engineering offices in São Paulo, Campinas, Belo Horizonte and Rio de Janeiro (Brazil); Palo Alto and Miami plus Washington DC and Durham (US); and **Toronto (Canada)**. Also Mexico City and Bogotá for the regional businesses.

Remote policy: **Hybrid, not remote.** Minimum two days per week in a named office, described as "strategic days" for in-person collaboration. There is no fully-remote track in evidence for engineering.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — effectively disqualifying for a Kimberley, BC candidate.** Nubank does have a Canadian entity and a Toronto engineering office, and several roles are Toronto-based with CAD compensation — but every one of them is **hybrid, requiring physical presence in the Toronto office at least twice a week**. That is a relocation requirement, not a remote role, and relocation is off the table. Toronto is also Eastern time, two hours ahead of Mountain. **These roles should not be treated as Canada-remote-eligible**; the Canadian location on the posting reflects an office address, not remote hiring.

Engineering & tech: Nubank is well known for a **Clojure + Datomic** functional-programming stack running a large microservices estate on AWS/Kubernetes, with Kafka for eventing and substantial data and ML platforms — genuinely large-scale, high-integrity financial systems work with strong engineering culture and public conference presence. Roles span core platform, SRE, data engineering and ML engineering. Postings note that "Our recruitment process may involve the use of artificial intelligence–enabled tools, such as automated interview transcription and analysis," with final decisions made by human reviewers.

Compensation (stated on their own Toronto JDs): Staff Software Engineer | Credit Cards — "Toronto base salary range (CAD): $192.000 - $288.000" (the same JD lists "Palo Alto base salary range (USD): $230.400 - $288.000"). Senior Software Engineer (Toronto) — "Base salary range: $147,996 - $185,004". Total compensation additionally includes RSUs.

Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Software Engineer - Credit Card Core Platforms (Palo Alto/Toronto/Brazil/US); Staff Software Engineer | Credit Cards (Toronto and others); Staff Software Engineer (São Paulo et al.); Senior Software Engineer (Toronto); Tech Lead Software Engineer (Toronto); Staff Software Engineer, Data; Staff Site Reliability Engineer; Staff Software Engineer | NuPay; Staff Software Engineer - Payments Gateway.
