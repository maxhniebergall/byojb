# Aera Technology — research
Provider/key: lever:aeratechnology | company_type: product

What they do: Aera Technology sells "decision intelligence" / agentic enterprise-decision software. Its platform, Aera Decision Cloud, "unifies data, analytics, AI, and automation to optimize decisions across the enterprise" — digitizing, augmenting and automating decision-making across supply chain, procurement, finance, sales and HR for large enterprises. Its own JD claims Leader placement in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for 2026 (their claim, not independently verified here). Customers are described as "the best-known brands in the world" / market leaders; effectively a large-enterprise B2B SaaS vendor.

How they describe themselves: They frame decision intelligence as a new category and want it to become "the operating system of global enterprises," enabling organizations to "anticipate change, act with confidence, and create lasting value" in the AI era. Employees are branded "Aeranauts." The JD closes with "If you share our passion for building a sustainable, intelligent, and efficient world, you're in the right place… So join us, and let's build this!" No explicit list of company values was found on the pages fetched — the tone is category-creation marketing rather than a values/handbook culture. The one culture claim that is concrete: "We believe in a flexible working environment, to allow our Aeranauts to perform at their best, ensuring a healthy work-life balance."

Size / stage / funding: Global team of over 400 (their JD, "and we're growing"). Privately held and VC-funded; the JD self-describes as "a series C start-up," founded 2017. Investors listed on their own site: NEA, Georgian Partners, NewView Capital, DFJ Growth, Silver Lake Waterman. No funding total, valuation or profitability disclosed on the pages fetched.

Locations / HQ: HQ Mountain View, California. Offices per the JD: Mountain View and San Francisco (CA), Bucharest and Cluj-Napoca (Romania), Paris (France), Munich (Germany), London (UK), Pune (India), Sydney (Australia). Their site's office list additionally includes Singapore and York (UK). Notably no Canadian office in either list. Heavy engineering presence in Romania and India.

Remote policy: No company-wide remote policy statement was found (unverified). Benefits language mentions "a flexible working environment" and describes what you get "when you're working from the office" (fully-stocked kitchen), which reads as office-anchored-with-flexibility rather than remote-first. The one live relevant posting is, however, listed "Remote US, USA."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No, on the available evidence.** The Senior Software Engineer (CALC engine) posting is scoped "Remote US, USA" and the posted range is USD. Aera has no Canadian entity or office in any list they publish. Nothing found indicates they employ Canada-based remote workers.

Engineering & tech: The relevant role is deep systems work — a complex Java-based high-performance OLAP calculation engine. It is a multi-dimensional calculation engine with its own modelling language, where "the model declaration is dynamically compiled into efficient runtime objects." Required depth: strong Core Java (threading, allocation, garbage collection, performance tuning, low-latency Java), understanding of JVM internals, and domain knowledge in database engines, compiler design, or investment-bank front office. Nice-to-haves: language design, Spring basics, multi-dimensional modelling, Lambda Architecture, planning software. Stated engineering ethos for this team is "correctness-first," with quality weighted equally against calculation performance. They explicitly expect use of AI tools, naming Claude Code.

Notable / other: The CALC-engine team is an unusually specialized pocket inside an AI/automation product company — the surrounding company narrative is agentic AI for enterprise decisions, but this specific role is compiler/database-engine work. Benefits: competitive salary plus company stock options, comprehensive medical/vision/dental, 401K, flexible PTO, generous parental leave.
