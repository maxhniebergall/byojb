# Cboe — research
Provider/key: workday:cboe/external_career_cboe | company_type: product

What they do: Cboe Global Markets is a publicly traded (Cboe: CBOE) operator of securities and derivatives exchanges. It runs options, equities, futures, FX and digital-asset markets across North America, Europe and Asia-Pacific, and is best known as the home of the VIX index and SPX options. Alongside the exchanges it sells market data and analytics. Engineering here is exchange infrastructure: matching engines, market-data distribution, clearing/billing systems and the surrounding platform.

How they describe themselves: The JDs emphasise partnership and in-person team connection above all — the phrase "To support strong partnership and team connection, this role follows a four day in office work model" appears verbatim on essentially every posting. Benefits language is conventional corporate: "Fair and competitive salary and incentive compensation packages with an upside for overachievement," and a "competitive, transparent, and market-informed total rewards program." Their Europe arm is described as operating "in a flexible and hybrid work environment, with our teams working regularly from our offices in London and Amsterdam." No distinctive values manifesto was reachable without spending fetches on a company that is geographically disqualifying.

Size / stage / funding: Large, public, profitable exchange operator; long-established and not runway-dependent. Exact headcount not verified here.

Locations / HQ: HQ Chicago, IL. Engineering roles in this batch sit in Chicago IL, Overland Park KS, New York, London UK and Amsterdam NL.

Remote policy: Explicitly and uniformly office-based — a stated four-day-per-week in-office model on every posting read. Cboe Europe is described as "flexible and hybrid" but still office-anchored to London and Amsterdam.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — verified from the JDs. Every US posting carries a four-day in-office requirement plus "This position is not eligible for visa sponsorship. Candidates must be legally authorized to work in the United States without the need for employer sponsorship now or in the future." The remaining roles are London-based. Nothing in this portal is workable from British Columbia.

Engineering & tech: Roles span Python engineering (London), site reliability, build-and-release management, cloud engineering, infrastructure engineering, an AI platform team (Chicago or Overland Park), and a Principal Software Engineer for Data and Billing QA who "help[s] lead QA activities for our billing platform, where we shadow and validate over $1B in annual revenue" and works on "streaming data testing, agentic coding workflows, and AI-driven tools." Real low-level, mission-critical, high-reliability engineering — the sort of "boring, mission-critical domain" work that is otherwise attractive.

Notable / other: Pay is disclosed on US postings under transparency law. Observed base ranges: Senior AI Platform Engineer $119,000-$169,400; Infrastructure Engineer $131,750-$170,500; Senior Cloud Engineer $119,000-$154,000 (Kansas) / $130,900-$169,400 (Chicago) / $148,750-$192,500 (New York); Principal Software Engineer - Data and Billing QA and Sr Software Engineer, Build and Release Management both $110,500-$143,000. London roles state no range but mention a Cboe-funded pension contribution of up to 7% of base with no employee contribution required.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior AI Platform Engineer; Infrastructure Engineer; Senior Cloud Engineer; Engineer - Site Reliability (London); Principal Python Software Engineer (London); Sr Software Engineer, Build and Release Management.

Sources: JD bodies for the nine live postings under https://cboe.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/external_career_cboe (entity confirmed as Cboe Global Markets from the JD text, including the VIX/options and billing-platform references).
