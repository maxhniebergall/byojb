# General Dynamics Mission Systems — Canada — research
Provider/key: smartrecruiters:gdmsi | company_type: product (defence prime / systems integrator)
**Entity note:** the registry name is "General Dynamics Missions System International", but the JD text names the employer as **"General Dynamics Mission Systems-Canada"**. Same corporate family, but the registry label is wrong and should be corrected.
What they do: Defence electronics and mission systems for the Canadian Armed Forces. The postings describe delivering "Intelligence, Reconnaissance, Target Acquisition and Surveillance" (ISTAR) capabilities to the Canadian Army under the LC4ISR programme — "a fully integrated tactical network, capable of providing flexible, multi-role and combat-capable communications, command and control." Other roles cover Air & Naval Sonar and Canadian Air programmes.
How they describe themselves: The postings are programme-descriptive rather than values-led; no mission/values statement was reachable. The stated way of working is Agile/Scrum: "Participate in all Agile processes: sprint planning, backlog refinement, stand-ups, reviews, and retrospectives", and engineers are expected to "write clean, testable, maintainable code against established coding standards — and enforce those standards in peer code reviews." Notably absent from the JD language: any hustle, "fast-paced" or growth framing.
Size / stage / funding: Subsidiary of General Dynamics (NYSE: GD), a large publicly traded US defence prime. Not runway-dependent; multi-year government programme funding. Exact Canadian headcount unverified.
Locations / HQ: Canadian sites across Ottawa/Nepean ON, Calgary AB, and Cole Harbour NS (Halifax area). Ottawa is the centre of gravity for the ISTAR work.
Remote policy: **Onsite.** The ISTAR JD states plainly: "This role requires on-site work at our Ottawa office and customer locations within the National Capital Region, with flexibility to work remotely when necessary. Occasional travel may be required to support customer field trials."
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — disqualifying.** Canada-based employment is obviously fine (it is a Canadian entity), but every relevant posting is anchored to a specific office (Ottawa, Calgary, Cole Harbour) with mandatory on-site presence and customer-site work. There is no remote-from-BC path. Classified/Controlled Goods work is structurally incompatible with fully-remote employment.
Engineering & tech: Java-heavy enterprise and desktop engineering — "Java 8+; Java 17 preferred", "Java 2EE/Jakarta EE fundamentals: lifecycle management, dependency injection, concurrency, and JMS", "MVVM or MVC in a Java desktop context (JavaFX, Swing, or equivalent)", message buses (ZeroMQ, ActiveMQ, RabbitMQ), protobuf/JSON/REST, SDK and Interface Control Document (ICD) design. Explicitly "desktop or embedded Java applications (not exclusively web/microservices)". Typical ask is 5-8 years' experience plus a technical bachelor's degree.
Security requirements: A hard gate. "It is a requirement that General Dynamics Mission Systems-Canada be registered with the Canadian Controlled Goods program and that all of its workforce be security assessed. Successful applicants must meet all applicable security requirements, including but not limited to the ability to obtain and maintain a Canadian government security clearance."
Notable / other: The domain is the definition of "boring and mission-critical" — long programme cycles, defect discipline, field trials, no product-market-fit churn. Also runs co-op programmes (Co-op Fall 2026 - Software Developer - 8 Months, Cole Harbour).
Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineer (Ottawa); Software Engineering Developer, Air & Naval Sonar (Ottawa); Senior ISTAR Software Engineer (Nepean); Software Engineers, Canadian Air (Ottawa / Cole Harbour); Software Engineering Developer, DevOps (Ottawa); Software Engineering, Developer (Calgary).
