# Masabi — research
Provider/key: ashby:masabi | company_type: product

What they do: Masabi builds Justride, a Fare Payments-as-a-Service platform for public transport. Transit agencies use it for mobile ticketing, barcode and smartcard ticketing, account-based ticketing and open/contactless bank-card payments ("tap to ride"), plus Mobility-as-a-Service integrations. It is deployed with roughly 150-200 transit agencies and operators across 9+ countries; named deployments include the MBTA (Boston), San Joaquin RTD and Transdev Blazefield. Millions of passengers transact on it. They also acquired Passenger, another transit software firm.

How they describe themselves: They position themselves as "leading the movement away from closed legacy fare collection systems", enabling "connected journeys for all", and describe Justride as "a robust, secure, and reliable Account-Based Ticketing platform". Careers messaging emphasises building a tangible product in "a trusting environment that empowers employees to develop their own career path within an open, supportive culture that encourages two-way communication", and describes the team as "a network of innovators from all walks of life; a group of passionate individuals who consistently deliver."

Size / stage / funding: Roughly 51-200 employees (third-party listings). Private, London-founded, has been operating since the late 2000s with 10+ years in fare payments; not a seed-stage company. Funding/ownership detail is not published on their own site.

Locations / HQ: HQ London, UK. Additional presence in Boston, USA; hires remotely in the UK and Canada. Their own benefits copy mentions the office shuts between Christmas and New Year.

Remote policy: Remote-friendly with location-scoped remote roles rather than global remote. Their live Principal Engineer postings are tagged "London - Remote" and "Canada - East Coast". Benefits stated on the Canadian JD are quoted in CAD: 20 days vacation plus public holidays (28+ days total with the Christmas shutdown), CAD$1,300/yr training allowance, CAD$325/yr home-office allowance, CAD$50/month team-building.

Remote-Canada eligibility: VERIFIED YES for Canada, but geographically restricted. Their Principal Engineer JD states verbatim: "This role is available in a fully remote model for candidates based in Canada within Central or Eastern time zones." A Canadian entity and CAD-denominated benefits clearly exist. British Columbia / Mountain Time is explicitly outside the stated eligible zone.

Engineering & tech: Cloud-native SaaS platform (Justride) with account-based ticketing, payments processing, validators and agency-facing tooling. Roles on offer are Principal Engineer and Principal Engineer (Payments) — deep, well-scoped senior IC positions rather than generalist ones. No public engineering handbook or blog beyond blog.masabi.com marketing content.

Notable / other: The domain is unusually stable and "boring" in the good sense — public-transit fare collection is mission-critical, regulated, long-contract infrastructure with agency (not consumer-growth) customers. Neither Principal Engineer posting states pay.

Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Engineer (Canada - East Coast); Principal Engineer (Payments) (London - Remote).
