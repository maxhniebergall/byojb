# Newrich Network — research

Provider/key: workable:newrich-network | company_type: product

**What they do:** Newrich Network builds a creator-economy platform: SaaS tooling plus e-learning and a "social learning platform" for business, health and wellness **coaches** to run their creator businesses and grow their audiences. Their words: "we are building the next generation creator platform and software tools to not only enable business, health, and wellness coaches to more efficiently and cost effectively run their creator business — but also to grow their audiences with improved visibility and algorithmic ranking on our social learning platform." The product is described as "a one-stop ecosystem designed for creators, entrepreneurs, and dreamers."

**How they describe themselves:** Heavily mission-rhetorical and aspirational. "Come and Join The New Rich!" The overview frames the company against "the economic inequality crisis… one of the most defining challenges of our time", asserts "the old institutions that we all used to depend on are slowly crumbling — and a new way of life is taking its place", and states "we believe that education, inspiration, and motivation are the only way to create economic opportunity, alleviate poverty and end economic suffering for everyone." On team and culture: "Our team spans the Americas and Europe… We're outdoor enthusiasts, musicians, artists, athletes, parents, and adventurers — 4 nationalities strong and growing"; "We're a **fast-growing** team driven by one vision: a world where anyone can work remotely, chase their passion, and live life on their own terms." The engineering roles are pitched as culture-shaping and generalist: "a hands-on role for someone who **thrives in a dynamic environment** and takes ownership of getting things done… This role is not just about shaping our product — it's also about **shaping our culture** as well."

**Size / stage / funding:** Small. "4 nationalities strong and growing" and "fast-growing team" are the only size signals; no headcount, funding stage, investor or profitability information is given, and none was verified. Assume early-stage.

**Locations / HQ:** No HQ stated. Team "spans the Americas and Europe." Postings are tagged Toronto, Canada (Hybrid); Austin, United States (Remote); and United States (Remote).

**Remote policy:** Mixed and country-locked. Each posting carries a hard gate: **"Please note: Only candidates who reside within the country of the advertised role will be considered."** The two Canadian postings (Senior Software Engineer, AWS DevOps Engineer) are explicitly `Workplace: hybrid` in **Toronto**; the US postings are `remote` within the US.

**Remote-Canada eligibility: Effectively no — and this is the key finding.** Canada *is* a hiring country, but the Canadian roles are **Toronto hybrid**, requiring physical presence in Toronto. The fully-remote roles are US-resident-only ("Only candidates who reside within the country of the advertised role"). A Kimberley, BC candidate who cannot relocate qualifies for neither: **not remote-Canada eligible despite the Canadian postings.**

**Engineering & tech:** Full-stack generalist. Stated stack: **PHP 8.2 / Laravel 10**, Node.js, Golang, and a React front end. The senior/staff roles are explicitly cross-cutting — "contribute across the product — building new features, improving existing systems, and helping the team work more efficiently." A separate AWS DevOps Engineer role covers the infrastructure side. No engineering blog, handbook, architecture detail or async-work statement appears in any posting.

**Notable / other:** Registry data quality note — the two "Senior Software Engineer" postings (Toronto hybrid and Austin remote) carry near-identical body text, and the Toronto "Senior Software Engineer" posting's description actually reads "We are looking for a **Staff** Software Engineer", so titles and bodies are copy-pasted between listings. No compensation is posted on any role.

**Open relevant roles (sample):** Senior Software Engineer (Toronto, Canada — Hybrid); AWS DevOps Engineer (Toronto, Canada — Hybrid); Staff Software Engineer (United States — Remote); Senior Software Engineer (Austin, United States — Remote).
