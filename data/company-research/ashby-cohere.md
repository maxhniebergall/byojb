# Cohere — research

Provider/key: ashby:cohere | company_type: product

What they do: Cohere is a Canadian frontier-AI company building foundation models and enterprise AI
products. In their own words: "Cohere is the leading security-first enterprise AI company. We build
cutting-edge foundation AI models and end-to-end products that are designed to solve real-world
business problems. We're training and deploying frontier models for enterprises who are building AI
systems." Product lines: **workplace systems** — North (enterprise AI/agent platform) and Compass
(intelligent search and discovery); **generative models** — Command, Transcribe (speech
recognition), North Mini Code (coding model); **retrieval models** — Embed (multimodal search) and
Rerank (semantic search). Their positioning is deliberately enterprise and deployment-flexible
(on-prem, VPC, "hybrid/multi-cloud deployments", "sovereign AI" for national governments) rather
than consumer chatbot. Customers span technology, financial services, healthcare, manufacturing,
energy, public sector and telecommunications.

How they describe themselves: Mission — **"scale intelligence and serve humanity."** Three stated
values on their careers page:
- **Momentum**: "We move quickly, trust each other, and push forward with purpose."
- **Openness**: "We embrace inclusivity and welcome diverse perspectives."
- **Autonomy**: "We support every team member with the flexibility and ownership they need."

From their JDs: "We obsess over what we build. Each one of us is responsible for contributing to
increasing the capabilities of our models and the value they drive for our customers. Cohere is a
team of researchers, engineers, designers, and more, who are all passionate about their craft."
They describe themselves as "a global technology company co-headquartered in Toronto and San
Francisco." Roles are titled "Member of Technical Staff" (MTS) in the frontier-lab convention.

Size / stage / funding: Founded **2019 in Toronto**. Private. Raised **nearly $1 billion across
four rounds (Series A–D) between 2021 and 2024**; specific valuation not disclosed on their own
pages. Headcount not disclosed. Leadership: CEO/co-founder **Aidan Gomez** (a co-author of the
Transformer paper), co-founders Nick Frosst and Ivan Zhang; **Joelle Pineau** as Chief AI Officer,
**Phil Blunsom** as CTO, François Chadwick as CFO — an unusually heavyweight research bench.
Revenue and profitability not disclosed.

Locations / HQ: **Co-headquartered Toronto and San Francisco.** Offices in Toronto, San Francisco,
New York City, London, Montreal, Paris and Seoul, with "more coming soon." Founded in Canada and
still substantially Canadian — most engineering postings list Toronto and Montreal among their
locations.

Remote policy: **Remote-friendly rather than remote-first.** Stated verbatim on their JDs: "Cohere
is remote-friendly. We have offices in Toronto, San Francisco, New York City, London, Paris,
Montreal, and more coming soon. For those in the office: a daily lunch program, plenty of snacks,
and regular community and social events. **For those not near an office: a co-working benefit so
you can work alongside others in your city.** Everyone receives a $500 home office stipend to set
up your workspace properly." The careers page adds that employees "can work from wherever they're
most productive" and that remote staff get meal stipends and a monthly co-working benefit. There is
a "budget for traveling to other offices if you are remote, plus an annual company offsite." No
stated days-per-week in-office requirement was found; every occurrence of "hybrid" in their JDs is
technical ("hybrid serving", "hybrid/multi-cloud deployments"), not a work-location policy.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, strongly.** Cohere is a Canadian company with a Canadian
entity: JDs state a separate "For candidates in Canada, the Compensation Range is: $165,000 -
$460,000 [CAD]" alongside the US range, and benefits include **RRSP matching**. Several relevant
roles are posted with country-level (not city-level) Canadian locations — "Member of Technical
Staff - Sovereign AI" (Canada, Toronto); "Software Engineer, GPU Infrastructure (HPC)" (Canada,
United States); "Senior/Staff Software Engineer, Developer Experience" (London, Canada, United
States); "Software Engineer, Adoption" and "Software Engineer, Agents & Automations" (London,
Europe, Canada, United States); "Member of Technical Staff, Search" (United States, San Francisco,
Europe, Canada, London, New York, United Kingdom, Toronto, Montreal); "Senior Software Engineer,
Agent Infrastructure" (Toronto, Canada, United States); "Data Engineer, Data Foundations" (New
York, Canada, United States); "Software Engineer, Data Infrastructure" and "Software Engineer,
North for Finance" also list Canada. Caveat: many other roles list only cities (Toronto, Montreal,
SF, NY, London, Paris), and Cohere is remote-*friendly* rather than remote-first — a
non-office-city Canadian hire is plausible and supported by the co-working/travel benefits, but the
roles listing "Canada" as a whole are the safest targets. Timezone: Toronto/Montreal centre of
gravity means Eastern hours, workable from Mountain Time.

Engineering & tech: A genuine mix of frontier research and hard infrastructure. The infrastructure
side is substantial: **Agent Infrastructure** — "the next generation of agentic AI infrastructure…
This team sits at the intersection of ML systems, distributed infrastructure, and developer
experience, creating the platform that powers autonomous AI agents at scale. You'll work on hard,
forward-looking problems with few established patterns, including secure code execution, agent
state management, model routing, identity and authentication, and resource management for
long-running agent workflows." Named workstreams there: "secure execution environments for
agent-generated code", "identity, authentication, and trust boundaries for agents", "model routing
and orchestration across different model types and environments", "rate limiting, quotas, and
resource management for agent workflows", "state management, memory, and filesystem abstractions
for agents". Skills sought: "production ML infrastructure with strong systems fundamentals",
"scalable, fault-tolerant distributed systems and Kubernetes", "model routing and LLM provider
frameworks". Other infra teams: Inference Infrastructure (including an SRE role and a Staff SWE
role), GPU Infrastructure (HPC), Training Infra, Training Performance, Model Efficiency, Data
Infrastructure, Developer Experience, and Security (including "Safety and Security for Agents" and
"Security Agents"). The research side covers Pre-Training Data, Post-Training, Modeling,
Multilingual, Multimodal, Synthetic Data, Web Data, Search, and RL/Integration. Bridge role: "Turn
emerging ML research ideas into production-ready infrastructure… Partner with research teams to
shape infrastructure based on what future agent systems will need."

Notable / other: Benefits are among the strongest seen in this queue — "6 weeks of paid vacation
(30 working days!)", "100% Parental Leave top-up for up to 6 months, for either parent", "full
health and dental benefits, including a separate budget for mental health", RRSP matching / 401K /
pension scheme depending on geography, a "$75/£75 or equivalent" weekly lunch stipend, annual
enrichment benefits (arts & culture, fitness/wellness, quality time, workspace improvement credit),
an education/learning stipend for conferences, courses and coaching, and a $500 home-office
stipend. Recruiting notes: they disclose that "we may use AI-enabled tools to screen and assess
applicants against the criteria for this position," and warn about recruitment scams (legitimate
roles only on the Cohere careers page and LinkedIn; emails only from @cohere.com or @cw.cohere).
Interview process: application review → recruiter conversation → possible take-home → hiring
manager → team interviews, conducted by video conference.

Compensation (posted, first-party): Cohere posts dual US/Canada ranges with the statement "Cohere
is committed to fair and transparent pay practices. The salary range listed for this role reflects
the expected base compensation. Actual compensation offered will be determined by factors such as
location, level, job-related knowledge, skills, education, and experience." On the "Member of
Technical Staff, Multilingual" JD: **US $110,000 – $370,000 USD; Canada $165,000 – $460,000 CAD**
base. These ranges are extremely wide (2.8x) because the MTS title spans multiple levels, so they
function as a company-wide envelope rather than a level band. Other bands already on record from
Cohere's own JDs: Senior/Staff Software Engineer, Developer Experience — $180,000–$250,000 CAD;
Software Engineer, GPU Infrastructure (HPC) — $127,000–$336,000 CAD. The great majority of Cohere's
relevant postings state no range at all.
