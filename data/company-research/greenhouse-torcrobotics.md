# Torc Robotics — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:torcrobotics | company_type: product

What they do: Torc Robotics builds autonomous driving software for long-haul Class 8 freight trucks —
perception, motion planning, learned planning/RL, calibration, vehicle diagnostics, the on-vehicle
operating system, plus the cloud/data platform (simulation, autonomy data pipelines, release
metrics, cloud automation) that supports it. It is a subsidiary of Daimler Truck (this is the
company's well-known status; not re-verified from a fetched first-party page this session), which
matters because it means the capital behind it is a large industrial parent rather than a
venture runway.

How they describe themselves: In their own JD words the culture line is "Our culture is
collaborative, energetic, and team focused", and employees are called "Torc'rs" ("Perks of Being a
Full-time Torc'r"). No values/principles page was fetched this session, so the full stated values set
is UNVERIFIED.

Size / stage / funding: Established autonomous-trucking programme with a large multi-site engineering
org (18 postings captured in the registry, 4 currently live and relevant). Headcount and exact
financials unverified this session.

Locations / HQ: US offices in Blacksburg VA, Ann Arbor MI and Fort Worth TX; a Canadian office in
Montreal, Quebec. JDs state office work is "in a hybrid capacity" for those who are local.

Remote policy: Hybrid for office-local employees, plus a genuine remote track. Standard JD wording:
"For this position, we are open to hiring in [office] work locations in a hybrid capacity. We are
also open to hiring Remote in the United States." Most postings say Remote in the United States only.

Remote-Canada eligibility: PARTIAL — verified yes on at least one live req, no on most. The live
"Software Engineer II, Calibration" JD (8503434002) states verbatim: "we are open to hiring in either
the Torc Montreal, Quebec (Canada) or Ann Arbor, MI (U.S.) office work locations in a hybrid
capacity. We are also open to hiring Remote in the United States or Canada", and the JD carries a
separate "Perks of Being a Full-time Torc'r (Canada)" benefits section — so a Canadian remote
employment entity demonstrably exists. However the senior ML and staff roles (3D Perception,
End-to-End, Learned Planning/RL, Motion Planning, Metrics for Release) all say "Remote in the United
States" with no Canada clause. JD text is written "exclusively in English" and the Canadian site is
in Quebec. Timezone is Eastern-centric (Ann Arbor / Montreal / Blacksburg), i.e. Mountain Time means
an early start.

Engineering & tech: Autonomy stack — 3D perception, end-to-end ML, learned planning and
reinforcement learning, motion planning in unstructured environments, sensor calibration, device
drivers, vehicle diagnostics, the autonomy operating system. Supporting platform work: cloud
automation, release pipelines, release metrics implementation, autonomy simulation and data
engineering (Python/C++). Senior ML roles are research-adjacent applied ML on safety-critical
systems.

Notable / other: Torc posts explicit USD ranges on its own JDs under a "Hiring Range for Job Opening"
block — a strong pay-transparency signal. Observed live ranges: Senior ML Engineer (End-to-End, and
Learned Planning/RL) $226,400-$271,700 USD; Senior ML Engineer 3D Perception $177,300-$212,800 USD.
Ranges are described as part of "a competitive compensation package, in addition to a full range of
medical, financial, and/or other benefits". Note that these are US ranges; the Canadian entity's
bands are not published.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior, Machine Learning Engineer - 3D Perception; Senior, Machine
Learning Engineer - End-to-End; Senior Machine Learning Engineer - Learned Planning/Reinforcement
Learning; Senior Software Engineer - Metrics for Release, Implementation; Senior Software Engineer -
New AI Initiatives; Software Engineer II, Calibration (Remote - Canada / Montreal); Staff, Software
Engineer - Motion Planning.
