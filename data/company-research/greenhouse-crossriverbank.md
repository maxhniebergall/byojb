# Cross River — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:crossriverbank | company_type: product
What they do: Cross River is a US bank and fintech infrastructure provider. In their own words: "Cross River builds the infrastructure behind the world's most innovative financial products. Our technology and capital solutions power payments, cards, lending, and digital asset capabilities that move money safely, instantly, and inclusively — trusted by leading fintechs, enterprises, and disruptors across the globe." It is the sponsor bank behind many well-known fintech lending and payments programs, and it also runs crypto/digital-asset capabilities.
How they describe themselves: Mission: "to build the financial infrastructure that expands access and opportunity for all." Stated core values are five: humility, integrity, creativity, compassion, and responsibility. Culture framing: "Guided by a culture of collaboration, curiosity, and purpose... where innovation and integrity drive everything we do." Their JD also asks for someone who "thrives in a collaborative, fast-paced environment."
Size / stage / funding: 900+ employees across 36 US states and 2 countries. Founded 2008. Private, and a chartered bank rather than a burn-rate startup — a genuinely stable, regulated balance sheet.
Locations / HQ: HQ Fort Lee, New Jersey. A significant development team in Jerusalem, Israel. Offices throughout the United States.
Remote policy: Mixed — "both in-office and remote work options"; the Israel team is on a "flexible hybrid work model" and the New Jersey HQ is amenity-heavy. Notably, the live "Senior Software Engineer" requisition is listed as location "Remote" yet is tagged #LI-Hybrid in the JD body, which suggests the remote label is not absolute.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO evidence of it, and the balance of evidence is against. The two countries they employ in are the United States and Israel. As a US-chartered bank the compliance surface for foreign remote employment is high. Nothing on the careers page or in any JD mentions Canada. Treat as not Canada-eligible unless they say otherwise.
Engineering & tech: .NET 10 and C# (8+ years asked), PostgreSQL and SQL Server, nHibernate / Entity Framework, RabbitMQ, Docker/containers, AWS. Domain-Driven Design, distributed systems and event-driven architecture with MassTransit or NServiceBus, and explicit attention to idempotency. Separate crypto/digital-asset and data-platform engineering tracks exist.
Notable / other: Named one of American Banker's Best Places to Work in Fintech repeatedly (2018-2022) and a Brandon Hall Gold Award winner. Salary ranges are stated openly on their US requisitions. Domain is regulated banking — stable and mission-critical, the opposite of runway-driven.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (Remote/#LI-Hybrid, $160,000–$200,000); Senior Software Engineer, Data Platform ($150,000–$200,000); Staff Software Engineer, Crypto ($190,000–$220,000); Senior Engineer, Information Security; Senior AI/ML Engineer.
