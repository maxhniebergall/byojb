# Wynd Labs — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:wyndlabs | company_type: product

What they do: In their own words, "Wynd Labs is an early-stage startup that is on a mission to make public web data accessible for AI through contributions to Grass." Grass is described by them as "a network sharing application that allows users to share their unused bandwidth. Effectively, this is a residential proxy network that directly rewards individual residential IPs for the bandwidth they provide. Grass will route traffic equitably among its network and meter the amount of data that each node provides to fairly distribute rewards." In plainer terms they add: "Grass unlocks everyone's ability to earn rewards by simply sharing their unused internet bandwidth on personal devices (laptops, smartphones)." The Rust JD frames the wider ambition as "developing a web-scale crawler and knowledge graph that allows ordinary people to participate in the process, and share in the benefits of AI development." So: a crypto-token-incentivised residential proxy / web-scraping network sold as AI training-data infrastructure.
