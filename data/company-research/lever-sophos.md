# Sophos — research
Provider/key: lever:sophos | company_type: product

What they do: Sophos is an established cybersecurity vendor. Their own description: "Sophos is a cybersecurity leader defending 600,000 organizations globally with an AI-driven platform and expert-led services… Its solutions combine machine learning, automation, and real-time threat intelligence with frontline human expertise from Sophos X-Ops to deliver advanced, 24/7 threat monitoring, detection, and response." Portfolio: industry-leading managed detection and response (MDR) plus "endpoint, network, email, and cloud security, extended detection and response (XDR), identity threat detection and response (ITDR), and next-gen SIEM", alongside advisory services. They sell through a partner ecosystem — "Managed Service Providers (MSPs), Managed Security Service Providers (MSSPs), resellers and distributors, marketplace integrations, and cyber risk partners." Note this is a **product** company that also sells managed services; it is not a staffing or outsourcing firm.

How they describe themselves: Five stated core values — **Simplicity, Empowerment, Passion, Innovation, Authenticity** — which they say guide product development, teamwork and decision-making. Careers messaging emphasises internal advancement: mentorship programmes, structured leadership development, and "promotion from within." JD tone varies sharply by team: the Security-features Software Engineer role describes "a fast-paced, high-performing engineering team that designs, architects, and builds systems from the ground up", with end-to-end ownership "from understanding requirements, through design, implementation, testing, and deployment", responsibility for "the full lifecycle of the code you ship, including production support and continuous improvement", and serving "as an on-call first responder for the services your team owns"; they add "We value curiosity, flexibility, and creative problem solving." By contrast the AI Security req is explicitly "a high-agency, low-process role. You won't wait for specs. You'll define problems, make decisions, and move fast with strong engineering judgment."

Size / stage / funding: Large, mature private company (thousands of employees), owned by private-equity firm Thoma Bravo. Long-established, revenue-generating, not runway-dependent — the stability profile of an incumbent vendor rather than a startup. PE ownership brings its own cost-discipline and reorganisation risk.

Locations / HQ: Headquartered in Oxford, United Kingdom ("Sophos is headquartered in Oxford, U.K."). Global engineering footprint; the live requisitions in the registry are located across India (Bangalore, Ahmedabad), the United Kingdom, the United States and **Canada**.

Remote policy: Their careers page states "remote-first" options for most roles, with hybrid models for teams needing in-person collaboration, and flexible scheduling — employees can adjust start/end times or shift their workday. Benefits called out first-party: parental leave of "12 weeks for birthing mothers and 6 weeks for all other parents"; Wellbeing Days (extra paid time off); Learning Days — "3 paid days (or 24 hours) for personal learning" per year; long-service recognition with paid leave and monetary awards; quarterly Sophos Values Awards.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** Three live requisitions are typed "Canada, remote": Software Engineer, Software Engineer 2, and Senior Software Engineer – AI Security (the latter "Canada, remote, United States"). No metro is attached and the careers page states remote-first for most roles. Sophos has a long-standing Canadian engineering presence, so employment infrastructure exists. Timezone caveat: the company is Oxford-anchored with major engineering in India, so meeting overlap skews to UK/IST hours, which is the worst direction for a Mountain-Time worker; and the security-features role requires being "an on-call first responder", inside an organisation whose service promise is "24/7 threat monitoring, detection, and response."

Engineering & tech: Named work across the Canada-eligible reqs:
- *Software Engineer (Security features team)* — designing, architecting and building systems from the ground up, full lifecycle ownership including production support, on-call first responder, mentoring juniors, "help raise the bar on quality and developer experience."
- *Software Engineer 2* — a platform/release-engineering charter: "supporting, maintaining, and incrementally improving the automation, tooling, and release processes that help Sophos engineering teams deliver cloud-based services safely and consistently", working across "CI/CD pipelines, cloud infrastructure, Kubernetes-based environments, and developer tooling", troubleshooting Linux, "writing practical automation in Groovy, Python, and Bash." Explicitly welcomes "approved AI-assisted engineering tools" used responsibly.
- *Senior Software Engineer – AI Security* — "build and ship AI-powered products (LLM-driven workflows, automation, intelligent systems) across the stack — frontend, backend", owning end-to-end product development.
Other (non-Canada) reqs show the depth of the systems work: C/C++ and networking, kernel programming, Golang, NSG firewall.

Notable / other: The Software Engineer 2 role is explicitly framed as "hands-on and execution-oriented" for someone with "solid technical fundamentals" who "is eager to learn" — a mid-level charter despite the volume of senior-titled reqs elsewhere. None of the Canadian reqs post a salary range.
