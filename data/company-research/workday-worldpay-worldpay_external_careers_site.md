# Worldpay — research
Provider/key: workday:worldpay/worldpay_external_careers_site | company_type: product

What they do: Worldpay is one of the largest global merchant-acquiring and payment-processing companies — card acquiring, gateway, POS/terminal software, fraud and risk, and treasury/settlement for merchants worldwide. It was carved out of FIS (majority sold to GTCR in early 2024) and operates again as a standalone payments company. Customers are merchants and payment partners; the product is transaction-processing infrastructure.

How they describe themselves: The captured JD bodies are boilerplate-heavy Workday postings and contain no substantive values/mission statement worth quoting. No first-party culture page was fetched this session (search budget exhausted; ATS host is not a source). Treat culture as **uncharacterized**.

Size / stage / funding: Very large, private-equity-controlled (GTCR majority, FIS minority) after the 2024 carve-out from FIS. Tens of thousands of employees. Not runway-dependent, but PE-owned carve-outs typically carry cost-discipline and restructuring pressure — noted as a structural fact, not a verified event.

Locations / HQ: Global. The 19+ live relevant postings cluster in **Cincinnati OH; Boston MA; London, Cambridge and Birmingham UK; Bucharest, Romania; and Pune / Bengaluru / Indore, India**. No Canadian location appears in any live posting.

Remote policy: Extracted facets on multiple live postings say `remote_policy: hybrid` (Platform Engineer II Cincinnati, DevOps Engineer London, Java SWE Cambridge x2, Payment Terminal SWE Birmingham) and one says `onsite` (Software Engineer II Boston). Nothing in the registry indicates a remote-first posture; the pattern is office-anchored hybrid per country.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — no evidence of any kind.** Zero Canadian postings, zero remote-designated postings, and the roles that state a policy state hybrid or onsite tied to a named office in the US, UK, Romania or India. Effectively disqualifying for a Kimberley-BC-based candidate.

Engineering & tech: Enterprise payments stack — Java/Spring Boot microservices and monoliths, .NET/C#, AWS, Kubernetes, Golang (Platform Engineer II), Terraform, CI/CD with Ansible, Salesforce platform work, Snowflake/Python/ETL for data engineering, and an ML/MLOps group in London (Senior MLOps Engineer, ML Ops Senior Manager, AI Engineer). Also embedded/terminal software in Birmingham. A large share of the volume is offshore delivery-shop-style hiring in Pune/Bengaluru/Indore with explicit years-of-experience bands in the titles.

Notable / other: The title conventions ("_6-10 Yrs_Bangalore/Pune/Indore", "Evergreen - Senior Java Software Engineer") indicate high-volume, pipeline-style enterprise recruiting rather than scoped team-by-team hiring. There is genuine platform and ML-infrastructure work in Cincinnati and London, but it sits inside a very large enterprise IT organisation.
