# Pulumi — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:pulumicorporation | company_type: product

What they do: Pulumi builds infrastructure-as-code tooling that lets engineers define cloud infrastructure in general-purpose programming languages (TypeScript/JavaScript, Go, Python, C#, Java) rather than a bespoke DSL. The open-source IaC engine is the flagship; the commercial product is Pulumi Cloud (SaaS + self-hosted), which adds Pulumi Deployments (CD for infrastructure), Pulumi ESC (configuration and secrets management), Pulumi IDP (internal developer platform / golden paths, reusable components and templates) and Pulumi Copilot, an LLM-powered assistant layered across those. Customers are platform-engineering teams at cloud-native companies; the stated community is 350,000+ members and 3,250+ customers.

How they describe themselves: "Pulumi is reimagining how teams build cloud software, enabling developers and infrastructure experts to work better together through a unique combination of programming languages, tools, and systems innovation." Mission framing is "democratize the cloud" / "program the cloud." The full stated value set, quoted from their own JD:
- "We succeed as a team and support each other through challenges."
- "Inclusive environments help the best ideas rise to the top."
- "Our customers guide the decisions we make."
- "Openness and honesty help us move forward, even when conversations are difficult."
- "We value thoughtful action and focus on delivering meaningful results."
- "Curiosity keeps us learning and evolving with technology."
- "We aim to think big and help define the future of cloud engineering."
Tone is engineering-credible rather than promotional, with one hustle-flavoured line in the closing boilerplate: "If you thrive in a fast-paced, high-performance, we want to work with you to accelerate Pulumi's impact." Their own culture summary elsewhere is "technical excellence, passion for teamwork, and customer obsession."

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2017 by Joe Duffy and Eric Rudder — both long-tenured developer-platform veterans (ex-Microsoft). Private, venture-backed, in the low-hundreds headcount range. Not a fresh startup: nine years old with an established open-source position and a paid SaaS line. Describes itself as in "rapid growth."

Locations / HQ: Headquartered in Seattle, WA (downtown office retained). Teammates across 20+ U.S. states and 12+ countries.

Remote policy: Remote-first and fully remote since March 2020 — stated in their own JD: "While Pulumi is headquartered in Seattle, WA., we are dedicated to being a remote-first company" and "Pulumi has been fully remote since 2020… We support flexible work with asynchronous collaboration and an annual 'work from anywhere' stipend." Explicit async-collaboration language.

Remote-Canada eligibility: Unclear — not verified. The live posting (Senior Software Engineer, IaC Cloud) is listed simply as "Remote" with no geographic restriction stated, and the company employs people in 12+ countries with benefits language that distinguishes "U.S. employees" from international ones ("Internationally, we comply with local healthcare requirements"), so non-US employment clearly exists. But no page seen names Canada. The posted base range is USD and the 401(k) is US-only, so a Canadian offer's currency/structure is unknown. Worth confirming with a recruiter before investing.

Engineering & tech: Full stack across Angular/TypeScript, Node.js and Go (API development), plus SDK/provider work spanning TypeScript/JavaScript, Go, Python, C#, and Java. Distributed systems and modern cloud architecture are core to the role. Deployment orchestration and IaC tooling experience expected. The Senior SWE role explicitly asks for shipping production LLM features ("incorporate state-of-the-art LLMs," "build production-grade products"), consistent with the Pulumi Copilot product line. The Cloud team's remit is platform-engineering productivity, self-service capabilities and automated infrastructure operations. Senior bar is 8+ years.

Compensation & benefits (from their own JD): Base salary hiring range $180,500 – $227,850 for the Senior SWE, IaC Cloud role; all full-time offers include base + bonus/variable + equity ("All employees receive equity"). Unlimited PTO with a mandatory minimum of three weeks off annually plus 13 U.S. holidays; 20 weeks paid leave for birthing/primary caregivers and 16 for non-birthing/secondary; no-cost medical/dental/vision for U.S. employees; 401(k) with match (U.S.); annual learning-and-development budget; monthly wellness fund; quarterly happiness fund; annual work-from-anywhere stipend.

Notable / other: Pioneered LLM features in the IaC space with Pulumi Copilot. The open-source engine gives them a durable developer-mindshare position against Terraform/OpenTofu. Explicit equal-opportunity and accommodation statement.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, IaC Cloud (Remote).
