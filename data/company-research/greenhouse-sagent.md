# Sagent — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:sagent | company_type: product

What they do: Sagent builds mortgage/loan **servicing** software. In their own words: "Sagent is transforming the mortgage servicing industry by bringing the modern experience customers now expect from loan originations to loan servicing. Our platform lets customers manage their home-owing lives from anywhere while giving servicers lower costs, scale compliance and higher servicing values through full market cycles." Customers are mortgage servicers; the domain is heavily compliance-bound financial services.

How they describe themselves: Mission framing is "make loans and homeownership simpler and safer for all consumers", with the recruiting slogan "Sagent powers servicers and consumers. You power Sagent!" and "You could work anywhere. We know you are talented and looking for something inspiring and impactful." They also say "We hire innovators and doers to disrupt the last and most complex frontier of lending and housing. We're growing fast and need you to help shape our future." The engineering-culture description in the JD is notably more grounded than the marketing copy: "You value automation, documentation, and simplicity. You are pragmatic about technical debt and know when to fix it versus when to ship… You hold a high bar for reliability, security, and operational hygiene — you proactively identify and resolve issues before they become incidents."

Size / stage / funding: A joint venture that "combines Fiserv Inc.'s decades of market-leading fintech expertise with Warburg Pincus' skill in growing technology companies" — i.e. privately held, PE/strategic-backed rather than venture-runway dependent. Headcount not verified (no first-party site fetched; the registry only carries the ATS board, which is not fetchable).

Locations / HQ: Not verified this session. Benefits (401(k) matching, "protected veteran status" EEO language) establish it as a US employer.

Remote policy: The posting carries the `#LI-REMOTE` tag and its location field is "Remote". Company-wide, benefits list "Remote/Hybrid workplace options", plus Unlimited Flexible Time Off, "Focus Time Fridays", tuition reimbursement, paid family leave, family planning services and pet insurance, all eligible from day one.

Remote-Canada eligibility: UNCLEAR, leaning no. The role is genuinely remote, but every benefit and compliance marker is US-specific (401(k), FMLA-style leave language, US veteran EEO text) and Sagent's business is US mortgage servicing. Nothing in the JD states a country restriction explicitly, so this is not verified either way — but the default assumption should be US-remote until Sagent confirms otherwise.

Engineering & tech: The Cloud Engineering team "builds the foundation that every engineering team at Sagent runs on… internal platforms and products that make shipping to production fast, secure, and repeatable" — a self-service Internal Developer Portal (Backstage), a centralized CI/CD platform, reusable infrastructure modules, and policy-as-code. The environment is a large-scale event-driven microservices platform on GKE: multi-region GKE clusters hosting hundreds of microservices; Istio service mesh (mTLS, VirtualServices, AuthorizationPolicies); cert-manager, external-dns, RBAC, HPA/KEDA autoscaling, HashiCorp Vault secret injection, Helm; Terraform across multiple IaC repos (GKE, Shared VPC, Cloud NAT, Private Service Connect, Cloud SQL, Cloud Storage, Dataproc, Cloud Composer, Vault); Azure DevOps YAML CI/CD with trunk-based development; Confluent Kafka (Connect/JDBC sources, consumer-lag KEDA autoscaling); Redis Enterprise on Kubernetes; observability via Grafana Cloud (Alloy, Loki, Mimir, Tempo, Pyroscope), kube-prometheus-stack, Google Managed Prometheus, OpenTelemetry, Beyla, Kubecost; security via NetworkPolicies, Pod Security Standards, admission policy, CrowdStrike Falcon, Lacework, kube-bench; data services Cloud SQL/Postgres, Dataproc (Spark), Cloud Composer (Airflow), Matillion CDC, Snowflake, BigQuery; multi-cloud DNS (Azure DNS, Cloudflare, GCP Cloud DNS), Azure APIM and Cloudflare CDN/WAF. Scripting in Bash, Python or Go. Seniority bar: 7+ years.

Notable / other: This is one of the most concretely specified platform JDs in the batch — it names the actual tools in production rather than generic categories, which is itself a signal of engineering maturity. The role explicitly straddles platform engineering and direct application-team support ("partner directly with application development teams to troubleshoot deployment failures… resolve Kafka consumer lag").
