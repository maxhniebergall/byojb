# Assured (Assured Insurance Technologies Inc.) — research
Provider/key: ashby:assured | company_type: product
What they do: AI-powered claims processing for property & casualty insurance. Sells to large insurers: self-service claim filing (FNOL), voice AI agents, messaging, backend fraud detection, and catastrophe management. Positions itself as "the engine that powers claims processing for some of the largest insurers in the world", processing "tens of millions of claims a year".
How they describe themselves: Mission framing is "to modernize insurance" — claims processing is "the foundation of the entire industry… currently highly manual, involving phone calls, faxes, and gut instinct, costing tens of billions of dollars a year. We can do better." Product philosophy on their site: combine "agentic AI with the determinism, explainability, and guardrails that claims demand", prioritizing accuracy, auditability and control because claims are high-stakes regulated decisions. JD tone is systems-engineering-forward: "solve real-world complexity with well-designed systems… measure success by how much easier they make it for others to ship software", "think in terms of systems, abstractions, and leverage". They call life at Assured "dynamic, collaborative, and rewarding". No published list of corporate values was found.
Size / stage / funding: Not disclosed on the site or in the JDs; not verified in this pass. Private; scale claim is "tens of millions of claims a year, trusted by the nation's top insurers". Benefits (401k with 3% non-elective contribution, platinum medical) read like a funded, past-seed company rather than a pre-product startup.
Locations / HQ: Not disclosed on the pages read. Company operates from official @assured.claims email addresses; site now resolves to assured.com.
Remote policy: Remote company — "We're remote, but we regularly get together" (team events & offsites). WFH benefits: lunch stipend 2x/week, monthly phone stipend, home-office perks. All five live engineering postings are listed Remote.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear — not verified, leaning US-only.** The benefits block is entirely US-shaped (401(k) with employer contribution, platinum US medical/dental/vision, FSAs and HSAs) and pay is quoted in USD with no non-US tier. No Canada or international-hiring statement appears on the site or in any JD. Do not assume Canada eligibility without asking them.
Engineering & tech: AWS, GitLab CI/CD, TypeScript-based services, Docker/Kubernetes, multi-environment production infrastructure. Organized into "Zones" — a Platform Engineering Zone with separate CI, infrastructure and SRE ownership. Work described is genuine platform plumbing: release orchestration, artifact promotion, environment-aware deployment, rollback and safety checks, paved paths and self-service tooling, observability of the release lifecycle, "the last mile of the platform". Also ML-driven decisioning on "billions of dollars of claims payments", and stated curiosity about "agentic, spec-driven development".
