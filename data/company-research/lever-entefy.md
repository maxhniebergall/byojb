What they do: Entefy describes itself as "an advanced AI software and automation company" delivering "multisensory AI technology" to enterprises. Its core is the **Mimi AI Engine**, wrapped in a suite of vertical applications: Digital Supply Chain Hub, Credit & Risk Decisioning, Report Analyzer, Communicator, Automated Data Scientist, and Crypto Market Intelligence. Stated customers are "U.S./global private and public companies with 200 to 200,000+ employees," from SMEs to "Fortune 100+" organizations. The original consumer premise was unified communication/"digital interaction" — the Senior ML JD still says the role will "shape the future of communication and digital interaction," which sits oddly next to the current enterprise-automation positioning.

"The next phase of technology will be defined by advanced computing, machine intelligence, and hyperautomation."
