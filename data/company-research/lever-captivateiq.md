# CaptivateIQ — research
Provider/key: lever:captivateiq | company_type: product

What they do: CaptivateIQ builds sales commission / incentive compensation management (ICM) and sales-performance SaaS. Their platform automates commission calculation, sales planning, territory management and quota setting, and is marketed as "a unified platform that connects compensation, planning, and AI in a way legacy tools can't replicate." Customers are enterprise and mid-market revenue/finance organizations — they state "over 1,000 customers."

How they describe themselves: Positioning is operator-led — "We built it because we lived it. Now we're sharing what we know." Their marketing centres on "revenue resilience," the ability to change comp strategy when business conditions shift. Their own engineering framing (from the Principal Software Engineer JD) is notably platform-oriented rather than hype-driven: the company is "transitioning from a single-product company to a multi-product, interconnected modeling platform — where every enterprise business model running on CIQ compounds the value of every other." The platform is described as serving two audiences: "enterprise customers who need fast, reliable, and trustworthy modeling experiences — and CIQ product teams who need to compose new products on shared primitives without rebuilding foundations." No public values list was found on the pages fetched.

Size / stage / funding: Private, VC-backed. 1,000+ customers. Their Principal JD states the Platform org alone is "~25 engineers today, scaling to 35+" and that there are "three Principal Engineers at CaptivateIQ" — implying a mid-size engineering organisation (low hundreds of engineers total, inferred, not stated). Funding stage and headcount are not stated on their site; unverified.

Locations / HQ: Not stated on the site (unverified). Their live postings imply an Austin, Texas hub (a Staff role is tagged Austin/hybrid, plus Nashville and Raleigh), a Toronto presence tagged on multiple postings, and a Poland engineering site (two roles are "Poland, remote").

Remote policy: Mixed. Live postings show fully-remote roles ("Remote USA", "Poland, remote", "Remote") alongside at least one explicitly hybrid role (Austin, Texas, hybrid). No published remote-policy statement was found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear — not verified.** Several postings carry a "Toronto" location tag alongside "Remote USA"/"Remote," which suggests a Canadian employing entity exists, but no posting states Canada-wide remote eligibility and none names British Columbia. The Toronto tag is as consistent with a Toronto-anchored role as with country-wide remote. Would need to be confirmed with the company before treating as BC-remote eligible.

Engineering & tech: The platform is organised across six named architectural layers — "Modeling, Orchestration, Identity, State Management, AI, and Frontend & Design Systems." The core differentiator is "SmartGrid," described publicly as an "ELT & Calculation Engine" for real-time data modeling and commission processing, with a no-code authoring surface. Senior/Staff/Principal ICs are expected to be "both architect and builder," setting multi-year technical strategy while staying hands-on. There is an explicit AI Platform team (multiple "AI Platform" postings) and a "Modeling Platform" team.

Notable / other: Pay is posted on the US/Toronto-tagged roles (rare and useful) and is high — Staff AI Platform is posted at $181,289–$285,272 USD. The Poland-based roles post no range. The company is clearly investing in a platform re-architecture, which is where the senior openings sit.

Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Software Engineer (Poland, remote); Staff Software Engineer, Modeling Platform (Remote/Toronto); Staff Software Engineer - AI Platform (Austin hybrid / Toronto / Nashville / Raleigh); Senior Software Engineer - AI Platform (East Coast) (Remote USA / Toronto).
