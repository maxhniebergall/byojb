# NielsenIQ (NIQ) — research

Provider/key: smartrecruiters:nielseniq | company_type: product

What they do: NielsenIQ (NIQ) is a global consumer-intelligence and retail-measurement
company — the "what consumers buy" business spun out of Nielsen in 2021 and since merged with
GfK. It builds and sells syndicated measurement products from retail point-of-sale feeds and
consumer panels: **Homescan** (household purchase panel) and **OmniShopper** (shopper
behaviour), plus e-commerce measurement and analytics for CPG manufacturers and retailers.
Its engineering problem is fundamentally large-scale data ingestion, validation and
measurement methodology rather than consumer product.

How they describe themselves: The scanned postings are functional rather than values-heavy;
tone extracts as balanced across all four live relevant roles, with no hustle language.
NIQ publicly positions itself around "the Full View" of consumer behaviour — complete,
unbiased measurement of what consumers buy and watch.

Size / stage / funding: Very large (tens of thousands of employees globally), private-equity
backed (Advent International et al.) following the Nielsen split and the GfK combination.
Structurally this is a mature, cost-managed, PE-owned data business — stable in the sense of
scale and revenue, but with the reorganization and cost-discipline dynamics that ownership
implies rather than founder- or profit-driven stability.

Locations / HQ: Global; Chicago and Illinois-area US presence, major engineering and research
concentrations in **Spain (Madrid, Barcelona, Valladolid)** and India, plus a **Toronto,
Ontario** presence. Registry postings for this batch are Spain-heavy with one Toronto role.

Remote policy: Mixed and geography-specific. Several roles are explicitly home-office /
remote-within-country ("AI Principal Engineer (Spain Home Office Based)", "Senior AI Research
Scientist … (Spain Remote)"), and the Toronto role is posted as remote. There is no single
company-wide remote-first statement in the scanned material; policy appears to be set per
requisition and per country.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Partial — one confirmed role, not a general policy.** The
*Principal Engineer – CPS DataValidation Platform (OmniShopper & Homescan)* posting states
"Location: Toronto, ON, ca (remote)" and extracts as `remote` / `canada` / `America/Toronto`.
That is a genuine remote-in-Canada requisition. The other three relevant live roles are Spain
-based (Madrid/Barcelona/Valladolid) and are not Canada-eligible. So NIQ *can* employ a
Canada-based remote worker, but the current opportunity is a single req, and it is anchored to
Eastern Time (2 hours ahead of Mountain) with a European-heavy wider org.

Engineering & tech: Data-platform-centric. Live roles cover a CPS **DataValidation Platform**
for the OmniShopper and Homescan panels (principal, platform_infra), applied AI research
(NLP, information retrieval, LLMs, SLMs, GenAI, agentic AI), MLOps/data analysis, and
conventional Java/Spring/Angular and Python/Oracle/SQL engineering. The data-validation
platform work is the most infrastructure-shaped of these.

F2F interview Diversity Hiring on 13th June"), which is a useful signal that the global
