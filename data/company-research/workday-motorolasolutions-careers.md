# Motorola Solutions — research
Provider/key: workday:motorolasolutions/careers | company_type: product

What they do: Motorola Solutions is a large public company building public-safety and enterprise
security technology: land-mobile-radio (LMR) and mission-critical communications infrastructure,
P25/broadband networks, 911/PSAP command-centre software (CAD, records), video security and access
control (Avigilon, Pelco), body cameras and evidence management, and increasingly AI for emergency
response. Also has a defence/R&D arm (e.g. networked electronic warfare). Customers are police, fire,
EMS, 911 centres, governments, militaries, schools, hospitals and enterprises.

How they describe themselves (their own Workday JD boilerplate, verbatim): "At Motorola Solutions,
we believe that everything starts with our people. We're a global close-knit community, united by the
relentless pursuit to help keep people safer everywhere. We build and connect technologies to help
protect people, property and places. Our solutions foster the collaboration that's critical for safer
communities, safer schools, safer hospitals, safer businesses, and ultimately, safer nations. Connect
with a career that matters, and help us build a safer future." Postings are published bilingually
(English/French) for Canadian roles. On AI they state: "We expect engineers to use AI agents in their
own work. But because this is mission-critical software, humans still own quality, judgment, and what
ships."

Size / stage / funding: NYSE-listed (MSI), ~$10B+ annual revenue, tens of thousands of employees.
Highly stable, profitable, and acquisitive — this is the opposite of a runway-dependent startup.

Locations / HQ: HQ Chicago, Illinois. Large global footprint. Canadian sites relevant here:
**Vancouver, BC** (the biggest Canadian engineering site — C++, enterprise software, platform),
**Toronto, ON** (ONT40 — architecture/data), **Gatineau, QC** (radio infrastructure). Also US sites
(Richardson TX, Colorado, Southern California) and a substantial **Brazil** engineering org.

Remote policy: Mixed and location-bound. Some JDs advertise a "Remote-first culture with flexible
work arrangements"; others state a "hybrid work schedule" or "remote work depending on location".
Crucially, remote postings are **province- or state-scoped**: "Ontario Remote Work", "Brazil Remote
Work", "Colorado Remote Work", "California Southern Remote Work". Some roles offer relocation.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Effectively NO for a British Columbia candidate outside Vancouver.**
Motorola clearly employs Canadians, but the live openings split into two buckets, neither of which
works from Kimberley, BC:
1. **Vancouver, Canada** — in-office/hybrid roles tied to the Vancouver site (Senior Software
   Developer C++, Software Developer II, Principal Software Engineer, Systems and Solutions
   Enterprise Software Developer). Vancouver is in BC but ~850 km from Kimberley; these require
   relocation, not remote work. Toronto and Gatineau roles are likewise site-based.
2. **Ontario Remote Work** — genuinely remote, but scoped to the province of Ontario (Sr Software
   Engineer, AI Agent Platform ×2; Data Engineering Co-Op). A BC-resident is outside the stated
   hiring province, and these are Eastern-time teams.
The remaining remote roles are Brazil-remote or US-state-remote and are not open to Canadians at all.
So: Canada-friendly employer, but no Canada-wide remote posting in the current set.

Engineering & tech: Broad. C++ systems and radio infrastructure (Vancouver, Gatineau); Java/Spring
Boot cloud platforms and SRE (Brazil); C#/React and enterprise software (Vancouver); Python/DevOps
and message brokers (Richardson TX); Salesforce platform engineering; data architecture (Toronto);
and defence R&D. The most interesting current work is **Hyper**, a conversational/agentic-AI company
Motorola announced it acquired on 9 April 2026, building AI that handles non-emergency 911 calls to
reduce PSAP load. Its AI Agent Platform team works on "real-time voice AI systems that support human
911 telecommunicators at scale" — low-latency audio, streaming, telephony, LLM orchestration and
human-in-the-loop AI workflows, with an explicit reliability/availability/security/performance bar.
That is genuine AI-platform infrastructure, but it is posted as Ontario-remote.

Notable / other: Acquired Hyper (agentic AI for 911) April 2026. Public-safety and defence exposure
including networked electronic warfare — a values consideration. Compensation at the Canadian sites
is noticeably below US levels for the same seniority.
