# Prime Intellect — research
Provider/key: ashby:primeintellect | company_type: product

What they do: Prime Intellect builds what it calls the **"open superintelligence stack": "the infrastructure frontier AI labs build internally, made available to every ambitious AI team."** Their platform, **Lab**, "unifies compute, environments, evaluations, secure sandboxes, high-performance training, and deployment into one full-stack system for post-training at frontier scale — from SFT and RL to tool use, agent workflows, and continuously improving production models." Product surfaces on their site: **Lab** (post-training, 2,500+ RL environments, evals, hosted training), **Inference** (dedicated or serverless deployment with LoRA adapter support), **Compute** (on-demand GPU access from 1-256 GPUs plus large reserved clusters), and **Research** (open-source models and datasets including INTELLECT-3 and SYNTHETIC-2). They also run decentralised/distributed training research and publish it openly.

How they describe themselves: Tagline **"Own Your Intelligence."** From their JDs: "We are building open frontier AI: open-source models trained end to end for long-horizon tasks like autonomous research, and the full-stack platform our own research team uses to build them." Their pitch to candidates: "We are looking for people who want to build at the intersection of **frontier research, real infrastructure, and go-to-market for a category that does not fully exist yet**", hiring "the most ambitious developers", and asking applicants for "examples of exceptional work". The GPU-infrastructure JD states: "**We value expertise and customer obsession**." Mission language: "join us in our mission to democratize access to planetary scale compute."

Engineering & tech: Genuinely deep systems and ML-infrastructure work.
- *Inference role* (spans cloud LLM serving, inference optimisation and RL systems): multi-tenant LLM serving across cloud GPU fleets; GPU-aware placement and scheduling for heterogeneous accelerators; multi-region/zone failover and traffic shifting; autoscaling, routing and load balancing to throughput/latency SLOs; model distribution and cold-start optimisation; contributing to **vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM**; tuning tensor/pipeline/expert parallelism, prefix caching and memory management; integrating inference into the RL training stack.
- *GPU Infrastructure role* (explicitly "**This customer-facing role**"): designing GPU cluster architectures for clients from 100 to 10,000+ GPUs, capacity planning, technical proposals and executive presentations; **SLURM and Kubernetes**; **InfiniBand, RoCE, NVLink**; parallel filesystems (Lustre, BeeGFS, GPFS); NVIDIA driver/CUDA/Fabric Manager/DCGM; container GPU runtimes (Docker, Containerd, Enroot); Linux kernel tuning; Ansible/Terraform; power and cooling for high-density deployments. Nice-to-haves: 1000+ GPU deployments, DGX/HGX/SuperPOD certification, PyTorch FSDP/DeepSpeed/Megatron-LM. It also names "primary technical escalation point for customer infrastructure issues" and runbook authorship.
