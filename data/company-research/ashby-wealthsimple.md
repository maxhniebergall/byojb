# Wealthsimple Technologies — research
Provider/key: ashby:wealthsimple | company_type: product

What they do: "Wealthsimple is Canada's leading financial innovator. The company offers a full suite of simple, sophisticated financial products across managed investing, do-it-yourself trading, cryptocurrency, tax filing, spending and saving." It serves more than 4 million Canadians and holds over $125 billion in assets under administration. Founded 2014 by financial experts and technology entrepreneurs.

How they describe themselves: They maintain a public Culture Manual built on six principles, in their own words: "The client, the client, the client" (client needs above internal convenience); "Ship it" (bias toward action and rapid iteration over perfect planning); "We are maker-owners" (employees act as owners, with accountability, and "ship the code"); "Naive ambition" (pursuing bold, transformative ideas despite uncertainty); "Be human" (compassion, directness and kindness); and "Keep it simple" (reducing complexity in products, processes and communication). They describe "building amazing things fast" and favour people who "launch, learn, and iterate." On pace, the manual is candid and bidirectional: "There are moments when important work needs attention at night, on a weekend, or during a holiday" — balanced by an expectation that personal time is protected when circumstances demand it. "Build with AI" is stated as an operating expectation across all roles. Their own JD adds: "We move quickly and build thoughtfully... we're always looking for better ways to work — whether that's new tools, AI, or rethinking how we approach a problem."

Size / stage / funding: Private, late-stage and substantial — over $125B in assets under administration and 4M+ clients, majority-backed by Power Corporation of Canada. Roughly 1,000+ employees. This is an established financial institution rather than an early startup, though it still describes itself in startup terms.

Locations / HQ: Headquartered in Toronto, Canada. Canada-focused business.

Remote policy: Remote-first, with output valued over face time — employees can work from anywhere in Canada given internet access, with outcomes-over-hours norms, asynchronous collaboration, infrequent onsites and periodic focus days. They also run a "90 Days Away" program letting employees work from abroad up to 90 days annually. Recognized as a Canada's Top 100 / Top Employer.

Remote-Canada eligibility: VERIFIED YES for most roles, but posting-by-posting. The registry shows the majority of relevant requisitions tagged "Remote (Canada)" — including Staff Software Engineer, Observability Platform; Senior Software Developer, Financial Risk; Bank & Brokerage Linking; Book of Record (Leverage); and Platform Engineering. A minority are tagged "Toronto Headquarters" only (Senior Software Developer - STAR, Ledger, Clients & Accounts), and those are effectively disqualifying for a Kimberley BC candidate who cannot relocate. A few are dual "Remote (Canada), Remote (US)". Since the company is Canada-only in its business and remote-first in policy, the Canada-remote requisitions are genuine. Mountain Time against a Toronto-anchored company is a 2h offset — workable.

Engineering & tech: Deep, well-resourced platform organization. The Observability Platform team is explicitly infrastructure-as-a-product: "The Observability Platform team exists to make production legible to every engineer and AI agent at Wealthsimple... We treat the platform as a product, with our internal engineering teams as our customers. Our mission is to provide the best, most consistent logging, metrics, and tracing experience." They "instrument on open standards so that switching costs are a configuration change rather than a rewrite," "optimize for the questions engineers did not anticipate rather than for pre-built dashboards," and "design for a world where both humans and AI agents investigate production through fast, high-cardinality data." Concrete scope includes wide-event data models, high-cardinality storage, high-throughput ingestion and query pipelines (columnar/streaming), telemetry SDKs and golden paths, OpenTelemetry conventions and sampling standards, Model Context Protocol (MCP) access patterns for AI agents investigating incidents, and SLI/SLO design. Languages named: Kotlin and Ruby. Bonus skills: ClickHouse or columnar stores, Kubernetes, progressive delivery. They name Claude Code specifically as a tool they expect daily proficiency with. Other teams cover ledger, brokerage, stablecoin, financial risk, book of record, quality/developer tooling and production engineering.

Compensation: Highly transparent on Ashby — nearly every relevant senior requisition posts CA$151.2K–189K base plus equity. Staff-level requisitions (Observability Platform, Production Engineering) post no range.

Notable / other: Regulated fintech environment. Culture Manual and "90 Days Away" are publicly documented. AI tooling is used in their hiring process, disclosed on the JD, with human final decisions.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer, Observability Platform (Remote Canada); Staff Software Developer, Production Engineering (Remote Canada); Senior Software Developer, Financial Risk; Senior Software Developer, Book of Record (Leverage); Sr Software Developer - Platform Engineering.
