# Hungryroot — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:hungryroot | company_type: product
What they do: Direct-to-consumer grocery and meal-recommendation service. In their own words: "Hungryroot is using AI to build the most consumer-centric food and wellness company to ever exist. We act as your personal assistant for healthy living—getting to know your goals, lifestyle, and budget, and recommending and delivering healthy groceries, easy recipes, and essential supplements for you and your family." Subscription e-commerce with physical fulfilment and supply chain.
How they describe themselves: Stated beliefs, quoted: "we believe food is the foundation of health, convenience should not mean compromise, and that everyone is unique in how they eat and live. That's why we're building a future in which healthy living is both easy and enjoyable." On working there: "Hungryroot is a distributed team of top talent across 28+ U.S. states. While we have a headquarters in New York City, our remote-first culture emphasizes collaboration, team-building, and flexibility. Expect regular virtual team events, strong ownership and accountability, and an annual company retreat." Engineering-culture language from the Platform JD is demanding and evidence-oriented: engineers should have "proven records being the tech lead on complex, cross-team projects, proactively raising issues, and resourcefully unblocking themselves to ensure projects are delivered with high quality and within the expected timeline" and "can constructively debate any issue, persuade others with hard evidence while staying objective and open to changing their mind."
Size / stage / funding: Headcount not stated; distributed across "28+ U.S. states". Private and VC-backed (equity is offered in the benefits list); stage, funding round and profitability not stated in any source read.
Locations / HQ: Headquarters in New York City; otherwise fully distributed across US states.
Remote policy: Remote-first, stated verbatim in the benefits of both live JDs: "Remote-first: work from home, work from our NYC office, work from anywhere in the U.S. - you decide!" Both postings carry the #LI-REMOTE tag.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO. "anywhere in the U.S." is the stated boundary, the team is described as spanning "28+ U.S. states", and the Data Engineer JD adds "The employer will not sponsor applicants for work visas." Nothing indicates a Canadian entity.
Engineering & tech: Two named teams in the live postings. Data Engineering (reports to a Senior Director, Data Platform & Governance) builds the "Hungryroot Analytics data model" used for financial decision-making, product development and inventory management — Spark (Python) on Databricks, ETL development and data modelling, production pipeline ownership, close partnership with analysts across Food Analytics, Operations, Marketing and Web/App. The Platform team builds "foundational systems and developer integration tooling" for the API, internal-services and data-science backend teams, working alongside a separate DevOps team on "reliable infrastructure, effective automation, and self-service" — Python/Django (Flask/FastAPI acceptable), strong SQL, AWS, Datadog/CloudWatch observability, on-call rotation, Terraform, Docker, Celery, workflow orchestration (they name "Dyrygent"), React as a nice-to-have. Both JDs explicitly expect use of AI coding tools; the Platform one names Claude Code.
Notable / other: Benefits: equity, unlimited vacation, universal paid parental leave, monthly Hungryroot grocery credit, full health/vision/dental/life, 401(k) with match, work-from-home setup stipend, annual company retreat. Both live postings publish pay ranges directly ($151,000-$189,000 for Senior Data Engineer; $160,000-$180,000 for Senior Software Engineer, Platform).
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Data Engineer (Remote, US); Senior Software Engineer, Platform (Remote, US).
