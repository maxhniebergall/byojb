# DiliTrust — research
Provider/key: lever:dilitrust | company_type: product

What they do: DiliTrust is a global legal-tech SaaS vendor ("Editeur SaaS mondial") selling an integrated suite of legal and governance solutions to corporate legal departments — from generating shareholder-meeting/board reports to AI-assisted contract lifecycle management. Their stated vision is "to digitalize legal departments across the world" and to become the global leader in Legal Tech.

How they describe themselves: Their own words (JD is in French): growth of more than 40% per year since 2020; "notre ambition est de devenir le leader mondial de la Legal Tech." They position themselves as the first legal-tech to integrate AI features, since 2022, and claim recognition for "un environnement de travail positif et entrepreneurial," citing the "Happy at Work" and "Tech at Work" labels every year since 2019. Framing is growth-and-ambition oriented, though without US-style hustle vocabulary.

Size / stage / funding: Private; headcount and funding stage not stated in available sources. Scale indicators from their own copy: 12 offices, 2,400 clients across 64 countries, of which 80% are companies listed on major markets (Europe, North America, Middle East). >40% annual growth since 2020.

Locations / HQ: 12 offices across France, the United States, Mexico, Dubai, Morocco, Germany, Spain, Italy and Canada. The infrastructure team of ~10 people is split across France, Canada and Spain. The open DevOps role is based in Montreal, Downtown.

Remote policy: The open role is explicitly hybrid and tied to the Montreal downtown office ("Le poste est localisé à Montréal, Downtown"). No company-wide remote policy statement found. The registry records this posting's location as "Montreal, hybrid."

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO for this role. The company does employ in Canada — that part is real, there is a Montreal office and Canadian members of the infra team — but the posting is a Montreal-downtown hybrid position, not remote-anywhere-in-Canada. From Kimberley, BC (Mountain Time, no relocation possible) this is effectively disqualifying. It should not be counted as Canada-remote-eligible.

Engineering & tech: Infrastructure team of ~10 engineers spread across France, Canada and Spain, working in an international context (Europe, NorAm, EMEA). Automated management of an infrastructure of 350+ assets hosted with their cloud providers. Working language for the Montreal role appears to be French (the JD is written in French).
