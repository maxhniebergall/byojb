# Trellis Technologies (Savvy) — research
Provider/key: ashby:savvyinsurance-trellis | company_type: product

What they do: Trellis is an insurance-technology company: "Trellis is rewriting the insurance experience from the inside out. We're the tech company behind Savvy, our licensed insurance agency." The ATS slug (`savvyinsurance-trellis`) confirms the Trellis/Savvy pairing, so the entity in the registry matches the JDs — no name collision. The business is consumer insurance shopping and distribution: comparison/quoting, carrier and agency integrations, and a conversational-AI layer over the shopping journey. Product surfaces named in JDs: real-time bidding, provider/carrier integrations, and a conversational AI assistant.

How they describe themselves: "With powerful tools, clean design, and a customer-first mindset, we're making insurance shopping refreshingly effortless." On culture and pace: "We're a profitable, fast-growing Series A startup backed by General Catalyst, QED, NYCA, and Amex Ventures. As a remote-first team, we move quickly, experiment boldly, and build with intention." And: "If you're craving meaningful impact, energized by ambiguity, and are ready to build alongside exceptional teammates…"
Their stated "what sets Trellis apart" list: "A transparent, collaborative culture where ideas win, not titles"; "Big opportunities to take ownership and chart your growth"; "Competitive compensation (75th+ percentile)"; "Fully remote across the US & Canada"; "Quarterly virtual and/or in-person events that keep us connected."
The engineer profile they publish is an explicit values set: **Meticulous** ("you pay attention to detail and take pride in what you deliver"), **Articulate**, **Kind, not (just) nice** ("you provide tough feedback in a clear and concise manner that does not diminish or belittle the recipient"), **Opinionated** ("bring strong opinions… but are willing to change your mind"), **Product-minded** ("focus on the 'why' just as much as the 'what' or 'how'"), **Collaborative**, **Autonomous** ("self-motivated… can generate high-value work with little direct supervision").

Size / stage / funding: Series A, and self-described as **profitable** — an unusual and positive combination at that stage. Investors: General Catalyst, QED, NYCA, Amex Ventures. Headcount not stated in the sources read. Claims the product is one "millions rely on."

Locations / HQ: Not stated in the JDs; the company operates as a distributed remote-first team. All live postings are listed "Remote."

Remote policy: Remote-first and explicitly stated: "Fully remote across the US & Canada." Async-leaning signals: they ask specifically for people who "communicate clearly and concisely, particularly in Slack and technical documents." In-person contact is limited to "quarterly virtual and/or in-person events."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes — verified from the company's own JD text.** "🏡 Fully remote across the US & Canada" appears verbatim in the benefits block of both the Senior and Staff Conversational AI postings, and the extracted facets on the Provider Integrations role record `geo_eligibility: canada`. No timezone restriction is stated, so Mountain Time is not obviously excluded, though no explicit overlap requirement was published either (unverified either way).

Engineering & tech: Node.js and TypeScript backend; RESTful APIs and "codifying business logic on top of data stores"; Google Cloud stack (GCP, with Looker and BigQuery named as a plus); "LLM-driven infrastructure" for the Conversational AI team; ML-based ranking and prediction ("normalize, rank, and evaluate results using ML-based predictions to determine optimal user paths"), with a Data Science team using DataRobot for retention forecasting. Engineering reports into a Head of Conversational AI Engineering (John Hartley, named in both JDs). Ownership model is full-lifecycle: "Own projects through the entire software development lifecycle — from planning and development to validation, iteration, and maintenance," with tech plans written for review by technical leadership.

Compensation: Unusually transparent and stated as policy: "As a remote team, we set our salaries at the 75th percentile of national market data in our industry… We review compensation twice a year." Posted USD base ranges — Senior Backend Engineer, Conversational AI: $159K–$210K (plus equity and bonus); Senior Backend Engineer, Provider Integrations: $159K–$210K (plus equity and bonus); Staff Backend Engineer, Conversational AI: $220K–$235K (plus bonus). Benefits: flexible vacation, health insurance from day one, wellness programming, 401(k) and HSA contributions, FSAs, bonuses and equity, paid parental leave. Note the 401(k)/HSA framing is US-centric even though they hire in Canada — the Canadian benefits equivalent is unverified.

Notable / other: All employees must complete a background check prior to starting. Equal-opportunity employer statement present.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Backend Engineer, Conversational AI; Senior Backend Engineer, Conversational AI; Senior Backend Engineer, Real-Time Bidding; Senior Backend Engineer, Provider Integrations.
