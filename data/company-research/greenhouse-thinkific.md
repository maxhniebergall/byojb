# Thinkific — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:thinkific | company_type: product
What they do: Thinkific is a Vancouver-based learning-commerce SaaS platform. In their own words it "helps learning businesses turn knowledge into impact. By bringing together community, courses, and content with commerce, we power transformative learning experiences that help businesses grow their revenue—and reach millions of learners around the world." Customers are creators, coaches and training businesses selling online courses/communities; the platform spans course authoring, community, payments and commerce. Also operates Thinkific Payments and an app/partner ecosystem.
How they describe themselves: "We're a team of 300+ Thinkers building products that matter. Every role at Thinkific contributes to raising the bar for online learning, supporting learning businesses, and creating real-world impact. You'll work alongside curious, collaborative teammates who care deeply about what they build and who they build it for." They stress a "fair, inclusive, and human hiring experience" with a public Candidate Hub, and lean on the "Thinker" identity ("Thinker Holidays", "an amazing team of talented, passionate, and kind Thinkers"). Culture claims are externally certified: "Certified Great Place to Work and one of Canada's Most Admired Corporate Cultures by Waterstone". They also open postings with a land acknowledgement for the Musqueam, Squamish and Tsleil-Waututh Nations. Notably absent from their own copy: hustle/"fast-paced"/rocketship language.
Size / stage / funding: 300+ employees. Publicly traded (TSX: THNC) — comp package explicitly includes "base salary, equity, team-wide bonuses, and an Employee Share Purchase Plan", which only a public issuer offers.
Locations / HQ: HQ in Vancouver, British Columbia. Postings are listed as "Distributed - Canada".
Remote policy: Remote-first / "Flexible Work". Their own words: "Choose to work from home from anywhere in Canada, at our Vancouver HQ, a co-working space, or anywhere there's wifi for a change of scenery." Company-owned MacBook Pro plus a home-office budget is provided.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified YES — and Canada is the only geography.** Roles are posted as "Distributed - Canada" and the benefits section states work-from-home from anywhere in Canada. BC HQ means Pacific/Mountain-workable hours. This is one of the few companies in the batch where a Kimberley, BC candidate is squarely in the hiring pool.
Engineering & tech: R&D org with a Lead Architect, Directors of Engineering, and a senior→staff→senior-staff→principal→architect ladder (the Principal Engineer JD describes leading "20+ Engineers" across multiple teams). SaaS on a "shared code and infrastructure model", cloud-native architecture, one or more web frameworks (Rails heritage). Principals own architecture, technical roadmap and SaaS unit economics — the JD explicitly makes them "accountable for the overall costs, reliability, and scalability of your domains", i.e. COGS ownership. Public technical presence is expected: "regularly contributing to the engineering blog, giving talks at conferences". On-call rotations are part of the role. Data Engineer role exists alongside, so there is a real data platform.
Notable / other: Pay transparency is unusually good — JDs post a three-point CAD band (min – midpoint – max) and state that "Most new hires start between the" lower part of the range. Benefits: comprehensive health/vision/dental from Day 1, $3,000 mental health care, $900/yr spending account, up to 32 weeks parental top-up, fertility coverage, flexible PTO with an encouraged 4-week minimum, Thinker Holidays, 3 paid volunteer days, $2,000/yr Learn and Grow fund.
Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Software Engineer (Distributed - Canada); Data Engineer (Distributed - Canada).
