# Skimmer — research
Provider/key: ashby:skimmer | company_type: product
What they do: Skimmer sells a SaaS pool-service platform to pool and spa service/repair businesses — route and job management, billing, customer communication, plus a mobile app used by technicians in the field. In their words the mission is "to modernize the pool and spa service and repair industry." 7,000+ customers, in a market they size at "over 70,000 pool service companies in this surprisingly large 'niche' space."
How they describe themselves: Values stated verbatim on the JD — at Skimmer we: "Realize our customers' success is our success"; "Build humble, diverse teams who have fun winning"; "Drive results with urgency." Tone elsewhere: "We're looking for big thinkers with small egos, so let's dive in!", "collaborative culture, focus on customers, and emphasis on having a good time together", "we work hard, celebrate wins, and learn as we go".
Size / stage / funding: Private-equity backed. Recognition claimed: Inc. 5000 fastest-growing companies, Deloitte Fast 500, Built In Austin's Best Startups, Austin's Best Places for Working Parents. Self-reported NPS 68. Exact headcount not stated; the engineering org is small enough that a "Principal Software Engineer" leads "a small team" reporting directly to the CTO.
Locations / HQ: Downtown office space in Austin, TX (primary/HQ) and Toronto, ON.
Remote policy: Stated verbatim: "Skimmer has downtown office spaces in Austin, TX and Toronto, ON, and remote-first employees around the US. Right now we can hire hybrid employees in Austin and Toronto, or remote employees in the following states: Alabama, California, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Indiana, Kansas, Kentucky, Maryland, Michigan, Missouri, North Carolina, Ohio, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, and Virginia." Benefits list says "Remote work friendly."
Remote-Canada eligibility: **NO — Canada is hybrid-Toronto only.** The only Canadian hiring channel they state is hybrid employees in the Toronto office; every remote option they list is a US state. There is no remote-Canada employment path stated, and none for British Columbia in any form.
Engineering & tech: Node.js and PostgreSQL are the stated core stack. Heavy emphasis on AI-assisted development — "Proficiency leveraging and implementing AI tools (preferably Claude) for development productivity" — and on replatforming: "Drive the replatforming of existing features to be AI-first and interactive for our pros and their customers." The Principal role is an explicit **player/coach people-leadership** role over a New Product Development team, reporting to the CTO, partnering with Product, Finance, Customer Success and Support.
Notable / other: Benefits are substantive — 100% employee medical/dental/vision premium and 50% for dependents, immediate 401(k) with match, flexible PTO with a 10-day annual minimum, 12 weeks paid parental leave for the birthing parent / 6 weeks for the partner, learning budget. Two live relevant roles, neither stating pay.
Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Software Engineer (Remote); Data Engineer (Remote).
