# Doppel — research
Provider/key: ashby:doppel | company_type: product

What they do: Doppel describes itself as "the world's first platform for Social Engineering Defense" and "the first platform built to dismantle digital deception at scale". Its AI-native platform unifies three products — Digital Risk Protection (detecting and taking down phishing sites, impersonation domains, fake social accounts and marketplace listings), Human Risk Management (phishing simulation and security awareness training), and Email Security (advanced phishing detection plus takedown of the attacker infrastructure behind it). They claim to "scan over 150 billion entities daily" and to run "continuously adaptive AI SOC agents, paired with expert human analysts". Named customers on their site include Coinbase, Shopify, Notion, OpenAI and Tripadvisor.

How they describe themselves: "We're not just another cybersecurity company. We're defining the future of social engineering defense, where trust is protected, and deception becomes unprofitable." Their culture statement, quoted verbatim from the JDs: "A mission-driven culture with low ego, high ownership, deep customer obsession, and exceptional talent density". They also say "Our team combines deep cybersecurity expertise, operational rigor, and startup velocity" and are "growing fast", seeking people "driven to solve real-world problems with bold technology". Perks listed: "Free lunch and dinner in the office", "Flexible PTO", "Quarterly team offsites".

Size / stage / funding: "a rapidly growing Series C startup", "Backed by leading investors including Andreessen Horowitz and Bessemer Venture Partners". Headcount is not disclosed on their site or JDs. Not profitable/established — explicitly venture-stage.

Locations / HQ: US-headquartered (San Francisco Bay Area, per the non-TOR postings) with a distinct **Toronto** engineering team — several roles are suffixed "(TOR)" and one JD says "As part of our Toronto team, you'll…". A New York ML role is also posted. HQ city is not stated on the pages fetched.

Remote policy: **Office-based.** No remote policy is stated anywhere on their site or in the JDs, and the benefits list ("Free lunch and dinner in the office", quarterly offsites) presumes daily office attendance. All engineering postings carry a specific city (Toronto, NY, SF), not "Remote".

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — Canada-eligible but not remote-Canada.** Doppel demonstrably employs in Canada (a Toronto entity paying in CAD), but the Toronto roles read as in-office Toronto positions: "As part of our Toronto team", office meals as a headline perk, and no remote language of any kind. Nothing found supports hiring a remote employee elsewhere in Canada. For a candidate who cannot relocate to Toronto this is effectively closed.

Engineering & tech: "designing scalable systems that monitor billions of domains, social media accounts, apps, dark web forums" with AI agents identifying and neutralising threats. Backend languages: "Python, Go, Java, or Node". Work includes "high-throughput ingestion, enrichment, scoring and alerting pipelines, APIs and dashboards, threat-linking infrastructure", batch and streaming data pipelines, warehouses/OLAP, SQL + NoSQL, caching, message queues, rate limiting, retries, sharding, idempotency, backpressure, plus "structured logging, tracing, dashboards, SLOs/alerts". Their Infrastructure role lists recent concrete work: "Built self-hosted Elasticsearch infrastructure on Kubernetes, enabling real-time search across millions of alerts"; "Defined core infrastructure using Terraform (IaC)"; "Stood up a dedicated staging environment"; "Introduced observability and tracing (metrics, logging, distributed tracing)". Engineers are expected to be on-call and to own "design → implementation → testing → monitoring → oncall".

Notable / other: Compensation is posted openly and is unusually high for Toronto — CAD ranges with equity on every TOR role. Roles combine deep backend with heavy cross-functional exposure: "Product ownership and excellent cross-functional communication — you'll collaborate with Operations, Customer Success, Sales, to deliver measurable outcomes" and "directly collecting customer requirements". Senior engineers are expected to "Mentor junior engineers, make key product decisions, and foster a high-performing team culture".

Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineer, Infrastructure (TOR); Software Engineer, Detection (TOR); Machine Learning Engineer, Detection (TOR); Software Engineer, Simulation (Toronto, ON); plus SF/NY roles in Autopilot, Platform and Detection.
