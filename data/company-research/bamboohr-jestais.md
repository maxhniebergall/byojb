client base includes many recognizable brands and continues to grow." The flagship product is the
**Vision Suite**, described internally as "a mission-critical ERP and supply-chain platform",
delivered both as SaaS and as customer-hosted deployments.

Engineering & tech: Microsoft/Oracle enterprise stack. **Azure DevOps** (Pipelines, Repos, Boards)
with YAML pipelines is the centre of the role; plus Git, Docker, Terraform/ARM for IaC, Oracle

scripting. Jira, Confluence, Outlook and Teams for process. The work itself is real platform
engineering: CI/CD pipeline architecture and reusable templates, release orchestration with
automated rollback, quality gates, environment provisioning, DevSecOps (secrets rotation,
least-privilege, SBOM validation, SOC2/GDPR alignment), developer self-service tooling and
documentation, observability and incident automation, and FinOps/cloud governance. AIOps appears
repeatedly as a "plus" — "Incorporate AI/AIOps insights to detect pipeline inefficiencies, predict
failures, and suggest optimizations." Requires 5+ years DevOps/CI/CD; AZ-400/AZ-104 certifications
preferred.
