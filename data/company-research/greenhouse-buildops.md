# BuildOps — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:buildops | company_type: product

What they do: BuildOps sells a vertical SaaS platform for **commercial contractors** — in their words, "a groundbreaking software solution, purpose-built to support today's commercial contractors. From helping our customers to manage their service all the way to project management." The product spans field service management and project management for commercial trades (HVAC, mechanical, electrical and similar), increasingly framed around "AI-driven tools." They describe the customer base as "the backbone of our economy."

How they describe themselves: The self-description leans hard on pace and ambition, in their own words:
- "We love driven, self-motivated folks who **thrive in fast-paced environments**. Could you be our next hire?"
- "At BuildOps, we're **changing the game** and doing the best work of our careers."
- Their investors' money "is fueling our **aggressive growth** and our commitment to equipping contractors with AI-driven tools to **conquer chaos, boost efficiency, skyrocket profitability**."
- "If you're ready to **tackle big challenges**, work with a passionate team, and **build something extraordinary**, BuildOps is the place for you. 🚀"
- The Senior SWE posting adds: "**fast-paced startup environment where priorities evolve** and strong judgment matters" and "Fast-paced, collaborative, and dynamic work environment."
No values list, engineering principles, or handbook was published in the JD text; the culture content is entirely growth/pace framing.

Size / stage / funding: Venture-backed growth-stage startup. Named investors and backers include **Next47**, former Twitter CEO **Dick Costolo**, former Salesforce President **Gavin Patterson**, and Boost Mobile CEO **Stephen Stokols**. Explicitly in an "aggressive growth" phase. Headcount and profitability not stated and unverified.

Locations / HQ: US-headquartered (Los Angeles area; the JD cites the California Fair Chance Act, confirming a California nexus). Offices named in the JD include **Toronto** and **Raleigh**, "with hybrid work schedules and lunch provided for in-office days." Both relevant postings are located in **Toronto, Ontario, Canada**.

Remote policy: **Hybrid, mandated and specific.** Stated as a requirement in the Staff SWE JD's own qualifications list: "Ability to work a hybrid schedule — **Monday/Friday WFH, Tuesday – Thursday, in office**." The Senior SWE posting repeats it: "hybrid schedule with work from home on Monday and Friday and in-office collaboration Tuesday through Thursday." Benefits mention "Flexible PTO and hybrid work schedules."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified NO.** BuildOps clearly can employ Canadians — there is a Toronto office and both relevant roles are Toronto-located with CAD pay ranges. But three mandatory in-office days per week is written into the role requirements, not offered as a preference. Unworkable from Kimberley BC.

Engineering & tech: Explicitly **full-stack**, and the JD is emphatic about it. The Staff role's primary focus is "to architect, build, and optimize our industry-leading platform **across the entire stack**, from scalable frontend applications to high-performance backend services." Named stack: **React.js** with state management, **Node.js**, **GraphQL** (Apollo, Relay) and REST, SQL (PostgreSQL/MySQL), **Material-UI** design system, event-driven architecture and messaging (Kafka, RabbitMQ), real-time data processing, Docker/Kubernetes, AWS/GCP/Azure, CI/CD, Jest and React Testing Library; Python "a plus." Responsibilities include owning "full-stack observability," database design and query optimisation, defining testing strategy, mentoring engineers, running design reviews and pair programming, and "lead technical initiatives that impact multiple teams." Requirements: 8+ years with "a strong focus on full-stack development in modern JavaScript/TypeScript," plus a B.S./M.S./Ph.D. in CS or related. They also expect communication "with engineers, product managers, **customers, partners**, and other leaders."

Notable / other: Benefits include a generous equity grant ("become an owner in our company"), a comprehensive benefits package, flexible PTO, and lunch on in-office days. The JD carries an unusually detailed recruiting-fraud warning directing candidates to verify openings at buildops.com/careers.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (Toronto, ON); Staff Software Engineer (Toronto, ON); Senior Backend Engineer.
