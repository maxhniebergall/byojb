# Block, Inc. — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:block | company_type: product

What they do: "Block, Inc. (NYSE: XYZ) builds technology to increase access to the global economy. Each of our brands unlocks different aspects of the economy for more people. **Square** makes commerce and financial services accessible to sellers. **Cash App** is the easy way to spend, send, and store money. **Afterpay** is transforming the way customers manage their spending over time. **TIDAL** is a music platform that empowers artists to thrive as entrepreneurs. **Bitkey** is a simple self-custody wallet built for bitcoin. **Proto** is a suite of bitcoin mining products and services. Together, we're helping build a financial system that is open to everyone." On Cash App specifically: "It all started with an idea at Block in 2013. Initially built to take the pain out of peer-to-peer payments, Cash App has gone from a simple product with a single purpose to a dynamic ecosystem, developing unique financial products, including Afterpay/Clearpay, to provide a better way to send, spend, invest, borrow and save to our **50+ million monthly active customers**." Domain: consumer and merchant fintech, payments, lending, tax filing, bitcoin infrastructure.

How they describe themselves: The unifying line is "**Block is one company built from many blocks, all united by the same purpose of economic empowerment**." Their careers copy frames the mission as building "simple, powerful tools that make progress towards an economy that's truly open to all." On the corporate/platform side they describe "foundational teams — People, Finance, Counsel, Hardware, Information Security, Platform Infrastructure Engineering, and more — [that] provide support and guidance at the corporate level. They work across business groups and around the globe, spanning time zones and disciplines… Every challenge creates possibilities, and we need different perspectives to see them all. Bring yours to Block." Cash App's own framing: "a culture geared toward innovation, collaboration and impact". Notably absent across all four JD bodies read: any "fast-paced", "hustle", "scrappy", "rocketship" or "wear many hats" language.

Size / stage / funding: Publicly traded on the NYSE under ticker XYZ — an established public company, not runway-dependent. "Cash App has thousands of employees working globally" and Block overall is a multi-brand organisation in the tens of thousands. Precise current headcount and profitability were not verified first-party in this session. **Unverified caution:** large public fintechs including Block have gone through well-publicised workforce reductions in recent years; that was not confirmed here and should be checked before treating stability as settled.

Locations / HQ: US-headquartered, global. Live Canadian engineering postings in the registry are in **Toronto, Ontario** and **Vancouver, British Columbia**. Their careers site filters by "Remote" as a location option.

Remote policy: **Distributed by default.** Cash App's own words: "Today, Cash App has thousands of employees working globally across office and remote locations… **We've been a distributed team since day one, and many of our roles can be done remotely from the countries where Cash App operates.** No matter the location, we tailor our experience to ensure our employees are creative, productive, and happy." Benefits copy leads with "Remote work, medical insurance, flexible time off, retirement savings plans, and modern family planning". Their compensation structure is explicitly geo-zoned ("Zone A" / "Zone B" bands on every JD), which is characteristic of an employer that expects people to live away from an office.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **YES, with one caveat worth checking.** Canada is plainly a country where Cash App operates and hires (live postings in both Toronto and Vancouver), and the company states most roles "can be done remotely from the countries where Cash App operates". Every Canadian posting quotes its pay in CAD with two geographic zones, which only makes sense if location within the country varies. **Caveat:** the individual JDs are tagged to a city (Toronto / Vancouver) rather than "Remote, Canada", so remote-from-British-Columbia is a strong inference from their stated distributed policy and the existence of a Vancouver, BC posting, not an explicit per-posting guarantee. A candidate in Kimberley, BC would fall under the same provincial employment setup as the Vancouver posting; whether "Zone B" or a non-metro location is accepted should be confirmed with a recruiter.

Engineering & tech: Distinct organisations with genuinely different characters. **Cash App product engineering** — e.g. Tax Engine Server Engineering: "build and enhance the codebase that powers our tax filing experience", "translate complex tax rules and requirements into clean, maintainable, and well-tested code", "build tools and guardrails that enable non-technical contributors to make code changes safely that maintain our quality standards", "drive architectural and design decisions, ensuring our tax software remains scalable, secure, and maintainable". **Business Platform (Bizplat)** — an internal automation platform org: "Block's Business Platform (Bizplat) organization powers operational automation across the company, focused on Risk & Compliance, Customer Support, GTM and Sales, Finance, and Hardware Supply Chain… as we scale from isolated automation initiatives to a company-wide automation platform and operating model, we face a critical architectural challenge: too many services and too much fragmentation." The Tech Lead there is "the overall systems architect for Bizplat — someone who can see across our sprawling service landscape, identify redundancy and fragmentation, and drive consolidation onto well-architected central systems… the technical decision-maker responsible for turning down the overwhelming number of services we've accumulated and replacing them with unified, scalable, and governable platform capabilities. This is a role for a strong technical leader who pairs deep systems design expertise with the judgment to make hard tradeoffs across automation domains." A separate "Platform Infrastructure Engineering" foundational team is named in their corporate copy.

Notable / other: Hiring process specifics stated on every JD: "Candidates may submit up to **9 active applications within a 60-day period**. Reapplications to the same role are accepted **90 days** after a previous application has been reviewed." They also disclose AI use in screening: "We may use automated AI tools to evaluate job applications for efficiency and consistency. These tools comply with local regulations, including bias audits." Benefits are published at https://block.xyz/employee-benefits. The registry shows a broad Canadian engineering footprint including ML and SRE roles (Principal Engineer AI Systems; Senior ML Engineer, AI Personalization; Senior ML Engineer, Model Risk Management; Senior Site Reliability Engineer; Staff Applied ML Engineer – Fraud & Abuse).

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Cash App Taxes (Toronto, ON); Staff Software Engineer, Cash App Banking (Toronto, ON); Tech Lead, Business Platform Engineering (Bizplat) (Toronto, ON); Software Engineer, Cash App – Lending Products (Vancouver, BC).
