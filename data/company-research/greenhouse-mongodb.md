# MongoDB — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:mongodb | company_type: product

What they do: MongoDB builds the general-purpose document database of the same name and MongoDB Atlas, its managed cloud data platform. In their own words: "MongoDB is built for change, empowering our customers and our people to innovate at the speed of the market. We have redefined the data platform for the AI era, enabling builders to create, transform, and disrupt industries with software. MongoDB's unified data platform, the most widely available, globally distributed data platform on the market, helps organizations modernize legacy workloads, embrace innovation, and unleash AI. Our cloud-native platform, MongoDB Atlas, is the only globally distributed, multi-cloud data platform and is available across AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure." Products in the current job set span the core server, Atlas Search (full-text and vector search over the MongoDB Query Language), Atlas cluster fleet management, application migration/modernization tooling, code generation, and internal business systems.

How they describe themselves: They cite over 67,000 customers including 75% of the Fortune 100 plus AI-native startups, and say "we're powering the next era of software." Their stated cultural anchor is the "Leadership Commitment" — "Our compass at MongoDB is our Leadership Commitment, guiding how and why we make decisions, show up for each other, and win. It's what makes us MongoDB." On people: "To drive the personal growth and business impact of our employees, we're committed to developing a supportive and enriching culture for everyone," with employee affinity groups, fertility assistance and a generous parental-leave policy. They also disclose that "AI is used to review applications based on job-related criteria and does not replace human decision-making. The hiring team decide who moves forward." Team-level JD language is notably unhurried and craft-oriented rather than hustle-flavored: "a collaborative team that prioritizes sound technical decision-making and building systems that our customers love and that we are proud of as engineers," with explicit 3/6/12-month success measures and "the opportunity to lead projects and own subsystems."

Size / stage / funding: Public company (NASDAQ: MDB), large and long-established, offices worldwide. Not runway-dependent. Employee benefits referenced in Canadian JDs indicate a mature HR function: equity, employee stock purchase program, flexible PTO, 20 weeks fully-paid gender-neutral parental leave, fertility and adoption assistance, RRSP with employer match, mental-health counseling, backup child and elder care, and health/dental/vision.

Locations / HQ: New York City HQ, offices worldwide. Canadian engineering presence in Toronto (large) plus roles open to Montreal, Vancouver, Calgary and province-wide postings across Alberta, British Columbia, Manitoba, Nova Scotia, Ontario and Quebec. US hubs across Austin, Boston, Chicago, Denver, Miami, NYC, Philadelphia, Pittsburgh, Raleigh, San Francisco, Seattle and Washington DC; European office in Dublin.

Remote policy: Mixed and set per-requisition — this is the single most important nuance for this company. Some roles are explicitly hybrid and city-bound ("This role is based in Toronto, ON hybrid"); others are explicitly remote ("remote from anywhere in North America", "remotely in the North America region", "remote or hybrid anywhere in the USA or Canada", "remotely in Canada or the USA"); others are remote but time-zone-bound ("remotely in the Canada while physically based in an Eastern or Central time zone", "remote while working from a location based in either Eastern or Central"). One JD notes the team supports "remote work and align projects with convenient work hours for each" team member. Each requisition must be read individually.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes, and specifically BC-eligible on several requisitions.** MongoDB employs directly in Canada (RRSP with employer match, Canadian base-salary bands posted under Canadian pay-transparency practice). Roles explicitly listing British Columbia include Senior Staff Engineer, AMP (British Columbia); Software Engineer, Code Generation (British Columbia; Calgary); Staff Engineer, Code Generation (Alberta; BC; Manitoba; Nova Scotia; Ontario; Quebec); and Staff Software Engineer, Query Optimization (same province list). Server Security is "remote or hybrid anywhere in the USA or Canada." **Caveat:** the SRE roles are largely Eastern/Central-time-bound or Toronto-hybrid, so most of the SRE volume is NOT Mountain-Time workable. The Atlas Search and Documentation Platform SWE3 roles are Toronto-based (one explicitly hybrid).

Engineering & tech: Distributed-systems and database internals work. Atlas Search team builds "cloud-based distributed systems software responsible for the lifecycle of search indexes including: data ingestion, index building, partitioning, performance, availability, and backup management," in Java ("experience with at least one modern statically typed programming language, and interest in working with Java"). Other areas: query optimization internals, storage-layer services, cluster fleet management, deployment automation, application migration/modernization, code generation (LLM-adjacent), server security, and business-systems/GTM engineering (NetSuite, FP&A integrations — a distinctly different, IT-flavored track). Teams describe owning subsystems, providing roadmap input and determining architecture.

Notable / other: MongoDB posts base-salary ranges on Canadian requisitions as well as US ones, and the Canadian ranges are stated in CAD — an unusually good pay-transparency posture for a Canada-based candidate. Canadian ranges observed in the current live set: $108,000–$149,000 (Software Engineer 3 / Software Engineer level); $137,000–$189,000 (Senior Software Engineer, Server Security); $144,000–$200,000 (SRE Senior-or-Staff roles); $159,000–$221,000 (Staff Engineer, Code Generation and Staff Software Engineer, Query Optimization); $209,000–$290,000 (Senior Staff Engineer, AMP). US ranges for comparable roles run $106,000–$249,000 USD, with Senior Staff AMP at $211,000–$363,000 USD. Note that the "Senior Engineer — Business Systems / GTM Tech / NetSuite" postings are enterprise-IT roles, not product engineering, despite matching on title keywords.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Staff Engineer, AMP (British Columbia); Staff Engineer, Code Generation (AB/BC/MB/NS/ON/QC); Staff Software Engineer, Query (Optimization) (AB/BC/MB/NS/ON/QC); Software Engineer, Code Generation (British Columbia; Calgary); Senior Software Engineer, Server Security (Toronto / remote USA or Canada); Site Reliability Engineering, Fabric (Mid, Senior, or Staff) (Toronto; Vancouver); Software Engineer 3, Atlas Search Systems (Toronto, hybrid).
