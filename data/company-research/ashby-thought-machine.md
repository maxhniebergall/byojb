# Thought Machine — research
Provider/key: ashby:thought-machine | company_type: product

What they do: Thought Machine builds cloud-native core banking and payments technology. Their flagship product is Vault, a core banking platform intended to replace banks' legacy mainframe cores. In their words: "Thought Machine's mission is bold – to properly and permanently rid the world's banks of legacy technology. To achieve this, we have developed the foundations of modern banking through core and payments technology which run natively in the cloud. What we are attempting is hard and means we need great people working together to build great technology." Customers "range from the world's largest financial institutions to smaller regional banks."

How they describe themselves: Engineering-excellence-forward. "We have created a culture that enables our team to produce the best work in the industry while ensuring we have fun along the way." On engineering: "Software Engineering is a key role within Thought Machine. As an engineering-focused company, we take pride in our engineering excellence, adopting best practices, and utilising modern technologies." And on scope: "Software engineers work across the stack to solve complex engineering problems that have not been solved before... while development at Thought Machine is fast-paced, you will be expected to develop code to the highest standards that scales, is secure, performant, and provides a great user experience. Software Engineers will take complete ownership of a requirement, converting it into an engineering design, task breakdown, and implementation. You'll be responsible for building both backend services and frontend experiences." They also expect engineers to "bridge the gap between back-end and front-end" and note "Interest in working on client-facing projects, conducting scoping and defining deliverables" as a requirement. Back End Engineering specifically: "we pride ourselves in excellence in this role, adopting the best practices in continuous deployment monorepo style development." Note the phrase "fast-paced" appears twice, verbatim.

Size / stage / funding: "We have grown rapidly in the past few years – growing our team to more than 550 individuals across offices in London, New York, Singapore, Sydney and our newly established Engineering Hub in Lisbon. We have raised more than £500m in funding and our investors include Molten Ventures, Eurazeo, Intesa Sanpaolo, Temasek, Nyca Partners, JPMorgan Chase Strategic Investments, Standard Chartered Ventures, and more." So: ~550+ people, private, heavily funded, with strategic bank investors.

Locations / HQ: HQ London. Offices in London, New York, Singapore and Sydney, plus a newly established Engineering Hub in Lisbon. All 11 live engineering postings are London or Lisbon only.

Remote policy: Onsite-heavy and explicit about it. One JD states flatly: "This is a full-time, permanent position based in our Lisbon office, requiring four days a week onsite." No remote-first or async signal anywhere; the model is office-based with limited flexibility.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — definitively. Every live posting is "United Kingdom, London" or "Portugal, Lisbon", the roles are explicitly office-based four days a week, and a full-text scan of every fetched Thought Machine JD returned zero occurrences of "Canada"/"Canadian". There is no North American engineering hiring at all (the New York office does not appear in any engineering req), and even if there were, the timezone gap from Mountain Time to London/Lisbon is 7-8 hours.

Engineering & tech: Python and Golang are the required languages ("Experience with either Python or Golang"). Practices: microservices ("Design, implement and develop scalable, performant microservices using best practices"), continuous deployment, monorepo development, automated unit and integration testing as "an integral part of the development cycle", and engineers "Managing and debugging your deployments from testing environments all the way to production". Roles cover Back End, Infrastructure, and SRE. Expectation is genuinely full-stack despite the backend titles — "crafting delightful experiences that delight all our clients" — plus "Translating customer requirements into trackable tickets."

Notable / other: Externally well-regarded — "regularly cited as having a fantastic workplace culture", "recognised by Sifted magazine as having one of the highest Glassdoor ratings for a UK fintech company and the industry's most generous employee share package", "Named one of the world's most innovative fintechs by Global Finance Magazine", "recognised by the Financial Times as one of Europe's fastest-growing companies for two consecutive years—and a UK Best Employer for 2026" (all their own claims). Pay is posted in EUR for the Lisbon roles: Back End Engineer EUR 55-75K; Senior Back End Engineer I EUR 75-100K; Senior Software Engineer II EUR 100-120K; Senior Software Engineer (Infrastructure) EUR 80-120K; Software Engineer (Infrastructure) EUR 55-80K. Their internal ladder is confusing: "Senior Software Engineer I" sits below "Senior Software Engineer II", and both sit above plain "Software Engineer".

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (Infrastructure) (London / Lisbon); Software Engineer (Infrastructure) (London / Lisbon); Senior Back End Engineer (London); Senior Back End Engineer I (Lisbon); Senior Software Engineer II (London / Lisbon); Back End Engineer (London / Lisbon).
