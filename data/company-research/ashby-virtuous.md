# Virtuous — research
Provider/key: ashby:virtuous | company_type: product

What they do: Virtuous builds a "modern software platform" — a CRM+ / fundraising suite for mid-sized nonprofits, covering fundraising, marketing, volunteerism and online giving. Customers are charities and nonprofit organizations. Their stated mission is "to inspire global generosity by helping nonprofits build better relationships with their donors." They are currently pushing an "AI-first" product direction on top of the CRM and building an "AI-ready" internal data platform.

How they describe themselves: "Our talented team is driven to disrupt the status quo in the nonprofit sector. We are hungry, humble, and committed to delivering best-in-class software solutions, customer success interactions, and sales experiences." They emphasise "radical generosity" — volunteering at nonprofits, "going the extra mile." Tone: "We take our work seriously, but we don't take ourselves too seriously. We believe that life is too short not to love what you do." Their published behavioural values (from the JDs, listed in full):
- Asking questions with a spirit of curiosity
- Giving feedback freely with candor & grace, welcoming it in return
- Displaying a passion for philanthropy and technology
- Serving with joy. "Everyone is willing to make the coffee!"
- Celebrating the wins & milestones of others
- Assuming good intent & demonstrating trust in others
- Pursuing relationships with people different from themselves & creating space to be human

Size / stage / funding: Self-described as "a high-growth SaaS company with a 30-person EPD team" (engineering + product + design). Total headcount, funding stage and profitability are **not verified** — no first-party page was fetched (see Sources).

Locations / HQ: Phoenix, Arizona. Postings are listed as "Phoenix, AZ, Remote, USA" / "Remote, USA, Phoenix, AZ".

Remote policy: Remote is offered, but every posting scopes it to "Remote, USA". No async / handbook signals found; the CRM+ role explicitly expects customer-discovery conversations, RCAs and "occasional customer demos", i.e. a customer-facing engineering posture.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — not eligible (high confidence).** Both live postings say "Remote, USA" and give no non-US option. This is a US-only remote employer on the available evidence.

Engineering & tech:
- CRM+ product: C#/.NET (latest version), Vue (React/Angular transfers), SQL Server (schema design, query optimization), Azure. Datadog and Playwright mentioned as pluses. Frequent deploy cadence (multiple times per week), CI/CD.
- Data platform: Snowflake + dbt, dbt/Snowflake CI-CD, role-based access control incl. row- and column-level security, SSO/SCIM integration with an enterprise IdP, warehouse cost governance. The Lead Data Platform Engineer role reports to a Director of Data Operations and is explicitly framed as durable platform work: "This is not a traditional data engineering role focused on building one-off pipelines, dashboards, or ad hoc reports… designing and building durable platform capabilities — security models, access patterns, cost controls, and shared data foundations."
- Strong stated AI-assisted-development mandate: the Senior SWE role requires "Genuine enthusiasm for AI-assisted development. Hands-on experience using Claude Code as part of your engineering workflow."

Notable / other: The CRM+ role is framed as "AI-Forward" and "thinks like a Product Engineer" — "You won't just implement stories… operate with high agency and ownership within your squad" and "We move at the speed of a startup." Benefits mentioned: market-competitive pay "leveraging Carta data", Bonusly recognition, 401(k). The data-platform role wants "a highly motivated self-starter who is comfortable with ambiguity."

Open relevant roles (sample): Lead Data Platform Engineer (Phoenix, AZ / Remote USA); Senior Software Engineer - CRM+ Platform (Remote USA / Phoenix, AZ).
