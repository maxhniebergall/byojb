# A-CX — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:acx | company_type: consulting
What they do: A-CX is a software design and development services firm, not a product company. Its own words: "A-CX is a software design and development partner for progressive companies, delivering innovative services through dedicated teams and customized solutions." It supplies outsourced/dedicated engineering teams across frontend and backend development, AI, cloud architecture and DevOps. Its JDs place engineers on client projects ("you would be a part of the team implementing solutions for our client"), and it describes its clients as "brands you recognize, Tier 1 leaders in their field, including high-tech companies and financial services". It is a recognized Microsoft and AWS partner for building AI solutions on their clouds.
How they describe themselves: "We're a team of dynamic, diverse, and fun individuals"; "scalable custom services of the highest quality, exceeding expectations"; "award-winning products"; a "collaborative, trusting, and curiosity-driven culture" maintained through "regular face-to-face events in addition to our online and VR meetings".
Size / stage / funding: Not stated; private services firm. Headcount unverified.
Locations / HQ: "A-CX operates in Vancouver, B.C.; Silicon Valley, CA; Dallas, TX; and Finland." Postings are listed in Vancouver, British Columbia.
Remote policy: Mixed and explicitly office-leaning. "Our team works remotely, but we arrange regular face-to-face events" and "we prefer people who live in the Greater Vancouver area"; the DevOps posting states "many of our roles involve a hybrid model: working directly with client teams onsite in Vancouver, combined with A-CX team collaboration across our global offices" and closes by welcoming applicants who can "contribute to this collaborative, partly onsite way of working".
Remote-Canada eligibility: They employ in Canada (Vancouver, CAD salaries posted), but roles are Greater-Vancouver-preferred and partly onsite at client sites — **not a remote-from-Kimberley arrangement.**
Engineering & tech: Client-project work. C++ role: cross-platform desktop audio software, plugin standards (VST3, AU, AAX), DAW/NLE workflows, embedded and distributed systems, Azure and/or AWS. DevOps role: Azure-centric — CI/CD (Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions), Docker/Kubernetes, IaC via Terraform/Bicep/ARM, monitoring with Prometheus/Grafana/ELK, Zero Trust security models, hybrid/multi-cloud, agile/scrum ceremonies.
Notable / other: They recruit both contractors and full-time employees for "long-term projects". Both open postings state CAD salary ranges (see comp record).
Open relevant roles (sample): Software Developer, C++ (Vancouver); DevOps Engineer (Vancouver).
