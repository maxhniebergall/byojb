# Sun Life — research
Provider/key: workday:sunlife/experienced-jobs | company_type: product

What they do: Sun Life Financial is a large, long-established Canadian financial services and
insurance group (life & health insurance, group benefits, wealth and asset management), operating
internationally with a major Canadian and Asian footprint plus US group-benefits and dental
businesses. Publicly traded (TSX/NYSE: SLF). The engineering org here is an in-house corporate IT
and platform function serving the insurance and wealth businesses — not a software product company.

How they describe themselves: The JD boilerplate on every posting is client-and-colleague framed:
*"You are as unique as your background, experience and point of view. Here, you'll be encouraged,
empowered and challenged to be your best self. You'll work with dynamic colleagues - experts in
their fields... Every day, you'll have new and exciting opportunities to make life brighter for our
Clients - who are at the heart of everything we do."* Stated values emphasize diversity and
inclusion: *"Diversity and inclusion have always been at the core of our values at Sun Life. A
diverse workforce with wide perspectives and creative ideas benefits our Clients, the communities
where we operate and all of us as colleagues."* Tone is corporate, benefits-forward, low-hype —
notably free of "fast-paced"/"hustle" language.

Size / stage / funding: Very large public company (tens of thousands of employees globally);
established, profitable, dividend-paying. The opposite of runway risk.

Locations / HQ: Toronto, Ontario HQ. Canadian tech postings cluster in Toronto, North York and
Waterloo, Ontario; several roles list "2/3/4 Locations" which resolve to Ontario cities.

Remote policy: Explicitly **hybrid**, stated on every JD: *"We are proud to be a hybrid
organization that offers our employees the choice and flexibility to work from both the office and
virtually based on the needs of the business, our Clients and you! Several work options are
available and can be discussed throughout the selection process depending on the role requirements
and individual needs."*

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No, not for British Columbia.** This is Canada-based employment, but
the tech roles are anchored to Ontario offices (Toronto / North York / Waterloo) under a hybrid
office-attendance model. "Hybrid" here means splitting time with a named Ontario office, which is
not workable from Kimberley, BC. The flexibility language is about days-in-office, not geography.

Engineering & tech: AWS-centric. The Senior AI Engineer role covers API infrastructure on AWS,
AWS Lambda, Python and Java, GenAI piloting, RAG and chatbot/agent development, model component
versioning across dev and prod, IaC and infrastructure automation, CI/CD ownership and DevOps
guardrails ("enforce software guardrails, increase automation, help engineers build code faster,
at a lower cost, and lower risk"), plus post-production support. Work is architected top-down —
engineers "work closely with the Enterprise Architect to design and implement solutions that align
with the platform capabilities roadmap." Elsewhere the stack is enterprise Java: Kafka, APIs,
Informatica Cloud, and there is still **mainframe** and even **Lotus Notes** maintenance work in
the portfolio, which tells you a lot about the estate.

Notable / other: Job category on these reqs is "IT - Application Development". Postings disclose
that *"We may use artificial intelligence to support candidate sourcing, screening, interview
scheduling."* Base pay is supplemented by discretionary incentive plans.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Engineer; Lead Software Engineer - MLOps; Senior AI Engineer;
Lead Machine Learning Engineer; Senior Specialist - Cloud DevOps; Senior Data Engineer I; Senior
Software Engineer, Operations; Senior Java Developer (Kafka/API); Security Platform Engineer -
EDR/Proxy; Software Engineer (Mainframe).

Pay (stated on their own JDs): Sun Life posts a salary range on its Workday reqs. Senior AI
Engineer (Toronto): **CAD 90,000–140,000** base, plus discretionary incentive plans. Note this is
low for a senior AI/ML infrastructure role by market standards.
