# NexHealth — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:nexhealth | company_type: product

What they do: NexHealth builds healthcare integration infrastructure. In their words: "NexHealth's mission is to accelerate innovation in healthcare by connecting patients, providers, and developers. We're building the infrastructure layer for modern healthcare, connecting thousands of fragmented, on-premise, and closed EHR systems into a single, modern platform that powers software, APIs, payments, and patient experiences across the ecosystem." Concretely there are two products on one base: "the world's most advanced healthcare integration API that synchronizes in real-time" with electronic health record systems — used by health-tech developers at companies including Swell, Truelark, Quip and Cherry — plus "our own doctor facing SaaS product on top of our own API" (patient scheduling, reminders, payments for dental/medical practices).

How they describe themselves: Their framing is the analog-healthcare gap: "Our healthcare system remains frustratingly analog. When you live in a world of one-tap car rides, instant meal delivery, and unlimited streaming, why do you still have to call to schedule a doctor's appointment and fill out a clipboard in the waiting room?" They position as "the infrastructure layer that modern healthcare needs" and are "Trusted by tens of thousands of providers and hundreds of health-tech developers." Growth is the recurring theme in the engineering JDs — "our exponentially growing customer base", "dev ops for our next 100x growth", "unlock 100x growth in the number of practices, patients, and developers", "ambitious engineering goals."

Size / stage / funding: Stated first-party on their JDs: Founded 2017; Headquarters San Francisco, CA; Funding "$177M Series C"; Employees "200+". Private, venture-backed, late-stage growth rather than early startup. Profitability not stated.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco. Live engineering postings are Seattle, Washington (the majority) and Toronto, Ontario. A Vancouver option is referenced in the Canadian JD. They are actively opening a Canadian presence: the Toronto role notes "You'll [be] one of NexHealth's first engineers based in Canada — setting the technical and cultural tone for what will become a larger team as we expand our footprint north of the border."

Remote policy: **Hybrid, in-office anchored, and stated explicitly.** The Canadian JD: "This role will be based out of Toronto or Vancouver and embraces a hybrid working model that balances remote and onsite work (the specifics of which are at the discretion of NexHealth management)." Note the discretion clause — the onsite share is not fixed and is management's call. Extracted facets read `remote_policy: hybrid`. The US roles are Seattle-based. One title in the registry reads "Senior Software Engineer, Remote", but no live posting body supports a fully-remote option.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No.** Canada hiring exists but is metro-anchored: Toronto or Vancouver, hybrid, with the onsite balance at management's discretion, and an explicit expectation of setting "the technical and cultural tone" for an in-person Canadian team. That is not workable from Kimberley, BC. The `geo_eligibility: canada` facet on this posting means Canadian employment, not remote-anywhere-in-Canada. AODA/Ontario Human Rights Code accommodation language confirms an Ontario employment relationship.

Engineering & tech: Backend/platform-heavy and a genuinely good technical match on paper. From the Senior SWE JD, the work is: "building an open data lake for health records", "building dev ops for our next 100x growth", "defining and building the next generation of health record system integrations for the industry", "scaling our data processing and storage layer from terabytes to petabytes and beyond", and "Build and maintain bidirectional, real-time integrations with dozens of EHRs, scaling to hundreds or thousands of integrations." Requirements: 5+ years backend/platform, "Deep experience integrating and scaling storage systems, cloud-native services, and related open source projects, including the Kubernetes ecosystem, AWS, and PostgreSQL", DevOps tooling, quality and security engineering principles, and "Excellent systems-level thinking." Roles span platform, security, data engineering and staff-level IC.

Compensation: Posted directly on their JDs. Toronto Senior Software Engineer: $156,000–$188,000 CAD. Seattle Senior Software Engineer and Senior Software Engineer, Security: $165,000–$230,000 USD. Staff Software Engineer: $180,000–$240,000 USD. Four band rows already on record in `data/company-comp.jsonl`, all `direct`/`jd_posted`. Notable / other: Real infrastructure identity — an API/integration layer over closed on-prem EHR systems is hard, boring, high-moat plumbing, and the "open data lake for health records" work is substantive. The counterweights are the pervasive 100x-growth framing, a dual identity as both an API platform and a doctor-facing SaaS product, and the hybrid location requirement. Being "one of NexHealth's first engineers based in Canada" also implies expectations around in-person culture-setting.
