# State Street — research

Provider/key: workday:statestreet/global | company_type: product

What they do: State Street Corporation is one of the world's largest custodian banks and asset
servicers, plus a major asset manager. Two engineering-relevant halves show up in the
requisitions: the **custody / wealth custody & clearing** and corporate-functions technology
organizations, and **State Street Investment Management** (the asset-management arm, described
in its own JDs as "one of the largest asset managers in the world … With over four decades of
experience and trillions of dollars in assets under management … pioneers in index, ETF, and
ESG investing"). Engineering supports investment analytics, financial data engineering,
custody platforms, and the internal cloud/data platform estate.

How they describe themselves: The JDs are functional and corporate rather than values-led;
the recurring self-description is scale, rigor and a "rigorous, research-driven investment
process". Titles are banded to the bank's officer ladder (Officer → AVP → VP → VP III /
Senior Associate), which is the dominant cultural signal: seniority is expressed in bank
rank, not engineering ladder.

Size / stage / funding: Public (NYSE: STT), ~50,000 employees, ~230 years old. Maximum
institutional stability; zero runway risk. Correspondingly bureaucratic.

Locations / HQ: HQ **Boston, Massachusetts**. Engineering sited across Boston, Quincy MA,
Burlington MA, Princeton NJ, **Toronto, Ontario**, London (England), Hyderabad (India), and a
number of multi-location ("2/3/5 Locations") reqs. Very large offshore engineering presence
in India.

Remote policy: **Hybrid-mandated, office-anchored.** Their own JD language: "This role
requires a hybrid work model - at least 3 days in the office/week" (Java Developer & Team
Lead); "Onsite 4 days a week in office and one day remote" (Sr. Databricks Data Engineer —
with "Onsite" literally in the job title); "Hybrid remote working and office" (Senior Network
Infrastructure Engineer, London). Every relevant posting is tied to named office cities.
No remote-first, async, or handbook culture in evidence.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No.** There is a Canadian presence — a Toronto, Ontario req
(*Sr. Software Engineer, AVP II*, $91,000–$140,500 annual) — so the bank can employ Canadians,
but on an in-office Toronto basis. The stated policy across the estate is 3–4 days a week in
the office. A Kimberley, BC candidate who cannot relocate is not eligible for anything here;
Toronto hybrid is effectively disqualifying, and it is also Eastern Time.

Engineering & tech: Java/Spring-heavy enterprise backend; AWS and Azure cloud platform
engineering; Kubernetes; Snowflake, Databricks, data virtualization and open data platforms;
encryption-as-a-service and security platform engineering; and an emerging agentic-AI /
LLMOps line ("GHR AI Engineer – Agentic Integrations (MCP / A2A / Python / APIs), AVP";
"Sr Principal - Agentic Architecture and LLMOps Engineering, VP III"). The infrastructure
and data-platform work is real and well-resourced; the domain is regulated and internal-facing.

Notable / other: State Street posts a salary range on most US requisitions. The ranges are
extremely wide (often a $70–100k spread) because they are officer-band ranges spanning
multiple locations rather than role-specific bands — treat any single number with caution.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior AI Infrastructure Platform Engineer, AVP; Data Platform
Engineering Principal, VP; Cloud Platform Engineer, Officer; AWS Platform Engineer, AVP;
Senior Lead Data Platform Engineer, AVP (State Street Investment Management); Sr Principal -
Agentic Architecture and LLMOps Engineering, VP III; Senior Security Software Engineer - Data,
AVP; Sr. Software Engineer, AVP II (Toronto).
