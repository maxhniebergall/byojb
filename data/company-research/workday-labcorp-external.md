# Labcorp — research
Provider/key: workday:labcorp/external | company_type: product
What they do: Labcorp is a global leader in clinical diagnostics and drug-development services — laboratory testing at enormous scale for healthcare providers, patients, researchers, pharmaceutical companies and health systems. Their own words: "Through our unparalleled science, data, technology and laboratory network, we advance diagnostics, accelerate innovation and help address some of the world's most important health challenges." The engineering roles in the registry sit in **Labcorp Genetics**, specifically the Variant Knowledge Platform, and in **Personal Genome Diagnostics (PGDx)**, a Labcorp business "Empowering the Fight Against Cancer by unlocking actionable information from the genome," which builds tissue-based and non-invasive liquid-biopsy genomic products for laboratories worldwide.
How they describe themselves: "Labcorp is a leader in diagnostics, drug development and healthcare innovation. Across every role, we harness data and AI to work smarter, move faster and create breakthrough solutions that improve health outcomes for people. With our global scale and deep expertise, you'll do meaningful work, grow your career and make a real impact. Together, we're improving health and improving lives." The mission framing is repeated consistently: "Join us in our mission to improve health and improve lives." Notably the engineering JDs themselves are written in an unusually substantive, systems-first register — talking about correctness, versioning, durability, schema drift and ADRs rather than pace or hustle.
Size / stage / funding: Very large, publicly traded (NYSE: LH), global, long-established. Tens of thousands of employees worldwide. Exact current headcount/revenue not verified in this pass. Stability is not in question.
Locations / HQ: US HQ in Burlington, North Carolina; the engineering roles here are based in Durham NC (Labcorp Genetics / Variant Knowledge Platform) and Baltimore MD (PGDx). A closed posting in the registry was "IND - Remote - Bangalore Based Entity," indicating an Indian engineering entity as well.
Remote policy: Mixed, and **stated per role**. The Bioinformatics Software Engineer II posting says outright: "Labcorp is seeking a US based remote Bioinformatics Software Engineer II to join our team!" — so genuine US-remote roles exist. The Principal Software Engineer posting is tied to Durham NC and states no remote provision. Working hours are specified conventionally: "a full-time, exempt (salaried) position assigned to a First Shift schedule, with standard business hours of Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. **in your time zone**. Business needs may occasionally require flexibility in work hours ... with reasonable notice provided when possible." The "in your time zone" phrasing is genuinely accommodating of a distributed workforce.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — not for these roles.** Where remote is offered it is explicitly "US based remote"; the other engineering entity referenced is India (Bangalore). Labcorp does have operations outside the US, but none of the live postings in the registry is Canada-eligible, and none mentions Canada. Treat as US-only for hiring purposes here.
Engineering & tech: Python as the primary backend language for data-intensive services; AWS cloud services; REST API contract design with versioning, deprecation strategy and secure service-to-service auth via Okta; event-driven and asynchronous messaging (AWS SQS) with REST gateways in front; Snowflake and analytics data ecosystems, with explicit awareness of batch/lagged vs real-time trade-offs; multi-tenant platform-as-a-product systems; large-scale data migration and backfill with provenance and validation guarantees. Engineering practice signals are strong: a "you build it, you run it" operating model with owned on-call, multi-tenant observability and health monitoring; Architecture Decision Records; an Architecture Review Forum; named principal-level cross-team technical leads. The domain imposes real rigor — "Identity or schema drift in this domain can corrupt classifications platform-wide," and because variant classification flows into clinical reports the systems "must be correct, versioned, and durable," in a regulated/clinical environment with version pinning and validation requirements.
Notable / other: Pay is posted on the JDs: Principal Software Engineer - Variant Knowledge Platform $180,000-$205,000 annually (application window closing 8/7/2026); Bioinformatics Software Engineer II $100,000-$140,000 annually (USD). Offers are stated to be based on "a candidate's skills and prior relevant experience, applicable degree." Domain expertise in genomics/bioinformatics (variant interpretation, HGVS nomenclature, gene/transcript annotation, evidence-based classification) is preferred but not required for the principal role.
Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Software Engineer - Variant Knowledge Platform (Durham NC); Bioinformatics Software Engineer III - Genome Diagnostics; Bioinformatics Software Engineer II (Baltimore MD / US remote).
