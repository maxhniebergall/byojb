# Cloudera — research
Provider/key: workday:cloudera/external_career | company_type: product

What they do: Enterprise data & analytics platform ("hybrid data platform") — the commercial home of a large slice of the open-source big-data stack: Apache Spark, Iceberg, Trino, Kafka, Hive, Impala, NiFi. Sells to large regulated enterprises (Global 2000: banks, telcos, government) that want an on-prem/hybrid/multi-cloud lakehouse rather than a pure-SaaS warehouse. Recent positioning pivots to "private AI" — running inference and AI workloads on the customer's own data, in the customer's own environment.

How they describe themselves: "enable global organizations to use data and AI to solve the impossible today by transforming any data anywhere into real-time, predictive, and prescriptive insights." The current marketing frame is three pillars — **"AI Anywhere"** (development to inference with private AI), **"Cloud Anywhere"** (data center to edge with full control), **"Data Anywhere"** (automated and AI-ready for real-time insights). They describe themselves as "well-established big data pioneers" and claim "the world's largest enterprise organizations, across all industries, trust Cloudera."

Size / stage / funding: Roughly 3,000 employees. Reports "more than $1 billion in annual recurring revenue" and "more than 25 exabytes of data" under management. Formerly NYSE-listed (CLDR); **taken private in October 2021 by KKR and Clayton, Dubilier & Rice** in a ~$5.3B deal, and privately held since. Mature, revenue-heavy, not runway-dependent — but a PE-owned mature company carries its own cost-discipline pressure, and Cloudera has run repeated restructurings.

Locations / HQ: HQ Santa Clara, California. Large engineering centres in **Budapest, Hungary** and **Prague, Czech Republic**; further global offices including Spain, Korea, Japan, China, India, and a Costa Rica remote hub.

Remote policy: Officially flexible — "employee-preferred work settings" spanning office, remote and hybrid, "to align with strategic goals and ensure compliance with relevant country regulations." Benefits page advertises flexible hours, remote work, "Unplugged Days Off" (an extended weekend every 3 weeks), generous PTO, phone/internet reimbursement, and up to $5,000/yr tuition reimbursement. **Counter-signal:** press coverage has the CEO publicly encouraging all 3,000+ employees to come into the office as often as possible, framing remote work as a state where "you effectively become a faceless factor of production." Stated policy and leadership preference are not aligned.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Not demonstrated — likely no.** Across the 12 postings on record for this portal, the geographies are Hungary–Budapest, Czech Republic–Prague, US-California-Remote, US-Washington-Remote, US-Texas-Remote, Costa Rica-Remote and UAE-Dubai. **No Canadian location appears on any posting**, and their remote roles are country-scoped ("US-…-Remote"), meaning a Canadian-resident hire would need a Canadian employing entity that is nowhere in evidence. Every one of the currently relevant infra/data roles is either an EU engineering-centre role or a US-remote role.

Engineering & tech: Java and Scala dominate (the JVM big-data lineage), with Rust appearing on newer "Anywhere Cloud" work and Go/Python around the edges. Engineering is organized by open-source component — dedicated teams own Spark, Iceberg, Trino, Kafka/streaming, Public Cloud, Cloud Connectors. This is genuinely deep, well-scoped systems work with real upstream open-source participation; Cloudera engineers are committers on several ASF projects. Ladder runs Senior → Staff → Sr. Staff.

Notable / other: One of the defining companies of the Hadoop era; merged with Hortonworks in 2019, then went private in 2021. Business has since repositioned from "Hadoop distribution" to hybrid lakehouse and, most recently, private/on-prem AI. Domain is unglamorous, mission-critical, long-lived infrastructure — the opposite of a hype product.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer - Trino; Staff Software Engineer - Apache Iceberg; Staff Software Engineer - Apache Spark; Senior Software Engineer - Kafka/Streaming Team; Senior Software Engineer, Cloud Connectors (Java); Senior Software Engineer - Public Cloud (Java); Staff Software Engineer, Anywhere Cloud (Rust); Sr. Staff Engineer.
