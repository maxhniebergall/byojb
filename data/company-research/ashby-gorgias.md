# Gorgias — research
Provider/key: ashby:gorgias | company_type: product
What they do: Gorgias builds a conversational-AI customer-experience platform for ecommerce — AI agents, helpdesk, voice support and SMS for online retailers (heavily Shopify-ecosystem). In their words: "we're building the platform that makes this real: a unified AI agent that sells, supports, and re-engages customers across the entire journey. Conversational Commerce is the future of ecommerce, and we're leading that shift."
How they describe themselves: Mission — "to turn every interaction between a brand and its customers into a relationship: personal, seamless, and intelligent." They say they focus relentlessly on three things: **Quality** ("conversations that feel authentic and on-brand"), **Experience** ("effortless shopping from chat to checkout") and **Re-engagement** ("personal, 1-1 dialogue instead of noisy marketing"). The careers page lists five company values: "Always Put Our Customers First", "Strive for Excellence", "Win as a Team", "Maximize Your Impact", "Be Honest, Direct, and Kind". They call themselves "an AI-first company building products powered by LLMs and agent-based systems", and describe internal AI use as pervasive — premium AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Granola) for everyone, a #powerup Slack channel, per-team "AI champions" demoing in weekly company-wide standups.
Size / stage / funding: 400+ employees per their careers page; private growth-stage SaaS. Funding round detail not verified here.
Locations / HQ: Offices in Toronto, San Francisco, New York City, Belgrade and Paris. HQ not explicitly stated on the page fetched (the company has French origins and a strong SF/NYC presence). The Toronto office is described on the careers page as a "sales & customer success hub".
Remote policy: **Hybrid, not remote-first.** The careers page frames benefits as "5 vacation weeks & 12 remote days" and states teams gather in offices roughly 2x per week. Onboarding brings new hires to the Toronto office for a week (flights and accommodation paid). They do provide up to $900 CAD for a home workstation and up to $2,600 CAD/year of learning material; quarterly company-wide summits and annual team/company retreats.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **They employ in Canada (Toronto) but the model is hybrid, not remote-Canada.** Canadian employment is confirmed by CAD-denominated benefits and RRSP matching up to 4% on the JD. However, the Principal AI Engineer posting is located in Toronto and the stated norm is ~2 days/week in office, so a Kimberley-BC-based fully-remote arrangement is not supported by anything they publish.
Engineering & tech: The Principal AI Engineer role is squarely AI-platform work: architect an internal evaluation "factory" — moving from manual testing to an automated lifecycle covering LLM-as-a-judge creation through production monitoring; partner with the Orchestration team on infrastructure to run evals and agentic simulations at scale; build the "paved road" so product squads self-serve agent quality; own the production-insight feedback loop into eval datasets. They explicitly want "beyond the wrapper" experience — multi-stage AI orchestration, temporal systems/state machines/event-driven architectures, vector databases, semantic caching, third-party tool APIs — and describe the challenge as "not the prompt, but the orchestration, state management, and reliability of the agent's actions". 8+ years, Staff-level-first framing.
Notable / other: Hiring process for the Principal role is recruiter screen → HM interview → system design → AI case study take-home (~1-2 hours) → technical deep dive → final leadership interview. Benefits include paid sick leave, 16 weeks paid parental leave, MacBook Pro, private health insurance, RRSP with 4% match.
