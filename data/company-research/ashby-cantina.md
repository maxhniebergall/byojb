# Cantina (Cantina Labs) — research
Provider/key: ashby:cantina | company_type: product
What they do: Cantina Labs is a consumer "social AI" company building real-time generative models (speech, images, video, character bots) and a flagship social AI platform, Cantina, where AI characters interact with people. Products centre on expression, personality and realism for storytelling and social interaction. They describe themselves as building and powering "ecosystems", with Cantina as "just the beginning".
How they describe themselves: From their JDs: "Cantina Labs is a social AI company, developing a suite of advanced real-time models that push the boundaries of expression, personality, and realism. We bring characters to life, transforming how people tell stories, connect, and create." Recruiting tone is mission/hype-forward — "If you're excited about the potential AI has to shape human creativity and social interactions, join us in building the future!" No published values list was reachable. Role framing is high-bar and specialist ("8+ years… you know the language spec, not just the idioms"; "Member of Technical Staff" titling borrowed from frontier labs), not "wear many hats".

Engineering & tech: Python, PyTorch, Kubernetes-based training infrastructure, AWS (S3, DynamoDB) plus on-demand GPU compute (RunPod); distributed data processing for video-model training data; annotation pipelines (MTurk, Prolific). Client side: Kotlin Multiplatform + Compose Multiplatform across Android/iOS/wasm, Koin DI, Ktor, SQLDelight, Gradle multiplatform, CI/CD for multiplatform builds. ML surface spans speech, images, real-time video generation and joint audio-video modelling.
