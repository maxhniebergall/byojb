# 7shifts — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:7shifts | company_type: product

What they do: 7shifts is "a scheduling and payroll platform designed to help restaurant teams thrive. With an easy-to-use app and industry-specific solutions, 7shifts saves time, reduces errors, and helps keep costs in check for **over 55,000 restaurants**." Their careers page adds that the platform serves "over 1.5 million restaurant workers." Mission, in their words: "to simplify team management and improve performance for restaurants, with a long-term vision of creating a thriving restaurant industry through the power of connected & engaged teams."

How they describe themselves: Values are the **B.E.A.M.S.** set, published on their careers page:
- **B**e radically candid
- **E**mbrace new challenges
- **A**ct like an owner
- **M**ake every experience an 11
- **S**olve with simplicity

Their "commitment to our Shifties" section spells out five more pillars in their own words: *Opportunity* ("our product is evolving in exciting ways… you'll be supported and empowered to own your growth"); *Challenge* ("We're tackling real problems in a fast-moving, complex industry. **The work is scrappy and ambiguous**, but meaningful"); *Culture* ("recognized among the best workplaces in North America… we **move fast** to solve meaningful problems," in-office and remote events, team offsites called "Sparks"); *Equity* ("We're a Series C, VC-backed SaaS company, and we believe everyone should share in the success they help create"); *Health and Wellness*; *Flexibility*; and *Support* (latest Apple tech, home-office setup, coaching, feedback, development planning). They also state pay philosophy openly: ranges "reflect the full growth path for the role, from building core skills, to accomplished performance, through to role mastery. **We typically target the accomplished level when hiring externally.**"

Size / stage / funding: **Series C, VC-backed** private SaaS (stated first-party). Growth stage; not profitable/established by their own framing, and equity is part of the pitch. Headcount not published on the pages reached.

Locations / HQ: Canadian — offices in **Saskatoon, SK** (origin) and **Toronto, ON**. Postings are dual-tagged "Toronto, ON, Saskatoon, SK."

Remote policy: **Hybrid, with an explicit hiring preference.** Their own words: "Our team is intentional around how and where we work, whether remotely, in person, or a mix of both. **We prioritize candidates who thrive in a hybrid work environment with offices in Saskatoon and Toronto**, ensuring in-person collaboration and connection when possible." They offer a flexible vacation policy and a **"90 Day Shift" program** letting staff "work from (almost) anywhere" for up to 90 days.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Canadian employer, but hybrid-preferred and office-anchored — not verified as remote-BC-eligible.** The language stops short of banning remote ("whether remotely, in person, or a mix"), but both live roles are tagged to the two office cities and the company explicitly prioritizes hybrid candidates. Facet extraction records `remote_policy: hybrid`, `geo_eligibility: canada`. Treat as Saskatoon/Toronto hybrid unless they confirm otherwise; Saskatchewan is at least closer to Mountain Time than Toronto is.

Engineering & tech: **Elixir** backend (notable and unusual), backend services and REST APIs, relational databases, plus modern web front-end work. AI tooling is expected in the workflow — "AI is part of the toolkit here, you use it actively, you know when to reach for it, and you hold the bar on what ships," with Copilot and Claude named. Squad model with PDE (product/design/engineering) pods reporting to an Engineering Manager. The senior role sits in the **Growth squad**: "shipping experiments that improve our in-product activation, retention, and monetization… acting as a key driver in how we test, learn, and scale," with A/B experimentation tooling (Growthbook, Statsig, LaunchDarkly) as a nice-to-have, plus pricing and feature-entitlement experiments.

Notable / other: Publishes CAD salary ranges on its JDs and explains its banding philosophy. Benefits: health and dental, lifestyle spending accounts, flexible parental leave, flexible vacation, Apple hardware and home-office setup, coaching and development planning. Claims recognition "among the best workplaces in North America." Facet extraction rates culture **hustle** and autonomy medium.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Developer, Growth (Toronto/Saskatoon, CA$130,000–$180,000); Software Developer (Toronto/Saskatoon, CA$80,000–$120,000, Elixir).
