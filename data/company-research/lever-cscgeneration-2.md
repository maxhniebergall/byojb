# CSC Generation — research
Provider/key: lever:cscgeneration-2 | company_type: product

What they do: CSC Generation is a retail holding company that acquires and operates consumer/e-commerce brands and runs their technology in-house. The Lever board mixes postings branded for distinct portfolio banners — one JD opens with "Adventure is our Culture ... At Backcountry" (the outdoor-gear retailer), another with "Design Your Future With Us. Creativity lives here. We celebrate craftsmanship, design, and inspired living." Engineering work is e-commerce web applications, fulfillment systems, and site reliability for these retail brands. UNVERIFIED beyond the JD text: no first-party corporate site was fetched this session (WebSearch budget was exhausted), so the portfolio list and ownership structure are not independently confirmed here.

How they describe themselves: Two distinct employer-brand voices appear in their own JDs. Backcountry-branded: "Join a team that celebrates a lifestyle as bold as the terrain we love... rooted in adventure, recognition, and wellbeing on and off the mountain... a team that thrives on energy, exploration, and connection." Home/design-branded: "Creativity lives here... your voice and vision matter... a culture of flexibility, storytelling, and opportunity." Both are lifestyle/brand-marketing framing rather than engineering-culture framing; no engineering values, handbook, or principles are stated anywhere in the postings.

Size / stage / funding: Not stated in any local source. Private retail-brand operator. Unverified.

Locations / HQ: Not stated in the postings. Hiring geos named in JDs are Remote — United States, Remote — Costa Rica, Remote — India, and (per the registry's location field) Remote — Canada.

Remote policy: All six live engineering postings are remote. The heavy majority route to Costa Rica: Principal Software Engineer ("Location: Costa Rica (Remote)"), Senior Backend Engineer – Fulfillment Systems ("Remote (Costa Rica)/ Remote (United States)/ Remote (India)"), Site Reliability Engineer (Costa Rica), and a Costa-Rica-specific Senior Software Engineer, Ecommerce Web Applications.

Remote-Canada eligibility: PARTIAL, and the registry and the JD disagree on the flagship role. "Senior Software Engineer, Ecommerce Web Applications (US/Canada)" is explicitly Canada-eligible (title and location both say so) — that one is genuinely open to a Canada-based remote worker. But "Lead Software Engineer (Team Lead)", which the registry lists as "Remote - Canada", states inside its own JD body: "Location: Remote, United States". Treat the Lead role as US-only until the company says otherwise. The Principal, Senior Backend and SRE roles are Costa Rica (± US/India) and are not Canada-eligible.

Engineering & tech: E-commerce web applications, fulfillment systems, site reliability. Extracted facets on the Lead role list Java, C#, PHP and Angular — a mixed legacy/enterprise stack. The Lead Software Engineer reports to the Chief Growth Officer (not to an engineering leader); the Principal Software Engineer and Senior Backend Engineer report to a Director of Engineering.

Notable / other: Compensation is posted on the two Ecommerce Web Applications roles and is low for the stated seniority: US/Canada senior at USD $75,000–$105,000, Costa Rica senior at USD $64,000–$80,000. A "Senior" title at USD $75–105K converts to roughly CAD $100–145K — beneath a Canadian senior-backend market rate. The fetched JD bodies are unusually thin (header + location only for several roles), so much of the postings' substance is not in local data.

Open relevant roles (sample): Lead Software Engineer (Team Lead); Principal Software Engineer; Senior Backend Engineer – Fulfillment Systems; Senior Software Engineer, Ecommerce Web Applications (US/Canada); Site Reliability Engineer.
