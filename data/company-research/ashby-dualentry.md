# DualEntry — research
Provider/key: ashby:dualentry | company_type: product

What they do: DualEntry builds an "AI-native ERP" / general ledger — in their words, "the OS for finance." The product automates accounting from data entry through close for finance teams. They frame the market as the ~$220B financial-system-of-record space "still running on software from the 1990s, with no new entrants in 30 years." Customers range from $5M-ARR companies to publicly traded ones.

How they describe themselves: Mission framing is "reinvent a $220B industry" / "fix the boring, think the hard, and build the impossible, so we become the most robust engineering platform for accounting the world has ever seen" (Ignacio Brasca, Head of Engineering). Their stated hiring philosophy: "We hire outliers… people with a track record of accomplishing difficult things," citing colleagues who "competed at the highest levels of sport, performed on Broadway, trained with UFC champions."
Their explicit "How You Operate" principles, quoted from their own JDs:
- **Pragmatic** — "make decisions based in reality (not theory). We don't debate if Cassandra has the most theoretical scalability - we use Postgres until it breaks"
- **Hard working** — "'You can work long, hard, or smart, but [here] you can't choose two out of three' - Jeff Bezos"
- **Curious** — "you love to learn, are highly curious about new frameworks"
- **Fast-moving** — "you deploy daily, iterate quickly, and never wait for permission"
Requirements lists open with "Hardcore work ethic and high agency." The "Meet the Engineering Team" section celebrates engineers who "spent most weekends… shipping what was never on the roadmap" and who "rebuilt [the bank connection engine] from scratch over a weekend." Culture claim: "High-speed, high-trust environment with high expectations."

Size / stage / funding: Roughly 2 years old. States "$100M+ raised" from Lightspeed, Khosla Ventures, Contrary and Google Ventures. Headcount not stated on the JDs; the eng team is described by naming a handful of individuals, suggesting a small org. Early-stage — "You're joining early and will grow with DualEntry."

Locations / HQ: New York City is the anchor (they offer "visa sponsorship and relocation support for a move to NYC after 2 years' tenure"). Team is distributed across EMEA/EU/LATAM/UK/Nordics/Canada. In-person meetups held in Lisbon, Vienna, Warsaw, Cyprus.

Remote policy: "Remote-first team — the fastest team you will have ever worked with." Perks include home internet, co-working space and office-setup support; flexible PTO (27 days incl. public holidays); unlimited AI token usage; L&D budget.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified YES.** Two live JDs list Canada explicitly in their location field — "Senior/Staff Backend Engineer: Remote (EMEA, EU, LATAM, UK, NORDICS), Remote (Canada)" and "Senior Site Reliability Engineer: Remote (EU, LATAM, Canada)." (The Senior AI Engineer role is EU/LATAM only.) Timezone expectations are not stated anywhere I could find; the team's centre of gravity is NYC + Europe, so Mountain Time overlap is **unverified**.

Engineering & tech: Python-preferred backend (FastAPI/Flask/Django named as a bonus), PostgreSQL/SQL/ORMs/migrations/schema design, AWS, CI/CD. Infra managed in Terraform; observability standardised on OpenTelemetry. Homegrown integration framework, webhooks, a transaction search engine, and a bank-connection engine. On-call rotation exists (named as a requirement for the SRE role). Engineers "ship features end to end — architecture, CI/CD, testing, monitoring."

Notable / other: Explicit AI-tooling investment ("unlimited AI token usage," "AI skills development & enablement"). Comp is unusually transparent for a startup — base and equity ranges are posted on every JD (see Comp below).

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior/Staff Backend Engineer (Remote incl. Canada); Senior Site Reliability Engineer (Remote incl. Canada); Senior AI Engineer (Remote EU/LATAM); Analytics & Data Engineering (Lead or Head); Senior Backend Engineer (NYC).
