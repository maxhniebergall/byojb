# Expedia Group — research
Provider/key: workday:expedia/search | company_type: product

What they do: Online travel marketplace operating Expedia, Hotels.com, Vrbo, Orbitz and a large B2B travel-supply/distribution business. Revenue is transactional (bookings) plus a fast-growing advertising/media arm. Public company (NASDAQ: EXPE).

How they describe themselves: No first-party about/values page was fetched this session, so their stated mission and value set are **unverified** here. The registry's JD titles show where they are investing: "GenAI Platform", "Agentic AI Experience", "ML Platform", "Advertising Technology", "Distribution & Supply", "Partner Connectivity".

Size / stage / funding: Large public company, tens of thousands of employees. Well past any stability/runway concern, though the sector is cyclical and Expedia has run multiple restructurings historically (not verified from a first-party source this session).

Locations / HQ: HQ Seattle, Washington ("Washington - Seattle Campus" is the literal location string on most reqs). Other engineering sites visible in the registry: Montreal, Quebec (Canada) and Gurgaon, India. Austin and London also exist as Expedia sites (unverified from the registry).

Remote policy: Every relevant req in the registry names a physical campus rather than "Remote". Expedia has publicly operated a return-to-office posture, and no remote-designated engineering req appears among the 13 relevant postings on record. Treat as onsite/hybrid by campus.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No.** The Canadian roles are all "Canada - Quebec - Montreal" campus reqs (the Software Developer II/III Content and Partner Connectivity roles). These are Montreal-based hybrid positions, not remote-Canada roles — effectively disqualifying for someone in Kimberley, BC who cannot relocate. The senior/ML roles that would actually match the target archetypes are all Seattle-campus and would require US relocation and work authorization.

Engineering & tech: Backend services at large scale, ML platform and inference infrastructure, agentic/GenAI platform work, data platform (a "Senior Data Platform Engineer (Data Lake and Catalog)" req is open), advertising ML. This is a genuine large-scale platform organization, not a thin AI-wrapper shop.

Notable / other: Comp transparency is good — Washington and Canadian reqs post ranges by law, which is why five bands already exist in company-comp.jsonl. The Seattle senior band (USD 184.5K–258K base) is strong; the Montreal bands (CAD 86K–152K) are markedly weaker, which is the crux for a Canadian candidate: the well-paid roles are in the geography that isn't available.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Development Engineer (Backend + ML); Senior Software Development Engineer - ML Platform; Senior Software Development Engineer - Agentic AI Experience; Senior Data Platform Engineer (Data Lake and Catalog); Software Development Engineer III - Back End, GenAI Platform; Développeur(-euse) de logiciels III (contenu) — Montreal.
