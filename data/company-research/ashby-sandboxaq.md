# SandboxAQ — research
Provider/key: ashby:sandboxaq | company_type: product

What they do: SandboxAQ builds what it calls **Large Quantitative Models (LQMs)** — models grounded in physics, chemistry and scientific data rather than text. Their positioning line: "LLMs generate content. LQMs generate results", and "AI that understands the physical world." The stated aim is "Quantitative AI" combining AI and quantum techniques. Their own description: "SandboxAQ is a high-growth company delivering AI solutions that address some of the world's greatest challenges. The company's Large Quantitative Models (LQMs) power advances in life sciences, financial services, navigation, cybersecurity, and other sectors." Named product lines include drug and materials discovery, **AQtive Guard** (cryptographic/cyber posture management), **AQMed**, and **AQNav** (magnetic-anomaly navigation). Sectors served: pharmaceuticals, energy, defense, chemicals, materials.

How they describe themselves: "We are a global team that is tech-focused and includes experts in AI, chemistry, cybersecurity, physics, mathematics, medicine, engineering, and other specialties." On culture, verbatim: "At SandboxAQ, we've cultivated an environment that encourages creativity, collaboration, and impact. By investing deeply in our people, we're building a thriving, global workforce poised to tackle the world's epic challenges. Join us to advance your career in pursuit of an inspiring mission, in a community of like-minded people who value **entrepreneurialism, ownership, and transformative impact**." And: "We are committed to fostering a culture of belonging and respect, where diverse perspectives are actively sought and valued. Our multidisciplinary environment provides ample opportunity for continuous growth — working alongside humble, empowered, and ambitious colleagues ready to tackle epic challenges." No numbered values list is published.

Size / stage / funding: "The company emerged from Alphabet Inc. as an independent, growth capital-backed company in 2022, funded by leading investors and supported by a braintrust of industry leaders." Investors named on their site include T. Rowe Price, Breyer Capital and Allianz, with Yann LeCun and Jim Breyer as notable backers. They also cite a **$500 million CHIPS R&D Award** from the US government. Very well capitalised and multi-hundred headcount, but self-described as "high-growth" and growth-capital-backed rather than profitable. Partners: Google Cloud, Microsoft, AWS, Deloitte, EY, Accenture. Customers cited: US Air Force, US Department of Health and Human Services, Mount Sinai, UCSF, Dow, P&G.

Locations / HQ: US-headquartered (Bay Area) with a global team; requisitions in the registry are typed at country level, including **Canada**.

Remote policy: Distributed/global with country-typed reqs. Benefits are US-centric in the JD text (401k matching at 50% up to the IRS maximum; twelve weeks fully paid parental leave "in the US"), but Canadian requisitions carry their own CAD compensation tiers, indicating a real Canadian employment path. Stated perks: "Unlimited PTO plus a summer and winter break (one week each)", "$750 equipment, software, and office furniture budget", "$100 per month for wellness (physical or mental) and $100 for home office bills", medical/dental/vision at 95% premium coverage for employee and dependents, Carrot family-planning support, "Competitive salary, equity and annual bonus."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** Multiple live reqs are located simply "Canada" with compensation posted in CAD and explicit geographic tiering — "CAN Tier 1" and "CAN Tier 2" bands. Country-level location with no metro attached means a BC-based candidate is eligible; the tiering implies pay is adjusted by Canadian region, so BC would need checking against which tier applies. Note the two-tier structure is itself the evidence they employ across Canadian geographies rather than in one city.

Engineering & tech: The relevant senior role is Machine Learning Engineer on the **SAIGE** (SandboxAQ AI Generation Engine) team, described as owning "the end-to-end ML lifecycle, from initial data exploration and model development to scalable production deployment", central to "designing and rapidly building AI-first products that incorporate Large Quantitative Models (LQMs) and sophisticated agentic frameworks." Named responsibilities: "Design, construct, and manage robust data pipelines for the training, validation, and continuous retraining of Large Quantitative Models (LQMs) and agentic frameworks"; developing and testing novel models with defined metrics; feature engineering on large-scale datasets; "deep analysis of model behavior, performance, and failure modes"; and enforcing "exceptional engineering standards for code quality, system efficiency, and security in a prototyping environment." Required: BS + 5+ years; "experience developing highly-available, performant, scalable ML systems, including large-scale data processing pipelines"; strong Python with PyTorch/TensorFlow/JAX/NumPy/Pandas; and "Deep proficiency in MLOps and software best practices, including CI/CD for ML, experiment tracking (e.g., Weights & Biases, MLflow), automated testing, and version control for both code and datasets." Desired: MS/PhD, financial/risk simulation, cloud (GCP, AWS). The role is explicitly a founding-team position: "This person will be one of the founding engineers to join the SAIGE team and will be the bridge between cutting-edge AI concepts and functional, real-world MVPs", with the primary goal "to rapidly iterate on different potential solutions to build and evaluate new models, focusing on speed and tangible outcomes."
