# Capital One — research
Provider/key: workday:capitalone/capital_one | company_type: product

What they do: Capital One Financial is a large US bank holding company — credit cards, consumer and commercial banking, auto lending — that has spent a decade rebuilding itself as a technology organisation. It was an early and unusually complete enterprise migration to public cloud (all-in on AWS, closed its own data centres), and it operates a very large in-house engineering org building card platforms, identity, session management, messaging, travel tech, data platforms and a substantial internal AI/ML and GenAI platform estate. Publicly traded (NYSE: COF). Separate legal entities operate by country: **Capital One Canada**, Capital One Europe (UK), and Capital One Philippines Service Corp — "any position posted in Canada is for Capital One Canada."

How they describe themselves: Their req copy leans on wellbeing and total rewards — full coverage for spouses, domestic partners and dependents, up to $3,000/yr mental health coverage and up to $5,000/yr tuition subsidies, "Capital One is committed to helping you live your best life." They publicly declare themselves a **hybrid work company** (their newsroom posts "Capital One Declares Future as a Hybrid Work Company" and "Capital One's U.S. Offices Fully Hybrid"). On AI they state: "We embrace the responsible use of artificial intelligence (AI) to enhance the candidate experience and streamline our recruitment processes. However, no hiring decisions are made using AI as every hiring decision is made by our hiring managers, business interviewers, and recruitment professionals." They also disclose that they "may, for certain purposes, render a decision based exclusively on automated processing of your personal information as a part of the candidate screening process." Capital One Canada states it is "an equal opportunity employer committed to fostering a diverse and inclusive work environment."

Size / stage / funding: Public (NYSE: COF), Fortune 100 bank, tens of thousands of employees, thousands of engineers. Maximum institutional stability; heavily regulated.

Locations / HQ: HQ McLean, Virginia. Major US tech hubs at McLean/Richmond VA, New York, Plano TX, San Jose and San Francisco CA, Cambridge MA, Wilmington DE, Chicago. **Toronto, Ontario** for Capital One Canada.

Remote policy: **Hybrid-first, not remote-first.** Company-wide the expectation is roughly three days a week in office (commonly Tuesday–Thursday, with Monday and Friday work-from-home); at major US locations this is administered as a badge-swipe target (reported as ~20 swipes per rolling 13 weeks) rather than a rigid daily mandate. A minority of roles are classified fully remote and are labelled as such on the posting — several US reqs in this batch carry "(Remote Eligible)" in the title. An accommodations process exists for medical circumstances.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Effectively no — this is the disqualifier.** The two Canadian reqs in the registry (Staff Software Engineer - Capital One Travel Tech - Backend, R244497 and R244505) are posted to **Toronto, ON** against the Capital One Canada entity, and Capital One is an explicitly hybrid, office-attendance company. A Toronto hybrid role is not workable from Kimberley BC and no relocation is possible. Separately, every "(Remote Eligible)" req in this batch — the Distinguished AI Engineer and Agentic AI Platform roles — is a **US** posting under the US entity and therefore requires US work authorisation, which is not available. There is no Canada-remote posting location in evidence on this board. Timezone would also be Eastern, two hours ahead of Mountain.

Engineering & tech: Very large and genuinely infrastructure-heavy. Java, Python, Go, Scala, Node.js on AWS; REST and GraphQL APIs; GraphDB and API platform work; WAF and HTTP-protocol depth; CI/CD at Distinguished-engineer scale; Cloud Operations Resilience Engineering; messaging dispatch; identity and session management; enterprise data engineering. The AI estate is deep and platform-shaped rather than wrapper-shaped: **MLX / MLXT**, "AI Foundations (LLM Core and Agentic AI)", "GenAI Platform, Agentic Infrastructure", "GenAI Platform Services", "FM Hosting, LLM Inference", and "SDKs: Gen AI Evaluation and MCP" — foundation-model hosting, inference serving, agent infrastructure, eval SDKs and MCP tooling. Ladder is Senior → Lead → Senior Lead / Sr. Lead → Distinguished → Senior Distinguished.

Notable / other: 46 live relevant postings in the registry — the highest volume in this batch by a wide margin. Uses automated processing in candidate screening. Benefits are strong (mental health and tuition subsidies quantified on the req).

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer - Capital One Travel Tech - Backend (Toronto, ON); Sr. Lead AI Engineer (FM Hosting, LLM Inference); Senior Lead AI Engineer (SDKs: Gen AI Evaluation and MCP); Distinguished AI Engineer - Agentic AI Platform (Remote Eligible, US); Lead Platform Engineer; Sr. Lead Data Engineer (Enterprise Platforms Technology); Lead Software Engineer, Back End (Cloud Operations Resilience Engineering).

Pay: Capital One posts salary ranges on its reqs under US and Canadian practice. The Toronto Staff Software Engineer (Travel Tech, Backend) req states **CAD 157,400–179,600**, plus eligibility for performance-based incentive compensation including cash bonuses. US bands already on record for this company run from roughly USD 179K–204K (senior SRE/DevOps) up to USD 244K–333K at Distinguished/principal level.
