# Redis — research
Provider/key: ashby:redis | company_type: product

What they do: Redis is the company behind the Redis in-memory data store, now positioned as a broader real-time data platform. Core product is a sub-millisecond in-memory database used for caching, session management, streaming, search, feature stores and vector/RAG workloads. Recent product line is heavily AI-oriented: Redis Iris (real-time context for agents), LangCache (token optimization), Agent Memory, and Context Retriever. Sold as fully-managed cloud, on-premises software, and open-source. Customers span financial services, e-commerce/retail, gaming, healthcare and telco; the company claims 10,000+ customers and describes itself as the "#1 cited brand for caching solutions."

How they describe themselves: Mission is stated simply as **"We help you build fast apps—fast."** Four core values, verbatim:
- **"Be real"** — "We are truth tellers and truth seekers, always."
- **"Be unreasonable"** — "Greatness isn't born from playing it safe."
- **"Simplicity is everything"** — "Simplicity is the highest form of sophistication."
- **"Speed is our superpower"** — "We act decisively, learn quickly, and move faster than anyone else."
Externally-sourced descriptions of their hiring bar emphasize candidates who are level-headed and can succeed in a "fast-paced, ever-evolving environment" with a service-oriented attitude. The company dropped "Labs" from its name and branding.

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, growth stage. Investors named on their own site: Bain Capital Ventures, Francisco Partners, GS Growth, Dell Technologies Capital, TCV. CEO is Rowan Trollope. Open-source origins date to 2011. Their own company page rendered employee count and download figures as placeholder "0+"/"0M" values, so headcount could not be confirmed first-party. Third-party trackers report ~1,400 employees, ~$272M–$300M ARR, $355M raised, ~$2B valuation. Co-founders Ofer Bengal (Chairman) and Yiftach Shoolman (President) are still in post. Not a runway-stage startup, but not disclosed as profitable either.

**Layoffs (July 2026):** press reports (Calcalist / Times of Israel) describe a cut of roughly 70 roles, concentrated in Tel Aviv (~25% of the Israeli workforce), despite the ~$300M revenue run rate. The company says it does not plan to close the Israeli R&D centre.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco. Additional offices in London, San Jose CA, Austin TX, Tel Aviv, Sofia (Bulgaria), and Singapore. Bulgaria appears explicitly in role titles (e.g. "Principal Golang Software Engineer – AI Services – Bulgaria"), and Israel is a major engineering centre.

Remote policy: Mixed — employees do a combination of remote and on-site work, and remote policy varies by role and team. No company-wide remote-first declaration was found on their own pages. Individual engineering postings are nonetheless listed with `remote` policy and multi-country eligibility.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes — verified.** Multiple live engineering postings in the local registry carry location "United States, Canada" with a `remote` policy and `geo_eligibility: canada`, including Senior Software Engineer, Feature Store; Senior Software Engineer, Context Engine; and Senior Principal Software Engineer, Feature Store. Timezone-wise, HQ is Pacific (UTC−8/−7), an easy overlap with Mountain Time; the Tel Aviv and Sofia engineering centres are far ahead of MT, which may affect some teams.

Engineering & tech: Genuinely infrastructure/database engineering. Stack signals across their postings: C/C++ and Rust for the core AI and Search team, Go for AI Services, Python for data/ML work; Kafka, Flink, Spark for streaming and data pipelines; AWS, GCP and Azure for deployment; RAG and vector infrastructure. Teams are narrowly scoped and named (Redis Cloud Unit, Redis AI Services, AI and Search, Feature Store, Context Engine, AI Search & Vector Infrastructure), which suggests deep specialization rather than generalist product work. Strong developer-experience posture: Dev Hub, first-party client libraries for Python, Node, Java, Go and .NET, Redis University training, and a large open-source community.

Compensation: No public compensation-philosophy page found. Individual JDs post explicit USD ranges (Ashby, US/Canada-eligible): Senior Software Engineer, Feature Store and Senior Software Engineer, Context Engine both at $196.9K–$246.1K base (the Feature Store role also offers equity); Senior Principal Software Engineer, Feature Store at $250K–$300K base.

Notable / other: Long-running, widely deployed open-source project — one of the most-used pieces of infrastructure software in existence. Positioning has pivoted hard toward AI/agent memory and vector search, and a large share of current openings are on AI-adjacent teams. Externally-reported employee sentiment (Glassdoor, ~267 reviews) sits around 84% recommend, 3.9 work-life balance, 4.1 culture and values.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Feature Store; Senior Software Engineer, Context Engine; Principal Engineer – AI Search & Vector Infrastructure; Senior Golang Software Engineer – AI Services; Software Engineer, AI and Search Team (C/C++ or Rust); Senior Principal Software Engineer, Feature Store.
