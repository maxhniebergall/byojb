# Smile Digital Health — research
Provider/key: lever:smiledigitalhealth | company_type: product

Research method: NO web fetches were possible for this company. WebSearch was exhausted for the
session (200/200 global cap) and the only URL on record is a client-rendered ATS board, which returns
an empty shell. Everything below is grounded in the company's OWN job-description text already captured
locally in `data/posting-research/`; anything not stated there is marked UNVERIFIED rather than guessed.

What they do: Smile Digital Health sells a FHIR-based health-data platform — "our leading FHIR-based data
liberation platform" — that lets healthcare organizations collect, exchange and manage clinical data.
Their own summary: "At its heart, the Smile platform enables people and organizations to better manage
healthcare data. We help generate and liberate structured healthcare data to ensure effective delivery
across care teams and health systems." Customers are healthcare stakeholders (payers, providers, health
systems) and the platform is stated to be in use "in over 20 countries".

How they describe themselves: Mission-led and health-outcome framed. Repeated first-party language across
every JD: "supporting our mandate for #BetterGlobalHealth. We strive towards this goal every day, and the
results can be seen in the impact of our innovative health data platform and data management solutions";
"bringing #BetterGlobalHealth to patients everyday!"; "Apply today and find plenty of reasons to SMILE!"
The tone is warm and mission-first with no hustle or rocketship vocabulary anywhere in the eight JD bodies
on record. A separate values page was not reachable — the above is the complete set available.

Size / stage / funding: Growth-stage private company; ranked "#19 on Deloitte's Technology Fast 50 Ranking
for 2024" (their own citation). Headcount, funding round and profitability UNVERIFIED from local sources.

Locations / HQ: Toronto, Ontario is the anchor location on the Canadian postings; US remote roles are also
posted, and the platform is deployed in 20+ countries.

Remote policy: Remote, and the job titles say so explicitly rather than leaving it to interpretation —
"(Remote Canada)", "Remote Ontario", "(Remote US)". No office-day requirement appears in any JD body.
Async practices are not described — UNVERIFIED.

Remote-Canada eligibility: YES — the strongest and most explicit in this batch. Multiple live roles are
titled "Remote Canada" with CAD salary bands; one is scoped narrower as "Remote Ontario". Whether the
"Remote Canada" roles extend to British Columbia / Mountain Time specifically is not stated, but the
company is clearly set up to employ remote Canadian staff, and note that at least one requisition is
province-restricted to Ontario.

Engineering & tech: Health-data platform and cloud/SRE work.
- Azure Platform Engineer: "designing, deploying, and operating production-grade Azure Kubernetes Service
  (AKS) clusters, with a strong focus on cluster internals, networking, security hardening, and
  reliability… owns the platform end-to-end from cluster architecture and CI/CD through to performance
  tuning, observability, and incident response for critical systems", acting as SME for Kubernetes
  deployments and production issues, working with application, security and platform teams.
- Cloud Performance Engineering / SRE: multi-cloud, CI/CD, observability, performance testing, FHIR, SLAs.
- Application development: FHIR healthcare data platform and data management; senior roles include
  gathering product requirements, designing, implementing and testing system components, plus guiding and
  mentoring associate-level developers.
Domain is standards-heavy (FHIR/HL7), regulated, and internal-platform in character.

Notable / other: Pay is posted on every JD, which makes the ladder unusually legible:
Software Developer Intermediate (Remote Canada) CAD 90–110K; Software Developer, Senior – Support (Remote
Canada) CAD 90–110K; Senior Software Developer (Remote Canada) CAD 115–135K; Cloud Engineer / Azure
Platform Engineer (Remote Ontario) CAD 115–130K; Cloud Performance Engineering SRE (Remote Canada) CAD
110–125K; Staff Software Developer (Remote Canada) CAD 155–165K; Senior Cloud Architect (Remote US) USD
140–160K; Senior Cloud Engineer (Remote US) USD 120–135K. So: Canadian senior bands run ~CAD 110–135K, and
only the Staff level clears CAD 150K, while the equivalent US-remote roles are paid materially more.

Open relevant roles (sample): Cloud Engineer (Azure Platform Engineer) – Remote Ontario; Cloud Performance
Engineering – Site Reliability Engineer (Remote Canada); Senior Software Developer (Remote Canada);
Software Developer Intermediate (Remote Canada); Senior Cloud Architect (Remote US); Senior Cloud Engineer
(Remote US).
