# nesto / Nesto Cloud — research
Provider/key: rippling:nestocloud | company_type: product

What they do: nesto is a Canadian digital mortgage platform — origination, funding and servicing, plus consumer-facing shopping/renewal/refinance flows, calculators and educational content. Per its own JD, nesto is "Canada's leading provider of mortgage technology and financing solutions, managing $73B+ CAD in residential and commercial mortgages, with 1,000+ team members across 12 offices nationwide", and it powers "major financial institutions across Canada" as a technology provider. Nesto Cloud is the B2B platform arm; at its centre is **Maestro**, an AI-powered document-validation and productivity system for mortgage sales, underwriting and loan servicing.

How they describe themselves: Mission (own site): "We're on a mission to offer a positive, empowering and transparent property financing experience, simplified from start to finish." Stated culture in the JD is "an entrepreneurial culture built on **trust, speed, uncomfortable ambition, being stronger together, and a relentless obsession with our clients**." Who they want: "Builders. Problem-solvers. Curious minds. **People who move fast**, embrace innovation, and believe we're stronger together." Recruiting copy leads with accolades: "Canadian Rocketship 2025" (TechTO), Deloitte Fast 50 three years running, CLA Lending Company of the Year four consecutive years, CMP Top Mortgage Employer 2025, and describes the company as "disrupting a 2.1 Trillion-dollar mortgage industry at light speed."

Size / stage / funding: 1,000+ team members, 12 offices across Canada. Backed by **Diagram** (a fintech venture launchpad), which is financed by **Portag3**, with institutional backing from Power Financial, Great-West Lifeco, IGM Financial and National Bank. Private, venture/corporate-backed, in declared hypergrowth. Profitability not stated.

Locations / HQ: Canada; 12 offices nationwide (Montreal is nesto's historic base — not re-verified this run). Postings are country-level remote.

Remote policy: Not stated on the about page. Both live engineering postings are labelled "**Canada Remote**" on the Rippling board, which is first-party ATS metadata.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes — verified.** Both relevant postings are explicitly "Canada Remote" with no city qualifier. A Canadian employer with Canadian payroll and country-wide remote postings; BC/Mountain Time should be workable, though the office footprint is eastern-Canada-weighted so meeting hours will likely sit in Eastern business hours (i.e. early mornings from Kimberley). No timezone requirement is stated.

Engineering & tech: Squad-structured teams, "several major tech projects progressing in parallel at a high delivery pace." Stack from the AI Stream JD: **Python, FastAPI** (or comparable), relational databases (MySQL named), agile/testing/design-patterns fundamentals. AI side: Maestro combines a **deterministic rules-based validation engine** with AI document classification and matching using **Gemini, Claude and OpenAI models**, **Vertex AI**-based classification and **embedding-based name/address matching** — explicitly "at the boundary between conventional backend systems and AI-assisted processing." The JD is careful that "no ML expertise required, but curiosity about how deterministic and AI-based systems interact."

Notable / other: B Corp certified since 2022; advisors are salaried and rewarded on customer satisfaction rather than commission, and the company markets rate transparency without lender bias — genuine ethical-posture signals. The "Senior Backend Engineer - AI Stream" JD asks for only **3–5 years** of experience, includes "Lean on senior engineers for guidance on novel or ambiguous architectural problems", and frames the role as growing into the domain — the scope described is mid-level despite the Senior title, while simultaneously asking the hire to mentor juniors.
