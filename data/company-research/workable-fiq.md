# Fulfillment IQ — research
Provider/key: workable:fiq | company_type: consulting

What they do: Fulfillment IQ (FIQ) is a supply-chain engineering and transformation firm. Its own site brands it an "Award Winning Supply Chain Tech Consultancy": it sells consulting (supply-chain audits, roadmaps, fractional CSCO), WMS/OMS implementations and integrations, and custom software development for brands, retailers and 3PLs — alongside a few proprietary platforms (Zynque iPaaS, 3PL IQ, PPX, vMeasure). Claimed record: "500+ successful software implementations", "$10 billion in GMV enabled", "50M+ square feet of warehouse supported", clients including Nordstrom, FedEx, Foot Locker, ShipBob, NFI.

How they describe themselves: The JD framing is client-delivery-first — "We work at the intersection of strategy, operations, and technology"; "delivering outcomes through consulting, systems implementation, and proprietary platforms that accelerate time-to-value and reduce delivery risk"; "If you enjoy working in complex environments, **partnering closely with clients**…". Marketing tagline "#bethedisruptor"; mission language about "people, processes, and technology".

Size / stage / funding: Privately held consultancy; headcount not published. Product line (Zynque) is described as "live in production with active customers onboarding" after about a year of development — i.e. early.

Locations / HQ: Atlanta, GA is cited in a July 2026 press release on their site; the engineering roles in the registry are Toronto, Canada.

Remote policy: Not remote. All three live postings state "Toronto (Onsite/ Hybrid)" and Workable's workplace field is "hybrid".

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO. Verified from the JDs themselves — "Location: Toronto (Onsite/ Hybrid)" on both the Backend Engineer III and Principal Engineer postings, workplace field "hybrid". A BC-based fully-remote candidate is not eligible.

Engineering & tech: Python (FastAPI/Flask/Django, SQLAlchemy, Alembic), PostgreSQL and relational modelling, REST APIs, multi-tenant SaaS, Docker/CI-CD, Next.js/TypeScript on the Zynque side, Kafka/event streaming, workflow/DAG pipelines, and "AI-assisted engineering workflows; AI is deeply embedded in how we build". The Principal role is genuine platform-hardening work: tenant isolation, idempotency, backpressure, SLOs, capacity planning.

Notable / other: Compensation is stated openly in the JD text — a rarity: Backend Engineer III "$105-125K CAD per year (negotiable)"; Principal Engineer - Zynque "$175 – $210K CAD per year (negotiable)". Roles report to a "Head of R&D" / "General Manager at Zynque"; the Principal role asks for "founder-level ownership; comfortable in low-process, high-ambiguity environments".

Open relevant roles (sample): Backend Engineer III; AI Engineer; Principal Engineer - Zynque.
