# Bold Commerce — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:boldcommerce | company_type: product
What they do: Bold Commerce builds "repeat commerce" apps for Shopify merchants — rePete (reorder nudging), Subscriptions (recurring billing + churn prevention), Upsell, Custom Pricing, Memberships and Discounts. The pitch is increasing retention, order frequency and average order value for existing customers. They cite reach across "800K merchants" on Shopify, are Shopify Plus Certified, and describe themselves as the first Shopify front-end app developer.
How they describe themselves: Positioning is "open-first by design" — tools that integrate with a merchant's existing stack rather than creating vendor lock-in — plus "10+ years" of operating history and analysis of "millions of repeat orders".
