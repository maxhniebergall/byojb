# Swift Navigation — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:swiftnav | company_type: product
What they do: Swift Navigation sells centimeter-accurate GNSS positioning. Flagship is Skylark, a 24/7 cloud GNSS-corrections service (global reference-station network, redundant data-center architecture, monitoring algorithms) used for ADAS/autonomous driving, plus the Swift Automotive Suite (vehicle localization software) and NxRTK network-RTK. Customers are 20+ automotive OEMs/suppliers and mobile-handset OEMs; their JD claims programs covering 10M+ ADAS/AV vehicles and 8M+ current users, processing 1.4B satellite signals/hour.
How they describe themselves: Positioning framed around "accuracy, reliability, safety, scalability, affordability" — making high-integrity precise positioning accessible and affordable at mass-market scale. The JD leans on safety-of-life reliability (99.9% guaranteed availability, "fewer than 1 failure per 1,142 years of driving") and on standards compliance (ISO 26262, 27001, 20000, 21434). The homepage cites a 2026 AutoTech Award for ADAS & Safety and a recent ISO 26262 certification. No published values list was reachable.
