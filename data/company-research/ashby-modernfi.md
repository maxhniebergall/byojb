# ModernFi — research
Provider/key: ashby:modernfi | company_type: product
What they do: Operates **institution-owned deposit networks** for banks and credit unions — infrastructure that lets financial institutions offer reciprocal deposit insurance, optimize funding and manage liquidity. Two products: the NBID network for banks (700+ member banks) and the ModernFi CUSO for credit unions (130+). Their own site reports **761 member institutions managing $2.8 trillion in deposits across all 50 states**, with institutions receiving "preferential pricing, profit share, and oversight through a bank-majority board". They describe their ambition as serving "as systemically important financial infrastructure that drives the American economy".
How they describe themselves: Verbatim from the Platform Engineer JD — "The caliber and character of the ModernFi team set us apart. We value competence and drive over experience, and look for compassionate people with diverse backgrounds. We appreciate technical ability, dedication, and humble confidence. Above all, we value **respect** — for our colleagues, our clients, our partners, and ourselves. Our team pushes each other to be better every day, supports each other when things go wrong, and grows together on this journey. Because we are a close-knit team, we are selective and considerate with every hire. The people at ModernFi could be doing anything in the world, yet they choose to be here." They also want "high level of empathy, integrity, and calmness under pressure" and someone who makes small teams "faster and more reliable rather than introducing process drag". Their hiring process is "focused on cultural and personality fit".
Size / stage / funding: Small and early — 8 open roles at scan time, and the platform role is explicitly a **founding** Platform Engineering position (i.e. no dedicated platform function exists yet). Funding stage, investors and headcount are NOT published on the pages read — treat as unverified. The business itself (deposit-network infrastructure with 761 member institutions) has real, non-speculative revenue mechanics, which is a meaningfully more stable posture than a pre-product startup.
Locations / HQ: New York City. Benefits include "Dinner in the office", "Quarterly team outings" and **"Relocation to New York"** — the company is office-centred with remote tolerated.
Remote policy: Both relevant postings are titled "**NYC | Remote**". No written remote/async policy was found; the perks list points strongly toward an in-office default.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unverified and doubtful.** No country qualifier appears on the postings, but everything else is US-anchored: a US-only product ("all 50 states", "the American economy"), US-specific benefits (401(k) with fund selection), SOC 1/2 and US bank-regulatory compliance work, and "Relocation to New York" offered as a benefit. There is no statement of Canadian eligibility anywhere. Ask before applying; do not assume yes.
Engineering & tech: Published stack, verbatim — AWS with **Pulumi** IaC; PostgreSQL (RDS), ECS, Docker; **Temporal**; Python (FastAPI, SQLAlchemy); Datadog, PagerDuty, GitHub Actions; Auth0, Vanta, Fern. The founding platform role owns reliability posture (observability, incident response, SLOs, on-call practice), a more advanced release pipeline (merge queues, feature flags, per-domain release gates), the cloud platform (Pulumi modules, AWS account structure, identity, networking), and the **security/compliance posture** (Vanta remediation, SOC 1/2 readiness, vulnerability scanning, secrets and key management) in partnership with the CCO and external advisors.
Notable / other: Benefits — "Top-of-market comp, equity from day one", 100% employee-covered health/dental/vision, 401(k) with a broad fund selection, unlimited PTO, fully paid parental leave, $100 monthly wellness stipend, uncapped equipment. Applications are routed through careers@modernfi.com.
Open relevant roles (sample): Platform Engineer (NYC | Remote); Software Engineer (NYC | Remote).
Pay: posted on their own JDs (USD base + equity) — Platform Engineer **$160K–$240K**, Software Engineer **$150K–$240K**, both "Offers Equity". Unusually wide bands, consistent with hiring across levels. Both slots already have bands on record.
