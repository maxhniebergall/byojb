# Gumloop — research
Provider/key: ashby:gumloop | company_type: product
What they do: Gumloop sells an AI-agent platform companies deploy so non-engineers can build and share agents securely — "the piece of AI infrastructure companies roll out to let everyone build agents securely". It handles access control over company data, choice of AI models, and self-hosting in the customer's own cloud. Use cases marketed include data analysis, customer support, CRM management, meeting prep and call analysis. Named customers: Gusto, Ramp, Shopify, Samsara, Instacart, Opendoor and Webflow. SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant; multi-model support is pitched as avoiding vendor lock-in.
How they describe themselves: "We're a very small team building as fast as humanly possible." The role is framed as "high-ownership... for someone who wants to solve hard infrastructure problems at a company at the edge of AI", with the hire expected to "work directly with founders daily", "speak directly with customers to inform priorities", and be comfortable "in a small team where ownership and speed are expected". Their marketing premise is that "understanding a task should be the only prerequisite to automating it".
Size / stage / funding: Very small team. The JD says they have "raised over $70M from Benchmark, First Round Capital, Y Combinator, Nexus Ventures"; the homepage separately highlights a $50M Series B led by Benchmark. Venture-stage and scaling fast.
Locations / HQ: San Francisco office and a Vancouver office — the relevant posting is listed for "San Francisco Office, Vancouver Office".
Remote policy: Office-based. Benefits explicitly include "15 days of temporary remote work flexibility per year", which is a strong statement that the role is expected to be in-office the rest of the time. PTO is 24 days for SF employees, 20 days globally.
Remote-Canada eligibility: NO for a fully-remote hire. They do have a Canadian presence (Vancouver office) and hire there, but the posting is an office role and the stated remote allowance is 15 days per year. Kimberley, BC is roughly a 10-hour drive / a flight from Vancouver; this is not a remote-eligible position.
Engineering & tech: Kubernetes, Docker, Redis, GCP, Terraform, Prometheus, CI/CD. The role owns cluster design, CI/CD pipelines and cloud infrastructure, high availability, security and performance, and helps define the technical roadmap. Bonus: AI/ML infrastructure and model serving.
Notable / other: Compensation posted for the SF tier: estimated base $220K–$300K USD plus equity — the highest band in this batch. Also $350/month wellness benefit and a 401k match up to 6% (US-centric benefits).
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Infrastructure Engineer (6 postings on the board, 1 relevant).
