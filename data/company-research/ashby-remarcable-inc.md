# Remarcable, Inc. — research
Provider/key: ashby:remarcable-inc | company_type: product

What they do: Remarcable is a vertical B2B SaaS and marketplace for trade contractors — primarily electrical contractors — covering procurement, tool management, inventory, prefabrication and warehouse operations, with AP automation and direct integrations into contractor accounting/ERP systems and supplier catalogs. In their own words: "our platform eliminates procurement and operational friction for the construction industry—especially electrical contractors", and their "award-winning, SOC 2 Type II–certified B2B marketplace has processed billions in material purchases, connecting contractors, suppliers, and accounting systems in one seamless workflow."

How they describe themselves: Mission, stated verbatim: "To advance the adoption of technology in the construction industry and bring better efficiency, visibility, and communication to our customers." Founding story is domain-first: "Founded by an engineer to solve the inefficiencies he faced in the field", with a founding team of "former foremen, purchasers, project managers, and software engineers." They describe themselves as a "builder-led, high-ownership team" and pitch the market as "an underserved, multi-billion-dollar industry" that is "long overdue for innovation". The site frames the product around "reducing waste, saving time, and streamlining collaboration" for "hardworking professionals", built and tested with real users rather than as generic software. The DevOps JD asks for someone "comfortable operating autonomously in an agile, fast-paced startup environment with a high degree of ownership."

Size / stage / funding: Series A, backed by Insight Partners. "We're scaling fast—fueling product innovation, deep ERP integrations, and rapid go-to-market growth." Headcount not disclosed. SOC 2 Type II certified. Profitability unstated.

Locations / HQ: HQ not disclosed on the about page, which says only that the team is "located coast to coast" across North America. However, **both live engineering postings are located "Vancouver, BC (Hybrid)"**. A US phone number (216 area code, Cleveland OH) is given for applicant verification.

Remote policy: Not stated as a company-wide policy, and it does not matter for these roles: the two open engineering positions are explicitly **hybrid in Vancouver, British Columbia**. No async or documentation-culture signals appear.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Canada yes, remote no — and that is disqualifying here.** These are Canadian roles with Canadian dollar salary bands, so the employment entity is not the problem. But "Vancouver, BC (Hybrid)" requires in-office presence in Vancouver, roughly 800 km from Kimberley, which is not commutable and cannot be satisfied without relocation. Unless they will convert the role to fully remote within BC, both open roles are effectively closed.

Engineering & tech: **DevOps Engineer** — CI/CD pipeline ownership across multiple services, AWS (required) and Azure infrastructure, Terraform/Ansible IaC with "modular, reusable patterns", container orchestration (ECS, Kubernetes or similar), observability with ELK/Datadog/Prometheus/OpenTelemetry, IAM/networking security, cloud cost optimization, and ownership of SOC 2 controls and evidence collection. 3–5+ years DevOps/SRE/infrastructure; Django or Python web frameworks a nice-to-have. **Data Engineer** — AWS Lakehouse (S3, Redshift, Athena, Glue), ELT pipelines, dbt transformation models, multi-layer data architecture, data quality/governance and PII handling via AWS Lake Formation and IAM, ownership of SaaS metric pipelines (ARR, churn, NRR, product usage), plus "AI/ML data infrastructure including feature stores, training datasets, and embedding pipelines supporting SageMaker and Bedrock workflows". 3+ years, strong SQL and Python.

Notable / other: Both roles post CAD salary bands with bonus: DevOps Engineer CA$140K–CA$180K, Data Engineer CA$130K–CA$150K. Benefits: Medical, Dental, Vision, STD and Life insurance 100% company-paid for the employee, 401(k) with company match (a US instrument — the Canadian equivalent is not described), and bonus potential. **PTO is explicitly "Two weeks per year in the 1st year"**, which is unusually low and stated plainly. The JDs carry a phishing-scam warning and note that "Remarcable is not associated with info-remarcable.com" — a name-collision caution issued by the company itself. Entity confirmed: the Data Engineer JD links https://www.remarcable.com/aboutus directly, matching the registry entry.

Open relevant roles (sample): DevOps Engineer (Vancouver, BC — Hybrid); Data Engineer (Vancouver, BC — Hybrid).
