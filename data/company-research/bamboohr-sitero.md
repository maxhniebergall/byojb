# Sitero — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:sitero | company_type: product

What they do: Sitero is a clinical-trial solutions provider — a hybrid of services organisation and software vendor. In their own words they are "a next-generation clinical trial solutions partner, working with more than 200 Pharmaceutical, Biotech, and Institutional Research Organizations globally" who "use technology to drive safety, compliance, quality, and efficiency in research and clinical trials, helping customers bring life-changing treatments to market safer and faster." Their site positions four service lines — Clinical Services, Drug Safety, Biosafety/IBC services, and eClinical technology solutions — under the tagline "One Partner. One Platform. Complete Trial Delivery." They claim study startup "25% faster than the standard startup time."

How they describe themselves: Mission stated as being "driven by a mission to advance clinical research through a technology-enabled delivery model", leveraging "a deep well of knowledge across Technology, Clinical Operations, Biosafety, and Drug Safety." On the "Why Sitero?" section of their JD: "At Sitero, you will work on projects that have a direct impact on human health. We offer a **fast-paced environment where you can switch teams and projects as our business grows.**" They state they are "looking for engineers who are versatile, display leadership qualities, and are enthusiastic to take on new problems **across the full stack**." Values emphasis on their site is patient safety and biosafety compliance as central to the mission, and integrated single-partner delivery. No formal values/principles list is published.

Size / stage / funding: Established rather than early — "25+ years of expertise", "trusted by 200+ organizations globally", multiple global offices. Private; funding stage, ownership and headcount are not published and are **unverified**. Note the hybrid model: a meaningful share of the business is clinical *services* delivery, so headcount is not a proxy for engineering scale.

Locations / HQ: HQ is Coral Gables, Florida (3119 Ponce de Leon), with "multiple global offices". The Canadian engineering presence is in the Greater Toronto Area — the live posting is Markham, Ontario, and the JD itself states "Location: Ontario, Canada (Mississauga area preferred)".

Remote policy: **Not stated anywhere reachable.** The company site publishes no remote-work policy, and the JD gives a province plus a preferred sub-region rather than a remote designation.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Partial / not for BC — unclear at best.** Sitero clearly employs in Canada, but the live role is scoped to a specific province with a preferred metro: "Ontario, Canada (Mississauga area preferred)", listed in the ATS as Markham, Ontario. A province-scoped posting naming a preferred metro is a location requirement, not a remote-anywhere-in-Canada posting, and nothing on the JD or the site indicates otherwise. There is **no evidence** this role is open to a British Columbia resident, and the preference for the Mississauga area implies at least occasional office presence in the GTA. Also note Ontario is Eastern Time, two hours ahead of Mountain. Treat as not BC-eligible unless the company states otherwise.

Engineering & tech: The one live engineering role, **Software Engineer, Machine Learning**, is substantive ML-infrastructure work — the JD explicitly places it "at the intersection of AI infrastructure, model optimization, and large-scale software engineering", building "next-generation AI technologies that power our clinical trial platforms." Scope as stated:
- *AI & ML Infrastructure* — design, develop and deploy large-scale ML infrastructure and state-of-the-art AI (LLMs, multi-modal models) for clinical trial efficiency and safety.
- *Systems Architecture* — technical leadership for high-throughput, low-latency data pipelines and storage (data loading, caching, **vector stores**) for massive datasets.
- *AI-Augmented Engineering* — actively use and advocate for AI coding assistants and refactoring agents to accelerate the SDLC, generate boilerplate, write tests and optimise legacy codebases. This is an unusually prominent requirement, repeated in the qualifications.
- *Cross-Functional Leadership* — translate clinical-operations/product/research requirements into engineering solutions and influence the direction of Sitero's AI platforms.
- *Mentorship* across the org in distributed systems, ML engineering and AI-native workflows.
Requirements: bachelor's or equivalent; **8+ years** software development in Python, C++ or similar; **7+ years** leading technical project strategy and optimising industry-scale ML infrastructure (model deployment, evaluation, fine-tuning); 2+ years with LLMs/RAG/computer vision and PyTorch/TensorFlow/JAX; demonstrated use of LLMs for code generation, debugging, architectural validation and documentation; experience designing and scaling distributed storage, data lakes or high-performance ETL. Preferred: master's or PhD in computer science. Model optimisation "on hardware accelerators" is called out in the role summary.

Compensation: **Posted directly on the JD — CAD $175,000–$200,000.** This is a company-stated range on its own live posting and is already recorded in data/company-comp.jsonl under `ml_eng`/`staff` with derivation `direct`.
