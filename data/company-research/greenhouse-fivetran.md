# Fivetran — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:fivetran | company_type: product

What they do: Fivetran builds automated data movement infrastructure — managed ELT connectors that replicate data from hundreds of SaaS applications, databases and file sources into cloud data warehouses and lakes, with schema drift handling, normalization and incremental sync managed for the customer. The engineering surface is connectors (SaaS APIs, Oracle/Postgres/MySQL and other core databases), a transport/platform layer, data lakes, and the reliability infrastructure underneath. It is a pure data-infrastructure company.

How they describe themselves: Stated cultural themes are that the company shares in the pains and successes of customers, partners and colleagues; that it seeks teammates with different perspectives and that people go above and beyond for each other; that the team "actively moves the needle forward daily, works hard and follows through, and takes initiative and accountability"; and that it does "the right thing even when harder in the short run" and challenges the status quo "by reasoning from first principles". Their work-model framing is "not one-size-fits-all because every team is different".

Size / stage / funding: Large late-stage private company, heavily venture-funded, a well-known name in the modern data stack. Not an early startup.

Locations / HQ: US headquarters in Oakland/San Francisco Bay Area, with a substantial Toronto, Ontario office plus offices and remote staff in EMEA (including Germany) and elsewhere.

Remote policy: Hybrid by default in office cities, fully remote in some designated roles. From their own live JDs: "This is a full-time, hybrid position based out of our Toronto office. Our hybrid work model offers a blend of remote flexibility and in-person collaboration, including two days in the office each week to connect and build as a team." Other roles are tagged #LI-REMOTE and say "This is a full-time, fully remote position" or "a full-time remote position based out of Germany". So remote roles exist — but they are attached to specific non-Canadian geographies.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO for a British Columbia candidate. Fivetran's Canadian roles are Toronto-office hybrid with a stated two-days-per-week in-office requirement — both live Canadian postings ("Senior Software Engineer, R&D Business Systems" and "Staff Software Engineer") say so explicitly and are tagged #LI-HYBRID. The fully-remote postings are scoped to the United States (Remote, Colorado / Remote, New York) and to Germany/EMEA. There is no remote-anywhere-in-Canada option in the registry. Since Kimberley BC is not commutable to Toronto and relocation is excluded, Fivetran's Canadian roles are effectively closed to this candidate despite the company being otherwise remote-capable.

Engineering & tech: Deep data-infrastructure engineering — database replication internals (Oracle, core databases, C-level programming on some postings), SaaS connector development, a transport/platform layer, data lakes, and SRE at senior and senior-staff levels. Registry titles include Principal Software Engineer, Senior Site Reliability Engineer, Senior Staff Site Reliability Engineer, Senior Software Engineer - Core Databases / Database Connectors / SaaS Connectors / Java Backend / C Programmer, and Software Engineer II - Oracle Databases. One US remote posting explicitly values having "worked asynchronously as part of a fully-remote, distributed team", so async competence is recognised somewhere in the org.
