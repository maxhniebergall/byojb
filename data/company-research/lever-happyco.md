# HappyCo — research
Provider/key: lever:happyco | company_type: product

What they do: HappyCo builds SaaS for the **multifamily residential property-management industry** (PropTech). The origin product replaced paper-based inspections, checklists and audits for property operations teams; the platform has since grown into three product lines: **Happy Asset** (asset-management workflows for evaluating and closing deals), **Happy Property** (onsite operations management for property managers), and **Happy Force** (remote/centralized maintenance operations). Recent positioning emphasizes AI in property maintenance management. Customers are property owners, operators, asset managers and onsite maintenance teams in residential communities.

How they describe themselves: Mission — **"Raising the quality of living"**, transforming "the lives of people living and working in residential communities" by building products that "redefine multifamily living." Their five stated core values, verbatim:
- **Get Good Sh*t Done (GGSD)** — results-driven, high standards, accountability
- **Customer Obsessed** — deeply understand customer challenges and co-create solutions
- **Kaizen** — inquisitive, curious, authentic, committed to continuous growth
- **Better Together** — "teamwork, vulnerability, and trust are critical to success"; lead with empathy and respect, encourage honest debates, honor commitments
- **Make Happiness** — "happy energy, kindness, positivity and enthusiasm"
Other stated culture claims: "a company where people can grow their careers and work with like minded people, with no egos or politics"; values-driven with a "flexible, supportive culture"; "an open and honest culture which promotes an entrepreneurial spirit, with a flat structure"; committed to "real work-life balance" and a "high-purpose work environment." Tagline: "Join HappyCo and Make Work Happy!" They describe themselves as a **"fast-growth company"** in PropTech.

Size / stage / funding: Private. Founded **2011 in Adelaide, Australia**. Has raised approximately **$84M** across many rounds, from 42 investors including 29th Street Management, BAM Companies, Bonaventure Realty Group, Camber Creek and Cerity Partners Ventures — notably a heavy concentration of real-estate and property-industry strategic investors rather than pure software VCs. Exact headcount not disclosed on their own pages; the LinkedIn/aggregator picture is a few hundred employees.

Locations / HQ: Australian origin (Adelaide) with a US presence; the company describes its "team [as spanning] the globe" across the **USA, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and beyond**. No single office is presented as the centre of gravity.

Remote policy: A **"flex work culture"** — employees can work remotely from anywhere with internet connectivity, and the workforce is described as remote throughout the U.S., Canada and Australia. Annual company retreats (past locations Bali, Mexico, Tahoe). Benefits include a one-month paid sabbatical at the five-year anniversary, generous PTO, 15 days paid parental and pregnancy-loss leave, 401(k) matching (US), a $100/month phone-and-internet stipend, a $100/month wellness benefit, equity/stock options for all employees, life insurance and an Employee Assistance Program.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** There is a dedicated Canada req — "Lead Software Engineer", location "Canada, remote" (https://jobs.lever.co/happyco/8fc93b78-f344-4f4c-b4d0-a0d446570a21) — and it posts a **CAD** salary range, which is direct evidence of Canadian employment. Their careers page independently lists Canada among the countries the team spans. A parallel US-remote version of the same req exists.

Engineering & tech: Product, Design & Engineering are described as "the heartbeat" of the software, "constantly pushing boundaries and challenging the status quo," organized on an **"Empowered Teams" model** with "fearless collaboration." The open Lead Software Engineer req describes an explicitly **hands-on, execution-focused technical leadership role, not people management** — leading technical direction, prioritization and decision-making, working closely with Product and Design to "turn customer needs into pragmatic solutions, balancing speed, quality, and long-term sustainability **across multiple products and codebases**." The stated environment is "a fast-moving startup environment" motivated by "shipping valuable software quickly" and delivering "functionality that is immediately useful to customers." Specific stack details are not published on their careers pages. A second req, "Senior Engineer, Platform," is also open.

Notable / other: The investor base skewing toward real-estate operators (BAM, Bonaventure, Camber Creek, 29th Street) suggests deep industry distribution but also a customer-and-owner overlap that keeps the company tightly coupled to property-market conditions. The company also hires non-engineering operational roles such as "Virtual Maintenance Technician," reflecting the Happy Force remote-maintenance service line. Fourteen-plus years old but still self-described as a startup.

Comp: The Canada Lead Software Engineer req posts **CAD $150,000–$200,000 base** on the company's own JD.

Open relevant roles (sample): Lead Software Engineer (Canada, remote); Lead Software Engineer (United States, remote); Senior Engineer, Platform.
