# FastSpring — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:fastspring | company_type: product

What they do: FastSpring is a **merchant of record** ecommerce platform for digital-goods companies — AI, SaaS, gaming, software and digital-product businesses sell through FastSpring, and FastSpring takes on the whole payment stack for them: checkout, payment processing, subscription billing, settlement, refunds, global tax/VAT compliance and reporting. In their own words, "FastSpring is how AI, SaaS, gaming, software, and digital product companies sell online in more places around the world. We handle all payment needs from checkout to taxes so you can go farther faster." They report supporting 3,500+ companies across 200+ countries, over 10 million transactions annually, and more than a billion dollars in worldwide transaction volume per year.

How they describe themselves: Three stated values on the careers page — **"Make a Difference," "Seek to Understand," "Act With Urgency."** Culture is described as working hard while keeping a "fun, collaborative, and casual atmosphere." Benefits listed include 401K matching, flexible PTO, robust healthcare, work-from-home equipment reimbursement, education support, paid parental leave, volunteer opportunities, and bi-weekly "FastSpring Friday" half-days.

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2005; privately owned, backed by growth-equity firm AKKR; described as profitable and an Inc. 5000 honoree ("one of America's fastest-growing companies"). Mid-size, established, not an early-stage startup.

Locations / HQ: HQ Santa Barbara, California (801 Garden St.). Offices/registered entities in Amsterdam (Netherlands), Bicester (England), Belfast and Dublin (Ireland), Austin (Texas), Singapore, and **Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada** — the Canadian entity is registered as "SalesRight Technologies ULC d.b.a. FastSpring" (5475 Spring Garden Road, Suite 600), the legacy of their SalesRight acquisition.

Remote policy: Distributed workforce across those offices; the careers page provides work-from-home equipment reimbursement, and the live Senior Software Engineer - Payments posting is listed simply as "Remote" with "flexible hours to work with local and remote team members." The careers page does not state a formal company-wide remote/hybrid/onsite policy, and some third-party employee reviews mention concerns about in-office flexibility.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Plausible but unverified.** They operate a real Canadian legal entity in Halifax, NS, which means they can employ Canadians. However, the live Senior Software Engineer - Payments JD carries a **USD** pay range and no stated country scope, which usually signals a US-scoped remote req. Halifax is Atlantic Time — a Mountain-Time worker would be 4 hours behind that office and 1 hour ahead of Santa Barbara. Confirm country eligibility with the recruiter before investing effort.

Engineering & tech: Java-first backend (the Senior Payments role asks for 10+ years delivering commercial SaaS in Java), PostgreSQL plus NoSQL, RESTful service-oriented architectures with public APIs, AWS (EC2, S3, RDS, DynamoDB, Lambda), Linux, Docker, plus Angular/Vue/JS on the front end. Agile teams. Notably, the JD states engineers use **Claude Code day-to-day** to accelerate design, implementation and review — comfort with AI-assisted development tooling is an explicit requirement. Payments work carries real regulatory substance: PCI-DSS, PSD2/SCA, 3DS2, working with Product, Risk, Finance and Compliance. Engineers are expected to respond to production issues on money-movement flows.

Notable / other: They publish an AI Transparency Statement about the use of AI in recruiting (AI assists with communications, scheduling, application organization and interview documentation but is "not used as the sole determinant of hiring outcomes"). They also state pay ranges under pay-transparency practice. The merchant-of-record model means the company itself absorbs payments, tax and fraud complexity — a genuinely deep back-end domain.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer - Payments (Remote).
