# Lightning Labs — research
Provider/key: ashby:lightning | company_type: product

What they do: Lightning Labs builds the core open-source software of the Bitcoin Lightning Network. In their own words on lightning.engineering: "We develop software that powers the Lightning Network. Our open source, secure, and scalable Lightning systems enable users to send and receive money more efficiently than ever before." They describe themselves as bridging "the world of open source software and the next-generation of bitcoin financial software." Their principal artefact is **lnd**, the Lightning Network Daemon — one of the two dominant Lightning implementations. Commercial/user-facing products include **Lightning Terminal** ("Simple Lightning node management", a web UI with a network explorer and liquidity tools for node operators) and **Pool** (a liquidity marketplace).

How they describe themselves: Values stated on the site are technical rather than cultural — "open source, secure, and scalable". The JDs reinforce this: they want "a pragmatic senior software engineer who embraces the DevOps mindset", a "security and adversarially-oriented mindset", and "a strong passion for Bitcoin and a desire to make using Bitcoin cheap, fast, and simple for everyone". Notably, the JDs contain **no** hustle, rocketship, "fast-paced" or hype vocabulary at all — they are terse, concrete bullet lists of systems responsibilities. The framing of the infrastructure role is explicitly about lowering operator burden: the tools "will lower the barrier to entry for operating routing nodes and enable existing routing node operators to more effectively manage their infrastructure."

Engineering & tech: Deeply systems-oriented. Languages: **Go** primarily (lnd and most of the ecosystem), plus C/C++ and Python/Rust mentioned. Platform role: Kubernetes clusters in production (management and troubleshooting), cloud and cluster technologies composing an internal platform, IaC, Linux/networking/security fundamentals, stateful backend services including **PostgreSQL, etcd, and Bitcoin/Lightning nodes**, observability (logging/monitoring/tracing) which they "operate and extend", Helm charts and k8s lifecycle support for dev teams, and robustness/security work via "operators and overlay networks". Preferred: GitOps, DevSecOps, service mesh. Infrastructure role: automated channel management and autopilot algorithms, capital-management and fee-optimisation algorithms, monitoring and alerting for routing nodes, graph- and node-level network-health metrics, liquidity systems, and public API design. Applied cryptography and the Lightning protocol are relevant throughout. Public repositories they point candidates at: lightningnetwork/lnd, lightninglabs/lndinit, lndmon, aperture, pool, neutrino, faraday. They also run an engineering blog at blog.lightning.engineering.

Notable / other: Both open roles are internal-platform / operator-tooling work rather than customer-facing product — the platform role's stated purpose is "to improve our developer experience", with internal dev teams as the customer. The company's entire codebase is public, which is an unusually strong transparency and code-quality signal and lets a candidate assess the work directly before applying. Neither posting states a salary range.
