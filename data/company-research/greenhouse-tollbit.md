# TollBit — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:tollbit | company_type: product

What they do: TollBit is AI-infrastructure for the "agentic internet" — it lets content publishers monitor, control and monetize AI-agent access to their sites. Its own site describes it as "your complete web stack for the agentic internet", offering analytics on bot traffic, licensing tools, paywalls for bots, and agent-specific content delivery. Their JD frames it as "a tollbooth on the internet": "The future is AI-powered interfaces, where bots and agents become the primary way we navigate and access information. TollBit is building the key infrastructure that makes this possible, ensuring that these intelligent agents pay for the content they consume."

How they describe themselves: A "dual mission: to empower developers with cutting-edge technology to build groundbreaking AI applications and to create a thriving ecosystem where creators are compensated for their data." Site tagline: "Long live the programmable web." Self-described as "pioneers in the AI infrastructure space" and "a fast-paced, innovative team pushing the boundaries of what's possible." The engineering culture line is explicit: "You thrive in a fast-paced environment and are not afraid to wear multiple hats, and do not hesitate to jump on problems on the business or product side." They also state a quality-over-quantity hiring stance: "We are looking for quality of experience over quantity."

Size / stage / funding: Venture-backed startup — "raised over $31M from leading investors in publishing, AI, and tech" (their own site), and the JD says "backed by top VCs", linking an Axios piece from October 2024 about the raise. Headcount not stated anywhere fetched; the language ("small", multiple hats, startup experience preferred) points to an early-stage team.

Locations / HQ: New York, NY (stated on their website footer/contact area).

Remote policy: "Remote work friendly" / "Remote friendly" — stated in the JD twice. No async or handbook culture claims. No formal remote-policy page was found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — verified negative. The JD lists as a hard requirement: "Located and eligible to work in the US. Remote work friendly." Remote is US-scoped.

Engineering & tech: Backend in **Go** ("Design, develop, and maintain robust and scalable backend systems using Go and other modern technologies"), with React on the frontend that engineers are expected to flex into ("a backend engineer who can occasionally flex to the frontend"). Distributed systems and scaling architectures, databases and data management, Git, AWS preferred, CI/CD, unit and integration testing. The role owns "the entire development lifecycle of assigned features, from initial design and implementation to testing, deployment, and monitoring", and includes direct customer interaction: "Interact directly with customers to gather feedback and understand their needs." Bar is 6+ years. Open-source contributions and side projects are called out as bonuses.

Notable / other: Scale claims on their own homepage: 987B+ website visits analyzed and 22B+ AI bot scrapes detected. Benefits stated in the JD: unlimited PTO, health/dental/vision, equity. Pay is disclosed directly in the JD (see data/company-comp) — "150-200K base salary, equity and benefits."

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Engineer, Backend.
