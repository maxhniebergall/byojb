# Salesforce — research
Provider/key: workday:salesforce/external_career_site | company_type: product

What they do: Salesforce is a large public enterprise SaaS company — CRM plus an ecosystem of clouds (Sales, Service, Data Cloud, Slack, MuleSoft, Tableau, and the acquired Informatica data-governance/MDM lines, which appear in current postings). Its current strategic framing is agentic AI: in its own words, "Salesforce is the #1 AI CRM, where humans with agents drive customer success together" and "Agentforce is the future of AI, and you are the future of Salesforce."

How they describe themselves: From their own JD boilerplate — "Here, ambition meets action. Tech meets trust. And innovation isn't a buzzword — it's a way of life. The world of work as we know it is changing and we're looking for Trailblazers who are passionate about bettering business and the world through AI, driving innovation, and keeping Salesforce's core values at the heart of it all." Also: "Ready to level-up your career at the company leading workforce transformation in the agentic era?" and "At Salesforce, you'll be limitless in all areas of your life. Our benefits and resources support you to find balance and be your best, and our AI agents accelerate your impact so you can do your best." They disclose AI use in recruiting: "Salesforce uses artificial intelligence (AI) tools to help our recruiters assess and evaluate candidates' resumes and qualifications throughout the recruiting process. Humans will always make any candidate selection and hiring decisions." Strong equal-opportunity and accommodations statements. They also ask candidates to "consider applying for a maximum of 3 roles within 12 months".

Size / stage / funding: Large, public, mature. (Specific headcount/revenue not fetched first-party this session — WebSearch was exhausted; the JD boilerplate is the only company-authored source used here.) Registry sees 82 postings on this tenant, 42 of them captured in the research file with a high churn rate — 14 of 42 already dead — indicating a fast-moving, high-volume req pipeline.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco-centred, with heavy engineering presence in Washington State (Bellevue and Seattle appear on the majority of postings), plus California, Georgia (Atlanta), Virginia (Herndon), Massachusetts (Burlington), Hyderabad (India) and Vancouver, Canada. Most postings are multi-location ("2 Locations", "3 Locations", up to "7 Locations").

Remote policy: Predominantly hub/office-anchored. The Stage-3 extractor read most postings with a stated policy as "hybrid" and geo "us_only"; exactly one live posting on file is marked remote — "Senior / Lead / Principal Software Engineer - Foundations Team (Infrastructure/Platform)" — and that one is us_only. No async or remote-first language appears anywhere in the captured JD text.

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO as a general matter, and unclear-at-best for the single Canadian role. Of 28 live relevant postings, exactly one is Canadian: "Software Engineering SMTS" in Canada - Vancouver (JR343396). Its JD body states no remote or hybrid arrangement at all; it names a British Columbia salary range, so it is a BC-employed role, but it is office-anchored in Vancouver with nothing indicating remote-from-anywhere-in-Canada. Every other relevant posting is US-located and several are explicitly us_only. From Kimberley BC this is effectively a relocation-to-Vancouver proposition, not a remote opportunity.

Engineering & tech: Genuine deep-infrastructure work exists. The Vancouver role is on the Ajna team, which "builds and operates Salesforce's internal Kafka-as-a-Service platform — the distributed messaging backbone that powers real-time data pipelines, alerting, analytics, and event streaming across every major Salesforce product and cloud environment", serving "internal engineering teams across commercial, GovCloud, and sovereign cloud environments globally". Other live reqs cover observability (Slack), Flink, big-data platform and distributed systems, enterprise IAM, FinOps and capacity platform, release engineering for the agentic platform, AI platform / ML engineering, and an SRE systems role requiring TS/SCI clearance. Ladder uses MTS / SMTS / LMTS / PMTS titles.

Notable / other: Pay transparency is thorough — US postings carry state-law ranges and the BC posting carries a BC range, so the registry already holds ten comp rows for this company. Postings churn quickly (a third of captured reqs went dead). The "Agentforce"/agentic-era framing saturates the boilerplate on every posting regardless of the underlying role, so it is not a reliable signal about any individual team's work.

Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineering SMTS — Canada - Vancouver (Ajna / Kafka-as-a-Service); Senior / Lead / Principal Software Engineer - Foundations Team (Infrastructure/Platform) — remote, US only; Sr. Software Engineer, Observability - Slack; Software Engineering Architect - FinOps & Capacity Platform; Big Data Platform & Distributed Systems (Mid/Senior/Lead/Principal); Flink Lead Software Engineer.
