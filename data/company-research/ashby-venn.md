# Venn — research
Provider/key: ashby:venn | company_type: product
What they do: Venn is a Canadian B2B fintech offering a digital business-banking platform for Canadian entrepreneurs and small businesses — multi-currency accounts, international money transfers/FX, virtual and physical cards, and (per the JD roadmap) lending. It integrates with banking partners, card processors, payment systems and accounting tools. In its own words it is "the ONLY digital banking platform in Canada offering a truly modern solution", positioned against the incumbent Canadian big-five banks.
How they describe themselves: From their own JDs: "Venn is the fastest-growing B2B fintech in Canada. We're on a mission to transform business banking for Canadian entrepreneurs and are looking to hire **A-players** interested in disrupting traditional banking practices... we have seen accelerated growth—scaling to support more than 10,000 Canadian businesses in under two years. Business owners are tired of the big banks' monopoly and are looking for modern tools that replace the legacy systems that have been their only option for over 100 years." Hiring language asks for a "Quick learner with an **ambitious and results-driven** personality" and an "**Entrepreneurial spirit**, as you'll be joining on the ground floor and building the business", plus experience "shipping products in a **fast-paced environment like an early-stage startup**". One JD closes with "This role is for an existing vacancy and we are looking to fill this role immediately." No separate published values page was reachable.
Size / stage / funding: Early-stage/growth. "Raised over $26.5M from Tier 1 investors such as Gradient, Left Lane Capital, Intact, and XYZ Venture Capital." 10,000+ business customers in under two years. Headcount not stated; the "ground floor" framing implies a small team. Venture-funded and therefore runway-dependent — not profitable so far as any first-party source shows.
Locations / HQ: Toronto. All five live postings are listed simply "Toronto".
Remote policy: **Not stated anywhere in their own JDs.** Neither remote, hybrid nor onsite is described; the perks list is minimal ("Competitive salary. Latest tech and equipment.").
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Unclear, leaning no.** Every posting carries a bare "Toronto" location with no remote qualifier, and nothing in the JDs mentions distributed or remote work. Combined with the "joining on the ground floor and building the business" framing, the working assumption should be a Toronto-based (onsite or hybrid) team. Not verified either way — no first-party careers page was fetched because no company domain is derivable from the registry URLs.
Engineering & tech: **Java** is the primary backend language ("5+ years of professional experience with Java", "6+ years of experience in backend development"). Backend work is financial-services product build-out — transfers, cards, lending — plus integration with banking partners and third-party APIs for virtual card issuing, international money transfer and financial-data accuracy. The data side owns "Venn's data infrastructure, including pipelines, models, warehouses, and reporting layers", ETL/ELT from the core product, banking partners, card processors, payment systems, accounting integrations, CRM and marketing tools; data modelling, data quality, observability, lineage, testing and governance; event schemas and tracking architecture with engineering. Notably the data role also covers "AI-assisted workflows, MCPs, and trusted data tools", dashboards for transaction volume/card spend/FX/balances/revenue, **and** marketing/growth analytics — attribution, paid-media incrementality, web analytics, CRO, lifecycle analytics, SEO and organic acquisition reporting.
Notable / other: The engineer is explicitly expected to be "Operating very independently and working directly with product owners" — autonomy is high but so is exposure to product pressure. The data role is effectively data-engineering-plus-analytics-plus-marketing-measurement in one seat. Java backend and Postgres-style data-pipeline work do line up with the candidate's actual toolchain.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Backend Engineer (x2), Senior Software Engineer, Backend; Data Engineer; Senior Software Engineer, Data — all Toronto.
