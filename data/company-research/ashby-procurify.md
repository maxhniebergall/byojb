# Procurify — research
Provider/key: ashby:procurify | company_type: product

What they do: Procurify is "the Intelligent Spend Management company." Its platform unifies data across procurement, accounts payable and expenses so finance and operations teams can "capture, analyze, and optimize every dollar of spend." Customers are mid-market organizations modernizing how they manage business spend. B2B finance SaaS — a back-office, mission-critical domain rather than a consumer growth product.

How they describe themselves: "We're on a mission to give all organizations unprecedented visibility and control over their business spend." On culture: "Procurify is a remote-first company with a big heart and a strong ambition to modernize the way organizations manage business spend. We're looking for talented and team-driven individuals to join us on our journey to build a place where the best people want to work – a place where every team member is empowered to apply their best thinking, push boundaries thoughtfully, and drive high performance together." Note the explicit "high performance" framing alongside the "thoughtfully" hedge.

Size / stage / funding: ~150-160 employees. Private; ~$102M raised across 6 rounds, including a $50M Series C, with a $20M equity round most recently (Oct 2024). Backed by leading investors. Growth-stage, not early.

Locations / HQ: Headquartered in Vancouver, British Columbia. Team distributed across Canada — Vancouver, Calgary, Toronto, Ottawa, Montreal.

Remote policy: **Remote-first with flexible working hours.** Their own JD: "Flexible working: We're a remote-first organization with flexible working hours. Work anywhere from within Canada!" Employment is restricted to within Canada for tax purposes.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes — Canada is the only eligible geography.** The Staff Backend Engineer posting is listed as "Canada Remote" and the JD explicitly says work anywhere within Canada. HQ is in BC, so British Columbia and Pacific/Mountain hours are well within norms. No relocation or office attendance implied.

Engineering & tech: Backend/platform engineering on a spend-management data platform — procurement, AP and expense data unification, which implies integrations, workflow engines, and financial data correctness work. They are also hiring a Senior AI Engineer, indicating an applied-AI effort layered on their spend data ("Intelligent Spend Management").

Compensation (first-party): The Staff Backend Engineer JD states a base salary range of **$160,000–$200,000 CAD**, explicitly "not inclusive of any commission, benefits or equity."

Notable / other: Canadian-owned and Canadian-employing throughout, which removes the cross-border employment friction that disqualifies many US companies.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Backend Engineer (Canada Remote); Senior AI Engineer.
