# Numeric — research
Provider/key: ashby:numeric | company_type: product

What they do: Numeric builds an AI-powered accounting/close-automation platform — "the modern data platform for accounting and finance." It automates journal entries, reconciliations, flux analysis and SOX-compliance workflows for enterprise finance teams, ingesting events and building "a unified graph of financial data." Customers include Anthropic, Notion, Brex, OpenAI, Cursor, Plaid, Wealthfront, Mercury, Figma and Asana.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "Empower every business to have perfect financial clarity." Their stated values, in their own words: **Brick by Brick** (execution and action over ideation; speed as competitive advantage), **Love the Game** (care deeply about craft and collaborative work), **Own the Outcome** (autonomy with accountability; roles extend beyond traditional scope), **SALY** (reject "Same As Last Year" thinking; first-principles over legacy process), **Earn the Right** (progression based on demonstrated capability, hiring for trajectory over credentials). JD tone is engineering-serious: "We tackle the hard problems. Accounting does not need more duct tape." / "As engineers, we do our homework."

Size / stage / funding: ~118 employees (Apr 2026). $51M Series B raised late 2025; ~$89M total across 4 rounds. Investors: IVP, Menlo Ventures, Founders Fund, 8VC, plus founders/execs from Ramp and Segment. Founded 2020 by Parker Gilbert, Andrew Bihl, Anthony Alvernaz.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco; offices New York and London. Engineering also hiring in Vancouver, BC.

Remote policy: Explicitly an **in-person company** — the about page emphasizes co-located teams and in-person collaboration. No stated remote policy. Roles are tied to a named office city (the Developer Platform JD states flatly "Location: Vancouver, BC").

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — not remote-eligible.** The Canadian presence is an in-office Vancouver, BC site, not a remote-Canada hire. Verified from the company's own about page ("in-person company") plus the Developer Platform JD's single-city location line. For a candidate in Kimberley, BC who cannot relocate, this is effectively disqualifying.

Engineering & tech: AWS; in-house tooling for remote development environments, internal tools and networking, shared AI tools (Dev Platform team). Product side is heavy data-systems work — event ingestion, transformation, value-movement matching, a unified financial data graph; "Numeric Intelligence" AI layer; NetSuite and bank-API integrations at scale. Hires from Stripe, Asana, Segment, Dropbox, MongoDB. Dev Platform team exists specifically to accelerate internal developers — genuine platform/utility work.

Notable / other: Series B momentum with a high-profile AI-company customer base. Dev Platform JD asks for 7+ years, AWS, networking/security/infrastructure depth, and explicitly rewards an advanced personal AI development workflow.

Open relevant roles (sample): Software Engineer, Developer Platform (Vancouver); Software Engineer, Product (San Francisco / Vancouver / New York).
