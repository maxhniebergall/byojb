# Upbound — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:upbound | company_type: product

What they do: Upbound is the **creator and primary maintainer of Crossplane**, the CNCF-graduated open-source framework for building cloud-native control planes on top of Kubernetes. Commercially it sells the **Intelligent Control Plane** / Upbound Platform, including **Upbound Spaces** — multi-control-plane management software that runs in Upbound Cloud or self-hosted on customers' premises. The pitch is a platform layer that makes infrastructure "programmable, autonomous, and composable" so platform-engineering teams can provision, operate and adapt infrastructure at scale. Customers are Fortune 500 companies and platform engineers across 100+ countries. This is pure infrastructure/developer-tooling — the buyer is a platform team, not an end user.

How they describe themselves: Mission is "to power the AI-native enterprise with a foundational platform layer that helps teams provision, operate, and adapt infrastructure at scale — so platforms are ready for **both humans and AI agents**." Product philosophy: make infrastructure "as programmable, composable, and scalable as software itself," and build "infrastructure platforms at the speed of AI, safely and with trust." Their careers page states **seven values**: accountability; decisive collaboration; craftsmanship; community care; customer advocacy; ownership mindset; and vulnerability / psychological safety — with the framing "We operationalize our values with intent and care to ensure we have a healthy culture where every UpBounder can thrive." On craftsmanship specifically: "A true craftsperson is a lifelong learner who strives to deliver the best possible result." The current positioning leans hard on "the Agentic AI Era" as a market narrative.

Size / stage / funding: **Series B**, **$69M raised to date**, backed by **GV (formerly Google Ventures), Altimeter Capital, and Intel Capital**. Headcount is not disclosed on any of their public pages. Not stated to be profitable. Open-source traction is the strongest proof point: Crossplane has **100M+ downloads**, **1,000+ teams / production users**, and **13,000+ Slack community members**, and is a **CNCF graduated** project — the highest CNCF maturity tier, which is a meaningful durability signal independent of the company's commercial position.

Locations / HQ: "Globally distributed team." No HQ is stated on the about or careers pages (the company originated in Seattle). Benefits are administered "by country," with specific US-only items called out.

Remote policy: **"Work From Anywhere"** with a home-office stipend. Globally distributed; the live engineering role is tagged `#LI-REMOTE` and titled "Senior Software Engineer **[REMOTE]**". No stated core hours or return-to-office expectation. On-call rotation exists to support Upbound Cloud.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** The live Senior Software Engineer posting is located "**North America - Remote**" and — decisively — its posted salary range is denominated in **CAD**, which means the company is set up to pay and employ in Canada rather than merely tolerating it. Combined with "Work From Anywhere" and country-by-country benefits administration, Canada is clearly in scope. No stated timezone pin on the role, so Mountain Time appears workable.

Compensation: The live Senior Software Engineer [REMOTE] posting states a range of **CAD $160,000 – $220,000 base** (recorded in `data/company-comp.jsonl` as a `direct` / `jd_posted` band for platform_infra / senior). Other stated benefits: **stock option equity grants**, **100% premium coverage for healthcare** (US plans include HSA, FSA, PPO, HMO), 401(k) for US employees, flexible PTO plus 10+ observed holidays, and home-office stipends. Benefits are localised by country. No public compensation-philosophy or levels page.

Engineering & tech: **Go, Kubernetes, and Crossplane** — writing operators, controllers and add-ons that interface with the Kubernetes API server, with heavy emphasis on reconciliation loops, API rate limits and controller performance. Multi-tenant SaaS operations plus self-hosted customer distributions. Observability stack named explicitly: **Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry, distributed tracing**. The Spaces team both builds and operates: full project lifecycle "discovery, analysis, architecture, design, review, documentation, building, migration, automation, deployment, production-readiness, and ongoing operational support," plus on-call, post-incident reviews, runbooks and design documents. Substantial **open-source contribution to Crossplane** is part of the job. Written culture is strong — "thoughtful and thorough design documents" and "detailed post-incident reviews" are explicit responsibilities, as is authoring documentation, user guides and blog posts.

Notable / other: The engineer is expected to work **directly with customers** — "comfortable working directly with customers to understand, reproduce, and resolve complex technical issues in their environments," and to debug inside customer environments including self-managed deployments. The "Why Upbound?" section is oddly revenue-flavoured for an engineering role: "help shape the systems and strategies that drive predictable, scalable growth in a product-led company embracing usage-based models... directly impact how revenue is generated and scaled," alongside "if you're excited to build from the ground up." Values language around psychological safety is echoed in the JD: "help create a safe environment where everyone can contribute, learn from failures, share on-call knowledge."
