# d1g1t — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:d1g1t | company_type: product

What they do: d1g1t is a Toronto fintech selling an enterprise wealth-management platform. In their own
words: "d1g1t built the industry's first enterprise wealth management platform powered by
institutional-grade analytics and risk management tools. Founded and led by veteran fintech engineers
who've built portfolio systems for global banks, hedge funds, and regulators, d1g1t gives wealth
management firms capabilities they've never had before." The platform "replaces fragmented legacy
systems with a unified solution for portfolio management, advanced analytics, risk management,
reporting, and operational workflows across the wealth advisory lifecycle." Customers are "Top
Multi-family Offices, RIAs, Broker-Dealers, and Bank Advisor Networks", with RBC Wealth Management
($1.4 trillion AUM), CI Financial and Raymond James named on their site.

How they describe themselves: Mission — "Empowering wealth management firms to scale up the high-value,
human services that set them apart in a digital world." Their careers page lists six values in full:
"Be Human" (leading with empathy), "Be Impactful" (making a positive difference), "Be Courageous"
(taking calculated risks), "Be Innovative" (using science and creativity), "Be Accountable" (valuing
details and facts), and "Be Transparent" (fostering trust through honest communication). A JD restates
these: "You are innovative in your problem-solving, courageous in bringing new ideas to the table,
accountable for your team's success, transparent in your communication, impactful in your delivery, and
above all, genuinely human in how you treat others." Employee quotes on the careers page describe
"a fast-paced environment where I am able to innovate and propose my own solutions", and a JD closes on
"A genuinely collaborative culture at a fast-growing fintech."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, small-to-mid (headcount not disclosed on their site).
Originated out of the Fields Institute in 2016-2017. Self-described as "fast-growing" and "Ranked among
Canada's top growing companies." Exact funding stage and investor list were not disclosed on the pages
read.

Locations / HQ: Toronto, Ontario. All three live postings are Toronto-scoped.

Remote policy: Hybrid, outcome-framed but office-anchored: "We want people to do their work from where
the best outcomes are achieved. In the office for collaboration and at home where deep work is
required." One JD softens this to "Remote friendly position with occasional office time as needed."
Employees may "work abroad for up to two months a year."

Remote-Canada eligibility: UNCLEAR, leaning NO for a candidate outside Toronto. All three live postings
carry location "Toronto, Ontario"; the extracted facets mark the Backend role as hybrid/Canada. The
"remote friendly... with occasional office time as needed" line on the Integrations JD leaves a crack
open, but "in the office for collaboration" is the stated company model and there is no evidence of
hiring anywhere in Canada outside the GTA. A candidate in interior BC would be a two-time-zone,
flight-distance exception to their own policy.

Engineering & tech: Python-centric backend and data engineering on a mature codebase. Backend role:
"Design, build, test, deploy, and maintain backend features using Python, Django, PostgreSQL, and
related technologies", "Navigate and improve a large, mature codebase with years of production
history", "Hands-on experience with PostgreSQL, including indexing, query optimization, and diagnosing
slow queries", plus Celery, Redis, REST APIs, and NumPy/pandas as a nice-to-have. Integrations role:
"Lead the design and development of robust, scalable ETL processes using Python and Airflow", internal
tooling, "managing/mentoring pods of 1-2 other engineers", with pandas/polars, Airflow and AWS as
pluses. Both ask 5+ years. AI expectation is stated as curiosity rather than a product mandate: "You are
comfortable with AI development and have an eager desire to learn, explore, and push the boundaries of
how we can leverage new AI technologies."

Notable / other: Benefits are itemised on the careers page — medical/dental/vision, mental-health and
wellness allowances, vacation/sick/personal days, birthday day off, a $500+ annual professional
development fund, bi-annual company events; plus a fitness & wellness credit and an Employee & Family
Assistance Program per the JD. Domain (portfolio analytics, risk, regulatory reporting) is
mission-critical and data-heavy.

Pay (first-party, stated on their own JDs): "Senior Software Engineer, Integrations" — "$120,000 -
$140,000" CAD base; "Senior Software Engineer, Backend" — "$120,000 - $160,000" CAD base, with
performance bonus.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Backend (Toronto); Senior Software Engineer,
Integrations (Toronto); Software Engineer, Reports.
