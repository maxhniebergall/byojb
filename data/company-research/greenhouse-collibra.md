# Collibra — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:collibra | company_type: product

What they do: Collibra sells a unified **data and AI governance** platform — data cataloguing, lineage, quality, policy/compliance management and, increasingly, AI-governance tooling for enterprises. It is a classic enterprise-infrastructure/"boring but mission-critical" software business: customers are large regulated organisations that need to know what data they have and who may use it. Their site claims **700+ customers, 100+ of the Fortune 500, and 300+ service partners**.

How they describe themselves: The about page emphasises "moving fast, thinking big, collaborative teamwork, high accountability, and building lasting impact," with leadership framed as action-oriented. The engineering JDs lead with a named company value: "**Embrace and Drive Change**," glossed on both postings as "we foster a collaborative culture focused on continuous improvement that **never settles long for good enough**." Both JDs describe the platform team as "geographically diverse" and set out explicit 1-month / 3-month / 6-month "Measures of Success," which is an unusually structured and legible onboarding expectation.

Size / stage / funding: Approximately **1,000 employees globally** per their own about page. Private; ownership and funding status are not disclosed on the page fetched, so stage is **unverified** — though at ~1,000 staff with a Fortune 500 enterprise base it is an established company rather than an early startup.

Locations / HQ: Dual-headquartered in **New York and Brussels**, with operations across the US and Europe. No Canadian office is stated; the only Canadian mention on their about page is **TELUS as a customer** case study.

Remote policy: Not stated as a company-wide policy on the about page. The postings themselves are remote-designated: "Senior Platform Engineer — Remote, USA" and "Staff Platform Engineer — Remote, Europe; Remote, United Kingdom," the latter stating in-body "This is a remote role based in UK or Europe." So remote is normal for this team, but scoped to specific regions per requisition. Both roles include on-call participation.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — verified negative, and unusually explicit.** The Senior Platform Engineer JD states: "Must be eligible to work in the USA without requiring sponsorship" **and** "Because this role supports the US government, it is required that this candidate be a **US citizen who resides on US soil**." The Staff Platform Engineer JD states it "is a remote role based in **UK or Europe**." Both relevant openings therefore exclude a Canada-based worker by their own terms. This is a hard, documented bar rather than an inference.

Engineering & tech: The Platform Infrastructure Engineering team "builds and operates the essential cloud foundation for all Collibra services," reporting to a Senior Manager of Platform Infrastructure Engineering. Stack stated verbatim: **multi-cloud (AWS and GCP, Azure also mentioned), Kubernetes, IaC via Terraform/Helm, Golang automation, GitOps (ArgoCD), Istio service mesh**. Scale is stated as "between tens and hundreds of Kubernetes clusters." Work includes writing **Kubernetes controllers** and automation patterns, developer enablement and golden paths, CI/CD, observability (Datadog, Grafana, Honeycomb; spans/traces), cloud networking (VPCs, routing, load balancing, WAFs, network policies), cloud security (IAM, security groups), and operating **shared data-platform services — Go operators, Kafka, Neo4j/GraphDB** — for product and analytics teams. Preferred certs: CKA/CKAD. The senior role also lists "demonstrated proficiency in leveraging AI tools (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Copilot)" as a requirement. Senior asks 3+ years in platform/SRE/infra; Staff is scoped to leading platform architecture and mentoring.

Notable / other: Some Collibra work **supports the US government**, which is what drives the citizenship restriction on the senior requisition. Benefits, DEI and accommodations pages are published under collibra.com/us/en/company/careers/. Pay is posted transparently on the US requisition but not on the Europe/UK one.

Compensation: Senior Platform Engineer (Remote, USA) posts a **standard base salary range of $168,000–$210,000 USD**, explicitly not eligible for commission, plus "equity ownership at every level, bonus potential, a Flex Fund monthly stipend, pension/401k." Already recorded in `data/company-comp.jsonl`. The Staff Platform Engineer (Europe/UK) posting states no range.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Platform Engineer (Remote, USA); Staff Platform Engineer (Remote, Europe/UK); Software Engineer, DevOps; Senior AI Engineer; Senior AI Engineer, Unstructured AI; Data Engineer.
