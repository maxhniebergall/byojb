# Openly — research
Provider/key: ashby:openly | company_type: product

What they do: Openly is a personal-lines insurance provider (primarily homeowners insurance)
distributed through independent agents. Their words: "Openly has redefined the insurance
experience—replacing decades of complexity with clarity. We're the insurance provider for people who
expect more and the partner independent agents have been waiting for." They summarise their
differentiators as: "Technology That Delivers: We leverage cutting-edge data to modernize underwriting
and deliver tailored insurance solutions"; "Superior Protection: Highly customizable coverage designed
to match the unique needs of modern homeowners"; "A Seamless Experience: Frictionless workflows for
independent agents and fast, human-centered care for policyholders." So: an insurance carrier that
builds its own technology, with independent agents as the distribution channel.

How they describe themselves: "Building a better future for personal lines insurance requires diverse
expertise and thoughtful execution. We bring together dedicated problem-solvers who are excited to
challenge the status quo and do meaningful work."
Core values, verbatim: "At Openly, our people are just as important as our product. Collaboration,
communication, and work-life balance aren't just nice-to-haves—they are core to who we are. We believe
a great company is built on a foundation of shared values, which is why we seek out individuals driven
by **integrity, empathy, teamwork, curiosity, and a healthy sense of urgency**."
On DEI: "We embrace individuality and know that diverse teams build better products. Our commitment to
inclusion across race, gender, age, religion, identity, and background drives our culture and our
business forward every day."
On hiring: "We believe interviewing is a two-way street. We've designed our hiring process to be
transparent, respectful, and human. At every step, you'll have space to showcase your strengths, ask
tough questions, and discover if Openly is the right place for you."

Locations / HQ: Not stated on the postings. All live roles are labelled "Remote - United States".

Remote policy: Remote-first and explicitly long-standing: "**Remote-First Culture:** We supported
remote work long before it became standard, and we remain dedicated to a flexible, distributed team."
Remote-work tooling is named in the stack (Slack, Zoom, Donut). Home-office support: "$1,500 upfront

Remote-Canada eligibility: **NO — verified negative.** Both live JDs state verbatim: "Applicants must

Engineering & tech: A well-specified, modern, boring-in-the-good-way stack, stated identically on both
JDs:
- Backend: Go and PostgreSQL
- Frontend: browser-based VueJS, Webpack, Nuxt, Tailwind
- Research/Data Science: R, ArcGIS, Jupyter Notebooks, Python
- Data: GCP GCS, BigQuery, Composer/Airflow, Cloud Functions, Postgres, SQL, Python, Go, Aiven
  Debezium and Kafka, Fivetran
- Infrastructure: Google Cloud (Cloud Run, Kubernetes, Pub/Sub, BigQuery, CloudSQL) managed with
  Terraform; GitHub for code hosting, DataDog for monitoring, CircleCI for CI/CD
The DevOps/SRE II role: build internal tooling "to help other engineers and the rest of the company
understand and operate our system"; design and implement security best practices; "reduce toil through
automation, including building and maintaining CI/CD infrastructure"; infrastructure as code with
declarative provisioning; "develop high signal-to-noise ratio monitoring and alerting policies and
technology to help us meet our SLOs"; lead incident response and postmortems; contribute to
architectural decisions (microservices vs monoliths, deployment techniques, policies). Bar: 2+ years
of professional infrastructure-automation experience, Python or Go scripting, Terraform preferred,
cloud experience.
The Senior Backend Engineer (Payments & Billing) role: build core systems and APIs; internal tooling
and APIs to make business and engineering processes "easier, faster, and safer"; systemic changes to
align existing systems with architectural goals; integrate external services for payments, documents
and claims; integrate data providers for real-time property-characteristic and risk data; mentorship;
architectural collaboration. Bar: 4+ years backend, professional Go experience required (the interview
includes a Go code challenge), billing/payments system experience highly preferred. Team rituals named:
daily stand-ups, weekly 1:1s, biweekly retros.

Notable / other: Benefits are unusually well documented: comprehensive medical/dental/vision; life and
disability including voluntary supplemental; up to 8 weeks (320 hours) paid parental leave for
eligible employees with additional disability coverage for birthing parents; 401(k) with a 3% company
contribution regardless of personal contributions; $1,500 home-office allowance and $75/month
internet; $2,000/year professional development; a "Be Well" $50/month wellness stipend; volunteer
hours and referral rewards. "Competitive Pay & Equity: Top-tier base salary plus ownership in Openly's
growth." Some roles may carry cash incentives or sales commissions.
