# Safe Software — research
Provider/key: ashby:safe | company_type: product
What they do: Safe Software makes **FME**, described in their own words as "the only All-Data Any-AI Enterprise Integration Platform connecting all your data, anywhere, at any velocity." FME is a long-established spatial/enterprise data integration and ETL platform, historically strongest in GIS and geospatial data but positioned now as general enterprise data integration. Two product lines appear in the postings: **FME Form** (the authoring product) and **FME Flow** (their "enterprise platform for automations, scheduling, and web services"). "With over 30 years of expertise and 25,000+ enterprise customers across 125+ countries, we simplify your data journey, wherever it leads."
How they describe themselves: Five stated core values on their careers page — "**Humans First**" (prioritising human needs in a data-driven context), "**Say it. Do it**" (accountability across all stakeholder relationships), "**Go Beyond**" (pushing past conventional boundaries), "**Act with integrity**" (honest, forthright conduct), and "**Have Fun**" (camaraderie and an enjoyable work environment). Their own JD elaborates the employment offer: "**Meaningful Work**: Make a profound impact across our business, workplace and data integration product"; "**A Supportive Environment**: Feel empowered to share your ideas and implement them with **high autonomy** and team support"; "**Social Responsibility**": paid volunteer time off through Safe-organised community initiatives; "**Learning & Career Development**": an annual learning budget and Safe-paid training; "**Flexible Working Hours**: Flexible and remote-friendly work arrangements to fit your lifestyle. Work when you want"; and an explicit anti-burnout stance — "Maintain a healthy balance of your personal and work life without splitting yourself in two."
Size / stage / funding: Founded **1993**; "headquartered in Surrey, BC with over 300 team members and counting" (JD), and the careers page states "350 Safers across 10 provinces" and "34 Teams". Privately held and independent — there is no VC-funding narrative anywhere in their materials. **Bi-annual profit sharing** plus RRSP/TFSA matching implies sustained profitability. **B Corp certified**, and named "one of BC's Top Employers for 2026".
Locations / HQ: HQ in Surrey, British Columbia. Distributed across "10 provinces" per the careers page. Posting locations in the registry are province-scoped rather than city-scoped: "British Columbia" and "Canada Wide - Excluding Quebec, British Columbia".
Remote policy: "Flexible and remote-friendly work arrangements to fit your lifestyle. Work when you want" (their JD). The careers page fetched did **not** state an explicit remote/hybrid split, so the precise policy is unverified. The office-related perks in the JD — "An Accessible Commute: Regardless of your commute method, we're located close to public transit, and provide complimentary parking and bike storage for our team!" — indicate a real Surrey office culture alongside the flexibility, i.e. likely hybrid for those near HQ.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes for Canada broadly (verified); unclear-to-concerning for BC specifically.** The careers page's "350 Safers across 10 provinces" and the posting location "Canada Wide - Excluding Quebec, British Columbia" together confirm they employ across most of Canada remotely. But that same location string is ambiguous in an important way: it may mean BC candidates are handled separately because BC is the HQ province and expected onsite/hybrid at Surrey — which would be a problem for Kimberley (in BC, but ~8 hours' drive from Surrey and in **Mountain Time**, not Pacific). The AI Enablement role is posted as plain "British Columbia", consistent with a Surrey-anchored arrangement. **This needs direct confirmation with the company** — it is the single question that decides whether Safe is viable. Quebec is excluded outright from the Canada-wide posting.
Engineering & tech: **Java and TypeScript** full-stack on FME Flow, with Python and other scripting as a nice-to-have; CI/CD pipelines and automated testing; REST APIs, webhooks and cloud services (AWS). The AI Enablement function is unusual and worth noting: "AI Enablement is a matrixed function at Safe Software, with engineers embedded across the FME product groups. The mission is to explore the frontier of AI-enabled software development and to transform how we build FME — reshaping our codebase, tooling, and engineering practices so we can take full advantage of what modern AI makes possible. **This is not primarily a feature-development role.**" The work is codebase transformation for AI-assisted development — structure, documentation and test coverage — using tools named explicitly as "Claude Code, Copilot, Cursor, or similar", under a Principal Software Developer for AI Enablement. Teams work with QA, UX and product; sprint and project planning is part of the role. There is also a DevOps ladder.
Notable / other: Benefits are unusually strong and concrete: 3 weeks' vacation plus 6 additional paid seasonal days; extended health, dental, health-or-lifestyle spending account and counselling **from day 1 with no waiting period**; a Parental Leave Top-Up Program; bi-annual profit sharing; RRSP/TFSA matching. They disclose using "automated tools to help us process applications... The final hiring decision is always made by a member of our team." Pay is posted on their JDs, which is a good transparency signal. Recorded/observed bands from their own live postings: **Senior Software Developer, FME Form — CA$128.1K–142.7K base plus bonus**; **DevOps II — CA$81.9K–91.2K base plus bonus**. Both are notably modest for the levels.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Developer, FME Form (Canada Wide – Excluding Quebec, British Columbia); Software Developer II, AI Enablement – FME Flow (British Columbia); DevOps II (British Columbia).
