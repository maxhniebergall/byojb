# CapIntel — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:capintel | company_type: product

What they do: B2B SaaS for wealth management. "CapIntel is a software platform built for wealth
management enterprises to help financial advisors explain complex investment strategies to their
clients. Advisors at some of the biggest banks across North America are winning trust by using
CapIntel to easily compare investments and create compelling, educational presentations." Their own
careers page frames the product as delivering "magical moments to advisors, investors and
customers" — investment presentation and comparison software, plus client onboarding tooling. End
customers are large banks and asset managers; the buyer is an enterprise wealth firm.

How they describe themselves: The careers page emphasises attention to detail, transparent
communication, accountability, collaborative teamwork, and an inclusive, "safe-to-fail" culture.
The JD adds: "We care most about mindset: your drive, curiosity and commitment to delivering great
work. While experience matters, we know that careers aren't always linear." And: "We're a team built
on trust, respect, and collaboration. This powers everything we do and creates a space to challenge
and elevate each other as we work towards our shared vision." The growth framing is prominent:
"Since launching in 2019, CapIntel has seen rapid adoption and industry recognition, earning top
placements in Deloitte's Technology Fast 50 Canada and Fast 500 North America in 2025… To support
this momentum, we're growing our team rapidly."

Size / stage / funding: Founded/launched 2019. Headcount, funding stage and profitability are not
stated on the careers page or in the JD — **unverified**. Deloitte Fast 50/Fast 500 placement in
2025 indicates high revenue growth, which typically implies venture-backed scale-up rather than
profitability. Actively hiring across functions (a Lead Product Manager req was visible alongside
engineering).

Locations / HQ: Canadian-centred with a US presence. Offices listed on the careers page: **Halifax
(NS), Toronto (ON), Gatineau (QC), Montreal (QC), San Francisco (CA), New York City (NY)**. The
engineering posting in the registry is filed as "Montreal, QC (Remote)".

Remote policy: **Hybrid, not remote-first.** In their own words, a "flexible work environment that
balances remote work with meaningful in-person connection", and depending on the role employees may
be required on-site for "cross-functional collaboration, periodic team meetings, or company events
throughout the year."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Partially — Canada-eligible but likely city-anchored.** The company is
Canadian with four Canadian offices, so employment and timezone are not obstacles in principle. But
the posting is tied to Montreal and the stated policy expects periodic on-site presence. The
company's offices are all Eastern/Atlantic time; nothing indicates a Western-Canada or
Mountain-Time hire. Whether a fully-remote BC-based hire is possible is **unverified and doubtful**
given the "balances remote work with in-person connection" framing.

Engineering & tech: TypeScript/JavaScript backend, MongoDB, REST APIs and backend services, AWS,
CI/CD and automated testing. The specific role is integration-flavoured: "Build and maintain backend
services and integrations that support client onboarding", "Implement client-specific backend
solutions", "Support integrations with client systems and assist in resolving technical issues",
and "occasionally work with client technical teams to support integrations and resolve technical
challenges." Collaboration is with Implementation, Data and Product teams. Requirements: "4+ years
of professional experience in backend or full-stack software development" and being "comfortable
collaborating with cross-functional teams in a fast-paced environment."

Compensation: Their careers page promises a "competitive, equitable salary presented upfront", but
no range is posted on this JD and no figure is on record. Benefits: day-one coverage, group RSP,
healthcare and lifestyle spending accounts with flex credits, vacation increasing with tenure, plus
"life-care days", mentorship and leadership development.

Notable / other: The single relevant live posting is a mid-level ("Software Developer", 4+ years)
requisition dated 2026-05-20 and last seen 2026-06-26 — it may already be stale.

Open relevant roles (sample): Software Developer — Engineering (Montreal, QC (Remote)).
