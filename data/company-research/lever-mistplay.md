# Mistplay — research
Provider/key: lever:mistplay | company_type: product

What they do: Mistplay operates a mobile-gaming loyalty/rewards platform — "the #1 loyalty app for mobile gamers". Users discover and play mobile games through the Mistplay app, earn points for time and money spent in those games, and redeem points for gift cards and sweepstakes entries (Amazon, Walmart, Google Play, Visa are named on their site). The business is effectively a user-acquisition and engagement channel sold to mobile game publishers, monetised through the players it routes into partner titles. Their site reports over 10 million users, $205,074,442 in total reward value redeemed to date, and a 4.5 average app-store rating across 945k+ ratings.

How they describe themselves: Mission stated as "to be the best way to play mobile games for everyone everywhere!" Their consumer-facing positioning leans on legitimacy and trust — testimonials emphasise that the app is "legit", pays out reliably, and helps players discover games. No formal values/principles list was reachable on the pages fetched; the corporate site content available was consumer-marketing rather than culture material. The JD's own tone is notably sober and engineering-serious rather than hype-driven (see below).

Size / stage / funding: Established private company with a large user base (10M+ users). Specific headcount, funding stage and profitability were **not disclosed** on the pages fetched and are unverified here. The company is bilingual French/English in its Canadian operations (the Staff/Principal JD is published in full in both languages), consistent with a substantial Quebec presence.

Locations / HQ: Canada — Toronto (400 University Ave) and Montreal (1001 Blvd. Robert-Bourassa) offices, both named explicitly in the JD. The bilingual posting and the Montreal address suggest Montreal is the primary Canadian engineering base.

Remote policy: **Hybrid, mandatory.** Stated verbatim at the top of the JD in both languages: "In Canada 🇨🇦, Mistplay follows a 2 days/week in-office hybrid model in Toronto (400 University Ave) & Montreal (1001 Blvd. Robert-Bourassa)". This is a company-level policy statement, not a role-specific one.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No for a BC-based worker — verified negative.** Mistplay is a Canadian employer and clearly employs Canadians, but its Canadian work model requires two days per week physically in a Toronto or Montreal office. Kimberley, BC is neither, and there is no stated remote exception. Additionally the office hours are Eastern Time, three hours ahead of Mountain.

Engineering & tech: The Staff/Principal Engineer, Data Platform role describes a genuine platform organisation, reporting to a Director of Data Platform, and is explicit that "this role is not analytics-focused, but rather large-scale data systems engineering." Scope covers: ingestion and pipeline infrastructure for batch and streaming sources, with schema evolution, data contracts and end-to-end lineage; warehouse/lakehouse architecture including storage-layer, partitioning and access-pattern strategy; a transformation and orchestration layer (dbt, Spark named) with data-quality contracts and test frameworks; a low-latency data serving/access layer with caching, materialisation and API strategies plus freshness/consistency/query-performance SLAs; data observability — quality monitoring, anomaly detection, freshness checks, data SLOs, incident response and post-mortems; data catalog, metadata management and discoverability; and platform tooling evolution and migrations (Spark, dbt, Airflow, Kafka, data catalogs named). Requirements: 10+ years building and operating production data platforms; strong Python, Scala or Go; distributed data systems at scale. The role is framed as a **strong individual contributor** partnering with Data Science, ML Platform and Backend — not a management track. Named/implied stack: Spark, dbt, Airflow, Kafka, lakehouse/warehouse, data catalogs.

Notable / other: No salary is posted on the role and the ATS compensation field is empty. Company is consumer/gaming/rewards in domain, which brings ad-tech-adjacent incentive dynamics, but the data-platform work itself is internal-facing infrastructure. Entity confirmed from the JD text (mobile-gaming loyalty app, Toronto/Montreal offices) against the registry record.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff/Principal Engineer, Data Platform (Montreal, hybrid).
