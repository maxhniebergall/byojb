# ActBlue — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:actblue | company_type: product
What they do: A **nonprofit** that builds and operates the online small-dollar fundraising platform used by Democratic campaigns and progressive organizations in the US. Their words: "building change through the power of people" — "if you've donated to a Democratic campaign or a progressive organization online, you've probably used our platform". Customers range from local candidates to national movements; the platform is high-stakes and extremely spiky (election-night peak load).
How they describe themselves: Vision stated verbatim — "We envision a democracy where everyone looking to make progressive people-powered change can easily and effectively deploy their resources, energy, and creativity to shape our country and futures." Their careers page lists five value themes: collaborative community, courageous and principled service, responsible innovation, respect and inclusivity, and reliability and trust. Engineering is described as "cross-functional teams that operate independently in alignment with ActBlue's broader organizational goals", made of "empathetic and passionate people". No hustle/rocketship language anywhere in their JDs; the emphasis is on documentation, monitoring, mentorship and paying down tech debt.
Size / stage / funding: Nonprofit, not venture-backed — no runway/burn dynamic. Exact headcount not stated on the pages read. 10 open roles at scan time. **Technical staff are unionized**: "The terms and conditions of this position are subject to a collective bargaining agreement with the Communications Workers of America, the exclusive bargaining agent of covered ActBlue Technical Services employees." Note the political exposure: as a partisan-infrastructure nonprofit it has been the subject of US political and regulatory attention.
Locations / HQ: US-based (Somerville/Boston-area roots — perks include a Boston Globe subscription); "our teams of diverse talent are based all across the country".
Remote policy: **Remote-first**, with flexible schedules and unlimited PTO. From the JD: "Employees can expect to work with distributed teams across all U.S. time zones." $1,000 home-office setup allowance for new full-time remote employees. Not an async-handbook shop, but explicitly distributed.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — treat as US-only.** Every location signal is US ("across the country", "all U.S. time zones"), and the JDs state plainly: "**ActBlue is unable to sponsor work visas at this time.**" A CWA collective-bargaining agreement covering "ActBlue Technical Services employees" is a further US-employment structure. Nothing suggests a Canadian entity.
Engineering & tech: Backend Ruby on Rails + PostgreSQL + Sidekiq + Node.js; frontend React with JavaScript/TypeScript; REST and GraphQL APIs; Yarn/Webpack, ESLint/Prettier. Data platform: Python-first pipelines, dbt, Fivetran, Hightouch (reverse ETL), Redshift **migrating to Databricks**, Vertex AI and Databricks for ML/MLOps, Datadog for observability, AWS cloud-native, CI/CD for data pipelines, Pytest/dbt tests. They explicitly welcome agentic AI tooling ("Claude Code and OpenAI Codex") and value regulated-environment experience.
Notable / other: Benefits are unusually strong — fully paid, trans-inclusive health/dental/vision for employees *and* families, fully paid HRA, fully paid short- and long-term disability, unlimited time off, automatic 2% employer 401(k) contribution plus up to 6% match, minimum three months paid medical/family/parental leave for all new parents including adoption, quarterly snack deliveries, Boston Globe and NYT subscriptions. Background check at offer. They anti-phish their own process (only actblue.com / actbluetech.com email).
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Data Engineer II (Remote); Staff Software Engineer, Extensibility and Integration (Remote); Senior Software Engineer I, Community Building.
Pay: transparent three-point ranges on their own JDs — Senior Data Engineer II $173,676 / $192,209 / $210,741; Staff Software Engineer $191,679 / $216,566 / $241,452. They state "ActBlue is committed to consistent compensation practices across our organization", with offers set by experience, interview performance and team salary parity — i.e. a real levelled scale, likely union-negotiated. Bands already on record.
