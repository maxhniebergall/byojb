# Klue — research
Provider/key: ashby:klue | company_type: product

What they do: Klue builds competitive-intelligence / "competitive enablement" software for B2B revenue teams — it collects and curates competitor signals (news, press releases, webpage changes, Slack posts, emails, reviews, CRM data) into battlecards and insights for sales. Products include a Compete Agent, a Win-Loss analysis suite, and AI features such as Auto Insights and Deal Tips.

How they describe themselves: Mission is "defining the category of Competitive Enablement". Values as stated on their careers page and JDs: "Unlock Your Potential. Take Risks. Take Action. Innovate"; they say they support employees to "unlock what they're truly capable of" and that teams should "have their back when they take a risk and fail". The JD is explicit about pace and intensity: "The environment here feels more startup than corporate. You'll roll up your sleeves, build, iterate, and level up"; "if you're looking to join a category-defining company… where you own big outcomes, influence real customers, and work with smart, gritty people… you'll thrive here"; and "High Performance Culture. We reward high performance and growth through career development, coaching, and annual performance reviews."

Size / stage / funding: Growth-stage private Vancouver SaaS company; headcount and current funding stage are not published on the careers page. Repeat Deloitte Technology Fast 50 winner plus assorted workplace-culture awards.

Locations / HQ: Vancouver, BC (HQ). Stated hiring hubs: "🇨🇦 Vancouver (HQ), 🇨🇦 Toronto, 🇬🇧 London".

Remote policy: Office-first hybrid, stated verbatim: "We work Office-First, with a hybrid touch, and work together in our vibrant offices on Mondays, Wednesdays & Thursdays to collaborate, brainstorm, and build together."

Remote-Canada eligibility: NO — not for a candidate outside a hub city. Verified from the JD text itself: three fixed in-office days per week in Vancouver, Toronto or London, and postings are located "Vancouver, British Columbia" and "Toronto, Ontario". Being in British Columbia is not sufficient; the requirement is commuting distance to the Vancouver office. This is effectively disqualifying from Kimberley.

Engineering & tech: Python backend; LLM agents at scale — multi-agent orchestration and sub-agent design, retrieval pipelines, RAG, prompt design, caching and latency optimization, inference-cost optimization at scale; evaluation frameworks (automated, offline and human-in-the-loop) measuring relevance, quality, latency and end-to-end task success; human-in-the-loop review and correction workflows. Infrastructure: Elasticsearch, Pinecone, PGVector, PostgreSQL, Temporal, PydanticAI, Logfire, Docker, Kubernetes, GCP, CI/CD. They explicitly expect daily use of AI coding tools ("Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code, or similar") and can speak to how it changed how you build.

Notable / other: Compensation is posted openly on the ATS: Senior Software Engineer (Agents / AI) CA$140K–CA$230K plus equity; Software Engineer II, AI CA$115K–CA$149K plus equity. Benefits: extended health & dental from day 1, Employee Stock Option Plan for all full-time employees, "Take The Time You Need" vacation, Mac or PC of choice. The JD stresses customer-orientation heavily — "Ship with the customer in mind… You're energized by understanding how customers use the product."

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Agents (Vancouver); Senior Software Engineer, AI (Toronto); Software Engineer II, AI (Toronto, since closed).
