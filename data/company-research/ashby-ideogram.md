# Ideogram — research
Provider/key: ashby:ideogram | company_type: product

What they do: In their own words, "Ideogram's mission is to make world-class design accessible to everyone, multiplying human creativity. We build proprietary generative media models and AI native creative workflows, tackling unsolved challenges in graphic design." They train and serve their own text-to-image/generative-media foundation models (notably strong at rendering text within images) and sell consumer, enterprise and custom-model offerings via ideogram.ai. This is a first-party model company, not an API reseller or prompt-wrapper.

How they describe themselves: Mission as above. On pedigree: "Our team includes builders with a track record of technology breakthroughs including early research in Diffusion Models, Google's Imagen, and Imagen Video. We care about design, taste, and craft as much as research and engineering – shipping experiences that creatives actually love." On culture, verbatim: "We're a team of exceptionally talented, curious builders who love solving tough problems and turning bold ideas into reality. We move fast, collaborate deeply, and operate without unnecessary hierarchy, because we believe the best ideas can come from anyone. Everyone at Ideogram rolls up their sleeves to make our products and our customers successful. We thrive on curiosity, creativity, and shared ownership. We believe that small, dedicated teams working together with trust and purpose can move faster, think bigger, and create amazing things." They also state a commitment to "welcoming everyone — regardless of gender identity, orientation, or expression." Role requirements repeat the theme: "Deep sense of ownership," "Thrives in fast-moving, ambiguous environments with a strong bias toward action," "The grit and adaptability to solve complex technical challenges that evolve day to day."

Size / stage / funding: "We've raised nearly $100M, led by Andreessen Horowitz and Index Ventures" (their own statement). Engineering & tech: Genuinely substantive infrastructure work, not a wrapper. ML Data Infrastructure role: "Building robust data infrastructure that powers foundation models at petabyte scale, ensuring reliability and performance across multi-modal training pipelines"; "Optimizing data processing workflows for massive throughput, working hands-on with distributed systems, TPU infrastructure, and large-scale storage solutions"; partnering with research scientists to turn data requirements into "production-grade systems." ML Infrastructure role: "build the systems that power training and serving for our generative AI models at scale... from designing distributed training infrastructure to optimizing inference pipelines that serve millions of users, with a relentless focus on reliability, speed, and efficiency," including "worker scaling for training or inference workloads." Stack, stated: backend primarily Python, with Kubernetes, GCP (Bigtable, BigQuery, Spanner, Pub/Sub), Docker and Terraform; GPU/TPU workloads on Kubernetes clusters; complex Linux environments.

Seniority: Notably junior for the titles. The ML Infrastructure role asks "1-4 years developing and shipping large-scale production infrastructure"; ML Data Infrastructure asks "2-5 years." Both are plain "Software Engineer" titles with no senior/staff designation.

Stated benefits: 4 weeks vacation; health, vision and dental from day one; RRSP/401(k) with employer match up to 4%; top-of-the-line equipment; daily in-office lunches and dinners; "Autonomy to explore and experiment."
