# Luxury Presence — research
Provider/key: lever:luxurypresence | company_type: product

What they do: Luxury Presence, Inc. builds "the AI growth platform for real estate" — websites and design, SEO/local search, social media, paid advertising, an AI-powered CRM, lead nurturing and client-collaboration tools for real-estate agents, teams and brokerages. Their own JD claims "More than 90,000 real estate professionals, including over 30% of the WSJ Real Trends top 100 agents in the United States"; their website claims "nearly 40% of the RealTrends Verified top 100 agents and teams", 20,000+ real-estate businesses on the platform and $450B in client transaction volume. Named brand associations include SERHANT., Compass, Coldwell Banker Realty and Sotheby's International Realty agents. (Note the 90,000-professionals vs 20,000-businesses figures come from two different pages and are not reconciled.)

How they describe themselves: "We don't just use AI. We build with it, ship with it, and think with it. We're in the top 1% of companies applying AI effectively, not just to our products, but to how we build them. With an unlimited budget for Anthropic tokens, our engineers use AI agents to write, review, and ship production code every day. We're building toward a world where humans design systems and AI builds them, and we're already further along that path than almost anyone else." Wanted attributes, verbatim: "You already build with AI daily. You use Claude Code as a core part of your workflow, not as a novelty"; "You have strong opinions, loosely held"; "You think in systems"; "You communicate clearly and directly"; "You're energized by ambiguity and speed. You thrive in a fast-growing company where the roadmap evolves and ownership is real"; "You like to have fun at work… We celebrate wins, crack jokes, and genuinely enjoy building together." Staff engineers are framed as "force multipliers" who "define how we build, not just what we build".

Size / stage / funding: Series C, backed by Bessemer Venture Partners "and other top investors", and stated to have "hit $100M in annual recurring revenue". Growth stage, venture-funded; profitability not stated.

Locations / HQ: Los Angeles, California (site lists a 310 number). Hires distributed across US, Canada and LATAM — postings are explicitly triplicated per region.

Remote policy: Remote by region. Every engineering req is posted three times — "- CANADA (Remote)", "- US (Remote)", "- LATAM (Remote)" — with matching location fields ("Canada, remote"). No office-attendance requirement appears in the JD text.

Remote-Canada eligibility: YES — verified. They post dedicated Canada-remote reqs (e.g. "Staff Software Engineer - AI Marketing - CANADA (Remote)", "Sr./Staff Data Engineer - CANADA (Remote)", "Staff DevOps Engineer — Developer Infrastructure - CANADA (Remote)"). Nothing in the JDs restricts to a particular Canadian province or timezone, though the company is LA-based (Pacific), which is Mountain-Time friendly.

Engineering & tech: Frontend React, Tailwind, Apollo; backend Node/TypeScript microservices; data Postgres, Redis, Elasticsearch, DynamoDB; infrastructure AWS, Kubernetes, Lambda, Temporal; messaging Kafka and SQS; GraphQL; multi-tenant platform architecture serving thousands of real-estate businesses; an AI-powered site-builder pipeline. Data-engineering side (from the Data Engineer reqs): Python, Spark Streaming, Kafka, Iceberg, Airflow, Pydantic. Their differentiator is AI-native development itself: agents integrated into the development workflow, unlimited Anthropic tokens, staff engineers shaping "engineering standards, review practices, and developer tooling".

Notable / other: There is a genuine Developer-Infrastructure/DevOps ladder ("Staff DevOps Engineer — Developer Infrastructure"), which is closer to utility work than the AI-marketing product roles. Several Canada reqs in the registry have already gone non-live, so postings churn quickly. Perks language is energetic ("unlimited Anthropic tokens", "energized by ambiguity and speed").

Open relevant roles (sample, live): Staff Software Engineer - AI Marketing - CANADA (Remote); Staff Software Engineer - AI Marketing - US (Remote); Senior DevOps Engineer - LATAM (Remote). Recently closed: Sr./Staff Data Engineer and Staff DevOps Engineer — Developer Infrastructure, in all three regions.

Pay (stated on their own JDs, USD base, US postings): Staff Software Engineer - AI Marketing $200,000–$250,000; Staff Data Engineer $200,000–$250,000; Staff DevOps Engineer — Developer Infrastructure $200,000–$250,000; Sr. Data Engineer $150,000–$190,000. The CANADA and LATAM versions of the same reqs post NO range, so the Canadian band is unknown — do not assume parity with the US figure.
