# Achievers — research
Provider/key: lever:achievers | company_type: product
What they do: Achievers builds employee-recognition, rewards and employee-voice (pulse survey) SaaS. Its platform lets organisations run peer-to-peer recognition programmes tied to company values, with a rewards marketplace, and measures engagement through pulse surveys. Customers are large enterprise HR organisations. Founded 2002.
How they describe themselves: "Achievers' employee voice and recognition solutions bring your organization's values and strategy to life by activating employee participation and accelerating a culture of performance… we help the world's best-run businesses build cultures where employees feel seen, heard, and appreciated—every day." Five stated values, verbatim: "It's the People" (celebrating diversity and individual contribution), "One Team" (collaboration and authentic communication), "Be Bold" (creativity and innovation), "Own the Outcome" (acting with urgency and accountability), and "Win with Customers" (prioritising customer success). Their careers page describes an "A-player culture" with a "strong sense of connection" and "exceptional leadership".
Size / stage / funding: 501–1,000 employees. Private, long-established (2002); owned by Blackstone since 2015. Great Place to Work-Certified; has won Best Workplaces in Canada (Top 50), Best Workplaces for Women and Best Workplaces for Inclusion awards.
Locations / HQ: Headquartered in Toronto with a San Francisco co-HQ; further offices in the United Kingdom, Australia, Singapore and the USA.
Remote policy: Hybrid, stated as such: "With our hybrid work environment, we understand the importance of adapting to modern work styles and providing our team with the best of in-office and remote work." The careers page does not publish the number of required office days. Postings bear this out — most engineering reqs are tagged Toronto/hybrid, with a minority tagged Canada/remote.
Remote-Canada eligibility: Mixed, and mostly negative for a BC-based candidate. Of the eight live engineering postings, six are Toronto (hybrid) — including both Senior Software Engineer reqs and the Intermediate reqs — and two are tagged "Canada, remote" (an Intermediate Software Engineer and one of the two Staff Software Engineer reqs; a second Staff req is "Canada, hybrid"). A Toronto hybrid role is not workable from Kimberley, BC. So Canadian employment is certain, but full-remote eligibility applies to only a couple of specific reqs.
Engineering & tech: Platform services at real scale — the Staff Software Engineer req covers "the platform's User Domain and HRIS integrations, where we connect and synchronize employee data from enterprise systems to power critical business workflows. Our services manage millions of user records, support large-scale integrations with leading HR platforms, and provide the foundation for user lifecycle, access, and organizational data across the product ecosystem." Stack described as "modern cloud technologies, distributed systems, APIs, and event-driven architectures while tackling complex scalability and reliability challenges."
Notable / other: States a "total rewards philosophy… built on transparency, equity, and market data. We clearly define pay ranges — so you know how compensation is set and how it can grow" — yet no salary range appears on any of its live Lever postings, so nothing citable was available. Unlimited/flexible vacation, up to 12 weeks paid parental leave, mentorship programmes, promote-from-within stance.
Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer (Canada, remote — User Domain / HRIS integrations); Staff Software Engineer (Canada, hybrid); Senior Software Engineer (Toronto, hybrid); Intermediate Software Engineer (Canada, remote).
