# Bettermode — research
Provider/key: bamboohr:bettermode | company_type: product

What they do: Bettermode is an all-in-one **customer community platform** — companies use it to launch branded community sites with discussions, events, feedback/wishlist boards and knowledge bases, and to tie that engagement to retention and advocacy metrics. The pitch is no-code and fast: "Launch fast with no code and turn engagement into measurable retention and growth." It integrates with Salesforce, HubSpot, Intercom and Segment. Customers named on their own site and careers page include IBM, Lenovo, Mercedes, Webflow, HubSpot, DeepL, Logitech, SafetyCulture, Jane App, Xano and FlutterFlow. They describe their platform as one that "empowers businesses to seamlessly craft powerful web apps with engagement tools in its core."

How they describe themselves: "At Bettermode, we are redefining how businesses streamline customer experiences and foster strong relationships." "Backed by Silicon Valley investors and trusted by brands like Lenovo, Mercedes, and IBM, we're proud to connect millions of end-users daily." On culture: "we're dedicated to empowering our team to thrive — both professionally and personally… Our culture is built on ownership and trust, giving you real influence over how we scale and succeed." They offer "location-based, competitive compensation that reflects your expertise and impact, with annual reviews." Team framing is "a globally diverse and collaborative team." Their engineering framing is builder-first: the Staff Backend role is "for a builder who carries a product from scrappy prototype, through real users in beta, to production-grade — same hands the whole way through," with "high autonomy and high technical standards" as "the default," and they want someone who "thrive[s] in a startup where requirements evolve."

Size / stage / funding: Private venture-backed startup, "backed by Silicon Valley investors." Small — the JD describes "a small team of seasoned developers." SOC 2 Type II compliant, with enterprise offerings. Stage not disclosed publicly on their site; treat as growth-stage startup, runway-dependent.

Locations / HQ: Toronto, Canada. Their office is "downtown Toronto… less than a 15-minute walk from Union Station, with a free shuttle running throughout the day," with snacks, coffee, video games and board games.

Remote policy: Their own JD states it plainly: "Location: Remote within Canada, or Hybrid in our Downtown Toronto office (3 days per week — Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday) if you're based in the Greater Toronto Area. Timezone: Eastern Standard Time." So remote-Canada is the default for anyone outside the GTA; the 3-day hybrid applies only to GTA residents.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes, verified.** "Remote within Canada" is stated on the live JD, benefits are Canadian ("From your very first day, you and your family are covered by comprehensive Canadian health benefits — dental and vision included"), and compensation is quoted in what is evidently CAD. The one caveat is the stated **Eastern time zone** expectation — from Mountain Time that is a two-hour offset, workable but it means the team's core hours start early.

Engineering & tech: TypeScript and **NestJS** backend; event-driven systems on **Kafka**; **PostgreSQL** and **MongoDB**; AI pipelines integrating LLMs into customer-facing workflows. Small team, so backend engineers are expected to "reach into the frontend when the problem demands it." They are aggressively AI-tooling-forward internally: "You work with Claude Code, Cursor, or a custom rig as a daily force multiplier, not just autocomplete."

Compensation & benefits: The JD posts "Compensation: 175K - 200K+" directly. Benefits: Canadian health/dental/vision from day one, unlimited paid vacation, paid parental leave, bereavement leave, equipment provided or an interest-free hardware stipend under a Device Upgrade Policy repayable via payroll deduction, and a monthly Tech & Appreciation Stipend.

Notable / other: They highlight a public "Showcase page" of customer communities. Mentorship is framed as core to the staff role, not optional: "Your code reviews shape how mid-level engineers grow… Mentorship and review are part of the job, not stretch goals."

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Backend Engineer (Remote within Canada / Toronto hybrid).
