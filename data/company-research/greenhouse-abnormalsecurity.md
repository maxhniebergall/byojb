# Abnormal AI (Abnormal Security) — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:abnormalsecurity | company_type: product

What they do: AI-native "human behavior security" platform. Uses behavioral ML to model what "normal" looks like inside an organization and stop socially-engineered inbound email attacks, account takeover and compromised accounts across email and connected SaaS apps. Positioned as a Secure Email Gateway (SEG) displacer selling to enterprises; product surface is expanding from inbound detection into outbound mailflow (SMTP relay, DLP, DMARC, encryption, archiving) and identity security.

How they describe themselves: Their stated mission framing is "using good AI to fight bad AI before it reaches the people who depend on them." Their careers/culture page presents the following as their operating principles: **AI-native operations** (rebuild the work around AI from the ground up, not bolted on); **remote-first flexibility** (home, hub office, or company-provided WeWork depending on role and region); **async-first collaboration** (reduce meeting dependency, designated synchronous overlap); **high autonomy / high accountability** ("no micromanagement, but clear expectations — you always know where you stand"); **impact-driven growth** ("advancement is a reflection of what you build and the impact it creates, not how long you've been there"); **transparent career progression**; and **customer obsession**. Pace is described explicitly and proudly in compressed terms: "what used to take quarters into weeks," and "one person does what used to take a team."

Size / stage / funding: Private, late-stage venture-backed cybersecurity company, several hundred employees; not disclosed on the pages fetched. Registered HQ is Las Vegas, NV (per third-party listings).

Locations / HQ: Las Vegas, NV (HQ) plus hub offices and WeWork access by region. Live engineering postings are geo-labelled Remote-USA, Remote-Canada, Remote-UK and Remote-Singapore.
