# Varicent — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:varicent | company_type: product
What they do: Varicent builds Sales Performance Management (SPM) / revenue performance SaaS — incentive compensation ("Varicent Incentives"), territory and quota planning, and an ELT data application that "simplifies data workflows, enabling organizations to extract insights faster". Named customers in its own JDs: T-Mobile, ServiceNow, Wawanesa Bank, Shaw Industries, Moody's, Stryker "and hundreds more".
How they describe themselves (their own words): "we're not just transforming the Sales Performance Management (SPM) market—we're redefining how organizations achieve revenue success". Four stated pillars: "Innovate with Purpose: Build impactful solutions for customers worldwide", "Join Excellence: Work in a diverse, collaborative, and innovative team", "Shape the Future: Lead in redefining revenue optimization", "Grow Together: Unlock your potential in a supportive environment". Analyst positioning is heavily foregrounded: leader in the 2025 Forrester Wave for SPM, 2023 Ventana RPM Value Index, Gartner Peer Insights, 2024 Gartner SPM Market Guide, and G2. Engineering copy is measured rather than hype-driven — "a strong focus on quality, scalability, and performance", "modern, reliable, and built for the future".
Size / stage / funding: Established private company (originally the IBM/Varicent SPM lineage, spun out and later private-equity backed). Exact headcount, ownership and profitability were not verifiable here — varicent.com/about-us returned HTTP 404 and no other first-party corporate page was fetched. Treat size/ownership as unverified.
Locations / HQ: Toronto, Ontario is the centre of gravity. Live reqs also sit in Vancouver BC and in Mexico (remote), plus Canada-wide remote reqs.
Remote policy: Hybrid/office-anchored for most Canadian reqs, with genuine remote reqs alongside. Registry locations include "Toronto, Ontario, Canada", "Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada", "Canada, remote", "Canada, Remote" and "Mexico, Remote". No stated company-wide remote policy was readable.
Remote-Canada eligibility: YES, partially and verifiably. Two live reqs are posted as Canada-remote — "Staff Software Developer" (Canada, remote) and "Staff Backend Developer" (Canada, Remote) — which is workable from Kimberley BC. However the majority of the Canadian backend reqs are Toronto- or Vancouver-anchored and would be disqualifying; the Generative AI roles are Mexico-remote only. Whether "Canada, remote" tolerates Mountain Time is not stated.
Engineering & tech: Two distinct stacks. Incentives: React/Redux single-page app over a C# REST API on AWS (Lambda, S3, DynamoDB, Redshift), CloudFormation IaC, CI/CD automation. ELT platform: Python, TypeScript/Node.js and C# backends, AWS (EC2, EKS, Lambda, S3, DynamoDB, RDS, Step Functions), distributed batch/stream processing with Spark, Dask, EMR, Glue and Kafka. Work is explicitly data-intensive — "massive datasets", "high-throughput backend architectures", "high throughput and low-latency". Teams are multidisciplinary (developers, designers, data scientists, business) and partner with PM/Design/QA.
Notable / other: Pay transparency is applied to Canadian reqs — the Staff Backend (TS/Node/AWS) and Staff Software Developer reqs both state C$121,200 - C$159,000 base plus a variable compensation program. The postings disclose that "This hiring process utilizes artificial intelligence tools to assist in candidate" evaluation. English proficiency at B2+ is a stated requirement, reflecting the Mexico-based teams.
Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Developer (Canada, remote); Staff Backend Developer (Canada, Remote / Toronto); Staff Software Engineer - Backend, Typescript/Node.js/AWS (Vancouver, Toronto); Lead Backend Developer (Toronto).
