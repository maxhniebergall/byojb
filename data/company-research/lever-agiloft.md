# Agiloft — research
Provider/key: lever:agiloft | company_type: product

What they do: Agiloft sells **data-first Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM)** software. In its own words: "the most trusted global leader in data-first contract lifecycle management (CLM) software, Agiloft helps organizations manage the end-to-end process of proposing, negotiating, signing, and leveraging contracts using our flexible Data-first Agreement Platform (DAP). With contract data as the foundation, customers quickly and collaboratively reach agreement and leverage contract visibility to thrive with competitive advantage. Employing powerful, pragmatic artificial intelligence as a legal force multiplier, and robust integration capabilities as a data liberator, organizations around the world trust Agiloft's certified implementers to deliver connected, intelligent, and autonomous solutions across the entire contract lifecycle." The platform is **no-code**, "easily managed and administered by business users". Over 3 million users worldwide. In December 2025 it launched an AI-driven Obligation Management suite.

How they describe themselves: The centrepiece is their working philosophy **"EX = CX": "when employee experience is excellent, so is customer experience."** Also stated: "We believe that the way to build the strongest, most vibrant place to work is to bring in individuals from all walks of life, and to support them in bringing their authentic selves to their day, every day." They support multiple Employee Resource Groups and "offer a working environment that supports **healthy work/life balance**, including **floating holidays and a quarterly, no-questions-asked wellness day**." On the business they are confident but measured: "Top analysts like Gartner, Forrester, and IDC agree, all showing Agiloft as a leader in the CLM space… Ours is a growing, vibrant, successful company that is at the forefront of a market that is becoming a must-have for all organizations." Notably absent from their own copy: any "fast-paced", "hustle", "move fast" or "wear many hats" language.

Size / stage / funding: Private, 201–500 employees. Total funding **$45M in a single private-equity round from FTV Capital in August 2020** — unusual for a company of this age and scale, and a strong sign of capital efficiency rather than burn dependence. Customer metrics they publish: "nearly a full 100% of new customers are satisfied with their initial implementations, and some 97% of customers renew every year." A 97% gross renewal rate is a genuine stability indicator.

Locations / HQ: US-headquartered (Redwood City, CA) and global. Certified in 2025 for the *100 Best Midsize Places to Work in San Francisco* list.

Remote policy: Remote-friendly to remote-first in practice — named in 2025 to the **100 Best Remote Midsize Places to Work** list and Great Place To Work certified for 2025, with a culture that "prioritizes employee experience and work-life balance". Live reqs are posted per-geo as fully remote ("United States, remote" / "Canada, remote").

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** They run the Senior Platform Engineer req twice — one located **"Canada, remote"** and one "United States, remote" — so a Canada-based remote hire is explicitly supported for exactly the role of interest. No timezone overlap requirement is stated; the company is California-anchored, so Pacific-leaning hours, which is easy from Mountain Time.

Engineering & tech: The Senior Platform Engineer role spans building and maintaining **cloud-native enterprise software** within the product suite; developing and improving **infrastructure-as-code, CI/CD pipelines and monitoring tools**; improving **internal developer tools, workflows and engineering best practices**; collaborating with AI engineers to **integrate and optimise commercial LLM APIs**; mentoring junior engineers; and participating in **incident response and disaster recovery**. So it is a genuine hybrid: about half platform/developer-experience infrastructure, about half product-adjacent application engineering with product managers and designers. The product itself is a no-code configurable platform with heavy integration surface.

Notable / other: The CLM market is a mature, compliance-driven enterprise category — "boring, mission-critical" in the useful sense, and Agiloft is an analyst-recognised leader in it rather than a challenger. The AI angle here is applied LLM API integration inside a legal-document product, not foundation-model work. Sole PE backing means an eventual sale or recapitalisation is a plausible future event.

Compensation: **No figure found.** Neither of the live Senior Platform Engineer reqs states a salary range (Lever postings with no pay-transparency block), and no company pay-transparency or compensation-philosophy page was located. No citable band exists; only a parametric estimate is possible.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Platform Engineer (Canada, remote); Senior Platform Engineer (United States, remote).
