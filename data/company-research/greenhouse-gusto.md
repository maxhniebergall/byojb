# Gusto, Inc. — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:gusto | company_type: product
What they do: "At Gusto, we're on a mission to grow the small business economy. We handle the hard stuff — payroll, health insurance, 401(k)s, and HR — so owners can focus on their craft and their customers. With teams in Denver, San Francisco, and New York, we support more than 500,000 small businesses nationwide." Payroll/benefits/HR SaaS for US small businesses.
How they describe themselves: Mission-first framing around the small-business economy, plus "building a workplace that reflects the people we serve". Compensation framing: "All full-time employees receive competitive base pay, benefits, and equity (RSUs) — because everyone who helps build Gusto should share in its success. Offer amounts are determined by role, level, and location." A company-wide AI mandate is stated on every JD: "AI is a fundamental part of how work gets done at Gusto. We expect all team members to actively engage with AI tools relevant to their role and grow their fluency as the technology evolves." The AI Developer Tools team describes engineers as its customers: "We treat our fellow engineers as our primary customers, prioritizing developer experiences that are not just powerful, but absolutely delightful to use."
Size / stage / funding: Large late-stage private company (500,000+ business customers, teams in three US cities); exact headcount, valuation and profitability not stated in reachable sources. Their Total Rewards page (https://gusto.com/about/careers/total-rewards) returned HTTP 403 to my fetch, so the compensation philosophy could not be read first-hand — UNVERIFIED beyond the JD boilerplate.
Locations / HQ: Denver, San Francisco and New York. Postings list Denver CO, New York NY, Atlanta GA, Seattle WA, San Francisco CA, "United States - Remote", and — notably — "Ontario, CAN - Remote" and "Toronto, Ontario, CAN - Remote".
Remote policy: Hybrid-plus-remote; individual postings enumerate specific office cities alongside a "- Remote" option, so remote eligibility is decided per role rather than company-wide.
Remote-Canada eligibility: PARTIAL — YES for Ontario specifically, and only on some roles. The "Staff Software Engineer, Business Money" posting lists "Ontario, CAN - Remote;Toronto, Ontario, CAN - Remote", proving a Canadian employing entity exists. But the listing is province-scoped to Ontario, and the more relevant "Staff Software Engineer, AI Developer Tools" role lists only US locations plus "United States - Remote". For a candidate in British Columbia this is not currently eligible on the roles on record; whether Gusto can employ outside Ontario is unconfirmed.
Engineering & tech: The AI Developer Tools team sits within Developer Productivity and builds internal platform infrastructure: "Design, scale, and maintain the internal platform and APIs that integrate LLMs into the developer workflow, with a heavy focus on autonomous agents, custom retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and smart context-delivery systems"; "robust evaluation frameworks (evals), guardrails for code safety, and strategies for optimizing model latency and token spend"; "self-heal CI/CD pipelines"; "automating sprawling codebase refactors". They explicitly distinguish platform from wrapper work: "You don't just build wrappers; you think deeply about data privacy, security, cost management, and systemic reliability." Requires "8+ Years of Systems Expertise" navigating "massive, complex codebases".
Notable / other: Comp on record in this registry: a posted base range of $163,000-$204,000 USD for Staff Software Engineer, Business Money (already captured in data/company-comp.jsonl under ml_eng/staff). Payroll/HR is a stable, regulated, non-cyclical domain with sticky customers.
