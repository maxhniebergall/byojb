# Chromatic — research
Provider/key: ashby:chromatic | company_type: product

What they do: Chromatic builds cloud UI testing and review tooling that complements **Storybook**, the open-source frontend component workshop (Chromatic's team are the maintainers/creators behind Storybook). The product runs visual regression testing, interaction testing, accessibility testing and UI review on top of an elastic browser-rendering grid on AWS taking millions of snapshots daily. Customers are frontend teams at BBC, Shopify, Adobe, GitHub. Business is both self-serve and enterprise.

How they describe themselves: Mission stated on their own JD as "to improve the UX of the internet and safeguard the world's pixels… building tools that make it impossible to ship broken pixels." Their careers page lists the values as: be **respectful**; take **pride in your work**; **invest in each other**; **inspire growth**; **outcomes over ego**; **don't overthink it**; and **have fun**. Tone on the JD is calm and technical — "motivated by tackling complex technical challenges alongside smart, capable, and empathetic people" — with no hustle/rocketship language.

Size / stage / funding: Private, roughly 60+ people per the "Who we are" section of the careers page. Describes itself as **profitable and scaling**. Small, developer-tools business with a large open-source footprint (Storybook) rather than a burn-rate startup.

Locations / HQ: Fully distributed; no meaningful office story. Annual company retreats.

Remote policy: "Work from anywhere" listed as a headline benefit; fully remote, distributed and asynchronous. The engineering JD explicitly says "This is a fully remote position on a cross-functional team." Nice-to-have on the DevOps req: "Have worked effectively in a distributed, remote-first team environment."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes — explicit.** The live Senior DevOps Engineer JD states verbatim: "This is a fully remote position on a cross-functional team for individuals based in the **mainland US or Canada**." The registry location field is likewise "Remote | US or Canada."

Engineering & tech: AWS + Terraform (IaC), Docker and container orchestration, CircleCI and GitHub Actions for CI/CD, Tailscale for zero-trust network access, MongoDB and/or PostgreSQL. The rendering grid is the core infrastructure problem — resilience, consistency and performance across millions of daily browser sessions. Engineering team runs its own on-call rotation. Team pedigree includes contributors to Meteor, Apollo GraphQL and Storybook. Current infra priorities are security posture (access controls, secrets management, vulnerability triage, audit logging, SOC2/GDPR), and hardening against an expanding attack surface from agentic workflows.

Notable / other: Benefits include annual retreats, health insurance including dependents, parental/family policies, monthly wellness and internet stipends, home-office and learning budgets, unlimited vacation, 401(k). The DevOps JD explicitly welcomes non-traditional paths into software.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior DevOps Engineer (Remote, US or Canada).

Pay: The Senior DevOps Engineer JD states its own range: **US $155,000–190,000 + equity**, and links to a published compensation philosophy page.
