# Dispel — research
Provider/key: workable:dispel | company_type: product

What they do: Dispel builds **secure remote access and network infrastructure for operational technology (OT) / industrial control systems**. Their own framing in the JD: *"Dispel builds secure, private network infrastructure for critical industries. The people who depend on our products need reliable, auditable, and secure access to sensitive systems."* The website positions the product as enabling vendors, OEMs and operators to reach critical infrastructure *"without legacy complexity."* Product lines named on dispel.com: the **Dispel Zero Trust Engine** (core platform), **OT Secure Remote Access**, **Industrial Data Streaming**, **OTFusion**, and the **Wicket Industrial Gateway**.

How they describe themselves: Engineering-serious and delivery-disciplined. The Senior Backend JD emphasises scoping into *"well-defined milestones,"* estimating and hitting timelines, code *"clear enough that other engineers can reason about it and extend it,"* and being *"a go-to expert in at least one area of the codebase."* Collaboration framing: *"Engineering at Dispel is a collaborative effort and those that show up trying to get things done and help others will receive support from the team."* But they also state the pace expectation unusually bluntly: *"Dispel has high aspirations and we are growing quickly. We have noticed that people that have been in such environments before tend to adapt better. If you have not been in an environment like this before you would do well to try and convince us that you can handle it during your interviews."*

Size / stage / funding: Private and, by their own account, **growing quickly**. Headcount, funding stage and profitability were not recoverable — the homepage carries no about/company facts, and no other first-party page was fetched. Treat size and stage as **unverified**. Third-party-style claims displayed on their own site: 4.8-star ratings on Gartner and G2, Control Engineering 2025 Bronze Product of the Year, and recognition as a fastest-growing cybersecurity company (2025).

Locations / HQ: **Not stated on any page reached — unverified.** (Dispel has historically been associated with New York City and Austin, but this could not be confirmed first-party in this pass.) The company's own domain is dispel.com; dispel.io 301-redirects there.

Remote policy: Remote roles are posted and the ATS marks the Senior Backend role **"Workplace: remote."** No company-wide policy statement was found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified NO for the live relevant roles.** The Senior Backend Engineer is scoped *"United States (Remote)"*, and the other openings are titled explicitly *"Senior Staff Software Engineer - Remote (US Based)"* and *"Staff Engineer: DevOps - Remote (US Based)"* — the US-based restriction is written into the job titles themselves. Given the OT/critical-infrastructure customer base (energy, water utilities, defence-adjacent manufacturing), US-person requirements are plausibly a customer/compliance constraint rather than an administrative one, which makes it unlikely to be negotiable.

Engineering & tech: The JD describes work spanning *"software, infrastructure, networking, and security"* layers — a deliberately full-stack-of-the-machine scope rather than application development. Named responsibilities: architecture through implementation and testing; owning *"reliability, monitoring, logging, and instrumentation of the systems you build"*; ensuring performance, scalability and security *"particularly as they relate to network infrastructure and cloud environments"*; incident response and root-cause analysis; improving automation and operational maturity. Bar is **6+ years** professional experience with a track record of delivering complex systems, mapped to an internal **IC6** level (a second posting is titled "Senior Backend Engineer (IC6)"). Expectations include informal mentoring of IC1/IC2 engineers, model documentation others extend, and participating in interview loops.

Notable / other: Pay is posted on the ATS: **USD 150,000–169,000** for Senior Backend Engineer (posted 2026-05-28). The published IC1/IC2/IC6 ladder terminology suggests a formalised levelling framework. The domain — auditable, secure access to sensitive industrial systems — is about as mission-critical and "boring-important" as software gets.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Backend Engineer (United States, Remote); Senior Backend Engineer (IC6); Senior Staff Software Engineer - Remote (US Based); Staff Engineer: DevOps - Remote (US Based).
