Its technology organisation is substantial and builds in-house: search and browse (NLP, vector search, LLMs), recommendation systems, an AI/ML platform, supply-chain and fulfilment systems, and Target.com web infrastructure. The live postings in the registry are concentrated in applied AI/ML and AI-platform engineering, plus SRE for Target.com.

Locations / HQ: Minneapolis, Minnesota (postings reference the Brooklyn Park, MN campus at 7000 Target Pkwy N). A second technology site in Sunnyvale, CA (100 Mathilda Place).
