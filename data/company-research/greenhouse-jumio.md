# Jumio — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:jumio | company_type: product

What they do: AI-powered identity verification and fraud intelligence, sold B2B. The platform runs
identity checks using document capture, biometrics, liveness detection and machine learning across a
customer's whole lifecycle. Product line includes Identity Verification, Risk Signals,
Cross-Transaction Risk, AML Screening, selfie.DONE (reusable identity verification), Doc Proof,
Premium Liveness Detection, Video Verification and Authentication.

How they describe themselves: The website is product/proof-point led rather than culture led — no
values or careers-culture statement was reachable on the pages fetched. The JDs carry a "Jumio
Values" block, and the technical JDs put unusual weight on **fairness and ethics**: "Awareness of
algorithmic bias and willingness to learn domain-specific fairness practices" and, at the more
senior level, "You understand the sources of algorithmic bias in Computer Vision and have practical
experience measuring and mitigating disparate impact." "Rigorous fairness analysis and benchmarking
of biometric models across various datasets and operating conditions." Tone is technical and
measured; no hustle vocabulary in any of the seven live JDs.

Size / stage / funding: Not stated on the pages fetched — **unverified**. Scale proof points they do
publish: "120 transactions per second", "over 1 billion total transactions", a network of "30+
million known identities", support for "5,000+ global ID types", "300+ patents", and an Identity
Graph drawing on hundreds of data sources. Named customers include Alaska Airlines, United
Airlines, Webull, Stanleybet and Personal Pay, spanning financial services, gaming, crypto,
healthcare and travel. Background knowledge (unverified here): founded 2010, US-headquartered,
private, roughly 700-1,000 employees, with an engineering footprint in Vienna, Montreal and
Bangalore — the posting geographies corroborate that footprint.

Locations / HQ: HQ not stated on the site. Live postings are anchored to **Montreal** (3 ML roles),
**India (remote)** (3 roles) and **Austria (remote)** (1 ML role).

Remote policy: Selective and geography-specific. The board explicitly labels the Indian and Austrian
roles "(remote)"; the three Montreal roles carry **no remote qualifier at all**. No company-wide
remote or async policy statement was found.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — not on the current evidence.** All three Canadian roles are
plainly labelled "Montreal" while the same board labels roles in two other countries "(remote)".
The company demonstrably tags remote roles when they are remote and did not do so for Montreal.
That reads as office-based or hybrid in Montreal, which is a disqualifier from Kimberley BC — and
Montreal is Eastern time besides. Treat Canadian openings here as Montreal-resident roles unless
Jumio says otherwise.

Engineering & tech: Serious production computer vision. Models built in **PyTorch, TensorFlow
and/or JAX**; Python with Pillow/OpenCV; end-to-end ML pipelines from data ingestion to deployment
with **Airflow** orchestration; multi-GPU training clusters; and real inference-side engineering —
"Optimize models for low-latency inference (quantization, distillation, TensorRT/ONNX) and manage
deployment on AWS (SageMaker, EC2, EKS)". Nice-to-haves reveal the rest of the stack: vector
databases and ANN search (Milvus, Faiss), GANs/diffusion for synthetic face generation, and edge
deployment via CoreML/LiteRT/TFLite. Publications at CVPR/ICCV/ECCV/FG are a listed plus. The
Montreal roles sit on the Biometrics / Computer Vision team; job level is decided post-interview
("The final job level for this role will be determined following the interview process").

Compensation: No range on any of the seven live postings, and none on record. The Montreal roles
are not in a pay-transparency jurisdiction. See `data/company-comp/` for a parametric estimate,
marked as such.

Notable / other: The domain is regulated, mission-critical anti-fraud infrastructure — "boring" in
the good sense and not cycle-chasing. The ML work is model-building and CV research-adjacent rather
than LLM/agent platform work.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Machine Learning Engineer (Montreal); Senior Machine Learning
Engineer - (Biometrics) (Montreal); Machine Learning Engineer - (Computer Vision) (Montreal);
Software Development Engineer III - MLOps (India, remote); DevOps Engineer IV (Obs) (India,
remote); Machine Learning Engineer (Austria, remote).
