# Roblox — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:roblox | company_type: product
What they do: Roblox operates a user-generated 3D immersive platform: "Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators." The company builds the engine, tools and platform its creator community builds on — "we're building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life."
How they describe themselves: Vision — "to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device"; mission — "to connect a billion people with optimism and civility". "A career at Roblox means you'll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone." Role-level values in the JDs: "Foundation oriented mindset: You believe that strong 3D math skills and a well thought out code architecture are important"; "Performance focus"; "Personally Motivated: Self-sufficient, self-organized and able to take ownership of a project throughout its full lifecycle"; "A Team player mentality".
Size / stage / funding: Public company (NYSE: RBLX), several thousand employees, established platform at very large scale.
Locations / HQ: Corporate HQ San Mateo, CA. The registry's relevant postings are at Roblox's **Vancouver, BC, Canada** office, which the JDs describe as "our headquarters in Vancouver, BC, Canada" for pay-range purposes.
Remote policy: Office-based with a fixed core-day requirement, stated verbatim: "Roles that are based in an office are onsite Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday, with optional presence on Monday and Friday (unless otherwise noted)."
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No for these roles.** All four live engineering postings are Vancouver-office roles carrying the three-day-onsite requirement. (One non-engineering posting in the registry, a short-term Talent Business Partner, was listed as "Remote; San Mateo, CA" — evidence Roblox does post some remote roles, but not these, and that one is US-located and now closed.)
Engineering & tech: C++ (primary, "ability to write high quality, performant code"), Lua as a plus, the Roblox game engine, character animation systems and tooling, geometry, physics, UGC validation; strong 3D math; mobile-class performance constraints. Teams named in the registry: Avatar Animation, Geometry, UGC Validation, Physics.
Compensation (stated on their own Vancouver JDs): Senior Software Engineer, Avatar Animation — $230,020–$279,120 (labelled USD on that posting); Senior Software Engineer, Geometry — $230,020–$279,120 CAD; Principal Software Engineer, Physics — $279,120–$332,440 CAD. All base only, and "All full-time employees are also eligible for equity compensation". **Data-quality note: two Vancouver postings carry the identical numeric range but label it USD on one and CAD on the other — the currency on the senior band should be treated as uncertain.** Total-rewards page (careers.roblox.com/total-rewards) is linked from the JDs but returned a timeout.
Notable / other: Senior roles here ask 8+ years with character-animation/engine specialization — deep, narrow specialist ladders.
Open relevant roles (sample): Principal Software Engineer, Physics; Senior Software Engineer, Avatar Animation; Senior Software Engineer, Geometry; Senior Software Engineer, UGC Validation — all Vancouver, BC.
