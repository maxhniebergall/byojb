# Magical — research
Provider/key: ashby:magical | company_type: product
What they do: Magical is "an agentic automation platform transforming healthcare with state-of-the-art AI—delivering AI agents that work in real production environments." It sells "AI employees" that automate repetitive administrative healthcare workflows — specifically named: **prior authorizations and medical coding** — while keeping humans in the loop. Target market is framed as "healthcare—a $4 trillion industry overwhelmed by administrative complexity". Customers are healthcare provider organizations; the stated model is a 7-day proof-of-concept leading to expansion into broader workflows.
How they describe themselves: From their own JD: "We believe the future of healthcare will be shaped not by replacing people, but by amplifying their talents. Magical empowers healthcare organizations to scale their top experts across every workflow, driving greater accuracy, efficiency, and care throughout operations." Traction claims in their words: "Customers are expanding from initial workflows into broader use cases after seeing reliable production results"; "7-day proof-of-concept projects demonstrate real value quickly, where months are typical in the industry"; "Our self-healing automations deliver production-grade reliability at scale". Closing line: "We're building the best self-serve agentic automation platform for healthcare, and we're just getting started." Hiring language asks for someone who "favor[s] action—shipping, learning, prioritizing, and iterating quickly", "take[s] ownership of your work and enjoy[s] helping teammates grow", and has "integrated AI tools into your developer workflow and enjoy[s] experimenting with new approaches". No separate published values list was reachable.

Engineering & tech: The platform description is more substantive than most agentic-AI marketing: "Under the hood, Magical orchestrates long-running, high-stakes healthcare workflows involving **queues, run state, browser automation, model calls, evaluations, observability, and human-in-the-loop recovery**." The role covers backend and full-stack systems for AI agents; workflow creation, pathways, testing, editing and production run reporting; and "reliability with better run state management, queues, error classification, alerts, and dashboards". Stack is **TypeScript/Node** or similar. Wanted-extras name event-driven architectures, workflow engines, observability and runtime reliability, plus LLM integration into production backends and healthcare revenue-cycle-management background. The team also converts "messy customer inputs—SOPs, decision trees, portal behaviors, edge cases—into reliable automations", working with product, design and deployment engineering.
