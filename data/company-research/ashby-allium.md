# Allium — research
Provider/key: ashby:allium | company_type: product
What they do: Allium is building "the System of Record for Onchain Finance" — an enterprise blockchain-data platform that ingests, verifies and standardizes data across 140-150+ blockchains and "30+ petabytes of history," turning raw chain events into financial concepts institutions can rely on. They frame it as closing four gaps: the Semantic Gap ("translating raw events into financial concepts like payments, trades, deposits, and staking income"), the Standardization Gap ("mapping thousands of protocols into a single canonical cross chain schema"), the Infrastructure Gap ("read optimized, globally distributed data at web scale") and the Accountability Gap ("auditable methodology, SLAs, and SOC 1 and 2 compliance").
How they describe themselves: Analytical and thesis-driven rather than hype-driven — the JD opens with a structural argument ("Blockchains are optimized for writes, not reads... Blockchains are computers, not databases") before describing the product. Positioning: "Finance is moving onchain. Allium is the system of record it runs on," with the explicit analogy to Bloomberg or DTCC. On the work: "We love engineers who love solving new problems every single day." They also frame data quality as an identity: "We pride ourselves on our data quality — how can we ensure our data is consistent across every copy and every region 24/7?"
Size / stage / funding: Recently closed a $40M Series B led by Amplify Partners, with Kleiner Perkins and Theory Ventures participating. Headcount not stated. Customers/users named across their site and JD: Visa, Stripe, G-SIB banks, Big 4 accounting firms, BCG, Coinbase, Phantom, Uniswap, a16z crypto, Grayscale, MetaMask, MoonPay — and their data is cited by the Federal Reserve.
Locations / HQ: HQ not stated on the site. People are based in New York, Seattle, Singapore and Australia; the posting lists "Remote, San Francisco, Singapore, New York."
Remote policy: Globally remote with a narrow overlap requirement, stated verbatim: "Note: The sun never sets on Allium - we hire from any geographical location as long as you are willing to overlap 2 hours overlap on NYC mornings Mon-Thurs from 10am-12pm ET. We have people based in New York, Seattle, Singapore and Australia."
Remote-Canada eligibility: YES, on the company's own words — "we hire from any geographical location." The overlap window (10am-12pm ET, Mon-Thu) is 8-10am Mountain Time, which is comfortably workable from Kimberley, BC. Caveat: employment mechanics for Canada (direct employment vs. contractor/EOR) are not stated.
Engineering & tech: Serious data-infrastructure problems, described concretely in the JD — data egress economics ("How does one transport 100s of TBs of data around the world without breaking the piggybank?", citing Delta Sharing / Cloudflare R2), high-traffic serving ("support... 100,000 QPS at peak traffic and not go down"), behavioral botnet detection, Sybil/fraud detection heuristics, customer-defined transformations over real-time data streams ("Bring Your Own Transformation"), and cross-region data-consistency governance. Public technical writing at allium.so/post (JUP airdrop at Phantom, botnet report, Wormhole Sybil-detection deep dive, Visa x Allium stablecoins) and developer docs at docs.allium.so.
Notable / other: SOC 1 and SOC 2 compliant. The role title is simply "Software Engineer" with no level attached, so leveling and comp are unknown from the posting.
