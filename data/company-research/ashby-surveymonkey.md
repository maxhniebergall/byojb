# SurveyMonkey — research
Provider/key: ashby:surveymonkey | company_type: product
What they do: In their own words, "SurveyMonkey is the world's most popular platform for surveys and forms, built for business—loved by users. We combine powerful capabilities with intuitive design, effectively serving every use case, from customer experience to employee engagement, market research to payment and registration forms." They position an AI angle: "With built-in research expertise and AI-powered technology, it's like having a team of expert researchers at your fingertips." Customer base spans "millions—from startups to Fortune 500 companies". Adjacent brands include GetFeedback and Wufoo.
How they describe themselves: Curiosity is the organising value — "Come join us and see where your curiosity can take you", and their values "are core to how we hire, collaborate, and grow. They help shape everything from our culture to our business decisions." They describe offices and perks in first-party terms: "Our teams work in a flexible, hybrid environment with thoughtfully designed offices and programs like the CHOICE Fund to help employees thrive in work and life." Standard EEO language: "we celebrate the unique differences of our employees because that is what drives curiosity, innovation, and the success of our business."
Size / stage / funding: Mature SaaS, formerly NASDAQ-listed (SVMK/MNTV) and taken private in 2023 by Silver Lake and STG. Now PE-owned rather than venture-stage. Roughly 1,000–1,500 employees.
Locations / HQ: HQ San Mateo, California. Real Canadian engineering presence in **Ottawa, Ontario**. Also hires in Europe (live Italy - Remote postings) and has an office footprint in Portland and Dublin.
Remote policy: Mixed. Their own careers copy says "flexible, hybrid environment with thoughtfully designed offices", but individual JDs are posted with genuinely remote locations ("Canada - Remote", "Italy - Remote"). The remote JDs carry the caveat: "While this role is remote, it may require in-person participation. You will be encouraged to attend company events throughout the year. These events will take place at a designated SurveyMonkey office."
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified YES for at least some roles.** A live Senior Software Engineer II is posted with location "Canada - Remote" and a CAD band, which is direct evidence the Canadian entity employs remote workers nationwide, not only in Ottawa. Caveat: several sibling postings for the same title are Ottawa-located, and remote roles carry an expectation of periodic in-person attendance at a designated office. Timezone is the softer risk — Ottawa is Eastern, so a Mountain-Time worker sits 2 hours behind the Canadian engineering hub.
Engineering & tech: There is a dedicated **Machine Learning Platform team (MLP)**, described in the Senior Software Engineer II JD as building "the secure, highly scalable, and high-performance pipelines that govern the end-to-end lifecycle of ML models", working "at the intersection of Data Science and DevOps, building the 'connective tissue' that empowers our product portfolio to leverage" ML. That is a first-class ML-infrastructure/MLOps function rather than a prompt-wrapper team. A Staff Machine Learning Engineer role sits alongside it. The company also notes it "may use artificial intelligence (AI) tools to support parts of the hiring process, such as sourcing candidates, reviewing applications, analyzing resumes, or summarizing interviews" — with human review retained.
Notable / other: Pay is disclosed through the ATS in CAD for Canadian roles, so the Canadian bands are first-party. Accommodations are offered for applicants with disabilities; a Global Applicant and Candidate Data Privacy Notice is published.
