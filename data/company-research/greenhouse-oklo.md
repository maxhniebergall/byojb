# Oklo — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:oklo | company_type: product

What they do: Oklo designs and deploys advanced fission power plants ("the next generation of nuclear
reactors"). The engineering work spans modelling, simulation, design and deployment of advanced
fission power technology, with stated goals of new levels of safety, efficiency and resiliency, and an
interest in "closing the nuclear fuel cycle". The software roles exist to support reactor design and
analysis — this is an energy/hardware company with an internal software function, not a software
vendor.

How they describe themselves: "Come be a part of powering the future with advanced fission power
plants to provide clean, reliable, affordable energy." They describe themselves plainly as "a
fast-moving startup where you will be responsible for tackling a wide range of challenges in reactor
design and analysis." Their "Who you are" section is an unusually explicit values statement:
- "A startup person: You aren't driven by titles or hierarchy, and prefer efficiency to excess
  process. You don't need or expect to have a lot of guidance but you enjoy working in a fast-paced
  team. If you prefer the culture and feel of a large organization, that is great, but you likely
  won't enjoy working with us!"
- "Motivated: You are self-motivated... a sense of passion that goes beyond clocking in and clocking
  out. This isn't about a fake or arbitrary 'pieces of flair' mentality or lack of work-life balance!"
- "A team-player: Oklo genuinely is a team. We aren't about taking credit for ourselves, and we aren't
  about pushing blame to others."
- "An excellent communicator: ...not only technically competent but also a clear and upbeat
  communicator."
- "Creative: ...when things fall outside clear scopes or processes or problems arise without clear
  solutions, you are able to... invent ways to solve a problem or fill a need without micromanagement...
  solving open-ended problems which may change day-by-day."
- "Detail-oriented: This focus is a big part of excellence, consistency, and quality."

Size / stage / funding: Publicly traded (Oklo Inc., US-listed) and self-described as a startup;
headcount not stated in available material. Pre-revenue reactor deployment stage as far as the JDs
indicate. UNVERIFIED beyond that — no company site was fetched.

Locations / HQ: Headquarters in Santa Clara, CA. Both software roles are listed "Santa Clara, CA or
Remote".

Remote policy: Remote is allowed but with hard in-person obligations, stated verbatim: "Oklo requires
remote employees to travel to headquarters (Santa Clara, CA) twice a quarter annually, based on
business or team needs, including attendance at team meetings, off-sites, and other company events or
gatherings. For the first two weeks of onboarding, employees are required to be in person at
headquarters in Santa Clara, CA."

Remote-Canada eligibility: NOT STATED, and effectively doubtful. There is no explicit US-only clause
in the JD text, but this is a US nuclear company (an industry with heavy US export-control and
security constraints on personnel), with mandatory quarterly-plus travel to Santa Clara and a two-week
onsite onboarding. Nothing indicates a Canadian employing entity. Treat as US-only unless they
confirm otherwise.

Engineering & tech: Software supports reactor physics and design. Responsibilities on the Senior role:
lead design of systems for storing and retrieving reactor data and physics results at scale; architect
and own CI/CD pipelines for multiphysics analyses with reliability, observability and reproducibility;
build tools/platforms letting reactor engineers visualise, compare and evaluate reactor designs;
develop physics kernels; build reactor optimisation schemes and statistical experiments; mentor
engineers and set technical direction. Stack and skills: Python (fluency required), Git, CI/CD (GitLab
CI/CD a bonus), with Rust and TypeScript/JavaScript (especially Svelte) as bonus proficiencies. Bonus
experience: large data collection/analysis pipelines, optimisation/statistics/DoE, software QA
programs in safety-critical industries, and working in regulated environments (energy, aerospace,
medical, automotive). Nuclear background explicitly not required. Senior bar is 8+ years.

Notable / other: Compensation is stated openly on the JDs. Benefits: flexible time off, equity,
competitive pay, 401k, health insurance, FSA, flexible work arrangements. The domain is genuinely
"boring and mission-critical" in the good sense (regulated, safety-focused, long-horizon) while the
culture copy is explicitly startup-flavoured — an unusual combination.
