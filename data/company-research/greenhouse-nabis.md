# Nabis — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:nabis | company_type: product

What they do: Nabis is a licensed **cannabis wholesale distribution platform** — it moves product from brands to licensed retailers, with software for ordering, logistics, compliance and payments layered on top. From their own JD: "Nabis is the #1 licensed cannabis wholesale platform in the world, supplying **$1B+ worth of cannabis products annually** from hundreds of brands to retailers across **California, New York, and Nevada**." So: a physical distribution business with a technology platform, operating in a heavily regulated, state-by-state legal market.

How they describe themselves: "Our mission is to empower the world to discover cannabis by providing choice, access, and innovation… Through modern, scalable infrastructure." And: "We're at the forefront of this movement, building an innovative, technology-first platform to scale the entire cannabis industry… rapidly expanding across the U.S. with the goal of becoming the largest and most influential cannabis distribution network globally." The DevOps JD's own framing of the work is refreshingly concrete and self-aware: "We've built the core (the 0-to-1) — now we need someone who has done the **1-to-2** before: taking **proven-but-scrappy** systems and making them resilient, secure, and repeatable as the organization grows… This is a hands-on senior role with real ownership: you'll set standards, not just follow them."

Size / stage / funding: Private startup, **Y Combinator**-backed. Named angel/investor list from their own JD: DoorDash co-founder Stanley Tang, NFL Hall of Famer Joe Montana, Gmail creator Paul Buchheit, Twitch co-founder Justin Kan. Headcount not disclosed; 34 total live postings in the registry. GMV of $1B+ annually is a distribution gross figure, not company revenue, and says little about profitability. Funding stage and runway are **unverified**.

Locations / HQ: Not stated in the captured JDs; operations span California, New York and Nevada. No first-party Nabis domain was reachable from the registry (the registry carries only the Greenhouse board URL), so HQ is unverified.

Remote policy: Remote-first for engineering — the SWE posting says "This is an **entirely remote** role." But remote is bounded by a hard state whitelist, quoted verbatim from both JDs: "This role is open to candidates based in the following states: **CA, CO, FL, GA, IA, ID, IL, KS, MA, MD, ME, MI, MO, NJ, NV, NY, TN, TX, UT, VA, or WA. Applicants residing outside of these states will not be considered at this time.**" No async or handbook signals stated.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — verified negative, explicitly.** The eligibility clause is an enumerated list of US states with a stated refusal to consider anyone outside it. Canada is categorically excluded. Note also that cannabis remains federally illegal in the US, which is a structural reason this employer will not hire cross-border.

Engineering & tech: Genuinely substantial platform work on the DevOps role — "a platform engineering team operating the infrastructure behind a data and application platform on AWS." Scope stated verbatim includes: CI/CD ownership and an **in-flight GitLab CI → GitHub Actions migration**; operating and hardening **EKS** clusters (upgrades, networking, autoscaling, security); an **RDS/Aurora Postgres** fleet (performance tuning, replication, connection management, backup/restore); **Kafka self-hosted on Kubernetes via Strimzi** plus **Debezium CDC** pipelines; a data lake of **Apache Iceberg tables in S3** fed by that CDC, with **Snowflake** as query/compute and **dbt** for transformations, including cost controls (warehouse sizing, auto-suspend, credit monitoring); **Terraform** IaC; security engineering (secrets management, least-privilege IAM, network segmentation, dependency/image scanning, OWASP-aligned CI/CD); **SOC 2** posture — access and change management, audit logging, evidence automation; on-call and incident-response formalisation; observability (metrics, logging, tracing, alerting); AWS cost efficiency. Requires 5+ years hands-on AWS production operations, with the JD explicitly "weighing depth of operational experience over breadth of tools on a résumé."
The Senior Software Engineer role is a different animal: **full-stack** — React, Node.js, NestJS, REST, PostgreSQL (5+ yrs), 2+ yrs Kafka or streaming, Docker/Kubernetes microservices, AWS exposure — with ownership "of the entire development lifecycle" and collaboration with product on roadmap and customer engagement.

Notable / other: Compensation is stated only on the SWE role and only as a floor. Benefits listed: unlimited PTO, medical/dental/vision, 401(k) with match. The company publishes its stack on StackShare (linked from its own JD).

Compensation (stated on their own JD): Senior Software Engineer — "**Base Salary starting at 145,000**" USD. No range and no figure at all on the Senior DevOps Engineer posting.
