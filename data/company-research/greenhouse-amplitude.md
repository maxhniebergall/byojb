# Amplitude — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:amplitude | company_type: product

What they do: Amplitude is a digital/product analytics platform. Their own words: "Amplitude is the
leading AI analytics platform, helping over 4,700 customers—including Atlassian, Burger King,
NBCUniversal, and Square—build better products and digital experiences. With powerful AI Agents embedded
across our platform, teams can analyze, test, and optimize user experiences faster than ever. Ranked #1
across multiple categories in G2's Winter 2026 Report, Amplitude is the best-in-class solution for
product, data, and marketing teams." The product spans behavioural analytics, experimentation, session
replay and customer data infrastructure.

How they describe themselves: Stated values, verbatim: "As an organization, we deliver for our customers
by living our values. We operate from a place of humility, take ownership of problems and successes,
approach challenges with a growth mindset, and put our customers at the center of everything we do."
They also publish a DEI commitment: "Amplitude believes that diversity enables the creation of better
products, improves the ability to solve complex problems, and drives more powerful solutions. We strive
to create an environment of inclusion—one focused on psychological safety, empathy, and human
connection—that will allow employees of all backgrounds to thrive." The JD describes the team as
"a global and fast-growing team" — the one growth-velocity phrase; otherwise the tone is measured and
engineering-serious, with no hustle vocabulary.

Size / stage / funding: **Public company** (NASDAQ: AMPL), listed via direct listing in 2021.
Roughly 800–1,000 employees. The registry's extracted facets record company_stage: public. Revenue in
the several-hundred-million range with the usual public-SaaS profile — growth moderating, profitability
pressure, but audited financials and no runway risk.

Locations / HQ: San Francisco headquarters. Their own JD lists the office footprint: "We have employees
around the world and offices in San Francisco (HQ), New York, Vancouver, Amsterdam, London, Paris,
Singapore, and Tokyo." Vancouver, BC is a real engineering site — the relevant open role is based there.

Remote policy: Office-anchored hybrid for this role. The Senior Software Engineer (Data Pipeline) JD is
tagged **`#LI-Hybrid`** at the foot of the posting and is located "Vancouver, BC, Canada". The extracted
facets independently record remote_policy: hybrid. Amplitude maintains offices in eight cities and
attaches roles to them; it is not a remote-first company.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Canada yes, remote no.** Amplitude clearly employs in Canada — it runs a
Vancouver office and the posting is a Canadian req — so the employment entity is not the obstacle. But
the role is explicitly hybrid and Vancouver-anchored (`#LI-Hybrid`). Kimberley, BC is roughly 800 km
and a full day's travel from Vancouver, so despite being in the same province this is **not** workable
as a remote-from-Kimberley role. Same-province should not be mistaken for eligibility here. On the
positive side, a Vancouver anchor means Pacific time — only one hour off Mountain, the best timezone
alignment in the batch, if a remote arrangement were ever negotiable.

Engineering & tech: This is the strongest infrastructure content in the batch. From the JD: "Our
infrastructure teams build and operate the backend systems that power Amplitude's products at massive
scale — handling more than 1 trillion events per month. We work on the systems that move, store,
transform, and serve customer data: real-time and batch ingestion, identity resolution, data import and
export connectors, privacy and compliance, and the operational reliability of everything in between."
The role: "you'll take on complex infrastructure challenges: designing for extreme throughput,
optimizing for millisecond latencies, and building resilient systems that run with close to zero
downtime." Stack, verbatim: "primarily Java and Python, running on Kafka, Temporal, DynamoDB, Amazon S3,
Snowflake, Kubernetes, and Terraform." Scope includes technical anchoring: "beyond your own delivery
you'll be a technical anchor for the team: leading design discussions, mentoring engineers, and raising
the bar for how we build." Extracted autonomy: medium.

Notable / other: No salary figure is posted on the Vancouver JD (BC pay-transparency legislation applies
to BC postings, so this is a gap worth noting). Heavy current investment in "AI Agents embedded across
our platform". The broader posting list shows a large, layered engineering org — Cloud Platform, Data
Management, Data Pipeline, Experiment, SDK, Data Warehouse, FinOps, Staff AI Engineer — indicating real
platform/infra specialization rather than generalist product teams.

Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer (Data Pipeline) [Vancouver]; Senior Platform
Engineer (Cloud Platform); Senior Software Engineer, Data Management; Staff Engineer, FinOps;
Staff AI Engineer; Software Engineer II, Data Warehouse.
