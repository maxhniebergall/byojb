What they do: YuJa develops cloud-based video, digital-accessibility and compliance software, primarily for education. Product portfolio from their own site: Lumina Video Platform (all-in-one video with LMS integration), Panorama LMS Accessibility, Verity Test Proctoring (automated and live), EqualGround website-accessibility scanning and governance, CivicGuard document remediation for government entities, plus Lecture Capture, Intellio "AI Knowledge", FeedbackFlow assessment and Engage student response. They state they serve over 1,000 organizations across higher education, K-12, healthcare, finance and government; named customers on the site include the University of Birmingham, Angelo State University, the University of Delaware and various state university systems.
