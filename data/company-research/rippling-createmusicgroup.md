# Create Music Group — research
Provider/key: rippling:createmusicgroup | company_type: product

What they do: Create Music Group is a Los Angeles music and entertainment company founded in 2015 — simultaneously a record label, a digital distribution and rights-management business, and a technology/media platform. It monetises and administers rights across roughly 12–15 billion music streams per month on DSPs (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube etc.) on behalf of artists and labels, and owns a large catalogue of IP. Its current engineering push is **CreateOS**, described as "a next-generation operating system for modern record labels," with AI at the centre.

How they describe themselves: On their own careers portal — "passionate music lovers and technology fanatics" who are "revolutionizing the music industry and how the world hears their favorite songs," inviting people to "join us to be a part of our growing company." No formal published values list.

Size / stage / funding: ~323–400 employees (2026). Privately held, PE/growth-backed: completed a **$450M equity-and-debt raise in March 2026 at a $2.2B valuation**, bringing total funding to ~$615M. Previously ranked #2 on the Inc. 5000 fastest-growing companies list (2020). Revenue-generating and scaled, but firmly in a hyper-growth posture rather than a steady-state one.

Locations / HQ: HQ Hollywood / Los Angeles, CA. Physical-office culture is prominent in employee reviews (workspace amenities, office culture). At least some engineering roles are posted as Canada Remote.

Remote policy: No published remote-work policy. Reviews describe an office-centred LA culture with managers noted as "extremely flexible with working conditions." Individual postings do set remote locations.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes for at least some roles — but thinly evidenced.** Their live "Lead Data Engineer" posting on their own Rippling ATS carries the location **"Canada Remote,"** which is direct first-party evidence that they will employ a Canada-based remote engineer. There is no company-wide statement backing this up, no province named, and no stated timezone expectation — with an LA headquarters, Pacific-time overlap should be assumed (workable from Mountain Time). Treat as promising but confirm at first contact.

Engineering & tech: Building CreateOS, an internal/label-facing operating system — royalty and rights data at very large scale (billions of monthly streams), which is genuinely data-infrastructure-shaped work. ML/AI roles cover forecasting and anomaly detection over music/streaming data, full model lifecycle ownership, and "development of agentic AI systems." Detailed stack is not published.

Notable / other: Glassdoor sentiment is decent — 78% would recommend, 4.1/5 work-life balance, 4.1 culture and values, 4.2 career opportunities.
