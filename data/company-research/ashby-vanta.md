# Vanta — research
Provider/key: ashby:vanta | company_type: product

What they do: Vanta builds a trust management / security-compliance automation platform. Its software continuously monitors a customer's infrastructure and controls, automates the evidence collection and workflows behind SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR and similar frameworks, manages security risk (including third-party/vendor risk), and gives customers a Trust Center to prove their security posture to their own buyers and auditors. Customers are primarily internet/SaaS businesses, from startups through enterprise. Domain is security, GRC, and compliance infrastructure.

How they describe themselves: Mission is to "help businesses earn and prove trust" — building security tools that "help internet businesses get secure and prove their security." Their stated company principles, in their own words:
- **Put customers first** — "center their needs in our work and invest in long-term relationships"
- **Bias for action** — make decisions quickly despite uncertainty; treat most decisions as reversible
- **Win as one team** — prioritize the whole of Vanta over individual achievement
- **Decide with frameworks** — make thoughtful decisions using documented criteria, clearly articulated ("framework thinking")
- **Lead with resilience** — operate effectively amid rapid change and ambiguity
- **Do what it says on the tin** — highest ethical standards; be transparent and accountable
They also emphasize impact, ownership and a growth mindset, and state explicitly that they do not micromanage: "we hire talented people, give them context and autonomy, and trust them to deliver."

Size / stage / funding: Private, venture-backed, late-stage (Series C and beyond) with a multi-billion-dollar valuation; roughly 1,000 employees. Not a pre-revenue startup — a well-capitalized category leader in compliance automation, competing with Drata and Secureframe.

Locations / HQ: HQ San Francisco, USA. Additional offices: New York (USA), Dublin (Ireland), London (UK), Sydney (Australia).

Remote policy: "Remote-first setup if you're in the US, or go hybrid at our international offices." They describe themselves as remote-first with team members across multiple time zones who "communicate asynchronously, document thoroughly, and trust each other to deliver." Benefits include a remote workspace stipend.

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Verified yes.** Multiple live postings in the registry are explicitly located "Remote - Canada", including a Canada-specific req: "Software Engineer, AI Product (Canada)" (Remote - Canada), "Principal Software Engineer, Platform" (Remote - Canada), "Staff Data Engineer" (Remote U.S., Remote - Canada), "Staff Engineer, Third Party Risk Management - Trust" (Remote U.S., Remote - Canada) and "Senior Software Engineer, Trust and Third Party Risk Management" (Remote U.S., Remote - Canada). The last of these splits its posted comp into a tier explicitly titled "US Compensation" with a "US Based Cash Range", confirming a separate non-US (Canadian) pay tier exists. The public careers page text mentions only "US remote / international hybrid" and does not name Canada, so the JDs are the stronger and more current evidence.

Engineering & tech: Engineering is organized into product-and-platform teams with clear domain ownership — visible team names in live reqs include Core Platform, Identity, Developer Experience, Enterprise Resilience, Privacy & Data Security, GRC, Trust / Third Party Risk Management, AI Product, Data, and GTM Engineering. That mix means a genuine platform/infrastructure surface (Core Platform, Identity, DevEx, data engineering) alongside product-facing squads. Compliance-automation work is heavy on continuous integrations with customer cloud accounts and SaaS vendors, evidence pipelines, and multi-tenant data security. There is a distinct AI Product line. The ladder runs Senior → Staff → Principal, and all levels are actively hiring.

Notable / other: Employee sentiment is mixed-positive — reviewers repeatedly describe a talented, smart, humble team and a CEO regarded as thoughtful and principled, but also a meeting-heavy culture with work spilling into nights and weekends; work-life-balance ratings sit around 3.3/5. Benefits: 401(k) with matching, comprehensive medical/dental/vision, paid parental leave, health-and-wellness stipend, generous PTO and holidays, remote workspace stipend, equity on essentially all engineering reqs.

Pay transparency: Vanta posts explicit salary ranges on nearly all of its own reqs (see data/company-comp/ashby-vanta.md). Observed base ranges are strong: Senior ~$195K–$263K USD, Staff $213K–$306K USD, Principal $321K–$378K USD.
