# ZipRecruiter — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:ziprecruiter | company_type: product
What they do: Online employment marketplace connecting job seekers and employers, using AI-driven "smart matching" across mobile, web and email products. Public company (NYSE: ZIP), US-centric marketplace at large scale.
How they describe themselves: Mission stated on every JD — "To actively connect people to their next great opportunity." They describe themselves as "a leading online employment marketplace… powered by AI-driven smart matching technology" connecting "millions of all-sized businesses and job seekers". Tone in the JDs is pragmatic and engineering-substantive rather than hype-driven; the ML JDs describe concrete problems ("given a training set of job postings, and known salaries, build a system that predicts salary for novel jobs") and explicitly note that "while building models is fun, it's also just one step in the process" — i.e. an ML-systems/productionization emphasis, not research.
Size / stage / funding: Publicly traded (NYSE: ZIP); no size/stage data gathered beyond what the JDs state. Not verified in this pass.
Locations / HQ: Santa Monica, CA (most postings are Santa Monica or Remote-US).
Remote policy: Stated verbatim at the top of every JD: "We offer a hybrid work environment. Most US-based positions can also be performed remotely (any exceptions will be noted in the Minimum Qualifications below.)" Several roles carry the #LI-Remote tag.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — not verified as eligible, and the JD language points the other way.** Remote is framed only for "US-based positions", and the pay disclosure says the posted range "reflects the minimum and maximum target for new hire salaries for the position across all US locations." No Canadian entity or Canada-remote language appears anywhere in the six live JDs.
Engineering & tech: Big Data and Machine Learning engineering teams; work includes large-scale data pipelines and production ML (salary prediction, matching/relevance). Senior and Staff IC ladders are both open.
Notable / other: Pay transparency is applied consistently across postings (CA requirement), including a statement that equity, bonus and commission may be layered on top of base.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Big Data; Senior Software Engineer, Machine Learning; Staff Software Engineer, Machine Learning; Software Engineer III.
Sources: the company's own live JDs in the registry — https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/ziprecruiter/jobs/6557032, /5167472, /5167480 (read from locally cached JD bodies; the Greenhouse board itself was not fetched). No first-party ziprecruiter.com page was fetched in this pass.
