# Squarepoint Capital — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:squarepointcapital | company_type: product (quantitative investment manager — engineering is in-house for its own trading, not sold)
What they do: Squarepoint Capital is a global quantitative investment management firm. Its technology organisation builds the in-house data and trading infrastructure the firm trades on: "The Data Infrastructure team manages the service-oriented data storage infrastructure for the firm. We implement microservices that provide high performance solutions to access large data sets required in trading and research." Adjacent teams visible from open roles cover core data, data pipelines, data platform, data products and services, data reliability, market-data integration, order gateway, market access, and risk technology/risk data pipelines. Engineers "work closely with data engineers, data scientists, quantitative researchers and other stakeholders."
How they describe themselves: The JDs are terse and unsentimental — no mission statement, no values list, no culture manifesto; the framing is engineering craft plus stakeholder proximity ("Excellent communication skills, comfortable interacting directly with stakeholders", "Contribute to the overall software architecture", "Participate in code reviews to ensure code quality and consistency"). The one explicit culture signal is a requirement: "Ability to work in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment." No public values/handbook page was fetched.
Size / stage / funding: Established multi-strategy quant fund, privately held, with a large multi-office technology headcount — 9+ live relevant engineering openings in this scan alone. Revenue comes from trading, so it is not runway-dependent, but headcount and AUM were not verified here.
Locations / HQ: Offices referenced across the live postings: London, Montreal, Singapore, Hong Kong, Bangalore, Madrid, Warsaw, and New York (implied HQ, unverified). **Montreal is the only Canadian office** and it appears on nearly every open role.
Remote policy: **Onsite.** The Stage-3 facet extraction classifies these postings as `onsite`, and every posting is enumerated by specific office cities rather than by country or "remote". No remote or hybrid policy is stated anywhere in the JD text.
Remote-Canada eligibility: Canada — yes as an employer, but **only in Montreal, and onsite**. There is no evidence of a remote-Canada hiring path, and quant funds generally require desk presence for trading-adjacent systems. **Effectively disqualifying** for someone in Kimberley, BC who cannot relocate — this should not be treated as Canada-eligible in any useful sense. Montreal is also Eastern Time, two hours off Mountain.
Engineering & tech: Deep systems work, primarily **C++** on Linux — "designing, developing, and maintaining the data infrastructure components that support our data processing and analytics pipelines", optimizing "microservices for performance and scalability". Named technologies: microservices and asynchronous multithreaded applications, Boost ASIO, Apache Arrow / Parquet, Google Protocol Buffers, Flatbuffers, gRPC, HTTP/REST and WebSocket, SQL databases, Google Cloud and AWS, Docker/Kubernetes, highly-available distributed systems and large datasets. Also in the stack across sibling teams: Python, Rust, and KDB+/Q (a dedicated "Data Services (KDB+/Q)" role is open). Bar is "Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related subject" plus "4+ years of professional software engineering experience".
Notable / other: This is close to a pure infrastructure/plumbing shop — internal customers (researchers and traders), well-scoped systems, and no external product pressure, which is unusual and appealing on paper. The blocker is entirely geographic. Postings state no salary; the firm applies for roles via its own site (squarepoint-capital.com/open-opportunities) fronted by Greenhouse.
