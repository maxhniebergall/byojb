# Mark43 — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:mark43 | company_type: product

What they do: Mark43 builds cloud-native public-safety software for law enforcement and emergency
response — an integrated Records Management System (RMS), Computer-Aided Dispatch (CAD), analytics,
and a federated "Universal Search" data-integration layer. Customers are police departments and
government agencies: Boston PD, Seattle PD, San Antonio PD, the U.S. Secret Service, U.S. Capitol
Police, the Defense Intelligence Agency, the U.S. Department of the Interior, and UK forces such as
Cumbria Constabulary. FedRAMP-authorized; sells to federal as well as municipal buyers.

How they describe themselves: Their vision is "to revolutionize the world of public safety by
building the software communities deserve," framed on their site as "Sworn to Save" and setting "a
new standard in public safety." Three stated focus areas: **Connect Communities** (be the
"operational backbone" for agencies), **Save Lives** (interdepartmental data sharing for emergency
response), **Stop Crime** (real-time analytics and accountability dashboards). Their JDs repeatedly
frame engineering work as "support[ing] life-saving decision making for our public safety users."
On pay they state a value directly: "We believe in equal pay for equal work, and transparency in
compensation is a core value" — and every JD carries a posted range.

Size / stage / funding: Founded 2012 by Scott Crouch, Matt Polega and Florian Mayr. Private,
venture-backed, late-stage (secondary shares trade on pre-IPO marketplaces such as EquityZen).
Described as "a global team"; no public headcount on their own site. Government/municipal contract
revenue — a long-cycle, sticky, recession-resistant customer base rather than burn-driven growth.

Locations / HQ: HQ New York City. Additional engineering presence in Boston, MA (the CAD team) and
a UK/international footprint.

Remote policy: Remote-friendly with team-level exceptions. Most engineering postings are listed
"Remote", "Remote, New York" or "Remote, New York City". A technology and remote-work stipend is
offered. Not uniformly remote: the CAD engineering team "is based in Boston, MA and operates on a
3 day hybrid schedule," and the "Lead Software Engineer - CAD" posting states "Candidates must be
located in the United States."

Remote-Canada eligibility: **YES — verified, first-party.** Every Mark43 JD carries the boilerplate:
"Mark43 is approved to hire in Canada, the UK, and 36 U.S. states…" (their careers page currently
says 40 states). Canada is an approved hiring country at the company level. Caveat: individual
roles can be narrower — the CAD role is US-only and Boston-hybrid, so it is not Canada-eligible
despite the company-level approval. The Remote-listed Universal Search, Data Engineering, DevOps
and Staff Engineer roles are the Canada-viable ones. Working hours are US Eastern-centric (NYC HQ),
which is a 2-hour offset from Mountain Time.

Engineering & tech: Distributed systems work with real substance — real-time data indexing,
authorization-aware retrieval, search reliability, and service-boundary architecture across RMS,
CAD and analytics. A dedicated "Universal Search" platform domain (3rd-party search and data
integration), a DevEx/DevOps team, a data-engineering function, and an Enterprise AI team. Security
is a first-class concern: all employees must follow security policies, protect credentials, and
report incidents — expected for a FedRAMP/CJIS-adjacent vendor.

Notable / other: Recent marquee win — the U.S. Department of the Interior launched an enterprise
public-safety platform on Mark43 (with Simple Technology Solutions) in a landmark federal
deployment. Domain is law-enforcement technology, which some candidates weigh on ethical grounds.

Open relevant roles (sample): Lead Software Engineer - Universal Search (Remote), Senior Software
Engineer - Universal Search (Remote), Lead Data Engineer (Remote / NYC), DevOps Engineer, DevEx
(New York, Remote), Staff Engineer (Remote, New York), Senior Software Engineer - Enterprise AI
(Boston), Lead Software Engineer - CAD (Boston, hybrid, US-only).

Posted pay (their own JDs, USD base): Lead Data Engineer $190,000–$220,000; Staff Engineer
$180,000–$240,000; Lead SWE - CAD $170,000–$210,000; Senior Software Engineer $155,000–$215,000;
DevOps Engineer, DevEx $140,000–$170,000. All plus bonus, stock options and benefits; they note the
top of the range is reserved for the New York metro and Bay Area.
