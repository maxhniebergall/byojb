# Speechify — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:speechify | company_type: product
What they do: Speechify builds text-to-speech and voice-AI products. Their own mission statement: "Speechify is the easiest way to listen to the world's information. Articles on the web, documents in the cloud, books on your phone. We absorb it all and let you listen to it at your desk, on the go, at your own speed, and with tools that make learning easier, deeper, and faster." The product suite spans read-aloud apps across platforms, voice-typing dictation, AI voice generation, dubbing, voice cloning, and AI work-delegation tooling. They frame the category by analogy: "What streaming services have done for audio entertainment, we're doing for audio information."
How they describe themselves: Founder-narrative-driven and accessibility-focused. Founder Cliff Weitzman frames the ethos as "Your mission is to be who you needed most when you were growing up", grounded in his own dyslexia — "I took full responsibility for my own accommodations and education" — and claims that "millions of people have been able to function in school and society because of Speechify." Growth framing is prominent and unhedged: "We're #1 in our category, and experiencing exponential growth."
