# Equinix — research
Provider/key: workday:equinix/external | company_type: product

What they do: Equinix is "the world's digital infrastructure company®" — the largest global operator of carrier-neutral colocation data centres and interconnection (IBX facilities, Equinix Fabric, Network Edge, Metal). It is the physical and network substrate that enterprises, clouds and networks plug into. Publicly traded on NASDAQ (EQIX) and structured as a **REIT**; roughly 13,000 employees across 30+ countries. Alongside the core data-centre business it runs a corporate/product software organisation building the platform, networking and now agentic-AI systems that operate the business.

How they describe themselves: From their own JD copy: "Equinix is the world's digital infrastructure company®, shortening the path to connectivity to enable the innovations that enrich our work, life and planet. A place where tech thinkers and future builders turn bold ideas into breakthrough experiences, we welcome your unique perspective. Help us challenge assumptions, uncover bias, and remove barriers—because progress starts with fresh ideas. You'll find belonging, purpose, and a team that welcomes you—because when you feel valued, you're empowered to do your best work." Their stated corporate values are **competitive, inclusive, sustainable, connected and efficient**. Equinix is an Equal Employment Opportunity and (in the US) Affirmative Action employer, and publicly discloses that it uses artificial intelligence in its hiring process.

Size / stage / funding: Public (NASDAQ: EQIX), REIT structure, ~13,000 employees. Mature, dividend-paying, capital-intensive infrastructure business — the opposite of a runway-dependent startup.

Locations / HQ: HQ Redwood City, California. Global footprint; the reqs in this batch are anchored to **Toronto (Office TRO)**, Dallas Infomart, Redwood City, Madrid, London, Warsaw and a **"Remote Location – Canada"** posting location.

Remote policy: Not published as a single policy statement on a page reachable without a 403 (equinix.com/careers/working-at-equinix blocks automated fetches). Their reqs advertise a mix of remote, flexible-hours and hybrid arrangements. Most Canadian engineering reqs in this batch are anchored to the **Toronto office**, but Equinix's Workday board also carries an explicit **"Remote Location – Canada"** location, used for e.g. Senior Staff Engineer, Product Software (JR-160180).

Remote-Canada eligibility: **Yes as an employer, with a geographic caveat.** Equinix plainly employs Canadians and posts CAD-denominated ranges for Canadian roles; it also maintains a literal "Remote Location – Canada" posting location, which is direct evidence that fully-remote Canadian employment exists. However the majority of the AI/ML and platform reqs here are pinned to **Toronto Office TRO**, which is Eastern Time and, for a Toronto-anchored req, likely expects proximity to that office. A Toronto-office-anchored posting is effectively out of reach from Kimberley BC; the "Remote Location – Canada" reqs are the ones that actually work. Timezone note: Toronto-anchored teams run on Eastern, two hours ahead of Mountain.

Engineering & tech: The standout work is **Equinix Incubation**, which builds "the agentic systems that run the software delivery lifecycle end to end: from intake and business case through design, code, test, release, and value tracking, with humans directing the work and owning every gate." The Staff / Senior Staff AI Agent Engineering charter is unusually specific and infrastructure-shaped: design and ship LLM-powered production agents; engineer the scaffolding — **tool use via MCP, agent-to-agent handoffs (A2A), event-driven orchestration**, deep Jira and enterprise-system integration; build on Equinix's enterprise **AI gateway, orchestration, audit and access control**; design and automate **eval suites** and make passing evals the release gate; define **guardrails, human-in-the-loop approval points and escalation paths**; instrument agent quality, latency, cost and adoption; build **retrieval layers** over process libraries, decision histories, code and delivery data; establish reusable prompt patterns and context standards; contribute to the orchestrator and dashboards. It explicitly insists on classical craft: "The fundamentals still decide whether this works: architecture, code quality, testing, CI/CD, and cloud-native design." Beyond that the org runs product software, network platform, software-defined dataplane, enterprise networking architecture and DevSecOps platform lines.

Notable / other: Canadian benefits disclosed on reqs include a Defined Contribution Pension Plan (DCPP), Group RRSP and TFSA, plus vacation and paid holidays. Accessibility accommodation process published.

Open relevant roles (sample): Staff / Senior Staff Engineer, AI Agent Engineering (Toronto; also London); Principal Machine Learning Engineer (MLE) (Toronto); Principal Software Engineer (Toronto); Senior Machine Learning Engineer (Toronto); Senior Staff Engineer, Software Defined Dataplane (Remote Location – Canada); Sr Principal Engineer, Enterprise Networking Architecture; AI DevOps Engineer.
