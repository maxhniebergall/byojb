ENTITY NOTE / DATA QUALITY: the registry stores this company as name "Sec". That is the Workday tenant
slug (`sec.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/samsung_careers`), not a company name. The JD bodies confirm the

specifically the Samsung Austin Research and Development Center (SARC) and the Advanced Computing Lab
(ACL). The display name should be corrected to "Samsung (SEC)" or similar.

What they do: Semiconductor and silicon IP. In their own words: "Samsung, a world leader in advanced
semiconductor technology, is founded on a simple philosophy - the endless pursuit of excellence will
create a better world for all. At Samsung Austin Research and Development Center (SARC) and Advanced
Computing Lab (ACL), we are building a center of excellence for Intellectual Property (IP) that is
applied to high-performance computing devices (mobile, automotive, and other custom market segments)
consumed by millions of people around the world. Come build with us!" The GPU work is concrete:
"The GPU Architect team delivers whole system architecture-level design for Samsung's current and
next-generation mobile GPUs... Our Xclipse GPU is the first mobile GPU with ray tracing technology that
enables console-level graphics for Samsung Galaxy smartphones."

How they describe themselves: Mission language is "the endless pursuit of excellence"; team language
emphasises architecture-level ownership, collaboration across hardware/software/architecture teams, and
"the ability to navigate ambiguity in a fast-paced, global team environment" (their words — a mild
pace/red flag). Work is framed as deep, long-horizon silicon R&D rather than product iteration.

Size / stage / funding: Part of Samsung Electronics, a very large publicly traded multinational. No
runway risk. Specific SARC/ACL headcount not stated in the material available.

Locations / HQ: Registry postings are at 3655 N 1st St, San Jose, CA; 3900 N Capital of Texas Hwy,
Austin, TX; One Pennsylvania Plaza, New York, NY; and 565 Great Northern Way, Vancouver, Canada. The
North-American semiconductor org is anchored in San Jose and Austin.
