# MaintainX — research
Provider/key: ashby:maintainx | company_type: product
What they do: MaintainX sells a mobile-first CMMS / "Asset and Work Intelligence" platform for industrial and frontline workers — maintenance, repair and operations (MRO) work orders, safety procedures, asset management and IoT-connected equipment monitoring. In their words: "Our mission is to deliver one platform for maintenance, repair & operations teams to keep the physical world running." Customers are large industrials and retailers: "MaintainX powers operational excellence for 12,000+ businesses, including Duracell, Univar Solutions Inc., Titan America, McDonald's, Brenntag, Cintas, Xylem, and Shell" (other JDs say "14,000+ customers", so their own numbers are inconsistent across postings).
How they describe themselves: The published values are Accountability — "Entitled to nothing, grateful for everything"; Quality — "Excellence is our standard", framed with Olympic-level performance expectations; Teamwork — "Collaboration over ego"; and Adaptability — "Embrace challenges, find opportunity", about moving quickly and staying optimistic through pivots. The careers page describes a "high-growth, high-trust" environment where teams "stay flat, lean, humble", made of "builders who aren't afraid to roll up their sleeves", offering "real ownership" and "stretch opportunities". JDs describe the culture as "Smart Humble Optimists" and say "We believe in meritocracy, where ideas and effort are publicly celebrated." Every JD opens with "We're growing fast and hiring the talent to match."
Size / stage / funding: Series D, well capitalized, high growth. "We recently completed a $150 million Series D funding round, bringing our total funding to $254 million and valuing the company at $2.5 billion", led by Bessemer Venture Partners and Bain Capital Ventures. Named to the Forbes 2025 Cloud 100. Engineering org size is stated indirectly: the internal developer platform has "internal customers (150+ developers)" and is planning for growth "from where we are now to 2-3x the size". Profitability not disclosed.
Locations / HQ: Montreal is the primary engineering hub, with Toronto also used; the company operates globally ("folks across the globe") and has US operations (401(k) offered alongside RRSP). Live engineering reqs are tagged Montreal, Toronto, "Montreal, Toronto", or simply "Canada".
Remote policy: "Hybrid work" is what the careers page lists under flexibility. No remote-first claim is made anywhere, and most engineering reqs carry a specific city tag. Async or documentation-culture signals were not found.
Remote-Canada eligibility: PARTIAL / UNCLEAR — this needs verifying before applying. Canadian employment is certain (RRSP program, Montreal-headquartered engineering, bilingual postings such as "Gestionnaire du développement logiciel, DevOps"). But the company describes itself as hybrid and nearly every relevant req names Montreal and/or Toronto. The one exception in the registry is "Senior Software Developer, API Platform and Ecosystem", whose location is simply "Canada" with no city — the only posting that plausibly supports remote-from-BC. Note the JD bodies themselves contain no remote-policy language at all, so this reading comes from the location fields, not from their words. Treat Montreal/Toronto-tagged reqs as effectively disqualifying (in-office or near-office) unless they say otherwise.
Engineering & tech: A real, well-resourced platform organization — this is the standout technical signal. The Backend Platform team sits "within our Platform Engineering division" with "an unusual mandate inside MaintainX: we treat our internal developer platform as a real product, with internal customers (150+ developers), adoption metrics, and a roadmap shaped by what product teams actually need. We are not an ops team." Its charter is golden paths: "spinning up a new backend service, getting it to production, and operating it involves too many manual steps, too much tribal knowledge... We want golden paths that make the right thing the easy thing" — developer portal, local development, domain-service templates and scaffolders, GraphQL, event-driven and OpenAPI golden paths, CI/CD workflows. The API Platform team owns "public-facing REST APIs" and platform semantics: "authentication/authorization, rate limiting, versioning, filtering, pagination, idempotency, error semantics, and backwards compatibility", bulk/batch operations for enterprise data sync, contract testing to decouple REST from the GraphQL monolith, SDKs and developer documentation, plus an integrations marketplace (Workato, Tray) and ERP connectors (SAP S/4HANA and BTP, Oracle EBS/NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics D365). The SRE role is an enablement function: SLOs, error budgets, incident management, observability standards, "Enable development teams to become autonomous with their service deployment, support, and infrastructure", explicitly measuring "success by reduced dependency on you". Languages and tools: TypeScript/Node.js primarily, with Go and C# mentioned; GraphQL at scale; MySQL/PostgreSQL; cloud-native infrastructure-as-code; CI/CD. Production ownership is expected — "monitoring, on-call, incident response, root-cause analysis". Strong stated AI emphasis ("Bring AI native and add on workflows into your daily work and into the platform", "Strong AI mindset"), and a separate Applied ML track (Senior Applied Machine Learning Engineer, Asset Intelligence).
Notable / other: No salary figure appears on any of the ten JD bodies captured — all say only "Competitive salary and meaningful equity opportunities." Benefits: healthcare/dental/vision, 401(k)/RRSP, "Take what you need PTO". Seniority bars are moderate: 4+ years for the API platform role ("seniority flexible based on impact and scope"), 5+ years distributed systems for backend platform, 3-5+ years for SRE.
