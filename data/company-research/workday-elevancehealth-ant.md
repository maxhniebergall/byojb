**What they do:** Elevance Health is a very large US health-benefits company — health plans and insurance services (the former Anthem, Inc.), operating Blue Cross Blue Shield plans across many US states plus pharmacy and care-delivery businesses. Their careers site frames the business as "improving healthcare accessibility and outcomes through technology and human-centered solutions."

**How they describe themselves:** Stated purpose is "improving the health of humanity", with the line that "every role connects to our purpose." The culture language on the careers site emphasizes **"collaboration, inclusivity, and continuous learning"**, employee voice, community impact, and professional development. It is corporate-enterprise in tone — no hustle/grind language, but also no engineering-specific handbook, async-work statement, or engineering blog surfaced from the careers landing page.

**Size / stage / funding:** Public company (NYSE: ELV), Fortune-top-tier US health insurer serving tens of millions of members. Not stated on the careers page; this is general knowledge and the specific figures here are **unverified from a first-party source in this pass**. What the careers site does show is scale of hiring: "255+ open positions" across many US states.

**Locations / HQ:** US only. HQ Indianapolis, Indiana. Careers site lists major presence in Georgia (Atlanta — 77 positions), Florida (73), Colorado (Denver), California, Connecticut, Indiana (Indianapolis) and Virginia. **"No international locations are mentioned on this careers page."**

**Remote policy:** Not stated explicitly for most roles on the careers landing page. The AI/engineering postings in the registry are all anchored to physical US office addresses (e.g. "GA-ATLANTA-740 W PEACHTREE ST NW", "VA-RICHMOND-2015 STAPLES MILL RD", "IN-INDIANAPOLIS-220 VIRGINIA AVE") with multi-site tags ("7 Locations", "13 Locations", "17 Locations"), which is the signature of hybrid/office-anchored hiring across a US site network rather than location-agnostic remote.

**Remote-Canada eligibility: No.** All operations and all listed hiring locations are within the United States; no international locations appear on the careers site and no posting mentions Canada. A US health insurer's engineering roles are also subject to US healthcare data/PHI handling constraints. Treat as disqualifying.

**Engineering & tech:** Not verifiable in detail — none of the six registry postings had a fetchable JD body (Workday job pages are client-rendered), so the technical content is unknown beyond the titles. The AI job family is clearly a build-out: "Gen AI Engineer", "AI-Native Software Engineer", "AI-Native Software Engineer Advisor Senior", "Distinguished AI Engineer" (a principal-level ladder rung, indicating a formal engineering ladder), and a "Staff Vice President (VP), Agentic AI" leadership post — i.e. a funded, executive-sponsored agentic-AI program rather than a one-team experiment. A "Senior Data Engineer" role across 17 locations was live in late July and has since closed.

**Notable / other:** The presence of a Distinguished-engineer rung and a VP-level agentic-AI mandate suggests the AI work has real institutional weight and budget. Healthcare payer is exactly the kind of "boring, mission-critical, regulated" domain that tends to be stable and low-chaos. Compensation is not posted on any of these listings, though as a large US employer it would post ranges in transparency states (CO, NY, WA, CA, IL) — those listings were not reachable in this pass.
