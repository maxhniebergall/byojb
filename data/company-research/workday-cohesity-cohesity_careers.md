# Cohesity — research
Provider/key: workday:cohesity/cohesity_careers | company_type: product
What they do: Enterprise data protection / backup & recovery and "AI-powered data security". Its platform (Cohesity Data Platform, SpanFS — a scale-out filesystem combining NFS, SMB and S3 interfaces with global dedup, unlimited snaps/clones) protects data on-premises, in cloud, and at the edge. In 2024 it combined with Veritas' enterprise data protection business. Customers are large enterprises: its own JD text claims "over 13,600 enterprise customers, including over 85 of the Fortune 100 and nearly 70% of the Global 500"; its site says 12,000+ customers, 200+ exabytes protected, 1,700 patents.
How they describe themselves: "We protect, secure, and provide insights into the world's data." Leadership framing on the company page: "As cyberthreats rise, our mission is to protect, secure, and unlock the value of the world's data. We operate as One Team—driven by bold innovation, speed, and a culture rooted in belonging, accountability, and impact." JDs are self-congratulatory ("the leader in AI-powered data security", "named a Leader by multiple analyst firms") and use mild hustle framing ("passionate and driven", "speed").
Size / stage / funding: Large late-stage private company, post-Veritas-DPB acquisition; leadership drawn from Google, Nutanix, VMware, Palo Alto Networks, Splunk, Cisco. Strategic investors named in its own JD boilerplate: NVIDIA, IBM, HPE, Cisco, AWS, Google Cloud. Not profitable/public status not stated on the pages fetched — unverified. An IPO has been widely anticipated but I could not verify that from a first-party source in this session.
Locations / HQ: HQ Santa Clara, CA; "offices around the globe". Live engineering postings in this registry are Pune and Bangalore, India.
Remote policy: Not stated on the company page. Both live relevant JDs are tagged to physical India offices ("Pune - Panchshil - India (Office)", "Bangalore - India (Office)"), i.e. office-attached roles.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No / not evidenced.** Both live relevant postings are India-office roles; nothing on the fetched pages offers remote hiring in Canada. Treat as ineligible unless a Canada/remote req appears later.
Engineering & tech: Deep systems work — C++, distributed storage, distributed filesystems, consensus protocols, dedup/compression/replication, object storage, cloud services, async programming, performance optimization. Staff role asks 10+ years in infrastructure/backend systems at scale. Genuinely infra-flavoured, well-scoped specialist engineering. A second team ("Data Protect Product Engineering") is more customer-experience oriented — "beyond writing code", ease of sale, deployment and maintenance.
Notable / other: Formed from Cohesity + Veritas enterprise data protection; integration of two large product lines is the dominant engineering context. No pay-transparency statement found; no CO/NY/WA/CA-jurisdiction live JD in the registry to read a range from.
Open relevant roles (sample): Staff Software Engineer: C++, Distributed Storage, filesystems & cloud skills (Pune); Senior Software Engineer (Bangalore); other historical titles: Security Software Engineer, Senior Performance Engineer (Data Protection & Security Platform Engineering), Senior IT Software Engineer.
