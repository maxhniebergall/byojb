# Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE) — research
Provider/key: workday:hpe/wfmathpe | company_type: product
NOTE ON DUPLICATE KEYS: HPE appears in the registry three times — `workday:hpe/acjobsite`, `workday:hpe/jobsathpe` and `workday:hpe/wfmathpe` — which are three Workday site tenants publishing the **same** postings (identical titles, identical requisition numbers with different suffixes, identical bodies). They are one company, not three. This dossier applies to all three.
What they do: HPE is a global enterprise IT vendor — servers, storage, networking (Aruba/Juniper), HPC/supercomputing, and hybrid/private cloud software. Their self-description: "Hewlett Packard Enterprise is the global edge-to-cloud company advancing the way people live and work. We help companies connect, protect, analyze, and act on their data and applications wherever they live, from edge to cloud, so they can turn insights into outcomes at the speed required to thrive in today's complex world." The engineering roles in the registry sit in **Private Cloud Engineering**, specifically the **HPE Morpheus** hybrid-cloud management platform and **VM Essentials** — HPE's KVM-based virtualization and CloudOps software portfolio, which is their play for VMware-displacement workloads.
How they describe themselves: "Our culture thrives on finding new and better ways to accelerate what's next. We know varied backgrounds are valued and succeed here. **We have the flexibility to manage our work and personal needs.** We make bold moves, together, and are a force for good. If you are looking to stretch and grow your career our culture will embrace you. Open up opportunities with HPE." And repeatedly: "HPE is always seeking to be a force for good and push for better!" The tone is corporate-optimistic rather than hustle-driven, though one DevOps posting does state the role "requires a hands-on technical leader who thrives in a fast-paced environment."
Size / stage / funding: Very large, publicly traded (NYSE: HPE), tens of thousands of employees globally, decades-old (the enterprise half of the 2015 Hewlett-Packard split). Financially stable; not runway-dependent. Exact current headcount/revenue not verified in this pass. Worth noting HPE has run periodic cost-reduction and restructuring programs over the years — normal for a company of this type but a stability caveat at the team level rather than the company level.
Locations / HQ: HQ in Spring, Texas. The postings in the registry are concentrated in **Ft. Collins, Colorado** (Morpheus / private-cloud engineering) and **San Juan, Puerto Rico** (AI software development), with pay bands also referencing North Carolina and Texas — so those are additional engineering sites for these roles. HPE operates globally.
Remote policy: **Hybrid, mandated.** Every relevant JD carries the same line: "This role has been designed as 'Hybrid' with an expectation that you will work on average 2 days per week from an HPE office." That is a hard requirement attached to the specific office in the posting, not a preference.
Remote-Canada eligibility: **No — verified negative for these roles.** Two days per week in a named HPE office (Ft. Collins CO, or San Juan PR) is required by the JD text itself, and the posted pay bands are indexed to Colorado, North Carolina and Texas. HPE as a corporation does have a Canadian presence, but none of the live postings in the registry is Canada-based or remote-eligible.
Engineering & tech: Two clusters. (1) **Private cloud / virtualization platform (Morpheus, VM Essentials)** — Java, Groovy, Ruby and/or Python; Linux; backend development, RESTful APIs, server-side logic, SQL and NoSQL databases; expert-level core compute, storage and networking; KVM-based virtualization and the surrounding ecosystem — automation, observability, software-defined networking (SDN) and hyperconverged infrastructure (HCI) storage; Kubernetes platforms; enterprise-scale infrastructure automation and operational excellence via automation, observability and cloud-native technology. (2) **AI engineering** — end-to-end delivery of AI features: LLM integrations, RAG pipelines, agents, tool/function calling and prompt strategies; fine-tuning with evaluation harnesses and metrics; A/B testing tracking accuracy, latency, robustness and task success; error analysis and feedback-efficacy loops; ETL/ELT pipelines with dataset curation, metadata, lineage and validation; vector indexing (chunking, embeddings, reranking, chunk-size/overlap tuning); data governance including PII handling, redaction, consent and auditability; MLOps — Docker containerization, Kubernetes/Helm orchestration, CI/CD for ML (train → evaluate → package → deploy → monitor → rollback), model/agent registries and experiment tracking. Networking knowledge is called out as important for the AI roles, reflecting the infrastructure context.
Notable / other: **Pay is posted under Colorado transparency law** and is broad: Principal-level (HPE Morpheus Enterprise Principal Software Engineer, Technical Cloud Architect) "Annual Salary USD 160,000 - 303,000 in Colorado // 152,000 - 349,000 in North Carolina & Texas"; Senior-level (Morpheus Senior Software Engineer, Senior DevOps Engineer) "USD 144,000 - 273,000 in Colorado // 137,000 - 315,000 in North Carolina [& Texas]." HPE adds: "The listed salary range reflects base salary. Variable incentives may also be offered," and "Actual offer may vary from this range based upon geographic location, work experience, education/training, and/or skill level." One caveat on role mix: the "Technical Cloud Architect (Virtualization & Cloud Infrastructure)" role — despite the title and the platform_infra classification — is explicitly **customer-facing pre-sales**: "a trusted technical advisor to our largest strategic customers ... a customer-facing, high-impact role that sits at the intersection of pre-sales engineering, solutions architecture, and product strategy," with time spent in "technical whiteboard sessions, hands-on proof of concepts, and writing the design guidance." Several of the pure engineering roles also mention assisting on customer issues and collaborating with sales engineering.
