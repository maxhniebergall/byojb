# Braze — research
Provider/key: greenhouse:braze | company_type: product
What they do: Braze is a customer-engagement / marketing-automation platform (public company, NASDAQ: BRZE). It describes itself as "the leading customer engagement platform" with the tagline "Be Absolutely Engaging.™", bringing "all your data, channels, and orchestration needs in one place" — data activation, AI-powered tooling and cross-channel messaging for industries including retail, financial services and gaming. Product surfaces in the open roles include Content Cards (in-product messaging: message definition, composition, targeting, delivery semantics and analytics, plus a dashboard for brands and UX teams), reporting, and "BrazeAI Operator".
How they describe themselves: "At Braze, we have found our people. We're a genuinely approachable, exceptionally kind, and intensely passionate crew. We seek to ignite that passion by setting high standards, championing teamwork, and creating work-life harmony as we collectively navigate rapid growth on a global scale while striving for greater equity and opportunity — inside and outside our organization. To flourish here, you must be prepared to set a high bar for yourself and those around you. There is always a way to contribute: Acting with autonomy, having accountability and being open to new perspectives are essential to our continued success. Our deep curiosity to learn and our eagerness to share diverse passions with others gives us balance and injects a one-of-a-kind vibrancy into our culture. If you are driven to solve exhilarating challenges and have a bias toward action in the face of change, you will be empowered to make a real impact here." Careers-page line: "Find your people. Make real impact."
Size / stage / funding: Public, post-IPO, ~1,500+ employees globally (headcount not verified from a first-party page here); "rapid growth on a global scale" in their own words. Not runway-dependent.
Locations / HQ: HQ New York City. Offices listed on the JDs: "Austin, Berlin, Bucharest, Chicago, Dubai, Jakarta, London, Paris, San Francisco, São Paulo, Singapore, Seoul, Sydney and Tokyo" — note this enumeration does not include Toronto, yet the roles in the registry are posted in Toronto with Ontario pay-transparency ranges, so a Canadian entity clearly exists.
Remote policy: **Hybrid.** The JDs say "From offering comprehensive benefits to fostering hybrid ways of working, we've got you covered so you can prioritize work-life harmony", and separately reference an "in-office employee experience, designed to foster community, team connections, and innovation". No specific days-per-week figure was found on the pages fetched (braze.com/company/careers and /careers/how-we-hire both returned only marketing copy).
Remote-Canada eligibility: **Employs in Ontario (Canada) but the model is hybrid and the roles are Toronto-located.** Ontario pay-transparency ranges are quoted on all three postings, so a Canadian employing entity exists; however every relevant posting is Toronto and the company's own language is hybrid/in-office. There is no published evidence of a fully-remote BC/Mountain-Time arrangement — treat as not eligible unless confirmed with a recruiter.
Engineering & tech: The Content Cards team "build[s] the systems that power in-app engagement across customers' websites and applications — covering message definition, composition, targeting, delivery semantics, and analytics", plus the customer-facing dashboard. They ask for 7+ years of **full-stack** development, technical leadership "driving complex projects from ambiguity to delivery", and strong system design; bonus points for "building software at immense scale, especially customer-facing platforms with high reliability requirements" and for Ruby, Sidekiq, TypeScript/JavaScript and React. So: Ruby/Sidekiq backend with a React/TypeScript front end. Other open roles cover site reliability engineering, platform infrastructure at Senior Staff level, ML, and reporting.
Notable / other: Compensation is unusually transparent and high for Ontario — Content Cards senior: CA$153,815-277,431 base with OTE CA$172,000-308,400; the ML and Reporting "Senior Software Engineer I" roles: CA$153,815-250,039 base, OTE CA$172,000-279,600. All include RSU equity grants ("so that you will own a piece of our company"), a retirement plan and an Employee Stock Purchase Plan; benefits vary by country and are documented at braze.com/company/careers/how-we-hire#benefit-section.
Open relevant roles (sample): Senior Software Engineer, Content Cards (Toronto); Senior Software Engineer I, ML (Toronto); Senior Software Engineer I, Reporting (Toronto); Senior Site Reliability Engineer; Senior Staff Platform Infrastructure Engineer.
